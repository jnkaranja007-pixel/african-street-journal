Equatorial Guinea Senate passes energy law reform

The new law modernizes the energy sector, enhances environmental protection, and boosts local content.

Source: Ahora EG · 2026-10-29 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Equatorial Guinea's Senate approved a reform of the Hydrocarbons Law on October 28, 2026, to modernize the national energy sector and enhance environmental protection. This reform, alongside the recent Mining Law, updates the regulatory framework for extractive industries, aligning with the PNDES-Horizonte 2035 economic diversification plan.

Minister Delegate Domingo Mba Esono outlined five strategic directives: flexible contracting models, industrial natural gas use, legal stability for foreign investors, modulated royalties based on field specifics, and reinforced national content requirements for local employment and technology transfer. A National Environmental Monitoring Fund will oversee ecological impact and field abandonment financing.

The reform received support from the PDGE parliamentary group, which highlighted the hydrocarbon sector's role in national development, and the Coalition group, which urged balanced fiscal competitiveness and local technical training. The government committed to submitting model contracts to the Senate for review and holding voluntary periodic appearances to report on investments, contracts, and national employment.

Why it mattersThe reform aims to attract foreign investment and ensure local participation in the energy sector.
