Canal Sol launches UrekaTV to help local creators monetise work

The platform brings films, series, documentaries and digital productions together for audiences in Equatorial Guinea and abroad.

Source: Ahora EG · 2026-11-22 · 1 min read

Canal Sol has launched UrekaTV, a web platform for films, series, documentaries and digital productions, aimed at viewers in Equatorial Guinea and overseas. The service is already operating, the company said.

Virginia Esther Mayé Mbá, Canal Sol’s general director, said UrekaTV is intended to showcase Equatorial Guinean creativity internationally and help creators move from building followers to earning money from their work. The platform includes a dedicated section for the domestic creative sector, where creators’ work is often limited to occasional advertising commissions for companies and brands.

Canal Sol said its audiovisual complex, Sol Estudio, provides technical and creative support for national production. Creators who attended the launch welcomed the initiative.

Why it mattersEquatorial Guinean creators have a dedicated platform section intended to help them earn from projects beyond occasional advertising commissions.
