fire kills six in malabo family home

Salvador Nsue Nguema lost his wife, three children, and two grandchildren in the blaze.

Source: Ahora EG · 2026-10-23 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

A fire in the Semu-Salomé neighborhood of Malabo killed six members of Salvador Nsue Nguema's family on October 22, 2026. The victims included Nguema's wife, three of his children, and two grandchildren. Nguema himself survived but was hospitalized with burns.

The family had been using candles for light after an electricity outage, and investigators believe a candle may have started the fire. Nguema stated that security bars on the windows and doors hindered rescue efforts. The blaze also destroyed all the family's belongings, prompting Nguema to seek assistance.

This tragedy echoes a similar incident months prior in Malabo, where Cosme Nguema Ondo lost his pregnant wife and six children when a retaining wall collapsed on their home. Both Nguema and Ondo originate from Nkahale Yebiveñ, a village in the Añisok district, linking these two family disasters.

Why it mattersSalvador Nsue Nguema and his surviving family members lost loved ones and property in a fire.
