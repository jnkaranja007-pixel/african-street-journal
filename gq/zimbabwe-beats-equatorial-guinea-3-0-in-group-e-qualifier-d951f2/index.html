Zimbabwe beats Equatorial Guinea 3-0 in Group E qualifier

The result leaves Nzalang Nacional under pressure in the race for places at the 2027 Africa Cup of Nations, after the teams had drawn 0-0.

Sources: Ahora EG and Real Equatorial Guinea · 2026-11-18 · 1 min read

Zimbabwe beat Equatorial Guinea 3-0 in Harare on Monday in the fourth round of Group E qualifying for the 2027 Africa Cup of Nations. The defeat leaves Equatorial Guinea needing results in its remaining matches to keep its qualification hopes alive.

Thandolwenkosi Ngwenya put the hosts ahead in the fourth minute at the National Sports Stadium. Equatorial Guinea created chances through Cristian Makaté, Santiago Eneme and Rony Mbomio, but Zimbabwe goalkeeper Nelson Chadya made important first-half saves, Ahora EG reported.

Before the match, Equatorial Guinea and Zimbabwe each had four points, while the Democratic Republic of Congo led the group with nine. The sides had drawn 0-0 on the 12th of the month; the tournament’s final stage is scheduled for Kenya, Tanzania and Uganda.

Why it mattersEquatorial Guinea must respond in its remaining Group E matches to keep its place in contention for the 2027 tournament.
