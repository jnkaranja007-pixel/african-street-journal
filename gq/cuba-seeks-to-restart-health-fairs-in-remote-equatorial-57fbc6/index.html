Cuba seeks to restart health fairs in remote Equatorial Guinea

The Cuban ambassador says the events would bring specialist care and early disease detection to isolated communities.

Source: Real Equatorial Guinea · 2026-11-28 · 1 min read

Cuba wants to restart health fairs in remote parts of Equatorial Guinea, bringing medical care to communities that are difficult to reach. The plan is intended to detect illnesses early through consultations, tests and medical check-ups.

Cuban ambassador Ángel Gustavo Suárez Cordero said the fairs would be organised with the country’s health and interior ministries and regional authorities. He said they would bring specialist care to people in isolated areas who need it most.

Suárez Cordero cited Annobón, where six Cuban medical cooperators have served residents over the years. Cuban doctors also work in Luba, other remote areas and the mainland, he said.

Why it mattersResidents of remote communities stand to receive specialist consultations, tests and check-ups through the proposed fairs.
