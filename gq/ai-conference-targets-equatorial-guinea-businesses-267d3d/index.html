AI Conference Targets Equatorial Guinea Businesses

Malabo will host an international conference on October 28 and 30, 2026, focusing on integrating artificial intelligence into business operations.

Source: Ahora EG · 2026-10-20 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Fundación Identic will present an international conference on Artificial Intelligence for businesses in Malabo on October 28 and 30, 2026. The event at KOIA Innovación aims to show entrepreneurs and professionals how to use AI for greater efficiency, new opportunities, and adaptation to a digital economy. Jorge Figueredo, CEO of Líderes Digitales in Latin America with over 20 years of experience in digital transformation and generative AI for business, will participate.

The conference, titled AI Business Malabo 2026, is designed for business owners, entrepreneurs, and professionals, emphasizing that AI is accessible to all company sizes. Attendees will learn to view AI as a business co-pilot, helping them discover new work methods and foster business growth. Tickets are priced at 40,000 xaf for executives and 65,000 xaf for premium access.

The event seeks to prepare local businesses for the transformative impact of artificial intelligence. Information is available by contacting 555 844 161, with tickets on sale at Fundación Identic, Canal Sol, and KOIA Innovation HUP. The conference underscores the growing importance of AI in the current economic landscape.

Why it mattersBusiness owners and professionals in Malabo can learn how to integrate AI to improve efficiency and foster growth.
