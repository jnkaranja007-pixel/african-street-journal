Equatorial Guinea closes first Venice Biennale appearance

The country’s pavilion at the 61st contemporary art Biennale also hosted a business meeting on investment opportunities in its market.

Source: Real Equatorial Guinea · 2026-11-26 · 1 min read

Equatorial Guinea closed its first participation in the Venice Biennale on Tuesday, 24 November, ending its appearance at the 61st contemporary art exhibition in Venice. The country presented “The Forest | The Undergrowth” at Palazzo Donà dalle Rose, with works by Equatorial Guinean and international artists.

The exhibition focused on people’s relationship with nature and the forest’s symbolic and spiritual meanings, according to Real Equatorial Guinea. Visitors included students, university professors, businesspeople, diplomats, art critics, cultural specialists, tourists and researchers. The organisers said the presentation introduced audiences to elements of Equatorial Guinea’s identity.

A business meeting held for Equatorial Guinea’s National Day brought together entrepreneurs interested in investment opportunities and the country’s market. The outlet said the meeting connected cultural promotion with business contacts and possible cooperation.

Why it mattersEntrepreneurs interested in Equatorial Guinea’s market met at a business session on National Day to discuss investment opportunities.
