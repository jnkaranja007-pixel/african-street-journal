GETESA sponsors AI Business Malabo 2026 event

The event, held October 28-30, focused on artificial intelligence opportunities for businesses.

Source: Ahora EG · 2026-11-01 · 1 min read

Equatorial Guinea's telecommunications company, GETESA, sponsored and participated in the AI Business Malabo 2026 event held on October 28 and 30 at the KOIA Innovation Hub.

The event, organized by the IDENTIC Foundation, KOIA Innovation Hub, and Elevate Events, brought together institutional representatives, business leaders, and entrepreneurs to explore artificial intelligence for business optimization. Carolina Obama Elonga represented GETESA on a panel discussing AI's role in improving efficiency and decision-making.

Reginaldo Boriko Okomo, Director General of New Technologies and Civil Aviation, stressed the need to extend AI tool benefits across all economic sectors. Jorge Figueredo, a specialist in generative AI, noted its potential to boost productivity without replacing human judgment.

Why it mattersGETESA's sponsorship of AI Business Malabo 2026 signals its engagement with technological advancements impacting Equatorial Guinea's business sector.
