Equatorial Guinea importers say they could not pay suppliers abroad

A 2021 finance ministry document records companies' complaints that they could not transfer money abroad for goods in the run-up to Christmas.

Source: Diario Rombe · 2026-11-25 · 1 min read

Importing companies in Equatorial Guinea told officials they could not transfer money abroad to buy essential goods as Christmas approached, according to a government document dated 4 November 2021 in Malabo.

The two-page document, titled "Summary of the meeting," was prepared by the secretariat of the then Ministry of Finance, Economy and Planning and addressed to the minister. Diario Rombe said it had obtained the document, which records the companies' concern about being unable to move foreign currency out of the country.

The excerpt gives no figures for the firms affected or for the value of the transfers blocked.

Why it mattersCompanies that depended on foreign currency to pay overseas suppliers were left unable to restock essential goods before the holiday season.
