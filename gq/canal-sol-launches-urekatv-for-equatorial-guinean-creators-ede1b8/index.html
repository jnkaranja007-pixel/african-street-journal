Canal Sol launches UrekaTV for Equatorial Guinean creators

The platform brings films, series, documentaries and digital productions together, with a dedicated section for local creators to pursue monetisation.

Source: Ahora EG · 2026-11-23 · 1 min read

Canal Sol has launched UrekaTV, an audiovisual platform for audiences in Equatorial Guinea and abroad. It brings films, series, documentaries and digital productions together in one online service, the company’s general director, Virginia Esther Mayé Mbá, said.

Mayé Mbá said the platform is intended to showcase Equatorial Guinean creativity internationally and help locally created projects compete in the global digital market. She said creators should be able to earn from their work, rather than rely only on building followers. The service includes a dedicated section for the national creator sector.

Creators at the launch welcomed that provision, saying their work is often limited to occasional advertising assignments for companies and brands. UrekaTV is operational online; Canal Sol also has Sol Estudio, an audiovisual complex supporting national production.

Why it mattersLocal creators have a dedicated section and a route to earn from their work, beyond occasional advertising assignments for companies and brands.
