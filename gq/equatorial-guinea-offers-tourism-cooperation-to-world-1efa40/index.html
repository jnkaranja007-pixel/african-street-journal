Equatorial Guinea offers tourism cooperation to World Tourism Forum Institute

The institute's president accepted the government's proposal, which followed the country's natural tourism award at a November 9 gala in Brussels.

Source: Ahora EG · 2026-11-26 · 1 min read

Equatorial Guinea's government has proposed tourism cooperation with the World Tourism Forum Institute, and a delegation from the institute is due to visit the country soon. The proposal was presented to the institute's president, Bulut Bagci, who expressed his agreement, according to Ahora EG.

A representative of the government described progress on port, airport and hotel infrastructure financed with oil revenues. The visit is intended to explore tourism development opportunities. Bagci said the institute has more than 16 years of experience and works in African countries including Angola and Mozambique.

The contacts followed Equatorial Guinea's receipt of the Best Natural Tourism Destination award at the World Tourism Forum's 2026 annual meeting, held on November 9 at the Royal Museum of Fine Arts in Brussels. According to the country's diplomatic mission, the award increased government interest in exploring new cooperation.

Why it mattersTourism operators and the state must now plan around a delegation that will assess which port, airport and hotel projects can attract visitors.
