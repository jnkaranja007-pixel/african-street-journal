Chinese hospital ship ends Bata mission after treating 8,616 patients

The Jixiang Fang Zhou also carried out 9,119 consultations and 17 surgeries during its stay in Equatorial Guinea from 20 to 25 November.

Sources: Ahora EG and Real Equatorial Guinea · 2026-11-27 · 1 min read

China’s hospital ship Jixiang Fang Zhou ended its medical mission in Bata, Equatorial Guinea, after treating 8,616 patients from 20 to 25 November, according to Real Equatorial Guinea.

The outlet reported that the team conducted 9,119 consultations and 17 surgeries. The ship arrived in Equatorial Guinea on 19 November and began providing care the following day.

At a farewell event, ship commander Zhou said the mission had been completed in record time and expressed hope that medical assistance could continue. Equatorial Guinea’s deputy defence minister, Alfredo Abeso Nvomo Onguene, represented Vice-President Teodoro Nguema Obiang Mangue.

Why it mattersThe 8,616 patients treated in Bata received care during the ship’s six days of medical work there.
