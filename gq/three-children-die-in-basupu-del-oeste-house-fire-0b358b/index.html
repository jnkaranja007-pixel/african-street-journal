Three children die in Basupú del Oeste house fire

A resident tried to rescue the minors but was prevented by flames.

Sources: Ahora EG and Real Equatorial Guinea · 2026-11-13 · 1 min read

A fire in Basupú del Oeste on Wednesday, November 11, killed three children in a semi-permanent house. The blaze started around seven in the evening and completely consumed the dwelling, which was divided into three sections each with a different owner. Residents could not save any belongings.

One resident told Ahora EG that she heard the children screaming as she escaped. She attempted to reach them but the intense flames blocked her path. The house lacked electricity, and residents were using a homemade lamp called a 'Bakoko', made from a bottle with kerosene and a wick, for light. This lamp reportedly fell onto a mattress, spilling kerosene and igniting the fire.

The youngest victim was a baby, and one of his siblings was six years old. The fire spread rapidly through the three sections of the house. All residents lost everything they owned in the fire.
