Chinese hospital ship offers care for up to 600 patients daily in Bata

The Jixiang Fangzhou will provide specialist consultations and surgery through 26 November, with 50 Equatorial Guinean health workers joining its team.

Sources: Ahora EG and Real Equatorial Guinea · 2026-11-20 · 1 min read

China’s hospital ship Jixiang Fangzhou arrived in Bata, Equatorial Guinea, on Thursday to provide free medical care. The vessel, also known as the Ark of Good Omen, will remain in the country through 26 November and has capacity for about 600 patients a day in two shifts.

The ship has 376 crew members and offers consultations and procedures in fields including cardiology, paediatrics, eye care and surgery. Equatorial Guinea’s government is coordinating patient identification, medical services and transport, while 50 local health professionals will work alongside Chinese specialists.

The mission is aimed especially at people with limited resources or difficulty obtaining specialist treatment. The team is also scheduled to visit the National University of Equatorial Guinea’s Faculty of Medicine and Nursing Sciences in Bata for academic activities with students.

Why it mattersPatients with limited resources can seek specialist treatment aboard the ship in Bata until 26 November, with the government coordinating access and transport.
