Equatorial Guinea finance and tourism ministries review national lottery plan

A preliminary feasibility report on creating a national lottery was examined at a joint meeting of the two ministries on Wednesday, 11 November.

Sources: Ahora EG and Real Equatorial Guinea · 2026-11-13 · 1 min read

The ministries of Finance and Tourism in Equatorial Guinea held a working meeting to analyse a preliminary feasibility report on creating a National Lottery. The session was chaired by the Minister Delegate of Finance, Milagrosa Obono Angue, alongside the Secretary of State for Information, Press and Tourism, María Milagrosa Nchama, and the Vice Minister of Finance for Tax Administration, José Domingo Ndong Bakale.

Officials examined the legal framework, the economic implications and the tax obligations the system would bring. Obono Angue proposed a joint interdepartmental technical commission to study the project further, review its components and recommend changes to fit national priorities. Tax and state contracting directors also joined the discussion.

The technical commission will also examine and validate the final feasibility report. Whether the lottery goes ahead depends on that evaluation, which will determine if the proposal meets the legal, economic and fiscal conditions needed for implementation.

Why it mattersThe national lottery would bring new tax obligations and state revenue, and it will only proceed if the technical commission validates the final feasibility report.
