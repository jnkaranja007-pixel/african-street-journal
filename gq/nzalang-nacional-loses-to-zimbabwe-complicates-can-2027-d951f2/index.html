Nzalang Nacional loses to Zimbabwe, complicates CAN 2027 qualification

Equatorial Guinea's national team suffered a 3-0 defeat against Zimbabwe in Harare, impacting their chances for the Africa Cup of Nations.

Sources: Ahora EG and Real Equatorial Guinea · 2026-11-17 · 1 min read

The Equatorial Guinean national team, led by Italian coach Vincenzo Alberto Annese, lost 3-0 to Zimbabwe on Monday in Harare. This defeat in the fourth round of qualification matches for the Africa Cup of Nations (CAN) 2027 complicates their aspirations in Group E.

Zimbabwe took an early lead in the 4th minute through Thandolwenkosi Ngwenya. Despite efforts from Cristian Makaté, Santiago Eneme, and Rony Mbomio, the Nzalang Nacional failed to score. Zimbabwean goalkeeper Nelson Chadya made key saves in the first half.

Before this match, Equatorial Guinea and Zimbabwe shared four points in Group E, with the Democratic Republic of Congo leading with nine points. The Nzalang Nacional must now win their upcoming matches to keep their hopes of qualifying for the CAN 2027 finals alive.
