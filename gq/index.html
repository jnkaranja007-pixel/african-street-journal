Police arrest man for attempted cathedral fire

Severo Oburu Ekong Afang, 54, is accused of trying to set fire to Malabo Cathedral after his warnings were ignored.

National Police in Malabo have arrested Severo Oburu Ekong Afang, 54, for an attempted fire at the city's cathedral on October 1. The arrest followed a month-long search involving public assistance.

Afang allegedly warned three officials from the Bishopric of Malabo about a situation he described as "maximum alert" but felt his concerns were not addressed. Investigations suggest this is not his first incident at the cathedral; he allegedly broke windows there in May 2024 for similar reasons.

The police consider these events to be of particular seriousness. The recent incident did not cause major damage due to the timely intervention of bystanders.

Why it mattersSevero Oburu Ekong Afang faces charges for an attempted arson at a religious site in Malabo.

Fire destroys eight homes in Malabo

Over 10 families lost all belongings after a short circuit sparked a blaze in the Héroe Luba neighbourhood.

A fire destroyed more than eight houses in Malabo's Héroe Luba neighbourhood on Sunday, November 1, leaving over 10 families homeless. The blaze, believed to be caused by a short circuit, spread rapidly through the semi-permanent structures.

Residents rescued an elderly man with reduced mobility and worked to extinguish the flames, but reported delays from protection services allowed the fire to spread further. Affected families are now appealing for aid, with some stating they are unemployed and unsure how to rebuild.

The community is seeking solidarity from the public and relevant authorities to help those who lost their homes and belongings in the incident.

Equatorial Guinean goalkeeper injures opponent

Jesús Lázaro Owono's collision with Jesús de Miguel during a Spanish Segunda División match caused immediate concern.

Equatorial Guinean footballer Jesús Lázaro Owono was involved in a serious incident on Monday, November 2, during a match between CD Tenerife and FC Andorra. The game, part of LaLiga Hypermotion's twelfth round, was tied 2-2 in its final minutes when the FC Andorra goalkeeper Owono left his area. He attempted to intercept a long ball and collided with his knee against Tenerife's forward, Jesús de Miguel.

Jesús de Miguel fell to the ground after the impact, prompting an immediate reaction from players on both teams who requested medical attention. The severity of the collision caused significant concern on the pitch. Owono is the goalkeeper for FC Andorra, and de Miguel plays for CD Tenerife in Spain's Second Division.

The match was played in the final minutes with the score at 2-2. The incident occurred during the twelfth round of LaLiga Hypermotion. No further details on the condition of either player were immediately available from the match report.
