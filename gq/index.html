Fire kills three children in Basupú del Oeste house

A resident tried to reach the children next door but flames blocked her path as the fire spread through the divided house.

Three children died in a fire that broke out at about seven in the evening on Wednesday, November 11, in Basupú del Oeste. The house was made of semi-permanent materials and split into three parts, each owned by a different person, and the flames consumed all of it.

A resident told Ahora EG that she was pulling children out when she heard the minors in the next section shouting that they were burning. She tried to reach them but could not get through the flames. One of the dead was a six-year-old, the brother of a baby who was in the house.

The household had no electricity after its power was cut, so residents lit the rooms with an artisanal lamp called a 'Bakoko', a bottle of petroleum with a wick. The resident said the lamp fell onto a mattress on the floor, spilling oil that set it alight. None of the residents could save their belongings.

Why it mattersResidents of the house lost all their possessions, and the three children who died leave their families with no home or belongings.

Equatorial Guinea joins Africa health data forum

The nation participates in the first African Health Data Forum to improve health information systems.

Equatorial Guinea is attending the first African Health Data Forum, an event organised by the World Health Organization (WHO) and partners under the theme «Better Data, Better Health for All». The forum aims to strengthen health information systems across Africa and promote the use of reliable data for public health planning and decision-making.

The country's delegation is led by Valero Ondo Nguema, the national director of the Health Information System at the Ministry of Health and Health Infrastructure. Representatives from health ministries, regional bodies, technical institutions, and international partners are participating in the forum.

Key priorities include strengthening health data governance, digital platform interoperability, and the responsible use of artificial intelligence in health, with a goal to develop a continental roadmap for integrated health information systems. Equatorial Guinea's participation supports its efforts to enhance its National Health Information System and advance towards Universal Health Coverage.

Why it mattersEquatorial Guinea is working to improve its national health information system by participating in a continental forum focused on data governance and digital health.

Equatorial Guinea considers national lottery

Government ministries met to review a preliminary feasibility study for a national lottery.

The ministries of Finance and Tourism in Equatorial Guinea have met to review a preliminary feasibility study for the creation of a National Lottery. The meeting, led by Deputy Finance Minister Milagrosa Obono Angue, examined the legal framework, economic implications, and tax obligations of such an initiative.

Officials also discussed ensuring the lottery would serve state interests and benefit the population. To further analyze the project, Obono Angue proposed forming a joint technical commission to refine the proposal and identify areas for improvement.

The commission will review the project's components and validate the final feasibility report. The potential launch of the National Lottery will depend on the outcomes of this evaluation process, which will determine if the proposal meets the necessary legal, economic, and fiscal conditions for implementation.

Why it mattersThe Ministry of Finance and the Ministry of Tourism are evaluating the potential economic and fiscal benefits of establishing a national lottery.
