Equatorial Guinea to host continental campaign against HIV/AIDS

The event from December 14-17 will focus on strengthening women's and girls' resilience to climate change and conflict.

Equatorial Guinea will host the continental campaign of the Organization of African First Ladies against HIV/AIDS (OPDAD) from December 14 to 17. The initiative aims to bolster the resilience of women and girls facing climate change and conflict.

Minister of Gender Equality and Social Affairs, María Consuelo Nguema Oyana, discussed the campaign details with First Lady Constancia Mangue de Obiang in Malabo. The campaign will address challenges faced by vulnerable women, girls, and communities, particularly their response to climate and humanitarian crises.

Key issues include child protection, education access, women's empowerment, and community resilience. This campaign is part of OPDAD's actions in different African countries.

Equatorial Guinea court sentences 13 to prison

Judgments include 13 years for homicide, nine years for fraud, and five years for rape.

The Provincial Court of Litoral in Equatorial Guinea has issued sentences in recent criminal trials. Josué Mba Micha received 13 years in prison for homicide, and José Ramón Nvo Mangue was sentenced to 10 years for the same crime. Rodrigo Félix was given nine years for three fraud offenses and ordered to repay over two million CFA francs to victims.

Tikou Divian Tiku faces seven years and eight months for robbery and another seven years and eight months for serious injury. Fidel Ndong Nguema Abuy was sentenced to five years and three months for raping a 13-year-old girl, along with a fine of 2,525,000 CFA francs. Joaquín Ovono Nkuna received a five-year prison term.

Bonifacio Pedro Esono Ondo will serve three years and four months and pay a 2,525,000 CFA franc fine, while Bernardo Obiang Nkua got three years of major arrest. Other sentences include six months for Benito Alogo Nvele for injuries, one year for Juan Carlos Oyono for drug offenses, and two years for Virginia Mikue for serious injuries. Ramón Owono Obiang received three years for fraud. The court also issued acquittals in several cases, including one for alleged HIV transmission.

Why it mattersSeveral individuals in Equatorial Guinea have received prison sentences for crimes including homicide, fraud, robbery, and rape.

Equatorial Guinea appoints first ambassador to Botswana

Domingo Mituy Edjang Adugu presented credentials to President Duma Gideon Boko, aiming to boost economic ties.

Domingo Mituy Edjang Adugu has become Equatorial Guinea's first ambassador to Botswana, presenting his credentials to President Duma Gideon Boko. The two nations established diplomatic relations in 2006 and reaffirmed them in 2025.

Upon accreditation, Ambassador Edjang met with the Botswana Investment and Trade Centre to promote Equatorial Guinea as an investment destination and invited Botswana firms to explore its market. He also held a technical meeting with Botswana's Ministry of International Affairs to review bilateral cooperation.

The ambassador's initial activities signal a focus on economic diplomacy and institutional outreach. Equatorial Guinea aims to foster investment and strengthen ties with the southern African nation.

Why it mattersThe appointment of the first ambassador and subsequent meetings aim to foster economic diplomacy and investment between Equatorial Guinea and Botswana.

Equatorial Guinea parliament backs goalkeeper after fatal clash

The Chamber of Deputies expressed solidarity with national player Jesús Lázaro Owono and mourned Spanish forward Jesús de Miguel.

The Chamber of Deputies of Equatorial Guinea expressed solidarity with national goalkeeper Jesús Lázaro Owono on Thursday, November 5. President Salomón Nguema Owono conveyed the institution's support following the incident that led to the death of Spanish forward Jesús de Miguel.

Nguema Owono stated that no one wants to witness such events on a football field, especially with devastating consequences. He extended condolences to the family of Jesús de Miguel and expressed the Chamber's sorrow over the loss. The accident occurred during a match between CD Tenerife and FC Andorra when Owono's challenge resulted in a collision with de Miguel.

Jesús de Miguel was taken to the hospital and later died in intensive care despite medical efforts. Equatorial Guinean football authorities may participate in tributes and funeral services for de Miguel as a gesture of respect.

Why it mattersThe parliament of Equatorial Guinea has officially backed a national player involved in a fatal on-field collision and sent condolences to the opposing player's family.

Confucius Institute marks 10 years in Malabo

The UNGE institute has trained 2,636 students, with 97.76% being Equatoguinean nationals.

The Confucius Institute at the National University of Equatorial Guinea (UNGE) celebrated its tenth anniversary on Wednesday at the UNGE central campus in Malabo. The event featured an academic and cultural atmosphere, highlighting the institute's established presence in the country. The institute received a donation of books from China's Zhejiang University.

Director Fernando Panadez García reported that 2,636 students have passed through the institute over the decade, with 97.76% being Equatoguinean. The student body also includes individuals from Cuba, Spain, Cameroon, Nigeria, Madagascar, Gabon, Guinea, Brazil, China, Lebanon, Mali, and Russia. Women constitute the majority of students, with 1,456 enrolled compared to 1,180 men.

The institute aims to foster connections between Chinese and Equatoguinean cultures through language and academic exchange. Chinese Ambassador Wang Wengang acknowledged the institute's role in strengthening institutional development and pedagogical models. The UNGE Confucius Institute operates under the joint guidance of UNGE and Zhejiang University of Foreign Languages.

Why it mattersEquatoguinean students and the general population are affected by the opportunity to learn Chinese language and culture.
