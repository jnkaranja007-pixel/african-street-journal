GETESA sponsors AI Business Malabo 2026 event

The event, held October 28-30, focused on artificial intelligence opportunities for businesses.

Equatorial Guinea's telecommunications company, GETESA, sponsored and participated in the AI Business Malabo 2026 event held on October 28 and 30 at the KOIA Innovation Hub.

The event, organized by the IDENTIC Foundation, KOIA Innovation Hub, and Elevate Events, brought together institutional representatives, business leaders, and entrepreneurs to explore artificial intelligence for business optimization. Carolina Obama Elonga represented GETESA on a panel discussing AI's role in improving efficiency and decision-making.

Reginaldo Boriko Okomo, Director General of New Technologies and Civil Aviation, stressed the need to extend AI tool benefits across all economic sectors. Jorge Figueredo, a specialist in generative AI, noted its potential to boost productivity without replacing human judgment.

Why it mattersGETESA's sponsorship of AI Business Malabo 2026 signals its engagement with technological advancements impacting Equatorial Guinea's business sector.

MYCHIC Project connects entrepreneurs in Malabo

The MYCHIC Project event in Malabo offered training in financial education and digital marketing.

The MYCHIC Project held an event on October 30 at the Hotel Bisila Palace in Malabo to help entrepreneurs develop their businesses. The meeting focused on entrepreneurial mindset, financial education, and digital marketing. Attendees presented their projects and made professional contacts.

Jennifer, the founder of MYCHIC Project, explained that the association aims to create a supportive space for learning and connection. The initiative grew from her personal experiences helping others overcome difficulties in starting their ventures. The project emphasizes guidance, mutual support, and personal growth.

MYCHIC Project has members in Equatorial Guinea, Spain, and London, with plans to expand connections. Jennifer highlighted self-love and valuing one's capabilities for personal and professional development. The event encouraged attendees to examine their aspirations and decision-making.

Why it mattersEntrepreneurs in Malabo gained skills and connections to advance their personal and professional development.

Equatorial Guinea treasury vice-minister outlines priorities

Montserrat Mbasogo Edu Angue met staff to discuss open communication, confidentiality, and punctuality.

Equatorial Guinea's new vice-minister of the Treasury, Montserrat Mbasogo Edu Angue, met with her department's staff on October 30 to outline her priorities. She emphasized working together with officials for the institution's good functioning, fostering open communication, and building trust.

Angue stressed the importance of technical staff, stating their knowledge is essential for institutional objectives. She also highlighted the need to protect institutional information, announcing that all staff will sign confidentiality agreements to prevent unauthorized disclosure of internal data. Violators may face disciplinary action.

The vice-minister also addressed punctuality, reminding staff of the importance of respecting work hours as a sign of responsibility and commitment. She encouraged staff to face challenges with dedication and a service spirit, noting that institutional results depend on staff involvement.

Why it mattersThe new vice-minister of the Treasury in Equatorial Guinea has set expectations for staff regarding communication, data security, and punctuality.

Spanish tax agency embargoes Equatorial Guinean minister's sister's property

Cándida Nfumu Angue Nchama's Madrid building faces over 320,000 euros in tax liens.

Spanish tax authorities have placed two embargoes totaling over 320,000 euros (209,906,240 CFA francs) on a building in Torrejón de Ardoz, Madrid. The property is owned by Cándida Nfumu Angue Nchama, the elder sister of Equatorial Guinea's Minister Delegate of Finance, Milagrosa Obono Angue.

The Spanish Public Treasury has placed the liens on the real estate, which is registered under Nfumu Angue Nchama's name. She is also the sister-in-law of Marcelino Owono Edu, further linking her to prominent figures within Equatorial Guinea's government.

This action follows an investigation by Diario Rombe into the property holdings of individuals connected to the minister's family. The Spanish property registry confirms the preventive embargoes against the building.

Why it mattersThe sister of Equatorial Guinea's Minister Delegate of Finance has had a property in Spain embargoed by tax authorities.

Equatorial Guinea sets six-month deadline for cocoa factory

The government will launch a cocoa processing factory to produce paste for chocolate.

The government of Equatorial Guinea has given itself six months to launch a factory for processing national cocoa production. This factory will produce cocoa paste, a raw material for chocolate.

The decision was made in Malabo during a meeting with the prime minister and ministers of Agriculture, Hacienda, and Tesorería. Companies ANONEY SERVICE and GETRANSCAO will supply quicklime and copper sulfate, with prices negotiated down by up to 10%. The government will also directly hire a shipping company for transport.

This initiative aims to move Equatorial Guinea from selling raw cocoa to processing its own production. The strategy seeks to strengthen the agricultural sector, improve cocoa and coffee production, create jobs, add value, and reduce reliance on imports.

Why it mattersFarmers in Equatorial Guinea will benefit from a new factory that processes their cocoa, adding value to their crops and potentially creating new employment opportunities.
