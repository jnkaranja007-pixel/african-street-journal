Business School Opens 2026-2027 Enrollment for Diverse Programs

Complutense International Business School in Malabo and Bata is now accepting applications for its upcoming academic year, offering a wide range of professional training courses.

Complutense International Business School has announced the opening of enrollment for the 2026-2027 academic year at its Malabo and Bata campuses. The institution offers a broad spectrum of professional training designed to enhance career prospects and job opportunities. Current offerings include specializations in fields such as Nursing, Clinical Laboratory, Pharmacy, Administration and Finance, Marketing, Human Resources, IT, International Trade, Banking, Accounting, Business Administration, International Relations, Journalism, Aviation, Hospitality, Agriculture, and Artificial Intelligence, among others.

The school emphasizes practical, up-to-date training with qualified instructors, modern facilities, equipped classrooms, laboratories, and a library. Students can choose from morning, afternoon, or evening shifts to accommodate varying schedules. The curriculum aims to blend theoretical knowledge with hands-on experience, preparing students for a rapidly evolving job market. Classes are scheduled to commence in October 2026, and early registration is advised due to limited capacity.

Interested individuals can contact Complutense International Business School via phone or WhatsApp at +240 222 192 490 for registration details, information on available specializations, schedules, and the enrollment process. The school operates from its centers located in Malabo and Bata, aiming to equip students with the skills to become leaders and contribute to future development. The motto for the new course is "We train leaders, we transform the future."

Why it mattersProspective students seeking professional development and enhanced employment opportunities are affected by the opening of enrollment for a wide array of specialized courses.

ONCIGE Offers Free Eye Exams to Students

The National Organization for the Control of Eye Diseases is providing free vision screenings and discounted glasses throughout September.

The National Organization for the Control of Eye Diseases (ONCIGE) has launched a free visual health campaign for students across Equatorial Guinea. The initiative, supported by the First Lady, will run for the entire month of September and targets students from primary to university levels, as well as vocational training centers. Screenings will be available at ONCIGE headquarters in Malabo for the Insular Region and in Bata for the Continental Region.

To qualify for the free consultations, students must present proof of enrollment, such as a student ID or academic record. Minors require accompaniment by a parent or guardian. Students diagnosed with vision problems needing correction will be eligible for prescription glasses at a 50% discount off the usual price. ONCIGE emphasizes that undetected vision issues can negatively impact academic performance and contribute to school failure.

This campaign is part of broader social actions aimed at preventing visual problems that can hinder learning. ONCIGE urges parents and guardians to utilize this opportunity for their children's check-ups and encourages older students to take an active role in their eye health. The organization highlights the link between vision and academic success, calling for proactive care.

Why it mattersStudents are affected by this initiative through free eye examinations and discounted glasses, which can improve their academic performance.

Ebola Outbreak Surpasses 6,000 Cases in DRC

The Democratic Republic of Congo's Ebola outbreak has now exceeded 6,000 confirmed cases and is nearing 3,000 deaths as it spreads rapidly.

Health authorities reported 6,041 confirmed cases and 2,911 deaths as of August 31, with 1,366 individuals recovering from the disease. Declared on May 15, the outbreak has now reached six provinces and dozens of health zones in the north and east of the country. This epidemic's scale has surpassed the previous record of 3,317 cases recorded between 2018 and 2020.

The World Health Organization has characterized the current outbreak as the largest ever reported in the country and one that is expanding faster than previous epidemics. The Bundibugyo virus, a rare strain of Ebola without an approved vaccine or specific treatment, is causing the outbreak, complicating transmission control. Ituri province remains the epicenter, with cases also detected in North Kivu, South Kivu, Haut-Uélé, Tshopo, and Bas-Uélé.

The WHO noted that the outbreak had reached 60 health zones by August 26. The current fatality rate stands at approximately 48%, highlighting the disease's severity and challenges in timely detection and treatment. Despite this, over 1,300 people have recovered, offering some encouragement to health officials. The WHO has warned that this outbreak could potentially surpass the 2014-2016 West Africa Ebola epidemic, which caused over 11,000 deaths.

Why it mattersThe Ebola outbreak in the DRC affects people by causing over 6,000 infections, nearly 3,000 deaths, and complicating treatment due to the lack of specific vaccines or approved therapies.

Grand Hotel Djibloho Offers Tourism Package

The hotel's World Tourism Day promotion bundles accommodation, dining, and excursions for groups.

El Grand Hotel Djibloho has launched a special weekend package to promote national tourism, coinciding with World Tourism Day. The initiative aims to showcase Djibloho's attractions through a comprehensive program that includes round-trip transport from Bata, two nights' accommodation with breakfast, and a "Taste of the World" dinner. This all-inclusive experience is designed to offer a diverse range of activities for visitors seeking domestic travel options.

The package incorporates a wellness component with an outdoor Afro Fitness session and a cultural excursion to Ndong Mba and Malamba, intended to immerse participants in the local environment and heritage. Entertainment is provided through a White Party at Ozean Pool Bar, featuring live music and a DJ, to conclude the experience. This offering targets groups of at least 10 people.

Pricing for the package is set at 205,000 XAF per person for single occupancy and 290,000 XAF for double occupancy. Reservations and further information can be obtained via WhatsApp at +240 555 502 359. The hotel aims to stimulate local tourism by providing a curated experience that highlights regional offerings.

Why it mattersThe Grand Hotel Djibloho's tourism package affects businesses by creating a new revenue stream and promoting local attractions.

Woman Kills Husband After Economic Hardship

A woman in Douala, Cameroon, faces charges for allegedly killing her husband after claiming the relationship became unbearable due to financial struggles.

Monkam Tedem Maëva, identified as the accused, has been detained in Douala, Cameroon, following the death of her husband. The couple had been living together for approximately three years in the Bonassama district of Bonaberi. The incident has caused shock in this part of the city. According to the detained woman's statement, she stated that the relationship had deteriorated to a point she found unbearable, attributing her actions to her husband's inability to meet their economic needs.

The accused claims that she attempted to end the relationship, but her partner allegedly forced her to stay, causing significant psychological distress. She asserts that she acted under the weight of this unbearable suffering. The woman is currently in custody while judicial investigations are underway to determine the exact circumstances of the death and establish legal responsibility.

The case highlights the severe psychological impact that prolonged economic difficulties can have on individuals within a relationship. The investigation will focus on verifying the claims made by the accused and understanding the sequence of events that led to this tragic outcome. The judicial process aims to bring clarity to the domestic dispute that ended in violence.

Why it mattersA woman in Cameroon is accused of killing her husband due to economic hardship and an unbearable relationship, highlighting the severe psychological impact of financial struggles.
