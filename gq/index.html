Secret gold contract involves $30 million bank guarantee

A secret contract involving 1,000 tons of gold and a $30 million bank guarantee was allegedly managed by a relative of Constancia Mangue. Diario Rombe reports the circuit operated outside of state controls and included facilities to purify raw gold.

Why it mattersThe use of an unofficial circuit for gold trade bypasses state regulatory controls and tax collection.

Agriculture · 2026-08-20

Scientific Committee validates V Population and I Agricultural censuses

The Scientific Committee has approved the strategic documents for the fifth population census and the first agricultural census. The review took place at the Ministry of Finance, Budgets and National Planning.

Why it mattersThe validated technical instruments will allow the government to collect updated statistical data on the country's population and farming sector.

Education · 2026-08-20

Media Law proposes new information sciences internship institute

A proposed Media Law includes the creation of an internship institute for Information Sciences. Government delegates told deputies the institute is intended to organize the training of journalists in Equatorial Guinea.

Why it mattersThe institute would change how journalism students and professionals receive formal training and certification.
