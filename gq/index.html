Malabo police seize 28,250mg of tramadol from Cameroon ship

Two Cameroonian crew members of the vessel Queen Rebeca were arrested as they allegedly prepared to hand over the painkiller for sale.

Malabo National Police seized 28,250 milligrams of tramadol from a commercial vessel that had sailed from Limbe, Cameroon. The operation on Friday led to the arrest of two Cameroonian men, Cristian Nnen and Musa Illiasu.

The police intercepted the two men as they allegedly prepared to deliver the tramadol to a buyer for resale in Equatorial Guinea. Both are sailors on the Queen Rebeca, which runs sea routes between Malabo and Limbe.

The two suspects remain in detention while investigators work to establish the circumstances of the case and identify any others involved. The file will then be passed to the competent judicial authorities.

Why it mattersThe two sailors face judicial proceedings in Equatorial Guinea over the alleged smuggling of the painkiller into the country.

Sport · 2026-11-15

Equatorial Guinea hosts national boxing championship

Over 70 boxers from 14 clubs will compete in Malabo for the National Boxing Championship 2026.

Malabo will host the 2026 National Boxing Championship, an event organized by the Equatorial Guinean Boxing Federation (FEGUIBOX). More than 70 boxers from 14 clubs across the country are expected to participate.

The championship will take place in two venues: the Malabo Sports Center for preliminary bouts and semifinals, and the Caracolas Gymnasium for the finals on December 6. Nine clubs are based in Malabo, with five from the Continental Region.

Competitors will vie for titles in eight weight categories ranging from 50 to 85 kilograms, aligning with World Boxing's Olympic boxing standards. The event aims to assess national boxers' skill levels and discover new talent.

Why it mattersThe national boxing championship will take place on December 6 in Malabo, involving over 70 boxers.

Education · 2026-11-15

Malabo French school wins national VÍA 2026 final

The winning school will now represent Equatorial Guinea in the programme's international phase, scheduled for December.

The Colegio Francés de Malabo won the national VÍA 2026 programme on Saturday, 14 November, defeating the Centro Privado Anciano Luis Ndong de Bata in the final at the French Cultural Centre in Malabo. The victory allows the school to represent Equatorial Guinea in the international phase of the programme, which was due to take place in December.

Students Sara Ngua and Esperanza Oke represented the Malabo school, while Nicolás Nsue Ndong Bendomo and Juan Santiago Obiang Obiang Andeme spoke for the Bata school. Each team presented final proposals to a jury, pitching ideas to promote safer mobility around schools. The Malabo school received a prize of 5,000 euros, plus a further 500 euros for a safe-mobility awareness action that its students will carry out.

The programme aims to train young people as ambassadors for safe mobility, equipping them to identify everyday travel risks, especially around schools, and to propose ways to improve road safety.

Why it mattersThe winning students will carry out a safe-mobility awareness action funded with 500 euros, on top of the 5,000-euro prize for their school.
