Malabo Students Register for September University Entrance Exams

Registration for the extraordinary September 2026 university entrance exams opens Tuesday in Malabo, offering a second chance for students.

The registration period for the extraordinary September 2026 university entrance exams begins Tuesday, September 1, in Malabo. Students will be able to complete their enrollment from nine in the morning to twelve noon, and again from two in the afternoon until four in the afternoon, with schedules staggered by educational center. This exam session provides a crucial opportunity for students who did not pass the initial examinations to qualify for higher education.

To register, students must present specific documentation including a national identity document or passport, or a literal birth certificate for those under eighteen. A passport-sized photograph is also required, along with a photocopy of the enrollment receipt for students transferring from Bata. The registration fee includes effects timbrados or policies valued at two thousand five hundred CFA francs, and four thousand CFA francs in cash.

This extraordinary session follows the national recovery tests, which also commence tomorrow. Following these exams, registration details for official students who successfully completed their second year of high school in September will be announced. The primary aim of these additional examinations is to enable students to pass the pending test and gain access to university studies.

Why it mattersStudents seeking university admission are directly affected by the registration process and fees for the September entrance exams.

Politics · 2026-09-01

President Replaces Development Fund Manager

Gaudencio Mohaba Mesu has been removed from his post as National Authorizing Officer for the European Development Fund.

President Obiang Nguema Mbasogo has removed Gaudencio Mohaba Mesu from his position as National Authorizing Officer for the European Development Fund. The decree, Number 99/2026, cited the convenience of better service as the reason for the change. The President exercised his authority under Article 41, subsection h of the Fundamental Law of the State to enact this dismissal.

In the same decree, Jerónimo Carlos Osa Osa Nsang has been appointed to the role of National Authorizing Officer for the European Development Fund. The appointment was made based on the circumstances present in Mr. Nsang’s person, also under the authority granted by Article 41, subsection h of the Fundamental Law of the State. The presidential decision was made in Malabo on August 24, 2026.

The European Development Fund manages financial aid and cooperation projects between the European Union and partner countries. The National Authorizing Officer is responsible for overseeing the implementation and management of these funds within the country. This change in leadership may affect the administration and disbursement of ongoing and future development projects.

Why it mattersThe change in the National Authorizing Officer for the European Development Fund could impact the management and allocation of development aid, affecting government projects and financial flows.

Sport · 2026-09-01

Messi Retires From Argentina National Team

The football star announced his departure from the national team via social media, citing a lack of further contribution.

Lionel Messi has officially announced his retirement from the Argentine national team, stating he has nothing more to give. The announcement came via a letter posted on his social media channels, two days after Argentina's World Cup final loss to Spain.

This marks the second time Messi has retired from the national team, having previously done so in 2016 after Argentina lost the Copa América to Chile. The 39-year-old player expressed that the decision was painful but felt it was the right moment, acknowledging the emergence of younger talent.

Messi thanked fans for their support over the past 20 years, stating he always gave his all for the jersey and aimed to make Argentinians proud through football. He mentioned he would miss hearing the crowd from within the team.

Why it mattersFootball fans and followers of the Argentine national team are directly affected by Lionel Messi's decision to retire.
