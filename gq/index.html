Prosecutor seeks life sentence for sergeant accused of murder

The military court in Micomeseng heard charges against Sergeant Rafael Edjang Edjo for allegedly killing two women.

A military court in Micomeseng has heard the case against Sergeant Rafael Edjang Edjo, who is accused of murdering two women at the Meban Nkan military facility. The prosecution is seeking a life sentence for Edjang.

The court, presided over by Rear Admiral Tiburcio Ngomo Ndong, also considered the case of Corporal Juan Pablo Obiang Ndong, who faces charges of abandonment of service, fraud, and disobedience. The prosecution requested four years in prison and permanent dismissal for Obiang Ndong, who was allegedly asleep and intoxicated during the incident. The Ministry of Defence was also asked to arrange compensation for the victims' families.

Another case, involving Corporal Isidro Edu Ondo for a charge of punishable recklessness connected to a homicide, was also heard. Both proceedings have concluded and await sentencing by the War Council.

Why it mattersThe Ministry of Defence must arrange compensation for the families of two women allegedly murdered at a military facility.

Equatorial Guinea reviews Securiport border control fees

Vice President Nguema Obiang Mangue chairs meeting in Malabo to assess company's economic proposal.

Equatorial Guinea's government reviewed Securiport's economic proposal for border control modernization in Malabo on Tuesday. Vice President Nguema Obiang Mangue led the meeting, attended by the prime minister and ministers of Transport, Security, and Finance. The group examined the fees and services offered by the U.S. company, which previously operated in the country.

Obiang Mangue requested a comparison of current and past contract conditions to ensure new fees align with planned services. He stressed that the project must meet national needs without unnecessary expenses that could burden passengers or deter tourism. A pilot phase at Malabo and Bata airports is recommended to test the system's effectiveness before wider implementation.

Securiport first proposed modernizing airport security in November 2025. Authorities are negotiating financial terms to enhance border controls and traveler safety without imposing undue costs on the state or citizens. The goal is to strengthen security at all national airports, ports, and border crossings.

Why it mattersVice President Nguema Obiang Mangue is overseeing a review of border control fees that could impact passenger costs and tourism.

Malabo French institute marks 40 years

The ICEF in Malabo celebrated its anniversary with a cultural event attended by ambassadors and officials.

The French Expression Institute of Malabo (ICEF) celebrated its 40th anniversary with social and cultural activities. The event brought together ambassadors from various diplomatic missions in Malabo and representatives from Equatorial Guinea's Ministry of Foreign Affairs, International Cooperation, and Diaspora.

ICEF director Rémy Béhanzin welcomed attendees before Sonia Doña Pérez, the French ambassador to Equatorial Guinea, spoke. Ambassador Pérez highlighted the cooperation between France and Equatorial Guinea since 1986, describing the ICEF as an open house that offers French language training and music courses.

The institute also provides services including a library, children's Saturdays, dance workshops, and film screenings. The celebration reaffirmed the ICEF's role as a center for training and cultural exchange.

Why it mattersEquatorial Guineans benefit from cultural and language training programs offered by the French Expression Institute.

Ceiba Intercontinental keeps Malabo-Bata flights running

The airline continues national operations on Mondays, Wednesdays, Fridays, and Sundays due to low passenger demand.

Ceiba Intercontinental is continuing its domestic flights connecting Malabo and Bata, operating on Mondays, Wednesdays, Fridays, and Sundays. The airline attributes this reduced schedule to the current low season, which has resulted in fewer passengers. This adjustment also accommodates the annual maintenance program for the company's fleet.

Commercial management expects to return to the usual flight frequency by mid-November, once the planned maintenance is completed. Ticket prices remain unchanged, with fares starting at 52,600 CFA francs. The company has assured customers that operations will resume their normal pace shortly.

The airline's commercial direction stated that the current schedule is a temporary measure. Passengers are advised to check the company's official channels for any updates regarding flight schedules and availability. The maintenance program is planned for the fleet.

Why it mattersPassengers traveling between Malabo and Bata will experience reduced flight options until mid-November.

Tree falls on taxi in Bata

Intense rains caused a large tree from Santa Cruz cemetery to collapse onto a moving vehicle.

A taxi carrying three people was trapped under a large tree that fell from the Santa Cruz cemetery on Wednesday in Bata. The incident occurred as the vehicle was driving near the cemetery, with the tree impacting the taxi and partially covering it.

The three occupants of the taxi were taken to the Bata Regional Hospital for medical attention. Nearby citizens helped remove branches and part of the trunk to free the vehicle and assist with the rescue.

The intense rainfall was cited as the cause for the tree's collapse. The incident happened in the city of Bata.

Why it mattersThree people in Bata were injured and required medical attention after a tree fell on their taxi.
