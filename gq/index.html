Security · 2026-11-16

Vehicle kills 17-year-old student in Luba

Emilia Mekina Edu, a pupil at the Colegio María Inmaculada de Batete, died at the scene after a Toyota RAV4 struck her on the road to Boloko.

A 17-year-old student, Emilia Mekina Edu, was killed in Luba after a Toyota RAV4 with registration AN-679-E hit her while she was walking towards Boloko. The impact was so violent that she died where she was struck, and local outlets report that one of her lower limbs was thrown into a nearby wooded area, where a search was started.

She was the daughter of Juan Edu Edu, a professor and director of the Centro Integrado de Luba, and Cirila Andeme. The driver, reportedly Jaime Alfaro Ndjemua from Musola, was detained by police after the crash. Sources say the collision may have happened while the driver was overtaking, but authorities have not confirmed this.

The circumstances remain under investigation, and officials will determine how the crash happened and who is responsible. The search for the missing limb had no reported outcome at the time of writing, and the Colegio María Inmaculada de Batete is mourning one of its students.

Why it mattersThe driver faces an official inquiry that will decide responsibility for the death of a pupil and the family of the victim await its findings.

Health · 2026-11-16

Chinese hospital ship offers free medical care in Bata

The Jixiang Fangzhou will provide consultations, diagnostics, and surgeries from November 21 to 26.

The Chinese hospital ship Jixiang Fangzhou, named Arca del Buen Augurio, will dock in Bata on Thursday, November 19, offering free medical services until November 26 as part of China's Harmony Mission. Patient care begins Saturday, November 21, with an estimated capacity of 600 people daily across two shifts.

The mission targets the general population, with a focus on individuals and families facing financial difficulties in accessing medical treatments. Chinese specialists will offer consultations, general check-ups, clinical analysis, diagnostic tests, and treatments for various illnesses, including potential surgical interventions based on patient condition.

Beyond direct healthcare, the mission includes academic exchanges with local medical personnel and a visit to the Faculty of Medicine and Nursing at the National University of Equatorial Guinea. Chinese specialists will conduct training sessions for students on sexually transmitted infections and new medical techniques.

Why it mattersEquatorial Guineans can access free medical consultations, diagnostics, and treatments from November 21 to 26.

Education · 2026-11-16

Colegio Francés de Malabo wins national VÍA 2026 road-safety final

The school takes 5,000 euros and will represent Equatorial Guinea in the international phase of the programme next month.

El Colegio Francés de Malabo won the national final of the VÍA 2026 programme on Saturday, 14 November, defeating the Centro Privado Anciano Luis Ndong of Bata. The Malabo school now represents Equatorial Guinea in the programme's international phase, which is scheduled for December.

The final was held at the French Cultural Centre in Malabo, where two pupils from each school pitched proposals for safer travel around schools. Sara Ngua and Esperanza Oke represented the winners, and Nicolás Nsue Ndong Bendomo and Juan Santiago Obiang Obiang Andeme represented Bata. The winning school received 5,000 euros, and the Bata centre, placed second, received 1,000 euros.

The winners also receive an extra 500 euros to fund a road-safety awareness action that they will run themselves. The VÍA programme trains young people as safe-mobility ambassadors, teaching them to spot everyday risks on their journeys, especially near schools.

Why it mattersThe prize money and the December trip abroad go to the Malabo school's pupils, who must now run their own awareness action.

Sport · 2026-11-16

Equatorial Guinea hosts national boxing championship

More than 70 boxers from 14 clubs will compete in Malabo for the National Boxing Championship.

Malabo will host the 2026 National Boxing Championship, organized by the Equatorial Guinean Boxing Federation (FEGUIBOX). Over 70 boxers from 14 clubs across the country are expected to participate.

The championship will use two venues: the Malabo Sports Center for preliminary bouts and semifinals, and the Caracolas Gymnasium for the finals on December 6. Nine clubs will represent Malabo, while five will come from the Continental Region.

Competitors will vie for supremacy in eight weight categories, ranging from 50 to 85 kilograms, aligning with World Boxing standards. The event aims to assess local talent and foster the sport's growth within Equatorial Guinea.

Why it mattersThe national boxing championship will take place in Malabo, with finals scheduled for December 6.
