VidaSeguros EG offers Oil & Gas talent retention solutions

The insurance company presented life, retirement, and education plans to address employee turnover and financial security in the energy sector.

VidaSeguros EG showcased its specialized insurance solutions at African Energy Week 2026, aiming to help Oil & Gas companies protect their workforce and retain talent. The company highlighted collective life insurance, supplementary retirement plans, and study insurance as key offerings designed to enhance employee benefits and reduce costs associated with staff rotation.

These solutions are intended to complement human resources policies, foster employee commitment, and build trust-based labor relations. By providing protection for families, financial security during retirement, and support for children's education, VidaSeguros EG seeks to improve the overall value proposition for employees in a demanding sector.

The insurer also used the event to connect with energy sector professionals and investors, identify collaboration opportunities, and present its capabilities as a specialized national insurer. VidaSeguros EG positions itself as a strategic partner for companies in Equatorial Guinea seeking to stabilize their teams and promote sustainable growth.

Why it mattersVidaSeguros EG's new insurance products aim to reduce costs for Oil & Gas companies by improving employee retention and financial security.

Man Faces Jail for Illegally Obtaining Nationality

Michael Germán Tsanga Bindang is accused of fraud and improperly acquiring citizenship, with the state as the injured party.

A court is considering charges against Michael Germán Tsanga Bindang for alleged fraud and the improper acquisition of Equatoguinean nationality. The Public Prosecutor's office stated that the alleged actions undermine public trust and the legal framework governing nationality. They are seeking a three-year prison sentence and a fine of 5,750,000 CFA francs for each offense, along with compensation for fraud victims.

The defense has denied the accusations, arguing for the defendant's acquittal due to a lack of conclusive evidence. The court also heard cases involving alleged drug trafficking, with prosecutors seeking three-year sentences and fines for two other defendants, Sunday Ozoagu and Federico Pedro Mandumbi. One admitted to being a regular marijuana user.

These proceedings are part of the Litoral Provincial Court's efforts to resolve pending cases. The court will now review the evidence and arguments presented by both the prosecution and defense before issuing its rulings in the coming days.

Why it mattersA man faces jail time and fines for allegedly obtaining Equatoguinean nationality improperly.

AI Conference Targets Equatorial Guinea Businesses

Malabo will host an international conference on October 28 and 30, 2026, focusing on integrating artificial intelligence into business operations.

Fundación Identic will present an international conference on Artificial Intelligence for businesses in Malabo on October 28 and 30, 2026. The event at KOIA Innovación aims to show entrepreneurs and professionals how to use AI for greater efficiency, new opportunities, and adaptation to a digital economy. Jorge Figueredo, CEO of Líderes Digitales in Latin America with over 20 years of experience in digital transformation and generative AI for business, will participate.

The conference, titled AI Business Malabo 2026, is designed for business owners, entrepreneurs, and professionals, emphasizing that AI is accessible to all company sizes. Attendees will learn to view AI as a business co-pilot, helping them discover new work methods and foster business growth. Tickets are priced at 40,000 xaf for executives and 65,000 xaf for premium access.

The event seeks to prepare local businesses for the transformative impact of artificial intelligence. Information is available by contacting 555 844 161, with tickets on sale at Fundación Identic, Canal Sol, and KOIA Innovation HUP. The conference underscores the growing importance of AI in the current economic landscape.

BOCAMANDJA uses theater to promote women's rights in Bioko

A new micro-project will stage 20 performances and discussions across Bioko Island to raise awareness about gender-based violence.

The BOCAMANDJA Theater Company, funded by FRS-European Union, has launched a micro-project aimed at promoting the rights and well-being of women and girls on Bioko Island. The initiative will span several months and cover both Bioko Norte and Bioko Sur provinces, targeting adolescents, youth, and the general public. This project aligns with a broader effort to foster community awareness and encourage attitudinal changes regarding gender-based violence.

The project includes twenty theatrical performances, twenty discussion sessions, and the distribution of informational leaflets. Following each play, a facilitated dialogue will address questions and topics arising from the performance. BOCAMANDJA stated its commitment to human rights, gender equality, and the welfare of women and girls in Equatorial Guinea, emphasizing theater's role in transforming perspectives.

The discussions are designed to encourage open and participatory debate around gender-based violence and its various forms. This initiative is part of FRS's main project, specifically Result 4, which focuses on community sensitization for behavioral change concerning gender-based violence.

Why it mattersThe project aims to raise awareness and promote dialogue about gender-based violence and women's rights on Bioko Island.

Designer Launches Fashion Brand Inspired by Equatorial Guinean Traditions

Eulalia Loribo Apo, known as Jelonny, unveiled Mía Wi’o, a new fashion line blending ancestral heritage with contemporary designs.

A new fashion brand, Mía Wi’o, inspired by Equatorial Guinean traditions, was launched by designer Eulalia Loribo Apo, known artistically as Jelonny. The presentation included institutional representatives, artists, and guests, with the Minister of Information, Press, Culture, and Tourism in attendance. Minister Juan José Ekua Esono Mangue expressed his department's support for initiatives that promote culture, engage youth, and preserve national traditions.

Jelonny stated that Mía Wi’o aims to reclaim her roots and translate ancestral legacies into modern proposals, transforming cultural symbols into elements that express Equatoguinean identity in daily life. The debut collection, 'Raíces Vivas' or 'Mis Raíces,' features contemporary clothing like t-shirts and bags inspired by traditional materials and techniques, alongside traditional wedding attire. Musical and artistic performances accompanied the launch.

The event concluded with a fashion show displaying traditional wedding garments integrated with new creative designs. Jelonny's initiative, Mía Wi’o Cultura Viva, seeks to disseminate Equatorial Guinea's cultural heritage and foster pride in the nation's roots and diversity through fashion and design.

Why it mattersThe launch of Mía Wi’o provides a platform for cultural expression and the promotion of national heritage through contemporary fashion.
