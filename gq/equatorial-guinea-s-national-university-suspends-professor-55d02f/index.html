Equatorial Guinea’s national university suspends professor and expels student

The measures followed a recorded confrontation in which the student broke a pair of glasses and the professor slapped the student.

Sources: Ahora EG and Real Equatorial Guinea · 2026-11-25 · 1 min read

The National University of Equatorial Guinea (UNGE) suspended a professor from all teaching activity and expelled a student after a confrontation at its Papa León XIV Campus in Basupú. The university announced the measures on Tuesday, 24 November, applying the rules that govern the institution.

In a recording, the professor asks the student to hand over a pair of glasses; the student breaks them in front of the professor, who then slaps the student. The university rector’s office said the decisions form part of a zero-tolerance policy on indiscipline and violence on campus.

The rector’s office said the university should serve as a place for education, culture and values in support of national development.

Why it mattersThe professor can no longer teach at the university, while the student has been expelled under its disciplinary rules.
