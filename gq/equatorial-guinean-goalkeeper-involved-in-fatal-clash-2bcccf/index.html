Equatorial Guinean goalkeeper involved in fatal clash

Jesús de Miguel, a 30-year-old forward for CD Tenerife, died after colliding with goalkeeper Jesús Owono.

Source: Ahora EG · 2026-11-04 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Jesús de Miguel Alameda, a 30-year-old forward for CD Tenerife, has died following a collision with Equatorial Guinean goalkeeper Jesús Lázaro Owono. The incident occurred on Monday, November 2, during a LaLiga Hypermotion match between CD Tenerife and FC Andorra at the Heliodoro Rodríguez López stadium. The game was suspended in the 108th minute with the score tied at 2-2 after Owono collided with De Miguel while attempting to intercept a long ball.

Owono, the FC Andorra goalkeeper, reportedly impacted De Miguel with his knee, causing the forward to collapse on the field. Medical services attended to De Miguel for several minutes before he was taken off on a stretcher and transported to a hospital. The referee issued Owono a direct red card for the play. CD Tenerife confirmed De Miguel's death on Tuesday morning at 08:00 hours, stating his condition had worsened overnight after initial hospital admission.

The club expressed deep sorrow and offered condolences to De Miguel's family and loved ones, requesting privacy. The news has sent shockwaves through Spanish football, with numerous clubs and sports entities sharing their condolences. De Miguel had been in intensive care, described as stable but serious, before his neurological condition deteriorated.

Why it mattersThe death of footballer Jesús de Miguel, following a collision with Equatorial Guinean goalkeeper Jesús Owono, has caused widespread shock in Spanish football.
