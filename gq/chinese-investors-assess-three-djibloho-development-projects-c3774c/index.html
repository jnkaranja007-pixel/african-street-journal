Chinese investors assess three Djibloho development projects

The proposals include 4,500 social homes, a road and sites for a planned ecological city, pending a technical review of viability.

Sources: Ahora EG and Real Equatorial Guinea · 2026-11-21 · 1 min read

10 Chinese investors are assessing development projects in Djibloho, Equatorial Guinea, before deciding whether to finance them. The province’s governor, Marcelo Castro Ngua Nkulu, presented three proposals: 4,500 social homes, a road and sites for a planned ecological city.

The delegation is in Ciudad de la Paz to evaluate 10 government proposals under a cooperation initiative between Equatorial Guinea and China. Castro Ngua Nkulu and specialists from GEPROYECTOS, a project body involved in the presentation, shared a development plan covering the city’s current situation, construction progress and growth plans. The investors asked about technical needs and local conditions.

The technical review is preliminary; its results are intended to establish whether the proposals are viable before financing decisions are made.

Why it mattersEquatorial Guinea’s government must wait for the preliminary technical review to determine whether financing decisions can proceed on the three Djibloho projects.
