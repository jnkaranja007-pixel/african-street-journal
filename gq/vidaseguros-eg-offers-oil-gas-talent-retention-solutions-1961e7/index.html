VidaSeguros EG offers Oil & Gas talent retention solutions

The insurance company presented life, retirement, and education plans to address employee turnover and financial security in the energy sector.

Source: Ahora EG · 2026-10-21 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

VidaSeguros EG showcased its specialized insurance solutions at African Energy Week 2026, aiming to help Oil & Gas companies protect their workforce and retain talent. The company highlighted collective life insurance, supplementary retirement plans, and study insurance as key offerings designed to enhance employee benefits and reduce costs associated with staff rotation.

These solutions are intended to complement human resources policies, foster employee commitment, and build trust-based labor relations. By providing protection for families, financial security during retirement, and support for children's education, VidaSeguros EG seeks to improve the overall value proposition for employees in a demanding sector.

The insurer also used the event to connect with energy sector professionals and investors, identify collaboration opportunities, and present its capabilities as a specialized national insurer. VidaSeguros EG positions itself as a strategic partner for companies in Equatorial Guinea seeking to stabilize their teams and promote sustainable growth.
