GETESA discusses AI impact at Malabo conference

The telecommunications firm shared insights on artificial intelligence in human resources and business efficiency.

Source: Real Equatorial Guinea · 2026-11-02 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Equatorial Guinea Telecommunications S.A. (GETESA) participated in the "AI Business Malabo 2026" international conference held in Malabo from October 28 to 30. The company's administrative section head, Carolina Obama Elonga, spoke as a panelist on artificial intelligence's role in human resources.

Elonga shared GETESA's views on how AI can boost efficiency, optimize processes, and enhance customer experience and service quality. She stated that GETESA supports responsible digital transformation where technological innovation and human talent advance together, emphasizing the company's ethical approach to technological developments.

The conference, organized by the IDENTIC Foundation at KOIA Innovation, aimed to bring practical AI applications to businesses and professionals. GETESA's participation as a sponsor reaffirmed its commitment to the responsible use of technology and digital transformation for improved daily operations.

Why it mattersGETESA, a telecommunications company, is exploring how artificial intelligence can improve its business operations and services.
