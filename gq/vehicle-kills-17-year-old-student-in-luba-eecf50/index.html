Vehicle kills 17-year-old student in Luba

Emilia Mekina Edu, a pupil at the Colegio María Inmaculada de Batete, died at the scene after a Toyota RAV4 struck her on the road to Boloko.

Sources: Ahora EG and Real Equatorial Guinea · 2026-11-16 · 1 min read

A 17-year-old student, Emilia Mekina Edu, was killed in Luba after a Toyota RAV4 with registration AN-679-E hit her while she was walking towards Boloko. The impact was so violent that she died where she was struck, and local outlets report that one of her lower limbs was thrown into a nearby wooded area, where a search was started.

She was the daughter of Juan Edu Edu, a professor and director of the Centro Integrado de Luba, and Cirila Andeme. The driver, reportedly Jaime Alfaro Ndjemua from Musola, was detained by police after the crash. Sources say the collision may have happened while the driver was overtaking, but authorities have not confirmed this.

The circumstances remain under investigation, and officials will determine how the crash happened and who is responsible. The search for the missing limb had no reported outcome at the time of writing, and the Colegio María Inmaculada de Batete is mourning one of its students.

Why it mattersThe driver faces an official inquiry that will decide responsibility for the death of a pupil and the family of the victim await its findings.
