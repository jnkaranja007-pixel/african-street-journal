Equatorial Guinea appoints first resident ambassador to Botswana

Domingo Mituy Edjang presented credentials in Gaborone on November 3, aiming to boost bilateral ties.

Source: Real Equatorial Guinea · 2026-11-08 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Equatorial Guinea has established its first resident ambassador in Botswana, with Domingo Mituy Edjang presenting his credentials to Botswana's President on November 3. This appointment aims to strengthen diplomatic relations between Ciudad de la Paz and Gaborone.

Ambassador Mituy Edjang also met with Botswana's Ministry of International Affairs to review bilateral cooperation and identify priority sectors. The following day, he met with the Botswana Investment and Trade Centre (BITC) to promote economic diplomacy, trade, and investment opportunities.

During the BITC meeting, Mituy Edjang highlighted Equatorial Guinea's economic potential and invited Botswanan investors to explore business prospects. The ambassador also presented the country's tourism and cultural attractions.

Why it mattersEquatorial Guinea's appointment of its first resident ambassador to Botswana aims to foster stronger diplomatic and economic ties between the two nations.
