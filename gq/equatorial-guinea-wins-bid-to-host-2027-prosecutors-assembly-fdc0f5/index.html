Equatorial Guinea wins bid to host 2027 prosecutors' assembly

The Portuguese-speaking community's prosecutors approved the country's candidacy by consensus, with nine votes in favour and none against, on 11 November.

Sources: Ahora EG and Real Equatorial Guinea · 2026-11-18 · 1 min read

Equatorial Guinea will preside over and organise the twenty-fourth Assembly of Attorneys General of the Community of Portuguese Language Countries (CPLP) and the Prosecutor of the Macao Special Administrative Region (RAEM) in 2027, according to Ahora EG. Attorney General Anatolio Nzang Nguema presented the bid to the plenary at the twenty-third assembly in Macao, held from 9 to 14 November 2026.

The prosecutors and the RAEM prosecutor reached their decision on 11 November, after reviewing the country's candidacy memorandum. Real Equatorial Guinea reported that Nzang Nguema returned home on Monday 16 November.

The bid process began five years ago. Nzang Nguema stressed the country's growing role in the community's legal cooperation activities, the outlet reported. The 2027 meeting was originally scheduled to take place on the date set out in the assembly's programme.
