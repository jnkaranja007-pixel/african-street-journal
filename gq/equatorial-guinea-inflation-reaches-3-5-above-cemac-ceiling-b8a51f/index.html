Equatorial Guinea inflation reaches 3.5%, above CEMAC ceiling

Consumer prices rose 0.7% from September, with Malabo at 5.2% and dairy prices up 14.5% over the year.

Sources: Ahora EG and Real Equatorial Guinea · 2026-11-20 · 1 min read

Equatorial Guinea’s inflation rate rose to 3.5% in October from 3.0% in September, exceeding the 3% reference ceiling set by the Central African Economic and Monetary Community (CEMAC). The consumer price index showed underlying inflation of 4.0%, while prices overall rose 0.7% in a month.

Food and non-alcoholic drink inflation was 3.5%; dairy prices rose 14.5%, coffee, tea and cocoa 12.1%, and cebú beef 10.5%. Transport inflation was also 3.5%, including a 15.6% increase in car prices. Malabo recorded the highest city rate, 5.2%, followed by Ebibeyin at 5.1%.

Evinayong’s rate was 2.9%, while Mongomo recorded 2.0% and Bata 1.8%. Transport had the largest monthly price increase, at 2.6% from September.

Why it mattersHouseholds in Malabo face the country’s highest reported inflation rate, 5.2%, while food prices rose 3.5% nationally in October.
