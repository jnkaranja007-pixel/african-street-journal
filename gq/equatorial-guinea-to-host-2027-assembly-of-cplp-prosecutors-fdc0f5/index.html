Equatorial Guinea to host 2027 assembly of CPLP prosecutors general

The Lusophone bloc's prosecutors voted nine to none to give the country the XXIV Assembly, which it will preside over and organise.

Sources: Ahora EG and Real Equatorial Guinea · 2026-11-17 · 1 min read

Equatorial Guinea will host the XXIV Assembly of Prosecutors General of the Community of Portuguese Language Countries (CPLP) and of Macao's prosecutor in 2027. The decision was taken on November 11 at the XXIII Assembly in Macao, held from November 9 to 14, 2026, after a process that began five years ago.

Anatolio Nzang Nguema, the country's Prosecutor General, presented the candidacy to the plenary. The prosecutors general voted by consensus, nine in favour and none against, after examining the bid dossier. The country will preside over and organise the next assembly.

Nzang Nguema returned home on Monday, November 16, after taking part in the Macao meeting. The outlet describes the win as consolidating the country's role in international legal cooperation.

Why it mattersEquatorial Guinea must now preside over and organise the 2027 assembly, a role that puts its prosecutors at the centre of the bloc's legal cooperation.
