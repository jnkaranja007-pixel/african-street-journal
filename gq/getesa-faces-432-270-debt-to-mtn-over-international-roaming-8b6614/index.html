GETESA faces $432,270 debt to MTN over international roaming

Diario Rombe reports that the state telecom operator faces roaming cuts and possible court seizures over unpaid debt to the South African company.

Source: Diario Rombe · 2026-11-18 · 1 min read

Equatorial Guinea’s state telecom operator GETESA owes MTN $432,270, according to Diario Rombe, which reported the debt on Tuesday. The outlet said the unpaid amount threatens international roaming services used by citizens abroad.

Citing sources close to GETESA’s new management, Diario Rombe reported that the company faces possible interruptions to roaming, inclusion on global debtors’ registers and threatened court seizures. MTN is a South African telecommunications company.

The report describes GETESA as facing a severe financial and operational crisis. It does not provide a date for any roaming cut or court action.

Why it mattersEquatorial Guineans abroad who rely on GETESA roaming face possible service interruptions, while the company also faces threatened court seizures over the $432,270 debt.
