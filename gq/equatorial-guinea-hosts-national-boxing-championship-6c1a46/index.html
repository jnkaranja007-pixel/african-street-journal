Equatorial Guinea hosts national boxing championship

Over 70 boxers from 14 clubs will compete in Malabo for the National Boxing Championship 2026.

Source: Ahora EG · 2026-11-15 · 1 min read

Malabo will host the 2026 National Boxing Championship, an event organized by the Equatorial Guinean Boxing Federation (FEGUIBOX). More than 70 boxers from 14 clubs across the country are expected to participate.

The championship will take place in two venues: the Malabo Sports Center for preliminary bouts and semifinals, and the Caracolas Gymnasium for the finals on December 6. Nine clubs are based in Malabo, with five from the Continental Region.

Competitors will vie for titles in eight weight categories ranging from 50 to 85 kilograms, aligning with World Boxing's Olympic boxing standards. The event aims to assess national boxers' skill levels and discover new talent.

Why it mattersThe national boxing championship will take place on December 6 in Malabo, involving over 70 boxers.
