Equatorial Guinea and Singapore agree to continue cooperation talks

Vice-President Nguema Obiang Mangue delivered President Teodoro Obiang Nguema Mbasogo’s message to Singapore’s president during an official visit.

Sources: Ahora EG and Real Equatorial Guinea · 2026-11-19 · 1 min read

Equatorial Guinea’s Vice-President Nguema Obiang Mangue delivered a personal message from President Teodoro Obiang Nguema Mbasogo to Singapore President Tharman Shanmugaratnam on 17 November, during an official visit to Singapore. The message expressed the Equatorial Guinean president’s wish to strengthen friendship and expand cooperation between the countries.

Shanmugaratnam highlighted Equatorial Guinea’s opening of an embassy in Singapore, saying the diplomatic mission would ease contact between the two governments and the development of joint projects. The two presidents’ offices did not announce a specific project at the audience.

Shanmugaratnam and Nguema Obiang Mangue agreed to continue talks on the cooperation initiatives they had discussed.

Why it mattersEquatorial Guinea’s government must continue discussions with Singapore to define the cooperation initiatives examined at the 17 November audience.
