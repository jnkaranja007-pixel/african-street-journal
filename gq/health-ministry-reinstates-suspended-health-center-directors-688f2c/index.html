Health Ministry Reinstates Suspended Health Center Directors

Officials returned to their posts at five health centers and a hospital emergency unit, reversing interim appointments.

The Ministry of Health and Sanitary Infrastructures has reinstated previously suspended directors at health centers in Evinayong, Djibloho, Mongomo, Ebibeyin, and the Bata Zonal Hospital Emergency Unit. This action invalidates appointments made during an interim period and aims to ensure more efficient health management, service continuity, and adherence to administrative legality.

Manuel Eribó Elobo, Inspector General of Sanitary Services for the Continental Region, presided over the formal handover of duties. He emphasized that the government's commitment to strengthening the national health system for the benefit of the population underpins this decision. The Ministry reaffirms its commitment to transparency and efficiency in public health services.

The move is intended to bolster the management of these health facilities. The Inspector General guaranteed a formal administrative delivery and stressed that the intervention aligns with governmental directives to improve public health services. This reintegration seeks to restore established administrative processes and strengthen the operational capacity of the affected health centers.

Why it mattersSuspended health directors have been reinstated, affecting the management and operational continuity of five health centers and one hospital emergency unit.
