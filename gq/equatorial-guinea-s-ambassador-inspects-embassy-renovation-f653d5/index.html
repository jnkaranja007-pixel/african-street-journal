Equatorial Guinea's ambassador inspects embassy renovation in Brussels

Ambassador Miguel Oyono Ndong Mifumu reviewed the progress of works on the 450-square-metre government-funded building, begun in June 2026.

Source: Real Equatorial Guinea · 2026-11-19 · 1 min read

Equatorial Guinea's ambassador to Belgium, Miguel Oyono Ndong Mifumu, inspected renovation and reconstruction work at the country's embassy in Brussels on Tuesday, 17 November. The state-owned building, on Place Guy d'Arezzo, covers 450 square metres and is being renovated by the company Jazz UP.

During the visit he toured the building's floors and departments and met the company's director, Benedicte Thonnard, and its technical staff, who explained the progress of the work and showed images of how the premises will look when finished. He congratulated Thonnard and the technical team on the work done.

The works, financed by the Equatorial Guinean government, began in early June 2026 and cover the full rehabilitation, reconstruction and modernisation of the building. Members of the diplomatic mission accredited to Belgium, the Netherlands, Luxembourg, Denmark and the European Union also took part in the inspection.

Why it mattersStaff at the mission in Brussels will work from a building that is being fully rebuilt and modernised, financed by the government in Malabo.
