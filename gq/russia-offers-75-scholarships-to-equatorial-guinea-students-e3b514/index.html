Russia offers 75 scholarships to Equatorial Guinea students

Alexander Kulikov highlights university training, Russian language learning, and cultural exchange projects.

Source: Real Equatorial Guinea · 2026-11-10 · 1 min read

Alexander Kulikov, an African studies expert and literary translator, has proposed new avenues for educational and cultural cooperation between Equatorial Guinea and Russia. He emphasized opportunities in university training, scholarships, Russian language learning, and cultural and literary exchange projects that could foster closer ties between the two nations.

Kulikov, who holds a doctorate from St. Petersburg State University, noted that Russia annually offers 75 scholarships to citizens of Equatorial Guinea. He recalled a June 2026 visit by a delegation from Moscow Technical University of Communications and Informatics to explore collaboration in telecommunications, cybersecurity, and artificial intelligence.

The expert believes studying in Russia can equip young Equatoguineans with professional skills and international connections. Russian President Vladimir Putin also mentioned the training of Equatoguinean officials at St. Petersburg State University and the Russian Presidential Academy during an October 27 meeting in Moscow.

Why it mattersYoung Equatoguineans can access higher education and professional development through Russian scholarships and exchange programs.
