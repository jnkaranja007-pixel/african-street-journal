Equatorial Guinea appoints first ambassador to Botswana

Domingo Mituy Edjang Adugu presented credentials to President Duma Gideon Boko, aiming to boost economic ties.

Source: Ahora EG · 2026-11-06 · 1 min read

Domingo Mituy Edjang Adugu has become Equatorial Guinea's first ambassador to Botswana, presenting his credentials to President Duma Gideon Boko. The two nations established diplomatic relations in 2006 and reaffirmed them in 2025.

Upon accreditation, Ambassador Edjang met with the Botswana Investment and Trade Centre to promote Equatorial Guinea as an investment destination and invited Botswana firms to explore its market. He also held a technical meeting with Botswana's Ministry of International Affairs to review bilateral cooperation.

The ambassador's initial activities signal a focus on economic diplomacy and institutional outreach. Equatorial Guinea aims to foster investment and strengthen ties with the southern African nation.
