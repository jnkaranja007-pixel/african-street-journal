SPLM team arrives in Eastern Equatoria to reorganize party

A high-level committee will engage members and organize structures from bomas to payams and counties.

Source: Radio Tamazuj · 2026-11-03 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

A high-level committee from the Sudan People’s Liberation Movement (SPLM) Party arrived in Eastern Equatoria State on Friday to reorganize the party’s structures from the grassroots.

Mary Paul Unde, secretary of the organizing committee, stated the mission aims to strengthen internal organization before primary elections, which will nominate governors and MPs. Akile Mboya Peter, the SPLM state secretary for Eastern Equatoria, welcomed the committee’s arrival as an important step for the party’s congress programme.

The committee's stay duration depends on the congress process progress across the state, covering boma, payam, and county levels. This exercise marks the beginning of the SPLM's internal reorganization in Eastern Equatoria as it prepares for future political activities.

Why it mattersThe SPLM party is reorganizing its internal structures and preparing for primary elections to nominate political leaders.
