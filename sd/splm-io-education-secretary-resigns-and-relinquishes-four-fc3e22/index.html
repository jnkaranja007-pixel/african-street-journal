SPLM-IO education secretary resigns and relinquishes four positions

Gatkuoth Lok Gatwich Diet said he would leave party politics for academic, professional and other interests outside the movement.

Source: Radio Tamazuj · 2026-11-28 · 1 min read

Gatkuoth Lok Gatwich Diet, the Sudan People’s Liberation Movement-in-Opposition (SPLM-IO) secretary for Education, Science and Technology, has resigned from the party, effective immediately. His resignation letter, dated November 23, also relinquished his other party roles.

In an email to Radio Tamazuj on Friday, Lok said he was leaving four positions: education secretary in the General Secretariat, secretary of the National Liberation Council Standing Committee on Education, Science and Technology, a council membership and party membership. He cited personal reasons and said he planned to pursue academic, professional and other interests outside active politics.

The SPLM-IO remains split between factions led by interim leader Regina Joseph Kaba and Oyet Nathaniel Pierino after a leadership dispute following chairman Dr. Riek Machar’s detention in Juba in March 2025. South Sudan’s general election is scheduled for February 11, 2027.

Why it mattersSPLM-IO leadership must address vacancies across four party roles after Lok’s immediate departure.
