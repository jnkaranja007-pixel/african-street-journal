Sudan's army urges international action over alleged executions in Blue Nile

The military says footage appeared to show captured soldiers being killed after RSF fighters took them during fighting in Dinring on Friday.

Sources: Radio Tamazuj, Sudan War Monitor and Anadolu Africa · 2026-11-23 · 1 min read

Sudan’s army on Sunday accused the paramilitary Rapid Support Forces (RSF) of executing captured soldiers in Blue Nile state and called for urgent international action to protect those still held. The RSF had released videos showing the capture of several army personnel during fighting in Dinring on Friday, 20 November.

In a statement carried by the state-run Sudan News Agency, the military condemned the alleged killings but gave no death toll. The army asked the United Nations, the African Union and other international and regional bodies to protect prisoners and ensure accountability. The RSF had not immediately responded to the allegations.

Emergency Lawyers, a Sudanese legal group, said it was examining videos circulated on November 20 to verify the footage and establish the circumstances. It said confirmed executions could amount to war crimes and urged all parties to respect prisoners’ safety and allow humanitarian access.

Why it mattersThe army is asking international bodies to act to protect soldiers who remain in captivity.
