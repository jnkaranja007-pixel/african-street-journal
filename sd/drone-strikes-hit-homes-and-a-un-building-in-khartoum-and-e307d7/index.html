Drone strikes hit homes and a UN building in Khartoum and Omdurman

Drones struck the capital at dawn on Sunday, damaging a United Nations building while its staff were present.

Sources: Radio Dabanga and Africanews · 2026-11-23 · 1 min read

Drone attacks struck Khartoum and Omdurman early on Sunday, Radio Dabanga reported, injuring at least 10 people and damaging homes and a United Nations building in the capital. Residents across Khartoum woke to successive explosions at dawn.

In Omdurman's El Thawra neighbourhood, a drone believed to belong to the paramilitary Rapid Support Forces crashed into a house in Block 95, killing four children from a family displaced from En Nuhoud in West Kordofan. In Jabal Awliya, south of Khartoum, a drone struck a family home in the El Assal area. The UN Resident and Humanitarian Coordinator in Sudan, Denise Brown, said UN staff were present when the building was hit and that humanitarian personnel and assets are protected under international humanitarian law.

In Arkaweet, a projectile struck a family home, causing damage but no casualties. Security services and an explosives team began removing the wreckage and projectile from another crash site in the eastern villages area.

Why it mattersDisplaced families in Omdurman and Khartoum face continued drone strikes, and UN premises with staff inside were damaged.
