African Union chief backs Ethiopia's proposed Sudan dialogue

Mahamoud Ali Youssouf says any talks must be Sudanese-led and preserve Sudan's sovereignty and unity, while the transitional government says it is ready to engage.

Sources: Sudan War Monitor and Altaghyeer · 2026-11-27 · 1 min read

The African Union Commission Chairman, Mahamoud Ali Youssouf, said on Thursday, 26 November, that the bloc was interested in Ethiopia's proposal to host an inclusive Sudanese dialogue. In a statement from Addis Ababa, he reaffirmed the union's commitment to a peaceful, comprehensive resolution of Sudan's conflict through talks and negotiations.

Youssouf said any such process must be led and owned by Sudanese people and aim at lasting peace while preserving Sudan's sovereignty, unity and territorial integrity. Ethiopian Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed announced the invitation on 23 November, calling on Sudanese military, political and civilian groups to take part. Sudan's transitional government welcomed the offer, its information minister and spokesman Khalid Ahmed Danaa said.

The African Union also said it would support Burundi's President Évariste Ndayishimiye, the bloc's chair, in pursuing a lasting peaceful solution with relevant partners.

Why it mattersSudanese military, political and civilian groups now face a decision on whether to join a dialogue Ethiopia will host.
