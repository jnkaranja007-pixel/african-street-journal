Drone attacks kill civilians in Sudan’s Blue Nile cities

The resistance committees say strikes hit homes in Damazin and Roseires on Tuesday, killing about 14 civilians and injuring 15.

Sources: Altaghyeer and Sudan War Monitor · 2026-11-26 · 1 min read

Drone attacks attributed to Sudan’s Rapid Support Forces struck residential areas in Damazin and Roseires, in Blue Nile state, on Tuesday evening, killing about 14 civilians and injuring 15, Altaghyeer reported.

The Resistance Committees of Sennar said three civilian families were killed in the Salha area, northwest of Damazin. They also reported strikes in Rayh Balak and Airport District in Damazin, and the Southern District of Roseires. The committees called for an independent, transparent investigation to identify those responsible and ensure accountability.

The committees urged a nationwide ban on military aircraft and drones, an end to attacks on civilian areas and facilities, and an immediate ceasefire with humanitarian access.

Why it mattersThe Resistance Committees of Sennar are seeking an independent investigation into the attacks and accountability for those responsible.
