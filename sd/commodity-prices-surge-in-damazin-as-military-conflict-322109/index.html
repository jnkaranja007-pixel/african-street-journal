Commodity prices surge in Damazin as military conflict spreads

Essential goods become scarce and household incomes dwindle as fighting expands to Bau locality.

Source: Radio Tamazuj · 2026-11-17 · 1 min read

Residents of Damazin, the capital of Sudan's Blue Nile Region, face soaring commodity prices and severe hardship as military operations spread to Bau locality. The Jebel Al-Tin and Mayful areas came under attack on 13 November by forces affiliated with the Tasis Alliance, expanding hostilities that already encompass Kurmuk, Geissan and Wad Al-Mahi localities.

Rami Hassan, a local resident, stated that livelihoods have been disrupted and sources of income have virtually disappeared. Market surveys by Radio Tamazuj established that a kilogram of beef increased from SDG 32,000 to 48,000, and a liter of milk rose from SDG 2,500 to 4,000.

A liter of cooking oil climbed from SDG 10,000 to 12,000, while a malwa of onions rose from SDG 16,000 to 20,000. A sack of sugar increased from SDG 415,000 to 450,000, and a 50-kilogramme sack of flour went from 367,000 to SDG 397,000.

Why it mattersResidents in Damazin face severe price hikes, with a sack of sugar rising to SDG 450,000 and beef reaching SDG 48,000 per kilogram amid spreading military confrontations.
