Rebel forces push within about 40 kilometers of Sudan's Damazin

Allied fighters took the Bau locality headquarters and entered Madina 11, while some Damazin residents have begun leaving for safer areas.

Sources: Sudan War Monitor and Radio Tamazuj · 2026-11-22 · 1 min read

On Thursday and Friday, fighters from the Rapid Support Forces and the SPLM-North took Bau, the administrative seat of Bau locality, and moved into Madina 11 in Geisan locality. Videos filmed by RSF fighters and verified by Sudan War Monitor show them outside the Bau headquarters building.

The army pulled back from Bau to Miganza, about 20 kilometers to the north, according to Sudan War Monitor, which said the Geisan advance has brought the fighting to within about 40 kilometers of Damazin. Some residents have begun leaving, and a resident, Elham Kamal, told Radio Tamazuj that others want to go but cannot afford to travel to safer areas.

Sennar state, to the east, has imposed a night curfew and restrictions on goods transport and hot-drink sales from 8 p.m. to 5 a.m., because of fears the fighting could spread there. Sennar's governor issued Emergency Order No. 6 of 2026 on Saturday.

Why it mattersDamazin residents are leaving as the fighting approaches, and some who want to flee cannot afford the journey to safer areas.
