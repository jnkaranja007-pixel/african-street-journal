White Nile launches sorghum harvest as yields top 17 sacks per feddan

A Radio Tamazuj survey found sorghum selling for up to SDG 1.05 million a sack in Rabak on 16 November.

Source: Radio Tamazuj · 2026-11-20 · 1 min read

White Nile State launched the summer sorghum harvest on Wednesday in its Nile Pump Irrigation Schemes, with yields above 17 sacks per feddan in some areas. The state cultivated about 2,488,902 feddans during the 2026 summer season.

Al-Mahi Abdallah, a resident of the Um Sangour Al-Shati area, said yields there exceeded 16 sacks per feddan despite challenges affecting the season. A Radio Tamazuj survey in Rabak on 16 November found sacks of Tabat, Wad Ahmed and hybrid sorghum at SDG 950,000, while Feterita reached SDG 1.05 million.

State Agriculture and Production Minister Abdel-Samie Musa said the ministry was exploring alternative energy for farm operations and prioritising higher productivity and self-sufficiency in seed production across the state.

Why it mattersSorghum buyers in White Nile markets now pay SDG 950,000 to 1.05 million a sack, and the harvest is the first test of whether supply eases those prices.
