Sudan's army uses El Obeid as base for westward push against RSF

Former rebels, civilian volunteers and Islamist fighters are helping government forces advance, and the alliance itself is raising concerns about what follows the war.

Source: The Africa Report · 2026-11-25 · 1 min read

El Obeid, a strategic city in central Sudan, has become the launchpad for the Sudanese Armed Forces' westward offensive against the Rapid Support Forces (RSF), according to The Africa Report.

The outlet's reporting from the city finds that former rebels, civilian volunteers and Islamist fighters are fighting alongside government forces as they gain ground against the RSF.

The Africa Report says the alliance could become a headache long after the fighting ends, and it gives no timetable for the offensive.
