RSF and Tasis announce capture of Bau headquarters in Blue Nile

Their 20 November claim covers Bau Locality and Village 12, while Sudan’s army says it repelled an attack at Dering.

Source: Radio Tamazuj · 2026-11-24 · 1 min read

Sudan’s Rapid Support Forces (RSF) and the Sudan Founding Alliance, known as Tasis, said on 20 November they had captured the administrative headquarters of Bau Locality and Village 12 on the Geissan front in Blue Nile State. The Sudanese Armed Forces (SAF) said its Fourth Infantry Division repelled an attack at Dering on the Kurmuk front.

The latest fighting is part of an escalation in recent weeks across areas along the Kurmuk and Geissan fronts, with control of positions shifting. Village 12 is about 40 kilometers from Damazin, the regional administrative and military center; Bau and Dering are forward positions on an axis leading toward the capital.

Tasis forces said in early November that they had taken Sali, on the road linking Kurmuk and Damazin and near mining areas. Retired Air Force Lieutenant Al-Tayyib Malkabi told Radio Tamazuj that resources and supply routes are increasingly shaping military operations as diplomatic initiatives stall and displacement and humanitarian conditions worsen.

Why it mattersForces contesting the Kurmuk-Damazin road and nearby mining areas face changing control of positions as fighting spreads on two fronts.
