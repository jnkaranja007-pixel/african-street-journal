Sudan's army retakes Al-Khuwai and pushes toward Al-Nahud

Government forces have taken the town from the Rapid Support Forces, which had held it since May 2025.

Source: Sudan War Monitor · 2026-11-19 · 1 min read

Government forces and allied armed groups have returned to West Kordofan State, capturing Al-Khuwai and advancing toward Al-Nahud. The move has forced the Rapid Support Forces (RSF) to pull back from several positions west and southwest of El-Obeid, the capital of North Kordofan State, after an offensive that began on November 5.

By November 13 the army had entered Al-Khuwai, the administrative headquarters of Al-Khuwai Locality, and taken Serif Ankoush, Umm Labana, Al-Dudiya and other villages nearby. The advance resumed the next day toward Al-Morkib, about 60 kilometres east of Al-Nahud, where the RSF keeps one of its main positions in West Kordofan.

Fighting continues across North Kordofan. The army captured Abu Haraz in southern Sheikan Locality on Wednesday, but its push south along the road from El-Obeid toward South Kordofan, through Kazgil and Al-Hamadi, did not secure lasting control.

Why it mattersResidents of Al-Khuwai now live under army control after the RSF-held town was taken on November 13.
