Rebel coalition captures Sali town in Blue Nile

Rapid Support Forces and allied fighters advanced from two directions to take the army-held town on Tuesday.

Source: Sudan War Monitor · 2026-11-04 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

The Rapid Support Forces (RSF) and allied Sudan People’s Liberation Movement-North (SPLM-N) fighters captured Sali in southern Blue Nile State on Tuesday. The fighters advanced from two directions, moving north from Kurmuk and eastward from Sirkum, after repeated attempts to take the army-held town. RSF soldiers were seen outside the Sali health centre, confirming their entry into the town.

The Sudan Founding Alliance (Ta’sis), which includes the RSF and SPLM-N, announced its forces had captured the strategic Sali military base. The statement claimed that fighting left a large number of army personnel dead or captured, with weapons and military equipment seized. Ta’sis coalition forces stated their commitment to protecting civilians in liberated areas and urged residents to stay away from military operations.

The Ta’sis coalition called these victories a significant shift in the fighting and a strategic step towards ending the dominance of extremist groups. They reaffirmed their determination to continue advancing until remaining strongholds are liberated. The coalition stated these victories represent another step towards the dawn of liberation and the construction of a Sudanese state founded on justice, equality and the rule of law.

Why it mattersFighting in Blue Nile State has resulted in rebel forces capturing the town of Sali and seizing military equipment.
