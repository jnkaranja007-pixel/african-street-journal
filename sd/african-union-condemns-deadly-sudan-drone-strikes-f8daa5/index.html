African Union condemns deadly Sudan drone strikes

The AU chairperson calls for an immediate humanitarian ceasefire after over 250 reported deaths.

Source: Radio Dabanga · 2026-10-24 · 1 min read

The African Union has condemned drone attacks that killed scores of civilians across Sudan and the disputed Abyei area. Reports indicate over 250 people have died in strikes over several days. The AU Commission chairperson, Mahmoud Ali Youssouf, called for an immediate humanitarian ceasefire.

Drone attacks on Monday in Abyei reportedly killed dozens of civilians, including women and children, near Amiet and Todach markets. Media reports citing local and medical sources say about 80 people were killed in El Dibb, West Kordofan, on October 18, while 23 died in Nyala, South Darfur, on October 16. At least three children were killed in Khartoum on October 19.

In Abyei, a drone attack on Ameet market on October 19 has reportedly killed more than 150 people. Youssouf urged all parties to protect civilians and humanitarian personnel, and ensure unhindered humanitarian access. He also stressed the need to respect Sudan’s sovereignty and territorial integrity.

Why it mattersDrone attacks have killed civilians, caused displacement, and disrupted access to food and healthcare in Sudan.
