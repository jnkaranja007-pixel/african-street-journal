University of Zalingei finalizes preparations to reopen

The university, closed after insecurity and fighting in the city, is addressing campus security, electricity, drinking water and displaced residents.

Source: Radio Tamazuj · 2026-11-28 · 1 min read

The University of Zalingei in Sudan’s Central Darfur state has completed preparations to reopen and resume academic activities, its administration announced. The university has been closed following insecurity and armed confrontations in the city.

Chancellor Awadallah Al-Douma said the administration and the state government reviewed campus security and essential services, including electricity and safe drinking water. Central Darfur government Secretary-General Amer Hamdan said representatives of the State Security Committee and the Sudanese Agency for Relief and Humanitarian Operations also discussed reopening measures. He said forces operating under the Tasis Alliance had adopted the arrangements.

Hamdan said the relief agency was responsible for helping internally displaced people sheltering on campus relocate safely to designated sites in eastern Zalingei. The university still needs facility rehabilitation, restored services and safety measures for students and staff before classes resume.

Why it mattersDisplaced people sheltering on campus are to be moved safely to designated sites in eastern Zalingei by the relief agency.
