Damazin transport fares climb as residents seek to leave

Bus tickets to Khartoum cost up to SDG 220,000 as fewer vehicles and security fears leave families struggling to find seats.

Source: Radio Tamazuj · 2026-11-23 · 1 min read

Transport fares from Damazin, the capital of Sudan’s Blue Nile Region, have risen as residents seek to leave amid security fears and fewer available vehicles. Radio Tamazuj found bus tickets to Khartoum priced at SDG 180,000 to 220,000, while a private intercity taxi cost about SDG 400,000 per passenger; hiring the whole taxi cost as much as SDG 4 million.

Residents told Radio Tamazuj that fares had jumped over the past few days. Damazin resident Rami Kamal said he could not secure a ticket despite the high prices. Crowding at terminals and difficulty finding tickets are affecting families trying to leave, while another resident, Tariq Al-Tayyib, said many could not afford the journey.

Blue Nile State government spokesperson Saif Al-Nasr Minallah said government forces were advancing and urged residents to continue daily activities. He described the region’s security as stable, while residents also raised concerns about the safety of travel routes.

Why it mattersFamilies who cannot afford fares of about SDG 400,000 per taxi passenger are left stranded in Damazin while those who can pay leave.
