Sudan lifts ban on 46 imports amid rising inflation

The Sovereignty Council chairman cancelled a cabinet decision as the dollar climbed to SDG 10,300 and annual inflation hit 28.29%.

Source: Radio Dabanga · 2026-10-23 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Sudan has lifted import restrictions on 46 goods as the dollar reached SDG 10,300 and annual inflation rose to 28.29%. Sovereignty Council chairman Abdelfattah El Burhan cancelled a cabinet decision that had banned these imports, aiming to ease pressure on consumers facing soaring food prices.

Finance minister Jibril Ibrahim stated the decision considers consumer needs while protecting domestic producers. Traders reported an immediate price fall for some goods. The National Importers’ Chamber welcomed the move but requested a clear decision to cancel Resolution No. 74, which had imposed the ban and disrupted markets.

Foreign-exchange dealers reported the dollar rose to SDG 10,200 for buying and SDG 10,300 for selling on Thursday, an increase of SDG 100. Annual inflation reached 28.29% in September, up from 26.14% in August, with food and drink prices rising 17.26% monthly.

Why it mattersSudanese consumers face continued pressure from rising prices and a depreciating currency.
