Founding Alliance claims control of five Blue Nile areas and captures troops

The group said it seized 27 vehicles and six tanks; the state government says the army is working to restore security.

Sources: Sudan War Monitor and Altaghyeer · 2026-11-21 · 1 min read

The Founding Alliance, a Sudanese armed group, said it captured soldiers and officers and took control of five areas in Blue Nile state, including Khormak and Al Madina 10. Its claims were reported by Sudan War Monitor on Friday, 20 November.

The outlet cited a field commander from the Rapid Support Forces in a social-media video saying the alliance inflicted heavy losses on the army. It also reported that the group seized 27 vehicles, six tanks and two armoured vehicles and burned 70 vehicles. These battlefield claims were not independently confirmed in the report.

Blue Nile state government spokesman Saif al-Nasr Min Allah said the government was coordinating with the army’s Fourth Infantry Division to restore security in the south of the state. He rejected claims circulating online that the force was close to Damazin, the state capital.
