Armed robberies spread across seven Damazin neighbourhoods

Residents say gunmen are targeting pedestrians after sunset, with reports of incidents continuing through the night.

Source: Radio Tamazuj · 2026-11-21 · 1 min read

Armed robberies have become more frequent across seven residential neighbourhoods in Damazin, the capital of Sudan’s Blue Nile Region, residents told Radio Tamazuj. Armed groups have stopped pedestrians at gunpoint and taken money and personal belongings, according to the report.

Jamal Noreen, a resident of Al-Taif, said robberies had increased since scheduled electricity outages began at 8 p.m. Abdallah Suleiman, of the Al-Rabee Neighborhood Committee, said incidents typically start around 6:30 p.m. and continue through the night. Civil society activist Ahmed Al-Tayyib said the reports had restricted movement after dark and disrupted daily activities; he is documenting complaints from across the city.

Residents have urged security agencies to intervene, but Radio Tamazuj said it had not obtained a response from authorities. The report said fighting between Sudan’s armed forces and allied forces and forces affiliated with the Tasis Alliance continues in several Blue Nile localities, including Bau more recently.

Why it mattersDamazin residents are limiting movement after dark as armed robberies spread across neighbourhoods, and security agencies face calls to intervene.
