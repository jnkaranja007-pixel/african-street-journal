Sudan accuses RSF of executing captured soldiers in Blue Nile

The government says the alleged killings violate international humanitarian law and urges protection for prisoners still in captivity.

Sources: Radio Dabanga and Anadolu Africa · 2026-11-24 · 1 min read

Sudan’s government accused the Rapid Support Forces (RSF) of executing captured Sudanese Armed Forces personnel in the Blue Nile region, calling for accountability. The allegation concerns soldiers taken prisoner during fighting there.

The government said killing a prisoner violates international humanitarian law and appealed for urgent protection for those still held. Activists circulated images they said showed Lieutenant Colonel Osama El Sadig after his capture and execution, but Radio Dabanga said it could not verify them or obtain an immediate response from the RSF spokesperson.

The Tasees Alliance, which includes the RSF and the Sudan People’s Liberation Movement led by Abdelaziz El Hilu, said its forces had taken Bao and seven other areas about 50 kilometres from Ed Damazin. Blue Nile’s regional government said the attacking forces had withdrawn. Yasser Arman of the Sudan People’s Liberation Movement – Democratic Revolutionary Current said the alleged executions, if confirmed, would constitute a war crime.

Why it mattersSudanese forces still held in Blue Nile are the people the government says need urgent protection while it seeks accountability for alleged executions.
