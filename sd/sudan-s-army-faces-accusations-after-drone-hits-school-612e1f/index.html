Sudan's army faces accusations after drone hits school sheltering displaced people in W...

Kordofan Observatory says a drone struck the Musab bin Umair Western School in Ghubaysh, which was sheltering hundreds displaced by fighting around En Nuhud, killing 16.

Source: Radio Tamazuj · 2026-11-22 · 1 min read

A drone struck a displacement shelter in Ghubaysh, West Kordofan State, on Tuesday night, killing at least 16 internally displaced people and injuring 10 others, according to a statement from Kordofan Observatory reported by Radio Tamazuj. The observatory blamed the Sudanese Armed Forces (SAF), saying the drone was allegedly theirs.

The observatory said the drone hit Musab bin Umair Western School at about 8 p.m., and that the school was sheltering hundreds of people who had fled fighting around En Nuhud. Imad Abdel Hadi, executive director of Ghubaysh Locality, said the army had repeatedly used drone strikes against towns and populated areas, and that Ghubaysh had come under successive drone attacks for three consecutive days.

Mohamed Hamid, a Ghubaysh resident, said army drones had repeatedly struck markets, residential neighbourhoods and schools, and called on the army to respect the civilian status of such sites and take precautions to protect civilians during military operations.

Why it mattersDisplaced families who fled En Nuhud are now at risk in Ghubaysh's schools and markets, which residents say are being hit by repeated drone strikes.
