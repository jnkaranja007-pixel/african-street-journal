Sudan loses OPCW council seat as pressure mounts over weapons allegations

The OPCW chief urged countries with evidence to share it and called for Sudan to circulate findings from its national investigation.

Source: Radio Dabanga · 2026-11-26 · 1 min read

Sudan lost its seat on the Organisation for the Prohibition of Chemical Weapons (OPCW) Executive Council during the organisation’s 31st Conference of the States Parties in The Hague, Netherlands. The change follows allegations that chemical weapons were used in Sudan’s ongoing war.

OPCW Director-General Sabrina Dallalfior said Sudan’s case remained before the council and urged countries holding relevant evidence to share it, including confidentially. She said she had offered technical assistance to Sudanese representatives for a national investigation and hoped its findings would be sent to member states.

Britain called on Sudan to complete an impartial investigation and share the results with the OPCW. The United States concluded in April 2025 that the Sudanese Armed Forces had used chemical weapons and imposed sanctions; in March 2026, Washington threatened to reconsider Sudan’s council position.

Why it mattersSudanese officials must complete a national investigation and share its findings with the OPCW; Britain called for the results to be sent in full.
