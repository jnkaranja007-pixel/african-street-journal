Police seek help identifying 11 bodies at Harare hospital mortuary

Police say the unclaimed bodies, recovered from road and mine accident sites and public places, are overwhelming the mortuary's capacity.

Source: NewsDay Zimbabwe · 2026-11-15 · 1 min read

Police in Harare have appealed to the public to help identify 11 bodies held at the Parirenyatwa Group of Hospitals mortuary. Some were recovered from road and mine accident sites, while others were picked up from public places.

In a statement, police said the bodies were stretching the mortuary's carrying capacity. The appeal asks anyone with information about a missing relative or friend to come forward so the dead can be identified.

Until the bodies are identified, families cannot claim them for burial.

Why it mattersUntil families come forward, the bodies cannot be released, and the mortuary has no room to keep them.
