Zimbabwean gamers see streaming chance in GTA VI launch

The first major Grand Theft Auto (GTA) instalment since GTA V, released more than a decade ago, lands on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S.

Source: NewsDay Zimbabwe · 2026-11-19 · 1 min read

Grand Theft Auto VI, the first major instalment in the franchise since GTA V more than a decade ago, is set for release on November 19 on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, NewsDay Zimbabwe reported.

The outlet said the release gives Zimbabwe's growing gaming community a chance to tap into the global esports and digital content economy, with gamers and streamers able to build audiences around the game.

The report said the launch follows years of anticipation, delays and trailer analysis. It gave no figures for local earnings or for the number of Zimbabwean gamers and streamers.
