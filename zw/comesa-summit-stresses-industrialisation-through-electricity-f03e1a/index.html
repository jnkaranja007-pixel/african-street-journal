Comesa summit stresses industrialisation through electricity

African leaders at the 25th Comesa Summit in Harare agreed on the need to transform natural resources into prosperity.

Source: The Standard Zimbabwe · 2026-10-29 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

The 25th Comesa Summit in Harare highlighted Africa's need to move beyond resource extraction and invest in infrastructure for sustainable prosperity. The summit's message focused on the strategic importance of electricity generation for the continent's industrialisation.

As an electrical engineer, the summit's emphasis on transforming natural resources into wealth through infrastructure was a key takeaway. Tanzania's investment in electricity generation was cited as an example of this strategic approach.

The summit's discussions underscored the necessity for Africa to develop its capacity to process raw materials into finished goods, thereby creating more value and fostering economic growth across the continent.

Why it mattersAfrican nations attending the Comesa Summit in Harare are urged to invest in electricity generation to industrialise and create sustainable prosperity.
