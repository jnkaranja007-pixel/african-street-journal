Zimbabwe releases Muchekeranwa finger millet variety with yields up to 2.9 tonnes

Developed by ICRISAT and Marondera University Seed Company, the variety could bring farmers about US$956 more gross revenue per hectare at April 2026 prices.

Sources: Farmers Review Africa, Zimbabwe Independent and The Standard Zimbabwe · 2026-11-19 · 1 min read

Zimbabwe approved Muchekeranwa, a new finger millet variety, for release on 12 November 2026. Developed by the International Crops Research Institute for the Semi-Arid Tropics (ICRISAT) with Marondera University Seed Company Pvt Ltd, it has recorded yields of up to 2.9 tonnes per hectare.

Against a national average of 0.28 tonnes, farmers reaching those yields could earn roughly US$956 more in gross revenue per hectare, before production costs, at April 2026 prices of US$364.75 per tonne. ICRISAT Director General Himanshu Pathak said the variety could improve the economics of finger millet production for farmers, seed producers and food processors.

The variety matures early, which could help farmers cope with shorter and less predictable growing seasons as erratic rainfall and recurring droughts threaten production across Zimbabwe.
