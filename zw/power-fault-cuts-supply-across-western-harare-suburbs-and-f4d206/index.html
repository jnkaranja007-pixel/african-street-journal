Power fault cuts supply across western Harare suburbs and industrial areas

The outage reaches households and businesses in Workington, Aspindale and Tynwald, where production depends on a stable electricity supply.

Source: Pindula News · 2026-11-23 · 1 min read

A fault at the Warren 132kV substation disrupted electricity supply across a large swathe of western Harare, the state electricity company, ZESA Private Limited, said on Saturday. The affected areas include high-density and low-density suburbs as well as industrial zones.

The company said its technical teams were working to repair the fault and restore supply. The interruption affects households and businesses in Workington, Aspindale and Tynwald, where production depends on stable electricity. ZESA warned customers to treat all electrical circuits as live during repairs, because power may return without notice.

The affected places include Kuwadzana, Budiriro, Glen View, Glen Norah, Highfield, Southerton, Mabelreign, Marlborough, Westgate and parts of Belvedere. Customers can call 704 or 08688003485/6 or 0242704040, or WhatsApp +263715519387, for information.
