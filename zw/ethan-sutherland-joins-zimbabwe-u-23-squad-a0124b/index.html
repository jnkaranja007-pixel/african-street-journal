Ethan Sutherland joins Zimbabwe U-23 squad

Wolverhampton Wanderers starlet Ethan Sutherland has reported for duty with the Young Warriors ahead of their Olympic qualifier.

Source: NewsDay Zimbabwe · 2026-11-10 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Zimbabwe has secured the commitment of Wolverhampton Wanderers player Ethan Sutherland for their Under-23 team. Sutherland has officially joined the Young Warriors squad in preparation for their 2027 Los Angeles Olympic Games qualifier against Mali.

The Zimbabwe Under-23 side will host Mali on Wednesday at the National Sports Stadium for their opening qualifier. Sutherland's arrival provides a significant boost to the team before the crucial first leg match.

The Young Warriors will travel to Bamako for the return fixture next Tuesday. This development comes as the team prepares for the challenging qualification campaign.

Why it mattersYoung Zimbabwean footballers will compete in an Olympic qualifier against Mali.
