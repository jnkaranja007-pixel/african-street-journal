ZB Financial Holdings profit after tax falls 31% despite balance sheet growth

The Zimbabwean financial services group's core earnings rose sharply even as its headline profit fell, according to figures reported this week.

Source: Zimbabwe Independent · 2026-11-11 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

ZB Financial Holdings, a Zimbabwean financial services group, reported that its profit after tax fell 31%, to ZiG610 million in the comparable period last year, from ZWG420 million in the latest period. Revenue also declined 10%, to ZWG2.57 billion from ZWG2.86 billion.

The drop in headline profit was offset by sustainable profit, which strips out exchange gains and fair value adjustments. That measure rose to ZiG215 million from ZiG40 million, a sign that earnings from the group's core operations improved.

The Zimbabwe Independent and NewsDay Zimbabwe both reported the results on 11 November 2026, with NewsDay giving the group's profit after tax as ZWG420 million against ZWG610 million a year earlier.

Why it mattersInvestors tracking the group will see that its core operating earnings grew even as reported profit shrank, which changes how its results should be read.
