Harare City win NRSL title and return to PSL

The Sunshine Boys secured promotion after a 3-0 victory over Jumbo Giants at Callies Ground.

Source: NewsDay Zimbabwe · 2026-11-08 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Harare City have been crowned Northern Region Soccer League (NRSL) champions, securing an immediate return to the Premier Soccer League (PSL). They achieved this by defeating Jumbo Giants 3-0 at Callies Ground.

This victory concluded a successful season for the team, coached by Try Ncube, who finished with 83 points from 42 matches. Their win ended second-placed Black Rhinos' promotion hopes in a close title race.

Harare City's triumph means they will compete in the top-tier PSL next season, a significant achievement after their previous relegation.

Why it mattersHarare City Football Club has won the NRSL championship and will play in the Premier Soccer League.
