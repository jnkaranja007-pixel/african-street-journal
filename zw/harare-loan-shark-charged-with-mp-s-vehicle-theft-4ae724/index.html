Harare loan shark charged with MP's vehicle theft

Cleophas Chinonje Chiwaya allegedly stole a Toyota Land Cruiser belonging to Mazowe Central MP Maxmore Njanji.

Source: NewsDay Zimbabwe · 2026-11-07 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Harare-based loan shark Cleophas Chinonje Chiwaya appeared in court on Friday facing charges of theft of trust property. Chiwaya is accused of stealing a Toyota Land Cruiser vehicle owned by Mazowe Central Member of Parliament Maxmore Njanji. Magistrate Sheunesu Matova granted Chiwaya US$300 bail.

The State alleges that in January 2023, an individual identified as [...] entered into an agreement with Chiwaya for the purchase of the vehicle. However, Chiwaya is said to have unlawfully taken possession of the Toyota Land Cruiser without the owner's consent.

The court case is proceeding with further details to be presented. The incident highlights ongoing legal disputes involving property and financial transactions within Harare.
