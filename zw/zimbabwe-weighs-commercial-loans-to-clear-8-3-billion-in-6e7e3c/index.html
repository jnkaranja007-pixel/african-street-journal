Zimbabwe weighs commercial loans to clear $8.3 billion in arrears

Finance Minister Mthuli Ncube told the World Bank that Zimbabwe may borrow at higher rates if concessional funding falls short of its March 2027 deadline.

Source: Pindula News · 2026-11-24 · 1 min read

Zimbabwe is seeking World Bank help to find cheaper loans to repay overdue debts to international lenders by March 2027, but Finance Minister Mthuli Ncube said the government may turn to more expensive commercial borrowing if that funding falls short.

In a letter dated 10 November to World Bank operations chief Anna Bjerde, Ncube said the government was considering both options. Zimbabwe’s public debt stood at US$21.7 billion in June 2026; US$8.3 billion of the US$11.7 billion owed to foreign lenders was arrears and penalties.

The government plans to seek a bridging-loan partner in London, Paris and Berlin and hold talks with Algeria. Treasury says clearing arrears could restore access to development finance; meanwhile, Zimbabwe has accumulated more than US$1.3 billion in debt to local contractors for projects including roads and energy.
