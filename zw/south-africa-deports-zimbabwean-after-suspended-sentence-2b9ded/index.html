South Africa deports Zimbabwean after suspended sentence over Ukraine plans

Wesley Sothole, 24, pleaded guilty to planning to travel to Ukraine; four other Zimbabweans arrested with him have yet to enter pleas.

Source: NewsDay Zimbabwe · 2026-11-25 · 1 min read

South Africa deported Wesley Sothole, a 24-year-old Zimbabwean, after a court handed him a five-year suspended prison sentence for planning to fight in the Russia-Ukraine war. He pleaded guilty at Kempton Park Magistrates' Court.

South African media reports said Sothole was one of five Zimbabweans arrested at OR Tambo International Airport in Johannesburg on November 8. Prosecutors have not said which side the group allegedly planned to fight for.

Court dates now fall to the other four, whose case returns to Kempton Park on December 1. Since the start of this year, a Ukrainian intelligence assessment has put the number of Africans recruited to fight for Russia at more than 1 700, from 36 countries.

Why it mattersSothole has been deported, and his four co-accused face further court proceedings on December 1.
