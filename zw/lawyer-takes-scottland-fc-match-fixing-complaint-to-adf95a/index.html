Lawyer takes Scottland FC match-fixing complaint to Parliament

Lonias Rozvi Majoni says ZIFA and the PSL have not acknowledged his formal complaints, despite a seven-day ultimatum he set them.

Source: NewsDay Zimbabwe · 2026-11-15 · 1 min read

Harare lawyer Lonias Rozvi Majoni has petitioned Parliament over alleged integrity concerns in Zimbabwean football involving Scottland FC. He says the Zimbabwe Football Association (ZIFA) and the Premier Soccer League (PSL) have not acknowledged the formal complaints he lodged about the club's alleged misconduct.

Majoni submitted his complaint to ZIFA and the PSL on November 2, 2026. Neither body has formally acknowledged receipt, even after the seven-day ultimatum he gave them expired. He is now asking Parliament for legislative and oversight intervention on matters he says fall within the State's jurisdiction, while maintaining that football's internal disciplinary processes must stay independent.

The petition places the football governing bodies' handling of the complaint before lawmakers, with Majoni arguing the State should act where he believes the sport's own processes have stalled.
