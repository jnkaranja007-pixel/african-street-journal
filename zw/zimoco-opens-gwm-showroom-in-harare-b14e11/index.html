ZIMOCO opens GWM showroom in Harare

The launch event was attended by government ministers and motoring industry executives.

Source: Zimbabwe Independent · 2026-10-30 · 1 min read

ZIMOCO officially opened its new Great Wall Motors (GWM) showroom in Harare on October 29, 2026. The launch event was a significant development in Zimbabwe’s automotive retail sector, drawing a crowd of government ministers, motoring industry executives, financial institutions, and media.

The ceremony featured entertainment from ZIMOCO brand ambassador Tammy Moyo. The event was described as having pomp and fanfare, marking a notable occasion for the automotive industry in the country.

Guests included members of the media and invited guests who witnessed the opening of the new facility.
