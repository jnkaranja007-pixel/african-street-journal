Zimbabwe arrests two agents over alleged lithium export fraud

The clearing agents allegedly used documents that labelled lithium bound for China as chrome concentrate and named Goromonzi as its source.

Source: Pindula News · 2026-11-19 · 1 min read

Zimbabwe’s Anti-Corruption Commission has arrested two clearing agents in Mutare over an alleged attempt to export lithium to China using documents that described it as chrome concentrate. The suspects, Moses Chipfuwamiti, 30, and Despard Robson Mamvura, 32, appeared before the Mutare Magistrates’ Court on Monday.

The commission alleges the agents supplied export papers for two drivers to process at Forbes Border Post. The documents gave Goromonzi as the mineral’s source, while investigators said the lithium had been collected in Domboshava. The State told the court the case would proceed by summons.

The commission said investigators intercepted two trucks near Forbes Border Post and seized them. They were moved to the Zimbabwe Revenue Authority’s dry port for storage, pending sample extraction, metallurgical examination and further investigations.

Why it mattersThe two accused agents face a court process by summons, and investigators are holding the trucks until mineral samples are examined.
