Zimbabwe approves state-funded bank for veterans’ businesses

The licensed microfinance bank will offer loans for farming, orders and emergencies before expanding into a deposit-taking institution over three to four years.

Source: Pindula News · 2026-11-28 · 1 min read

Zimbabwe’s Cabinet has approved a government-funded microfinance bank to lend to veterans of the liberation struggle, Information Minister Zhemu Soda said. The Veterans Microfinance Bank is intended to provide affordable credit for businesses using land, mining claims and farms allocated to ex-fighters.

Soda said a lack of affordable finance had limited veterans’ ability to use those assets. The bank will offer agribusiness and order-financing loans, personal and group lending, and emergency loans, alongside financial-literacy programmes and digital services. Women and young veterans are among its primary beneficiaries.

The bank will begin as a licensed microfinance institution under the Reserve Bank of Zimbabwe, then develop over three to four years into a deposit-taking, digitally enabled bank. It will be wholly owned by Veterans Investments Holding Company, with technical backing from the People’s Own Savings Bank.

Why it mattersVeterans seeking to turn allocated land, mining claims and farms into businesses will have access to the bank’s planned agribusiness and order-financing loans.
