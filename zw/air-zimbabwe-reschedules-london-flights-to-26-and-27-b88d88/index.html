Air Zimbabwe reschedules London flights to 26 and 27 November

The carrier says the London-to-Harare service will resume on 26 November after a flight was rescheduled, while the Harare-to-London flight moves to 27 November.

Sources: Pindula News and Zimbabwe Independent · 2026-11-25 · 1 min read

Air Zimbabwe said its London-to-Harare flight will resume on 26 November, after a service scheduled for 23 November was rescheduled for operational reasons. The Harare-to-London flight, originally set for 25 November, will now operate on 27 November, the airline said in an update issued on 24 November.

Plus Ultra Líneas Aéreas, the Spanish carrier that supplies the aircraft and crew under a wet lease, suspended all flight operations on 23 November until further notice, grounding its last scheduled service between Spain and Caracas. Air Zimbabwe sells tickets and manages the route commercially, while Plus Ultra provides the aircraft, crew, maintenance and insurance. The route uses a 302-seat Airbus A330-300.

Air Zimbabwe restarted direct Harare-London flights on 22 July 2026, ending a 14-year absence from the route. The service links Harare's Robert Gabriel Mugabe International Airport with London Gatwick's South Terminal.

Why it mattersPassengers booked on the route must follow the revised departure dates, with the London-to-Harare service set for 26 November and the return for 27 November.
