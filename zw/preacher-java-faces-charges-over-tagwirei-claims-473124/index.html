Preacher Java faces charges over Tagwirei claims

Batsirai Java, 43, was arrested for allegedly publishing false statements about Kudakwashe Tagwirei's influence.

Sources: Pindula News and Bulawayo24 · 2026-11-16 · 1 min read

Harare preacher Batsirai Java, 43, appeared in court on Friday facing a charge of publishing false statements prejudicial to the State. Java handed himself to police on Thursday after allegedly recording a telephone conversation with Everson Mushava, who is still at large. The State claims the two published the recording on social media platforms between May 25 and May 28, 2026.

The State alleges that Java and Mushava falsely claimed that ZANU PF Politburo member Kudakwashe Tagwirei is now Zimbabwe's kingmaker and controls all government business. They reportedly claimed the President consults Tagwirei on ministerial appointments and that all ministers and senior security officials report to him. The State contends Java published these statements knowing they were false, intending to undermine public confidence in state institutions.

The case was referred back to the police during vetting at the Harare Magistrates Court due to a lack of a cyber report. Java has since been released from custody, with prosecutors ordering further investigation into the matter. The Herald and Bulawayo24 also reported on the case.
