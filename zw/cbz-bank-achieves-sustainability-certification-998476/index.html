CBZ Bank Achieves Sustainability Certification

The financial institution aims to embed sustainability into its core operations and decision-making processes, strengthening its support for enterprise development.

Source: Zimbabwe Independent · 2026-09-06 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X

CBZ Bank has received a Sustainability Certification as it integrates environmental and social considerations into its business strategy. CBZ Holdings announced the certification on Friday, stating it will enhance the bank's capacity to foster enterprise development and investment within Zimbabwe.

The bank acknowledges its significant role in shaping the economy through capital allocation and investment choices. CBZ Holdings emphasized that sustainability must be central to the bank's governance, business practices, and value creation for stakeholders, moving beyond peripheral considerations.

This certification provides a framework for CBZ Bank to leverage its financial strength and market position to contribute to enterprise growth and investment, supporting the long-term economic competitiveness of Zimbabwe. The bank views this as a foundational step, not an endpoint, with future impact to be measured by its success in strengthening businesses and creating lasting value.

Why it mattersCBZ Bank's new sustainability certification aims to strengthen its ability to support enterprise development and investment, potentially benefiting businesses and the broader Zimbabwean economy.
