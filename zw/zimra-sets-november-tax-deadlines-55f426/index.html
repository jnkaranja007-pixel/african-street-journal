ZIMRA sets November tax deadlines

Taxpayers must submit returns by November 5 and payments by November 10.

Source: Zimbabwe Independent · 2026-11-03 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

The Zimbabwe Revenue Authority (ZIMRA) has announced deadlines for tax returns and payments for October 2026. All taxpayers must submit their returns by November 5, 2026, according to Statutory Instrument 81 of 2025.

Payments for various tax types, including Pay As You Earn (PAYE), Withholding Taxes, Mining Royalties, and Plastic Carrier Bag Tax, are due by November 10, 2026. This notice applies to returns filed for the month ending October 31, 2026.

Other taxes with the same deadlines include Value Chain Withholding Tax, Presumptive Tax Return, Gaming Operators’ Tax, Fast Foods Tax, Remittance of Levy on Specified Minerals, Return for the Remittance of Tobacco Levy, and Presumptive Tax on Rental Income.

Why it mattersZimbabwean taxpayers must meet specific deadlines for submitting tax returns and making payments to avoid penalties.
