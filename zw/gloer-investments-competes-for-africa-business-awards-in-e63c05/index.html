Gloer Investments competes for Africa business awards in Johannesburg

The Zimbabwean engineering and energy firm reached the final stage after ranking first in the national leg of the competition.

Source: Zimbabwe Independent · 2026-11-25 · 1 min read

Zimbabwean engineering, procurement and construction firm Gloer Investments is set to compete for continental honours at the inaugural Standard Bank Africa Business Awards in Johannesburg, South Africa. The awards have reached their final stage, with the contest scheduled for Wednesday, according to the Zimbabwe Independent.

Trading as Gi Glo Energy & Water, Gloer ranked first in Zimbabwe's leg of the competition. The contest launched in September and closed late last month, the report said.

Gloer provides energy and water services, including electrical power infrastructure, solar projects and farms, borehole drilling and equipment, water heating systems and water pumping solutions.

Why it mattersA first-place finish in Zimbabwe lets Gloer, a firm selling power and water systems, go on to the continental final in Johannesburg.
