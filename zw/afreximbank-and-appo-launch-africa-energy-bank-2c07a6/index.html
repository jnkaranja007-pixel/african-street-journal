Afreximbank and APPO launch Africa Energy Bank

The new bank aims to close the continent's energy financing gap, with its first meeting held in Abuja, Nigeria.

Source: Zimbabwe Independent · 2026-11-02 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

The African Export-Import Bank (Afreximbank) and the African Petroleum Producers’ Organisation (APPO) have officially established the Africa Energy Bank (AEB). This initiative is designed to address the growing energy financing gap across the continent. The announcement followed the AEB's inaugural general meeting, which took place in Abuja, Nigeria, on Saturday.

Afreximbank stated on Saturday that the creation of the Africa Energy Bank demonstrates African governments' commitment to securing their own energy future. The establishment of the bank was formalized during the meeting in Abuja.

The Africa Energy Bank's establishment aims to provide financial solutions for energy projects within Africa. Its first official meeting convened in Nigeria's capital city.

Why it mattersAfrican governments are taking steps to secure their own energy future through a new financing institution.
