Hardrock FC clinches Zimbabwe title in debut top-flight season

The Kwekwe side beat FC Platinum 1-0 at Chahwanda Stadium to finish on 73 points, two clear of Scottland FC.

Source: NewsDay Zimbabwe · 2026-11-22 · 1 min read

Hardrock FC won Zimbabwe's Castle Lager Premier Soccer League title on Saturday, beating FC Platinum 1-0 at Chahwanda Stadium. The Kwekwe club secured the championship in its first season in the top flight.

Prince Dube scored the only goal in the first minute, finishing a team move set up by Thubelihle Mavuso. The win took Hardrock to 73 points, NewsDay Zimbabwe reported.

Second-placed Scottland FC finished two points behind Hardrock. The title win prompted celebrations in Kwekwe, a mining town.

Why it mattersHardrock's players and supporters in Kwekwe celebrate the club's first top-flight title, secured with a 73-point finish.
