Invictus Energy faces funding squeeze for Musuma drilling

The Australian-listed firm has A$7,97 million in cash but quarterly outgoings of A$2,85 million.

Source: Zimbabwe Independent · 2026-11-01 · 1 min read

Australian firm Invictus Energy faces a funding challenge for its Musuma-1 drilling in northern Zimbabwe. As of September 30, the company had A$7,97 million (US$5,6 million) in cash reserves.

Quarterly expenditures stood at A$2,85 million (US$2,0 million), meaning current cash could cover less than three quarters of planned spending. Invictus also reported no available loan or credit facilities at the end of the reporting period.

This situation raises questions about how the oil and gas explorer will finance its ongoing drilling programme. The company is preparing to drill its Musuma-1 exploration well.
