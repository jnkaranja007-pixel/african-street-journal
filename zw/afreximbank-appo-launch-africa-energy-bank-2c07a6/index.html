Afreximbank, APPO launch Africa Energy Bank

The new bank aims to close the continent's energy financing gap, with its first meeting held in Abuja.

Source: Zimbabwe Independent · 2026-11-01 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

The African Export-Import Bank (Afreximbank) and the African Petroleum Producers’ Organisation (APPO) have officially launched the Africa Energy Bank (AEB). This initiative seeks to address the growing energy financing shortfall across the continent. The announcement followed the AEB's inaugural general meeting in Abuja, Nigeria, on Saturday.

Afreximbank stated on Saturday that the establishment of the Africa Energy Bank demonstrates African governments' commitment to securing their own energy future. The bank's creation is a direct response to the continent's energy financing challenges.

The AEB's first general meeting took place in Abuja on Saturday, marking a formal step in its operationalization. The bank's objective is to provide financing for energy projects within Africa.

Why it mattersAfrican governments are determined to secure their own energy future through the establishment of the Africa Energy Bank.
