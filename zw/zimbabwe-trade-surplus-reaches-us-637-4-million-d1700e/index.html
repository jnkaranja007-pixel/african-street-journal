Zimbabwe trade surplus reaches US$637.4 million

Exports rose significantly, reversing a US$763.9 million deficit recorded in the first nine months of 2025.

Source: NewsDay Zimbabwe · 2026-11-07 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Zimbabwe's external trade position has improved, moving from a US$763.9 million deficit in the first nine months of 2025 to a US$637.4 million surplus for the same period in 2026. Buy Zimbabwe welcomed this turnaround in the country's trade balance.

Figures from the Zimbabwe National Statistics Agency (ZIMSTAT) show that exports increased substantially. This positive shift in trade figures indicates a stronger performance in the country's export sector.

The improvement in the trade balance is a key indicator for the national economy. Further details on the specific sectors contributing to the export growth are expected.

Why it mattersZimbabwe's economy benefits from a trade surplus, which can strengthen the national currency and improve foreign exchange reserves.
