Harare court issues arrest warrant for Chatunga Mugabe after no-show

The youngest son of Zimbabwe's former president missed a routine remand hearing in a robbery and property-damage case.

Source: Pindula News · 2026-11-24 · 1 min read

Harare Magistrate Isheanesu Matova issued an arrest warrant for Chatunga Bellarmine Mugabe after he failed to appear in court on Tuesday for routine remand in a robbery and malicious damage case. Mugabe, 29, was due in court with four co-accused.

The four appeared and were remanded to 10 December 2026. Prosecutors allege the men confronted Zimbabwe Anti-Corruption Commission investigator Panyika Lloyd Mupanduki at a Harare guest lodge on 3 November, took his service pistol and smashed his phone. The State says police recovered the pistol from co-accused Terence Johannes Chinyemba.

Mugabe is the youngest son of late former President Robert Mugabe. All five were granted US$100 bail and ordered to report to police weekly and avoid interfering with State witnesses.

Why it mattersMugabe must respond to the warrant, while his four co-accused are due back in court on 10 December 2026.
