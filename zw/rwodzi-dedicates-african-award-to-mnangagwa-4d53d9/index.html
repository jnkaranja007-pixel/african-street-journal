Rwodzi dedicates African award to Mnangagwa

Tourism Minister Barbara Rwodzi received the Unstoppable African Minister of the Year Award.

Source: The Standard Zimbabwe · 2026-11-02 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Zimbabwe's Tourism and Hospitality Industry Minister Barbara Rwodzi dedicated her Unstoppable African Minister of the Year Award to President Emmerson Mnangagwa and First Lady Auxillia Mnangagwa.

The award ceremony recognized ministers for their contributions. In November 2017, armoured personnel carriers rolled down Samora Machel Avenue in Harare to end Robert Mugabe’s 37-year reign.

The military intervention at that time was framed as a swift, surgical strike to remove a corrupt cabal encircling the president, pitting Emmerson Mnangagwa’s Lacoste faction against first lady Grace Mugabe’s Generation 40 (G40) cabal.
