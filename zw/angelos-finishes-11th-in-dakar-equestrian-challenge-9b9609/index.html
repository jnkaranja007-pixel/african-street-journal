Angelos finishes 11th in Dakar Equestrian Challenge

Terry Angelos recorded eight penalties in 49.46 seconds riding Cayenne 2000 Z.

Source: NewsDay Zimbabwe · 2026-11-07 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Zimbabwean equestrian athlete Terry Angelos finished 11th in the equestrian competition at the Dakar 2026 Youth Olympic Games. Angelos gained valuable experience competing against some of the world’s best young riders.

Riding Cayenne 2000 Z, Angelos recorded eight penalties in 49.46 seconds. This performance secured him 11th place in a competition that presented both challenges and important lessons.

The event provided Angelos with significant experience against elite young competitors. He gained valuable insights from his participation in the Dakar 2026 Youth Olympic Games.

Why it mattersTerry Angelos participated in the Dakar 2026 Youth Olympic Games equestrian competition.
