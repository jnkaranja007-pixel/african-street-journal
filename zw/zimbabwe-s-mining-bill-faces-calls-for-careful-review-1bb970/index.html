Zimbabwe’s mining bill faces calls for careful review

The Zimbabwe Independent says the 1961 Mines and Minerals Act no longer reflects the realities of the mining industry.

Source: Zimbabwe Independent · 2026-11-21 · 1 min read

Zimbabwe’s Mines and Minerals Bill should be considered carefully before it is passed, the Zimbabwe Independent said in an editorial published on Friday. The proposed law would replace the Mines and Minerals Act of 1961, which the outlet said no longer reflects the realities of the country’s mining industry.

The editorial said Zimbabwe had waited too long for a modern mining law, but argued that the sector’s importance to the economy makes haste an unsuitable guide to the bill’s passage. It did not identify a timetable or specific provisions in the bill.

The outlet’s argument was that lawmakers should weigh the legislation carefully as they consider replacing the decades-old act.

Why it mattersLawmakers considering the mining bill must balance replacing the 1961 act with careful scrutiny because mining is important to Zimbabwe’s economy.
