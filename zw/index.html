Zimbabwe encourages local vehicle assembly

Government aims to revive motor industry by reducing import duties on vehicle kits.

Zimbabwe is encouraging the local assembly of vehicles to boost its economy. The government has supported the importation of completely knocked-down kits to reduce the cost of finished vehicles. This policy aims to restart idle factories in the motor industry.

The 2021 National Budget Statement outlined plans for duty-free importation of completely knocked-down kits. This measure is intended to revive the country's motor industry capacity. It also seeks to strengthen connections between different industrial sectors.

Supporting local assembly is seen as a way to make economic sense for Zimbabwe. The country has long aimed to increase its manufacturing capabilities through such initiatives.

Why it mattersZimbabwe's motor industry and related sectors are affected by government policy on vehicle assembly.

Zimbabwe tourism to add 66,100 jobs by 2036

The World Travel & Tourism Council forecasts a rise in travel and tourism employment from 167,510 jobs in 2025 to 245,009 by 2036.

Zimbabwe's tourism industry is expected to create 66,100 new jobs over the next decade, according to research by the World Travel & Tourism Council (WTTC). This expansion will increase the sector's economic contribution.

The WTTC's Economic Impact Research 2026 estimates that travel and tourism supported 167,510 jobs in 2025, representing 2.8% of total national employment. By 2036, this figure is projected to grow to 245,009 jobs.

This projected growth means travel and tourism will account for 3.4% of Zimbabwe's workforce by 2036, up from 2.8% in 2025.

Why it mattersZimbabweans working in or seeking employment in the tourism sector will see job numbers increase.

Omoda & Jaecoo launch SUVs in Zimbabwe

The Chinese car brands introduce four new sport utility vehicles to the Zimbabwean market.

Chinese automotive brands Omoda and Jaecoo officially launched their sport utility vehicles in Zimbabwe on October 27, 2026. The new arrivals include the Omoda C5 Luxury X, positioned in the compact to crossover segment. The Omoda C7 Elegance targets the mid-range market.

Jaecoo also introduced two models: the J5 Glacier, also in the mid-range section, and the J7 Inferno AWD, a large SUV with offroad capabilities. The launch brings a variety of SUV options to Zimbabwean consumers.

The entry of these brands into the Zimbabwean market signifies increased competition in the automotive sector. Further details on pricing and availability were not immediately released.

Why it mattersZimbabwean consumers gain new sport utility vehicle options with the arrival of Omoda and Jaecoo brands.
