Local government director faces fraud charges

Khonzani Ncube and five officials appeared in court for allegedly defrauding the state of ZWG360 644 and US$19 419.

Ministry of Local Government and Public Works Chief Director Khonzani Ncube and five other officials appeared in court on Friday facing fraud charges. They are accused of double-claiming travelling and subsistence allowances, causing prejudice to the State of ZWG360 644 and local authorities of US$19 419.

The six officials appeared before Harare magistrate Learnmore Mapiye at the Rotten Row Magistrates Court. Their arrest followed an investigation by the Zimbabwe Anti-Corruption Commission (ZACC).

The court appearance took place on Friday. The specific amount of ZWG360 644 represents the State's loss, while US$19 419 is the amount lost by local authorities.

Harare City win NRSL title and return to PSL

The Sunshine Boys secured promotion after a 3-0 victory over Jumbo Giants at Callies Ground.

Harare City have been crowned Northern Region Soccer League (NRSL) champions, securing an immediate return to the Premier Soccer League (PSL). The team achieved this by defeating Jumbo Giants 3-0 at Callies Ground.

Coached by Try Ncube, Harare City finished the season with 83 points from 42 matches. This victory ended second-placed Black Rhinos’ hopes of promotion after a competitive title race.

The Sunshine Boys' championship win confirms their place in the top-tier league for the upcoming season.

Why it mattersHarare City Football Club has won the NRSL championship and earned promotion back to the Premier Soccer League.
