ZB Financial Holdings profit drops 31%

The financial services group reported a profit after tax of ZiG610 million, down from the previous year.

ZB Financial Holdings saw its profit after tax fall by 31% to ZiG610 million in the latest reporting period compared to the same period last year.

Despite the overall profit decline, the company's sustainable profit, which excludes exchange gains and fair value adjustments, increased significantly to ZiG215 million from ZiG40 million.

This rise in sustainable profit suggests an improvement in earnings derived from the group's core business operations, even as the balance sheet grew.

Dolphin targets fibre blind spots in Zim broadband

The telecommunications group plans fixed wireless internet services for underserved areas.

Dolphin Technologies Group plans to introduce fixed wireless internet services in Zimbabwe, targeting households and businesses beyond the reach of fibre networks. This move signals the telecommunications group's ambition to expand beyond its mobile virtual network operator business and capture more of the country's demand for internet connectivity.

The company intends to use fixed wireless access (FWA) to extend internet services into areas where fibre connectivity is unavailable. This strategy positions Dolphin to serve customers who have struggled to access reliable broadband, opening a new revenue stream for the group.

This expansion aims to address limited traditional broadband infrastructure in specific locations across the country.

Why it mattersZimbabweans in areas without fibre will gain access to internet services.

Business · 2026-11-12

Mataranyika launches blog series on childhood inspirations

Nyaradzo Group founder Philip Mataranyika reflects on influential figures from his youth.

Philip Mataranyika, founder and Chief Solutions Officer of Zimbabwe's Nyaradzo Group, has started a new series on his personal blog called “Titans of My Childhood”. The series will explore the lives of people he admired while growing up.

Through these blog posts, Mr Mataranyika aims to revisit individuals whose values, achievements, and legacies helped shape his entrepreneurial journey. He stated that these figures influenced his ambitions and outlook on life.

The series highlights the power of inspiration and mentorship in pursuing aspirations. It offers readers a glimpse into the foundations of Mr Mataranyika's work and the principles guiding him.

Why it mattersZimbabwean business mogul Philip Mataranyika is sharing personal reflections on his blog about the people who inspired his career.
