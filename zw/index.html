Mnangagwa faces court challenge over term limits

Zimbabwe's Constitutional Court will hear three challenges in November to President Mnangagwa's bid to serve beyond two terms.

President Emmerson Mnangagwa's plan to stay in office beyond his pledged two terms faces a legal challenge in November. The Constitutional Court is scheduled to hear three separate cases questioning his eligibility to serve further. Mnangagwa signed the CAA3 law in July, even as these legal challenges were already before the court.

The legal challenges will test Mnangagwa's intention to remain in power beyond the two terms he initially promised. The Constitutional Court's decision in November will determine the immediate future of his presidency. The CAA3 law was enacted despite the ongoing legal scrutiny.

The court's hearing in November represents a significant legal hurdle for President Mnangagwa's political future. The outcome will clarify the constitutional limits on presidential terms in Zimbabwe.
