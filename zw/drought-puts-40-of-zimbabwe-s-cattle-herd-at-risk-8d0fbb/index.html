Drought puts 40% of Zimbabwe’s cattle herd at risk

A Cabinet update links the threat to El Niño, and Treasury now projects growth slowing to 1.2% in 2027 from 5% this year.

Source: Zimbabwe Independent · 2026-11-26 · 1 min read

At least 40% of Zimbabwe’s cattle herd is at risk from drought linked to El Niño, according to a Cabinet update. Deteriorating conditions are already putting pressure on cattle farmers, and some are disposing of their herds.

The livestock sector is among the agricultural value chains facing heightened exposure, the update said. Latest Treasury projections put economic growth at 1.2% in 2027, down from 5% this year, amid headwinds largely linked to El Niño and other global shocks.

The update raises the prospect of significant livestock losses and further pressure on agricultural output.

Why it mattersFarmers forced to sell herds now face losses that will weigh on the agricultural output the economy depends on.
