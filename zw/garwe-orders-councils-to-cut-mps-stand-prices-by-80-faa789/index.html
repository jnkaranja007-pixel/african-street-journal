Garwe orders councils to cut MPs’ stand prices by 80%

The local government minister’s circular requires every council to apply the discount and keep records of the stands and payments.

Sources: Pindula News and The Standard Zimbabwe · 2026-11-23 · 1 min read

Zimbabwe’s Ministry of Local Government and Public Works has directed councils nationwide to cut the approved purchase price of residential stands allocated to eligible MPs by 80%. The directive comes in a circular dated 8 September from Daniel Garwe, the minister responsible for local government and public works.

Garwe told councils to give qualifying MPs the same discount whether they obtain stands directly from local authorities or through other government housing arrangements. Councils must record each allocation and discount, then send the ministry periodic reports covering beneficiaries, stand locations and sizes, approved and reduced prices, and outstanding payments.

The circular does not set a number of stands, identify locations or spell out individual eligibility criteria. In April 2025, Garwe said 70 stands had been allocated to sitting and former legislators in Killarney, Penrose and Mabelreign; he said more than 250 had been allocated since the programme began in 2013.

Why it mattersCouncils must track the reduced prices and outstanding payments for each eligible MP’s stand and report them periodically to the ministry.
