Harare residents urge council to map flood risks and clear drains

The Combined Harare Residents Association says high-density and low-lying areas face the worst flooding as the rainy season intensifies.

Source: Pindula News · 2026-11-28 · 1 min read

The Combined Harare Residents Association (CHRA), a residents’ group, has urged Harare City Council to map flood-prone areas and clear blocked drains as flooding worsens across the city during the rainy season. In a statement issued on Thursday, 26 November, it said high-density suburbs and low-lying areas were worst affected.

CHRA said flooded homes, damaged roads and disrupted movement recur each rainy season, while water contamination raises public-health risks. Flooded roads and public spaces in the city centre have also disrupted businesses and travel, the association said. It called for drains and channels to be cleared and repaired, roads to be maintained, and areas most at risk to be mapped so the council can direct limited resources to exposed communities.

The association also urged the council to prevent developments that block natural waterways and build new infrastructure where existing systems cannot cope. It said mapping should guide rehabilitation and investment priorities.

Why it mattersHarare communities exposed to flooding face disrupted homes, roads and businesses, while the council is being urged to direct limited resources using risk maps.
