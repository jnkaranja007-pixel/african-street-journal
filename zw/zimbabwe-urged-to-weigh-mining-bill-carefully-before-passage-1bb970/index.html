Zimbabwe urged to weigh mining bill carefully before passage

The Zimbabwe Independent says the 1961 Mines and Minerals Act no longer reflects the mining industry, while mining remains important to the economy.

Source: Zimbabwe Independent · 2026-11-20 · 1 min read

Zimbabwe’s Mines and Minerals Bill should be considered carefully before lawmakers pass it, the Zimbabwe Independent said in an editorial published Friday. The outlet argues that the proposed law needs to replace a statute dating to 1961, which it says no longer reflects the realities of the mining industry.

The editorial says Zimbabwe has waited too long for a modern mining law, but urges decision-makers not to rush the bill. It gives no details of the bill’s provisions, timetable or the lawmakers responsible for considering it.

The outlet points to mining’s importance to Zimbabwe’s economy as a reason for careful scrutiny. It does not identify a next step or date for the bill’s passage.

Why it mattersLawmakers considering the bill must balance replacing a law from 1961 with careful review, because mining is important to Zimbabwe’s economy.
