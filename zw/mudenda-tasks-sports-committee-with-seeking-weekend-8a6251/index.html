Mudenda tasks sports committee with seeking weekend Warriors matches

The speaker wants the committee to ask FIFA to move national-team games from weekdays, after Zimbabwe beat Equatorial Guinea 3-0 on Monday.

Source: Pindula News · 2026-11-22 · 1 min read

Zimbabwe’s National Assembly Speaker Jacob Mudenda has tasked the Parliamentary Portfolio Committee on Sports with asking FIFA to consider moving national-team matches from weekdays to weekends. He said the change would allow more fans to attend.

Mudenda raised the issue after committee chairman Farai Jere congratulated the senior national soccer team for its 3-0 win over Equatorial Guinea in a 2027 Africa Cup of Nations qualifier played on Monday. Jere said FIFA decides match dates, prompting Mudenda to assign him the task of engaging the governing body.

Mudenda said FIFA president Gianni Infantino should consider fans’ expectations, adding that football is a business and match scheduling should take the market into account. The committee’s next step is to approach FIFA.

Why it mattersThe sports committee must now approach FIFA to seek weekend scheduling so fans can attend national-team games in larger numbers.
