UK seeks coffee investment in Uganda

Britain wants to boost Uganda's coffee sector through processing and market access.

Source: Nile Post · 2026-11-11 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

The United Kingdom is seeking to increase investment and trade in Uganda's coffee sector, focusing on processing, value addition, and market access. British High Commissioner Lisa Chesney stated that the UK aims to support Uganda's coffee exports through commercial partnerships and investment across the entire value chain. This initiative aims to connect international buyers with Ugandan farmers, cooperatives, and exporters.

Uganda exported approximately 8.1 million bags of coffee valued at US$2.1 billion last year, highlighting the sector's importance for foreign exchange. Chesney noted that stronger commercial ties could help Ugandan businesses capitalize on growing global demand and encourage investment in processing, packaging, and logistics. She pointed to British company Hats Hats as an example of successful foreign investment in Uganda's coffee market.

The UK itself represents a significant export market, with daily consumption of around 103 million cups of coffee. Accessing this market requires consistent supply, quality, and traceability. The Uganda-UK and Ireland Coffee Familiarisation Trip aims to build these connections, bringing producers closer to international buyers and fostering lasting commercial relationships and investment opportunities.

Why it mattersUgandan coffee farmers and businesses can benefit from increased investment in processing and direct access to the UK market.
