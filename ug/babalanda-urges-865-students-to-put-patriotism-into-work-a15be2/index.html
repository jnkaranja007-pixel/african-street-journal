Babalanda urges 865 students to put patriotism into work

The minister told graduates in Iganga to support family businesses and encourage parents to join government wealth-creation programmes.

Source: Watchdog Uganda · 2026-11-23 · 1 min read

Uganda’s Minister in Charge of the Presidency, Babirye Milly Babalanda, urged 865 students from two secondary schools to demonstrate patriotism through productive work, unity and respect for the Constitution. She spoke on Sunday at a training pass-out ceremony at Bukoyo Senior Secondary School in Iganga District.

The graduates had completed training in patriotism and ideological mindset change, Babalanda said. She urged them to support family enterprises in agriculture, livestock, poultry, fisheries and trade, and to encourage their parents to take part in the Parish Development Model, a government programme intended to move households from subsistence production into the money economy.

Babalanda also told students to use school holidays to learn business skills and modern production methods, and to report child abuse to parents, teachers, local leaders, police or other authorities.

Why it mattersThe 865 graduates were asked to help their families build productive enterprises and encourage parents to join the Parish Development Model.
