Dfcu Bank offers up to Shs40,000 cashback on VISA spending

Cardholders can earn up to 8% back on eligible purchases, and discounts of up to 20% apply to flights and online shopping until December 31, 2026.

Sources: Softpower News, Nile Post and The Independent Uganda · 2026-11-14 · 1 min read

Up to Shs40,000 is available to dfcu VISA cardholders for every complete Shs500,000 they spend, through a festive campaign that runs until December 31, 2026. Eligible spending at any merchant point of sale that accepts dfcu cards earns the cashback, which works out to as much as eight per cent back on purchases.

Isaac Bigirwa, dfcu Bank's Head of Cards and Payments, said the end of the year is when households and travellers spend most, so customers can earn value on purchases they were already planning. Cardholders can also get up to 20 per cent off online shopping and on flights with Emirates, Qatar Airways and Ethiopian Airlines.

Groceries, fuel, dining, retail, hospitality, travel and online purchases are all covered. The campaign, called "Pay. Save. Get More.", is aimed mainly at existing cardholders who use their cards only occasionally.

Why it mattersHolders of dfcu VISA cards who spend through the end of the year can recover part of their outlay, and travellers booking with three named airlines can cut their fares.
