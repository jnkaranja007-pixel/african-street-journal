Museveni challenges African creators on historical missions

Ugandan President Museveni met content creators from five African nations to discuss promoting Africa's history and identity.

Sources: Watchdog Uganda and Nile Post · 2026-11-13 · 2 min read

President Yoweri Museveni has urged African content creators to champion five historical missions for the continent's transformation: liberation, democracy, economic prosperity, strategic security, and African unity. He met a delegation of creators from Ghana, Zambia, Tanzania, Kenya, and Uganda at State House, Entebbe, on Thursday. Museveni stated that understanding societal challenges is key to finding solutions, drawing parallels between patients and doctors.

The President explained that Africa possesses strong foundations for wealth creation, including agriculture, craftsmanship, and indigenous knowledge, alongside a rich cultural heritage and linguistic diversity. He noted that historical divisions, poor governance, and conflicts weakened the continent, offering lessons for present-day leaders and the youth on unity and cooperation. The first mission discussed was liberation from colonial rule, with the formation of movements like the ANC in 1912 cited as a milestone.

Museveni also highlighted democracy as the second historical mission, emphasizing the importance of governance systems reflecting the political will and interests of African people. He invited the creators after watching their discussions on African history, culture, and languages, seeing an opportunity to share historical knowledge to enrich their storytelling about the continent. The delegation included creators such as Berthold Kobina Ackon from Ghana and Daniel Wambua Ndambuki from Kenya.

Why it mattersPresident Museveni has tasked African content creators with promoting the continent's history and identity through their platforms, affecting how Africa's narrative is shaped.
