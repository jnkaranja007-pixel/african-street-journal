Ugandan comedian Kapere dies in Buddo

Popular comedian Yokana Mbuuse, known as Kapere Lufotose, died on Saturday, November 1, 2026, at a medical facility in Buddo.

Source: Watchdog Uganda · 2026-11-02 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Popular Ugandan comedian Yokana Mbuuse, known as Kapere Lufotose, died on Saturday, November 1, 2026, at a medical facility in Buddo, Wakiso District. News of his death emerged on social media and was confirmed by entertainment journalist Kasuku and former presidential candidate Robert Kyagulanyi Ssentamu, also known as Bobi Wine.

Kapere rose to national prominence with the Amarula Family comedy group, known for his self-deprecating humour and distinctive stage presence. He also featured in Bobi Wine's music video for the hit song 'Mr. Money'. Bobi Wine described Kapere as a man who brought happiness during difficult moments and loved his country.

Kapere, a father of three, had spoken about his difficult childhood and a childhood hernia condition. In recent years, he advocated for Ugandan entertainers facing economic challenges, highlighting financial instability and limited booking opportunities in the comedy industry.

Why it mattersThe death of comedian Yokana Mbuuse, known as Kapere, affects fans and fellow entertainers who found happiness in his performances and who heard his advocacy for artists' economic challenges.
