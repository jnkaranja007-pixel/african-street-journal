Museveni directs Uganda to identify island site for proposed resort

The UK-based investor says it is considering tourism projects, while three international hotel groups are in talks to build hotels in Uganda.

Sources: Softpower News, Nile Post and Watchdog Uganda · 2026-11-28 · 1 min read

President Yoweri Museveni directed Uganda’s prime minister to identify a Lake Victoria island for a proposed resort during a meeting with UK-based International Tourism Investment Corporation Limited at his Kisozi Farm on Friday, November 27. The company’s delegation, led by chief executive Ibrahim Ayoub, expressed interest in resorts, hotels and other tourism infrastructure.

Prime Minister Robinah Nabbanja said Ayoub had returned to Uganda to advance investment discussions and formalise arrangements with government ministries. She said he was in talks with three international hotel groups about building hotels in the country. Museveni said the proposed resort would require about 100 acres and that the government could identify suitable land.

Museveni asked the company to consider less-developed islands in the Ssese group, which comprises about 82 islands, as well as islands in Buvuma and Mukono. He also suggested tourism facilities along the River Nile and near national parks.

Why it mattersUganda’s prime minister must identify an island and government ministries must advance arrangements for a proposed resort requiring about 100 acres.
