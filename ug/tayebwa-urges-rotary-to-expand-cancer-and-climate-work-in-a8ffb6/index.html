Tayebwa urges Rotary to expand cancer and climate work in Uganda

The deputy speaker said Parliament contributed USh3 billion to the Rotary Cancer Run this year and called for larger environmental programmes.

Sources: The Independent Uganda and Nile Post · 2026-11-28 · 1 min read

Uganda’s Deputy Speaker Thomas Tayebwa urged Rotary International to expand its work on cancer and climate change during a meeting at Parliament in Kampala on Thursday. He said Parliament had contributed USh3 billion to the annual Rotary Cancer Run this year, part of more than USh10 billion given over the years.

Tayebwa said the cancer burden was growing in Uganda and other African countries, and that the initiative could grow. He also urged Rotary to scale up reforestation, renewable energy, sustainable agriculture, freshwater conservation and waste management, and to mobilise young people on environmental conservation.

Rotary International President Olayinka Babalola thanked the government and Parliament for their role in immunisation and Uganda's elimination of indigenous wild poliovirus. Rotary directors and delegations are in Uganda for the 7th All-Africa Rotary Institute at Speke Resort Munyonyo.
