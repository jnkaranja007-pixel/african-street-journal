EACOP opens latrine and gives sports gear to Kabonera

The pipeline company also donated footballs, jerseys, and netball posts to the community.

Source: Watchdog Uganda · 2026-11-02 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

The East African Crude Oil Pipeline (EACOP) Ltd. has opened a new four-stance drainable pit latrine block in Kabonera Village, Kyotera District. The facility includes a rainwater harvesting tank and aims to improve public health and sanitation for communities and schools.

EACOP also handed over sports equipment for football, netball, and volleyball, including goalposts, nets, jerseys, and balls. Matred Murungi, EACOP’s Social Economic Investment Manager, stated the initiative supports the company's Social Economic Investment programme under the Community Health and Safety pillar.

The handover ceremony in Kabonera village was attended by local leaders, including Resident District Commissioner Mr. Apollo Mugume. Mugume praised the project for nurturing young talent and promoting unity, commending EACOP for its corporate citizenship.

Why it mattersThe Kabonera community in Uganda received new sanitation facilities and sports equipment from EACOP, improving hygiene and recreation.
