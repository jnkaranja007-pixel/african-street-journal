Huawei ICT competition opens at Kyambogo University

Ugandan students can register for the 11th annual competition until mid-December, with chances to compete regionally and globally.

Sources: The Independent Uganda and Nile Post · 2026-11-15 · 1 min read

Huawei Technologies Uganda launched its 11th annual ICT Competition at Kyambogo University on November 13, inviting students to develop practical technology skills. The event, themed “I.C. The Future,” aims to encourage registration for the 2026–2027 competition, which covers Network, Cloud, and Computing categories.

Students can register until mid-December 2026, with preliminary exams in December and national finals in January 2027. Successful candidates will train for regional examinations in March 2027, with top teams advancing to global finals in Shenzhen, China, in May 2027, competing for cash prizes from RMB5,500 to RMB15,000.

Dickson Mugerwa from Kyambogo University’s Faculty of Engineering urged students to gain practical ICT skills and certifications for a competitive job market. Amon Nasasira, a former contestant, shared that the competition helped him build professional connections and valuable skills in networking and cloud technologies.

Why it mattersStudents can register for the competition until mid-December 2026, with opportunities to win cash prizes and gain valuable ICT skills.
