Coca-Cola returns as AFCON sponsor ahead of 2027 East Africa tournament

The renewed CAF partnership comes as Uganda, Kenya and Tanzania prepare to host the competition under the AFCON Pamoja 2027 arrangement.

Sources: The Observer Uganda, Nile Post and Softpower News · 2026-11-24 · 1 min read

Coca-Cola has rejoined the Confederation of African Football (CAF) as an official sponsor of the Africa Cup of Nations (AFCON), nearly 30 years after its previous involvement. The partnership comes ahead of the 2027 tournament, which Uganda, Kenya and Tanzania will jointly host.

Luis Felipe Avellar, president of Coca-Cola’s Africa operating unit, said the company plans to link the competition with music, creativity and local cultural expression, and give fans ways to take part in its activities. CAF president Patrice Motsepe said the agreement would support football’s development and the commercial interests of both organisations.

The tournament will be branded AFCON Pamoja 2027. Motsepe said sponsors’ and investors’ renewed confidence reflected changes to CAF’s governance and business practices, as well as the competitiveness of African football.

Why it mattersUgandan fans and local businesses will see Coca-Cola's marketing, music events and fan activities across the three host countries as the 2027 tournament approaches.
