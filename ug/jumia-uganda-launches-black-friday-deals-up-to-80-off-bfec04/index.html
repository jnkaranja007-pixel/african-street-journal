Jumia Uganda launches Black Friday deals up to 80% off

The online marketplace offers discounts on electronics, appliances, and fashion from October 30 to November 30.

Source: The Independent Uganda · 2026-10-30 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Jumia Uganda has launched its annual Black Friday shopping campaign, offering discounts of up to 80% on selected items. The promotion, which runs from October 30 to November 30, aims to help consumers manage household spending by providing reduced prices on electronics, smartphones, home appliances, fashion, beauty products, and household essentials.

Steven Lamony, chief executive officer of Jumia Uganda, stated that the campaign, themed “Jumia Black Friday — Your dreams, now on deal!”, is designed to offer customers more value for their money. He acknowledged that many Ugandan households are being more deliberate with their spending and that Jumia wants to facilitate access to attractive offers on everyday products. The campaign officially began at 11:59 p.m. on October 29.

Customers can find specific deals such as an SPJ 15-kilogram twin-tub washing machine for Shs699,000 and a Pixel 65-inch QLED Android television for Shs999,000. A Pixel 280-litre double-door refrigerator with a water dispenser is also available for Shs399,000, subject to availability. Shoppers can access these offers through the Jumia website or mobile application.

Why it mattersUgandan consumers can access discounted prices on a wide range of products to help manage household budgets amid rising living costs.
