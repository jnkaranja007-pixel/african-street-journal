Stellenbosch selects Uganda’s Winnie Byanyima for honorary commerce doctorate

The university named the outgoing UNAIDS chief among seven recipients, citing her work on social justice, women’s rights and global inequality.

Sources: The Independent Uganda, Nile Post and Softpower News · 2026-11-25 · 1 min read

Stellenbosch University in South Africa has selected Winnie Byanyima, Uganda’s outgoing Executive Director of the Joint United Nations Programme on HIV/AIDS (UNAIDS), for an honorary Doctor of Commerce. The university named her among seven recipients of honorary degrees.

Byanyima, an engineer and United Nations Under-Secretary-General, served three terms in Uganda’s Parliament and founded the Forum for Women in Democracy, which promotes women’s leadership. She also held senior roles at the African Union Commission, the United Nations Development Programme and Oxfam International before taking the UNAIDS post in 2019.

Her previous honorary degrees came from the University of Manchester, Mount Saint Vincent University in Canada, Cranfield University and the University of the Free State in South Africa. The other recipients include Nigerian journalist Dele Olojede and retired South African judge Dennis Davis.

Why it mattersByanyima’s selection recognises a career that includes three terms in Uganda’s Parliament and leadership of UNAIDS since 2019.
