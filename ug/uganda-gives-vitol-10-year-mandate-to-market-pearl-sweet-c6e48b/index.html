Uganda gives Vitol 10-year mandate to market Pearl Sweet crude

The international trader will seek buyers and competitive prices, while Uganda National Oil Company retains oversight of each sale.

Sources: Watchdog Uganda and Nile Post · 2026-11-21 · 1 min read

Uganda has signed a 10-year agreement appointing international energy trader Vitol to market Pearl Sweet crude oil abroad. The deal was signed at State House in Entebbe on Friday by the Uganda National Oil Company (UNOC) and the Ministry of Energy and Mineral Development.

President Yoweri Museveni said UNOC must know who buys the crude and be satisfied with each transaction. Energy Minister Monica Musenero said Vitol will initially work alongside UNOC, offering technical guidance, training and access to international buyers as the national company builds its marketing capacity.

The agreement sets up sales arrangements ahead of commercial production. Musenero said Uganda needed to prepare for the international market before production could lead to sales, and described the deal as the last step before a further announcement about first oil.

Why it mattersUNOC must review the buyers and reasons for each crude sale, while building its marketing capacity with Vitol’s guidance and training.
