Tayebwa backs UWA’s expanded forest role and seeks resources

The deputy speaker pledged Parliament’s support as the wildlife authority takes over central forest reserves and seeks a helicopter for its operations.

Sources: Watchdog Uganda and Nile Post · 2026-11-25 · 1 min read

Uganda’s Deputy Speaker, Thomas Tayebwa, pledged Parliament’s continued support for the Uganda Wildlife Authority (UWA) on November 24, 2026, as it takes on management of central forest reserves. He met UWA Executive Director James Musinguzi at Parliament.

Musinguzi asked Tayebwa to help secure a helicopter for aerial surveillance, wildlife protection and emergency response, and said UWA needed more funding for its expanded duties. Tayebwa said the authority’s performance had informed Parliament’s support for adding the forests to its responsibilities.

Tayebwa also pledged to mobilise colleagues for a rhino-naming ceremony scheduled for January 16, 2027, and urged UWA to habituate more chimpanzee groups in Maramagambo Forest to expand tracking tourism. He committed to supporting tourism initiatives, particularly chimpanzee tourism.

Why it mattersUWA must obtain a helicopter and more funding to carry out its expanded forest-management duties.
