Equity banks insist on auction of Dei properties over loan debt

Lenders maintain the sale of commercial and industrial assets to recover over Shs 148 billion is lawful.

Source: The Observer Uganda · 2026-11-17 · 1 min read

Equity Bank Uganda and Equity Bank Kenya said the planned auction of properties belonging to companies owned by businessman Dr Mathias Magoola will proceed to recover over Shs 148 billion in loans. The lenders, represented by Katende, Ssempebwa and Company Advocates, said in a public notice that the sale is lawful under the Mortgage Act and that no court order has stopped it.

The notice responded to a November 9, 2026 warning from Muwema and Company Advocates urging buyers to avoid the purchase. JALD (U) Ltd advertised the intended sale on November 5, listing a pharmaceutical manufacturing facility occupying about 20.673 acres in Matugga, a wheat milling plant in Luzira, and properties in Kampala and Wakiso districts.

The Dei companies argue the auction is premature because the debt remains contested and court proceedings over a loan audit are not concluded. The dispute touches government investment of Shs 723.4 billion in Dei BioPharma, which the Parliamentary Committee on Presidential Affairs has previously questioned.

Why it mattersBuyers of the listed Kampala, Wakiso and Matugga properties face a sale the companies say is premature while the audit dispute is unresolved.
