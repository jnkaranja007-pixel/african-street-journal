Museveni backs Luwero and NEC defence systems built at home

Engineers at two Nakasongola facilities showed threat-detection systems, armoured vehicle work and aircraft maintenance to the president on Tuesday.

Sources: The Independent Uganda, Nile Post and Softpower News · 2026-11-26 · 1 min read

President Yoweri Museveni praised Luwero Industries and National Enterprise Corporation (NEC) Technologies during a Tuesday tour of their defence facilities in Nakasongola District. Engineers demonstrated technology, equipment and systems under development as Uganda expands domestic defence manufacturing and seeks to reduce reliance on foreign suppliers.

At NEC Technologies, its manager, Maya Huang, said the facility was established in 2025 and began training in September this year. She said engineers were developing systems to detect and neutralise potential threats. Museveni said the technologies covered both offensive and defensive capabilities, and said Luwero's innovations would let the Uganda People's Defence Forces defend the country with its own means.

Luwero Industries, a subsidiary of NEC, manufactures and repairs military equipment, assembles armoured vehicles and carries out aircraft maintenance. Its NEC-STREIT partnership with the UAE-based Streit Group runs an armoured vehicle factory in Nakasongola, commissioned in 2022.

Why it mattersLuwero and NEC engineers must move locally developed threat-detection and armoured vehicle systems toward use by the Uganda People's Defence Forces, cutting the army's dependence on foreign suppliers.
