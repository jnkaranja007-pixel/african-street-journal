Stanbic Uganda wins Titans award for reporting

The company received a Gold award in the Titans Category at the 2026 Financial Reporting Awards for sustained excellence.

Source: Watchdog Uganda · 2026-11-08 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Stanbic Uganda Holdings Limited (SUHL) received a Gold award in the Titans Category at the 2026 Financial Reporting (FiRe) Awards in Kampala. The awards, organised by the Institute of Certified Public Accountants of Uganda (ICPAU) and its partners, promote excellence in financial reporting, corporate governance, and sustainability disclosure. This year's ceremony focused on "Driving Transparent Sustainability Reporting through Technology and Innovation."

The Titans category honours institutions with a legacy of sustained excellence in corporate reporting. SUHL earned this distinction by accumulating eight Gold Awards across various categories since the FiRe Awards began in 2011. Mark Ocitti Ongom, Chief Executive of SUHL, stated that strong reporting builds stakeholder trust and confidence in a listed entity.

Ronald Makata, Chief Finance and Value Management Officer at SUHL, highlighted that credible reporting is central to maintaining confidence among shareholders, regulators, and investors. The FiRe Awards Committee noted that organisations recognised as Titans have demonstrated the ability to compete regionally and won at least three Gold accolades. The awards ceremony was held on November 4, 2026.

Why it mattersStanbic Uganda Holdings Limited's recognition as a Titan at the FiRe Awards highlights its consistent high standards in financial reporting, impacting stakeholder trust and confidence.
