Uganda is preparing for first oil production and revenues in 2026-27, with production at the Lake Albert basin scheduled for late 2026 and construction continuing on the East African Crude Oil Pipeline to the Tanzanian coast, despite financing difficulties and environmental objections.

Youth unemployment at 43% as 90,000 of 700,000 graduates find formal work

Uganda's overall unemployment rate stands at 12.6% while youth unemployment is 43%, with only about 90,000 of the roughly 700,000 graduates each year finding formal-sector employment. Unemployment and corruption rank as the leading concerns among voters.

Why it mattersThe graduate-to-job gap is the central pressure behind migration, informal work and political discontent.
