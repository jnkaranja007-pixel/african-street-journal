Health · 2026-11-12

Four pupils die in suspected poisoning

Ta-Alim Primary School in Kisasi has been closed as police investigate the deaths of four young girls.

Four pupils at Ta-Alim Primary School and Quran Memorisation Centre in Kisasi, Kampala, have died after developing stomach complications. Preliminary police findings suggest suspected poisoning as the cause. The deceased pupils were identified as Mariam Nakitende, Rwahim Kiruta, Nasuna Nusufa, all 11 years old, and nine-year-old Nabeta Failuzi.

Kampala Metropolitan Police spokesperson Rachel Kawala stated that investigations are ongoing, with detectives planning to visit the area where the pupils reportedly consumed oranges. Police medical teams are conducting postmortem examinations at Mulago City Mortuary to determine the exact cause of death. The school has been closed and declared a crime scene by homicide detectives.

The incident began when one pupil complained of stomach pain late Monday night and her condition worsened, leading to other pupils showing similar symptoms. The school director and matron initially took affected pupils to a nearby clinic. Some parents collected their children as their conditions deteriorated, while others were taken to hospitals where doctors suspected food poisoning after ruling out malaria.

Why it mattersFour young children have died from suspected poisoning, impacting their families and the school community.

Politics · 2026-11-12

Uganda court hears Shs60bn bank fraud trial

Nine finance officials face charges over alleged diversion of $14 million to foreign firms.

The trial of nine finance ministry officials accused of diverting over $14 million (about Shs 60 billion) to foreign companies began at the Anti-Corruption court in Kampala on Tuesday. Prosecutors allege the officials manipulated payment files from the Integrated Financial Management System (IFMS) in March 2024, sending funds meant for the World Bank Group and the African Development Bank to unintended recipients.

Those charged include former Accountant General Lawrence Ssemakula and IT systems officer Paul Nkalubo Lumala. Investigators claim payment instructions were altered before reaching the Bank of Uganda. The first prosecution witness, Dr Edward Ssengozi Damulira, the ministry's under secretary, testified that Shs 13 billion in supplementary funding was released in the 2022/2023 financial year for system upgrades.

Damulira stated the ministry had requested Shs 26 billion to upgrade the IFMS. The upgrade covered the Programme Budgeting System, IFMS, and the Electronic Government Procurement System. Minutes from a November 4, 2022, meeting showed the Accountant General recommended continuous user training after the upgrades.

Why it mattersNine finance ministry officials are on trial for allegedly defrauding the government of Shs 60 billion through manipulated payment systems.

Business · 2026-11-12

Uganda coffee leaders plan economic expansion

A two-day think tank in Ntungamo will explore coffee's role in industrialisation and tourism.

Uganda's coffee sector leaders, policymakers, and investors will convene on November 13 and 14 for the second Coffee Roundtable Think Tank in Ntungamo. The meeting aims to broaden the economic impact of coffee beyond farming and exports, focusing on industrialisation, tourism, and investment. Organisers want to move beyond measuring success solely by production volumes, export earnings, and farmer prices.

Dr Nelson O. Tugume, CEO of Inspire Africa Group, stated that Uganda must view coffee as a resource with potential across multiple sectors, suggesting opportunities in coffee tourism and local processing. He highlighted Mbale as a model for coffee tourism, where visitors could experience coffee culture and explore investment. "Perhaps the question is no longer how much coffee Uganda can produce, but how much of the global economy Uganda can build around coffee," Tugume said.

Africa Coffee Park, the host, will focus on unlocking opportunities, promoting value addition, and strengthening the coffee value chain to position Uganda as a global coffee player. The event will include senior government officials from trade, finance, agriculture, foreign affairs, tourism, and science ministries, alongside private sector representatives like tourism entrepreneur Amos Wekesa.

Why it mattersThe think tank aims to boost Uganda's economy by developing new industries and tourism opportunities around coffee.

Business · 2026-11-12

MTN Uganda funds 25 community projects with Sh500 million

The telecommunications company's foundation will support initiatives in health, education, and environment.

MTN Uganda Foundation has committed Shs500 million to support 25 community-led initiatives across Uganda, with each receiving Shs20 million in kind. The announcement in Kampala on November 10 marks the fourth phase of the MTN Changemakers programme. This funding brings the foundation's total investment in the programme to Shs2 billion.

The 25 selected organisations, chosen from 2,607 applications, focus on challenges in health, education, water, sanitation, environmental sustainability, and economic empowerment. MTN Uganda CEO Sylvia Mulinge stated that the programme supports individuals and organisations developing practical, community-based solutions. One beneficiary, Beles Bubu Africa, received ICT equipment to strengthen its educational programmes for refugees.

The fourth phase builds on previous rounds where MTN Uganda Foundation supported 75 initiatives with Shs1.5 billion, benefiting over 119,000 people. The latest beneficiaries are spread across Uganda's five regions, including the National Christian Students Association and Women and Children With Albinism in Uganda.

Why it mattersThe MTN Changemakers programme provides Shs20 million in in-kind assistance to 25 community organisations across Uganda, supporting their work in areas like health and education.

Tech · 2026-11-12

Huawei launches 2026-2027 ICT competition in Uganda

The annual competition will test university students in networking, cloud, computing, and AI innovation.

Huawei Technologies Uganda, with the Ministry of Science, Technology and Innovation, launched the 2026–2027 Huawei ICT Competition in Kampala on November 10, 2026. This initiative aims to develop Uganda's ICT talent by connecting industry needs with academic training and national digital priorities.

The competition, now in its 11th edition, offers students a platform to enhance skills in networking, cloud computing, computing, and AI innovation. Top performers will advance to regional finals in South Africa and potentially global finals in Shenzhen, China. Mountains of the Moon University and Uganda Martyrs University students, who previously won global awards, were recognized at the launch.

Minister for Science, Technology and Innovation, Hon. Eng. Jonard Asiimwe Akiiki, stressed the importance of technology skills for national development and urged collaboration with industry players like Huawei. He encouraged young innovators to develop practical solutions for local challenges.

Why it mattersUgandan university students will gain advanced technical skills and international competition experience.
