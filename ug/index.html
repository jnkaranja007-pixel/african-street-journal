Health · 2026-11-15

Museveni pledges tax exemption for doctors' salaries

President Museveni also promised Shs125 billion for a doctors' Sacco and urged a shift to preventive healthcare.

President Yoweri Museveni pledged on Friday to exempt doctors' salaries from taxation and increase pay for other health workers. He made the remarks at the 10th Uganda Medical Association (UMA) Grand Doctors’ Conference at Soroti University. The government is also prepared to increase salaries for government health workers.

Museveni announced a Shs125 billion capitalisation pledge for the UMA Savings and Credit Cooperative Organisation (Sacco) to support regional medical hubs and postgraduate training. UMA president Dr Frank Asiimwe had asked for staffing levels to increase from 23 per cent to 50 per cent by the next election cycle and for increased allowances for medical interns and residents to Shs2.5 million.

The President urged health workers to prioritise disease prevention through public education, immunisation, safe water, and sanitation, stating that prevention is cheaper than treatment. Prof Francis Omaswa called for stronger regulation of medical training institutions, while Vice President Jessica Alupo noted Uganda's efforts to strengthen its domestic healthcare system with partners like India.

Why it mattersDoctors and health workers will retain their current earnings as salaries become tax-exempt, and a Shs125 billion Sacco is planned to support their financial stability and professional development.

Security · 2026-11-15

Rebels from Zaire strike Kasese border towns, killing about 50

Roughly 600 fighters of the Allied Democratic Forces crossed the Mpondwe-Lhubiriha river on November 13, 1996, in the group's first major offensive against Uganda.

On November 13, 1996, at about 6am, around 600 heavily armed fighters from the Allied Democratic Forces (ADF) crossed the Mpondwe-Lhubiriha river from Kasindi in eastern Zaire, now the Democratic Republic of the Congo. Within the same hour they hit Mpondwe Customs Post, Bwera township and nearby Karambi in Kasese District, swamping local defences.

The attack reportedly killed about 50 people and drove an estimated 25,000 residents out of the border corridor. As they pulled back towards Zaire after Ugandan military reinforcements arrived, the fighters carried off supplies, wrecked infrastructure and seized civilians to serve as porters and recruits.

Uganda's army, the Uganda People's Defence Force (UPDF), first sent Colonel Peter Kerim to retake Mpondwe and Bwera, with Colonel Katumba Wamala taking over soon after. Within days the UPDF crossed into Zaire, took the border town of Kasindi and struck ADF rear camps, pulling Uganda into the wider conflict later called the First Congo War.

Why it mattersResidents of the Kasese border corridor were forced to flee their homes, and civilians seized by the fighters were taken across the border as porters and recruits.

Politics · 2026-11-15

Five parties agree to fold Saleh's democracy plan into national dialogue

The proposal, co-authored by President Museveni's younger brother Gen. Salim Saleh and Prof. Olara Otunnu, would be added to a 2018 framework that has stalled.

Five political parties have agreed to merge the consociational democracy proposal of Gen. Salim Saleh, President Yoweri Museveni's younger brother, with Prof. Olara Otunnu into Uganda's stalled 2018 National Dialogue Framework. The combined effort is meant to open a broader national engagement process.

Saleh, who is also Chief Coordinator of Operation Wealth Creation, linked the proposal to his recent effort to reach opposition veteran Dr Kizza Besigye, who remains on trial. He said he found Besigye "almost dead in prison" and first secured his release for a proper medical examination. He told leaders of parliamentary committees on November 1 at Kapeeka Industrial Park that the aim was to keep younger Ugandans from inheriting the country's generational quarrels.

The engagement came after a public appeal from Gen. Muhoozi Kainerugaba, the president's son and Chief of Defence Forces, who has publicly indicated an intention to run for president in 2031. President Museveni began a new five-year term after winning the January 2026 election.

Why it mattersParties that adopt the merged framework will carry the consociational proposal into a national engagement process that has been stalled since 2018.

Tech · 2026-11-15

Huawei ICT competition opens at Kyambogo University

Ugandan students can register for the 11th annual competition until mid-December, with chances to compete regionally and globally.

Huawei Technologies Uganda launched its 11th annual ICT Competition at Kyambogo University on November 13, inviting students to develop practical technology skills. The event, themed “I.C. The Future,” aims to encourage registration for the 2026–2027 competition, which covers Network, Cloud, and Computing categories.

Students can register until mid-December 2026, with preliminary exams in December and national finals in January 2027. Successful candidates will train for regional examinations in March 2027, with top teams advancing to global finals in Shenzhen, China, in May 2027, competing for cash prizes from RMB5,500 to RMB15,000.

Dickson Mugerwa from Kyambogo University’s Faculty of Engineering urged students to gain practical ICT skills and certifications for a competitive job market. Amon Nasasira, a former contestant, shared that the competition helped him build professional connections and valuable skills in networking and cloud technologies.

Why it mattersStudents can register for the competition until mid-December 2026, with opportunities to win cash prizes and gain valuable ICT skills.

Agriculture · 2026-11-15

Nile Breweries honours 500 Sebei farmers

The company celebrated farmers in Kween District for their contribution to Uganda's barley value chain.

Nile Breweries Limited (NBL) celebrated over 500 barley farmers in Uganda's Sebei sub-region on Saturday at its Cheminy Drying Facility in Kween District. The event recognised farmers for their role in the country's barley value chain and shared practices to boost yields and reduce post-harvest losses. NBL supports approximately 25,000 farmers nationwide, sourcing more than 98% of its raw materials locally and spending over UGX 156 billion annually.

Joseph Luzinda, NBL's Agro Services Manager, stated that the brewery procures about 10,000 metric tonnes of barley annually and is developing a new barley seed variety with the National Agricultural Research Organisation (NARO). Sanjay Tanna, Minister of Trade, Industry and Cooperatives, noted that a ready market encourages farmer investment and that local processing creates employment. The minister commended NBL's partnership for contributing to national development.

NBL invested UGX 4 billion in the Cheminy barley cleaning and drying facility, opened in 2023, to help Sebei farmers improve grain quality after harvest, especially when rain affects drying. Nkanyiso Mncwabe, NBL Managing Director, highlighted the company's 75-year presence in Uganda and its commitment to supporting farmers through seed access, agricultural advice, and harvest handling. The "Cheers to Farmers" campaign celebrates this joint progress.

Why it mattersNile Breweries' annual spending of over UGX 156 billion on local sourcing directly impacts farmers by providing a ready market for their barley.
