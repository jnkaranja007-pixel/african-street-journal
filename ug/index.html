Germany grants Uganda €50 million for development

The funds will support water, electricity, agribusiness, and refugee communities.

Germany has committed €50 million in grants to Uganda to fund development projects. The funding, announced after two days of negotiations in Kampala on November 3, 2026, includes €30 million in Financial Cooperation and €20 million in Technical Cooperation.

Of the total, €35 million will go to water and electricity projects, with €10 million allocated to agribusiness and local economic development. An additional €5 million will support refugee-hosting communities. Finance Minister Henry Musasizi stated the funding aligns with Uganda’s National Development Plan IV.

The German delegation, led by Joachim Schmitt, noted the partnership supports Uganda’s ambition to graduate from Least Developed Country status. Schmitt encouraged greater participation by Ugandan, German, and European businesses to unlock economic potential and create jobs.

Why it mattersUganda will receive €50 million in grants from Germany to finance development projects across several sectors.

Kyaggwe wins maiden Airtel Masaza Cup title

Kyaggwe defeated Gomba 1-0 in a closely contested final at Wankulukuku stadium.

Kyaggwe has won their first-ever Airtel Masaza Cup after beating Gomba 1-0 in the 2026 final at Muteesa II Memorial Stadium, Wankulukuku, on Saturday, October 31. George Isingoma Kato scored the decisive goal for Kyaggwe, securing the historic title after a goalless first half. The Kabaka of Buganda, Ronald Muwenda Mutebi II, presented the trophy to the victorious team.

Airtel Uganda Sales and Distribution Director Ali Balunywa congratulated Kyaggwe and reaffirmed the company's commitment to its partnership with the Buganda Kingdom. He stated that the Masaza Cup promotes youth talent, discipline, teamwork, and community engagement. The Katikkiro of Buganda, Charles Peter Mayiga, also commended Kyaggwe's players, technical team, and supporters for their commitment and determination.

This victory marks a significant milestone for Kyaggwe, who were finalists in 2024 but lost to Buddu. The tournament, revived in 2004, brings together thousands of football fans from various counties within the Buganda Kingdom. Princess Katrina Ssangalyambogo also attended the final match.

Why it mattersKyaggwe county's football team won their first major trophy, bringing pride to their players, officials, and supporters.

Winnie Byanyima to chair environment institute board

The UNAIDS Executive Director will lead the International Institute for Environment and Development from January 2027.

Engineer Winnie Byanyima, the Executive Director of UNAIDS, has been appointed chairperson of the board of trustees for the International Institute for Environment and Development (IIED). Byanyima will start her new role in January 2027, following her departure from UNAIDS on December 31, 2026. The IIED is a policy and research organisation focused on climate change, nature loss, inequality, and sustainable development.

Byanyima, a former Mbarara Member of Parliament, will lead an institution that collaborates with governments, communities, and civil society on development and environmental issues. UNAIDS congratulated her on November 3, acknowledging her leadership in steering the global HIV/AIDS programme through significant challenges and transformations.

She expressed honour at joining IIED during a challenging period for sustainable development and multilateralism, stating a shared belief in partnership for lasting change. IIED Executive Director Tom Mitchell noted that Byanyima's experience and commitment to social justice would strengthen the organisation's mission to advance fairer and more sustainable futures.

Why it mattersUgandan Winnie Byanyima will lead a global institute addressing climate change and sustainable development.

Politics · 2026-11-04

Uganda minister halts Masaka city hall project

Minister Justine Nameere questions engineer's qualifications and ongoing work after halting Shs14 billion project.

Minister of State for Local Government Justine Nameere Nsubuga halted the Shs14 billion Masaka City headquarters construction on Tuesday. Nameere questioned the project engineer's qualifications and the use of manual excavation, noting that work continued despite a prior stoppage directive.

Minister Nameere expressed surprise that construction was ongoing after she had ordered a halt, stating, "The engineers and Town Clerk are questionable." She specifically questioned project engineer Musa Mukiibi, who stated he holds a Bachelor’s degree in Civil Engineering from Kyambogo University, graduating in December 2023.

The Minister also alleged that Mayor Florence Namayanja had persistently demanded Shs14 million, though the context of this demand remains unclear. The construction works were reportedly halted on September 2, and Masaka Town Clerk Uthuman Ssebadduka was arrested following the Minister's inspection.

Why it mattersUganda's Minister of State for Local Government has halted a Shs14 billion construction project and questioned officials, affecting local governance.

Man kills wife, then lynched by mob in Kanungu

Doreen Kirabo, 20, was killed by her husband Aaron Akandinda, 25, who was then beaten to death by villagers.

A man identified as Aaron Akandinda, 25, allegedly killed his wife, Doreen Kirabo, 20, with an axe on Monday, November 2, 2026, in Kakyenke Cell, Kanungu District. Kirabo had reportedly returned to their home after visiting her sick parents.

Following the attack on Kirabo, an angry mob gathered and pursued Akandinda, assaulting him until he died. Kigezi Region Police spokesperson Nelson Tumushime stated that no arrests have been made for the mob killing, and investigations are ongoing to identify the perpetrators.

Police condemned both the domestic murder and the mob killing, urging the public to report disputes to authorities instead of resorting to violence or taking the law into their own hands.

Why it mattersA mob lynching occurred in Kanungu District following a domestic murder.
