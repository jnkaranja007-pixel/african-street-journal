Health · 2026-11-09

Uganda minister promises more aid for Lacor Hospital

Health Minister Chris Baryomunsi will review financial support and tax exemptions for St. Mary’s Hospital Lacor.

Health Minister Chris Baryomunsi pledged to review government financial support for St. Mary’s Hospital Lacor in Gulu and consider tax exemptions for the institution. The commitment came during the launch of a €12.75 million (about Shs58.3 billion) health project in Kampala on November 7, funded by Italian development cooperation and implemented by AVSI Foundation Uganda.

Dominique Corti, President of the Piero and Lucille Corti Foundation, appealed for increased government assistance, noting that the foundation provides Shs7 billion annually to subsidise patient fees by about 70%. Lacor Hospital treats approximately 200,000 patients yearly and employs around 700 Ugandans.

Corti requested exemptions on PAYE, VAT, and import duties for donated goods, citing potential Shs1 billion in duties for electricity cables. Minister Baryomunsi stated he would recommend tax exemptions and examine government allocations to Lacor to strengthen financial support.

Why it mattersUgandan citizens needing affordable healthcare at St. Mary's Hospital Lacor will benefit from increased government financial support and tax exemptions.

Politics · 2026-11-09

Museveni names 13 new Permanent Secretaries

Appointments span ministries of Foreign Affairs, Defence, Public Service, and ICT.

President Yoweri Museveni has appointed 13 new Permanent Secretaries across key Ugandan government ministries and agencies. The changes, announced on Sunday, November 8, 2026, by the Presidential Press Unit, affect institutions including those responsible for foreign affairs, defence, public service, works and transport, agriculture, and water and environment.

Notable appointments include Prof. Stephen Akandwanaho replacing Dr. Aminah Zawedde at the Ministry of ICT and National Guidance, and Edith Butuuro taking over from Rosette Byengoma at the Ministry of Defence and Veteran Affairs. Emmanuel Freddie Mugunga is the new Permanent Secretary in the Office of the Prime Minister, replacing Alex Kakooza. These appointments are made under Article 174(2) of the 1995 Constitution.

The reshuffle aims to strengthen leadership and improve program implementation. Edward Sengonzi Damulira will replace Adolf Mwesige as Clerk to Parliament, a move occurring as Parliament transitions to its 12th session. Barbara Namugambe is the new Permanent Secretary at the Ministry of Agriculture, Animal Industry and Fisheries.

Why it mattersUgandans in public service roles have been replaced, affecting administrative efficiency and program implementation.

Politics · 2026-11-09

Museveni drops clerk Mwesige amid corruption probes

Edward Sengonzi Damulira nominated to replace Adolf Mwesige, who faced scrutiny over Shs27 billion corruption scheme.

President Yoweri Museveni has dropped Adolf Mwesige as Clerk to Parliament, ending his five-year tenure. Edward Sengonzi Damulira, an economist with a PhD, was nominated on Sunday to replace Mwesige. This change comes after Mwesige faced investigations into alleged corruption within parliamentary administration.

Mwesige's departure follows raids on his offices in May 2026 by State House investigators probing an alleged Shs27 billion corruption scheme. He was considered a potential state witness but was not formally charged. His tenure also saw scrutiny over a Shs1.7 billion service award paid to commissioners, leading a High Court judge to direct disciplinary action.

Damulira's appointment is subject to assessment by the Public Service Commission. President Museveni did not state Mwesige's next assignment. Senior press secretary Sandor Walusimbi confirmed that the full list of permanent secretary assignments will be shared later.

Why it mattersAdolf Mwesige was dropped as Clerk to Parliament following corruption investigations and scrutiny over parliamentary administration.

Business · 2026-11-09

Uganda hospitality sector urged to embrace green investment

Minister Nyamutoro launched a national initiative with Equity Bank to cut hotel operating costs and attract investment.

Uganda's hospitality industry is being challenged to turn its green energy ambitions into profitable investments. Minister of State for Energy and Mineral Development Phiona Nyamutoro unveiled a national initiative to reduce operating costs, attract investment, and strengthen tourism competitiveness. The National Green Hospitality Initiative, launched recently at Serena hotel Kampala, aims to make hotels, lodges, resorts, and conference facilities drivers of sustainable economic growth.

Co-hosted by the ministry and Equity Bank Uganda, the initiative focuses on affordable financing, clean-energy technology, and government policy. Minister Nyamutoro stated that renewable energy offers opportunities to lower operating costs, improve service reliability, and create new business opportunities. Savings from energy efficiency can be reinvested in staff, facilities, and visitor experiences, while also generating demand for related service providers.

The initiative aligns with Uganda’s Fourth National Development Plan and the Energy Policy for Uganda 2023. Key deliverables include identifying facilities for energy assessments, developing Green Hotel Standards, and establishing a financing window for green projects. Minister Nyamutoro called on businesses to develop bankable projects, financial institutions to unlock capital, and public institutions to create an enabling investment environment.

Why it mattersThe hospitality sector in Uganda is encouraged to adopt green energy to lower operational costs and attract investment, boosting tourism.

Culture · 2026-11-09

Amber Grace Birungi wins Miss Glamour Look International Uganda

The Taibah International School student also aims to study law at Harvard University and become a voice for the voiceless.

Amber Grace Birungi, a student at Taibah International School, secured the Miss Glamour Look International Uganda title. Birungi, who is also a young entertainer and youth advocate, celebrated women with her song "Woman" earlier this year.

The beauty pageant win is another step for Birungi, who previously won Miss High School Uganda in 2024 and was first runner-up in Miss Teen Pearl of Africa in 2025. Her mother, Dr Olive Enid Nabiryo, described her as disciplined and hardworking.

Birungi, born May 31, 2008, aspires to study law at Harvard University and dreams of becoming a voice for the voiceless. She also leads the Queen Birungi Amber Foundation, focusing on youth empowerment and mentorship.

Why it mattersAmber Grace Birungi's win and her stated ambitions will inspire young Ugandans.
