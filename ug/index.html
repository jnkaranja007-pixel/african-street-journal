Uganda police probe comedian Kapere's sudden death

The popular Amarula Family comedian collapsed at his daughter's school after complaining of a severe headache.

Police in Uganda have opened an investigation into the sudden death of comedian Yokana Mbuuse, known as Kapere Lufotose, aged 56. He collapsed on Sunday at Jolivo Day Care and Junior School in Wakiso district while attending a school event. Kapere complained of a severe headache before falling to his knees and was later pronounced dead at Royale Health Services Hospital.

Kampala Metropolitan Police spokesperson Racheal Kawala confirmed that a post-mortem examination will be conducted to determine the cause of his death. Kapere's wife, Lydia Nakandi, stated that he had been experiencing financial difficulties and had expressed despair over rent payments shortly before his collapse. He rose to fame with the Amarula Family comedy group and later appeared on WBS TV's Side Mirror programme.

Kapere was also an outspoken supporter of the National Unity Platform (NUP) and participated in party activities. NUP president Robert Kyagulanyi Ssentamu paid tribute to him as a loyal friend. Kapere's death has prompted mourning from fans and colleagues in both the entertainment and political spheres.

Politics · 2026-11-03

Uganda arrests retired general Peter Elwelu over subversive activities

Military says 15 guns and fatigues were recovered from the UPDF officer who was on the run in Soroti City.

Retired UPDF officer General Peter Elwelu was arrested in Soroti City on Monday evening for alleged subversive activities and plans to destabilize the Teso region. UPDF spokesperson Colonel Chris Magezi confirmed the arrest on X, stating Elwelu had been on the run. Security forces recovered 15 guns, military fatigues, and two pickup trucks linked to him.

Colonel Magezi described Elwelu as a "renegade UPDF officer" who will face charges and prosecution in court. The military announced the arrest after a weeks-long manhunt, with Elwelu's home in Kalaki District reportedly abandoned and guarded by security operatives.

Elwelu, who previously served as Deputy Chief of Defence Forces, built a significant military career before his arrest. Details regarding his court appearance and specific charges were not immediately available as of Monday evening, with security tightened in Soroti City.

Sport · 2026-11-03

Kyaggwe wins Masaza Cup after defeating Gomba

Centenary Bank sponsored the tournament for the 13th year, contributing UGX 200 million.

Kyaggwe County won the 2026 Masaza Cup after beating Gomba 1-0 in the final at Muteesa II Stadium, Wankulukuku. This marks Kyaggwe's first title since the tournament restarted in 2004. Kato George Isingoma scored the winning goal in the second half.

Centenary Bank, which has sponsored the tournament for 13 years, provided UGX 200 million for this year's event. The bank also awarded UGX 1 million to top scorer Kasule Mohamed and Herbert Masaba for best goalkeeper. Centenary Bank Managing Director Godfrey Byekwaso stated the cup nurtures talent and promotes unity.

The Masaza Cup has become a platform for identifying talent and preserving Buganda's cultural heritage, attracting thousands of supporters. Centenary Bank's support for the tournament is part of its broader community development initiatives, which include education and youth empowerment.

Why it mattersUgandan football fans and young players in the Buganda Kingdom are directly affected by the tournament's continuation and Centenary Bank's support.

Culture · 2026-11-03

Kampala Crème reality show returns for season four

MultiChoice Uganda and Uganda Breweries partner to launch the new season featuring five cast members.

The reality television series Kampala Crème launched its fourth season on Sunday in Kampala, Uganda. MultiChoice Uganda, a CANAL+ company, partnered with Uganda Breweries Limited (UBL) to host the event, which showcased the show's cast and new storylines.

Colin Asiimwe, Head of Marketing at MultiChoice Uganda, stated that the new season reflects a growing demand for local content about contemporary Ugandan women. He noted that three new cast members join returning stars Sheena Holm and Tamara Kainembabazi, introducing fresh dynamics and relatable stories.

Uganda Breweries provided a Tusker Cider experience at the launch event. Nancy Nansikombi, Brand Manager RTD at UBL, confirmed the company's support, noting that two of their influencers, Zeta and Sheena, are part of the Kampala Crème cast.

Why it mattersUgandan viewers will see new dynamics and relatable stories from five cast members in the fourth season of the Kampala Crème reality show.

Business · 2026-11-03

Coca-Cola rewards 26 Ugandan student creators

The beverage company awarded cash prizes to university students for digital content produced during a month-long competition.

Coca-Cola has rewarded 26 university students in Uganda with cash prizes for their digital content creation during the Essawa Yona Kisoboka Coca-Cola Content Creation Challenge. The awards ceremony took place on Saturday, October 31, 2026, at the Makerere University Freshers’ Ball held at Impis Rugby Grounds.

The month-long challenge invited students to produce original digital content inspired by their interests, including football, music, and food, focusing on English Premier League matchday experiences, meals, and Coca-Cola moments. Winners like Kalema Benjamin from MUBS and Nakityo Deborah from Kyambogo University received prizes for their creative output.

Barry Otieno, Coca-Cola Uganda’s Senior Marketing Manager, encouraged young creators to remain consistent and innovative, noting further prize opportunities in November. The initiative is part of Coca-Cola’s broader campaign to create earning opportunities for young people through social media platforms like TikTok and Instagram.
