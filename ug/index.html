Health · 2026-11-07

Uganda secures $25 million health grant

The funding will upgrade outbreak detection, laboratory capacity, and frontline worker support.

Uganda has received a $25 million grant from the international Pandemic Fund to strengthen its public health system. Vice President Jessica Alupo announced the funding, which amounts to approximately UGX 92 billion, on behalf of President Yoweri Museveni. The grant aims to enhance the country's capabilities following the 2026 Bundibugyo Ebola outbreak.

The funds are earmarked for tangible upgrades including faster detection and containment of viral threats like Ebola and Mpox, equipping regional labs for quicker specimen analysis, and providing targeted training and equipment for frontline health workers. Dr. Jean Kaseya, Africa CDC Director General, communicated the development during a briefing chaired by Vice President Alupo.

Ugandan Minister for Health Dr. Chris Baryomunsi will join the Africa CDC Governing Board as Chair, while epidemiologist Dr. Henry Kyobe Bosa has been appointed Africa CDC Director for Uganda. The Africa CDC, an accredited Implementing Entity for the Pandemic Fund, will channel financing directly to national projects. Implementation breakdowns are expected from the Ministry of Health and Africa CDC soon.

Why it mattersUgandans will benefit from faster detection of diseases, better equipped labs, and improved support for healthcare workers.

Pearl Bank launches Wendi mobile wallet campaign

The Wendi Ku Win campaign offers customers weekly chances to win prizes for digital transactions over sh10,000.

Pearl Bank launched its Wendi mobile wallet campaign, "Wendi Ku Win," on November 6, 2026, at Old Taxi Park in Kampala. The campaign aims to encourage digital financial transactions by rewarding customers who make weekly transactions exceeding sh10,000 via the Wendi mobile wallet.

The Wendi mobile wallet, developed by Pearl Bank, allows users to save, send, and manage money without a bank account or smartphone. Andrew Kabeera, Executive Director at Pearl Bank, stated that the campaign demonstrates Wendi's commitment to empowering customers financially and ushering more Ugandans into the digital money economy.

Customers making transactions over UGX. 10,000 can win fuel vouchers, smartphones, household appliances, and cash rewards up to UGX.100,000. This initiative aligns with the National Financial Literacy Strategy for 2023-2028, aiming to promote economic growth through accessible financial services.

Business · 2026-11-07

UEDCL replaces 1,110 transformers to boost electricity supply

Uganda Electricity Distribution Company Limited is strengthening its network capacity to meet a 71% customer growth over five years.

Uganda Electricity Distribution Company Limited (UEDCL) is implementing measures to improve electricity supply and address infrastructure limits as demand grows rapidly. The company's customer base expanded by 71% in five years, from 1.64 million in 2021 to about 2.8 million currently, straining the distribution network. UEDCL is initiating work on an additional eight-kilometre line in Gayaza to transfer demand to a substation with available capacity and is working with UETCL to relieve pressure on the Masaka Road network.

Acting Managing Director Eng. Joselynne R. Rwabwogo stated that UEDCL is prioritising immediate interventions for pressing supply challenges while pursuing longer-term infrastructure improvements. Network reconfiguration in Mubende reduced outages from 12 to three in one month, and emergency repairs to underground cables in Entebbe, Namasuba, and Kisubi are complete. The company also replaced 1,110 distribution transformers during the year ending July 2026, exceeding its target of 853.

UEDCL is increasing technicians and standby personnel to improve fault response capacity. Average transformer replacement times decreased from 38 days to under 14 days by the end of August. The company acknowledges customer concerns and is committed to enhancing the reliability of electricity supply.

Why it mattersUganda Electricity Distribution Company Limited is undertaking network improvements and transformer replacements to address increased demand and improve electricity supply reliability for its 2.8 million customers.

Austria, UNICEF commit Shs15 billion to Uganda water crisis

The funding will improve water, sanitation, and hygiene services for over 72,000 people in West Nile.

Austria has committed Shs14.96 billion to address water shortages in Uganda's West Nile region. The funding, channeled through the Austrian Development Agency and UNICEF, will support a three-year program in Yumbe and Madi-Okollo districts. This initiative aims to improve water, sanitation, and hygiene services for more than 72,000 direct beneficiaries, including children and women.

The program will provide improved access to safe, climate-resilient water and sanitation for approximately 50,000 people through five health centers. Another 8,000 community members will benefit from two new water supply systems designed to withstand climate disruptions. Sanitation and hygiene campaigns are expected to reach over 1.3 million people, including more than 400,000 refugees.

The program, named Strengthening the Humanitarian-Development-Peace Nexus through WASH and Climate Action in West Nile, runs from October 1, 2026, to September 30, 2029. Inadequate sanitation in the region means 71 pupils share a single school latrine, nearly twice the recommended standard, and one in three health facilities lack adequate water services.

Why it mattersOver 72,000 people, including children and women in West Nile, will gain improved access to safe water and sanitation services, addressing health risks and resource strain.

Ugandan chef Catherine Nalule to fly flag at World Tapas Championship

Chef Catherine Nalule secured third place at the Africa Tapas Competition, earning her a spot in Spain's global culinary event.

Kampala-based chef Catherine Nalule will represent Uganda at the 10th World Tapas Championship in Valladolid, Spain, on November 11. Nalule, also known as Chef Cathy, earned her place by finishing third at the inaugural Africa Tapas Competition held on October 3 in South Africa. She competed against seven chefs from across the continent, creating small, sophisticated dishes within a strict budget of USD 1.90 per portion.

Nalule, who works as a pastry instructor at the 7star Culinary Institute in Kampala, is the first Ugandan to ever compete in the World Tapas Championship. The Africa Tapas Competition featured chefs from seven African nations, with South Africa's Sasha-Lee Small winning first place and Egypt's Adham Khaled taking second. Organizers will cover all travel and accommodation expenses for the 16 finalists.

Ugandan TV host Douglas Lwanga and model Judith Heard have publicly celebrated Nalule's achievement, calling it a significant moment for the nation. Her participation aligns with the Ministry of Tourism, Wildlife and Antiquities' efforts to promote Uganda's food culture as a tourism driver through chef training and showcasing traditional dishes.

Why it mattersUgandan chef Catherine Nalule's participation in the World Tapas Championship promotes Ugandan gastronomy internationally as part of a government push to boost tourism.
