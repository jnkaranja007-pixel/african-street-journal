Uganda and SANY Group agree to develop green investment proposals

The proposed work covers urban sanitation, recycling infrastructure and electric trucks, but the meeting produced no funding commitment or project timetable.

Sources: Watchdog Uganda and Nile Post · 2026-11-27 · 1 min read

Ambassador Abbey Walusimbi, Uganda's senior presidential adviser on diaspora affairs, met representatives of the China-based SANY Group, a heavy-machinery and equipment maker, and Gogwa Corp, a Uganda-focused diaspora investment company operating from Sweden, at the President's Office in Kampala. They discussed investment in urban sanitation, waste management, recycling and electric mobility. The parties agreed to develop practical proposals, but announced no investment amount.

Reuben Chen, SANY's international sales and marketing manager for East Africa, and Namakajo Gogwa, chief executive of Gogwa Corp, took part. Gogwa said sorting and recycling could turn garbage into an economic resource. The discussions also covered recycling infrastructure, electric trucks and other environmentally friendly vehicles.

Gogwa said Ugandans abroad are active partners in attracting serious investors and bringing practical solutions home. The proposed collaboration is intended to promote cleaner cities and create green jobs, but the report gave no schedule for the proposals.

Why it mattersSANY Group and Gogwa Corp must now turn discussions into investment proposals, with no amount or deadline yet set.
