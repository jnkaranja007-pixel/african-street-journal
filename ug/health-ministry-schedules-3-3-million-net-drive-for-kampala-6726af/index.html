Health Ministry schedules 3.3 million-net drive for Kampala and Wakiso

The three-day distribution, set for November 20 to 22, will send 1,168,700 nets to Kampala and 2,000,216 to Wakiso.

Source: Nile Post · 2026-11-18 · 1 min read

Uganda’s Ministry of Health has scheduled its final 2026 mosquito-net distribution phase for Kampala and Wakiso District, with approximately 3.3 million insecticide-treated nets due to be distributed from November 20 to 22. The ministry says the campaign targets vulnerable people, particularly children under five and pregnant women.

Kampala is allocated 1,168,700 nets for an estimated two million residents, while Wakiso will receive 2,000,216 for about 3.9 million people. The distribution will run through 32 operational units aligned with administrative divisions and municipalities, with access prioritised for low-income urban communities. Acting malaria control programme head Dr Richard Kabanda said Uganda recorded more than 11.7 million malaria cases in 2025 and over 2,300 deaths; more than half of those who died were children under five.

Kabanda said consistent, proper net use can cut malaria transmission by about 50 per cent and reduce childhood mortality by up to 25 per cent. The Kampala-Wakiso phase follows the distribution of more than 17 million nets across several sub-regions since January. The ministry advises recipients to hang new nets for 24 hours before use and seek medical care promptly for malaria symptoms.
