Kyaggwe wins Masaza Cup after defeating Gomba

Centenary Bank sponsored the tournament for the 13th year, contributing UGX 200 million.

Source: Watchdog Uganda · 2026-11-03 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Kyaggwe County won the 2026 Masaza Cup after beating Gomba 1-0 in the final at Muteesa II Stadium, Wankulukuku. This marks Kyaggwe's first title since the tournament restarted in 2004. Kato George Isingoma scored the winning goal in the second half.

Centenary Bank, which has sponsored the tournament for 13 years, provided UGX 200 million for this year's event. The bank also awarded UGX 1 million to top scorer Kasule Mohamed and Herbert Masaba for best goalkeeper. Centenary Bank Managing Director Godfrey Byekwaso stated the cup nurtures talent and promotes unity.

The Masaza Cup has become a platform for identifying talent and preserving Buganda's cultural heritage, attracting thousands of supporters. Centenary Bank's support for the tournament is part of its broader community development initiatives, which include education and youth empowerment.

Why it mattersUgandan football fans and young players in the Buganda Kingdom are directly affected by the tournament's continuation and Centenary Bank's support.
