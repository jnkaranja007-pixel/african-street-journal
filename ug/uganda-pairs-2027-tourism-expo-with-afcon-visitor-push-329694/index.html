Uganda pairs 2027 tourism expo with AFCON visitor push

The May event will bring together two regional expos weeks before Uganda co-hosts the football tournament with Kenya and Tanzania.

Source: The Independent Uganda · 2026-11-28 · 1 min read

Uganda’s tourism board launched the 2027 Pearl of Africa Tourism Expo, to be held jointly with the East African Community Regional Tourism Expo in Kampala. The two events are scheduled for May 6-8 at Speke Resort Convention Centre, weeks before Uganda, Kenya and Tanzania co-host the Africa Cup of Nations from June 19 to July 17.

Tourism Board chief executive Juliana Kagwa said the campaign is designed to turn football visitors into longer-staying guests. At the previous expo, held over three days in May, 124 hosted buyers generated 7,745 business meetings and commitments worth about USh1.8 billion. The board remains in contact with 60% of those buyers, below its 75% target, Kagwa said.

Uganda Wildlife Authority executive director James Musinguzi called for more accommodation and domestic flights to destinations including Kidepo and Kisoro, and said some roads remain in poor condition. He said the authority would diversify wildlife-related products for the tourism board to market.

Why it mattersTourism businesses must prepare rooms and visitor services for the June-July tournament, while the tourism board is still following up with only 60% of the previous expo’s hosted buyers.
