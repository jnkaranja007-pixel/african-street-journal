Uganda's Dei BioPharma says it is ready to supply Africa's medicine market

Founder Matthias Magoola says the company has more than 500 products and will launch six antimalarial medicines within two weeks.

Sources: Watchdog Uganda and The Independent Uganda · 2026-11-27 · 1 min read

Ugandan drugmaker Dei BioPharma is ready to supply medicines and vaccines across Africa, founder Matthias Magoola said at a public health conference in Addis Ababa on Wednesday. He said the company had more than 500 products available and needed a market for its manufacturing capacity.

Magoola said those products cover 70% to 80% of the molecules Africa imports. The company's campus in Matugga, Wakiso District, covers more than 400 acres, with more than 30 factories under development. He said it would make medicines from active ingredients through to finished products, using local raw materials such as cassava, maize, potatoes and sugarcane.

The company plans to launch six antimalarial medicines within two weeks, Magoola said, and has invited Africa CDC officials to the launch. He also said it had filed more than 100 patent applications in the United States, with more than 72 published.

Why it mattersAfrican health buyers who take up its products would give Dei BioPharma the market it says it needs, starting with six antimalarials due within two weeks.
