Qatar lifts Ebola travel restrictions on Ugandan passengers

Qatari authorities announced the change on November 23, and Uganda no longer appears on the country's restricted-entry list in the International Air Transport Association (IATA).

Source: The Observer Uganda · 2026-11-24 · 1 min read

Qatar has restored entry for travellers arriving from or transiting through Uganda, after revising temporary restrictions linked to Ebola. Qatari authorities announced the change on November 23, and Uganda was removed from the relevant entry restrictions in the International Air Transport Association (IATA) travel database.

The revision opens travel for Ugandans going to Qatar for employment, education, business, tourism and family visits, as well as passengers connecting through its aviation hub. A November 20 advisory from immigration consultancy Fragomen had listed Uganda, the Democratic Republic of Congo and South Sudan among countries whose travellers were barred.

Uganda declared its Bundibugyo-virus outbreak over on July 28, after reporting 20 confirmed cases and completing 42 days without further cases. The health ministry continues screening at airports and land borders; returning Ugandans with recent travel to the Democratic Republic of Congo face 21 days of quarantine.

Why it mattersUgandans travelling for jobs, education and family visits can again enter Qatar, while the health ministry continues screening and 21-day quarantine for some returnees from the Democratic Republic of Congo.
