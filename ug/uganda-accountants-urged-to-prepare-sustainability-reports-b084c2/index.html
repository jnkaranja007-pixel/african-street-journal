Uganda accountants urged to prepare sustainability reports and address stigma

At ACCA Uganda’s convention in Kampala, speakers called for early environmental data collection and an end to silence around mental health.

Source: Softpower News · 2026-11-22 · 1 min read

The Association of Chartered Certified Accountants (ACCA) Uganda concluded its 2026 members convention at Speke Resort Munyonyo in Kampala, urging accounting professionals to address mental health stigma and prepare for sustainability reporting requirements. The two-day event brought finance professionals, business leaders and industry experts together to discuss changes in the profession.

Joseph Owolabi, Rubicola Consulting’s chief executive and a former ACCA president, urged organisations to collect environmental data early and run parallel reports before requirements become mandatory. He said reliable information would help businesses respond to regulators, investors and customers. Lydia Nankabirwa, who manages standards and technical services at Uganda’s Institute of Certified Public Accountants, said a committee would oversee a phased plan to implement international reporting standards and provide guidance to members.

Psychiatry professor Noeline Nakasujja of Makerere University said pressure to appear competent can keep professionals from acknowledging psychological difficulties. Convention discussions also covered green finance, carbon markets and sustainability in financial institutions’ lending, investment and risk management.

Why it mattersUgandan organisations preparing sustainability data will need to respond to regulators, investors and customers as international reporting standards move toward phased implementation.
