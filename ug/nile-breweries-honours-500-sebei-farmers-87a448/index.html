Nile Breweries honours 500 Sebei farmers

The company celebrated farmers in Kween District for their contribution to Uganda's barley value chain.

Sources: Softpower News and Nile Post · 2026-11-15 · 1 min read

Nile Breweries Limited (NBL) celebrated over 500 barley farmers in Uganda's Sebei sub-region on Saturday at its Cheminy Drying Facility in Kween District. The event recognised farmers for their role in the country's barley value chain and shared practices to boost yields and reduce post-harvest losses. NBL supports approximately 25,000 farmers nationwide, sourcing more than 98% of its raw materials locally and spending over UGX 156 billion annually.

Joseph Luzinda, NBL's Agro Services Manager, stated that the brewery procures about 10,000 metric tonnes of barley annually and is developing a new barley seed variety with the National Agricultural Research Organisation (NARO). Sanjay Tanna, Minister of Trade, Industry and Cooperatives, noted that a ready market encourages farmer investment and that local processing creates employment. The minister commended NBL's partnership for contributing to national development.

NBL invested UGX 4 billion in the Cheminy barley cleaning and drying facility, opened in 2023, to help Sebei farmers improve grain quality after harvest, especially when rain affects drying. Nkanyiso Mncwabe, NBL Managing Director, highlighted the company's 75-year presence in Uganda and its commitment to supporting farmers through seed access, agricultural advice, and harvest handling. The "Cheers to Farmers" campaign celebrates this joint progress.

Why it mattersNile Breweries' annual spending of over UGX 156 billion on local sourcing directly impacts farmers by providing a ready market for their barley.
