Stanbic Bank retains Uganda’s Bank of the Year title

The bank won four honours at Kampala’s 2nd Annual Bankers’ Awards, including Best Trade Finance Bank and two awards for its staff.

Sources: The Independent Uganda and Watchdog Uganda · 2026-11-22 · 1 min read

Stanbic Bank Uganda retained the Bank of the Year title for a second consecutive year, winning four awards at the 2nd Annual Bankers’ Awards in Kampala. The bank also received Best Trade Finance Bank, while two of its staff won individual honours.

Cathy Adengo, the bank’s head of sustainability, was named Banker of the Year. Grace Muwanguzi, its head of learning and development, received the Sector Leadership and Capacity Development Recognition Award. Mumba Kalifungwa, Stanbic’s chief executive, said the awards reflected the work of employees and customers’ trust in the bank.

The awards were presented by Henry Musasizi, Uganda’s state minister for finance, planning and economic development. Adengo also chairs the Uganda Bankers’ Association Sustainability Committee.

Why it mattersStanbic’s trade-finance award recognises its work providing businesses with financial services for domestic, regional and international trade.
