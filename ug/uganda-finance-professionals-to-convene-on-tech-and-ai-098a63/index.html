Uganda finance professionals to convene on tech and AI

The fifth ACCA Uganda Members Convention on November 19-20 will gather accountants and leaders to discuss profession-wide changes.

Source: Softpower News · 2026-11-06 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Finance and accounting professionals will gather in Kampala for the fifth ACCA Uganda Members Convention on November 19 and 20, 2026. The two-day event at Speke Resort and Convention Centre will focus on how technology, artificial intelligence, and evolving business demands are reshaping the profession. Organised by ACCA Uganda, the convention's theme is “Navigating Change: Flexible and Inclusive Career Pathways.”

The convention will convene accountants, business leaders, policymakers, ACCA members, affiliates, and students to discuss adapting to automation, AI, data-driven business, and global standards. Finance Minister Henry Musasizi is expected to officiate the opening, while National Planning Authority Executive Director Dr Joseph Muvawala will discuss accountants' role in Uganda’s growth. Speakers will also cover global opportunities and sustainability reporting.

Discussions will include AI in public financial management, Uganda’s sustainability roadmap, IFRS S1 and S2 adoption, green finance, and carbon markets. Topics also cover labour market changes, digital transformation, talent management, professional wellbeing, and mentorship. ACCA Uganda Country Manager Charlotte Kukunda stated the event will help professionals examine changes impacting their work and businesses.

Why it mattersFinance and accounting professionals in Uganda will examine changes impacting their roles and businesses, including new technologies and global standards.
