Uganda signs 10-year crude marketing deal with Vitol

The trader will sell Pearl Sweet crude to international buyers and train the state oil company as exports are expected in early 2027.

Sources: The Independent Uganda, Nile Post and Softpower News · 2026-11-22 · 1 min read

Uganda signed a 10-year agreement with international energy trader Vitol on Friday, November 20, allowing the company to market the country’s Pearl Sweet crude to international buyers. President Yoweri Museveni witnessed the signing at State House in Entebbe, alongside officials from the Uganda National Oil Company (UNOC) and the energy ministry.

Vitol will provide UNOC with technical guidance, training and access to buyers as the state company builds its own crude-marketing capacity. Energy Minister Monica Musenero said commercial arrangements and market readiness had to be in place before production could lead to international sales.

Under the initial terms, Uganda will sell crude on a Free on Board basis at the export terminal in Tanga, Tanzania, where the East African Crude Oil Pipeline ends; buyers will arrange transport from there. The government will later assess whether to extend the deal to cover transport to final destinations. Exports were expected to start in early 2027.

Why it mattersUNOC must build the technical and commercial capacity to market crude independently during the 10-year agreement, with exports expected in early 2027.
