Uganda AIDS Commission chief Nelson Musoba dies at 5:45 a.m.

The commission's director general died in a Kampala hospital after treatment for cancer-related complications, the minister for the Presidency said.

Sources: The Observer Uganda, Nile Post and The Independent Uganda · 2026-11-23 · 1 min read

Nelson Musoba, director general of the Uganda AIDS Commission, died at 5:45 a.m. on Sunday at Medipal Hospital in Kampala after cancer-related complications, the minister for the Presidency, Milly Babalanda, said. He had been receiving treatment at the hospital.

Babalanda announced the death in a post on X and said it was a loss to Uganda’s HIV/AIDS response. Musoba joined the commission in 2014 as director of planning and strategic information, became acting director general in March 2017 and was confirmed in February 2018.

Before joining the commission, he coordinated the Global Fund for AIDS, Tuberculosis and Malaria’s Uganda office from 2007 to 2012 while working as a senior health planner at the Ministry of Health. Uganda is due to mark World AIDS Day on December 1.

Why it mattersUganda AIDS Commission staff must continue coordinating the HIV response without their director general as the country prepares to mark World AIDS Day on December 1.
