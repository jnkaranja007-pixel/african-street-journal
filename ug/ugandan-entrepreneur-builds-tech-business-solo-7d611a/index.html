Ugandan Entrepreneur Builds Tech Business Solo

Andrew Kasi, known as 256 Genuine Gadgets Kampala, established a successful electronics business in Uganda through self-reliance.

Andrew Kasi, 31, has built a reputation for authentic tech sales in Uganda, operating independently from his base in Pioneer Mall. Known online as 256 Genuine Gadgets Kampala, Kasi chose a path of solitary effort in a market where partnerships are common. His business philosophy is rooted in the integrity and reputation emphasized by his father, Andrew Mubiru, during his upbringing in Naguru.

Kasi’s journey began with a deep curiosity about how machines function, leading him to pursue a Diploma in Electrical Engineering. This technical background provided him with expertise, differentiating him from merchants who simply buy and sell electronics. He aimed to be a master of his trade, understanding the intricacies of the technology he deals with.

After gaining experience in international trading hubs like the United Arab Emirates, Kasi returned to Uganda with a mission to redefine authenticity in the local tech market. His success, evidenced by a significant online following, demonstrates the viability of his independent approach and commitment to genuine products.

Why it mattersUgandan entrepreneurs are affected by the success of individuals like Andrew Kasi who build businesses independently, offering a model for navigating competitive markets.
