Kiira Motors builds Uganda’s vehicle industry from a university prototype

The company’s Jinja plant is designed to produce up to 5,000 vehicles a year as it advances electric buses and regional-market plans.

Source: Nile Post · 2026-11-25 · 1 min read

President Yoweri Museveni launched Uganda’s first homegrown electric vehicle, the two-seat Kiira EV, at Makerere University on November 24, 2011. Students and staff developed the battery-powered prototype through the university’s Centre for Research in Transportation Technologies.

The vehicle was a proof of concept, not a commercial model. Kiira Motors Corporation was established in 2014 as a state enterprise jointly owned by the Ugandan government and Makerere University. Its Jinja plant, in the Jinja Industrial and Business Park, is designed for annual output of up to 5,000 vehicles, with shops for bodywork, paint, chassis, trim and final assembly.

The company has moved towards commercial production with its Kayoola electric buses and has outlined plans to expand their use in Ugandan cities and pursue regional markets. It also plans to raise local content, develop domestic suppliers and explore battery manufacturing.

Why it mattersKiira Motors must develop domestic suppliers and pursue regional markets as it moves from a university prototype towards vehicle production.
