Women become majority of Rwanda's tertiary students

Women made up 51.8% of tertiary-level students in 2024, reversing the balance recorded in 2017.

Source: KT Press · 2026-11-23 · 1 min read

Women accounted for 51.8% of students in Rwanda's tertiary education institutions in 2024, overtaking men, according to the National Institute of Statistics of Rwanda (NISR). Tertiary education covers learning after secondary school at universities, polytechnics, colleges and other higher learning institutions.

The Measuring Gender-in-Trade Statistics – Country Report: Rwanda recorded the change. In 2017, men made up 53.4% of tertiary students and women 46.6%, the report said.

Higher education enrolment also grew: 153,318 students were enrolled in the academic year ending July 2025, compared with 130,474 the previous year, according to the Ministry of Education's 2024/25 Education Statistical Yearbook.

Why it mattersStudents and institutions are part of an expanded higher education system, with enrolment reaching 153,318 in the academic year ending July 2025.
