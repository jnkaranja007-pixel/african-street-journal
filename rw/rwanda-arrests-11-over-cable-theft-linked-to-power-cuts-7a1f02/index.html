Rwanda arrests 11 over cable theft linked to power cuts

Two Ugandan nationals are among those detained for stealing electricity infrastructure worth an estimated Rwf20 million.

Sources: KT Press and Taarifa Rwanda · 2026-11-05 · 1 min read

Rwanda Investigation Bureau (RIB) arrested 11 people, including two Ugandans, for stealing and destroying electricity cables. The criminal operation is linked to ongoing power outages across the country. The suspects are accused of theft, damaging infrastructure, and forming a criminal gang. The value of the stolen property is estimated at Rwf20 million.

RIB spokesperson Dr. Thierry Murangira B. stated the suspects operated as an organized network, with specific roles in stealing, transporting, and selling the cables. Nuwagaba Isaac and Mugisha Fred, the two Ugandan nationals, are accused of buying stolen cables and arranging their sale outside Rwanda. Motorcycle taxi operators and drivers allegedly assisted in transporting the stolen materials.

The recovered stolen cables weigh over one tonne. The suspects are currently detained at RIB stations in Nyarugenge and Kicukiro as investigations continue. This operation is part of RIB's efforts to tackle economic crimes and protect public infrastructure.
