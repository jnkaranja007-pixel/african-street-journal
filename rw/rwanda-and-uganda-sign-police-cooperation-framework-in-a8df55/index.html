Rwanda and Uganda sign police cooperation framework in Kigali

The framework will guide the two forces in carrying out a memorandum of understanding they signed last year.

Source: KT Press · 2026-11-18 · 1 min read

Rwanda and Uganda's police chiefs signed an implementation framework on Tuesday, November 17, during a bilateral meeting at Rwanda National Police headquarters in Kacyiru, Kigali. The framework is aimed at strengthening cooperation between the two countries' law enforcement institutions.

Rwanda's Inspector General of Police, CG Felix Namuhoranye, received Uganda's IGP Abbas Byakagaba and his delegation for talks on strengthening the existing relationship between the two police institutions. The discussions focused on cooperation for the mutual benefit of both countries.

The framework will guide implementation of a memorandum of understanding the two institutions signed last year. The Rwanda National Police and the Uganda Police Force said the meeting was part of efforts to deepen their institutional ties in areas of mutual interest.

Why it mattersPolice officers in both countries will work under a shared framework that sets out how last year's memorandum is put into practice.
