Kagame, Antigua PM seek expanded Africa-Caribbean trade

Rwandan president and Antigua and Barbuda prime minister discussed boosting commercial ties between the regions.

Source: Taarifa Rwanda · 2026-11-06 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

President Paul Kagame met with Antigua and Barbuda Prime Minister Gaston Browne on November 3 to discuss expanding trade and investment between Africa and the Caribbean. The leaders held discussions during the 2026 Commonwealth Heads of Government Meeting on Jumby Bay Island.

Browne encouraged greater engagement between African and Caribbean businesses, seeking mutually beneficial commercial opportunities. He stated that current global challenges offer chances for Global South countries to deepen cooperation and build stronger economic partnerships. Browne affirmed Antigua and Barbuda’s support for Rwanda on matters of mutual interest.

Kagame congratulated Browne on Antigua and Barbuda hosting CHOGM 2026 and expressed Rwanda's interest in closer relations with the country and the wider Commonwealth. Browne also serves as Commonwealth Chair-in-Office.
