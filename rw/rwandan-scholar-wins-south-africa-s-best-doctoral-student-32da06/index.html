Rwandan scholar wins South Africa's Best Doctoral Student award

Dr. Claudine Nkeragutabara Ndimurukundo received the honour at the Council for Scientific and Industrial Research's 2026 awards.

Source: Taarifa Rwanda · 2026-11-24 · 1 min read

Dr. Claudine Nkeragutabara Ndimurukundo, a Rwandan scholar with a PhD in Human Genetics, received the Best Doctoral Student Award at South Africa's 2026 Council for Scientific and Industrial Research (CSIR) Excellence Awards on 20 November.

Nkeragutabara said she was honoured to be recognised for her contributions to microbiome research. Rwanda's High Commission in South Africa said the award celebrated Rwandan talent contributing to scientific advancement in South Africa and highlighted opportunities for research and innovation collaboration between the two countries.

The annual awards recognise contributions to scientific research, innovation and technological advancement that address societal challenges. The ceremony took place at the CSIR International Convention Centre under the theme "Celebrating EPIC Achievements".

Why it mattersRwandan researchers working in South Africa's science system gain a public example of recognition, and the High Commission has pointed to wider research collaboration between the two countries.
