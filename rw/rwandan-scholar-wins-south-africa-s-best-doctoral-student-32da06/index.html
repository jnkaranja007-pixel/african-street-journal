Rwandan scholar wins South Africa’s Best Doctoral Student Award

Dr. Claudine Nkeragutabara Ndimurukundo received the honour at the Council for Scientific and Industrial Research’s 2026 awards.

Source: Taarifa Rwanda · 2026-11-25 · 1 min read

Dr. Claudine Nkeragutabara Ndimurukundo, a Rwandan scholar with a PhD in Human Genetics, won the Best Doctoral Student Award at South Africa’s 2026 Council for Scientific and Industrial Research Excellence Awards on 20 November.

Nkeragutabara said the award recognised her contributions to microbiome research. Rwanda’s High Commission in South Africa said her recognition celebrated Rwandan talent contributing to scientific research in South Africa and pointed to opportunities for collaboration between the two countries.

The annual awards recognise contributions to research, innovation and technology addressing societal challenges. South Africa’s Deputy Minister of Science, Technology and Innovation, Dr. Nomalungelo Gina, addressed scientific sovereignty and AI governance at the ceremony.

Why it mattersRwandan researchers and institutions now have a recognised award-winning scholar contributing to research in South Africa, with the High Commission citing opportunities for bilateral collaboration.
