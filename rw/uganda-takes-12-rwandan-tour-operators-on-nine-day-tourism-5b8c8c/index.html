Uganda takes 12 Rwandan tour operators on nine-day tourism trip

The Uganda High Commission in Kigali organised visits to wildlife parks and other sites to promote the country as a destination.

Source: Taarifa Rwanda · 2026-11-22 · 1 min read

Uganda is hosting 12 Rwandan tour operators on a nine-day familiarisation trip to its tourism sites, organised by the Uganda High Commission in Kigali. The itinerary includes Ziwa Rhino Sanctuary, Murchison Falls, Kibale, Queen Elizabeth National Park and Bwindi.

Uganda’s Foreign Affairs Ministry outlined activities including rhino tracking, chimpanzee and gorilla trekking, and wildlife safaris. The group is also due to visit Lake Bunyonyi and Kampala. The initiative is intended to promote Uganda as a tourism destination and strengthen cross-border partnerships, according to Taarifa Rwanda.

The trip is also linked to Uganda’s Tenfold Growth Strategy, the outlet reported. The source did not provide a financial target or say whether tour operators had agreed commercial deals.

Why it mattersThe 12 Rwandan operators are visiting Ugandan sites over nine days to assess tourism offerings and build cross-border partnerships.
