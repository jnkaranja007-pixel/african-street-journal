Rwanda hosts Ugandan and Rwandan commanders for cross-border security talks

Division commanders from Uganda and Rwanda have opened the 9th UPDF/RDF Proximity Commanders' Meeting, aimed at military cooperation and cross-border security.

Source: Taarifa Rwanda · 2026-11-25 · 1 min read

Officers of the Uganda People's Defence Forces (UPDF) arrived in Rwanda on Tuesday for the three-day 9th UPDF/RDF Proximity Commanders' Meeting. The meeting is aimed at strengthening military cooperation and cross-border security.

The Ugandan delegation is led by Major General Paul Muhanguzi, commander of the UPDF's 2 Division. The Rwanda Defence Force (RDF) delegation is led by Maj Gen Ruki Karusisi, commander of the RDF's 2 Division.

Eastern Province Governor Prudence Rubingisa accompanied both delegations and joined residents of Tabagwe Village in a civil-military cooperation (CIMIC) tree-planting exercise.

Why it mattersThe meeting gives the two divisions' commanders a formal forum to address security along their shared border over three days.
