Rwanda court sets December 8 resumption for Ingabire trial

The High Court in Kigali gave the defence until November 23 to file written responses to the prosecution’s submissions.

Source: Taarifa Rwanda · 2026-11-18 · 1 min read

Rwanda’s High Court in Kigali will resume proceedings on December 8, 2026, in the case involving opposition figure Ingabire Victoire and several co-accused. The court kept that date after their defence lawyers asked for more time to answer the prosecution’s submissions.

The court granted the defence until November 23, 2026, to submit written responses. Defence lawyer Me Gatera Gashabana asked the court to reconsider the hearing date, citing a planned trip that could affect his attendance, but the court declined to change it.

The defence is expected to file its responses before the next session in December, when the case returns to court on the date originally scheduled.

Why it mattersIngabire's defence team must file its written answers to the prosecution by November 23, ahead of the December 8 session.
