Rwanda’s farm insurance pays out Rwf11.6 billion since 2019

Crop farmers received more than Rwf6.7 billion and livestock keepers over Rwf4.9 billion under the national programme.

Source: Rwanda Dispatch · 2026-11-20 · 1 min read

Rwanda’s agricultural insurance programme has paid more than Rwf11.6 billion to farmers and livestock keepers since 2019, the Ministry of Agriculture and Animal Resources said on November 17. The total includes more than Rwf6.7 billion for crop farmers and over Rwf4.9 billion for livestock keepers.

At a payment exercise in Rwamagana District, BK General Insurance and the Rwanda Agriculture and Animal Resources Development Board disbursed Rwf204 million to beneficiaries. Joseph Museruka, the ministry official responsible for crop and livestock insurance, said payouts helped farmers recover from losses that could have wiped out their investments. The government pays 40% of premiums; farmers and livestock keepers pay the rest.

The programme has attracted 266,402 farmers and livestock keepers annually, and more than Rwf22 billion has been spent on premiums. It operates in all 30 districts and covers crops and livestock including rice, maize, cattle, poultry and fish farming. A cattle vaccination campaign accompanied the latest payments.

Why it mattersFarmers and livestock keepers pay 60% of premiums, while the scheme has paid them more than Rwf11.6 billion since 2019.
