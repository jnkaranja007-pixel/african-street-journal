Rwanda seeks hundreds of millions in investment with Raffles Family Office

The Asian wealth management firm will connect international investors with priority projects including Formula 1.

Source: Rwanda Dispatch · 2026-10-23 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Rwanda has partnered with Raffles Family Office, an Asian wealth management firm, to attract hundreds of millions of dollars in private investment. The agreement with the Rwanda Development Board (RDB), announced Oct. 22, 2026, aims to connect international investors with priority economic development projects. Raffles Family Office will promote opportunities in aviation, tourism, real estate, and infrastructure, including developments for the country’s planned Formula 1 Grand Prix.

The partnership seeks to connect wealthy individuals and families through Raffles Family Office’s network with projects like Kigali International Airport in Bugesera and Airport City. RDB Chief Executive Jean-Guy Afrika stated that private-sector participation is important for realizing investment opportunities associated with Formula 1, which is expected to catalyze business development and job creation.

Raffles Family Office Chief Executive Chi Man Kwan highlighted the potential for international capital to support long-term investment in Rwanda and Africa. The firm appointed Jean de Dieu Uwihanganye, a former Rwandan minister of state, as principal for emerging markets in September 2026 to expand its engagement across the continent.

Why it mattersRwanda seeks to attract hundreds of millions of dollars in private investment for national development projects.
