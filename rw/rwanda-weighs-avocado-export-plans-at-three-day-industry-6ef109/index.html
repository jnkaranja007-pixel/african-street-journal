Rwanda weighs avocado export plans at three-day industry meeting

Growers, exporters and officials are reviewing production, quality and market access as Rwanda seeks more foreign exchange from avocados.

Source: Taarifa Rwanda · 2026-11-25 · 1 min read

Rwandan avocado growers, exporters and officials have begun a three-day meeting to discuss ways to increase production, improve quality and reach more international markets. The discussions cover the chain from cultivation and harvesting through processing, packaging and distribution.

Sandrine Urujeni, deputy chief executive for operations at the National Agricultural Export Development Board (NAEB), said the board would work with government agencies, development partners and private businesses to develop commercial avocado farming. Participants are also reviewing findings from an avocado farming census intended to show industry performance, gaps and investment needs.

The census is expected to inform measures to raise productivity, increase value addition and diversify export destinations. The report says Rwanda’s landlocked position makes transport costs and logistics important to export competitiveness.

Why it mattersGrowers and exporters are using the census findings to identify productivity gaps and investment needs across Rwanda’s avocado industry.
