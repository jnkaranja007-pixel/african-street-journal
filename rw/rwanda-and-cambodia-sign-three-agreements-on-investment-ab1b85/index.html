Rwanda and Cambodia sign three agreements on investment, tourism and air services

Kagame and Cambodia's prime minister witnessed the signings at the Peace Palace in Phnom Penh during Kagame's state visit.

Source: Taarifa Rwanda · 2026-11-19 · 1 min read

President Paul Kagame and Cambodian Prime Minister Hun Manet watched the signing of three agreements covering investment promotion, tourism and air services at the Peace Palace in Phnom Penh. Kagame arrived in Cambodia on November 12 with a senior Rwandan delegation for a state visit and the 20th Francophonie Summit.

Both governments discussed Rwanda as a possible entry point for Cambodian goods into African markets, and Cambodia as a possible entry point for Rwandan businesses into Southeast Asia. The air services agreement creates the legal basis for future flight connections, but no direct Kigali–Phnom Penh flight has been announced.

During the visit Kagame also met Cambodia's most senior political figures: Senate President Hun Sen, National Assembly President Khuon Sudary and Prime Minister Hun Manet.

Why it mattersRwandan and Cambodian businesses now have a legal framework for air links and a government-backed route into each other's markets, though travellers must still connect through third countries.
