Rwanda seeks solar energy deals in India

A Rwandan delegation is in New Delhi for the International Solar Alliance assembly to find new partnerships and investments.

Source: Taarifa Rwanda · 2026-11-04 · 1 min read

Rwanda is attending the 9th General Assembly of the International Solar Alliance in New Delhi, India, from November 2-5 to boost solar energy partnerships. State Minister for Infrastructure Armand Zingiro leads the delegation, which includes Ambassador Jacqueline Mukangira, aiming to expand access to affordable and sustainable solar power.

The assembly offers a platform to review global solar progress, strengthen cooperation, and find solutions for financing and infrastructure challenges. Rwanda seeks to attract investment and gain technical expertise to accelerate solar projects and increase electricity access in underserved areas.

Discussions will also cover mobilizing funds for solar projects, encouraging private investment, and promoting technology transfer. The event runs alongside renewable energy and green hydrogen conferences, potentially offering Rwanda opportunities to engage investors and technology providers for its energy transition.
