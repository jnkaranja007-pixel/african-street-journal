Rwanda pitches luxury tourism to Asia-Pacific buyers in Sydney

A delegation led by tourism chief Irène Murerwa is promoting premium trips to buyers and advisers at Luxperience 2026, running through November 20.

Source: Taarifa Rwanda · 2026-11-20 · 1 min read

Rwanda is promoting its luxury travel experiences to buyers and advisers from Australia, New Zealand and other markets at Luxperience 2026, a travel trade event in Sydney running from November 17 to 20. The delegation is led by Rwanda Development Board Chief Tourism Officer Irène Murerwa and includes local tour operators and travel agents, working with the Rwandan High Commission in Singapore.

The delegation is presenting gorilla trekking in Volcanoes National Park, safaris in Akagera and chimpanzee tracking and canopy walks in Nyungwe. Its aim is to secure travel partnerships and encourage companies to include Rwanda in premium holiday packages. The event brings together luxury tourism buyers, travel advisers and industry players.

Rwanda is also promoting leisure and cultural tourism around Lake Kivu. The report says partnerships with international advisers could bring bookings and visitor spending to hotels, tour operators, transport providers, restaurants and community-based tourism initiatives.

Why it mattersRwandan hotels, tour operators, transport providers and restaurants are being pitched for bookings through partnerships with international travel advisers.
