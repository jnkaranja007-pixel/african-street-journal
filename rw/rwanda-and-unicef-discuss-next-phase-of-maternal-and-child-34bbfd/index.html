Rwanda and UNICEF discuss next phase of maternal and child health work

Health Minister Sabin Nsanzimana and UNICEF regional director Etleva Kadilli reviewed four decades of cooperation and community health support.

Source: Taarifa Rwanda · 2026-11-20 · 1 min read

Rwanda’s Health Minister Dr Sabin Nsanzimana and Etleva Kadilli, UNICEF’s regional director for Eastern and Southern Africa, discussed continuing cooperation on maternal and child health at a meeting in Rwanda. The government is seeking to strengthen its partnership with the United Nations Children’s Fund (UNICEF) and expand investment in human capital development.

The talks reviewed UNICEF’s 40 years of work with Rwanda, including support for maternal and child health services and efforts to build community capacity. Nsanzimana said the partnership helps strengthen those services and communities; both sides discussed sustaining cooperation and improving community-level interventions.

UNICEF is marking 80 years of global operations. The two sides also discussed priorities for the next phase of cooperation, with maternal and child health among the areas under consideration.

Why it mattersHealth workers and communities involved in maternal and child services are the focus of the next phase of cooperation after 40 years of UNICEF support.
