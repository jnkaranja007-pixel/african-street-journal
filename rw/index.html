Mushikiwabo seeks third term as Francophonie leader

Rwanda's Louise Mushikiwabo faces competition from DRC and other candidates in November 16 election.

Rwandan Louise Mushikiwabo is seeking a third term as the leader of La Francophonie, with elections scheduled for November 16 in Phnom Penh. She faces competition from Juliana Amato Lumumba of the Congo, Coumba Bâ of Mauritania, and Dacian Cioloș of Romania. The race is complicated by ongoing tensions between Rwanda and the Democratic Republic of Congo, with Kinshasa accusing Kigali of aiding rebels.

Mushikiwabo, who has held the position since 2018 and was re-elected in 2022, benefits from incumbency and familiarity with the organisation's mechanisms. French President Emmanuel Macron previously backed her, stating she has "all the skills for the job." Rwandan President Paul Kagame has been actively lobbying member states to support her re-election bid.

The Democratic Republic of Congo has threatened to withdraw from the organisation if Mushikiwabo secures a third term, fielding its own candidate, Juliana Amato Lumumba. Lumumba's candidacy aims to leverage the DRC's demographic weight, but her campaign may be hindered if seen as a move to oppose Kigali.

Why it mattersThe Democratic Republic of Congo threatens to withdraw from the Francophonie if Rwanda's candidate Louise Mushikiwabo wins a third term.

Equity Bank Rwanda launches EMMA digital wallet

The new app allows users to make payments, save money, and access credit based on transaction history.

Equity Bank Rwanda launched its EMMA digital wallet app on November 5 in Kigali, enabling users to make payments, send and receive money, save, and access credit. The app connects to Rwanda’s payments network, including eKash, MTN MoMo, and Airtel Money, and does not require customers to have an existing Equity Bank account to register.

Managing Director Hannington Namara stated that the app aims to help money move lives forward, building on Rwanda's financial inclusion progress. Diane Uwase, a Kigali small business owner, found the app useful for managing everyday business payments directly from her phone, simplifying transactions that previously required bank visits.

FinScope 2024 data shows 96% of Rwandan adults are financially included, with 22% holding bank accounts. Namara emphasized that EMMA's success will be measured by active use and saving habits, not just downloads, and that customer transaction activity may influence eligibility for overdrafts and short-term loans.

Why it mattersRwandan adults, particularly small business owners, can now manage daily transactions and potentially access credit more easily through their mobile phones.

Seven miners killed in Kigali mine flood

Water flooded underground tunnels at a mineral extraction site operated by Calliane Mines Ltd in Nyarugenge District.

Seven miners died on Thursday, November 5, when water flooded underground tunnels at a mineral extraction site in Nyarugenge District, Kigali City. The site is operated by Calliane Mines Ltd.

The incident happened in Mageragere Sector, near the Nyabarongo River, as miners extracted minerals underground. Water suddenly entered the tunnels around 4:00 p.m., trapping seven miners who could not escape. About 200 people were present at the mining site when the flooding occurred. Local authorities and security agencies retrieved the seven bodies.

The official cause of the flooding remained undetermined by Friday. Miners at the site suspected that excavation activities might have breached an underground water source linked to the Nyabarongo River. Nyarugenge District officials visited the site on Friday and confirmed the recovery of all seven bodies, with arrangements being made for their burial.

Agriculture · 2026-11-07

Rwandan farmer boosts maize harvest tenfold

Anatholie Ayinkamiye increased her yield from four bags to 35 bags after joining a support program.

Anatholie Ayinkamiye, a 39-year-old farmer from Nyagatare District, increased her maize harvest from four bags to 35 bags after joining the Tubura program in 2021. Previously, she farmed one hectare under the traditional Tugabane arrangement, yielding only enough to replant and feed her family. Access to financing, improved seeds, quality inputs, and training transformed her production.

Ayinkamiye's increased yield allowed her to complete a loan for a house, a significant financial security improvement. Tubura supports farmers like Ayinkamiye through over 1,800 field officers offering inputs and training within walking distance. The organization also distributed over 145 million trees since 2016 to build farm resilience.

The program also connects farmers to markets, with Odette Mukabarame of Gisagara District now selling avocados reliably. Last year, Tubura aggregated and exported 378 metric tonnes of produce. A new avocado oil processing facility, Avocado Oil Industries, can process over 200 metric tons weekly, retaining value and creating jobs.

Why it mattersRwandan farmers like Anatholie Ayinkamiye and Odette Mukabarame are improving their incomes and financial security through access to better farming inputs, training, and market connections.

Kigali Universe blends cinema with arcade games

FrameLAB's Flick Movie Night offers interactive entertainment combining gaming and film screenings.

Kigali Universe is offering a new entertainment experience by merging movie screenings with arcade gaming. The initiative, called Flick Movie Night and presented by FrameLAB, aims to create a more interactive social outing for residents.

Prince Ishimwe, Head of Arcade at Kigali Universe, stated the goal is to provide an evening where visitors can engage with activities and each other before a film. Activities begin at 5pm, with movie screenings starting at 6:30pm, two days a week.

Organizers have introduced various ticket packages, including options for individuals and pairs, to cater to different preferences and budgets. These packages range from movie-only access to combined cinema and arcade gaming experiences.

Why it mattersResidents in Kigali can now enjoy a combined cinema and arcade gaming experience for leisure.
