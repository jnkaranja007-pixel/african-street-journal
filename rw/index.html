Rwanda Seeks Investors for 200MW Floating Solar Plant

The Energy Development Corporation Limited is inviting independent power producers to develop a 200-megawatt floating solar plant with battery storage on Lake Sake.

Rwanda is seeking private investors to develop a 200-megawatt floating solar power plant on Lake Sake in Ngoma District, Eastern Province. The project will include a battery storage system capable of supplying electricity for four hours. The Energy Development Corporation Limited (EDCL), a subsidiary of the Rwanda Energy Group, has invited independent power producers to submit applications for prequalification by November 12, 2026.

The selected investor will be responsible for the design, financing, construction, installation, and operation of the facility, including grid connection infrastructure. The plant will combine floating solar panels with a battery energy storage system rated at 200MW/800 megawatt-hours to store excess daytime electricity for peak evening demand. The project is expected to be completed and commissioned within 18 months.

Under a proposed public-private partnership, the developer will operate and maintain the plant for 25 years through a long-term power purchase agreement with EDCL. Lake Sake was chosen due to the Eastern Province's abundant sunshine throughout the year, according to an EDCL project manager. A site visit for interested investors is scheduled for October 23.

Why it mattersInvestors are sought to finance, build, and operate a new 200MW floating solar power plant and battery storage system.

Rwanda Shuts 250 Alcohol Factories for Substandard Production

Health Minister Nsanzimana stated 250 factories were closed for producing substandard alcoholic drinks, with 200 more awaiting licenses.

Rwanda has closed 250 factories producing substandard alcoholic drinks, locally known as ibyuma, with an additional 200 businesses that applied for licenses not yet operational, according to Health Minister Dr. Sabin Nsanzimana. He noted that the production of these drinks expanded during the Covid-19 pandemic, with some producers switching from bananas to ethanol mixed with powdered flavorings due to rising banana prices. Authorities intervened to halt operations at the 250 identified substandard factories.

The minister clarified that the 200 businesses awaiting licenses are distinct from those already producing the drinks. While some producers were reportedly operating without any licenses, a specific figure was not provided. Nsanzimana advised those affected by the closures and restrictions to explore alternative business ventures, such as food processing, non-alcoholic beverages, nutritious products, and agriculture.

During a meeting of the National Consultative Forum of Political Organisations, representatives questioned the licensing process, specifically how so many factories obtained permits from the government agency responsible for regulating medicines and food products. The forum aimed to address concerns surrounding the proliferation and regulation of such businesses within the country.

Why it matters250 factories producing substandard alcoholic drinks were closed, affecting businesses and their owners.

Rwanda cyclists target medals at African Championships

A squad of 29 Rwandan riders will compete in the African Cycling Championships in Kisumu, Kenya, aiming to improve on last year's performance.

Rwanda's 29 cyclists began their campaign at the African Cycling Championships in Kisumu, Kenya, today, with the individual time trials and junior races commencing the five-day event. The Rwandan team, comprising riders from junior to elite categories, aims to secure medals and surpass their previous year's results. Coach Félix Ssempoma stated that the large selection of riders was a strategic decision to increase their chances of reaching the podium, facing strong competition from South Africa, Eritrea, Algeria, and Morocco.

Defending junior women's champion Akimana Donatha, who has achieved multiple gold medals recently, leads Rwanda's challenge alongside last year's bronze medallist Yvonne Masengesho. In the elite men's category, Moise Mugisha, who placed third last year, heads the experienced Rwandan contingent, while Eritrea's Merhawi Kudus and South Africa's Hayley Preen are defending their elite titles in the men's and women's races, respectively. The championships feature 282 riders from 33 countries across various age categories.

The championships, running from October 21 to 25, include individual time trials, a mixed team relay, and road races. Kisumu's hot lakeside climate poses a potential challenge, particularly for the longer road races, with organizers providing medical support and water stations. Rwanda's participation follows their recent trip to the UCI Road World Championships in Montréal, where their riders gained experience against top international competition.

Rwanda U-17 Team Qualifies for 2027 AFCON Tournament

The junior Wasps secured their spot in the continental competition by defeating Ethiopia 2-1 in a decisive playoff match.

Rwanda's U-17 national team has qualified for the 2027 Africa Cup of Nations (AFCON) in Morocco after a 2-1 victory over Ethiopia at Kigali Pelé Stadium. The young Amavubi juniors scored twice in the first 16 minutes, with Heylen Jayden Shema opening the scoring in the fifth minute and Isaac Ndizeye adding another from a corner in the 15th minute. Ethiopia managed to pull one goal back before halftime, but Rwanda held on through sustained second-half pressure to secure their qualification.

This achievement marks a significant milestone for Rwandan youth football, with head coach Jimmy Murisa highlighting the result as a testament to years of investment and development. The team entered the match having lost to Somalia in the semi-finals, making Monday's playoff a crucial opportunity to achieve their primary objective of reaching the continental tournament.

Shema, who was given a starting role after often being a substitute, made an immediate impact, scoring the opening goal from an assist by Pacifique Bahati. The team's early offensive pressure led to the corner from which Ndizeye scored the second goal, setting the stage for their successful defense of the lead.

Why it mattersYoung Rwandan footballers are affected by this qualification as it represents a major achievement in their development and a chance to compete on a continental stage.

Rwanda, Benin, Ghana Link Digital ID Systems for Cross-Border Testing

Smart Africa's platform connects national digital identity systems, enabling travelers to use IDs across borders for services and travel.

Rwanda, Benin, and Ghana are preparing to test a shared platform, the Smart Africa Data Exchange (SADX), designed to verify digital identities issued by one country to citizens of another. The platform has already integrated the three nations' national ID systems, with real-world trials planned but not yet dated. Initial tests will focus on verifying identities for travelers between Benin and Rwanda by air, and between Ghana and Benin at land borders.

The initiative, announced by Smart Africa on X, aims to create a seamless and secure way for Africans to use their digital identities across borders for purposes such as travel, work, and accessing services. SADX operates under the Smart Africa Trust Alliance (SATA), which promotes trusted identity checks and personal data sharing among member countries while allowing each nation to retain control over its own digital identity infrastructure.

Technical meetings in Kigali earlier this year laid the groundwork for this integration, addressing infrastructure linkage, trust mechanisms via public key infrastructure, and the use of application programming interfaces for data exchange. The upcoming trials will assess the secure and reliable cross-border verification capabilities of these interconnected systems, with wider rollout contingent on trial results and government agreements.

Why it mattersFarmers may benefit from easier access to services or inputs if digital identity systems streamline cross-border trade and administrative processes.
