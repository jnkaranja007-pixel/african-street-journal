Kagame to attend Francophonie summit amid leadership contest

Rwanda's candidate Louise Mushikiwabo seeks a third term as OIF Secretary-General against DRC's Juliana Lumumba.

President Paul Kagame is expected to attend the 20th Francophonie Summit in Phnom Penh, Cambodia, on November 15 and 16. Rwanda will contest the election for the Secretary-General position for the 2027-2030 term. Rwanda's current leader, Louise Mushikiwabo, who has held the post since 2019, is seeking re-election.

Mushikiwabo faces competition from Juliana Lumumba of the Democratic Republic of Congo, Coumba Ba of Mauritania, and former Romanian Prime Minister Dacian Ciolos. The election will place Rwanda and the DRC in opposition, with Kinshasa promoting Lumumba based on the DRC's population and French-speaking status. Rwanda is campaigning on Mushikiwabo's record of reforms. The summit's theme is “Reinvest in Peace for Shared Prosperity and Lasting Stability.”

Kagame's attendance could bolster Rwanda's campaign during negotiations among the OIF's 53 full member states. The organisation typically seeks consensus for its Secretary-General, but a secret ballot is possible if no agreement is reached. The summit, Cambodia's first, will also discuss international cooperation, education, culture, digital innovation, governance, and sustainable development.

Why it mattersPresident Kagame's presence at the summit will support Rwanda's bid for its candidate to lead the International Organisation of La Francophonie.

Rwanda signs nuclear deal with Russia

The agreement aims to develop a small modular reactor and a Centre for Nuclear Science and Technology.

Rwanda and Russia have signed a new cooperation agreement to develop a small modular reactor and a Centre for Nuclear Science and Technology. Rwanda’s Ambassador to Moscow, Joseph Nzabamwita announced the deal, signed by the Rwanda Atomic Energy Board (RAEB) and Russia’s Rosatom during the Third Russia-Africa Summit in Moscow.

Ambassador Nzabamwita stated that construction works will soon commence in Rwanda, creating jobs for the country's youth. This partnership advances Rwanda's multi-year program to diversify electricity generation, support industrial expansion, and apply nuclear technology in healthcare, agriculture, and mining.

Rwanda and Russia began formal cooperation on peaceful nuclear energy in December 2018. In July 2026, RAEB and Rosatom signed a cooperation roadmap on SMRs, covering project development, human resources, nuclear infrastructure, and regulatory requirements.

Why it mattersRwanda's nuclear program will be advanced through a new agreement with Russia, potentially impacting electricity generation and industrial expansion.

Bank of Kigali marks 60 years with football and music tour

The bank's anniversary celebrations began in Rubavu with a BK Pro League match and concerts by local artists.

Bank of Kigali (BK) launched its 60th anniversary nationwide tour in Rubavu on October 31, 2026, bringing football and live music to fans. The celebration kicked off with a BK Pro League weekend featuring APR FC defeating Etincelles FC 2-0. Following the match, artists Riderman and Juno Kizigenza, with DJ RY, performed for the crowd.

The tour aims to celebrate six decades of service by taking the festivities to communities across Rwanda, not just Kigali. BK, as the title sponsor of the BK Pro League, is using match days to provide fans with a full day of sport and entertainment. This initiative is part of the bank's year-long BK@60 celebration.

The Rubavu event continues on November 1, 2026, with Marine FC hosting Rayon Sports FC, accompanied by entertainment from DJ June. Kitoko Bibarwa is scheduled to headline the evening concert. BK stated the anniversary celebration belongs to every Rwandan and aims to unite people through football and music.

Why it mattersRwandans in Rubavu experienced a weekend of football and live music as part of Bank of Kigali's 60th anniversary tour.

Politics · 2026-11-02

RPF members in Europe meet in Warsaw

Young party members from 19 European countries gathered to discuss contributing to Rwanda's development and Vision 2050 goals.

Members of RPF-Inkotanyi and friends of the organisation met in Warsaw, Poland, for a retreat focused on strengthening social cohesion and contributing to Rwanda’s development. The gathering on Saturday, October 31, brought together young party members from 19 European countries.

RPF Secretary-General Christophe Bazivamo urged participants to use their skills, knowledge, and resources to support Rwanda’s development and its Vision 2050 goals. He stated that achieving the country’s ambition to eliminate poverty by 2050 requires faster economic growth and contributions from Rwandans at home and abroad.

A panel themed “From Heritage to Responsibility” explored how young Rwandans could draw on opportunities and experience in their countries of residence to contribute to Rwanda. The discussions aimed to foster stronger ties and collaboration for national progress.

Why it mattersRwandans living in Europe discussed how to contribute their skills and resources to Rwanda's development and poverty elimination goals by 2050.

Sport · 2026-11-02

Rwanda trains 70 coaches in football GPS technology

The national football federation aims to improve player preparation and reduce injury risks through data analysis.

The Rwanda Football Federation (FERWAFA) trained 70 coaches and football professionals in GPS tracking technology on October 29 and 30. This technology monitors players' physical performance during training sessions.

The workshop, held at the FERWAFA Hotel in Remera, taught participants how to interpret data on player movement, speed, and acceleration. Experts stated this data helps manage fatigue and injury risks by adjusting training intensity.

Claudio Altieri, Amavubi's performance specialist, and three other experts facilitated the sessions. FERWAFA seeks to integrate sports science into coaching practices, mirroring methods used by top international clubs.

Why it matters70 Rwandan football coaches and professionals learned to use GPS technology to enhance player performance and prevent injuries.
