Agriculture · 2026-08-21

AfDB plans $400 million for Rwanda agriculture

The African Development Bank plans to invest more than $400 million into Rwanda's agriculture sector over the next three years. The bank stated it seeks to shift some of the sector's focus.

Why it mattersThe funding provides capital to modernize farming practices and increase crop yields for Rwandan farmers.

Health · 2026-08-21

Rwanda low birth weight rate reaches 8.8%

The proportion of babies born weighing less than 2.5 kilograms reached 8.8% in 2025. The National Institute of Statistics of Rwanda reported the figure in the Rwanda Demographic and Health Survey.

Why it mattersThe increase in low birth weight infants requires higher medical intervention and neonatal care for affected newborns.

WFP delivers 5,000 metric tonnes of rice to three nations

DP World and the United Nations World Food Programme delivered a 5,000-metric-tonne rice shipment to support Rwanda, the DRC, and Burundi. The shipment arrived at the Port of Dar es Salaam from India.

Why it mattersThe rice shipment provides immediate food relief to communities in Rwanda facing acute food insecurity.

Kirehe district ranks first in national exams

Kirehe district in the Eastern Province ranked first nationally in both national examination results released Thursday. The results were issued for 2026.

Rwanda and Japan discuss mining investment

The Rwanda Development Board met with a delegation of Japanese mining investors on Wednesday. The discussions focused on deepening economic cooperation in the mining sector.
