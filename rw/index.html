Rwanda raises social sector spending to Rwf1.711 trillion

Education, health, social protection and water and sanitation together take 22% of the national budget for 2026/2027, a 4.2% rise on the previous year.

Rwf1.711 trillion, or 22% of Rwanda's total national budget, is now allocated to social sectors for the 2026/2027 financial year, a 4.2% increase on the previous year, according to a new report by UNICEF and the Ministry of Finance and Economic Planning (MINECOFIN). The money covers education, health, social protection and water, sanitation and hygiene (WASH).

The report was presented in Kigali at an event jointly organised by UNICEF and MINECOFIN, attended by government institutions and development partners. It examines whether the extra spending is improving access to quality schooling, healthcare, social protection and basic sanitation, by setting budget figures against development outcomes. UNICEF and MINECOFIN said reliable data is needed to make sure public investment delivers measurable benefits to children and their families.

The findings are expected to feed into discussions on the 2027/2028 budget, where officials will look at how resources can be directed toward children's needs. The report also assesses results achieved through investments in children and identifies areas that need more attention.

Why it mattersFamilies and children in Rwanda will see whether the Rwf1.711 trillion translates into better schools, clinics and clean water when the 2027/2028 budget is drafted.

Business · 2026-11-13

Uganda Airlines to fly Kigali-Entebbe daily

The national carrier launches a new route on November 18, aiming to boost regional trade and tourism.

Uganda Airlines will begin daily flights between Kigali, Rwanda, and Entebbe, Uganda, on November 18, 2026. The new service aims to enhance tourism, trade, business travel, and regional integration between the two East African nations.

Lillian Kyategwa, Uganda Airlines Country Director, stated at a Kigali trade event on November 12 that the airline intends to strengthen existing ties and facilitate the movement of people and goods. She highlighted Rwanda's growing role as a conference hub as a key opportunity for the airline.

Michael Bulwaka, Counsellor at the Uganda High Commission in Rwanda, noted that the flights will promote trade, tourism, and investment, advancing East African integration. The airline views the route as part of a larger goal to improve travel across Africa and foster economic relationships.

Why it mattersThe new daily flights between Kigali and Entebbe, starting November 18, aim to ease the movement of people and goods, promoting trade and regional integration.

Climate · 2026-11-13

Rwanda seeks $3bn in climate finance for electricity and urban resilience

At COP31 in Türkiye, the government presented an estimated US$3.05 billion in investment needs for its electricity ambitions, including US$1.3 billion for grid infrastructure.

Rwanda is seeking greater international investment in electricity infrastructure, urban resilience, green industrialisation and climate-smart agriculture. Prime Minister Justin Nsengiyumva outlined the country's priorities at the COP31 climate conference in Türkiye, where Rwanda called for stronger partnerships with multilateral development banks and development finance institutions.

At a High-Level Event on the Global Grids Accelerator on November 12, presided over by United Nations Secretary-General António Guterres, the Prime Minister highlighted the need to expand transmission capacity by 70 per cent and raise regional electricity trade from 100 megawatts to 200 megawatts. The discussions included Mission 300, an initiative that aims to connect 300 million people in Africa to electricity by 2030.

On November 11, Nsengiyumva took part in a dialogue on green industrial opportunities, where Rwanda pointed to renewable energy, clean cooking, electric mobility, sustainable agriculture and the circular economy as areas for investment and jobs.

Why it mattersRwanda's grid investment case depends on multilateral lenders and development finance institutions agreeing to fund the transmission network.

Climate · 2026-11-13

Rwanda seeks $12 billion to cut emissions

The country aims to reduce greenhouse gas emissions by 53 per cent by 2035.

A $12 billion climate investment bill, aimed at cutting greenhouse gases by 53 per cent by 2035, is what Rwanda is now asking the world to help finance. Prime Minister Dr Justin Nsengiyumva made the case on Thursday, November 12, at the COP31 climate summit in Antalya, Türkiye, reading the statement on behalf of President Paul Kagame.

The sum equals roughly 65 per cent of the country's projected 2026 GDP of $18.4 billion, and more than twice its entire 2026/27 national budget of Rwf7.8 trillion (about $5.2 billion). Government says 80 per cent of the money depends on international support, so access to climate finance will decide whether the 2035 target is met.

The goal forms part of Rwanda's third Nationally Determined Contribution, its climate action plan under the Paris Agreement. Nsengiyumva urged donors to provide financing that is predictable, easy to access and sufficient for both adaptation and cutting emissions, and said developing countries need better access to capital.

Why it mattersTraders and public services depend on the 80 per cent of the plan that rests on foreign money, so a shortfall there would stall the 2035 target.

Politics · 2026-11-13

Kagame arrives in Cambodia for OIF summit

Rwandan President Paul Kagame is in Phnom Penh for a state visit and the 20th Francophonie Summit.

Rwandan President Paul Kagame and First Lady Jeannette Kagame arrived in Phnom Penh, Cambodia, on Thursday for a state visit and the 20th Francophonie Summit.

Cambodian Prime Minister Hun Manet welcomed the Rwandan leaders, and President Kagame is scheduled for a royal audience with King Norodom Sihamoni on November 13. Discussions will focus on strengthening bilateral relations and expanding cooperation, with several agreements expected to be signed covering trade, investment, aviation, and tourism.

The 20th Francophonie Summit, hosted by Cambodia, will take place in Phnom Penh on November 15 and 16, where the next Secretary-General of the Organisation Internationale de la Francophonie (OIF) will be elected.

Why it mattersPresident Kagame will meet Cambodia's top leaders and witness the signing of bilateral cooperation agreements on November 13.
