Business · 2026-11-10

Kigali hosts Africa's Business Heroes summit

10 entrepreneurs will pitch for $1.5 million in grant funding at the December 1-2 event.

Rwanda's capital, Kigali, will host the 8th Africa's Business Heroes (ABH) Summit and Grand Finale on December 1-2. Over 24,000 applications from across Africa were received, with 10 founders reaching the final stage.

The finalists will pitch their established businesses live to judges for a share of $1.5 million in grant funding, including $300,000 for the overall winner. The entrepreneurs represent eight countries, with four women among the finalists, and their average age is 35.

Day one of the summit will focus on "Defining Africa’s Intelligence," exploring AI and innovation, followed by the live pitch competition on day two. Judges include Dr. Diane Karusisi, CEO of Bank of Kigali, and Shola Akinlade, Co-founder & CEO of Paystack.

Rwanda receives genocide fugitive Jean Leonard Teganya from U.S.

Teganya, a former medical student, arrived at Kigali International Airport on a chartered flight on Monday, November 9, 2026.

Rwanda received genocide fugitive Jean Leonard Teganya from the U.S. on Monday, November 9, 2026. His chartered flight landed at Kigali International Airport around 7pm local time, following a delay from an earlier 11am schedule.

Police agents escorted Teganya down the plane's stairs and immediately placed him into a waiting RIB detainee van. This arrival marks the end of a decades-long evasion of justice for the former National University of Rwanda medical student.

The U.S. handed over the fugitive to Rwandan authorities. Teganya had been sought for his alleged involvement in the genocide.

Rwandan students showcase culture at Abu Dhabi festival

Students presented Rwanda's heritage and tourism at Afro Fest 2026 held at New York University Abu Dhabi.

Rwandan students presented Rwanda's rich cultural heritage and tourism attractions at Afro Fest 2026. The event took place at New York University Abu Dhabi on December 4-5.

Afro Fest 2026, an annual celebration of African culture and diaspora contributions, featured Afrobeats, Amapiano, and live performances. The festival strictly admitted attendees above 21 years old.

The festival, held during the Formula 1 Race Weekend, provided a platform for university community members from across the African continent and diaspora to share their heritage.

Agriculture · 2026-11-10

Sina Gérard builds value-added agriculture in Nyirangarama

Entrepreneur Dr. Sina Gérard transforms local produce into over 30 products, including juices and biscuits, at his Rulindo District enterprise.

Entrepreneur Dr. Sina Gérard is developing an agro-processing ecosystem in Nyirangarama, Rulindo District, by transforming local agricultural produce into over 30 value-added products. His enterprise, SINA GÉRARD/ ENTREPRISE URWIBUTSO, produces items like juices, chilli oil, yoghurt, biscuits, and wine, aiming for affordability for all Rwandans.

Dr. Sina established vineyards in 2005 and later invested in fruit processing, creating the Agashya juice plant to demonstrate the commercial viability of local commodities. The enterprise has since expanded to include grape wine, chilli oil, yoghurt, biscuits made from local wheat and potatoes, and honey, with some products reaching markets in Europe.

The entrepreneur's broader objective is to build a complete ecosystem linking farming, livestock, processing, skills development, and manufacturing, while also investing in young people through education initiatives like Collège Fondation/SI Gérard. This approach aims to foster a generation capable of creating opportunities through agriculture and enterprise.

Why it mattersDr. Sina Gérard's initiative in Nyirangarama creates value-added agricultural products and employment opportunities within Rwanda.

Politics · 2026-11-10

Rwanda candidate vies for OIF job amid DRC threat

The Democratic Republic of Congo threatens to leave La Francophonie if its candidate does not win the secretary general post.

Rwanda and the Democratic Republic of Congo are competing for the secretary general position at the La Francophonie organisation in Cambodia. The election for the role, held by Rwanda's Louise Mushikiwabo, is scheduled for November 16 in Phnom Penh. Kinshasa has declared it will withdraw from the organisation if its candidate, Juliana Lumumba, is not successful.

Diplomatic sources indicate that Paris is increasing engagement with both Kinshasa and Kigali as the Phnom Penh summit nears. The DRC has sent a delegation of 100 officials to the event, a move that contravenes OIF rules limiting accompanying persons for presidents to three. Organisers are reportedly questioning the purpose of such a large Congolese delegation.

Kinshasa political strategists have devised a plan for the Phnom Penh OIF elections. The DRC's presidential delegation of 100 members arrived in advance of the summit.
