Politics · 2026-11-01

RPP youth in Europe meet in Warsaw

Young RPF members from 19 European countries gathered to discuss Rwanda's development goals.

Members of the RPF-Inkotanyi party and their supporters in Europe convened in Warsaw, Poland, for a retreat. The gathering focused on strengthening social cohesion and contributing to Rwanda’s development.

On Saturday, October 31, young party members from 19 European countries discussed the role of Rwandans abroad in the country’s transformation. RPF Secretary-General Christophe Bazivamo urged participants to use their skills and resources to support Rwanda’s Vision 2050 goals.

A panel titled “From Heritage to Responsibility” explored how young Rwandans could leverage opportunities and experiences from their countries of residence to contribute to Rwanda’s ambition to eliminate poverty by 2050.

1,729 graduates urged to create jobs in Rwanda

Rwanda aims to create 1.25 million new jobs by 2029, requiring innovation and entrepreneurship from graduates.

INES-Ruhengeri graduated 1,729 students on Thursday, October 30, in Musanze district. The graduates completed bachelor’s and master’s programs at the Institute of Applied Sciences. University leaders encouraged the graduates to create their own employment opportunities rather than solely seeking them.

Dr. Olivier Habimana, Director in charge of Technology and Research at the Higher Education Council, told graduates that innovation and entrepreneurship are essential for meeting Rwanda's employment goals. He noted that government institutions have limited vacancies, urging graduates to use their skills to start businesses and employ others.

Rwanda has set a target of creating 1.25 million new jobs by 2029. Dr. Habimana stated that all these jobs will require innovation to address the country’s ambitious vision and limited formal sector positions.

Rwanda farmers urged to innovate against climate change

Minister Ndabamenye calls for new methods to secure food and livestock production from weather threats.

Rwanda's farmers must adopt innovative agricultural methods to counter threats to food and livestock production from changing weather patterns. Agriculture and Animal Resources Minister Dr Telesphore Ndabamenye made this call on Friday, October 30, during World Food Day celebrations in Gatsibo District.

Minister Ndabamenye urged farmers to increase productivity for household consumption and market supply, identifying climate change as a major challenge. He advised adopting farming techniques that reduce reliance on rainfall and prepare for disasters.

Farmers were also encouraged to follow best practices like timely planting, correct fertiliser application, and proper harvesting and storage to minimize losses. Livestock farmers should ensure sufficient animal feed and consider crop and livestock insurance.

Politics · 2026-11-01

Kagame tells officials to assess performance

President Paul Kagame urged public officials to regularly assess their performance and improve public service.

President Paul Kagame urged public officials to regularly assess their performance and improve how they serve citizens, stating that their decisions impact ordinary people. Kagame spoke on Friday, October 30, during the swearing-in of four senior officials at Village Urugwiro in Kigali.

The newly sworn-in officials include Julienne Uwacu as Minister of Public Service and Labour, Judith Uwizeye moving to the Senate, Claudette Mukashyaka as Auditor General of State Finances, and Gratien Kubwimana as Deputy Auditor General. Kagame stated that changes in responsibilities should prompt leaders to examine past performance and identify areas for improvement.

The president emphasized that this self-reflection should extend to all individuals with government responsibilities, regardless of whether they are taking on new roles or continuing existing ones. He stressed that public service should be evaluated by its impact on others, not solely by the positions held.

Why it mattersPublic officials in Rwanda are being held accountable for their performance and its impact on citizens.
