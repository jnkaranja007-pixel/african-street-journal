Health · 2026-08-20

Rwanda low birth weight rate rises to 8.8%

The proportion of babies born weighing less than 2.5 kilograms reached 8.8% in 2025. The National Institute of Statistics of Rwanda reported the figure in the Rwanda Demographic and Health Survey (RDHS7).

Why it mattersThe increase in low birth weight affects neonatal health outcomes for newborns and their mothers.

Rwanda Primary Leaving Examination cohort grows by 50,000

The 2025-2026 Primary Leaving Examination cohort increased by more than 50,000 candidates compared to the previous year. Results from the national examinations show an increase in successful students.

WFP delivers 5,000 metric tonnes of rice to three nations

A 5,000-metric-tonne rice shipment from India was delivered to the Port of Dar es Salaam to support Rwanda, the DRC, and Burundi. The delivery was coordinated by DP World and the United Nations World Food Programme.

Why it mattersThe shipment provides food relief to communities in Rwanda facing acute food insecurity.
