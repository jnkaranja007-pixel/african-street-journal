Rwanda security minister studies Suez Canal operations in Egypt

Vincent Biruta and Rwanda's ambassador to Egypt received briefings on the waterway's operations, trade role and maritime security.

Source: Taarifa Rwanda · 2026-11-22 · 1 min read

Rwanda’s Minister of Internal Security, Vincent Biruta, visited Egypt’s Suez Canal with Rwanda’s ambassador to Egypt, Commissioner General Dan Munyuza, to learn about its operations and role in trade and security. The delegation was received by Suez Canal Authority Chairman Osama Rabie, who briefed them on the waterway’s history and operations.

Biruta was shown canal activities and visited the Suez Canal Museum, where he heard about the waterway’s construction and expansion to handle larger vessels. He said the delegation valued learning about the canal’s history, operations and contribution to international trade and maritime security.

The canal links the Mediterranean and Red seas, connecting shipping between Europe, Asia and other markets. The visit was intended to give the Rwandan delegation insights into managing strategic infrastructure.

Why it mattersBiruta and Munyuza have been briefed on canal management, trade and security, giving Rwanda's officials material to consider for their work.
