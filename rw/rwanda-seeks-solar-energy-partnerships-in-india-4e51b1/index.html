Rwanda seeks solar energy partnerships in India

A Rwandan delegation is in New Delhi for the International Solar Alliance assembly to attract investment and expertise.

Source: Taarifa Rwanda · 2026-11-03 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Rwanda is pursuing new solar energy partnerships at the 9th General Assembly of the International Solar Alliance in New Delhi, India. State Minister for Infrastructure Armand Zingiro is leading a delegation, including Ambassador to India Jacqueline Mukangira, to the November 2–5 meeting. They aim to expand access to affordable and sustainable solar power.

The assembly offers a platform to discuss challenges in solar technology deployment, such as financing and infrastructure. Rwanda seeks to attract investment and gain technical expertise to accelerate solar projects and increase electricity access. Discussions also cover mobilising funds and encouraging private sector involvement.

The event runs alongside the Bharat Renewable Energy Summit & Expo and the Green Hydrogen Conference. These parallel events could offer Rwanda opportunities to engage investors and technology providers. Potential outcomes include new cooperation, investment prospects, and knowledge sharing to diversify Rwanda's energy mix.
