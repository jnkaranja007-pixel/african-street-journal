Burundi minister attacks Rwanda's OIF candidate

Burundi's foreign minister threatened withdrawal from the OIF if Rwanda's candidate is re-elected.

Sources: Taarifa Rwanda and KT Press · 2026-11-16 · 2 min read

Burundi's Foreign Affairs Minister Edouard Bizimana attacked Rwanda's candidature for the Secretary General of the Francophonie organisation, stating that a third term for the current leader would "bury" the International Organisation of La Francophonie (OIF). Bizimana claimed the attempt to impose a candidacy served particular interests and threatened that Burundi would withdraw from the organisation if Rwanda's candidate, Louise Mushikiwabo, is re-elected. The announcement came as the OIF prepares to announce its next Secretary General tomorrow morning.

Bizimana's comments come amid frosty relations between Burundi and Rwanda, with Burundi having closed its doors since 2015 and accusing its northern neighbour of interference. Political pundits suggest the contest in Cambodia may mirror a 2025 precedent where Rwanda withdrew from the Economic Community of Central African States (ECCAS) after opposition blocked its expected rotating presidency. Insiders claim French leader Emmanuel Macron urged Rwanda to withdraw Mushikiwabo's candidacy, an attempt described by a commentator as blackmail.

An African Intelligence source claims the Democratic Republic of Congo (DRC) President Felix Tshisekedi has instructed his information minister to prepare a letter announcing the DRC's withdrawal from the OIF should Mushikiwabo win. This threat has reportedly been presented to several African ministers in Phnom Penh to underscore the DRC's intent. Rwanda has stated it will not withdraw Mushikiwabo's candidature.
