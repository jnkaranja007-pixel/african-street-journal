RPF youth meet in Poland to discuss development

RPF Secretary General Christophe Bazivamo urged young members in Europe to take practical action for Rwanda's development.

Source: KT Press · 2026-10-31 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Young members of Rwanda's ruling RPF-Inkotanyi party living in Europe convened in Warsaw, Poland, for a retreat focused on their identity and contribution to Rwanda’s development agenda. The meeting brought together party members from across Europe to discuss guiding values and methods for contributing to the country's transformation.

RPF Secretary General Christophe Bazivamo opened the retreat, conveying greetings from President Paul Kagame, who also chairs the party. Bazivamo thanked attendees for their involvement in Rwanda’s development efforts and challenged them to think ambitiously and set examples in their communities.

The retreat provided an opportunity for participants to reflect on their beliefs and renew their commitment to the country’s development goals. Bazivamo stated that RPF members are committed to leading the country's rebuilding and development regardless of their location.

Why it mattersYoung Rwandan RPF party members living in Europe are discussing their role in the country's development.
