British Embassy proposes Rwanda as a James Bond filming location

The proposal highlights Volcanoes National Park, Lake Kivu and Musanze’s mountain roads as possible settings for the next film.

Source: Taarifa Rwanda · 2026-11-19 · 1 min read

The British Embassy in Kigali has proposed that scenes for the next James Bond film be shot in Rwanda, with Volcanoes National Park among the suggested locations. The proposal also names Lake Kivu and the winding roads around Musanze, in northern Rwanda.

Volcanoes National Park is home to endangered mountain gorillas and volcanic scenery, while Lake Kivu offers islands and lakeside landscapes. The suggestion identifies possible settings; it does not confirm that the film’s producers have chosen Rwanda.

A production could bring attention to tourism operators, hotels, transport companies, local creatives and other visitor-economy businesses, according to the report. It also describes a chance to attract more international film productions and develop Rwanda’s creative industry.

Why it mattersTourism operators, hotels, transport companies and local creatives are identified as potential beneficiaries if the production brings international attention to Rwanda.
