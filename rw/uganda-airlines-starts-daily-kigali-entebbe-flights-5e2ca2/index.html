Uganda Airlines starts daily Kigali–Entebbe flights

The route began on 18 November, linking Rwanda's capital to the airline's wider network through Entebbe.

Sources: Rwanda Dispatch and Taarifa Rwanda · 2026-11-19 · 1 min read

Uganda Airlines began daily direct flights between Kigali and Entebbe on 18 November, adding the Rwandan capital to its regional network. The service gives travellers between Rwanda and Uganda another direct option by air.

Chief Executive Officer Girma Wake said the route was long overdue, citing the two countries' economic, social and cultural ties. He said passengers flying from Kigali can connect through Entebbe to destinations in West Africa, the Middle East, Asia and Europe. Company Secretary Suzan Batuuka said the service would support business travel and help families stay connected.

With Kigali, Uganda Airlines now serves 18 destinations. The carrier began flights to Accra, Ghana, on 27 October. Rwanda's Minister of State for Infrastructure, Col Claudien Bizimungu, said Rwanda was committed to working with Uganda to improve transport links and economic cooperation.

Why it mattersBusiness travellers and families moving between Rwanda and Uganda can now fly direct each day, and Kigali passengers gain onward flights through Entebbe.
