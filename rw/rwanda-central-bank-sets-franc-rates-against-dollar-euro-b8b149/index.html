Rwanda central bank sets franc rates against dollar, euro and regional currencies

The National Bank of Rwanda quoted the dollar at Rwf1,472.39 to buy and Rwf1,482.39 to sell on Tuesday, November 17.

Source: Taarifa Rwanda · 2026-11-18 · 1 min read

The Rwandan franc traded at rates published by the National Bank of Rwanda (BNR) on Tuesday, November 17, 2026. The dollar was quoted at Rwf1,472.39 to buy and Rwf1,482.39 to sell; the euro at Rwf1,620.21 and Rwf1,631.22, respectively; and the British pound at Rwf1,942.29 and Rwf1,955.49.

Among regional currencies, the Kenyan shilling was Rwf11.32 to buy and Rwf11.39 to sell. The Ugandan shilling stood at Rwf0.36 for both transactions, while the Tanzanian shilling was Rwf0.56 for both, according to BNR’s figures.

The central bank said the rates provide guidance to financial institutions and the wider economy. It noted that commercial banks and foreign-exchange bureaus may offer different rates, advising customers to check their quotes before completing a transaction. The published rates took effect on Tuesday.

Why it mattersBusinesses settling import payments and exporters converting foreign-currency earnings use the published rates as a reference, while commercial-bank and bureau quotes can differ.
