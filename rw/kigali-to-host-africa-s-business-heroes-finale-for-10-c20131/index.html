Kigali to host Africa’s Business Heroes finale for 10 entrepreneurs

The finalists from eight African countries will compete for grant funding at the Kigali Convention Centre on December 1–2.

Source: Taarifa Rwanda · 2026-11-23 · 1 min read

Kigali will host the 2026 Africa’s Business Heroes Grand Finale on December 1–2, with 10 entrepreneurs from eight African countries competing for a share of $1.5 million in grant funding. The two-day event is scheduled for the Kigali Convention Centre.

The finalists were chosen from more than 24,000 applications submitted across all 54 African countries, after business evaluations, due diligence and live pitching sessions in Nairobi on August 21–22, 2026. Their businesses work in healthcare, agriculture, manufacturing, education and technology.

Four of the 10 finalists are women, and the entrepreneurs have an average age of 35. The event will bring together founders, investors and corporate leaders, according to Taarifa Rwanda.

Why it mattersFinalists from Egypt, Kenya, Nigeria, Ethiopia, South Africa, Tanzania, Benin and Côte d’Ivoire will share the grant pool, with investors and corporate leaders attending in Kigali.
