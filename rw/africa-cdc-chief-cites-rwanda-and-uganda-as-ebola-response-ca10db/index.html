Africa CDC chief cites Rwanda and Uganda as Ebola response models

Dr Jean Kaseya made the call at a virtual briefing marking six months since the Ebola outbreak was declared in the Democratic Republic of the Congo.

Source: KT Press · 2026-11-16 · 1 min read

Africa CDC Director General Dr Jean Kaseya has named Rwanda and Uganda as examples of countries that prepare for and manage public health emergencies well. He spoke at a virtual media briefing marking six months since the Ebola outbreak was declared in the Democratic Republic of the Congo, which is still fighting the disease.

Kaseya said controlling an outbreak needs more than medical supplies and treatment facilities. He called for stronger government leadership, greater community involvement and closer cooperation between African countries, and said governments must invest in preparedness, train health workers and strengthen public health systems.

International partners, he added, should coordinate their support under national government leadership.

Why it mattersGovernments that act on his advice would commit money to training health workers and strengthening clinics before the next outbreak arrives.
