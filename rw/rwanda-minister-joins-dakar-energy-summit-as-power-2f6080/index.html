Rwanda minister joins Dakar energy summit as power investment tops agenda

Armand Zingiro will meet officials from four other countries as Rwanda pursues universal electricity access by 2029 and a 60% renewable mix by 2030.

Source: Rwanda Dispatch · 2026-11-28 · 1 min read

Armand Zingiro, Rwanda's minister of state for infrastructure, is scheduled to attend MSGBC Oil, Gas & Power 2026 in Dakar, Senegal, from December 1–3. He will join energy ministers and senior government officials from Yemen, Russia, Iran and Saudi Arabia at the summit.

Organisers say electricity access has reached 85.4% of Rwandan households. The country's targets are universal access by 2029 and a 60% renewable energy mix by 2030. The three-day meeting is set to bring together governments, energy companies, investors and development partners.

The agenda includes investment, infrastructure financing, natural gas development, electricity generation and technical cooperation. Energy Capital & Power is organising the summit with Senegal's Ministry of Energy and Petroleum, under the high patronage of President Bassirou Diomaye Faye.

Why it mattersRwandan officials and energy companies have investment, financing and power-infrastructure discussions scheduled in Dakar from December 1–3.
