Congolese minister blames Rwanda for hunger affecting 28 million

Eve Bazaiba says fighting in eastern Congo has spread hunger across all 24 provinces, while critics point to domestic policy failures.

Source: KT Press · 2026-11-24 · 1 min read

Eve Bazaiba, the Democratic Republic of Congo’s minister of state for social affairs and humanitarian action, blamed Rwanda for hunger affecting 28 million people. She said the war in the east had spread hunger across all 24 provinces.

The accusation has drawn criticism from some of Bazaiba’s supporters, according to KT Press, as Congolese call for answers and food. The report says the country depends heavily on food imports and has struggled to build reliable domestic supply chains.

It also cites poor rural roads, limited investment in farmers, inadequate storage and weak market access as longstanding problems. Bazaiba’s remarks come as the government faces calls to address the crisis at home.

Why it mattersRwanda is being drawn into the dispute as Congolese officials link it to a crisis affecting 28 million people across 24 provinces.
