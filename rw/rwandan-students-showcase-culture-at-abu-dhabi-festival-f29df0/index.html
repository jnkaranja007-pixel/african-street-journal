Rwandan students showcase culture at Abu Dhabi festival

Students presented Rwanda's heritage and tourism at Afro Fest 2026 held at New York University Abu Dhabi.

Source: Taarifa Rwanda · 2026-11-10 · 1 min read

Rwandan students presented Rwanda's rich cultural heritage and tourism attractions at Afro Fest 2026. The event took place at New York University Abu Dhabi on December 4-5.

Afro Fest 2026, an annual celebration of African culture and diaspora contributions, featured Afrobeats, Amapiano, and live performances. The festival strictly admitted attendees above 21 years old.

The festival, held during the Formula 1 Race Weekend, provided a platform for university community members from across the African continent and diaspora to share their heritage.
