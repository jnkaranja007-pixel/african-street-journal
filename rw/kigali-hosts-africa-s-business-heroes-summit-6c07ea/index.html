Kigali hosts Africa's Business Heroes Summit

10 finalists will pitch for $1.5 million in grant funding at the December 1-2 event.

Source: Taarifa Rwanda · 2026-11-11 · 1 min read

Rwanda's capital, Kigali, will host the 8th Africa's Business Heroes (ABH) Summit and Grand Finale on December 1-2. Organisers received over 24,000 applications from across Africa for the competition.

10 finalists, representing eight countries and including four women, will present their established businesses to judges for a share of $1.5 million in grant funding. The overall winner will receive $300,000.

The summit's first day will focus on "Defining Africa’s Intelligence," exploring AI and innovation, followed by the live pitches on the second day. Judges include Dr. Diane Karusisi, CEO of Bank of Kigali, and Shola Akinlade, Co-founder & CEO of Paystack.

Why it mattersRwanda is hosting a continental business competition that will award $1.5 million in grants to entrepreneurs.
