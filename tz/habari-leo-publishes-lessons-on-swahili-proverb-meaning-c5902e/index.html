Habari Leo publishes lessons on Swahili proverb meaning

New educational columns in Tanzanian newspapers examine the literal and figurative meanings of the traditional Swahili saying.

Sources: Habari Leo and Daily News Tanzania · 2026-11-17 · 1 min read

Habari Leo and Daily News Tanzania published educational features explaining the traditional Swahili proverb Usilolijua ni sawa na usiku wa giza. The publications stated that the literal meaning compares an unknown thing to a dark night. The outlets noted that the figurative meaning reflects how a lack of knowledge can prevent people from seeing the truth or making sound judgments.

According to the newspapers, the saying teaches three lessons which include seeking knowledge, avoiding premature judgments, and remaining open to learning. The reports added that not knowing something does not automatically mean a person is wrong.

The publications concluded that the enduring wisdom of the proverb encourages readers to understand situations before judging them, rejecting opportunities, or making decisions.

Why it mattersReaders across Tanzania can access the published language lessons in Habari Leo and Daily News Tanzania to study Swahili proverbs.
