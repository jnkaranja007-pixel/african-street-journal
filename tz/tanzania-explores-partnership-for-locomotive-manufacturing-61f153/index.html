Tanzania explores partnership for locomotive manufacturing plant

Samia announced the proposal in Uganda and urged East African countries to build complementary industries rather than compete for investment.

Sources: The Chanzo and The Citizen · 2026-11-21 · 1 min read

Tanzania is exploring partnerships to build a railway locomotive manufacturing plant, President Samia Suluhu Hassan said in Wakiso, Uganda, on Friday, the final day of her state visit. She made the announcement after commissioning and touring Double Q’s truck assembly plant with Ugandan President Yoweri Museveni.

Samia did not name a partner, location or construction timetable for the proposed plant. She said Tanzania wanted to expand local manufacturing and urged East African countries to create industries that complement one another. Double Q said its Ugandan facility could assemble 4,000 trucks a year and described plans to make vehicle parts and electric vehicles.

Samia also said regional rules for vehicle assembly and manufacturing were being developed. Museveni cited Tanzania’s natural gas and Uganda’s iron ore as resources the neighbours could combine.

Why it mattersTanzania’s government must identify a partner, site and timetable before the proposed locomotive plant can advance; Samia supplied none of those details.
