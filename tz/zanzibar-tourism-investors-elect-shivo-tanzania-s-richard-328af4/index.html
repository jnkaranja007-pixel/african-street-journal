Zanzibar tourism investors elect Shivo Tanzania's Richard Ashby to board

Ashby won 47 votes in a contest with 10 candidates, taking one of four places on the incoming board of the Zanzibar Association of Tourism Investors.

Sources: Daily News Tanzania and The Citizen · 2026-11-21 · 1 min read

Richard Ashby of Shivo Tanzania won a place on the Zanzibar Association of Tourism Investors' incoming board at its annual general meeting and board elections held in Zanzibar on Wednesday. He received 47 votes in a field of 10 candidates and was one of four people elected.

The association, which represents private-sector tourism investors across Zanzibar, announced the results on November 19 after verifying ballots cast by participating members. Nafat Haroub Seif topped the poll, followed by Barbara Lexmond and Alphonce Marandu Gura.

The association's secretariat congratulated the four directors and thanked all 10 candidates. The new board is expected to advance the association's work advising and connecting investors and advocating for sustainable tourism policies.

Why it mattersZATI's incoming directors must represent tourism investors across Zanzibar and carry forward the association's work on policy and business growth.
