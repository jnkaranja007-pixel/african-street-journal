Tanzania aims for top 10 global food producer status

Prime Minister Nchemba announced the goal at the Borlaug Dialogue in Des Moines, USA, citing Vision 2050.

Sources: Daily News Tanzania and The Citizen · 2026-10-24 · 1 min read

Prime Minister Mwigulu Nchemba stated Tanzania's ambition to join the world's top 10 food-producing nations by 2050. This goal is part of Tanzania’s Development Vision 2050, aiming for leadership in African food production. Nchemba announced this during the Norman Borlaug International Dialogue in Des Moines, United States.

Tanzania has achieved a 130 per cent food self-sufficiency level through its agricultural initiatives. Agricultural exports increased from US$2.1bn in 2021/2022 to US$3.7bn in 2024/2025. Agriculture contributes 24.6 per cent to Tanzania’s GDP and employs 65 per cent of the workforce.

Nchemba urged increased investment in farmers, science, youth, women, and resilient food systems to support Africa's agricultural potential. He stressed the need for action and innovation to build food systems for present and future generations, under the theme 'Before Hunger Defeats Us'.
