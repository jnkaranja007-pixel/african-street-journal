TANAPA launches campaign across 21 parks and five heritage sites

The authority says it will promote lesser-known attractions and encourage Tanzanians to take part in domestic tourism.

Source: Daily News Tanzania · 2026-11-23 · 1 min read

Tanzania National Parks Authority (TANAPA) has launched the third edition of its Festive Season Celebrations campaign at Rubondo Island National Park in Geita Region, aiming to encourage domestic tourism.

Jully Bede Lyimo, TANAPA’s head of business development, said the campaign covers 21 national parks and five heritage sites managed by the authority. It will promote attractions that have received less attention and identify new sites in the Lake and Western zones and villages near parks.

Rubondo chief park warden Imani Kikoti said the park has about 300 bird species. He urged Tanzanians to support the development of protected areas and the protection of heritage sites.

Why it mattersTANAPA must promote attractions across 21 parks and five heritage sites, including locations in the Lake and Western zones and villages near parks.
