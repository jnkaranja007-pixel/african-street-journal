Tanzania builds sports facilities for global events

The nation is investing in diverse venues beyond football to bid for major competitions like the All-Africa Games.

Source: Daily News Tanzania · 2026-11-09 · 1 min read

Tanzania is accelerating investment in sports infrastructure to become a hub for international sporting events, according to Neema Msitha, Executive Secretary of the National Sports Council. The country aims to bid for major competitions, including multi-sport events, leveraging its peace, hospitality, and cultural attractions. Previously, a lack of facilities hindered hosting bids, but the government is prioritizing modern sports complexes to meet international standards.

Msitha stated that completing these projects will open new opportunities for local athletes and shift the focus to stakeholders utilizing the enhanced facilities. She cited Morocco, Egypt, South Africa, and Algeria as examples of nations successful in hosting due to their established sports infrastructure. The government is establishing the Sports Infrastructure Management Agency (SIMA) to oversee these developments.

The infrastructure program includes multipurpose venues, athletics tracks, basketball and netball courts, tennis facilities, swimming pools, and indoor arenas. The Benjamin Mkapa Stadium in Dar es Salaam already has a World Athletics-certified track. New facilities are planned for Kawe, Arusha, and Dodoma, with recreation centres also slated for Dar es Salaam and Dodoma.
