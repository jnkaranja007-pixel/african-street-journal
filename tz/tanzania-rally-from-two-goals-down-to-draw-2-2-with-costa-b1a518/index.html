Tanzania rally from two goals down to draw 2-2 with Costa Rica

Faraji Kayanda scored twice in the Serengeti Boys’ FIFA U17 World Cup debut in Doha, giving Tanzania a point in Group I.

Sources: Africanews and The Citizen · 2026-11-22 · 1 min read

Tanzania’s U17 team drew 2-2 with Costa Rica in Doha in its FIFA U17 World Cup debut, coming back from two goals down. The result gave the Serengeti Boys a point in Group I.

Forward Faraji Kayanda scored both goals and was named player of the match. Coach Elieneza Nicolaus Nsanganzelu praised the team’s mentality, while captain Kassim Selemani Juma said better concentration helped secure the draw.

Tanzania’s next Group I fixture is against Brazil on the 24th. Supporters in Doha said they remained hopeful ahead of that match.
