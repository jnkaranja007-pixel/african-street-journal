Tanzania finds 223 billion cubic feet of natural gas

Energy Minister Juma Homera announced the discovery at the Kasa-1X ST2 well in Mtwara's Mnazi Bay area.

Source: The Chanzo · 2026-10-28 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Tanzania's Energy Minister Juma Homera announced on October 27 the discovery of an estimated 223 billion cubic feet (Bcf) of natural gas at the Kasa-1X ST2 well in Mtwara's Mnazi Bay area. This find follows a three-well campaign by the Tanzania Petroleum Development Corporation (TPDC) and French operator Maurel & Prom (M&P). The government stated the discovery will add to domestic gas supply.

The 223 Bcf figure announced by Homera is an early estimate and not yet a certified recoverable reserve. M&P's own technical update on October 22 estimated gas volumes at approximately 200 Bcf gross, or 120 Bcf net to its 60 per cent interest, without classifying it as a reserve. The two production wells, MB-5 and MS-2, are already on stream, increasing Mnazi Bay's output to about 130 million cubic feet a day.

Gas from the Mnazi Bay field, one of Tanzania's two producing gas fields, is used for power generation, industry, transport, and households. The three-well programme was expected to add 45 to 55 million cubic feet a day to the field's average output of 110 million cubic feet a day. First production from the Kasa exploration well is envisaged as early as November.

Why it mattersTanzania has discovered a significant natural gas find that will bolster domestic supply for power, industry, transport, and households.
