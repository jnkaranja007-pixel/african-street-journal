Samia backs Uganda rail link and MV Mwanza service

The Tanzanian president called for faster delivery of transport agreements as Museveni warned that trade barriers constrain producers.

Sources: The Chanzo and The Citizen · 2026-11-20 · 1 min read

Tanzania’s President Samia Suluhu Hassan said on November 18 that her government was ready to jointly build a railway connection with Uganda and bring the passenger and cargo vessel MV Mwanza into service between Mwanza and Uganda’s Port Bell. She made the proposals at a joint press conference in Entebbe during her state visit.

Ugandan President Yoweri Museveni said restrictions on Tanzanian rice would limit farmers’ ability to expand by selling into Uganda. Samia said bilateral trade had recently declined, citing connectivity and logistics among the reasons, and called for faster implementation of commitments on trade, investment, energy and infrastructure.

Tanzania is extending its standard gauge railway towards Mwanza and Kigoma, while work continues on a proposed route from Isaka through Lusahunga, Murongo and Kikagati to Mpondwe in Uganda. Samia said Tanzania was ready to cooperate on that route.

Why it mattersTanzanian rice farmers face a restricted outlet in Uganda, which Museveni said limits their ability to expand sales and production.
