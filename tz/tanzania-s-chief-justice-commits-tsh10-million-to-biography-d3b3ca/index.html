Tanzania’s chief justice commits TSh10 million to biography of first woman magistrate

George Masaju urged the judiciary to budget for more books about retired judges as he launched a biography of Eusebia Munuo in Dodoma.

Source: The Chanzo · 2026-11-21 · 1 min read

Tanzania Chief Justice George Masaju launched a biography of retired Court of Appeal justice Eusebia Munuo in Dodoma on Thursday, saying his office would buy copies worth TSh10 million. Munuo was the country’s first woman magistrate.

The book, published by the judiciary’s training institution, recounts that Munuo became a resident magistrate on July 1, 1970, at age 24. Masaju called on the judiciary to keep budgeting for books documenting retired judges. Senior advocate Eric Ngimaryo, one of the book’s reviewers, said Munuo’s approach helped lead to the formation of the Tanzania Women Judges Association.

Ngimaryo said the book reports women hold 50% of deputy registrar and resident magistrate-in-charge posts, while the judiciary has not reached parity among judges. A second reviewer, Prof Ruth Meena, praised the biography but said it should go further.

Why it mattersThe judiciary must continue budgeting for books about retired judges, while Masaju’s office will spend TSh10 million on copies of Munuo’s biography.
