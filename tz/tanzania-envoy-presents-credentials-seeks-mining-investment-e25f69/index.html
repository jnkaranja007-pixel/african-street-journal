Tanzania Envoy Presents Credentials, Seeks Mining Investment

Tanzania's High Commissioner to Australia, Macocha Moshe Tembele, presented his credentials and discussed strengthening bilateral ties, particularly in the mining sector.

Source: Daily News Tanzania · 2026-10-21 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X

Tanzania's High Commissioner to Australia, Macocha Moshe Tembele, formally presented his Letters of Credence to Governor-General Sam Mostyn on October 20, 2026, in Canberra. During their discussion, Governor-General Mostyn acknowledged Tanzania's historical contributions and expressed satisfaction with the strong bilateral relationship, highlighting cooperation in the mining sector.

Governor-General Mostyn encouraged increased engagement with Australia's mining industry, noting significant interest from Australian companies in Tanzania's investment climate. She also welcomed the rise in Australian tourism to Tanzania and called for enhanced cooperation to facilitate travel for visitors. Ambassador Tembele thanked the Governor-General and congratulated Australia on the election of Milton Dick as President of the Inter-Parliamentary Union.

Ambassador Tembele commended Australian investments in Tanzania, especially in mining, and stated his intention to visit Perth to meet with interested companies. He also requested Australia to expand scholarship and capacity-building programs for Tanzanians, underscoring a desire for deeper collaboration across various sectors.

Why it mattersInvestors are affected by discussions aimed at strengthening cooperation in the mining sector and highlighting favorable investment opportunities in Tanzania.
