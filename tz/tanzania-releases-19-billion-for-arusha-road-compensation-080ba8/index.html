Tanzania releases 19 billion/- for Arusha road compensation

The works minister ordered the roads agency to pay eligible residents within two to three days and told contractors to speed up construction.

Source: Daily News Tanzania · 2026-11-24 · 1 min read

Tanzania has released 19 billion/- to compensate residents whose land and properties are affected by road projects in Arusha ahead of the 2027 Africa Cup of Nations (AFCON). Works Minister Masanja Kungu Kadogosa directed the Tanzania National Roads Agency (TANROADS) to start paying eligible residents immediately.

Kadogosa said the funds were available and payments should be made within two to three days. He also ordered contractors to add machinery, equipment and workers, and keep construction going day and night. TANROADS Arusha regional manager Reginald Massawe said contractors had fully mobilised equipment and deployed the required technical staff.

The 13-kilometre AFCON section of the 70-kilometre Mbauda-Losinyai Road was 54.4% complete, project supervisor Hophine Mollel said. The government says the roads are intended to benefit residents beyond the tournament.

Why it mattersResidents whose land and properties are affected are due compensation within two to three days, while contractors must increase resources and maintain round-the-clock work.
