Tanzania joins Indian Ocean states in talks on maritime security and trade

Ministers and senior officials from the Indian Ocean Rim Association are meeting in New Delhi under India's chairmanship for 2025 to 2027.

Source: Daily News Tanzania · 2026-11-26 · 1 min read

Tanzania is taking part in the 25th Council of Ministers meeting of the Indian Ocean Rim Association (IORA) in New Delhi, India, where it is represented by its High Commissioner to India, Anisa Mbega. The meeting brings together foreign affairs ministers and permanent secretaries from member states to discuss maritime security, sustainable development and the prosperity of the Indian Ocean region.

The meeting was preceded by the 29th Meeting of the Committee of Senior Officials, held from November 25 to 26, 2026, and the Council of Ministers meeting is scheduled for November 27. The agenda covers maritime security and transport, trade and investment, the blue economy and the sustainable management of marine resources, under the theme "Innovation, Openness, Resilience and Adaptability in the Indian Ocean Region".

Delegates are also reviewing the implementation of previous decisions and discussing ways to keep trade and supply chains moving smoothly. The association, founded in 1997, has 23 member states and 12 dialogue partners, and member states are expected to develop joint strategies on the region's challenges.

Why it mattersTanzanian shipping, fisheries and traders who depend on Indian Ocean routes will see any joint strategies on maritime security and trade flows agreed at the meeting, which is scheduled for November 27.
