Tanzania calls for digital infrastructure to get equal priority in Africa

Communications Minister Angellah Jasmine Mbelwa Kairuki made the case at an investment dialogue in Singapore, where financing and public-private partnerships were also discussed.

Source: Daily News Tanzania · 2026-11-22 · 1 min read

Tanzania's Communications and Information Technology Minister, Angellah Jasmine Mbelwa Kairuki, asked that digital infrastructure be given the same priority as conventional projects in Africa's development agenda. Speaking at the Africa-Singapore Infrastructure Investment Dialogue in Singapore, she said information and communication technologies are now essential to energy, electricity grids, ports and transport.

Kairuki said stronger partnerships between African and Asian governments and private firms could attract investment and speed up infrastructure building. Singapore's Minister of State for Foreign Affairs and National Development, Alvin Tan, pledged his country's cooperation with Africa in infrastructure, energy, transport, the digital economy, fintech and cross-border payments.

Tan also said talks are continuing on a Free Trade Agreement between Singapore and the East African Community, which Tanzania belongs to. The dialogue explored innovative financing and public-private partnerships, and Invest Africa's chief executive, Chantelé Carrington, said further cooperation is expected in 2027, when Singapore chairs ASEAN.

Why it mattersInvestors and banks weighing African infrastructure deals are being pointed to digital projects alongside roads and power, with financing and partnership models now on the table.
