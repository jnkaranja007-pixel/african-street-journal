Alute Lissu, lawyer and brother of Tundu Lissu, dies

The constitutional lawyer died early Sunday after a long illness, his family announced.

Source: The Chanzo · 2026-11-02 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Alute Lissu, a constitutional and administrative lawyer, died early Sunday after a long illness, his family confirmed. He had been admitted to Sani Hospital and his condition worsened before his death. His family expects the burial to take place in Singida, but will announce further arrangements after attending the treason case judgment of his brother, Tundu Lissu, on Friday.

Vincent Lissu Mughwai, Alute’s younger brother, described the deceased as a father figure and role model, particularly to Tundu, stating that Alute’s example inspired Tundu to become a lawyer. Alute Lissu, who leaves five children, had his wife pass away many years ago. He was known within the family as Lute.

Alute Lissu, who had spent over four decades in courtrooms, worked for the State before private practice, and saw public legal education as central to citizenship. His legal work focused on constitutional access and state accountability. He graduated from the University of Dar es Salaam’s Faculty of Law in 1982.

Why it mattersAlute Lissu's death affects his five children and his brother Tundu Lissu, a politician whose treason case judgment the family plans to attend.
