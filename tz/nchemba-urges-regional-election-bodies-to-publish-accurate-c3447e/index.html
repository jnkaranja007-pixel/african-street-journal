Nchemba urges regional election bodies to publish accurate information

The Tanzanian prime minister said information gaps let rumours spread and called on commissions to prepare for artificial intelligence risks.

Source: The Chanzo · 2026-11-24 · 1 min read

Tanzania’s Prime Minister Mwigulu Nchemba urged Southern African electoral commissions to make accurate information readily available, opening their 28th annual conference in Dar es Salaam on November 23. He represented President Samia Suluhu Hassan at the two-day meeting, which focused on election management and social media.

Nchemba said rumours can spread when people lack reliable information, damaging confidence in electoral institutions and elections. He called on commissions to strengthen communication and build capacity in artificial intelligence, while developing strategies to address its risks. He also said digital channels can support voter education, registration information and public participation.

Justice Jacobs Mwambegele, chair of Tanzania’s Independent National Electoral Commission, said more than 108 participants from 15 commissions in 14 countries attended. Delegates were to exchange experience, assess challenges and develop shared strategies; the programme also included elections for the forum’s leadership and consideration of resolutions.

Why it mattersElectoral commissions across the region are being urged to improve public communication and prepare AI strategies, with delegates from 14 countries meeting in Dar es Salaam.
