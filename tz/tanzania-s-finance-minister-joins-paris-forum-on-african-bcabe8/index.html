Tanzania’s finance minister joins Paris forum on African debt

Khamis Mussa Omar will join a session on access to affordable, sustainable debt as African governments face rising debt-service obligations.

Source: Daily News Tanzania · 2026-11-24 · 1 min read

Tanzania’s Finance Minister Khamis Mussa Omar arrived in Paris to lead the country’s delegation to the 25th International Economic Forum on Africa, scheduled for November 25. The meeting at the Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development (OECD) Conference Centre will discuss economic transformation and development financing.

Omar is due to join the first session, on access to affordable and sustainable debt during global crises. The discussion will examine how African governments can balance debt costs and risks with financing needs, and the role of international partners in improving access to funding, according to Daily News Tanzania.

The forum will also launch Africa’s Development Dynamics 2026: Managing Debt for Transformation, a report prepared jointly by the OECD Development Centre and the African Union Commission. Participants are expected to discuss domestic revenue, investment conditions and financing options.

Why it mattersOmar must set out Tanzania’s position in a session examining how African governments can secure affordable debt while meeting transformation financing needs.
