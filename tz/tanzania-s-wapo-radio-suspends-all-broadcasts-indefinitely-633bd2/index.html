Tanzania's WAPO Radio suspends all broadcasts indefinitely

Management cites operational and technical reasons for the immediate halt without giving a return date.

Source: The Chanzo · 2026-11-17 · 1 min read

WAPO Radio in Tanzania has halted all broadcasts until further notice, taking effect immediately. Sylvester Gamanywa, the general overseer of the station, described the sudden suspension as an operational and technical matter. In a notice directed to listeners, customers and stakeholders, station management did not provide a specific reason for the interruption or name any authority responsible for ordering it.

Speaking to DW Kiswahili on November 16, 2026, Gamanywa declined to elaborate on the exact cause while internal discussions continued, stating that giving details would be inappropriate at that stage. The station is owned by WAPO Mission International in Tanzania. Management apologised for the disruption while thanking supporters for their patience and trust, and appealed for prayers from the public before addressing the technical issues.

Independent Tanzanian broadcasters face severe financial pressures, including more than 18 statutory levies, high electricity costs and demands for unpaid airtime from government agencies. Amos Ngosha, chairperson of the National Independent Broadcasters Association, stated in an August 6 report that several station owners had voluntarily shut down because they could no longer afford to operate.

Why it mattersListeners, customers and stakeholders face an indefinite loss of broadcasts from WAPO Radio without any scheduled date for services to resume.
