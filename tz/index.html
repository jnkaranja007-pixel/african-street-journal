Business · 2026-08-20

Hydropower generates 63.85% of Tanzania's national grid electricity

Hydropower provided 63.85% of electricity from the National Grid in 2025, according to the Tanzania Economic Survey 2025. Natural gas accounted for 31.48% of generation. Hydropower had an installed capacity of 2,718.27 MW compared to 1,140.72 MW for natural gas.

Why it mattersThe energy mix supports a national target to reach 3,000 kWh per capita by 2050.

Tanzania shutters Mwanahalisi news outlet for six months

The state has suspended the Kiswahili investigative weekly Mwanahalisi for six months. The closure follows a report by the outlet regarding the Vice President, according to The Chanzo.

Why it mattersThe suspension restricts the availability of investigative reporting in the Kiswahili language.
