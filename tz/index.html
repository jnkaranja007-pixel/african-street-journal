Business · 2026-08-05

Tanzania targets $7,000 GDP per capita by 2050, up from $1,318

Tanzania has set out a blueprint to grow its economy tenfold by 2050 and lift GDP per capita to $7,000 from $1,318, African Business reported, a path that would move it into upper-middle-income status.

Why it mattersThe 2050 target frames which sectors and infrastructure the state will back for a generation.

Russian agribusiness Betaren enters Tanzanian market after Samia's Moscow visit

Russian companies including Betaren have begun entering the Tanzanian market following President Samia's three-day working visit to Russia in June, allAfrica reported. Sky Business and Investment Company has partnered with Betaren to distribute hybrid seeds, fertilisers and crop protection products to farmers.

Tanzania to co-host AFCON 2027 with Kenya and Uganda

Tanzania will host AFCON Pamoja 2027 alongside Kenya and Uganda, President Samia said, the first Africa Cup of Nations to be held in East Africa and the first jointly hosted by three countries.

Why it mattersTournament hosting drives hotel, transport and stadium spending across the three countries.
