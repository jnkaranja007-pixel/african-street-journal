MUHAS draws more than 700 to health-awareness event

Participants from Tanzania and abroad took part in walks and runs promoting exercise, good nutrition and prevention of noncommunicable diseases.

Source: Habari Leo · 2026-11-22 · 1 min read

Muhimbili University of Health and Allied Sciences (MUHAS) brought more than 700 people to charity walks and runs at its Dar es Salaam campus on November 21, 2026, promoting exercise, good nutrition and health checks to help prevent noncommunicable diseases. Participants included students, alumni, staff and health-sector stakeholders from Tanzania and abroad.

Chief Government Medical Officer Prof. Bruno Sunguya said the government encourages people to exercise and eat well. MUHAS Vice-Chancellor Prof. Appolinary Kamuhabwa said the event was in its fifth season and included participants from the United States, Sweden and Germany. Races covered one, five, 10 and 15 kilometres, with certificates given to winners in each group.

Kamuhabwa said MUHAS would continue the programme as noncommunicable diseases increase.
