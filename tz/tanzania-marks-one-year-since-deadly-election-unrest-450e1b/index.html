Tanzania marks one year since deadly election unrest

Official figures state 518 people died and over 2,000 were injured in the October 29, 2025 events.

Sources: The Chanzo, The Citizen and Africanews · 2026-10-30 · 1 min read

One year after the October 29, 2025 election day violence, stakeholders gathered on October 29, 2026, to assess the nation's progress. Official figures report at least 518 people died and over 2,000 were injured during the events.

Human rights advocate Dr. Helen Kijo-Bisimba, civil society executive Lutgard Kokulinda Kagaruki, lawyer Jebra Kambole, and others participated in the Kivulini Talks session. The discussion, moderated by Khalifa Said, focused on what is known and unknown about the unrest, and the steps taken since.

President Samia Suluhu Hassan established two commissions following the events. While official figures cite around 500 deaths, unofficial reports suggest thousands were killed and injured, with significant property destruction.

Why it mattersTanzanians are affected by the violence that resulted in hundreds of deaths and injuries following the October 29, 2025 election.
