Tanzania banks partner on carbon project finance

CRDB Bank and NCMC sign deal to boost green investment and economic growth.

Source: Daily News Tanzania · 2026-11-15 · 2 min read

Tanzania is seeking domestic financing for carbon projects to boost green investment and economic growth. Minister of State Hamad Yussuf Masauni urged CRDB Bank and the National Carbon Monitoring Centre (NCMC) to collaborate on financing, developing, and promoting these projects. This call came on November 13, 2026, during the signing of a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) between NCMC and CRDB Bank in Antalya, Türkiye, on the sidelines of the UN Climate Change Conference (COP31).

Minister Masauni stated that climate change requires innovative financing and strategic partnerships, identifying carbon trading as a key area for economic transformation in the National Development Vision 2050. He emphasized that carbon trading should deliver tangible economic, social, and environmental benefits, including improved livelihoods and job creation. Masauni also called on CRDB Bank to develop financial products like loans and guarantees to help carbon project owners access capital.

NCMC Executive Director Kathryn Kigaraba believes the partnership will allow carbon projects to access local financing, strengthening the country's capacity for carbon trading. CRDB Chief Executive Officer Abdulmajid Nsekela added that the agreement will attract capital and facilitate projects with economic and social benefits. Permanent Secretary Richard Muyungi noted the partnership supports Development Vision 2050 by using carbon trading to attract investment and stimulate productive activities.

Why it mattersCRDB Bank and NCMC will collaborate on financing carbon projects, aiming to attract investment and stimulate productive activities according to the National Development Vision 2050.
