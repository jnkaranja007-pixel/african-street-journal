Tanzania minister meets King Charles III at CHOGM

Ambassador Mahmoud Thabit Kombo discussed investment and economic growth with leaders in Antigua.

Source: Daily News Tanzania · 2026-11-03 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Tanzania's Minister for Foreign Affairs, Ambassador Mahmoud Thabit Kombo, attended the Commonwealth Heads of Government Meeting (CHOGM) in Antigua and Barbuda. The meeting aimed to boost investment, economic resilience, and job creation across member states, with discussions covering infrastructure, renewable energy, agriculture, and climate change effects. Ambassador Kombo represented President Samia Suluhu Hassan at the event.

On the sidelines of the CHOGM, Ambassador Kombo held discussions with King Charles III of the United Kingdom, British Prime Minister Andy Burnham, and Antigua and Barbuda Prime Minister Gaston Browne. Tanzania used the platform to strengthen international relations and advance economic diplomacy, seeking to attract investment and promote economic growth. The country also highlighted the importance of private sector involvement in its National Development Vision 2050.

The meeting brought together leaders of Commonwealth member states and company chief executives to explore opportunities for promoting investment and strengthening economic resilience. Participants emphasized practical cooperation between governments and investors, alongside greater private sector participation in member states' development.

Why it mattersTanzania's participation in the CHOGM aimed to attract investment and promote economic growth, benefiting the country's development goals.
