Tanzania and Cuba advance university research cooperation

Universities in both countries are implementing collaboration under agreements that include a 2023 framework between Dar es Salaam and Havana.

Source: Daily News Tanzania · 2026-11-25 · 1 min read

Tanzania and Cuba are advancing joint research and academic exchanges, with universities implementing areas of collaboration, Tanzania’s ambassador to Cuba, Lieutenant General Mathew Mkingule, said. He pointed to artificial intelligence, biotechnology, robotics and digital technologies as fields for closer work.

The countries signed two memoranda of understanding in September 2023, including one between the University of Dar es Salaam and the University of Havana. In January 2024, Tanzania’s Sokoine University of Agriculture signed a separate agreement with the University of Artemisa ‘Julio Díaz González’.

About 315 Tanzanian students had graduated from Cuban universities by 2020, including 209 medical personnel and 10 people trained in sports, Mkingule said. The Cuban government also built and donated three schools in Tanzania in the 1970s.

Why it mattersTanzanian universities now have implementation underway under agreements with Cuban institutions, including the University of Dar es Salaam’s framework with Havana.
