Tundu Lissu jailed for four years for incitement

The High Court acquitted the CHADEMA chairman of treason but found him guilty of a lesser charge.

Source: The Chanzo · 2026-11-08 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

The High Court in Dar es Salaam sentenced CHADEMA national chairperson Tundu Lissu to four years in prison on November 6, 2026, for incitement. A three-judge panel, led by Judge Dunstan Ndunguru, found that the prosecution failed to prove the treason charge but that Lissu’s statements advocating for the disruption of the 2025 election constituted incitement.

Lissu, who conducted his own defence, insisted on his innocence and accused the judges of political persecution, stating he was not charged with incitement. The court maintained that trial evidence focused on incitement, giving Lissu notice and an opportunity to defend himself against the lesser offence. The courtroom was full with relatives, journalists, and dozens of advocates.

The prosecution had alleged Lissu intended to mobilise the public to obstruct the election to intimidate the executive, based on statements made at an April 3, 2025 meeting in Dar es Salaam during CHADEMA’s No Reforms, No Election campaign. The treason charge carried a potential death sentence, while the incitement conviction did not secure his immediate release.
