Tanzania Seeks Investors for Nala Techno Valley Project

The nation is leveraging an AI conference in South Korea to attract investment for a new technology hub in Dodoma.

Source: Daily News Tanzania · 2026-10-21 · 2 min read

Tanzania is actively seeking investors and development partners for its Nala Techno Valley project during an international Artificial Intelligence conference in Seoul, Republic of Korea. Deputy Minister for Communication and Information Technology Dr. Switbert Mkama is leading the Tanzanian delegation at the event, which focuses on how AI and digital technologies can drive economic growth and improve public services. The proposed techno valley, planned for Nala in Dodoma Region, has been allocated approximately 400 acres by the government to serve as a hub for AI, robotics, data processing, and digital skills training.

Dr. Mkama highlighted Tanzania's commitment to developing technology that addresses citizen needs and local contexts during a discussion on AI in public services. He emphasized the country's ongoing efforts to collaborate with international entities and technology firms to secure investment, expertise, and technological solutions essential for its digital transformation. The conference provides a platform for Tanzania to showcase its vision for technological advancement and innovation.

The Nala Techno Valley project aims to establish a significant technology and innovation center, fostering startup development and providing specialized digital skills training. By participating in this global AI forum, Tanzania aims to forge partnerships that will accelerate the realization of this ambitious project and contribute to the nation's broader digital agenda.

Why it mattersInvestors are sought to fund the development of a technology and innovation hub in Tanzania.
