Tanzania urges insurers to seek ISO quality certification

The finance ministry’s call came as the insurance regulator received ISO 9001:2015 certification for its management system in Dodoma.

Source: Habari Leo · 2026-11-19 · 1 min read

Tanzania’s finance ministry has urged insurance companies to strengthen quality management and seek ISO 9001:2015 certification, following the insurance regulator’s certification in Dodoma on November 18. The call was made by ministry Permanent Secretary Natu El-Maamry Mwamba at a handover ceremony for the Tanzania Insurance Regulatory Authority (TIRA).

Mwamba said the regulator’s certificate reflected its effort to improve operations, accountability and customer service. She told TIRA to make the certificate a basis for quality work, rather than a document kept in an office, and urged staff to follow laws, rules and procedures. Insurance Commissioner Baghayo Saqware said the certification process began after TIRA applied to the Tanzania Bureau of Standards for registration.

Saqware said TIRA received guidance and an implementation plan after submitting its application. The ministry said better service should be timely, transparent and professional.

Why it mattersTIRA staff must put the new certification into practice by following laws and procedures, while insurers are being urged to seek certification.
