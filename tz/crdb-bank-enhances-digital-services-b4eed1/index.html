CRDB Bank enhances digital services

Customers can now self-register for internet banking and unlock accounts without visiting a branch.

Source: Habari Leo · 2026-11-06 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

CRDB Bank has upgraded its internet banking services in Dar es Salaam, introducing systems for faster, easier, and more secure transactions. Customers can now register for the service themselves, eliminating the need to visit a bank branch.

Bonaventure Paul, CRDB Bank's Director of Customer Service, stated that these improvements aim to remove challenges for internet banking users and boost efficiency. The service now operates 24 hours a day, seven days a week, allowing customers to access banking services anytime.

A key enhancement is the self-registration system for customers with a CRDB account linked to an ATM card. Additionally, customers can now unlock their internet banking accounts if they enter the wrong password multiple times, without needing to visit a branch for verification.

Why it mattersCRDB Bank customers in Tanzania can now access and manage their internet banking services more conveniently and securely.
