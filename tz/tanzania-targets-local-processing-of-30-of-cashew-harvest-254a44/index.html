Tanzania targets local processing of 30% of cashew harvest

The Cashewnut Board says domestic processors handled 27% of the crop in the 2025/2026 season, against a 60% goal by 2030.

Source: Daily News Tanzania · 2026-11-20 · 1 min read

Tanzania is targeting local processing of 30% of its projected 750,000 tonnes of raw cashew nuts during the 2026/2027 harvest season. The government wants more of the crop processed in the country rather than exported raw.

Revelian Ngaiza, the Cashewnut Board of Tanzania’s director of marketing and quality control, said domestic processing stood at 27% in the 2025/2026 season. The country has 26 cashew factories, whose capacity covers 20% of raw-nut output, and two large-scale factories are due to begin operating this year, he said.

The government is also developing large-scale factories at Maranje Industrial Park in Mtwara Region. It aims to raise local processing to 60% by 2030 and is facilitating working capital for small processors and a steadier supply of raw nuts.

Why it mattersThe government is facilitating working capital for small-scale processors and plans to stabilise raw-nut supply as it targets local processing of 30% of the 2026/2027 crop.
