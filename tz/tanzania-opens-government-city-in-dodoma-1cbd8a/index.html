Tanzania opens government city in Dodoma

The new administrative capital in Mtumba now houses ministries and includes electric vehicle charging stations.

Source: Daily News Tanzania · 2026-10-31 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Tanzania officially opened new government office buildings and road infrastructure in its new capital city in Mtumba, Dodoma on October 30, 2026. The move aims to strengthen public administration and consolidate government operations.

Minister of State Kitila Mkumbo stated the project marks a milestone in establishing Dodoma as the administrative capital, building on efforts from previous administrations. The Government City incorporates smart-city features, including electric vehicle charging and natural gas refuelling stations, to promote cleaner energy use.

Tanzania has invested Sh700 billion from domestic resources in the construction of the Government City. The relocation process to Dodoma began in December 2017 and was completed in October 2019.

Why it mattersTanzanians will see government operations consolidated in the new capital city of Dodoma.
