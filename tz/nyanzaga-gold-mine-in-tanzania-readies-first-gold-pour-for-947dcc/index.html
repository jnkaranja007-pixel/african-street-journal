Nyanzaga gold mine in Tanzania readies first gold pour for January 2027

The Sengerema project, 80% owned by Perseus Mining, has paid local suppliers and the government 457.7 billion/- in its build-up since July 2024.

Sources: Daily News Tanzania and Habari Leo · 2026-11-22 · 1 min read

Nyanzaga, a gold project in Sengerema District, Mwanza Region, is targeting its first gold pour in January 2027 after 17 years of exploration and construction. Sotta Mining Corporation Limited, a joint venture between Perseus Mining, which holds 80%, and the Government of Tanzania, which holds 20%, is developing the project.

Country Manager Isaac Lupokela told the Seventh Tanzania Mining and Investment Conference (TMIC 2026) in Dar es Salaam that the company is preparing to commence production. Between July 2024 and March 2026, the project contributed 457.7 billion/- directly to Tanzania, including 371.6 billion/- spent on local suppliers and 60.7 billion/- paid to the government. More than 3,600 people work at the site, including 281 direct employees, most of them Tanzanians.

Nyanzaga is expected to produce an average of more than 200,000 ounces of gold a year from the 2028 to 2035 financial years, making it Perseus Mining's fourth gold mine in Africa.
