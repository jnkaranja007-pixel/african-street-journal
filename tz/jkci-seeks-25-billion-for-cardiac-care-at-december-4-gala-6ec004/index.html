JKCI seeks 25 billion/- for cardiac care at December 4 gala

The institute plans to fund heart surgery, build a House of Hope and buy diagnostic equipment for school screening programmes.

Sources: Daily News Tanzania and The Citizen · 2026-11-28 · 1 min read

Tanzania’s Jakaya Kikwete Cardiac Institute (JKCI) and the Heart Team Africa Foundation are organising a fundraising gala for December 4 at Malaika Beach Resort in Mwanza Region. JKCI has asked President Samia Suluhu Hassan to attend as guest of honour. The organisers aim to raise 25 billion/- for cardiac healthcare services over five years.

JKCI Executive Director Dr Peter Kisenge said the money would help children from low-income families obtain treatment and surgery. The plans include a fund for heart operations, a House of Hope, diagnostic equipment for school screening and support for research. Kisenge said about 1,500 children need heart surgery, which costs between 10m/- and 30m/- per operation.

The government covers more than 70% of treatment costs, Kisenge said. JKCI performs minimally invasive heart surgery on about 400 children and 400 adults each year; more than 3,000 patients receive minimally invasive procedures. The institute says 97% of such surgeries are now performed in Tanzania.

Why it mattersChildren from low-income families are among those the proposed fund would help access surgery costing 10m/- to 30m/- per operation.
