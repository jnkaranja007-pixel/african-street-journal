Tanzania secures US$85 million for maternal and newborn care

The five-year Beginnings Fund partnership will support 210 mainland facilities and allocate US$9.6 million to Zanzibar.

Sources: The Chanzo and The Citizen · 2026-11-27 · 1 min read

Tanzania has announced a five-year US$85 million partnership with the Beginnings Fund to improve maternal and newborn care, as President Samia Suluhu Hassan called for stronger regional accountability. The announcement came at a reproductive, maternal, newborn, child and adolescent health conference in Dar es Salaam on November 26, 2026.

Beginnings Fund chief executive Alice Kang’ethe said US$75.4 million would go to mainland Tanzania, supporting 210 high-volume facilities and aiming to reach about 7.3 million women and newborns. Funding will cover health workers, equipment, medicines and delivery systems, with implementation led by the government through the Health Ministry and the Prime Minister’s Office.

The government also launched a 2026–2030 health plan and a mortality report. Samia said maternal deaths in 2024/25 stood at 76 per 100,000 live births; newborn mortality was 17 per 1,000, and under-five mortality was 28 per 1,000.

Why it mattersWomen and newborns across mainland Tanzania are the intended beneficiaries of support to 210 facilities, with about 7.3 million targeted over five years.
