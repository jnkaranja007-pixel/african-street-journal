Tanzania and Russia explore new trade and investment opportunities

Engineer Mashauri Jofrey Sabwoya highlights potential for economic and technological cooperation following recent high-level visits.

Source: Habari Leo · 2026-11-01 · 1 min read

Tanzania and Russia are entering a new phase of relations, with opportunities in trade, investment, education, science, technology, energy, agriculture, and industry identified as key areas for cooperation. These potential benefits stem from recent diplomatic engagements, including President Samia Suluhu Hassan's visit to Russia in June 2026 and Prime Minister Dr. Mwigulu Nchemba's participation in the Third Russia-Africa Summit in Moscow in October 2026.

Engineer Mashauri Jofrey Sabwoya, who serves as the Chama Cha Mapinduzi (CCM) Diaspora Secretary in Russia and Chairman of the Tanzania Students' Federation there, believes these high-level meetings open doors for enhanced collaboration. He emphasizes the need to translate these diplomatic efforts into tangible benefits for Tanzanian citizens, moving beyond mere agreements and discussions.

Sabwoya noted that the historical relationship between Tanzania and Russia, which began with support for liberation movements, now has the potential to evolve into a partnership yielding direct advantages for the populace. He specifically pointed to the opportunities in sectors such as mining, industry, and technology as areas where Russia's experience and knowledge could accelerate Tanzania's development.

Why it mattersTanzanians, including students and business people, can benefit from expanded economic and technological cooperation between Tanzania and Russia.
