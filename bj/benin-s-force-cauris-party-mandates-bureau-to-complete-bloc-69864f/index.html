Benin's Force cauris party mandates bureau to complete Bloc républicain merger

Delegates at the party's congress in Parakou authorised its political bureau to carry out a merger-absorption with the Bloc républicain led by Abdoulaye Bio Tchané.

Sources: La Nouvelle Tribune and Benin Web TV · 2026-11-23 · 1 min read

Benin's Force cauris pour un Bénin émergent (FCBE), a party in the presidential camp, has tasked its political bureau with completing formalities for a merger-absorption with the Bloc républicain. Delegates approved the mandate at the party's ordinary congress, which opened in Parakou on Saturday, 21 November, and was due to end on Sunday.

The decision gives the bureau authority to carry out the process with the Bloc républicain, led by Abdoulaye Bio Tchané, according to Benin Web TV. The party's interim executive secretary, Garba Yaya, told delegates the FCBE had held 22 meetings with other political formations, including the Union progressiste and the Bloc républicain.

The congress also examined the party's position within the presidential camp. Yaya, interim executive secretary for several months, was expected to be confirmed in the role, succeeding former leader Paul Hounkpè, now a senator; the source reports no final decision on that appointment.

Why it mattersThe bureau must now complete the merger formalities with the Bloc républicain, which will decide how the FCBE's remaining members are organised within the presidential camp.
