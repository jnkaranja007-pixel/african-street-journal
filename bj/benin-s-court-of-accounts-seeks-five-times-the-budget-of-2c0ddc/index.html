Benin’s Court of Accounts seeks five times the budget of the High Court of Justice

The two courts requested CFA5,88 billion combined, with the Court of Accounts seeking 83,7% of the total for 2027.

Source: Benin Web TV · 2026-11-21 · 1 min read

Benin’s Court of Accounts requested 4 922 308 CFA636 for 2027, more than five times the 960 869 CFA262 sought by the High Court of Justice. The two courts presented budgets totalling CFA5,88 billion to the National Assembly’s budget committee on Wednesday 18 November.

The Court of Accounts, which oversees public accounts and the management of bodies under its authority, allocated 2 245 708 CFA636 to personnel and 2 666 600 CFA000 to goods and services. Its president, Ismath Bio Tchané Mamadou, also proposed an information system for planning, tracking work and securely sharing audit data, with staff training.

The High Court of Justice, which tries the president and government members for certain offences linked to their duties, reported that by 31 October 2026 it had committed 81,78% of its budget and completed 88,25% of its activities. The proposed budgets remain under consideration; the Assembly is scheduled to vote on the 2027 finance bill on 8 December 2026.

Why it mattersBoth courts need lawmakers to approve their proposed 2027 allocations before the finance bill vote scheduled for 8 December 2026.
