Benin's social security fund moves employer filings and payments online

From 1 December, employers must use the national public-services portal for registration certificates and social-contribution payments.

Sources: La Nouvelle Tribune and Benin Web TV · 2026-11-28 · 1 min read

Benin’s National Social Security Fund will require employers to submit applications for registration certificates and pay social contributions through the national public-services portal from 1 December 2026. The fund announced the change in a statement on 27 November.

The new rule ends in-person submission of those applications at the fund’s agencies. Employers needing a registration certificate or paying contributions must send their files and complete payments online, the fund said.

The fund urged employers to prepare for the change before it takes effect. The requirement covers all employers seeking the certificates or settling social contributions.

Why it mattersEmployers handling registration documents and social contributions must complete both procedures online from 1 December 2026.
