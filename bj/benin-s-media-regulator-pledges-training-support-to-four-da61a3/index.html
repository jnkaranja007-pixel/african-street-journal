Benin's media regulator pledges training support to four outlets

Stations in Tchaourou, Savè and Dassa-Zoumè were also reminded to follow the broadcasting terms they accepted.

Source: Benin Web TV · 2026-11-22 · 1 min read

Benin's High Authority for Audiovisual and Communication (HAAC) said it would keep helping media outlets with training after visits to four stations in the Borgou and Collines departments on Friday, 20 November 2026. Media professionals in the two regions asked for stronger training during the sixth day of the regulator's national tour.

The delegation visited Tchaourou FM, Idadu FM in Savè, La Voix des enfants of SOS Children's Village and Soha TV, both in Dassa-Zoumè. HAAC president Édouard Loko reminded the radio and television stations to respect the specifications they had committed to, and talks covered their facilities, broadcast hours and programmes.

The training requests followed similar appeals from three northern stations on 19 November during the tour's fifth day. The national tour was due to continue on Saturday, 21 November.

Why it mattersThe four visited broadcasters must follow the specifications they committed to, and the HAAC says it will keep contributing to their staff training.
