Benin allocates CFA11,59 billion to digital ministry for 2027

Minister Mahuna Akplogan presented the plan to lawmakers, with two-thirds of the money earmarked for investment in public services, connectivity and innovation.

Sources: Matin Libre and La Nouvelle Tribune · 2026-11-19 · 1 min read

Benin's Ministry of Digital Transformation and Innovation has proposed a 2027 budget of 11,59 milliards de FCFA. Minister Mahuna Akplogan presented the plan to lawmakers, the first budget under the ministry's current name after a government reorganisation.

The proposal sets aside 7 736 213 CFA232 for investment and 3 853 335 CFA221 for operating costs. The ministry says the spending will support simpler public services, better-connected municipalities and innovation. By 30 September 2026, it had committed 71,3 % of domestically funded credits and put 68,5 % into payment.

The total is down 29,69 % from 2026 because an externally funded project, worth 6,5 milliards that year, has ended. Excluding external funding, national spending rises 16 %. Plans for 2027 include Innobox innovation centres in Parakou and Porto-Novo, to be followed by other communes.

Why it mattersThe ministry must deliver its 2027 investment and operating plans, with Innobox centres planned for Parakou and Porto-Novo.
