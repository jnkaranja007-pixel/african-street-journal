Benin police seize 1 150 cartons of Timaking 250 in Cotonou

Three people were arrested between Agontinkon and Abomey-Calavi as police seized 73,30 kilograms of tablets and cash.

Source: Benin Web TV · 2026-11-20 · 1 min read

Benin’s Republican Police seized 1 150 cartons of Timaking 250 psychotropic tablets, weighing 73,30 kilograms, in Cotonou on Wednesday 18 November 2026. Three people were arrested between Agontinkon and Abomey-Calavi, according to a police account released Thursday.

Police said an anonymous tip described suitcases regularly unloaded from a bus arriving from the north and quickly moved into a car at Agontinkon. Officers from Cotonou’s 11th arrondissement, supported by Godomey police, watched the transfers. The tablets were found in luggage and beneath the car seats. Officers also seized 1 410 CFA100 in cash and four phones, and immobilised the car.

The case was sent to the Central Office for the Repression of Illicit Drug and Precursor Trafficking. The Beninese Medicines and Other Health Products Agency is to analyse the tablets, and police announced cooperation with Interpol to investigate possible links outside Benin.

Why it mattersThe three arrested people are now under investigation by the central drug-trafficking office, while the medicines agency is to analyse the seized tablets.
