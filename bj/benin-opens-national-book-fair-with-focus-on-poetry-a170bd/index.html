Benin opens national book fair with focus on poetry

The Cotonou event runs through 22 November, bringing together book professionals from Benin, other African countries, the Caribbean and Europe.

Sources: La Nouvelle Tribune and Benin Web TV · 2026-11-20 · 1 min read

Benin opened its fifth National Book Fair on Thursday, 19 November, at the Palais des Congrès in Cotonou, with poetry as its theme. The fair brings together writers, publishers, booksellers and other book professionals from Benin and countries in Africa, the Caribbean and Europe.

Bienvenu Hounwanou, chief of staff at the Culture Ministry, said the book preserves memory and develops ideas. He called for stronger support for book professionals, more accessible bookstores and greater visibility for Beninese works beyond the country's borders. The programme includes workshops, literary meetings, round tables and book signings.

Events continue through Sunday, 22 November, with discussions on African poetry, slam and the role of African languages in distributing works. The programme also includes a poetry contest, a tribute to Olympe Bhêly-Quenum and meetings between invited authors and students.

Why it mattersBeninese book professionals have through 22 November to discuss access to books and ways to increase the visibility of local works abroad.
