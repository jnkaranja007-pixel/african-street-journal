Benin leaves Rohr's future open as contract expires on 31 December

Government spokesman Wilfried Léandre Houngbédji said the coach's employers must discuss his future with him, without confirming an extension.

Sources: La Nouvelle Tribune and Benin Web TV · 2026-11-23 · 1 min read

Benin's government has left the future of national football coach Gernot Rohr undecided ahead of his contract's expiry on 31 December 2026. Government spokesman Wilfried Léandre Houngbédji said on Peace FM on Sunday that the decision rests with Rohr's employers.

Houngbédji said they would discuss the matter calmly with the Franco-German coach, and did not rule out a temporary arrangement for the competition phase, which he placed six or seven months away. Asked whether Rohr would stay, he said he could not say. Rohr, who has led the Guépards since February 2023, said he had begun no discussions and wanted to leave the team in good shape if he left.

Benin beat the Central African Republic 4-1 on 12 November and has 10 points in qualifying for the 2027 Africa Cup of Nations. Houngbédji said state subsidies to sports bodies had risen from CFA75 million in 2016 to nearly 5 billion, with almost 4 billion mobilised for national team qualifying campaigns.

Why it mattersRohr's employers must settle his next contract before the 2027 Africa Cup of Nations phase, with no extension yet confirmed.
