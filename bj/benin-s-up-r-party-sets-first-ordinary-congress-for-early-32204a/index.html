Benin's UP-R party sets first ordinary congress for early 2027

The party's National Council approved preparations for the congress, which will end its transitional period and update its foundational texts in Cotonou.

Source: Benin Web TV · 2026-11-15 · 1 min read

The Union progressiste le Renouveau (UP-R), a Beninese party led by Joseph Djogbénou, held sessions of its Political Bureau and National Council at the Palais des Congrès in Cotonou on Saturday, November 14, 2026. The members adopted resolutions to prepare the party's first ordinary congress, planned for the first quarter of 2027 in Cotonou.

Djogbénou called the first National Council meeting historic, saying it was the first time the party had gathered in that body. The congress is meant to end the party's transitional period, update its founding texts and strengthen its territorial structures. Nearly 400 women were named village and neighbourhood chiefs during the same meeting.

The National Council endorsed the Political Bureau's recommendations on delegates. The party has 118 sections, and each may name up to three extra delegates to the congress, on top of members with automatic seats. The final number of participants will depend on the funds the party raises.
