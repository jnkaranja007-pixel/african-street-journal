Benin's army allocates CFA20 million in farm equipment to women's groups

China's donation includes rice and shea-processing machines for Ségbana and a powered tiller for Karimama, with delivery planned for December.

Sources: La Nouvelle Tribune and Benin Web TV · 2026-11-23 · 1 min read

Benin's army is preparing to distribute CFA20 million' worth of farm equipment donated by China to women's groups in Ségbana and Karimama. The equipment is intended for village groups and cooperatives, and the army says it will be given free.

In Ségbana, where peanuts and shea are produced, the package includes rice hullers, a shea-nut crusher and an oil press. Karimama, described by an army official as a rice-producing area, is due to receive a powered tiller with a trailer. China's ambassador Zhang Wei and Brigadier General Abdoul-Baki Sanni Bachabi, a defence ministry cabinet director, signed the transfer documents, according to the state broadcaster, the Radio and Television of Benin (SRTB), which reported the announcement on 21 November.

The army plans to move the machines to the communities in the week of 7 December. It will provide training in their use and maintenance, along with spare parts for breakdowns.

Why it mattersWomen's village groups and cooperatives are due to receive free machinery, training and spare parts when the equipment is transported in the week of 7 December.
