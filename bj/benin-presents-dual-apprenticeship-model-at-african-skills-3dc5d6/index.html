Benin presents dual-apprenticeship model at African skills forum

At a ministerial roundtable in Oran, Awaou Baco described national qualifications and a unified ministry linking training, employment and business.

Source: Matin Libre · 2026-11-19 · 1 min read

Benin’s Small and Medium Enterprises and Employment Promotion Minister, Awaou Baco, presented the country’s dual-apprenticeship training system at a ministerial roundtable in Oran, Algeria, on 17 November. The African Skills Policy Forum runs from 16 to 19 November.

Baco said the model combines apprenticeships with national certifications, under unified oversight linking vocational training, employment and businesses in one ministry. She also cited the Azôli programme, which supports young people’s placement in companies, including through a link to the Glo-Djigbé industrial zone, and outlined plans to modernise training centres and widen youth inclusion.

The forum brought together representatives from 29 African countries and more than six international organisations, with support from the African Union. On its sidelines, Baco met her Algerian counterpart; the two ministers discussed prospects for cooperation and partnerships in vocational training.

Why it mattersBeninese vocational-training officials now have prospects for bilateral cooperation with Algeria after the ministers discussed partnerships on the forum’s sidelines.
