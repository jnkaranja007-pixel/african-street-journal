Adjohoun adopts first budget under Mayor Hounkanrin

The Benin commune of Adjohoun will operate with a 1.92 billion FCFA budget for 2027.

Source: La Nouvelle Tribune · 2026-11-13 · 1 min read

The commune of Adjohoun has adopted its first budget under Mayor Félicien Hounkanrin, setting a preliminary budget of 1.92 billion FCFA for the 2027 fiscal year. This budget, balanced in revenue and expenditure, was approved during the communal council's fourth ordinary session of the year.

The councilors also approved the 2027 Annual Work Plan (PTA) and the 2027 Annual Investment Plan (PAI). The PTA will guide activity programming and performance monitoring, while the PAI outlines infrastructure projects aimed at meeting population needs and improving living conditions.

The supervision council presented its third-quarter 2026 activity report during the session. The adoption of these financial and planning documents signifies the new elected team's commitment to enhancing administrative efficiency and local public action in Adjohoun.
