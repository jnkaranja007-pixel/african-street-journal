Benin police report 1 415 arrests in security operations

The campaign, launched on 12 November 2026, also dismantled 177 ghettos and seized 18 firearms, police officials said in Cotonou.

Sources: La Nouvelle Tribune and RFI Afrique · 2026-11-25 · 1 min read

Benin’s Republican Police arrested 1 415 people and dismantled 177 ghettos during its fifth special security operation, officials said in Cotonou on Tuesday 24 November 2026. The campaign began on 12 November and targeted crime-prone areas and networks involved in possessing, using and selling psychotropic drugs.

Police also seized 18 firearms in operations intended to prevent violent theft. On roads, officers recorded 19 126 speeding violations involving light and heavy vehicles, alongside 2 668 traffic-light violations and 2 066 cases of riding without a helmet. Police leaders said checks would continue on main roads.

The wider police report also recorded seizures of 8 931 kg of counterfeit pharmaceutical products, more than five tonnes of cannabis and 77 403 CFA625 linked to various trafficking activities.

Why it mattersDrivers on Benin’s main roads face continued police checks after officers recorded 19 126 speeding violations involving light and heavy vehicles.
