Benin's new envoy consults defence and media officials before Moscow posting

Malick Gomina discussed military cooperation and security with the defence minister, and media-regulation ties with Benin's broadcasting regulator.

Sources: Benin Web TV and La Nouvelle Tribune · 2026-11-26 · 1 min read

Benin’s newly appointed ambassador to Russia, Malick Gomina, met the country’s deputy defence minister and broadcasting regulator in Cotonou on Wednesday, 25 November. He discussed Benin’s positions on military cooperation and security with Defence Minister Gildas Habib Agonkan, before speaking with the High Authority for Audiovisual and Communication about links with counterpart institutions in Russia and nine other countries under his diplomatic jurisdiction.

The regulator said their discussion covered media oversight and institutional cooperation. Gomina had met Supreme Court President Victor Dassi Adossou on Tuesday to consider judicial cooperation between Benin and foreign institutions, especially those in Russia; the court pledged support for his mission.

Gomina was appointed on 4 November to replace André Okounlola Biaou. He must present his credentials to Russian authorities before formally taking up the post.

Why it mattersGomina must present his credentials to Russian authorities before he can formally begin work on the cooperation agenda discussed with Benin's defence, media and judicial institutions.
