Benin’s media ministry seeks 24.38% budget increase for 2027

The proposed CFA12,39 billion allocation includes funding for audiovisual production, professional training and wider broadcast coverage.

Source: Benin Web TV · 2026-11-25 · 1 min read

Benin’s Communication Ministry has asked for a 24.38% increase in its 2027 budget, seeking 12 390 094 CFA890 compared with 9 961 132 CFA921 in 2026. Communication Minister Aurélie Adam Soulé Zoumarou presented the request to the National Assembly’s budget committee on Tuesday, 24 November.

The proposed increase is 2 428 961 CFA969, with more than three-quarters of the rise attributed to purchases of goods and services, according to details reported by L’Événement Précis. The ministry allocated 11 225 937 CFA037 to its “Communication and media” programme and 1 164 157 CFA853 to management and support.

The plan includes improving broadcasts in remote areas, expanding programmes in national languages, supporting Beninese audiovisual production and training media professionals. Parliament has yet to vote on the proposed budget.

Why it mattersMedia businesses and professionals are included in a proposed 11 225 937 CFA037 programme allocation, subject to Parliament’s vote.
