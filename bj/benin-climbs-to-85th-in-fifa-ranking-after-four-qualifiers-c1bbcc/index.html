Benin climbs to 85th in FIFA ranking after four qualifiers

The Guépards gained four world places and moved from 19th to 17th in Africa after taking 10 points from four matches.

Sources: La Nouvelle Tribune, Benin Web TV and Matin Libre · 2026-11-19 · 1 min read

Benin’s national football team, the Guépards, climbed four places to 85th in the FIFA world ranking published on 18 November 2026. The team also rose from 19th to 17th in Africa, after collecting 10 points in four 2027 Africa Cup of Nations qualifying matches.

Benin drew 1-1 away to Burkina Faso on 25 September, beat Mauritania 2-1 in Abidjan and defeated the Central African Republic 4-1 on 12 November. It then won 1-0 away, with Joseph Ede Oloko scoring in added time. The team had already secured qualification for the tournament, according to Benin Web TV.

Morocco leads African teams at fifth in the world ranking; Benin’s best-ever position is 59th, while its lowest is 165th.

Why it mattersBenin’s players have secured qualification for the 2027 Africa Cup of Nations, with two qualifying matches still to play, according to Matin Libre.
