Benin says Dogobis dam project is ready for implementation

Government spokesman Wilfried Léandre Houngbédji says the 2026-2033 plan includes a 128 MW hydroelectric plant as power cuts continue.

Source: La Nouvelle Tribune · 2026-11-19 · 1 min read

Benin government spokesman Wilfried Léandre Houngbédji said on Peace FM on 18 November that preparations for the Dogobis hydroelectric dam are ready for implementation. The project is part of the government’s 2026-2033 action plan to improve electricity supply.

Several localities are experiencing power interruptions. On 11 November, the government said domestic generation meets about 40% of demand, with the remainder imported, including from Nigeria and Ghana. A Nigerian supplier is facing problems at its facilities, and repairs could take several months, the government said.

The planned plant has a capacity of 128 MW. Houngbédji said a project steering team attached to the presidency had begun to be formed; the project’s implementation framework was adopted by the Council of Ministers on 17 December 2025.

Why it mattersElectricity users in affected localities continue to face interruptions while domestic production covers about 40% of demand.
