Benin, Senegal and Djibouti set plans for out-of-school youth training

A Cotonou meeting reviewed the three-country training scheme and outlined national rollout plans, trainer preparation and field monitoring.

Sources: La Nouvelle Tribune and Benin Web TV · 2026-11-27 · 1 min read

Benin, Senegal and Djibouti are preparing national plans to train professionals who support young people outside school, after a meeting in Cotonou on Wednesday, 25 November. The meeting was organised by the Conference of Education Ministers of French-speaking Countries (CONFEMEN), a body that groups the ministers responsible for education in francophone states.

Benin's secondary education minister, Adéyèmi Clément Kouchadé, opened the talks and said the next steps include national rollout plans, training of relays and supervisors, new training sessions and monitoring of skills in the field. Participants reviewed national workshops and a knowledge-sharing meeting in Dakar before drawing up an action plan for each country.

The meeting was due to end on 26 November and to prepare a regional framework for 2026-2028 to anchor the scheme in the three countries. CONFEMEN secretary-general Abdel Rahamane Baba-Moussa said alternative education pathways have become essential for young people facing difficulties.

Why it mattersEducation officials in Benin, Senegal and Djibouti must turn the review into national rollout plans under a regional framework for 2026-2028.
