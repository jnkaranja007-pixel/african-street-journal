Coris Bank Bénin opens share sale ahead of listing on West Africa's regional stock exch...

The bank is offering 4,1 million existing shares for CFA19,19 billion, with public subscriptions scheduled for 7 to 11 December.

Sources: La Nouvelle Tribune and Benin Web TV · 2026-11-25 · 1 min read

Coris Bank International Bénin launched a public share sale in Cotonou on Tuesday 24 November, the first step toward listing on the Bourse régionale des valeurs mobilières (BRVM), the regional stock exchange of West African francophone states. The offer covers 4,1 million existing shares held by Coris Holding, for a total of CFA19,19 billion.

Public investors will pay 4 CFA700 per share; Coris Group employees have a separate offer at 3 CFA760. Buyers need a securities account through Coris Bourse or a member of the placement syndicate. The West African Monetary Union's financial markets authority approved the operation on 11 November.

At the end of 2025, the bank had total assets of CFA850,3 billion and ranked third among 14 banks in Benin. It reported net income of CFA4,5 billion for the first half of 2026, up 80% from a year earlier.

Why it mattersInvestors must open or hold a securities account to subscribe during the 7 to 11 December window, at the stated public share price.
