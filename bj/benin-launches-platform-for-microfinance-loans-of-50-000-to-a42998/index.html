Benin launches platform for microfinance loans of 50 000 to CFA50 million

The national financial-inclusion platform lets registered clients choose offers, with repayment periods from three to 36 months and terms shown before signing.

Sources: Benin Web TV and La Nouvelle Tribune · 2026-11-25 · 1 min read

Benin’s National Microfinance Fund launched the National Financial Inclusion Platform on Tuesday 24 November, offering loans from 50 000 to CFA50 million through the government’s 48-hour credit programme. The loan amount depends on the applicant’s profile.

Applicants must first be registered by a participating microfinance institution, which sets the client’s borrowing ceiling. After registering at an agency, customers use their personal identification number or unique borrower ID to access the platform and select an eligible offer. Monthly instalments appear before electronic signing, which requires a code sent by SMS.

Lenders have 48 hours from contract signature to transfer funds to a bank account or electronic-money wallet. Repayment terms run from three to 36 months; interest depends on the offer, insurance fees may apply, and each contract states the withdrawal period. 14 institutions participate, including FECECAM, PADME, Finadev, Coris Meso Finance and Africa Finances; assistance is available on 7320.

Why it mattersRegistered customers of participating microfinance institutions receive disbursement to a bank account or electronic-money wallet within 48 hours of signing.
