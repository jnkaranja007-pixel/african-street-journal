Beninese cultural space hosts Quebec artists

Le Monde de Sica in Cotonou welcomes 10 artists from Benin and Quebec for a residency focused on identity and eco-materials.

Source: Matin Libre · 2026-11-04 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Le Monde de Sica, a cultural space in Cotonou, is hosting a residency bringing together 10 artists from Benin and Quebec. The program, titled "Regards Croisés Québec/Bénin 2026," focuses on the theme "Traversées, identités et éco-matériaux" and began on October 22.

The space, led by Sica Christelle Yaovi, emphasizes creative freedom and cultural exchange. Artists like painter Mekudi Pamela Chablis and photographer Nauld Adjahuime have noted the inspiring atmosphere and opportunities for collaboration. Quebecois artist Jean Martin created a sculpture from salvaged materials at the new National Assembly site.

This residency continues Le Monde de Sica's long-standing tradition of hosting artists, with initial residencies dating back to 2014. The current program aims to foster intergenerational exchange, solidarity, and mutual learning among participants.

Why it mattersBeninese and Quebecois artists are collaborating on artistic projects exploring identity and eco-materials in Cotonou.
