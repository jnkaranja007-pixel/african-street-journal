Benin proposes CFA174,826 billion for health in 2027

Health Minister Benjamin Ignace Bodounrin Hounkpatin told lawmakers the budget would fund emergency care, hospitals and staffing.

Source: La Nouvelle Tribune · 2026-11-26 · 1 min read

Benin’s Health Minister Benjamin Ignace Bodounrin Hounkpatin presented a proposed CFA174,826 billion health budget for 2027 to lawmakers in Porto-Novo on Wednesday, 25 November. The plan includes emergency care for patients in immediate danger, efforts to reduce deaths among mothers and newborns, new zone hospitals, and upgrades and equipment for existing facilities.

The ministry also plans to recruit more than 500 contract workers, train more than 200 specialist doctors and train 60 hospital interns. Hounkpatin set out the spending as lawmakers consider the 2027 finance bill, which has been sent to the National Assembly.

The proposed health allocation is part of a national budget set at 4 CFA757 billion. The finance bill also prioritises domestic resources, territorial development and digital technology.

Why it mattersThe health ministry must plan for more than 500 contract hires and training for more than 200 specialist doctors and 60 hospital interns in 2027.
