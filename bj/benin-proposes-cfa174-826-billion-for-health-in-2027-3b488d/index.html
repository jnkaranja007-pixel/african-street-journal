Benin proposes CFA174,826 billion for health in 2027

Health Minister Benjamin Ignace Bodounrin Hounkpatin told lawmakers the funds would support urgent care, new hospitals and existing facilities.

Source: La Nouvelle Tribune · 2026-11-27 · 1 min read

Benin’s health minister, Benjamin Ignace Bodounrin Hounkpatin, presented a proposed CFA174,826 billion allocation for 2027 to lawmakers in Porto-Novo on Wednesday, 25 November. The plan covers emergency care for patients in immediate danger, reducing maternal and newborn deaths, building new zone hospitals, and upgrading and equipping existing facilities.

The ministry also plans to recruit more than 500 contract agents, train more than 200 specialist doctors and train 60 hospital interns. The proposed health allocation was presented as lawmakers considered the 2027 finance bill, which has been sent to the National Assembly.

The finance bill totals 4 CFA757 billion. Other proposed ministry allocations include CFA11,59 billion for digital affairs, down about 30%, and CFA20,778 billion for foreign affairs, up 13,79% from 2026.

Why it mattersMore than 500 contract agents are due to be recruited, alongside training for more than 200 specialist doctors and 60 hospital interns.
