Beninese presenter Inès Facia seeks divorce from Steve Facia

She told a podcast she had left the marital home after years of difficulties, including infidelities she says her husband committed.

Sources: La Nouvelle Tribune and Benin Web TV · 2026-11-24 · 1 min read

Inès Facia, known as Tata Inès, has announced that she is in divorce proceedings with Steve Facia after nearly 25 years together. She made the disclosure on a podcast hosted by Déborah Mutund, broadcast on Sunday, 22 November 2026, and said she had left the couple's home.

In the interview she described several years of suffering and disappointment, and accused her husband of infidelity. She said she still loves him but is sure she no longer wants to live with him. Social media users offered her support, while others urged caution and said Steve Facia's side should be heard.

Beninese rapper Blaaz asked people in relationships to consider whether their partner was happy, and actress Princesse Beni sent Inès Facia a message of support and prayed for her recovery.

Why it mattersSteve Facia now faces a divorce request and must answer the allegations of infidelity made against him in public.
