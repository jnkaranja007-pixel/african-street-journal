Benin sets 2027 secondary education budget at 124.838.383.589 francs CFA

Minister Clément Kouchadé presented a plan that includes teacher recruitment, school repairs and adult education centres.

Source: La Nouvelle Tribune · 2026-11-23 · 1 min read

Benin's minister for secondary education, Clément Kouchadé, presented a 2027 ministry budget of 124.838.383.589 francs CFA to parliament during budget proceedings. The total is 5,5% higher than the 2026 budget, according to La Nouvelle Tribune.

The plan allocates CFA118.517.856.000 to ordinary spending and CFA6.320.000.000 to capital spending. It includes recruiting and training teachers, strengthening support for adult education (AME) staff and deputy teachers, and expanding pedagogical supervision, the report said. It also lists school infrastructure rehabilitation and reproductive health education.

The ministry plans to strengthen the Educ-Master platform and use digital tools, including artificial intelligence, while implementing general education and literacy measures. Its 2027 plans also include making adult education centres available; parliamentarians praised the government's efforts, the report said.

Why it mattersTeachers are slated for recruitment and training, while the ministry plans to make adult education centres available in 2027.
