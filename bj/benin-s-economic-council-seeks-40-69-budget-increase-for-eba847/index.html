Benin's economic council seeks 40.69% budget increase for 2027

The CFA5.295 billion request includes costs from the former Republic Mediator's duties, now folded into the council's remit.

Sources: La Nouvelle Tribune and Benin Web TV · 2026-11-28 · 1 min read

Benin’s Economic and Social Council (CES) asked parliament’s finance and trade committee for CFA5.295 billion for 2027, a 40.69% increase on its 2026 budget of 3.764 billion. The council presented the proposal on Nov. 26, saying its expanded responsibilities include work previously handled by the Republic Mediator.

Pascal Essou, the CES’s second vice-president, presented the request on behalf of council president Abdoulaye Bio Tchané. The proposed funds cover staff, goods and services, and transfers. In 2026, the council adopted eight studies and continued four more, covering areas including agriculture, employment, health and mobility.

The CES also plans to review the 2028 budget guidance note and corresponding finance bill in 2027. Its 2027-2031 strategic plan sets out a framework for expanding its own-initiative studies.

Why it mattersThe council must fund newly added responsibilities and its 2027 work from a proposed CFA5.295 billion, up 40.69% from 2026.
