Burkina Faso records 580 Beninese among returnees from Guinea

The government says talks with the relevant embassies are under way to organise the return of people expelled from Guinea’s gold-mining sites.

Source: Benin Web TV · 2026-11-22 · 1 min read

Burkina Faso recorded 580 Beninese among people received at a transit centre in Bobo-Dioulasso after Guinea expelled gold miners. The Burkinabe government announced the figure on Friday 20 November 2026, as it said its repatriation operations had ended.

The centre took in 1 994 foreign nationals from 11 countries, according to the Burkinabe government’s information service. Togo accounted for 983 people, followed by Benin, Ivory Coast with 150 and Niger with 133. The service said discussions with the countries’ embassies were under way to arrange their return.

The last convoy from Guinea arrived during the night of 18 to 19 November. Burkina Faso then ended reception and registration at Bobo-Dioulasso and Koloko; regional emergency-relief councils remain available for any remaining cases.

Why it mattersThe 580 Beninese recorded in Bobo-Dioulasso are awaiting return arrangements being discussed with their embassies.
