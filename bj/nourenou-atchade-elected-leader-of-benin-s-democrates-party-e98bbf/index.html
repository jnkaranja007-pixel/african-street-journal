Nourénou Atchadé elected leader of Benin's Démocrates party

Atchadé was chosen by consensus at the party's congress in Porto-Novo, facing internal challenges.

Source: Jeune Afrique · 2026-11-07 · 1 min read

Nourénou Atchadé became the new leader of the opposition party, Les Démocrates, on October 11 in Porto-Novo. He was elected by consensus during the party's second congress. Atchadé, formerly the second vice-president, affirmed the party's opposition status to supporters.

The new leader inherits a party experiencing internal crises, including legal disputes and financial difficulties. Atchadé's succession took place under tense circumstances. He celebrated his victory with loyal party members.

The Démocrates party is described as ostracized and facing a political and financial desert. Atchadé's election occurred on October 11, 2026.

Why it mattersNourénou Atchadé has been elected leader of the opposition party Les Démocrates in Benin, a development that affects the party's internal structure and political standing.
