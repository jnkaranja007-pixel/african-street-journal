Benin diaspora seeks return for around 300 nationals in Mali

The group brought food and water to Béninois who left Guinea after operations against illegal gold mining.

Sources: La Nouvelle Tribune and Benin Web TV · 2026-11-20 · 1 min read

A delegation from Benin’s community in Mali visited Ouinzindougou earlier in the week to assist Béninois who left Guinea, according to La Nation. The visitors delivered rice, bread, pasta, sugar, tinned sardines and water to the site, where the group is staying.

Sofiath Adjeodah, president of the Mali section of the High Council of Beninois Abroad, said an initial count put about 300 Béninois, aged 13 to 36, at the site. Identification is continuing to establish the number and their needs. Toupac Transport also donated food, a cow and an envelope of 500 CFA000.

Adjeodah called for continued humanitarian aid, medical care for sick compatriots and support from authorities to repatriate them to Benin. The group left Guinea after authorities acted against illegal gold mining.

Why it mattersThe roughly 300 Béninois at the site need medical care and continued aid while officials identify them and assess their needs.
