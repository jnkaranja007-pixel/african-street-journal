Benin police seize CFA4,83 million in counterfeit notes at Péhunco

A search of a kiosk owner's home found the notes after police detained its manager in Tokoro over suspected counterfeit medicines and plant material.

Source: Benin Web TV · 2026-11-20 · 1 min read

Police in Ouassa-Péhunco, in Benin's Atacora department, seized CFA4,83 million in counterfeit notes after searching the home of a kiosk owner on Monday 16 November. The notes were in 10 000-franc denominations, according to a police report made public on Thursday 19 November.

The search followed a police check at a kiosk in Tokoro, where officers found suspected counterfeit stimulants and anabolic steroids, along with 28 packets containing a plant resembling cannabis. The kiosk manager was detained after trying to flee, and reportedly told officers he sold the products to local young people for the owner.

The manager is due to be presented to the public prosecutor. The suspected owner had left home before police arrived and remains wanted for questioning about the counterfeit money and sale of the seized products. In separate operations on 17 and 18 July, Péhunco police seized 296 kg of smuggled pharmaceutical products.

Why it mattersThe detained kiosk manager must now appear before the public prosecutor, and police are seeking the owner for questioning about the seized cash and products.
