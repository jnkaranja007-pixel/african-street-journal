Benin president replaces military high command

General Abou Issa appointed as the new head of the military on November 4, 2026.

Source: Jeune Afrique · 2026-11-05 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Benin's President Romuald Wadagni replaced the entire military high command on November 4, 2026. General Abou Issa, an officer who previously survived an attempted coup, was appointed to lead the new military leadership.

This extensive reshuffling of the military leadership was confirmed in a Council of Ministers meeting. The move follows months of speculation within the Beninese army about a potential reorganization.

The government also appointed special envoys to the prefects of Benin's 12 departments during the November 4 Council of Ministers. Separately, Haiti's army chief visited Benin to explore military training cooperation.
