Benin wins four Africa Tourism Awards

The nation secured top honors including 'Tourist Destination of the Year' and 'Cultural Destination of the Year'.

Benin secured four major awards at the 2026 Africa Tourism Awards ceremony held in London on November 1. The country was named "Tourist Destination of the Year" and "Cultural Destination of the Year." Bénin Tourisme also received the "National Tourism Agency of the Year" award, alongside "Tourist Video of the Year."

These accolades recognize Benin's efforts in developing tourism and promoting the destination continentally. The awards ceremony gathered tourism officials, public decision-makers, hospitality professionals, investors, and media to highlight African tourism excellence. The government of Benin has prioritized tourism as a driver for economic growth and cultural affirmation.

The awards follow previous international recognition, including being named a top destination by Lonely Planet in 2024 and by Afar magazine in 2025. Benin's cultural events like the Vodun Days and its diverse landscapes from Ganvié to Abomey have attracted growing interest.

Why it mattersBenin's tourism sector has been recognized for its development and promotion, boosting the economy and cultural visibility.

Haiti army chief asks Benin for military training

Lieutenant-general Derby Guerrier seeks Benin's help to train Haitian officers to combat gangs.

Haiti's army chief, Lieutenant-General Derby Guerrier, has asked Benin to train Haitian soldiers to help combat the country's growing gang problem. The Haitian armed forces, remobilized in 2018, lack sufficiently trained officers to address the urgent security situation in the west of the country where gangs control territory. Guerrier is visiting Benin this week with a delegation to explore international cooperation options.

Guerrier believes Benin can provide solutions to Haiti's security challenges. His delegation is touring several military garrisons and academies in Benin to assess training capabilities. The Haitian army is focused on reconstruction and needs skilled personnel to regain control.

The visit aims to strengthen bilateral ties and secure training support for Haiti's military. Guerrier's delegation will spend the entire week in Benin. This cooperation seeks to bolster Haiti's capacity to confront armed groups.

Benin Senate has powers France's does not

Benin's upper house can suspend or withdraw political rights, a power absent in the French Senate.

Benin and France each now have a Senate, but the two upper houses differ significantly in their composition and powers. While the French Senate represents local authorities and scrutinizes government actions, Benin's Senate primarily regulates political life and holds distinct legislative authority.

A key distinction is Benin's constitutional provision allowing for the suspension or withdrawal of political or civic rights from certain individuals. This sanctioning power is unique to Benin's Senate and has no equivalent in France.

The French Senate's functions include participating in lawmaking and controlling the government, whereas Benin's Senate has a unique role in political regulation and legislative powers that differ from France's system.

Why it mattersBenin's Senate possesses unique powers to suspend or withdraw political rights from its citizens, affecting their civic participation.

Beninese cultural space hosts Quebec artists

Le Monde de Sica in Cotonou welcomes 10 artists from Benin and Quebec for a residency focused on identity and eco-materials.

Le Monde de Sica, a cultural space in Cotonou, is hosting a residency bringing together 10 artists from Benin and Quebec. The program, titled "Regards Croisés Québec/Bénin 2026," focuses on the theme "Traversées, identités et éco-matériaux" and began on October 22.

The space, led by Sica Christelle Yaovi, emphasizes creative freedom and cultural exchange. Artists like painter Mekudi Pamela Chablis and photographer Nauld Adjahuime have noted the inspiring atmosphere and opportunities for collaboration. Quebecois artist Jean Martin created a sculpture from salvaged materials at the new National Assembly site.

This residency continues Le Monde de Sica's long-standing tradition of hosting artists, with initial residencies dating back to 2014. The current program aims to foster intergenerational exchange, solidarity, and mutual learning among participants.

Why it mattersBeninese and Quebecois artists are collaborating on artistic projects exploring identity and eco-materials in Cotonou.

Business · 2026-11-04

Benin mini-stations sell fuel at higher price

Formalized kpayo sellers charge 850 FCFA per litre, 100 FCFA more than official stations.

Mini-stations in Benin, which formalize the informal "kpayo" fuel trade, are selling gasoline at 850 FCFA per litre. This price is 100 FCFA higher than the 750 FCFA per litre charged at official service stations, according to a report by 24 Heures au Bénin on November 3, 2026.

The official fuel price of 750 FCFA per litre has been in effect since October 8, 2026, as stated by the Ministry of Industry and Commerce. While informal "kpayo" sellers previously traded fuel around 800 FCFA, these new mini-stations now compete with approved stations on price, despite their aim to formalize and secure sales.

Despite the higher price per litre, these mini-stations offer customers the advantage of buying small quantities, with transactions starting as low as 300 FCFA. This flexibility contrasts with some service stations where smaller purchases may not be possible. Some attendants are reportedly trying to obscure the displayed prices from surprised customers.

Why it mattersBenin residents buying fuel from informal sellers now face higher prices at formalized mini-stations, impacting their daily transport costs.
