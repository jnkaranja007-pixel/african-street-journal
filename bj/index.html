Microsoft restores global services after outage

Microsoft announced Saturday the full restoration of its services after a global outage that began Thursday, affecting over 70 countries.

Microsoft announced Saturday the complete restoration of its services after a global outage that began Thursday. The company stated that interventions to fix the problem causing disruptions are finished, and users of Outlook, Teams, and Copilot should no longer experience issues.

The company attributes the incident to a technical problem introduced during an increase in its network capacity. Microsoft has apologized to its clients and confirmed that the outage was not caused by a cyberattack or an artificial intelligence agent. The disruptions affected over 70 countries, impacting businesses, administrations, and individuals.

The return to normal was gradual. Microsoft had acknowledged issues with access to Exchange Online mailboxes in the Americas on Thursday at 17:54 GMT. The company plans to publish a detailed report on the incident after completing its review.

Why it mattersBusinesses and individuals in Benin using Microsoft Outlook, Teams, or Copilot experienced disruptions to their work and communication starting Thursday.

Benin coach Gernot Rohr focuses on CAN 2027 qualification

The national team secured a 4-1 victory against the Central African Republic, boosting their chances for the tournament.

Benin's national football team, the Guépards, defeated the Central African Republic 4-1 on Thursday, November 12, in Abidjan. This win places Benin at seven points after three matches in their CAN 2027 qualifying group.

Coach Gernot Rohr stated his priority is qualifying Benin for the CAN 2027 tournament, not his contract, which ends on December 31, 2026. He has not yet discussed an extension with Benin's football leaders. Imourane Hassane scored the first goal for Benin, with Rodolfo Aloko and Aiyegun Tosin adding two more.

The Guépards now have two wins and one draw in Group F, while the Central African Republic has no points. Benin will play the Central African Republic again on Monday, November 16, in Douala, Cameroon.

Why it mattersThe national football team's victory improves their standing in the CAN 2027 qualifiers, with their next match scheduled for November 16.
