Benin U-16 Handball Team Finishes 8th in Nantes

The young Guépards showed progress in their third appearance at the Nantes International Handball Cup, securing a spot in the top eight.

Benin's U-16 handball team concluded the Nantes International Handball Cup in 8th place out of 12 participating teams. This result marks an encouraging progression for the young Guépards, who competed against several European squads. After an initial victory against Entente Val de Loire (19-18), the team faced a defeat from FC Porto (15-23) in the preliminary round, but still qualified for the main tournament.

In the main round, Benin lost to CS Dinamo Bucharest (25-21) and Academia Claret of Portugal (18-19). Despite these losses, the team demonstrated competitive play, often matching their opponents but succumbing to minor errors and lapses in control. The final match for 7th and 8th place against Pulpo of Spain was a narrow 13-14 loss.

This third participation highlights the team's development. In 2022, Benin finished 11th out of 12, and in 2023, 7th out of 8. While the 2026 ranking is slightly lower than the previous year, the team competed in a larger tournament and achieved a top-eight finish. The experience of facing high-level international competition is seen as a significant positive for the players' development.

Why it mattersYoung handball players from Benin are gaining international experience and improving their skills by competing against European teams.

Wadagni Adopts Presidential Role Amidst Talon's Shadow

One hundred days into his presidency, Romuald Wadagni is asserting his leadership with a focus on social measures and diplomatic engagement.

Romuald Wadagni, now president, is making his mark on Benin's governance with a style characterized by sobriety, the implementation of social measures, and active diplomatic efforts. This period follows his inauguration and comes as Patrice Talon transitions to the role of Senate president.

However, Wadagni's presidency operates under the significant influence of Patrice Talon, whose election to the Senate on August 6 has revived concerns about the extent of Wadagni's autonomy. Observers are questioning whether the new president will have the freedom to govern effectively.

The political landscape in Benin is marked by these dynamics, with a portion of political observers closely monitoring the relationship between the new president and the former president, now Senate president, as Wadagni navigates his initial months in office.

Why it mattersBenin's political leadership transition affects the country's governance and diplomatic activities.

Designer Reimagines Amazon Warrior Attire for U20 Team

Jerry Sinclair created ceremonial outfits for Benin's U20 women's team, drawing inspiration from historical female warriors.

Benin's U20 women's football team will wear new ceremonial attire designed by Jerry Sinclair for their upcoming world competition. The Ministry of Sports and Civic Engagement commissioned Sinclair to create outfits that reflect the nation's identity, building on his previous work for the national men's team. This project aims to capture the spirit of the Agojie, the female warriors of the ancient Kingdom of Dahomey, reinterpreting their martial silhouette for contemporary athletes without resorting to historical costume.

The design features a tunic with an officer's collar and long sleeves, intentionally deconstructed shoulders for flexibility, and six slits at the hem for ease of movement. This is paired with dark blue trousers and a wide belt that combines leather and elastic for comfort and fit. The long sleeves are a practical adaptation for cooler climates, such as Poland, where the competition will be held, while maintaining the outfit's distinct look.

Artisanal indigo fabric from Couleurs Indigo, a structure led by Nadia Adanlé, forms the core material. The creative team visited the Indigo Village to select the 'Raphia' motif, emphasizing the handmade quality and artisanal craftsmanship. Bronze-finished armbands and bracelets complement the attire, extending the thematic exploration of the Amazon warrior figure.

Why it mattersThe new ceremonial attire for the U20 women's team draws inspiration from Benin's historical female warriors, connecting contemporary athletes to national heritage.
