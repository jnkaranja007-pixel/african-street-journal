Benin U15 team beats Cook Islands 10-0

The young Guépards secured a decisive victory in their second match of the U15 World Cup and Festival in Baku.

Benin's U15 team, the young Guépards, defeated the Cook Islands 10-0 on Sunday, October 25, 2026. The match took place at the Hövsan Sports Complex in Baku, Azerbaijan, as part of the U15 World Cup and Festival.

This win followed an earlier 3-2 loss to Qatar, bringing Benin's total goals scored to 12 and conceded to three in their first two tournament games. The tournament features eight-a-side matches for players born in 2011 and 2012.

Benin is scheduled to play Italy and Turkmenistan on Monday, October 26, to conclude their group stage. The tournament's first edition runs from October 24 to October 30, with winners of the top-tier 'A' level advancing to claim the championship title.

Benin and UAE sign economic partnership accord

The agreement aims to reduce tariffs and facilitate market access for services and investors.

Benin and the United Arab Emirates signed a global economic partnership agreement in Abu Dhabi on Sunday, October 25, 2026. The deal, signed by Thani bin Ahmed Al Zeyoudi and Olushegun Adjadi Bakari, seeks to ease trade and investment between the two nations.

The accord will reduce or eliminate customs duties on many goods and improve market access for services and investors, though these benefits depend on ratification and the text coming into force. This agreement follows Benin's president's visit to the UAE, announced on October 23.

Bilateral trade in non-oil goods reached $2.15 billion in 2025, a 65% increase from 2024, according to UAE figures. The first half of 2026 saw trade at $1.29 billion, up 37.4% from the same period in 2025. Both countries also committed to cooperation in consular, diplomatic, and digital areas.

Why it mattersBenin and the UAE have signed an economic partnership agreement that will facilitate trade and investment.

Benin launches stroke awareness campaign

The 'Détourner l’AVC' campaign runs from October 25 to November 1, 2026, with daily information and a public dialogue.

An awareness campaign on stroke, or accident vasculaire cérébral (AVC), is underway in Benin from October 25 to November 1, 2026. The initiative, named 'Détourner l’AVC', is led by Hervé Prudence Hessou, president of the Union of Media Professionals of Benin. The campaign aims to inform the public about stroke warning signs, risk factors, and prevention methods through daily posters and fact sheets.

Beyond symptom recognition, the campaign also addresses post-stroke rehabilitation and support for loved ones. It includes activities coinciding with the World Stroke Day on October 29 and a dedicated day on October 30 for life after a stroke. A public dialogue event, 'Dialogue Cerveau & Voix', is scheduled for October 31 at 10 AM at the Maison des médias Thomas Mègnassan in Cotonou.

Survivors, journalists, doctors, speech therapists, and physiotherapists are expected to participate in the October 31 dialogue. The campaign's daily information will cover alert signs, risk factors, and prevention, as well as rehabilitation and family support.

Why it mattersBenin residents are affected by a stroke awareness campaign providing information on prevention, symptoms, and rehabilitation.

Benin grants nationality to 53 Afro-descendants

Recipients from Haiti, France, and Martinique received their certificates at a Cotonou ceremony.

53 Afro-descendants received their Beninese nationality certificates on Saturday, October 24, 2026, at the Cotonou Judicial Complex. The recipients came from countries including Haiti, France, and Martinique. This ceremony follows a law adopted in 2024 aimed at reconnecting with descendants of individuals deported during the slave trade.

Florentin Gbodou, director of cabinet for the Minister of Justice, and Jocelyn Flenon, director of cabinet for the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, presided over the event. Béatrice Toni, a French recipient, expressed her intention to initiate formalities for her relatives to also obtain Beninese nationality. The government launched the My Afro Origins platform on July 4, 2025, to process these nationality applications.

This event is part of a law passed on July 31, 2024, which recognizes Beninese nationality for Afro-descendants. A previous ceremony on August 29, 2026, saw 10 people, mainly from Guadeloupe, receive their attestations at the same judicial complex.

Benin military and civilians trained in maritime security

35 participants completed over 150 hours of training on maritime security and illicit trafficking.

35 military and civilian personnel from the Gulf of Guinea and partner countries completed maritime security training in Cotonou on October 23, 2026. The training took place aboard the French Mistral helicopter carrier.

The fifth edition of SIREN, organized by the French Navy and the Yaoundé architecture, focused on illicit trafficking, illegal fishing, pollution, and search and rescue. Participants received over 150 hours of instruction, including land-based phases in France and practical exercises at sea.

The training aims to improve coordination and information sharing among maritime security actors, including navies, customs, and police. The Mistral's visit to Cotonou, part of the Corymbe cooperation program, includes discussions on ship boarding operations and diving.
