Beninese TikToker Billy Acquitted of Internet Fraud Charges

Arsène Kplédo, known as Billy, was acquitted by the CRIET on Thursday, August twenty-seventh, 2026, due to insufficient evidence in a case of alleged internet fraud.

The Court of Repression of Economic Offenses and Terrorism (CRIET) acquitted Beninese content creator and TikToker Billy, whose real name is Arsène Kplédo, on Thursday, August twenty-seventh, 2026. He was cleared due to a lack of conclusive evidence regarding alleged internet fraud.

Kplédo had been awaiting the court's decision after being prosecuted for suspected internet fraud. The public prosecutor, during a previous hearing on July sixteenth, 2026, stated that the collected evidence did not establish the accused's guilt with certainty.

The CRIET followed the special prosecutor's analysis in its decision, ending the proceedings against Arsène Kplédo in this case. This ruling concludes the judicial episode for the content creator.

Why it mattersIndividuals accused of internet fraud, like Arsène Kplédo, are directly affected by the court's decision, which determines their legal status and freedom.

Benin military chief meets Pentagon to boost security cooperation

General Fructueux Gbaguidi, head of Benin's armed forces, met with US military leadership to deepen their partnership against regional security threats.

Benin's Chief of General Staff, General Fructueux Gbaguidi, met with General Christopher J. Mahoney, Vice Chairman of the US Joint Chiefs of Staff, at the Pentagon on Tuesday, August 25, 2026. Their discussions focused on security threats in West Africa, American assistance to Benin, and strengthening cooperation between the two armies.

The meeting reaffirmed the "strong and lasting" nature of the security partnership between Washington and Cotonou, according to an official US military report published the following day. Both officials addressed regional threats and the need for increased coordination with other West African partners.

This engagement follows recent joint activities, including a five-day exercise in late July where Beninese and American soldiers trained in detecting and neutralizing improvised explosive devices. The US considers Benin's stability important for regional security balance.

Why it mattersThe enhanced military cooperation directly affects the safety and security of Beninese citizens by targeting armed groups and transnational criminal networks.

UBA Benin recruits legal assistant for compliance and litigation management

UBA Benin is hiring a Legal Assistant to manage litigation, contracts, and legal advice, ensuring the subsidiary's compliance with group standards and local regulations.

UBA Benin is seeking a Legal Assistant to work under the Director of Legal Affairs. The role involves adapting the subsidiary's legal policies and procedures to group standards while ensuring adherence to all local legal requirements in Benin. This position will also manage relationships with the bank's legal partners, including lawyers and bailiffs.

The new assistant will be responsible for drafting legal documents, such as service and maintenance contracts, and monitoring litigation involving the bank and third parties or administrations. They will also provide specialized support on legal and intellectual property matters, and advise the General Management and other bank components on legal issues.

Key duties include managing legal reporting for the bank's affairs to General Management and regulatory bodies, identifying and evaluating legal and regulatory risks, and keeping management informed of local and international legal developments affecting the business. The assistant will also participate in negotiations and discussions, offering legal and strategic advice to the subsidiary.

Why it mattersThis recruitment directly affects UBA Benin's operational efficiency and legal risk management, impacting its financial stability and service delivery to customers.

Abdoulaye Bio Tchané visits artisan fair in Nikki

The President of the Economic and Social Council (CES), Abdoulaye Bio Tchané, toured the third regional artisan fair in Nikki, engaging with 75 exhibitors.

Abdoulaye Bio Tchané, President of the Economic and Social Council (CES), visited the third edition of the regional artisan fair in Nikki, Northern Benin, which runs from August twenty-first to August thirtieth, 2026. This visit followed his participation in the Gaani 2026 festivities and focused on understanding the local artisans' skills and working conditions.

The fair, organized by the Ministry of Small and Medium-Sized Enterprises and Employment Promotion, through the Artisan Development Fund, in collaboration with the Chamber of Trades and Crafts of Benin (CMA-Bénin), showcases textiles, sculptures, pottery, and jewelry. It aims to increase the visibility and commercialization of artisan products by leveraging the attendance of the Gaani cultural event.

Artisans expressed appreciation for the CES President's interest and advice, according to Biaou François Atchade, a Borgou departmental councilor for the CES and an artisan sector actor. They hope for continued support and the extension of the fair, aligning with the CES's renewed mission to engage with the population and address their realities.

Why it mattersArtisans in Northern Benin are directly affected by the visit of the CES President, as he listened to their concerns and offered advice, potentially leading to increased support for their sector.

Benin U20 women's team loses 3-2 to France in World Cup warm-up

The Beninese U20 women's national team, known as the Amazons, fell to France's U20 squad in a preparatory match for the 2026 U20 Women's World Cup.

Benin's U20 women's football team suffered a narrow 3-2 defeat against France at the National Football Centre in Clairefontaine. Romaine Gandonou scored both goals for Benin, who are preparing for the 2026 U20 Women's World Cup. The team, coached by Abdoulaye Ouzérou, initially took the lead in the eleventh minute.

France quickly responded, overturning the score to lead 2-1 by halftime. In the second half, Benin showed renewed determination, with Gandonou scoring her second goal in the seventy-fourth minute to equalize. However, France secured the winning goal ten minutes before the end of the match.

This match follows two significant victories for Benin against Sarcelles (7-2) and Fleury U19 (6-0). The team is scheduled to play one final preparatory match on Sunday against Paris FC U19 as they continue their training in France.

Why it mattersThe national team's performance in international matches affects national pride and the visibility of Beninese athletes on a global stage.
