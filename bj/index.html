Romuald Wadagni sworn in as Benin's president, succeeding Patrice Talon

Romuald Wadagni, Benin's long-serving finance minister, took office as the country's ninth president on May 24, 2026, succeeding Patrice Talon. Wadagni, the ruling coalition's chosen candidate, moved quickly to form a new government after a landslide election win, according to reporting compiled by allAfrica.

Why it mattersA smooth handover after a decade of Talon-era economic policy signals continuity for investors and donors watching West Africa's coup-hit region.

South African court delays Kemi Seba extradition case to Benin until Aug. 11

A Pretoria court has postponed proceedings in the extradition case of pan-African activist Kemi Seba to August 11, 2026, leaving him in pretrial custody. Benin is seeking his return over allegations of incitement to rebellion tied to the failed December 2025 coup attempt against President Talon.

Why it mattersThe ruling will test how neighboring judiciaries handle politically charged coup-related cases and could reshape ties between Cotonou and Pretoria.

Benin jails about 30 people, mostly soldiers, over foiled December coup

Beninese authorities jailed roughly 30 people, most of them soldiers, on charges linked to the failed December 7, 2025 attempt to oust President Talon, according to legal sources cited by AFP. The alleged ringleader, Lieutenant Colonel Pascal Tigri, and other mutineers remain at large.

Why it mattersOngoing prosecutions and a manhunt for the coup leader keep security risk elevated for businesses and travelers in Cotonou.

Politics · 2026-08-05

Ruling coalition sweeps all 109 seats in Benin's January legislative vote

Two parties backing President Talon won every one of the 109 seats in parliament in the January 11, 2026 legislative elections, after the opposition failed to clear the 10% national vote threshold required to sit, according to the World Bank. The vote preceded the April 12 presidential election that brought Wadagni to power.

Why it mattersA one-party legislature removes a check on the new president's agenda heading into 2026 policy decisions.
