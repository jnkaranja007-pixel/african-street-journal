Benin judge orders mass for Lionel Messi's father

Supreme Court judge Césaire Kpenonhoun requested the ceremony in Aklampa after being moved by the footballer's grief.

Césaire Kpenonhoun, a judge at Benin's Supreme Court, arranged a memorial mass in Aklampa on October 4 for Jorge Horacio Messi, the father of footballer Lionel Messi. The judge explained on November 8 that he was moved by Lionel Messi's mourning and sought a spiritual comfort for the deceased.

The requiem mass was held at the Catholic church in Aklampa by Father Jules Hodonou. Jorge Messi, who was 68, passed away in Rosario, Argentina. Judge Kpenonhoun had previously sent condolences to Lionel Messi via email on August 17 and postal mail on August 24.

The initiative was also marked by a friendly match between Aklampa FC veterans and Éléphants FC, which the latter won 3-0 after a minute of silence. This event occurred two days before Argentina's national team defeated Benin 3-0 in a gala match in Buenos Aires.

Why it mattersA Benin Supreme Court judge organised a memorial mass for the father of football star Lionel Messi, an act that also coincided with a football match between Argentina and Benin.

Sport · 2026-11-09

Messi wins second Golden Boot

Lionel Messi scored 26 goals in 31 games for Inter Miami in the 2026 season.

Lionel Messi became the first player in Major League Soccer history to win the Golden Boot in consecutive seasons. The Inter Miami forward finished the 2026 regular season with 26 goals in 31 matches, the MLS announced on Saturday, November 7.

Messi's 26 goals put him ahead of FC Dallas forward Petar Musa, who scored 22, and Nashville SC's Sam Surridge, with 21. The 39-year-old Argentinian also led the league with 43 offensive contributions and scored multiple goals in six games.

In the final match on November 7, Rodolfo Aloko, a Benin international, scored a 90th-minute equalizer for Charlotte in their 3-3 draw against Miami. Aloko's goal came after a shot from Tyger Smalls was parried by the post.

Why it mattersBenin international Rodolfo Aloko scored a late equalizer in a Major League Soccer match.
