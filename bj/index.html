Agriculture · 2026-11-17

Benin prefects mobilised to protect farmland

Benin's government is launching a national offensive to stop the loss of agricultural land to urbanisation.

Benin's government is acting against the shrinking of its agricultural land, which fell from 43% in 2021 to 41% in 2023. Prefects from all departments met on Friday, November 13, 2026, in Abomey to make the protection of farmland a national priority.

Antoine Houédji, technical advisor for land affairs at the Ministry of Agriculture, Livestock and Fisheries (MAEP), said that 2% of agricultural land was lost in two years. The MAEP and the Ministry of Decentralisation and Local Governance (MDGL) jointly organised the workshop to secure agricultural land against investment projects. Ministers Adin Yéton Bloukounon Goubalan and Janvier Yahouédéhou presided over the talks.

As part of the Benin Vert 2033 strategy, the state plans to identify and secure several hectares of land in each commune for the "One Commune, One Green Lung" initiative. Laurent ZomaÏ, Prefect of the Zou department, said prefects must intensify, mobilise and secure agricultural land reserves. The sessions also covered legal protection, mapping, boundary demarcation and cadastre work.

Why it mattersEvery commune must set aside several hectares of land as a reserve under the Benin Vert 2033 strategy, with prefects leading the effort.

Climate · 2026-11-17

Benin launches climate resilience project

The seven-billion CFA franc project will support rural populations in nine vulnerable communes facing ecological threats.

The integrated project to strengthen the climate resilience of vulnerable and border communes (Pirrcf) has officially launched in Sèmè-Podji, Benin. The seven billion 150 million CFA franc program aims to provide sustainable solutions for rural populations severely impacted by climate change. Nine communes across the national territory are directly involved, addressing recurrent ecological threats that endanger food security and impoverish local producers.

The plan includes developing protective structures, supporting adapted agricultural practices, and reinforcing water resources to stabilize living conditions. The project's implementation relies on collaboration between the state, local authorities, and international organizations, with financial support from the United Nations Development Programme. Minister of Agriculture, Livestock, and Fisheries Adin Yéton Bloukounon Goubalan presided over the launch, emphasizing the need for effective field implementation and urging communal authorities to actively monitor construction sites.

Field interventions are set to begin in the coming weeks to implement initial commitments to the affected communities. Experts highlighted that the vulnerability of rural and border areas requires sustained attention and investment, stressing that shared responsibility between ministries and mayoralties will ensure the durability of the initiated actions.

Why it mattersThe Pirrcf project will implement protective structures and support adapted agricultural practices in nine communes, starting in the coming weeks.

Politics · 2026-11-17

Benin institute urges leaders to put human dignity before growth figures

The IAJP, a Catholic justice and peace institute, calls for policies on purchasing power, social justice and essential services, not infrastructure alone.

The Institut des artisans de justice et de paix (IAJP), a justice and peace institute in Benin, has urged political leaders not to reduce development to growth indicators and infrastructure projects. Closing its 29th annual symposium, held on 13 and 14 November 2026 with the Konrad Adenauer Foundation, the institute issued the appeal on Saturday, 14 November.

Its director, the Reverend Arnaud Éric Aguénounon, said living conditions lag behind economic performance in several francophone African countries, particularly in outlying and rural areas. The appeal calls for policies that put the human person at the centre, with attention to purchasing power, social justice, freedom of conscience and citizen participation.

The IAJP also wants stronger funding for health, education, social protection and human security, and an equitable sharing of resources. It denounces the embezzlement of public funds and asks for transparent public communication on the economy, public works and mining contracts.

Why it mattersCitizens in rural areas and outlying regions must see water, electricity, food and basic healthcare turned into concrete improvements, according to the institute's call for funding and fair distribution.

Sport · 2026-11-17

Benin beats Central African Republic 1-0 to take a big step toward AFCON 2027

Benin moves to 10 points at the top of Group F after four matches, with Burkina Faso seven points behind.

Benin won 1-0 away to the Central African Republic on Monday, 16 November 2026, at the Japoma stadium in Douala, in the fourth round of qualifying for the 2027 Africa Cup of Nations. The winner came in the third minute of added time, when substitute E. Oloko, who had come on in the 89th minute for Aiyegun Tosin, scored after a tight, cautious match.

Coach Gernot Rohr's side had the more dangerous attack, with 10 shots on goal against seven for the Central African Republic, although the hosts had slightly more of the ball. The result follows Benin's heavy win in Abidjan four days earlier.

Benin sits first in Group F with 10 points, ahead of Burkina Faso on seven, Mauritania on three and the Central African Republic on none. Benin could confirm its place in the finals as early as this week, depending on the result of Burkina Faso's match against Mauritania.

Sport · 2026-11-17

Benin edges closer to Afcon 2027 finals

A late goal secured a crucial 1-0 victory against Central African Republic.

Benin is on the verge of qualifying for the 2027 Africa Cup of Nations after a dramatic 1-0 win over Central African Republic. Ede Oloko scored in second-half stoppage time to secure the victory. This win places Benin at the top of their group with 10 points after four matches.

The team will advance to the finals in Kenya, Tanzania, and Uganda if other group results favour them on Tuesday. Nicolas Jackson, who has 12 goals in 18 appearances for Aston Villa this season, also scored for Senegal in their 4-2 victory over Sudan. Cameroon, five-time Afcon champions, defeated Namibia 3-0.

Elsewhere, Jordan Ayew's penalty gave Ghana a 1-0 win against Somalia. The match was played in Sekondi-Takoradi due to Somalia lacking a suitable international stadium. Ghana had previously beaten Somalia 2-0 in the reverse fixture.

Why it mattersBenin's football team is one step away from qualifying for the 2027 Africa Cup of Nations finals.
