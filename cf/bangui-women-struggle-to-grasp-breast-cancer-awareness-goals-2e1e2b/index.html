Bangui women struggle to grasp breast cancer awareness goals

Many women in the capital city remain unaware of the importance of early detection for breast cancer.

Source: Radio Ndeke Luka · 2026-11-10 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Despite the conclusion of the October Rose campaign in Bangui, many women report a lack of full understanding regarding its breast cancer awareness objectives. Some women still do not know why early screening is important because they have not received enough information.

Edda Lotine Djiko spoke with women in the capital about their comprehension of the campaign. The women's statements indicate a gap in the effective communication of vital health information during the awareness drive.

The campaign aimed to educate women about breast cancer and the benefits of early detection. The ongoing lack of understanding highlights a challenge in reaching all segments of the population with public health messages.
