Bangui hosts second African boxing tournament

The capital city will see boxers from Cameroon, Gabon, and Congo compete on October 31.

Source: Oubangui Medias · 2026-10-23 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

The Central African Republic capital, Bangui, is preparing to host the second African Boxing Tournament on October 31 at the Omnisports stadium. Organized by Bamara Boxe Pro Academy, Almadina Groupe, and Maison Russe, the event aims to promote boxing in the country and will feature professional, semi-professional, and amateur bouts. Athletes from Cameroon, Gabon, and Congo will compete alongside local boxers.

A key match will pit Central African fighter Kevin Dasso against Cameroon's Kola Sucré in the 75kg category. The program also includes two semi-professional and four amateur fights, showcasing young talents like Hergy Betoumbam and Merveil Amoloma. Maison Russe stated its commitment to supporting athletes' progress and increasing the sport's visibility.

Kevin Dasso expressed confidence and called for support from sponsors and fans. The tournament's ambition is to provide Central African boxers with an international platform and prepare them for future competitions, including the Olympic Games. The event is held under the patronage of President Faustin-Archange Touadéra.

Why it mattersCentral African boxers will compete for international recognition and Olympic preparation.
