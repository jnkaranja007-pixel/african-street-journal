Rising living costs strain households in two Bangui districts

Residents in the 2nd and 6th districts say they struggle to afford essentials and make ends meet.

Source: Radio Ndeke Luka · 2026-11-24 · 1 min read

Rising living costs are squeezing households in Bangui's 2nd and 6th districts, where residents say they are finding it hard to meet essential needs.

Residents interviewed by Radio Ndeke Luka said they were struggling to cover necessities and make ends meet. The report, by Bolyvice Massambaye, focused on popular neighbourhoods of the Central African Republic's capital.

The report gave no figures for the price increases or for the number of households affected.

Why it mattersFamilies in these two districts of the capital are cutting back on necessities, according to residents interviewed for the report.
