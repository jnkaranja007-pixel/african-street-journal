Corridor 13 project nears 85% road completion

The 168 billion CFA franc project funded by the African Development Bank includes ports, schools, health centers, and roads linking Congo, CAR, and Chad.

Source: Corbeau News Centrafrique · 2026-11-17 · 1 min read

The Corridor 13 project, connecting the Republic of Congo to the Central African Republic and Chad, is nearing 85% completion for its main road component. The African Development Bank (AfDB) is funding the 168 billion CFA franc initiative, which includes road works, port construction, schools, and health centers. A joint evaluation mission confirmed progress, with the section between Mbaïki and Gouga being the only one fully underway and showing concrete advances.

Former Minister of Equipment and Public Works, Éric Rokosse-Kamot, expressed satisfaction with most of the work, despite localized delays on unassigned lots. The project's seven components include the construction of the Moungoumba port with warehouses and economic activity zones, and support for its management. Related activities involve building schools, health centers, and a women's center to foster local development.

Future steps for Corridor 13 involve awarding contracts for the Gouga-Moungoumba section, which includes a 380-meter bridge over the Lobaye River, and a 30 km stretch to the Congolese border. Work is also planned for the Mbaïki-Bossongo section, though population compensation issues are delaying land acquisition. The project also includes rehabilitating the PK9 bridge for pedestrians and non-motorized users while constructing a new double-lane bridge for vehicles.

Why it mattersThe Corridor 13 project will improve transport and local development through new roads, a port, schools, and health facilities, with road works at 85% completion.
