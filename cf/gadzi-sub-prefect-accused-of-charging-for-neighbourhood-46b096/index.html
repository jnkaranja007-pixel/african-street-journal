Gadzi sub-prefect accused of charging for neighbourhood chief posts

Corbeau News Centrafrique reports that five or six houses now suffice to create a neighbourhood and appoint a chief, with payment demanded from those who want the post.

Source: Corbeau News Centrafrique · 2026-11-26 · 1 min read

In Gadzi, in the Mambéré prefecture of the Central African Republic, a neighbourhood can now be declared with only five or six houses, according to Corbeau News Centrafrique. The outlet reports that sub-prefect Lucien Yika is demanding money from people who want to become neighbourhood chiefs.

The outlet says the small number of houses is enough for an area to be declared a neighbourhood and given a chief. Its report does not specify the amount demanded or name anyone who has paid.

The account rests on information gathered by the outlet's editorial team. No official response to the allegations is reported.

Why it mattersResidents seeking a neighbourhood chief post in Gadzi must now pay sub-prefect Lucien Yika, and the amount he demands is not yet known.
