Central African Republic committee begins nationwide price checks

Created by the prime minister on 10 November, its three commissions will monitor essential goods, transport fares and petroleum supplies.

Source: Radio Ndeke Luka · 2026-11-26 · 1 min read

A joint committee tasked with monitoring prices and social measures in the Central African Republic began work on 25 November in Bangui. The prime minister established it on 10 November to address the cost of living.

Committee president Arnaud Djoubaye Abazène said its three commissions will check prices of essential goods, urban and intercity transport fares, and petroleum supplies across the country. He discussed the committee’s work in an interview with Radio Ndeke Luka’s Bolyvice Massambaye.

The committee’s remit covers all national territory, including fuel supply.
