Former minister criticizes CAR flood response

Marie-Reine Hassen says authorities lack lasting solutions for recurring deadly floods in Bangui.

Source: Corbeau News Centrafrique · 2026-10-28 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Former Foreign Affairs Minister Marie-Reine Hassen has criticized the Central African Republic's authorities for their inaction on deadly, recurring floods in the capital, Bangui. In an analysis published on October 26, 2026, she pointed to successive public policies and demanded accountability for urban planning, sanitation, and investments meant to protect residents.

Hassen specifically questioned the management of urban development and sanitation projects, stating that these have failed to provide lasting solutions to the recurrent flooding that affects the population. She called for transparency regarding the funds allocated for disaster prevention and mitigation efforts.

The former minister's statement highlights ongoing concerns about the city's vulnerability to extreme weather events and the effectiveness of government measures. The report did not specify any immediate next steps or reactions from current officials.
