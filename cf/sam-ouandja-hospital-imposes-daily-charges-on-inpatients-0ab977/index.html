Sam-Ouandja hospital imposes daily charges on inpatients

A Central African Republic report also records allegations that an elderly woman died after being left without sufficient care in her bed.

Source: Corbeau News Centrafrique · 2026-11-25 · 1 min read

Sam-Ouandja’s hospital in Haute-Kotto, Central African Republic, is charging patients a daily fee to stay in its inpatient ward, according to an investigation by Corbeau News Centrafrique. The hospital’s new building was funded by the United Nations Development Programme (UNDP).

The outlet said it gathered serious testimony about the charges and care at the hospital. Its investigation reported that an elderly woman died after being left in her bed without sufficient assistance. The report did not provide the fee amount or say how many patients are affected.

Sam-Ouandja is a small locality where much of the population depends on farming, and the report says access to care is already difficult for many families. The UNDP-funded building was intended to improve hospital accommodation and inpatient conditions.

Why it mattersPatients in Sam-Ouandja face a daily inpatient charge, while many local families already find access to care difficult.
