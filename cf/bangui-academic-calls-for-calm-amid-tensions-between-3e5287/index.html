Bangui academic calls for calm amid tensions between government, opposition and civil s...

Arnaud Yaliki, a public law lecturer at the University of Bangui, urged citizens to respect differing opinions on the International Day of Tolerance.

Source: Radio Ndeke Luka · 2026-11-17 · 1 min read

Arnaud Yaliki, a lecturer in public law at the University of Bangui, called for calm on November 16, the International Day of Tolerance. He appealed amid tensions between the government, the opposition and some civil society organisations in the Central African Republic.

Yaliki said respect for divergent opinions is essential to building a lasting peace. He answered questions put to him by Christ Roi Jordan Bandéngbé for Radio Ndeke Luka.

Other details of the interview were not reported in the source.

Why it mattersCitizens and civil society groups in Bangui are the audience he is addressing, at a time when tensions with the government and opposition are running high.
