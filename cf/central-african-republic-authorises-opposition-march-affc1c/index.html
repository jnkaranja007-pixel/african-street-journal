Central African Republic authorises opposition march

The government has approved a demonstration planned for November 13 in Bangui against the high cost of living.

Sources: Radio Ndeke Luka and Corbeau News Centrafrique · 2026-11-11 · 2 min read

The Central African Republic government has authorised a march by the Republican Bloc for the Defence of the Constitution (BRDC) scheduled for Friday, November 13, in Bangui. The demonstration aims to protest the high cost of living and rising fuel prices. This decision follows recent statements from the government spokesperson that suggested opposition to the planned mobilisation.

The Minister of Public Security formally notified the BRDC coordinator, Me Crépin Mboli-Goumba, on November 9, granting approval for the march. The Minister noted that the BRDC is not formally registered with the Ministry of Territorial Administration but cited a commitment to public liberties and democratic freedoms for the decision. The march is set to begin at 6 AM at the Marabéna roundabout, proceeding through specific avenues to the United Nations roundabout, with an emphasis on maintaining a peaceful and orderly event.

The BRDC plans to use the march to denounce the increased cost of living and fuel prices. They are also demanding the release of five civil society leaders detained since October 12, following a call for a 'dead city' operation. The Ministry of Public Security has assured the BRDC that law enforcement will secure the demonstration and expects adherence to public safety regulations and the free movement of citizens along the route.
