Bangui school fair urges youth to question career advisers

The third national education and career guidance fair was held on Wednesday 11 November at the Alliance française in Bangui, with Youth Power RCA as organiser.

Source: Oubangui Medias · 2026-11-19 · 1 min read

The third national education and career guidance fair was held on Wednesday 11 November at the Alliance française in Bangui, under the theme of guiding Central African youth for tomorrow's challenges. Students from several schools attended. Youth Power RCA organised the event with the Alliance française and under the high patronage of the education ministry.

Porcia Deya Abazene, the fair's patron, urged students, teachers and university students to visit the stands and ask questions. She said guidance should reflect young people's aspirations and abilities as well as the realities of the job market. Didier Colletin, director of the Alliance française in Bangui, said his own modest family background had shaped his path.

The stated aim is to steer young people towards careers that strengthen the country's human capital. Speakers named engineering, teaching, pastoral work, crafts, digital entrepreneurship and agricultural technicians among fields young people might consider.

Why it mattersStudents choosing courses were urged to weigh their abilities against job-market realities when picking a career path.
