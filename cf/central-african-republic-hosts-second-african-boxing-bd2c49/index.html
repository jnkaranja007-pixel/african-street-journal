Central African Republic hosts second African boxing tournament

Bangui prepares to welcome athletes from Cameroon, Gabon, and Congo for a multi-category boxing event.

Source: Oubangui Medias · 2026-10-22 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

The Central African Republic's capital, Bangui, is set to host the second edition of the African Boxing Tournament. The event, organized by Bamara Boxe Pro Academy, Almadina Groupe, and Maison Russe, will feature professional, semi-professional, and amateur bouts. Athletes from Cameroon, Gabon, and Congo will compete alongside local boxers, aiming to elevate the sport's profile in the nation.

A key highlight will be the 75kg professional fight between Central African Republic's Kevin Dasso and Cameroon's Kola Sucré. The program also includes two semi-professional and four amateur fights, showcasing emerging talents like Hergy Betoumbam and Merveil Amoloma. The tournament aims to provide Central African boxers with an international platform and prepare them for future competitions, including the Olympics.

The tournament is scheduled for October 31 at the Omnisports Stadium in Bangui. Kevin Dasso expressed confidence and called for support from sponsors and fans. Maison Russe, a partner, stated its commitment to athlete development and increasing the sport's visibility. The event seeks to establish the Central African Republic as a hub for sporting talent on the continent.

Why it mattersCentral African boxers will gain international exposure and preparation for major events like the Olympics.
