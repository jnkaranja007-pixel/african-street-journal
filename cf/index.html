Business · 2026-08-05

Bangui fuel shortage stretches into fourth week, driving black-market prices higher

Queues at filling stations in Bangui have persisted for nearly a month, according to Radio Ndeke Luka, with drivers waiting hours as pumps run dry. In towns outside the capital, a litre of fuel is selling for 3,000 to 5,000 CFA francs, several times the official price, while authorities have offered no public explanation for the disruption. The National Assembly held a debate on the crisis as landlocked CAR remains dependent on fuel shipped through ports in Cameroon and the Republic of Congo.

Why it mattersProlonged fuel scarcity raises transport costs and threatens to slow trade and food distribution nationwide.

News · 2026-08-05

Gunmen kill Swiss national and soldier near Bangui in weekend attack

A Swiss national and a Central African army soldier were killed on August 1 in an armed attack on the village of Imohoro, about 50 kilometers north of Bangui, according to Radio France Internationale. No group has claimed responsibility and the identity of the attackers remains unknown. The Central African armed forces have increased patrols and launched search operations in the area.

Why it mattersAn attack this close to the capital signals insecurity is spreading beyond the country's volatile rural periphery.

News · 2026-08-05

Armed groups kill around 10, wound UN peacekeepers in northeast border raid

Armed groups attacked Am-Dafock sub-prefecture near the Sudanese border on June 30, killing roughly 10 people and injuring three UN peacekeepers, according to the International Crisis Group. The United Nations said the assailants included fighters affiliated with the Popular Front for the Renaissance of the Central African Republic and the Democratic Movement for the Rally of the Central African People.

Why it mattersThe raid underscores how instability along the Sudan border continues to threaten civilians and peacekeepers despite gains elsewhere.

UN Security Council extends CAR arms-group sanctions to July 2027

The UN Security Council unanimously renewed sanctions targeting armed groups in the Central African Republic for one more year, according to Radio Ndeke Luka. Resolution 2827 also extends the mandate of the panel of experts monitoring enforcement, keeping the measures in place until July 31, 2027.

Why it mattersContinued sanctions keep pressure on armed factions but signal the Council still views the security situation as unresolved.

Heavy rains flood Bangui neighborhoods, damaging homes and markets

Torrential rain on July 12 flooded the Miskine and Boy-Rabe districts in Bangui's 4th and 5th arrondissements, according to Radio Ndeke Luka. Several areas remained inundated afterward, with standing water reported in market aisles and damage to homes.

Why it mattersRecurring urban flooding highlights gaps in Bangui's drainage infrastructure as the rainy season continues.

Health ministry battles growing cholera outbreak, watches for Ebola spillover

The Central African Republic is contending with an expanding cholera epidemic, according to Radio Ndeke Luka. The health minister also called for vigilance against Ebola in Obo, near the border with the Democratic Republic of Congo, where an Ebola outbreak has spread to additional provinces.

Why it mattersA dual disease threat near a porous border could strain the country's already limited health infrastructure.
