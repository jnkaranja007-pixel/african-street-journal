CAR government reports 49 dead in Zamboye mine collapse

A total of 49 people died following earth collapses on Tuesday, Aug. 18, at the "Moscou-RCA" mining site in Zamboye. Minister of Mines and Geology Rufin Benam-Beltoungou reported the figure after visiting the site in Nana-Mambéré.

Why it mattersThe disaster results in the immediate loss of 49 lives at the mining operation.

Politics · 2026-08-21

CAR presidency says courts can probe French ambassador

The presidency of the Central African Republic stated that national justice may take up a case involving French Ambassador Bruno Foucher. Presidential spokesperson Albert Yalokkè Mokpème issued the statement following reports by Le Canard Enchaîné alleging Foucher had sexual relations with young women at his residence in Bangui.

Why it mattersThe statement opens the door for legal proceedings against a foreign diplomat in Bangui.

Business · 2026-08-21

GTSC group denounces UNOPS over road and bridge projects

The GTSC group alleges that UNOPS failed to meet commitments for the rehabilitation of the Ndélé-Ouadda road and the construction of four bridges between Mbrès and Ndélé. Spokesperson Paul Crescent Béninga stated that the failures occurred despite the disbursement of funds by the World Bank.

Why it mattersThe lack of completed infrastructure hinders movement and transport between Mbrès and Ndélé.

CAR Communication Minister attends media forum in Beijing

Minister of State for Communication and Media Evariste Ngamana arrived in Beijing on Aug. 18, 2026. Ngamana is attending the 7th China-Africa Media Forum.

Why it mattersThe visit aims to strengthen diplomatic and media cooperation between the Central African Republic and China.
