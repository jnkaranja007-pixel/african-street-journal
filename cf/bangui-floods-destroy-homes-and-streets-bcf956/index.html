bangui floods destroy homes and streets

The capital city of the Central African Republic is submerged, with streets and homes destroyed by severe flooding.

Source: Corbeau News Centrafrique · 2026-10-28 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Bangui, the capital of the Central African Republic, is submerged in water after catastrophic floods destroyed homes and streets in the city center and peripheral neighborhoods. Images circulating on social media show vehicles trapped and buildings damaged by the raging torrents.

The scale of the recurring disaster has led residents to question the cause, with some asking if it is a divine deluge or the result of an irresponsible regime. Calls to God are multiplying as the city grapples with the aftermath.

This year's flooding has transformed the capital's streets into furious torrents, engulfing houses and demolishing walls. The recurring nature of these events raises questions about past warnings that went unheeded.

Why it mattersResidents of Bangui are directly affected by the destruction of their homes and streets due to severe flooding.
