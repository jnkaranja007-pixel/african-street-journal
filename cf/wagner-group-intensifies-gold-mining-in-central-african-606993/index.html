Wagner group intensifies gold mining in Central African Republic

While Ndassima gold activities draw political attention, Wagner's industrial gold extraction also expands in Yidéré.

Source: Corbeau News Centrafrique · 2026-11-15 · 1 min read

The Russian Wagner group is intensifying industrial gold mining operations in Yidéré, located in the Nana-Mambéré prefecture of the Central African Republic. Field observations describe a large-scale mining site with crushing facilities and continuous activity, with helicopters and planes linked to Wagner making regular rotations to the site.

Corbeau News Centrafrique's field team estimates that the Yidéré installations may surpass those at Ndassima in scale, based on direct observations. While detailed public data on exploited areas or production volumes is unavailable, the Yidéré site exhibits characteristics of industrial exploitation, including significant logistical means and regular air traffic.

The Nana-Mambéré region hosts several foreign operators, including Chinese actors and interests linked to Wagner, according to information gathered by Corbeau News Centrafrique. Precisely identifying the companies present, their authorizations, and their respective responsibilities is necessary to understand the conditions under which the country's resources are being exploited.

Why it mattersThe Wagner group's industrial gold mining operations in Yidéré involve significant logistical means and regular air traffic, impacting the exploitation of the Central African Republic's natural resources.
