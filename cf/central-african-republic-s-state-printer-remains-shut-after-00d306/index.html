Central African Republic’s state printer remains shut after four months

Staff are protesting over 34 months of unpaid wages at the public company responsible for printing official government documents.

Sources: Radio Ndeke Luka and Corbeau News Centrafrique · 2026-11-21 · 1 min read

The Central African Republic’s public printing company has been out of operation for four months as staff protest over unpaid wages. The company prints official state documents, and its shutdown has halted its usual work.

Radio Ndeke Luka reported that workers are demanding payment of 34 months of salary arrears. The report did not give a value for the debt or say how many employees are involved.

The company’s routine printing work remains stopped while the protest continues. The report gave no further details about a payment plan or when operations might resume.

Why it mattersThe printing company’s staff are demanding payment for 34 months of unpaid salaries while the state document service remains halted.
