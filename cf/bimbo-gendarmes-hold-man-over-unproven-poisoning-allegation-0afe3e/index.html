Bimbo gendarmes hold man over unproven poisoning allegation

Radio Ndeke Luka reported that the wife and four children said to be victims were alive, while investigators had not established an attempted poisoning.

Sources: Corbeau News Centrafrique and Radio Ndeke Luka · 2026-11-24 · 1 min read

Gendarmes in Bimbo, near Bangui, detained a man on Sunday over an allegation that he put a toxic substance in juice consumed by his wife, four children and mother-in-law. An initial report said the six family members had died; Radio Ndeke Luka later reported that none had died.

The broadcaster said it found all the people described as victims alive and going about their activities. The man’s wife told the station that one of her sons alerted her to the alleged substance in the juice. The man said he had drunk the same juice as his children and denied trying to poison anyone. Bimbo’s gendarmerie said the attempted poisoning remained unproven.

The gendarmerie was holding the suspect as investigators worked to establish what happened. Radio Ndeke Luka said the man and his wife had argued several days before the allegation.

Why it mattersThe detained man remains in gendarmerie custody while investigators seek proof of an attempted poisoning.
