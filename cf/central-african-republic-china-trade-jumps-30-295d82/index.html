Central African Republic-China trade jumps 30%

Bilateral trade reached 123 million dollars between January and September 2026, driven by China's zero-tariff regime.

Source: Oubangui Medias · 2026-11-15 · 1 min read

Trade between the Central African Republic (CAR) and China surged by 30.0% in the first nine months of 2026, reaching 123 million US dollars. This increase follows China's implementation of a zero-tariff regime for 53 African countries on May 1, 2026, which accelerates access to the Chinese market.

The CAR's exports to China during this period were overwhelmingly dominated by timber, accounting for 99.9% of the 30.2 million dollars in exports. While raw timber sales saw a slight increase, sawn timber exports experienced a significant rise of 263.3%.

The CAR's total trade with Beijing in nine months nearly matched the 130 million dollars recorded for all of 2025. The country now ranks 180th among China's trading partners, up from 183rd last year.

Why it mattersCentral African Republic producers and economic operators should leverage China's zero-tax policy to boost exports, with timber remaining the primary commodity.
