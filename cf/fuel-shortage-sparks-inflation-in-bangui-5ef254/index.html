Fuel shortage sparks inflation in Bangui

Transport costs and food prices are rising as stations struggle with supply for three weeks.

Source: RFI Afrique · 2026-11-10 · 1 min read

Fuel shortages are disrupting daily life in Bangui, the capital of the Central African Republic. For three weeks, multiple service stations have faced supply difficulties, leading to a scarcity of transport options.

The lack of fuel has caused transportation fares to increase significantly. This situation is directly impacting the cost of living for residents who rely on these services for their daily commutes and movement of goods.

The rising prices extend beyond transport, with certain food items also experiencing an upward trend in cost. This inflation is a direct consequence of the ongoing fuel supply disruptions affecting the city.

Why it mattersResidents of Bangui face higher costs for transport and food due to a persistent fuel shortage.
