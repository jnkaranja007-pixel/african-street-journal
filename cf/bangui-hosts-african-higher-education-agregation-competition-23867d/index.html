Bangui Hosts African Higher Education Agrégation Competition

Over 300 candidates from 15 African nations are competing in the 23rd CAMES aggregation contest, focusing on rigorous professional training.

Bangui is hosting the aggregation contest of the African and Malagasy Council for Higher Education (CAMES) for the first time. More than 300 candidates from 15 African countries are participating in this 23rd edition, which commenced on Monday. The competition spans five fields, including human medicine, pharmacy, and veterinary medicine, aiming to foster rigor and excellence in professional training.

The event is designed to promote high standards in the education of future professionals across the continent. Participants are vying for recognition in specialized areas, underscoring the importance of advanced academic and practical skills in these critical sectors.

This marks a significant occasion for the Central African Republic to showcase its capacity to host international academic events and contribute to the advancement of higher education standards across Africa.

Why it mattersThe competition affects professionals in medicine, pharmacy, and veterinary fields by promoting rigor and excellence in their training.
