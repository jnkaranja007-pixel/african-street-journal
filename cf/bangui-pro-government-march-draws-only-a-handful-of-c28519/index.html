Bangui pro-government march draws only a handful of onlookers

The event on Independence Avenue paired a march with music, while government spokesman Évariste Ngamana criticised the BRDC’s mobilisation.

Source: Corbeau News Centrafrique · 2026-11-19 · 1 min read

A pro-government march in Bangui drew only a handful of curious onlookers on Independence Avenue on 13 November 2026, according to reporters for Corbeau News Centrafrique. The march was paired with music and entertainment aimed at attracting residents.

Government spokesman Évariste Ngamana said Central Africans had largely ignored a call by the Bloc républicain pour la défense de la Constitution de 2016 (BRDC), which held its march on the same date. His public remarks did not mention the small turnout at the pro-government event, the outlet reported.

The BRDC’s march and the pro-government gathering used different approaches: the outlet said the latter featured music rather than demands about fuel prices or speeches on civil liberties.

Why it mattersThe government and the BRDC each held a public demonstration in Bangui on 13 November, and Ngamana must address the turnout at the pro-government event.
