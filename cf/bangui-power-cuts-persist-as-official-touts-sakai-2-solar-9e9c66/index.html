Bangui power cuts persist as official touts Sakaï 2 solar project

Maxime Balalou said the plant would double maximum capacity and serve 300 000 households, while outages continue across the capital.

Source: Corbeau News Centrafrique · 2026-11-20 · 1 min read

Maxime Balalou, a former Central African government spokesman, said at a February 9, 2026, press conference that the Sakaï 2 solar plant would double maximum capacity and provide electricity to 300 000 households in Bangui. Residents in the capital have continued to report outages.

In January 2026, ENERCA’s director general cited problems at the Boali 2 site to explain prolonged power cuts. Radio Ndeke Luka reported on February 14 that residents in several neighbourhoods were complaining about electricity distribution problems that were holding back economic and social activity.

Power cuts had affected Galabadja 3, Gobongo, PK12, the Boali road and part of the 6th district in late November 2025. Galabadja 3 had been without electricity for more than two weeks, according to the report.

Why it mattersResidents and businesses in several Bangui neighbourhoods face disrupted electricity distribution that Radio Ndeke Luka said was holding back economic and social activity.
