FAFECA adviser pledges funding appeal for women entrepreneurs

Mabelle Nabana says she will ask the Economic Council to back financing for exhibitors whose products she said have improved since last year.

Sources: Oubangui Medias and RFI Afrique · 2026-11-24 · 1 min read

Mabelle Nabana, an economic adviser and representative of the Federation of Associations of Women Entrepreneurs (FAFECA), said she would ask the Economic Council to support financing for women entrepreneurs at Bangui’s fourth “Consommons local” campaign. She visited the exhibitors on 17 November, buying their products and encouraging them.

Nabana said the women had improved their products since last year, when their work was entirely artisanal. The campaign, organised by FAFECA with support from the ministry responsible for small and medium-sized enterprises, runs from 2 to 30 November and promotes local production and processing.

Nabana said several entrepreneurs had the capacity to do more but lacked the money. She said she would advocate for support so they could achieve next year what they had done this year.

Why it mattersThe women entrepreneurs Nabana represents lack the funds to expand, and she says she will seek Economic Council support for their work before next year’s edition.
