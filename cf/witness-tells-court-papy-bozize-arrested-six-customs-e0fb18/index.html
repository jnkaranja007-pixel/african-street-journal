Witness tells court Papy Bozizé arrested six customs officers

At the Special Criminal Court in Bangui, the witness said the officers were detained in Bouar in 2012 while working as customs officers.

Sources: Radio Ndeke Luka and Oubangui Medias · 2026-11-25 · 1 min read

A protected witness told the Special Criminal Court in Bangui on 23 November that Papy Bozizé arrested six customs officers in Bouar in 2012. The witness, identified as BOS-06, said the officers were detained while carrying out their duties and that he did not know why he had been arrested.

The witness said Papy Bozizé, then commander of Military Region No. 3, had taken over customs duties, and that the officers opposed him during an operation. He said the six were taken to Bossembélé prison. Defence lawyers argued that their release on judicial instructions showed their clients were not responsible for the detention; prosecutors said judicial authorities had no control over the prison at the time.

The witness also said magistrate Éric Tambo visited regularly and brought the document ordering their release. The case concerns charges of murder, torture, cruel and degrading treatment, and enforced disappearances as crimes against humanity; further witnesses are due to testify.

Why it mattersThe court must assess defence and prosecution accounts of judicial control over the prison as it hears evidence about the six officers' detention.
