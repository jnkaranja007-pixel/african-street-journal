Central African forces launch offensive operations

Army units, supported by Russian specialists, dismantled illegal checkpoints and neutralized armed group members.

Source: Oubangui Medias · 2026-11-10 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Central African Republic's security forces are adopting a more offensive stance to reclaim and secure national territory. The army, police, and gendarmerie are increasing their operational presence across the country. This shift aims to enhance the nation's security apparatus to anticipate threats, protect citizens, and defend territorial integrity.

The national army is undergoing a transformation, moving from a defensive posture to a more mobile and offensive intervention capability. In October 2026, FACA forces, with Russian military specialists, conducted a preventive operation in the Vakaga prefecture near Nabagai-Tissi to thwart an incursion from the tri-border area. Operations in Vakaga on October 28 and 30 led to the dismantling of an illegal checkpoint and the discovery of an armed group's base.

In a separate operation on November 5, 2026, south of Pendéré in Nana-Mambéré prefecture, nine members of an Anti-Balaka group, including their alleged leader Marcel Ndalé, were neutralized with Russian support. This operation secured the Bouar-Béloko trade route. Security cooperation with Russia and Rwanda, including training and intelligence sharing, is bolstering national forces. In June 2026, MINUSCA provided logistical support for redeploying over 300 FACA elements to Vakaga.

Why it mattersCentral African citizens are affected as the national army shifts to a more proactive strategy to combat armed groups and secure territory.
