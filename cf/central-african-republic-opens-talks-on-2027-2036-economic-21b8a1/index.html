Central African Republic opens talks on 2027-2036 economic reform strategy

Finance Minister Hervé Ndoba says the government and its partners must agree how the reforms will be sequenced and carried out.

Source: Oubangui Medias · 2026-11-19 · 1 min read

The Central African Republic's government and its technical and financial partners opened a consultation workshop in Bangui on Monday, 16 November 2026, on a 10-year strategy of economic and financial reforms covering 2026-2027. The finance and budget ministry, the economy and planning ministry, the Bank of Central African States (BEAC) and the partners organised the meeting, according to Oubangui Medias.

Finance Minister Hervé Ndoba said the meeting should agree the direction, sequencing and implementation arrangements of the strategy for 2027-2036, in line with the national development plan and the conclusions of government consultations held between May and October 2026. A representative of the International Monetary Fund said the country already has many diagnoses and recommendations, and that the main task is now moving from diagnosis to execution.

The IMF and the World Bank Group are finalising, at staff level, a proposal for a long-term joint engagement framework with the Central African Republic.

Why it mattersPartners and ministries must settle the order and delivery method of the decade-long reforms, which will shape how public finances are run.
