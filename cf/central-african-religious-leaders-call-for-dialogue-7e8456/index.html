Central African religious leaders call for dialogue

Religious leaders in Bangui urged calm and dialogue amid socio-economic and political tensions.

Source: Radio Ndeke Luka · 2026-11-13 · 1 min read

Religious leaders in the Central African Republic called for calm and dialogue on Thursday, November 12, in Bangui. The Platform of Religious Confessions of the Central African Republic (PCRC) expressed concern over the deteriorating social fabric and tensions between the government, opposition, and civil society.

The leaders highlighted the fuel shortage and rising cost of living as urgent issues requiring government attention. Apostle Nicolas Guéret-Koyame noted the improving security but warned of a worsening socio-political climate, stating, "We are witnessing a widening crisis between political power, opposition parties, and civil society."

Cardinal Dieudonné Nzapalaïnga urged the youth and media to act responsibly and transparently. The PCRC announced a nationwide campaign for peace and coexistence to help ease social tensions.

Why it mattersReligious leaders are urging dialogue to address the rising cost of living and political tensions affecting most Central Africans.
