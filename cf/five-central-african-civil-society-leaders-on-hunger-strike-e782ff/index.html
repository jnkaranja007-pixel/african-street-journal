Five Central African civil society leaders on hunger strike

Activists arrested on October 15 are now in gendarmerie custody, their health declining.

Source: Corbeau News Centrafrique · 2026-10-23 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Five Central African civil society leaders arrested on October 15 in Bangui have begun a hunger strike in gendarmerie detention. Their deteriorating health has caused concern among investigators, who are reportedly angry with police colleagues over the transfer. The arrests occurred on October 15.

President Faustin-Archange Touadéra stated on Wednesday, October 21, that he would not intervene for the release of the five activists. He believes the decision rests with the justice system, which he expects to operate independently. The activists' current condition is worrying.

The leaders were detained in Bangui, the capital city. Their transfer to gendarmerie custody has led to anger among some investigators. President Touadéra's refusal to intervene highlights the justice system's expected autonomy.
