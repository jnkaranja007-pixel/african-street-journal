Hundreds march in Bangui against fuel price rise and high cost of living

The Bloc Républicain pour la Défense de la Constitution (BRDC) led the peaceful protest along Avenue des Martyrs on Friday morning.

Sources: Corbeau News Centrafrique and Radio Ndeke Luka · 2026-11-15 · 1 min read

Several hundred people marched in Bangui on Friday, 13 November 2026, to protest rising fuel prices and the high cost of living in the Central African Republic. The march was called by the Bloc Républicain pour la Défense de la Constitution (BRDC), a group of opposition parties, which said the government had raised fuel prices without consultation.

The demonstrators set off from the Marabéna crossroads, walked along Avenue des Martyrs and ended at the Martyrs roundabout. National security forces and the United Nations stabilisation mission, MINUSCA, escorted the march, which went ahead peacefully.

Organisers say protests of this kind are a legitimate way to contest government decisions and press for change in everyday living conditions.

Why it mattersHouseholds already short of cash now face dearer fuel and food, and the opposition has shown it can gather hundreds on the streets over the cost of living.
