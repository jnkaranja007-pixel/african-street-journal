Newtech Institut graduates celebrate academic success

The Bangui-based institute awarded diplomas from bachelor's to master's degrees, recognizing top students and a robotics champion.

Source: Oubangui Medias · 2026-10-22 · 1 min read

Newtech Institut in Bangui celebrated its 2026 academic year with an open house, solemn opening ceremony, and graduation for the Émilie Béatrice Epaye class on Saturday, October 17. Diplomas were conferred for studies ranging from Baccalaureate to Master's levels. The event also honored Charline Girardot, a student recognized for her achievements in a South Korean robotics competition.

Serge Singha Bengba, President Founder of Newtech, congratulated students on their excellent national exam results, emphasizing education as a crucial investment for the country's future. He also acknowledged the dedication of teachers and parents. Charline Girardot, the institute's top student, was highlighted for her academic excellence, including a Baccalaureate in the challenging Series C, and her representation of the Central African Republic in the international robotics contest.

The institute's strategic decision to integrate robotics and artificial intelligence into its scientific curriculum from the secondary level was reiterated. The promotion was named in honor of Émilie Béatrice Epaye, recognizing her distinguished career as a four-time elected deputy, minister, and her past achievements as a track and field athlete and academic.

Why it mattersStudents and their families are directly affected by the academic achievements and recognition of their educational progress.
