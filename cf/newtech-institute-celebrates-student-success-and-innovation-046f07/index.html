Newtech Institute Celebrates Student Success and Innovation

The institution awarded diplomas from Baccalaureate to Master's degrees and honored a robotics champion.

Source: Oubangui Medias · 2026-10-21 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X

Newtech Institute in Bangui held a celebratory open house and solemn opening ceremony for its 2026 academic year on Saturday, October 17. The event included the graduation of the Émilie Béatrice Epaye class, with students receiving diplomas ranging from Baccalaureate to Master's levels. A special recognition was given to champion Girardot thanks Charline Reine for her achievements in a robotics competition held in South Korea.

Serge Singha Bengba, President and Founder of Newtech, congratulated the students on their excellent results in national exams for 2026. He emphasized education as a crucial investment for the youth and the nation's future, also acknowledging the dedication of teachers and parents. Charline Girardot, recognized as the institute's top student, was celebrated for her academic excellence and her representation of Central African youth at the international robotics event.

The institute's strategic choice to integrate robotics and artificial intelligence into its scientific curriculum from the high school level was highlighted. The promotion was named in honor of Émilie Béatrice Epaye, recognizing her distinguished career as a former deputy, minister, and her past achievements as an athlete and academic.

Why it mattersStudents in the Central African Republic are recognized for academic achievements and participation in international STEM competitions.
