Bangui's 6th arrondissement mayor reviews first 100 days in office

Crépin Ngoko-Nzengue outlined actions already under way and his plans to improve the arrondissement's organisation and development.

Source: Radio Ndeke Luka · 2026-11-24 · 1 min read

Crépin Ngoko-Nzengue, mayor of Bangui's 6th arrondissement, has reviewed his first 100 days leading the municipality, Radio Ndeke Luka reported on Tuesday. He also set out the actions the municipality has begun.

Ngoko-Nzengue presented his perspectives for improving how the arrondissement is organised and for contributing to its development, the station said. The report gave no details of the actions, targets, costs or timetable.

The 6th arrondissement is one of the municipal districts of Bangui, the capital.

Why it mattersResidents of the 6th arrondissement will judge the mayor by whether the organisational and development plans he presented are carried out.
