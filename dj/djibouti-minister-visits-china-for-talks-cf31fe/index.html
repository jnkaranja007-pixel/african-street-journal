Djibouti minister visits China for talks

Djibouti's foreign minister met with China's Wang Yi to deepen political and economic cooperation.

Source: Agence Djiboutienne d'Information · 2026-10-23 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Djibouti's Minister of Foreign Affairs, Abdoulkader Houssein Omar, arrived in Beijing for an official visit at the invitation of Chinese Foreign Minister Wang Yi. The visit, scheduled to conclude on October 24, aims to strengthen political dialogue and deepen cooperation between the two governments.

Discussions focused on political, economic, and institutional partnership, as well as infrastructure, investments, and trade prospects. The ministers also addressed regional and international issues of mutual concern, agreeing to continue consultations in bilateral and multilateral forums. Omar was accompanied by a delegation including Djibouti's ambassador to China and senior officials from various ministries.

The delegation includes key figures such as the ambassador Abdallah Abdillahi Miguil, the secretary general of the foreign ministry Mohamed Ali Hassan, and the secretary general of the budget ministry Simon Mibrathu.
