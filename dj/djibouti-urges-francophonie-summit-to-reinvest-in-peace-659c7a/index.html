Djibouti urges Francophonie summit to reinvest in peace

Djibouti's foreign minister told heads of state at the Francophonie summit in Phnom Penh that lasting prosperity needs justice, dialogue and solidarity.

Source: Agence Djiboutienne d'Information · 2026-11-16 · 1 min read

Djibouti's foreign minister, who is also the government spokesman, spoke on behalf of President Ismaïl Omar Guelleh at the Francophonie summit, held under the theme "Reinvesting in peace for shared prosperity and lasting stability." He told leaders that peace means more than the absence of conflict, and that no lasting prosperity is possible without justice, dialogue and solidarity.

The minister said reinvesting in peace means investing in education, health, employment and young people, and refusing to let inequality and exclusion become the seeds of division and instability. Abdoulkader Houssein Omar urged the Organisation internationale de la Francophonie (OIF) to become a more visible, inclusive and results-focused political, cultural and human space, and said Djibouti stands ready to contribute.

The summit is due to name the next secretary-general of the OIF for the 2027-2030 term. Louise Mushikiwabo, from Rwanda, has held the post since 2019.

Why it mattersDjibouti's call for education, health and jobs spending at the summit sets out the priorities it will press within the OIF's next leadership.
