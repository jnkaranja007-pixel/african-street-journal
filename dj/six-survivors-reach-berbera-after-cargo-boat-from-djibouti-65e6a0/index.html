Six survivors reach Berbera after cargo boat from Djibouti sinks

The Somaliland Ports Authority said two crew members died and medical teams are caring for the six rescued survivors at the port.

Source: Horn Diplomat · 2026-11-18 · 1 min read

Six crew members rescued after the cargo boat Alfatah sank near the Bab al-Mandeb Strait arrived at Somaliland’s Berbera Port on Nov. 17. The boat had sailed from Djibouti, according to Horn Diplomat.

The Somaliland Ports Authority said medical personnel and emergency teams were providing care to the survivors. Two other crew members died when the boat sank.

The arrival brought the six survivors to safety at Berbera, the port authority said. The report did not give further details about the boat or its voyage.
