tropical storm rain risk for Djibouti

A tropical disturbance in the Arabian Sea is influencing weather in the Horn of Africa, with potential rain for Djibouti.

Source: Agence Djiboutienne d'Information · 2026-10-23 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Djibouti could experience rain and thunderstorms on Friday and Saturday. A tropical disturbance formed on October 19 in the southwest Arabian Sea and moved towards Somalia, influencing weather in the Horn of Africa.

Cloud formations associated with the disturbance have reached the Gulf of Aden, opening the possibility of rainfall across Djibouti. The intensity and geographical distribution of the rain remain uncertain, depending on the disturbance's trajectory and local atmospheric conditions.

Meteorological services are monitoring the situation and may revise forecasts as the disturbance evolves. The coming days will identify zones susceptible to being hit.

Why it mattersDjibouti residents face the possibility of rain and thunderstorms on Friday and Saturday.
