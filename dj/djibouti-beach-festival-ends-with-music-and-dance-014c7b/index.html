Djibouti beach festival ends with music and dance

The three-day event featured local and diaspora artists, traditional dances, and family activities.

Source: Agence Djiboutienne d'Information · 2026-11-09 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Djibouti's first Beach Festival concluded after three nights of seaside music, drawing crowds who danced to international hits and local creations. Performers included singer Wafa and her band, Sharmarke and his dancers in traditional costumes, and reunited music figures Saad Coolio and Racine J. The festival also showcased national talents like rapper Waydara Boy, alongside artists Fafy, Yahya, Ikhran, Harbouy, Houssein Hayle, and Abayazid Ali.

The event included artists from the Djiboutian diaspora, such as Danka Boy and Arido, and an Ethiopian guest, Haymanot. Arido paid tribute to Nasser Abdallah by performing his song « Yinti Yinti », a moment described as the festival's most moving. Beyond the music, the festival site offered a family space with children's games, craft stalls, and entrepreneurship exhibits.

Organizers reported the event proceeded without incident, with minor electricity issues on the second night quickly resolved. The festival, blending sport, leisure, tourism, culture, and creative economy, served as a platform for local talent and a gathering point for youth. Observers noted the public's enthusiasm indicated a need for more leisure and creative spaces for young people.

Why it mattersDjiboutian youth and families can enjoy new leisure and creative spaces.
