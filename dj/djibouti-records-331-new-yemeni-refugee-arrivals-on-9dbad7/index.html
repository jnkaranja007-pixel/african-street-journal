Djibouti records 331 new Yemeni refugee arrivals on Wednesday

The new arrivals included 146 children, as women and children made up nearly three-quarters of those reaching the country.

Sources: Agence Djiboutienne d'Information and RTD · 2026-11-20 · 1 min read

Djibouti’s National Office for Assistance to Refugees and Disasters recorded 331 people arriving from Yemen on Wednesday, 18 November, adding to a growing refugee influx. The group included 146 children, 112 women and 73 men.

The office counted 459 arrivals overall that day, including 61 people already registered by 02:00. The arrivals followed 26 people on 16 November and 41 the next day. Women and children accounted for nearly three-quarters of those arriving, according to Agence Djiboutienne d’Information.

The new arrivals add to about 3,840 people who had fled Yemen for Djibouti since Houthi rebels launched an offensive on the Red Sea coast in September. Djibouti warned in late September that arrivals could reach 10,000 and called for more international aid.

Why it mattersRefugee assistance agencies in Djibouti must respond to arrivals that have added 331 people in one day to a caseload of about 3,840 since September.
