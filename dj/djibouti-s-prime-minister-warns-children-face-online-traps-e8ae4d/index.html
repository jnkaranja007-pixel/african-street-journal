Djibouti’s prime minister warns children face online traps

Abdoulkader Kamil Mohamed praised public awareness work by the justice ministry, ANC and security forces in recent weeks.

Source: Agence Djiboutienne d'Information · 2026-11-23 · 1 min read

Djibouti Prime Minister Abdoulkader Kamil Mohamed warned that the internet can become a trap for children, speaking at the People’s Palace during the World Children’s Day celebration. He also urged people not to forget children killed in Gaza.

Kamil Mohamed said the online world should offer learning and personal development, and praised awareness work in recent weeks by the Justice Ministry, the ANC and security forces. He told children at the event that they have rights to learn, be protected, heard and respected.

The celebration, held each year on 20 November, carried the theme “My day, my rights, my future.” The prime minister said parents, educators, public officials and civil society share responsibility for children.

Why it mattersParents, educators, public officials and civil society are being asked to share responsibility for children’s rights and online safety.
