Djiboutian media creator wins IGAD award for local-language migration work

Migane Mohamed Megag received the Kampala trophy for work on migration and human mobility produced in a local language.

Source: Agence Djiboutienne d'Information · 2026-11-23 · 1 min read

Migane Mohamed Megag, a Djiboutian photographer, presenter and graphic designer, received an award in Kampala for work on migration and human mobility in a local language. Winston Agaba David, director general of Uganda’s public broadcaster, presented the trophy.

Megag also works in 3D modelling and co-founded Buuti.tv, where she served as chief camera operator and director of photography. The award was part of the fourth edition of the regional competition, which recognised 20 winners from member states of the Intergovernmental Authority on Development (IGAD), an organisation headquartered in Djibouti.

The competition’s theme covered free movement, decent work, protection and shared prosperity. Its local-language category addressed audiences including nomadic communities, displaced families and residents along migration routes, who often have limited access to media in English, French or Arabic.

Why it mattersMegag’s recognition connects Djibouti’s media work to an IGAD award that honoured 20 winners from member states.
