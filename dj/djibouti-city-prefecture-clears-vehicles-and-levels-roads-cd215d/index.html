Djibouti City prefecture clears vehicles and levels roads in three districts

The campaign also cleaned the EEGA (Escale Ecologique Grand Arrosage) canal and its banks, with police, road-agency and company equipment supporting the work.

Source: Agence Djiboutienne d'Information · 2026-11-22 · 1 min read

Djibouti City's prefecture began a clean-up campaign across three sub-prefectures, removing abandoned vehicles, levelling urban roads and clearing the EEGA canal and its banks. In the 2nd arrondissement, sub-prefect Awaleh Chideh Farah supervised the removal of disused vehicles from main roads, including a road in Dar-el-Hanan; the vehicles were taken to an impound yard.

In Djebel, in the 3rd arrondissement, teams working under sub-prefect Isman Robleh Souldan levelled dirt roads to ease residents' movement and help the city's rubbish-collection trucks enter the neighbourhood. The Djibouti Roads Agency lent a grader and loader. In the 4th arrondissement, sub-prefect Awaleh Fod Mohamed's team cleared the canal and plastic waste with SOS Environnement; Chinese company CCECC supplied machinery. National police provided personnel and equipment for the vehicle operation.

The prefecture said the interventions would be progressively intensified, with priority given to the capital's main roads.

Why it mattersResidents of Djebel gain levelled roads intended to ease movement and let city rubbish trucks enter the neighbourhood.
