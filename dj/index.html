Djibouti seeks solar expansion support from International Solar Alliance

Djibouti's energy minister met with the ISA director general to discuss rural electrification and solar infrastructure development.

Djibouti's Minister of Energy, Dr. Djama Mohamed Hassan, met with the Director General of the International Solar Alliance (ISA), Ashish Khanna, to advance solar energy deployment. The discussions focused on enhancing the partnership between Djibouti and the ISA, with specific attention to training, rural electrification, and the development of solar infrastructure. Djibouti requested ISA support for deploying solar mini-grids in twenty priority areas as part of its national rural electrification strategy.

The meeting also addressed the establishment of a regional STAR-C center in Djibouti, intended to serve as an expertise and training hub. This center aims to standardize photovoltaic equipment, strengthen quality standards, and develop regulatory frameworks to encourage solar investments. The initiative is expected to improve the oversight of photovoltaic installations' compliance and durability within the country.

ISA Director General Ashish Khanna highlighted the Alliance's initiatives for Africa, including a guarantee fund increased from seventy-one million euros to two hundred million euros to stimulate private investment in decentralized energy production. Khanna affirmed the ISA's continued support for realizing solar projects in Djibouti, commending the government's ambitious vision for sustainable development.

Why it mattersDjibouti's government is seeking support for solar mini-grids and a STAR-C center to improve rural electrification and regulate installations.

Djibouti Red Cross disburses funds to storm victims

The Djibouti Red Cross mobilized thirteen million, four hundred forty thousand Djiboutian francs to aid families in Mouloud whose roofs were damaged by high winds.

The Djibouti Red Cross has provided financial assistance totaling thirteen million, four hundred forty thousand Djiboutian francs to households in Mouloud affected by severe winds. Each family received eighty thousand Djiboutian francs to help with roof repairs or rehabilitation. This initiative is part of a multisectoral response to the violent winds in Mouloud, implemented with support from the International Federation of Red Cross and Red Crescent Societies.

The distribution followed field assessments by Red Cross teams and volunteers, who identified homes with destroyed or severely damaged roofs. Vulnerable families, including those headed by women, large families, the elderly, people with disabilities, and those with limited economic resources, received particular attention. The process involved coordination with local authorities and community structures.

The ceremony was attended by various officials, including the Secretary General of the Djibouti Red Cross, Amina Houssein Farah, and the deputy prefect of the Dikhil region, Idriss Houssein Abass. This financial transfer represents the culmination of efforts to support affected residents in rebuilding their homes and restoring their living conditions after the storm damage.

Why it mattersFamilies in Mouloud are directly affected by receiving eighty thousand Djiboutian francs per household to repair storm-damaged roofs.

Health Minister Meets New UNFPA Chief

Djibouti's Health Minister and the new UNFPA head reviewed cooperation on maternal and reproductive health services.

Djibouti's Health Minister held a first meeting with the new head of the United Nations Population Fund (UNFPA) office to discuss future cooperation. The discussions focused on maternal and reproductive health, family planning, and adolescent and youth health. Both sides explored ways to improve access to quality health services for women, girls, and communities.

The Minister praised the UNFPA's commitment to supporting her ministry and reaffirmed the authorities' desire to strengthen the partnership. The goal is to achieve sustainable improvements in the population's health and well-being. The new UNFPA representative pledged her organization's support for the ministry's national priorities and ongoing initiatives.

The UNFPA has been a long-standing partner of the Djiboutian government. This meeting signals a continued commitment to enhancing health outcomes across the nation, particularly for vulnerable groups. The collaboration aims to ensure better health services and support for reproductive and family planning needs.

Why it mattersThe meeting between the Health Minister and the UNFPA chief directly affects the population by aiming to improve access to quality health services, focusing on maternal and reproductive health, family planning, and adolescent health.
