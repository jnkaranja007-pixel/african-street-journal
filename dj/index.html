Djibouti, Tanzania discuss energy and maritime security

Djibouti's Prime Minister met with a Tanzanian delegation to explore renewable energy and address Red Sea geopolitical tensions.

Djibouti's Prime Minister Abdoulkader Kamil Mohamed met with a Tanzanian delegation in Djibouti on October 28, 2026. The delegation included representatives from Tanzania's civil aviation and maritime security authorities, as well as climate change services. Discussions focused on renewable energy, electric mobility, and potential cooperation between the two nations.

The Prime Minister urged increased vigilance regarding geopolitical tensions in the Red Sea and the Strait of Hormuz, noting their impact on maritime security, supply chains, and international trade. He also highlighted Djibouti's ambition to balance energy transition, sustainable development, and natural heritage preservation, referencing the tourist and environmental potential of Lake Assal.

The meeting also covered South-South cooperation based on expertise sharing and African solidarity for the continent's energy sovereignty. The Tanzanian delegation's program included meetings with aeronautical and port authorities, a ship visit, and presentations on carbon governance and registration.

Why it mattersDjibouti's Prime Minister is addressing geopolitical tensions affecting maritime security and supply chains, and exploring renewable energy cooperation.

Balbala town hall extension opens

Moroccan diplomat and Djiboutian ministers attend inauguration of expanded communal headquarters.

The communal headquarters in Balbala, Djibouti, has been extended and improved. The inauguration ceremony was attended by the mayor of Balbala, Mohamed Hassan Saïd, and the chargé d'affaires of the Moroccan embassy, Mohamed Mdarhi. Djiboutian Minister of Economy and Finance, Ilyas Moussa Dawaleh, and Minister of Higher Education, Nabil Mohamed Ahmed, were also present.

The project stems from a 2024 memorandum of understanding between Balbala and Rabat, Morocco, for the extension, beautification, and public space development. According to the mayor's office, the new facilities will enhance staff working conditions, improve user services, and provide a better environment for neighbourhood councils and associations.

Mayor Saïd Daoud Mohamed thanked Morocco's King Mohammed VI and the Moroccan government for the development. Minister Dawaleh linked the project to President Ismaïl Omar Guelleh's decentralisation policy, stating that cooperation should serve local development and the population.

Why it mattersDjiboutian residents of Balbala will benefit from improved public services and facilities at their communal headquarters.

Djibouti arrests four for online blackmail and child abuse material

Authorities seized digital content and arrested suspects facing up to 10 years in prison.

Djibouti's national cybersecurity agency, in collaboration with the Gendarmerie, arrested four individuals suspected of online crimes. The arrests followed investigations into digital offenses, including three cases of alleged online blackmail and one case involving the detention and distribution of child pornography. Digital content was seized during the operations.

The suspects have been transferred to the Public Prosecutor following the completion of investigations. The charges they face carry potential sentences ranging from six to 10 years of criminal imprisonment. This action is part of a broader awareness campaign against online abuses launched by the cybersecurity agency.

The announcement comes less than a month after a similar arrest on October 7 for online child sexual abuse. Authorities have recently increased calls for public vigilance against offenses committed on social networks.

Djibouti president congratulates Turkish counterpart

President Guelleh expressed a wish for further development and prosperity for the Turkish people.

Djibouti's President Guelleh sent a telegram to Turkish President Erdogan on Turkey's national day. He wished the Turkish people more development, prosperity, and fulfillment. The message noted that Djibouti and Turkey share fruitful ties built on history and shared culture.

President Guelleh also expressed satisfaction with the rapid development in economic and commercial exchanges between the two nations. He reiterated Djibouti's determination to strengthen the relations of friendship and cooperation between the two countries and their peoples.

The exchange highlights ongoing diplomatic ties between Djibouti and Turkey, emphasizing shared historical and cultural connections.

Why it mattersDjibouti's president congratulated Turkey's president on their national day, reinforcing diplomatic relations.
