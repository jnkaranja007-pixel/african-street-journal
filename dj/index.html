RPP Leaders Convene in Ali Sabieh for Strategic Political Retreat

The ruling party is holding a three-day meeting to discuss national, regional, and global challenges following the recent presidential election.

The RPP party has opened a political retreat in Ali Sabieh, focusing on future challenges and national, regional, and global issues. Prime Minister Abdoulkader Kamil Mohamed emphasized the need to listen to the population's expectations and to "lucidly look at the challenges ahead." The meeting, themed "What party in the face of new stakes and national, regional, and global challenges?", aims to develop orientations for the party to better meet public demands.

This gathering follows the April 10 presidential election, which saw the re-election of President Ismaïl Omar Guelleh. The Prime Minister acknowledged the welcome from local authorities and the population, particularly the youth, and thanked the prefecture, regional council, security forces, and organizing teams. He stressed that a great party serves its people and nation, urging RPP officials to use the three days for reflection and preparation.

The RPP's deliberations are intended to address the evolving regional and international environment and their implications for Djibouti, a strategically positioned country at the entrance to the Red Sea. The party seeks to adapt its approach to better serve the nation's interests and the aspirations of its citizens in a changing global landscape.

Why it mattersThe RPP party is holding a strategic retreat to discuss future challenges and adapt its approach to better meet the population's expectations.

Djibouti Adopts AI Strategy Focused on Skills and Governance

The nation's artificial intelligence roadmap prioritizes cybersecurity, training, research, and innovation to prepare its workforce for emerging technologies.

Djibouti has launched a national strategy to guide the development of artificial intelligence, emphasizing governance, cybersecurity, training, research, innovation, and inclusion. The plan follows discussions on the country's readiness for AI, with a UNESCO RAM report presented during the second day of deliberations. Experts from UNESCO and ESCWA, alongside local academics, contributed to shaping the strategy.

Key areas of focus include data governance, digital security, institutional frameworks, and national priorities. Participants highlighted that successful AI transformation hinges on developing appropriate skills, rather than solely on technology. The strategy aims to equip both public and private sectors with necessary expertise and foster a new generation of researchers and entrepreneurs in emerging technologies.

Discussions also covered essential elements for implementation, such as evolving educational programs, teacher training, research development, computing infrastructure, and university partnerships. Five panels were dedicated to elaborating on these strategic directions, underscoring a commitment to practical application and capacity building.

Why it mattersThe country's focus on AI skills development and governance affects businesses and public sector entities by preparing them for technological advancements.

Djibouti Social Protection Model Inspires Comorian Delegation

Comorian officials are examining Djibouti's social welfare system, including its national social security fund and the Al Shifa hospital.

A delegation from the Comoros is visiting Djibouti to study its social protection and health systems, seeking opportunities for cooperation. The Comorians are particularly interested in the operational framework of Djibouti's National Social Security Fund (CNSS), which oversees social welfare and the recently opened Al Shifa hospital.

Discussions focused on the governance of social protection, coverage, healthcare facility management, and human resources. The delegation also inquired about the management and operational strategies of the Al Shifa hospital, which began operations in late June under the CNSS. Djibouti's Minister Yonis Ali Guedi highlighted recent reforms in social protection and emphasized the value of South-South cooperation.

Minister Guedi stated that sharing experiences could strengthen cooperation and lead to solutions tailored to each nation's circumstances. He assured the Comorian delegation that Djibouti's Ministry of Labor and the CNSS are open to continued dialogue and exploring potential areas for collaboration.

Why it mattersComorian officials are seeking to replicate Djibouti's social protection and healthcare management systems to improve services for their citizens.
