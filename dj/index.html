Climate · 2026-10-24

Djibouti expects moderate rain on Saturday

Temperatures will remain mild across the country, with Dikhil seeing the highest highs.

Djibouti will experience moderate rain and cloudy skies on Saturday, according to the National Meteorological Agency (ANM). The clouds are expected to gradually clear throughout the day, leaving partially cloudy conditions across the entire territory.

Temperatures in Djibouti-ville will range between 27°C and 30°C, with Obock experiencing the same range. Tadjourah will see temperatures between 27°C and 32°C.

Dikhil is forecast to have the highest temperatures, from 25°C to 34°C. Ali-Sabieh will experience temperatures from 23°C to 29°C, and Arta from 23°C to 28°C.

Djiboutian swimmer wins silver at Zone 3 championships

Houmed Houssein Barkat secured a second-place finish in the 100m backstroke event.

Djiboutian swimmer Houmed Houssein Barkat won a silver medal in the 100m backstroke at the Africa Aquatics Zone 3 Championships. He finished second behind a top East African swimmer in a competitive race.

The Secretary of State for Sports, Fatouma Ali Abdallah, congratulated Barkat and the Djiboutian Swimming Federation. She encouraged them to continue their efforts for the prestige of Djiboutian swimming.

Barkat's second-place finish confirms his progress in regional sports.

Politics · 2026-10-24

Djibouti fund backs 15 community projects

The Community Development Fund has now supported 52 local initiatives since its launch.

Djibouti's Agency for Social Development (ADDS) has signed agreements with 15 associations to fund local development projects. The Community Development Fund (FDC) allocated 20 million Djiboutian francs (FDJ) for these 15 sub-projects, each proposed by an association from the program's intervention zones. The initiative aims to support local efforts and increase resident involvement in neighborhood development.

The signing ceremony was attended by parliamentarians, the mayor of Balbala commune, local officials, and directors from ADDS and the French Development Agency (AFD). Representatives from the beneficiary associations were also present. Speakers praised the quality of the selected projects and urged the recipients to manage the funds responsibly and execute their plans effectively.

This marks the third call for projects by the FDC, bringing the total number of funded initiatives to 52 since its inception. The cumulative amount disbursed for these projects now stands at 57.97 million FDJ.

Why it matters15 local associations in Djibouti will receive funding for community development projects.
