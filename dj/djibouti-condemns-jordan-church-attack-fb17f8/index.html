Djibouti condemns Jordan church attack

Djibouti expresses solidarity with Jordan and rejects all forms of violence and extremism.

Source: Agence Djiboutienne d'Information · 2026-10-26 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Djibouti has strongly condemned an attack on a church in Jordan's Karak governorate. The Republic of Djibouti stated its categorical rejection of all violence, terrorism, and extremism, and any attacks on civilians and places of worship.

The Djiboutian government expressed its full solidarity with the Hashemite Kingdom of Jordan during this difficult time. Djibouti extended its sincere condolences to the families of the victims and to the Jordanian government and people, wishing a speedy recovery to the injured.

Djibouti's condemnation reiterates its stance against acts that harm innocent people and sacred sites.

Why it mattersDjibouti's government has condemned an attack in Jordan, expressing solidarity with the affected nation.
