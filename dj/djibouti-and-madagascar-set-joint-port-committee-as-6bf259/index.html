Djibouti and Madagascar set joint port committee as condition for maritime cooperation

President Ismaïl Omar Guelleh proposed exchanges on port management, training and digital logistics, as well as transport and cargo-handling infrastructure.

Sources: Agence Djiboutienne d'Information and RTD · 2026-11-25 · 1 min read

Djibouti and Madagascar agreed to establish a joint port committee as a condition for pursuing maritime cooperation, Djibouti's President Ismaïl Omar Guelleh said during a meeting with Madagascar's President Michaël Randrianirina. Guelleh proposed exchanges on port management, training, digital logistics operations and transport and cargo-handling infrastructure.

Guelleh cited existing maritime ties, including the Djibouti Code of Conduct, a regional agreement against piracy adopted in 2009 under the International Maritime Organization, and a regional maritime information-fusion centre based in Madagascar that monitors traffic in the western Indian Ocean. Madagascar's delegation included Hertiana Randriamâhoaka, director general of Toamasina port, the island's main port.

Guelleh also proposed that the two countries coordinate their participation in international carbon markets. Randrianirina said agreements signed during his visit would give the trip concrete content.

Why it mattersPort operators and logistics firms in both countries will see any cooperation depend on the joint committee being set up first.
