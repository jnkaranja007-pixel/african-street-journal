Darar and Demil win Air Djibouti 10 km races

Aden Waberi Darar finished the men's race in 27 min 36 sec, while Kaira Mohamed Demil won the women's event in 33 min 40 sec.

Source: Agence Djiboutienne d'Information · 2026-11-28 · 1 min read

Aden Waberi Darar of Garde-Côtes and Kaira Mohamed Demil of Gendarmerie Rapide, known as GR, won the men's and women's Air Djibouti 10 km races in Djibouti. Darar finished in 27 min 36 sec; Demil crossed in 33 min 40 sec.

Moutar Isman Samri, Darar's Garde-Côtes teammate, was second in 28 min 29 sec, and Ayed Farah Aye took third in 28 min 40 sec. Neima Youssouf of the Forces Armées Djiboutiennes club, known as FAD, finished second among the women in 34 min 34 sec, 54 seconds behind Demil. Deka Ahmed Mahamoud, also of FAD, was third in 34 min 36 sec.

The race started at 16h00 at Place Menelik. Air Djibouti and the Djiboutian Athletics Federation organised the competition, which also included a 2 km race for men with special needs, won by Abdoulrazack Bouh Roble in 6 min 02 sec.

Why it mattersGarde-Côtes secured the first two places in the men's race, while GR and FAD athletes took the top three women's places.
