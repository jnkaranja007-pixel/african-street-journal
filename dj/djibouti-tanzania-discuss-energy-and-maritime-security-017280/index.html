Djibouti, Tanzania discuss energy and maritime security

Djibouti's Prime Minister met with a Tanzanian delegation to explore renewable energy and address Red Sea geopolitical tensions.

Source: Agence Djiboutienne d'Information · 2026-10-29 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Djibouti's Prime Minister Abdoulkader Kamil Mohamed met with a Tanzanian delegation in Djibouti on October 28, 2026. The delegation included representatives from Tanzania's civil aviation and maritime security authorities, as well as climate change services. Discussions focused on renewable energy, electric mobility, and potential cooperation between the two nations.

The Prime Minister urged increased vigilance regarding geopolitical tensions in the Red Sea and the Strait of Hormuz, noting their impact on maritime security, supply chains, and international trade. He also highlighted Djibouti's ambition to balance energy transition, sustainable development, and natural heritage preservation, referencing the tourist and environmental potential of Lake Assal.

The meeting also covered South-South cooperation based on expertise sharing and African solidarity for the continent's energy sovereignty. The Tanzanian delegation's program included meetings with aeronautical and port authorities, a ship visit, and presentations on carbon governance and registration.

Why it mattersDjibouti's Prime Minister is addressing geopolitical tensions affecting maritime security and supply chains, and exploring renewable energy cooperation.
