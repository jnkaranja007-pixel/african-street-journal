Djibouti funds 15 community development projects

The Community Development Fund has now supported 52 local initiatives since its launch.

Source: Agence Djiboutienne d'Information · 2026-10-23 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Djibouti's Agency for Social Development (ADDS) signed agreements today with 15 associations to fund local development projects. The initiative, backed by 20 million Djiboutian francs (FDJ), aims to support local initiatives and boost resident participation in neighborhood improvements.

The ceremony was attended by parliamentarians, local officials, and representatives from the French Development Agency (AFD). Speakers praised the selected projects and urged careful fund management and effective execution by the winning associations.

This marks the third call for projects, bringing the total number of funded initiatives by the Community Development Fund to 52, with a cumulative investment of 57.97 million FDJ since its inception.

Why it matters15 local associations in Djibouti will receive funding for development projects.
