Djibouti and Russia to deepen ties

Djibouti's Prime Minister and the Russian Ambassador met to strengthen bilateral cooperation after the Africa-Russia Summit.

Source: Agence Djiboutienne d'Information · 2026-11-13 · 1 min read

Djibouti's Prime Minister met with the Russian Ambassador on November 11 to discuss strengthening relations between the two countries following the third Africa-Russia Summit.

The meeting focused on Djibouti's role as a logistical hub in a region facing multiple crises, with the Prime Minister highlighting the nation's efforts to maintain regional connectivity and host refugees. He stressed the need for international solidarity to address these challenges.

The Russian Ambassador, drawing on extensive diplomatic experience in Africa, expressed a commitment to deepening dialogue and revitalizing bilateral cooperation for the benefit of both populations.
