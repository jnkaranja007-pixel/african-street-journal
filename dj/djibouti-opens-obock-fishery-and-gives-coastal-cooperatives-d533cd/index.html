Djibouti opens Obock fishery and gives coastal cooperatives gear

Fishing groups in Godoria and Khor Angar received nets and an engine as residents raised water access and infrastructure needs with the minister.

Source: Agence Djiboutienne d'Information · 2026-11-23 · 1 min read

Djibouti inaugurated the Obock fishery and handed nets and an engine to fishing cooperatives in Godoria and Khor Angar, two coastal communities in the region. Fisheries Minister Radwan Abdillahi Bahdon visited the fishery as part of a programme in Obock, where many households depend on the sea.

The government also donated fish to the Markazi refugee camp through the National Office for Assistance to Refugees and Disaster Victims. Opened in 2015, the camp hosts mainly Yemenis who fled war across the Bab-el-Mandeb strait. At a meeting with community representatives, drinking water and a shortage of water infrastructure dominated discussion.

Local leaders also raised agriculture, livestock and economic development. Bahdon acknowledged the need for coordinated local development that takes account of available natural resources and residents’ needs.

Why it mattersResidents raised drinking-water access and missing water infrastructure directly with the minister, alongside demands on agriculture and livestock.
