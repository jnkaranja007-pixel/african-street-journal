Djibouti prepares World Bank business climate assessment

Officials and businesses will review indicators for the 2027 B-READY evaluation.

Source: Agence Djiboutienne d'Information · 2026-11-02 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Djibouti is preparing for the World Bank's B-READY assessment, a process aimed at improving the business climate and national attractiveness. The evaluation examines regulatory frameworks, public services for businesses, and rule enforcement across 10 areas including company creation, finance, employment, and international trade. Ali Houmed Aboubaker, secretary-general of MIDDP, presided over a meeting to launch this preparation.

Various administrations, banks, essential service providers, professional organizations, and companies are participating in this initiative. Their role involves understanding the B-READY methodology, analyzing specific indicators and questionnaires, and appointing focal points responsible for data collection.

Following data collection, the process will move to processing the 2027 edition questionnaires. The next steps include identifying priority reforms and developing a roadmap to implement them.

Why it mattersDjibouti's business climate and attractiveness are being assessed through the World Bank's B-READY evaluation, involving government and private sector participants.
