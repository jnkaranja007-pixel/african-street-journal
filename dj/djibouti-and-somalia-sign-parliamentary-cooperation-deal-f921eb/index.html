Djibouti and Somalia sign parliamentary cooperation deal

Djibouti's Dileita Mohamed Dileita and Somalia's Abdoulkadir Mohamed Nour agreed to share expertise and build institutional partnerships.

Source: Agence Djiboutienne d'Information · 2026-11-03 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Djibouti and Somalia signed a memorandum of understanding on February 11, 2026, to strengthen parliamentary cooperation. The agreement aims to deepen relations through exchanges of experience and expertise, and the establishment of institutional partnerships between the two legislative assemblies.

Dileita Mohamed Dileita, president of the Djiboutian assembly, praised the discussions with the Somali delegation led by his counterpart. He highlighted positive perspectives for both nations emerging from the talks. The Somali president of the People's Assembly expressed Mogadishu's commitment to reinforcing parliamentary ties with Djibouti.

"We intend to strengthen the links between the two nations through a process of greater exchanges of expertise and partnerships between the two legislative assemblies," stated Abdoulkadir Mohamed Nour. He also thanked Djibouti for the welcome and hospitality extended to his delegation.

Why it mattersDjibouti's parliament and Somalia's parliament will share expertise and build partnerships, deepening relations between the two countries.
