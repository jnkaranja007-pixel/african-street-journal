Shipping lines' route changes lift Djibouti's port sector

Carriers facing costly detours are beginning to return to the Suez route, a shift Jeune Afrique says favours Djibouti's container terminal at Doraleh.

Source: Jeune Afrique · 2026-11-21 · 1 min read

Shipping companies are reorganising their routes because of insecurity in the Persian Gulf, and Jeune Afrique reports that the shift is benefiting Djibouti. Faced with the added cost of detours, some carriers are also beginning to return to the Suez route.

Djibouti's Doraleh container terminal sits in a port ranked third in Africa for container performance, behind Tanger in Morocco and Port Said in Egypt, according to a World Bank and S&P Global Market Intelligence index published in June 2026.

The account gives no figures for traffic, revenue or the size of the gain, so the scale of the benefit is not yet measured.

Why it mattersCarriers paying for longer detours around the Gulf are choosing Djibouti's terminals as they weigh a return to Suez.
