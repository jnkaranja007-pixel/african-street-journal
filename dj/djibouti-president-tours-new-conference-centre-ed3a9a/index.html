Djibouti president tours new conference centre

President Guelleh inspected the modern facility equipped with new technologies and a main hall for large meetings.

Source: Agence Djiboutienne d'Information · 2026-11-11 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

President Guelleh visited Djibouti's new international conference centre on November 10, 2026, accompanied by his government.

The modern infrastructure includes a main hall for large gatherings, a press gallery, and four rooms for heads of state and government meetings. It also has five rooms for smaller meetings, spacious offices, and equipped stands for translators.

The centre is connected to an autonomous generator and features a kitchen offering a variety of meals.

Why it mattersThe new international conference centre will host state and government meetings, impacting diplomatic engagements.
