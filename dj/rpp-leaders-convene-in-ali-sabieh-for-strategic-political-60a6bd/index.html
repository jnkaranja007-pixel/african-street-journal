RPP Leaders Convene in Ali Sabieh for Strategic Political Retreat

The ruling party is holding a three-day meeting to discuss national, regional, and global challenges following the recent presidential election.

Source: Agence Djiboutienne d'Information · 2026-09-06 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X

The RPP party has opened a political retreat in Ali Sabieh, focusing on future challenges and national, regional, and global issues. Prime Minister Abdoulkader Kamil Mohamed emphasized the need to listen to the population's expectations and to "lucidly look at the challenges ahead." The meeting, themed "What party in the face of new stakes and national, regional, and global challenges?", aims to develop orientations for the party to better meet public demands.

This gathering follows the April 10 presidential election, which saw the re-election of President Ismaïl Omar Guelleh. The Prime Minister acknowledged the welcome from local authorities and the population, particularly the youth, and thanked the prefecture, regional council, security forces, and organizing teams. He stressed that a great party serves its people and nation, urging RPP officials to use the three days for reflection and preparation.

The RPP's deliberations are intended to address the evolving regional and international environment and their implications for Djibouti, a strategically positioned country at the entrance to the Red Sea. The party seeks to adapt its approach to better serve the nation's interests and the aspirations of its citizens in a changing global landscape.

Why it mattersThe RPP party is holding a strategic retreat to discuss future challenges and adapt its approach to better meet the population's expectations.
