Djibouti advocates for inclusive African digital future

Djibouti called for a continental digital ecosystem that is inclusive, sovereign, and innovative at a Libreville meeting.

Source: Agence Djiboutienne d'Information · 2026-11-01 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Djibouti defended an "inclusive, sovereign, innovative" African digital future at a ministerial meeting in Libreville, Gabon. The gathering, which opened on Thursday, brought African states together to discuss digital transformation, communication infrastructure, and the governance of emerging technologies.

The meeting, held at the Palais des Congrès, aimed to strengthen cooperation among member states as digital transformation becomes a lever for growth and modernization. Participants reviewed the implementation of decisions from the November 2023 session in Addis Ababa and defined next steps, including electing a new bureau for the STC-CICT.

The STC-CICT, an AU technical body, harmonizes the continent's digital policies and guides decisions for the pan-African organization. The Libreville works concluded on Friday, with Djibouti reaffirming its commitment to enhancing African cooperation and building a digital ecosystem focused on the future.

Why it mattersDjibouti's participation in the Libreville meeting influences its position on continental digital policy and cooperation.
