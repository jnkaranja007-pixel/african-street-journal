Djibouti recommends four more coaching days for 24 farm cooperatives

A review of the pilot backed by the International Finance Corporation proposes extra coaching, stronger trainer preparation and new teaching materials.

Source: Agence Djiboutienne d'Information · 2026-11-18 · 1 min read

Djibouti’s agriculture value-chain project unit hosted a review of the Agricultural Leadership Programme, backed by the International Finance Corporation, the World Bank’s private-sector arm. The programme’s pilot covered 24 cooperatives across the country.

The review mission recommended extending the pilot, with four additional days of coaching for each cooperative, more training for trainers and new learning materials. Abdoulkader Issé, the programme’s focal point at the project unit, said the programme was intended to strengthen cooperatives and their economic weight.

Participants discussed which institution would lead the programme and how responsibilities and coordination would be organised. A second phase could start in early March 2027; they also raised a national study of cooperatives and the programme’s links to legal reforms underway.

Why it mattersThe 24 cooperatives covered by the pilot are slated for four more coaching days each under the mission’s recommendation.
