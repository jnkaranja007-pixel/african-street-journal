Djibouti shows Madagascar president Damerjog port and 30 km² industrial zone plan

Port officials presented the terminal’s capacity and expansion plans; its oil quay can receive vessels of up to 120,000 tonnes deadweight.

Sources: Agence Djiboutienne d'Information and RTD · 2026-11-26 · 1 min read

Madagascar’s President Michaël Randrianirina visited Djibouti’s Damerjog port, accompanied by Djibouti’s infrastructure minister, Saïd Nouh Hassan. Aboubaker Omar Hadi, president of Djibouti’s ports and free zones authority, presented the terminal’s facilities, operations, capacity and expansion plans.

Damerjog handles diesel, kerosene, petrol, heavy fuel oil, liquefied natural gas, liquefied petroleum gas and edible oil. Its oil quay has 18 metres of water depth at low tide and can accommodate ships of up to 120,000 tonnes deadweight, according to the port authority’s presentation.

Officials also presented a proposed 30 km² industrial zone around the port, with planned energy, petrochemical, port and water-treatment facilities, as well as cement and construction-material factories. The delegation went on to the quay to view loading installations.

Why it mattersPort authorities must advance a proposed 30 km² industrial zone planned to include energy, petrochemical, water-treatment and manufacturing facilities.
