Djibouti proposes permanent secretariat for African official gazettes

A forum is due to consider a cooperation charter, shared publication standards and a database for laws issued across the continent.

Source: Agence Djiboutienne d'Information · 2026-11-22 · 1 min read

Djibouti is seeking a permanent secretariat to coordinate cooperation among African official gazettes, following the first forum in 2025. The government-organised forum is being held under the patronage of President Ismail Omar Guelleh.

Organisers say participants are expected to sign a cooperation charter and adopt a “Declaration of Djibouti”. The proposed secretariat would track commitments and keep dialogue going between member states. The forum is also due to advance shared publication standards and links between national platforms, which currently do not communicate with one another.

The discussions include artificial intelligence, cybersecurity and electronic archiving. Djibouti says its e-JO platform has digitised all its published legal texts dating to 1901 and offers search and alerts for new texts; artificial-intelligence tools are due to be added.

Why it mattersMember states must decide whether to adopt the charter and declaration, with a proposed secretariat tasked with tracking their commitments.
