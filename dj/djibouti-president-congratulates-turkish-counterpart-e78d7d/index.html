Djibouti president congratulates Turkish counterpart

President Guelleh expressed a wish for further development and prosperity for the Turkish people.

Source: Agence Djiboutienne d'Information · 2026-10-29 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Djibouti's President Guelleh sent a telegram to Turkish President Erdogan on Turkey's national day. He wished the Turkish people more development, prosperity, and fulfillment. The message noted that Djibouti and Turkey share fruitful ties built on history and shared culture.

President Guelleh also expressed satisfaction with the rapid development in economic and commercial exchanges between the two nations. He reiterated Djibouti's determination to strengthen the relations of friendship and cooperation between the two countries and their peoples.

The exchange highlights ongoing diplomatic ties between Djibouti and Turkey, emphasizing shared historical and cultural connections.

Why it mattersDjibouti's president congratulated Turkey's president on their national day, reinforcing diplomatic relations.
