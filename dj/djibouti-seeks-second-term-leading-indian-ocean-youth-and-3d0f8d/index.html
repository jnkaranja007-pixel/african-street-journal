Djibouti seeks second term leading Indian Ocean youth and sports commission

The country, currently holding the presidency, declared its candidacy as ministers met in Djibouti City to assess programmes and regional cooperation.

Source: Agence Djiboutienne d'Information · 2026-11-24 · 1 min read

Djibouti declared its candidacy for a second term as president of the Indian Ocean Commission for Youth and Sports (CJSOI) during a ministerial meeting in the capital. The presidency is to be assigned at the end of the talks.

The meeting at the Sheraton hotel brought together member-country delegations, youth and sports officials and sports federation representatives. Djibouti's Youth and Culture Minister Fardouza Moussa Egueh, the commission's current president, reviewed her term and made recommendations on cooperation among member states.

Delegates also assessed programmes from the past term and discussed future cooperation. Ministers from Comoros, Mauritius and Seychelles attended, while France was represented through Mayotte and Réunion by its ambassador to Djibouti.

Why it mattersDjibouti's bid now depends on the member delegations' decision on the presidency at the close of their ministerial work.
