Djibouti police academy trains 17 African risk prevention officers

The ESIPC concluded a risk prevention course for trainees from nine African nations at its police academy.

Source: Agence Djiboutienne d'Information · 2026-11-02 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

The ESIPC concluded a risk prevention training on October 29, graduating 17 trainees from nine African countries including Djibouti. The course, held at the "Idriss Farah Abaneh" Police Academy, focused on enhancing skills in analyzing, anticipating, and managing threats to people and property. French lieutenants Colonel Jules Mazart and Olivier Ménard led the instruction.

The graduates came from Benin, Cameroon, Ivory Coast, Djibouti, Gabon, Guinea, Madagascar, Mauritania, and Senegal. The ceremony was attended by Djibouti's National Director of Civil Protection, Abdoulaker Moussa Abayazid, Police Commander Mahdi, and ESIPC Director Commander Hassan Ibrahim Ismail. This session aimed to improve capabilities and share best practices among African civil protection institutions.

The ESIPC stated the training is part of a skills development and cooperation initiative between African civil protection services. The school intends to strengthen international partnerships and improve operational capacities through such programs. The certificate awarding marked the culmination of the course.
