Rumbek Central paramount chief Madol Mathok dies at 87

The influential leader, known for supporting the SPLA, died in Juba after a battle with illness.

Sources: Radio Tamazuj and Sudans Post · 2026-10-23 · 1 min read

Prominent Rumbek Central Paramount Chief Madol Mathok Agolder, 87, died in Juba on Wednesday after battling illness. Born in 1939, Mathok succeeded his brother as chief in 1976 and was known for his contributions to the SPLM/A liberation struggle.

Rumbek Central County Commissioner Manyiel Riak Manyiel confirmed the chief's death, stating it caused shock across Lakes State. Emmanuel Mading Manon, chairperson of the Nyang Community, said Mathok died from a heart attack and was a "dictionary and a Bible" for the community.

Arrangements are underway to transport the body to Rumbek for burial, with a grave being prepared near Bros Hotel. Mathok was a strong supporter of the liberation struggle, mobilizing youth and contributing food to the SPLA.
