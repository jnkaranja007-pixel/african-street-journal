South Sudan's Red Cross partner weighs in on missing persons bill

Talks in Juba covered a proposed law that would govern how the country tracks people who have vanished.

Source: Sudans Post · 2026-11-21 · 1 min read

Talks on South Sudan's proposed Missing Persons Bill took place in Juba between the government and the International Committee of the Red Cross (ICRC), Sudans Post reported on Friday. More than 6,200 missing-person cases remain active in the country.

Humanitarian Affairs Minister Peter Mayen Majongdit held the meeting with Ramin Hashempour, who heads the ICRC's programmes and prevention work and is acting head of its delegation in the country.

No timetable for the bill or further details of the meeting were reported.

Why it mattersFamilies still waiting on more than 6,200 open cases now depend on a bill that the government and the ICRC are still shaping.
