Emmanuel Christian University graduates 131 in Yei

The graduates included 60 from Business Administration, 47 from Education and 24 from Theology at the university’s 18th ceremony.

Source: Radio Tamazuj · 2026-11-18 · 1 min read

Emmanuel Christian University in Yei Town, Central Equatoria State, graduated 131 students last Friday, awarding qualifications across three faculties at its 18th graduation ceremony.

Vice Chancellor Dr. Daniel Ohide said the university had grown from three faculties to five and offered about 13 programmes. It has added Agriculture and Social Work, including Public Administration, and plans to establish an Information Technology faculty in 2027 focused on Artificial Intelligence and digital skills.

Yei River County caretaker commissioner Emmanuel Taban Seme said the county government was working to secure more scholarships and improve access to higher education. Central Equatoria education minister Lupai Satimon cited inadequate funding and teacher shortages at primary, secondary and technical schools across the state.

Why it mattersStudents seeking higher education in Yei have new Agriculture and Social Work faculties, while the county government is working to secure more scholarships.
