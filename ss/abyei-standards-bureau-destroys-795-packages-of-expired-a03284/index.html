Abyei standards bureau destroys 795 packages of expired goods

The seized stock from shops at Mulmul Market included 718 flour bags, soft drinks, alcohol and jangaro.

Sources: Eye Radio and Sudans Post · 2026-11-28 · 1 min read

South Sudan’s Bureau of Standards in the Abyei Special Administrative Area destroyed 795 packages of expired goods seized from shops in Mulmul Market, Abyei Municipality. The bureau acted over concerns about public health and safety.

Among the seized stock, officials counted 718 flour bags, 63 cartons of soft drinks, 10 cartons of alcohol and four bags of jangaro. Caretaker Mayor Bakor Mayar said the goods had expired and were poorly stored, leaving them damaged and infested with insects that could put consumers at risk.

Mayar urged residents to check expiry dates and traders to inspect stock regularly and report expired products to authorities. Caretaker Information Minister Yohona Akol said the government would remove expired and unsafe goods from local markets.

Why it mattersMulmul Market traders are being urged to inspect their stocks regularly and report expired goods to local authorities.
