Azande king urges South Sudanese to register for elections

Atoroba Peni Rikito Gbudue registered in Yambio and called on citizens to choose leaders through elections rather than armed conflict.

Sources: Radio Tamazuj, Sudans Post and Eye Radio · 2026-11-24 · 1 min read

The King of the Azande Kingdom, Atoroba Peni Rikito Gbudue, urged South Sudanese citizens to register to vote after signing up at Nabima Registration Centre in Yambio, Western Equatoria State, on Monday. He called on eligible people from all communities to take part in the ongoing exercise and choose leaders through elections rather than armed conflict.

Gbudue said he registered to set an example for traditional and church leaders and community members, and said he had received calls from Juba urging traditional leaders to encourage their communities to register. John Gemenze, head of the Yambio Town Registration Centre, said more than 2,500 people had registered there between November 14 and 22.

The caretaker governor of Western Equatoria, Badagbu Daniel Rimbasa, also registered on Tuesday and pledged government support for security and the completion of registration, including in Nagero County, where logistical and security problems have affected the exercise. The state's High-Level Election Commission chairperson, Simon Bakama, said registration was progressing despite problems with transport, electricity and insecurity.

Why it mattersVoters in Western Equatoria must register before the exercise closes on November 28, and Nagero County faces logistical and security obstacles.
