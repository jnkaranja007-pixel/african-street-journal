South Sudan opens 14 oil blocks to investors

Caretaker Petroleum Minister Awow Daniel Chuang made the offer in a national address, as the government seeks to lift crude production above 200,000 barrels a day.

Source: Sudans Post · 2026-11-20 · 1 min read

Investors can now take up 14 oil exploration blocks in South Sudan, as the government looks to push crude production above 200,000 barrels per day within two years. The offer was announced by Caretaker Petroleum Minister Awow Daniel Chuang in a national address on Wednesday.

Chuang made the announcement ahead of the South Sudan Oil and Power 2026 event. The address concerned the exploration blocks themselves, and did not name any company that has taken them up.

The blocks are on offer, but the government has not said any have been awarded, and no production increase has yet been reported.

Why it mattersOil firms weighing the 14 blocks now face a two-year government target to lift output above 200,000 barrels a day.
