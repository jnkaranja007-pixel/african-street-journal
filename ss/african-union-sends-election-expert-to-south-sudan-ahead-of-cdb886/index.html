African Union sends election expert to South Sudan ahead of February 2027 polls

Juba received the expert on Wednesday, who will work with the National Elections Commission while voter registration continues nationwide.

Sources: Sudans Post and Radio Tamazuj · 2026-11-20 · 1 min read

Juba received an African Union election expert on Wednesday, to help the National Elections Commission prepare for polls in February 2027. The expert will work from the union's offices in the capital.

Registration is under way across the country, and the expert will be working alongside the commission as it signs up eligible voters.

In Yei Town, former Central Equatoria State governor Emmanuel Adil Anthony registered on Wednesday at Freedom Square. He is contesting the state chairperson post in the SPLM primary, and the winner will carry the party's flag in the general elections.

Why it mattersVoters registering now, and the commission's work with the expert, will determine who is eligible to take part in the February 2027 polls.
