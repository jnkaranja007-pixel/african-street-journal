South Sudan government to boost flood response in Unity State

The government will strengthen coordination with partners to address displacement and flood risks, focusing on preparedness and recovery.

Source: Eye Radio · 2026-10-23 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

The South Sudan government is enhancing collaboration with humanitarian and development partners to tackle the needs of displaced people and communities affected by floods in Unity State. The focus will be on preparedness, recovering livelihoods, and finding lasting solutions.

Speaking at a meeting with United Nations agencies on Thursday in Bentiu, Kuyok Abol Kuyok, caretaker Minister of General Education and Instruction, stated the government's commitment to protecting those impacted by displacement and environmental shocks. He highlighted that the Ministry of Humanitarian Affairs and Disaster Management is working with institutions and partners to coordinate emergency aid, including food, shelter, and healthcare, depending on available resources.

The government acknowledges challenges such as recurrent flooding, limited finances, poor infrastructure, and logistical problems that hinder humanitarian efforts. Minister Kuyok called for increased investment in drainage systems, raised roads, and flood-protection structures, alongside support for agriculture and small businesses to help displaced families rebuild their lives and reduce reliance on aid.

Why it mattersThe government is strengthening its response to displacement and flood risks in Unity State, affecting communities and their livelihoods.
