Kon to challenge South Sudan election timetable in court on Monday

The presidential aspirant wants judges to overturn a delay that moved the vote from Dec. 22, 2026, to Feb. 11, 2027.

Source: Radio Tamazuj · 2026-11-21 · 1 min read

South Sudanese presidential aspirant Kornelio Kon Ngu said on Friday he would challenge the National Elections Commission’s revised timetable in court on Monday. The commission did not answer his 72-hour ultimatum, issued Wednesday, to withdraw the schedule postponing the general election from Dec. 22, 2026, to Feb. 11, 2027.

Kon, a former deputy speaker of parliament who leads a faction of the National Alliance of Political Parties, announced the planned case at a news conference in Juba. He said he would ask the court to overturn the commission’s decision, arguing that no institution could change the election date set by law. He urged other parties opposing the timetable to join the case.

Kon also said unidentified people in civilian clothes tried to take him for questioning in Juba on Thursday, but left after a crowd gathered. He did not identify them or accuse any security service.

Why it mattersKon’s alliance must bring its challenge to court on Monday, while voters face a revised election date of Feb. 11, 2027.
