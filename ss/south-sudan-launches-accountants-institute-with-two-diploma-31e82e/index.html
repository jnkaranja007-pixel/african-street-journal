South Sudan launches accountants' institute with two diploma levels

The Institute of Certified Public Accountants of South Sudan says it plans to develop a full certification course from next year.

Source: Radio Tamazuj · 2026-11-19 · 1 min read

South Sudan launched the Institute of Certified Public Accountants of South Sudan in Juba on Wednesday, offering diploma studies at two levels in accounting and business.

The Ministry of Finance and Planning says the country needs more qualified accountants to improve financial management and reduce reliance on external experts. Benjamin Ayali Konyongwo, the ministry's undersecretary for planning, said trained professionals are needed across government, private businesses, financial institutions and development organisations. The foundation level covers core accounting and business principles; the advanced level covers financial reporting, taxation and management accounting.

David Mogga, the institute's chief executive, said it had begun implementing an Accounting Technician Diploma for South Sudan. He said development of a full professional accountancy course, the Certified Public Accountant (CPA) qualification, was planned for next year.

Why it mattersPeople studying through the institute can progress from its two diploma levels toward a full professional certification planned for next year.
