South Sudan mourns General Joseph Lagu Yanga

A tribute remembers the late General Joseph Lagu Yanga, who died in 2026, for his courage and call for peace.

Source: Radio Tamazuj · 2026-11-14 · 1 min read

South Sudan is remembering General Joseph Lagu Yanga, who died in 2026. A tribute published by Radio Tamazuj on November 13, 2026, honors his life and service.

The tribute describes General Yanga as a hero who walked where maps end and taught others to stand for survival. It highlights his courage and duty, noting that war's cost is too high and its glory purchased with broken bodies.

The writer calls for an end to bloodshed that profits the powerful and empties the poor, urging a collective work for peace. The tribute concludes by stating that General Yanga's name moves like a star in the nation's darkness and he will not be forgotten.

Why it mattersThe nation remembers General Joseph Lagu Yanga, a hero who called for peace and an end to bloodshed.
