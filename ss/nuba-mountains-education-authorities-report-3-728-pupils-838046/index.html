Nuba Mountains education authorities report 3,728 pupils sat exams in South Sudan

The candidates took primary certificate examinations at nine centres in Ruweng, while insecurity kept 66 registered pupils away.

Source: Radio Tamazuj · 2026-11-22 · 1 min read

A total of 3,728 pupils from Sudan’s Nuba Mountains region sat primary school certificate examinations at nine centres in South Sudan’s Ruweng Administrative Area, regional education director-general Jamal Abdallah Kalo said. The exams ran from 16 to 20 November, with centres in Pariang and Yida.

Kalo said 3,794 pupils had registered, including 1,913 boys and 1,815 girls; 66 candidates were absent, among them 25 boys and 41 girls. He attributed the absences to insecurity in Heiban County, where schools remained closed amid displacement that began in May. Schools reopened for the first term on 1 October, but some in the region faced full or partial closures.

Secondary certificate exams for 1,213 registered students, from 15 schools across 14 counties, were scheduled to begin on 23 November. Kalo said shortages of school supplies and teaching materials, damaged in events in Kauda, and the closure of teacher training institutes were among the region’s education challenges.

Why it mattersThe 66 pupils absent because of insecurity in Heiban County missed the primary examination sitting held from 16 to 20 November.
