South Sudanese youth urge government to expand jobs and political voice

After a six-day visit to Senegal for the 2026 Youth Olympic Games, Deng Wiyual Nyang called for investment in young people at home.

Sources: Radio Tamazuj and Sudans Post · 2026-11-24 · 1 min read

Deng Wiyual Nyang, a 24-year-old member of a youth protection team in Juba, urged South Sudan’s government to create more work opportunities and give young people a voice in parliament after returning from a six-day visit to Senegal for the 2026 Youth Olympic Games.

Speaking to Radio Tamazuj on Monday, Deng said he saw sports complexes, stadiums and tourism sites in Dakar that he hoped South Sudan could develop. He said unemployment and limited opportunities were driving young people to risk dangerous routes through Libya and across the Mediterranean Sea to Europe. The teams he works with identify young people’s challenges and report them to community and government leaders.

Deng said the teams also engage unemployed and vulnerable youth through community activities and online platforms. Another team member, Nyanuer Wiyual Manytap, said they were excited to promote peace through sport and development.

Why it mattersUnemployed and vulnerable young people are engaged by Deng’s teams through community activities and online platforms, while he is urging the government to create more work and parliamentary opportunities.
