African Union deploys election expert to South Sudan ahead of February 2027 polls

The expert will support the National Elections Commission while voter registration continues across the country’s 10 states and three administrative areas.

Sources: Eye Radio and Sudans Post · 2026-11-19 · 1 min read

The African Union has deployed an electoral expert to South Sudan to support preparations for the February 2027 general elections. The expert was announced at a Wednesday meeting with the National Elections Commission at its headquarters in Juba.

Commission chair Gabriel Bol Deng said the deployment followed an AU commitment made in Addis Ababa. Registration began on November 14, and Bol said it was progressing smoothly despite logistical challenges. He reported no violence at registration centres as of Wednesday’s briefing. The exercise is intended to let the commission compile and publish a preliminary voters’ register.

The AU delegation urged the commission to maintain independence, transparency and inclusivity. The meeting also covered electoral security and consultations with political parties; AU official Prosper N. Addo reaffirmed support for peaceful elections.

Why it mattersThe National Elections Commission must complete registration and publish a preliminary voters’ register before the February 2027 general elections.
