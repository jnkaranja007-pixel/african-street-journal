Western Equatoria trains 619 police recruits for 2027 elections

The recruits, including 37 women, are training at three sites as police prepare to secure polling stations instead of the army.

Sources: Radio Tamazuj and Sudans Post · 2026-11-25 · 1 min read

Western Equatoria State is training 619 police recruits in Yambio, Maridi and Mvolo ahead of South Sudan's planned February 2027 elections. The group includes 37 women, and 57 trainers are overseeing the programme, Musa Daud Kodi, its chairperson, said.

Registration and mobilisation ran from August 10 to August 20, 2026, and training had been underway for just over two months, Kodi said. Caretaker Governor Daniel Badagbu Rimbasa said police would secure polling stations and be deployed across the state to maintain public order and protect communities.

During a Tuesday visit to recruits at the State Police Headquarters in Yambio, Badagbu handed over uniforms and boots. He said the state would refer issues requiring national intervention to authorities in Juba and arrange medical personnel when needed; medical supplies had already arrived from Juba.

Why it mattersWith the army barred from polling stations, the trained recruits will be the force responsible for order at the ballot in Western Equatoria.
