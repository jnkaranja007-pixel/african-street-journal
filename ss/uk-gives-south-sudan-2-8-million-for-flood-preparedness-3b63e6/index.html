UK gives South Sudan $2.8 million for flood preparedness

Funding will support early warning systems and aid for communities in Jonglei, Unity, and Upper Nile states.

Source: Eye Radio · 2026-11-01 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

The United Kingdom has provided 2.8 million US dollars to the UN Food and Agriculture Organization and the World Food Programme to help vulnerable communities in South Sudan prepare for climate-related disasters. The funding will support early flood preparedness and response activities in Jonglei, Unity, and Upper Nile states. The programme will provide early-warning information, cash assistance, livelihood support, community public works, and emergency supplies.

UK Development Director Sebastian Fouquet stated that climate shocks are worsening the country’s humanitarian situation, and acting on early warnings helps families protect their assets. WFP Country Director Mutinta Chimuka said early action is essential to prevent predictable climate shocks from becoming humanitarian emergencies. FAO Acting Representative Felix Dzvurumi added that the support will help farmers, livestock keepers, and fishing communities protect their livelihoods and improve food security.

The programme will run until March 2027 and will also strengthen community-based early-warning systems and coordination with government authorities. This contribution brings the UK’s total funding for El Niño-related early action in South Sudan to over £2.85 million. The initiative aims to reduce losses and protect livelihoods before disasters strike.

Why it mattersCommunities in Jonglei, Unity, and Upper Nile states will receive support to prepare for and respond to climate-related disasters like floods and drought.
