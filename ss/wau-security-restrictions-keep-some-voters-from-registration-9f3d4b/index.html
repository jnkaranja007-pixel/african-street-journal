Wau security restrictions keep some voters from registration

South Sudan’s elections commission says some centres lack materials, with the nationwide registration period ending on November 28.

Sources: Radio Tamazuj and Eye Radio · 2026-11-26 · 1 min read

Security restrictions are preventing election officials from reaching some parts of Wau County, in Western Bahr el Ghazal state, and some registration centres have not received materials, the state elections commission chairman said Wednesday. South Sudan’s nationwide voter registration began on November 14 and is scheduled to end on November 28.

Arkangelo Wudo Agany, chairman of the National Elections Commission in the state, said opposition SPLA-IO forces controlling parts of the county had denied commission staff access. County Commissioner Joseph Daniel Dongo said registration was operating at four established centres and urged residents to use them. He also said limited public awareness was reducing participation.

Civil society activist Jackline Nasiwa has asked the commission to extend registration, citing remote communities and South Sudanese students abroad. The commission said an extension requires a board decision and could affect candidate nominations and other scheduled electoral activities. The general election is scheduled for February 11, 2027.

Why it mattersResidents in areas without registration services have until November 28 under the current timetable, while any extension requires a commission board decision.
