South Sudan starts secondary exams for 53,301 candidates

The national examinations run through December 1, with candidates from 596 schools, including 1,009 refugees and 4,216 accelerated-programme learners.

Source: Eye Radio · 2026-11-23 · 1 min read

South Sudan began its Certificate of Secondary Education examinations on November 23, with 53,301 candidates from 596 schools taking part. The exams, administered by the South Sudan National Examinations Council, are scheduled to run through December 1.

The Ministry of General Education and Instruction said 31,037 candidates are male and 22,264 female. The candidates also include 1,009 refugees, 4,216 learners in the Accelerated Secondary Education Programme, 168 technical candidates and 24 commercial candidates. The ministry urged supervisors, invigilators, monitors and security personnel to act professionally and protect the exams’ credibility.

The registered total is approximately 14% higher than the 45,799 candidates recorded in 2025. The council will proceed with the next stages of the examination process after the exams conclude.
