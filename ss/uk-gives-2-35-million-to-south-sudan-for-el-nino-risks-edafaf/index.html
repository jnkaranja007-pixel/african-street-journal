UK gives £2.35 million to South Sudan for El Niño risks

The funding aims to protect food production and vulnerable communities from climate shocks.

Source: Sudans Post · 2026-10-23 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

The United Kingdom has announced an additional £2.35 million to help mitigate El Niño-related risks in South Sudan. This funding will assist communities facing threats to their food production and livelihoods from climate shocks. UK Ambassador Rebecca Fabrizi announced the new support during the start of her tenure in the country.

Ambassador Fabrizi stated that the funding is intended to address the impacts of climate shocks. The support targets vulnerable communities whose livelihoods are at risk. The announcement was made in a statement released in Juba.

The additional funding from the UK comes as El Niño weather patterns can disrupt agricultural cycles. This support aims to build resilience against such climate-related challenges.

Why it mattersSouth Sudanese farmers and vulnerable communities will receive support to mitigate the risks of El Niño.
