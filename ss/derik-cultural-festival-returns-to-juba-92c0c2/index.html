Derik Cultural Festival returns to Juba

The event will run from November 7 to 14 at the University of Juba’s Main Campus.

Source: Sudans Post · 2026-11-03 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

The sixth Derik Cultural Festival is scheduled to take place in Juba from November 7 to 14. Organisers aim to foster peace, reconciliation, and civic engagement through various cultural activities at the University of Juba’s Main Campus.

The festival is being held under the theme “Advancing Peace Through Culture.” It honors the late Derik Uya Alfred, who was known for championing cultural development across Sudan.

This event seeks to use cultural expression as a tool for building bridges and understanding within South Sudan.

Why it mattersSouth Sudanese citizens attending the festival can engage in activities promoting peace and reconciliation.
