Somalia to start issuing EAC e-passport in January, ahead of South Sudan

Somalia will become the latest East African Community partner state to issue the regional travel document, and South Sudan has yet to begin.

Source: Sudans Post · 2026-11-27 · 1 min read

Somalia is set to begin issuing the East African Community (EAC) electronic passport in January 2027, the report said from Arusha. The rollout puts Somalia ahead of South Sudan, which joined the bloc in 2016 but has yet to begin issuing the document.

The planned launch leaves South Sudan as the partner state still without the regional passport, even though it has been a member of the bloc for a decade. The report gives no further details on South Sudan's timetable.

The EAC electronic passport is a regional travel document for the bloc's partner states.

Why it mattersSouth Sudanese travellers who need the regional document must keep using their national passports for trips within the bloc until their government starts issuing it.
