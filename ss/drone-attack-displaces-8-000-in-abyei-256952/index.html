Drone attack displaces 8,000 in Abyei

More than 8,000 people fled their homes after a drone strike killed over 220 civilians at Amiet Market, according to local authorities.

Sources: Eye Radio, Radio Tamazuj and Sudans Post · 2026-10-22 · 2 min read

Over 8,000 people have been displaced from northern Abyei following a drone attack on Amiet Market that killed more than 220 individuals. The incident occurred on Monday, October 19, in the disputed area between South Sudan and Sudan. Local authorities reported that 220 people were killed and dozens injured, with some victims buried in mass graves. The South Sudanese government has attributed the attack to the Sudan Armed Forces and summoned the Sudanese ambassador.

Caretaker Minister of Information and Spokesperson for Abyei, Yohana Akol, stated that the displaced are seeking refuge in Abyei town and surrounding areas, with many sheltering under trees. He appealed to humanitarian organizations and United Nations agencies for urgent assistance. Residents of the Abyei Administrative Area staged a peaceful protest on Tuesday to condemn the strike and demand better civilian protection. They submitted a petition to the UN Interim Security Force for Abyei (UNISFA) urging an investigation and referral to the UN Security Council.

The attack has prompted condemnation from the South Sudanese government, with President Salva Kiir's office calling for an investigation and accountability. The government has summoned the Sudanese ambassador to discuss the incident. The protest, involving community leaders, civil society, youth, and women, highlighted concerns over civilian safety and the impact of the attack.

Why it mattersOver 8,000 people are displaced and seeking urgent humanitarian aid following a deadly drone attack.
