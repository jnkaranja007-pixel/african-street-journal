South Sudan pitches investors on farming, livestock and fisheries

At a trade forum in Ethiopia, Trade Minister Labanya Margret Mathya cited land, livestock and a fisheries sector she said could earn US$300 million annually.

Source: Eye Radio · 2026-11-25 · 1 min read

South Sudan’s caretaker trade minister, Dr. Labanya Margret Mathya, urged African and international investors to explore the country’s farming, livestock and fisheries sectors at the Africa Development Trade Forum 2026 in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia, on Monday. She said South Sudan has approximately 30 million hectares of potentially arable land, with about 2.57 million hectares currently used for agriculture.

Mathya said the country has more than 18.8 million cattle and approximately 38.4 million domestic animals in total, and urged investors to consider meat processing, leather and other livestock businesses. She also cited World Bank figures putting fisheries’ annual potential at approximately US$300 million and called for partnerships with fishing and fish-processing companies.

The minister cited annual revenue potential of approximately US$550 million from shea butter, alongside investment prospects in gum Arabic, petroleum, gold and cobalt.

Why it mattersInvestors are being asked to develop fisheries with an annual potential of approximately US$300 million, according to figures Mathya attributed to the World Bank.
