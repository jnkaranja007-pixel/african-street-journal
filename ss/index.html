News · 2026-08-21

Violence at Bentiu IDP camp kills 9

At least nine people were killed and 23 others wounded during violence at the Bentiu internally displaced persons camp in Unity State. Sudans Post reports the clashes began when authorities attempted to arrest a self-proclaimed prophet named Taban Puol.

Why it mattersThe unrest disrupts the safety of displaced populations in Unity State.

IRC delivers health and education facilities in Aweil South

The International Rescue Committee handed over two renovated primary schools and health facilities to authorities in Aweil South County. Sudans Post reports the project was funded by the World Bank.

Why it mattersThe new facilities increase local community access to essential medical and educational services.

Politics · 2026-08-21

Nyangwara community seeks halt to Nyamini land demarcation

The Nyangwara-Pojulu-Tijor community has called for a stop to land allocation and demarcation in Juba County. Eye Radio reports the dispute over the land has lasted more than 13 years.

Why it mattersThe ongoing conflict prevents the legal resolution of competing land claims for residents in Nyamini.

Governor Adil vows crackdown on illegal mining

Central Equatoria State Governor Emmanuel Adil Anthony announced a crackdown on illegal mining and armed groups. Radio Tamazuj reports the measure was announced Thursday.

Why it mattersThe initiative aims to curb unauthorized extraction of mineral resources within the state.

Former finance minister Kosti Manibe dies
