Security · 2026-11-17

Gunmen free 3 abducted Yei aid workers for $2,500 ransom

Three humanitarian staff seized along the Yei-Maridi Road were released after their captors received a cash payment.

Three aid workers seized at gunpoint along the Yei-Maridi Road in Central Equatoria State on 9 November regained their freedom after a ransom was paid. Police Commissioner Major General Yoasa Lujang Kamba stated that the abductors initially demanded 8,000 US dollars before accepting 2,500 US dollars to release the captives.

The freed staff include a driver and two data collectors working for an international humanitarian organization based in Yei Town. General Kamba confirmed the trio were reunited with their families after being held by armed individuals who claimed affiliation with the National Salvation Front, though authorities have not independently verified this group's identity.

General Kamba also reported a separate roadside kidnapping involving three other individuals near the tobacco company along the same corridor. Police stated that security forces are continuing operations to identify and apprehend the perpetrators behind both attacks.

Why it mattersHumanitarian workers and travelers navigating the Yei-Maridi corridor face renewed security risks following multiple roadside abductions.

Education · 2026-11-17

Yei records 1,918 primary candidates despite insecurity

Yei River County's Primary Eight candidates rose from 1,825 last year to 1,918 this year, even as insecurity forces some learners to sit exams elsewhere.

Monday saw 1,918 Primary Eight candidates in Yei River County, Central Equatoria State, begin their Certificate of Primary Education examinations, up from 1,825 last year. Philip Taban Issa, the county education director, said 892 girls and 1,025 boys are sitting the tests across 59 schools, including four Alternative Learning Programme Centres.

Insecurity has moved some candidates: those from Lasu, Tore and Otogo payams are sitting in Yei Town, while Mugwo Payam candidates are at Payawa Centre. Mary Ding Foster, the chief examination field monitor, warned candidates and invigilators against malpractice, and Emmanuel Taban Seme, the caretaker commissioner, urged learners to stay committed to their studies.

Elsewhere, 393 Primary Eight students in Panyijiar County, Unity State, have missed their national exams after the government cancelled tests in opposition-controlled areas. In Jonglei State, the government will transport 534 Akobo candidates to Bor, where it says they will be housed until the exams end.

Why it mattersCandidates in Panyijiar and Akobo have lost or are being moved from their usual exam centres because of security concerns, and Akobo pupils will be housed in Bor until the exams end.

South Sudan protests exclusion from UNISFA mandate drafting

South Sudan objected to being left out of consultations for a United Nations peacekeeping force mandate extension.

South Sudan protested its exclusion from consultations on a United Nations Security Council resolution renewing the mandate of the United Nations Interim Security Force in Abyei. The government stated that its concerns were overlooked and not adequately reflected in the decision.

The United Nations Security Council adopted Resolution 2838 (2026) on November 13, extending the peacekeeping force's mandate without the country's direct participation in the drafting process.

Officials in Juba raised the objection following the formal adoption of the text by the international body.

Why it mattersSouth Sudan officials must navigate renewed security arrangements in Abyei following the adoption of Resolution 2838 (2026) without their direct participation.

Politics · 2026-11-17

Ateny vows action against crimes targeting journalists

South Sudan's information minister pledged investigations following the killing of journalist Veronica Lucy Gordon.

South Sudan’s Minister of Information, Communication, Technology and Postal Services, Ateny Wek Ateny, pledged government action against crimes targeting journalists during a belated commemoration of the International Day to End Impunity for Crimes Against Journalists on Monday. He said perpetrators must be investigated and held accountable under the law.

Ateny condemned the killing of journalist Veronica Lucy Gordon, describing it as a cold-blooded act, and offered condolences to her family. He commended the Union of the Journalists of South Sudan (UJoSS) and its partners for marking the day under the national theme "Strengthening Accountability, Digital Safety and Protection of Journalists."

Ateny said the role of journalists will become especially important as elections slated for February 2027 draw closer. He urged all stakeholders to respect press freedom and called for stronger cooperation among government institutions, law enforcement, the judiciary and media regulators to improve journalists' safety.

Why it mattersJournalists in South Sudan face intimidation and physical threats while reporting, and the minister has pledged that crimes against them will be investigated and punished under the law.

Sport · 2026-11-17

South Sudan signs sports infrastructure deal with Chinese firm

The ministry partners with Bai Chuan Water Conservancy and Electric Power Construction Company to build a national basketball stadium.

South Sudan’s Ministry of Youth and Sports signed an agreement on Monday with Chinese company Bai Chuan Water Conservancy and Electric Power Construction Company to develop youth infrastructure and sports facilities across the country. Dr Monica Agum Awet Akot, the ministry’s Undersecretary, and Mr Gan Hong Jin, the company’s chairman, signed the memorandum of understanding in a ceremony witnessed by Peter Mayen Majongdit, the Caretaker Minister of Humanitarian Affairs and Acting Youth and Sports Minister.

A national basketball stadium holding at least 15,000 spectators and meeting International Basketball Federation standards was named as the first project under the partnership. Before construction starts, both parties will carry out feasibility studies, site allocation, architectural designs and financing arrangements.

The project also includes building Olympic training centres, youth centres with vocational and digital skills training, technology and innovation hubs, and entrepreneurship facilities.

Why it mattersThe Ministry of Youth and Sports and Bai Chuan Water Conservancy and Electric Power Construction Company must complete feasibility studies, site allocation, architectural designs and financing arrangements before building the stadium.
