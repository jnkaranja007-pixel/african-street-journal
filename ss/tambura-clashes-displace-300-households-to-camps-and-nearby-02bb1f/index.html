Tambura clashes displace 300 households to camps and nearby communities

Officials report 100 households in Maboiku and 200 in Sinawiri as South Sudan's government plans an assessment of those who fled.

Source: Radio Tamazuj · 2026-11-19 · 1 min read

Renewed clashes between government and opposition forces on Tuesday displaced at least 300 households in Tambura County, Western Equatoria State, to two camps and nearby communities. Opposition forces entered areas hosting displaced people in Nabanga, caretaker commissioner Mupaya James said.

Lucy Edward, an official with the county Relief and Rehabilitation Commission, said 100 households had reached Maboiku and 200 had taken refuge in Sinawiri. Families need food, plastic sheeting and other supplies, she said. Three people injured in the fighting were being treated at Jumbura Hospital; James said he had received no reports of deaths.

State Information Minister Noah Issa Philip said the government would assess how many people had fled and identify their needs with humanitarian partners. He said five soldiers had been injured and evacuated to Juba by Médecins Sans Frontières; opposition casualties had not been independently verified.

Why it mattersFamilies in Maboiku and Sinawiri need food and shelter supplies while the government assesses the number displaced.
