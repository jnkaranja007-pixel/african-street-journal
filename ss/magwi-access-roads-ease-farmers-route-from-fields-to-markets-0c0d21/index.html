Magwi access roads ease farmers’ route from fields to markets

Residents say a community road linking farming areas to the main route is helping vehicles carry produce without getting stuck.

Source: Radio Tamazuj · 2026-11-22 · 1 min read

Rehabilitated access roads in parts of Magwi County, Eastern Equatoria State, are making it easier for farmers to move produce from their fields to markets, residents say. A community road connects farming areas to the main route, while work on the Magwi-Palotaka feeder road is ongoing.

Olaraga Simon Ogenga, a 35-year-old farmer, said vehicles had previously become stuck when carrying produce from farms. Aloyo Josephine, a 32-year-old farmer and mother of four, said the road had improved movement between farming areas, markets and homes even before work was complete. She said local leaders organised residents, including women, young people, widows and people with disabilities, to clear trees and other obstacles. Josephine said many women worked for three to four hours, but their payment was too little for the time and effort.

Ogenga said opening the road also gave people without jobs a chance to earn money. Josephine said the connection was especially important to farmers relying on produce from Palotaka, an area known for agricultural production.

Why it mattersFarmers in Magwi can move produce between farms and markets more easily, while community workers who cleared the road say their pay was too little for three to four hours of work.
