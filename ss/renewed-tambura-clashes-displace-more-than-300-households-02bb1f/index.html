Renewed Tambura clashes displace more than 300 households

Families fled camps and shelters after fighting between government and opposition forces, with local officials organising temporary accommodation and an aid assessment.

Sources: Radio Tamazuj and Eye Radio · 2026-11-20 · 1 min read

Renewed clashes between South Sudanese government and opposition forces displaced more than 300 households in Tambura County, Western Equatoria State, on Tuesday. Families fled camps and shelters in Nabanga to Sinawiri, Maboiku and other nearby areas, according to local officials.

Tambura County commissioner Mupaya James told Radio Tamazuj that authorities were registering and verifying those affected. Lucy Edward, an official with the Relief and Rehabilitation Commission, said 100 households had reached Maboiku and 200 had gone to Sinawiri. Three people injured in the fighting were being treated at Jumbura Hospital, while five government soldiers were evacuated to Juba, the state information minister said.

The commissioner said local authorities were working with camp leaders to arrange temporary shelter. The state government plans an assessment with humanitarian partners to confirm the number displaced and identify urgent needs; families were reported to need food, shelter materials and other supplies.

Why it mattersNewly displaced households in Maboiku and Sinawiri need food and shelter while officials verify the total number affected.
