South Sudan targets December finish for 44-kilometre Magwi–Palotaka road

Work on the Eastern Equatoria feeder road began in October under a programme linking rural infrastructure with agricultural production.

Source: Radio Tamazuj · 2026-11-20 · 1 min read

South Sudan’s Ministry of Agriculture and Food Security is overseeing rehabilitation of the 44-kilometre feeder road between Magwi and Palotaka in Magwi County, Eastern Equatoria State, with completion expected by December. Work began in October under the South Sudan Livelihoods Resilience Program, which combines rural infrastructure with support for agricultural production.

Peter Nyerenda Chidumula, project manager at the United Nations Office for Project Services (UNOPS) in South Sudan, said officials and development partners were inspecting the road, community access routes and farming activities. He said the team was checking whether farmers were producing goods they could transport on the improved routes to markets.

The programme is implemented by the ministry and UNOPS, with support from the International Fund for Agricultural Development, the Global Agriculture and Food Security Program and the Netherlands government. The ministry’s acting project manager, Peter Mazede, said government and beneficiary contributions are recorded in the programme’s funding arrangements.

Why it mattersFarmers in Magwi County are due to gain a route linking their communities to farms and markets when the 44-kilometre road is completed by December.
