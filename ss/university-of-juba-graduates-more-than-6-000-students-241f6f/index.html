University of Juba graduates more than 6,000 students

The 23-school class includes 1,503 master's graduates and 16 PhD holders, as officials urged them to serve South Sudan.

Source: Eye Radio · 2026-11-22 · 1 min read

The University of Juba graduated more than 6,000 students from 23 schools on Saturday, 21 November, at its 28th convocation in South Sudan. President Salva Kiir Mayardit, the university's chancellor, presided over the ceremony.

Among the graduates are 1,325 diploma holders and 3,655 bachelor's degree recipients, alongside 277 postgraduate diploma holders, 1,503 master's graduates and 16 PhD holders. Vice Chancellor John Akech said some students had taken part-time jobs to pay tuition while facing financial difficulties and other challenges.

Caretaker General Education and Instruction Minister Kuyok Abol Kuyok urged graduates to contribute through service, innovation and leadership, saying families and communities had sacrificed to support their education. Government officials, university staff, students and parents attended.

Why it mattersThe graduates are expected to repay the sacrifices of their families and communities through service, innovation and leadership, the education minister said.
