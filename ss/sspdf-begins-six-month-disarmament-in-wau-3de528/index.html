SSPDF begins six-month disarmament in Wau

Operation aims to remove illegal firearms and improve security, but civil society warns of looting and civilian safety concerns.

Sources: Sudans Post and Radio Tamazuj · 2026-11-15 · 1 min read

The South Sudan People’s Defence Forces (SSPDF) has started a six-month operation in Wau County to disarm civilians and remove illegal firearms. Major General Wol Dhel Theip, the SSPDF’s Fifth Infantry Division Deputy Commander, confirmed the operation began to improve security in the region.

Civil society groups in Western Bahr el Ghazal State are calling for oversight of the disarmament forces due to reports of looting. Stephen Robo Musa, regional coordinator for the Community Empowerment for Progress Organization (CEPO), stated that soldiers found abusing their authority should face legal action. The Paramount Chief of Greater Bagari Payam reported looting, including from hospitals and a court.

Concerns have also been raised about the impact of the insecurity on pupils preparing for Primary Eight examinations, scheduled to begin on Monday. Robo Musa urged the state Ministry of Education to assess examination centres in Wau County and ensure they are safe, suggesting candidates be moved to safer venues within Wau Municipality if necessary.
