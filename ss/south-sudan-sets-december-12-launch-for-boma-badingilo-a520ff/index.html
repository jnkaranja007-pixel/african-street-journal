South Sudan sets December 12 launch for Boma-Badingilo heritage site

The landscape was inscribed in July as the country’s first UNESCO World Heritage Site, but is also listed as being in danger.

Source: Eye Radio · 2026-11-28 · 1 min read

South Sudan will officially launch the Boma-Badingilo Migratory Landscape on December 12, five months after it joined the UNESCO World Heritage List. Julia Amon Wantok, the caretaker undersecretary at the Ministry of Culture, Museums and National Heritage, announced the date on Friday in Juba.

The launch will be organised with the Ministry of Wildlife Conservation and Tourism, UNESCO, African Parks and other partners, Amon said. The She Stand Women’s Museum will also take part. The landscape includes Boma and Badingilo national parks, wetlands, floodplains and savannahs, and is home to an estimated six million migratory antelopes.

UNESCO has also placed the site on its List of World Heritage in Danger, citing threats including poaching, insecurity and infrastructure development. The antelope species there include white-eared kob, tiang and Mongalla gazelles.

Why it mattersThe Culture Ministry and its partners have to carry out the launch on December 12 while the site remains on UNESCO’s danger list.
