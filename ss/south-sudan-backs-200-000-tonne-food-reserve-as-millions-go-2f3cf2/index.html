South Sudan backs 200,000-tonne food reserve as millions go hungry

The Council of Ministers has cleared a 200,000-metric-tonne stockpile as food insecurity spreads across the country.

Source: Sudans Post · 2026-11-21 · 1 min read

The Office of the President announced on Friday that the Council of Ministers had cleared a plan for a 200,000-metric-tonne food reserve, to be built up in South Sudan.

Officials said the reserve is meant to shore up national food stocks at a time when millions of people across the country are short of food, according to the president's office.

The announcement gave no timetable for building the stockpile and did not say how it would be stocked.

Why it mattersHouseholds short of food across the country are the ones the reserve would reach if it is filled and released.
