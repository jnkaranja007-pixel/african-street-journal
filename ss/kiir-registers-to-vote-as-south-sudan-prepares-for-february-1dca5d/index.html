Kiir registers to vote as South Sudan prepares for February polls

The president received his voter card six days after nationwide registration began, with the exercise scheduled to close on November 28.

Sources: Eye Radio, Radio Tamazuj and Sudans Post · 2026-11-21 · 1 min read

South Sudanese President Salva Kiir Mayardit registered to vote on Friday at the Hai Gonya Voter Registration Centre in Constituency No. 1, Juba, ahead of general elections scheduled for February 11, 2027. He received his voter card.

Kiir urged eligible citizens to register and collect their cards before the polls, saying registration was the first step towards exercising their constitutional right to vote. The National Elections Commission said 22,672 election personnel had been deployed nationwide to support registration.

The exercise began on November 14 and is scheduled to run through November 28 across South Sudan’s 10 states and three administrative areas.

Why it mattersEligible South Sudanese voters have until November 28 to register and collect their cards before the February 11, 2027 elections.
