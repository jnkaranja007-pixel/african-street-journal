Juba council arrests 14 over soak pit violations in Hai Sujun

Enforcement teams are stepping up action against homes and businesses that discharge wastewater onto public streets.

Sources: Sudans Post and Eye Radio · 2026-11-25 · 1 min read

Juba City Council arrested about 14 people in Hai Sujun, a neighbourhood in South Sudan's capital, on Tuesday for violating public health regulations that require households and businesses to install soak pits and septic tanks. The arrests followed the council's November 11 compliance deadline.

Enforcement teams later moved to Munuki, where they told restaurant operators that businesses must also comply with the regulations. The council said properties without proper systems were discharging wastewater onto public streets.

The action follows a weekly cleaning campaign in Hai Cinema led by Deputy Mayor for Environment and Waste Management Stephen Lomude, who raised concerns over properties lacking proper disposal systems. The council says it is stepping up enforcement.

Why it mattersHouseholds and businesses that lack proper soak pits and septic tanks now face arrest, and restaurant operators in Munuki have been warned to comply.
