Leer police hold five men over alleged hospital thefts

The suspects are accused of taking money, phones and other property from a hospital in Unity State, and no recoveries have been reported.

Sources: Radio Tamazuj and Sudans Post · 2026-11-26 · 1 min read

Police in Leer County, Unity State, are holding five men after their arrest on suspicion of taking money, mobile phones and other property from a local hospital. The county's caretaker commissioner, Stephen Taker Riek Dong, told Radio Tamazuj on Wednesday that the men are being kept at Leer Police Station while investigations continue.

Taker said the arrests followed a visit by 17 people to the hospital, 10 of them armed. Three of those detained are suspected of taking part in the theft, and a fourth allegedly agreed that hospital property should be moved to his home. Taker gave no details of the items taken and did not say whether any of it had been recovered.

Taker said arrests would continue one by one, as authorities respond to insecurity in the county. The five suspects remain in custody.

Why it mattersThe five men are held at Leer Police Station while investigators look into the alleged theft from a hospital that serves local communities.
