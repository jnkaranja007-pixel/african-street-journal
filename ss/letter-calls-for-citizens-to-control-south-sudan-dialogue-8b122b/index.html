Letter calls for citizens to control South Sudan dialogue agenda

The writer says a proposed Citizens’ Assembly needs independent conveners and representatives chosen by citizens, with leaders limited to presenting their views.

Source: Radio Tamazuj · 2026-11-22 · 1 min read

A letter published by Radio Tamazuj on Saturday calls for citizens to control the agenda of a proposed Citizens’ Assembly on South Sudan’s Future. The writer says the assembly needs independent conveners and citizens must be free to choose their representatives and raise their concerns.

The letter says political and military leaders may present their views, but the agenda should belong to citizens. It argues that people with greater power have greater duties, and calls on commanders in government and opposition to end attacks on civilians, open roads and allow aid through.

The writer also points to a 10 November 2026 call by the UN Mission in South Sudan for civilian protection and humanitarian access, saying people must be able to participate safely.

Why it mattersCitizens invited to the proposed assembly need independent conveners and the freedom to choose representatives and set the agenda.
