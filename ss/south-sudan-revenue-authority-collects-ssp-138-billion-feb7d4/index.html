South Sudan Revenue Authority collects SSP 138 billion

The Domestic Tax Revenue Division gathered the funds in the first 15 days of October.

Source: Sudans Post · 2026-10-29 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

The South Sudan Revenue Authority collected SSP 138 billion in the first 15 days of October 2026. The authority is increasing efforts to register more taxpayers and improve compliance with tax laws.

Angelo Aguot, acting commissioner for Domestic Tax Revenue, stated that the division has recently seen improvements in its revenue collection. He noted these gains occurred as the authority works to expand taxpayer registration and enhance compliance.

The collected revenue is part of the authority's ongoing efforts to boost domestic tax collection. Further details on the specific improvements in compliance and registration were not provided in the initial report.

Why it mattersThe South Sudan Revenue Authority collected SSP 138 billion in 15 days, impacting national revenue collection.
