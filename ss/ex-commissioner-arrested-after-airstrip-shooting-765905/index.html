Ex-commissioner arrested after airstrip shooting

Former Rubkona commissioner Stephen Salam Maluit allegedly fired shots at Bentiu airstrip when denied entry.

Source: Eye Radio · 2026-10-23 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Former Rubkona County commissioner Stephen Salam Maluit was arrested on Thursday morning after allegedly firing shots at Bentiu airstrip. The incident occurred as gubernatorial candidate Michael Chanjiek Geay arrived at the location. Maluit arrived with over 20 armed individuals but was stopped by security forces.

Unity State Information Minister Nyakenya Johannes Keah stated that Maluit opened fire when security personnel advised him to leave. The confrontation led to an exchange of gunfire between Maluit's armed group and security forces. No injuries or property damage were reported following the incident.

Minister Keah urged political aspirants to reject violence ahead of the country's elections, emphasizing the need for peaceful participation and stability in Unity State. She called on community members to promote peace and coexistence amid current challenges.

Why it mattersThe arrest of a former commissioner and gunfire at an airstrip disrupt political campaigning and threaten stability in Unity State.
