Mchacha rejects reports he is seeking a ministerial post

The Democratic Progressive Party governor says his work to build support in the Southern Region is aimed at strengthening the party.

Source: Malawi24 · 2026-11-23 · 1 min read

Charles Mchacha, the Democratic Progressive Party’s (DPP) governor for Malawi’s Southern Region, says he is not seeking a ministerial position. He says his priority is to strengthen and expand the party.

Mchacha was responding to reports that his work to consolidate DPP support in the region was intended to impress President Professor […]. He rejected that interpretation and said his focus was party-building.

The article gives no further details about the reports or any next step. Mchacha’s comments address speculation about his political ambitions and the purpose of his regional organising.

Why it mattersDPP supporters in the Southern Region are the constituency Mchacha says he will focus on building, rather than pursuing a ministerial position.
