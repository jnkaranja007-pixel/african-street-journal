Malawi wins seat on OACPS mining governance working group

Frank Tumpale Mwenifumbo says the role will let the country promote its rare earths to investors and shape talks on mineral governance.

Source: Nyasa Times · 2026-11-24 · 1 min read

Malawi has won a place on a 10-member working group of the Organisation of African, Caribbean and Pacific States (OACPS), which focuses on resource governance, local processing and value addition. The delegation’s team leader, Frank Tumpale Mwenifumbo, said the country secured the seat at OACPS parliamentary meetings in Brussels, Belgium, held from November 5 to 13, 2026.

Mwenifumbo, a Karonga Central legislator for the National Development Party, said Malawi would use the role to present itself as a rare earths destination and attract foreign direct investment. He said the working group would also give Malawi a voice on mineral governance, energy production, industrialisation and value addition in the OACPS-European Union agenda.

Mining expert Grain Malunga urged the government to engage the private sector for Malawi’s participation to bear fruit. Members also discussed ways to give the OACPS Parliamentary Assembly sustainable financing independent of external funding.

Why it mattersMalawi’s government must engage private firms to make the country’s new role in mineral governance productive, as mining expert Grain Malunga urged.
