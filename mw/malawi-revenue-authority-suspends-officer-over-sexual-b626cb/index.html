Malawi Revenue Authority suspends officer over sexual misconduct complaint

The authority says Jonathan Sulumba will remain suspended while it investigates an Irish visitor's allegation about an incident at its Lilongwe office.

Source: Nyasa Times · 2026-11-28 · 1 min read

Malawi's revenue authority has suspended officer Jonathan Sulumba while it investigates a sexual misconduct complaint from a woman visiting its Lilongwe office on November 24, 2026. The woman, who is from Ireland, was seeking a certificate when she alleged she was sexually abused.

The woman described the experience as upsetting and reported it to the station manager. In a statement issued Friday evening, signed by Head of Corporate Affairs Wilma Chalulu, the authority said it was committed to establishing the facts and circumstances of the incident.

The authority said it does not condone any form of misconduct by its officers, and that appropriate action would follow the outcome of the investigation.

Why it mattersSulumba stays off duty while the investigation runs, and any sanction depends on its outcome.
