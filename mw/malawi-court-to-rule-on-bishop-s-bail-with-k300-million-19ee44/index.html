Malawi court to rule on bishop’s bail, with K300 million bond proposed

The State does not oppose Medram Felix Chirwa’s release if conditions are imposed, but his lawyer disputes how often he should report.

Source: Malawi24 · 2026-11-20 · 1 min read

Malawi’s High Court in Lilongwe is expected to rule on Friday on whether Fountain of Victory Church Bishop Medram Felix Chirwa should be released on bail. Chirwa is accused of murdering Tathokoza Salimu, an employee of FDH Bank.

The State said it would not oppose bail if conditions were imposed. Its lawyer, Hassan Ayami, proposed that Chirwa report to the Directorate of Public Prosecutions’ Criminal Registry every fortnight from November 21, 2026. Chirwa’s lawyer, Joseph Chiume, said monthly reporting would be sufficient and more manageable. The State also proposed registered property worth at least K300 million as bond, two sureties bound at K20 million each without cash, surrender of travel documents, court permission for foreign travel and no interference with witnesses or investigations.

Chirwa was arrested on October 6, 2026, after Salimu’s body was found in her vehicle at Chinkhota Village, near Tatolonga Bridge in Dowa District, on September 21.

Why it mattersIf released, Chirwa must provide registered property worth at least K300 million and comply with any reporting and travel conditions the court sets.
