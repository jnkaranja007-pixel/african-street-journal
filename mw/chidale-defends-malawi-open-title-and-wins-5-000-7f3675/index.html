Chidale defends Malawi Open title and wins $5 000

The Malawian golfer beat a field that included 35 foreign players at the four-day tournament in Lilongwe.

Source: The Nation Malawi · 2026-11-24 · 1 min read

Malawi’s top-ranked professional golfer Paul Chidale defended the SeedCo Malawi Open title on Sunday at Lilongwe Golf Club, winning $5 000 (about K8.75 million). The tournament drew players from South Africa, Zambia, Zimbabwe, Namibia, Kenya and Tanzania, among other countries.

Chidale finished ahead of fellow Malawian Victor Kachepatsonga and Zimbabwean Clive Nguru, who tied for second and each received $2 350 (about K4.1 million). SeedCo Malawi managing director Boyd Luwe said the sixth edition recorded 800 rounds of golf over four days and included 35 foreign golfers. Chidale said he was pleased to defend the championship after playing four days.

Youth, Sports and Culture Minister Alfred Gangata said investment in golf could help young people earn a living and boost tourism. The Professional Golfers Association of Malawi also praised Chidale’s title defence.

Why it mattersChidale takes home $5 000, while Kachepatsonga shares second place and receives $2 350.
