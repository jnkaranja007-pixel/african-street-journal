Malawi airport tells passengers to arrive four hours early amid check-in fault

Kamuzu International Airport has used manual check-in since Nov. 17 after heavy rain and strong winds disrupted its automated service.

Sources: Malawi24 and Nyasa Times · 2026-11-22 · 1 min read

Passengers travelling through Kamuzu International Airport in Malawi have been told to arrive at least four hours before departure after a technical fault disrupted check-in. Airport authorities have switched from automated processing to manual check-in, increasing the risk of delays.

Airport Development Limited (ADL), which manages Malawi’s airports, said heavy rainfall and strong winds affected the area on Nov. 17 and caused the fault. Spokesperson Wezzie Gondwe Lawrence said ADL was working to repair the system and restore normal operations. Passengers have also been asked to bring required travel documents, including valid passports and visas.

ADL gave no date for the return of automated check-in. The four-hour advisory is intended to give travellers time to complete the manual process.

Why it mattersPassengers flying through Kamuzu International Airport must allow four hours for check-in while ADL works to restore the automated system.
