Kukoma Diamonds take Southern crown into national netball finals

The Blantyre club beat Tigresses 51-46 to retain its regional title and will face defending champions Blue Eagles among the national contenders.

Source: Nyasa Times · 2026-11-26 · 1 min read

Kukoma Diamonds retained the Southern Region netball crown with a 51-46 win over Tigresses in the play-off final at Blantyre Sports Arena. The Blantyre side will represent the South at the One NICO Top 12 Netball Cup National Finals at Don Bosco in Lilongwe, where they aim to win the national title.

Diamonds lost last year's national final 37-29 to defending champions Blue Eagles. Coach Noel Mussa said the team had defended its regional title but had failed to win the national championship, and that they had vowed to fight hard for the national crown this year. Diamonds also lead the FDH Premier Netball League.

The South's other representatives at the finals are Tigresses and Giants NC. Blue Eagles, Civonets and MAFCO Soldiers represent the Centre; Prison Queens, Airforce Falcons and Genius the East; and Mzimba Queens, Karonga Queens and FLC Netball Club the North.

Why it mattersDiamonds must convert their regional win into a national title after last year's 37-29 final defeat, and the finals are being held in Lilongwe.
