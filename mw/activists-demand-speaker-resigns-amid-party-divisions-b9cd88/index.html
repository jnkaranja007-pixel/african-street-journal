Activists demand Speaker resigns amid party divisions

Civil society groups are intensifying calls for Parliament Speaker Sameer Suleman to step down following controversial remarks on religion.

Malawian civil society activists are demanding the resignation of Parliament Speaker Sameer Suleman, accusing him of lying and attacking the Agriculture Minister. Billy Malata, chairperson of Mbadwa Zokhudzidwa Malawi, made these accusations during a press briefing in Lilongwe. Social media users have questioned Malata's motives and suggested he may have been paid to target the Speaker.

The controversy has exposed divisions within the ruling Democratic Progressive Party (DPP). One DPP parliamentarian has publicly defended Suleman, breaking ranks with those calling for his removal. This internal dissent highlights a growing row over Suleman's remarks on religion.

The calls for Suleman's resignation are intensifying as the debate over his statements on religion continues. The situation has led to public questioning of the motives behind the demands for the Speaker's departure.

Why it mattersMalawians are questioning the motives and potential payment behind demands for the Speaker of Parliament's resignation.
