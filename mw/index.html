Malawi collects K1.05 trillion in domestic tax for first quarter

Domestic tax revenue reached K1.05 trillion in the first quarter of the 2026-27 financial year. Pay As You Earn (Paye) contributed more than K261 billion, or about 25 percent of the total, according to Malawi Revenue Authority figures.

Why it mattersThe high contribution from Paye shows the government relies heavily on a small labor market to fund public spending.

Malawi to open gold buying centres to curb US$700 million annual smuggling

The government plans to open gold buying centres in major mining areas starting in September. Malawi Mining Investment Company CEO Leonard Kalindekafe estimated that more than US$700 million worth of gold leaves the country illegally each year.

Why it mattersThe new centres provide miners with legal markets to reduce the amount of mineral wealth lost to smuggling.

High Court releases K383 million to Nema Farm Seeds

The High Court of Malawi's Commercial Division lifted a preservation order that had frozen more than K383 million. The court ruled there were no grounds to suspect the funds were linked to criminal activity after an order by the Financial Intelligence Authority.

Why it mattersThe ruling restores access to capital for the businessperson after the funds were previously unavailable due to the freeze.
