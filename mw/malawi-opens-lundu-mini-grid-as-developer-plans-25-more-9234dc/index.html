Malawi opens Lundu mini-grid as developer plans 25 more

The 40 kWp solar facility in Lilongwe has connected some households and businesses, with 25 more customers registered for connection.

Source: Nyasa Times · 2026-11-20 · 1 min read

Malawi's Energy Minister Jean Mathanga has called for more investment in off-grid power as she opened the Lundu mini-grid in Lilongwe. The project, the first in Malawi by Differ Community Power, has 40 kilowatt-peak of solar generation and 102 kilowatt-hours of battery storage.

Differ chief executive Kjetil Røyne said the mini-grid has already connected several households and businesses, with 25 more customers registered. The facility can expand to 120 kWp. Mathanga said off-grid solutions could play a major role in reaching rural communities, as the government aims to raise national electricity access from 25.9% to 70% by 2030.

Financing is the main obstacle to Differ's plans for 25 more mini-grids in Malawi in the coming year, Røyne said. The company is prioritising trading centres and small businesses, and also offers solar home systems, business batteries, electric motorcycles and three-wheelers, and electricity-powered water pumping and irrigation.

Why it mattersThe 25 customers registered for connection and the businesses already linked to the mini-grid stand to gain power as Differ seeks financing for 25 further projects.
