Malawi construction regulator appoints acting chief as CEO moves to university

Board chair Peter Matipwiri says Gerald Khonje’s secondment to teach at Mubas took effect immediately under a letter dated November 23.

Source: Nyasa Times · 2026-11-25 · 1 min read

Gerald Khonje, chief executive of Malawi’s Construction Industry Regulatory Authority (Cira), has been seconded to teach at the Malawi University of Business and Applied Sciences (Mubas). The move took effect immediately, according to a November 23 letter to Cira staff from board chair Peter Matipwiri.

Cira has named Dorothy Nyong’a, its director of regulatory and compliance services, as acting chief executive until further notice. Khonje is among several chief executives deployed to Mubas; the others lead the Central Region Water Board, Blantyre Water Board, Southern Region Water Board and Electricity Supply Corporation of Malawi.

Benedicto Kondowe of the Civil Society Education Coalition said placing chief executives without academic backgrounds in universities undermines standards and distorts human-resource planning. Other senior officials have been sent to Mzuzu University and the University of Malawi, as well as public colleges.

Why it mattersCira’s regulatory and compliance director must lead the construction watchdog as acting chief executive until further notice.
