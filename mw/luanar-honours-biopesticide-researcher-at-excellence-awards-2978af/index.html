LUANAR honours biopesticide researcher at excellence awards

Dr Trust Donga’s natural pest-control formulations have cleared Malawi’s agricultural technology review and are in final registration.

Source: Nyasa Times · 2026-11-22 · 1 min read

Lilongwe University of Agriculture and Natural Resources (LUANAR) honoured students, researchers, innovators and academic departments at its 2025/26 Vice-Chancellor’s Excellence Awards on 20 November 2026. The awards covered student academic performance, innovation, resource mobilisation, research publications and departmental research productivity.

Dr Trust Donga won the Outstanding Excellence in Innovation Award for developing natural biopesticides and agroecological crop-protection methods. LUANAR said the products are intended to help smallholder farmers tackle pests including fall armyworm and insects that damage stored crops. The university said her formulations had been reviewed and cleared by Malawi’s Agricultural Technology Clearing Committee, with support from its Technology Transfer Office.

The products are progressing through final registration with the Pesticides Control Board. Vice-Chancellor Professor Emmanuel Kaunda said the awards recognised work in research, innovation and academic performance.

Why it mattersSmallholder farmers are the intended users of Donga’s pest-control formulations, which are progressing through final registration with the Pesticides Control Board.
