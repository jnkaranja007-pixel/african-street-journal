Malawi’s black-market dollar reaches K5,000 as official rate holds at K1,751

Spot checks in major cities found vendors buying dollars for K4,500 to K4,700 and selling them for K5,000.

Source: Nyasa Times · 2026-11-27 · 1 min read

The US dollar was selling for K5,000 on Malawi’s parallel market, while the official exchange rate remained at K1,751, Nyasa Times reported Friday. The widening gap leaves buyers paying far more through unauthorised vendors than at official institutions.

Spot checks in major cities found vendors buying dollars for between K4,500 and K4,700 before reselling them at K5,000. One forex dealer said he bought $3,000 from Tanzanian merchandise sellers at K4,550 each on Wednesday.

The dealer said customers seeking dollars from him would have to pay K5,000 per dollar.

Why it mattersPeople seeking dollars from parallel-market vendors face a quoted selling price of K5,000 per dollar.
