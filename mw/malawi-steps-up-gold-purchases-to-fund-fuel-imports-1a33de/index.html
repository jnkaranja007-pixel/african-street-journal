Malawi steps up gold purchases to fund fuel imports

Information Minister Shadric Namalomba says the government expects fuel supplies to improve, but cannot give a date for ending the crisis.

Sources: Times Group Malawi and Nyasa Times · 2026-11-18 · 1 min read

Malawi is increasing purchases of locally mined gold to earn foreign exchange for importing fuel and other essential goods, Information Minister Shadric Namalomba said Tuesday. The government expects fuel supplies to improve significantly as the purchases rise.

Speaking on Times Radio’s Kulinji Programme, Namalomba said the government had increased investment in local gold buying. He said the Ministry of Finance had released US$30 million for fuel procurement; Petroleum Importers Limited had also bought fuel.

Namalomba said the government could not give a firm date for ending the fuel crisis. The gold purchases are intended to add foreign exchange for imports, but he gave no timetable for improved supply.

Why it mattersFuel buyers and importers still have no firm end date for the crisis, while the government has released US$30 million for procurement.
