Malawian actress Yvonne Scott wins role in Nigerian film

The creative team behind Ikenga: The Last Festival selected Scott after her performances in Malawi’s entertainment industry.

Source: Malawi24 · 2026-11-28 · 1 min read

Award-winning Malawian actress Yvonne Scott has been selected to appear in Ikenga: The Last Festival, an upcoming Nigerian film. The role gives Scott an opportunity to take her work beyond Malawi.

Dzuwa Brands announced the selection. The creative team behind the Nigerian production chose Scott following her performances in Malawi’s entertainment industry, according to the announcement.

The film is described as upcoming; no release date or further details about Scott’s role were provided in the announcement.
