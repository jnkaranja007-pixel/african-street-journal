Luanar produces certified maize seed on 150 hectares for Malawi subsidy programme

The university plans to buy packaging machinery before supplying the crop to the Agriculture Ministry for distribution under the Farm Inputs Subsidy Programme.

Source: The Nation Malawi · 2026-11-20 · 1 min read

Lilongwe University of Agriculture and Natural Resources (Luanar) produced certified maize seed on 150 hectares for Malawi’s Farm Inputs Subsidy Programme (Fisp), the university’s vice-chancellor said. The production took place on land within Illovo Sugar’s estate at Dwangwa, in Nkhotakota District.

Vice-Chancellor Emmanuel Kaunda said Luanar used its holding company to grow the seed after an intervention by the agriculture minister. The university is planning to acquire packaging machinery, then supply the seed to the Agriculture Ministry for distribution through Fisp.

Kaunda said Luanar had acquired 750 hectares in Kasungu for expanded seed production and commercial agriculture. The university is also entering commercial macadamia and coffee production, which he said could earn foreign exchange through exports.

Why it mattersLuanar must secure packaging machinery before it can process the 150-hectare crop for distribution to Fisp beneficiaries.
