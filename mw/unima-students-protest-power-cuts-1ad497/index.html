UNIMA students protest power cuts

Students say ongoing blackouts are hindering exam preparation and causing security fears.

Source: Malawi24 · 2026-11-11 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

University of Malawi (UNIMA) students are frustrated by continuous electricity blackouts that disrupt their studies and assignment completion. Collidah Kachale, a fourth-year student, stated that power outages prevent students from charging devices needed for research and group discussions, impacting their academic performance.

The disruptions are particularly worrying during the examination period, with students like Kachale appealing to the Minister of Education, Bright Nsaka, to intervene. Another student, Staphel Nyirenda, highlighted security concerns in dark areas and difficulties in preparing meals due to the frequent power interruptions.

Students are struggling to study adequately and fear that the blackouts will negatively affect their examination results. They have urged authorities to address the persistent electricity outages urgently.

Why it mattersUniversity students in Malawi are facing academic difficulties and security risks due to prolonged electricity outages.
