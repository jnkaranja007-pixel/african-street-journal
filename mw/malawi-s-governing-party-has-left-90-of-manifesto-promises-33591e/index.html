Malawi's governing party has left 90% of manifesto promises unstarted, assessment finds

An assessment of the Democratic Progressive Party's 2025-30 manifesto was published a year after the party won power in Malawi's September 2025 elections.

Source: Times Group Malawi · 2026-11-19 · 1 min read

An assessment of Malawi's government found that 90% of promises in the Democratic Progressive Party's 2025-30 election manifesto had not begun, Times Group Malawi reported. The finding comes a year after the party took power following the September 16, 2025 presidential, parliamentary and local government elections.

The assessment measured the government's performance against the commitments in the manifesto, the party's programme for the 2025-30 term. The report does not say which promises have been started or what the government plans next.

The report gives no further figures, timelines or official response.

Why it mattersThe government faces public scrutiny of its election pledges, with nine in 10 still unstarted one year into its term.
