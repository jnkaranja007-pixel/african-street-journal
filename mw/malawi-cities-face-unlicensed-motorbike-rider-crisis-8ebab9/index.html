Malawi cities face unlicensed motorbike rider crisis

Motorcycle taxis are vital for transport and livelihoods in Lilongwe, Blantyre, Mzuzu and Zomba.

Source: Malawi24 · 2026-11-02 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Unlicensed motorcycle riders are causing serious road safety concerns in Malawi's cities. Motorcycle taxis, known as kabaza, are a key part of urban transport in Lilongwe, Blantyre, Mzuzu, and Zomba. These bikes provide affordable mobility and support thousands of jobs.

The growing number of riders without licenses is raising questions about regulatory enforcement and owner responsibility. Road safety advocates are worried about the potential for accidents and lack of accountability on the roads.

The Malawi24 article highlights the dual nature of kabaza services: essential for daily commutes and income, yet operating outside of established licensing regulations.
