Malawi starts K131 billion upgrade of 31-kilometre Chitipa-Ilomba road

The bitumen-standard project is due to take 24 months and is intended to improve access to markets and cut transport costs.

Sources: The Nation Malawi and Malawi24 · 2026-11-24 · 1 min read

Malawi has launched rehabilitation of the 31-kilometre Chitipa-Ilomba Road, a project valued at K131 billion. Transport and Public Works Minister Jappie Mhango announced the works on Saturday at Lwakwa Primary School in Chitipa District.

The road will be upgraded from earth to bitumen standards by China Civil Engineering Corporation, Mhango said. Roads Authority board chairperson Witness Kuotcha said the authority would intensify monitoring and supervision to support quality work and timely completion.

Mhango said the road was expected to improve access to markets, schools and health facilities, while easing movement of people and goods. He said it would strengthen cross-border trade and Malawi’s economic links with Tanzania and the wider East African region.

Why it mattersFarmers, traders and businesses using the route are due to gain improved access to markets when the K131 billion upgrade is completed within 24 months.
