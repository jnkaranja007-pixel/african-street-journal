Prophet Hara's book launch raises K70 million

Renowned Prophet Justice Hara launched four books at a Lilongwe event that also celebrated his birthday.

Source: Times Group Malawi · 2026-10-21 · 1 min read

Prophet Justice Hara's book launch and birthday celebration at the Pearl Convention Centre in Lilongwe generated K70 million over two days. The event, held from Wednesday through Thursday, attracted religious leaders and creatives. Among the notable attendees, Prophet Shepherd Bushiri purchased a book for K8 million, and Pastor Khumbo Joram, founder of the Golden Nation Church, bought another for K7.5 million.

Bushiri described Hara's books as "treasures of wisdom" that will guide believers, emphasizing that ministry involves documenting truth for the future. Joram stated his purchase was to encourage Hara's continued production of knowledge for the church, noting the current need for such resources. The launched titles included "The Ever Increasing Success," "Who is Man?" and "Destiny is a Mystery."

Hara, also known as "The Game Changer," explained that the books stem from revelations he has received since secondary school. He expressed gratitude for the grace enabling him to share these insights, which he said teach about enduring success in Christ, human identity and authority, and encourage believers to pursue greater vision. The event featured musical performances by Joe Ikon, Onesimus, and Kamuzu Barracks Gospel Singers.

Why it mattersThe book launch generated K70 million, indicating a significant financial transaction and interest in the published works.
