Malawi disaster agency has 109 327MT of maize for El Niño response

Dodma says that is about a third of the 342 696MT required, as 6.5 million people face potential food insecurity.

Source: The Nation Malawi · 2026-11-20 · 1 min read

Malawi’s Department of Disaster Management Affairs (Dodma) has secured 109 327MT of maize against the 342 696MT required for its El Niño response, leaving a shortfall of 233 369 metric tonnes. The department said 6.5 million people face potential food insecurity.

Commissioner Wilson Moleni gave the figures at a briefing to the Parliamentary Women’s Caucus in Lilongwe on Tuesday. Dodma has also mobilised K164 billion of the K488 billion needed for its revised preparedness plan, leaving a gap of about K324 billion. It has identified Nsanje, Mangochi and Zomba for assistance to vulnerable households before the anticipated weather shocks.

The National Food Reserve Agency is expected to procure an additional 77 000MT, which would raise projected stocks to 186 327MT. The combined maize requirement, including lean-season interventions expected to affect an additional 2.6 million people, is 440 583MT; the planned procurement would leave a 254 256MT deficit.

Why it mattersHouseholds in Nsanje, Mangochi and Zomba are identified for anticipatory support, but Dodma has mobilised only K164 billion of the K488 billion required for its plan.
