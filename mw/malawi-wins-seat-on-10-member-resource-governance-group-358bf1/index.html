Malawi wins seat on 10-member resource governance group

Frank Tumpale Mwenifumbo says the place will help promote Malawi's rare earths to foreign investors and shape mineral policy.

Source: Nyasa Times · 2026-11-23 · 1 min read

Malawi has been elected to a 10-member working group of the Organisation of African, Caribbean and Pacific States (OACPS), a bloc of former colonies in Africa, the Caribbean and the Pacific, focused on resource governance, local processing and value addition. Delegation leader Frank Tumpale Mwenifumbo said the country secured the seat at meetings in Brussels, Belgium, from November 5 to 13, 2026.

Mwenifumbo, a legislator for Karonga Central, said Malawi would use the group to promote itself internationally as a rare earths destination and attract foreign direct investment. He said the seat would also let Malawi press its interests in the bloc's agenda with the European Union on minerals, energy production, industrialisation and value addition.

Mining expert Grain Malunga urged the government to engage the private sector for Malawi's participation to bear fruit. Members also discussed financing the OACPS Parliamentary Assembly independently and sustainably, reducing its dependence on external funding.

Why it mattersMalawi's government must work with private companies to turn its seat into investment and local mineral processing, as mining expert Grain Malunga urged.
