Bullets and Wanderers win FDH Bank Premiership matches

FCB Nyasa Big Bullets beat Masters 2-1 and Mighty Wanderers defeated Goshen City Dedza Dynamos 2-0.

Source: Times Group Malawi · 2026-11-02 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

FCB Nyasa Big Bullets and Mighty Wanderers secured victories in the FDH Bank Premiership on Sunday. Bullets earned a 2-1 win against Masters at Bingu National Stadium in Lilongwe. Mighty Wanderers defeated Goshen City Dedza Dynamos 2-0 at Mpira Stadium in Blantyre.

The wins keep both teams' paces in the league title race. Bullets claimed maximum points in an entertaining contest against Masters Football Club. Wanderers secured their win at Mpira Stadium.

The FDH Bank Premiership title race has tightened following these results and other fixtures. Both FCB Nyasa Big Bullets and Mighty Wanderers picked up crucial wins.
