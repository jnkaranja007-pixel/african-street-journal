Tunisia’s SECADENORD develops AI tool to cut pumping costs

Aqua Min 360 analyses water volumes and electricity use, recommending lower-tariff pumping periods and the most efficient pumps.

Source: La Presse de Tunisie · 2026-11-25 · 1 min read

Tunisia’s Société d’exploitation du canal et des adductions des eaux du Nord (SECADENORD) has developed Aqua Min 360, an artificial-intelligence application to manage water resources and reduce electricity use at its pumping stations. The tool was examined at a working session chaired by Agriculture Minister Ezzeddine Ben Cheikh on Monday.

The application continuously analyses pumped-water volumes and electricity consumption, then recommends when to pump, how many pumps to operate and which equipment performs best. It also tracks water quality, pump condition and availability. SECADENORD’s stations at Sidi El Barrak, Tarf, Fondouk Jedid and Béjaoua are among the company’s most energy-intensive facilities.

Aqua Min 360 uses a national artificial-intelligence model to identify lower-priced electricity periods, including at night. Participants said its development should continue and its use be extended to the relevant administrations and technical bodies.

Why it mattersSECADENORD must continue developing the application and extend its use to the administrations and technical bodies concerned.
