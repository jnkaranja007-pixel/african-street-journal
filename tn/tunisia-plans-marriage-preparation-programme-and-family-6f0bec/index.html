Tunisia plans marriage preparation programme and family mediation body

The family ministry will coordinate training with three ministries and prepare a bill to create an institution for resolving marital disputes.

Sources: Webdo and La Presse de Tunisie · 2026-11-24 · 1 min read

Tunisia’s family ministry is preparing a national programme to brief people planning to marry on family responsibilities and relationships. Family Minister Asma Jebri announced the plan during a parliamentary session on the ministry’s 2027 budget. The programme is to be developed with the interior, justice and health ministries.

The ministry plans video awareness campaigns for future spouses and training for officials in 74 municipalities in the second region before the end of December 2026. Municipalities and notaries are among those expected to take part, according to the report.

Jebri also said the government plans a bill to establish a family mediation institution, citing a rise in divorce cases. The ministry’s 2027 budget is 331.690 million dinars, up 15.60% from 2026; children’s services account for 71% of the budget.

Why it mattersOfficials in 74 municipalities are due to receive training before the end of December 2026 to support the marriage preparation programme.
