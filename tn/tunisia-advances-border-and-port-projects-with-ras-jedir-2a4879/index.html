Tunisia advances border and port projects, with Ras Jedir work due in December

Melloula is 70% complete, while a 252 million-dinar project at Ras Jedir is scheduled to start next month.

Source: Webdo · 2026-11-21 · 1 min read

Tunisia’s transport minister, Rachid Amri, presented infrastructure updates to lawmakers on Thursday, 19 November, during scrutiny of the 2027 transport budget. Work at the Melloula border post is 70% complete and is estimated to cost 52 million dinars; construction at Ras Jedir is scheduled to begin in December 2026, with 252 million dinars allocated.

At Radès port, the modernisation programme includes 12 port tractors, two truck weighbridges, three large forklifts and three unloading cranes for the Tunisian Stevedoring and Handling Company. It also includes digitising port services. At Zarzis, the ministry has opened a tender to dredge the access channel and basin, with bids due by 27 November 2026.

A contract to update studies for the planned deep-water port at Enfidha was scheduled for signing on 23 November. The selected consultants are to prepare tender documents for the construction company, while the ministry seeks financing from several lenders. Tunis-Carthage airport expansion and a city-centre link remain at the study stage.

Why it mattersPort operators and border users are awaiting projects at different stages, including a Ras Jedir construction start scheduled for December 2026 and Zarzis bids due by 27 November.
