German firms to explore Tunisia water partnerships

A delegation of German companies will visit Tunisia from November 9-11 to discuss water and environmental technologies.

Source: Webdo · 2026-11-08 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

German companies specializing in water and environmental technologies will visit Tunisia from November 9 to 11, 2026. The mission, organized by the Tunisian-German Chamber of Commerce and Industry (AHK Tunisie), includes a conference on water management and meetings with Tunisian economic actors. This initiative is supported by the German Federal Ministry for Economic Affairs and Energy to promote exports of eco-technologies.

A conference on water management is scheduled for November 10 in Tunis, where German firms will present their technologies. This will be followed by professional exchanges and bilateral meetings on November 11. The goal is to foster commercial contacts and explore cooperation in water and environmental protection, involving the Technical University of Berlin.

The mission occurs as Tunisia faces increasing pressure on its water resources, requiring significant investment in hydraulic infrastructure and management technologies. This visit follows a recent partnership between Mabrouka Holding and UMIDA for underground irrigation technology, highlighting interest in technological solutions for water constraints.

Why it mattersTunisian businesses and the country's water sector will engage with German companies to find technological solutions for water management challenges.
