Tunisia forecasts cooler temperatures and northern rain on Friday

Highs will range from 20°C to 25°C in the north and centre, while the sea will be very rough in the north.

Sources: La Presse de Tunisie and Webdo · 2026-11-20 · 1 min read

Tunisia’s weather service forecast a notable temperature drop on Friday, 20 November, with morning rain in the far north and relatively strong winds near the coast. Maximum temperatures are expected to range from 20°C to 25°C in the north and centre, and reach about 18°C in highland areas.

In the south, highs will range from 25°C to 29°C. The far north is expected to have periods of very cloudy skies and some rain in the morning, followed by cloudy spells across most regions. Winds will come from the northwest in the north and centre and from the east in the south.

The sea is forecast to be very rough in the north and slightly rough to rough along the rest of the coast; inland winds will be light to moderate.

Why it mattersPeople travelling or working along Tunisia’s northern coast face very rough seas on Friday, while coastal winds will be relatively strong.
