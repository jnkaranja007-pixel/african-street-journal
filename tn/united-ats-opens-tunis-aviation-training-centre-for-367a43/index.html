United ATS opens Tunis aviation training centre for regional clients

The company says local training, priced in Tunisian dinars, can replace services that aviation customers currently buy abroad.

Source: Kapitalis · 2026-11-28 · 1 min read

United ATS Tunisia has opened an aviation training centre in the Lac district of Tunis, aiming to serve local and regional demand for air traffic control and other specialist skills. The company says Tunisia could become a regional training hub, with potential to attract clients from African and French-speaking markets.

Sofiane Bejaoui, the centre’s chief instructor, said its courses would be offered locally at competitive costs and in Tunisian dinars, replacing services now purchased abroad. He said clients from Iraq, Libya and Kuwait already come to Tunisia, and that the country has qualified instructors and aviation professionals with international experience.

Ghofran Ben Ameur, United ATS Tunisia’s training coordinator, said shortages of qualified air navigation and air traffic control staff affect Europe, the United States and other countries. The company has also delivered technical training at international centres and for aviation businesses in Libya and Egypt.

Why it mattersAviation trainees and customers from Iraq, Libya and Kuwait can access instruction in Tunisia, where United ATS says services will be priced in dinars.
