UTICA seeks zero duties and VAT on solar equipment

The Tunisian business union proposed the exemption as lawmakers review the 2027 finance bill, alongside a digital one-stop shop for renewable-energy investment.

Source: Kapitalis · 2026-11-23 · 1 min read

Tunisia’s Tunisian Union of Industry, Trade and Handicrafts (UTICA), a business association, has proposed cutting customs duties and value-added tax (VAT) to 0% on solar equipment, batteries and inverters under the 2027 finance bill. The proposals were presented during a joint hearing on Friday 20 November 2026, as lawmakers examined the bill.

UTICA representatives also called for a digital one-stop shop linked to the government presidency to simplify renewable-energy investment procedures. They said solar projects were about 2 800 megawatts behind planned capacity, blaming complex administration and long waits for permits.

Lawmakers said tax relief should lower final equipment prices while protecting domestic industry and technical standards. They called for a joint hearing with relevant government bodies to clarify energy policy choices.

Why it mattersSolar equipment importers and renewable-energy investors would face lower import taxes if lawmakers adopt the proposed exemption in the 2027 finance bill.
