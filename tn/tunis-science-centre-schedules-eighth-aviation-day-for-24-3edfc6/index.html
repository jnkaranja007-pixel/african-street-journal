Tunis science centre schedules eighth aviation day for 24 December

The public event will feature aviation professionals, flight simulators and activities aimed especially at young people.

Source: Kapitalis · 2026-11-25 · 1 min read

The Cité des Sciences à Tunis, a science centre, will hold its eighth Aviation Day on 24 December under the theme “Les Ailes de la Cité’26”. The event is open to the public, with particular attention to young people.

The centre is organising the programme with Tunisia’s defence and interior ministries, the Centre of Excellence in Aeronautical Industry Trades and the Tunisian Aeronautics Association, alongside other institutions and businesses. Organisers have scheduled discussions led by aviation professionals, exhibitions, a documentary on the history of aviation in Tunisia, competitions and flight-simulator sessions.

The event is also being held in connection with International Civil Aviation Day, which falls on 7 December. The organisers say the programme is intended to introduce young people to aviation careers and technology.

Why it mattersStudents and young visitors can try flight simulators and question aviation professionals directly, with the day intended to spark interest in aviation careers.
