Tunisia solidarity event backs Chaima Issa and political prisoners

The Tunis gathering is part of a campaign running through 10 December, after rights groups said Issa began a hunger strike on 16 November.

Source: Kapitalis · 2026-11-26 · 1 min read

Organizers scheduled a solidarity event for women prisoners at the Nawaat association’s headquarters in Tunis on Thursday, 26 November, at 17:00. The gathering is part of the United Nations’ 16 Days of Activism against Gender-Based Violence campaign and focuses on detained women’s rights and access to a fair trial.

The event will include testimony, readings and remembrance, with organizers expressing support for Chaima Issa and other political and opinion prisoners. On 19 November, human rights and feminist groups announced a support committee for Issa, a National Salvation Front activist, and said she had been on an open-ended hunger strike since 16 November to protest her continued detention.

The groups said authorities arrested Issa on 29 November 2025 during a march for freedoms. They demanded her immediate release; solidarity activities are scheduled to continue through 10 December, Human Rights Day.

Why it mattersIssa’s support committee and the organizers must sustain public solidarity through 10 December while she remains detained and on hunger strike, according to the groups.
