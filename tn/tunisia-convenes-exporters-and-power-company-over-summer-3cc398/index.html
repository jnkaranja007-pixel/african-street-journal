Tunisia convenes exporters and power company over summer outages

Nearly 90 export manufacturers are meeting the state power company and officials in Tunis to discuss supply security and prepare for summer 2027.

Source: La Presse de Tunisie · 2026-11-20 · 1 min read

Tunisia’s mixed-chambers council brought nearly 90 Tunisian and foreign export manufacturers, the state power company Steg and public officials together in Tunis on Thursday to discuss electricity supply security after frequent outages during summer 2026. The talks are intended to identify the difficulties businesses faced and plan for industrial energy needs in 2027.

The organiser said the summer disruptions interrupted production, put equipment at risk and delayed deliveries, with consequences for exporters’ competitiveness. Representatives of the Industry Ministry, Economy Ministry, renewable-energy sector, textile federation and automotive association were also invited.

Interim Industry Minister Slah Zouari told parliament he would seek to avoid summer outages. He said a programme would reduce electricity demand from 6 000 megawatts to 5 700 megawatts, a saving of 300 megawatts. The meeting’s organisers said they wanted better coordination between industry and the authorities.

Why it mattersExport manufacturers faced production interruptions, equipment risks and delivery delays during the 2026 summer outages, and are discussing how to secure supply for 2027.
