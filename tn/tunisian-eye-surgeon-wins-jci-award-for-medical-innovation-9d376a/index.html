Tunisian eye surgeon wins JCI award for medical innovation

Yassine Belghith was named among the 10 young global figures of 2026 for work expanding specialist eye surgery at a public hospital in Gabès.

Source: Webdo · 2026-11-20 · 1 min read

Tunisian ophthalmic surgeon Yassine Belghith won the 2026 Junior Chamber International (JCI) TOYP award for medical innovation at the organisation’s world congress in Clark, the Philippines, on 16 November. He was also selected among the 10 young personalities of the year.

Belghith, an assistant professor at Sfax Faculty of Medicine, has helped build specialist eye surgery at Mohamed Ben Sassi University Hospital in Gabès. He has performed more than 1500 operations in two years and helped establish a dedicated unit to offer procedures requiring specialised equipment and expertise in the public sector. The hospital announced a first cornea transplant in June 2025, following an operation using amniotic tissue for a deep corneal ulcer in September 2024.

Belghith also trains resident doctors and joins medical caravans in more than 20 rural areas. Two other Tunisians received JCI TOYP awards: Emna Mizouni for cultural achievement and Khadija Maalej for childhood, peace and human rights.

Why it mattersPatients in southern Tunisia have access to specialist public eye surgery at Gabès, where Belghith has performed more than 1500 operations in two years.
