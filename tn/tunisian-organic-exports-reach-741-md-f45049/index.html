Tunisian organic exports reach 741 MD

Organic olive oil and dates drove a 32.7% year-on-year increase in export revenue for Tunisia's organic products.

Source: Webdo · 2026-10-23 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Tunisian organic product exports generated 740.92 million dinars (MD) in the first nine months of 2026, a 32.7% rise from the previous year.

Organic olive oil accounted for 90.1% of this revenue, with 51,290 tonnes exported, while organic dates contributed 105.8 MD from 9,843.6 tonnes.

These organic goods were sold in 46 countries, with Italy, the United States, and Spain being the top destinations for olive oil, and Germany leading for dates.

Why it mattersTunisian farmers and exporters earned more revenue from organic products sold abroad.
