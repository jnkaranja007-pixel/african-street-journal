Tunisia plans 32.6 billion dinars investment for 2027

The country aims for a 14.1% increase in total investments, with 1.2 billion dinars allocated to road infrastructure.

Source: Webdo · 2026-10-31 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Tunisia plans to increase its total investment volume to 32.6 billion dinars in 2027, up from 28.6 billion expected in 2026. This represents a 14.1% progression, with the investment rate projected to reach 16.4% of GDP. Foreign direct investment is estimated to rise by 20% to 4.8 billion dinars.

A significant portion, 1.2 billion dinars, is earmarked for road infrastructure, including highway projects like the Tunis-Jelma route and the doubling of national road 13. Agricultural investments are expected to reach 1.532 billion dinars, a 7.1% increase. Industrial investments are set to grow by 17.8%, with 180 hectares of industrial zones planned for completion.

Energy projects, such as the Sousse combined cycle power plant and solar facilities, are also included. The government anticipates a 8.8% growth in services, driven by transport investments. These figures are projections, and their realization depends on the execution of scheduled projects.

Why it mattersTunisia's planned investments in infrastructure, agriculture, industry, and energy will affect businesses and economic growth.
