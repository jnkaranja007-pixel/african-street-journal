Orange Tunisie Uses Sport to Teach Teens Online Safety

The company organized a day of games and sports to educate 120 adolescents about digital risks.

Source: Webdo · 2026-10-21 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X

Orange Tunisie, in partnership with the Tunisian School and University Sports Federation, hosted its second #ForGoodConnectionsDay on October 17, 2026. The event in Tunis gathered 120 adolescents, aged 11 to 15, for sports and games aimed at promoting safer and more responsible internet use. Activities included football matches, olympiads, and card games designed to help participants identify online risks and develop appropriate responses.

The initiative connects the values of sportsmanship, such as respect and solidarity, to online interactions, emphasizing that these principles apply to digital exchanges. This approach aims to equip young people with practical guidance for navigating cyberbullying, inappropriate content, and hyperconnectivity. Since June 2025, the #ForGoodConnections program has reached nearly 10,000 beneficiaries across Tunisia, including students, parents, and teachers.

The event highlighted the importance of collaboration between businesses and educational bodies in preventing digital risks. Orange Tunisie employees volunteered alongside federation supervisors and children of Orange Tunisie staff. This shared responsibility approach underscores the growing need for awareness as digital tools become central to adolescents' daily lives.

Why it mattersAdolescents aged 11 to 15 are directly affected by learning to navigate online risks through sports and games.
