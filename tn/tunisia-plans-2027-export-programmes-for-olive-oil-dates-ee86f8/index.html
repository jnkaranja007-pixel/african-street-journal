Tunisia plans 2027 export programmes for olive oil, dates and handicrafts

Trade Minister Samir Abid also outlined a plan to promote Tunisian olive oil in the United States, Canada and some Asian markets.

Sources: La Presse de Tunisie and Webdo · 2026-11-21 · 1 min read

Tunisia’s Trade and Export Development Ministry plans targeted 2027 programmes to support exports of olive oil, organic olive oil, dates and handicrafts, Trade Minister Samir Abid told lawmakers in Tunis on Saturday. He said the programmes are intended to improve the trade balance and expand the products’ presence abroad.

The ministry also plans to reactivate its Export Promotion Fund, create a platform to assist exporting businesses and complete the digitisation of export procedures. Abid said a plan to promote Tunisian olive oil targets the United States, Canada and some Asian markets.

For imports, the ministry plans to match purchases to domestic needs and tighten controls, while activating trade-defence measures to protect local industry from unfair practices, Abid said. The measures form part of the ministry’s 2027 programme.

Why it mattersExporters of olive oil, dates and handicrafts are slated for targeted support in 2027, alongside a fund and platform intended to assist export businesses.
