Tunisia forecasts partly cloudy skies and 30°C highs

Temperatures will range from 23°C to 30°C across the country on Friday, October 23, 2026.

Source: La Presse de Tunisie · 2026-10-23 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Tunisia will experience partly cloudy skies on Friday, October 23, 2026, with sunny spells expected during the day. Maximum temperatures will reach 30°C in the south, while northern and central regions will see highs between 23°C and 27°C. The wind will blow from the southeast with low to moderate intensity.

The national meteorological institute forecasts generally calm seas, becoming locally agitated on some coasts. Overnight, cloudy skies are expected nationwide with scattered showers on southern coastal areas. Strong winds are predicted near the coast, with moderate winds inland.

Nighttime temperatures are forecast between 16°C and 21°C, dropping to around 13°C in the western mountainous zones. The sea will be particularly agitated north of the country overnight.

Why it mattersTunisians will experience varied weather conditions with mild to warm temperatures across the country.
