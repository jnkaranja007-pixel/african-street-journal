Club Africain beats CAB 3-2 in delayed match

Hamza Khadraoui scored twice and added an assist to help Club Africain secure a vital win.

Source: Webdo · 2026-10-29 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Club Africain secured a dramatic 3-2 victory over Club Athlétique Bizertin (CAB) on Thursday, October 29, in a rescheduled sixth-round Ligue 1 match. The win allows Club Africain to improve its standing in the league table. Hamza Khadraoui was instrumental, scoring two goals and providing an assist. This victory follows Club Africain's qualification for the African Champions League group stage.

The match against CAB, who had won their previous game 3-1, ended a positive run for the visitors. In another delayed fixture, Club Sportif Sfaxien (CSS) and Espérance de Tunis played to a goalless draw (0-0) at the Taïeb Mhiri stadium. Before this round, Espérance de Tunis had 12 points from five matches, while CSS had eight points from four games.

The third delayed match saw Stade Tunisien defeat Espérance de Zarzis 1-0. This win marks a return to form for Stade Tunisien after a previous loss. The results mean neither CSS nor Espérance de Tunis made a decisive move in the race for the top positions in the league.

Why it mattersTunisian football fans will be interested in the performance of their clubs in the national league.
