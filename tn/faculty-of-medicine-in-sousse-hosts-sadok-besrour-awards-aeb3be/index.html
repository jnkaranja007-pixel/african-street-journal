Faculty of Medicine in Sousse hosts Sadok Besrour awards

Two researchers will receive 5,000 Canadian dollars each during the ceremony on Friday, 20 November 2026.

Source: Kapitalis · 2026-11-17 · 1 min read

The Faculty of Medicine of Sousse will host the official ceremony for the Dr Sadok Besrour Excellence Awards in medicine and health sciences on Friday, 20 November 2026, according to Kapitalis. Created in 2007, the prizes recognize teachers and researchers contributing to medical research and health training in Tunisia through a partnership involving the Dr Sadok Besrour Foundation, the University of Montreal, and the Tunisian Ministry of Higher Education and Scientific Research.

Two distinctions will be awarded during the event, each carrying a value of 5 000 Canadian dollars. One prize honors excellence in health sciences pedagogy, while the other focuses on fundamental research and its impact. The ceremony will also feature plenary conferences, including a presentation by Professor Latifa Lassouad from the University of Sousse and a conference from the Centre for Pedagogy applied to Health Sciences of the University of Montreal.

The event forms part of international health partnerships, following a University of Montreal agreement announced in January 2025 to host Tunisian medical academics for specialized training. In March 2026, the University of Oxford announced 2 million pounds sterling in support from the Dr Sadok Besrour Foundation to strengthen primary health care research and leadership in Tunisia.
