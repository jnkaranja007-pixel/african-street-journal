EU-backed programme mobilises €52.3 million for 396 Tunisian businesses

The €170 million Tunisia Economic Recovery programme has reached companies in 23 governorates, with financing still open to applicants.

Sources: Webdo and Leaders · 2026-11-27 · 1 min read

Tunisia’s EU-backed Tunisia Economic Recovery programme has mobilised €52.3 million for 396 businesses across 23 governorates, Webdo reported on Friday. The programme has a total allocation of €170 million and is aimed mainly at small and medium-sized enterprises seeking funds to invest or expand.

The support includes long-term financing, grants, guarantees and technical assistance, according to Webdo. The programme also targets young entrepreneurs, women and priority governorates as it seeks to narrow regional gaps in access to finance.

Financing remains available to Tunisian businesses proposing investment, expansion or development projects. The programme is intended to support lasting job creation and strengthen the country's business base.

Why it mattersThe 396 businesses already funded share €52.3 million, while Tunisian companies can still submit investment, expansion or development projects.
