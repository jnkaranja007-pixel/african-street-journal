Tunis Pasteur Institute amphitheatre rehabilitation begins

The historic science venue will receive a new design and modern equipment while preserving its architectural identity.

Source: Leaders · 2026-11-16 · 1 min read

The Institut Pasteur de Tunis began rehabilitation and modernization work on its amphitheatre on November 16, 2026. The Fondation Dr Sadok Besrour is supporting the project in partnership with the UNESCO.

The institution aims to offer researchers a space for exchange that matches their standards and the building's history. The project includes a new design and equipment adapted to technological advancements.

Dr Sadok Besrour's conviction that the future is built where knowledge is shared underpins the renovation. The amphitheatre is described as one of Tunisia's most iconic scientific locations.

Why it mattersResearchers at the Institut Pasteur de Tunis will gain a modernized space for sharing knowledge starting November 16, 2026.
