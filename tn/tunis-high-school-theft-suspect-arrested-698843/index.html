Tunis high school theft suspect arrested

Police recovered a stolen television, projector, and computer from a suspect in Jebel Lahmar.

Source: Kapitalis · 2026-11-07 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Tunisian police arrested a suspect on Friday, November 6, 2026, in connection with a theft at a Tunis high school. The individual, who resides in Jebel Lahmar, is accused of stealing equipment during the night of November 4 to 5.

The stolen items included a large television, a projector, and a computer, according to a source from the National Security Directorate (DGSN). The DGSN source stated that the investigation led to the arrest of the main suspect and the recovery of all stolen property.

The arrested individual has been taken into custody as the investigation continues.
