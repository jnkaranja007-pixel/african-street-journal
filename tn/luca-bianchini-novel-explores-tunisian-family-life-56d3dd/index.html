Luca Bianchini novel explores Tunisian family life

The book "Le ragazze di Tunisi" details Sicilian emigration to Tunisia and a mother's struggles in the 1959-1961 period.

Source: Kapitalis · 2026-10-29 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Luca Bianchini's novel "Le ragazze di Tunisi" portrays the lives of the Brancata family, Sicilian emigrants in cosmopolitan Tunisia between 1959 and 1961. The story follows Maria, a seamstress raising three daughters as Tunisia approaches independence. Bianchini's work captures the city of Tunis suspended between past charm and the dawn of independence, weaving together first loves and the impending farewells.

The novel focuses on Maria, a 38-year-old seamstress in the Borgel neighborhood, who supports her family while her husband works in the countryside. Her eldest daughter, Anna, 16, attends a good school thanks to her uncle Jojo and dreams of love, while her sisters Vitina and Pupetta have their own preoccupations. Anna finds companionship with her French friend Marinette, who introduces her to cinema and fashion.

Bianchini researched his mother's family history for the novel, noting that the Brancatas, like many Sicilian families in Tunisia, sought opportunity. The family lived harmoniously with French residents, Tunisians, Jews, and Maltese. The narrative captures a period of transition, with the approaching independence of Tunisia signaling an imminent departure for many.

Why it mattersThe novel "Le ragazze di Tunisi" by Luca Bianchini explores the lives of Sicilian emigrant families in Tunisia during a period of significant political change.
