Tunisia lists 11,271 state properties, targets full inventory by 2030

The state property minister says the register will support better protection and management, while legal disputes complicate the status of some land.

Source: Webdo · 2026-11-21 · 1 min read

Tunisia had catalogued 11,271 state properties by the end of September 2026, and aims to complete a full inventory by 2030. State Property and Land Affairs Minister Wajdi Hedhili gave the figures on Friday, 20 November, during a session on his ministry’s 2027 budget.

Hedhili said the ministry wants a reliable database to establish the properties’ status and improve their management and use. He called for more staff and equipment for the Land Court, which handles land regularisation and clarifies ownership rights.

The minister said periodic inspections of state properties would continue and called for legal reforms and tougher penalties for encroachment. He also urged closer coordination with governors and stronger capacity for the State Litigation institution to defend public property.

Why it mattersThe ministry must establish the legal status of state properties as it works toward a complete register by 2030.
