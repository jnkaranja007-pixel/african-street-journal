Pasteur Institute of Tunis starts amphitheatre renovation

Backed by the Dr Sadok Besrour Foundation and Unesco, the work will upgrade audiovisual systems, ventilation and accessibility while preserving the building’s character.

Source: Kapitalis · 2026-11-19 · 1 min read

The Pasteur Institute of Tunis began renovating and modernising its amphitheatre on 16 November, with support from the Dr Sadok Besrour Foundation and Unesco. The project will update the venue while retaining its architectural identity.

The institute said planned work includes improvements to acoustics, electrical and audiovisual systems, ventilation, waterproofing, fire safety and accessibility. The changes are intended to make the hall better suited to researchers, students, doctoral candidates and scientific partners.

The amphitheatre hosts training, conferences, seminars, thesis defences and international meetings. The institute, which conducts research on diseases affecting Tunisia and the region and produces vaccines and sera for national needs, said technical studies will guide the renovation.

Why it mattersResearchers, students, doctoral candidates and scientific partners who use the hall will receive upgraded audiovisual, safety and accessibility facilities.
