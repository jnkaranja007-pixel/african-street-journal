Tunisia Telecom and Ericsson honour green hackathon winners

About 100 participants competed over two days at the Tunisian-Swedish event for gaming concepts linked to sustainability and recycling.

Source: La Presse de Tunisie · 2026-11-26 · 1 min read

Tunisia Telecom, Ericsson Tunisia and Sweden’s embassy in Tunisia closed the Tunisian-Swedish Games and Innovation Days at the El-Ghazala technology park, presenting awards to winners of its green hackathon. The two-day competition put about 100 participants forward to develop gaming concepts for sustainability solutions.

A statement distributed to journalists said the event was open to young project creators and explored gaming’s potential for economic development, training and international links. Sihem Mhissen, a senior Tunisie Telecom technology and information-systems official, said the operator’s contribution included developing digital skills and improving connection performance.

Swedish ambassador Cecila Wramsten said the country’s game studios export to hundreds of millions of users worldwide, while video games account for 3% of Sweden’s services exports. The event also brought together the Ministry of Communication Technologies, according to the report.

Why it mattersTunisia Telecom and Ericsson Tunisia are expected to support young digital creators after about 100 people competed in the two-day hackathon.
