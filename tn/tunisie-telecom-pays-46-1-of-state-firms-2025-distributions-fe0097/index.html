Tunisie Telecom pays 46.1% of state firms’ 2025 distributions

The telecom operator contributed 49.7 million dinars of 107.9 million, while total distributions fell 73% from 2024.

Source: Webdo · 2026-11-25 · 1 min read

Tunisie Telecom paid 49.7 million dinars in dividends for 2025, accounting for 46.1% of the 107.9 million dinars in dividends and surpluses recorded for the Tunisian state from public companies. The figures cover the 2025 financial year.

An analysis by TeraFinances, published on 23 November and based on a report attached to the 2027 draft finance law, ranks the National Agricultural Bank second with 25.9 million dinars and the Tunisian Banking Company third with 22.2 million. Together, the three firms supplied 90.7% of the total. State distributions were 107.9 million dinars in 2025, down from 398.9 million in 2024 and 605.3 million in 2023.

The report’s 2025 figures exclude dividends from the Tunisian Petroleum Activities Company and the National Petroleum Distribution Company, both major contributors in 2024. The total remains provisional.

Why it mattersTunisie Telecom and the two leading public banks account for 90.7% of the state’s recorded 2025 distributions, with the total still provisional.
