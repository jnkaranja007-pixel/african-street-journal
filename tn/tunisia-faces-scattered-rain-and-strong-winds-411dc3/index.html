Tunisia faces scattered rain and strong winds

Coastal areas will experience rough seas as temperatures range from 23 to 28 degrees Celsius.

Source: La Presse de Tunisie · 2026-10-28 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Tunisia will experience cloudy skies on Wednesday, October 28, 2026, with a chance of scattered rain in the north and parts of the center. The national meteorological institute forecasts winds from the north, blowing strongly near the coasts and moderately inland. Daytime high temperatures are expected to be between 23 and 28 degrees Celsius, dropping to around 20 degrees in the western highlands.

Overnight, temperatures will range from 13 to 17 degrees Celsius in western northern and central areas, and between 17 and 22 degrees elsewhere. The sea will be rough in the north and slightly agitated along the rest of the coastline. These conditions are predicted to affect the northern and central regions of the country.

The meteorological institute also reported that the sky will be partly cloudy across the country, becoming denser on the northern coasts by the end of the night. This forecast covers the weather for the upcoming night and the following day.

Why it mattersTunisians will experience varied weather conditions including rain, wind, and rough seas.
