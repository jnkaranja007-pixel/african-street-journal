JTC schedules Indian dance performance in Tunis for 26 November

Rima Kallingal directs a work on Kerala’s hand-weaving heritage, set for 19h30 at the Théâtre des Régions.

Sources: Kapitalis and Webdo · 2026-11-24 · 1 min read

The Carthage Theatre Days (JTC) will present the Indian dance performance “Neythe – Dance of the Weaves” at 19h30 on Thursday 26 November at the Théâtre des Régions in Tunis’s Cité de la Culture Chedli Klibi.

Rima Kallingal directs the work, produced by Mamangam Dance Company. Through dance and body movements, it recreates stages in turning thread into cloth and pays tribute to the hand-weaving heritage of Kerala, in India, and the craft of its artisans.

Tickets are available at the usual sales points. The twenty-seventh edition of the Carthage Theatre Days runs until 28 November.

Why it mattersPeople attending the Tunis performance can buy tickets through the usual sales points for the 19h30 show on 26 November.
