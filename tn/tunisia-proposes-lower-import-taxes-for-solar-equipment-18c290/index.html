Tunisia proposes lower import taxes for solar equipment

The 2027 finance bill includes a 10% duty on solar sensors and tax exemptions for battery imports.

Source: Webdo · 2026-11-10 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Tunisia's draft 2027 finance law proposes a 10% import duty on solar sensors and tax exemptions for batteries used in electricity storage. The government also plans to expand financing options for companies undertaking environmental upgrades. These measures aim to reduce equipment costs and encourage investment in renewable energy.

Prime Minister Sarra Zaâfrani Zenzri presented these proposals, detailed in articles 49, 50, and 51 of the draft law, to the Assembly of people's representatives and the National council of regions and districts on Monday, November 9. The measures require final adoption of the finance bill.

The proposed fiscal incentives are part of Tunisia's energy transition strategy. The government seeks to facilitate the adoption of renewable energy technologies by making them more affordable for businesses and consumers.

Why it mattersTunisian businesses investing in renewable energy equipment will see reduced import costs.
