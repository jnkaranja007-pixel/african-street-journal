Tunisia rises to 61st in English ranking as score falls eight points

The 2026 index puts Tunisia above the global average, but still in the low-proficiency category, with a 71-point gap between Ariana and Sidi Bouzid.

Source: La Presse de Tunisie · 2026-11-24 · 1 min read

Tunisia ranked 61st among 117 countries and regions in the 2026 English Proficiency Index, published on 18 November by Education First (EF), an international education and language-learning group based in Zurich, Switzerland. Its score was 490, down eight points from 2025, when Tunisia placed 66th. The global average fell to 485 from 488.

The report found a 71-point difference between Ariana and Sidi Bouzid. It also breaks results down by city, region, age, sex and profession. More than 2.3 million adults took the free online English test from EF, known as EF SET, in 2025 for the index, which assesses reading, listening, writing and speaking.

Nearly half of the tests for the 2026 edition assessed all four skills together. The report says participation is voluntary, so results describe those tested and may not represent the whole population; Tunisia remains in the low-proficiency category.

Why it mattersLearners and education policymakers must account for a 71-point gap between Ariana and Sidi Bouzid when reading the country's English results.
