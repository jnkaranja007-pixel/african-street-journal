Tunisia podcasts festival returns after three years

The event will feature creators from Algeria and Marseille, alongside Tunisian podcasters.

Source: Kapitalis · 2026-10-29 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

The Tunisia Podcasts Festival, known as Podcast Days, will take place in Tunis on Friday, November 6, and Saturday, November 7, 2026. The festival will be held at the Institut français de Tunisie and L’Art Rue, celebrating sound creation through meetings, masterclasses, and public recordings. Entry is free but requires registration.

This marks the festival's return after a three-year absence, with a more condensed format than its 2023 debut. Raouia Kheder, the festival's founder, stated that Tunisian podcasts have grown significantly since 2023, finding their voices and audiences. The event aims to provide a space for creators to connect and observe international trends.

Guests from Algiers and Marseille are expected to attend, highlighting the event's continental and cross-Mediterranean reach. The program includes a mix of professional development and immersive experiences for attendees.

Why it mattersTunisian podcasters and sound creators will benefit from networking and learning opportunities at the festival.
