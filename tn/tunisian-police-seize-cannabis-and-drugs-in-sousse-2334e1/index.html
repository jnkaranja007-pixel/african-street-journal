Tunisian police seize cannabis and drugs in Sousse

Authorities arrested three dealers and confiscated approximately 250 grams of cannabis resin and psychotropic pills.

Source: Kapitalis · 2026-11-05 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Tunisian anti-narcotics police in Sousse seized about 250 grams of cannabis resin and a quantity of psychotropic pills on Wednesday, November 4, 2026. Officers also confiscated a sum of money and a precision scale during the operation in the Laouina neighborhood.

The security operation led to the arrest of three individuals, including one person from a sub-Saharan African country. Following consultation with the public prosecutor, the dealers were placed in custody as the investigation continues.

The regional brigade for the fight against narcotics in Sousse conducted the operation.
