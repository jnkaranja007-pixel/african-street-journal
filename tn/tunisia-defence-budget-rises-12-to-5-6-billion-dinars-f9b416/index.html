Tunisia defence budget rises 12% to 5.6 billion dinars

Tunisia's national defence budget for 2027 will increase by 12% to 5.6 billion dinars, with investment spending up 15%.

Source: La Presse de Tunisie · 2026-11-04 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Tunisia's national defence budget for 2027 will reach 5.6 billion dinars, a 12% increase from 2026. This budget aligns with the 2026-2030 development plan and the ministry's 10-year strategy. Investment spending will rise by 15%, making up 28% of the total budget.

Minister of National Defence Khaled Sehili presented the budget to joint committees of the Assembly of Representatives of the People and the National Council of Regions and Districts. He emphasized rationalizing expenses and strengthening governance to balance national security needs with state finances. The budget covers military intervention, logistics, personnel support, and command programs.

Discussions also covered human resources, the military service system, military health, and scientific research. Deputies raised questions about military vocational training, digitalization, innovation, and defense industry development. The ministry aims to improve public spending efficiency and enhance public investment quality.
