Tunisia energy deficit hits 10.48 billion dinars

Foreign exchange reserves fell to cover 93 days of imports by October 23, 2026.

Source: Webdo · 2026-10-30 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Tunisia's energy trade deficit reached 10.48 billion dinars, nearly half of the country's total trade deficit. Foreign exchange reserves dropped to cover 93 days of imports as of October 23, 2026, down from 107 days a year earlier. These figures were presented on October 29 by Finance Minister Mechkat Salma Khaldi during a budget execution hearing.

Imports grew by 13.1% while exports increased by 8.6%, widening the trade balance pressures and increasing external financing needs. The energy deficit alone accounted for approximately 50% of the overall trade deficit. The minister also noted potential impacts from the Middle East geopolitical crisis on trade and energy prices.

Despite these challenges, tourism revenue rose by 3.7% and remittances from Tunisians abroad increased by 4.4%, helping to finance part of the external deficit. The government is also developing a plan to integrate informal economic activities into the legal framework to broaden the formal economic base and improve tax compliance.

Why it mattersTunisia's economy faces significant pressure from a large energy deficit and declining foreign exchange reserves, impacting its financial stability.
