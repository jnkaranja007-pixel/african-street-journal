Tunisia reviews appointments for vacant mosque posts

Religious Affairs Minister Ahmed Bouhali says candidates are assessed by regional commissions as his department works to shorten delays.

Sources: La Presse de Tunisie and Webdo · 2026-11-22 · 1 min read

Tunisia has 20 628 religious officials across 11 functions, Religious Affairs Minister Ahmed Bouhali told lawmakers during a joint session on his department’s 2027 budget. He outlined measures to fill vacant posts in mosques, speed up appointments and improve officials’ professional and social conditions.

Bouhali said officials are not assigned automatically to mosques with vacancies. Residents of the localities concerned propose candidates, who must complete administrative procedures and meet requirements including scientific and physical aptitude and commitment to Islamic values. A regional commission assesses each candidate’s skills and ability to perform the religious role.

Asked about delays between applications and appointment decisions, Bouhali said administrative procedures take time. He said the ministry is working to process applications faster to meet places of worship’s needs and maintain religious activities.

Why it mattersApplicants proposed by local residents must pass administrative checks and a regional commission’s assessment before the ministry makes appointments.
