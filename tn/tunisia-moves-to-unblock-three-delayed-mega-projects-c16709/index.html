Tunisia moves to unblock three delayed mega-projects

Tunis Financial Port, Tunis Sports City and the Sama Dubai scheme have all stalled for years, and the government says it is working to clear the obstacles.

Source: Webdo · 2026-11-16 · 1 min read

Tunisia's Ministry of Economy and Planning has restarted efforts to unblock three large investment projects that are running well behind schedule: the Tunis Financial Port, Tunis Sports City and La Porte de la Méditerranée, known as Sama Dubai. The ministry is working with the bodies involved to resolve administrative and technical problems holding them back.

Economy and Planning Minister Samir Abdelhafidh presented the status of the three files on Saturday, 14 November 2026, during a joint plenary session on the ministry's 2027 budget at the Bardo palace. For the Tunis Financial Port, he said the company behind the project would be asked to revise its timeline and phases and to propose an amendment to its investment agreement. For Tunis Sports City, electricity and telecommunications network studies have been validated, and funding for connecting the project to the external natural gas network has received an agreement in principle.

Sama Dubai, a large urban and property development around Lake Sud in Tunis, has been stalled since 2011. The minister confirmed it is among the three projects the ministry is monitoring but gave no further details on measures or a relaunch timetable. The agrément committee remains responsible for steering all three files.

Why it mattersInvestors in the three schemes now face a revised timetable and amended agreement for the Financial Port, and the Sports City gas connection funding is still only approved in principle.
