Tunisia sets weekend food discounts, chicken at 7.500 dinars

The 28-29 November offers cover produce and poultry in supermarkets and shops in Kef and Jendouba, with direct sales in two towns.

Source: Webdo · 2026-11-27 · 1 min read

Tunisia’s Trade and Export Development Ministry scheduled food discounts for 28 and 29 November at large supermarkets and shops in Kef and Jendouba. Supermarkets will offer Thomson oranges for 1.980 dinars a kilogram, local apples for 4.500 dinars and potatoes for 2.300 dinars. Turkey cutlets will cost 16.750 dinars a kilogram.

Shops in the two governorates will cut prices on poultry products, including ready-to-cook chicken, thighs and breast, by between 1.450 and 3 dinars per kilogram; the ministry did not give final prices for each item. A direct-sales outlet opened in Ghardimaou on 26 November, and a mobile outlet is scheduled for Jerissa on 28 November, selling ready-to-cook chicken for 7.500 dinars each.

A ministry official said earlier promotions sold 225 tonnes of poultry and more than 110 tonnes of farmed fish over five weeks.

Why it mattersShoppers in Ghardimaou and Jerissa can buy ready-to-cook chicken directly for 7.500 dinars each, while discounts run in Kef and Jendouba.
