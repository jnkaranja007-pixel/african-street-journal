Tunisia faces Libya in Tripoli for Africa Cup of Nations qualifier

The national football team plays away after a previous defeat in Sfax.

Sources: Webdo and La Presse de Tunisie · 2026-11-17 · 1 min read

Tunisia plays away against Libya on Tuesday, 17 November 2026, during the fourth matchday of the Africa Cup of Nations qualifiers in group H. The fixture starts at 17h00 at the June Eleventh Stadium in Tripoli, following a 0-2 defeat against the same opponent on Friday in Sfax. Webdo reported that the match will be played behind closed doors because of a disciplinary sanction issued against the Libyan Football Federation by the Confederation of African Football.

Viewers across the region can watch the broadcast live on beIN Sports ar 1 for MENA and beIN Sports fr 2 in France. Supporters inside Tunisia can also tune in through the digital terrestrial network on Al Wataniya 1, alongside streaming options on beIN CONNECT and TOD.

The team seeks recovery in this return fixture to revive qualification prospects after losing the previous encounter. Additional television coverage details were confirmed by La Presse de Tunisie ahead of the kickoff in Libya.

Why it mattersTunisian football supporters can watch the televised qualifier broadcast on Al Wataniya 1 or stream it via digital platforms starting at 17h00.
