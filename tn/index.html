Tunisia awards Elmed cable contract

Prysmian secures 460 million euro deal for 200-kilometer subsea power link to Italy.

Tunisia and Italy's Elmed electricity interconnection project has entered a decisive phase with the awarding of key contracts. The project, linking the two Mediterranean shores via the Strait of Sicily, will have a 600-megawatt bidirectional capacity and an estimated total cost of 1.42 billion euros. Hitachi Energy will build conversion stations in Tunisia and Sicily for approximately 770 million euros, while Prysmian secured the 460 million euro contract for the 200-kilometer subsea cable.

The Elmed project's second phase involves strengthening Tunisia's national grid to integrate renewable energy and transmit power from southern regions. This includes a 400-kilovolt overhead line between Grombalia and Kondar, covering about 85 kilometers, and 20 kilometers of additional connection lines. The European Bank for Reconstruction and Development (BERD) signed a 43 million euro financing agreement on January 15, 2026, for this infrastructure, with the loan to Steg to be repaid over 18 years.

Parliamentary review of the financing guarantee agreement is underway, with a request for urgent examination. The Elmed project is supported by the European Union with a 307.6 million euro grant, alongside funding from the World Bank, BERD, the European Investment Bank (BEI), and Germany's KfW. The subsea link's commissioning is currently planned between 2028 and 2030.

Why it mattersTunisian electricity infrastructure and national grid are being upgraded and financed through international agreements, impacting the national electricity company Steg.

Tunisia expects heavy rain up to 70mm

Stormy weather and hail are forecast for eastern northern and central regions from Saturday afternoon until Sunday morning.

Tunisia anticipates heavy, stormy rains in several regions from Saturday afternoon, November 7, 2026, until Sunday morning, November 8. The Institute of Meteorology (INM) reported that eastern northern and central areas will experience the brunt of the rainfall. Amounts are expected to range from 30 to 50 millimeters in Zaghouan, Monastir, Mahdia, and Sfax.

The INM forecasts that Nabeul and Sousse could locally receive up to 70 millimeters of rain, with particularly significant precipitation expected. Hail and strong winds are also predicted to accompany the storm cells. These conditions are expected to persist into the early hours of Sunday.

The weather on Sunday, November 8, will feature cloudy spells across most of Tunisia, according to the INM. Rain is specifically anticipated over Cap Bon and the Sahel, while other areas will see generally cloudy skies. The forecast indicates these weather patterns will continue through Sunday.

Why it mattersFarmers and residents in eastern northern and central Tunisia face disruptions from heavy rainfall, hail, and strong winds.

German firms to explore Tunisia water partnerships

A delegation of German companies will visit Tunisia from November 9-11 to discuss water and environmental technologies.

German companies specializing in water and environmental technologies will visit Tunisia from November 9 to 11, 2026. The mission, organized by the Tunisian-German Chamber of Commerce and Industry (AHK Tunisie), includes a conference on water management and meetings with Tunisian economic actors. This initiative is supported by the German Federal Ministry for Economic Affairs and Energy to promote exports of eco-technologies.

A conference on water management is scheduled for November 10 in Tunis, where German firms will present their technologies. This will be followed by professional exchanges and bilateral meetings on November 11. The goal is to foster commercial contacts and explore cooperation in water and environmental protection, involving the Technical University of Berlin.

The mission occurs as Tunisia faces increasing pressure on its water resources, requiring significant investment in hydraulic infrastructure and management technologies. This visit follows a recent partnership between Mabrouka Holding and UMIDA for underground irrigation technology, highlighting interest in technological solutions for water constraints.

Why it mattersTunisian businesses and the country's water sector will engage with German companies to find technological solutions for water management challenges.

Sport · 2026-11-08

CS Sfaxien defeats Espérance de Tunis in volleyball

CS Sfaxien secured a 3-1 victory over Espérance de Tunis in the second round of the Tunisian National A volleyball championship.

The second day of the Tunisian National A volleyball championship featured six matches played on November 7.

In Pool A, CS Sfaxien defeated Espérance de Tunis 3-1, US Carthage lost 0-3 to Saydia Sidi Bou Saïd, and AS La Marsa beat Fath Hammam El Ghezaz 3-2.

Pool B saw Étoile du Sahel win 3-1 against US Transport de Sfax, Mouloudia de Bousalem beat AS Télécommunications de Sfax 0-3, and CO Kélibia secure a 3-1 victory over CS Hammam-Lif.

Why it mattersTunisian volleyball teams continue their journey in the first phase of the national championship after the second day's matches.
