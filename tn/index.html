Tunisia defence budget rises 12% to 5.6 billion dinars

Tunisia's national defence budget for 2027 will increase by 12% to 5.6 billion dinars, with investment spending up 15%.

Tunisia's national defence budget for 2027 will reach 5.6 billion dinars, a 12% increase from 2026. This budget aligns with the 2026-2030 development plan and the ministry's 10-year strategy. Investment spending will rise by 15%, making up 28% of the total budget.

Minister of National Defence Khaled Sehili presented the budget to joint committees of the Assembly of Representatives of the People and the National Council of Regions and Districts. He emphasized rationalizing expenses and strengthening governance to balance national security needs with state finances. The budget covers military intervention, logistics, personnel support, and command programs.

Discussions also covered human resources, the military service system, military health, and scientific research. Deputies raised questions about military vocational training, digitalization, innovation, and defense industry development. The ministry aims to improve public spending efficiency and enhance public investment quality.

Tunisia delays digital tax account launch to 2027

The finance minister announced the new deadline for the unified digital tax system, with only 37,000 electronic invoices settled.

Tunisia's unified digital tax account, designed to centralize taxpayer information, will now launch in the first quarter of 2027, a year later than planned. The electronic invoicing platform has processed nearly 1.8 million invoices, with approximately 37,000 settled, according to Finance Minister Michket Salama Khaldi. This announcement came during a parliamentary finance committee meeting on Tuesday, November 3, 2026, discussing the state budget for 2027.

The delay means the digital tax system, intended to simplify tax procedures and improve access to fiscal information, will be implemented a year behind the initial schedule. The electronic invoicing system has seen a significant volume of transactions, but the number of settled invoices represents about 2.1% of the total processed.

The minister provided these figures while examining the Ministry of Finance's role in the 2027 state budget. The unified tax account aims to streamline taxpayer interactions with the administration, while electronic invoicing seeks to dematerialize invoice exchanges. The government is now working to meet the revised deadlines for these digital transformation initiatives.

Why it mattersTunisian taxpayers will face a delayed implementation of a new digital system for managing their tax obligations and will see a low settlement rate on electronic invoices.

Tunisia arrests student with 2kg cannabis

Jendouba authorities detained a 19-year-old from sub-Saharan Africa for alleged drug trafficking near schools.

Tunisian authorities arrested a 19-year-old student from sub-Saharan Africa in Jendouba for alleged cannabis trafficking. The national guard conducted the operation with the public prosecutor near the Court of First Instance. The student was found with two kilograms of cannabis.

The judiciary issued a detention order against the student. An investigating judge near the tribunal ordered the detention as the investigation continues to find potential accomplices. The alleged trafficking targeted schools and universities in the Jendouba region.

The investigation aims to identify further individuals involved in the alleged drug distribution network.

Tunisia hosts agricultural investment expo

The 16th SIAT expo in Tunis focused on resilient and smart farming for food sovereignty.

Tunisia held its 16th International Agricultural Investment and Technology Show (SIAT) from October 28-31, 2026, at the Parc des Expositions du Kram. Organized by the Agricultural Investment Promotion Agency (APIA), the event showcased innovations and facilitated partnerships for agricultural professionals.

Under the theme 'Investing in resilient and smart agriculture to strengthen our food sovereignty,' the expo aimed to connect investors, suppliers, and industry experts. This biennial event is a key platform for developing agricultural ventures within Tunisia and internationally.

SIAT's focus on resilient and smart agriculture addresses the need to bolster food sovereignty. The event provided a space for discovering new technologies and fostering collaborations essential for the sector's future.

Why it mattersTunisian farmers and investors gathered to explore innovations for resilient and smart agriculture to enhance national food security.

Tunisia temperatures to rise to 25°C

Cloudy skies are forecast across most regions with light to moderate winds.

Temperatures in Tunisia will climb on Wednesday, November 4, 2026, with maximums expected to reach 25°C according to weather forecasts.

Cloudy skies will be present across most of the country, with clouds becoming locally more abundant but no rain is predicted. Winds will blow from the southwest with low to moderate intensity.

Seas will be relatively calm, ranging from slightly rough to locally agitated. The weather service issued these forecasts for the country.

Why it mattersTunisians will experience warmer weather with highs of 25°C and cloudy conditions.
