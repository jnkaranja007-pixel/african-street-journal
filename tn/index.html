Politics · 2026-08-21

Tunisia records 1,101 protests in July

Tunisia saw 1,101 protest movements in July 2026, the highest monthly total since July 2016. The Tunisian Social Observatory reported that more than 60% of these mobilizations were linked to social problems.

Why it mattersThe record level of unrest indicates increasing public instability driven by social grievances.

Agriculture · 2026-08-21

Black Sea tensions pressure Tunisian wheat imports

Tunisia remains dependent on imported wheat despite a strong domestic cereal campaign. Webdo reports that attacks on ports and ships in the Black Sea are disrupting one of the world's primary grain basins.

Why it mattersSupply chain disruptions in the Black Sea expose the country to food security risks due to its reliance on foreign wheat.

Business · 2026-08-21

Tunisian government reviews 2027 economic balance

Prime Minister Sara Zaafrani Zenzri presided over a ministerial council on Aug. 19 to examine the draft economic balance for 2027. The meeting focused on the major orientations of the country's economic policy.

Why it mattersThe document will establish the fiscal and developmental priorities for the Tunisian state in 2027.

Culture · 2026-08-21

Tunis Medina schedules Mouled procession for Aug. 25

A large procession will start at 9:30 a.m. on Tuesday, Aug. 25, at the zaouia of Sidi Mahrez. According to Webdo, the march will proceed toward Rue du Pacha and end at the zaouia of Sidi Ben Arous.

Why it mattersThe event will restrict normal movement and traffic within the Tunis Medina for participants and residents.

Bottled mineral water shortages persist in Tunisia

A shortage of bottled mineral water continues across the country. Kapitalis reports that the scarcity remains visible in numerous supermarkets and retail points.

Why it mattersThe ongoing shortage limits the availability of drinking water for consumers at retail outlets.
