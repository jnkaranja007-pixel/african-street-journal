Orange Tunisie hosts forum on AI and cybersecurity

Nearly 400 Tunisian business leaders and experts met in Tunis to discuss digital transformation and innovation.

Orange Tunisie organized the first Orange Business Forum 2026 in Tunis, gathering nearly 400 decision-makers and experts to discuss the digital transformation of Tunisian businesses. The event focused on artificial intelligence, data valorization, and cybersecurity as major challenges for companies. Discussions covered securing digital environments, integrating AI into professional activities, and using innovation to improve business performance.

The forum explored the evolution of cyber threats in the age of AI, new detection and protection methods, and the potential of trustworthy AI. Orange Tunisie presented solutions from Orange Cyberdefense, Orange Business, and Sofrecom Tunisie, including demonstrations of Dynamic SOC for cybersecurity, Live Intelligence for AI, and Mon IA Pro for innovation. The AI4IT approach, which uses AI to automate software development, was also highlighted.

Orange Tunisie emphasized the synergy between its global expertise and local skills to meet the technological needs of Tunisian companies. The event also stressed the importance of trust as a central issue in digital transition, alongside reliable data, secure systems, and adapted solutions for organizations.

Why it mattersTunisian business leaders and experts gathered to explore how AI and cybersecurity can drive innovation and performance in their companies.

Tunisian police seize cannabis and drugs in Sousse

Authorities arrested three dealers and confiscated approximately 250 grams of cannabis resin and psychotropic pills.

Tunisian anti-narcotics police in Sousse seized about 250 grams of cannabis resin and a quantity of psychotropic pills on Wednesday, November 4, 2026. Officers also confiscated a sum of money and a precision scale during the operation in the Laouina neighborhood.

The security operation led to the arrest of three individuals, including one person from a sub-Saharan African country. Following consultation with the public prosecutor, the dealers were placed in custody as the investigation continues.

The regional brigade for the fight against narcotics in Sousse conducted the operation.

New book explores Tunis consular quarter

Adnen El Ghali's book details European consular representations in Ottoman Tunis and their urban impact.

Adnen El Ghali has published a new book titled "Quand la diplomatie fait la ville. Le quartier consulaire du Tunis ottoman, XVIIe-XIXe siècles" with Cérès Editions.

The book examines the architectural, urban, and symbolic significance of European consular representations in the medina of Tunis during the Ottoman regency.

Drawing from his 2021 doctoral thesis at the Free University of Brussels, El Ghali reconstructs an active capital where fondouks, consular houses, and places of worship shaped the urban space.

Why it mattersThe book provides historical insight into the urban development of Tunis influenced by diplomatic activities.

Stade Tunisien goalkeeper Nourddine Farhati repels attempts

The 26-year-old goalie turned away multiple attempts after conceding an early goal in a 2-1 victory.

Stade Tunisien goalkeeper Nourddine Farhati, 26, was in goal for his team's 2-1 win against Club Africain on Tuesday, November 3. Farhati, who joined Stade Tunisien in the summer of 2025, conceded a goal in the 12th minute but later repelled a 51st-minute attempt by Hamza Khadhraoui. His performance was rated 7.3/10 by FootballTunisien.

Despite the early goal, Farhati's efforts helped his team stay in contention during the match. Skander Sghaier and Fakhreddine Ben Youssef scored the goals that secured the victory for Stade Tunisien. This performance continues a solid start to the season for Farhati, who has now conceded three goals in four matches.

Farhati's current record stands at three goals conceded over four games played. The goalkeeper, nicknamed 'Noura', is solidifying his position in the Stade Tunisien lineup. His consistency will be tested in upcoming matches.

Why it mattersFootball fans in Tunisia will be interested in the performance of Nourddine Farhati, a player for the Stade Tunisien team.
