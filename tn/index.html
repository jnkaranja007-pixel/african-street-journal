Politics · 08/20/2026

Tunisia records 1,101 protests in July

Tunisia saw 1,101 protest movements in July 2026, the highest monthly total since July 2016. The Tunisian Social Observatory reported that more than 60% of these mobilizations were linked to social problems.

Why it mattersThe record level of unrest indicates increasing public dissatisfaction with social conditions.

Black Sea tensions pressure Tunisian wheat imports

Tunisia remains dependent on imported wheat despite a strong domestic cereal campaign. Webdo reports that attacks on ships and ports in the Black Sea expose the country to supply disruptions.

Why it mattersInstability in this global grain basin threatens the availability of a primary food staple for the population.

Bottled mineral water shortages persist in Tunisia

A shortage of bottled mineral water continues across the country. Kapitalis reports the scarcity remains visible in numerous supermarkets and retail points.

Tunisian government reviews 2027 economic balance

Prime Minister Sara Zaafrani Zenzri presided over a ministerial council on Aug. 19 to examine the draft economic balance for 2027. The document serves as the primary orientation for the country's economic policy.

Why it mattersThe project establishes the fiscal and economic priorities that will govern state spending and business planning for 2027.
