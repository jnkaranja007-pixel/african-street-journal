12 farm workers injured in tunisia road accident

a collision between two light trucks on regional road 59 injured 12 agricultural workers and two drivers on october 28, 2026.

12 agricultural workers were injured on Wednesday, October 28, 2026, when two light trucks collided on regional road number 59. The accident occurred in the Bulla Regia area, near Jendouba, as the workers were traveling to their fields. The injured were transported to the University Hospital of Jendouba for treatment.

The collision involved two light trucks, and in addition to the 12 farm laborers, the drivers of both vehicles sustained injuries. Five of the workers and one driver were kept under medical observation after receiving initial care. Civil protection agents responded to the scene to assist the victims.

This incident highlights the daily risks faced by agricultural workers in Jendouba governorate who rely on collective transport to reach their workplaces. The exact circumstances of the crash and any resulting responsibilities have not yet been determined.

Why it matters12 agricultural workers were injured when the transport they were using to reach their fields was involved in a road accident.

Tunisian journalist Zied El Heni jailed for four years

A Tunis court sentenced journalist Zied El Heni to four years in prison on charges including terrorism.

Journalist Zied El Heni received a four-year prison sentence on Tuesday, October 27, 2026, from the terrorism court at the Tunis Court of First Instance. The National Syndicate of Tunisian Journalists (SNJT) condemned the ruling, stating it was linked to his profession and that the trial conditions were unfair. El Heni was prosecuted on four charges under anti-terrorism legislation, including forming a terrorist organization and disclosing secrets to one.

The conviction stems from an audio recording case involving Mondher Ounissi, former interim president of Ennahdha, and journalist Chahrazed Akacha. Ounissi received nine years in prison, and other defendants were sentenced to 25 years. El Heni, initially a witness after verifying audio recordings in September 2023, was later accused. The SNJT argues these proceedings endanger source confidentiality, a vital aspect of journalism and the public's right to information.

The SNJT also reported that defense lawyers could not present their arguments and El Heni did not attend his trial in person, participating via videoconference from Mornaguia prison. Technical issues reportedly disrupted the hearing. El Heni is already serving a one-year sentence from another case confirmed in June 2026. The syndicate has called for a retrial and an end to what it deems abusive prosecutions against journalists.

Why it mattersJournalist Zied El Heni was sentenced to four years in prison by a Tunisian court.

Tunisia earthquake registers magnitude 3.5 near Tazarka

The tremor was felt by residents close to the epicenter in Nabeul governorate early Wednesday.

An earthquake with a magnitude of 3.5 on the Richter scale shook the Nabeul governorate on Wednesday, October 28, 2026. The tremor occurred at 4:18 AM, with its epicenter located southeast of Tazarka. The National Institute of Meteorology (INM) reported the seismic event and its details.

According to the INM's initial analysis, the earthquake's epicenter was precisely at 36.47 degrees north latitude and 10.99 degrees east longitude. While specialists classify this magnitude as weak, residents living very close to the epicenter reported feeling the shaking. No material or human damage has been reported so far.

The INM also noted that a possible aftershock could occur in such situations, though it is not systematic. The seismic surveillance stations of the INM detected the tremor.

Why it mattersResidents near Tazarka felt a weak earthquake that registered 3.5 on the Richter scale.

Tunisia faces scattered rain and strong winds

Coastal areas will experience rough seas as temperatures range from 23 to 28 degrees Celsius.

Tunisia will experience cloudy skies on Wednesday, October 28, 2026, with a chance of scattered rain in the north and parts of the center. The national meteorological institute forecasts winds from the north, blowing strongly near the coasts and moderately inland. Daytime high temperatures are expected to be between 23 and 28 degrees Celsius, dropping to around 20 degrees in the western highlands.

Overnight, temperatures will range from 13 to 17 degrees Celsius in western northern and central areas, and between 17 and 22 degrees elsewhere. The sea will be rough in the north and slightly agitated along the rest of the coastline. These conditions are predicted to affect the northern and central regions of the country.

The meteorological institute also reported that the sky will be partly cloudy across the country, becoming denser on the northern coasts by the end of the night. This forecast covers the weather for the upcoming night and the following day.

Why it mattersTunisians will experience varied weather conditions including rain, wind, and rough seas.

AI predicts Kais Saïed win in 2029 Tunisian election

Three powerful AI models suggest Tunisian President Kais Saïed has a high probability of securing a third term if he seeks re-election.

Three leading artificial intelligence models have predicted Tunisian President Kais Saïed is likely to win a third term if he runs in the 2029 presidential elections. Gemini, Meta.ai, and ChatGPT assigned probabilities ranging from 65% to 85% for his re-election, surprising those hoping for a change in power.

These AI predictions challenge the opposition's belief that Saïed's record on unemployment, inflation, purchasing power, and shortages of basic goods would lead to his defeat. The AI models suggest the country's complex sociological and political reality escapes human logic.

The author of the analysis noted that Kais Saïed's previous article on the president's chances was widely read, raising questions about the credibility and intelligence of AI tools. This current analysis was prompted by the AI models' surprising conclusions regarding the 2029 election.

Why it mattersTunisian citizens hoping for political alternation in 2029 face continued leadership under Kais Saïed based on AI predictions.
