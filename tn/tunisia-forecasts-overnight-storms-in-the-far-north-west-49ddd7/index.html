Tunisia forecasts overnight storms in the far North-West

The meteorological institute expects daytime highs of 26°C to 31°C across most regions, reaching 33°C locally in the Centre.

Sources: La Presse de Tunisie and Webdo · 2026-11-19 · 1 min read

Tunisia’s National Institute of Meteorology forecast partly cloudy skies across the country on Thursday, 19 November, with temperatures rising slightly before conditions worsen in the North. Highs are expected to range from 26°C to 31°C in most regions, reaching 33°C locally in the Centre.

The institute said clouds would thicken in the North late in the day, with temporarily stormy rain expected overnight in the far North-West. Temperatures on the western highlands will be around 24°C. Westerly winds will be relatively strong to strong near the northern coast and light to moderate elsewhere.

The sea is forecast to be very rough to rough in the North, and slightly rough to rough along the rest of the coast.

Why it mattersPeople travelling or working along the northern coast face strong westerly winds and very rough seas, while the far North-West is forecast to get storms overnight.
