L'Espérance nears new airline sponsorship deal

The football club is in advanced discussions for a significant financial partnership with an airline.

Source: La Presse de Tunisie · 2026-11-07 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

L'Espérance Sportive de Tunis is nearing a major commercial agreement with an airline company. The club, known as Sang et Or, is in advanced negotiations for a sponsorship deal that could significantly boost its finances.

This potential partnership aims to strengthen the club's resources and diversify its funding streams. If the negotiations are successful, the agreement would represent a substantial new commercial operation for L'Espérance.

The club is actively working to consolidate its economic standing through such ventures.
