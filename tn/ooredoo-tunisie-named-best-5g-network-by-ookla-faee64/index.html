Ooredoo Tunisie named best 5G network by Ookla

The operator received the Speedtest Award for its performance in the first half of 2026, based on nearly two million user tests.

Source: Leaders · 2026-11-10 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Ooredoo Tunisie has been named the best 5G network in Tunisia by Ookla, a global leader in connectivity intelligence. The award is based on analysis of consumer-initiated tests conducted via Speedtest during the first half of 2026.

The operator achieved a Speedtest Connectivity Score of 73.74, surpassing competitors based on factors including download speed, web browsing, and video streaming. Ookla's evaluation combined these elements, with speed accounting for 50% of the final score.

Ooredoo Tunisie launched its commercial 5G services in February 2025, investing in infrastructure to meet growing demand. The company aims to continue strengthening its network and exploring new digital uses.

Why it mattersTunisian consumers using Ooredoo's 5G network will experience superior digital performance, including faster speeds and better streaming.
