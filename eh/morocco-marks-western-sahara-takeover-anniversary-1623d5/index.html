Morocco marks Western Sahara takeover anniversary

Hundreds of pro-Moroccan Sahrawis gathered in Laayoune to celebrate the 1975 Green March.

Source: Africanews · 2026-11-09 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Morocco observed the anniversary of the Green March on Friday, commemorating the 1975 mobilization that led to its control over most of Western Sahara. In Laayoune, hundreds of pro-Moroccan Sahrawis gathered to celebrate, waving Moroccan flags.

The anniversary occurs as a U.N. peacekeeping mission concludes after 35 years, entering a new diplomatic phase for the territory's conflict. Pro-Morocco demonstrator Layla Choraifi stated, “He proposed the autonomy initiative for the Saharan provinces, which, in our view, is the most appropriate proposal for resolving this issue.”

For pro-independence Sahrawis, the day signifies the start of Morocco’s takeover and the displacement of thousands. Families remain divided between Moroccan-controlled cities and refugee camps in Algeria.

Why it mattersThe anniversary marks the beginning of Morocco's takeover and the displacement of thousands of Sahrawis, dividing families.
