Polisario rejects MINURSO after UN Security Council declines renewal

At a Madrid conference, its Europe delegate said the mission had failed to deliver the referendum it was created to organise in 1991.

Source: ECS Saharaui · 2026-11-21 · 1 min read

The Polisario Front rejected the loss of the United Nations Mission for the Referendum in Western Sahara (MINURSO) after the Security Council declined to renew it in late October. Mansur Omar, the movement’s delegate for Europe, spoke at the 50th European Conference of Solidarity and Support for the Sahrawi People in Madrid on Friday.

Omar said MINURSO was established in 1991 to organise a referendum that never took place and had become a shield for Morocco’s occupation. He said the Polisario would not mourn its departure and warned that a new war could spread beyond Western Sahara towards Rabat. An Algerian parliamentary delegation urged that the process continue without escalation and that a political vacuum be avoided.

The conference also heard Algeria’s solidarity committee pledge support for the Polisario. Its president, Said Ayachi, missed the Madrid meeting because he could not obtain a visa, according to the committee representative.

Why it mattersThe Polisario and Sahrawis now face the end of a UN mission that was established to organise a referendum in 1991, while Algeria’s delegation urges the process to continue without a political vacuum.
