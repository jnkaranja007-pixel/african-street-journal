Sahrawi protesters reject UN resolution draft

Tindouf residents demonstrated against a US-backed UN proposal for Western Sahara on October 28, 2026.

Source: Jeune Afrique · 2026-10-31 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Sahrawi residents in the Tindouf camps protested a proposed UN resolution on October 28, 2026. Women and children marched under security supervision against the American draft, with images shared on pro-Polisario media to show unity.

The demonstration occurred five days before the UN Security Council's vote on Western Sahara. Media close to the Polisario Front broadcast images of the protest, aiming to portray a unified movement.

The proposed resolution has created tensions and strategic debates within the Polisario Front, potentially reshaping the movement's future and leadership.

Why it mattersSahrawi residents in Tindouf camps are directly affected by the UN resolution vote and subsequent negotiations.
