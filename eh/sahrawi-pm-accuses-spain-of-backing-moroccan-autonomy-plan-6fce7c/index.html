Sahrawi PM accuses Spain of backing Moroccan autonomy plan

Bucharaya Hamudi Beyun said the Polisario Front will use all legal, military and non-military tools to defend Sahrawi rights, ahead of the 50th EUCOCO meeting in Madrid.

Source: ECS Saharaui · 2026-11-16 · 1 min read

Sahrawi Prime Minister Bucharaya Hamudi Beyun accused Pedro Sánchez's government, together with the United States, of backing an attempt to impose Morocco's autonomy plan for Western Sahara. Speaking at a press conference on Friday before the 50th EUCOCO in Madrid, he said the Polisario Front was ready to use "all legal, military and non-military instruments" to defend its rights.

Beyun said Madrid, the capital of the former colonial power, had ended that period without completing the decolonisation process. He compared Sánchez's stance on a bill granting Spanish nationality to Sahrawis with the position of Franco's government under Arias Navarro, and said the US ambassador to the UN had publicly said Spain was asked to offer nationality to Sahrawis to help make autonomy the solution.

Beyun also described a Security Council push in late October by Morocco and its Western allies to make Moroccan autonomy the only solution and to turn the MINURSO mission into a cheap one. He said Russia vetoed the text, and that Moroccan troops are being moved and concentrated along the wall, which he said could intensify fighting.

Why it mattersSahrawis in the diaspora and in the territory face a possible return to fighting, since the Polisario says Morocco is massing troops along the wall.
