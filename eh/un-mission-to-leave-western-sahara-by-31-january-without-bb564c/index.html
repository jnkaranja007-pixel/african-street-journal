UN mission to leave Western Sahara by 31 January without holding referendum

MINURSO spent 35 years without organising the vote, while helping monitor the 1991 ceasefire between Morocco and the Polisario Front.

Source: ECS Saharaui · 2026-11-27 · 1 min read

The United Nations Mission for the Referendum in Western Sahara (MINURSO) is due to leave the territory by 31 January 2027 without organising the self-determination referendum in its mandate, ECS Saharaui reported. The mission was created 35 years ago to let Sahrawis decide the territory's political future.

The referendum process stalled over disputes including the voter census and identification. Morocco opposed repeated efforts to update the census drawn up during Spanish rule, the report said. MINURSO also monitored the 1991 ceasefire between Morocco and the Polisario Front for decades.

Hostilities resumed in November 2020 after a Moroccan military intervention in the El Guerguerat area. Spanish journalist Ignacio Cembrero, who has followed the conflict for four decades, told RFI he fears a military escalation once the mission is gone.
