Morocco moves thousands of troops to Western Sahara wall

Thousands of Moroccan troops, tanks, and armored vehicles have been moved to at least nine points along the military wall dividing Western Sahara.

Source: ECS Saharaui · 2026-11-08 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Morocco reinforced at least nine points along the military wall in Western Sahara this week, accumulating thousands of troops, tanks, and armored vehicles near the separation line. A battalion of elite Moroccan Army troops was sighted in Guelta-Zemmur and Amgala, with similar large-scale troop movements occurring in the northern, central, and southern sectors of the territory.

A high-ranking Frente POLISARIO official stated that these troop movements are a response to continuous Polisario attacks, intended to "show muscle" rather than signal a full invasion of the liberated territories. Military sources reported that Sahrawi People's Liberation Army (ELPS) fighters attacked a Moroccan mobile unit shortly after these movements were detected, with no visible reaction from the targeted unit reported.

The Moroccan military's actions follow the UN General Assembly's scheduled debate on November 10 regarding a veto that led to the closure of MINURSO. A high-ranking source suggested Morocco could attempt to exploit the security vacuum east of the wall to expand its control over territories administered by the Frente POLISARIO.

Why it mattersTensions are rising in Western Sahara as Morocco increases its military presence along the separation wall, potentially leading to renewed conflict.
