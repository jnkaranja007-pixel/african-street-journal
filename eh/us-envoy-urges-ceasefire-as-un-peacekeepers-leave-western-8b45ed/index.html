US envoy urges ceasefire as UN peacekeepers leave Western Sahara

Mike Waltz called on Morocco and the Polisario Front to avoid escalation after the Security Council failed to renew the mission's mandate.

Source: Africanews · 2026-11-19 · 1 min read

Mike Waltz, the US ambassador to the United Nations, urged respect for the ceasefire in Western Sahara on Tuesday as UN peacekeepers began leaving the disputed territory. He made the appeal in Rabat, Morocco's capital.

Waltz called on all parties to avoid provocation and escalation, and urged Morocco to remain committed to the truce. He also asked the Algeria-backed Polisario Front, which has pushed for independence for the mineral-rich territory for decades, to rejoin and fully honour the ceasefire.

The UN peacekeeping mission, known as the Mission for the Referendum in Western Sahara (MINURSO), saw its mandate expire last month after the Security Council failed to adopt a resolution renewing it. The Polisario declared in 2020 that it would resume its armed struggle. Waltz said Washington would keep working with all parties and the United Nations towards a lasting settlement.

Why it mattersThe Polisario Front is being pressed to honour the ceasefire as UN peacekeepers leave after the mission's mandate expired without renewal.
