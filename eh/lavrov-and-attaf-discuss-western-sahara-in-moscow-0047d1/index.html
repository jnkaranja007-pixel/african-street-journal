Lavrov and Attaf discuss Western Sahara in Moscow

Russian and Algerian foreign ministers met amid UN Security Council talks on a US draft resolution.

Source: ECS Saharaui · 2026-10-29 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Russian Foreign Minister Sergey Lavrov met Algerian counterpart Ahmed Attaf in Moscow on October 27, 2026, to discuss the Western Sahara issue. The meeting occurred on the sidelines of the Gas Exporting Countries Forum (GECF) summit.

The talks addressed bilateral relations and regional matters, including Western Sahara, during a sensitive diplomatic period. Consultations are ongoing at the UN Security Council regarding a revised US draft resolution on the territory and the MINURSO mandate.

Russia, a permanent Security Council member, maintains its stance for a just political solution within the UN framework, emphasizing self-determination. Moscow has abstained on Western Sahara resolutions since 2018, citing disagreements with the US approach to drafting them.

Why it mattersThe discussions between Russia and Algeria directly involve the political future of Western Sahara and its people's right to self-determination.
