Polisario Front calls general mobilisation as it accuses Morocco of massing troops

The movement's leadership, led by Brahim Ghali, made the call on 10 November along the defence wall that divides Western Sahara.

Source: Jeune Afrique · 2026-11-18 · 1 min read

The Polisario Front, the movement seeking independence for Western Sahara, called for general mobilisation on 10 November. Its leadership, led by Brahim Ghali, accused Morocco of gathering troops along the defence wall.

The Sahrawi People's Liberation Army is organised into seven military regions and has infantry, armoured units and artillery, according to Jeune Afrique. The magazine describes the army's arsenal as ageing.

The report raises the question of how many men the movement can actually mobilise, and of the support it receives from Algeria. It gives no figures for troop strength, weapons or the scale of that support.

Why it mattersFighters and families in the camps and along the wall now face a formal mobilisation call, with no public figures yet showing how many people it would draw in.
