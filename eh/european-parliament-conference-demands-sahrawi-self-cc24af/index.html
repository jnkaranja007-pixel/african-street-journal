European Parliament conference demands Sahrawi self-determination

MEPs and experts met in Brussels to discuss the exploitation of natural resources and tourism under occupation.

Source: ECS Saharaui · 2026-11-09 · 2 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

A conference at the European Parliament in Brussels on Wednesday called for the decolonization of Western Sahara and demanded that the Sahrawi people be guaranteed their right to self-determination, freedom, and independence. Participants warned of the consequences of prolonging the conflict for regional security and stability. Oubbi Bouchraya stated that the Sahrawi people will not renounce their right to self-determination and sovereignty over their natural resources.

The conference, titled "The exploitation of Sahrawi natural resources and tourism under occupation," was organized by the European Parliament's Intergroup on Solidarity with the Sahrawi People and the Sahrawi Commission for Natural Resources and Legal Affairs. Attendees, including MEPs, lawyers, and international law experts, recalled that the Sahrawi issue is a decolonization process on the UN agenda. They emphasized that the UN's responsibility to complete decolonization and end the occupation remains, even with the non-renewal of MINURSO's mandate.

The meeting also presented a report on "Tourism under the context of occupation: the case of the occupied city of Dajla," which details Morocco's continued exploitation of Sahrawi resources, particularly through illegal agreements with the EU. The report examines tourism investments, including Ryanair flights between Madrid and Dajla, highlighting tourism's role in consolidating economic occupation and attempts to legitimize it through foreign investment.

Why it mattersThe Sahrawi people are directly affected by the ongoing conflict and the exploitation of their natural resources and territory.
