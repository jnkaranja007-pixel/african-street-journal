UN mission to leave Western Sahara by 31 January 2027

The mission was created 35 years ago to organise a referendum on the territory's political future, but it will end without holding one.

Why it mattersResidents of Western Sahara lose the only United Nations force keeping the Morocco-Polisario ceasefire in place after 31 January 2027.
