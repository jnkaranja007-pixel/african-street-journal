Polisario Front calls for general mobilisation as UN mission prepares to leave

The independence movement tells all Sahrawis to be ready to defend their land after the Security Council ends the Minurso mandate.

The United Nations peacekeeping mission in Western Sahara, Minurso, is preparing to leave the disputed territory after the Security Council declined to renew its mandate, ending a presence of more than three decades.

The Polisario Front has called on all Sahrawis to stand ready to defend their land and their right to self-determination. Political scientist Khadija Mohsen-Finan said the moment is a rupture: Minurso is no longer on the ground, and diplomatic efforts have reached an impasse.

Mohsen-Finan said the threat is real because the two belligerents now face each other directly, with no UN force between them.

Why it mattersWith Minurso gone, Sahrawis in the territory face a direct confrontation between the two belligerents, according to the political scientist quoted in the report.

Politics · 2026-11-12

Polisario declares general mobilization amid Moroccan troop movements

The Polisario Front announced a general mobilization on Tuesday, citing Moroccan military deployments along the wall.

The Polisario Front declared a state of general mobilization on Tuesday, with its National Secretariat meeting urgently under Brahim Ghali. The Sahrawi leadership called on "all sons and daughters of the Sahrawi people" to prepare for "conspiracies and maneuvers of the enemy," citing large deployments of Moroccan troops, weaponry, and material at various points along the wall. This move is seen as a contradiction to Rabat's ceasefire commitment, prompting calls for the UN and African Union to assume their responsibilities.

The mobilization announcement followed a Moroccan drone strike that destroyed a Mauritanian tanker truck in the Ahfir area, near the Mauritanian border, killing one passenger. Mauritanian press confirmed the truck was traveling from Polisario-controlled territory towards Atar. Sahrawi sources also reported another drone attack on a vehicle near Aklab El Haulilia, with no victim data available. These drone attacks have been frequent since 2020, often targeting civilians.

Hours after the mobilization, the Sahrawi army reported attacking Moroccan bases and troop concentrations in the Agad Argan area, in the Mahbes sector, causing significant losses. This is the third such report since the end of the MINURSO mission. Moroccan military aircraft have been making continuous flights to El Aaiún, Bojador, and Guelmim since October 30, coinciding with visible military movements, including a convoy of approximately 60 tanks heading south.

Why it mattersThe Polisario Front's general mobilization and subsequent military actions directly impact the Sahrawi people and the region's stability.
