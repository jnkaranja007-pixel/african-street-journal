Ethiopia’s central bank holds forex supply at $140 million as bids exceed it

Banks bid $160.76 million on November 24, while the birr’s marginal rate rose to 159.40 per dollar from 159.18 at the previous auction.

Source: Capital Ethiopia · 2026-11-25 · 1 min read

The National Bank of Ethiopia kept its foreign-currency allocation at $140 million in an auction on November 24, while bids from commercial banks totalled $160.76 million. The marginal exchange rate rose to 159.40 birr per US dollar, from 159.18 in the previous auction on November 10.

The 32 banks that submitted bids were up from 31 in the prior round, and 28 secured allocations, compared with 26 previously. The weighted average rate for successful bids increased to 160.73 birr per dollar from 159.70, according to the central bank’s auction results.

Total bids were 2.57% higher than the $156.73 million submitted two weeks earlier. The November 24 auction was the fourth of six scheduled for the October–December quarter; the bank plans to offer $140 million in each round, for a quarterly total of $840 million.

Why it mattersCommercial banks that submitted bids received allocations in the $140 million auction, against $160.76 million in demand on November 24.
