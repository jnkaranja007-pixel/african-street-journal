Ethiopian Airlines investigates ShebaMiles customer data breach

The loyalty programme says some customer information may have been affected, but it has found no evidence that financial data was accessed.

Source: Capital Ethiopia · 2026-11-19 · 1 min read

Ethiopian Airlines is investigating a cybersecurity incident on its systems that may have affected customer information held by ShebaMiles, its loyalty programme. The airline disclosed the incident in a public notice, describing it as limited in scope and recent.

ShebaMiles said there was no evidence that customers' financial information was accessed. The notice did not say how many customers were affected, what personal data may have been exposed, or when the incident was detected. The airline said it was working with cybersecurity partners and relevant authorities to determine its full impact.

ShebaMiles urged customers to stay alert to suspicious or unsolicited communications. It did not say whether customers should change passwords or take other security measures, and said it would provide further updates as more information became available.

Why it mattersShebaMiles members are urged to be alert to suspicious or unsolicited communications while the airline investigates which customer information was affected.
