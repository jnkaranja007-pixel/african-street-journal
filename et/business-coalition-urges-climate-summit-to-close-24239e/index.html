Business coalition urges climate summit to close electrification gap

We Mean Business Coalition wants COP31 to focus on turning electrification ambition into actual deployment.

Source: Capital Ethiopia · 2026-11-09 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

The We Mean Business Coalition has called on the COP31 Presidency to prioritize closing the electrification implementation gap at the upcoming climate summit in Antalya. The coalition, a non-profit focused on business action for climate, stated that while electrification is central to COP31's Action Agenda with a 35% global share goal by 2035, the focus must now shift to deployment. Recent research by WMBC has repeatedly identified this implementation gap.

According to the coalition, a failure to accelerate progress leaves countries vulnerable to energy insecurity and fluctuating prices, while also harming industrial competitiveness and investment. Businesses are experiencing increased costs, project delays, and difficult decisions regarding capital allocation. The Electrification Competitiveness Index revealed that policy and infrastructure in most economies analysed are more advanced than on-the-ground deployment.

The Electrification Competitiveness Index found China leading in actual electrification, while the US faces risks of losing momentum. Major European economies like Germany, the UK, Italy, and Poland are also reportedly struggling to translate strong policy frameworks into comparable progress. The COP31 summit is scheduled to begin in one week.

Why it mattersBusinesses face higher costs, delayed projects, and tougher decisions about where to put capital due to the electrification implementation gap.
