Ethiopia backs UAE diplomat for UN chief role

Addis Ababa welcomes Lana Nusseibeh's candidacy, citing her experience and commitment to global cooperation.

Source: Horn Diplomat · 2026-10-31 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Ethiopia has endorsed Emirati diplomat Lana Nusseibeh for the position of United Nations secretary-general. The Ethiopian foreign ministry announced its support, highlighting Nusseibeh's diplomatic experience and dedication to multilateral cooperation. Ethiopia is a founding member of the UN.

The ministry stated its position in a message posted on X. Ethiopia views Nusseibeh's candidacy as important for the United Nations. The announcement came from Addis Ababa, the country's capital.

Nusseibeh's bid aims to make her the first woman to hold the UN's top job. Ethiopia's support adds to her campaign as the international community considers potential candidates for the role.

Why it mattersEthiopia's government has publicly supported a candidate for a top international position.
