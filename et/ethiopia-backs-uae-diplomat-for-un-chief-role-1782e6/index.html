Ethiopia backs UAE diplomat for UN chief role

Addis Ababa welcomes Lana Nusseibeh's candidacy, citing her experience and commitment to global cooperation.

Source: Horn Diplomat · 2026-11-01 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Ethiopia has announced its support for Emirati diplomat Lana Nusseibeh's bid to become the next United Nations secretary-general. The foreign ministry stated on X that the country values Nusseibeh's extensive diplomatic experience and dedication to multilateral cooperation.

The ministry's statement highlighted Ethiopia's position as a founding member of the United Nations and its strong belief in the importance of international collaboration. This endorsement comes as the global body prepares to select its next leader.

Ethiopia's support signals its engagement in international diplomacy and its willingness to back candidates it believes can strengthen global governance. The UN is expected to announce further details regarding the selection process in the coming months.

Why it mattersEthiopia's government is actively participating in the selection of a new UN secretary-general, influencing international leadership.
