Tana Forum discusses Red Sea security threats

African and international diplomats met in Bahir Dar, Ethiopia, to address escalating security concerns in the Red Sea region.

Source: Horn Diplomat · 2026-10-26 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Senior African and international diplomats gathered in Bahir Dar, Ethiopia, on Friday for the Tana Forum’s Special Envoys’ Dialogue. The meeting focused on rising security threats in the Red Sea, geopolitical rivalries, and regional cooperation within the Horn of Africa.

The dialogue brought together diplomats to discuss the escalating security situation. The discussions aimed to foster cooperation and address the complex geopolitical landscape affecting the region.

The Tana Forum is an annual event that convenes leaders to discuss critical issues facing the African continent. This year's focus on the Red Sea highlights the growing international concern over maritime security and regional stability.

Why it mattersDiplomats and leaders from African and international nations met in Ethiopia to discuss security threats.
