Ethiopia's Abiy reviews nuclear energy progress

Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed visited the Ethiopian Nuclear Energy Commission to assess the nation's nuclear power development.

Source: Horn Diplomat · 2026-10-23 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Ethiopian Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed visited the Ethiopian Nuclear Energy Commission on October 22, 2026, to review the country's nuclear energy program. He highlighted the role of advanced technology in Ethiopia's long-term economic transformation.

During his visit, Abiy stated that Ethiopia must advance beyond meeting its current needs. The Prime Minister's tour focused on the progress of the nation's nuclear energy development.

The government aims to integrate advanced technological solutions into its national development strategy.
