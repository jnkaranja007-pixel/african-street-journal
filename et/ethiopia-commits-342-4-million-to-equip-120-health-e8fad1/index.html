Ethiopia commits $342.4 million to equip 120 health facilities

The three-year maternal and child health programme combines a $20 million foundation pledge with partner funding matched by the government.

Source: Capital Ethiopia · 2026-11-27 · 1 min read

Ethiopia will equip around 120 health facilities with medicines, medical equipment and lifesaving technologies over three years under a $342.4 million reproductive, maternal, newborn and child health programme. The funding is intended to improve care across the country, particularly for pregnant women, newborns and children.

Health Minister Mekdes Daba said the foundation's $20 million pledge to Ethiopia's health compact helped mobilize another $151.2 million from development partners, which the government matched. Planned purchases include artificial intelligence-enabled ultrasound equipment to help staff identify pregnancy complications, as well as antibiotics to reduce infection risks, including sepsis.

The wider initiative, announced by the Mohamed bin Zayed Foundation for Humanity at a public health conference in Addis Ababa, is expected to benefit more than 500 facilities in nine African countries. The foundation said its $135 million commitment is expected to attract more than $550 million in further financing.

Why it mattersHospitals and clinics at about 120 facilities are due to receive new ultrasound equipment and antibiotics, which will change how staff treat pregnant women and newborns.
