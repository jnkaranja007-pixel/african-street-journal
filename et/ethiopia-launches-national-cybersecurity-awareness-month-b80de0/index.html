Ethiopia launches national cybersecurity awareness month

The Seventh National Cybersecurity Awareness Month focuses on making citizens vigilant for a secure nation.

Source: Capital Ethiopia · 2026-11-10 · 1 min read

Ethiopia is reinforcing digital security through its Seventh National Cybersecurity Awareness Month, themed “An Alert Citizen for a Secure Nation.” This initiative encourages collective vigilance to protect citizens, businesses, and public institutions as they increasingly use mobile financial services, e-commerce, and online platforms. The campaign aims to build trust in the digital economy by safeguarding personal information and transactions.

The "Secure by Habit" call to action urges everyone to integrate cybersecurity into daily behavior, moving beyond an annual focus. This push comes as digital transformation creates new opportunities alongside sophisticated cyber threats like phishing, ransomware, and AI-driven scams. International findings from the World Economic Forum and Verizon highlight the growing risks from third parties and software vulnerabilities, underscoring the need for strong systems and informed decisions.

A secure digital environment is presented as essential for technology adoption, inclusion, innovation, business continuity, and national development. The campaign emphasizes that consistent safer choices, not just awareness, are the true measure of success in protecting against cyber intrusions. The WannaCry ransomware attack in 2017, which affected over 200,000 computers globally, serves as a reminder of how a single security weakness can disrupt essential services and undermine public trust.

Why it mattersEthiopian citizens are directly affected by the need to make safer choices online to protect their personal information and transactions in the growing digital economy.
