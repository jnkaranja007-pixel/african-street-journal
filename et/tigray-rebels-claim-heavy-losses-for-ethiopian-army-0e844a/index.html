Tigray rebels claim heavy losses for Ethiopian army

The TPLF reported killing 500 soldiers and wounding 800 more during clashes near Aksum on Saturday.

Source: Africanews · 2026-11-04 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Ethiopia's rebel Tigray People's Liberation Front (TPLF) claimed on Monday to have inflicted heavy losses on the country’s armed forces. The group stated it killed 500 soldiers and wounded 800 others during clashes with federal troops and allied militias in the northern region. These confrontations occurred on Saturday near the town of Aksum, according to TPLF-affiliated Dimtsi Weyane television.

The TPLF also reported capturing territory and seizing weapons, ammunition, food supplies, and medical equipment. The rebels accused federal forces of using artillery and drones and noted disruptions to telephone and internet services. Ethiopia’s government has not commented on the casualty figures, which could not be independently verified.

The clashes follow weeks of escalating fighting in northern Ethiopia. Government troops had captured several towns, including Tigray's regional capital, Mekelle, in early October, but TPLF fighters have continued mounting counterattacks.

Why it mattersFighting in the northern region has led to rebel claims of significant casualties among Ethiopian soldiers.
