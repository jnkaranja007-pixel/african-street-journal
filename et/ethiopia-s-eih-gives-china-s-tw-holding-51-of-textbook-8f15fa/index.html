Ethiopia's EIH gives China’s TW Holding 51% of textbook venture

The proposed $30 million first phase would print, bind, package and distribute school textbooks and teachers’ guides in Ethiopia.

Source: Capital Ethiopia · 2026-11-26 · 1 min read

Ethiopia Investment Holdings (EIH), the state-owned investment holding company, has signed a shareholders’ agreement with China-based TW Holding Limited to establish a textbook printing and publishing joint venture in Ethiopia. TW Holding will own 51% and EIH 49%; the project’s first phase is estimated to require $30 million.

The facility is planned to print, bind, finish, package and distribute school textbooks, teachers’ guides and other books. EIH chief executive Brook Taye said the partnership is intended to address gaps in textbook printing and bring international expertise and technology into the industry. TW Holding will provide machinery, production technology and technical expertise, and lead development and operations.

The project is proposed as a response to growing textbook demand and limited domestic printing capacity, which have contributed to reliance on imported finished books and educational materials. EIH said the production platform could also serve markets beyond Ethiopia.

Why it mattersEIH must establish the joint venture with TW Holding, which will lead operations and provide machinery and production technology for the $30 million first phase.
