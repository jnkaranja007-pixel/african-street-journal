Nigeria warns citizens about travel via Addis Ababa airport

Abuja advises travellers with health conditions to be cautious due to the airport's high altitude.

Source: BBC Africa · 2026-11-15 · 1 min read

Nigeria's government has cautioned its citizens, particularly those with medical conditions, against travelling via Ethiopia's Bole International Airport due to its high altitude.

The foreign ministry in Abuja stated that 16 Nigerians with underlying health problems had died this year while transiting through Addis Ababa, which sits at 2,334m above sea level. The ministry urged travellers with conditions exacerbated by high altitudes to seek medical guidance before journeys through the major hub.

Bole International Airport serves millions of passengers annually as a key transit point between Africa, the Middle East, and Asia. The Nigerian ministry stressed it did not intend to discourage travel through the Ethiopian capital.

Why it mattersTravellers with cardiovascular and respiratory conditions are at increased risk when transiting through Addis Ababa's high-altitude airport.
