Ethiopia and DHL boost coffee export capacity

A new deal will train exporters in international trade, logistics, and customs procedures.

Source: Capital Ethiopia · 2026-11-04 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

The Ethiopian Coffee and Tea Authority (ECTA) and DHL Express Ethiopia signed an agreement on November 3 to improve the export capacity of the country's coffee sector. The partnership, part of DHL’s GoTrade programme, aims to enhance knowledge and skills in international trade, logistics, and customs for Ethiopian coffee businesses.

The deal, facilitated by the United Nations Industrial Development Organization (UNIDO), will see ECTA and DHL conduct training for coffee exporters and professionals. DHL will offer expertise in shipping and cross-border trade, while ECTA will identify participants and sector priorities. ECTA Director Office head Sefissa Ababu stated the partnership equips businesses with practical skills for international markets.

DHL Express Ethiopia Commercial Manager Yonas Sbhat noted that while Ethiopian coffee has strong demand, customs and logistics challenges can hinder market access. A recent nine-month capacity-building programme under this cooperation already trained about 30 participants from private coffee enterprises and ECTA.

Why it mattersEthiopian coffee exporters and businesses will gain practical knowledge and skills to access global markets more effectively.
