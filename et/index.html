Brazil Opens Addis Ababa Office to Boost Trade in Agribusiness, Energy, and Tech

The Brazilian Trade and Investment Promotion Agency's new office aims to deepen commercial ties and facilitate investment in key sectors.

Brazil's Trade and Investment Promotion Agency (ApexBrasil) has launched its first permanent office in Addis Ababa, signaling a strategic expansion into Africa. The office, inaugurated following ApexBrasil's Southern Africa trade mission, aims to foster tangible trade and investment opportunities, building on prior diplomatic exchanges between Ethiopia and Brazil. Ambassador Meles Alem stated the office will elevate bilateral commercial ties, leveraging the BRICS platform for knowledge transfer and mutual growth.

The new Addis Ababa branch will serve as a permanent platform for Brazilian companies to navigate the local market, identify partners, and access market intelligence. This initiative follows the Ethiopia-Brazil Economic Cooperation Forum, which facilitated numerous business-to-business sessions. The office will prioritize agribusiness value addition, industrial manufacturing, green energy, logistics, and technology transfer, aligning with Ethiopia's recent authorization for a range of Brazilian animal products and agricultural inputs.

While bilateral trade remains nascent, with Brazilian exports to Ethiopia totaling $31 million in 2025, officials see significant potential for diversification within Ethiopia's $8.9 billion import market. ApexBrasil has identified 59 export opportunities for Brazilian firms in Ethiopia, and the office will also support Ethiopian businesses seeking access to the Latin American market. This expansion occurs amid Ethiopia's projected 8.3 percent annual economic growth from 2026 to 2030.

Why it mattersEthiopian agribusiness and manufacturing sectors can benefit from Brazilian expertise and market access, while investors gain a dedicated point of contact for trade and investment facilitation.

MIDROC Signs Deal for Austrian Machinery for Gold Plant

MIDROC Investment Group will receive machinery from CEMETC for a new gold processing plant capable of processing 1.25 million tonnes of ore annually.

MIDROC Investment Group has signed an agreement with Austrian firm CEMETC to supply machinery for its planned gold processing plant in Metekel Zone. The plant will have the capacity to process 1.25 million tonnes of gold-bearing ore each year. CEMETC is expected to manufacture, install, and deliver the required equipment within one year.

The deal is part of MIDROC Investment Group's strategy to expand its industrial and mining investments and contribute to Ethiopia's economic development, according to a statement released by the group. CEMETC specializes in industrial technology for the cement and mining sectors, offering solutions to enhance factory operations and production efficiency.

This new technology and equipment are intended to help MIDROC Gold establish a more efficient production system, increase processing capacity, and boost overall productivity. MIDROC Gold, a subsidiary of MIDROC Investment Group, is a significant gold producer in Ethiopia, contributing to the nation's mineral output and foreign exchange earnings.

Why it mattersInvestors are affected by the deal as it signals expansion and modernization in Ethiopia's mining sector, potentially increasing production and foreign exchange earnings.

Aman Properties Unites Brands, Launches Expo with Discounts

Three real estate firms merge under Aman Properties, offering 250 homes at up to 30% off during a four-day expo.

Aman Properties publicly debuted in Addis Ababa, consolidating New Life Properties, Lafto Life Properties, and Sky View Properties into a single entity. The company, which has five years of experience and three delivered development sites, announced Aman Addis Expo 2026, a real estate event at Laphto Mall from September 4 to 7. Through its constituent companies, Aman Properties has already provided homes for over 300 households and handed over three sites with more than 56 villas.

The newly unified Aman Properties is part of Amanet Group, which has a decade-long presence in Ethiopia's trade, manufacturing, and technology sectors. The company benefits from an in-house construction firm and direct access to materials, enabling faster delivery and flexible payment options. Its current projects include a residential community linked to Laphto Mall and a mixed-use development in Kazanchis featuring commercial space and apartments.

The Aman Addis Expo coincides with Ethiopia's New Year, a period traditionally associated with new beginnings. During the expo, 250 homes will be available with New Year discounts ranging from fifteen to thirty percent. Aman Properties CEO Ato Biniyam Wondimu stated that five years of experience form the foundation for the company's stronger structure and clear future plans.

Why it mattersPotential homebuyers and investors are affected by the new company's consolidation and the expo offering discounted homes.
