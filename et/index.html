Ethiopia joins African Circular Economy Facility implementation

Ethiopia has entered the active implementation phase of the African Circular Economy Facility (ACEF). The African Development Bank (AfDB) manages the multi-donor trust fund.

Why it mattersThe initiative aims to transform waste into economic value to foster green industrial development.
