Ethiopian PM urges African parties to build consensus on growth

Abiy Ahmed called for unity, accountability, and inclusive governance to improve lives.

Ethiopian Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed urged African political parties to agree on long-term national priorities, saying unity, accountability, and inclusive governance were essential to improving people’s lives. Abiy delivered the remarks at the second African Political Parties Summit in Addis Ababa on November 6.

The Prime Minister stated that political parties should look beyond electoral competition to build consensus on growth. The summit brought together political leaders from across the continent.

The second African Political Parties Summit aimed to foster dialogue on national priorities.

Why it mattersEthiopian Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed spoke at a summit in Addis Ababa about improving citizens' lives through political consensus.

Business coalition urges climate summit to close electrification gap

We Mean Business Coalition wants COP31 to focus on turning electrification ambition into actual deployment.

The We Mean Business Coalition has called on the COP31 Presidency to prioritize closing the electrification implementation gap at the upcoming climate summit in Antalya. The coalition, a non-profit focused on business action for climate, stated that while electrification is central to COP31's Action Agenda with a 35% global share goal by 2035, the focus must now shift to deployment. Recent research by WMBC has repeatedly identified this implementation gap.

According to the coalition, a failure to accelerate progress leaves countries vulnerable to energy insecurity and fluctuating prices, while also harming industrial competitiveness and investment. Businesses are experiencing increased costs, project delays, and difficult decisions regarding capital allocation. The Electrification Competitiveness Index revealed that policy and infrastructure in most economies analysed are more advanced than on-the-ground deployment.

The Electrification Competitiveness Index found China leading in actual electrification, while the US faces risks of losing momentum. Major European economies like Germany, the UK, Italy, and Poland are also reportedly struggling to translate strong policy frameworks into comparable progress. The COP31 summit is scheduled to begin in one week.

Why it mattersBusinesses face higher costs, delayed projects, and tougher decisions about where to put capital due to the electrification implementation gap.

Business · 2026-11-09

AfDB chief urges African finance to aid businesses

Sidi Ould Tah proposed a new framework to align financial institutions with business needs.

African Development Bank Group President Sidi Ould Tah called on the continent's financial sector to better support businesses at the Africa Financial Summit in Luanda on November 3.

Ould Tah stated that for Africa's financial strength to become economic power, financial institutions must match the pace of the businesses they serve. He introduced the New African Financial Architecture for Development (NAFAD) as a framework to improve collaboration and rethink capital mobilisation and risk sharing.

He highlighted a disconnect between the reporting cycles and investment horizons of financial institutions and the business cycles and talent retention needs of companies, identifying this as a cause of lost economic growth in Africa.

Why it mattersAfrican businesses face a disconnect with financial institutions, hindering their ability to translate financial strength into economic power.
