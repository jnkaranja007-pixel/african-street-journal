ApexBrasil Opens Addis Office to Boost Ethiopia-Brazil Trade

The Brazilian Trade and Investment Promotion Agency will establish a permanent base in Ethiopia's capital to enhance economic cooperation and connect businesses.

The Brazilian Trade and Investment Promotion Agency, ApexBrasil, is opening a permanent office in Addis Ababa to strengthen economic ties between Brazil and Ethiopia. This initiative, announced following a meeting between Ethiopian Investment Commission (EIC) Commissioner Zeleke Temesgen and ApexBrasil President Laudemir André Müller, aims to create a platform for improved trade, investment promotion, and business connections.

According to the EIC, the new office will act as a strategic bridge, translating bilateral cooperation into tangible trade and investment opportunities. The Ethiopian Investment Commission has expressed readiness to collaborate with ApexBrasil in key sectors including agribusiness, industrial manufacturing, green energy, logistics, and technology transfer.

Commissioner Zeleke stated that the EIC is prepared to offer comprehensive support to Brazilian companies looking to invest in Ethiopia, with a focus on developing high-impact projects. The announcement was made at a press briefing attended by government officials, representatives from the Brazilian Embassy, ApexBrasil, and members of the private sector and diplomatic missions.

Why it mattersBrazilian companies seeking to invest in Ethiopia will have a dedicated office for support and connection, potentially leading to concrete trade and investment projects.

Austrian Firm to Equip MIDROC Gold Plant

CEMETC will supply and install machinery for a new gold processing facility in Ethiopia's Benishangul-Gumuz region.

MIDROC Investment Group has contracted Austrian company CEMETC to provide machinery for its planned gold processing plant in Metekel Zone, Benishangul-Gumuz Regional State. The agreement specifies that CEMETC will manufacture, install, and deliver equipment for a plant capable of processing 1.25 million tonnes of gold-bearing ore annually. This new facility is expected to be operational within one year, according to the announcement.

CEMETC, specializing in industrial technology for the cement and mining sectors, will provide advanced solutions aimed at improving operational efficiency and production monitoring. MIDROC Investment Group stated that the deal represents a significant step in expanding its industrial and mining investments and contributing to Ethiopia's economic development. The new technology is intended to enhance processing capacity and overall productivity for MIDROC Gold.

MIDROC Gold, a subsidiary of MIDROC Investment Group, is identified as one of Ethiopia's leading gold producers. The company plays a role in the country's mineral output and contributes to foreign-exchange earnings. The acquisition of new processing machinery is anticipated to bolster its production capabilities and its contribution to the national economy.

Why it mattersEthiopian investors and companies like MIDROC will benefit from new technology to increase gold processing capacity and efficiency.

Ethiopia's National Dialogue Backs Executive Presidency Plan

More than 4,000 delegates overwhelmingly endorsed constitutional changes that would merge prime minister and president roles into one powerful executive position.

Ethiopia's national dialogue has concluded with delegates overwhelmingly backing constitutional changes to create a powerful executive presidency. This proposed system would merge the current roles of prime minister and president, consolidating significant power into a single office. The recommendations, emerging from discussions on political tensions and ethnic divisions, are expected to be presented to parliament when it reconvenes in October.

Critics of the proposal, including political analyst Befekadu Hailu, warn that the reforms could allow Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed to further consolidate his control, especially given his party's recent landslide election victory. While supporters argue the executive presidency could foster more unifying national leadership, opponents fear the changes could transform the political system to serve a single individual.

The president under the proposed system would be chosen by a parliament currently dominated by members of Abiy Ahmed's ruling party. Observers anticipate that Abiy Ahmed would be the leading candidate for the new position if the changes are adopted, potentially concentrating executive power in one office.

Why it mattersThe proposed executive presidency could concentrate power in the hands of the prime minister, potentially altering the country's political landscape and governance structure.

Deloitte Secures Investment Bank License in Ethiopia

The Ethiopian Capital Market Authority granted Deloitte a license to operate as an investment banking service provider, making it the second foreign firm to receive the permit.

Deloitte has been licensed to operate as an investment banking service provider in Ethiopia, becoming the second foreign entity to secure the permit. The Ethiopian Capital Market Authority (ECMA) announced the granting of the license to D and T Ethiopia Management Consulting Plc, a subsidiary of Deloitte, on Friday. The regulator stated the decision followed a rigorous assessment, two years after the firm reentered the Ethiopian market as a securities investment advisor.

The new license allows D and T Ethiopia to provide non-brokerage investment banking services, including facilitating mergers and corporate organizations. ECMA noted that this transition from a Securities Investment Advisory role demonstrates the market's growth capacity and trust. However, the license does not permit the firm to act as a Securities Broker or Securities Dealer.

This upgrade makes Deloitte the second foreign entity authorized for investment banking services, bringing the total number of licensed Investment Banks to nine. United Capital Financial Services Plc, a Nigerian subsidiary, was the first foreign investment bank licensed in Ethiopia on June 5, 2026, a move ECMA described as a historic milestone for the country's capital market.

Why it mattersInvestors are directly affected as Deloitte can now offer investment banking services, facilitating mergers and corporate organizations, and increasing competition in the sector.
