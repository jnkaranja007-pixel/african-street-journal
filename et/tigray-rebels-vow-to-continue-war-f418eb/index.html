Tigray rebels vow to continue war

TPLF leader Debretsion Gebremichael claims tactical victories and says the region will be a graveyard for enemies.

Source: Africanews · 2026-10-24 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

The leader of Ethiopia’s rebel Tigray People’s Liberation Front (TPLF), Debretsion Gebremichael, stated on Wednesday that the northern state remains at war with federal forces.

Gebremichael claimed the TPLF has achieved several tactical victories, including neutralizing hundreds of federal soldiers last week in northern and central Tigray, and vowed the region will be a "graveyard for its enemies."

This comes after the army recaptured the regional capital Mekelle earlier this month, and analysts warn the advance could increase the risk of war with Eritrea, following a drone strike that killed four people in southern Tigray.

Why it mattersThe conflict in Ethiopia's Tigray region continues, affecting civilians and potentially escalating into a wider regional confrontation.
