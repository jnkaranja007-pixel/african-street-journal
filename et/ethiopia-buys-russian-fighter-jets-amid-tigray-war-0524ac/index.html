Ethiopia buys Russian fighter jets amid Tigray war

Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed's military purchases raise questions about how the debt-strapped nation is financing them.

Source: The Africa Report · 2026-10-30 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Ethiopia has acquired Russian fighter jets and air defence systems, according to reports on October 29, 2026. The new military hardware could significantly alter the country's air power capabilities.

The acquisition of these advanced aircraft occurs while fighting continues in Ethiopia's northern Tigray region, which began last month. Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed's government is facing scrutiny over the financial means used for these substantial military purchases, given the country's existing debt.

Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed also met with Russian President Vladimir Putin in Moscow on October 29, 2026, during the third Russia–Africa Summit. Their discussions included expanding cooperation in nuclear energy, health, and trade.

Why it mattersEthiopians are directly affected by the acquisition of new fighter jets amid an ongoing internal conflict and questions about national debt.
