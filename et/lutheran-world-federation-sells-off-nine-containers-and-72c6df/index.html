Lutheran World Federation sells off nine containers and aluminium profiles

Sealed bids for the nine containers and nine types of aluminium profiles close on 10 December in Addis Ababa.

Source: Capital Ethiopia · 2026-11-19 · 1 min read

The Lutheran World Federation is inviting sealed bids for nine containers, including 20-foot units, and nine types of aluminium profiles. The auction runs from 19 November to 10 December, with each lot going to its highest bidder.

Bidders must lodge 50,000 Ethiopian birr in certified payment order security and attach a valid trade licence or national ID. Bids are due by 11:00 AM on 10 December and will be opened at 2:00 PM at the Federation's country office at 5 Kilo in Addis Ababa. Bids may cover one lot or both, but every item in a lot must be priced.

The items are sold as is, without warranty, and can be inspected at the Federation's premises during working hours. Winning bidders must pay in full within five working days of the award notice and remove the goods within 10 working days of payment, at their own cost and risk.
