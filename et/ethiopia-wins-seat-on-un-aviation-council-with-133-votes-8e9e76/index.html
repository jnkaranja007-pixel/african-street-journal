Ethiopia wins seat on UN aviation council with 133 votes

The country was elected alongside Türkiye, Oman and Indonesia in a ballot that filled four new seats on the council of the United Nations aviation agency.

Source: Horn Diplomat · 2026-11-24 · 1 min read

Ethiopia won a seat on the governing council of the International Civil Aviation Organization (ICAO), the United Nations agency for aviation, receiving 133 votes in a ballot on Nov. 20. The vote took place during an extraordinary session of the ICAO Assembly and filled four new places on the council.

Türkiye, Oman and Indonesia were also elected in the ballot, according to Horn Diplomat. The four countries were chosen for the expanded council; the report did not give the total number of votes cast or the length of their terms.

The report named no further steps or dates following the vote.
