Southern Ethiopia chamber signs Emirates Holidays deal to promote trade

The agreement sets out joint marketing of meetings and events, with chamber members offered conference access and discounted travel.

Source: Capital Ethiopia · 2026-11-24 · 1 min read

The Southern Ethiopia Regional Chamber of Commerce and Sectoral Association signed a memorandum of understanding with Emirates Holidays in Addis Ababa to promote business and tourism ties with the United Arab Emirates. The agreement focuses on meetings, incentives, conferences and exhibitions between Ethiopia, Dubai and other destinations served by Emirates.

The chamber’s president, Bogale Bitane, said the partnership would promote regional business opportunities and help local enterprises reach foreign markets. The two sides plan coordinated campaigns, events and business initiatives, and will exchange updates on visas, travel rules, tourism and markets.

Chamber members are expected to gain access to international trade and investment conferences and discounted air travel to destinations in the Middle East and Asia. Emirates Holidays business development manager Maitha Albutehi also said the company was open to cooperation with Ethiopian Airlines to connect arriving international passengers with domestic destinations and other African travel.

Why it mattersChamber members are set to gain access to international conferences and discounted air travel to the Middle East and Asia.
