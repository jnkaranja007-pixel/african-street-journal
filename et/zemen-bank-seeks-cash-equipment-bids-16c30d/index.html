Zemen Bank seeks cash equipment bids

The Addis Ababa bank is procuring note detectors, safes, generators, and TVs.

Source: Capital Ethiopia · 2026-11-12 · 1 min read

Zemen Bank S.C. is seeking bids for various cash-related equipment and branch opening items. The procurement includes multi-currency detecting machines, note counters, fire-resistant safes of different sizes, generators, and LED TVs with receivers. A one-year contract for the supply and installation of electrical and data cables is also included.

Interested bidders can obtain bid documents from Zemen Bank's headquarters in Addis Ababa. The non-refundable fee for a complete set of documents is Birr 230.00, payable in cash. Bid proposals must be submitted by November 24, 2026, at 5:00 PM, accompanied by a bid security ranging from ETB 150,000.00 to ETB 700,000.00 per lot.

Bid opening sessions are scheduled for November 25, 2026, starting at 9:00 AM. Zemen Bank reserves the right to reject any or all bids submitted.

Why it mattersBusinesses that supply cash handling equipment, safes, generators, or electrical and data cabling services can bid for contracts with Zemen Bank.
