Ethiopian Jews mark Sigd holy day in Jerusalem

Thousands gathered in Jerusalem on Monday for the Sigd observance, which Kessim religious leaders led as a day of prayer and reflection.

Source: Africanews · 2026-11-11 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Thousands of Ethiopian Jews gathered in Jerusalem on Monday to observe the holy day of Sigd. Kessim, the community's religious leaders, led the observance, which is meant as a day of introspection and prayer seeking a return to Israel.

Most Ethiopian Jews now live in Israel, and the community comes together in Jerusalem each year to mark the day and celebrate its culture. Religious leader Michael Maharat said the community has integrated well and made progress this generation, and he voiced hope that the Ethiopian Jews who have not yet immigrated will be able to do so.

In 1991, while Ethiopia was in civil war, Israel carried out Operation Solomon, airlifting some 14,500 Ethiopian Jews out in less than two days. The Ethiopian Jewish community in Israel now numbers 180,000.

Why it mattersEthiopian Jews living in Israel, and those still waiting to immigrate, are the people directly affected by the observance and its emphasis on return to Israel.
