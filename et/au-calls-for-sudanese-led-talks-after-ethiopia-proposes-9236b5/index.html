AU calls for Sudanese-led talks after Ethiopia proposes dialogue

African Union Commission Chairperson Mahmoud Ali Youssouf said Sudanese stakeholders should lead any talks to end the war after Ethiopia offered to convene them.

Source: Horn Diplomat · 2026-11-26 · 1 min read

The African Union called for Sudanese stakeholders to lead any talks to end Sudan's war after Ethiopia proposed convening a dialogue among them. Mahmoud Ali Youssouf, chairperson of the AU Commission, made the call on Wednesday.

Youssouf said the proposal was important to efforts to promote peace, according to Horn Diplomat. His position was that Sudanese stakeholders, rather than outside parties, should set the terms of any negotiations.

The report did not give a date for the proposed dialogue.

Why it mattersEthiopia's offer to host Sudanese stakeholders now has to fit the AU's position that Sudanese parties, not outsiders, lead any negotiations.
