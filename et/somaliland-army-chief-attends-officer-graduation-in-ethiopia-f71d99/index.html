Somaliland army chief attends officer graduation in Ethiopia

Major General Niman Yusuf Osman joined the ceremony for Somaliland officers who studied at Ethiopia's Defence Command and Staff College.

Source: Horn Diplomat · 2026-11-15 · 1 min read

Major General Niman Yusuf Osman, Somaliland's army chief, was present at the graduation of Somaliland military officers on Saturday. The Somaliland military said the officers had finished their studies at Ethiopia's Defence Command and Staff College, a military institution in Ethiopia. The announcement was made on the same day.

The army commander is on a working visit to Ethiopia and joined the ceremony in person. The officers trained at the college before receiving their qualifications. The report does not give the number of officers who graduated.

Osman's attendance comes during his working visit, which the Somaliland military described in its statement. No further engagements were listed in the report.

Why it mattersSomaliland officers who completed training in Ethiopia now hold qualifications from the college, with the army chief present to mark the result.
