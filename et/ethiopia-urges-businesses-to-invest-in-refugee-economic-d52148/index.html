Ethiopia urges businesses to invest in refugee economic inclusion

At a forum in Addis Ababa, a state minister called for commercially viable partnerships and better access to jobs, finance and markets.

Source: Capital Ethiopia · 2026-11-19 · 1 min read

Ethiopia urged African governments, businesses and financial institutions to expand investment and economic opportunities for refugees at the fourth Africa Forum on Displacement, which opened in Addis Ababa on November 17. State Minister for Political and Economic Diplomacy Hadera Abera said refugees should be treated as potential contributors to host communities and national economies, not only as people needing humanitarian aid.

Abera said Ethiopia’s Refugees Proclamation No. 1110/2019 widened refugees’ access to employment, education, movement and other socioeconomic opportunities. The government has also introduced measures to support work permits, business registration, licensing and access to services, he said, but reforms must lead to decent jobs, viable businesses and finance. Ethiopia hosts more than 1.1 million refugees.

The two-day forum focused on building an inclusive economic system. Abera called for investment in infrastructure, energy, agriculture, education, connectivity and financial services, saying these could create opportunities for refugees and host communities.

Why it mattersEthiopia’s more than 1.1 million refugees and host communities need policy changes to translate into jobs, finance and viable businesses.
