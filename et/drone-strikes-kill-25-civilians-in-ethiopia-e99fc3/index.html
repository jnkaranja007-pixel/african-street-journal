Drone strikes kill 25 civilians in Ethiopia

Witnesses report multiple strikes hit northern regions since Thursday, with children among the victims in Amhara.

Source: Africanews · 2026-11-02 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

At least 25 civilians, including children, died in multiple drone strikes in Ethiopia's northern regions since Thursday, according to witnesses and a medical source.

In Amhara, a witness described four drone strikes hitting a camp for displaced people on Thursday, killing 14 people at the center with two more dying later. Two other residents reported 14 and 15 deaths respectively, with children among them.

The strikes occurred about 400 kilometers northeast of Addis Ababa, as students were returning from school, one witness said, adding his sister-in-law's body was dismembered.

Why it mattersCivilians in Ethiopia's Amhara and Tigray regions are directly affected by drone strikes during ongoing conflict.
