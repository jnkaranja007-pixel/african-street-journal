Ethiopia and AAMFI sign deal to host permanent secretariat in Addis Ababa

The Host Country Agreement, signed at the Foreign Ministry, sets the framework for the Alliance's permanent base and closer coordination with the African Union.

Source: Capital Ethiopia · 2026-11-28 · 1 min read

The Alliance of African Multilateral Financial Institutions (AAMFI), also known as the Africa Club, and the Government of Ethiopia have signed a Host Country Agreement to establish the Alliance's permanent Secretariat in Addis Ababa. The agreement was signed at the Ministry of Foreign Affairs by Ambassador Hadera, State Minister of Foreign Affairs, and Corneille Karekezi, chairperson of the AAMFI Governing Council and group managing director and chief executive of the African Reinsurance Corporation (Africa Re).

Ambassador Hadera said the agreement was an important step in strengthening cooperation between Ethiopia and AAMFI. He pointed to Addis Ababa's position as the seat of the African Union and numerous international organisations, and to Ethiopian Airlines' international connections, as advantages for the Alliance's continental and global work. Dr Karekezi said the Secretariat would help members work together, mobilise African capital and advance an African-led financial architecture.

The Secretariat will give the Alliance an institutional base to deepen collaboration among its members and with continental partners, and to pursue joint initiatives on Africa's development financing. The Alliance was launched in Addis Ababa on 17 February 2024 with the African Union Commission and brings together African-owned and African-controlled multilateral financial institutions.

Why it mattersMember institutions and continental partners will coordinate from a permanent Addis Ababa base, with the African Union nearby, instead of an ad hoc arrangement.
