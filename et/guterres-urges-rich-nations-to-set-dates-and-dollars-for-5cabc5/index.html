Guterres urges rich nations to set dates and dollars for climate adaptation

The UN chief spoke after the 31st Climate Change Conference (COP31) in Türkiye ended without a political agreement, while developing economies need about $305 billion a year.

Source: Capital Ethiopia · 2026-11-24 · 1 min read

United Nations Secretary-General António Guterres urged wealthy nations to put dates and dollars behind their climate adaptation pledges after the 31st United Nations Climate Change Conference (COP31) ended in Türkiye without an overarching political agreement. Negotiators were divided over climate finance and over whether to reaffirm the COP28 commitment to move away from coal, oil and natural gas.

According to the United Nations Environment Programme's 2026 Adaptation Gap Report, developing economies will need about $305 billion a year by 2035 for climate-resilient agriculture, water management, flood protection and early-warning systems. During the summit, Guterres pressed for at least $300 billion a year in climate finance by 2035, and clear plans to triple adaptation support within a $1.3 trillion goal.

Ethiopia will host the next conference, COP32, in Addis Ababa in November 2027. Discussions ahead of it are expected to focus on a proposed Antalya-to-Addis Ababa Roadmap on Adaptation and Resilience Finance.

Why it mattersEthiopia, host of COP32 in Addis Ababa in November 2027, will take up a proposed adaptation finance roadmap that starts from the unresolved funding dispute.
