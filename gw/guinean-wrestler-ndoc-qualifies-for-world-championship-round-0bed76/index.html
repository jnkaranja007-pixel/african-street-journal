Guinean wrestler Ndoc qualifies for world championship round

Wotna Cana Ndoc will face Russian Ibragim Ibragimov in a qualification match for the round of 32.

Source: O Democrata GB · 2026-10-30 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Guinean wrestler Wotna Cana Ndoc faces Russian Ibragim Ibragimov on Friday, October 30, 2026, in a qualification bout for the round of 32 at the World Wrestling Championship in Kazakhstan.

Ndoc, competing in the 70-kilogram category, is the first athlete from Guinea-Bissau in this edition of the championship. The Guinean Wrestling Federation reports the athlete is confident and determined to represent the nation.

Diamantino Iuna Fafé will compete on Saturday, October 31, entering directly into the round of 32 against the winner of a match between Moldova's Andrei Vitan and Kyrgyzstan's Abdymalik Karachov.

Why it mattersTwo Guinean wrestlers are participating in the World Wrestling Championship in Kazakhstan.
