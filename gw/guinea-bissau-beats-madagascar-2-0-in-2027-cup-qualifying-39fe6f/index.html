Guinea-Bissau beats Madagascar 2-0 in 2027 Cup qualifying

Renato Nhaga helped control midfield in the fourth-round victory at Bissau’s Estádio Nacional 24 de Setembro.

Source: O Democrata GB · 2026-11-22 · 1 min read

Guinea-Bissau beat Madagascar 2-0 in Bissau in the fourth round of qualifying for the 2027 Africa Cup of Nations. Renato Nhaga, the 19-year-old midfielder who plays for Turkey’s Galatasaray, was among the decisive players in the home side’s win.

Nhaga helped disrupt Madagascar’s passing, recover possession and protect the centre of the pitch, O Democrata GB reported. The victory followed his first goal for Guinea-Bissau, scored against Madagascar on 12 November in the teams’ third-round match.

Nhaga joined Galatasaray in February 2026 and won the Turkish league title in the 2025/26 season. He made his Champions League debut in September 2026.

Why it mattersNhaga’s role in Guinea-Bissau’s qualifying campaign follows his first national-team goal on 12 November and the 2-0 home win.
