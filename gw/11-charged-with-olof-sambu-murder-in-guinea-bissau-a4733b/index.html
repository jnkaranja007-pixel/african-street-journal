11 charged with Olof Sambú murder in Guinea-Bissau

Suspects face charges of murder and qualified damage following a public lynching on September 27, 2026.

Source: O Democrata GB · 2026-11-11 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Guinea-Bissau's Public Ministry has formally charged 11 suspects in connection with the death of Olof João Gomes Sambú. The incident occurred on September 27, 2026, in the Chapa area of Bissau. The accused are currently in preventive detention and have eight days to contest the charges.

The suspects are accused of murder and qualified damage under Guinea-Bissau's Penal Code. The Public Ministry stated that sufficient evidence has been gathered to show the 11 acted together in committing the crimes. Olof Sambú died after being falsely accused of attempting to kidnap a child, leading to a public lynching where his vehicle was intercepted and destroyed before he was fatally assaulted.

The National Guard Command clarified that Olof Gomes Sambú was not a member of their corps but a civilian employee of the General Directorate of Customs, serving as head of Anti-Fraud in the Gabú Delegation. If the suspects do not challenge the charges within the legal timeframe, the case will proceed to trial.

Why it matters11 individuals face trial for murder and property damage, impacting justice and public safety.
