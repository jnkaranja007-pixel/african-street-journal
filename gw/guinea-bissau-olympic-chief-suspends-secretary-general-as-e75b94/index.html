Guinea-Bissau Olympic chief suspends secretary-general as federations seek assembly

Fernando Arlete’s 16 November order ends Eugénio de Oliveira Lopes’s representative, administrative and communications duties for the committee.

Source: O Democrata GB · 2026-11-20 · 1 min read

Fernando Arlete, president of Guinea-Bissau’s Olympic committee, suspended its secretary-general, Eugénio de Oliveira Lopes, under an order signed on 16 November. The order ended Lopes’s representative, administrative and communications duties on behalf of the committee, according to O Democrata.

Arlete cited a breakdown in trust and alleged institutional disobedience, including correspondence with international partners without his knowledge and foreign travel without authorisation. The order also says Lopes challenged the current leadership with regional Olympic organisations. Arlete said the secretary-general’s role requires trust, loyalty and cooperation with the president.

Separately, affiliated federations requested an extraordinary general assembly to discuss the organisation’s internal situation. Their request, dated 19 November, was signed by the handball, athletics, basketball, judo, table tennis, tennis, aquatic sports, winter sports and taekwondo federations, and the Athletes’ Commission.

Why it mattersThe affiliated federations that signed the 19 November request are seeking an extraordinary assembly to discuss the committee’s internal dispute.
