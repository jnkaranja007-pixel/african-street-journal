Guinea-Bissau's prime minister urges peace at installation of new Bishop of Bissau

Dom Victor Luís Quematcha becomes the fourth bishop of the Diocese of Bissau, which marks its 50th anniversary this year.

Sources: O Democrata GB and Conosaba do Porto · 2026-11-17 · 1 min read

Prime Minister Ilídio Vieira Té attended the installation of Dom Victor Luís Quematcha as Bishop of Bissau on Sunday, 15 November, at the Sé Catedral. Representatives of several religious denominations took part, along with political leaders and congregants.

Vieira Té said the presence of different faiths reflected the ethnic and religious diversity of Guinea-Bissau. He asked the bishops of the regional episcopal conference, priests and lay faithful to keep praying for peace, forgiveness and reconciliation among Guineans, and he expressed the hope that the new bishop would play an active role in promoting peace and social cohesion.

The ceremony also opened the celebrations for the diocese's 50th anniversary. The diocese was created in 1975 and was first led by Dom Settimio Arturo Ferrazzetta. It was later headed by Dom José Câmnate na Bissign, the first Catholic bishop born in Guinea-Bissau, and then by Dom José Lampra Cá, who recently became bishop emeritus after resigning for health reasons.

Why it mattersGuinean Catholics in Bissau now have a new bishop, and the diocese's 50th-anniversary programme begins with his installation.
