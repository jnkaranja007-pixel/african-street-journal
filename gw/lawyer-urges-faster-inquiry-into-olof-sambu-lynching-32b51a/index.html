Lawyer urges faster inquiry into Olof Sambu lynching

Olof Sambu died on September 27, 2026, after being attacked by a mob on false kidnapping rumours.

Source: O Democrata GB · 2026-11-08 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

The lawyer for Olof João Gomes Sambú's family urged the Guinean state to speed up investigations into his death. Suleimane Cassamá stated that the state must take responsibility for clarifying the circumstances of the killing and holding perpetrators accountable. Cassamá spoke on Friday, November 6, 2026, after the family testified at the Ministry of Justice as part of the ongoing inquiry.

Olof João Gomes Sambú died on September 27, 2026, in the Chapa de Bissau area. He was allegedly lynched by a crowd after false information circulated accusing him of attempting to kidnap a child. Witnesses reported that the mob damaged his vehicle and assaulted him following these suspicions.

Cassamá described the family's testimony as encouraging, showing the case remains under investigation. The family hopes for justice and that the truth about the incident will emerge. The investigation is proceeding with witness testimonies to establish the facts of the case.

Why it mattersOlof Sambu was killed by a mob on false rumours, and his family is seeking justice through an accelerated investigation.
