Guinea-Bissau delays presidential and parliamentary elections until 2028

A decree moves the vote from 6 December to 26 November 2028 after the presidency said election preparations lacked sufficient resources.

Source: BBC Africa · 2026-11-27 · 1 min read

Guinea-Bissau’s transitional president, Gen Horta N’Tam, has postponed presidential and parliamentary elections from 6 December to 26 November 2028, delaying the planned return to civilian rule by two years.

The presidency said an assessment found insufficient technical, material and financial conditions to hold the polls as scheduled. It said the extra time would help ensure credible, transparent and inclusive elections, with equal opportunities for all candidates.

The announcement came a year after the military seized power and promised a return to civilian rule within 12 months. The presidency reiterated its commitment to the country’s first local government elections but set no date for them.

Why it mattersVoters and candidates must wait until 26 November 2028 for national elections, while the first local government polls have no set date.
