Guinea-Bissau charges 11 over Olof Sambú death

11 suspects face charges of murder and qualified damage for the September 27, 2026, public lynching.

Source: O Democrata GB · 2026-11-12 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Guinea-Bissau's Public Ministry has formally charged 11 suspects in connection with the death of Olof João Gomes Sambú on September 27, 2026, in the Chapa area of Bissau. The accused are in preventive detention and have eight days to contest the charges. The Public Ministry stated it has gathered sufficient evidence to prove the 11 acted together in committing the crimes of homicide and qualified damage, as defined by the Guinean Penal Code.

Olof Sambú died following a public lynching after being falsely accused of attempting to kidnap a child. Investigators found that a group of people pursued Sambú, intercepted and destroyed his vehicle before fatally assaulting him. The National Guard Command clarified that Olof Gomes Sambú was not a member of their corps but a civilian employee of the Directorate-General of Customs, where he served as head of Anti-Fraud in the Gabú Delegation.

If no legal challenges are filed within the deadline, the case will proceed to trial. The charges include murder and qualified damage. The investigation concluded that the victim was falsely associated with a child abduction attempt before the assault.

Why it matters11 individuals are facing trial for the murder of Olof Sambú, a civilian customs official, following a public lynching.
