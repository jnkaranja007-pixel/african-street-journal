Guinean wrestler Ndoc qualifies for world championship round

Wotna Cana Ndoc will face Russian Ibragim Ibragimov in a qualification match for the round of 32.

Guinean wrestler Wotna Cana Ndoc faces Russian Ibragim Ibragimov on Friday, October 30, 2026, in a qualification bout for the round of 32 at the World Wrestling Championship in Kazakhstan.

Ndoc, competing in the 70-kilogram category, is the first athlete from Guinea-Bissau in this edition of the championship. The Guinean Wrestling Federation reports the athlete is confident and determined to represent the nation.

Diamantino Iuna Fafé will compete on Saturday, October 31, entering directly into the round of 32 against the winner of a match between Moldova's Andrei Vitan and Kyrgyzstan's Abdymalik Karachov.

Why it mattersTwo Guinean wrestlers are participating in the World Wrestling Championship in Kazakhstan.

Guinea-Bissau minister urges realistic budgets

Communication Minister Abduramane Turé called for financial capacity assessment before state commitments.

Guinea-Bissau's Minister of Social Communication, Abduramane Turé, on Thursday, October 29, 2026, advocated for a strict evaluation of financial execution capacity before the state takes on new commitments. Speaking at the opening of the INACEP Activity Planning Retreat for 2027, Turé stated that planning must be based on clear priorities, realistic objectives, and resources aligned with the institution's financial capacity.

The two-day retreat, themed “Planning for the repositioning of INACEP as the State's national documentary security service,” aims to define the activity plan and budget for the upcoming year. Minister Turé emphasized that an effective plan requires a realistic diagnosis, identification of challenges, establishment of priorities, and measurable indicators. He highlighted technological modernization, digital transformation, operational capacity strengthening, and human resource development as priority areas.

Lemes Monteiro, the Director-General of INACEP, announced that the retreat will conclude with the development of the 2027 activity plan and budget. He acknowledged the institution's challenges, pointing to the regularization of retiring staff through social security as a priority. The minister urged the adoption of concrete solutions for identified problems, advocating for a financially sustainable and feasible planning document.

Why it mattersThe Minister of Communication and the Director-General of INACEP are discussing the institution's financial planning and operational priorities for 2027.

Politics · 2026-10-30

Firkidja di Pubis denounces regime crisis

Political movement calls for international sanctions against coup leaders and return to democracy.

The political movement Firkidja di Pubis denounced on Thursday, October 29, 2026, an alleged internal crisis within Guinea-Bissau's current regime. The group called on the international community to impose sanctions on those responsible for blocking the political process and demanded a return to democratic order and the release of political prisoners.

In a statement, Firkidja di Pubis described the situation as a "palace coup" within the regime that emerged from the November 2025 coup. They cited the disarmament of militias loyal to former President Umaro Sissoco Embaló, his prevented return to the country, changes in the Ministry of Interior, the Court of Accounts, and the Prosecutor General's Office, as well as military hierarchy adjustments, as evidence of internal disputes.

The movement stated that the division within the ruling bloc stems from a lack of political cohesion, disagreements over the election calendar, power struggles, and the pursuit of external support. Firkidja di Pubis also called for justice and transparency in managing the crisis and reiterated its opposition to the military high command remaining in power.

Why it mattersThe movement's call for sanctions and return to democracy affects Guineans abroad concerned about their country's stability and the release of political prisoners.

Politics · 2026-10-30

PSTGB leader criticises ECOWAS, demands elections

Sene Bacai Cassamá says ECOWAS consultations excluded parties and calls for simultaneous polls within six months.

Sene Bacai Cassamá, president of the Socialist Workers' Party of Guinea-Bissau (PSTGB), called for simultaneous elections within six months to choose a new President and Government. Cassamá made these statements on Wednesday, October 28, 2026, in Bissau.

The PSTGB leader criticised the Economic Community of West African States (ECOWAS) mission, stating that excluding political parties from consultations violates democratic principles. Cassamá expressed concern that the Community of Portuguese Language Countries (CPLP) should pressure for a return to constitutional normality, adding that the country is not on the right path.

Cassamá argued that extending the absence of elections by another year would be unjust given the population's growing difficulties. He denounced alleged restrictions on democratic freedoms and political activity, stating that parties lack freedom to speak and citizens cannot freely express divergent opinions.

Why it mattersThe leader of the PSTGB is calling for immediate elections and criticising regional bodies, affecting the political direction of Guinea-Bissau.
