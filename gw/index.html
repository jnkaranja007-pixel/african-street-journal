Chinese medical team offers free child health checks in Bissau

18 health professionals provided consultations and health education on scalp ringworm at SOS School.

The 21st Chinese Medical Team held a child health seminar at SOS School in Bissau on Friday, October 23, 2026, offering free medical consultations and promoting Traditional Chinese Medicine. The initiative involved 18 health professionals from the Military Hospital and Canchungo Regional Hospital, who also distributed footballs and school supplies to children.

The seminar focused on preventing scalp ringworm, a common childhood illness during the rainy season, detailing its symptoms, transmission, and the importance of early diagnosis and treatment. Doctor Wang Weicheng advised students to avoid contact with infected animals, refrain from sharing personal items, and maintain a clean, dry scalp, noting the disease's prevalence in the Bijagós Archipelago.

Agostinho Bolamu, director of SOS Basic School, thanked the Chinese medical team for their initiative, emphasizing its positive impact on the health and well-being of both children and teachers. Students also learned about Traditional Chinese Medicine through an exhibition of medicinal plants.

Guinea-Bissau police rescue 71 trafficking victims

Operation ASTRA dismantled a human trafficking ring that lured victims with promises of work abroad.

Police in Guinea-Bissau rescued 71 people from various West African nations on October 22, 2026, in an operation that dismantled a human trafficking network. The operation, named ASTRA, led to the arrest of four suspects. The victims, originally from Mali, Guinea, Burkina Faso, Togo, Senegal, and Sierra Leone, were lured with promises of jobs in Canada, Europe, and the United States.

According to the Judicial Police (PJ) spokesperson Braima Cissé, the victims were brought to Bissau and held against their will. PJ agent António Gomes told AFP that the victims faced physical and psychological pressure, including threats and abuse, and were forced into pyramid schemes to recruit new members via social media. The network allegedly provided false office details in Canada, the United States, and Portugal to appear legitimate.

Interpol's information aided the dismantling of the network, which investigators believe operated across several countries in the sub-region. The rescued individuals will be handed over to their respective embassies, with others needing to contact family for assistance to return home.

Why it matters71 people from several West African countries were rescued from a human trafficking ring in Guinea-Bissau.

Guinea-Bissau groups demand political prisoner release

Organisations sent a manifesto to ECOWAS mediators calling for democratic freedoms and an end to detentions.

Political and civic groups in Guinea-Bissau demanded the immediate release of all political prisoners on October 23, 2026. The groups, part of the Frente Única and the Rede Internacional de Solidariedade com a Guiné-Bissau, asked the Economic Community of West African States (ECOWAS) to intervene. They stated that continued detention contradicts court decisions.

The manifesto, delivered to ECOWAS mediators in Dakar and Senegalese embassies in five cities including Bissau, also called for the full restoration of fundamental freedoms and political activities. It specifically requested the reopening of political party headquarters and the lifting of restrictions on civil society and media organisations. The groups expressed dissatisfaction with the perceived lack of impartiality from the Senegalese mediation, accusing it of being too close to the current government.

Signatories include API-Cabas Garandi, PAIGC, and UNTG-CS, along with international solidarity groups. They urged ECOWAS to engage with the military command to restore constitutional order, respecting the popular vote from November 2025. The document also proposed individual sanctions against those obstructing the restoration of constitutional normality.

Politics · 2026-10-24

PRS youth wings agree to unified congress

Factions associated with Franco Ialá and Ednilson Alberto Nambeia have committed to ending internal divisions within the party.

The youth wing of Guinea-Bissau's Social Renovation Party (PRS), known as JRS, announced on October 23, 2026, that internal divisions have ended, forming a single youth organization. Representatives from factions linked to Franco Ialá and Ednilson Alberto Nambeia formalized this decision in Bissau, agreeing to work together for the party's reunification.

In a document, the youth declared their split "free, definitive and irreversible" and will act as facilitators for party reunification. They will form a Joint Youth Commission to draft a roadmap for a unified and inclusive congress, committing to cease mutual attacks on social media and public spaces, and to avoid participating in separate congresses.

The JRS is calling on the two wings of the party's Superior Directorate to negotiate a unified congress. The youth have pledged to uphold unity, dialogue, forgiveness, and the collective interest over personal or group ambitions for the full reunification of the PRS.

Why it mattersThe youth wings of the Social Renovation Party (PRS) have agreed to end internal divisions and work towards a unified congress, impacting the party's political future.
