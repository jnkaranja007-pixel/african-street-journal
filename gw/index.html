Guinea-Bissau beats Madagascar 2-0 to lead Group L with 10 points

Mauro Teixeira scored in the 69th minute in Bissau, giving the national side a three-point lead over second-placed Nigeria.

Guinea-Bissau beat Madagascar 2-0 on Tuesday, 17 November, at the Estádio Nacional 24 de Setembro in Bissau, moving to 10 points after four Group L qualifying matches for the 2027 Africa Cup of Nations.

Mama Baldé opened the scoring in the 32nd minute, and Mauro Teixeira added the second in the 69th minute from an Adramane Cassamá assist. Coach Emiliano Té’s side now leads Nigeria by three points.

Madagascar tried to respond after the break but could not get past Guinea-Bissau’s defence. The visitors were seeking their first win in this qualifying phase.

Why it mattersGuinea-Bissau’s national team has a three-point lead over Nigeria after four matches, with Group L qualification still under way.
