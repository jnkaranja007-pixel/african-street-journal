Univers-Sel seeks technical manager for rice projects

The international solidarity association needs someone with an agronomy degree and experience in rice cultivation for a two-year contract.

The international solidarity association Univers-Sel is recruiting a Technical Manager to oversee its rice and water management projects in Guinea-Bissau. The role involves participatory diagnostics, rehabilitation of rice paddies, and supervision of field teams. The position is based in Bissau with frequent travel to the field and occasional missions to The Gambia.

The candidate must have a higher education in agronomy, rural engineering, or agricultural hydraulics, with experience in rice cultivation or hydro-agricultural planning. Fluency in Portuguese is essential, while knowledge of French, English, and Guinea-Bissau Creole is an advantage. The contract is for a minimum of two years with a six-month probationary period.

Applications, including a CV, motivation letter, and two professional references, should be sent to Univers-Sel by November 30, 2026, for initial review. The position is to start as soon as possible and will remain open until filled.

Why it mattersFarmers in Guinea-Bissau will benefit from improved rice cultivation techniques and water management through this new technical role.

Sport · 2026-11-10

Mamadu Aliu Djaló wins West Africa draughts tournament

The Guinean player secured first place in Senegal after six matches in the Zone 2 competition.

Guinean draughts player Mamadu Aliu Djaló won the first Sub-Regional Draughts Tournament of Zone 2, held in Dakar, Senegal, from October 31 to November 5. The competition brought together players from six West African countries.

Djaló earned eight points from three wins, two draws, and one loss in the round-robin format. This victory reinforces his standing as a top player in the sub-region.

Born in Guinea-Conakry on October 10, 1979, Djaló has a history of winning tournaments in Bissau, including the National Draughts Championship in 2026. His win in Dakar is a source of pride for Guinean sport.

Why it mattersGuinean athlete Mamadu Aliu Djaló achieved a significant victory in an international sports competition.

Business · 2026-11-10

EAGB workers accuse management of harmful practices

Staff at Guinea-Bissau's electricity and water company cite poor decision-making and rights violations.

Employees of the Empresa de Eletricidade e Águas da Guiné-Bissau (EAGB) have reported alleged mismanagement, warning of a potential deep crisis impacting public services. The union president, António Ramalhano, publicly announced these concerns on Monday, November 9, 2026, in Bissau.

The EAGB's base workers' union accused the current director-general of concentrating excessive decision-making power, violating worker rights, and disregarding internal company rules. Ramalhano stated that a single person now makes all decisions, leaving directors without effective intervention capacity.

Unionists warned that years of accumulating management problems are compromising the company's normal operations and service quality. They claim the administration acts outside the law, ignoring company regulations and statutes.

Guinea-Bissau prosecutor investigates vicar's death

A special commission will spend 60 days investigating the killing of Vicar Luís Balanta.

Guinea-Bissau's Public Prosecutor's Office has formed an inquiry commission to investigate the murder of Vicar Luís Balanta and determine criminal responsibility.

The commission, established by Prosecutor-General Mário Yala, will conduct necessary investigations to clarify facts, identify perpetrators, and assign blame. The Public Prosecutor's Office stated its commitment to justice and fighting crime.

This commission has a legal deadline of 60 days to complete its work and present findings. The investigation will proceed with rigor and objectivity, respecting the principles of the rule of law.
