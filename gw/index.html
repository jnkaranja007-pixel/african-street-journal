Health · 2026-11-14

Guinea-Bissau official flags shortages of staff, equipment and medicines

Fodé Caramba Sanhá urged citizens, schools and religious groups to share the work of preventing diabetes.

Fodé Caramba Sanhá, the first vice-president of Guinea-Bissau's National Transition Council (CNT), warned on Friday, November 13, 2026, that the country's health system lacks staff, equipment and medicines, and that many people struggle to reach care.

He made the remarks at the opening of a technical debate on diabetes prevention and control, held at the National People's Assembly ahead of World Diabetes Day on November 14. He called diabetes one of the main public health challenges in the country and said the response needs the state, health workers, families, schools, civil society and religious bodies. "Each citizen must take responsibility for their own health," he said.

Sanhá called for stronger awareness campaigns, healthy eating, regular physical activity, screening and ongoing follow-up for people living with diabetes. He said efforts should focus on vulnerable groups and remote communities far from main health centres.

Why it mattersPeople in remote communities and those with diabetes are the ones who must now rely on screening and follow-up that the shortages put at risk.

Politics · 2026-11-14

Frente Popular asks new prosecutor to investigate activist's killing

The party wants a fast, independent probe into the murder of Vigário Luís Balanta and an end to military restrictions it calls unconstitutional.

Mário Yala, the newly appointed Attorney General, has been urged by the Frente Popular to ensure a swift, rigorous and impartial inquiry into the killing of the activist Vigário Luís Balanta, so that the facts come out and both the material and moral authors of the crime are held to account.

In a statement dated 13 November 2026, seen by the newspaper O Democrata, the party called for measures against delays, inaction and favouritism in the prosecution service. It also asked authorities to give the public regular updates on how the cases are progressing, within the limits of judicial secrecy.

The party also urged the高 Military High Command to lift restrictions it considers incompatible with the Constitution, and to respect the popular will expressed at the ballot box on 23 November 2025.

Why it mattersRelatives of Vigário Luís Balanta and the public are waiting on a probe that the party wants run by the new Attorney General, with regular updates promised.

Sport · 2026-11-14

Guinea-Bissau draws with Madagascar, keeps Group L lead

The national football team drew one-all with Madagascar in Morocco, maintaining their top position in CAN 2027 qualifying.

Guinea-Bissau drew one-all with Madagascar on Thursday, November 12, 2026, in their third match of the African Cup of Nations (CAN 2027) qualification Group L. The result means the Djurtus, who had won their previous two matches, maintain their lead in the group with seven points.

The match, played in Morocco where Madagascar hosts its home games, saw Guinea-Bissau's coach Emiliano Té make several changes to the starting lineup with less-used players. Despite controlling possession for much of the game, the team struggled to create clear scoring chances. Madagascar's Warren Caddy opened the scoring in the 59th minute, with Guinea-Bissau's goalkeeper Fernando Embadje making crucial saves throughout the encounter.

This draw prevents Guinea-Bissau from extending their lead over Nigeria, a strong contender for qualification. The team's performance in the first half was described as poor, with both sides showing difficulties in offensive build-up and frequent loss of possession. The next matchday will determine if they can solidify their position at the top of the group.
