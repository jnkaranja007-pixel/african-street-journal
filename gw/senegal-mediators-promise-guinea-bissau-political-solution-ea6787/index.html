Senegal mediators promise Guinea-Bissau political solution

Senegal's foreign minister announced a commitment to a peaceful and inclusive political solution for Guinea-Bissau.

Source: O Democrata GB · 2026-10-28 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Senegal's mediation mission in Guinea-Bissau has pledged to work towards a peaceful and inclusive political solution, respecting the nation's sovereignty. The commitment was stated in a communique released on October 27, 2026, by Senegal's Minister of African Integration, Cheikh Niang.

The Senegalese delegation met with Guinea-Bissau's Prime Minister Ilídio Vieira Té and the Head of the Armed Forces General Tomás Djassi. They also held separate discussions with representatives from various political parties, including PAIGC and MADEM-G15, as well as civil society groups.

The mission's findings and proposals will be submitted to the Economic Community of West African States (ECOWAS) for guidance on future mediation steps. Senegal is leading this effort as the current rotating president of ECOWAS, aiming to restore constitutional order in Guinea-Bissau.

Why it mattersGuinea-Bissau's political stability is being addressed through mediation efforts involving Senegal and ECOWAS.
