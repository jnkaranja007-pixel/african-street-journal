Guinea-Bissau military partially lifts political activity ban

Political parties can hold internal meetings from November 24 but public gatherings remain prohibited.

Source: O Democrata GB · 2026-11-08 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Guinea-Bissau's High Military Command announced on Friday, November 6, that some restrictions will be eased, allowing internal political party activities to resume from November 24, 2026.

Legal political parties can now conduct internal meetings, consultations, assemblies, and congresses, provided they notify authorities beforehand and adhere to security conditions. However, marches, demonstrations, rallies, and other public events that could threaten national security or public order are still forbidden.

The military stated that any violation of these measures could lead to intervention by competent authorities, urging political actors and the public to avoid inciting violence or disturbing peace.

Why it mattersPolitical party activities are partially restricted, affecting the ability of politicians and party members to organize and engage publicly.
