Guinea-Bissau sets presidential and legislative elections for 2028

Transition President Horta Inta-a cited missing technical, material and financial conditions for the vote, originally scheduled for 6 December 2026.

Sources: O Democrata GB and Conosaba do Porto · 2026-11-27 · 1 min read

Guinea-Bissau’s transition president, Horta Inta-a, has set 26 November 2028 for presidential and legislative elections, postponing the vote originally scheduled for 6 December 2026. The date is in Presidential Decree No. 35/2026, released on 26 November by the presidency’s communications office.

The decree says the country lacks the technical, material and financial conditions needed to hold the elections. It warns that voting without meeting legal, technical and operational requirements could undermine the process’s regularity, transparency, inclusion and credibility, as well as public and political actors’ confidence.

The decree calls for the electoral calendar to be reviewed with the relevant institutions and other participants. It also says the authorities should advance the establishment of local governments through the first municipal elections provided for in the transition political charter and applicable law.

Why it mattersVoters and political actors must wait until 26 November 2028 for presidential and legislative elections, rather than the vote scheduled for 6 December 2026.
