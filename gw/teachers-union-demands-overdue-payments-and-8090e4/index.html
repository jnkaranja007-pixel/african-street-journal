Teachers union demands overdue payments and reclassifications

Guinea-Bissau's national teachers union warns of class disruptions if overdue payments and reclassifications for 1,112 educators are not addressed.

Source: O Democrata GB · 2026-10-22 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

The National Union of Teachers (SINAPROF) in Guinea-Bissau has called on the government to finalize the reclassification of 1,112 teachers, whose process has been completed and validated by the Ministry of Education. SINAPROF president Domingos Carvalho stated that while the regularization of 5,000 contracted teachers is positive, these outstanding issues continue to affect educators. He also raised concerns about teachers over 45 being excluded from regularization, arguing that many were hired before reaching this age.

Carvalho criticized the financial burdens placed on teachers for submitting documentation for regularization, especially given past salary delays. According to union data, 95% of public schools are open, but only 56% are functioning normally. This is partly due to many contracted teachers traveling to the capital, Bissau, to manage their administrative regularization processes.

Beyond human resources, Carvalho highlighted the poor state of school infrastructure, reporting that 80% of public schools lack adequate buildings, with some operating out of makeshift structures. The union is negotiating with the government to find solutions for integrating these professionals into the education system and to address the infrastructure deficits.

Why it mattersTeachers and students in Guinea-Bissau are affected by potential class disruptions and inadequate learning environments due to unresolved administrative and infrastructure issues.
