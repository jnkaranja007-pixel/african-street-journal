Guinea-Bissau to compete in West Africa draughts tournament

The national draughts federation aims for a podium finish at the inaugural Zone-2 tournament in Dakar, Senegal.

Source: O Democrata GB · 2026-11-03 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Guinea-Bissau will participate in the first Sub-Regional Draughts Tournament of Zone-2, scheduled from October 31 to November 5, 2026, in Dakar, Senegal. The Senegalese Draughts Federation extended the invitation following recent achievements in the sport within Guinea-Bissau.

The country will be represented by Mamadu Aliu Djaló, a player described by the Guinean Draughts Federation president, Bacar Dabó, as one of the region's top talents with extensive international experience. Dabó stated that Djaló possesses strong reasoning skills and competitive composure, making him capable of competing at an equal level with other participants.

The tournament will also feature teams from Senegal, Burkina Faso, Mali, Mauritania, and Guinea-Conakri. Bacar Dabó, elected president of the federation in August, expressed high expectations for Djaló's performance, aiming for a top-three finish despite the inherent unpredictability of the game.

Why it mattersGuinea-Bissau's draughts player Mamadu Aliu Djaló will compete in an international tournament in Senegal.
