Guinea-Bissau health ministry seeks national consultants

The Ministry of Public Health is recruiting two national consultants to bolster its PNDS Management Unit.

Source: O Democrata GB · 2026-10-31 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Guinea-Bissau's Ministry of Public Health is hiring two national consultants to strengthen its PNDS Management Unit (CG-PNDS). This unit manages a 27,535,509 Euro grant for fighting HIV/AIDS, TB, and reinforcing the health system, allocated for the 202-2026 period. The consultants will support technical and financial management of grant GC#8, and enhance the unit's programmatic and health system reinforcement efforts.

Applications must be submitted in a sealed envelope to the Secretary of the CG-PNDS by November 26, 2026, around 12:00. The required documents include a dated recommendation letter from the last employer, an updated curriculum vitae with three references, authenticated copies of diplomas and certificates, and a copy of an identity card or passport. These applications are addressed to the Director-General of Health System Administration.

This recruitment aims to enhance the capacity of the CG-PNDS to manage the allocated funding effectively.

Why it mattersThe Ministry of Public Health is recruiting national consultants to manage a grant aimed at combating diseases and strengthening the health system.
