El Nasr targets 2,500 Egypt-assembled Dongfeng electric cars in 2027

Assembly of Dongfeng's MAGE electric SUV, a Chinese-designed model, has begun in Helwan, with sales planned for early 2027 and exports potentially starting in the second half.

Source: Egyptian Streets · 2026-11-23 · 1 min read

Egypt's state-owned El Nasr Automotive has begun assembling limited numbers of Dongfeng's electric SUV, known as the MAGE EV, at its Helwan factories. The model is a five-door electric SUV from China's Dongfeng, and SN Automotive, the project's commercial partner, is targeting production of at least 2,500 vehicles in 2027, with sales from early that year.

SN Automotive chief executive Yehia Abdel Halim said the company intends to meet domestic demand first, then expand into several African markets and eventually the Middle East. The vehicle is assembled from completely knocked-down components imported from abroad.

The company plans to raise annual output to about 20,000 vehicles towards the end of the decade. Exports could begin in the second half of 2028.
