Angola marks 51 years of independence at Cairo reception

Angola's ambassador to Egypt credits both presidents with strengthening ties, citing the Angola-Egypt Bilateral Commission as proof of the commitment.

Source: Daily News Egypt · 2026-11-12 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Angola's ambassador to Egypt, Maquento Sebastião Lopes, hosted a reception in Cairo on Wednesday to mark the 51st anniversary of Angola's independence and 50 years of diplomatic ties with Egypt. Lopes credited Presidents João Lourenço and Abdel Fattah al-Sisi with strengthening relations in recent years through regular high-level contact and dialogue.

Lopes said the two leaders had deepened political trust and identified new areas of co-operation, pointing to the institutionalisation of the Angola-Egypt Bilateral Commission as evidence of the will to turn political ties into benefits for both peoples. The reception, held at a Cairo hotel, drew members of the Egyptian government, diplomats, academics, students, business people and members of the Angolan community in Egypt.

The embassy screened a 10-minute documentary, "Angola and Egypt: 50 Years Building the Future", and Lopes and other Angolan diplomats laid a wreath at the monument to António Agostinho Neto, Angola's first president, in Heroes' Park in Zamalek. Lopes said the two countries were well placed to build partnerships in agriculture, industry, infrastructure, transport, energy, water and mineral resources.

Why it mattersMembers of the Angolan community in Egypt and Egyptian business people were among those invited to the reception marking the anniversary.
