Egypt interior minister promises social media complaint results

Minister Mahmoud Tawfiq stated the public has a right to know how online complaints are investigated and resolved.

Source: Egyptian Streets · 2026-10-24 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Egypt's Minister of Interior Mahmoud Tawfiq announced on October 21 that citizens have the right to know the outcomes of investigations into complaints shared on social media. Speaking at the Egypt Forum 2026, Tawfiq emphasized the ministry's commitment to balancing transparency with individual privacy.

Tawfiq explained that online complaints can become matters of public concern, especially if they relate to public security, but acknowledged some may be fabricated. The ministry plans to publish verified findings on its official social media pages, alongside the original complaint, to demonstrate how issues are addressed.

This approach aims to build trust between citizens and security authorities by showing the speed of response to public concerns. The ministry will continue to present these findings transparently while protecting personal privacy.

Why it mattersEgyptian citizens will be informed about the results of their complaints made on social media.
