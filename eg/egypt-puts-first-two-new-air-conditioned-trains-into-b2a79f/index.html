Egypt puts first two new air-conditioned trains into service on Metro Line 1

The trains are the first deliveries under a EUR 876 million contract with France's Alstom for 55 trains on Cairo's oldest metro line.

Sources: Egypt Independent and Egyptian Streets · 2026-11-17 · 1 min read

Transport Minister Kamel al-Wazir oversaw the entry into service of the first two new air-conditioned trains on Cairo Metro Line 1 on Sunday, 15 November. The trains are the start of deliveries under a contract with the French company Alstom to supply 55 trains for the Helwan–New El-Marg line.

Al-Wazir rode one of the new trains from Saray al-Qubba station to Sadat station, passing eight intermediate stations, and inspected its technical features. French Ambassador Éric Chevallier and National Authority for Tunnels chairman Tarek Goweili attended the ceremony. The trains include surveillance cameras in the driver's cab and carriages, screens showing the next stop, and spaces for wheelchairs.

The contract, signed in 2021, also covers eight years of maintenance, overhauls and spare parts. The line carries about 2.5 million passengers a day, and the new fleet was built at Alstom's plant in Valenciennes, France.

Why it mattersPassengers on the line, which carries about 2.5 million people a day, will get air-conditioned carriages and wheelchair spaces as more of the 55 trains arrive.
