Raya Cyber Solutions debuts 45+ cybersecurity services in Egypt

The Raya IT subsidiary is showcasing its cybersecurity portfolio for the first time at Cairo ICT 2026.

Source: Daily News Egypt · 2026-11-05 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Raya Cyber Solutions, a subsidiary of Raya IT, debuted at Cairo ICT 2026 with over 45 cybersecurity services. The company is exhibiting at the Egypt International Exhibition Centre from November 7 to 10, aiming to expand its reach in Egypt's expanding cybersecurity market.

The company presented more than 45 services across eight key areas, including network security, data protection, and AI security solutions. Marwa Abbas, CEO of Raya IT, stated that understanding and prioritizing risks is essential for organizations to manage operations and maintain trust.

Raya Cyber Solutions combines consulting, implementation, managed services, and training, supported by over 100 certified consultants. The company builds on Raya IT's 25 years of experience in Egypt and Gulf markets.

Why it mattersEgyptian businesses face growing cybersecurity risks that can lead to financial and reputational damage.
