Egypt inflation quickens to 14.9% in July, first rise since March

Urban consumer prices rose 14.9% year on year in July, up from 14.3% in June, Bloomberg reported. It was the first acceleration since March, breaking an easing trend that had held through much of the ongoing regional conflict.

Why it mattersFaster inflation erodes wages and pushes back the point at which borrowing costs start to fall.

Business · 2026-08-14

Central bank holds overnight deposit rate at 19% for a second meeting

The Monetary Policy Committee kept key rates unchanged at its third meeting of the year, a second consecutive hold. The overnight deposit rate stayed at 19% and the overnight lending rate at 20%, with the main operation and discount rates at 19.5%.

Why it mattersRates at these levels keep credit expensive for businesses and mortgage borrowers.

Business · 2026-08-14

CBE expects inflation to accelerate through the third quarter before easing

The Central Bank of Egypt expects annual headline inflation to accelerate through the third quarter of 2026 before declining gradually toward its target. It attributed the path to unfavourable base effects, supply-side pressure from regional conflict, exchange-rate movements and fiscal adjustment.

Why it mattersThe central bank's own forecast signals how long households should expect price pressure to last.

Egypt growth outlook cut to 5% as inflation delays rate cuts

BMI lowered its Egypt growth forecast to 5%, citing persistent inflation that is postponing interest rate cuts, Arab News reported.

Why it mattersA slower growth path with rates held high narrows room for job creation.
