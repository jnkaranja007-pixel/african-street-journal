Egypt condemns Israeli strike on Gaza hospital

Cairo joins 17 nations in condemning the attack and urging the US to enforce a ceasefire.

An Israeli airstrike struck the Nasser Medical Complex in Khan Younis, southern Gaza, shortly after midnight on Thursday, 12 November, and Egypt has condemned it, the foreign ministry said in a statement that day. At least six Palestinians were killed and dozens more wounded, and the hospital's surgical building and several medical departments were badly damaged.

According to Gaza's Health Ministry, the blast killed patients, medical staff and their companions, including a doctor and a child. Cairo described attacks on healthcare facilities as breaches of international humanitarian law and demanded that Israel stop breaching the ceasefire agreement immediately. The Israeli military acknowledged the strike and said it was aimed at a senior Hamas commander.

Egypt and 17 other Arab and Muslim-majority countries issued a joint statement calling the strike a violation of international humanitarian law, and warned that winter could expose Gaza's children and infants to deadly cold. The ministers called on the United States and the Board of Peace to press Israel to lift restrictions on aid, tents and medical supplies without delay.

Why it mattersEgypt and 17 other nations have called on the United States and the Board of Peace to press Israel to lift restrictions on aid, tents and medical supplies.

Business · 2026-11-15

Egypt pushes toward 20 million tourists this year, minister says

Growth of 20 percent, the fastest in the Middle East, has the government targeting 30 million visitors a year by 2030.

Egypt had received about 16.2 million tourists by the end of October, and Minister of Tourism and Antiquities Sherif Fathy expects the total to reach 20 million by the end of this year and 22 million next year. Visitor numbers have risen by a record 20 percent, the highest growth rate in the Middle East according to a regional report by the United Nations World Tourism Organization.

Fathy made the projections in statements on Monday. Tourism earned US$18.4 billion in the 2025–2026 fiscal year, a 10.3 percent rise on the year before, according to Central Bank of Egypt data. The government wants 30 million tourists a year by 2030, bringing in between $36 billion and $40 billion, which will require 240,000 to 250,000 new hotel rooms and more airport capacity.

An ATM Travel Trends Report 2026, released on November 9, found that 77 percent of travellers considering the Middle East prioritise luxury, which it now defines to include authentic cultural experiences and heritage events. It also found 55 percent of visitors to the region want to attend cultural events, a figure that places Egypt in a strong position with its heritage and the Grand Egyptian Museum.

Why it mattersHotel developers and airport operators must add capacity quickly, as the government's 2030 target depends on new rooms and expanded airports.

Holcim Egypt restarts cement line at Ain Sokhna after seven-year shutdown

The fifth production line at the Ain Sokhna plant is running again after an investment of more than EGP 1.3 billion, and is expected to create 560 jobs.

Holcim Egypt has restarted the fifth production line at its cement plant in Ain Sokhna, which had been shut for seven years, after investments exceeding EGP 1.3 billion. The restart was marked at a ceremony attended by Holcim Group chief executive Miljan Gutovic, Khaled El-Dakany, the group's regional head for Asia, the Middle East and Africa, and Holcim Egypt chief executive Medhat Ismail.

Egypt's Minister of Industry, Khaled Hashem, spoke through Mohamed Zada, Assistant Minister of Industry for Strategic Industries, and said the restarted line is expected to create 560 direct and indirect jobs. Hashem said Egypt ranks eighth globally and first in Africa among cement producers, and that the country has 19 multinational cement companies.

Hashem said energy availability and cost determine production costs and the competitiveness of finished products. He said the ministry encourages greater use of alternative fuels, particularly refuse-derived fuel, and pointed to Holcim's Geocycle platform, which works on alternative fuels.

Egypt builds 16 Sinai communities with 1,141 homes

The development includes 269 groundwater wells and 28 pumping units to support housing and agriculture.

Egypt has established 16 new residential and agricultural development communities in North and South Sinai, providing 1,141 homes and 269 groundwater wells. The Ministry of Water Resources and Irrigation stated that 28 pumping units are also part of the development. Each beneficiary receives a 200-square-metre home and five feddans of agricultural land.

10 of the communities are in North Sinai, where 128 wells and 11 pumping stations serve around 4,900 feddans. In South Sinai, the six remaining communities rely on 16 pumping stations, with the aim of irrigating 5,250 feddans. Electricity has been connected to all wells handed over so far, with work continuing on infrastructure.

Water Resources Minister Hani Sewilam has instructed officials to complete outstanding works and award operation and maintenance contracts to specialised companies. He has also called for tighter management of groundwater use, which he described as non-renewable, and for water-user associations to monitor pumping volumes and water quality. Four additional tenders are being prepared for the 2026/27 and 2027/28 fiscal years.

Why it mattersBeneficiaries are allocated a home and agricultural land, creating housing and employment opportunities in Sinai.

Sport · 2026-11-15

Salah becomes Egypt's all-time leading goalscorer in 5-0 win over Malawi

The 34-year-old forward reached 70 international goals with a Panenka penalty in stoppage time at Cairo International Stadium.

Mohamed Salah became Egypt's all-time leading goalscorer on Friday, 13 November, scoring twice in the national team's 5-0 win over Malawi in the 2027 Africa Cup of Nations (AFCON) qualifiers. The 34-year-old forward reached 70 international goals in his 123rd appearance for Egypt, passing the record of 69 held by former captain and coach Hossam Hassan.

Salah first drew level with Hassan's tally with a header in the 84th minute, from a cross by Ibrahim Adel. He then scored his 70th goal with a Panenka penalty in stoppage time, according to the Confederation of African Football. Nasser Maher, Omar Marmoush and Mohamed Hany also scored in the match at Cairo International Stadium.

The win was the first match under head coach Ali Maher, who replaced Hassan earlier in November. Salah made his Egypt debut in 2011.

Why it mattersSalah's 70 goals now stand as the national record, a mark that will be the benchmark for Egypt's future forwards.
