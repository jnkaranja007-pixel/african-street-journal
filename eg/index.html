Egypt plans local production of Samsung medical devices

Health Minister Khaled Abdel Ghaffar discussed manufacturing ultrasound and mobile X-ray units with Samsung Electronics in South Korea.

Egyptian Health Minister Khaled Abdel Ghaffar met with Samsung Electronics in South Korea to discuss local production of medical imaging equipment. The discussions included a proposal to build a factory in Egypt to manufacture ultrasound devices with Samsung’s local agent, Al-Radwan, and a study on producing digital mobile X-ray units locally. The minister also reviewed progress on upgrading 715 family health centres and discussed establishing a Samsung training academy.

The health ministry stated that the talks also covered the delivery of 150 Samsung ultrasound devices and plans to expand Samsung’s digital solutions in hospitals. These included digital displays, video communication systems, and artificial intelligence applications. The upgrades are part of the Decent Life initiative and aim to equip facilities with necessary digital tools.

The two sides agreed to explore the establishment of a Samsung training academy, with the Princess Fatma Academy for Professional Medical Education proposed as a potential location. The ministry is also working to develop five smart training centres with interactive screens and tablets. The investment value and timeline for the local manufacturing projects were not disclosed.

Why it mattersEgyptians will benefit from locally produced medical imaging devices and upgraded healthcare facilities.

Egypt raises $500 million by reopening dollar bonds

The Finance Ministry sold additional bonds maturing in 2030 and 2033 to diversify funding.

Egypt's Finance Ministry raised USD 500 million on Tuesday by selling additional bonds under two existing dollar issues. The sale added USD 150 million to a bond maturing in 2030 and USD 350 million to one maturing in 2033. Selling more of an already-trading bond, known as a reopening, allows the government to raise money without creating a new security.

The ministry stated the sale aims to diversify funding tools and markets, reach new types of investors, and lengthen the average life of the country’s debt. This occurred despite global economic challenges, geopolitical tensions, and heightened uncertainty. The ministry also renewed a pledge to cut external debt by USD 1 billion to USD 2 billion annually.

The government reduced external debt by approximately USD 2.4 billion in the last fiscal year. The new money raised has an average maturity of about five years. The Finance Ministry aims to repay more debt abroad each year than it borrows.

Why it mattersEgypt's government has diversified its funding tools and markets by raising USD 500 million through additional dollar bond sales.

Politics · 2026-11-04

Egypt plans rules for social media creators

Government met with 70 influencers to discuss a new framework for digital content and professional standards.

Egypt's Minister of State for Information Diaa Rashwan met with approximately 70 Egyptian content creators and social media influencers on Sunday, November 1, to discuss regulating the digital content sector. The meeting explored establishing an organizing framework and defining professional standards for online content.

The discussions followed recent Cabinet recommendations for new measures against harmful social media practices, including increased fines for spreading false news and a proposed digital safety law. Influencers suggested forming an Egyptian umbrella organization to set ethical standards and manage their relations with traditional media.

Rashwan stated the government would not create a syndicate for influencers but confirmed they could form their own association under the law. The ministry is also exploring ways to support creators and improve their access to official information.

Why it mattersEgyptian social media content creators face potential new regulations and professional standards impacting their work.

4.9 magnitude earthquake recorded north of Marsa Matrouh

The National Research Institute of Astronomy and Geophysics recorded the tremor at a depth of 16.60 kilometers.

A 4.9-magnitude earthquake struck 353 kilometers north of Marsa Matrouh on Tuesday. The National Research Institute of Astronomy and Geophysics (NRIAG) recorded the tremor at 1:03:44 PM local time.

The earthquake's epicenter was located at a latitude of 34.45° N and a longitude of 26.51° E, at a depth of 16.60 kilometers. NRIAG confirmed it received no reports of the earthquake being felt and no loss of life or property damage was recorded.

Data for the event was captured by the institute’s national seismic monitoring network. The institute issued a statement detailing the earthquake's parameters.

Why it mattersThe earthquake occurred offshore and no impact on people or property was reported.

Business · 2026-11-04

Adeer International targets $2bn in Egypt hospitality investments

Saudi Arabia's Sumou Holding Group forms alliance with Egyptian partners for hotel projects.

Adeer International, the investment arm of Saudi Arabia’s Sumou Holding Group, has allied with Hassan Allam, MIDAR for Investment and Urban Development, and Sumou Investment to develop hospitality projects in Egypt. The group plans to invest approximately $2bn in these ventures. This alliance is part of Adeer International’s expansion strategy in Egypt, announced during a media briefing in Cairo.

Bassel El-Serafy, Founder and CEO of Adeer International, stated that the company entered the Egyptian market after assessing its growth prospects and real estate sector development. Adeer began operations in Egypt with Adeer Plaza in Sheikh Zayed City, which opened in 2024. The company acts as a strategic investment partner, bringing capital and expertise to develop opportunities through tailored partnerships.

Ahmed Helmy, Chief Investment Officer at Adeer International, noted that the company signed strategic partnerships in 2024, including an agreement with Hassan Allam Properties for a mixed-use project in East Cairo. In 2026, the company launched Sumou Boulevard in Mostakbal City, Sumou Investment’s first major project in Egypt. Sumou Holding Group manages SAR 55bn across 15 real estate funds.

Why it mattersInvestors in Egypt's hospitality sector face new competition and potential development from a Saudi Arabian investment group.
