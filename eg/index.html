Egypt schedules Tanzania friendly before AFCON qualifiers

The national team will host Tanzania on October 5 as part of preparations for the 2027 Africa Cup of Nations and upcoming qualifying matches.

Egypt's national football team, under head coach Hossam Hassan, has arranged a friendly match against Tanzania on October 5 in Cairo. This fixture is a key component of the team's strategy to prepare for the 2027 Africa Cup of Nations (AFCON) and its qualifying campaign. The match will allow the technical staff to assess player fitness, tactical readiness, and integrate new talent into the squad.

The friendly against Tanzania follows Egypt's initial AFCON qualifying games against Angola and South Sudan in September. The team's preparatory camp begins on September 17. Egypt will first host Angola at Cairo International Stadium on September 25, followed by an away match against South Sudan in Juba on September 29. These early qualifiers are considered crucial for securing maximum points.

Following the September fixtures, Egypt is scheduled to play Malawi twice in November 2026, concluding their qualifying campaign in March 2027 with return matches against South Sudan and Angola. The technical committee views the friendly with Tanzania as a vital opportunity to finalize the starting lineup and ensure the team is match-ready for the tournament.

Why it mattersThe Egyptian national football team is preparing for the 2027 Africa Cup of Nations qualifiers, with a friendly match against Tanzania scheduled for October 5.

VR Exhibits Transport Visitors to Ancient Egypt in Buenos Aires

Cutting-edge virtual reality experiences allow users to explore the Great Pyramid of Giza and ancient Egyptian civilization.

History, science, and technology are converging at La Rural’s Frers Hall in Buenos Aires, where the DG Tech Lab is showcasing five virtual reality experiences. Among these, two VR journeys focus on ancient Egypt, allowing visitors to step inside the Great Pyramid of Giza and explore its chambers. These immersive experiences offer a hands-on look at ancient engineering and craftsmanship, transforming historical exploration from passive observation to active participation.

The VR exhibit features 'Horizon of Khufu,' which enables visitors to stand within the Great Pyramid and uncover its secrets, and 'Secrets of the pyramid builders,' placing users in the role of ancient architects. These attractions are proving to be a major draw, highlighting Egyptian civilization through hyper-realistic graphics and providing a glimpse into the future of education by making history and science tangible.

This project utilizes immersive VR as an educational tool, demonstrating that direct experience is a powerful way to understand complex subjects. Beyond ancient Egypt, other VR experiences include space exploration, medieval fortresses, and the evolution of life on Earth, collectively offering a diverse range of educational and entertaining journeys.

Why it mattersVisitors interested in Egyptian history and culture can virtually explore ancient sites and learn about their construction through immersive VR.

Central Bank of Egypt Assures Sector After US UAE Branch Sanctions

Measures targeting Banque Misr's UAE branch for Iran-related transactions will not affect Egyptian banks or domestic operations, the central bank stated.

The Central Bank of Egypt has confirmed that United States measures against Banque Misr’s branch in the United Arab Emirates will not impact the Egyptian banking sector or the bank’s domestic operations. These US actions are strictly limited to the UAE branch’s transactions with correspondent banks in US dollars and do not extend to any other institutions within Egypt.

The central bank and the Ministry of Foreign Affairs are in contact with US authorities to monitor the situation and address any repercussions. The statement aimed to reassure depositors, affirming the strength and resilience of all banks operating in Egypt and emphasizing the soundness of the Egyptian banking sector in the face of external developments.

The US measures specifically target dollar transactions conducted by the UAE branch, with the scope explicitly confined to that entity. No other foreign branches of Banque Misr or its domestic operations are affected by these restrictions.

Why it mattersInvestors are affected because US measures against a specific branch of Banque Misr could signal broader regulatory scrutiny or impact international financial dealings.
