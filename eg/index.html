Egypt launches $658m phosphoric acid complex in New Valley

Officials began construction Wednesday on a $658 million industrial complex at the Abu Tartour plateau. The facility is designed to produce 250,000 tonnes of commercial phosphoric acid annually.

Why it mattersThe plant will increase mining exports of fertilizer components via the Red Sea port.
