Egypt Health Ministry Denies Salmonella Outbreak

The Ministry of Health confirmed no unusual Salmonella outbreaks are currently detected nationally, despite ongoing monitoring of international incidents and supply chains.

Egypt's Ministry of Health has confirmed that no unusual Salmonella outbreaks are currently occurring within the country. Minister of Health Dr. Khaled Abdel Ghaffar stated that the ministry actively monitors local and international health conditions using epidemiological surveillance systems, particularly for foodborne illnesses. This proactive monitoring, which includes continuous risk assessments, is a precautionary measure and does not indicate an immediate risk within Egypt.

Deputy Minister of Health Dr. Amr Kandil emphasized that the Preventive Medicine and Public Health sector tracks daily infection patterns and assesses reports based on scientific standards. He confirmed that no unusual Salmonella outbreaks connected to recent international events have been recorded nationally. The ministry is tracking specific international incidents, including over 306 cases in Belgium and 207 cases with one death in the United Kingdom, which have led to product recalls in those regions.

The Ministry of Health is implementing response protocols that rely on swift case identification, epidemiological investigation, and supply tracing. They are also advising the public on essential food safety practices, such as thorough hand washing, separating raw and cooked foods, cooking food to safe temperatures, and storing perishables properly. The ministry reaffirmed that its epidemiological surveillance infrastructure remains proactive and operational to safeguard public health.

Why it mattersThe Ministry of Health's confirmation of no domestic Salmonella outbreaks means the public can continue to trust local food supplies without immediate health concerns.

Egypt National Team Schedules Tanzania Friendly Ahead of AFCON Qualifiers

The Pharaohs will face Tanzania on October 5 in Cairo as part of their preparation for the 2027 Africa Cup of Nations qualifiers, with coach Hossam Hassan aiming to assess.

Egypt's national football team will play a friendly match against Tanzania on October 5 in Cairo. This fixture is part of the team's strategy to prepare for the 2027 Africa Cup of Nations (AFCON) qualifiers. Head coach Hossam Hassan and his technical staff plan to use the match to evaluate player fitness, tactical readiness, and integrate new talent into the squad before subsequent qualifying rounds.

The friendly comes after Egypt's initial AFCON qualifying games against Angola and South Sudan, scheduled for late September. The team's preparatory camp begins on September 17. Egypt will host Angola on September 25 and then travel to face South Sudan on September 29, with the latter match considered a critical early test for securing maximum points.

Following the September fixtures, Egypt is set to play Malawi twice in November 2026, and conclude their qualifying campaign in March 2027 with return matches against South Sudan and Angola. The technical committee views the Tanzania match as a key opportunity to finalize the squad and ensure match readiness for the tournament.

Why it mattersThe scheduling of this friendly match directly impacts the Egyptian national football team's preparation and player selection for upcoming continental tournaments.

VR Exhibit Showcases Great Pyramid in Buenos Aires

An immersive virtual reality experience allows visitors to explore the Great Pyramid of Giza and learn about ancient Egyptian civilization.

History, science, and technology are converging at La Rural’s Frers Hall in Buenos Aires, where the DG Tech Lab is offering five virtual reality experiences. Among these, the "Horizon of Khufu" VR journey allows visitors to step inside the Great Pyramid of Giza, offering a hyper-realistic exploration of its inner chambers and ancient secrets.

This exhibition highlights Egyptian civilization as a significant draw, providing visitors with a rare, hands-on look at ancient engineering and craftsmanship. The VR experience is designed to be an educational tool, blurring the lines between entertainment and learning by enabling participants to walk through Khufu’s grand galleries.

The project aims to demonstrate the future of learning through immersive technology, proving that direct engagement can enhance understanding of history, science, and the universe. Other VR experiences include space exploration, medieval fortresses, and the history of evolution on Earth.

Why it mattersEgyptian civilization is presented as a major crowd-puller, offering a unique educational and entertainment experience through virtual reality.

Egypt Orders Legal Action Over Wrestler Defections

The Ministry of Youth and Sports is pursuing legal action against two wrestlers who defected during international travel, leading to the suspension of a delegation head.

Egypt's Ministry of Youth and Sports has initiated legal proceedings through the Public Prosecution concerning the defection of wrestler Mohamed Mustafa Ali El-Shami while in transit in Rome. A similar case involving wrestler Ziad Haggag, who disappeared during the World Junior Wrestling Championships in Slovakia, has also been referred for prosecution. Preliminary investigations revealed negligence and insufficient supervision of delegation members, resulting in the suspension of Ibrahim Adel Ibrahim Mustafa Al-Attar, head of the wrestling delegation, who was also referred to the Ethics Committee.

El-Shami's disappearance occurred during a layover in Rome en route to Cairo from Taranto. Despite the delegation leader's attempts to locate the athlete, the effort was unsuccessful. Consequently, the team coach remained in Rome while the remainder of the delegation returned to Egypt. The Ministry had previously directed the Egyptian Wrestling Federation to investigate Haggag's disappearance in Slovakia and take appropriate legal measures before the case was handed over to the Public Prosecution.

Despite these disciplinary actions, ministry officials highlighted the national wrestling team's strong performance at the Mediterranean Games, where they secured seven medals, including two gold. Officials stated these incidents should not detract from the team's achievements. El-Shami was eliminated in the preliminary rounds of the competition.

Why it mattersThe legal actions and investigations into the defections of wrestlers affect the oversight and accountability of sports delegations, potentially impacting future international travel and representation.

EgyptAir Partners FC Barcelona Through 2029

The national carrier secures exclusive airline rights for FC Barcelona's men's football, basketball, and handball teams, aiming to boost global visibility.

EgyptAir has finalized a sponsorship agreement with FC Barcelona, becoming the club's Official Exclusive Airline Partner until the 2028-2029 season. This multi-year deal grants EgyptAir global marketing rights across Barcelona's men's football, basketball, and handball teams. A key element of the partnership includes the airline's logo prominently displayed on the front of the handball team's match jerseys.

This collaboration is positioned by EgyptAir's Chairman, Capt. Ahmed Adel, as a significant milestone, merging the airline's 95-year history with FC Barcelona's international prestige. Capt. Mohamed Elyan, Chairman of EgyptAir Airlines, stated the partnership aligns with shared values of excellence and teamwork, intended to enhance the airline's competitive standing in global markets.

FC Barcelona's Global Sponsorship Director, Marc Broix, welcomed the agreement, viewing the partnership with Egypt's national carrier as a logical progression for the club's international growth strategy. The deal aims to leverage FC Barcelona's global fanbase to increase EgyptAir's international visibility and appeal.

Why it mattersThis deal affects investors by potentially increasing EgyptAir's market share and brand recognition through a high-profile international sports sponsorship.
