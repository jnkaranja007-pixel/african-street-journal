Egypt opens prosecution inquiry after fuel tanker fire kills 31 on Giza's Ring Road

Flames from the overturned tanker spread to passing vehicles, with preliminary estimates showing 27 cars and motorcycles burned.

Sources: Egyptian Streets and Egypt Independent · 2026-11-26 · 1 min read

Egypt's Ministry of Health and Population said the final toll from Wednesday evening's fire in Giza's Warraq district is 31 dead and 21 injured. The blaze began above Tunnel 10 on the Ring Road, on the side heading towards Haram near Al-Qawmeya metro station, after the tanker carrying petroleum products came to rest on its side and ignited.

Ministry spokesperson Hossam Abdel-Ghaffar confirmed the figure late on Wednesday, after earlier counts of 21 and then 30 deaths. The ministry sent 39 ambulances to the scene, and the injured were taken to Sheikh Zayed Specialised Hospital, Imbaba General Hospital and Warraq Central Hospital. A senior petroleum-sector official said the tanker belongs to a private contractor, not the Ministry of Petroleum.

Health Minister Khaled Abdel-Ghaffar ordered hospitals near the site to raise their readiness to the highest level. The chief prosecutor of the North Giza Prosecution led a team to inspect the site and establish the cause, while a second team questioned the injured at hospitals in Imbaba, Warraq and Boulaq El-Dakrour.

Why it mattersThe 21 injured were admitted to three hospitals, and the contractor that owns the tanker is central to the prosecution's inquiry into the cause.
