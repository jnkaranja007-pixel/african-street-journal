Salah becomes Egypt's all-time leading goalscorer in 5-0 win over Malawi

The 34-year-old forward reached 70 international goals with a Panenka penalty in stoppage time at Cairo International Stadium.

Source: Egyptian Streets · 2026-11-15 · 1 min read

Mohamed Salah became Egypt's all-time leading goalscorer on Friday, 13 November, scoring twice in the national team's 5-0 win over Malawi in the 2027 Africa Cup of Nations (AFCON) qualifiers. The 34-year-old forward reached 70 international goals in his 123rd appearance for Egypt, passing the record of 69 held by former captain and coach Hossam Hassan.

Salah first drew level with Hassan's tally with a header in the 84th minute, from a cross by Ibrahim Adel. He then scored his 70th goal with a Panenka penalty in stoppage time, according to the Confederation of African Football. Nasser Maher, Omar Marmoush and Mohamed Hany also scored in the match at Cairo International Stadium.

The win was the first match under head coach Ali Maher, who replaced Hassan earlier in November. Salah made his Egypt debut in 2011.

Why it mattersSalah's 70 goals now stand as the national record, a mark that will be the benchmark for Egypt's future forwards.
