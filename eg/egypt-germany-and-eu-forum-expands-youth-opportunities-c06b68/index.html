Egypt, Germany and EU forum expands youth opportunities

A Cairo forum marked five years of cooperation aimed at boosting young Egyptians' socio-economic participation.

Source: Daily News Egypt · 2026-11-17 · 2 min read

Cairo hosted the Socio-Economic Collaboration Forum (SECF) on Monday at the Hilton Grand Nile, convened by the German development agency GIZ Egypt for the German Government with the Ministry of Social Solidarity. More than 150 representatives from government, civil society, the private sector, academia, media and international development partners attended. They marked five years of work under the Equal Opportunities and Social Development (EOSD) project and discussed how to widen young people's socio-economic participation in Egypt.

The European Union co-finances the project, and its first phase focused on creating an enabling environment for young people, particularly young women and persons with disabilities. Christian Grün, Head of Cooperation at the German Embassy in Cairo, said the partnership between Egypt, Germany and the European Union aims to let young people realise their potential and access opportunities. Marguerite Saroufim, Deputy Minister of Social Solidarity, called the forum an effective model of cooperation tied to Egypt Vision 2030.

Saroufim urged the private sector to offer training aligned with labour market needs, and asked civil society groups to build solutions on community needs. Research institutions were asked to supply scientific evidence to guide decisions and measure impact. Grün said that when young people can take part, societies move forward.

Why it mattersYoung women and persons with disabilities are the groups the forum says the next phase must reach, through training tied to jobs.
