Egypt sets private-sector minimum wage at EGP 8,500 from 2027

The National Wages Council raised the floor from EGP 7,000, a rise of about 21.4 percent that takes effect on 1 January 2027.

Sources: Egyptian Streets and Daily News Egypt · 2026-11-17 · 1 min read

Egypt's National Wages Council approved on 15 November a minimum monthly wage of EGP 8,500 for private-sector employees, up from EGP 7,000, effective 1 January 2027. The EGP 1,500 increase is about 21.4 percent. The council also set a minimum net hourly wage of EGP 34 for workers in temporary and part-time jobs.

The decision followed a meeting chaired by Prime Minister Mostafa Madbouly, who stressed that the government will ensure private-sector businesses comply with minimum wage requirements. He noted that the sector employs the largest share of Egypt's workforce and said the government is working to improve incomes in line with directives from President Abdel-Fattah El-Sisi.

Private-sector employees will also get an annual periodic increase from the 2027 financial year, set by each establishment's accounting year. It must be at least three percent of the employee's social insurance contribution wage, with a minimum rise of EGP 350 a year.

Why it mattersPrivate-sector staff on the old floor will see their pay lifted to EGP 8,500 a month, and employers must add an annual rise of at least EGP 350.
