Egypt hosts building materials summit as exporters seek overseas buyers

The Convergence 2026 summit will bring about 100 industry, trade and investment leaders from 40 countries to Sharm El-Sheikh for a one-day gathering.

Source: Egyptian Streets · 2026-11-25 · 1 min read

Egypt will host the first Global Building Materials Summit, known as CONVERGE 2026, in Sharm El-Sheikh on 29 November, with around 100 industry, trade and investment leaders expected from 40 countries. Building materials are Egypt's largest non-oil export, but manufacturers have faced higher shipping costs and softer demand after a boom last year.

Ahmed Othman, a board member of the Chamber of Building Materials Industries who heads its insulation division and chairs the summit, said organizers want Egyptian manufacturers to find buyers, investors and partners abroad. He said the gathering would focus on building long-term partnerships among manufacturers, investors, distributors and policymakers, rather than routine networking.

The one-day agenda includes sustainability, advanced manufacturing, access to global markets and industrial franchising, which lets companies enter new markets while passing on technical know-how. Othman said the choice of Sharm El-Sheikh, often called the City of Peace, was meant to show that Egypt remains a safe place to do business.

Why it mattersEgyptian manufacturers facing higher shipping costs and softer demand are being connected with potential overseas buyers, investors and partners at the 29 November gathering.
