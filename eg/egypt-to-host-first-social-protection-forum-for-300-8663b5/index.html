Egypt to host first social protection forum for 300 participants

The November 23-24 meeting in the New Administrative Capital will be funded by UN agencies, the World Bank and private-sector partners.

Source: Daily News Egypt · 2026-11-19 · 1 min read

Egypt will host the first Global Forum on Social Protection on November 23-24 in the New Administrative Capital, bringing together around 300 officials, experts and international-organisation representatives. The Foreign Ministry and the Ministry of Social Solidarity are organising the two-day meeting, known as Hemaya.

Social Solidarity Minister Maya Morsy said the state budget would bear none of the costs; UN agencies, the World Bank and private-sector partners will cover them. Sessions will examine financing and governance, digital service delivery, disaster response and ways to help people move from assistance into economic participation.

Foreign Minister Badr Abdelatty said the forum would promote exchanges between countries and turn outcomes from UN and regional summits into practical partnerships. Egypt plans to hold the forum regularly. A parallel Diarna handicrafts and heritage exhibition will feature more than 30 exhibitors.

Why it mattersThe two organising ministries must deliver the forum without drawing on the state budget; external partners are covering its costs.
