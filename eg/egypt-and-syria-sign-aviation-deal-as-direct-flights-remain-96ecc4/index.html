Egypt and Syria sign aviation deal as direct flights remain out of service

The agreement allows technical exchanges and follows Egyptian inspections of Damascus and Aleppo airports after about 14 years without regular flights.

Source: Egyptian Streets · 2026-11-20 · 1 min read

Egypt and Syria signed an aviation cooperation agreement in Montreal, Canada, on the sidelines of the International Civil Aviation Organization’s Seventh Worldwide Air Transport Conference, which runs from 16 to 20 November. The countries have had no regular direct commercial flights for approximately 14 years.

Egyptian aviation authorities have inspected Damascus and Aleppo airports to assess whether they are ready to receive flights, Egyptian Streets reported. The agreement also provides for the two countries to share technical expertise and information and improve airport and aviation procedures to meet international safety standards.

The deal follows discussions between Egyptian and Syrian aviation officials on preparing to restore regular air connections. Egypt’s Civil Aviation Minister Sameh Elhefny described it as an important step towards closer cooperation. The report also noted growing diplomatic engagement between Cairo and Damascus.

Why it mattersEgyptian and Syrian aviation authorities must prepare airport procedures and readiness for a return to regular direct connections.
