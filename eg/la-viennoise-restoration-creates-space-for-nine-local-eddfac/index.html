La Viennoise restoration creates space for nine local businesses

The 130-year-old Downtown Cairo building's restored courtyard will host stores and creative spaces, including an Italian restaurant and an art gallery.

Source: Egyptian Streets · 2026-11-20 · 1 min read

La Viennoise, a 130-year-old heritage building in Downtown Cairo, is being renovated to accommodate nine local businesses in its restored courtyard. The project adds stores and creative spaces to the historic building.

The businesses include Andiamo, an Italian restaurant; Floating Cups, a specialty coffee shop; ANŪT, a homeware brand; and Le LAB, an art and design gallery. The remaining five businesses are not identified in the report.

The renovation is intended to retain the building's historic character while bringing in contemporary businesses. The report describes the site as a meeting place for visitors and Cairo residents.

Why it mattersThe nine businesses, including a restaurant, coffee shop and homeware brand, will operate from the restored courtyard.
