Egypt and Kuwait sign agreements at joint committee

The 14th session of the Egyptian-Kuwaiti Joint Committee ended with several agreements and memoranda of understanding signed in Kuwait on Sunday.

Source: Daily News Egypt · 2026-11-16 · 1 min read

Egypt's Foreign Minister Badr Abdelatty and his Kuwaiti counterpart, Sheikh Jarrah Jaber Al-Ahmad Al-Sabah, co-chaired the 14th session of the Egyptian-Kuwaiti Joint Committee in Kuwait on Sunday. The session ended with the signing of several agreements and memoranda of understanding in priority areas of co-operation, though the foreign ministry did not detail them.

Abdelatty called for more trade, two-way investment and closer ties between the two business communities, and said Egypt would remove obstacles faced by Kuwaiti investors. In a separate meeting, he met Kuwait's Crown Prince Sheikh Sabah Khalid Al-Hamad Al-Sabah. Egypt's foreign ministry spokesman, Ambassador Nader Zaki, confirmed the meeting.

The two delegations signed the political and technical minutes of the committee, which the ministry described as a road map for co-operation until the next session, due in Cairo in 2028. The two sides also discussed the Palestinian issue, the crises in Sudan and Yemen, and the security of navigation in the Gulf, the Red Sea and the Bab el-Mandeb Strait, and agreed to keep co-ordinating.

Why it mattersKuwaiti investors and Egyptian workers now have a framework of signed agreements, with the next joint session set for Cairo in 2028.
