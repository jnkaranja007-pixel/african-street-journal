Dutch Embassy in Cairo suspends public access after protests

The Netherlands’ foreign ministry ordered a temporary closure after dozens gathered over the detention of Egyptian activist Ahmed Nasser.

Source: Egyptian Streets · 2026-11-26 · 1 min read

The Dutch Embassy in Cairo closed its premises to the public on Tuesday, 24 November, after dozens of demonstrators gathered outside to protest the detention of Egyptian activist Ahmed Nasser.

The Netherlands’ Ministry of Foreign Affairs ordered the temporary precautionary closure amid demonstrations, the embassy said. Protesters called for Nasser’s immediate release and the expulsion of the Dutch ambassador to Cairo. The embassy said people with existing appointments would be contacted to reschedule.

VFS Global’s Cairo services continued independently, the embassy said. It advised people needing urgent assistance to call its emergency line at +31247247247; public access will resume after a further announcement.
