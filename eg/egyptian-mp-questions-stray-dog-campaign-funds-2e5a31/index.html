egyptian mp questions stray dog campaign funds

irene said filed a formal request for a briefing on public spending for stray dog control.

Source: Egyptian Streets · 2026-10-28 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Irene Said, a member of Egypt’s House of Representatives, demanded on October 27 that the government explain its spending on stray dogs. Residents continue to report growing numbers of stray animals in streets and residential areas, with packs gathering near schools and hospitals. Said filed a formal request for a briefing to the prime minister and ministers of agriculture, local development, environment, and health.

The lawmaker stated that the dogs have been linked to bites and attacks, posing risks to public safety and health. These incidents impose costs on the state for treating victims and preventing rabies. The government announced a national plan including sterilization and vaccination drives, but Said questioned its effectiveness and the sufficiency of assigned money and personnel.

Said pointed to testimony in June before the House Agriculture Committee regarding the General Authority for Veterinary Services. She asked how actual progress compared with stated targets for the national campaign.

Why it mattersan Egyptian MP is questioning government spending on a national campaign to control stray dogs.
