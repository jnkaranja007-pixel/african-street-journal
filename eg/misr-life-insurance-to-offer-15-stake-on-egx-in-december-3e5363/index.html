Misr Life Insurance to offer 15% stake on EGX in December

The sale covers 750 million existing shares held by state-owned Misr Insurance Holding, with the price set through a bookbuilding process.

Source: Daily News Egypt · 2026-11-18 · 1 min read

Misr Life Insurance plans to sell 15% of its shares on the Egyptian Exchange, with subscriptions and trading expected to begin in December 2026. The proposed initial public offering comprises 750 million existing ordinary shares held by Misr Insurance Holding Company, which is wholly owned by the Sovereign Fund of Egypt for Investment and Development.

The offer will include a private placement for qualified investors in Egypt and selected jurisdictions outside the United States, plus a public offer open to investors in Egypt. Both portions will have the same per-share price, set through bookbuilding. The selling shareholder’s remaining shares will be subject to lock-up restrictions under exchange rules and customary market practice.

Misr Life Insurance is seeking approvals from the Financial Regulatory Authority, including approval of the prospectus and registration of the shares, as well as approvals from the exchange. The December timetable depends on regulatory clearance and market conditions. EFG Hermes is the sole global coordinator and bookrunner.

Why it mattersInvestors in Egypt can subscribe to the public offer, while qualified investors can join the private placement; the price will be set through bookbuilding.
