Egypt allocates Red Sea land to cut public debt

Egypt's finance ministry will use about 26,690 feddans of state land in the Red Sea governorate to reduce public debt and issue sovereign sukuk.

Source: Daily News Egypt · 2026-11-12 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

President Abdel Fattah Al-Sisi has issued a decree allocating about 26,690 feddans of state-owned land in the Red Sea governorate to the finance ministry, to be used for reducing public debt and issuing sovereign sukuk. The decree, published in the Official Gazette, amends Presidential Decree No. 222 of 2026, which had allocated the same plot, about 112.1 million square metres, only for sovereign sukuk.

Prime Minister Mostafa Madbouly met Finance Minister Ahmed Kouchouk in the New Administrative Capital to review progress on debt targets. Kouchouk said the ratio of budget-sector debt to GDP had fallen by about 14 percentage points between June 2023 and June 2026, and that external debt of budget-sector bodies was cut by the equivalent of about 3.9% of GDP in 2025/26. Madbouly called debt reduction a top government priority.

The Egypt Forum 2026 recommendations set a path to bring public debt down to about 69.5% of GDP by 2030, with fixed ceilings on public debt, external debt and sovereign guarantees. Madbouly directed officials to draw up a detailed plan assigning specific tasks to each ministry and government body, to be presented to the president.

Why it mattersMinistries and government bodies must now each receive specific debt-reduction tasks under a detailed plan to be presented to the president.
