Egypt signs Greece business pact and Jordan aviation agreement

The Greece deal covers nine sectors, while the Jordan agreement seeks to expand air links and give airlines more operating opportunities.

Source: Food Business Africa · 2026-11-27 · 1 min read

Egypt signed two agreements in November: a business cooperation memorandum with Greece on 21 November in Athens, and an aviation memorandum with Jordan on the sidelines of a Montreal conference. The Egyptian Junior Business Association and the Athens Chamber of Commerce and Industry signed the Greece deal, which covers nine sectors.

The Greece agreement provides for trade missions, business meetings, sector forums and exchanges of economic information to help companies find partners. The aviation agreement, initialled by Egypt and Jordan, is intended to improve coordination between their authorities, update air transport regulations and support passenger travel, tourism and trade.

Bilateral merchandise trade between Egypt and Greece reached €1.45 billion in 2025, according to the Athens chamber. Jordan was Egypt’s third-largest individual market for processed food exports in the first eight months of 2026, with sales of US$228 million, Egypt’s Food Export Council reported.

Why it mattersEgyptian companies have scheduled trade missions and sector forums to pursue partnerships across nine industries; airlines also face updated bilateral rules.
