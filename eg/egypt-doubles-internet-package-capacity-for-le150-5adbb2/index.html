Egypt doubles internet package capacity for LE150

The National Telecom Regulatory Authority ordered providers to increase base data from 50 GB to 100 GB starting November 1.

Source: Egypt Independent · 2026-10-28 · 1 min read

Egypt's National Telecom Regulatory Authority (NTRA) has instructed internet providers to double the capacity of the LE150 base package to 100 GB, effective November 1. This move, without any additional cost, follows presidential directives to make telecommunications services more accessible for citizens. The initiative aims to improve access to digital government services and support the nation's digital transformation.

NTRA CEO Mohamed Shamroukh stated that this order reflects the authority's role in regulating and facilitating the sector. He explained the goal is to allow families to access quality internet services affordably, supporting their integration into digital society. An official source confirmed that companies are committed to implementing the decision without new fees, ensuring the increased data reaches beneficiaries.

This initiative is part of a broader plan to build a 'Digital Egypt' and enhance the competitiveness of the knowledge-based economy. The NTRA will monitor service providers to ensure the new package meets high standards of quality and efficiency. The change is intended to improve the quality of digital life for Egyptians and enable them to utilize various online services.

Why it mattersEgyptian families will receive double the internet data for the same monthly price, improving their access to digital services.
