Egypt keeps dozens of aid truckloads moving into Gaza daily

Egyptian Committee spokesperson Mohamed Mansour says Israeli obstacles are facing convoys as needs among displaced Palestinians grow.

Source: Egypt Independent · 2026-11-22 · 1 min read

Egypt is continuing efforts to deliver humanitarian aid to Gaza despite Israeli obstacles facing convoys, Egyptian Committee for Gaza Relief spokesperson Mohamed Mansour said in a phone interview with Nile News Channel on Friday, November 20,2026. The Egyptian Red Crescent sends dozens of truckloads into the territory daily, he said.

Mansour said Palestinian families’ needs are growing as conditions deteriorate and winter approaches. He warned that a weather depression expected in the coming days threatens displaced Palestinians with a new climate-related disaster. Machinery operated by the Egyptian Committee clears roads and rubble daily; in some neighborhoods it also demolishes buildings at risk of collapse.

The committee coordinates with UN organizations to get aid to people in need and responds to vulnerable families, particularly those living near the Yellow Line, Mansour said. He called for increased assistance and support for rebuilding Gaza.
