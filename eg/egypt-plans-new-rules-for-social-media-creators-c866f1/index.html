Egypt plans new rules for social media creators

Government met with 70 influencers to discuss a framework for digital content and professional standards.

Source: Egyptian Streets · 2026-11-03 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Egypt's Minister of State for Information Diaa Rashwan met with about 70 content creators and influencers on Sunday, November 1, to discuss regulating the digital content sector. The meeting explored establishing a framework, defining professional standards, and improving access to official information.

This discussion follows recent Cabinet recommendations for new measures against harmful social media practices, including increased fines for false news and a digital safety law. Creators proposed forming an Egyptian umbrella organization to set ethical standards and define professional creators.

Rashwan stated the government will not create a syndicate for influencers but supports their formation of an association. The ministry is exploring ways to support content creators, aiming to distinguish professionals from general users.
