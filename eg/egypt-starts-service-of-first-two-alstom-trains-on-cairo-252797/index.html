Egypt starts service of first two Alstom trains on Cairo Metro Line 1

The French-built trains are the first deliveries under a contract to supply 55 trains for the country's oldest metro line.

Source: Daily News Egypt · 2026-11-16 · 1 min read

Two new air-conditioned trains have entered service on Cairo Metro Line 1, the first deliveries under a contract with the French firm Alstom for 55 trains. Transport Minister Kamel Al-Wazir rode one of them from Saray El-Qobba station to Sadat station, passing eight intermediate stations, to check its technical specifications and passenger facilities.

The trains come with secure connections between carriages, CCTV cameras in the driver cabins and passenger carriages, LCD passenger information screens and spaces for wheelchair users. Al-Wazir said the trains are a major step towards modernising the line and improving efficiency, comfort and safety. The contract, which covers 495 carriages in total, also includes eight years of maintenance and spare parts.

Line 1 runs 44 kilometres between Helwan and New Marg and serves 35 stations. Alongside the new trains, 23 existing trains are being rehabilitated with a Spanish company, and a consortium of Egyptian and French companies is upgrading signalling, communications, track and power supply. The ministry is preparing to tender works for a new Sheikh Mansour station between Ezbet El-Nakhl and New Marg.

Why it mattersCairo Metro Line 1 passengers will ride the new trains as the rest of the 55-train fleet is delivered over the coming years under the contract.
