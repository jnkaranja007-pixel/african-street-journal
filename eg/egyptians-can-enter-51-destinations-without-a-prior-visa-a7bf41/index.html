Egyptians can enter 51 destinations without a prior visa

Henley & Partners data counts both visa-free entry and visas on arrival, with permitted stays generally ranging from 30 to 90 days.

Source: Egypt Independent · 2026-11-21 · 1 min read

Egyptian passport holders can travel to 51 countries and territories without obtaining a visa in advance, either through visa-free entry or a visa on arrival, according to the Henley Passport Index. The list includes Kenya, Jordan, Indonesia and Malaysia, as well as Barbados and Hong Kong.

Henley & Partners compiles the index using data from the International Air Transport Association. The index counts access without a prior visa; it does not remove border requirements. Travelers may need a passport valid well beyond their stay, proof of funds and a confirmed return or onward ticket.

Permitted stays generally range from 30 to 90 days, and conditions vary by destination. The report advises travelers to check current entry rules before booking; it also recommends travel medical insurance and an itinerary with accommodation details.

Why it mattersEgyptian travelers must check each destination’s current rules before booking, as permitted stays range from 30 to 90 days and entry conditions vary.
