Egypt's prosecutors open a probe after a fuel truck fire on Giza's Ring Road

Officials say 31 people died and 21 were hurt when the vehicle overturned and burst into flames, spreading to traffic above Tunnel 10.

Source: Egyptian Streets · 2026-11-27 · 1 min read

On Wednesday evening a truck carrying petroleum products overturned and caught fire on the Ring Road in Giza's Warraq district, Egypt's Ministry of Health and Population said. The blaze started above Tunnel 10, in the Imbaba area, on the Haram-bound side near Al-Qawmeya metro station, and reached vehicles on the road.

The Public Prosecution estimated that 27 cars and motorcycles burned. The ministry dispatched 39 ambulances and moved the injured to Sheikh Zayed Specialised Hospital, Imbaba General Hospital and Warraq Central Hospital, while Health Minister Khaled Abdel-Ghaffar ordered nearby hospitals to their highest state of readiness.
