Egypt gold prices rise as global ounce gains 2.98%

An 18-karat gram reached about LE 4,950, while a gold pound, the Egyptian local unit of gold, cost about LE 46,200 in local trading.

Source: Egypt Independent · 2026-11-28 · 1 min read

Spot gold rose about $106.25, or 2.98%, on Friday to $3,673.09 an ounce. The price ranged from $3,558.05 to $3,680.16 during the session, according to Egypt Independent.

In Egypt, a gram of 21-karat gold, the Egyptian local price, cost about LE 5,775, the Egyptian pound (LE), with craftsmanship fees of LE 100 to LE 150. A 24-karat gram was about LE 6,600, while an 18-karat gram reached about LE 4,950. The gold pound was priced at about LE 46,200.

Despite Friday's rise, gold was down 11.66% over the year and 17.62% over six months. It had fallen 9.55% over the month, while gaining 3.96% for the week.

Why it mattersEgyptian buyers of 21-karat jewellery pay about LE 5,775 a gram, with craftsmanship fees adding LE 100 to LE 150.
