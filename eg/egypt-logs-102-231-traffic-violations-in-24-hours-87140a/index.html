Egypt logs 102,231 traffic violations in 24 hours

The Interior Ministry says 42 of 997 drivers screened in nationwide campaigns tested positive for drugs, and legal action has been taken.

Source: Egyptian Streets · 2026-11-28 · 1 min read

Egypt’s Interior Ministry recorded 102,231 traffic violations in 24 hours during nationwide road inspections, the ministry said. Offences included driving without valid licences, speeding, illegal parking, using mobile phones while driving and licensing breaches.

Authorities screened 997 drivers for drug use, with 42 testing positive. Separate checks in construction zones along the Regional Ring Road recorded 433 violations, including unauthorised passenger transport and failures to meet vehicle safety standards; six of 151 drivers screened there tested positive. The ministry said legal action had been taken over the offences.

Authorities also apprehended seven people subject to 11 court rulings and impounded one vehicle. Similar campaigns have run throughout November; on 9 November, the ministry reported 104,292 violations and 45 positive results among 1,094 drivers tested.

Why it mattersDrivers who committed the recorded offences face legal action, while seven people subject to 11 court rulings were apprehended.
