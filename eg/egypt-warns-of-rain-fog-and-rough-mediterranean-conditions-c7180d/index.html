Egypt warns of rain, fog and rough Mediterranean conditions from Monday

The meteorological authority forecasts temperatures falling by up to 6°C, with rain of varying intensity in parts of northern Egypt.

Source: Egypt Independent · 2026-11-28 · 1 min read

Egypt’s meteorological authority warned that a low-pressure system will bring changing weather to parts of the country from Monday, November 30, with temperatures dropping by up to 6°C and rain expected. Forecasts for November 28 to December 2 put daytime conditions at mild autumn levels across most regions, but hot in Southern Upper Egypt, with cool to cold nights and early mornings.

The authority forecast fog from 4 a.m. to 9 a.m. on roads in northern Egypt, Greater Cairo, the Suez Canal cities, Central Sinai and Northern Upper Egypt; it could become dense on some routes. Light rain has an approximate 40% chance on Sunday in parts of the Northern Coasts, Northern Nile Delta, Red Sea mountain range, South Sinai and Gulf of Aqaba. From Monday, rain of varying intensity and active winds are forecast in parts of northern Egypt, with maritime navigation disrupted along sections of the Mediterranean coast.

Greater Cairo and the Nile Delta are forecast to reach 25°C on Sunday, then 22°C on Tuesday and Wednesday; lows are expected to fall from 16°C to 14°C over those days.

Why it mattersDrivers on named routes face fog between 4 a.m. and 9 a.m., while Mediterranean mariners face disrupted navigation from Monday.
