US Congress awards Anwar Sadat its highest civilian honour

Sadat’s son received the Congressional Gold Medal at the Capitol for the late Egyptian president’s role in advancing peace in the Middle East.

Source: Egyptian Streets · 2026-11-20 · 1 min read

The US Congress awarded late Egyptian President Mohamed Anwar Sadat the Congressional Gold Medal at a ceremony in Washington on Wednesday, 18 November. The medal is the US legislature’s highest civilian honour.

Gamal Sadat received it on his father’s behalf. Foreign Minister Badr Abdelatty led an Egyptian delegation representing President Abdel Fattah El-Sisi, and former Foreign Minister Mohamed El-Orabi attended. The ceremony recognised Sadat’s contributions to advancing peace in the Middle East, according to Egyptian Streets.

El-Sisi welcomed the award in a post on his official X account on Wednesday, thanking Congress. He said Sadat’s legacy showed that peace could be achieved through political will and decisive action.

Why it mattersEgypt’s foreign minister represented El-Sisi at the Washington ceremony, while Gamal Sadat accepted the medal on his father’s behalf.
