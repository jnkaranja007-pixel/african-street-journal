Cairo airport adds international driving permit service

Travelers can complete applications at Terminal 3 or Air Mall before departure, avoiding a separate trip to outside offices.

Source: Egypt Independent · 2026-11-26 · 1 min read

The Cairo International Airport Company has introduced an international driving permit service at Terminal 3 and Air Mall, allowing travelers to handle applications at the airport before leaving Egypt.

The service is for citizens planning to drive abroad. The airport company said applicants can use either location, avoiding the need to visit offices outside the airport beforehand.

The company described the launch as part of a plan to expand on-site passenger support services and reduce time spent on travel errands away from the airport.

Why it mattersCitizens preparing to drive abroad can submit permit applications at either of the airport’s two locations rather than visiting an outside office first.
