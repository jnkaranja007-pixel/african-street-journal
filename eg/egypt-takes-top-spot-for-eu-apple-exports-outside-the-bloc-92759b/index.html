Egypt takes top spot for EU apple exports outside the bloc

EU shipments rose 32% to 190,000 tonnes in 2025/26, putting Egypt ahead of the United Kingdom by about 36,000 tonnes.

Source: Food Business Africa · 2026-11-19 · 1 min read

Egypt became the European Union’s largest apple market outside the bloc in the 2025/26 season, receiving 190,000 tonnes and overtaking the United Kingdom. Shipments to Egypt rose 32% from the previous season and more than doubled over two seasons, according to a November 2026 report by the US Department of Agriculture.

Egypt took about 16% of the EU’s apple exports to non-member countries, while the UK imported 154,000 tonnes. Polish companies shipped 22,600 tonnes to Egypt in September and October 2026; Egypt accounted for around 45% of Poland’s non-EU apple exports in October alone.

The increase came alongside improving economic conditions in Egypt and a recovery in importers’ purchasing power, the report said. The 190,000 tonnes of European fruit also put domestic producers in competition with lower-priced imports during their own season.

Why it mattersEgyptian growers face lower-priced European apples during their own season, as imports reached 190,000 tonnes.
