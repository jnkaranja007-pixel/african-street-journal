Egypt MP says measures to block Grand Theft Auto VI are coming

Ahmed Badawy says talks with media and telecom regulators are under way, while fewer than one million people in Egypt play the game.

Source: Egypt Independent · 2026-11-26 · 1 min read

Ahmed Badawy, chairman of Egypt's parliamentary Communications Committee, said measures to stop Grand Theft Auto VI, a video game, from being available in Egypt would be introduced in the coming days. He said the game posed a danger to young people and violated the Information Technology Law.

Badawy said discussions had taken place with the Supreme Council for Media Regulation and coordination was under way with the National Telecommunications Regulatory Authority. He put the game's Egypt player count at far below one million, compared with nearly 40 million worldwide, and said blocking it would not replace parental supervision.

Badawy said a Digital Safety Law, under directives issued by President Abdel Fattah al-Sisi in January, would be enacted within a few weeks. He also said some players spend 14 hours a day on the game.

Why it mattersFamilies monitoring children who play Grand Theft Auto VI must also rely on parental supervision; Badawy says a Digital Safety Law is due within a few weeks.
