Egypt denies military rift with eastern Libya forces

Information Minister Diaa Rashwan said coordination with the Libyan National Army continues as usual after reports Egypt withdrew experts from Benghazi.

Source: Egyptian Streets · 2026-11-23 · 1 min read

Egypt denied on Sunday, 22 November, that it had withdrawn military experts from Benghazi, Libya, after reports of disagreements with the Libyan National Army (LNA). Minister of State for Information Diaa Rashwan called the claims circulating in media and on social media unfounded.

Under way as normal, according to Rashwan, are the links between Cairo and the LNA's General Command. He urged media outlets to check information before publishing claims that have not been substantiated, and restated Egypt's backing for Libya's stability, territorial integrity and national institutions.

Khalifa Haftar leads the LNA, which holds much of eastern and southern Libya, while the internationally recognised Government of National Unity sits in Tripoli under Prime Minister Abdul Hamid Dbeibeh. Egypt has kept close military and political ties with Haftar's forces and has widened diplomatic dealings with the Tripoli government.

Why it mattersEgypt's information minister must urge media outlets to verify claims about military coordination with the LNA before publishing them.
