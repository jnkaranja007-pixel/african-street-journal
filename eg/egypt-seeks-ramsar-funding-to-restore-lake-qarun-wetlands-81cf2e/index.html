Egypt seeks Ramsar funding to restore Lake Qarun wetlands

Environment Minister Manal Awad raised funding with Ramsar chief Musonda Mumba to tackle pollution and improve the lake’s ecological condition.

Source: Daily News Egypt · 2026-11-18 · 1 min read

Egypt’s Environment Minister Manal Awad discussed securing funding to restore wetlands and tackle pollution in Egyptian lakes, with particular focus on Lake Qarun, during talks with Ramsar Convention Secretary-General Musonda Mumba at COP31 in Antalya.

Mumba said the convention’s secretariat was seeking closer cooperation with Turkey, which chairs the Mediterranean Wetlands Working Group, to mobilise money for addressing chemical pollution and improving important lakes. She said Lake Qarun provides habitat for migratory birds and livelihoods and jobs, particularly for fishermen and farmers.

Egypt has four wetlands of international importance under the convention: Lakes Bardawil and Burullus, and the Wadi El-Rayan and Qarun protected areas. The country’s 2024–2030 biodiversity strategy sets 21 national targets, including restoring 20% of degraded ecosystems.

Why it mattersFishermen and farmers around Lake Qarun rely on its livelihoods and employment, while the talks focused on funding to address pollution.
