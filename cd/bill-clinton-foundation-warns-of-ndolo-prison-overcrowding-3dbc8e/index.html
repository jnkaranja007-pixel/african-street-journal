Bill Clinton Foundation warns of Ndolo prison overcrowding

The foundation urges Congolese authorities to take urgent action to alleviate severe overcrowding at the Ndolo military prison.

Source: Radio Okapi · 2026-11-03 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

The Bill Clinton Peace Foundation (BCPF) has raised concerns about detention conditions at the Ndolo military prison in Kinshasa. In a statement released on October 30, the organization called on Congolese authorities to implement immediate measures to reduce the prison's significant overcrowding.

The foundation's statement, reported by Radio Okapi on November 2, specifically targets the severe population density within the military facility. The BCPF is urging for urgent action to address the situation.

The organization's appeal highlights the critical state of the Ndolo prison. The BCPF's call for urgent measures underscores the immediate need for intervention by the Congolese government.

Why it mattersThe Bill Clinton Peace Foundation is concerned about the detention conditions and overcrowding at the Ndolo military prison in Kinshasa.
