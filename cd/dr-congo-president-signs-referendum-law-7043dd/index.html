DR Congo president signs referendum law

The new law sets conditions for a public vote on a potential new constitution.

Sources: Congo Independant, Radio Okapi and Actualite.cd · 2026-11-12 · 1 min read

President Félix Tshisekedi has signed into law a bill that establishes the conditions for organizing a referendum in the Democratic Republic of Congo. The presidential ordinance, dated November 9, 2026, was read on national television on November 11, 2026. This legislation outlines the rules for the referendum's object, procedure, initiative, and convocation.

Roger Kalenga, deputy spokesperson for the head of state, explained that the law defines a referendum as an expression of the people's sovereignty through a direct consultation. Citizens will vote yes or no on a specific text or question. Participation is considered a civic duty for all registered voters aged 18 and above who are in possession of their civil and political rights.

André Mbata, secretary general of the presidential majority, stated that the new referendum law opens the way for adopting a new constitution. He indicated that this process is now possible without being imposed. The promulgation of this law was a major focus in media reports on November 11, 2026.
