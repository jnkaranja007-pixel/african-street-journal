DR Congo opposition coalition backs Catholic leaders after march suppression

The Coalition Article 64 condemned restrictions on some religious activities and the response to a march organised by Lubumbashi’s archdiocese.

Sources: Radio Okapi, Eco News RDC and Congo Independant · 2026-11-26 · 1 min read

The Coalition Article 64 (C64), an opposition platform in DR Congo, voiced support on Tuesday, 24 November, for Cardinal Fridolin Ambongo, Archbishop Fulgence Muteba and Catholic faithful in the archdioceses of Kinshasa and Lubumbashi. The statement followed restrictions on some religious activities and the suppression of a march organised by the Lubumbashi archdiocese.

The archdiocese had planned the 23 November march to demand enforcement of a court ruling ordering the demolition of buildings on the Tshamalale seminary grounds in Lubumbashi. The city’s mayor, Joyce Tunda, said provincial authorities had invited the archbishop to talks, but he sent a priest without a mandate and did not attend a second invitation on Monday.

Tunda said the provincial government remained ready to continue dialogue, and police were deployed to prevent disturbances in the city.

Why it mattersWorshippers in Kinshasa and Lubumbashi now face restrictions on some religious activities, and the Lubumbashi demolition dispute remains unresolved between the archdiocese and provincial authorities.
