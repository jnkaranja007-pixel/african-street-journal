Tshisekedi to press central African summit on UN resolution 2773

The DR Congo president arrived in Malabo on Friday 27 November for the 27th summit of the Economic Community of Central African States (ECCAS), the regional bloc.

Source: Radio Okapi · 2026-11-28 · 1 min read

DR Congo President Félix Tshisekedi arrived in Malabo, Equatorial Guinea, on Friday 27 November for the 27th summit of the Economic Community of Central African States (ECCAS), the regional bloc. The Congolese presidency said he intends to seek full implementation of United Nations Security Council resolution 2773, concerning security in eastern DR Congo.

Adopted in February 2025, the resolution calls on the Rwandan Defence Forces to stop supporting M23 and withdraw immediately and without preconditions from Congolese territory. It also demands that rebels of the Front for the Refoundation of the Nation (FRN), which replaced the former AFC/M23 coalition, leave areas they control in DR Congo.

The summit is also set to discuss ECCAS's Vision 2050, its 2026-2030 strategic plan and funding mechanisms, as well as energy, health, maritime security and border governance.

Why it mattersThe Rwandan Defence Forces and the FRN face demands to end their support for, and their control over, areas of eastern DR Congo under resolution 2773.
