US midterm elections may alter Washington's DRC agreements

The November 3rd US midterm elections could change power dynamics in Washington, affecting future deals.

Source: Actualite.cd · 2026-11-02 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

The US midterm elections on November 3rd may reshape the balance of power in Washington. This shift could influence agreements between the United States and Kinshasa.

The election results will determine party control of the US Congress, impacting the president's ability to advance foreign policy. Changes in congressional leadership could lead to a review of existing bilateral accords.

The specific implications for agreements between Washington and Kinshasa depend on the final vote tallies and the legislative agenda.
