Ambongo accuses Kinshasa authorities of provoking Catholic Church

The cardinal spoke after a Christ the King mass was moved from Tata Raphaël stadium to Saint-Raphaël college grounds.

Sources: Actualite.cd and Radio Okapi · 2026-11-23 · 1 min read

Cardinal Fridolin Ambongo accused Kinshasa’s authorities of provoking the Catholic Church after a Christ the King mass was cancelled at Tata Raphaël stadium. The service was held instead on Sunday afternoon at the grounds of Saint-Raphaël college.

Ambongo made the accusation after the mass, according to Actualite.cd. He questioned what the authorities stood to gain from provoking the church. The report did not give a reason for the cancellation or say who ordered it.

The planned stadium celebration was replaced by the gathering at the college grounds; no further official response was reported.

Why it mattersAmbongo must respond to the cancellation of the planned stadium mass, which was instead held at Saint-Raphaël college grounds.
