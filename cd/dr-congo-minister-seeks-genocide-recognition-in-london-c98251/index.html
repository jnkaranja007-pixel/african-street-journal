DR Congo minister seeks genocide recognition in London

Human rights minister Samuel Mbemba urged UK institutions to sanction Paul Kagame and recognize Congolese genocides.

Source: Radio Okapi · 2026-11-15 · 1 min read

DR Congo's Human Rights Minister Samuel Mbemba urged the United Kingdom on Thursday, November 13, to take strong measures against Rwanda and recognize Congolese genocides. Speaking in London, Mbemba called on human rights defenders and civil society activists to follow the example of Edmond Morel, who exposed Belgian King Leopold II's atrocities in DR Congo.

Mbemba stated that the situation in eastern DR Congo, where he estimates over 10 million people have died, mirrors the historical injustices Morel denounced. He asked UK institutions to impose sanctions on Paul Kagame and pass resolutions acknowledging the genocides. "We are therefore here in England as the cradle of modern human rights," Mbemba said, adding that "women and children are terrorized by forces supported by the Government of Rwanda" in eastern Congo.

The DR Congo government, through the CIA-VAR and FONAREV under the Ministry of Human Rights, has established a scientific and technical team of professors and researchers. This team aims to achieve official recognition of the Congolese genocides and secure Rwanda's condemnation.

Why it mattersHuman Rights Minister Samuel Mbemba is seeking UK sanctions against Paul Kagame and recognition of Congolese genocides, impacting diplomatic relations and potential future actions.
