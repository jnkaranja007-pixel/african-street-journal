Wapinda youth group urges stronger Ebola response as suspected cases top 400

The group reports 7 confirmed cases and 2 deaths, and says health teams lack fuel, medicines and ambulances to reach patients.

Source: Radio Okapi · 2026-11-23 · 1 min read

The Wapinda Youth Parliament, a civic group in Yakoma territory in DR Congo’s North-Ubangi province, called on health authorities and response partners on Sunday, 22 November, to strengthen Ebola operations. It cited more than 400 suspected cases, including 7 confirmed infections and 2 deaths.

The group said local health facilities and response teams lacked essential supplies. It reported that the area had gone more than 45 days without operational assistance, with too little fuel and too few motorcycles, and no ambulances to transport patients. It also cited shortages of medicines, protective equipment and handwashing kits, and said Wapinda had no dedicated isolation facility or treatment centre for confirmed cases.

Jeannot Mbuna Kowenzu, the group’s president, urged authorities and partners to provide more material, financial and medical resources. The group also called for regular supplies and stronger support for communities.

Why it mattersWapinda’s response teams need fuel, motorcycles, ambulances and medical supplies after more than 45 days without operational assistance.
