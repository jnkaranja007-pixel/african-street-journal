Judith Suminwa calls for women's peace role in DR Congo

The Prime Minister urged women to be central actors for peace in the Great Lakes region.

Source: Actualite.cd · 2026-11-03 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Prime Minister Judith Suminwa called for women's participation to be a concrete tool for peace in the Great Lakes region. She stated that peace should be a lived reality, not a repeated promise.

Suminwa urged that women must be central actors in achieving peace. This call was made on Monday, February 11, 2026, in the Great Lakes region.

The Prime Minister's statement aims to make peace a tangible experience for all.
