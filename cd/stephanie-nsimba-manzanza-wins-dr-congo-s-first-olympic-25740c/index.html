Stéphanie Nsimba Manzanza wins DR Congo's first Olympic medal

The wrestler secured a bronze medal in beach wrestling at the Dakar 2026 Youth Olympic Games.

Source: Radio Okapi · 2026-11-08 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Stéphanie Nsimba Manzanza made history for the Democratic Republic of Congo on Saturday, November 7, in Dakar, by winning the country's first Olympic medal at the 2026 Youth Olympic Games.

Competing in beach wrestling in the under-55kg category, Nsimba Manzanza claimed the bronze medal after defeating Azerbaijan's Gunel Karimli in the third-place match.

This achievement marks a turning point for Congolese sport, with Nsimba Manzanza, already a double gold medalist at the African Wrestling Championships, proving her potential on the international stage.

Why it mattersStéphanie Nsimba Manzanza's historic bronze medal win provides a source of national pride and hope for the future of sport in the DR Congo.
