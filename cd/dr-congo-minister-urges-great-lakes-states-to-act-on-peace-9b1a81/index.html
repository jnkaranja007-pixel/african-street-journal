DR Congo Minister Urges Great Lakes States to Act on Peace Commitments

DR Congo's Minister for Regional Integration called on member states of the International Conference on the Great Lakes Region to turn their pledges into concrete actions.

Floribert Anzuluni Isiloketshi, the Democratic Republic of Congo's Minister of Regional Integration, has urged member states of the International Conference on the Great Lakes Region (ICGLR) to translate their commitments into tangible actions for peace.

The minister's call emphasizes the need for practical steps to address the ongoing challenges within the Great Lakes region, highlighting a gap between stated intentions and on-the-ground implementation.

This appeal comes as the region continues to grapple with various security and stability issues, underscoring the urgency for collective and decisive measures from all ICGLR member states.

Why it mattersThe Minister of Regional Integration of the DR Congo is urging ICGLR member states to take concrete actions for peace, affecting regional stability.
