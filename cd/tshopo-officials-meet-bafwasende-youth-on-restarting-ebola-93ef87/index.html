Tshopo officials meet Bafwasende youth on restarting Ebola response

Provincial ministers for the interior and health joined response workers for talks with young people in the Tshopo town.

Sources: Actualite.cd and Radio Okapi · 2026-11-28 · 1 min read

Provincial ministers for the interior and health in Tshopo met young people in Bafwasende on Monday, 23 November 2026, to discuss resuming activities in the response to Ebola. Health workers involved in the response also took part.

Actualite.cd reported that the exchange focused on restarting response activities. It gave no timetable and no specific measures agreed.

Radio Okapi separately reported that authorities were preparing to resume Ebola response work in Bafwasende, but did not say when that would begin.
