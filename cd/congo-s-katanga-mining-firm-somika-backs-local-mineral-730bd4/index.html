Congo's Katanga mining firm SOMIKA backs local mineral processing

The Société Minière du Katanga joined the first Makutano Mining forum in Kinshasa, where it promoted processing minerals in the country.

Sources: Actualite.cd and Eco News RDC · 2026-11-26 · 1 min read

SOMIKA, the Société Minière du Katanga and a pioneer company of the Vinmart Group in the Democratic Republic of Congo, took part in the first edition of the Makutano Mining 2026 forum in Kinshasa, Actualite.cd reported on Thursday. The company said it is backing local processing to create more value in the country.

The report did not detail a processing project, an investment amount or a timeline. A separate report by Eco News RDC quoted Louis Watum saying that the sector's attractiveness also rests on good governance, transparency, a stable tax regime and a predictable regulatory framework.

Investors weighing mining projects in the country will look for those commitments to be matched by concrete plans, though none were reported at the forum.
