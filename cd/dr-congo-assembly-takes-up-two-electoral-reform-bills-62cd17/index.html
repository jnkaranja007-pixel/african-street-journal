DR Congo assembly takes up two electoral reform bills

Lawmakers are examining proposals to reform the electoral commission and the electoral law as the September session nears its close.

Source: Actualite.cd · 2026-11-27 · 1 min read

The Democratic Republic of Congo’s National Assembly is examining two bills on electoral reform, bringing the election agenda back into debate in the weeks before the September 2026 ordinary session closes.

The proposals cover reform of the Independent National Electoral Commission (CENI), the body that organises elections, and changes to the electoral law, Actualite.cd reported on Friday. The report says the country’s institutions are turning their attention to the electoral agenda, but gives no details of the bills’ provisions or the Assembly’s timetable for considering them.

The report does not say when the session closes.

Why it mattersCongolese voters and candidates face possible changes to how the electoral commission runs elections, depending on what lawmakers approve before the session ends.
