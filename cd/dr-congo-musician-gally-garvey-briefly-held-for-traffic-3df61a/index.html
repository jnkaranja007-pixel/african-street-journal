DR Congo musician Gally Garvey briefly held for traffic offenses

Police in Kinshasa detained the artist Gally Garvey on Monday, November 2, 2026, for serious traffic violations.

Source: Actualite.cd · 2026-11-03 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Kinshasa police briefly detained musician Gally Garvey on Monday, November 2, 2026, for serious traffic violations. Police sources announced the arrest to ACTUALITE.CD.

The artist was stopped in Kinshasa for infractions that sources described as grave. The police did not immediately release further details on the nature of the violations or the duration of his detention.

This incident occurred as James Swan, the UN special representative and head of MONUSCO, began a visit to Goma in North Kivu for consultations in eastern DR Congo.
