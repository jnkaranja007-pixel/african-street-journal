DR Congo launches judicial digitalisation project

The justice ministry aims to modernise the system with support from the EU and UNDP.

Source: Actualite.cd · 2026-11-06 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

The Democratic Republic of Congo's Justice Ministry officially began digitalising judicial revenues and interconnecting justice services on Thursday, November 5, 2026. This initiative connects courts, public prosecutor offices, and penitentiary institutions across the country.

The reform, led by the Justice Ministry, receives technical assistance from the United Nations Development Programme (UNDP) and financial backing from the European Union (EU). Officials stated the goal is to modernise the judicial system and improve citizen services.

This digitalisation process is intended to enhance transparency and streamline services for citizens interacting with the justice system. The project was launched in Kinshasa.

Why it mattersCitizens will experience improved and more transparent services when dealing with the judicial system.
