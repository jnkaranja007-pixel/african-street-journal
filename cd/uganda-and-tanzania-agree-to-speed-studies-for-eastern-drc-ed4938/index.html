Uganda and Tanzania agree to speed studies for eastern DRC railway

The framework covers feasibility, design and financing work for a line linking the Central Corridor with eastern Congo.

Source: Food Business Africa · 2026-11-21 · 1 min read

On 18 November, Presidents Yoweri Museveni and Samia Suluhu Hassan watched Uganda and Tanzania sign a framework agreement at State House in Entebbe to fast-track a standard gauge railway into eastern Democratic Republic of Congo. The line would run from Isaka through Lusahunga and Murongo/Kikagati to Mpondwe.

The agreement binds both governments to expedite feasibility studies, technical designs and financing arrangements. Transport economist Richard Sendi, who heads planning and strategy at Uganda's SGR Development and Coordination Office, said no definitive cost estimate exists yet, and the Central Corridor Transit Transport Facilitation Agency will fund the studies.

The African Development Bank has backed preliminary preparation, and Italian financing interests are understood to be exploring the project. Eastern DRC, a market of about 116 million people, would gain a link to regional markets.

Why it mattersEastern DRC traders and mineral exporters must wait for studies to set the railway's cost and implementation schedule.
