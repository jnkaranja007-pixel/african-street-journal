Kinshasa governor targets December reopening for central market

Daniel Bumba says septic-tank repairs are done, but water connection and finishing work remain before the market can resume operations.

Source: Radio Okapi · 2026-11-24 · 1 min read

Kinshasa governor Daniel Bumba said the city’s central market, known as Zando, is due to reopen in December after years of closure for modernisation. He announced the target on Sunday, 22 November, after inspecting finishing work at the site.

Bumba said repairs to septic tanks, which had delayed an earlier planned July opening, are complete. Remaining tasks include backfilling and other finishing work, while connecting the market to water is among the final technical challenges before full operations begin.

Provincial authorities also said sanitation and improvement work has been carried out on nearby avenues, including Bokassa, Rwakadingi, Du Marais and Kasa-Vubu. The governor said the aim is to open before the end of the year.

Why it mattersTraders and shoppers in Kinshasa are awaiting access to the market, with water connection and finishing work still to be completed before the December target.
