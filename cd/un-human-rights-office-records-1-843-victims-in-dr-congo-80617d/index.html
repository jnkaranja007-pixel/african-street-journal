UN human rights office records 1 843 victims in DR Congo

Its September count rose by more than 41 % from August, with conflict-affected provinces accounting for 91 % of documented violations.

Source: Radio Okapi · 2026-11-20 · 1 min read

The United Nations Joint Human Rights Office recorded 1 843 victims of human rights violations and abuses in the Democratic Republic of the Congo during September 2026, Radio Okapi reported.

The office’s report said the count was up by more than 41 % from August. Provinces affected by armed conflict accounted for 91 % of documented violations, and North Kivu had the highest count, with 477 cases.

Ituri recorded 70 cases and South Kivu 65, the report said. The figures cover September 2026.
