DR Congo signs US$41 million contract for Ngandajika agro-industrial park

Construction is scheduled to start in January 2027, while separate private investment is being assessed for processing plants and logistics facilities.

Source: Food Business Africa · 2026-11-28 · 1 min read

The Democratic Republic of Congo has signed a US$41 million contract to build the Ngandajika agro-industrial park in Lomami province, with construction scheduled to start in January 2027. A Chinese company will do the work, Bruno Miteyo Nyenge, head of the president’s Civil Household, said in a message reported by 7sur7.cd.

The park covers 534 hectares, including 159 hectares for livestock farming, 51 for industrial activity and 32 for logistics, according to African Development Bank procurement documents. Planned facilities include slaughterhouses, a laboratory and an agricultural services centre. The contractor’s identity and the contract’s completion deadline have not been disclosed.

A separate 2026 government-AfDB financing agreement worth about US$155 million covers transport links. The AfDB extended the deadline for bids on two road contracts, covering 125 kilometres in total, from December 4 to December 18, 2026. Each road project is expected to take 30 months.

Why it mattersInvestors are being assessed for the processing plants, warehouses and logistics facilities, while the road bids close on December 18, 2026.
