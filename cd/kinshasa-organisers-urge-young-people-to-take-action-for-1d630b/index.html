Kinshasa organisers urge young people to take action for peace

At a 16 November conference, speakers called on Congolese youth to help prevent conflict and oppose violence against women and girls.

Source: Radio Okapi · 2026-11-19 · 1 min read

Organisers in Kinshasa urged young people in the Democratic Republic of Congo to take a more active role in peace and security at a conference on Monday, 16 November. Change Your World, the Kinshasa Youth Parliament and the United Nations mission in the country held the event under the theme “Youth in action for peace.”

The conference aimed to raise awareness of young people’s role in preventing conflict and strengthening social cohesion in their communities. Sarah Tambwe, Change Your World’s coordinator, said young people need spaces to express themselves and engage in peace efforts. Youth parliamentarian Jennifer Sarty urged them to oppose violence against women and girls and take part in peacebuilding.

The organisers called on participants to turn their discussions and commitments into concrete action in their communities.

Why it mattersYoung people who attended the conference are being asked to carry peace and anti-violence awareness into their communities.
