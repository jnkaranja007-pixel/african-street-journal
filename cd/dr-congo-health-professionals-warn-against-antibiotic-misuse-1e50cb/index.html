DR Congo health professionals warn against antibiotic misuse

The advice comes during a Kinshasa campaign running from 18 to 24 November on antimicrobial resistance in people and animals.

Source: Radio Okapi · 2026-11-21 · 1 min read

Health professionals in Kinshasa are warning against using antibiotics without medical advice, saying misuse can help bacteria become resistant to treatment. The warning comes during the 2026 World Antimicrobial Resistance Awareness Week, held from 18 to 24 November.

People with fever, sore throats or other infections sometimes buy antibiotics directly from pharmacies, reuse old prescriptions or follow advice from acquaintances, Radio Okapi reported. The professionals say antibiotics work only against some bacterial infections and recommend taking them only with a prescription, at the prescribed dose and for the full course.

A biologist explained that antibiotics can either kill bacteria or stop them multiplying. Specialists also cite self-medication, incorrect dosing, early treatment stoppage and poor hygiene as factors that encourage resistance.

Why it mattersPeople using antibiotics without medical advice are urged to obtain prescriptions and complete treatment at the correct dose and duration.
