Belgium backs new UN mission mandate and presses for ceasefire compliance

Belgian Foreign Minister Maxime Prévot raised the ceasefire and the demobilisation of Rwandan Hutu rebels with the head of the United Nations mission in Congo.

Sources: Actualite.cd and Radio Okapi · 2026-11-26 · 1 min read

Belgium pressed for compliance with the ceasefire and for the neutralisation and demobilisation of the Democratic Forces for the Liberation of Rwanda (FDLR), a Rwandan Hutu armed group operating in eastern DR Congo, while backing a new mandate for the United Nations Stabilisation Mission in the Congo (MONUSCO). Belgian Foreign Minister Maxime Prévot discussed the issues with James Swan, the mission's head.

Swan is continuing contacts and consultations under his responsibilities as head of the mission, according to Actualite.cd. The report did not give details of any agreement or decision from the meeting.

The current mandate is approaching expiry. Jeunes Ambassadeurs de la paix et du développement, a Congolese youth organisation, has called for stronger disarmament, demobilisation and reintegration provisions in the next mandate, Radio Okapi reported.

Why it mattersMONUSCO's leadership must continue consultations as the current mandate approaches expiry, while Belgium backs a new mandate and presses for FDLR demobilisation.
