WHO calls for immediate, safe evacuation of Ebola response team in Ituri

WHO Director-General Tedros Adhanom Ghebreyesus reported a worrying security situation around a hospital in Ituri province, near the outbreak's initial focus.

Source: Actualite.cd · 2026-11-23 · 1 min read

The World Health Organization (WHO) has called for the immediate and safe evacuation of its Ebola response team in Ituri province, in eastern Democratic Republic of Congo, after fighting broke out around a hospital.

WHO Director-General Tedros Adhanom Ghebreyesus reported a worrying security situation around the hospital, which is close to the initial focus of the Ebola outbreak. The call covers the team responding to the outbreak.

The report did not say whether the team had already been evacuated or set a timetable for its removal.

Why it mattersEbola responders working near the Ituri hospital must be moved out safely now, and no evacuation timetable has been set.
