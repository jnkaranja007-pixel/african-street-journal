Lawmaker announces no-confidence vote against agriculture minister

A national lawmaker plans to introduce a motion of no-confidence against the Minister of State for Agriculture and Food Security over alleged embezzlement of $57 million.

Source: Actualite.cd · 2026-10-22 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

National Assembly member Hermione Bolumbe announced on October 21, 2026, that she will soon file a motion of no-confidence against Muhindo Nzangi, the Minister of State for Agriculture and Food Security. The motion is based on suspicions of embezzlement totaling $57 million.

The announcement comes amid broader discussions in Congolese media about the country's security and political crises. Other headlines on October 22, 2026, included calls for peace from the LUCHA movement directed at former president Joseph Kabila and current president Félix Tshisekedi, as well as statements from Belgium regarding the FRN and threats made by Fils Mukoko.

The specific allegations against Minister Nzangi have not yet been detailed in public reports. The motion, if filed and passed, could lead to the minister's removal from office and potentially trigger a cabinet reshuffle.

Why it mattersThe agriculture minister faces a no-confidence vote, potentially impacting the ministry's operations and leadership.
