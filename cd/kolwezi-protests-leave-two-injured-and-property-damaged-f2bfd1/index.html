Kolwezi protests leave two injured and property damaged

Artisanal miners demonstrated for two days in Kolwezi, paralyzing economic activity and causing injuries and material damage.

Source: Radio Okapi · 2026-10-22 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

A gradual calm returned to several neighborhoods in Kolwezi, Lualaba province, on Wednesday, October 21, following two days of violent demonstrations by artisanal miners. Local sources reported that the unrest disrupted socio-economic activities and resulted in several injuries and significant material damage.

The protests, which lasted for two days, brought normal life to a standstill in the affected areas. The scale of the material damage is still being assessed, but initial reports indicate it is substantial. The number of injured has been confirmed by local sources, though specific details about their conditions were not immediately available.

The demonstrations by artisanal miners have had a direct impact on the local economy, halting daily business and trade. The return to calm suggests a de-escalation of the situation, though the full extent of the consequences of the two-day unrest is yet to be determined.

Why it mattersArtisanal miners are affected by the disruption of socio-economic activities and potential damage to their livelihoods during the protests.
