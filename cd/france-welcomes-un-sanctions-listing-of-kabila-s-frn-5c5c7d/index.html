France welcomes UN sanctions listing of Kabila's FRN movement

Paris reaffirms its support for DR Congo's sovereignty and territorial integrity in the east.

Sources: Actualite.cd and Radio Okapi · 2026-11-15 · 1 min read

France has welcomed the United Nations' decision to add the Front for the Refoundation of the Nation (FRN) to its sanctions list. The FRN is a politico-military movement led by former president Joseph Kabila Kabange, and the UN measures also cover the entire leadership of the AFC/M23.

France said the designation reflects its commitment to the sovereignty and territorial integrity of the Democratic Republic of Congo. The UN sanctions now apply to the FRN as a group and to its leaders, who were named in the listing.

Separately, the UNICEF associate director for public health emergencies, Dr Douglas Noble, visited Bunia this week and praised progress against the seventeenth Ebola outbreak in the country. He pledged the agency's continued support for the response in the city.

Why it mattersIndividuals and entities on the list face UN sanctions, which can restrict their dealings and movements in the region.
