DR Congo prime minister submits 2027 budget to assembly

Judith Suminwa Tuluka presented the draft finance law for the upcoming fiscal year on October 31, 2026.

Source: Actualite.cd · 2026-10-31 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Prime Minister Judith Suminwa Tuluka presented the draft finance law for the 2027 fiscal year to the National Assembly on October 31, 2026. She stated that every Congolese franc mobilized should lead to real progress in citizens' lives and every decision made should be a promise kept for the future of Congo.

The submission of the 2027 budget project occurred on October 31, 2026. The prime minister's statement emphasized the connection between mobilized funds and tangible improvements for the Congolese people. The proposed budget is now under examination by the National Assembly.

Separately, the President of the Democratic Republic of Congo participated in the third Russia-Africa Summit held in Moscow from October 28 to 29, 2026. This summit focused on strengthening political, economic, and diplomatic cooperation between Russia and African states.

Why it mattersThe prime minister has submitted the 2027 budget to the National Assembly, directly impacting the country's financial planning and future development.
