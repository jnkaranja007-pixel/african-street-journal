Tshisekedi sets four demands for Congo to process more minerals at home

At a Kinshasa economic forum, the president said reliable energy, infrastructure, skills and a fight against misconduct are needed for the shift.

Sources: Radio Okapi and Eco News RDC · 2026-11-25 · 1 min read

Democratic Republic of Congo President Félix Tshisekedi said on Monday, 23 November, that the country should move beyond producing minerals and build an economy able to process and manufacture more of them. He set out four requirements at the launch of the 12th Makutano Economic Forum in Kinshasa: more energy, durable infrastructure, investment in skills and a fight against misconduct.

Tshisekedi said reliable, competitively priced electricity was needed for domestic refining and mineral processing. He argued that factories should not face power shortages despite Congo's large electricity potential.

The forum was held alongside Makutano Mining 2026 under the theme “Mining sovereignty in action”. Tshisekedi said mineral sovereignty depended on turning underground resources into lasting economic wealth.

Why it mattersCongo's industrial power supply must meet the needs of factories seeking to refine and process minerals, the president said.
