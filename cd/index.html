News · 08/21/2026

NGO reports 10,000 fire victims lack aid in South Kivu

More than 10,000 people from 1,900 households are living without humanitarian assistance after their homes were burned, according to the Parlement citoyen pour la démocratie et la bonne gouvernance. The NGO issued the alert on Aug. 20.

Why it mattersThe lack of aid leaves thousands of displaced residents in the province without basic shelter or food security.

Health · 08/21/2026

DRC launches emergency Ebola measures along Congo River

The government established an emergency system on Thursday between Kisangani and Kinshasa to prevent the spread of the Bundibugyo virus. An official communiqué stated the measures target the country's primary waterway.

Why it mattersThe restrictions aim to stop the virus from traveling via river transport to the capital, Kinshasa.

Politics · 08/21/2026

AFC-M23 bans Kinshasa news in South Kivu zones

De facto governor Patrick Busu Bwa Ngwi Shombo prohibited the broadcast of political programs and information from Kinshasa in rebel-controlled areas. The order affects zones under AFC-M23 control in South Kivu.

Why it mattersResidents in these regions are now blocked from accessing official government communications and political discourse from the capital.

Education · 08/21/2026

Kindu school supply sales drop ahead of Sept. 1 start

Market activity for uniforms, notebooks, and bags has declined at the Kindu central market two weeks before the Sept. 1 school start date. Radio Okapi reports the slump is due to salary arrears.

Why it mattersParents unable to receive back pay cannot afford the necessary materials for their children to begin the school year.

Sport · 08/21/2026

Joël Kayamba joins Czech club FC Hlinsko

Joël Kayamba officially signed with FC Hlinsko in the Czech fourth division. The player, who has 7 caps with the Leopards, had been a free agent since leaving Atyrau in Kazakhstan in January.

Why it mattersThe transfer ends a seven-month period of unemployment for the national team player.
