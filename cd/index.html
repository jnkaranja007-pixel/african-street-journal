Cholera outbreak worsens in Masisi territory, eastern DR Congo

Nearly 400 cases and dozens of deaths have been recorded in the Katoyi health zone since mid-September.

Cholera is spreading in the Katoyi health zone of Masisi territory in North Kivu province. A woman and a child died on the night of Wednesday to Thursday, 12 November, at the Mangere health centre. Since the outbreak began in mid-September, nearly 400 cases and several dozen deaths have been recorded in the zone, which already faces insecurity and serious shortages of medical supplies.

Health facilities report a steady stream of new patients with acute diarrhoea and severe vomiting. Local health authorities say at least six health areas are affected, including Mangere, Biolo, Kirambo, Kikoma and Mahya. Health services have recorded at least 15 cholera deaths in the past six days across the affected localities.

17 cartons of intravenous fluids and other medical supplies were sent to the region, but the zone's chief doctor says they have been stuck for two weeks in Luke because of insecurity and the isolation of this part of Masisi. Health officials fear the situation will worsen in the coming weeks if treatment capacity is not reinforced.

Why it mattersPatients at the centres in the six affected health areas are waiting on treatment supplies that have been stuck in Luke for two weeks.

Climate · 2026-11-13

Heavy rains flood Kinshasa streets

Flooding disrupts traffic and damages homes in DR Congo's capital after heavy rainfall.

Heavy rains on Wednesday, November 11, caused significant flooding in several Kinshasa neighbourhoods, disrupting traffic and damaging homes. The downpour led to accumulation of runoff water and overflowing gutters, making many main roads difficult to navigate.

Residents reported water entering houses in various parts of the capital, with families attempting to save belongings and facing considerable material damage. The increased traffic jams made travel extremely difficult for commuters as the precipitation continued in some areas.

The authorities had not yet released an official damage report at the time of publication. The ongoing rainfall continued to worry residents facing the risks of further inundation.

Why it mattersResidents in Kinshasa are facing property damage and significant travel disruptions due to severe flooding.

Erdoğan and Tshisekedi meet in Antalya, Turkey reaffirms DR Congo's territorial integrity

Turkey's president backed the Democratic Republic of Congo's territorial integrity at a meeting with President Félix Tshisekedi on the sidelines of the COP31 climate conference.

Turkish President Recep Tayyip Erdoğan met his Congolese counterpart, Félix Tshisekedi, in Antalya on Thursday, November 12, 2026, on the sidelines of COP31, the United Nations climate change conference held in Turkey.

During the meeting, Erdoğan reaffirmed Turkey's support for the territorial integrity of the Democratic Republic of Congo, according to Actualite.cd. Tshisekedi had earlier presented the Kivu-Kinshasa Green Corridor at the conference as a lever for sustainable development, Radio Okapi reported.

Congolese press coverage the same day also focused on the security situation in the east of the country, which remains a major concern for Kinshasa.

Why it mattersKinshasa's position on eastern security and its territorial claims now carry explicit backing from Ankara, a counterpart with growing ties to Congo.

Sport · 2026-11-13

DR Congo beats Sierra Leone 3-1 in Kinshasa in CAN 2027 qualifier

Yoane Wissa scored in the fourth minute as the Leopards kept up pressure on the Leone Stars at the Martyrs stadium.

DR Congo beat Sierra Leone 3-1 on Thursday, 12 November 2026, at the Martyrs stadium in Kinshasa, in the third round of qualifiers for the 2027 Africa Cup of Nations (CAN). Yoane Wissa scored in the fourth minute, and the Leopards dominated the visitors for most of the first half.

Samuel Moutoussamy made it 2-0 about 30 minutes later, from a pass by Wissa. The score stayed 2-0 at half-time. Early in the second half, Sierra Leone's goalkeeper I. Sesay deflected a shot by B. Cipenga, and Edo Kayembe scored DR Congo's third goal in the 58th minute.

Sierra Leone scored a late consolation goal, making the final score 3-1. Radio Okapi reported that the heavy scoreline appeared to dishearten the Leone Stars on the pitch.

Why it mattersThe win moves DR Congo a step closer to the 2027 Africa Cup of Nations finals, with the next qualifiers still to decide the group standings.
