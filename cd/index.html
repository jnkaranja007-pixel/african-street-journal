ADF attack kills 35 civilians in Beni territory

Islamist rebels attacked the locality of Cantine-Aloya on November 6, 2026.

At least 35 civilians died in a new attack by the Allied Democratic Forces (ADF) in Beni territory, DR Congo. The attack occurred during the night of Thursday to Friday, November 6, 2026, in the locality of Cantine-Aloya.

The ADF, an Islamist group, carried out the assault in Cantine-Aloya, a location situated on the border between the Beni and Lubero territories. The victims were all civilians.

This incident follows a pattern of violence attributed to the ADF in the region.

DR Congo rangers attack Ugandan goat herders

Tensions rise between Sarambwe reserve and Bwindi park communities after April goat seizure.

Tensions persist between communities bordering the Sarambwe Nature Reserve in DR Congo's Rutshuru territory and those near Uganda's Bwindi Impenetrable National Park. Local activist Muhindo Kibunga Elias reported on Thursday, November 5, that Ugandan eco-guards violently assaulted several Sarambwe community trackers on Monday.

The incident follows a dispute that began in April when Sarambwe trackers seized about 30 goats believed to belong to Ugandan herders inside the protected area. The goats were taken to a reserve surveillance post, and their owners are still seeking information on their fate from Sarambwe reserve managers.

Elias stated that the lack of clear explanations from reserve managers fuels the tensions, warning that the situation could escalate. He called for swift intervention from nature conservation authorities on both sides, urging the ICCN and Uganda Wildlife Authority to find a joint solution and involving the Greater Virunga Transboundary Collaboration.

Why it mattersCommunities in DR Congo and Uganda bordering nature reserves face escalating tensions due to a dispute over seized goats and alleged assaults.

DR Congo launches cholera vaccination campaign for 1.5 million

The vaccination drive in Équateur province aims to curb the spread of the disease which has caused 18 deaths.

Health authorities in the Democratic Republic of Congo launched a large-scale cholera vaccination campaign on Friday, November 6, in Mbandaka. This first phase targets nearly 1.5 million people in nine health zones within Équateur province that are severely affected by the epidemic.

The campaign, which runs until November 12, seeks to vaccinate individuals aged one year and older. Since the outbreak began, the province has recorded 142 cholera cases and 18 fatalities. The official launch ceremony took place at the Mbandaka 2 market, presided over by Body Robert Ilonga Bompoko, secretary-general at the Ministry of Health.

Interim provincial governor Thomas Boyenge Ifaso urged local authorities, community leaders, and health partners to support the vaccination teams. He also expressed hope that future vaccination phases would extend to other affected health zones.

Why it mattersNearly 1.5 million people in DR Congo's Équateur province are being vaccinated against cholera, a disease that has already caused 18 deaths.

Business · 2026-11-07

DR Congo rights group demands crackdown on mining mafias

Justicia ASBL wants the government to pursue criminal networks beyond illegal miners and seized ore.

The human rights organization JUSTICIA ASBL is urging the Congolese government to target the masterminds behind illegal mining in the Haut-Katanga and Lualaba provinces. The group acknowledged government actions, such as arresting illegal miners and seizing mineral shipments, but stated these measures do not address the core issue.

JUSTICIA ASBL spokesperson stated that the current arrests only capture low-level operators, leaving the larger criminal organizations untouched. The organization insists that dismantling these mafias is crucial to truly combat mining fraud and ensure fair resource management in the region.

The government's ongoing efforts to curb illegal mining are being closely watched by rights groups. JUSTICIA ASBL plans to continue advocating for comprehensive investigations into the full scope of mining fraud networks operating in the affected provinces.

Why it mattersThe people of Haut-Katanga and Lualaba provinces are directly affected by illegal mining and the associated criminal networks.
