ADF attack sparks Ebola spread fears in North Kivu

Suspected Ebola patients and high-risk contacts fled a health center after rebels attacked Cantine, raising alarms.

Rebels from the Allied Democratic Forces (ADF) attacked the Cantine area in the Beni territory of North Kivu, DR Congo. The attack caused suspected Ebola patients and high-risk contacts to flee the Kelele health center in Cantine. The Centers for African Disease Control and Prevention (Africa CDC) condemned the attack and warned of potential Ebola spread.

The flight of patients occurred overnight from Thursday to Friday. The director of the Kelele health facility expressed concern that the virus could spread within the community. Africa CDC has issued an alert regarding the risks associated with this situation.

The attack and subsequent patient displacement raise fears of a wider outbreak. Health authorities are monitoring the situation closely following the incident in Beni.

Kinshasa deploys mixed brigade to ease traffic

A mixed brigade will regulate traffic on main roads starting Monday, November 9, to enforce circulation rules.

Kinshasa authorities announced measures to improve road traffic in the capital. Starting Monday, November 9, a mixed brigade will be deployed on main routes to regulate traffic. This unit will also ensure respect for circulation restrictions.

The mixed brigade's deployment aims to improve traffic flow and ensure adherence to traffic rules. Officials stated the goal is to enhance the movement of vehicles and people within the city.

The measures are intended to make daily commutes more predictable for residents.

Why it mattersResidents of Kinshasa will experience regulated traffic flow and enforcement of circulation rules on main roads.

Business · 2026-11-09

DR Congo and US to speed up strategic partnership

The two nations aim to boost investments in critical minerals, infrastructure, and energy sectors.

The Democratic Republic of Congo and the United States plan to accelerate their strategic partnership, focusing on critical minerals, infrastructure, and energy. This commitment was announced after the second meeting of their Joint Steering Committee on November 5 in Paris.

This meeting followed a strategic partnership agreement signed on December 4, 2025, and the first committee meeting on February 5, 2026. Both governments aim to translate commitments into concrete actions with clear responsibilities and measurable outcomes, particularly in developing secure and diversified critical mineral supply chains.

The partnership also seeks to move beyond raw material extraction, promoting local processing, refining, advanced manufacturing, and technology transfer. The Sakania-Lobito corridor was highlighted as a priority for boosting regional trade and investment, encouraging US companies to invest while emphasizing transparency and environmental standards.

Why it mattersThe Democratic Republic of Congo and the United States are working to increase investments in critical minerals, infrastructure, and energy, aiming for local processing and job creation.

Sport · 2026-11-09

Céleste FC beats Aigles du Congo 2-0

FC Céleste took sole leadership of Group B in the Illicocash Ligue 1 with a victory on Sunday, November 8.

FC Céleste defeated FC Les Aigles du Congo 2-0 on Sunday, November 8, at the Tata Raphaël stadium in Kinshasa. The win secured FC Céleste's sole leadership of Group B in the Illicocash Ligue 1.

The visitors scored an own goal in the 9th minute through Helton Kayembe of Aigles du Congo, giving Céleste the lead. Nzanga Malamu extended Céleste's advantage with a second goal in the 12th minute. Aigles du Congo attempted to reduce the deficit but failed before the match concluded.

FC Céleste now leads Group B with 11 points from five matches, holding a +4 goal difference. In another match, AF Ufalya Simba surprised by beating FC Renaissance du Congo 1-0, leaving Renaissance with 4 points from three games.

Why it mattersFootball fans in the DR Congo will be interested in the league standings and team performance.

Sport · 2026-11-09

Stéphanie Nsimba Manzanza wins RDC's first Olympic medal

The Congolese wrestler secured a bronze medal in beach wrestling at the Dakar 2026 Youth Olympic Games.

Stéphanie Nsimba Manzanza made history for the Democratic Republic of Congo on Saturday, November 7, at the Dakar 2026 Youth Olympic Games by winning the country's first Olympic medal.

Competing in beach wrestling in the under-55kg category, Nsimba Manzanza earned a bronze medal after defeating Azerbaijan's Gunel Karimli.

This historic achievement marks a significant moment for Congolese sport, with Nsimba Manzanza already a double gold medalist at the African Wrestling Championships.

Why it mattersThe Democratic Republic of Congo's athletes have achieved their first Olympic medal, bringing national pride.
