JB Mpiana returns to Paris's Accor Arena for 40-year career concert

Congolese music star Jean Bedel Mpiana Tshituka was scheduled to perform at the Accor Arena in Paris on Saturday, 14 November, marking 40 years in music.

Source: Radio Okapi · 2026-11-15 · 1 min read

Jean Bedel Mpiana Tshituka, leader of the band Wenge BCBG and known to fans as "Papa Chéri", was due to perform at the Accor Arena in Paris on Saturday, 14 November. The venue, formerly known as Bercy, was the site of his last show there 25 years earlier.

In an interview with Radio Okapi from Paris, Mpiana said he was happy to return to the stage and thanked his fans for their support. He said the preparations had gone well, crediting several years of hard work. He was to share the stage with former collaborators, and singer Deborah Ntoya was to pay tribute to his idol and mentor, the late Papa Wemba.

Mpiana urged fellow musicians to pursue excellence and love of their work, and called on Congolese people to put love of their homeland first.

Why it mattersCongolese fans in Paris were due to see a concert marking four decades of one of the country's best-known musicians.
