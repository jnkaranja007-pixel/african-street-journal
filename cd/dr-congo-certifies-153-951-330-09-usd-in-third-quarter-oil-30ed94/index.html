DR Congo certifies 153.951.330,09 USD in third-quarter oil claims

The Government says it will pay the certified claims and provide fourth-quarter advances as it keeps pump prices stable amid rising world oil prices.

Sources: Radio Okapi and Eco News RDC · 2026-11-20 · 1 min read

DR Congo’s Petroleum Products Price Monitoring Committee certified 153.951.330,09 USD in petroleum companies’ losses and shortfalls for the third quarter of 2026. The work took place on Tuesday and Wednesday, 18 November, and covered the country’s four supply zones.

The committee certified 14.497.027,71 USD for the South zone and 139.454.302,38 USD for the West and North zones, according to Radio Okapi. The East zone was also included in the assessment. Economy Minister Daniel Mukoko Samba said the Government intended to pay the certified claims as soon as possible.

The Government also intends to provide advances for the fourth quarter of 2026. The certification came as international petroleum prices rose amid the crisis in the Middle East; the Government said it was continuing its policy of stabilising pump prices to protect household purchasing power.

Why it mattersPetroleum companies are awaiting payment of their certified claims and fourth-quarter advances, which the Government says it intends to provide as soon as possible.
