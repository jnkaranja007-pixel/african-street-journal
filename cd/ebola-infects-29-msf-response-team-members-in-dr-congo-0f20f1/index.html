Ebola infects 29 MSF response team members in DR Congo

Three members of the teams have died since the country’s 17th Ebola epidemic began, according to Actualite.cd.

Source: Actualite.cd · 2026-11-27 · 1 min read

29 members of teams working for Médecins Sans Frontières (MSF) in the Democratic Republic of Congo have been infected with Ebola during the country’s 17th outbreak, Actualite.cd reported Friday.

The report said three MSF team members have died since the epidemic began. It described the infected workers as part of the health response to the outbreak.

The report did not give the date the epidemic began or further details about the affected workers.

Why it mattersMSF workers engaged in the response face 29 infections and three deaths since the epidemic began.
