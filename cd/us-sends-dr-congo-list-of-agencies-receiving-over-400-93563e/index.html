US sends DR Congo list of agencies receiving over $400 million for Ebola

Washington has handed Kinshasa the names of the agencies that will receive its Ebola response funding, estimated at more than $400 million.

Source: Actualite.cd · 2026-11-16 · 1 min read

The United States has passed to the Congolese government a list of the agencies that will receive its contributions to the Ebola response in the Democratic Republic of Congo, which are estimated at more than $400 million.

The move follows warnings from Dr Jean Kaseya, director general of Africa CDC, the African Union's continental disease control agency, about how money meant for the Ebola response in the DRC was being managed.

The list is the first step in tracking where the money goes once it reaches the agencies responsible for the response.

Why it mattersAgencies named on the list will control how more than $400 million in U.S. money is spent on the Ebola response in the DRC.
