Kinshasa road crews start concrete and drainage work on Lumumba Boulevard

The Office of Roads and Drainage (OVD) began rehabilitation work on Thursday, November 12, in the communes of Limete and Kalamu.

Source: Radio Okapi · 2026-11-17 · 1 min read

Work to rehabilitate a section of Lumumba Boulevard and the Saint-Christophe and des Maçons avenues started on Thursday, November 12, in the Limete and Kalamu communes of Kinshasa.

The Office of Roads and Drainage (OVD), the state agency that builds and maintains the capital's roads and drains, is carrying out the concrete and drainage work. The project is scheduled to last 45 days.

Residents and drivers on these routes should expect the works to continue until the 45-day schedule is complete.

Why it mattersDrivers and residents on Lumumba Boulevard and the two avenues will face the works for the next 45 days.
