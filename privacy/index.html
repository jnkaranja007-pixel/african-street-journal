Privacy

We do not use cookies, we do not ask you to sign in, and we do not run advertising trackers.

Analytics

We count visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics. It sets no cookies, stores nothing on your device to identify you and builds no profile of you across sites. It reports totals only: pages viewed, referring sites, browsers and countries.

If your browser sends a Do Not Track signal, the analytics script does not load.

Hosting

GitHub Pages serves the site, and Cloudflare manages its domain. Like any host, they may keep short-lived server logs, including IP addresses, for security and reliability. Our typefaces come from Google Fonts, which sees your IP address when it delivers them. See the GitHub privacy statement.

On your device

The site keeps a copy of its pages in your browser so that it opens quickly and works offline. That copy never leaves your device.

Your settings, such as the countries you follow and light or dark mode, are stored the same way and are never sent to us.

If a page hits an error, the site records the last few on your device so that "Report a problem" in the About menu can include them. Nothing is sent unless you send that email yourself, and you can delete any of it first.

Email

If you write to us, we use your address only to reply. We do not run a mailing list.
