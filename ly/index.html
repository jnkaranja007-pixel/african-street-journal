17 Italian companies to exhibit at Libya Construction Expo

The Italian Pavilion will feature companies in construction machinery, engineering, and furnishings at the Tripoli event.

17 Italian companies will exhibit at the Libya Construction Expo 2026 in Tripoli from November 8 to 11. The Italian Pavilion, organized by the Italian Trade Agency (ITA) Tripoli Office, will showcase products and services at the Tripoli International Fairgrounds.

Each of the 17 participating companies will have a 12 sq m booth within the 230 sq m Italian Pavilion. The firms operate in sectors including construction machinery, engineering, and furniture and design. An ITA institutional area will facilitate business-to-business meetings.

Libero Tessitore, Director of the ITA Tripoli Office, expressed pride in hosting the companies and highlighted the agency's role in promoting "Made in Italy" brands. Italy's Ambassador to Libya, Gianluca Alberini, stated that the Italian presence demonstrates how "Made in Italy" excellence can integrate into local development plans.

Why it mattersItalian products and services will be showcased for potential integration into Libyan commercial and residential projects.

French firms to join Libyan energy forum in Madrid

More than 25 French companies will attend the Libyan-European Energy Forum in Madrid on December 2-3.

Over 25 French companies, led by the energy sector association EVOLEN, will participate in the Libyan-European Forum for Oil, Energy and Gas in Madrid from December 2-3. Libya's General Union of Chambers of Commerce reported that EVOLEN represents professionals across oil, gas, and renewable energies.

The forum aims to strengthen Libyan-French and European cooperation in the energy sectors, focusing on technical support, investment opportunities, and expertise exchange. The General Union stated this event will enhance communication between Libyan and French institutions and companies.

EVOLEN's participation signals French companies' interest in the Libyan market to support its oil, gas, and energy sector development. The forum is expected to open new cooperation horizons and foster economic and investment partnerships.

Why it mattersLibyan institutions and companies will engage with French counterparts to explore cooperation and investment in the energy sector.

UN envoy meets UK minister on Libya developments

Hanna Tetteh and Stephen Doughty discussed political and economic progress in Libya during their London meeting.

Hanna Tetteh, the UN's acting special representative for Libya, met Stephen Doughty, the UK Minister of State for the Middle East and North Africa, in London on Tuesday. The officials discussed recent political and economic developments within Libya.

The meeting, held in London, focused on the current situation in Libya. Both Tetteh and Doughty emphasized their shared concerns regarding the country's progress.

The UN Support Mission in Libya, led by Tetteh, continues to engage with international partners on the ground. This discussion with the UK minister highlights ongoing diplomatic efforts concerning Libya's future.

Why it mattersLibya's political and economic developments are discussed by a UN envoy and a UK minister.

Libya detains 20 truck owners over essential goods

Law enforcement seized trucks and referred drivers to financial crimes agency for lacking official invoices.

Libya's Law Enforcement Department arrested 20 truck owners yesterday for allegedly transporting imported essential goods without proper invoices. The operation, directed by the Government of National Unity's Prime Minister, aimed to increase oversight on food supplies and combat smuggling.

The detained individuals are suspected of moving essential food items, imported via letters of credit, without documentation proving their legitimacy or origin. These arrests occurred during security patrols in Tripoli's suburbs, the Jebel region, and Shwerif.

Legal actions have been initiated against the drivers, who have been sent with their documentation to the Agency for Combating Financial Crimes. The trucks involved have been impounded as part of efforts to verify the movement of goods and prevent market disruption.

Why it matters20 truck owners were detained and their vehicles impounded for transporting essential goods without official invoices.

National Development Agency starts Sabha road works

Construction of a dual carriageway and paving of Martyrs’ Square begin in Sabha.

The National Development Agency has started construction of a dual carriageway in Sabha. The new road runs next to Tarek Bin Ziyad School, opposite the entrance to the main road in the Sabha University administration area. This project is part of ongoing urban development efforts in the city.

The agency is also paving and rehabilitating Martyrs’ Square as part of the same urban development initiative. The specific details of the paving and rehabilitation works were not immediately available.

These works are intended to improve infrastructure within Sabha. The National Development Agency is responsible for overseeing various development projects across the country.

Why it mattersLibyan citizens in Sabha will experience road improvements and a renovated public square.
