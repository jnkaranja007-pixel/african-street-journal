Agriculture · 2026-11-14

Libya seeks Islamic Development Bank funding for water and farm projects

Libyan ministers held talks with the bank's director general at the COP31 climate conference in Antalya, Turkey, on financing environmental, farming and water projects.

A Libyan government delegation met the Director General of the Islamic Development Bank at the COP31 climate conference in Antalya, Turkey, to discuss how to finance and carry out projects in the environmental, agricultural and water resource sectors. The stated aim is to strengthen food and water security in Libya.

The Ministers of Agriculture and Water Resources attended, along with an adviser from the Ministry of Environment and the Director General of the Government Communication Centre. Both sides reviewed Libya's sector priorities and the funding and technical support the proposed projects require.

The meeting also covered stronger partnerships with international institutions and faster implementation of national environment and climate strategies, including projects linked to sustainable development.

Why it mattersFarmers and water users would benefit if the proposed projects secure the funding and technical support Libya has asked the bank for.

Business · 2026-11-14

Libyan Economic Forum ends with growth recommendations

The two-day forum in Tripoli focused on investment opportunities and improving the business environment.

The Permanent Libyan Economic Forum, held under the banner 'Invest in Libya', concluded its two-day deliberations on November 12 in Tripoli. The event brought together government officials, policymakers, experts, and business leaders to discuss economic improvement.

Organized by the General Union of Chambers and supervised by the National Economic and Social Development Board, the forum featured panel discussions on investment opportunities and enhancing the business environment. Numerous development projects and strategic visions were presented to activate economic partnerships.

The forum adopted final recommendations aimed at translating dialogue outcomes into practical steps to support the national economy's stability and growth. These recommendations will guide future economic development efforts in Libya.

Why it mattersThe forum adopted final recommendations aimed at supporting the stability and growth of Libya's national economy.

Climate · 2026-11-14

Libya presents climate plans at COP31

Minister Mohamed Sidi Ibrahim presented national documents on mitigation and adaptation in Antalya, Turkey.

Libya's Minister of Environment, Mohamed Sidi Ibrahim, presented the country's climate change mitigation and adaptation plans at the 31st Conference of the Parties (COP31) in Antalya, Turkey, on Friday. The presentation included Libya's First National Communication, National Adaptation Plan, and Nationally Determined Contributions (NDC) document.

The documents were prepared by the Ministry of Environment with support from the United Nations Development Programme (UNDP) and the Deutsche Gesellschaft für Internationale Zusammenarbeit (GIZ). Minister Ibrahim stressed the need to move from planning to implementing practical projects and called for coordinated efforts among state institutions and stronger international cooperation.

The conference in Turkey runs from November 9 to 20. Minister of Oil and Gas, Khalifa Abdel-Sadig, and Minister of Water Resources, Hosni Owaidan, were also present at the presentation alongside UN and international officials.

Why it mattersLibya's Minister of Environment must coordinate state institutions and strengthen international cooperation to mitigate climate-related repercussions.

Business · 2026-11-14

Libya ministry sponsors entrepreneurship week

Global Entrepreneurship Week will run from November 16 to 22 across three Libyan cities.

Libya's Ministry of Youth announced its sponsorship of Global Entrepreneurship Week, an event scheduled to take place from November 16 to 22.

The ministry stated that activities will occur in three Libyan cities with the aim of connecting students with the professional world and turning ideas into economic contributions.

The week includes a Libya Hackathon 2026, where participants can build an idea in two days and present it to decisionmakers.

Why it mattersStudents can present their ideas to decisionmakers during Global Entrepreneurship Week, which runs from November 16 to 22.
