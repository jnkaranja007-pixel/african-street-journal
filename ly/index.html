Libya reviews rail projects with Russia and China

Transportation Ministry discusses resuming operations and a potential monorail for Tripoli.

Libya's Ministry of Transportation met today in Tripoli to review the status of rail projects with Russian and Chinese partners. Minister Mohamed Al-Shahoubi discussed ongoing dialogues to explore resuming operations with companies from both nations.

The meeting included officials from Libyan Railroads who reviewed executive and technical project details. They also considered a proposal for a monorail system in Tripoli, seen as a modern solution to traffic congestion and a way to improve student transport.

Libyan Railroads will submit a detailed report on the discussions to finalize an action plan. The monorail project aims to provide an efficient public transport system for the capital city.

Why it mattersLibyans will benefit from improved public transport and reduced traffic congestion in Tripoli.

Saddam Haftar condemns Trump remarks on Libya

General Haftar calls for a formal diplomatic protest to the United States over offensive statements.

General Saddam Haftar condemned remarks by U.S. President Donald Trump about Libya, calling them offensive and unacceptable. Haftar stated that domestic political debates do not justify insulting nations or their people.

Haftar reiterated Libya's commitment to friendly relations with the U.S. but stressed that mutual respect is the foundation for ties with all nations.

Libya shapes national blue economy strategy

A workshop in Benghazi reviewed findings and explored priorities for sustainable economic growth along the coast.

National institutions, universities, businesses, and civil society met in Benghazi on October 18 to help shape Libya’s first National Blue Economy Strategy. The workshop, co-organized by UNDP Libya and the National Blue Economy Committee, aimed to unlock the potential of Libya’s coastline for job creation and sustainable economic growth.

Discussions covered seven pillars, including water resources and wastewater management, emphasizing economic diversification, employment, environmental sustainability, biodiversity, and climate resilience. The United Nations Development Programme reported that the workshop outcomes will inform the draft strategy.

The strategy is being developed with support from the Government of Italy, with national partners working towards its adoption in early 2027. The UNDP stated that Libya’s coastline offers opportunities to strengthen coastal livelihoods and support sustainable economic growth while protecting marine ecosystems.

Why it mattersLibyans can expect new jobs and economic growth opportunities along the coast through the development of a national blue economy strategy.

Libya faces moderate to heavy rainfall

Autumn conditions bring intermittent rain to northern and southwestern Libya, with warnings of flooded valleys.

Moderate to heavy rainfall is expected across northern and southwestern Libya starting Sunday, October 25, 2026. Weather forecasts indicate autumn conditions with intermittent rain for the next two days. Coastal and inland areas, including the Western Mountains and Green Mountain regions, will experience varying rainfall levels.

Forecasters have issued warnings for potential flooded valleys due to the anticipated downpours. The heaviest rain is specifically forecast for these regions. Residents are advised to prepare for the changing weather patterns.

The forecast covers Sunday, October 25, 2026, and the subsequent two days. This period is expected to bring significant autumn weather to the northern parts of the country.

Why it mattersLibya's northern and southwestern regions will experience moderate to heavy rainfall and potential flooding.

New CBL governor meets department directors

Fawzi Abu Khazam held his first meeting to strengthen institutional operations and enhance performance.

Fawzi Abu Khazam, the new interim Governor of the Central Bank of Libya (CBL), held his first meeting with department directors at his office today. The meeting aimed to strengthen institutional operations and integrate efforts to enhance the CBL's overall performance.

Abu Khazam welcomed the directors and commended the efforts of outgoing Governor Naji Issa and the CBL staff. He stressed the necessity of continuing to work with a unified team spirit, ensuring efficiency and professionalism in carrying out assigned tasks.

The governor emphasized bolstering coordination and cooperation across departments to improve performance and support the CBL's advancement. This meeting reflects his commitment to direct communication and aligning efforts to serve the CBL's interests and institutional progress.
