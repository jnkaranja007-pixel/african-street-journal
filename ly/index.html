Libya central bank governor meets French ambassador

Fawzi Abukhazam and Thierry Vallat discussed cooperation prospects between Libya and France.

Fawzi Abukhazam, the Governor of the Central Bank of Libya, met with French Ambassador to Libya Thierry Vallat on Sunday in Tripoli. The officials discussed several common issues and prospects for cooperation between Libya and France.

Ambassador Vallat congratulated Abukhazam on his recent appointment as the Central Bank governor. He also conveyed his wishes for success in Abukhazam's new role.

The meeting focused on strengthening the relationship and exploring potential areas of collaboration between the two nations' financial institutions.

Why it mattersThe meeting between the Central Bank of Libya governor and the French ambassador signals potential future financial cooperation between the two countries.

Libya authorizes two PhD programs at University of Benghazi

The Ministry of Higher Education and Scientific Research has cleared the university to offer doctoral studies in two education fields.

The Ministry of Higher Education and Scientific Research has authorized the University of Benghazi to launch two new PhD programs. The doctoral courses cover Curriculum and Teaching Methods and Special Education, and will be run at the Faculty of Education in Al-Marj.

The ministry's go-ahead followed a review and approval by its General Committee for Graduate Studies, which cleared the university to begin implementing the programs. The authorization lets the university start enrolling doctoral students in these specialised fields.

Libyan students who want advanced qualifications in teaching and special education can now study for them at a Benghazi-based university, rather than travelling for doctoral training.

Why it mattersLibyan students seeking doctoral training in teaching and special education can now study for it at the University of Benghazi.

Health · 2026-11-11

Sabha mayor clears contractor to renovate sanitation lab

The municipal environmental sanitation laboratory in Sebha is being renovated so it can reopen and resume monitoring work.

Sebha Municipality has authorised a contractor to begin renovating the municipal environmental sanitation laboratory, according to the mayor, Ahfaf al-Aswad.

The work is meant to get the facility ready to reopen and to restart its monitoring and technical functions. The municipal council describes the renovation as part of its push to tighten health and environmental oversight in the city.

The packet does not give a timeline for completion or a budget for the project.

Why it mattersResidents of Sebha will depend on the laboratory's monitoring work once it reopens, and the packet gives no completion date.
