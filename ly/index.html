Libyan health center distributes 300 solar refrigerators

The National Centre for Disease Control distributed more than 300 solar-powered refrigerators. The move follows reports of medical supplies perishing due to summer power cuts.

Why it mattersThe equipment prevents the loss of temperature-sensitive medical supplies during blackouts.
