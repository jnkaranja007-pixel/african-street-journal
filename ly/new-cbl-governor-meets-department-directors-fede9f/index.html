New CBL governor meets department directors

Fawzi Abu Khazam held his first meeting to strengthen institutional operations and enhance performance.

Source: Libya Herald · 2026-10-26 · 1 min read

Fawzi Abu Khazam, the new interim Governor of the Central Bank of Libya (CBL), held his first meeting with department directors at his office today. The meeting aimed to strengthen institutional operations and integrate efforts to enhance the CBL's overall performance.

Abu Khazam welcomed the directors and commended the efforts of outgoing Governor Naji Issa and the CBL staff. He stressed the necessity of continuing to work with a unified team spirit, ensuring efficiency and professionalism in carrying out assigned tasks.

The governor emphasized bolstering coordination and cooperation across departments to improve performance and support the CBL's advancement. This meeting reflects his commitment to direct communication and aligning efforts to serve the CBL's interests and institutional progress.
