Libya's NOC reviews Southern Refinery project progress

The National Oil Corporation met with Zallaf Libya to track completion stages and discuss challenges for 2027 targets.

Source: Libya Herald · 2026-10-24 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Libya's National Oil Corporation (NOC) and Zallaf Libya Oil & Gas Co. met yesterday in Sebha to review the Southern Refinery project's progress. Officials discussed completion stages, technical challenges, and ways to accelerate work towards 2027 targets.

The meeting, held at Zallaf Libya's headquarters, assessed the project's current status, implementation phases, and financial requirements. Discussions also covered fuel storage and distribution systems to integrate the refinery into the national oil sector.

The project aims to stabilize domestic fuel supplies, reduce import reliance, and bolster energy security. Representatives from the NOC's General Directorate for Oil Industries and Zallaf Libya's board attended the review.

Why it mattersLibya's energy security and domestic fuel supply are directly affected by the Southern Refinery project.
