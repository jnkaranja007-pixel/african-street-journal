German ambassador discusses trade with Misrata Chamber

Libyan businesspeople could see easier visa access as Germany offers expertise.

Source: Libya Herald · 2026-11-05 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

The German Ambassador to Libya, Arvid Anders, met with the Misrata Chamber of Commerce today at its Misrata headquarters. He was accompanied by Dominik Lauf-Wilhelm, First Secretary for Political Affairs. The Chamber's Chairman, Fathi Al-Amin Al-Turki, and Yousef Abu Shaiba, Director of the Chamber’s Office of External Relations, welcomed the delegation.

Discussions focused on strengthening economic and commercial cooperation between Libya and Germany. The parties explored ways to use German expertise in various economic and industrial sectors. They also addressed facilitating visa procedures for Libyan businesspeople and resolving challenges faced by Chamber members.

Al-Turki highlighted the importance of increasing cooperation and opening new channels for communication and partnership between the business communities of both nations. The meeting aimed to foster broader avenues for collaboration.
