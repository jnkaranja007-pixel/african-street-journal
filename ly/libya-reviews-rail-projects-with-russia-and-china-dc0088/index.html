Libya reviews rail projects with Russia and China

Transportation Ministry discusses resuming operations and a potential monorail for Tripoli.

Source: Libya Herald · 2026-10-26 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Libya's Ministry of Transportation met today in Tripoli to review the status of rail projects with Russian and Chinese partners. Minister Mohamed Al-Shahoubi discussed ongoing dialogues to explore resuming operations with companies from both nations.

The meeting included officials from Libyan Railroads who reviewed executive and technical project details. They also considered a proposal for a monorail system in Tripoli, seen as a modern solution to traffic congestion and a way to improve student transport.

Libyan Railroads will submit a detailed report on the discussions to finalize an action plan. The monorail project aims to provide an efficient public transport system for the capital city.

Why it mattersLibyans will benefit from improved public transport and reduced traffic congestion in Tripoli.
