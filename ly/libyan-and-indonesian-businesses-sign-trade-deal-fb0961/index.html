Libyan and Indonesian businesses sign trade deal

A cooperation agreement signed in Semarang aims to boost direct trade and commercial ties between the two nations.

Source: Libya Herald · 2026-10-23 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Libyan and Indonesian business leaders signed a cooperation agreement on October 20 in Semarang, Indonesia, to facilitate trade between their countries. The deal aims to foster direct cooperation between private-sector companies from Libya and Indonesia.

The agreement follows a Libyan Business Forum held on October 16 in Jakarta, where 12 letters of intent for commercial deals worth US$1.8 million were signed. Bilateral B2B meetings were also held to explore export and import opportunities.

The Libyan Business Council is now working with Indonesian economic entities to develop trade exchange opportunities and convert the letters of intent into actionable commercial agreements. The Central Java region, where Semarang is located, is an industrial and export hub.

Why it mattersLibyan businesses will benefit from new trade opportunities and commercial agreements with Indonesian companies.
