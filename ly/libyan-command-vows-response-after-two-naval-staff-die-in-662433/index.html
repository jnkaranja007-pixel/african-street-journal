Libyan command vows response after two naval staff die in Benghazi attack

The General Command said the officers died from injuries sustained in an attack on Benghazi Port and explosions involving the targeted vessel.

Sources: Libya Herald and Libya Update · 2026-11-25 · 1 min read

Libya’s eastern-based General Command of the Libyan Arab Armed Forces said two members of its Naval Forces General Staff died after an attack on Benghazi Port. Chief Warrant Officer Tahir Abdullah Al-Sadiq and Lieutenant Ali Muhammad Al-Said succumbed to injuries sustained in the attack and subsequent explosions.

The command said the attack targeted a vessel at the port and pledged to seek justice for the two officers. It also said it would respond at a time and place of its choosing, without specifying a date or action.

Ukraine claimed responsibility for drone and unmanned sea vehicle attacks on Russian ships off the Benghazi coast and in the port early Monday, according to Libya Herald. The command said wounded people were recovering.

Why it mattersThe General Command must now decide how to carry out its pledge to seek justice for the two officers.
