Libya speaker discusses economic plan

Salehin Abdel Nabi met with the head of the National Planning Council to discuss reducing public spending.

Source: Libya Update · 2026-10-24 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Speaker of the House of Representatives Salehin Abdel Nabi met Thursday in Benghazi with Ahmed Khalifa Breidan, head of the National Planning Council. They discussed an emergency economic plan for Libya intended to reduce public spending and safeguard public funds.

The officials also considered revising the exchange rate of the US dollar against the Libyan dinar. This meeting focused on protecting public funds through spending cuts.

The National Planning Council will hold a meeting of experts within 10 days to further discuss the economic plan.

Why it mattersLibyan officials are discussing an economic plan to reduce public spending and adjust the exchange rate.
