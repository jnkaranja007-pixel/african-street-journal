Libya beats Ghana 1-0 in under-23 Africa Cup qualifier

Ghana assistant coach Baba Nuho Malam said his team respects Libya’s qualification ambitions but is not afraid of the hosts.

Source: Libya Update · 2026-11-19 · 1 min read

Libya beat Ghana 1-0 in an under-23 Africa Cup of Nations qualifier at Benina Martyrs Stadium in Benghazi on November 11. The result came against Ghana’s Black Meteors, the country’s under-23 national team.

Baba Nuho Malam, Ghana’s assistant coach, told the Ghana Football Association’s media centre that his players performed well but conceded at an unlucky moment. He said the team accepted the result, despite dissatisfaction with some refereeing decisions, and did not want to discuss them further.

Malam said a one-goal loss left Ghana’s players believing they could change the outcome. He said Libya should not be underestimated as it also seeks qualification, while insisting that respect for the hosts did not mean Ghana feared them.

Why it mattersGhana’s players are seeking to overturn the one-goal deficit, while Libya is also pursuing qualification.
