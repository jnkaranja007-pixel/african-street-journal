Libya braces for Mediterranean storm Agnetta

The storm is centered south of Italy and moving east with active winds and low pressure.

Source: Libya Update · 2026-11-03 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Libya is preparing for the impact of Mediterranean storm Agnetta, which is currently affecting central and eastern regions of the Mediterranean. The storm is located south of Italy and is characterized by low atmospheric pressure and strong winds around its core.

Fares Fathi, Director of the Geographic and Environmental Systems, stated that the storm is gradually moving eastward. The unstable weather conditions are developing across the region.

The storm's progression indicates potential weather changes for Libya as it moves closer.
