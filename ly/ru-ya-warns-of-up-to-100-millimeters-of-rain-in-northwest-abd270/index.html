Ru’ya warns of up to 100 millimeters of rain in northwest Libya

The foundation says the heaviest downpours are expected around Tripoli and along the coast, with flash floods and road disruption possible.

Source: Libya Update · 2026-11-28 · 1 min read

The Ru’ya Foundation for Space Sciences and Its Applications warned Thursday evening that a cold front would bring thunderstorms and heavy rain to northwest Libya from Thursday night into early Friday. Rainfall is forecast at 20 to 60 millimeters, with local totals approaching 100 millimeters, especially in some coastal areas.

The foundation’s bulletin identifies greater Tripoli and the coast from Sabratha to Khoms among the areas expecting the heaviest rain. It also lists south of Tripoli, parts of the Jafara Plain and eastern parts of the Western Mountains, including Gharyan and Tarhuna. Most rain could fall within a few hours, the bulletin says.

The foundation warned of flash floods in valleys and mountain streams, water accumulation in low-lying and coastal areas, reduced visibility and possible road closures. It urged residents to avoid flood-prone areas and called on authorities and emergency services to raise their readiness.

Why it mattersDrivers and residents in low-lying and coastal areas face possible road closures and flash floods as local rainfall totals approach 100 millimeters.
