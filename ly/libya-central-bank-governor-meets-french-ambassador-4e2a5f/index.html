Libya central bank governor meets French ambassador

Fawzi Abukhazam and Thierry Vallat discussed cooperation prospects between Libya and France.

Source: Libya Update · 2026-11-11 · 1 min read

Fawzi Abukhazam, the Governor of the Central Bank of Libya, met with French Ambassador to Libya Thierry Vallat on Sunday in Tripoli. The officials discussed several common issues and prospects for cooperation between Libya and France.

Ambassador Vallat congratulated Abukhazam on his recent appointment as the Central Bank governor. He also conveyed his wishes for success in Abukhazam's new role.

The meeting focused on strengthening the relationship and exploring potential areas of collaboration between the two nations' financial institutions.

Why it mattersThe meeting between the Central Bank of Libya governor and the French ambassador signals potential future financial cooperation between the two countries.
