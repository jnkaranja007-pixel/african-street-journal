Libya presents climate plans at COP31

Minister Mohamed Sidi Ibrahim presented national documents on mitigation and adaptation in Antalya, Turkey.

Source: Libya Herald · 2026-11-14 · 1 min read

Libya's Minister of Environment, Mohamed Sidi Ibrahim, presented the country's climate change mitigation and adaptation plans at the 31st Conference of the Parties (COP31) in Antalya, Turkey, on Friday. The presentation included Libya's First National Communication, National Adaptation Plan, and Nationally Determined Contributions (NDC) document.

The documents were prepared by the Ministry of Environment with support from the United Nations Development Programme (UNDP) and the Deutsche Gesellschaft für Internationale Zusammenarbeit (GIZ). Minister Ibrahim stressed the need to move from planning to implementing practical projects and called for coordinated efforts among state institutions and stronger international cooperation.

The conference in Turkey runs from November 9 to 20. Minister of Oil and Gas, Khalifa Abdel-Sadig, and Minister of Water Resources, Hosni Owaidan, were also present at the presentation alongside UN and international officials.

Why it mattersLibya's Minister of Environment must coordinate state institutions and strengthen international cooperation to mitigate climate-related repercussions.
