Libya food center attends quality compliance workshop

The Food and Drug Control Center joined regulatory agencies at the Oil Research Center for a workshop on product releases.

Source: Libya Update · 2026-10-29 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

The head of the Food Monitoring Department represented the Food and Drug Control Center at a workshop in Tripoli. The event, titled “Releases and Quality Compliance: Challenges and Solutions,” was organized by the Oil Research Center. Several regulatory and technical agencies participated.

The Food and Drug Control Center's representative attended the workshop. This meeting brought together various official bodies to discuss product releases and quality compliance.

The Food Monitoring Department's participation signifies the center's engagement with other agencies on standards.

Why it mattersLibyan regulatory agencies met to discuss challenges and solutions for product releases and quality compliance.
