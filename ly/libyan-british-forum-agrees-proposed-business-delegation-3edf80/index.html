Libyan-British forum agrees proposed business delegation visit to Libya

The London meeting also offered Libyan companies help registering in the UK and discussed a British firm’s proposed aluminium project with a local investor.

Source: Libya Herald · 2026-11-25 · 1 min read

The Libyan British Business Forum in London brought together companies and business leaders from Libya and the United Kingdom to discuss trade and investment cooperation. The newly launched Joint Libyan-British Business Council organized the event with the West London Chamber of Commerce.

The Libyan Business Council (LBC) proposed an exploratory visit to Libya by British companies and business leaders, with meetings planned with Libyan companies and investment authorities. The LBC said the visit would show delegates investment opportunities and existing projects. The West London chamber offered LBC-member companies help registering in the UK and the chance to join its membership.

Discussions also covered a British iron and steel firm’s interest in an aluminium-production project with a local investor. The LBC said the proposed visit would focus on oil and gas, renewable energy, real estate, industry, technology and professional training.

Why it mattersLBC-member companies seeking UK entry were offered registration assistance and access to the West London chamber’s business network.
