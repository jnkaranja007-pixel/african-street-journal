Libya's investment authority and state oil company sign deal with Oman's OQ partner

The agreement, signed in Tripoli, starts preliminary legal steps to explore oil and gas investment opportunities among the three parties.

Source: Libya Herald · 2026-11-27 · 1 min read

Libya's Investment Authority (LIA), Oman's OQ Exploration and Production (OQEP) and the National Oil Corporation (NOC) signed an agreement in Tripoli on November 24 to explore investment opportunities in the oil and gas sector. The signing took place during the LIBYES 2026 event, held from November 24 to 26.

The LIA said the agreement begins preliminary legal procedures for work with the National Oil Corporation. It sets up a framework for cooperation and the exchange of expertise, drawing on the parties' technical and investment capabilities.

The agreement implements a memorandum of understanding between the LIA and Oman's OQ group signed earlier this year. The LIA said it aims to grow the value of its assets through partnerships that benefit Libya's economy.

Why it mattersThe LIA, OQEP and the National Oil Corporation must start preliminary legal procedures before exploring oil and gas investment opportunities.
