French firms to join Libyan energy forum in Madrid

More than 25 French companies will attend the Libyan-European Energy Forum in Madrid on December 2-3.

Source: Libya Herald · 2026-10-28 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Over 25 French companies, led by the energy sector association EVOLEN, will participate in the Libyan-European Forum for Oil, Energy and Gas in Madrid from December 2-3. Libya's General Union of Chambers of Commerce reported that EVOLEN represents professionals across oil, gas, and renewable energies.

The forum aims to strengthen Libyan-French and European cooperation in the energy sectors, focusing on technical support, investment opportunities, and expertise exchange. The General Union stated this event will enhance communication between Libyan and French institutions and companies.

EVOLEN's participation signals French companies' interest in the Libyan market to support its oil, gas, and energy sector development. The forum is expected to open new cooperation horizons and foster economic and investment partnerships.

Why it mattersLibyan institutions and companies will engage with French counterparts to explore cooperation and investment in the energy sector.
