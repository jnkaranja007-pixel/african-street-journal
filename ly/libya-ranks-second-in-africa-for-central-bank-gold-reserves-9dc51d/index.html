Libya ranks second in Africa for central bank gold reserves

Algeria leads the continent with 173.6 tonnes, and the top four holders together hold 575.8 tonnes, a World Gold Council tally cited in a 20 November report.

Source: Libyan Express · 2026-11-22 · 1 min read

Libya holds 146.7 tonnes of gold in its central bank reserves, placing it second in Africa behind Algeria's 173.6 tonnes, according to World Gold Council figures.

Egypt ranks third with 130 tonnes and South Africa fourth with 125.5 tonnes. Tanzania is fifth with 39.6 tonnes, a figure announced by its minerals minister. Libya has almost no industrial gold mining, so the Central Bank of Libya has built its stock through purchases, including 30 tonnes bought in 2023.

A bne IntelliNews report of 20 November put the four leading countries' combined holdings at 575.8 tonnes, more than 14 times Tanzania's reported figure.

Why it mattersBecause Libya has virtually no industrial gold mining, its reserves depend on central bank purchases, such as the 30 tonnes acquired in 2023.
