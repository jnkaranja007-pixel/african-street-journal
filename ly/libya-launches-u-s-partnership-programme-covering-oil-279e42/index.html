Libya launches U.S. partnership programme covering oil, energy and transport

The plan also includes mining, infrastructure investment and technology transfer, while the Libyan delegation is due to seek agreements and implementation timelines.

Source: Libya Herald · 2026-11-21 · 1 min read

Libya's Government of National Unity launched an executive programme for a strategic partnership with the United States in Washington, D.C., the government announced on Friday. It covers oil and gas production and exploration, electricity and renewable energy, aviation and air transport, and mining and strategic minerals.

The programme also covers infrastructure and development investment, technology transfer and national capacity building. The Libyan delegation includes Economy and Trade Minister Suhail Abu Shiha, National Oil Corporation Chairman Masoud Suleiman and Libyan Investment Authority Chairman Ali Mahmoud.

The delegation's Washington agenda includes signing several Libyan-American partnership agreements, approving implementation timelines and holding high-level meetings with 21 U.S. government departments and institutions. The government said the programme is meant to support the national economy and diversify sources of income.

Why it mattersLibyan state oil and investment bodies will sign agreements and set timelines with 21 U.S. departments and institutions, opening deals in oil, power and transport.
