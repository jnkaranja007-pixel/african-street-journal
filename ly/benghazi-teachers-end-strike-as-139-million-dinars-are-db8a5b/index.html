Benghazi teachers end strike as 139 million dinars are allocated

The funds cover salary differences and session-based teaching allowances, with the first payments scheduled to start on November 30.

Source: Libya Update · 2026-11-28 · 1 min read

Teachers in Benghazi, Libya, have ended their strike and the new academic year has begun after the city's education supervisor received two checks totalling 139 million dinars for outstanding salary differences and teaching allowances.

Education supervisor Belaid Jumaa said 92 million dinars will cover salary differences, with each teacher due 2,000 dinars; distribution is scheduled to begin on Monday, November 30. A further 47 million dinars is for session-based teachers' allowances, with payments expected to reach their accounts in the coming days.

Jumaa said a unified salary schedule is postponed to allow parliamentary committees to review it before it goes to the House of Representatives. Graduates are due to be appointed to teaching shortages in several subjects from mid-December.

Why it mattersBenghazi teachers are due to receive 2,000 dinars each in salary differences from November 30, while session-based teachers await allowance payments.
