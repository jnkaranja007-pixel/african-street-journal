Libya’s Audit Bureau reports 2025 deficit of 5,385,015,697 dinars

The 19-chapter report puts revenue at 165,683,644,617 dinars and expenditure at 171,068,660,314 dinars.

Source: Libya Herald · 2026-11-24 · 1 min read

Libya’s State Audit Bureau published its 2025 Annual Report on Monday, setting out a deficit of 5,385,015,697 Libyan dinars. It recorded revenue of 165,683,644,617 dinars and expenditure of 171,068,660,314 dinars.

The report spans 19 chapters and over 1,000 pages, with findings from examinations, audits and evaluations of more than 500 entities across state sectors. The Bureau said the entities had received its observations, and that it reviewed and analysed their responses.

The report says government spending on salaries rose to 73.3 billion dinars in 2025 from 33.2 billion dinars in 2021, an increase of approximately 120%. It also covers public finance, banking, oil, investment, state projects and healthcare.

Why it mattersThe more than 500 audited entities have received the Bureau’s findings, while salary spending reached 73.3 billion dinars in 2025.
