Libya's state oil firm reports 30,792,522 USD loss from Hamada pipeline closure

The National Oil Corporation says an armed group's shutdown of the pipeline has cut crude output by 302,778 barrels over more than 20 days.

Source: Libya Herald · 2026-11-22 · 1 min read

Libya's National Oil Corporation said the closure of the Hamada pipeline by an armed Libyan group has cost 302,778 barrels of crude oil, valued at 30,792,522 USD, over more than 20 days. It reported an average daily loss of 14,418 barrels, equal to 1,470,636 USD in revenue.

The shutdown affects the Al-Hamada fields, known as NC5 and NC8, and the Al-Tahara field, known as NC4, all operated by the Arabian Gulf Oil Company. The corporation said the losses are based on the average crude price and are in addition to fixed operating costs at those fields.

The corporation urged relevant authorities and citizens to work toward reopening the pipeline and to end attacks on oil facilities. It said the closure has also caused a decline in production.

Why it mattersAuthorities and citizens are being urged to help reopen the pipeline after its closure caused losses valued at 30,792,522 USD.
