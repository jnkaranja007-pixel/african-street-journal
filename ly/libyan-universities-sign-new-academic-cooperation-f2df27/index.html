Libyan universities sign new academic cooperation agreements with U.S.

Massad Boulos said the memorandums will expand educational cooperation and strengthen partnerships with the University of Benghazi and the University of Tripoli.

Source: Libya Herald · 2026-11-20 · 1 min read

The University of Benghazi and the University of Tripoli have signed new memorandums of understanding with the United States, Massad Boulos, the U.S. senior adviser for Arab and African affairs, said on Thursday.

Boulos said the frameworks are intended to strengthen academic partnerships, widen educational cooperation and invest in Libya’s next generation. He congratulated both universities on signing the agreements.

Boulos also described the memorandums as evidence of a growing U.S.-Libya relationship and a shared commitment to a unified, stable and prosperous Libya.

Why it mattersThe two universities’ new frameworks set out to widen educational cooperation and invest in Libya’s next generation.
