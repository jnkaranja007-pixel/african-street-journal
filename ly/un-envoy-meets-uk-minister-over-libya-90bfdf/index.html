UN envoy meets UK minister over Libya

Hanna Tetteh and Stephen Doughty discussed political and economic progress in Libya during their London meeting.

Source: Libya Update · 2026-10-29 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Hanna Tetteh, the UN's acting special representative for Libya, met with UK Minister Stephen Doughty in London on Tuesday. The officials discussed recent political and economic developments within Libya.

The meeting focused on the current situation in Libya, with both Tetteh and Doughty emphasizing specific points during their discussion. The UN Support Mission in Libya (UNSMIL) was represented by Tetteh.

The United Kingdom's Minister of State for the Middle East and North Africa, Stephen Doughty, participated in the talks. The specific outcomes or next steps from this discussion were not detailed in the report.
