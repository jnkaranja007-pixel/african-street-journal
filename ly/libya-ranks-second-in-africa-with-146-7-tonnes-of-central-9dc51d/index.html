Libya ranks second in Africa with 146.7 tonnes of central bank gold

Algeria leads the continent with 173.6 tonnes, while Libya has virtually no industrial gold mining, according to figures cited in a report.

Source: Libyan Express · 2026-11-23 · 1 min read

Libya holds 146.7 tonnes of gold in its central bank reserves, placing it second in Africa behind Algeria's 173.6 tonnes, according to World Gold Council figures.

Egypt is third with 130 tonnes and South Africa fourth with 125.5 tonnes. Tanzania's minerals minister announced its holdings at 39.6 tonnes, fifth on the list. A bne IntelliNews report of 20 November put the combined total for the four leading countries at 575.8 tonnes, over 14 times Tanzania's figure.

Libya has almost no industrial gold mining, so the central bank has built its reserves through purchases, including 30 tonnes acquired in 2023.

Why it mattersThe central bank's gold stock is the result of buying rather than domestic mining, with 30 tonnes acquired in 2023 alone.
