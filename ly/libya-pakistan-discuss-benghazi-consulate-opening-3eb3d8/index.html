Libya, Pakistan Discuss Benghazi Consulate Opening

Libya's Foreign Ministry and Pakistan's ambassador reviewed steps to establish a Pakistani consulate in the eastern city of Benghazi.

Source: Libya Update · 2026-10-21 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X

Libya's Foreign Ministry is preparing to open a consulate in Benghazi with Pakistan. Saad Hassan Bakkar, Director of the Asia and Australia Affairs Department, met Sunday with Pakistan's Ambassador to Libya, Mumtaz Hussain Sakr, and his delegation. The officials discussed strengthening bilateral ties and the specific preparations needed to establish the Pakistani consulate in the eastern Libyan city.

The meeting, held in Benghazi, focused on the logistical and diplomatic steps required for the consulate's establishment. This move signals an intent to deepen economic and consular relations between the two nations. The Pakistani delegation's presence indicates a commitment to advancing this diplomatic initiative.

The opening of a Pakistani consulate in Benghazi is expected to facilitate consular services for citizens and streamline business interactions. This development is part of ongoing efforts to enhance diplomatic representation and cooperation between Libya and Pakistan, particularly in the eastern region.

Why it mattersInvestors may benefit from improved trade relations and easier access to consular services for business dealings in Benghazi.
