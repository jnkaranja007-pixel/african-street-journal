Libya faces moderate to heavy rainfall

Autumn conditions bring intermittent rain to northern and southwestern Libya, with warnings of flooded valleys.

Source: Libya Update · 2026-10-26 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Moderate to heavy rainfall is expected across northern and southwestern Libya starting Sunday, October 25, 2026. Weather forecasts indicate autumn conditions with intermittent rain for the next two days. Coastal and inland areas, including the Western Mountains and Green Mountain regions, will experience varying rainfall levels.

Forecasters have issued warnings for potential flooded valleys due to the anticipated downpours. The heaviest rain is specifically forecast for these regions. Residents are advised to prepare for the changing weather patterns.

The forecast covers Sunday, October 25, 2026, and the subsequent two days. This period is expected to bring significant autumn weather to the northern parts of the country.

Why it mattersLibya's northern and southwestern regions will experience moderate to heavy rainfall and potential flooding.
