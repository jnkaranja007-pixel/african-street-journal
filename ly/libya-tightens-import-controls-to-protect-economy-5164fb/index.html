Libya tightens import controls to protect economy

Customs and Economy Ministry officials met in Tripoli to discuss regulating goods and safeguarding national markets.

Source: Libya Herald · 2026-11-17 · 1 min read

Libyan customs officials met with the Minister of Economy and Trade in Tripoli on Monday to discuss strengthening import controls and protecting the national economy. The meeting focused on enhancing oversight of goods entering through customs ports, particularly food and pharmaceuticals.

Discussions also covered verifying shipment routes for essential goods financed by letters of credit, ensuring they comply with regulations. Officials explored tightening controls over transit goods to prevent violations as they move across Libyan territory.

This effort is part of a wider initiative to rationalize the use of Libya's hard currency deposits, following an agreement on November 12 between the Economy Minister and the Central Bank of Libya governor to prioritize import documentary credits.

Why it mattersThe Economy Ministry and Customs Authority are enhancing import oversight and regulating goods to safeguard the national economy and rationalize hard currency use.
