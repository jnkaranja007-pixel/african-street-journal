Libya and US discuss visa challenges

Officials from Libya's Foreign Ministry and the U.S. Embassy met to improve consular cooperation and address obstacles for citizens applying for visas.

Source: Libya Herald · 2026-10-22 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Libya's Foreign Ministry reported that officials met with Janet Robert, Head of Consular Affairs at the U.S. Embassy in Libya, to discuss strengthening consular communication. The meeting, which included representatives from both sides, focused on enhancing cooperation and facilitating matters of mutual interest. Both parties emphasized the importance of maintaining institutional dialogue and coordination channels.

A key topic of discussion was the challenges and obstacles faced by citizens of both Libya and the United States in their visa application processes. Views were exchanged on existing difficulties, and potential approaches were explored to overcome these hurdles. The aim is to further streamline procedures while ensuring compliance with the national legislation and legal requirements of both countries.

The discussions also covered several other issues of mutual interest, with an emphasis on continued consultation and coordination. These efforts are intended to develop practical solutions to the challenges discussed and to support the further development of bilateral relations between Libya and the United States, serving the interests of citizens from both nations.

Why it mattersLibyan and American citizens may face fewer difficulties when applying for visas to travel between the two countries.
