Tripoli court affirms judicial independence

The Tripoli Court of Appeals rejected attempts to fragment the judiciary and influence its rulings.

Source: Libya Herald · 2026-10-31 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

The Tripoli Court of Appeals affirmed its commitment to judicial independence and the separation of powers on Tuesday, October 27. The court stressed that judicial independence is a constitutional immunity and rejected any interference in its work or rulings.

In its General Assembly, the court also emphasized the binding authority of the Supreme Court's Constitutional Chamber. It rejected amendments to judiciary laws made outside the constitutional process, warning against exceptional measures that could harm judicial independence.

The court declared its adherence to the unity of the judicial body, stating that its strength lies in cohesion. It aims to remain separate from political and regional tensions, serving as a safe haven.

Why it mattersThe Tripoli Court of Appeals' actions directly affect the judiciary and its ability to operate independently of political influence.
