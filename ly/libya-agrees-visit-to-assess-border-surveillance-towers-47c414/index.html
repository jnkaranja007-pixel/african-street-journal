Libya agrees visit to assess border surveillance towers

The Customs Authority and the European Union mission will inspect sites near Tunisia and discuss repairs, equipment and a proposed joint monitoring centre.

Source: Libya Herald · 2026-11-27 · 1 min read

Libya’s Customs Authority agreed on 25 November to coordinate a field visit to surveillance towers in the Al-Assa area, near the Tunisian border, after meeting the European Union Border Assistance Mission (EUBAM) at its Tripoli headquarters. The talks covered cooperation on land and sea border monitoring.

The Customs Authority said the visit will assess the condition of the Bal-Sharaf, Tawilat al-Rutba and Al-Quradiyah towers and identify maintenance and equipment needs. Work on maintenance and camera installation at Bal-Sharaf has already been completed. The meeting was chaired by Major General Al-Tahir Khair, director of the Land Border Surveillance Department.

Officials also discussed specialist training for border personnel and a proposal for a joint surveillance centre involving the Customs Authority and the interior and defence ministries. They raised equipment and logistical needs at the Wazin crossing and the Nalut surveillance unit.

Why it mattersBorder surveillance teams must assess three towers and identify maintenance and equipment needs during the agreed field visit.
