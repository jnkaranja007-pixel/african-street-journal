Saddam Haftar condemns Trump remarks on Libya

General Haftar calls for a formal diplomatic protest to the United States over offensive statements.

Source: Libya Update · 2026-10-26 · 1 min read

General Saddam Haftar condemned remarks by U.S. President Donald Trump about Libya, calling them offensive and unacceptable. Haftar stated that domestic political debates do not justify insulting nations or their people.

In an official statement, Haftar declared Libya a sovereign state whose dignity is paramount. He called on Libyan authorities to deliver a formal protest to the United States, demanding the offense be addressed seriously.

Haftar reiterated Libya's commitment to friendly relations with the U.S. but stressed that mutual respect is the foundation for ties with all nations.

Why it mattersLibyan authorities are called to lodge a formal diplomatic protest with the United States.
