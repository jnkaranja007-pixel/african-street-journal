Mauritius court sentences Vedanand Seetul to 36 years in prison

A jury found the 52-year-old guilty by seven votes to two over his wife’s death in Lallmatie in 2018.

Sources: Ion News and Defi Media · 2026-11-26 · 1 min read

A court in Mauritius sentenced Vedanand Seetul, 52, to 36 years in prison on 20 November 2026 for the 2018 killing of his wife, Madhuri Seetul, in Lallmatie. The court convicted him of manslaughter.

A nine-member jury found Seetul guilty by seven votes to two on 7 November. Madhuri, 26, died after a dispute at the couple’s home on Tagore Road on 25 November 2018. Prosecutors had sought 37 to 42 years in prison, citing what they described as a lack of remorse and the impact on the couple’s three children.

The defence asked the court for leniency, referring to Seetul’s personal and social circumstances. Judge Prameeta Devi Rasheela Goordyal-Chittoo said a family home should remain a place of safety and voiced concern about rising femicides.

Why it mattersSeetul must serve the 36-year term imposed by the court, after the prosecution sought up to 42 years and the defence requested leniency.
