Lutchigadoo asks Mauritius court to cut 18-year drug sentence

His defence seeks a term of 13 to 15 years, while prosecutors argue the appeal has not shown the sentence was legally wrong.

Source: Defi Media · 2026-11-23 · 1 min read

Kelvy Lutchigadoo, 31, has asked Mauritius’s Supreme Court to reduce his 18-year prison sentence to between 13 and 15 years. His defence made the request on 10 November 2026, at a hearing of his appeal over an attempted possession of synthetic drugs. The prosecution opposes a reduction, and the court reserved its judgment.

Lutchigadoo was sentenced on 15 October 2025 after pleading guilty to attempting to possess 290,2 grams of synthetic cannabinoids intended for distribution, valued at Rs 4 353 000. His lawyer, Yanilla Moonshiram, argued that the offence involved an attempt, while prosecutor Pareemala Devi Mauree said the defence had not shown the sentence was legally wrong.

Moonshiram also challenged the trial court’s decision to give Lutchigadoo and co-accused Keniven Chocalingum identical sentences, saying Chocalingum had pleaded not guilty. She said Lutchigadoo had given authorities the name of the alleged importer.
