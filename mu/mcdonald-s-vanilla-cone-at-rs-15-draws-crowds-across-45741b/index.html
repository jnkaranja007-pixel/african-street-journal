McDonald's vanilla cone at Rs 15 draws crowds across Mauritius

Buyers at outlets from Port-Louis to Grand-Baie are choosing the Rs 15 cone while other McDonald's desserts top Rs 100.

Source: Ion News · 2026-11-20 · 1 min read

Crowds are queuing at McDonald's dessert counters and kiosks in Mauritius for the vanilla Ice Cream Cone, which still sells for Rs 15, Ion News reported on Friday. The outlet named Port-Louis, Grand-Baie, Bagatelle, Phoenix and Beau-Bassin among the places where people are buying it.

Workers, students and families are among the buyers, according to Ion News. Some pick up several cones at drive-throughs or shopping centres to share with colleagues or children. The report said the cone costs less than a bottle of cold water, while most other desserts and sundaes cost more than Rs 100.

Ion News said demand is rising as the hot months of December and January approach.

Why it mattersShoppers on a tight budget can still buy a cooling dessert for Rs 15, while other McDonald's desserts cost more than Rs 100.
