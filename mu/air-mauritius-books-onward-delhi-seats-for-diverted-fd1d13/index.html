Air Mauritius books onward Delhi seats for diverted passengers

Limited Mumbai-Delhi seats on domestic carriers, after a runway closure and a terminal evacuation drill, left the airline reserving places on other flights.

Source: Defi Media · 2026-11-20 · 1 min read

Air Mauritius has reserved seats on several available flights to Delhi for all passengers affected after its flight MK744 was diverted to Mumbai on Thursday, 19 November, because of a technical problem. The aircraft was scheduled to fly on to Delhi.

Seats on domestic carriers between Mumbai and Delhi were limited, partly because Mumbai airport's runways were temporarily closed for post-monsoon maintenance. A terminal evacuation exercise, held at the request of local authorities, added to the pressure on availability.

The Airbus A330-900neo landed safely at Mumbai's Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj International Airport at 3:05 a.m. Mauritius time. Under local requirements, passengers had to disembark and collect their bags, and the airline said its staff were helping with accommodation and onward travel.

Why it mattersPassengers stranded in Mumbai now have confirmed onward seats to Delhi on other flights rather than waiting for the next MK745 service.
