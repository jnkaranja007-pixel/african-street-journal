Police search for husband after woman found seriously injured in Goodlands

A 26-year-old woman was found with serious injuries in a parking lot near a gym in Goodlands on Monday night, accusing her husband of assault.

Police are searching for the husband of a 26-year-old woman who was found seriously injured in Goodlands on Monday night. The woman was discovered in a parking lot near a gym and has accused her husband of assaulting her, according to initial police findings.

The victim is currently admitted to SSRN Hospital in Pamplemousses, where her condition is described as serious. Investigators have not yet been able to take her full statement due to her health. An inquiry has been opened to establish the circumstances of the aggression.

The police are actively looking for the victim's husband in connection with the incident. The investigation is ongoing as authorities work to gather more details about the assault and locate the individual accused by the victim.

Why it mattersThis event affects the victim and her family directly by causing serious injury and initiating a police search for her husband.

Mauritian Civil Servants Train in India to Streamline Administration

Five hundred Mauritian civil servants will receive training in India over the next five years, focusing on governance and digital transformation to reduce administrative burdens.

Five hundred Mauritian civil servants are scheduled to undergo training in India over the next five years, focusing on governance and digital transformation. This program, launched under a March 2025 agreement between the National Centre for Good Governance and Mauritius's Ministry of Public Service, aims to enhance skills in areas like artificial intelligence in administration, complaint management, and improving the business climate for SMEs.

The initiative responds to the Mauritian government's goal of reforming public administration, with Prime Minister Navin Ramgoolam recently criticizing excessive procedures and permits. The training also covers investment, entrepreneurship, waste management, and local government operations, with 70 civil servants already having participated.

Public Service Minister Raj Pentiah is currently in India with a delegation of senior officials as part of this cooperation. The program seeks to simplify procedures, reduce delays, and increase the use of digital tools within the Mauritian administration, building on existing cooperation like the Indian Technical and Economic Cooperation (ITEC) program, which provides Mauritius with approximately 500 training slots annually.

Why it mattersMauritian citizens and businesses are affected by the government's efforts to reduce administrative burdens, potentially leading to simpler processes and faster service delivery.

Air Mauritius diverts flight MK017 to Rome due to medical emergency

Flight MK017, traveling from Paris-Charles-de-Gaulle to Mauritius, was rerouted to Rome on Monday, August 24, 2026, after a cabin crew member experienced a medical emergency.

Air Mauritius flight MK017 made an unscheduled landing in Rome on Monday, August 24, 2026, following a medical emergency involving a cabin crew member. The airline confirmed the diversion in a statement released on Tuesday, August 25.

Upon arrival at Rome's Leonardo da Vinci Airport, the affected crew member received immediate medical attention from local authorities. After completing necessary operational and regulatory procedures, the aircraft departed Rome at four twenty-seven AM, Mauritian time.

The flight was expected to arrive at Sir Seewoosagur Ramgoolam International Airport at two thirty PM on Tuesday, August 25. Air Mauritius stated it is in contact with the crew member and their family, offering support and wishes for a swift recovery, and thanked passengers for their patience.

Why it mattersPassengers on flight MK017 experienced a delay in their travel plans due to the unexpected diversion, and the airline's operations were impacted by the rerouting.

Donald Trump gains South Carolina primary win for supported candidate

Darline Graham, a candidate backed by Donald Trump, secured the Republican nomination in South Carolina, providing a boost to the former president after a series of defeats for.

Darline Graham, 62, won the Republican primary in South Carolina on Tuesday, August 25, according to CNN, CBS, and NBC. Graham, who replaced her brother Lindsey Graham after his death in July, defeated ultraconservative representative Ralph Norman. This victory offers a reprieve for Donald Trump, whose influence within the Republican party has been challenged by declining approval ratings and polls predicting Republican losses in the November elections.

Donald Trump actively supported Darline Graham, visiting South Carolina last week to campaign for her. On Tuesday, he praised her as “a spectacular person and a true American patriot.” He hopes to rely on her as a loyal ally in the Senate, where some members of his party have recently opposed him, and several candidates have been nominated without his endorsement.

Graham's candidacy, despite her previous work on disability issues in South Carolina, caused divisions within the state's Republican Party due to her lack of political experience. This inexperience was highlighted last week during a televised debate when she struggled with a question about Taiwan, stating, “National security is not my thing.” She faced opposition from Ralph Norman, also a Trump supporter but without presidential backing.

Why it mattersThis event directly affects Republican voters in South Carolina by determining their party's Senate candidate and influences the broader political landscape for Donald Trump's supported candidates.

Rodrigues validates 230 new words for Kreol Rodrige dictionary

The second edition of the Diksioner Kreol Rodrige, set for release in October, will include over 230 new words ahead of its integration into secondary schools in 2027.

The Diksioner Kreol Rodrige will expand its vocabulary with more than 230 new words in its second edition, expected in October. This update follows consultations with the local population to gather terms used in daily life by both older and younger generations. The dictionary, a collaboration between Rodriguans, authorities, and the University of Mauritius, aims to document the evolving language.

The new words were collected from various community groups, then reviewed and defined before their inclusion. This process ensures the dictionary reflects the current usage of Kreol Rodrige in Rodrigues. The first edition, published in 2021, contained approximately one thousand four hundred pages.

The updated dictionary is set to become a reference for teaching Kreol Rodrige when it enters the secondary school system in 2027. Its formalized and documented vocabulary will support the language's new role in education, marking a significant step for its preservation and development.

Why it mattersStudents in Rodrigues will use the updated dictionary as a reference for learning Kreol Rodrige in secondary schools starting in 2027.
