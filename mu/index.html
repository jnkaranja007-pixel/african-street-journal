Supreme Court annuls release of inspector in Rs 75.9M drug case

The Supreme Court overturned the release of inspector Vicky Luckmun involving the importation of Rs 75.9 million in synthetic drugs. The ruling was reported by Defi Media.

Why it mattersThe decision reinstates legal proceedings against the officer involved in the high-value narcotics seizure.

Agriculture · 2026-08-21

Mauritius agricultural price index drops 2.6% in Q2

The Producer Price Index-Agriculture fell 2.6% between the first and second quarters of 2026, moving from 96.2 in March to 94.0 in June. Statistics Mauritius published the data on August 19.

Why it mattersThe decline was driven by a 5.8% drop in crop product indices, affecting the revenue of farmers producing vegetable goods.

Climate · 2026-08-21

Mauritius water production falls 17.8% as reservoirs deplete

Overall water production decreased by 17.8% compared to the previous year, according to Central Water Authority figures reported in late July. Ion News noted that the Mare aux Vacoas reservoir has been particularly affected by rainfall deficits.

Why it mattersLower reservoir levels threaten the consistent supply of potable water to residents and businesses.

Mauritian call centers pivot after France bans phone solicitation

Mauritian call centers are adapting their operations following a ban on telephone solicitation in France. The shift was reported by Defi Media.

Why it mattersThe regulatory change in France forces these centers to find new revenue streams or change their outreach methods to avoid losing contracts.

News · 2026-08-21

Ministry distributes safety gear to fishers at 2 locations

The ministry of Agro-industry provided first aid kits, coolers, and distress beacons to fishers in Bambous-Virieux and Mahébourg on August 19. Minister Arvin Boolell stated the program will expand to all 14 fishing posts nationwide.

Why it mattersThe equipment allows fishers to venture further beyond the lagoon while maintaining safety and preserving catches.
