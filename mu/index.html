Mauritius court denies bail for alleged cannabis trafficker

Marie Nathalia Laviolette remains in jail after the Supreme Court rejected her appeal for judicial review.

Marie Nathalia Laviolette, a 37-year-old resident of L’Escalier, will stay in prison after the Supreme Court upheld a lower court’s decision to deny her bail. The judges rejected her request for judicial review on October 21, 2026, confirming the Mahébourg court's earlier refusal on March 10, 2026.

The case began in January 2026 with the sea-borne import of 20 kg of cannabis from Reunion Island, valued at 24 million rupees and hidden in Baie-du-Cap. Police arrested three suspects, including Laviolette’s husband, Jean Berty David Kardaree, on January 31, 2026. Another arrestee, Terry Toussaint, later identified Laviolette as "Sorenza," instructing her to collect the package and deliver it to Rose-Belle.

Laviolette faces charges of aggravated drug trafficking and incitement. Her lawyer argued for bail citing no drugs or suspicious money at her home, a clean record, her bakery business, and four minor children. However, judges Iqbal Maghooa and Renuka Devi Dabee denied bail, citing the gravity of the offense, flight risk, and potential for witness tampering or evidence destruction, including the disappearance of her phone and the cannabis.

Mauritian sculptor Dhyaneswar Dausoa dies at 82

Dausoa, a pioneer of the fine arts department at the Mahatma Gandhi Institute, passed away after a long illness.

Mauritian sculptor Dhyaneswar Dausoa died on Monday, November 9, at the age of 82 due to a long illness. Dausoa was a significant figure in Mauritian sculpture, known for his work in wood, stone, and metal, with his early nude sculptures dating back to the 1970s.

He was instrumental in establishing the fine arts department at the Mahatma Gandhi Institute (MGI) in Moka, invited in 1976 by Dr. Kissoonsingh Hazareesingh. Dausoa's art, which often explored nature, life, and the female form, was exhibited nationally and internationally, including at the inauguration of the Sir Seewoosagur Ramgoolam National Hospital in 1968.

His artistic journey included a government scholarship to study in India during the 1970s, where he exhibited in New Delhi. In 1997, he created a monumental iron sculpture in Tunisia. Dausoa was recognised for his cultural contributions when he was appointed Commander of the Order of the Star and Key of the Indian Ocean (CSK) on March 12, 2020.

Why it mattersThe death of Dhyaneswar Dausoa affects Mauritius's cultural heritage and artistic community.

ASVP targets survival amid financial strain

Mauritian club ASVP focuses on budget prudence and squad renewal for the new Super League season.

The Association Sportive de Vacoas/Phoenix (ASVP) begins the new Mauritian Super League season with a focus on financial caution and squad changes. The club finished eighth last season with 23 points, recording seven wins, two draws, and nine losses.

Facing major financial difficulties and the departure of key players, ASVP's 53-year-old coach Pramen Armoogum is working with a limited budget. The team has signed only one foreign player, a Malagasy student, and integrated young talent from local development centers.

ASVP's first match is against title contender Cercle de Joachim on Monday, November 9, 2026. The club's primary goal is to build a strong team to secure its position in the top league.

Why it mattersThe ASVP football club in Mauritius faces financial challenges and aims to secure its survival in the Super League.

Husband stabs wife before autistic son

A 7-year-old boy witnessed his mother being stabbed by his father on November 2.

A 7-year-old boy witnessed his mother being stabbed by his father at their home on November 2. The child, who has autism, reportedly saw his father attack his mother with a knife. His 62-year-old grandmother was also present during the violent incident.

The alleged attacker, a prison guard on leave, reportedly took a knife from the kitchen and threatened his wife before stabbing her in the right side. The victim was taken to Dr A. G. Jeetoo Hospital, where her condition is described as serious. Her sister transported her to the hospital after neighbours intervened to subdue the suspect.

The couple had been married for 12 years and have a son. Family members report that conflicts related to alcohol, finances, and the husband's gambling habits have escalated over the past three to four years, leading to the deterioration of their relationship.
