Mauritius extends police commissioner's contract for one year

Prime Minister Navin Ramgoolam confirmed in parliament that Rampersad Sooroojbally will stay on as the country's police chief.

Prime Minister Navin Ramgoolam told parliament on Tuesday, November 17, that the contract of Police Commissioner Rampersad Sooroojbally has been renewed for one year. Sooroojbally has headed the police force since November 15, 2024, and received an earlier one-year renewal in November 2025.

Ramgoolam made the confirmation in reply to a supplementary question from the opposition leader, Chetan Baboolall, during question time. ION News had reported the extension the previous week, but when Ramgoolam was asked on Sunday, he said "nothing has been decided yet".

The renewal keeps Sooroojbally in charge of the force for another year, according to the prime minister's statement to the House.

Culture · 2026-11-17

Port Louis Waterfront hosts second food festival with 30 vendors

The three-day event runs from 20 to 22 November, with food sold at no more than Rs 275 a dish and evening concerts.

The Port Louis Waterfront will host a second Food Festival from 20 to 22 November, following a first edition held in July. Visitors can eat and browse from noon until late each day, with more than 30 restaurateurs and food artisans taking part.

The menu covers Mauritian cuisine, Indian and Asian dishes, street food, grills, seafood and desserts. Dishes will be sold for no more than Rs 275. Among the vendors listed are Restaurant Le Bardo for curries and butter chicken, Kogi Kogi for Korean dishes, and Maison Dorée and Chocomelts for sweets. Radio Plus is co-hosting the event with games, prizes and meetings with its presenters, and a Kids' Corner has been set up for children during the school holidays.

Radio Plus programme director Venen Coolen said the festival answers Mauritians' wish to gather around food and entertainment. Revathi Narainen Cooroopdoss, the project's digital project manager, said it is meant to showcase local food vendors and entrepreneurs.

Why it mattersFood vendors selling at no more than Rs 275 a dish give families a low-cost night out during the start of the holiday season.
