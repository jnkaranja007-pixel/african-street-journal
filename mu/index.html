Detainee bites police officer at Line Barracks

A police constable required two days sick leave after being bitten on the arm during an incident at the Port-Louis detention centre.

A routine inspection turned violent at the Line Barracks detention centre in Port-Louis on Saturday, November 7. Around 11 AM, detainee Anne Mario Joseph Gaetan asked a patrolling police officer to use the ground floor toilet. The officer escorted him, but the detainee refused to return to his cell, becoming aggressive.

The officer called for backup, and a sergeant and another colleague arrived to help subdue the detainee. During the struggle, Gaetan bit the officer's left forearm, causing a bloody injury. The suspect was removed from the cell and taken to Dr A.G. Jeetoo Hospital for urgent care.

The injured constable received treatment and was granted two days of sick leave. A formal complaint has been filed at the Line Barracks police station, and an investigation into the incident is underway.

Mauritian sculptor Dhyaneswar Dausoa dies at 82

Dausoa, a pioneer of the fine arts department at the Mahatma Gandhi Institute, passed away after a long illness.

Mauritian sculptor Dhyaneswar Dausoa died on Monday, November 9, at the age of 82 due to a long illness. Dausoa was a significant figure in Mauritian sculpture, known for his work in wood, stone, and metal, with his early nude sculptures dating back to the 1970s.

He was instrumental in establishing the fine arts department at the Mahatma Gandhi Institute (MGI) in Moka, invited in 1976 by Dr. Kissoonsingh Hazareesingh. Dausoa's art, which often explored nature, life, and the female form, was exhibited nationally and internationally, including at the inauguration of the Sir Seewoosagur Ramgoolam National Hospital in 1968.

His artistic journey included a government scholarship to study in India during the 1970s, where he exhibited in New Delhi. In 1997, he created a monumental iron sculpture in Tunisia. Dausoa was recognised for his cultural contributions when he was appointed Commander of the Order of the Star and Key of the Indian Ocean (CSK) on March 12, 2020.

Why it mattersThe death of Dhyaneswar Dausoa affects Mauritius's cultural heritage and artistic community.

Plaine-Magnien Star defeats Entente Boulet Rouge 4-0

Plaine-Magnien Star secured a dominant victory in their opening National D1 match on November 9, 2026.

Plaine-Magnien Star began their National D1 season with a resounding 4-0 win against Entente Boulet Rouge on Monday, November 9, 2026. The visitors controlled the match from the start, opening the scoring in the 26th minute through Ally. Entente Boulet Rouge could not find a way back into the game before halftime.

In the second half, Plaine-Magnien Star extended their lead with a goal from Laboiteuse in the 62nd minute, effectively ending any hopes of a comeback for the home team. Toinette added a third goal in the 79th minute, and Laboiteuse completed his brace and the scoring in the 90th minute.

This decisive victory gives Plaine-Magnien Star a strong start to the 2026/27 campaign, building confidence early in the season. The team demonstrated both offensive effectiveness and defensive solidity throughout the 90 minutes.

Agriculture · 2026-11-10

Brothers offer mangoes with original sauces

Faiz and Fadil Khaidoo are modernizing a Mauritian tradition by pairing cut mangoes with unique flavour combinations.

Brothers Faiz, 31, and Fadil Khaidoo, 32, are offering a new way to enjoy summer mangoes in Mauritius. They sell cut mangoes with a choice of original sauces at their Port-Louis and Flic-en-Flac locations.

The Khaidoo brothers are building on a family tradition in fruit sales, adding their creative touch to the seasonal offering. Their concept allows customers to customize their fruit experience, drawing inspiration from the Mauritian habit of eating fruit with salt, chili, or coconut.

Faiz explained that his aim is not to replace tradition but to give it a new dimension, showing how familiar fruits like mangoes can become a unique taste experience with creativity.
