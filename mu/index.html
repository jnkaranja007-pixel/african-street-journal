Husband stabs wife in front of autistic son

A 7-year-old boy witnessed his father stab his mother at their home on November 2.

A prison guard, banned from his job, allegedly stabbed his wife on November 2 at their Terre-Rouge home. The 62-year-old grandmother also witnessed the attack. The suspect reportedly took a knife from the kitchen and threatened his wife before stabbing her in the right side.

The victim was taken to Dr. A. G. Jeetoo Hospital and is in serious condition. Her sister recounted hearing screams and finding her bleeding, stating her husband had stabbed her. Neighbours intervened and subdued the suspect before police arrived. The couple has a 7-year-old son with autism.

The family reports that marital problems, including alcohol, finances, and outside relationships, have worsened over the past three to four years. The husband allegedly spends his salary on gambling and neglects his responsibilities.

Why it mattersA 7-year-old boy with autism witnessed his father stab his mother, a traumatic event that will forever mark his childhood.

Mauritian dentist finds identity in service

Dr. Muhammad Ismaēl Rawoo's life is shaped by family, faith, and a dedication to community service.

Dr. Muhammad Ismaēl Rawoo, a 43-year-old Mauritian dentist, views his profession as a calling influenced by family, faith, and compassion. His work and commitment to serving vulnerable patients are seen as contributing to a shared national identity.

Rawoo's journey is marked by the influence of his late father, his supportive mother Aisha Bee, and his grandfather Ibrahim Boodhoo, a businessman who introduced several brands to Mauritius. He is married to Shamima, a medical doctor, and they have three children: Ibrahim, Eizzah, and Adam.

His eldest son, Ibrahim, shows interest in dentistry, while Eizzah is passionate about painting and teaching the Quran. Youngest child Adam enjoys helping in the kitchen. Rawoo also shares a close bond with his seven-year-old niece, Aisha, who aspires to be a dentist or doctor.

Why it mattersDr. Rawoo's personal journey and professional dedication are presented as integral to the fabric of Mauritian identity.

Driver hospitalised after Mont-Ida road crash

A 54-year-old man is in serious condition following a two-car collision on the royal road in Mont-Ida.

A road accident occurred on the royal road in Mont-Ida on Friday, November 6, when two cars collided. The drivers were a 24-year-old from Mont-Ida and a 54-year-old from Sébastopol.

The occupants of both vehicles, including the two drivers, were injured and taken to the hospital. The health of the 54-year-old driver is a concern, while the other driver and three passengers were treated and released. Alcootests for both drivers were negative.

An investigation into the collision is continuing.

Mauritius night market plan delayed to 2026

Minister Ranjiv Woochit will not implement the night market project in 2026 due to necessary preparations.

The proposed Night Market project in Mauritius remains under consideration, with no implementation planned for 2026. A consultation meeting was held on Friday, November 6, but further examination of several points is required before a decision can be made. Minister of Local Government Ranjiv Woochit stated that necessary precautions must be taken.

Key concerns for the Night Market project include public safety and health, requiring consultation with health services, firefighters, and ambulance personnel. The interests of merchants, shops, and other economic actors will also be reviewed, with authorities having already received proposals and grievances. Minister Woochit explained that the initial meeting aimed to consider all these proposals.

Further meetings are scheduled as the dossier requires more work before a final decision can be reached by the Cabinet. The minister indicated that many aspects need examination and preparation, and decisions cannot be made lightly, especially given letters and grievances from shops, mosques, and other traders. The Cabinet will make the ultimate collective decision.

Why it mattersThe Night Market project's implementation is delayed, affecting merchants, shops, and other economic actors.

Mauritius to host international Ramayana conference in 2027

The event will mark the silver jubilee of the Ramayana Centre and explore timeless Wisdom for Global Unity.

Mauritius will host the third edition of the International Conference on the Ramayana from May 22 to 24, 2027. The Council of Ministers approved the event, which will also celebrate the silver jubilee of the Ramayana Centre.

Under the theme "Ramayana: Timeless Wisdom for Global Unity and Shared Humanity," the conference aims to highlight the universal relevance of the spiritual work. Organizers stated that the Ramayana's teachings offer valuable perspectives on spiritual conduct, responsible leadership, peaceful coexistence, and mutual respect.

The government initiative seeks to foster intellectual dialogue, strengthen cultural exchanges, and promote international collaboration. Researchers, educators, cultural practitioners, students, and devotees from around the world are expected to attend.

Why it mattersMauritius will host an international cultural conference in May 2027, bringing together global scholars and devotees.
