Sergeant Taukoor Detained After Shooting Incident

A 57-year-old police sergeant will remain in custody until October 26 following a shooting incident involving a 27-year-old woman.

Sergeant Gautam Singh Taukoor, aged 57, appeared in the Rose-Hill district court on Monday, October 19. The hearing was conducted with significant security measures. Taukoor is suspected of firing a weapon at Élodie Thomas, 27, in Camp-Levieux on Friday, October 9. He faces provisional charges of "Using Firearm to Intimidate" and "Involuntary Wounds and Blows by Imprudence."

The court denied a request for Taukoor's release, ordering him to be returned to police custody. His detention is a precautionary measure while investigations continue into the exact circumstances of the shooting. The case has proceeded with discretion due to security concerns.

Taukoor's next court appearance is scheduled for Monday, October 26, when he will continue to be held. The incident occurred on October 9, and the charges were filed on October 10. The investigation into the event is ongoing.

Why it mattersThe sergeant's detention affects the safety and legal standing of the woman he is accused of intimidating and wounding.

Mauritius MP Questions Government on M4 Highway, Waste Transfer, and Water Permits

Joanna Bérenger will ask the Prime Minister and ministers about a missing Bangladeshi national, public consultations for the M4 highway, waste transfer station relocation.

Member of Parliament Joanna Bérenger has submitted several questions to the Mauritian government ahead of the National Assembly's reconvening on October 20. Bérenger, representing the 15th constituency, announced these queries via Facebook on October 19, focusing on four key issues. She plans to question Prime Minister Navin Ramgoolam regarding the investigation into the disappearance of a Bangladeshi caregiver who has been missing since April 3, 2026. Her questions aim to ascertain if the individual has left the country, been located, and if measures are in place for her safety.

Further inquiries will be directed to the Minister of National Infrastructure concerning the M4 highway project. Bérenger seeks the findings and recommendations from public consultations held on this infrastructure plan. Additionally, she will address the Minister of Environment, Rajesh Bhagwan, about a decision on relocating the La Brasserie waste transfer station to La Marie. The MP also intends to question the Minister of Energy and Public Utilities, Patrick Assirvaden, on water resource management.

Specifically, Bérenger is requesting a detailed report on the number of water drilling permits issued to date. This report should include the names of permit holders, issuance dates, the locations of the boreholes, and the intended use of the extracted water. These questions highlight Bérenger's focus on public safety, infrastructure development, environmental management, and resource allocation within Mauritius.

Why it mattersFarmers are affected by the potential relocation of a waste transfer station and the issuance of water drilling permits, which could impact water resources.

Bodha Calls for Social Harmony and Economic Revival

Rassemblement Mauricien leader Nando Bodha urges restoring trust and lessons from past unrest to rebuild the nation.

Nando Bodha, leader of the Rassemblement Mauricien, has called for urgent action to restore social harmony and rebuild trust following recent unrest. He emphasized the need to address the root causes of public discontent, questioning why young people are protesting and what underlying issues fuel their anger. Bodha stressed that the nation must find answers to these fundamental questions without resorting to communal divisions.

In a Facebook post, Bodha warned against the dangers of a society where public order breaks down, leading communities to seek their own solutions. He highlighted that social peace and national harmony have a price that must be paid. The leader also pointed to statements by the Prime Minister regarding drug traffickers and police fragility, suggesting these raise further questions about the reasons for public demonstrations.

Bodha insists on the necessity of relaunching the economy and learning from past mistakes to reconstruct a country where every citizen is treated with equal dignity and has the same opportunities to succeed. He advocates for a unified approach to national challenges, urging the population to reject violence and work towards a more equitable future for all children.

Why it mattersNando Bodha's call for social peace and economic revival directly impacts all Mauritians by addressing societal issues and future opportunities.

Motorcyclist Dies in Poudre-d'Or Collision

A motorcyclist died and a passenger was injured in a crash involving a motorcycle and a van on October 19.

A motorcyclist died instantly and another person was injured in a collision between a motorcycle and a van in Poudre-d'Or on Monday, October 19. Emergency services and the SAMU responded to the scene. The motorcyclist succumbed to injuries sustained during the impact.

The injured passenger was transported to SSRN Hospital in Pamplemousses for treatment. The exact circumstances leading to the accident are still under investigation by authorities.

This incident highlights ongoing road safety concerns. Investigations are underway to determine the cause of the crash and prevent future occurrences.

Why it mattersA motorcyclist died and a passenger was injured in a traffic accident.
