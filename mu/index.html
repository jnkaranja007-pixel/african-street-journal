Rodrigues man arrested with cannabis plants

Police seized 0.72 grams of cannabis, six seedlings, seven seeds, and a 70cm plant.

Police in Rodrigues arrested a 43-year-old mason on Monday, November 2, following a search of his home in Citron Donis. Officers from the Anti-Drug and Smuggling Unit (ADSU) conducted the raid around 10:15 AM.

During the search, officers discovered and seized 0.72 grams of cannabis, six cannabis seedlings, seven cannabis seeds, and a cannabis plant approximately 70 centimeters tall. The suspect was taken into police custody.

The investigation into the cultivation and possession of cannabis is ongoing. The man remains detained by the police as inquiries continue.

Mauritius men adopt festive attire for Divali

Men's fashion for Divali 2026 features natural fabrics like linen and silk in rich colours and intricate embroidery.

Mauritian men are preparing for Divali 2026 with a focus on elegant and comfortable festive wear. For morning rituals and family visits, outfits will include kurtas made from natural linen or Mul-Chanderi cotton in soft shades like mustard yellow, sage green, and ivory. These are paired with churidar or cigarette-style trousers, and a sleeveless bundi jacket in silk adds a polished touch.

As evening approaches and celebrations intensify, men's attire will shift to more striking pieces. Short sherwanis or embroidered Nehru jackets in Tussar silk or royal velvet will be popular. These are designed to be worn with draped or pre-stitched dhoti trousers, featuring subtle zari, chikankari, or zardozi embroidery that catches the light. Deep jewel tones such as midnight blue, emerald green, and intense plum will feature prominently.

The festive look will be completed with embroidered leather mojris or juttis, often detailed with gold thread. A final flourish can be added with an organza or silk dupatta draped casually over the shoulder. This year's Divali fashion emphasizes a blend of tradition and contemporary style for men.

Why it mattersMauritian men will wear specific traditional and modern clothing styles for Divali 2026 celebrations.
