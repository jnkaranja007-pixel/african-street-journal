Reform Party leader warns Mauritius rate rise will squeeze household budgets

Roshi Bhadain says the central bank's hike from 4.75% to 5% will add to borrowing costs as inflation reached 6.4% last month.

The Monetary Policy Committee raised the key interest rate from 4.75% to 5% in the course of this week to contain inflation, which reached 6.4% last month, according to the bank's announcement.

Roshi Bhadain, leader of the Reform Party, said in a video posted on Friday to his Facebook page that the increase would raise the repayments of households with bank loans or credit purchases. Citing figures he attributes to Statistics Mauritius, he said household debt already exceeded Rs 220 billion in September 2026, and that one household in two is in debt.

He also argued that the previous increase, from 4.50% to 4.75% in May, did not produce the expected results, and that the new rise will not solve the inflation problem. He said the decision should not have been taken at this stage.

Why it mattersHouseholds carrying bank loans or credit purchases will pay more on their repayments after the central bank raised its benchmark rate to 5%.

Mauritius reroutes Port-Louis traffic from Monday after road repairs stall flow

A revised diversion plan takes effect on Monday, 16 November 2026, to ease congestion around the closed section of Rue Monseigneur Leen.

The Council of Ministers reviewed urgent repairs on Rue Monseigneur Leen in Port-Louis on Friday, 13 November 2026. Work on the road surface and the retaining wall has closed a section of the street to cars and forced temporary alternative routes through the capital.

The first diversion, set up by the Ministry of Land Transport, caused heavy delays and disrupted traffic flow into and within Port-Louis in recent days. Motorists and public transport users have faced daily disruption, so the government has revised its traffic management strategy.

A revised diversion plan takes effect on Monday, 16 November 2026. The ministry is to publish an official statement setting out the new arrangements, and drivers near the works zone are urged to take care and follow official directions.

Why it mattersCommuters and bus users travelling through Port-Louis must adjust their routes from Monday, 16 November 2026, while the closed section stays shut.

Sport · 2026-11-14

Pamplemousses SC bid for first Super League win against Petite Rivière-Noire FC

The match opens the second round of Mauritius's Super League at St-François-Xavier Stadium in Port-Louis, after a goalless draw in the opening round.

Pamplemousses SC face Petite Rivière-Noire FC on Saturday 14 November at 15:30 at the St-François-Xavier Stadium in Port-Louis, the first of two matches on the opening evening of the second round of the Super League. Pamplemousses SC, coached by Colin Bell, drew 0-0 with the reigning champions in the first round and are looking for their first three points of the season.

Pamplemousses SC will rely on their attack, led by Ashley Nazira, the top scorer of last season, to break down the defence of the western club. Petite Rivière-Noire FC intend to spoil the plans of the favourites.

The Super League's second round continues on Sunday with the match in the capital.
