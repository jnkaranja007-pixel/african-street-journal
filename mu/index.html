Woman Accuses Husband of Assault in Goodlands, Police Search Underway

A 26-year-old woman found seriously injured in Goodlands has accused her husband of assault, prompting a police search for him.

A 26-year-old woman was found severely injured in a parking lot near a gym in Goodlands on Monday night. She was admitted to SSRN Hospital in Pamplemousses, where her condition is described as serious.

According to initial police findings, the woman stated that her husband attacked her. Investigators have not yet been able to take her full statement due to her health condition.

An investigation has been opened to determine the circumstances of the assault. Police are currently searching for the victim's husband in connection with the incident.

Why it mattersThis event directly affects the woman who was severely injured and her husband who is now sought by police, impacting their safety and legal status.

Medical Association Questions Health Minister's Private Doctor at Public Hospital

The Medical and Health Officers Association (MHOA) is seeking clarification on the circumstances surrounding Health Minister Anil Bachoo's surgery at a public hospital.

The MHOA issued a statement Tuesday morning requesting details on whether a private doctor, who allegedly practices exclusively in the private sector, was authorized to participate in Minister Bachoo's surgery at Dr A. G. Jeetoo Hospital. The association also wants to know if public infrastructure, equipment, or employees were used, and if such arrangements are available to other patients.

Minister Bachoo stated on his Facebook page that his private doctor was only an "observer" during the intervention, invited by the hospital's treating physician due to a decade-long medical history. He claims this is a "common practice" for patients followed by a private doctor for many years, ensuring continuity of care, and that the surgery was performed by the public hospital's surgeon.

However, former Health Minister Kailesh Jagutpal disputes Bachoo's account, stating that based on his information, it was not merely an observation. Jagutpal, who received information from concerned employees, claims such a protocol or established procedure for private doctors observing in public hospitals is unprecedented, though he made no comment on the minister's health.

Why it mattersThe controversy affects the public's trust in the healthcare system and raises questions about equitable access to medical services for all citizens.

Air Mauritius diverts flight MK017 to Rome due to medical emergency

Flight MK017, traveling from Paris-Charles de Gaulle to Mauritius, was rerouted to Rome on Monday, August twenty-fourth, 2026, after a cabin crew member experienced a medical.

Air Mauritius flight MK017 was diverted to Rome after a cabin crew member suffered a medical emergency, according to a statement released by the airline on Tuesday, August twenty-fifth. The aircraft landed at Leonardo da Vinci Airport in Rome, where the crew member received immediate medical attention from local authorities.

After completing necessary operational and regulatory procedures, the plane departed Rome at four twenty-seven AM, Mauritian time. It was expected to arrive at Sir Seewoosagur Ramgoolam International Airport at two thirty PM on Tuesday, August twenty-fifth.

Air Mauritius stated it is maintaining contact with the affected crew member and their family, offering support and wishes for a swift recovery. The airline also thanked passengers for their patience and understanding following the unexpected diversion.

Why it mattersPassengers on flight MK017 experienced a delay in their travel plans due to the unexpected diversion, and the airline's operations were disrupted.

Jack Bizlall challenges police action against Malina Cheeneebash

Jack Bizlall of the Democracy Observatory questioned the police's interpretation of activist Malina Cheeneebash's reaction during her protest against government pension decisions.

Jack Bizlall, representing the Democracy Observatory (ODD), sent a letter on August 18 to the police commissioner, disputing the characterization of Malina Cheeneebash's actions. Bizlall argues that Cheeneebash, who was protesting government pension decisions, should not face charges for assaulting police, stating she is "the victim and not the aggressor."

Bizlall's letter also suggests that the protest location, in front of Parliament and near a police station with no road traffic, was secure for a demonstration. He emphasized that the issue of pensions affects the entire population, including police officers, and called for police to distinguish between the state, government, and political regimes.

The ODD representative further asserted that police should avoid provocation during demonstrations and that the use of force must be regulated. Bizlall believes police should not act as a substitute for the judiciary and that interventions against protests require precise rules and methodology, reaffirming the right to contest government decisions.

Why it mattersThis event affects all citizens by raising questions about the right to protest and the role of law enforcement in public demonstrations.

Elephant seal "Jackie" reappears in Le Morne, prompting public safety warnings

The elephant seal, previously sighted in Port Louis, was spotted again in Le Morne, leading authorities to reinforce monitoring and advise the public to maintain distance.

The elephant seal known as "Jackie" was sighted in the Le Morne region on Monday, marking its second appearance this month after an initial sighting in Port Louis. The Albion Fisheries Research Centre identified the animal as the same one observed between August 4 and 10 at Les Salines, Fort-William.

Initial observations indicate that Jackie has no visible injuries and appears to be reacting normally to its environment. A veterinary check is planned to assess its health more precisely. Authorities have increased surveillance of the animal and will continue to monitor its behavior and condition in the coming days.

The public is advised not to approach, touch, or feed the elephant seal to limit human interaction and allow it to continue its journey without unnecessary stress. This measure aims to ensure the animal's well-being and prevent any potential harm from human interference.

Why it mattersThe reappearance of the elephant seal "Jackie" affects local residents and tourists by prompting public safety warnings and altering their interactions with the coastal environment.
