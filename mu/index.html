Two inmates escape Rivière-des-Anguilles police station

Police recaptured Stéphane Perrine, 29, and Jeff Wendy Leonel Domingue, 35, on November 1.

Police launched a manhunt after two inmates escaped from the Rivière-des-Anguilles police station on the evening of Saturday, October 31. Stéphane Perrine, 29, and Jeff Wendy Leonel Domingue, 35, fled their cell during a medical emergency involving another detainee. The escape occurred when a police officer was attending to a detainee requiring urgent medical care.

Specialized police units, including the CID, DCIU, and ADSU, searched the Rivière-des-Anguilles and Mahébourg areas extensively. Despite a full day and night of searching, the two fugitives had not been located as of Sunday, November 1. The police have been actively seeking Perrine and Domingue.

The two escaped inmates were recaptured on November 1. The investigation into the circumstances of their escape is ongoing.

MFA youth league sees over 50 goals scored

Flacq U15 boys secured the largest victory with a 7-0 win against Vacoas.

The Mauritius Football Association (MFA) youth championships delivered a goal-filled fifth day on Saturday, October 31, 2026. Across the island, over 50 goals were scored in matches featuring U15 and U17 boys, and U17 girls.

In the U15 boys' matches, Flacq dominated Vacoas with a 7-0 scoreline at La Caverne stadium, while Black River defeated Quatre Bornes 6-2 at Cascavelle. For the U17 boys, Grand Port achieved a significant 7-0 victory over Rivière du Rempart at Grand Bel Air, and Port Louis narrowly beat Savanne 1-0 at Souillac.

The U17 girls saw Curepipe secure a decisive 4-0 win against Pamplemousses at the CEB ground. Other U15 results included Grand Port 4-0 Rivière du Rempart and BBRH 4-1 Moka. U17 matches also featured Black River winning 4-1 against Quatre Bornes and BBRH drawing 1-1 with Moka.

Air Mauritius reports Rs 4.1 billion profit for 2025-2026

The national airline's equity has turned positive, reaching Rs 1.37 billion after being negative since the pandemic.

Air Mauritius announced a profit of Rs 4.1 billion for the 2025-2026 financial year. This result includes accounting adjustments related to the Covid-19 pandemic and a non-realised gain from re-evaluating monetary assets and liabilities.

The airline's audited annual financial statements were approved by its board on October 30, 2026. The profit accounts for re-evaluated provisions for ticket refunds, unused travel vouchers issued during Covid-19, and favourable fuel prices and a strong euro during the past financial year.

Air Mauritius's equity has become positive again, reaching approximately Rs 1.37 billion, a turnaround from its negative standing post-Covid-19. Despite these results, the board urged caution, noting that high fuel prices in 2026-2027 cannot be fully passed on to airfares.

Why it mattersAir Mauritius, the national airline, has achieved a significant profit and positive equity, though it cautions about future fuel costs impacting airfares.

Mauritius federation calls for national unity at Divali event

The Mauritius Sanatan Dharma Temples Federation unveiled plans for new spiritual projects and an expanded education program.

The Indira Gandhi Centre for Indian Culture in Phoenix hosted the Divali Mahotsav 2026 ceremony on Friday, October 30. Organized by the Mauritius Sanatan Dharma Temples Federation (MSDTF), the event aimed to foster social cohesion. MSDTF president Bhojraj Ghoorbin urged national unity, peace, and harmony, referencing recent island tensions and emphasizing Divali's symbolism of light over darkness.

Ghoorbin highlighted the federation's successful Chaar Dhaam Yatra and expanded Gurukul Shiksha Course, now offered in 24 temples across the island to teach Hindu traditions to youth. The federation also confirmed progress on two major projects: the Panch Mukhi Ganesh Mandir at Ganga Talao, set to open before Maha Shivaratri 2027, and the Sanatan Vatika cultural and environmental project in Réduit.

These initiatives aim to ensure the transmission of spiritual knowledge and encourage young Mauritians to preserve cultural heritage and social fabric.

Why it mattersThe Divali Mahotsav 2026 ceremony promoted national unity and unveiled spiritual and educational projects for Mauritians.

Mauritius unionists march on cost of living

Workers in Port-Louis protested rising prices, poor working conditions, and water shortages.

A peaceful march organised by the Platform Komin Syndikal took place in Port-Louis on Saturday afternoon. The protest focused on the high cost of living, difficult working conditions, water supply problems, and industrial relations tensions in several sectors.

Union leaders spoke at the march, which ended at the Jardin de la Compagnie. They denounced the expensive cost of living, the falling value of the Mauritian rupee, the use of foreign labour, and changes to the pension reform.

Speakers urged workers to remain mobilised and continue their collective struggle.

Why it mattersWorkers in Mauritius face higher living costs, poor working conditions, and changes to their pension system.
