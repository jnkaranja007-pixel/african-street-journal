Health · 2026-11-06

Woman shot in neck leaves hospital

Élodie Thomas, 27, returns home after a month of hospital care for a gunshot wound.

Élodie Thomas, 27, left ENT Hospital for home on Friday, November 6, almost a month after being shot in the neck. The young mother was injured on October 9 while returning from work at Winners supermarket. She underwent a seven-hour surgery at Dr. A. G. Jeetoo Hospital to remove the bullet.

A team of 13 foreign specialists cared for Thomas. She can now walk again and is reunited with her family, though she remains under strict medical supervision. Her husband, Jonathan, is with her as she begins her recovery. Investigators continue to question Sergeant Gowtamsing Taukoor, who is suspected of firing the shot.

Sergeant Taukoor faces two provisional charges: "using firearm to intimidate" and "involuntary wounds and blows by imprudence." Health Minister Anil Bachoo confirmed Thomas's 90% recovery.

Why it mattersÉlodie Thomas, a 27-year-old woman, is recovering at home after being shot and undergoing complex surgery.

Mauritius police arrest second murder suspect

Ridwaan Bauluck, 34, detained in connection with the killing of Selven Iyempermal on October 27.

The Major Crime Investigation Team (MCIT) arrested Ridwaan Bauluck, 34, on Thursday, November 5, as a second suspect in the murder of Selven Iyempermal. The victim was attacked by masked men with sabres and sticks in La Caverne, Vacoas, on the evening of Tuesday, October 27, and died from his injuries the next day. Police are investigating the motive as a possible settling of scores, with the victim suspected of involvement in drug trafficking in the area.

Bauluck, a father of one, denied involvement during questioning by the MCIT, stating he was at home with family. Investigators are verifying his alibi, and a relative is expected to be interviewed. He was placed under arrest and spent the night in a cell before appearing in court on Friday, November 6, for a provisional charge.

This arrest follows the apprehension of a first suspect, Imtiyaz Chady, 35, who has been in detention since October 30. The MCIT had previously conducted a police raid at the detoxification center in Vacoas as part of the ongoing investigation into Iyempermal's death.

Why it mattersTwo men have been arrested in connection with the murder of Selven Iyempermal, who was allegedly involved in drug trafficking.

Business · 2026-11-06

Business Mauritius launches water-saving tool

The organisation aims to encourage companies and employees to reduce water consumption through a six-week programme.

Business Mauritius launched a new Water-Saving Toolkit on November 4 at its Water Stewardship Forum in Ébène to promote water conservation among businesses. The forum, supported by the Mauritius Chamber of Agriculture, gathered private sector representatives and public officials to discuss responsible water management and cooperation.

Philippe Espitalier-Noël, president of Business Mauritius's Sustainable and Inclusive Development Commission, stated that water supply difficulties affect the entire community, impacting employees' daily lives and hindering business prosperity. The toolkit is designed as a six-week programme to encourage water-saving habits at work and home, adaptable for various company sizes.

Business Mauritius plans to engage in technical dialogue with the ministry and the Central Water Authority, proposing a joint committee on water to address immediate needs and long-term challenges. CEO Kevin Ramkaloan highlighted initiatives since 2019 to promote efficient water use, including the creation of the Water Stewardship Forum in 2023.

Why it mattersMauritian businesses and their employees face disruptions and increased costs due to water supply issues, affecting operations and living conditions.

CEB prepares for Divali power demand surge

The Central Electricity Board is deploying special teams and urging consumer caution to ensure uninterrupted electricity supply during the festival.

The Central Electricity Board (CEB) is activating special measures to manage an anticipated increase in electricity demand during Divali. The CEB announced on October 30 that its technical teams will be on standby to respond swiftly to any power outages, ensuring a continuous supply for the festival.

The CEB is advising consumers to use electrical installations prudently, warning against overloading circuits and advising against self-manipulation of wiring. They also recommend checking festive lights for damage and limiting the simultaneous use of high-consumption appliances like electric showers, kettles, and air conditioners when decorative lighting is active.

Customers experiencing power failures or supply issues are instructed to contact the CEB service at 130. The board's preparedness aims to prevent disruptions during the period of heightened electricity usage associated with Divali celebrations.

Why it mattersMauritian electricity consumers will be affected by potential power surges and are advised on safe electrical usage during Divali.

Health · 2026-11-06

French surgeon completes second Mauritius mission

Dr Bruno Zipoli consulted 80 patients and performed 15 major surgeries at Jeetoo Hospital.

French orthopedic surgeon Dr Bruno Zipoli has completed his second medical mission at Dr A. G. Jeetoo Hospital in Port-Louis, Mauritius. The specialist conducted consultations and performed surgeries for complex orthopedic conditions from the week of November 2, 2026. This initiative aims to enhance local access to specialized surgical care and foster expertise sharing with Mauritian medical staff.

During this mission, Dr Zipoli consulted 80 patients, performed 15 major surgeries, 10 shoulder infiltrations, and several specialized arthroscopic examinations. Health Minister Anil Bachoo met with the surgeon to review the mission and discuss the strengthening of orthopedic services. Dr Zipoli previously visited Mauritius from February 22 to March 2, 2026, during which he conducted 12 operations and numerous external consultations.

Minister Bachoo stated that this collaborative model will be extended to other medical disciplines. Dr Zipoli is scheduled to return to Mauritius in March 2027.

Why it mattersMauritian patients with complex orthopedic conditions receive specialized surgical care from visiting French surgeon Dr Bruno Zipoli.
