Mauritius football association suspends all matches amid referees’ protest

The referees are seeking better safety conditions at games and greater respect from club leaders and players.

Source: Defi Media · 2026-11-28 · 1 min read

The Mauritius Football Association (MFA) postponed all matches scheduled for this week, announcing the decision on Friday, 27 November, after referees began a protest over safety at games. The suspension applies across the island’s football competitions and has no announced end date.

The MFA said the protest concerns referees’ demand for better security during matches. Officials are also calling on club leaders and players to treat match officials with respect. The postponement covers the men’s and women’s Super Leagues, the first- and second-division championships and the MFA Cup.

The association plans to bring club leaders and referee representatives together early next week to assess the situation and seek solutions. It has not announced when matches will resume.

Why it mattersClubs and players must wait for the MFA’s meeting with referees early next week before the suspended fixtures can be addressed.
