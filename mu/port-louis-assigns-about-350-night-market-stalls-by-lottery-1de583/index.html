Port-Louis assigns about 350 Night Market stalls by lottery

The market opens at Champ-de-Mars on 18 December, with traders paying Rs 150 per place each day, as hundreds seek a stall.

Sources: Ion News and Defi Media · 2026-11-23 · 1 min read

Port-Louis will allocate about 350 stalls at its Night Market by lottery after registration, with hundreds of traders seeking places at the year-end fair. The market at Champ-de-Mars is due to open on 18 December, according to Ion News and Defi Media.

The municipal council will organise the event, and traders who get a stall will pay Rs 150 per day per place, the same rate as last year. The market is scheduled to run from 18 h to 1 h. Ion News reported questions over whether a random draw gives established vendors and new applicants equal chances.

Defi Media said site-preparation work was due to begin on Monday 23 November, including the installation of 15 columns. Ion News said the draw process would need to be transparent and understood by applicants.

Why it mattersApplicants who miss the lottery lose access to a trading place at the year-end fair, while selected vendors pay Rs 150 per day.
