Mauritius opens inquiry after inmate found dead at Melrose prison

The 52-year-old was found on his bed on 25 November, and drugs were later found under the bed and on partly burned paper.

Sources: Ion News and Defi Media · 2026-11-27 · 1 min read

Mauritius has opened an inquiry into the death of a 52-year-old prisoner found on his bed at Melrose prison on 25 November. A prison doctor who was called to the cell could only confirm the death.

Initial indications showed no signs of violence on the body. Closed-circuit television footage showed the man rolling a “ti-papie,” and Scene of Crime Office staff found partly burned paper with traces of a drug. A further search turned up drugs beneath the bed. He was serving a sentence in the Eastern High Security Prison for a drug case and was due for release in March 2027.

Investigators are working to determine the circumstances of the death. A separate report said a second prisoner died at Line Barracks within the same 24 hours, in different circumstances.

Why it mattersPrison staff and investigators must determine the circumstances of the death, with the inquiry now under way.
