Mauritius adds Rs 150 fee per parcel on small online orders

The fee applies to each declared personal parcel, so a Rs 900 basket split across three parcels adds Rs 600 before delivery.

Source: Ion News · 2026-11-23 · 1 min read

The Mauritius Revenue Authority (MRA) has applied an administrative fee of Rs 150 on each declared personal parcel in categories II and III since 1 September 2026. The exemption from value-added tax (VAT) and customs duty remains for personal parcels with a cost, insurance and freight (CIF) value of Rs 1 000 or less.

For a Rs 900 order split into three postal parcels, shoppers pay Rs 150 to the MRA and Rs 50 in postal handling for each parcel, according to the report. The total fees are Rs 600, taking the bill to Rs 1 500, a 67% increase. The postal handling charge for small non-taxable parcels has applied since 10 November 2026.

For a single electronic device priced at Rs 2 500, the Rs 1 500 above the exemption is subject to 15% VAT, or Rs 225. With MRA and delivery charges, the reported final cost is Rs 2 925 to Rs 3 175. The Bank of Mauritius also raised its key rate to 5% on 11 November.

Why it mattersShoppers receiving split postal orders pay Rs 600 in fees on a Rs 900 basket, as charges apply to each parcel.
