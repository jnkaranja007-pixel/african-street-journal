Mauritius farmers get 257.9 hectares for climate adaptation

Minister Arvin Boolell announced land allocation and a new roadmap to address irrigation, pesticide use, and labor shortages.

Source: Ion News · 2026-11-05 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Mauritius agriculture minister Arvin Boolell met with planters on November 3 in Réduit to discuss urgent climate adaptation needs. The session focused on managing irrigation deficits, controlling pesticide use, and addressing daily operational challenges. Minister Boolell stressed the need for local research and practical solutions, encouraging youth to join the sector.

To support agricultural expansion, 257.9 hectares of land will be allocated to planters under a new roadmap for project distribution. A consultant will also assist pig farmers with operational optimization. Discussions with India are underway to expedite the recruitment of agricultural labor.

Planters raised concerns about irrigation, chemical use, and farming practices, requesting a dedicated roundtable for ongoing dialogue. Technical clarifications were provided by ministry officials and the Food and Agricultural Research and Extension Institute (FAREI).

Why it mattersMauritian farmers are directly affected by the allocation of 257.9 hectares of land and new strategies to manage climate change impacts on their operations.
