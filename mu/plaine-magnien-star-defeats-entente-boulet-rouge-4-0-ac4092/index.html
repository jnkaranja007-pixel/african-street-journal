Plaine-Magnien Star defeats Entente Boulet Rouge 4-0

Plaine-Magnien Star secured a dominant victory in their opening National D1 match on November 9, 2026.

Source: Ion News · 2026-11-10 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Plaine-Magnien Star began their National D1 season with a resounding 4-0 win against Entente Boulet Rouge on Monday, November 9, 2026. The visitors controlled the match from the start, opening the scoring in the 26th minute through Ally. Entente Boulet Rouge could not find a way back into the game before halftime.

In the second half, Plaine-Magnien Star extended their lead with a goal from Laboiteuse in the 62nd minute, effectively ending any hopes of a comeback for the home team. Toinette added a third goal in the 79th minute, and Laboiteuse completed his brace and the scoring in the 90th minute.

This decisive victory gives Plaine-Magnien Star a strong start to the 2026/27 campaign, building confidence early in the season. The team demonstrated both offensive effectiveness and defensive solidity throughout the 90 minutes.
