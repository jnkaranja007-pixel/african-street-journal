Mauritius sets end-2028 deadline for 3G mobile networks

Operators have begun withdrawing the service on separate schedules, while the regulator requires them to protect coverage and inform subscribers.

Sources: Defi Media and Ion News · 2026-11-26 · 1 min read

Mauritius’s mobile operators have begun gradually withdrawing 3G service, which is due to disappear by the end of 2028, the Information and Communication Technologies Authority (ICTA) said in a statement on Wednesday, 25 November. Each operator is following its own timetable.

The government approved the transition on 3 October 2025 to support the expansion of 4G and 5G. ICTA says users of devices that work only on 3G—including some phones, vehicle tracking devices and business communication equipment—should replace them. The government had planned trade-in programmes to help consumers obtain compatible phones.

Operators must ensure the withdrawal does not reduce mobile coverage and inform subscribers of their shutdown schedules. ICTA also urged the public to stop importing or using devices compatible only with 3G.

Why it mattersPeople and businesses relying on 3G-only phones, vehicle trackers or communications equipment must replace them before service ends by the close of 2028.
