Port-Louis waterfront festival returns with more than 30 food stalls

The 20-22 November event caps dish prices at Rs 275 and adds nightly concerts, DJ sets and radio activities.

Source: Defi Media · 2026-11-18 · 1 min read

The Port-Louis Waterfront Food Festival will return from 20 to 22 November for its second edition, with more than 30 food stalls offering Mauritian dishes and food inspired by Indian, Korean, other Asian and Western cuisines. The event is organised by Défi Digital and Radio Plus, with Pocket Island Solutions Ltd and Coca-Cola as partners.

Organisers say prices for dishes will not exceed Rs 275. The stalls will sell options including biryani, curries, noodles, dumplings, street food and seafood, alongside desserts and drinks. The festival follows a first edition held in July.

Concerts, DJ sets and other music performances are scheduled each evening from 19 to 21 hours. Radio Plus will also run games and activities, distribute gifts and bring its presenters to the waterfront during the three-day event.

Why it mattersVisitors can buy festival dishes for no more than Rs 275, with more than 30 stalls operating from 20 to 22 November.
