Motorcyclist Dies in Poudre-d'Or Collision

A motorcyclist died and a passenger was injured in a crash involving a motorcycle and a van on October 19.

Source: Ion News · 2026-10-21 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

A motorcyclist died instantly and another person was injured in a collision between a motorcycle and a van in Poudre-d'Or on Monday, October 19. Emergency services and the SAMU responded to the scene. The motorcyclist succumbed to injuries sustained during the impact.

The injured passenger was transported to SSRN Hospital in Pamplemousses for treatment. The exact circumstances leading to the accident are still under investigation by authorities.

This incident highlights ongoing road safety concerns. Investigations are underway to determine the cause of the crash and prevent future occurrences.
