MFA youth league sees over 50 goals scored

Flacq U15 boys secured the largest victory with a 7-0 win against Vacoas.

Source: Ion News · 2026-11-02 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

The Mauritius Football Association (MFA) youth championships delivered a goal-filled fifth day on Saturday, October 31, 2026. Across the island, over 50 goals were scored in matches featuring U15 and U17 boys, and U17 girls.

In the U15 boys' matches, Flacq dominated Vacoas with a 7-0 scoreline at La Caverne stadium, while Black River defeated Quatre Bornes 6-2 at Cascavelle. For the U17 boys, Grand Port achieved a significant 7-0 victory over Rivière du Rempart at Grand Bel Air, and Port Louis narrowly beat Savanne 1-0 at Souillac.

The U17 girls saw Curepipe secure a decisive 4-0 win against Pamplemousses at the CEB ground. Other U15 results included Grand Port 4-0 Rivière du Rempart and BBRH 4-1 Moka. U17 matches also featured Black River winning 4-1 against Quatre Bornes and BBRH drawing 1-1 with Moka.

Why it mattersYoung footballers in Mauritius participated in competitive matches across multiple age categories.
