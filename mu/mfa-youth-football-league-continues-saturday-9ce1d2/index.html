MFA youth football league continues Saturday

The Mauritius Football Association's youth championships will feature U15 and U17 boys' matches across six grounds.

Source: Ion News · 2026-11-05 · 1 min read

The sixth round of the Mauritius Football Association (MFA) youth championships is scheduled for Saturday, November 7. Only the U15 and U17 boys' categories will compete, with the women's teams taking a break. Matches will take place on six fields: La Caverne Grand Bel Air, Bon Accueil, Guy Rozemont, Sir Gaëtan Duval, S. G. D Grand Gaube, and L’Agrément St Pierre.

For each venue, the U15 matches will kick off at 9:30 AM, followed by the U17 games at 12:00 PM on the same field. The fixtures include Vacoas versus Pamplemousses at La Caverne Grand Bel Air, and Flacq against Grand Port at Bon Accueil.

Other U15 and U17 pairings include Quatre Bornes vs Savanne at Guy Rozemont, Curepipe vs BBRH at Sir Gaëtan Duval, Rivière du Rempart vs Port Louis at S.G.D Grand Gaube, and Moka vs Black River at L’Agrément St Pierre.

Why it mattersYoung male footballers in Mauritius will compete in the MFA youth league on November 7.
