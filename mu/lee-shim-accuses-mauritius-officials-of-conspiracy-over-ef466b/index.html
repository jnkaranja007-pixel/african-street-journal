Lee Shim accuses Mauritius officials of conspiracy over racing and betting

The 72-year-old filed a police complaint naming the attorney general, the Financial Crimes Commission chief and Kulwant Kumar Ubheeram.

Sources: Ion News and Defi Media · 2026-11-19 · 1 min read

Mauritian businessman Jean-Michel Lee Shim filed a conspiracy complaint at Line-Barracks police station on Wednesday, 18 November, accusing Attorney General Gavin Glover, Financial Crimes Commission (FCC) director general Titrudeo Dawoodarry and Kulwant Kumar Ubheeram of trying to remove him from horse racing and betting.

Lee Shim, who says he consults for SMS Pariaz Ltd and promotes the Petit Gamin Horse Racing Project, alleges the three coordinated investigations into him and his financial interests. He says the FCC prepared an arrest document before hearing his account and wants police to investigate urgently; he submitted affidavits and a USB drive, according to Ion News.

The FCC investigation concerns SMS Pariaz’s operations, including Lee Shim’s fees and licensing conditions. He is expected at the commission’s Réduit Triangle headquarters early next week after his questioning was postponed over health-related unavailability.

Why it mattersLee Shim must answer FCC questioning at Réduit Triangle early next week, and his complaint asks police to investigate allegations that, if the FCC issues a provisional charge, would bar him from horse racing and betting under the Gambling Regulatory Authority Act.
