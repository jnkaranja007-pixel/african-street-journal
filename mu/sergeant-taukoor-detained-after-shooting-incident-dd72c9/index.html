Sergeant Taukoor Detained After Shooting Incident

A 57-year-old police sergeant will remain in custody until October 26 following a shooting incident involving a 27-year-old woman.

Sergeant Gautam Singh Taukoor, aged 57, appeared in the Rose-Hill district court on Monday, October 19. The hearing was conducted with significant security measures. Taukoor is suspected of firing a weapon at Élodie Thomas, 27, in Camp-Levieux on Friday, October 9. He faces provisional charges of "Using Firearm to Intimidate" and "Involuntary Wounds and Blows by Imprudence."

The court denied a request for Taukoor's release, ordering him to be returned to police custody. His detention is a precautionary measure while investigations continue into the exact circumstances of the shooting. The case has proceeded with discretion due to security concerns.

Taukoor's next court appearance is scheduled for Monday, October 26, when he will continue to be held. The incident occurred on October 9, and the charges were filed on October 10. The investigation into the event is ongoing.

Why it mattersThe sergeant's detention affects the safety and legal standing of the woman he is accused of intimidating and wounding.
