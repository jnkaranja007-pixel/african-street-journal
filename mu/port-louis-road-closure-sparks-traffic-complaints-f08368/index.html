Port-Louis road closure sparks traffic complaints

Emergency repairs on Rue Mgr Leen, closed since November 10, continue until December 21.

Source: Ion News · 2026-11-12 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

A temporary closure of a section of Rue Mgr Leen in Port-Louis, between Poulin and D’Alembert streets, is causing significant traffic disruption. The emergency roadworks, which began on November 10, 2026, aim to repair damaged pavement and a retaining wall following a landslide. The repairs are scheduled to last until December 21, 2026.

Motorists have complained about severe traffic jams and feeling unprepared for the closure. Activist Suttyhudeo Tengur addressed the criticism on Facebook on November 12, urging patience and stating that road safety must be prioritized over daily traffic inconveniences. He emphasized the necessity of the urgent repairs to prevent further damage and accidents.

Tengur refuted claims that drivers were not adequately warned, citing radio announcements and communiqués made days before the closure. He suggested that residents leave home earlier to mitigate delays, noting that the current school holiday period generally leads to lighter traffic in the capital.

Why it mattersRoad users in Port-Louis face extended travel times due to emergency repairs on Rue Mgr Leen.
