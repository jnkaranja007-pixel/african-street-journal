Port-Louis festival brings more than 30 food stalls to waterfront

The three-day event pairs Mauritian and international dishes with nightly music, while keeping each dish at no more than Rs 275.

Source: Defi Media · 2026-11-20 · 1 min read

The second Port-Louis Waterfront Food Festival opened Friday at the waterfront, bringing more than 30 food stalls to the site for three days. Visitors can choose from Mauritian dishes, including briani and faratas, alongside Korean food, street food and seafood.

Dish prices will not exceed Rs 275, according to the festival information reported by Defi Media. Radio Plus is adding interactive games, gifts and activities, while a Kids' Corner is set up for younger visitors during the school holidays.

Concerts, DJs and other music performances are scheduled each evening from 19 to 21 hours. The festival follows a first edition held in July.

Why it mattersFestival vendors are serving visitors for three days, with prices for dishes capped at Rs 275.
