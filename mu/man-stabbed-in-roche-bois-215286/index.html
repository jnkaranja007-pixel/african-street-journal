man stabbed in Roche-Bois

Police found a 25-year-old unemployed man bleeding from stab wounds near Roche-Bois garden.

Source: Ion News · 2026-10-26 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Police responded to an incident near Roche-Bois garden on the afternoon of Thursday, October 22. Officers discovered a 25-year-old unemployed man lying on the ground with significant stab wounds.

The victim was immediately transported to Dr. A. G. Jeetoo Hospital for treatment and observation. His condition has prevented investigators from taking his statement as an inquiry has been opened into the incident.

The attack occurred in the Roche-Bois area. The victim sustained serious injuries from a bladed weapon.
