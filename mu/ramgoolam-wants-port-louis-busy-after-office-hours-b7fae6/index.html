Ramgoolam wants Port-Louis busy after office hours

Speaking at the Caudan Waterfront's 30th anniversary, Prime Minister Navin Ramgoolam said the capital should be a place where Mauritians gather after work.

Source: Defi Media · 2026-11-21 · 1 min read

Prime Minister Navin Ramgoolam said on Friday evening that Port-Louis should stay lively after dark, speaking at the 30th anniversary of the Caudan Waterfront.

Speaking at the Caudan Arts Centre, Ramgoolam called for a capital that is more welcoming, more accessible and more active. He said Mauritians should meet in Port-Louis after work and that the city should regain its role as a place for social life and cultural activity.

The report gives no specific measures, funding or timetable for the plan.

Why it mattersBusinesses and cultural venues in Port-Louis would be the ones affected if the call for evening activity is turned into policy, but the report sets no measures or timetable.
