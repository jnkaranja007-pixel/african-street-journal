Mauritius banks raise lending rates by 0.25%

MCB’s prime lending rate is 7.15% from 23 November, while Absa’s rose to 7.95% on 18 November.

Sources: Ion News and Defi Media · 2026-11-25 · 1 min read

Mauritius banks are raising lending rates by 0.25% from late November after the central bank’s Key Rate rose to 5% on 11 November. The change affects borrowers with housing and car loans, as well as people seeking new bank credit.

The Mauritius Commercial Bank (MCB) set its Prime Lending Rate at 7.15% from 23 November, and Absa’s rate reached 7.95% on 18 November. Ion News reported that the higher rates will increase loan instalments and total borrowing costs, reducing households’ monthly disposable income. Savings account rates are also rising.

The State Bank of Mauritius had not announced its new rate by publication. MauBank’s committee was scheduled to meet on 26 November, with new rates due that afternoon. The Mauritius Housing Company had applied the increase from 16 November.

Why it mattersMauritian borrowers face larger monthly instalments and less disposable income as the 0.25% increase takes effect from late November.
