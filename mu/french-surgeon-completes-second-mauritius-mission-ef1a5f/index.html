French surgeon completes second Mauritius mission

Dr Bruno Zipoli consulted 80 patients and performed 15 major surgeries at Jeetoo Hospital.

Source: Ion News · 2026-11-06 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

French orthopedic surgeon Dr Bruno Zipoli has completed his second medical mission at Dr A. G. Jeetoo Hospital in Port-Louis, Mauritius. The specialist conducted consultations and performed surgeries for complex orthopedic conditions from the week of November 2, 2026. This initiative aims to enhance local access to specialized surgical care and foster expertise sharing with Mauritian medical staff.

During this mission, Dr Zipoli consulted 80 patients, performed 15 major surgeries, 10 shoulder infiltrations, and several specialized arthroscopic examinations. Health Minister Anil Bachoo met with the surgeon to review the mission and discuss the strengthening of orthopedic services. Dr Zipoli previously visited Mauritius from February 22 to March 2, 2026, during which he conducted 12 operations and numerous external consultations.

Minister Bachoo stated that this collaborative model will be extended to other medical disciplines. Dr Zipoli is scheduled to return to Mauritius in March 2027.
