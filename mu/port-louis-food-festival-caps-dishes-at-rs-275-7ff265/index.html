Port Louis food festival caps dishes at Rs 275

The three-day Waterfront event brings together more than 30 food stands, with concerts and DJ sets scheduled from 19 h to 21 h.

Source: Defi Media · 2026-11-22 · 1 min read

The second Port-Louis Waterfront Food Festival is running through Sunday evening in Mauritius’ capital, bringing food stands, music and leisure activities to the waterfront. Organised by Défi Digital and Radio Plus with the Port Louis Waterfront, the event began Friday.

More than 30 stands offer Mauritian, Indian, Asian and Western dishes, from briani and curries to dumplings, grilled food and desserts. Organisers set a maximum price of Rs 275 per dish. Radio Plus is running games and gift giveaways, while a Kids’ Corner provides activities for children.

Concerts and DJ sets are scheduled from 19 h to 21 h on Saturday and Sunday. Local restaurateurs and artisans are also presenting their specialties at the festival.
