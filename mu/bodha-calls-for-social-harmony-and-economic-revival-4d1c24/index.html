Bodha Calls for Social Harmony and Economic Revival

Rassemblement Mauricien leader Nando Bodha urges restoring trust and lessons from past unrest to rebuild the nation.

Source: Defi Media · 2026-10-21 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X

Nando Bodha, leader of the Rassemblement Mauricien, has called for urgent action to restore social harmony and rebuild trust following recent unrest. He emphasized the need to address the root causes of public discontent, questioning why young people are protesting and what underlying issues fuel their anger. Bodha stressed that the nation must find answers to these fundamental questions without resorting to communal divisions.

In a Facebook post, Bodha warned against the dangers of a society where public order breaks down, leading communities to seek their own solutions. He highlighted that social peace and national harmony have a price that must be paid. The leader also pointed to statements by the Prime Minister regarding drug traffickers and police fragility, suggesting these raise further questions about the reasons for public demonstrations.

Bodha insists on the necessity of relaunching the economy and learning from past mistakes to reconstruct a country where every citizen is treated with equal dignity and has the same opportunities to succeed. He advocates for a unified approach to national challenges, urging the population to reject violence and work towards a more equitable future for all children.

Why it mattersNando Bodha's call for social peace and economic revival directly impacts all Mauritians by addressing societal issues and future opportunities.
