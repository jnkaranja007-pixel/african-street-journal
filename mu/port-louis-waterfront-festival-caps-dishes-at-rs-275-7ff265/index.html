Port-Louis Waterfront festival caps dishes at Rs 275

More than 30 food stands are part of the second edition, with concerts and DJ sets scheduled from 19 h to 21 h on Saturday and Sunday.

Source: Defi Media · 2026-11-21 · 1 min read

The second Port-Louis Waterfront Food Festival is running through Sunday at the capital’s waterfront, combining food stands, music and leisure activities. More than 30 vendors are offering Mauritian, Indian, Asian and Western dishes in the event’s second edition.

Organisers set a ceiling of Rs 275 per dish, while visitors can choose from meals including briani, curries, seafood, street food and desserts. The three-day programme also includes a children’s corner and interactive games, giveaways and meet-and-greets hosted by Radio Plus.

Concerts and DJ sets are scheduled from 19 h to 21 h on Saturday and Sunday. Défi Digital and Radio Plus organised the festival with Port Louis Waterfront, with support from Pocket Island Solutions Ltd and Coca-Cola.

Why it mattersFood vendors at the waterfront must price each dish at no more than Rs 275 during the festival, which runs through Sunday.
