Port-Louis court grants bail to man charged in Iyempermal murder inquiry

Mohammad Reedwan Baulauck denies involvement and faces a provisional accusation of obstructing the course of justice.

Source: Defi Media · 2026-11-25 · 1 min read

A court in Port-Louis granted bail on Tuesday 24 November to Mohammad Reedwan Baulauck, who was arrested in the inquiry into Selven Iyempermal’s murder. Baulauck faces a provisional accusation of obstructing the course of justice and denies involvement.

The court set bail at Rs 75 000 and required Baulauck to sign a Rs 500 000 debt acknowledgment. He must report to the nearest police station to his home every Friday between 6 h and 18 h. The Major Crime Investigation Team arrested him on 5 November.

Iyempermal, 34, was attacked at a house in La Caverne, Vacoas, on the evening of 27 October and died at Candos hospital on 28 October. His friend told police that six armed, masked men had entered the house; an autopsy found he died from haemorrhagic shock caused by multiple injuries.

Why it mattersBaulauck must meet the court’s bail conditions, including weekly police reporting and the required Rs 75 000 bail and Rs 500 000 debt acknowledgment.
