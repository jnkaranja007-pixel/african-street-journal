Husband stabs wife before autistic son

A 7-year-old boy witnessed his mother being stabbed by his father on November 2.

Source: Defi Media · 2026-11-09 · 1 min read

A 7-year-old boy witnessed his mother being stabbed by his father at their home on November 2. The child, who has autism, reportedly saw his father attack his mother with a knife. His 62-year-old grandmother was also present during the violent incident.

The alleged attacker, a prison guard on leave, reportedly took a knife from the kitchen and threatened his wife before stabbing her in the right side. The victim was taken to Dr A. G. Jeetoo Hospital, where her condition is described as serious. Her sister transported her to the hospital after neighbours intervened to subdue the suspect.

The couple had been married for 12 years and have a son. Family members report that conflicts related to alcohol, finances, and the husband's gambling habits have escalated over the past three to four years, leading to the deterioration of their relationship.
