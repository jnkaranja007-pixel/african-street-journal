Mauritius opens 2027 salary talks as inflation reaches 4,3 %

The first technical meeting is set for Friday 27 November, with Statistics Mauritius presenting its 2026 inflation data and projections.

Sources: Ion News and Defi Media · 2026-11-26 · 1 min read

Mauritius begins consultations on 2027 salary compensation at 14 h 30 on Friday 27 November at Victoria House in Port-Louis. Labour and Industrial Relations Minister Reza Uteem will chair the first technical meeting, attended by government, employer and union representatives.

Statistics Mauritius will present statistics and inflation projections for 2026 before the social partners give their initial views. Official figures put overall inflation at 4,3 % in October 2026, up from 3,5 % a year earlier; annual inflation was 6,4 %, compared with 4,1 % at the same point in 2025. The Bank of Mauritius expects a 4,7 % rate for 2026 in December.

The partners must send their memorandums to the labour ministry by Friday 4 December. The discussions follow salary adjustments of Rs 635 in 2026 for employees earning less than Rs 50 000.

Why it mattersGovernment, employer and union representatives must send their positions to the labour ministry by Friday 4 December.
