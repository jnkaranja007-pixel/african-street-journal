Somalia agrees to buy Pakistani fighter jets

A Turkish defense outlet reported Somalia's agreement to purchase JF-17 Thunder jets from Pakistan, with no official confirmation.

Source: Somali Guardian · 2026-10-21 · 2 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Somalia has reportedly agreed to acquire JF-17 Thunder fighter jets from Pakistan, according to a Turkish defense outlet. Neither the Somali nor Pakistani governments have confirmed the existence of a contract, and details regarding the price, number of aircraft, or delivery timeline remain undisclosed. This claim follows months of unconfirmed reports, including a February assertion that Somalia was negotiating for up to 24 Block III variants in a deal valued at approximately $900 million, figures also unconfirmed by officials.

The potential acquisition comes after the U.N. Security Council lifted its arms embargo on Somalia's federal government in December 2023, allowing for the purchase of military equipment. However, the resolution urged Mogadishu to improve weapons management and prevent unauthorized access to imported arms. Analysts suggest that acquiring advanced fighter jets would necessitate significant investment in new airbase infrastructure, air traffic control systems, maintenance facilities, and pilot training programs.

Diplomatic engagements have included talks between Somalia's Defence Minister Ahmed Moallim Fiqi and Pakistani naval and air force chiefs in August, where Pakistan expressed readiness to support Somali Air Force modernization. Subsequently, Pakistan's air defence commander visited Mogadishu in September, meeting with President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud and military commanders. During the meeting, a model of the JF-17 jet was presented, though no purchase announcement was made.
