Somalia's president asks to visit Uganda again within two weeks

Hassan Sheikh Mohamud wants a one-day meeting with Yoweri Museveni, his second trip to Uganda in a fortnight.

Source: Somali Guardian · 2026-11-16 · 1 min read

Mogadishu has asked for a one-day visit by Somalia's President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud to Kampala, where he would meet President Yoweri Museveni, The Somali Stream reported on Sunday. The trip would be his second to Uganda in a fortnight.

On his late October visit, Mohamud asked Museveni to hold off pulling out Ugandan troops that are holding key strategic towns in the Lower Shabelle region under a bilateral agreement. Museveni turned the request down, and the drawdown has already begun. Uganda has been one of the largest troop contributors to African Union missions in Somalia since 2007, and it keeps a separate bilateral presence in sensitive areas.

It is not clear whether Mohamud would repeat the troop request if the visit is approved. The outreach comes as his government faces a Supreme Court ruling that the extension of his term was unlawful, and Mohamud has sent two special advisers to several countries to seek financial support from key African Union and European Union partners.

Why it mattersUgandan troops leaving the Lower Shabelle region will change which forces hold key towns there, and the outcome of the visit will decide whether that drawdown is reversed.
