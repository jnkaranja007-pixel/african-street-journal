Somalia orders UAE ambassador out within 72 hours, recalls envoy

The cabinet said the measures answer alleged Emirati interference in Somalia's sovereignty, territorial integrity and national security, and the UAE foreign ministry had not.

Source: Somali Guardian · 2026-11-20 · 1 min read

Somalia ordered the United Arab Emirates ambassador to leave within 72 hours and recalled its envoy from Abu Dhabi on Thursday, after a cabinet meeting chaired by Prime Minister Hamza Abdi Barre. Information Minister Abdifatah Kasim Mohamud announced the steps, saying the foreign ministry had been instructed to notify the Emirati ambassador and summon Somalia's ambassador in the UAE, Hussein Abdi Halane, for consultations.

The cabinet said it had reviewed a foreign ministry report on what it called continued Emirati interference in Somalia's internal affairs and security. President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud had accused the UAE of signing agreements on the ports of Berbera, Bosaso and Kismayo with regional authorities without federal consent.

Somalia annulled all agreements with the UAE in January, and authorities in Somaliland, Puntland and Jubbaland rejected Mogadishu's authority to cancel arrangements involving their territories. Somalia has since closed its airspace to Emirati military and cargo aircraft.

Why it mattersRegional administrations in Somaliland, Puntland and Jubbaland, which keep their own port and security ties with Abu Dhabi, now face a federal ban on those arrangements.
