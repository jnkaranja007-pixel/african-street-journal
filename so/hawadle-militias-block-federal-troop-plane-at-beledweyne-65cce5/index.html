Hawadle militias block federal troop plane at Beledweyne airport

Two Djiboutian peacekeepers were killed and two wounded in a clash as the aircraft aborted its landing and returned to its departure point.

Source: Somali Guardian · 2026-11-20 · 1 min read

Hawadle clan militias seized the runway at Ugaas Khalif Airport in Beledweyne, central Somalia, on Thursday to prevent a plane carrying federal troops from landing. Djiboutian peacekeepers based nearby tried to push them back, and the aircraft returned to its point of departure.

Local reports and a spokesman for the Macawisley, a militia drawn mainly from the Hawadle clan, said two Djiboutian peacekeepers were killed and two wounded. At least five Macawisley fighters were injured. Spokesman Mumin Mohamed Halane said the group would not allow another plane carrying federal troops to land.

The airport dispute follows weeks of tension between clan leaders and federal and Hirshabelle authorities. Federal efforts to ease the standoff have not persuaded clan leaders, and the militia’s seizure of the runway has disrupted troop movements by air.

Why it mattersDjiboutian peacekeepers and federal troops face a blocked route for air arrivals after the militia seized the runway and the plane turned back.
