Somali intelligence commander defects to Al-Shabaab

A senior NISA commander reportedly took sensitive intelligence files and equipment when he joined the militant group.

Source: Somali Guardian · 2026-11-09 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

A senior commander of Somalia’s National Intelligence and Security Agency (NISA) paramilitary Duufaan unit has defected to Al-Shabaab. The commander, identified as Luqman, led the Turkish-trained unit and is believed to have taken laptops, communication gadgets, and files with him to Al-Shabab-controlled territory in southern Somalia.

Analyst Rashid Abdi stated that NISA is now severely compromised, with Al-Shabaab potentially gaining access to critical intelligence data, including names and addresses of informants in sensitive areas. Abdi noted that NISA is attempting to manage the crisis, but it may be too late.

The defection escalates an intelligence war within Somalia. The Duufaan unit was trained by Turkey and operated under Somalia’s spy agency.

Why it mattersSomalia's national security is threatened by a potential intelligence breach following the defection of a senior NISA commander.
