Two Labour MPs quit Somaliland group over Israel and chair’s party ties

A third Labour MP has suspended her involvement, citing the chair’s link to Restore Britain and the group’s direction after Israel recognised Somaliland.

Source: Somali Guardian · 2026-11-19 · 1 min read

Two Labour MPs have resigned from the UK Parliament’s All-Party Parliamentary Group on Somaliland, while a third has suspended her involvement. Kim Johnson, the group’s former co-chair, and Abtisam Mohamed, its former vice-chair, quit; Kerry McCarthy said she would stay away until Conservative chair Sir Gavin Williamson steps down.

Johnson told Middle East Eye she left a May parliamentary event after learning an Israeli government official was expected. She said Israel’s December 2025 recognition of Somaliland had changed the group’s direction and deepened divisions in communities, with some people facing attacks for pro-Palestinian views. Mohamed said she left because of long-term differences over the group’s direction.

McCarthy objected to Williamson’s June signature on a letter from Restore Britain leader Rupert Lowe calling for mass deportations of refugees admitted through safe and legal routes. Williamson’s office said it did not invite the Israeli official and understood the invitation came from Somaliland’s UK mission. The group has not met since before Parliament’s summer recess.

Why it mattersSomaliland’s UK communities are divided over the group’s direction, while McCarthy is withholding involvement until Williamson leaves as chair.
