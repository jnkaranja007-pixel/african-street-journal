Turkish airstrike kills Somali woman in Jameeco

Ankara's air campaign supporting President Mohamud's government hits civilians near an SNA ambush site.

Source: Somali Guardian · 2026-11-05 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

An elderly Somali woman, Hawo Madow, died on October 27 when a Turkish airstrike hit the village of Jameeco, 160 kilometres northwest of Mogadishu. The strike killed five people, including Hawo, and critically injured another man, according to village resident Suley Ibrahim Dhere. This attack followed an ambush on a Somali National Army convoy near the same highway by Al-Shabaab militants.

Suley stated that Turkish aircraft carried out the bombardment, questioning the action as the village had done nothing to provoke an attack. Hawo Madow was preparing tea when the strike occurred. Her husband, Nasir, denied any Al-Shabaab presence in the village, stating, "Not at all! We did not even see a single Al-Shabaab fighter here."

Hawo Madow is survived by three sons and two daughters. Her niece, Mumino Osman Bashow, described the airstrike shattering the lives of villagers struggling with drought. Mumino dismissed the idea that the aircraft targeted militants, stating, "We are not Al-Shabaab, and there are no Al-Shabaab fighters here."

Why it mattersCivilians like Hawo Madow are killed and injured by airstrikes conducted by Turkey in support of the Somali president.
