Somalia leaves transport minister’s armed confrontation unanswered

A video showed State Minister Abdikarim Abdow Hayder carrying an AK-47 to the ministry gates amid a dispute over vehicle number-plate kickbacks.

Source: Somali Guardian · 2026-11-28 · 1 min read

A video circulated on Thursday showed Somalia’s State Minister of Transport Abdikarim Abdow Hayder carrying an AK-47 and wearing a tactical vest as he approached the Ministry of Transport and Civil Aviation in Mogadishu. Witnesses and journalists described it as an attempt to confront and kill Transport Minister Mohamed Farah Nuh.

Media reports linked the confrontation to a dispute over how the officials divided proceeds from vehicle number plates; Hayder reportedly believed he had been denied his share. More than 72 hours after the video appeared, the government had neither arrested nor dismissed him.

Days earlier, the civil aviation agency accused Nuh of entering its offices with armed troops and raised safety concerns. The ministry’s director general had also entered the minister’s office carrying an AK-47 during an earlier dispute, the report said.

Why it mattersThe government has yet to arrest or dismiss Hayder more than 72 hours after the video appeared, leaving officials in the transport ministry without a reported resolution to the dispute.
