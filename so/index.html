World Bank weighs suspending Somalia budget support after court ruling

The bank is considering a temporary funding halt after the Supreme Court said President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud's term ended on May 15, 2026.

Africa Intelligence reported on Monday that the World Bank is considering a short pause in budget support to Somalia, following a Supreme Court ruling that President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud's term has run out. The court said his four-year term ended on May 15, 2026, and that the mandates of both houses of parliament ended on April 14.

Issued on Nov. 5, the ruling gave political leaders 30 days to agree on an electoral framework and 90 days to hold elections, while restricting the government to routine caretaker duties. The court held that constitutional amendments extending terms from four years to five did not apply retrospectively. The presidency has disputed parts of the court's jurisdiction and findings.

Budget support has recently been projected at $150 million or more a year, with the World Bank a major provider. Somalia's 2026 budget framework projected about $1.3 billion in revenue and grants, including nearly $470 million in domestic revenue. A pause would hit the federal government's ability to meet recurrent costs, including salaries and transfers to federal member states.

Why it mattersFederal employees and member states face pressure on salaries and transfers if the World Bank suspends budget support projected at $150 million or more.

Politics · 2026-11-18

Mohamud accuses UAE of seeking to divide Somalia and Sudan

The Somali president said the Emirates had returned from Libya and Yemen divided, as Somalia faces disputes over elections and regional authority.

Somalia’s President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud accused the United Arab Emirates of seeking to divide Somalia and Sudan in a video message released by his office on Tuesday. He said the Emirates had returned from Libya and Yemen divided, and urged people dealing with it to study those experiences.

The remarks came weeks after Somalia’s Constitutional Court ruled that parliament’s extension of Mohamud’s term was unlawful and ordered elections within 90 days. The government rejected the ruling and occupied the court’s premises; the chief justice and five other judges then sought shelter at former President Sharif Sheikh Ahmed’s residence in Mogadishu after receiving death threats, according to Somali Guardian.

Mohamud’s government is also in dispute with opposition leaders, while relations with Puntland and Jubaland have deteriorated into intermittent military clashes. The report says there is no agreed election path; in the Southwest, forces loyal to ousted regional leader Abdiasis Laftagaren reclaimed five towns in Bay last month.

Why it mattersThe Constitutional Court ordered elections within 90 days, but the government rejected its ruling and no agreed election path has emerged.

Security · 2026-11-18

Somaliland detains five Americans over alleged national security threat

The intelligence agency said it monitored the US nationals after their arrival; a person familiar with the matter said they left on Monday.

Somaliland’s National Intelligence Agency said it briefly detained five US nationals over what it described as a threat to national security. In a post on X late Sunday, the agency said it had monitored them since they arrived and investigated their activities.

A person familiar with the matter said the five worked for Parallel Black, a US company, and left Somaliland on an Ethiopian Airlines flight on Monday afternoon. A November 4 letter seen by Reuters showed Somaliland’s foreign affairs minister had invited them to conduct feasibility studies for potential investments.

The agency’s director-general, Jama Mohamoud Egal, Somaliland’s foreign affairs ministry and the US State Department did not immediately respond to requests for comment. Parallel Black also did not immediately respond.

Why it mattersThe five Americans left Somaliland on an Ethiopian Airlines flight on Monday afternoon after the intelligence agency detained them.
