Somalia launches fourth cybersecurity forum

Mogadishu University and the National Communications Authority host a two-day event to boost digital security.

Source: Radio Dalsan · 2026-10-28 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Somalia's 4th National Cyber Security Forum began today in Mogadishu, organized by the National Communications Authority (NCA) and Mogadishu University. The two-day event brings together government, telecom companies, ISPs, banks, universities, and civil society to strengthen cybersecurity capabilities under the theme “Building Trust and Cyber Resilience Through Security by Design.”

Mogadishu University President Dr. Ibrahim Mohamed Mursal highlighted the university's new Cybersecurity and Artificial Intelligence department, stating universities are key to developing needed skills. NCA Director General Mustafa Yasin Sheikh noted progress including the Somali Computer Incident Response Team (SOMCIRT) launch and capacity-building programs, calling the forum a chance to share knowledge and develop solutions.

Minister of Communications and Technology H.E. MP Mohamed Adan Mo’allin officially opened the forum, commending the organizers and mentioning the planned National Cybersecurity Academy. He urged participants to develop practical recommendations to enhance national cybersecurity and public awareness, hoping for outcomes that benefit the Somali people.

Why it mattersThe forum aims to strengthen cybersecurity capabilities and promote secure digital systems for the Somali people.
