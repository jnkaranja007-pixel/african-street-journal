Somaliland detains five Americans over security threat

The five US nationals left the territory on an Ethiopian Airlines flight after being monitored by the intelligence agency.

Source: Somali Guardian · 2026-11-17 · 1 min read

The national intelligence agency in the self-declared Republic of Somaliland briefly detained five US nationals over national security threats, according to a post on X late on Sunday. The National Intelligence Agency of Somaliland stated that it monitored the group since their arrival and conducted a lengthy investigation into their activities.

The five individuals work for Parallel Black, a company registered in the US state of Delaware less than two months ago, a person familiar with the matter stated. They entered the territory following an invitation by Somaliland Foreign Affairs Minister Abdirahman Dahir Adam via a November 4 letter to conduct feasibility studies for potential investments.

The five Americans departed on an Ethiopian Airlines flight on Monday afternoon. Representatives for Parallel Black, the National Intelligence Agency Director-General Jama Mohamoud Egal, the Somaliland Ministry of Foreign Affairs, and the US State Department did not immediately respond to requests for comment.

Why it mattersFive US nationals working for Parallel Black were detained and then left Somaliland on a Monday afternoon flight following an intelligence investigation.
