Somalia’s speaker accuses Kenya, Ethiopia and Uganda of colluding with Al-Shabaab

Abdulkadir Mohamed Nur alleges Israel has assembled the countries, the militants and forces loyal to a former regional leader against Mogadishu.

Source: Somali Guardian · 2026-11-25 · 1 min read

Somalia’s parliamentary speaker, Abdulkadir Mohamed Nur, accused Kenya, Ethiopia and Uganda of colluding with Al-Shabaab in an address in Mogadishu. He alleged that Israel had assembled the three countries, the militants and forces loyal to former South West State leader Abdiaziz Hassan Mohamed Laftagareen into a front against Somalia’s federal government.

Nur also said unnamed foreign powers sought to colonise Somalia and described the security structure backing the government as compromised. His remarks come as Laftagareen’s forces hold towns in the Bay region, where Ethiopian troops serving with the African Union Support and Stabilisation Mission in Somalia remain deployed. Mogadishu has accused Laftagareen’s camp of links to Al-Shabaab; his supporters deny the allegations.

Nur, elected speaker in August, made similar accusations against Kenya in October and urged the government to seek explanations from Nairobi. Kenyan officials said then that they had received no formal diplomatic complaint.

Why it mattersThe federal government is being pressed by its parliamentary speaker to seek explanations from Nairobi, after Kenyan officials said in October they had received no formal complaint.
