Ericsson and YAS Madagascar launch technology training at Madagascar university

The programme, deployed at the University of Antsiranana from 18 November, targets students in computing, telecommunications and polytechnic studies.

Sources: Midi Madagasikara and L'Express de Madagascar · 2026-11-25 · 1 min read

Ericsson and YAS Madagascar, a subsidiary of the Yas group, have launched the Ericsson Educate 21st Century Technologies programme at the University of Antsiranana, in northern Madagascar. Deployed from 18 November, it targets university students, prioritising those studying computing, telecommunications and polytechnic subjects.

The programme provides access through computer laboratories equipped and supported by YAS on campus. Students can follow self-paced, interactive courses on mobile devices, using practical simulations to study 5G, artificial intelligence, the internet of things, automation, data science and cybersecurity.

Ericsson and YAS said the initiative is intended to prepare students for careers in science, technology, engineering and mathematics. It is linked to Madagascar's 2023-2028 Digital Strategic Plan, which prioritises strengthening human capabilities.

Why it mattersStudents in computing, telecommunications and polytechnic studies can access the courses through YAS-supported campus laboratories from 18 November.
