Madagascar deputies prepare to elect assembly president after 112 vote to oust Siteny

Six lawmakers are being discussed as possible successors, while negotiations and candidate filings continue ahead of Wednesday’s vote.

Sources: Madagascar Tribune and Midi Madagasikara · 2026-11-25 · 1 min read

Madagascar’s deputies are due to elect a new president of the National Assembly on Wednesday, 25 November, after voting to remove Siteny Randrianasoloniaiko. In a late-night sitting at the Tsimbazaza Palace, 112 deputies backed his removal and 37 opposed it, Midi Madagasikara reported.

A total of 149 lawmakers took part in the vote, which also removed two vice-presidents and the assembly’s third questor. Marco Tsaradia, a deputy and vice-president for the former province of Toliara, is among six lawmakers discussed as a possible successor, Madagascar Tribune reported.

The other names include lawmakers Fanomezantsoa Andrianjanahary, Zavamanitra Andriamiharivolamena, Fidèle Razara Pierre, Fetra Ralambozafimbololona and minister Hanitra Razafimanantsoa. Candidacies were due to close on Wednesday morning; deputies must also fill the other vacant bureau posts.

Why it mattersThe lawmakers must choose a new assembly president and fill three other bureau posts after the removals.
