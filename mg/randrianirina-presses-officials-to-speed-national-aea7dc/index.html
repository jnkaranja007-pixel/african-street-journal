Randrianirina presses officials to speed national concertation

Madagascar’s presidency says only 8.33% of funds for the process have been disbursed, despite resources being available.

Sources: Madagascar Tribune and Midi Madagasikara · 2026-11-21 · 1 min read

Madagascar’s transitional president, Colonel Michaël Randrianirina, pressed officials to address delays in the national concertation process at a meeting in Iavoloha on Thursday, 19 November. The presidency said only 8.33% of the funds allocated to the process had been disbursed, although the money was available.

The prime minister’s office, the ministry responsible for Refoundation, the Council of Christian Churches of Madagascar and the technical committee for the concertation attended. Participants discussed administrative procedures holding up public funds and ways to release them while maintaining financial transparency, the presidency said. Randrianirina reminded those responsible of his commitment to hold elections within two years.

Madagascar’s transition calendar calls for a return to constitutional order before the end of 2027. Political consultations held on 9 and 10 November set a constitutional referendum as the priority before other elections.
