Madagascar's electoral commission seeks partners' support for 2027 elections

Thierry Rakotonarivo presented the commission's needs to diplomats and international organisations in Antananarivo, but no funding commitments were announced.

Sources: Midi Madagasikara and Madagascar Tribune · 2026-11-20 · 1 min read

Madagascar's independent electoral commission is seeking technical, material and financial support from international partners as it prepares for elections in 2027. Its president, Thierry Rakotonarivo, met diplomats and representatives of international organisations in Antananarivo to outline the commission's work and needs.

Rakotonarivo described logistical and financial constraints, including the difficulty of running operations in remote localities where roads and communications are poor. The commission also wants stronger staff skills and financial backing. The United Nations Development Programme, the African Union and the International Organisation of La Francophonie were among those present, along with representatives of the European Union, the United States, the United Kingdom, Japan, South Korea, South Africa, Morocco and Turkey.

No aid amount or disbursement timetable was announced. The commission must keep pursuing partners while preparing the vote, and the final electoral calendar is still to be fixed.

Why it mattersThe electoral commission must secure resources for election operations across Madagascar, while the final calendar is still to be set.
