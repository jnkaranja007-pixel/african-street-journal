Madagascar sends Tendry to defend world pétanque shooting title

The team left for Khon Kaen, Thailand, where the championships run from 20 to 24 November and Madagascar will also contest the men's triplette title.

Sources: Midi Madagasikara and L'Express de Madagascar · 2026-11-20 · 1 min read

Madagascar's pétanque team left for Khon Kaen, Thailand, on Wednesday for the world championships, which run from 20 to 24 November. Tendry Fitia Fanjamanovofifaliana will represent the country in men's precision shooting as Madagascar seeks to defend its world title.

The world precision-shooting championship begins on 20 November with the delegations' arrival and draw; the men's shooting contest, from qualifying through the final, is scheduled for the following day. Tendry, a 2017 junior world champion and member of the Caisse d'Épargne-linked club CBC (Centre de Bien-être Communautaire, a Malagasy sports club), carries the country's hopes in the event.

Madagascar will also compete in the 52nd men's triplette world championship, starting on 22 November. The national team lost the 2024 final to Italy, 13-11; the finals and medal ceremony are scheduled for 24 November.

Why it mattersTendry must carry Madagascar's hopes in the shooting event, and the triplette side must reverse its 2024 final defeat to Italy by 24 November.
