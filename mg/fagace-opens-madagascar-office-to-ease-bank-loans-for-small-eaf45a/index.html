FAGACE opens Madagascar office to ease bank loans for small firms

The African guarantee fund can cover up to half of a loan, cutting the risk for lenders and easing collateral demands on borrowers.

Source: Madagascar Tribune · 2026-11-17 · 1 min read

The African Guarantee and Economic Cooperation Fund (FAGACE) opened its representative office in Antaninarenina on Monday, 16 November 2026. The office is meant to improve Malagasy companies' access to bank credit by reducing the risk that lenders carry.

According to the Central Bank of Madagascar, as relayed by the Prime Minister's office, FAGACE can guarantee up to 50% of a loan amount under certain conditions. The guarantee is aimed at small and medium-sized enterprises, along with farming, renewable energy and capital-market projects. At the ceremony, the Prime Minister stressed political stability as a condition for investor confidence, and the fund's president described the opening as a sign of confidence in Madagascar's economic outlook.

The access rules have yet to be set out: how guarantees are obtained, how beneficiaries are chosen, and which financing terms will actually be offered to entrepreneurs.

Why it mattersSmall and medium-sized firms can apply for bank loans that FAGACE guarantees up to 50% of, with the access criteria still to be published.
