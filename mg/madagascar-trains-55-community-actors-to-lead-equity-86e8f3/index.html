Madagascar trains 55 community actors to lead equity dialogues in Bemasoandro

The women's leadership network is using UNFPA support to build local discussions on parity in decision-making, with the same programme also reaching other towns.

Source: Midi Madagasikara · 2026-11-26 · 1 min read

The African Women Leaders Network (AWLN) Madagascar chapter, supported by the United Nations Population Fund (UNFPA), is running a training session in the commune of Bemasoandro Itaosy. The aim is to strengthen women's participation in decision-making and public affairs, including on climate change.

In Bemasoandro, 55 community actors from different neighbourhoods were selected to lead community dialogues and raise awareness of equity and parity. Men are also encouraged to take part, to support changes in relationships within communities.

AWLN Madagascar president Bodo Ramangason said development and climate adaptation cannot advance without respect for human rights, especially those of women and young people. Workshops on fundamental rights are to be organised for local residents soon.

Why it mattersThe 55 selected actors will carry equity and parity discussions into Bemasoandro's neighbourhoods, and residents will receive rights workshops.
