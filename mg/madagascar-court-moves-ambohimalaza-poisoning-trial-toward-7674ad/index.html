Madagascar court moves Ambohimalaza poisoning trial toward former health minister

Lawyers for the civil parties want the former health minister, doctors and gendarmes called as witnesses over the response to the poisoning that killed at least 35 people.

Sources: Madagascar Tribune and Midi Madagasikara · 2026-11-22 · 1 min read

Madagascar's Ordinary Criminal Court in Anosy is weighing calls to summon former health minister Zely Arivelo Randriamanantany in the Ambohimalaza poisoning trial. At least 35 people died after a birthday party on 14 June 2025 in Imerinkasinina, most of them young people.

The prosecutor's general office said the steps needed to issue the summonses were under way. Hospital officials, doctors from the Hôpital Joseph Ravoahangy Andrianavalona (HJRA), a state hospital in Antananarivo, and gendarmes who investigated the case could also be called. Lawyers want the court to examine the treatment protocols, the resources deployed and why some victims were not evacuated abroad for care.

Tojo Rahamefy, one of 14 survivors, told the court he still depends on breathing assistance and is waiting for surgery abroad after selling part of his property to pay for treatment. The trial resumes on Monday, 23 November.

Why it mattersThe court must decide whether to summon the former health minister and other officials when hearings resume on 23 November.
