Madagascar justice minister weighs broadcasts of three more trials

Fanirisoa Ernaivo says public interest will guide whether proceedings in sensitive cases, including one involving 11 Chinese nationals, go on air.

Sources: L'Express de Madagascar and Midi Madagasikara · 2026-11-23 · 1 min read

Madagascar’s Justice Minister Fanirisoa Ernaivo said Saturday that her ministry is considering live broadcasts of one, two or three more high-profile trials on the national channel. She named the case involving the release of 11 Chinese nationals and the Boeing 777 case among the sensitive proceedings under consideration.

Ernaivo said the decision would depend on public expectations and that broadcasts would let interested citizens follow court debates. She said officials would assess whether the effects were positive, and stressed that accused people’s faces should not be shown during public hearings. The minister said evidence in the food-poisoning case had already been sent to judges.

The trial over the poisoning at the Imerinkasinina crossroads, where 35 people died, was the third to be broadcast on national television. The proceedings began on Wednesday 18 November and continued on Monday, the source’s publication date.

Why it mattersThe ministry must assess the broadcasts’ effects and protect accused people’s faces before deciding whether to air more trials.
