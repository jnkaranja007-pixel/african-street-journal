Madagascar renames international karting trophy for Faly Andrianafetra

The competition's 26th edition is scheduled for 22 November 2026 at the SRK circuit in Imerintsiatosika, and counts toward the national championship.

Sources: Midi Madagasikara and L'Express de Madagascar · 2026-11-19 · 1 min read

Madagascar's International Trophy will be renamed the Faly Andrianafetra Trophy for its 26th edition, scheduled for 22 November 2026 at the SRK circuit in Imerintsiatosika. Serana Racing Kart (SRK), the club that organises the event, presented the name at Nexus Andranobevava on Wednesday evening.

The trophy honours Faly Andrianafetra, promoter of the SRK circuit and the club's first president, who died in 2020. The competition will also be the fifth round of Madagascar's championship, with Minime, Junior and Élite drivers racing on the circuit's long layout, which measures 1 850 metres.

Created in 2001, the event has previously featured drivers including Loïc Duval, later a winner of the 24 Hours of Le Mans and an endurance world champion. The club says the renamed trophy recognises Andrianafetra's work to develop karting and motorsport in Madagascar.

Why it mattersThe Minime, Junior and Élite drivers will contest a national championship round on 22 November 2026, giving the renamed trophy a role in the season standings.
