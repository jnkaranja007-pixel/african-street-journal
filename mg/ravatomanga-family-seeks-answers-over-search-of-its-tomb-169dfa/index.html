Ravatomanga family seeks answers over search of its tomb

Lawyers say people claiming a prosecutor’s mandate searched the family tomb in Moratsiazo and reported finding nothing.

Sources: Madagascar Tribune and Midi Madagasikara · 2026-11-28 · 1 min read

Lawyers for the family of Mamy Ravatomanga, a Malagasy businessman imprisoned in Mauritius, are demanding explanations from Madagascar’s judicial authorities after his family tomb was searched in Moratsiazo, Soamahamanina, on Thursday 26 November 2026. The family’s statement says people who claimed to have been authorised by the public prosecutor carried out the operation.

The lawyers said the visitors reported finding nothing, and the family’s statement did not report any seizure. They are asking who took part, which judicial authority ordered the search and what legal acts authorised it. The nature of the search was not specified.

The family said it may pursue legal remedies if irregularities are established. Madagascar Tribune reported that judicial authorities had not responded to the family’s statement.

Why it mattersThe family’s lawyers must identify the officials and legal authorisation for the search, and are considering legal remedies if they establish irregularities.
