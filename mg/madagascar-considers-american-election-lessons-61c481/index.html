Madagascar considers american election lessons

The nation's electoral system is being examined in light of recent democratic victories abroad.

Source: Madagascar Tribune · 2026-11-08 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Madagascar's political observers are reflecting on the recent midterm election victories by Democrats in the United States. These events are seen as examples of how political power can shift through electoral processes, even when facing significant challenges.

The article cites several instances of unexpected electoral victories, including Jacques Chirac's 1995 win in France, François Hollande's 2012 presidential victory, Emmanuel Macron's 2017 success without an existing party, and Donald Trump's 2016 campaign. It also mentions democratic alternations in Senegal with victories by Abdoulaye Wade, Macky Sall, and Bassirou Faye.

These examples are presented to illustrate that electoral success is possible through debate and adherence to electoral rules, contrasting with countries where institutions are manipulated for clan interests. The text argues that genuine democratization requires a fundamental shift in mentality, moving away from coups, corruption, and impunity, and emphasizes that democracy is defined by universally recognized principles, not localized versions.
