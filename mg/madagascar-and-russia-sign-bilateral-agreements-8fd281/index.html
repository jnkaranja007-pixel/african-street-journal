Madagascar and Russia sign bilateral agreements

Agreements cover agriculture, health, education, defense, and mining, with a potential $10 billion Eurasian fund.

Source: Madagascar Tribune · 2026-11-03 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Colonel Michaël Randrianirina announced several concrete outcomes from Madagascar's participation in the Russia-Africa Summit upon his return. Agreements were signed by accompanying ministers covering agriculture, health, higher education, defense, and mining resource management. Randrianirina stated that the visit opens new economic and diplomatic perspectives for Madagascar.

Specific projects include mobile medical laboratories for rural diagnostics and a joint agricultural training center. Russia also plans to increase scholarships for Malagasy students, with a focus on recognizing national diplomas for balanced university exchanges. Discussions also addressed strengthening military ties and combating illegal mining.

The visit included a meeting with Vladimir Putin on October 30, exploring additional projects and a potential $10 billion Eurasian financial mechanism for investments. Randrianirina emphasized pragmatic, equal cooperation, with technical negotiations and financing mobilization crucial for implementation in Madagascar.

Why it mattersMadagascar's government has secured agreements with Russia to advance sectors like agriculture, health, and education, potentially impacting citizens through improved services and opportunities.
