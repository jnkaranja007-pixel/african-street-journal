Madagascar lawmakers elect Marco Tsaradia Assembly president with 129 votes

The former supporter of Andry Rajoelina ran unopposed after other candidates withdrew and Firaisankina lawmakers left the chamber before voting.

Sources: Madagascar Tribune, L'Express de Madagascar and Midi Madagasikara · 2026-11-26 · 1 min read

Madagascar’s National Assembly elected Marco Tsaradia as its president on Wednesday, 25 November, giving him 129 of the 130 votes cast. The deputy from Ampanihy, in Toliara province, was the only candidate when lawmakers voted.

Tsaradia succeeds Siteny Randrianasoloniaiko, who was removed from the post on Monday by a majority of deputies. Several other lawmakers withdrew their candidacies, while deputies from Firaisankina, a parliamentary group led by Fidèle Razara Piera, left the chamber before the vote.

Tsaradia had backed Andry Rajoelina before the political changes of October 2026 and has since moved closer to the current government’s camp. He previously served as an Assembly vice-president for Toliara province.

Why it mattersAssembly lawmakers must now work with Tsaradia as president after his election by 129 votes, following the removal of Siteny Randrianasoloniaiko.
