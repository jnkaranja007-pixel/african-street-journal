Madagascar's ousted Assembly president asks court to annul his removal

Siteny Randrianasoloniaiko's petition also challenges the removal of three other bureau members, arguing the required serious grounds were not shown.

Sources: Madagascar Tribune and Midi Madagasikara · 2026-11-28 · 1 min read

Siteny Randrianasoloniaiko, the former president of Madagascar's National Assembly, filed a petition with the High Constitutional Court on Friday, 27 November, seeking annulment of the Assembly's vote that removed him and three other members of its permanent bureau. Lawmakers had elected Marco Tsaradia, the only candidate, to succeed him on 25 November.

The petition contests the Assembly's resolution and asks the court to check whether the constitution's requirement of a serious reason against each member was met. The other three removed members are Christian Afakandro, Issa Charles Ibrahim and Charlot Mamihaja. The Assembly voted 112 to 37 to remove Randrianasoloniaiko, above the 109 votes its rules required.

His lawyers, from the Fanomezantsoa Heriniaina firm, argue that the court's October 2025 decision keeping existing institutions in place should also keep him in office. The handover ceremony with Tsaradia, which had been set for 27 November, remains in suspense while the court considers the case.

Why it mattersThe four removed members now depend on the High Constitutional Court to decide whether the Assembly's vote met the constitutional conditions for removal.
