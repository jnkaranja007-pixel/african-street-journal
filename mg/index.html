Madagascar groups submit reform proposals

Youth, women, parties, and diplomats have presented numerous ideas for national reform.

Madagascar has seen a surge of proposals for national reform following a political rupture in 2025. Young people have put forward a 61-point resolution, while the CENI has outlined 11 electoral reform axes. Women leaders have demanded at least 40% representation in elected and appointed positions, and over 80 political parties reportedly met at Iavoloha. Diplomats have also drafted a doctrine for the future.

The national youth consultation concluded on August 1 with proposals ranging from a youth parliament to mandatory local processing of raw materials, sparking debate on a unitary versus federal state. Women leaders finalized a advocacy document after consultations in eight regions. The CENI held multi-stakeholder workshops on electoral reform, and national diplomacy discussions ended with recommendations for a white paper.

Technical discussions on a future national public investment policy began on October 19. While these initiatives vary in legitimacy and reach, they contradict the idea that Madagascar lacks ideas. The challenge lies in confronting, prioritizing, and translating these diverse proposals into actionable rules and policies.

Why it mattersMadagascar's citizens and various groups are actively participating in consultations to shape the nation's future reforms.

Madagascar arrests prison officials over Chinese escape

Three prison administrators face charges after 11 Chinese nationals disappeared from Avaradrano prison.

Madagascar has arrested three prison administration officials and placed 12 others under judicial supervision following the irregular release and subsequent disappearance of 11 Chinese nationals. The arrests followed their presentation to the Anosy prosecutor's office on Friday. The 11 Chinese men had left Avaradrano prison in August, reportedly as transfers, without proper authorization. Two extradition waves to China occurred in September, with two of these inmates expected to be among those sent back.

The disappearance of the inmates was only publicly revealed in October, prompting swift administrative and legal action. Among those detained are former Avaradrano prison director Mamisoa Tolojanahary, director of prison administration Jean De Kopy Rajaonarison, and the head of the Antanimora post. The first substitute prosecutor, Nirina Andrianarivony, stated that several suspects remain at large, including Mahomed Razafimbeloson, secretary-general of the prison agents' union, who is wanted on an arrest warrant.

Mahomed Razafimbeloson has accused Minister of Justice Fanirisoa Ernaivo of issuing instructions for the inmates' release, a claim the minister has categorically denied. The case has raised questions about the effectiveness of surveillance mechanisms and inter-service communication, especially given the speed of the current prosecutions compared to the months of silence. This controversy impacts the country's image, particularly concerning corruption concerns voiced by the president of the Refondation.

Why it mattersThree prison officials and 12 others face judicial proceedings after 11 Chinese nationals disappeared from custody, impacting the government's image.

Madagascar customs union decries accusations

The union says generalized criticism of customs officers is unfair and demands more resources.

The Syndicate of Customs Inspectors of Madagascar (SIDM) has responded to widespread criticism of the customs administration, particularly concerning gold trafficking allegations. The union stated that responsibilities should be determined through legal investigations, not public accusations that tarnish the reputation of all customs officers. The SIDM insists that facts must be verified before public statements are made, citing recent gold seizures as examples of how imprecise communication can be harmful.

Recent incidents, including the interception of nearly six kilograms of gold at Ivato on September 13, which led to charges against five customs officers and two airport security agents, have placed airport controls under scrutiny. Another seizure of 2.85 kilograms of gold on October 22 has renewed questions about surveillance effectiveness. The SIDM argues that these cases highlight a need for clearer division of responsibilities among border security agencies.

The SIDM reports that approximately 700 customs agents are currently responsible for border control, an insufficient number for the required level of inspection. A recruitment drive is planned to increase staffing. The union also seeks improved working conditions, clearly defined rules, and better support from management for its members, emphasizing that the fight against illegal practices must be balanced with protecting officers' rights and strengthening operational capacities.

Why it mattersMadagascar's customs officers face public scrutiny and demand more resources to perform their duties effectively.

Sport · 2026-10-24

Cosfa targets Madagascar Cup rugby double

Cosfa will face Uscar in the final after defeating TFA 91-29 in the semi-finals.

Cosfa will play Uscar in the final of the Madagascar Cup rugby union on Sunday at the Makis d’Andohatapenaka stadium. Cosfa won the Madagascar championship in September, beating Uscar 26-25 in the final.

Uscar reached the final by defeating TFMA 45-44 in a close semi-final match. Cosfa advanced after a dominant 91-29 victory over TFA in their semi-final.

Cosfa coach Noé Mboazafy Rakotoarivelo, known as coach Razily, aims for a historic double. Uscar captain Anthony Razafindratsimba stated his team is ready to overturn Cosfa.

Why it mattersRugby fans in Madagascar will watch the final match between Cosfa and Uscar.
