GNVB wins Coupe de Madagascar U23 doubles

The Gendarmerie Nationale Volleyball club secured both the men's and women's titles at the national U23 tournament in Antsirabe.

The Coupe de Madagascar U23 volleyball tournament concluded Saturday in Antsirabe, with nine men's and six women's teams competing. The Gendarmerie Nationale Volleyball (GNVB) achieved a significant double victory, winning both the men's and women's championships.

In the men's final, GNVB faced Akany Sambatra Itaosy (ASI). ASI led two sets to none before GNVB mounted a comeback, winning the next two sets. GNVB ultimately secured the national title in a close tie-break, winning 16-14. For the women, GNVB defeated ASI in three sets (25-20, 31-29, 25-21) to complete their club's double.

The men's third-place match saw Cosfa defeat Nadjy VB in four sets, while Stef’Auto took third in the women's competition by beating AGIR in straight sets. These victories confirm GNVB's strong youth program and its position as a leading Malagasy volleyball club for young players.

Why it mattersYoung Malagasy volleyball players from the GNVB club have won national titles, showcasing their talent and the club's development programs.

Madagascar president urges faster national talks

Colonel Michaël Randrianirina acknowledged delays in the national consultation process, which began in 2025.

Colonel Michaël Randrianirina stated on Sunday, November 8, that preparations for the national consultation are not progressing as expected. The head of state urged the Christian Churches Council of Madagascar (FFKM) and administration officials to speed up their work, noting that financial constraints should not hinder mobilization. This call follows successive postponements since the process officially launched in 2025.

The national consultation aims to gather public proposals for institutional and political reforms. The FFKM was tasked with leading this process on November 19, 2025, with an official launch ceremony on December 10, 2025, at the Ivato International Conference Centre. The process has faced repeated delays and organizational challenges since its inception.

Preparations began in October, with a plan to train facilitators for local discussions in six pilot districts. However, the nationwide rollout of training depends on equipment, including tablets expected from the Prime Minister's office. The High Constitutional Court provided legal clarifications on November 4 regarding two electoral deadlines.

Why it mattersThe president is pushing for faster progress on national consultations that will define the country's institutional and political direction.

Ladies Makis win Madagascar's first Olympic medal

The Madagascar women's rugby sevens team secured bronze at the Dakar Youth Olympic Games.

The Madagascar women's rugby sevens team, the Ladies Makis, returned home as heroes on Saturday morning after winning the nation's first-ever Olympic medal. They achieved this historic bronze at the Dakar Youth Olympic Games 2026. The team secured third place after a 26-10 victory against the Czech Republic in the bronze medal match.

The Ladies Makis' journey included three group stage wins against the United States, Spain, and the Czech Republic, followed by a 5-15 semi-final loss to Kenya. Marcel Rakotomalala, president of the Malagasy Rugby Federation, praised the team's intensive four-month preparation and highlighted their international performance. Coach Dina Orléan Razafindratsara noted that the third-place finish exceeded their initial goal of reaching the semi-finals.

Team captain Tracy Fitiavanomenjanahary expressed the team's initial disappointment after the semi-final loss but emphasized their resilience. She stated a desire for more friendly matches and international encounters to aid their progression. Jean Alex Harinelina Andriamanarivo, secretary general of the Malagasy Olympic Committee, confirmed the team's training camps in Japan and Tunisia as part of their solid preparation.

Mage 4 stages theatrical rock concert

The band Mage 4 performed a unique show at the Mahamasina Sports Palace, blending rock music with theatrical staging.

The band Mage 4 presented a theatrical rock concert at the Mahamasina Sports Palace on 11/09/2026. The performance featured elaborate staging that enhanced each song, creating an immersive experience for the audience.

Rayhas and his bandmates, including co-founder Lita, showcased their long preparation through the show. They performed songs like "Raha toa ka" and "Miandry aho," maintaining their established artistic direction. Guest performers Nini from Kiaka and Kix AmbondronA also joined Mage 4 on stage.

Orange Madagascar sponsored the event, recognizing the band's effort in promoting artistic creation. While the venue was not full, the concert demonstrated the enduring appeal and artistic quality of Mage 4's music.

Why it mattersFans of the band Mage 4 and rock music enthusiasts in Madagascar experienced a unique blend of performance art and music.

Antananarivo city council warns dog owners

Owners must control dogs in public spaces and protect passersby.

The Antananarivo Urban Community (CUA) has reminded dog owners of their responsibility to control their pets in public areas. Owners must ensure their dogs do not roam unsupervised and pose a risk to others on streets and in public spaces.

The CUA stated that allowing dogs to wander freely is prohibited. This measure aims to protect the public from potential harm caused by uncontrolled animals. The council also issued a warning about heavy rainfall expected in the northeast of Madagascar this week, with alerts for high tides and strong winds along the east and south coasts.

Meanwhile, the American embassy is hosting the "Freedom Festival" in Taolagnaro. This event celebrates the 250th anniversary of American independence and the 160th anniversary of Madagascar-US friendship. Young attendees can gain knowledge by visiting the festival.

Why it mattersDog owners in Antananarivo face responsibility for their pets' actions in public spaces.
