Fondation H marks 10 years with two art exhibitions

Two major exhibitions will open on November 13 at Ambatomena, featuring artists who have won awards from the foundation.

Fondation H will celebrate its 10th anniversary with two major art exhibitions opening on November 13 at its Ambatomena location.

The first exhibition, "Masoandro amam-bolana," will showcase the work of Temandrota, focusing on Malagasy culture, spirituality, and philosophy. The second exhibition, "Ranty hikambanana," will feature works by 21 artists who have received awards from the foundation over the past decade, under the direction of N’Goné Fall.

Artists whose work will be displayed include Yannick Tojonantenaina Andrianambonisoa, Finoana Rato­vo Andriantseheno, Mian­galy Elia Andriantsoa, Dina Nomena Andria­rimanjaka, and Olivia Bourgois, among others. This exhibition highlights individual creativity and the foundation's efforts over 10 years.

Why it mattersMalagasy artists and the public will experience two art exhibitions celebrating the 10th anniversary of Fondation H.

chinese national caught with 2.85 kg gold at Ivato airport

Customs agents intercepted the gold hidden in the passenger's rectum during a body scan at Ivato International Airport.

Customs agents at Ivato International Airport intercepted a Chinese national on Thursday attempting to leave Madagascar with 2.85 kg of gold hidden in her rectum.

Agents became suspicious of the passenger's unusual gait and discovered the gold during a body scan, prompting an immediate response from Prime Minister Mamitiana Rajaonarison and the Minister of Mines who visited the airport.

The seizure follows a presidential visit to the airport two days prior and a statement by Michaël Randrianirina that 100 tonnes of gold illegally leave Madagascar for Dubai annually, leading the state to announce strengthened measures against mining wealth trafficking.

Holitiana Rabemanantsoa publishes eight health books

The midwife and teacher released six books on health and two on etiquette in Malagasy.

Holitiana Vololoniaina Rabemanantsoa, a midwife and teacher, has published eight books in Malagasy covering health, maternity, and child-rearing. Her collection includes six volumes of "Ny Fahasalamako" focused on health and two books titled "Olo-mendrika" on etiquette. Her two latest publications, released on July 26, 2026, are dedicated to pregnancy and infants.

The book for pregnant women details pregnancy stages, baby development, maternal changes, and postpartum care, while the infant book covers newborn care, feeding, and hygiene. Rabemanantsoa's health books incorporate knowledge of natural remedies and local plants, aiming to aid public understanding and daily prevention. She stresses that her books do not replace medical consultations.

The author began developing this collection in 2011, with the first books appearing in 2013. Rabemanantsoa, who is also a doctoral student in education and social sciences, continues her work of knowledge transmission. She believes learning is a continuous process serving education, health, and daily life.

Why it mattersMadagascar residents can access practical health information in their own language.
