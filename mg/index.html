State and Oil Firms Mobilize 60 Million Liters of Diesel

An agreement between the State Procurement of Madagascar and four oil operators aims to secure diesel supply through a combination of loans and sales to distributors.

The State Procurement of Madagascar (SPM) has finalized an agreement with four petroleum operators to secure 60 million liters of diesel for the Malagasy market. This accord, signed on August 27, involves 20 million liters being loaned by SPM to distributors and 40 million liters sold to them. The participating companies are Galana Distribution Pétrolière, Jovena, TotalEnergies Madagascar, and Vivo Energy Madagascar. The loaned volume is equivalent to approximately one week of national consumption, crucial during peak demand periods.

This strategic reserve is intended to ensure distributors maintain adequate stock levels and continue normal diesel deliveries across the country while awaiting subsequent shipments. The measure comes as two fuel ships arrived simultaneously at the port of Toamasina, one carrying SPM's cargo and the other private operators' fuel. A temporary delay in unloading the private vessel, due to SPM's cargo taking priority, prompted this collaborative resource pooling to prevent disruptions at service stations and for consumers.

This public-private partnership mechanism, previously used to address temporary stock shortages, aims to guarantee diesel availability. Marc Renauld, Director General of Galana Madagascar, emphasized the central goal of ensuring supply continuity, noting that SPM's product meets quality standards. No price changes at the pump are anticipated, and the Malagasy Hydrocarbons Office will monitor market compliance. A separate shipment of gasoline is expected in early September.

Why it mattersThis action directly impacts investors by ensuring the continued availability of diesel fuel, preventing supply chain disruptions and maintaining market stability.

Madagascar Shifts Drought Response to Anticipation Policy

The nation presented its Drought Investment Plan at COP17, aiming to redirect funding towards sustainable resilience for vulnerable territories and populations.

Madagascar is transitioning from reactive drought crisis management to a proactive anticipation policy. At COP17 of the UN Convention to Combat Desertification in Ulaanbaatar, Mongolia, the country unveiled its Drought Investment Plan, developed with the Global Mechanism. This plan is designed to better channel financing into actions that build lasting resilience for at-risk territories and communities.

The international conference, themed "Restoring land, restoring hope," brings together 197 Parties, scientists, economic actors, civil society, and local communities. Madagascar participated in a ministerial dialogue on drought resilience on August 25. The Malagasy plan focuses on three key areas: ecosystem and forest restoration, including degraded watershed rehabilitation and mangrove preservation; agriculture and water, involving irrigation modernization, agroecological practices, and short-cycle, climate-resilient seeds; and community empowerment to reduce reliance on repeated humanitarian aid.

Minister of Environment and Sustainable Development Luk Aristide Nolave Andriatsihala emphasized investing in people, particularly women and youth, aligning with COP17 discussions on preventive financing. The initiative seeks to mobilize more capital for resilience, hydraulic infrastructure, and land restoration, with a new investment facility for drought resilience among the proposals. Madagascar's delegation included government officials, scientists, civil society members, and young negotiators.

Why it mattersFarmers will benefit from modernized irrigation, agroecological practices, and resilient seeds, while investors can fund resilience initiatives and infrastructure.

GNVB Volleyball Reaches Two Coupe de Madagascar Finals

The Gendarmerie nationale volleyball d’Analamanga team will compete in at least two finals this Friday at the Ampisikina complex in Mahajanga.

The Gendarmerie nationale volleyball d’Analamanga (GNVB) is set to compete in at least two finals of the Coupe de Madagascar for the U15 and U21 categories this Friday in Mahajanga. In the U21 girls' division, GNVB will face Bi’as Analamanga in the championship match. GNVB secured their spot by defeating Akany Sambatra Itaosy 3-1, with set scores of 25/13, 21/25, 25/21, and 25/18. Bi’as Analamanga advanced by beating MVBC Boeny 3-0.

The U15 boys' final will be an internal match between two GNVB teams. The third-place match in this category saw AMVB Mandroseza defeat MVBC 3-1. For the U15 girls, CRJS Atsinanana is scheduled to play MVBC in the final. CRJS Atsinanana reached the final by overcoming GNVB 3-1 in the semifinals, with set scores of 22/25, 26/24, 25/13, and 25/10. MVBC advanced by defeating EVBFI 3-0.

The U21 boys' final will be contested by the winners of the semifinals between GNVB-ECVB and ASSM-ASI, with the match scheduled for this afternoon. The GNVB team has demonstrated strong performance across multiple age groups, positioning themselves for potential victories in the national competition.

Why it mattersThe GNVB volleyball team's advancement to multiple finals in the Coupe de Madagascar directly impacts young athletes by providing them with opportunities to compete for national titles.

Justice Ministry Suspends Officials Amidst Corruption and Prison Break Investigations

Three magistrates from the anti-corruption unit and fifteen prison guards face suspension following official inquiries into professional misconduct and security lapses.

The Ministry of Justice has announced a crackdown on ethical breaches within its services, vowing to enforce laws and codes of conduct rigorously. This stance has led to the suspension of three magistrates from the Anti-Corruption Pole (PAC) in Antananarivo. These actions follow an investigation by the Central Bureau for Justice Control (BCCJ) and reports of unfulfilled duties, mishandling of cases, and disregard for professional obligations.

In parallel, fifteen agents from the high-security prison in Imerintsiatosika have been suspended after an attempted escape on August 7, 2026. Investigations by the Prison Inspection Directorate indicated negligence in their duties. The ministry confirmed the escaped detainee has since been recaptured, adhering to the Justice Minister's directives.

These suspensions, affecting a total of eighteen personnel, are presented by the ministry as measures taken in accordance with current legislation and administrative procedures. The stated objective is to uphold the integrity and proper functioning of judicial and penitentiary services, emphasizing that professional obligations must be met by all staff, regardless of their position.

Why it mattersMagistrates and prison agents face disciplinary action, impacting the administration of justice and prison security.

Rebika Band Plans 70-Song Performance

The popular Malagasy band Rebika is preparing for a major outdoor concert on August 30th at the Esca Antanimena school field, featuring an extensive setlist.

The band Rebika, known for its many songwriters, faces the challenge of selecting over 70 songs for an upcoming major performance. Nix, a band member, stated that the show will be lengthy, with more than 70 songs planned, and some fan favorites might not make the final cut.

Preparations for the concert, scheduled for Sunday, August 30th, at the Esca Antanimena school field, are underway. Band members have begun rehearsals, and Rivo confirmed that technical aspects are being coordinated to match the scale of the event. The band's return to the stage after a long break has been met with enthusiasm by fans.

Nix noted that this concert is the only major outdoor event in Antananarivo this year. Heritiana emphasized the difficulty in scheduling such gatherings, but the demand from fans was prioritized. The organizers from Lax Pro stated that working with Rebika has always been successful, and they aim to continue this trend.

Why it mattersThe concert by Rebika band affects fans by offering a large-scale outdoor performance with an extensive song selection.
