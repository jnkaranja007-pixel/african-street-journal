SADC urges Madagascar to restore constitutional normality

The Southern African Development Community issued recommendations on democratic functioning and human rights following its 46th Summit in Durban. The regional organization expressed support for the country's Refondation process while demanding inclusive governance. The group also noted consultations held under its aegis.

Why it mattersThe requirements for accountability and citizen participation set conditions for the regional bloc's continued support of the current transition.

Politics · 2026-08-21

Randrianirina promotes political dialogue for elections

President Michaël Randrianirina stated upon his return from the SADC summit in Durban that political consultations have been underway for several weeks. He said the process is part of electoral governance. The goal is to assist the CENI in preparing for upcoming elections.

Why it mattersThe dialogue is intended to accelerate the timeline for holding national elections.

Politics · 2026-08-21

French parliamentary delegation visits Madagascar

Michaël Randrianirina received Bruno Fuchs, chairman of the French National Assembly's foreign affairs committee, on Wednesday, August 19. The meeting focused on cooperation between the two nations. The visit occurs as Madagascar reconfigures its international relations.

Why it mattersThe meeting allows the new authorities to maintain diplomatic ties with France during a period of international restructuring.

Antananarivo residents face water and power outages

Water supply disruptions, including pressure drops and cuts, have returned to several neighborhoods in the capital over the last few days. These issues coincide with a return of electricity outages. JIRAMA announced new disruptions are expected to continue next week.

Why it mattersThe instability in utility services affects the daily operations of residents and businesses in the capital.

Mpox vaccinations begin at Polyteknika Vontovorona

Vaccination campaigns against Mpox are continuing today at Polyteknika Vontovorona. The initiative targets students through vaccination and awareness efforts. Some students have already participated in the program.

Why it mattersThe campaign aims to protect the student population from the spread of the Mpox virus.
