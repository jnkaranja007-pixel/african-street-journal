Madagascar president targets 70% electricity access by 2030

President aims for ambitious goal while capital faces repeated, lengthy power cuts.

Madagascar's President announced a goal to achieve 70% electricity access by 2030 from Antalya, Turkey, where he is attending COP31.

This target contrasts with current realities in Antananarivo, where residents have endured repeated, unpredictable power cuts lasting over four hours in some areas, disrupting daily life and businesses.

The national grid faces strain, compounded by the state's requisition of ENELEC facilities in Toamasina, raising questions about the government's capacity to manage energy emergencies amidst international climate talks.

Why it mattersResidents of Antananarivo are experiencing prolonged electricity outages that disrupt their daily lives and economic activities.

Culture · 2026-11-12

Seven artists turn Antananarivo airport into showcase of Malagasy culture

Ravinala Airports has unveiled the works of seven winners from the second Art’ÉROPORT contest, chosen from nearly 50 applicants.

Seven artists have transformed Antananarivo's international airport into a showcase of Malagasy culture through the second edition of the Art’ÉROPORT contest. Ravinala Airports, which runs the airport, organised the contest to mark 10 years of managing the site. The winning works were unveiled at a ceremony attended by jury members, representatives of the relevant ministries and Ravinala Airports officials.

Sandrine Rahantamalala, the company's communications director, said every person passing through the airport should feel welcomed and leave with a memory or an emotion. Nearly 50 candidates answered the call for projects, and seven were selected. Installations are placed in the departures area and the domestic arrivals zone, and include a depiction of Madagascar's landscapes and a piece made from recycled sandals and lamba landy under the message of unity.

A private television channel followed the contest as a reality show, letting the public meet the artists and see how they work. The jury gave its special favourite award to Maherisoa Rakotomalala for a colourful composition of several symbols of Madagascar.

Why it mattersTravellers passing through Antananarivo airport will see the seven works on display, and the artists gain a public showcase for their work.

Sport · 2026-11-12

Jauslin Laura finishes fifth in youth Olympic 200m

The Makis Ladies were the only Malagasy team to win a medal as the country's athletics programme at the Youth Olympic Games closed.

Madagascar's participation in the Youth Olympic Games (JOJ 2026) ended with the athletics competition. Jauslin Laura finished fifth in the 200 m race, clocking 27 seconds and 46 percent of a second.

The Makis Ladies were the only Malagasy delegation to bring home a medal from the Games. Rasoanaivo Laura will next compete in the under-70 kg judo category at the Grand Prix de Zagreb in Croatia, running from Thursday to Sunday.

The Federation Sport-Automobile de Madagascar closes its season with the Grand Rallye de Madagascar in Alaotra Mangoro, running from Friday to Sunday, where the 2026 Madagascar champion will be decided.

Why it mattersRasoanaivo Laura and the rally entrants will compete abroad and at home over the coming days, with the 2026 national champion decided at the end of the rally.
