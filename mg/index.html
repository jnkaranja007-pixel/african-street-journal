Madagascar signs Russian accords on health, education, mining

Colonel Michaël Randrianirina announced agreements for mobile medical labs and increased student scholarships following a summit in Russia.

Colonel Michaël Randrianirina announced several agreements with Russia upon his return to Madagascar on Saturday. The deals cover agriculture, health, higher education, defense, and mining resource management, aiming to open new economic and diplomatic avenues for the island nation. Ministers accompanying Randrianirina signed bilateral accords, according to the president of the Refondation.

Projects include mobile medical laboratories to improve rural diagnostics and a joint training center for agricultural development. Russia also plans to increase scholarships for Malagasy students, with a focus on mutual recognition of national diplomas. Randrianirina emphasized a cooperation of equals, contrasting it with other developed nations.

Discussions also addressed strengthening military ties and combating illegal mining. Randrianirina explored public service digitalization and AI for administrative transparency during a visit to a Russian presidential academy on October 28. A meeting with Vladimir Putin on October 30 discussed potential new projects and a $10 billion Eurasian financial mechanism for investments, though details are pending.

Why it mattersMadagascar's citizens will benefit from improved healthcare access, educational opportunities, and potentially more transparent governance.

Émile Ratefinanahary re-elected head of Madagascar karate federation

Ratefinanahary secured 11 votes for his second term after an election attended by 12 of 15 regional leagues.

Émile Ratefinanahary has been re-elected president of the Malagasy Karate Federation for a second term. The election took place in Fianarantsoa during the federation's general assembly, which was combined with the Madagascar Championship. 12 of the 15 regional leagues were represented, meeting the quorum requirement.

As the sole candidate, Ratefinanahary received 11 votes, with one league abstaining. Mara Boniface, president of the Androy league, stated that the league presidents conducted the election as the supreme body. Hervé Ranoroson, president of the Alaotra-Mangoro league, addressed social media controversies, asserting that claims of a collective of leagues were false and constituted usurpation of functions.

The election proceeded according to the federation's statutes, solidifying the legitimacy of Ratefinanahary's new mandate. The federation's next steps were not detailed in the report.

Why it mattersÉmile Ratefinanahary secured a second term as president of the Malagasy Karate Federation, with league presidents affirming their authority in the election process.

Madagascar tennis and volleyball figure Ravalitera Rafolomanantsiatosika dies

Ravalitera Rafolomanantsiatosika, known as Ravaly, trained generations of tennis players and coaches.

Ravalitera Rafolomanantsiatosika, a prominent figure in Malagasy sports, has died. He was known for his contributions to tennis and volleyball, reaching the position of national technical director for tennis.

Affectionately called Ravaly, he trained many tennis players and coaches at Ankatso, including his daughter Aina Rafolo, who went to the United States. He was also a skilled volleyball player with a notable vertical leap.

Rafolomanantsiatosika dedicated his life to sports, starting from his physical education studies at the École normale supérieure at the University of Ankatso.

Why it mattersThe Malagasy sports community mourns the loss of Ravalitera Rafolomanantsiatosika, who dedicated his life to developing tennis and volleyball.

Fanjiry association launches in Avaradrano

The wife of the President of the Republic officially established the association on October 31.

The Fanjiry association was officially established in Ambodivoara Ambohimangakely on Saturday, October 31, by Mrs. Randrianirina Elisa, wife of the President of the Republic.

Mrs. Randrianirina Elisa stated that Fanjiry aims to support vulnerable families and promote sustainable, just, and humane development, with a focus on women's independence.

16 communes in the Avaradrano district are ready to establish Fanjiry branches, and central and local government officials, along with various community organizations and NGOs, attended the launch.

Why it mattersThe Fanjiry association, launched by the wife of the President of the Republic, aims to support vulnerable families and promote development.
