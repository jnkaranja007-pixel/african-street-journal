Madagascar and US sign military logistics accord for maritime patrols

The deal lets both armed forces exchange supplies, services and logistical support for joint patrols, exercises and crisis response.

Madagascar and the United States signed a military acquisition and mutual assistance agreement in Antananarivo on Tuesday, creating a framework for the two armed forces to exchange supplies, services and logistical support. The accord can be used for humanitarian operations, military exercises and crises.

The arrangement also provides mutual support for joint maritime patrols and multinational exercises, including Cutlass Express. US Africa Command deputy operations director General Thomas M. Armas, who signed for the United States, said reliable logistics were needed for operations, including cyclone response and securing waters in the Mozambique Channel. Madagascar’s Armed Forces Minister, General Maminirina Ely Razafitombo, signed for Madagascar.

A roundtable after the signing discussed port infrastructure and bases for refuelling operations in Antsiranana, Toamasina and Mahajanga. 24 Malagasy troops received practical training in procurement, emergency fuel supply and air support. The signing followed a visit by AFRICOM deputy commander General John Brennan two weeks earlier; Madagascar is among more than 30 African partner countries using the agreement.

Why it mattersThe agreement provides mutual logistical support for joint maritime patrols, while port and base options were discussed for Antsiranana, Toamasina and Mahajanga.

FAGACE opens Madagascar office to guarantee up to 50% of loans

The fund says its Antananarivo representation will help small and medium-sized businesses secure bank credit by sharing lenders’ risk.

The African Fund for Guarantee and Economic Cooperation (FAGACE) opened a representative office in Madagascar on Monday, 16 November, at Antaninarenina. The fund can guarantee up to 50% of a loan, subject to conditions, according to Madagascar’s central bank, cited by the prime minister’s office.

The guarantee is intended to reduce the risk banks bear when lending to businesses, particularly small and medium-sized enterprises struggling to raise investment funds. Agriculture and renewable energy are among the sectors covered. The prime minister’s office said the fund’s local presence is intended to improve Malagasy businesses’ access to bank credit.

The office’s practical access rules, selection criteria and financing terms have yet to be set out. FAGACE said it intends to provide financial solutions for local businesses, beyond technical support.

Why it mattersSmall and medium-sized businesses seeking investment loans can apply for guarantees covering up to 50% of a loan, subject to conditions.

Ravinala Airports renovates Ivato terminal and adds passenger services

The airport operator has introduced free Wi-Fi, device-charging points and new rest areas at Madagascar’s national and international terminals.

Ravinala Airports and other airport operators have renovated the national terminal at Ivato airport in Antananarivo and expanded services for passengers. The changes were presented during the World Air Passenger Day celebration at the airport.

Travellers can use free Wi-Fi and charging stations, while rest areas include a game zone, a beauty and wellness space, areas for children and comfort zones. The airport community also presented measures to prevent corruption, without detailing them.

The fourth World Air Passenger Day was held at the international airports in Antananarivo and Nosy Be. At Ivato, the opening included an exhibition of raffia works by the association Made For A Woman, alongside cultural activities celebrating Malagasy art and craft.

Why it mattersPassengers at Ivato now have free Wi-Fi, charging points and dedicated rest areas while they wait for flights.

Madagascar Airlines critic says leadership change risks its recovery

Former chief executive Thierry de Bailleul says the airline had restored five ATRs to service and reached structural profitability before the funding programme was suspended.

Madagascar Airlines’ former chief executive, Thierry de Bailleul, says the carrier’s recovery was disrupted after authorities ended continuity in its leadership. In an opinion article, he says the Phénix 2030 restructuring plan, backed by the World Bank, had restored five ATR aircraft to operation, compared with one at the start of the effort.

De Bailleul says the airline achieved more than 82% punctuality, increased domestic capacity by 123% compared with 2022 and grew revenue by 60% in two years. He reports structural profitability of +5M$ excluding non-recurring items, and says the World Bank had been preparing 80 M$ in additional financing, including 40 M$ for the airline, subject to conditions including governance.

He says the financing programme was suspended the week after his departure. The former executive argues the carrier should focus on reliable domestic routes, regional services and international partnerships; he warns that operational deterioration observed over the past two months risks a relapse if it continues.

Why it mattersThe World Bank programme that included 40 M$ for Madagascar Airlines was suspended the week after de Bailleul left, while the airline’s recovery remains fragile.

Malagasy jiu-jitsu athletes win three silver medals at Abu Dhabi worlds

Nofy Chuck, Ranaivomanana Idealisoa and Ranarivelo Ando Mahasoa finished second in their events at the Brazilian jiu-jitsu championship.

Madagascar’s jiu-jitsu team won three silver medals at the world Brazilian jiu-jitsu championship in Abu Dhabi, with Nofy Chuck earning one on 10 November and juniors Ranaivomanana Idealisoa and Ranarivelo Ando Mahasoa adding two more.

Idealisoa lost the final in the under 32-kilogram category to Brazil’s Campos Chloé. Kazakhstan’s Aidaruly Zanggar beat Ando Mahasoa in the under 42-kilogram final.

The two juniors had also competed internationally in 2025: Idealisoa won gold and Ando Mahasoa took bronze.

Why it mattersIdealisoa and Ando Mahasoa moved from gold and bronze in 2025 to silver finishes at the Abu Dhabi championship.
