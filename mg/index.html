Tendry Rakotobe sets new Madagascar swimming record

The young swimmer finished ninth overall in the 50m backstroke at the Dakar 2026 Youth Olympics.

Madagascar swimmer Tendry Rakotobe set a new national record in the 50m backstroke at the Dakar 2026 Youth Olympics. He finished fourth in his heat with a time of 27.35 seconds, placing him ninth overall and narrowly missing a spot in the final.

Rakotobe's performance surpassed his previous personal best of 27.7 seconds, established two years ago. His teammate Océane Rakotonanahary competed in the 50m breaststroke, finishing fourth in her heat in 34.65 seconds and eleventh overall.

Two other Malagasy athletes are still competing: Francia Nomenjanahary Randriatiana in beach wrestling on November 8 and Jauslin Anjara Laura in the 200m athletics sprint on November 10.

Why it mattersTendry Rakotobe's new national swimming record at the Youth Olympics showcases Malagasy athletic achievement abroad.

Madagascar suspends Boeing 777 trial for new probe

The anti-corruption court suspended the trial of 38 people linked to the transfer of five Boeing 777s to Iran, sending it back for further investigation.

The trial concerning five Boeing 777 aircraft sent to Iran has been suspended by Madagascar's Anti-Corruption Pole (PAC) without a new date. The case returns to the investigative stage at the request of the prosecution, which deems further investigations necessary. This decision prolongs the uncertainty for the 38 individuals prosecuted.

The dean of investigative judges will resume investigations, examining new elements including complaints from the new management of the Civil Aviation of Madagascar (ACM), reports from the Independent Anti-Corruption Bureau (Bianco), and technical expertise from American specialists. These documents aim to clarify responsibilities in the administrative authorizations granted for the aircraft transfer.

The case, which erupted in July 2025, also faces a potential reclassification of offenses to criminal infractions, which would significantly alter the procedure. Former Transport Minister Valéry Ramonjavelo and former Senate President General Richard Ravalomanana are among those implicated in the affair.

Why it mattersThe trial suspension affects 38 individuals, including former high-ranking officials, and potentially alters the legal classification of the offenses related to the transfer of five Boeing 777 aircraft to Iran.

Politics · 2026-11-06

ORTM director clashes with communication minister over access

A dispute over security measures at public broadcasting premises escalates between the ORTM director general and the communication minister.

A dispute has erupted between the director general of the Office de la Radio et de la Télévision de Madagascar (ORTM), Lemana Elysée, and the Minister of Communication and Culture, Gascar Fenosoa, over access to the Havoria hall within the ORTM compound in Anosy. The conflict escalated on Wednesday evening when the ministry attempted to implement new access controls, leading to a confrontation that required the intervention of the Organe mixte de conception (OMC).

The disagreement stems from ORTM's decision to restrict access to Havoria, citing security concerns due to its shared premises with the national radio and television buildings, which are classified as red zones. The ORTM director general reportedly closed a connecting gate and increased security checks, disrupting events and causing cancellations. The ministry responded by installing metal barriers to separate the sites and reopen access to Havoria, a move the ORTM director allegedly opposed and dismantled.

Further complicating the situation, the ORTM management has reportedly given ministry services occupying ORTM offices one week to vacate the premises. These services include the Directorate of Public Relations and the regional communication directorate. The minister and the head of the OMC were present to oversee the barrier installation, while the ORTM director general allegedly called in Presidential Security elements during the standoff.
