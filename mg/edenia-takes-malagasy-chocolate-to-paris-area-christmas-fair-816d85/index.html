Edenia takes Malagasy chocolate to Paris-area Christmas fair

The Madagascar craft chocolatier plans to show about 15 products at the 19 December event organised by ASASOA France-Madagascar.

Sources: Midi Madagasikara and L'Express de Madagascar · 2026-11-27 · 1 min read

Edenia Chocolaterie Madagascar will exhibit at the second Foire de Noël Art et Gastronomie on 19 December at the Ferme du Manet in Montigny-le-Bretonneux, near Paris. The Malagasy craft chocolatier plans to bring a quince of references and nearly 40 kilos of chocolate.

Founder Achille Rajerison is due to fly to France from 30 November to prepare for the fair, organised by ASASOA France-Madagascar. Edenia’s recipes pair Malagasy cacao with vanilla, combava, wild tsiperifery pepper, lychee and cinnamon; the company grows some ingredients, including lychee and cinnamon, at Soatanana in Vatomandry district.

Rajerison said the fair would let the company present its products and meet the Malagasy diaspora. Edenia has already formed several partnerships in France, according to Midi Madagasikara.

Why it mattersEdenia’s preparations include taking nearly 40 kilos of chocolate to the 19 December fair, where the company will meet the Malagasy diaspora.
