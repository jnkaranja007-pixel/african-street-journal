Fondation H marks 10 years with two art exhibitions

Two major exhibitions will open on November 13 at Ambatomena, featuring artists who have won awards from the foundation.

Source: NewsMada · 2026-10-23 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Fondation H will celebrate its 10th anniversary with two major art exhibitions opening on November 13 at its Ambatomena location.

The first exhibition, "Masoandro amam-bolana," will showcase the work of Temandrota, focusing on Malagasy culture, spirituality, and philosophy. The second exhibition, "Ranty hikambanana," will feature works by 21 artists who have received awards from the foundation over the past decade, under the direction of N’Goné Fall.

Artists whose work will be displayed include Yannick Tojonantenaina Andrianambonisoa, Finoana Rato­vo Andriantseheno, Mian­galy Elia Andriantsoa, Dina Nomena Andria­rimanjaka, and Olivia Bourgois, among others. This exhibition highlights individual creativity and the foundation's efforts over 10 years.

Why it mattersMalagasy artists and the public will experience two art exhibitions celebrating the 10th anniversary of Fondation H.
