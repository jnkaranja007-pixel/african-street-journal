Émile Ratefinanahary re-elected head of Madagascar karate federation

Ratefinanahary secured 11 votes for his second term after an election attended by 12 of 15 regional leagues.

Source: Midi Madagasikara · 2026-11-02 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Émile Ratefinanahary has been re-elected president of the Malagasy Karate Federation for a second term. The election took place in Fianarantsoa during the federation's general assembly, which was combined with the Madagascar Championship. 12 of the 15 regional leagues were represented, meeting the quorum requirement.

As the sole candidate, Ratefinanahary received 11 votes, with one league abstaining. Mara Boniface, president of the Androy league, stated that the league presidents conducted the election as the supreme body. Hervé Ranoroson, president of the Alaotra-Mangoro league, addressed social media controversies, asserting that claims of a collective of leagues were false and constituted usurpation of functions.

The election proceeded according to the federation's statutes, solidifying the legitimacy of Ratefinanahary's new mandate. The federation's next steps were not detailed in the report.
