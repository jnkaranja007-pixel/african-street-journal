Miara Manorina proposes Madagascar plan through 2060

The movement sets growth targets of at least 6% from 2027 to 2032, about 9% from 2033 to 2045 and 10% from 2046 to 2060.

Source: Midi Madagasikara · 2026-11-24 · 1 min read

Miara Manorina, a movement founded by Rija Randriamalalaniaina, is proposing a national development plan for Madagascar through 2060. Its Vina Iraisana, or Common Vision Madagascar 2060, calls for priorities that continue across changes of government, with annual growth targets of at least 6% from 2027 to 2032, about 9% from 2033 to 2045 and 10% from 2046 to 2060.

Randriamalalaniaina told Midi Madagasikara that the figures are targets dependent on deep reforms, not assured forecasts. For the first phase, the movement names six priorities: human capital, the rule of law and security, public administration, productive infrastructure, wealth-creating sectors and macroeconomic stability.

The proposal calls for stronger transport and energy networks and growth in agriculture, industry, tourism and digital services. Miara Manorina says it wants citizens, entrepreneurs, experts, regions and the diaspora to take part in shaping the plan.

Why it mattersPotential participants, including investors and the diaspora, are being asked to help shape the first phase, which covers 2027 to 2032.
