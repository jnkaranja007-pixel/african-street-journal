Madagascar Approves 141 Vanilla Exporters for 2026-2027 Season

The Ministry of Commerce and Consumption has authorized a first group of companies to export vanilla, aiming to address market challenges.

Source: Madagascar Tribune · 2026-10-21 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X

The Ministry of Commerce and Consumption announced on Monday, October 19, the initial list of 141 authorized operators for the 2026-2027 vanilla export campaign. These selected companies and exporters can collect their accreditation certificates starting Wednesday, October 21. This move comes as the vanilla sector grapples with marketing difficulties, price volatility, and stringent international quality demands.

The approved list includes specialized agricultural product firms, import-export businesses, and individual operators, all vetted according to criteria established in a 2013 decree. The ministry emphasized that accreditations are contingent on verifying the administrative and fiscal standing of beneficiaries and ensuring the repatriation of foreign currency from their operations. Authorities also plan to increase oversight during the campaign, with potential sanctions including authorization withdrawal for non-compliance.

The vanilla sector, particularly vital for the SAVA region where it is a primary income source for many families, faces persistent economic challenges. Fluctuations in global prices directly impact producer revenues and collector stock sales. Issues with premature harvesting, preparation, and storage also compromise the product's market value, while international buyers prioritize traceability and consistent supply meeting specific quality standards.

Why it matters141 approved exporters will navigate market and quality challenges for the 2026-2027 vanilla season.
