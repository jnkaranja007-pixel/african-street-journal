Ravinala Airports and Okalou bring Light Festival to Ivato

The 21 November programme combines family shows, performances by Nate Tex, Mage 4 and Big MJ (a Malagasy rapper and performer), and a light show at 18:30.

Sources: Midi Madagasikara and L'Express de Madagascar · 2026-11-19 · 1 min read

Ravinala Airports and the event organiser Okalou will hold the first Light Festival at Antananarivo International Airport in Ivato on Saturday, 21 November. The one-day event will use the airport for performances and activities for passengers and other visitors.

The programme includes two family shows, "Le Voyage de Pumbaa" and "Timon et Les Lumières du Monde", alongside performances by Nate Tex, Mage 4 and Big MJ. Momota will run the children's area, and food stands will be set up at the airport. A light show is scheduled for 18:30, followed by a performance from Mazaval.

Ravinala Airports chief executive Daniel Lefebvre said the festival was intended to improve passengers' experience. The airport operator is marking 10 years of managing the airport this year.

Why it mattersPassengers and visitors to Ivato can attend a full day of shows, children's activities and food stands on 21 November.
