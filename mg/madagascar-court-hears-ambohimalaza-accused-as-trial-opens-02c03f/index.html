Madagascar court hears Ambohimalaza accused as trial opens

Fenohasina denied accusations over a 2025 birthday party linked to the deaths of 35 people, while nearly 50 civil parties seek compensation.

Sources: Midi Madagasikara and Madagascar Tribune · 2026-11-19 · 1 min read

Madagascar’s criminal court opened the Ambohimalaza case on Wednesday in Antananarivo, hearing Fenohasina, the organiser of a birthday party linked to a suspected food poisoning that killed 35 people. 11 accused appeared before the court, and none admitted responsibility, Madagascar Tribune reported.

Fenohasina rejected accusations against herself and her mother, saying she organised the invitations and food at the event on 14 June 2025 and had also become seriously ill after eating there. Nearly 50 people have joined the case as civil parties seeking compensation; some bereaved families have yet to be heard.

The hearings are due to continue. Another accused, Clarisse Randriamanga, denied putting a toxic substance in the food and alleged gendarmes used physical violence to obtain an earlier confession; the court will hear her claims.
