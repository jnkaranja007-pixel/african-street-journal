Madagascar civil society groups prepare joint statement on election process

The planned declaration would challenge the conduct of the 9 and 10 November electoral consultations at the Ivato International Conference Centre.

Source: Madagascar Tribune · 2026-11-22 · 1 min read

Several civil society organisations and members in Madagascar are preparing a joint statement on the electoral consultations held on 9 and 10 November at the Ivato International Conference Centre, according to Madagascar Tribune. The declaration has not yet been made public and could be released as early as next week.

The groups are expected to raise reservations about how the consultations were conducted and about institutional decisions that could shape the country's political future. They are also expected to press for stronger guarantees of transparency and independence in the mechanisms overseeing the coming polls, including a constitutional referendum announced before the 2027 elections.

The authorities of the Refoundation process intend to keep the first elections on schedule for 2027, despite persistent questions about how they will be organised. The organisations have not yet formally announced their initiative.

Why it mattersElectoral bodies and the authorities running the 2027 polls face demands for stronger transparency and independence guarantees before the referendum and elections proceed.
