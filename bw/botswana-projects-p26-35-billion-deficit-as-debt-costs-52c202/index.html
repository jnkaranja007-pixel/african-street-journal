Botswana projects P26.35 billion deficit as debt costs reach P38.46 billion

The Ministry of Finance says the 2026/2027 shortfall is approximately nine percent of GDP, while public debt is expected to reach 45% by March 2027.

Source: The Botswana Gazette · 2026-11-19 · 1 min read

Botswana’s Ministry of Finance projects a P26.35 billion fiscal deficit and P38.46 billion in debt-service payments for the 2026/2027 financial year, leaving the government facing a larger borrowing need alongside repayments.

The ministry’s 2027/2028 Budget Strategy Paper says the deficit is approximately nine percent of Gross Domestic Product (GDP), and public debt is expected to climb to 45% of GDP by March 2027. Total public debt, including guarantees, stood at P95 billion in June 2026, equal to 32% of GDP.

Domestic debt accounted for P62.83 billion of that total and external debt for P32.17 billion. The ministry warns that rising debt-service costs will constrain funding for essential services and development programmes.

Why it mattersPublic services and development programmes face tighter funding as the government’s debt-service bill includes interest and is projected at P38.46 billion for 2026/2027.
