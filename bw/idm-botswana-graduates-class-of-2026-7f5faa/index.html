IDM Botswana graduates Class of 2026

The Institute of Development Management will hold its 20th Joint Graduation Ceremony on October 30, 2026, in Tlokweng.

Source: Weekend Post · 2026-10-30 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

The Institute of Development Management (IDM) Botswana is hosting its 20th Joint Graduation Ceremony on Friday, 30 October 2026, at the Royal Aria Convention Centre in Tlokweng. The event will celebrate the academic and professional achievements of the Class of 2026 from Botswana, Lesotho, and Eswatini.

Minister for State President, Defence and Security, Honourable Moeti Ceasar Mohwasa, will deliver the keynote address. George, Earl of St Andrews, Chancellor of the University of Greater Manchester, United Kingdom, will confer postgraduate and undergraduate degrees awarded by his university.

The ceremony theme is “Thriving as a Continuous Learner in an Ever-Changing Environment,” highlighting lifelong learning and adaptability. IDM reaffirms its commitment to developing management and professional capacity across the region.

Why it mattersGraduands from Botswana, Lesotho, and Eswatini will receive degrees and celebrate their achievements.
