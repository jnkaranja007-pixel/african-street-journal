Masisi, Boko Forge Diamond Trade Lifeline to Dubai

Former and current presidents have transformed diamond beneficiation into a geopolitical and economic defense strategy.

Source: Sunday Standard · 2026-10-21 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X

Former President Mokgweetsi Masisi established a significant trade relationship with Dubai, valued in the billions of dollars. President Duma Boko has since expanded this initiative, institutionalizing it as a robust economic defense mechanism.

This continuity in policy between the two leaders has solidified Botswana's diamond beneficiation efforts, turning what was once viewed skeptically into a strong geopolitical asset.

The strategic move has converted a nationalistic endeavor, previously seen as a slow-moving failure by global observers, into a world-class strategic advantage for the nation.

Why it mattersInvestors are affected by the strengthening of Botswana's diamond beneficiation into a geopolitical and economic defense strategy.
