Researchers track slaty egret to assess Okavango wetland health

The bird’s population and breeding data are expected to help authorities plan protection for its habitat in Botswana’s Delta.

Source: Weekend Post · 2026-11-20 · 1 min read

Researchers and wildlife authorities in Botswana are tracking the slaty egret in the Okavango Delta to assess the condition of its wetland habitat. The vulnerable waterbird is found mainly in the Delta and neighbouring floodplains across the Okavango and Zambezi river basins.

Monei Onalethatai, a senior wildlife biologist at Botswana’s Department of Wildlife and National Parks, said the bird is sensitive to habitat disturbance. Researchers are seeking clearer data on its population, distribution and breeding success, which could inform conservation planning.

The Agreement on the Conservation of African-Eurasian Migratory Waterbirds is supporting a regional plan to coordinate protection of the egret and its wetland habitat. Conservationists are also concerned about climate change and water-management decisions across national borders.

Why it mattersBotswana wildlife authorities and researchers need population, range and breeding data to shape conservation planning for the Delta’s wetlands.
