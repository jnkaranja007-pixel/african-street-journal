Botswana businesses struggle to create jobs

The nation's economy is not growing fast enough to absorb new graduates.

Source: Weekend Post · 2026-11-12 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Botswana's recent graduation ceremonies celebrate many new graduates, but questions linger about their future employment prospects. The nation's economy faces a significant challenge in creating jobs at the same rate educated young people are entering the workforce.

While some argue universities produce inadequately prepared graduates, the primary issue may be the economy's demand side. Businesses need to grow, invest, and innovate to absorb the talent and creativity of graduates.

A stagnant private sector, undercapitalised and unwilling to expand, will perpetuate unemployment regardless of curriculum changes. Economic growth, driven by enterprises producing more goods and services, is essential for job creation.

Why it mattersGraduates face unemployment because Botswana's businesses are not expanding and creating enough new opportunities.
