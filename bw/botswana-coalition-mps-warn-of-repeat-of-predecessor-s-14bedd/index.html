Botswana coalition MPs warn of repeat of predecessor's election defeat

Two years after their historic election victory, some MPs in the Umbrella for Democratic Change (UDC) coalition are raising alarm about its future at the polls.

Source: Weekend Post · 2026-11-23 · 1 min read

Some MPs from the Umbrella for Democratic Change (UDC), the coalition that won a historic election victory in Botswana, are warning it could suffer the same electoral fate as its predecessor. The warning comes two years after that victory, according to the Weekend Post.

The MPs who raised the concern helped bring the UDC to power, the newspaper reported. The report does not say what prompted the warning or name the MPs involved.

The report gives no response from the coalition leadership and no next step by its MPs.

Why it mattersParties in the coalition now face pressure from within over whether they can keep the seats they won, which bears on the next national vote.
