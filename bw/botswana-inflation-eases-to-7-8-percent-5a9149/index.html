Botswana inflation eases to 7.8 percent

Botswana's annual inflation rate fell by 1.5 percentage points in September, offering some relief from rising costs.

Source: Sunday Standard · 2026-10-22 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Botswana's annual inflation rate declined sharply to 7.8 percent in September from 9.3 percent in August. The decrease of 1.5 percentage points offers some relief to consumers and businesses facing elevated operating costs. Statistics Botswana's Consumer Price Index report for September 2026 detailed the fall, noting that transport costs remained the primary driver of price increases across the economy.

The easing inflation rate provides a welcome respite for households and companies that have been struggling with the financial impact of rising prices. While the overall inflation has decreased, the persistent increases in transport costs continue to affect economic activity and household budgets.

The September figures indicate a positive trend in price stability, although the underlying causes of transport cost increases require ongoing attention. This development is a key indicator for economic health and consumer confidence in Botswana.

Why it mattersConsumers and businesses are affected by the reduced rate of price increases, though transport costs remain a significant factor.
