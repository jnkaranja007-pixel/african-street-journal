Gaborone launches electric bus service

New Metrobus initiative faces challenges due to lack of policy and network reforms.

Source: The Botswana Gazette · 2026-11-10 · 1 min read

Gaborone has launched an electric Metrobus service operated by Tana Holdings, a subsidiary of CEDA. The initiative aims to provide more accessible, efficient, and environmentally sustainable public transport in the capital city.

The success of the electric buses, however, hinges on policy and network reforms first proposed 15 years ago. Without a coordinated network plan and an accountable passenger transport authority, the project risks underperforming, according to the Botswana Gazette.

The September 2011 draft Integrated Transport Policy White Paper outlined necessary reforms, including bus priority schemes and accessible boarding facilities. Government has been advised to fund socially necessary services transparently and ensure operators remain commercially viable through proper fare policies and service contracts.
