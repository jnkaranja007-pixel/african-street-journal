Botswana youth demand 30 percent parliamentary quota

A democracy forum report released in November highlights youth calls for greater political influence following the 2024 general elections.

Source: The Botswana Gazette · 2026-11-17 · 1 min read

Young Batswana are demanding a 30 percent quota in Parliament for citizens under 35, according to a Democracy International report. This push aims to give the younger generation a greater stake in national decision-making.

The report, titled ‘Beyond Elections: What We Learned Together in Botswana,’ emerged from the 13th Global Forum on Modern Direct Democracy held in Gaborone from October 7–10. It suggests that political change alone, such as the 2024 election that saw the Umbrella for Democratic Change (UDC) take power, may not be enough to address youth representation.

Discussions at the forum also explored alternative methods for youth influence, including children's participation and the contribution of Kgotla ya Basha. The proposal for a 30 percent parliamentary seat reservation for those under 35 was a specific suggestion put forward during the international gathering.
