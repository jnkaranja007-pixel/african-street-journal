Botswana seeks P4.7 billion from investors in debt auction on November 27

The sale is a test of how much domestic appetite remains for the government's debt, with three-quarters of the money maturing within six months.

Source: Weekend Post · 2026-11-25 · 1 min read

The Bank of Botswana will go to the market on November 27 seeking P4.7 billion, offering five government securities under the state's P85 billion Government Note Programme. The central bank announced the auction on November 16, and the government borrows from domestic investors under the programme rather than from foreign lenders.

Treasury bills make up P3.4 billion of the offering, split evenly between a three-month bill maturing on March 3, 2027 and a six-month bill maturing on June 2, 2027. The remaining P1.3 billion comes from reopening three existing bonds: P100 million in BOTSGB0929 and P600 million each in BOTSGB0943 and BOTSGB1150.

Bidding closes at 10:00 on November 27, with results at noon and settlement on December 2.

Why it mattersInvestors who buy the three-month and six-month bills will be repaid in full at maturity, with the discount as their return.
