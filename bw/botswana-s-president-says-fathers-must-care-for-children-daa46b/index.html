Botswana's President says fathers must care for children named by mothers

President Duma Boko says a man named as father is presumed to be the father until a DNA test proves otherwise.

Source: Weekend Post · 2026-11-12 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Botswana's President, who is also Advocate Duma Boko, a lawyer, said this week that when a woman says a man is the father of her child, he is the father and must take care of the child. He made the remarks at a workshop with UNICEF officials and the Ministry of Child Welfare, convened to overhaul the country's children's legislation.

The workshop discussed a proposal raised in the House of Chiefs to make DNA testing compulsory. Boko described a man named by a mother as a putative, or presumed, father, and said only a DNA test proving otherwise can release him from the duty of care. He warned that compulsory testing could show some men have fathered children they never acknowledged.

Paternity tests compare short tandem repeat markers in a child's DNA with those of a potential father, excluding the mother's contribution. Laboratories typically report a probability of paternity of 99.99 percent or higher when the markers match. Boko said online that no child should suffer because of circumstances beyond their control.

Why it mattersMen named as fathers by mothers would be presumed responsible for child support unless a DNA test shows otherwise, under the reform Boko described.
