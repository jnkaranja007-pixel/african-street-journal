Botswana adopts revised turnover bands for small businesses

The gazetted definition sets annual turnover limits from P270,000 for micro-enterprises to P20 million for medium firms, with turnover deciding the category when measures differ.

Source: The Botswana Gazette · 2026-11-20 · 1 min read

Botswana has adopted a revised definition for micro, small and medium enterprises, replacing financial thresholds set in 1999. The government gazetted the new classification after the Local Enterprise Authority reviewed the limits and lobbied for their adoption.

Dynah Solani, the authority’s head of information and knowledge management, said the common framework will determine which businesses qualify for enterprise-specific programmes and government support. Micro-enterprises can employ up to six people and have annual turnover of no more than P270,000; small firms fall between P270,000 and P7 million, while medium firms generate P7 million to P20 million a year.

When staff numbers and turnover put a business in different categories, the new rules use turnover to decide. The previous financial thresholds had not been adjusted for inflation in 27 years, Solani said.

Why it mattersBusinesses seeking SMME-specific programmes will be classified by turnover when their employee numbers point to a different category.
