Young Zebras miss Ratshukudu for U-23 Cup qualifier

Botswana's Under-23 team faces Guinea without their top scorer, Losika Ratshukudu, who joins the senior squad.

Source: Weekend Post · 2026-11-12 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Botswana's Young Zebras will attempt to reach the U-23 Africa Cup of Nations without their leading scorer, Losika Ratshukudu, for their second-round qualifier against Guinea. The first leg match is scheduled for November 11 at Gaborone National Stadium, kicking off at 19:00. Coach Michael Mogaladi's squad selection omits Ratshukudu, who scored three goals in the previous round against Seychelles.

Ratshukudu's absence is due to his call-up to Botswana's senior national team for a 2027 Africa Cup of Nations qualifier against Uganda on November 14. This promotion signifies progress for Botswana football but presents a challenge for the Under-23 side, which must now find alternative attacking strategies against stronger opponents. Mogaladi aims to develop a more collective attacking effort, with remaining forwards and midfielders needing to increase their responsibility.

The return match against Guinea is scheduled for November 17 in Conakry. With only six days between the two legs, the coaching staff has limited time to address any tactical issues. The Young Zebras' qualification hopes may depend on their ability to adapt quickly to playing without their key finisher and secure a positive result in the home leg at Gaborone National Stadium.

Why it mattersThe Young Zebras' qualification hopes for the U-23 Africa Cup of Nations are directly impacted by the absence of their top scorer, Losika Ratshukudu, for crucial matches against Guinea.
