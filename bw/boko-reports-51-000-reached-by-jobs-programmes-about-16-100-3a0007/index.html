Boko reports 51,000 reached by jobs programmes, about 16,100 permanent

Botswana’s 2026 State of the Nation Address counted public works, insourcing and other schemes, with many placements still temporary.

Source: Weekend Post · 2026-11-20 · 1 min read

Botswana President Duma Boko told Parliament on 9 November that employment programmes had reached 51,000 people, but only about 16,100 positions were permanent-type. The figures appeared in his 2026 State of the Nation Address, which set out public works, public-service insourcing and other routes to employment.

The Ikageng Public Works Programme engaged nearly 27,600 young people, 79% of them women, and labour expenditure exceeded P820 million since 2025. More than P274 million went directly to young workers trained to build and maintain roads and water lines, according to the address. The Remote Area Development Programme placed 2,869 graduates in permanent jobs in 2025 and gave more than 10,600 young people temporary work.

The Public Service Insourcing Programme, launched on 1 April, is projected to employ about 9,380 people; 4,366 temporary employees had already moved to permanent posts. An internship programme placed 804 graduates in private firms, funded with P17.6 million from the Human Resource Development Fund.

Why it mattersPeople in the programmes face a clear divide between temporary placements and roughly 16,100 permanent-type positions across the schemes.
