Beauty Reset Brunch returns to Gaborone

Jessica Molefe's women-focused experience will feature sisterhood, wellness, and personal growth on February 27, 2027.

Source: The Botswana Gazette · 2026-11-08 · 1 min read

Jessica Molefe's Beauty Reset Brunch is scheduled to return to Gaborone on February 27, 2027. The event will be held at the Game City Rooftop, focusing on sisterhood, wellness, and personal growth.

Makeup artist and content creator Jessica Molefe announced that tickets are now on sale for the upcoming brunch. She stated that attendees can expect a day filled with beauty, meaningful conversations, inspiration, and unforgettable experiences designed to celebrate personal transformation.

Following a successful inaugural event earlier this year, the brunch aims to provide a space for women to discuss self-perception and personal development. Professionals from beauty, dermatology, fitness, and mindset sectors will share practical knowledge.
