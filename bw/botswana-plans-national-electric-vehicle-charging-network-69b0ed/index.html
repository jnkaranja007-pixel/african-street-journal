Botswana plans national electric-vehicle charging network for 2027

The state-linked programme vehicle that will build the network is now operational, and the plan must still be costed and measured.

Source: The Botswana Gazette · 2026-11-26 · 1 min read

Botswana plans to build a national electric-vehicle charging network in 2027, Dr Ecco Maje, managing director of eMobility Botswana (Pty) Ltd, told The Botswana Gazette. The special-purpose vehicle advancing the programme is now operational.

A reliable network would let motorists and freight operators charge beyond private facilities, including on journeys between Gaborone, Francistown, Maun and Kasane. The Gazette reports that a full shift to electric transport could eventually displace up to P16 billion in petroleum imports a year; that is a potential gross saving, not an immediate net benefit.

The Gazette says the ambition must become a costed and measurable commitment under Botswana's third nationally determined contribution (NDC 3.0), the country's climate pledge under the Paris Agreement. The International Energy Agency says public charging points worldwide topped five million in 2024, more than double the 2022 total.

Why it mattersInvestors in chargers and drivers planning long trips depend on this network, and it will only become a firm commitment once its costs and targets are set.
