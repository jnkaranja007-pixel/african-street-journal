Botswana's Orange Summer Challenge advances four AI business finalists

The teams were selected from 361 entries and developed products with commercial potential, including customer-service tools and a Setswana voice contact centre.

Source: Weekend Post · 2026-11-26 · 1 min read

Four teams presented AI-based business products at the Orange Summer Challenge 2026 closing ceremony in Gaborone, after being selected from 361 entries across Botswana. The finalists had spent months developing concepts with commercial potential, under the theme “AI as a Business Accelerator.”

The teams built tools for everyday services: reKwano created a feature to help users find Orange Money cash agents; Co.nect explored customer-service and financial tools; Leseding Engine AI made a voice-first contact centre in Setswana and English; and Kalon AI Platform developed a digital twin for managing telecom networks. Orange Botswana said participants tested ideas in weekly sessions with mentors and staff from Orange Money, MaxIt and its network teams.

Orange Botswana chief executive Nene Maiga said the company’s connectivity, data centre capacity, Orange Money and MaxIt could help founders reach customers. Orange Money serves more than 1.3 million customers. The company said more than 3,400 people completed free training at its Orange Digital Center between February and July 2026, 57% of them women.

Why it mattersFounders developing customer services can build on Orange Money’s network of more than 1.3 million customers, according to chief executive Nene Maiga.
