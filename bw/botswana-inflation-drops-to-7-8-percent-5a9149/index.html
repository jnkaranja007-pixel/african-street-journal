Botswana Inflation Drops to 7.8 Percent

The annual inflation rate fell by 1.5 percentage points in September, providing some relief from rising costs.

Botswana's annual inflation rate decreased to 7.8 percent in September, down from 9.3 percent in August. Statistics Botswana reported that the Consumer Price Index fell by 1.5 percentage points, offering some relief to consumers and businesses facing high operating expenses. Despite the overall decline, transport costs remained the primary driver of price increases across the economy.

The September 2026 Consumer Price Index report, released by Statistics Botswana, detailed the sharp decline in inflation. This easing of price pressures comes as a welcome development for households and companies that have been contending with elevated costs for goods and services over recent months.

While the overall inflation figure has improved, the persistent rise in transport costs continues to impact the economy. This sector remains the largest contributor to price increases, suggesting that the full extent of cost relief may not yet be felt by all segments of the population and business community.

Why it mattersConsumers and businesses may experience some relief from elevated operating costs due to the decrease in the inflation rate.
