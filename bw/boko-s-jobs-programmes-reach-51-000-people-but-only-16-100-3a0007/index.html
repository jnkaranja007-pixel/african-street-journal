Boko’s jobs programmes reach 51,000 people, but only 16,100 jobs are permanent-type

Botswana’s 2026 State of the Nation Address reports public works, insourcing and internship schemes alongside a push for private-sector growth.

Source: Weekend Post · 2026-11-21 · 1 min read

Botswana President Duma Boko told Parliament on 9 November that employment programmes had reached 51,000 people, but only about 16,100 positions were permanent-type. His 2026 State of the Nation Address cited public works, public-service insourcing and other schemes as routes to employment.

The Ikageng Public Works Programme has engaged nearly 27,600 young people since 2025, 79% of them women, with labour spending above P820 million. More than P274 million went directly to trained youth working on roads and water lines. Insourcing, launched on 1 April, is projected to employ about 9,380 people; 4,366 temporary workers have already moved into permanent posts.

The Remote Area Development Programme placed 2,869 graduates in permanent jobs in the defence force, police and wildlife department in 2025, while more than 10,600 young people took temporary work. The National Internship Programme placed 804 graduates in private firms at a cost of P17.6 million.

Why it mattersYoung people seeking work have access to programmes that have engaged nearly 27,600 through Ikageng, while 4,366 insourcing workers have gained permanent posts.
