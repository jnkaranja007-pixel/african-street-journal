Four Botswana teams pitch AI businesses after 361 entries

The Orange Summer Challenge finalists developed tools for payments, customer service and telecoms after months of work with mentors and industry teams.

Source: Weekend Post · 2026-11-27 · 1 min read

Four teams presented business ideas at the Orange Summer Challenge closing ceremony in Gaborone, after being selected from 361 entries across Botswana. The finalists had spent months developing products under the programme’s 2026 theme, “AI as a Business Accelerator.”

The projects include a tool to help users find Orange Money cash agents, AI services for customer support and financial tools, a Setswana- and English-speaking contact centre, and a digital twin for managing telecom networks. Orange Botswana said more than 3,400 people completed free training at its Digital Center between February and July 2026; 57% were women.

The teams received weekly guidance from mentors and staff linked to Orange Money, MaxIt and Orange network teams. Orange Money serves more than 1.3 million customers in Botswana, according to chief executive Nene Maiga.

Why it mattersThe four finalist teams now have to turn their prototypes into products customers will use, after advancing from 361 entries.
