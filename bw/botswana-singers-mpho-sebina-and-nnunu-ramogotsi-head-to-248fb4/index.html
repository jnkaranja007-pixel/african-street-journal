Botswana singers Mpho Sebina and Nnunu Ramogotsi head to Sandton

The two artists will perform their original music live at Artistry in Sandton on March 26, 2027, under the Music & Diplomacy initiative.

Source: The Botswana Gazette · 2026-11-16 · 1 min read

Botswana singers Mpho Sebina and Nnunu Ramogotsi are scheduled to perform at Artistry in Sandton, Johannesburg, on March 26, 2027. The showcase is meant to put Botswana's sound in front of a new audience in one of the region's hardest music markets.

Ramogotsi told Time Out that the invitation was a major step for them as they try to break into the South African market, which she described as very hard to penetrate. The appearance falls under the Music & Diplomacy initiative, which uses music to connect artists, audiences, cultural institutions and businesses across borders. Both artists will perform original material live.

A joint collaboration is not confirmed, but Ramogotsi said collaborations are being lined up at home and abroad. She is currently promoting her album Sephonono, which won Best Jazz Album of the Year 2026 and earned her the Best Achievement Award under BOMU.

Why it mattersSebina and Ramogotsi gain a showcase before a South African audience, a step toward building a fan base beyond Botswana.
