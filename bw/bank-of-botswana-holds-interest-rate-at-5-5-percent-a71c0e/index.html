Bank of Botswana holds interest rate at 5.5 percent

Inflation eased to 7.8 percent in September but remains above the central bank's target range.

Source: Weekend Post · 2026-11-10 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

The Bank of Botswana kept its main interest rate at 5.5 percent on Wednesday, a decision made by the Monetary Policy Committee chaired by Governor Lesego C. Moseki. This move aims to manage inflation without hindering the economy's recovery, which has seen growth for the first time in two years.

Consumer price inflation fell to 7.8 percent in September from 9.3 percent the previous month, though much of this decrease is attributed to a statistical base effect from a fuel price increase in September 2025. Underlying inflation remains above the bank's 3-to-6 percent comfort zone, with expectations of continued pressure from rising fuel and electricity costs.

Policymakers project inflation to average 8.1 percent for 2026 before dropping to 5.4 percent in 2027, with a return to the target band anticipated in the second quarter of next year. The committee noted upside risks including potential fuel price hikes, electricity tariff increases, and threats to food costs from El Niño-related weather events.

Why it mattersThe Bank of Botswana's decision to maintain interest rates affects borrowing costs for businesses and consumers, impacting economic activity.
