Boko backs Tamamyan for WHO leadership role

President Boko nominated Dr Gevorg Tamamyan, citing a need for effective global health leadership.

President Duma Boko has nominated Dr Gevorg Tamamyan for the World Health Organization's top position. Boko formally proposed Tamamyan for the role of Director-General at the World Health Assembly in May 2027 in a letter to the WHO Executive Board chairperson.

The President stated Botswana's health-system challenges underscore the need for effective global health leadership. He wrote that multilateral institutions under strain require a demonstrably effective WHO, not a diminished one. Boko's endorsement centres on Tamamyan's commitment to underserved communities and improving healthcare access.

Boko linked the nomination to Botswana's experience confronting its own health system limitations. He framed the endorsement around the WHO's ability to respond to countries' healthcare needs, particularly for those struggling to obtain care.

Why it mattersPresident Boko's nomination of Dr. Gevorg Tamamyan for WHO Director-General aims to improve global health leadership and healthcare access for underserved communities, reflecting Botswana's own health system challenges.

Beauty Reset Brunch returns to Gaborone

Jessica Molefe's women-focused experience will feature sisterhood, wellness, and personal growth on February 27, 2027.

Jessica Molefe's Beauty Reset Brunch is scheduled to return to Gaborone on February 27, 2027. The event will be held at the Game City Rooftop, focusing on sisterhood, wellness, and personal growth.

Makeup artist and content creator Jessica Molefe announced that tickets are now on sale for the upcoming brunch. She stated that attendees can expect a day filled with beauty, meaningful conversations, inspiration, and unforgettable experiences designed to celebrate personal transformation.

Following a successful inaugural event earlier this year, the brunch aims to provide a space for women to discuss self-perception and personal development. Professionals from beauty, dermatology, fitness, and mindset sectors will share practical knowledge.

Botswana artist wins Tokyo award

Katlego Twala received the Sponsor Award at the inaugural Toyota Tsusho Corporation African Art Award.

Botswana's Katlego C.L. Twala earned the Sponsor Award at the first Toyota Tsusho Corporation (TTC) African Art Award in Tokyo, Japan. The recognition celebrates her artistic excellence and perspectives connecting Africa with the world.

Twala was among young African artists from Botswana, South Africa, Zimbabwe, Ethiopia, and the Democratic Republic of the Congo exhibited in Tokyo. TTC established the award to support artists across Africa and provide a platform for them to showcase their work in Japan.

The Tokyo award adds to Twala's international profile, following her 2024 New Emergence Art Prize win and a sold-out solo booth at the 2025 Latitudes Art Fair.

Why it mattersBotswana artist Katlego Twala's creative vision is now part of an international conversation following her award in Tokyo.

Botswana cookbook named global finalist

Kenneth Middleton's 'Paradise' cookbook is one of eight worldwide contenders for the 2026 Gourmand World Cookbook Awards.

Kenneth Middleton's debut cookbook, 'Paradise', is among eight global finalists for the 2026 Gourmand World Cookbook Awards in the First Cookbook category. Published by Penguin Random House South Africa, the book features recipes ranging from Botswana's seswaa to Vietnamese pho and Korean fried chicken.

Middleton thanked his publishers and supporters, stating, "Until then, this office remains optimistically disposed towards a favourable verdict and we place our fate in the hands of providence." The cookbook celebrates indulgent, guilt-free cooking while incorporating regional flavours.

The winners will be announced in May 2027. 'Paradise' has already earned a spot among the world's notable debut cookbooks, showcasing Botswana's culinary creativity internationally.

Why it mattersBotswana's culinary culture is gaining international recognition through Kenneth Middleton's cookbook.
