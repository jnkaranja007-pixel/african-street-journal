Botswana inflation falls to 9.4% in July

Annual inflation declined to 9.4% in July from 10.7% in June, according to Statistics Botswana. The 1.3 percentage-point drop followed a 6.5% decrease in the transport index.

Why it mattersLower fuel prices reduced transport costs for consumers and businesses after a price surge earlier this year.

MMG adds 1.4 million tonnes of copper at Khoemacau mine

MMG Ltd discovered a copper-silver deposit at the Kgwebe site, adding 1.4 million tonnes of copper and 90 million ounces of silver to its mineral resources. The deposit is located six kilometers from the existing Zone 5 mine.

Why it mattersThe discovery strengthens the long-term growth prospects of the Khoemacau operation.

Botswana retains P1,500 daily per diem for public servants

The government maintained a P1,500 daily local per diem for public servants, according to the Hospitality and Tourism Association of Botswana. Starting Sept. 1, 2026, servants must provide receipts for accommodation components up to P900.

Why it mattersThe policy is expected to increase demand for the domestic hospitality industry.

African Development Bank values Botswana natural capital at $116 billion

The African Development Bank estimated Botswana's natural capital, including wildlife and water, at $116 billion as of 2020. The figure represents the value of assets over time rather than available public spending.

Why it mattersThe country is seeking these natural assets as alternative sources of growth and financing to reduce its dependence on diamonds.

Botswana working-age population projected to reach 67.6% by 2038

The population of people aged 15 to 64 is projected to rise to 67.6% of the total population by 2038. The projection is cited by the Sunday Standard.

Why it mattersThe demographic shift requires the country to generate more jobs and raise productivity to convert the population growth into economic growth.
