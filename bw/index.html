Journalist wins diamond at Botswana independence event

Veteran correspondent Joy Chikura received a diamond from Debswana at a national business conference dinner.

Duma FM news correspondent Joy Chikura won a diamond in a lucky draw at the Debswana-sponsored National Business Conference dinner last Monday. The mining giant awarded the gem to celebrate Botswana's 60-year Independence Anniversary. Chikura, who is from Zimbabwe, had never seen a diamond until this win.

Chikura, a veteran journalist, was assigned to cover the event when she was selected in the draw. Debswana Managing Director Andrew Maatla Motsumi was present. Chikura stated it was an honor and dedicated the diamond to journalists who inform the nation.

She has chosen to keep the diamond's value undisclosed. Chikura previously named her daughter Ruby, a name meaning red gemstone, and finds the win a happy coincidence. She mentioned it is the first time in many years Debswana has given out a diamond.

Three charged with possessing 20 uncut diamonds

Locals George Jeffries, Benjamini Gopolang, and Farai Koitheng appeared in court after a mall ambush.

Three Batswana, George Jeffries, 36, Benjamini Gopolang, 30, and Farai Koitheng, 47, appeared in Extension II Magistrates Court on Monday charged with unlawful possession of 20 uncut diamonds. Police apprehended the trio at The Fields Mall on Friday, October 23, acting on an informant's tip. The suspects were found with the diamonds during a midday sting operation witnessed by shoppers.

Prosecutor Kalako requested the accused be remanded in custody, stating that the weight and value of the diamonds are yet to be determined. Magistrate Kamogelo Mmesi described the allegations as serious and ordered the three to remain in custody for 14 days while police investigations continue. The accused include a purchased and inventory clerk, a mechanic, and an unemployed individual.

The case has generated public interest regarding how the individuals allegedly obtained the diamonds. The prosecution promised to provide further answers at the next court appearance, scheduled for November 9. The police are working to establish the full circumstances surrounding the alleged crime.
