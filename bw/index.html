BDC targets P5.5bn in investments and 25,000 jobs by 2031

Botswana has over P158 billion in pension assets, but only 45% of it is invested at home.

The Botswana Development Corporation (BDC) plans to deploy P5.5 billion into priority sectors and support the creation of 25,000 jobs by 2031. Managing Director Oteng Keabetswe set out the strategy at the BDC Investment Forum on Thursday.

Keabetswe described a "capital paradox" in which Botswana has plenty of savings but little capital reaching productive businesses. Private sector credit stands at about 30% of GDP, against 55% in comparable regional economies, and less than 5% of capital goes into private equity and venture capital.

Requirements for pension funds to invest about 50% of their assets domestically by 2027 could channel roughly P8 billion back into the economy, Keabetswe said. The BDC's targets depend partly on unlocking that domestic capital for businesses able to expand production.

Botswana writers take their craft to Joy City Park

A Writers Picnic on Sunday, 15 November, will bring authors outdoors to discuss writer's block, research and publishing.

Botswana's writers will meet outdoors at Joy City Park on 15 November 2026 for the Writers Picnic, organised by author Oduetse Tebogo. Tebogo said most literary forums are held in closed, formal settings, and she wanted to move the discussion into a more relaxed space.

The programme covers the practical side of writing: overcoming writer's block and self-doubt, researching, publishing and marketing books. Writer and communications practitioner Makarov Abotseng will speak on turning an idea into a manuscript, while Kabelo Nkwane will address publishing on platforms such as Amazon and Kindle.

Other sessions cover author branding, led by Henny Maruapula, and lessons from J.B.S. Mothowadimo's six-book journey. Gobotsamang Leshetla will speak on preserving culture, history and heritage through writing, and Bryan Saudu will facilitate the day.

Why it mattersAspiring and established writers in Botswana get a practical, low-cost chance to learn about publishing and marketing their books in one afternoon.

Grand Palm hosts final gin experience of 2026

Despite rain, Gaborone's gin enthusiasts gathered for premium pours, music, and social connection.

The Grand Palm Hotel in Gaborone concluded its 2026 Gin Experience events with a final gathering on Saturday. Gin enthusiasts attended the outdoor event, which ran from 15:00 to 23:00, to sample various brands and learn about gin's history and distilling methods. Brands such as Hendrick’s, Inverroche, Beefeater, Cruxland, and Tanqueray were available for tasting.

The scheduled tasting session lasted from 15:00 to 19:00, with an additional 15 minutes offered to guests. The event featured cocktails, live entertainment, and a relaxed social atmosphere, aiming to provide a comprehensive experience beyond just the drinks.

This gathering is part of the Grand Palm's ongoing entertainment and lifestyle programme celebrating its years of hospitality. The event received support from partners including Benju, Lengau, Heineken Botswana, and SMC Brands.

Why it mattersThe Grand Palm Hotel's entertainment programme celebrated its hospitality with a gin experience attended by local enthusiasts.
