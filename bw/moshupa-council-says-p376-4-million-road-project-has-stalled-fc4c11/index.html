Moshupa council says P376.4 million road project has stalled

Council chair Peter Sethibe said the contractor is unreachable and no work is under way on the 33.5-kilometre route.

Source: Weekend Post · 2026-11-24 · 1 min read

Construction on Botswana’s Moshupa–Manyana road has stopped at 47% completion, and the Moshupa District Council cannot reach Heinsite Construction, council chairperson Peter Sethibe told a Full Council meeting. The 33.5-kilometre project, awarded in 2024, missed its planned completion date of 17 October 2026.

The road was expected to provide another route from Moshupa through Manyana and Mmakgodi towards Gaborone, easing congestion on the A10. In July, Assistant Minister for Transport and Infrastructure Keoagile Atamelang said the work was 10 months behind schedule, with 42% physical progress against a planned 79%. He cited permit delays, 52 days lost to heavy rain and the contractor’s financial difficulties.

The government had paid P92,741,602.39 of the stated contract value of about P376.4 million, Atamelang said. The contractor had received a notice to accelerate work; Atamelang said the ministry was processing payments to support cash flow and still expected completion within budget.

Why it mattersThe council must now act on a stalled route intended to ease A10 congestion, while the contractor has missed its 17 October 2026 deadline.
