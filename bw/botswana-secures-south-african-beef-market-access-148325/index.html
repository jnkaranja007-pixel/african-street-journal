Botswana secures South African beef market access

The agreement allows for exports of processed beef and live cattle, providing farmers with a new market amid disease and drought concerns.

Source: Sunday Standard · 2026-10-22 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Botswana has successfully negotiated access to the South African market for its beef and live cattle exports. This development offers a crucial new avenue for the country's livestock farmers, who are currently facing pressure from foot-and-mouth disease (FMD) and the threat of drought.

Director of Veterinary Services Dr. Kobedi Segale confirmed that discussions with South African authorities commenced in August. The resulting agreement encompasses both processed beef and live cattle, provided they meet the necessary veterinary and export requirements stipulated by South Africa.

This market access comes at a critical time for Botswana's agricultural sector. The dual threats of FMD and potential drought have created an urgent need for alternative markets to support livestock producers and ensure the stability of the sector.

Why it mattersBotswanan farmers are affected by gaining a new market for their beef and live cattle exports.
