Botswana banks remain resilient, council finds

The Financial Stability Council reported on November 11 that Botswana's financial system is sound and supports economic activity.

Source: Weekend Post · 2026-11-17 · 1 min read

Botswana's financial system is resilient and supports economic activity, according to the Financial Stability Council (FSC). The council delivered its verdict in Gaborone on November 11, finding that the nation's banks and non-bank financial institutions are safe and sound.

The assessment is based on robust market infrastructures and effective supervisory machinery, regulators stated. The FSC concluded that the financial system is a sturdy vessel holding steady in increasingly choppy waters.

The council's findings indicate a stable financial environment, though the report cautioned that this calm is not self-sustaining. Further details on the sustainability of this financial resilience were not immediately available.
