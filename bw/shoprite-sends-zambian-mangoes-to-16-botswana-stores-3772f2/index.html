Shoprite sends Zambian mangoes to 16 Botswana stores

The first 10-ton consignment left Lusaka on 12 November, with 45 to 50 tons expected during the current mango season.

Source: Food Business Africa · 2026-11-19 · 1 min read

Shoprite Zambia is helping local supplier Chakanaka Investments export mangoes to Botswana, where the fruit is destined for 16 Shoprite, Checkers and Usave stores. The first consignment, 10 tons, left Lusaka on 12 November; 45 to 50 tons are expected to be exported during the current mango season.

Freshmark, Shoprite Zambia's produce procurement and distribution division, has worked with Chakanaka for several years, advising the grower on product specifications and quality requirements. The deal uses Zambia's harvest, which starts about two to three weeks before Botswana's, to supply the market before its local crop begins. Chakanaka is based in Chirundu, in Southern Province, and has supplied Shoprite Zambia since 2000.

Shoprite Zambia general manager Charles Bota said the retailer also sources produce from 126 registered Zambian farmers for 45 stores nationwide. Its local sourcing includes an avocado partnership with the Lusaka Avocado Multipurpose Cooperative Society, which represents about 500 farmers.

Why it mattersBotswana shoppers gain fresh mangoes from Zambia before the local crop ripens, and Zambian growers gain a route into regional trade through a retail buyer.
