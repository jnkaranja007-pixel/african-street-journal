First Capital Bank Botswana backs men's health run with P50,000

The sponsorship is part of the bank's Movember campaign, which aims to get men talking about their health and wellbeing.

Source: The Botswana Gazette · 2026-11-24 · 1 min read

First Capital Bank Botswana has committed P50,000 to The Blue Run, a men's health initiative organised by Village Oncology and scheduled for 14 November 2026. The bank said the sponsorship forms part of its Movember awareness campaign.

Otsile Sethunya Marole, the bank's head of marketing and communications, said the initiative was intended to encourage community participation and raise awareness of men's health issues. The Blue Run brought participants and community organisations together for health awareness and physical activity.

Mani Neb, the bank's head of treasury and a guest runner, said addressing men's health challenges required partnerships between businesses, healthcare providers, community organisations, families and individuals.

Why it mattersThe P50,000 from First Capital Bank Botswana gives Village Oncology funding to run the event that brought community groups into a men's health drive.
