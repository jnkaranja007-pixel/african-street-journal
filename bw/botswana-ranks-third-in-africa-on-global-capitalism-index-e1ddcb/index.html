Botswana ranks third in Africa on global capitalism index

The nation scored 72.7 out of 100, but faces persistent barriers to private-sector growth.

Source: Sunday Standard · 2026-10-22 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Botswana has been ranked as Africa 's third-highest-performing capitalist economy, according to the Global Capitalism Index. The country achieved a score of 72.7 out of 100 in the index released by the University of Virginia 's Democracy and Capitalism Lab. This places Botswana behind Mauritius and Seychelles, which ranked first and second respectively in Africa.

Globally, Botswana secured the 52nd position. Namibia followed in fourth place among African nations with a score of 69.7. Despite its strong overall performance, the index highlighted existing weaknesses in business formation and capital markets.

These identified weaknesses suggest that persistent barriers continue to hinder the growth of Botswana's private sector. The index aims to measure various aspects of capitalist economies, with scores reflecting their overall performance and health.

Why it mattersInvestors may find Botswana's high ranking on the global capitalism index appealing, but persistent barriers to business formation and capital markets could affect private sector growth.
