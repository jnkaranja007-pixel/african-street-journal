Botswana Housing Corporation reverses P101.7mn loss

The corporation anticipates a P49.58 million profit before tax for the 2025/2026 financial year, marking a significant turnaround.

Source: Sunday Standard · 2026-10-22 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Botswana Housing Corporation (BHC) expects to return to profitability in the 2025/2026 financial year, projecting a P49.58 million profit before tax. This follows a substantial P101.741 million loss recorded in the previous financial year. The anticipated improvement represents a P151.321 million turnaround, according to a cautionary statement issued by the corporation on October 13.

The turnaround is attributed to internal adjustments and improved financial management within the corporation. BHC has been undergoing restructuring aimed at enhancing operational efficiency and service delivery. The corporation's statement indicates a positive outlook for its financial performance in the coming fiscal period.

This financial recovery is crucial for BHC's ongoing projects and its ability to meet housing demands across the country. The projected profit will enable further investment in infrastructure development and housing initiatives, supporting the government's broader economic and social development agenda.

Why it mattersInvestors are affected by the Botswana Housing Corporation's return to profitability, which signals improved financial health and potential for future returns.
