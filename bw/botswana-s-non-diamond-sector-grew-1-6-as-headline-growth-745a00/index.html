Botswana's non-diamond sector grew 1.6% as headline growth hit 8.7%

Diamond production lifted the quarterly figure, while borrowing costs and inflation are squeezing businesses and household budgets.

Source: Weekend Post · 2026-11-25 · 1 min read

Botswana's economy grew 8.7% in the most recent quarter, but the non-diamond private sector expanded by just 1.6%, according to figures discussed at a Bank Gaborone roundtable in the capital. The non-diamond sector accounts for almost two-thirds of the economy.

Economist and former Bank of Botswana deputy governor Keith Jefferis said fluctuations in diamond production had strongly influenced headline growth, making it a poor guide to conditions for businesses and households. Private-sector credit declined about 3% over 12 months as high borrowing costs constrained expansion, while projected year-end inflation was approaching 10%.

Government borrowing rates were easing, offering modest, indirect relief on the price of credit. Bank Gaborone managing director Tsaone Raboloko told attendees that resilience needed to give way to transformation.

Why it mattersNon-diamond businesses, which account for almost two-thirds of the economy, are expanding at 1.6% while private-sector credit has fallen about 3% over 12 months.
