Gabon recalls Lemina and Ndong for Lesotho qualifiers

Coach Sébastien Migné named a 25-player squad for the 2027 Africa Cup of Nations qualifiers against Lesotho.

Gabon's national football team, the Panthers, will face Lesotho in two matches for the 2027 Africa Cup of Nations qualifiers. Coach Sébastien Migné announced a 25-player squad on Thursday, November 5, 2026. The team includes the returns of Mario Lemina and Meshak Babanzila, and the surprising inclusion of Didier Ibrahim Ndong, who is currently without a club.

The squad also features the first call-up for 19-year-old Clermont forward Jean William Nyindong. Gabon will host Lesotho on November 13 in Franceville, with the return leg scheduled for November 17 in South Africa. These matches are important for Gabon's qualification campaign for the 2027 CAN finals.

The selected players include goalkeepers Jean-Noël Amonome, François Bekalé, and Loyce Mbaba. Defenders are Anthony Oyono, Jérémy Oyono, Jacques Ekomie, Johan Obiang, Michel Mboula, Alex Moucketou, Jonathan Do Marcolino, and Chris Obiang. Midfielders are Mario Lemina, André Poko, Henrick Do Marcolino, Didier Ibrahim Ndong, Guelor Kanga, and Meshak Babanzila. Strikers are Denis Bouanga, Orphé Mbina, Junior Effaghe, Jim Allévinah, Shavy Babicka, Teddy Andami, Randy Matouti, and Jean William Nyindong.

Why it mattersGabon's national football team is preparing for important 2027 Africa Cup of Nations qualifying matches.

Nina Abouna leads Gabon's international exhibition efforts

Nina Abouna, Gabon's General Commissioner for Exhibitions, guided the nation's participation in Osaka in 2025, earning a Golden Prize.

Nina Abouna, currently the General Commissioner for Exhibitions for Gabon, led the nation's delegation to the Osaka International Exhibition in 2025, which received a Golden Prize.

Abouna also presides over the Information and Communication Commission of the Bureau International des Expositions since November 2025, representing Gabon in this international body.

Her career includes roles in investment promotion and government, and she previously served as Director General of APIEX and ANPI-Gabon, where she contributed to establishing a one-stop shop for investment and digitizing the Commercial Register.

Why it mattersNina Abouna's work aims to increase Gabon's visibility and attractiveness through international exhibitions and investment promotion.

Gabon names university after pioneering cardiologist

The University of Health Sciences in Libreville will now be known as the Pierre André and Maryvonne Kombila Koumba University.

The University of Health Sciences (USS) in Libreville has been renamed the "Pierre André and Maryvonne Kombila Koumba University" to honour the late Professor Pierre-André Kombila Koumba, Gabon's first cardiologist and the first Cameroonian professor of cardiology. The ceremony on November 5, presided over by Gervais Oniane, personal representative of the President, also commemorated his wife, who was also a professor of medicine.

Professor Kombila Koumba, 85, died on October 15 from a stroke. The Health Minister, Elsa Nkana Joséphine Ayo-Bivigou, a former student, praised his scientific rigour and dedication to national cardiology, recalling his role in establishing the first cardiology department at the former General Hospital. He was posthumously awarded the National Order of Merit for his contributions.

Kombila Koumba also served as Minister of Technical Education and Vocational Training, advocating for it as a tool for youth development. The renaming decision was announced by Oniane on behalf of President Brice Clotaire Oligui Nguema, recognizing the couple's significant legacy in medicine and education.

Why it mattersGabon's health and education sectors are honouring a pioneering medical professional and his wife, who was also an academic.

Business · 2026-11-07

Gabon invites Chinese investors to Nkok zone

President Oligui Nguema seeks partnerships for industrialization and raw material processing at Shanghai expo.

Gabon is the African guest of honor at the 9th China International Import Expo (CIIE) in Shanghai, where President Brice Clotaire Oligui Nguema is promoting the nation to Chinese investors. He aims to leverage this commercial event to accelerate industrialization and the local processing of raw materials. The president spoke on November 5, 2026, in Shanghai.

At the CIIE opening, President Nguema emphasized local raw material processing as central to Gabon's message. Gabon is participating for the first time with a national pavilion and was the only African president to address the opening ceremony, which was presided over by Chinese Vice-Premier Ding Xuexiang. He presented Gabon's presence as evidence of a long-standing friendship with China and the concrete results of trade agreements.

President Nguema invited Chinese investors to the Nkok Special Economic Zone, describing it as a strategic Atlantic gateway to Central Africa's 200 million consumers. He also highlighted opportunities in timber, manganese, iron, energy, water, health, digital, and agriculture sectors, noting five million hectares of arable land. The president assured investors of political stability, legal security, a new investment code, and reforms to mining and petroleum codes.

Why it mattersPresident Nguema is actively seeking Chinese investment to transform Gabon's economy by processing raw materials locally and creating jobs.

Politics · 2026-11-07

Gabon ex-PM Bilie-By-Nze questioned by judge

Alain-Claude Bilie-By-Nze faced his first judicial interrogation on November 5 after six months in detention.

Former Prime Minister Alain-Claude Bilie-By-Nze was interrogated by an investigating judge in Libreville on Thursday, November 5. He has been detained since April 16 and his lawyer, Me Sarah-Ognyane, confirmed the hearing took place after months of defense requests.

The interrogation focused on statements previously made by Baba Ramatou on April 27. Me Sarah-Ognyane stated that her client provided clarifications on the facts he is accused of and described the session as having gone very well. The procedure has now entered a new phase.

The next step could be a confrontation between Alain-Claude Bilie-By-Nze and Baba Ramatou. Following this, a character investigation will occur before the judge decides on a dismissal or a trial. Me Sarah-Ognyane hopes for a resolution before the next six-month detention period expires.
