Gabon repatriates 28 citizens from South Africa

Twenty-eight Gabonese citizens returned to Libreville on Wednesday via a Fly Gabon flight. The repatriation follows xenophobic tensions in South Africa, according to Gabon Actu.

Why it mattersThe move provides immediate safety for citizens, including those who were in South Africa under irregular status.

Agasa immobilizes 10 bread transport vehicles

The Gabonese Food Safety Agency (Agasa) has grounded approximately 10 vehicles in the Estuaire region since Aug. 14. The agency is conducting a national compliance campaign for vehicles transporting bread and bakery products.

Why it mattersBakery distributors using non-compliant vehicles face operational shutdowns until they meet safety standards.
