Gabon seeks balanced economic partnership with Russia

Vice-President Hermann Immongault called for mutual interests and state sovereignty at the Russia-Africa Summit in Moscow.

Source: Gabon Review · 2026-10-31 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Gabon's Vice-President Hermann Immongault, representing President Brice Clotaire Oligui Nguema, proposed a more balanced economic partnership with Russia at the 3rd Russia-Africa Summit in Moscow on October 29, 2026. He emphasized mutual interests, state sovereignty, and effective commitments in bilateral cooperation.

Immongault highlighted key areas for increased collaboration, including support for SMEs, special economic zones, trade in national currencies, and modernizing port and rail infrastructure. He also pointed to energy prospects in hydropower, natural gas, and civil nuclear power, alongside accelerating local processing of raw materials like manganese before a January 2029 export ban.

The vice-president also advocated for enhanced security cooperation against terrorism and piracy in the Gulf of Guinea, and discussed challenges with student scholarship fund transfers. Gabon is also seeking Russian support in artificial intelligence for digital sovereignty and inclusive development.

Why it mattersGabon's vice-president outlined priorities for economic, security, and technological cooperation with Russia, affecting the nation's development and international relations.
