Gabon votes to lift US embargo on Cuba

The UN General Assembly voted on October 28, 2026, to end the US embargo on Cuba.

Source: Gabon Review · 2026-10-31 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Gabon voted on Wednesday, October 28, 2026, in favor of a United Nations General Assembly resolution calling for the end of the United States embargo against Cuba. The vote took place in New York, with 130 countries supporting the resolution, 17 opposing, and 25 abstaining.

The resolution urges Washington to lift the economic, commercial, and financial sanctions that have been in place for over six decades. It also calls on other nations not to implement similar measures, citing violations of UN Charter principles and denouncing the extraterritorial effects of the 1996 Helms-Burton Act.

This diplomatic action occurs as Cuba faces severe economic and energy shortages, with power outages affecting essential services like hospitals. The vote shows a decline in international support compared to previous years, with 165 votes in favor in 2025 and 187 in 2024.

Why it mattersGabon's vote at the UN affects its diplomatic relations and stance on international sanctions.
