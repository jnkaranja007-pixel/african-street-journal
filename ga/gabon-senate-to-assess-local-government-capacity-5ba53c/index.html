Gabon Senate to assess local government capacity

Senate President Huguette Nyana Ekoumé presented plans for field missions to President Brice Clotaire Oligui Nguema in Libreville.

Source: Gabon Review · 2026-10-26 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Gabon's Senate will conduct field missions to evaluate the capacity of local governments to assume transferred responsibilities. Senate President Huguette Nyana Ekoumé presented this initiative to President Brice Clotaire Oligui Nguema on Friday, October 23, in Libreville. The goal is to accelerate the effective implementation of decentralization by understanding local realities and constraints.

These missions aim to assess the administrative and financial capabilities of local authorities. Senators will identify needs in human resources, funding, and budget management. The discussions also highlighted the necessity of training and professionalizing local agents, particularly in tax collection and revenue generation.

The initiative seeks to enable local governments to mobilize more resources and gain financial autonomy. This evolution is considered essential for them to develop projects tailored to population needs and ensure transparent public resource management. The Senate's planned visits could bring decision-making closer to local realities.

Why it mattersGabon's local governments will be assessed for their capacity to manage transferred responsibilities and resources.
