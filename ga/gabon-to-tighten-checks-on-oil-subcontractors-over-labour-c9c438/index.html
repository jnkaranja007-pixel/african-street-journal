Gabon to tighten checks on oil subcontractors over labour violations

Labour Minister Adrien Nguema Mba cited delayed wages, contract irregularities and unpaid social contributions at a meeting in Port-Gentil.

Source: Gabon Review · 2026-11-25 · 1 min read

Gabon’s government plans to strengthen inspections of oil subcontractors, Labour Minister Adrien Nguema Mba said at a meeting with companies and labour representatives in Port-Gentil on 23 November 2026. Petroleum and Gas Minister Clotaire Kondja also attended.

Nguema Mba said workers had repeatedly raised concerns about late wages, irregular contracts and social contributions that employers had not paid. He reminded the service companies of their legal duties and said the oil sector’s economic demands did not excuse violations of employees’ rights.

The ministers gave no inspection timetable or details of penalties in the report. The meeting focused on enforcing existing obligations and increasing oversight of the companies.

Why it mattersOil subcontractors must now meet their legal duties as the government strengthens inspections, while workers’ reported concerns include late pay and unpaid social contributions.
