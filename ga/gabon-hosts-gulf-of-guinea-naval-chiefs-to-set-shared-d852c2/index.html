Gabon hosts Gulf of Guinea naval chiefs to set shared maritime priorities

The 10th symposium brought nearly 150 participants to Libreville to improve cooperation against piracy, trafficking and illegal fishing.

Sources: Gabon Review and L'Union Gabon · 2026-11-20 · 1 min read

Gabon hosted the 10th Symposium of Gulf of Guinea naval chiefs and coast-guard commanders in Libreville from 18 to 19 November, bringing together nearly 150 participants. The regional meeting focused on cooperation against maritime piracy, drug trafficking, illegal fishing and other cross-border crime.

Defence Minister Brigitte Onkanoa said existing cooperation mechanisms, including the Yaoundé architecture, need to deliver more operational results. Organisers said the discussions also covered information-sharing, coordination of operations, freedom of navigation and international maritime law.

Military leaders were expected to agree common guidance on maritime governance and surveillance. The European Union was among the international partners supporting the meeting.

Why it mattersNaval and coast-guard leaders are expected to agree shared surveillance and governance guidance for the Gulf of Guinea at the meeting's close.
