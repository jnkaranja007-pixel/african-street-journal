Gabon's social security fund faces rescue plan

A task force has proposed 23 measures to address the CNAMGS's financial crisis and governance issues.

Sources: Gabon Review and L'Union Gabon · 2026-11-17 · 2 min read

Gabon's National Social Security and Health Insurance Fund (CNAMGS) is undergoing a recovery plan due to severe financial problems, governance failures, and significant debt to healthcare providers. A steering committee met on Monday, November 16, 2026, in Libreville, chaired by Vice-President Hermann Immongault, to review 23 proposals aimed at stabilizing the organization. The CNAMGS is struggling with accumulated debts, particularly for medical evacuations, and operational dysfunctions that impact service quality for its members.

After four months of work, the interministerial Task Force has identified critical issues including financial fragility, governance and control shortcomings, and legal constraints. Vice-President Immongault stressed that the task force is meant to provide concrete, high-level state responses to the CNAMGS's crisis. The Minister of Social Affairs, Raïssa Nana Ghogho, stated the meeting aimed to prioritize proposals and set an execution timeline to lift the CNAMGS out of its known 'quagmire'.

The proposed solutions fall into three phases: short-term actions to restore cash flow and ensure care continuity; medium-term goals focusing on modernizing governance and accelerating the Unique Social Registry; and long-term strategies for resource diversification through behavioral taxation and an environmental health contribution based on the polluter-pays principle. The task force expects 23 decisions to translate these directions into operational measures.

Why it mattersHealthcare providers are awaiting payment of accumulated debts to ensure the continuity of care for insured individuals.
