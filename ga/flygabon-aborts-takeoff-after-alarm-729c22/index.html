FlyGabon aborts takeoff after alarm

A technical alert during departure on Friday, November 13, 2026, halted a FlyGabon flight from Libreville to Franceville.

Source: Gabon Review · 2026-11-16 · 1 min read

A FlyGabon ATR 72-600 aircraft aborted its takeoff from Libreville on Friday, November 13, 2026, after a technical alarm sounded during the departure phase. The pilots immediately halted the plane, which had not yet left the ground, prioritizing passenger safety. The national airline stated that the incident, which occurred on the 10:30 flight to Franceville, was handled according to established procedures.

An inspection revealed a leak caused by a ruptured seal, which was promptly replaced. Passengers were then transferred to a replacement aircraft of the same type and continued their journey to Franceville without further incident. FlyGabon emphasized that rejecting a takeoff, known as RTO, is a planned procedure for which pilots are trained and regularly rehearsed on simulators.

The airline highlighted its fleet's average age of 4.2 years for its ATR aircraft and its maintained IOSA certification since 2020, an international audit assessing maintenance, operations, and safety management systems. FlyGabon stated that the incident demonstrates the effectiveness of its safety culture, which relies on vigilance, responsiveness, and transparency.

Why it mattersPassengers traveling on FlyGabon flights between Libreville and Franceville experienced a delay due to a technical issue, with a replacement aircraft provided.
