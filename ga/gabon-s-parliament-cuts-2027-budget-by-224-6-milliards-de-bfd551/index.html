Gabon’s parliament cuts 2027 budget by 224,6 milliards de francs CFA

Deputies approved the revised budget unanimously after lowering the initial envelope from 6 223,1 to 5 998,5 milliards de francs CFA.

Sources: Gabon Review and L'Union Gabon · 2026-11-20 · 1 min read

Gabon’s National Assembly adopted the state’s 2027 budget unanimously on 18 November, reducing the proposed envelope from 6 223,1 to 5 998,5 milliards de francs CFA. The vote took place at the Palais Léon-Mba after debate in plenary.

Jeanine Lydie Roboty-Mbou, chair of the Assembly’s Finance, Budget and Public Accounting Commission, presented its report. Deputies recommended broadening the tax base, controlling the public-sector wage bill, reducing public debt and monitoring budget execution. Finance Minister Thierry Minko proposed amendments to four articles, saying he wanted more flexibility in debt management.

The budget text followed committee work from 21 October to 16 November. The Assembly also unanimously adopted a bill setting conditions for appointment and service as a chief executive of Gabonese credit and microfinance institutions.

Why it mattersDeputies have set recommendations on tax collection, the public-sector wage bill, debt reduction and oversight as the government prepares to execute the 2027 budget.
