FlyGabon flight aborted after technical alarm

An ATR 72-600 plane returned to the gate at Libreville airport on Friday after a warning light, with passengers later transferred to a replacement aircraft.

Source: Gabon Review · 2026-11-15 · 1 min read

A FlyGabon flight from Libreville to Franceville was interrupted on Friday, November 13, 2026, when pilots aborted takeoff due to a technical alarm. The ATR 72-600 aircraft returned to the parking area at Libreville International Airport before leaving the ground. Passengers were later moved to a replacement plane of the same type and continued their journey to Franceville without further incident.

The airline stated that a technical inspection revealed a leak caused by a ruptured seal, which was promptly replaced. FlyGabon emphasized that interrupting a takeoff, known as a Rejected Take-Off (RTO), is a standard procedure for pilots trained to handle such situations. The company highlighted that this incident demonstrated the effectiveness of their safety protocols and the professionalism of their crews.

FlyGabon noted its fleet's average age is 4.2 years for its ATR aircraft and that it has maintained its IOSA certification since 2020. This international audit assesses maintenance, flight operations, crew training, and safety management systems. The company sees this event as proof of a safety culture built on vigilance, responsiveness, and transparency.

Why it mattersPassengers travelling between Libreville and Franceville on FlyGabon experienced a delay due to a technical issue with an aircraft.
