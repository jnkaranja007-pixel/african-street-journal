Gabon’s sports and youth institute graduates 302

The new cohort includes 183 youth and sports inspectors, alongside graduates in four other training fields.

Source: Gabon Review · 2026-11-24 · 1 min read

Gabon’s National Institute of Youth and Sports (INJS) graduated 302 people from its 44th cohort at a ceremony on 21 November. The graduates completed training in five fields for work in youth and sports.

The cohort includes 183 youth and sports inspectors, 29 certified teachers, 37 assistant teachers, 27 sports advisers and 26 youth advisers. Andy Roland Nziengui Nziengui, chief of staff to the youth and sports minister, told graduates their diplomas carried a responsibility to serve.

Some members of the cohort are law-enforcement officers trained to strengthen their support for children in conflict with the law. The graduates are expected to put their skills to work in youth guidance and national sport.

Why it mattersThe 302 graduates are expected to turn their training into youth guidance and sports work, including support for children in conflict with the law.
