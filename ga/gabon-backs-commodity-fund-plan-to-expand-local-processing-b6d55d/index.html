Gabon backs commodity fund plan to expand local processing

At a 17 November meeting in The Hague, Gabon endorsed the fund’s 2025-2035 strategy and sought technical support for agricultural and agro-industrial businesses.

Source: Gabon Review · 2026-11-19 · 1 min read

Gabon’s ambassador to Belgium, Régis Immongault Tatangani, backed the Common Fund for Commodities’ 2025-2035 strategy at the fund’s 38th governors’ session in The Hague on 17 November. He said Gabon wants local processing of its resources to support human development and ensure producers benefit more from the wealth created.

Tatangani expressed interest in the fund’s ACT Fund and technical-assistance facility, which the report said could support agricultural and agro-industrial value chains and strengthen domestic businesses. He urged the fund to send information and technical-support missions to national capitals to help countries access financing and develop projects aligned with their priorities.

He also called for producers, women, young people and local communities to have a greater role in value chains and economic decisions. The fund’s member-state representatives examined its strategic direction, financing tools and prospects for expanding support to producer economies.

Why it mattersGabonese producers and domestic businesses are the intended beneficiaries of the fund’s technical assistance and the push for local processing.
