Gabon schedules vocational training entrance exam for 5 880 candidates

The public-centre exam was moved from 16 November to 21 November, with candidates sitting tests in mathematics, French and general knowledge.

Source: Gabon Review · 2026-11-22 · 1 min read

Gabon’s public vocational training centres are holding their entrance exam on Saturday 21 November, with 5 880 candidates expected, according to Gabon Review. The National Agency for Vocational Training and Education (ANFEP) is organising the session after the exam was rescheduled.

In Libreville, candidates will sit the tests at the Basile-Ondimba school complex and Omar-Bongo University. Mathematics is scheduled for the morning, followed by French and general knowledge. Organisers said the tests will take place simultaneously at the centres concerned.

An organising official said candidates will not be admitted after the examinations begin, urging them to arrive on time because the tests use multiple-choice questions. The exam had originally been announced for 16 November before being moved for practical and technical reasons.

Why it mattersThe 5 880 candidates must attend the rescheduled exam on 21 November and arrive before the tests begin to be admitted.
