Gabon food agency warns of rising prices and unsafe food

The agency says inspections have been interrupted as mixed control brigades await full deployment, while hygiene conditions have worsened.

Source: Gabon Review · 2026-11-19 · 1 min read

Gabon’s food safety agency warned on 17 November that access to sufficient, safe food has deteriorated, with prices still rising and more food unfit for consumption on sale. The agency said the situation had persisted for three months.

The Gabonese Agency for Food Safety, known as Agasa, linked the problem to high food prices, low incomes and poverty. It also reported worsening hygiene at food production, processing, transport, storage and sales sites, blaming interrupted government inspections while mixed control brigades are being set up.

A decree creating the brigades took effect on 14 September. Agasa urged businesses to honour their commitments, called on relevant administrations to remain vigilant before year-end celebrations, and advised consumers to check food quality and compliance when shopping.

Why it mattersConsumers are being urged to check food quality and compliance at purchase as unsafe products become more common and inspections remain interrupted.
