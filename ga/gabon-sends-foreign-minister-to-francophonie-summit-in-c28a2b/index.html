Gabon sends foreign minister to Francophonie summit in Phnom Penh

Gabon wants a say in the direction of the Francophonie, as its foreign minister attends the 20th summit in Cambodia.

Source: Gabon Review · 2026-11-15 · 1 min read

Foreign Minister Marie-Édith Tassyla-Ye-Doumbeneny is representing President Brice Clotaire Oligui Nguema at the 20th Francophonie Summit in Phnom Penh, Cambodia, on 15 and 16 November 2026. Libreville wants to press its positions on the organisation's future, including peace, cooperation and shared prosperity. Long Yes, undersecretary of state at Cambodia's foreign ministry, welcomed her on arrival.

Before the summit she held a working meeting with her delegation, including Alfred Nguia Banda, Gabon's ambassador to France. The foreign ministry said the gathering is "not just one more summit", and that Gabon aims to help shape decisions on the future of the French-speaking space. The theme is "Reinvesting in peace for shared prosperity and lasting stability".

The 90 member states and governments will discuss political, economic, cultural and educational cooperation, and the Francophonie's role in multilateralism and crisis prevention. The election of the next secretary general of the Organisation internationale de la Francophonie (OIF) is also on the agenda.
