Gabon defends inequality observatory at UN forum

Justice Minister Augustin Emane presented Gabon's plan to measure anti-racism policies at the 5th Global Forum in The Hague.

Source: Gabon Review · 2026-11-03 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Gabon presented its strategy to combat racism and discrimination at the 5th Global Forum of the Unesco in The Hague on October 29. Justice Minister Augustin Emane detailed plans for an observatory of inequalities and a national action plan against hate speech. The minister stated that Gabon aims to move beyond declarations of principle to measurable results in public policies.

The observatory will audit structures, collect data, and assess inclusion policies, including a national survey to map socioeconomic disparities. This aims to improve access to citizenship, education, and justice for vulnerable and indigenous populations. The action plan includes prevention, sanctions, and the deconstruction of stereotypes in school programs.

Emane highlighted that Gabon's constitution guarantees cohesion among its diverse communities. He urged global leaders to make human diversity a foundation for a more just world. Gabon is committed to working alongside other nations in this endeavor.
