Cimaf-Gabon lifts cement capacity to 1,85 million tonnes

The 25 milliards de francs CFA investment adds one million tonnes of annual capacity before Gabon’s planned clinker import ban.

Sources: Gabon Review and L'Union Gabon · 2026-11-27 · 1 min read

Cimaf-Gabon inaugurated its third cement production line in Owendo on 26 novembre, taking the company’s annual production capacity from 850 000 to 1,85 million tonnes. President Brice Clotaire Oligui Nguema opened the line.

The 25 milliards de francs CFA investment adds one million tonnes of capacity. Authorities said the expansion is intended to meet demand from road, property and infrastructure projects and reduce reliance on imports. Cimaf-Gabon’s activity now accounts for nearly 1,000 direct and indirect jobs.

The new line comes before the planned 1er janvier 2027 ban on clinker imports, a raw material used to make cement. The investment was made under an agreement with the Gabonese state.

Why it mattersCimaf-Gabon’s activity accounts for nearly 1,000 direct and indirect jobs, while the new line adds one million tonnes of annual capacity ahead of the planned clinker import ban.
