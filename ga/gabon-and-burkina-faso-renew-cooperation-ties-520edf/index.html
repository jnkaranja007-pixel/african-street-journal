Gabon and Burkina Faso renew cooperation ties

14 years after their first meeting, officials from Gabon and Burkina Faso met in Libreville to boost bilateral relations and create tangible benefits for their populations.

Source: Gabon Review · 2026-11-13 · 1 min read

Gabon and Burkina Faso opened the second session of their Joint Cooperation Commission in Libreville on November 11, aiming to transform their long-standing relationship into an operational partnership. This meeting, 14 years after the first in Ouagadougou, seeks to create measurable benefits for the populations of both nations.

The session was officially launched by Valentin Louemba Bayonne, Secretary General of the Gabonese Ministry of Foreign Affairs, and Hermann Yerigouin Toé, his Burkinabe counterpart. Both officials emphasized the need to move beyond intentions and build a pragmatic cooperation with achievable, mutually beneficial projects.

The three-day meeting, held on November 12 and 13, will focus on updating the legal framework, examining about 20 cooperation instruments, and addressing key sectors including agriculture, trade, economy, professional training, higher education, research, culture, youth, sports, communication, security, and consular affairs.

Why it mattersOfficials from Gabon and Burkina Faso are meeting to update legal frameworks and explore cooperation in sectors like agriculture and trade, aiming for tangible benefits for their populations.
