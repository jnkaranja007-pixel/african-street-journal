Gabon, US Bolster Air Security Cooperation

Gabon's civil aviation agency and the U.S. embassy met to enhance airport security and state safety through API-PNR system implementation.

Source: Gabon Review · 2026-10-21 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Gabon's National Civil Aviation Agency (ANAC) and the U.S. Embassy in Libreville are strengthening their collaboration on airport and state security. A meeting on October 16th focused on technical assistance and expertise sharing as Gabon deploys the API-PNR system, designed to enhance air traffic control and threat detection. The system utilizes passenger identification and reservation data to anticipate risks and identify suspicious travel patterns, aiding in the fight against terrorism, drug trafficking, and transnational crime.

The discussions, led by ANAC Director General Éric Tristan Franck Moussavou and U.S. Embassy Regional Security Director Michael Lee, aimed to bolster national capabilities in airport security and territorial protection. The API-PNR system requires effective coordination among administrations and proficiency in data analysis tools, making the exchange of experience and technical support crucial for Gabon. Both parties expressed a commitment to deepening their relationship and developing concrete cooperation aligned with Gabon's priorities.

This enhanced partnership is particularly relevant as Gabon advances the implementation of the API-PNR system. The goal is to consolidate national expertise and better integrate airport security measures with national protection imperatives. The collaboration seeks to translate the shared willingness into operational actions to meet the growing demands of civil aviation security.

Why it mattersGabon's aviation security is being enhanced through a partnership with the U.S. Embassy, impacting border control and travel data management.
