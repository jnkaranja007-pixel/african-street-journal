Gabon opens cybersecurity forum with push for local data hosting

The Libreville event brought together about 400 participants to discuss digital sovereignty, artificial intelligence and cybersecurity.

Source: Gabon Review · 2026-11-28 · 1 min read

Gabon’s third Cybersecurity Forum opened on 27 November at the Aninf Tower in Libreville, with organisers calling for stronger national cybersecurity capacity and local data hosting. The event was organised by the National Agency for Digital Infrastructure and Frequencies (Aninf), ST Digital and CNConsulting.

About 400 people attended, including public decision-makers, business leaders, technology and security specialists, and diplomatic representatives. Aninf director-general Alberto Wenceslas Mounguengui Moudoki called for public and private sector cooperation. The forum’s stated goals also include encouraging technology partnerships.

ST Digital director-general Laïka Mba said the event had become a gathering for Gabonese working in technology and cybersecurity. Ambassador Noël Nelson Messone, a former minister of new technologies, delivered the opening lecture on the diplomatic stakes of cybersecurity in Africa.

Why it mattersPublic agencies and businesses are being urged to strengthen cybersecurity capacity and promote local data hosting through partnerships discussed at the forum.
