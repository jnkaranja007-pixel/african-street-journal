Gabon and Italy discuss industrial and commercial projects in Libreville

A 17 November round table brought business representatives together to identify sectors for cooperation and connect companies from both countries.

Source: Gabon Review · 2026-11-19 · 1 min read

Gabonese and Italian business representatives discussed industrial and commercial cooperation at a round table in Libreville on 17 November, during Gabon-Italy Week. The meeting was organised by Italy’s embassy in Gabon and the Italian Trade Agency to identify sectors for new partnerships between companies.

The discussions covered business introductions and possible joint projects, according to Dr Andrea Ferrari of the Italian Trade Agency. Organisers said the goal was to turn diplomatic relations into business partnerships and productive investment. The week’s programme, running from 16 to 20 November in Libreville, also included tourism, training, entrepreneurship and creative trades.

The initiative followed talks on 2 October between Gabon’s President Brice Clotaire Oligui Nguema and Italy’s ambassador, Stefano Moscatelli. Organisers said company-matching platforms discussed at the round table should help businesses make contact; the article reported no signed deals.

Why it mattersGabonese and Italian companies seeking partnerships must now turn the Libreville discussions into projects, with company-matching platforms proposed to help make contacts.
