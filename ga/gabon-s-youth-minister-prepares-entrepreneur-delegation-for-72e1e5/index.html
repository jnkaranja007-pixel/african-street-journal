Gabon's youth minister prepares entrepreneur delegation for Kigali summit

Paul Ulrich Kessany Zategwa met the UN Development Programme's resident representative to arrange travel and support for the selected delegates.

Source: Gabon Review · 2026-11-23 · 1 min read

Gabon's youth minister, Paul Ulrich Kessany Zategwa, received Rokya Ye-Dieng, resident representative of the United Nations Development Programme (UNDP) in Gabon, on 20 November to prepare the country's participation in the eighth YouthConnekt Africa Summit. The summit is due to take place from 25 to 27 November 2026 in Kigali, Rwanda.

The two discussed how the delegation will travel and how the young entrepreneurs chosen to represent Gabon will be supported. The aim, according to the ministry, is to help them showcase their initiatives, build networks and form partnerships for their projects.

The summit, launched in 2017, brings together young leaders, entrepreneurs, policymakers, investors and development partners. The ministry said the minister and Ye-Dieng reaffirmed their intention to keep working together on support for Gabonese youth beyond the Kigali event.

Why it mattersSelected Gabonese entrepreneurs gain networking and partnership opportunities with investors and development partners at the Kigali summit.
