Chinese firm CCECC seeks new infrastructure projects in Gabon

After renovating the Senate building, the China Civil Engineering Construction Corporation aims to expand its presence in Gabon's road, rail, and mining sectors.

The China Civil Engineering Construction Corporation (CCECC) is seeking new contracts in Gabon, building on its current work renovating the Omar Bongo Ondimba palace, which houses the Senate. A delegation from the Chinese group met with the vice-president of the government, Hermann Immongault, to present projects and expertise in infrastructure, particularly in the road, rail, and mining sectors. This move aims to transform the company's existing presence into new contracts, leveraging its experience from other African countries.

Discussions with the government focused on strategic sectors such as road and rail infrastructure, and mineral transport, aligning with Gabon's development ambitions. The Bélinga iron ore project was a key topic, requiring significant infrastructure for development and ore evacuation. CCECC intends to apply its experience in railway and aeronautics to potentially secure roles in future major Gabonese construction projects.

CCECC's strategy extends beyond current projects to identifying new markets as Gabon prepares for substantial infrastructure investments. The company plans further meetings with ministries of Transport, Public Works, Energy, and Mines to explore cooperation possibilities. The ongoing renovation of the Senate building serves as a reference point for CCECC's broader expansion plans in the country.

Why it mattersCCECC's pursuit of new infrastructure contracts in Gabon affects investors by potentially opening up new opportunities in major development projects.
