African first ladies’ advisers seek stronger reproductive-health coordination

Representatives from Gabon and five other countries met in Libreville on 19 and 20 November to share experience and discuss collaboration.

Source: Gabon Review · 2026-11-24 · 1 min read

Advisers to the Organisation of African First Ladies for Development met in Libreville on 19 and 20 November to discuss sexual and reproductive health and rights. Representatives from Gabon, Burundi, Congo, the Central African Republic, Senegal and Zimbabwe attended the two-day consultation, organised by the group.

Ruth Oumou Baldé, a special adviser to Gabon’s head of state, opened the talks. Participants called for better coordination among organisations working on the issues and discussed protecting women and supporting girls. They also visited Gabonese school and university programmes offering medical care and psychosocial support.

The advisers raised how programmes could continue after current funding runs out. Organisers said mapping the groups involved and strengthening partnerships were conditions for successful interventions.

Why it mattersThe participating countries’ advisers must coordinate their interventions, while organisers say mapping partners is needed to sustain programmes beyond available funding.
