Gabon defends inequality observatory and anti-hate speech plan

Minister Augustin Emane presented Gabon's strategy to combat racism and discrimination at the UNESCO forum in The Hague.

Source: Gabon Review · 2026-11-02 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Gabon is implementing an observatory for inequalities and a national action plan against hate speech to achieve measurable results. Justice Minister Augustin Emane presented this approach on October 29 in The Hague, Netherlands, at the 5th UNESCO Global Forum against Racism and Discrimination. He reaffirmed Gabon's support for the organization's Global Alliance.

The forum, themed "From Commitment to Accountability: Changing the System, Changing the Future," provided a platform for Emane to advocate for moving beyond declarations of principle to test public policies against facts, transparency, and measurement. Gabon's strategy involves an Observatory of Inequalities to audit structures and collect data on socioeconomic disparities, aiming to improve access to citizenship, education, and justice for vulnerable populations. A national plan will focus on prevention, sanctions, and deconstructing stereotypes in school programs.

Emane highlighted that Gabon's constitution guarantees the cohesion of a society with over 50 communities, aiming to make diversity a factor of inclusion and stability. He urged participants to make human diversity the foundation of a more just and fraternal world, stating Gabon is ready to work alongside them.
