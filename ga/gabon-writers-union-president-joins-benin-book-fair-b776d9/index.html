Gabon writers’ union president joins Benin book fair

Éric Joël Békalé took part in the four-day Cotonou gathering, where writers discussed African poetry, memory and writing in African languages.

Source: Gabon Review · 2026-11-25 · 1 min read

Éric Joël Békalé, president of Gabon’s writers’ union, attended the fifth National Book Fair of Benin in Cotonou from 19 to 22 November 2026. The event’s theme was “Poetry in all its forms.” Békalé joined authors from Togo, Congo, Cameroon, Haiti, Mauritania and Mali.

The four-day fair brought together writers, literary critics, teachers, journalists and readers for discussions on the renewal of African poetry, links between slam and poetry, and writing in African languages. Its opening conference asked whether poetry could help address contemporary challenges. Writer Roger Sidokpohou spoke about preserving individual and collective memories and called for a renewed role for the griot in passing on knowledge, myths, rituals and values.

The programme also included writing workshops for young people and children. Florent Couao-Zotti, the fair’s general delegate, said poetry could go beyond artistic expression.

Why it mattersBékalé represented Gabon’s writers at a four-day regional book fair, alongside authors from six other countries.
