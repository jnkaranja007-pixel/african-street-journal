Gabon’s RPM joins ruling UDB ahead of party congress

Alexandre Barro Chambrier’s party formalised its membership at an extraordinary congress in Libreville on 26 November.

Source: Gabon Review · 2026-11-28 · 1 min read

Gabon’s Rally for the Fatherland and Modernity (RPM), led by Alexandre Barro Chambrier, formally joined the Democratic Union of Builders (UDB) at an extraordinary congress in Libreville on 26 November. The UDB is the party founded by President Brice Clotaire Oligui Nguema.

The UDB’s secretary-general, Mays Mouissi, attended the gathering. The RPM says its political experience, local presence and cadre expertise will add to the UDB’s ranks; the two parties also cited shared convictions and political goals.

The UDB is preparing to hold its first ordinary congress. The report gave no details on how the RPM’s integration will change the party’s internal organisation.

Why it mattersUDB organisers must incorporate the RPM before the party’s first ordinary congress, following the formal decision on 26 November.
