Gabon prepares to split its state water and power utility by December

The government says no Gabonese employee should lose a job as officials work through contracts, debt and asset transfers before the split takes effect.

Source: Gabon Review · 2026-11-22 · 1 min read

Gabon is preparing to divide the Société d'énergie et d'eau du Gabon (SEEG), the state water and electricity operator, into two companies by December 2026: Électricité du Gabon (EDG) for power and La Gabonaise des Eaux (GDE) for water. Vice-President of the Government Hermann Immongault received Water and Energy Minister Olivier Mouckocko, along with SEEG's board chairman and managing director, on 19 November to review progress.

Minister Olivier Mouckocko said the instruction from President Brice Clotaire Oligui Nguema is that no Gabonese employee should lose a job by the end of the process. The meeting also sought approval for a committee to oversee the steps needed before separation. SEEG is a private-law company governed by OHADA, the business law framework covering much of francophone Africa, so the transfer must clarify employment contracts, responsibilities and asset transfers.

Officials said they must first establish the real level of SEEG's debt, take an inventory of state assets held in the company and give assurances to its shareholders. Continued dialogue with staff and social partners was identified as essential to the operation's success.

Why it mattersSEEG's Gabonese employees face contract transfers under the split, and the government has pledged that none will lose a job.
