Oligui Nguema joins CEEAC summit talks on regional integration

The Gabonese president is taking part in discussions in Malabo on security, trade across borders and cooperation in energy and health.

Sources: Gabon Review and L'Union Gabon · 2026-11-28 · 1 min read

Gabonese President Brice Clotaire Oligui Nguema left Libreville on 26 November for the 27th ordinary session of the Economic Community of Central African States (CEEAC) leaders’ conference in Malabo, Equatorial Guinea. The meeting is scheduled for Friday 27 November, with security, economic integration and cooperation among the listed subjects.

The leaders are due to discuss security crises, maritime security and border governance, as well as the CEEAC Vision 2050 and its 2026-2030 indicative strategic plan. Energy and health cooperation are also on the agenda, according to Gabon Review.

The trip follows a 10 November meeting in Libreville between Oligui Nguema and an envoy of Equatorial Guinea’s president, Teodoro Obiang Nguema Mbasogo. Their talks included aligning the two countries’ positions in CEEAC and the Central African Economic and Monetary Community.

Why it mattersGabon’s president is representing Libreville in regional discussions that include border movement, trade and security.
