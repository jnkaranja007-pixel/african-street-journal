Fitch keeps Gabon’s sovereign ratings at CCC- and CC

The agency forecasts the budget deficit will fall to 5.2% of gross domestic product in 2027, from 8.3% in 2026.

Source: Gabon Review · 2026-11-24 · 1 min read

Fitch Ratings kept Gabon’s long-term sovereign ratings at CCC- in foreign currency and CC in local currency, according to a statement issued by the economy ministry on 20 November. The agency forecasts the budget deficit will narrow from 12.4% of gross domestic product in 2025 to 8.3% in 2026 and 5.2% in 2027.

Fitch cited Gabon’s large financing needs, limited access to low-cost funding, dependence on oil revenue and high public debt relative to gross domestic product. The government said the forecast deficit path reflects measures in a 2026 supplementary budget law and adjustments in the 2027 budget bill, which parliament adopted on 18 November.

Fitch projects real economic growth of 3.2% in 2026 and 3.4% a year through 2028. Technical discussions with the International Monetary Fund are continuing toward a shared assessment of public finances and a possible staff-level agreement.

Why it mattersThe government must address large financing needs and high public debt while seeking an IMF assessment and a possible staff-level agreement.
