Port-Gentil police hold students as school imposes exclusion

Students from the Ogoula Mbeye Evangelical high school were taken to the central police station after videos spread on 19 November.

Sources: Gabon Review and L'Union Gabon · 2026-11-22 · 1 min read

Police in Port-Gentil have taken students from Ogoula Mbeye Evangelical high school into custody after videos described as obscene circulated on 19 November 2026. The school’s disciplinary council has also excluded the students identified in the recordings.

Pierre Onanga Ossounda, the provincial academic director for Ogooué-Maritime, said the school director, students involved and their parents were called in and heard. He said the academic directorate notified its ministry hierarchy and the national directorate for private education of the Evangelical Church of Gabon. The exclusion’s duration has not been set; the school’s official report is pending.

Onanga Ossounda said the students were taken to Port-Gentil’s central police station for the investigation. He said school rules and decrees 0857, 0858 and 0859 provide for disciplinary measures, and warned that repeat conduct would bring disciplinary and judicial sanctions.

Why it mattersThe students identified in the videos face both school exclusion and a police investigation, with the exclusion period still awaiting the school’s official report.
