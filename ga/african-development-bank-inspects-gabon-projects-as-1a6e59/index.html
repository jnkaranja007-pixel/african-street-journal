African Development Bank inspects Gabon projects as training works top 95%

The bank found the public health laboratory and health-training institute works complete, while the Nkembo and CIADFOR training centres remain unfinished.

Source: Gabon Review · 2026-11-20 · 1 min read

The African Development Bank inspected several projects it funds in Libreville on 18 November, including a public health laboratory and vocational training centres. The bank's country manager, Nouridine Kane Dia, led the delegation with Bussa-Bussambe, coordinator of the project to strengthen youth employability and social protection.

The National Public Health Laboratory's rehabilitation and extension, including a new R+1 building, are complete and accepted, director general Joël Djoba Siawaya said. Work at the National Institute for Training in Health and Social Action is finished, but its new R+2 building awaits equipment. Construction at the Nkembo vocational centres and at the Centre for Vocational Training in Gabon (CIADFOR) is more than 95% complete after a temporary pause over release of the final funding tranche; four R+3 buildings have been built there.

The project is scheduled to close on 31 March 2027. Kane said the bank was confident it would meet its goals, including creating jobs for young people and women in Gabon.

Why it mattersThe Nkembo and CIADFOR training-centre works remain unfinished after a pause over release of the final funding tranche, while the project closes on 31 March 2027.
