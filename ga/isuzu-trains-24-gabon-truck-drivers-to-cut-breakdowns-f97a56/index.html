Isuzu trains 24 Gabon truck drivers to cut breakdowns

The two-day Libreville course covered driving, maintenance and road safety, with special attention to onboard systems many drivers did not know.

Source: Gabon Review · 2026-11-21 · 1 min read

Isuzu Motors International, Sodim TP and Gabon’s transport ministry trained 24 Isuzu truck drivers in Libreville on 19 and 20 November, covering vehicle operation, maintenance and road safety. Experts sent from Dubai led the practical sessions, the second stage of a programme launched in March 2025.

The drivers practised braking, clutch use, gear changes, load management and cornering, and learned about onboard safety systems. Isuzu commercial director Collins Djimmo Tima said many drivers had not known those systems were fitted. Sodim TP’s Izzard Biyang Ndong said customers complained about clutch problems because drivers did not follow the supplier’s instructions.

Participants Paul Koumenic of ETS Christelle & Filles and Seuntio Colince of Makaco Sarl said the training showed them how their driving practices had contributed to wear and repair costs.

Why it mattersThe 24 trained drivers and their employers are being asked to use the trucks according to supplier guidance, after Sodim TP linked poor practices to clutch complaints.
