Gabon aviation fees rise 7.97%

Aviation revenue reached 1.547 billion FCFA by September 2026, up from the previous year.

Source: Gabon Review · 2026-11-06 · 1 min read

Aviation fees in Gabon generated 1.547 billion FCFA by September 2026, an increase of 7.97% compared to the same period in 2025. Other operating revenues also grew to 316 million FCFA, a 5.93% rise. These figures were presented on November 4, 2026, in Libreville during the 46th session on national aviation activities.

The overall budget realization rate stood at 59.2% as of September 30, 2026. Joachim Tchissambou M’Boundou, cabinet chief for Asecna Gabon's director general, reported that out of 12 decisions reviewed, four were fully executed, four are in progress, three partially executed, and one remains unexecuted. Two recommendations have not been acted upon.

Beyond financial indicators, participants discussed improving aviation service quality, safety, and continuity. The partnership between Gabon and Asecna, based on a management delegation contract signed on February 5, 2019, emphasizes shared responsibility. Enhancing airport facilities and ensuring decisions are implemented are priorities for strengthening public aviation services.

Why it mattersAviation revenue in Gabon has increased, impacting the national budget and the efficiency of aviation services.
