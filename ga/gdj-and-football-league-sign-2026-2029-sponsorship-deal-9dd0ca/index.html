GDJ and football league sign 2026-2029 sponsorship deal

The agreement covers Gabon’s first and second divisions, with clubs urged to build their own income and include women’s football.

Source: Gabon Review · 2026-11-25 · 1 min read

Gabonese gaming company Gabonaise des Jeux (GDJ) and the National Professional Football League signed a sponsorship agreement in Libreville on 23 November, covering the 2026-2029 seasons. It supports the country’s first- and second-division championships.

League president Brice Mbika said the deal is intended to improve competition organisation and support clubs. Sports Minister Paul Ulrich Kessany said clubs benefiting from the agreement must develop their own revenue to remain viable, and urged them to strengthen lower-age categories and women’s football.

GDJ marketing and communications director Stéphane Nwa said the partnership backs more sustainable organisation and a return of supporters to stadiums. The agreement’s stated goals also include strengthening clubs and improving the experience of people involved in Gabonese football.

Why it mattersClubs receiving sponsorship support must build a minimum of their own resources to secure their financial viability.
