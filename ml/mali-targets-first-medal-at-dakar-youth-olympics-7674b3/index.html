Mali targets first medal at Dakar Youth Olympics

Five Malian athletes aged 15-17 compete in athletics, fencing, judo, swimming, and taekwondo.

Source: Studio Tamani · 2026-11-02 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Mali is participating in the Youth Olympic Games (YOG) in Dakar, Senegal, from October 31 to November 13, 2026, aiming to win at least one medal. The Malian delegation includes five athletes, two of whom are women, competing in athletics, fencing, judo, swimming, and taekwondo. The team is led by Abdoul Wahab Zéromé, vice-president of the Malian National Olympic and Sports Committee.

Taekwondo is seen as a strong possibility for a medal, with past successes by athletes like Daba Modibo Keïta and Ismaël Coulibaly. Bintou Diakité's gold medal at the 2026 African Taekwondo Championships in Bamako further fuels these hopes. The young athletes have pledged to perform their best to represent Mali with honor.

This is the first edition of the YOG to be held on the African continent. The athletes are determined to achieve their goal of bringing a medal back to Mali.

Why it mattersMalian athletes are competing in the Youth Olympic Games in Dakar with the goal of winning a medal.
