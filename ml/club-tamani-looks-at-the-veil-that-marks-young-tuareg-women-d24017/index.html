Club Tamani looks at the veil that marks young Tuareg women's lives

Studio Tamani's programme traced where the practice began, when it starts in a girl's life and what it signifies in Tuareg society.

Sources: Bamada.net and Studio Tamani · 2026-11-22 · 1 min read

Club Tamani, a programme of Studio Tamani, examined the practice of young Tuareg women wearing the veil during the third International Tuareg Crafts Week, held at Mali's National Museum. The broadcast asked how the practice began, when it enters a young woman's life and what its stages are.

Mohamed Elmaouloud Ag Hamid, president of the Timidwa association and director of the National Tuareg Crafts Week, joined the programme to explain the tradition and its meaning in Tuareg society.

The full programme was broadcast on 20 November 2026 and is available to listen to on Studio Tamani's website.

Why it mattersFamilies and young women who follow the tradition now have a public account of its stages and meaning, aired on a national platform.
