Algerian army reports arms cache near Mali border

The army said it found the weapons on 21 November in Algeria’s far south, in an area bordering Mali.

Sources: RFI Afrique, Mali Actu and Bamada.net · 2026-11-23 · 1 min read

Algeria’s army said it found an arms cache on 21 November 2026 in the country’s far south, in an area that borders Mali. The discovery was made near the Malian frontier, the army said.

The army described the find as part of its efforts to secure Algeria’s borders. A separate report identified the location as Bordj Badji Mokhtar and said the cache included mortars, rocket launchers, machine guns, ammunition and two drones.

The reports did not give a quantity for the weapons or identify who owned the cache. Algeria’s defence ministry announced the discovery in a statement on Saturday.

Why it mattersAlgerian forces are tasked with securing the southern border near Mali after the army reported the cache discovery on 21 November.
