Mali journalists' murder case sees partial declassification

French judge receives partial access to classified French army note regarding the 2013 killings.

Source: Journal du Mali · 2026-11-03 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

13 years after the 2013 Kidal assassinations of RFI journalists Ghislaine Dupont and Claude Verlon, the judicial investigation in France continues. A French defense commission approved partial declassification of a note from the French Ministry of Armed Forces, dated April 24, 2024. Judge Valéry Muller, leading the investigation into kidnapping, sequestration, and assassination linked to terrorism, requested the note.

The decision allows some elements of the note to be added to the case file for examination by the magistrate and civil parties. This marks the fourth declassification procedure in the ongoing investigation. No trial has yet established full responsibility for the deaths of the two reporters, whose bodies were found near Kidal after an interview.

Seidane Ag Hitta is named in the case file as a suspected instigator, a qualification pending the investigation's outcome. Malian authorities seek Ag Hitta as a leader for the Group to Support Islam and Muslims in the Kidal region, offering a 500 million FCFA reward for information leading to his arrest in 2026. Civil parties also seek an international arrest warrant for Hamadi Ag Mohamed, cited as a suspected commando member.

Why it mattersJournalists and their families in Mali and abroad are affected by the ongoing pursuit of justice for the 2013 murders.
