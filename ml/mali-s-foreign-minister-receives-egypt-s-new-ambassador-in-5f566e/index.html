Mali's foreign minister receives Egypt's new ambassador in Bamako

Abdoulaye Diop met Al-Hassan Abou Elsaoud Abdou Soliman, who presented copies of his letters of credence on Monday, 16 November 2026.

Source: Bamada.net · 2026-11-17 · 1 min read

Mali's Foreign Minister, Abdoulaye Diop, received Egypt's newly designated ambassador to Mali, Al-Hassan Abou Elsaoud Abdou Soliman, on Monday, 16 November 2026. The ambassador presented copies of his letters of credence, the formal documents that accredit a diplomat to a host government.

During the meeting, the two men reviewed priority areas of bilateral cooperation, with emphasis on security partnership and socio-economic development. Diop also set out Mali's new vision of development based on endogenous growth and on partnerships grounded in the country's constitutional principles, and he addressed the security situation in the region, including counter-terrorism efforts within the Confederation of Sahel States.

Both sides said they wanted to consolidate and diversify ties along the Bamako-Cairo axis, in fields of shared interest. The ministry described the relationship as historic, built on common values, regular political dialogue and high-level visits.

Why it mattersThe ambassador's arrival gives Malian and Egyptian officials a channel to pursue joint security and development projects.
