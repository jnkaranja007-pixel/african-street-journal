Koutiala governor launches repairs on degraded roads

Regional authorities say some routes are impassable in places as the work aims to improve road safety and traffic flow.

Sources: Studio Tamani and Bamada.net · 2026-11-28 · 1 min read

Koutiala’s regional governor launched road repair work on Thursday, 26 November, as authorities seek to improve safety for road users and ease movement around the city.

Regional authorities said the repairs come after heavy deterioration of the road network, with some routes becoming impassable in places. The reports gave no cost, route list or timetable for the work.

The launch was reported by Studio Tamani and Bamada.net on Friday, 27 November. No further details about the repair programme were provided.
