Ségou court hands down terms of 10 to 20 years in seven cases

Two defendants received 20-year sentences and two others were acquitted at the chamber's first hearing, which ended on Thursday, 19 November.

Sources: Studio Tamani and Bamada.net · 2026-11-21 · 1 min read

The first hearing of the new criminal chamber of the high court in Ségou, in central Mali, ended on Thursday, 19 November, after seven cases were heard. Sentences ranged from 10 to 20 years in prison.

Abdoulaye Diallo and Yacouba Traoré were each sentenced to 20 years. Baba Yara was sentenced in absentia to 10 years and a warrant for his detention was issued. Demba Bouaré and Emmanuel Dembélé were acquitted.

The court president said he was concerned that five of the seven cases were rape cases.

Why it mattersFive of the seven defendants heard in Ségou now face rape charges that the court president has singled out, and two men face 20-year prison terms.
