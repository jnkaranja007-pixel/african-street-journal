Mali and Burkina Faso leaders meet to strengthen alliance

General Assimi Goïta and Captain Ibrahim Traoré discussed bilateral, regional, and international issues.

Source: Bamada.net · 2026-11-07 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Mali's transitional leader, General Assimi Goïta, visited Ouagadougou on Thursday, November 5, 2026, to meet with Burkina Faso's President, Captain Ibrahim Traoré. The visit aimed to reinforce the existing fraternity, friendship, and cooperation between the two nations.

During their meeting, the leaders discussed shared interests, including bilateral cooperation to address common challenges like fighting terrorism. They also instructed their foreign ministries to prepare for the 11th Joint Cooperation Commission meeting in Ouagadougou.

The presidents also highlighted the need for increased solidarity and partnership for peace and stability in the sub-region. They noted the positive reception of their citizens residing in each other's countries and praised the progress of the Sahel States Confederation in areas like defense and diplomacy.
