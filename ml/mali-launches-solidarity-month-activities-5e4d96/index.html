Mali launches solidarity month activities

Mali's solidarity month included training for cancer patients and disabled women, and delivery of maternal health kits.

Source: Bamada.net · 2026-10-31 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Mali launched its 31st Solidarity Month with several events from October 26 to 29, 2026, focusing on vocational training, maternal health, economic empowerment, and partner mobilization. On October 27, ministers Oumou Sall Seck and Assa Badiallo TOURÉ co-chaired a 10-day traditional and modern beadwork training for the Association Solidarité et Soutien aux Malades de Cancers, supported by FAFPA to boost their economic autonomy.

The Ministry of Health and Social Development also received clean delivery kits to improve care for pregnant women and newborns, presided over by Dr. Abdoulaye Guindo. In Niamakoro, ministers Oumou Sall Seck and Assa Badiallo TOURÉ launched a semi-industrial soap-making training for women with disabilities and widows, also backed by FAFPA for professional integration.

Additionally, 220 widows and orphans of military personnel received self-employment kits for trades like tailoring and solar energy installation after receiving training. Moov Africa Malitel supported vulnerable populations with food, medical supplies, solar equipment, and funding for income-generating activities. Kénéya Foot 2026 also met with the Health Minister to promote sports for health and civic engagement.

Why it mattersVulnerable populations in Mali, including cancer patients, disabled women, widows, orphans, pregnant women, and newborns, received training and essential supplies.
