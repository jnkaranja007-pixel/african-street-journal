Douentza gathering showcases grain produced by rural women

Women from different communes and localities in the region brought cereals from their own production to the arts and culture centre.

Sources: Studio Tamani and Bamada.net · 2026-11-20 · 1 min read

Rural women from across Mali’s Douentza region displayed grain from their own production at a gathering on Thursday at the Maison des arts et de la culture in Douentza.

Mamadou Bouya Sy, the governor’s adviser for economic and financial affairs, chaired the ceremony as the governor’s representative. Technical services and security forces were also present, Studio Tamani reported.

The gathering focused on recognising women’s contribution to rural activities. The report said the women came from different communes and localities in the region; it gave no further programme or decision.

Why it mattersWomen producing grain in Douentza’s communes and localities presented their crops at a regional gathering focused on their role in rural activities.
