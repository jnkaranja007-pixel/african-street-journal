Mauritanian police seize 1,7 tonne of cocaine near Mali border

The haul was intercepted in eastern Mauritania after three months of intelligence work; police also arrested two foreign nationals suspected of involvement.

Source: Mali Actu · 2026-11-24 · 1 min read

Mauritanian national police said they intercepted 1,7 tonne of cocaine in Hodh Ech Chargui, eastern Mauritania, near the Malian border. The force announced the seizure in a statement released on Saturday 21 November 2026.

Police said the operation followed three months of intelligence gathering and surveillance. Officers also arrested two foreign nationals suspected of links to the network, but withheld their identities and precise origins while investigations continue.

The police said investigators are pursuing possible accomplices and organisers operating in the sub-region. The report does not identify any arrests in Mali.

Why it mattersMauritanian police are pursuing possible accomplices and organisers in the sub-region after the seizure and arrests near Mali’s border.
