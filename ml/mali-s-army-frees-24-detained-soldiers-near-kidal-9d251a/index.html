Mali’s army frees 24 detained soldiers near Kidal

The soldiers were taken to Gao after a coordinated operation by the army and its partners in In-Tebezas on Wednesday.

Sources: Mali Actu and Bamada.net · 2026-11-19 · 1 min read

Mali’s armed forces and their partners freed 24 soldiers held by armed groups on Wednesday 18 November 2026 in In-Tebezas, about 110 kilometres south-east of Kidal. The operation began at around 13 hours, according to a statement from the General Staff of the Armed Forces.

The General Staff said an aerial reconnaissance flight located the armed groups and the place where the soldiers were held. Forces then struck several gathering points and used combat helicopters against guards and guard posts before helicopter-borne troops entered the detention area and freed the soldiers.

All 24 were taken to Gao, and the General Staff reported no losses among the forces involved.
