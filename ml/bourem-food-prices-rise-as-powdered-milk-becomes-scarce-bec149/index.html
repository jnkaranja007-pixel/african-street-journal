Bourem food prices rise as powdered milk becomes scarce

A 500-gram packet of powdered milk now costs CFA2250 or 3 CFA500 in some places, up from 1 CFA700.

Sources: Studio Tamani and Bamada.net · 2026-11-26 · 1 min read

Prices for several basic foods have risen at the market in Bourem, Mali, where powdered milk is also becoming scarce. A 500-gram packet costs CFA2250, and reaches 3 CFA500 in some places, compared with 1 CFA700 before.

Studio Tamani reported that a 5-litre container of oil now sells for 4 CFA000, up from 3 CFA500. A large tin of tomato paste has risen from 1 CFA500 to 2 CFA000.

The outlet said the increases are putting more pressure on household food budgets and making things harder for consumers.

Why it mattersBourem households pay more for staple foods, with a 5-litre container of oil now selling for 4 CFA000.
