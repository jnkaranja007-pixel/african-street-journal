Orange Mali Foundation donates 25 million FCFA for vulnerable people

The foundation provided food, equipment for women's groups, and medical consultations in Koulikoro.

The Orange Mali Foundation has allocated 25 million FCFA to support vulnerable populations in Koulikoro through donations of food, equipment for women's groups, and free medical consultations. This initiative, launched on Friday, October 23, 2026, aims to assist thousands of beneficiaries over two days.

On the first day, 17 million FCFA was dedicated to supporting social and associative structures, with aid intended to improve living conditions. Koniba Diarra, representing beneficiary associations, expressed gratitude for the foundation's commitment. Mohamed Koureichi, regional director of social development for Koulikoro, highlighted the importance of this contribution in supporting families and individuals facing social difficulties.

The foundation's general administrator, Djénébou Diallo, stated that this mobilization is part of 20 years of engagement with Malian populations. The total 25 million FCFA will fund two major actions in the Koulikoro region, with the first 17 million FCFA directly benefiting over 5,000 people.

Why it mattersVulnerable people in Koulikoro will receive food, equipment, and medical aid.

armed attack kills one in Mali's Bandiagara

Unidentified assailants killed one person and injured another in Danlekanda village on Thursday, October 22.

One person died and another was wounded by gunfire in an attack on Danlekanda village, in the Muétoumou commune of Bandiagara, on Thursday, October 22. Unidentified armed individuals carried out the assault.

The injured man was en route to the Bandiagara reference health center for treatment. This attack follows a similar incident last week in the nearby village of Dôolo, also in Muétoumou, where three people were killed. Security remains unstable in the region.

The commune of Muétoumou is located in the Bandiagara circle. The village of Dôolo experienced a fatal attack the previous week.

Why it mattersOne person was killed and another injured in an armed attack in the Bandiagara region.

Baye Guindo of PMU-Mali receives leadership award

The Barometer recognized the marketing director for his 15 years of expertise and teaching.

Baye Guindo, Director of Marketing and Communication at PMU-Mali, received an award for merit and leadership at a ceremony in Bamako. The event, organized by the Organization of Young Business Leaders (OJEP), celebrated national managerial talent. Guindo's recognition highlights his career achievements in the Malian business sector.

Dr. Cheick Oumar Soumano coordinated the Barometer awards ceremony held at the Hôtel de l'Amitié. Guindo, who has over 15 years of experience, was honored for his professional trajectory. He also teaches marketing and business development at institutions including ESC School of Management and ESCAE.

Guindo has led PMU-Mali's marketing and communication since December 2022, working under Director General Fassery Doumbia. His previous roles include sales management at Sotelma-Malitel and general management at Hôtel du Cinquantenaire.

Why it mattersBaye Guindo, a marketing director at PMU-Mali, was honored for his professional achievements and contributions to teaching.

Malian media figure Abdoulaye Benson Diakité dies

The former RFI journalist and institutional communication advisor passed away on October 23, 2026, in Bamako.

Abdoulaye Benson Diakité, a prominent Malian journalist and communicator, died on Friday, October 23, 2026, in Bamako. Diakité, a former presenter for Radio France Internationale (RFI), had a career spanning decades in media, institutional communication, and culture. His funeral was scheduled for Friday after prayers in Missabougou.

Born in Bancoumana, Diakité began his career in teaching before moving into media, working at Africa N°1 and Radio Télévision Gabonaise in Gabon. He later joined RFI in France from 1994 to 2009, co-hosting programs like "Plein Sud" and focusing on African societies, culture, and diasporas. After returning to Mali, he served as a technical advisor in the General Secretariat of the Presidency in January 2010 and later as a special advisor to President Amadou Toumani Touré in August 2011.

Diakité also contributed to Malian media, sharing his insights on TM1 in recent years. He remained active in cultural initiatives, notably spearheading the Festi’Bazin in 2014 to promote Malian bazin fabric and its artisans. His career bridged international newsrooms and Malian realities, emphasizing knowledge transmission and giving voice to others.

Business · 2026-10-24

Mali minister launches sixth 'Consume Local' month

Minister Moussa Alassane Diallo announced 100 million FCFA for local businesses and artisans.

Mali's Minister of Industry and Commerce, Moussa Alassane Diallo, launched the sixth national "Consume Local" month on October 15, 2026. The ceremony, held at the Chamber of Commerce and Industry, aimed to promote Malian products and economic sovereignty. The event also included the presentation of a locally made outfit to the Head of State.

Minister Diallo highlighted the government's commitment to supporting micro, small, and medium enterprises, artisans, and agricultural cooperatives. A budget of 100 million FCFA has been allocated this year to support these local actors. Public procurement will also be directed towards "Made in Mali" products.

The minister urged all institutions, partners, and citizens to make consuming local products a daily habit. This initiative supports the refoundation of Mali's economy and promotes regional integration.

Why it mattersMalian businesses and artisans will receive 100 million FCFA in support and preferential public procurement.
