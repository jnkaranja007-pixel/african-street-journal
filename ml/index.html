Education · 2026-11-16

Mali invests in school infrastructure

Education Minister Amadou Sy Savané announced significant funding for new classrooms and school facilities.

Mali's Minister of National Education, Dr. Amadou Sy Savané, announced on November 10 that substantial investments have been made to improve teaching conditions during 2026. He stated that the 2025-2026 school year demonstrated progress in exam organization, conduct, and results, achieved within record time.

For the 2026-2027 academic year, declared the Year of Education and Culture, the minister outlined a three-pronged program focused on refounding Malian schools, mobilizing national support, and enhancing training. Recent achievements include the construction and rehabilitation of hundreds of classrooms, the creation and equipping of 11 classical lycées, and two scientific and technological lycées.

Future priorities include building 15 new lycées within nine months, establishing four vocational training institutes and five teacher training institutes, extending a dozen existing lycées, and renovating three historic lycées. The minister also confirmed the acquisition of 13,393 student desks and benches, with funding secured for these projects.

Why it mattersThe minister confirmed funding is secured for the construction of 15 new lycées and the acquisition of 13,393 student desks and benches.

Politics · 2026-11-16

Mali ministers promise accountability on state TV

Government officials defended their departments' actions in special broadcasts from November 9 to 14, but questions remain about the gap between official figures and daily life.

Mali's government presented its actions on television in a series of special broadcasts called Mali Kura Taasira, number 4, between November 9 and 14, 2026. Five ministers appeared on the ORTM channel to discuss their departments' work, promising accountability and transparency to citizens. The broadcasts aimed to show how government indicators align with the reality faced by Malians.

The Minister of Religious Affairs, Worship and Customs, Dr. Mahamadou Oumar Koné, spoke on November 9, emphasizing religion's role in national stability and highlighting a September 28 meeting in Bamako with religious leaders. He stressed interreligious dialogue and preventing extremism, but avoided details on the costs and issues of the Hadj pilgrimage. The Minister of National Education, Dr. Amadou Sy Savané, presented his case on November 10, arguing that state investment in education leads to progress.

Despite the ministers' presentations, citizens questioned the effectiveness of dialogue in addressing ongoing crises. The Minister of Religious Affairs focused on continuous processes rather than specific figures. The Minister of National Education stated that the Malian school system is progressing due to significant state investment and reforms.

Why it mattersMalian citizens are questioning the effectiveness of government actions presented on television in addressing ongoing crises.

Politics · 2026-11-16

Mali's foreign minister Abdoulaye Diop, a career diplomat, backs the military transition

Diop has served under Ibrahim Boubacar Keïta and Assimi Goïta, moving through political changes without leaving the centre of power.

Abdoulaye Diop, Mali's minister of foreign affairs, has held his post through successive political changes, from the presidency of Ibrahim Boubacar Keïta to that of Assimi Goïta.

A career diplomat, he has become a tireless defender of the transition led by the military, and Jeune Afrique describes him as one of its principal civilian figures.

Jeune Afrique's profile recalls a press briefing at the foreign ministry in Bamako on Friday 15 September 2023, when journalists waited for Diop to speak and the agenda had not been announced.

Why it mattersDiop's continued presence in the cabinet gives the transition government a civilian face in its dealings with foreign partners.
