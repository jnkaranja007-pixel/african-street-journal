Mali hands air force seven aircraft for transport and support

The fleet includes one IL-76, four Mi-8 helicopters and two Mi-24 helicopters, funded from the national budget and acquired with Russia.

Mali handed the armed forces an IL-76 transport plane, four Mi-8 helicopters and two Mi-24 helicopters at Sénou airport on Monday, 16 November. The aircraft are assigned to heavy transport, troop mobility, air support and medical evacuations.

Brigadier General Alou Boï Diarra, head of the air force, said it recorded more than 7 000 sorties and nearly 26 000 flight hours from January to October 2026. It transported about 28 000 passengers and more than 2 000 tonnes of freight during that period.

The government said the aircraft were bought with Russia’s cooperation and paid for from the national budget. It did not publish the purchase cost, contract terms or full delivery schedule. Their regular use will depend on crew availability, maintenance and spare-parts supply.

Why it mattersAir-force operations carried about 28 000 passengers and more than 2 000 tonnes of freight from January to October 2026, while regular use of the new aircraft depends on maintenance and spare parts.

Mali begins demolitions on 9.3-kilometre Samé road section

The work between the GMS roundabout and Kati clears the route for a four-lane upgrade under a 92.661 billion CFA-franc contract.

Mali began demolishing buildings and installations on Monday, 16 November, along the route from the GMS roundabout through Samé to Kati. The section covers 9.3 kilometres and is planned as a two-by-two-lane road.

The transport and urban-planning ministries announced the operation, which affects families and businesses along the route. The road carries daily travel between Bamako and Kati, freight and local commerce, as well as heavy two-wheel traffic.

The road works, awarded to COVEC-Mali, form part of a wider contract approved in 2024 and valued at 92.661 billion CFA francs including taxes. The project also covers 11.481 kilometres on the Bamako–Koulouba–Kati road, a 1.7-kilometre spur to Point G Hospital and seven kilometres of roads in Kati. The contract period is 36 months.

Why it mattersFamilies and businesses along the GMS–Samé–Kati route face demolitions as the 9.3-kilometre section is cleared for construction.

Mali road agency reports 2 deaths and 33 serious injuries daily

ANASER director Ousmane Bah Maïga cited the daily toll at a Bamako training session for 60 journalists on the new road code.

Mali’s National Road Safety Agency (ANASER) director Ousmane Bah Maïga said 2 people die and 33 are seriously injured on the country’s roads each day. He gave the figures in Bamako on Sunday, 15 November 2026.

Maïga was speaking at a training session at the Maison de la presse for 60 journalists from traditional media and digital platforms. The session focused on involving journalists in explaining the new road code to the public.

The training was organised to help spread information about the code, according to Bamada.net, which cited L’Indicateur du Renouveau.

Why it mattersThe 60 journalists trained are expected to explain the new road code to the public, while the agency reports 33 serious road injuries each day.

Education · 2026-11-18

Kayes project gives 100 young people training kits and certificates

The recipients in Paparah trained in solar electricity and gum arabic seedling production under a project funded by the European Union.

100 young people in Paparah, in the rural commune of Liberté Dembaya, received training kits and certificates on Monday, 16 November, for courses in solar electricity and gum arabic seedling production.

The distribution is part of Chifinw Ka Baara Sira, a project funded by the European Union and implemented by LuxDev with the Kayes Regional Council. The initiative is intended to help participants enter work or become self-employed.

The project’s training covers two fields: solar electricity and growing gum arabic seedlings. The recipients received both kits and certificates.

Why it mattersThe 100 recipients received kits and certificates to support work or self-employment after training in two fields.

Sport · 2026-11-18

Mali seal 2027 Africa Cup of Nations place with 2-0 win in Kigali

Goals from Modibo Sagnan and Gaoussou Diarra gave the Eagles a fourth straight qualifying victory and secured top spot in Group K.

Mali qualified for the 2027 Africa Cup of Nations with a 2-0 win over Rwanda in Kigali on 17 November, securing a place at the tournament with two qualifying matches still to play. The result also confirmed Mali as Group K winners.

Modibo Sagnan opened the scoring in the 27th minute and Gaoussou Diarra added the second in the 88th. Mali have won all four of their qualifying matches and hold 12 points, six more than their nearest pursuer, according to Mali Actu.

The Eagles had beaten Rwanda 3-2 in Bamako on 13 November, after twice falling behind. Their two wins over Rwanda in November brought Mali six points and completed qualification.

Why it mattersMali's players have secured qualification with two group matches remaining, after collecting 12 points from four wins.
