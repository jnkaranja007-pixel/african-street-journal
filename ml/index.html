Mali and Türkiye discuss resuming Turkish Airlines flights to Bamako

Prime Minister Abdoulaye Maïga raised the restart of Turkish Airlines flights to Bamako with Turkish Vice President Cevdet Yılmaz in Antalya on Tuesday.

Malian Prime Minister Abdoulaye Maïga, a army general, met Turkish Vice President Cevdet Yılmaz in Antalya, Türkiye, on Tuesday 10 November 2026, and the two men discussed the resumption of Turkish Airlines flights to Bamako, according to a statement from the Malian Prime Minister's office.

The talks reviewed relations between Mali and Türkiye and considered ways to strengthen them. The meeting was held on the sidelines of the COP31 climate conference hosted in Antalya, where Maïga represents Mali's transitional president, General Assimi Goïta.

The packet does not report a date for the flights to restart or any decision on them. It also does not give details of the wider cooperation agreed during the meeting.

Why it mattersTravellers between Bamako and Istanbul, and the Malian families and businesses that rely on that route, have no confirmed date for the service to return.

Mali's five youth athletes finish Dakar 2026 Youth Olympics without a medal

Five Malian athletes in five disciplines failed to reach the podium but several set new personal bests in Dakar.

Mali leaves the Dakar 2026 Youth Olympic Games without a single medal. Its five athletes, competing in five disciplines, did not reach the podium at the first Olympic event held on African soil.

Fencer Thierno Hady Thiam, the last Malian in competition, was knocked out in the first round of the individual event. His coach, Raphaël Dembélé, praised his courage and effort despite difficulties during the competition. Athlete Awa Diarra finished last in the women's 800 metres final but cut nine seconds off her personal record. Taekwondo athlete Mariam Diallo reached the quarter-finals before being eliminated.

Judoka Moussa Koïta lost two bouts in a row in the under-55 kg category. Swimmer Younoussa Maïga improved his times in the 100 metres freestyle and the 50 metres butterfly. Mariam Diallo and Younoussa Maïga carried the Malian flag at the opening ceremony on Saturday, 31 October 2026.

Why it mattersThe athletes return home with improved personal records and experience against competitors from several countries.

Malian lawmakers join African parliamentary seminar in China

Three members of Mali's National Transitional Council are attending a parliamentary seminar in China from 6 to 12 November, and visited agricultural plantations on 10 November.

A delegation of three members of Mali's National Transitional Council, the CNT, is taking part in the Seminar of African Parliamentarians held in China from 6 to 12 November 2026. The seminar brings together African parliamentary representatives to discuss relations with China and to strengthen contacts between African and Chinese institutions.

The delegation includes Saran Keita, the council's fifth vice-president, Aïchata Cissé, who chairs the Mali-China Friendship Group, and council member Rahamatou Haïdara. On 10 November the Malian representatives visited plantations in Inyi province to learn about local farming practices.

The participants say the trip is meant to share African experience with Chinese counterparts and identify areas where cooperation could be expanded. The packet does not report any agreements signed or any further steps planned after the seminar.

Why it mattersThe trip gives Malian parliamentarians a channel with Chinese and African counterparts, and its agricultural visit exposes them to farming methods used in Inyi province.

Sport · 2026-11-11

Stade Malien wins two cup finals

The club secured both the men's and women's cup titles on Saturday.

Stade Malien's women's team defeated Djoliba AC 54-52 in a close basketball final on Saturday in the Salamatou Maïga hall. This victory avenges their July championship playoff loss to Djoliba, who retained the national league title. The men's team secured their cup with a decisive 70-53 victory over Attar Club of Kidal, having led throughout the match. This win completes Stade Malien's men's league and cup double, following their championship win against Djoliba in July.

The women's final saw scores of 62-57, 56-54, and 49-46 in the playoff series, indicating a narrowing gap before Saturday's close contest. For the men, Attar Club reached the final after a season that saw them advance from Kidal. The men's team will receive two million FCFA as finalists, while both winning teams, Stade Malien's men and women, each received four million FCFA.

Stade Malien's men's team had previously beaten Djoliba three games to one in the championship final, losing only the second match 91-55. The club replaces Djoliba (women) and CRBT (men) as cup holders from the 2025 season. Attar Club's journey to the final marks their achievement as national runners-up.

Why it mattersMalian basketball clubs Stade Malien and Djoliba AC, along with Attar Club of Kidal, competed for national cup titles and prize money.

Mali Council donates food to Guinean returnees

Five tonnes of rice and other supplies will aid young Malian returnees.

The Malian Shippers' Council (CMC), through the Ministry of Transport and Infrastructure, donated five tonnes of rice, 200 cartons of sardines, 100 cartons of corned beef, and 100 cartons of Lipton tea to support young Malian returnees from Guinea. The donation was officially handed over on Tuesday, November 10, 2026, to the Minister of Health and Social Development, Colonel-major Assa Badiallo Touré, at her ministry.

These food kits are destined for the reception site at Ouézindougou, aiming to meet the essential needs of the returnees. Minister Touré expressed gratitude for the solidarity shown by the Ministry of Transport and Infrastructure and the CMC, noting that this contribution enhances the reception and assistance conditions for the affected youth while they await their return to their home countries.

The Ministry of Health and Social Development has been coordinating humanitarian efforts, including medical support and supplies, alongside contributions from other partners. The government remains committed to mobilizing necessary resources for the returnees, with the Ministry of Health leading the coordination in collaboration with relevant structures and partners.

Why it mattersMalian youth returning from Guinea are receiving essential food supplies as part of a national solidarity effort.
