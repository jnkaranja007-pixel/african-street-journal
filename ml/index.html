Mali president attends Russia-Africa forum in Moscow

Discussions focused on technology transfer, food security, and youth training in Moscow on October 28, 2026.

Mali's transitional president, General Assimi Goïta, attended the opening of the Russia-Africa Economic and Humanitarian Forum in Moscow on October 28, 2026. The event, co-chaired by Russian President Vladimir Putin and African Union chair Évariste Ndayishimiye, focused on cooperation for sovereign development.

Mali's participation centered on exploring partnerships for technology transfer, food security, natural resource valorization, infrastructure, and youth training. The goal was to support national priorities while respecting the country's sovereignty and strategic choices.

President Putin stated that trade between Russia and Africa exceeded $27 billion in 2025 and aimed for over $50 billion. He also noted that 85% of these transactions were settled in roubles, facilitated by national currency payment channels.

Mali trains 60 health workers on malnutrition

Community health agents in Yorosso are learning to screen and treat malnutrition.

Over 60 community health agents, including 40 women, are attending a training session in Yorosso, Mali. The workshop focuses on the screening and management of malnutrition. The training aims to strengthen their skills and health service package.

The regional health directorate of Koutiala organized the event in partnership with UNICEF. The workshop began on Tuesday and concludes on Thursday, November 5.

The training is intended to reinforce the agents' skills and their health service package.

Mali military court hears eight cases

Accused include officers and civilians facing charges from desertion to fraud.

The Military Court of Bamako heard eight cases on Tuesday, November 3, 2026, involving military personnel and civilians. The hearings, presided over by magistrate Assama Dolo, addressed charges including fraud, false accusations, and desertion. Some cases involved alleged financial fraud reaching up to six million CFA francs.

Among those accused were Adjudant-Chef Major Oumar Ahmadou MAIGA and Adjudant Moussa DIANE, facing charges such as loss of arms and violation of military orders. Adjudant Yacouba TOGOLA was prosecuted for prisoner escape and related violations, receiving a six-month suspended sentence. Lieutenant-colonel Ogobara GUINDO, deputy prosecutor, stated that these hearings occur twice monthly.

Guindo urged soldiers to maintain discipline and warned against fraudulent recruitment practices where individuals solicit money for integration into the Malian Armed Forces. He stressed that such acts are punishable offenses. The court deferred judgment on some cases while proceeding with others.

Why it mattersMilitary personnel and civilians in Mali faced legal proceedings for various offenses, including fraud and desertion.

Macina women's groups get six tonnes of rice

The NGO Carrefour de Développement delivered the grain on November 3, 2026, through a UN Women-funded project.

Six women's associations in Mali's Macina circle received six tonnes of rice on Tuesday, November 3, 2026. The NGO Carrefour de Développement made the donation to women in the Kolongo and Kokry communes as part of the Month of Solidarity and Fight Against Exclusion. Each of the six associations received one tonne of the cereal.

This delivery was made through the project "Force féminine, agir pour la résilience de l’assistance humanitaire," which is financed by ONU Femmes. Local authorities and the women beneficiaries welcomed this initiative.

The project aims to build resilience through humanitarian assistance.

Mali hosts FMI delegation to discuss technical aid

The International Monetary Fund's West Africa technical assistance center director met with Mali's finance minister to identify aid needs.

Mali's Minister of Economy and Finance met with a delegation from the International Monetary Fund (IMF) on April 11, 2026. The delegation was led by Mr. Christian Ebeke, Director of the IMF's Regional Technical Assistance Center for West Africa (AFRITAC West). The meeting aimed to introduce the new AFRITAC West Director to Malian authorities and discuss Mali's technical assistance requirements.

Discussions focused on identifying the technical assistance needs of key Malian structures. Preparations for the next AFRITAC West Steering Committee meeting, scheduled for Bamako in June 2027, were also discussed. Mali will preside over this upcoming meeting. The meeting reinforced the partnership between Mali and the IMF to improve institutional capacity and public finance management.

The IMF delegation included Mr. Celaine Ngotta Kouame, an economist, and Mr. Moez Ben Hassin, the IMF Resident Representative in Mali. This visit signifies a shared commitment to strengthening Mali's financial governance.

Why it mattersMali's government is seeking technical assistance from the IMF to improve its public finance management and institutional capacity.
