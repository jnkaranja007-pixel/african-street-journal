Koutiala begins checks ahead of 2026-2027 cotton ginning season

Seasonal CMDT workers started a physical control operation in Koutiala on November 9, which runs until November 25.

Seasonal workers at the Malian textile company CMDT began a physical control operation in Koutiala on November 9, 2026, ahead of the 2026-2027 cotton ginning campaign. The operation is set to run until November 25.

A communiqué from the CMDT regional director, broadcast on radio, says the checks cover the factories in Koutiala, Kimparana and Karangana, as well as the garage and common services. The Kimparana and Karangana units did not run a ginning campaign last year because of insecurity in those areas.

The checks are the first step before ginning begins. The packet does not give a start date for the campaign itself.

Why it mattersSeasonal CMDT workers and the three ginning units in Koutiala, Kimparana and Karangana are the people directly affected by the checks and the coming campaign.

Mali council gives 5 tonnes of rice to youths sent back from Guinea

Food aid also includes 200 cartons of sardines, 100 of corned beef and 100 of Lipton tea for reception sites in Mali.

On Tuesday, November 10, 2026, the Malian Shippers Council (CMC) handed a food donation to Mali's Ministry of Health and Social Development for young people sent back from Guinea and now housed in Mali. The supplies are destined for reception sites, including Ouézindougou.

The package contains 5 tonnes of rice, 200 cartons of sardines, 100 cartons of corned beef and 100 cartons of Lipton tea. The donation was organised under the Ministry of Transport and Infrastructure and was received by Health and Social Development Minister, Medical Colonel-Major Assa Badiallo Touré, who praised the speed of the gesture and pledged that the government will mobilise all necessary resources to provide dignified conditions while the youths await their return to their countries of origin.

The contribution follows an emergency humanitarian response plan of nearly 228 million FCFA, drawn up at a meeting chaired by Touré on November 6, covering shelter, food, health, protection and transport. On November 9, Maliactu reported rice, medicine and bedding donations at Ouézindougou, and the minister said talks with diplomatic missions are under way to organise the voluntary, coordinated return of foreign nationals.

Why it mattersReturnees from Guinea depend on these sites for food, shelter and health care while their repatriation is arranged.

Gao women's groups receive tricycles and flour-processing kits

Three women's groups in Gao's circle received three tricycle motorcycles and kits to make enriched flour, handed over by the circle's prefect on Monday.

Three women's groups in the Gao circle received equipment and raw materials on Monday, November 9, 2026. The groups, from Tacharane, Bagoundjé 2 and Djidara, were given three tricycle motorcycles and kits for processing and packaging enriched flour.

The handover took place in Bagoundjé 2 under the presidency of the prefect of the Gao circle. The support is part of the MOMENTUM Integrated Health Resilience project and is backed by the NGO GRIDEV.

The aim, as stated, is to strengthen the women's economic autonomy and improve nutrition in their households.

Why it mattersThe three women's groups gain the tools to produce and sell enriched flour, which they can use to earn income and feed their households.

Mali U23s beat Zimbabwe 3-0 in Harare in qualifiers

Fousseni Diawara's Eagles Hopefuls took a three-goal lead into the return leg in Bamako on November 17 after the first-round win.

Mali's Eagles Hopefuls beat Zimbabwe 3-0 on Wednesday, November 11, 2026, in Harare, in the first leg of the second round of the 2027 U23 Africa Cup of Nations qualifiers. Aboubacar Sidibé opened the scoring in the 7th minute, Niama Pape Sissoko doubled the lead in the 22nd minute, and Seydou Dembélé added the third in the 53rd minute. Mali conceded nothing.

Coach Fousseni Diawara's side were exempt from the first round. Zimbabwe's Young Warriors had eliminated Mauritius by winning both legs, 2-0 and 4-1. Captain Hamidou Makalou led the team, which now holds a three-goal advantage.

The return leg is set for November 17 in Bamako. The winner of the tie will meet Algeria or Eswatini in the third and final qualifying round.

Why it mattersMali's team goes into the Bamako return leg with a three-goal lead, and the winner advances to the third qualifying round against Algeria or Eswatini.

Politics · 2026-11-12

Mali, Turkey agree to boost security cooperation

Malian security minister met with Turkish deputy defense minister to discuss training and counter-terrorism.

Mali's Minister of Security and Civil Protection, General Daoud Aly Mohammedine, met with a Turkish delegation led by Deputy Minister of National Defense Bilal Durdali on Wednesday, November 11, 2026. The meeting, held during the BAMEX 26 Defense and Security Exhibition, aimed to strengthen security cooperation between the two nations.

Both sides expressed a shared commitment to enhancing their partnership through training, experience sharing, and improving the operational capabilities of Mali's security forces. Discussions specifically addressed combating terrorism, drug trafficking, transnational crime, and civil protection efforts. Turkey indicated its readiness to support Mali in these areas with training programs and expertise.

General Mohammedine described Turkey as a strategic partner in Mali's fight against terrorism and emphasized the importance of continuing this collaboration to address security challenges. The meeting highlighted the strong bilateral relations and shared commitment to reinforced security cooperation.

Why it mattersMalian security forces will receive training and expertise from Turkey to combat terrorism and transnational crime.
