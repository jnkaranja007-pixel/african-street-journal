Mali signs 30 billion FCFA vehicle deal with Russian firm KAMAZ

The contract includes training for mechanics and drivers, with plans for a future assembly plant in Bamako.

Mali's transition government signed a deal with Russian manufacturer KAMAZ for 350 vehicles worth over 30 billion FCFA on October 7, 2026. The agreement was confirmed by President Assimi Goïta during a meeting with KAMAZ CEO Vasily TSYGANOV in Moscow on October 27, 2026. This partnership aims to establish a sovereign transport and logistics system for Mali and foster gradual industrialization.

The contract extends beyond vehicle supply to include training for future drivers, mechanics, and engineers who will operate within Mali's transport company, ESMTL. KAMAZ also plans to build a maintenance center and eventually an assembly plant in Mali. Minister of Transport and Infrastructure, Mme DEMBELE Madina SISSOKO, stated that both parties are working to meet the established roadmap, with KAMAZ viewing Mali as a West African hub.

The strategic partnership between Mali and Russia was discussed ahead of the 3rd Russia-Africa Summit. The deal aims to enhance Mali's transport and logistics capabilities through the construction of a maintenance center and assembly plant.

Why it mattersMali's government is investing in transport infrastructure and industrialization through a partnership with a Russian company.

Mali teachers strike over research pay

University lecturers in Mali began a five-day strike on October 26, 2026, demanding payment of research bonuses.

University lecturers in Mali began a five-day strike on Monday, October 26, 2026, demanding payment of their research bonuses. The National Union of Higher Education and Scientific Research (SNESUP) initiated the action, which is renewable if their demands are not met.

The strike has left university campuses empty, with empty lecture halls and closed offices. Students, like Souleymane Ongoïba, expressed concern about further delays to their academic programs, with some having started their degree in 2023 and still not finished. Mariam Konaté also noted disruptions to course preparation and exams.

Dr. Oumar Coulibaly, SNESUP's secretary general, stated that the union is open to dialogue but will renew the strike if the government remains unresponsive. No negotiations have yet begun, and the union warns that the mobilization will continue until their demands are satisfied, leaving students uncertain about their academic calendar.

Why it mattersMalian university students face disruptions to their academic year and potential delays in completing their degrees due to the ongoing strike.

Mali charges four detained pro-democracy activists

Four activists held for nearly six months by state security have reappeared and been jailed in Bamako.

Four Malian pro-democracy activists, Youssouf Daba Diawara, Aboubacar Soumaré, Moussa « Abba » Djiré, and Ibrahima Tamega, have reappeared after nearly six months of detention by state security. They were presented to the specialized judicial pole against cybercrime on Tuesday, October 20, and have now been imprisoned at the Bamako correctional facility.

The activists were held in unknown locations and outside any judicial framework since May. No news has emerged regarding lawyer and former minister Mountaga Tall, who was reportedly the first to be abducted by state security in May.

The detention and subsequent presentation to judicial authorities of these activists highlight ongoing concerns about civil liberties in Mali. The fate of Mountaga Tall remains unknown, with no updates provided on his whereabouts or legal status.

Why it mattersFour Malian pro-democracy activists have been charged and imprisoned after nearly six months of secret detention.

MSF offers free medical aid in Macina

The aid group will provide free medical assistance to vulnerable people and displaced individuals for several more months.

For one month, the NGO Doctors Without Borders (MSF) has been providing free medical assistance to the sick at the Macina community health center.

This initiative, planned to continue for several more months, specifically targets vulnerable individuals and internally displaced people. Local authorities and the population of Macina have praised the action.

MSF's medical support in Macina aims to address the health needs of those most in need in the region.

Why it mattersVulnerable people and displaced individuals in Macina are receiving free medical assistance.

Politics · 2026-10-28

Mali junta dissolves political parties

Since the 2020 coup, former political leaders face varied fates including joining the junta or facing legal issues.

Mali's transitional authorities dissolved all political parties in May 2025, impacting former leaders of now-disbanded parties.

These former political figures have experienced diverse outcomes since the 2020 coup, with some aligning with the current government led by Assimi Goïta, while others have fallen from grace or faced judicial proceedings.

The dissolution of parties marks a significant shift for Mali's political landscape, which had seen democracy established after the 1991 events.

Why it mattersFormer political leaders in Mali are struggling to find their place after the dissolution of their parties by the transitional authorities.
