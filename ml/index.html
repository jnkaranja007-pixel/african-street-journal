Human Rights Watch says Africa Corps killed 9 civilians in Mopti

Russian Africa Corps paramilitaries killed nine civilians, including four minors, on July 10 in Bombori. Human Rights Watch reported the deaths in a report published Thursday, alleging the operation also involved beatings and arson.

Why it mattersThe report documents violence committed by Russian forces operating alongside Malian soldiers in the center of the country.

San residents donate 1 million CFA to military forces

Residents of San delivered more than 1 million CFA to camp officials on Wednesday. The donation was made in the presence of regional governor Colonel-Major Ousmane Sangaré.

Why it mattersThe funds are intended to support defense and security forces following an attack on the camp.

Politics · 08/21/2026

Mali-Mètre poll shows 14.2 point drop in positive national outlook

The percentage of people who believe the general situation in Mali has improved over the last 12 months fell by 14.2 points in one year. The Friedrich-Ebert-Stiftung (FES) released the results of its 17th Mali-Mètre opinion poll on Wednesday.

Why it mattersThe decline in perception reflects continued citizen demand for better security, employment, and essential services.

Seven-year-old boy dies of suspected poisoning in Kita

A boy died on Tuesday after allegedly consuming a toxic substance from a bottle of milk. Police are questioning an unknown woman suspected of providing the drink in the Ségoubougouni neighborhood.

Why it mattersThe child died during an evacuation to Bamako, leaving the suspect in police custody for investigation.

FIFA President congratulates Djoliba AC on Mali title

FIFA President Gianni Infantino personally congratulated Djoliba AC for winning the 2025-2026 Mali championship. The club received the recognition from the international governing body following its title win.
