Mali President Calls for Dengue Prevention Measures

President Assimi Goïta urged citizens to maintain preventative measures against dengue fever following a cabinet meeting on public health.

President Assimi Goïta has called on the population to continue implementing preventive measures against dengue fever. The appeal follows a cabinet meeting chaired by the President, which reviewed the country's public health situation. The Minister of Health, Assa Badiallo Touré, presented an update on the nation's epidemiological status and Mali's participation in the World Health Organization's regional committee for Africa.

The health minister's report highlighted Mali's engagement at the 76th session of the WHO Regional Committee for Africa in Addis Ababa, where the nation held the outgoing presidency. Discussions focused on strengthening African countries' capacities to respond to health crises and reduce external dependence. The minister emphasized advancements in managing health emergencies, crucial for a continent regularly facing epidemics and emerging diseases.

Beyond emergency response, the meeting addressed the strategic importance of local production of medicines and vaccines. This initiative aims to enhance health sovereignty and ensure a secure supply of essential health products, particularly after international crises exposed vulnerabilities. Local production, supported by infrastructure and quality control investments, is seen as key to building a more resilient health system.

Why it mattersMalian citizens are directly affected by the call to maintain dengue prevention measures to safeguard public health.

Minister Attends International Fatwa Conference in Cairo

Dr. Mahamadou Koné is representing Mali at the 11th International Conference of Fatwa Bodies and Organizations in Egypt.

Mali's Minister of Religious Affairs, Cult, and Customs, Dr. Mahamadou Koné, is currently in Cairo, Egypt, to participate in the 11th International Conference of Fatwa Bodies and Organizations. The conference is scheduled to take place over two days, September 2nd and 3rd, 2026.

The minister's attendance signifies Mali's engagement in international religious discourse and cooperation. The event brings together various religious authorities and organizations from around the world to discuss matters related to fatwas, which are Islamic legal rulings. This participation highlights Mali's commitment to engaging with global religious bodies.

The conference provides a platform for dialogue on contemporary religious issues and the role of fatwa institutions in addressing them. Dr. Koné's presence underscores Mali's active role in international religious forums and its contribution to discussions on Islamic jurisprudence and practice.

Why it mattersMalian religious leaders are engaging in international discussions on Islamic legal rulings, potentially influencing religious guidance within the country.

Donations reach 200 displaced households in Ségou

NGOs and individuals provided food and essential items to families who fled their homes in the Saro district.

Two hundred households of displaced people received food and essential supplies in Ségou on Monday. The recipients had fled from the Saro district. The aid package included rice, millet, oil, sugar, soap, dignity kits, and four head of cattle.

The initiative was organized by non-governmental organizations and private citizens. The goal of the effort was to provide relief to the families who have been displaced from their homes. The distribution took place in Ségou.

The donations aim to alleviate the immediate needs of the displaced families. The provision of food staples and hygiene products, alongside livestock, offers a measure of support for their sustenance and well-being.

Why it mattersDisplaced households in Ségou received food and essential items, including rice, millet, oil, sugar, soap, dignity kits, and four cattle.
