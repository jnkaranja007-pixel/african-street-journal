Malians remember Madou Dakolo 12 years after his death

The entrepreneur, known for his generosity, built a significant business in the hydrocarbon sector.

Source: Bamada.net · 2026-11-08 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Malians are remembering Mamadou Lah, known as Madou Dakolo, on the twelfth anniversary of his death on November 8, 2026. Dakolo, who passed away on November 8, 2014, is remembered for his entrepreneurial success and generosity. His legacy continues to inspire admiration and recognition.

Madou Dakolo, a prominent businessman in the hydrocarbon sector through his company Hydro-Mali SARL, was known for his significant investments and wealth. He was also recognized for his charitable contributions and support for those in need, embodying human and social values.

His life serves as an example for young Africans seeking success and determination. Dakolo's business acumen and philanthropic spirit left a lasting impression on Mali and its people.
