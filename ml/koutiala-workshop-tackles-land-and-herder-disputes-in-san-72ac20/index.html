Koutiala workshop tackles land and herder disputes in San and Koutiala

The workshop, held in Koutiala in Mali's south, focused on preventing violence over natural resources and climate change in San and Koutiala.

Sources: Studio Tamani and Bamada.net · 2026-11-19 · 1 min read

A workshop on cultural practices, natural-resource governance and peaceful conflict management was held in Koutiala, in southern Mali, on Wednesday, 18 November. Participants discussed land conflicts linked to field boundaries, inheritance and succession, as well as tensions between farmers and herders.

The meeting was organised by the non-governmental organisations AMEDD (Association Malienne d'Éducation pour un Développement Durable) and HELVETAS, a Swiss development organisation. It forms part of a project to prevent violence linked to natural-resource conflicts and climate change in the regions of San and Koutiala.

The reporting does not record any decisions or agreements reached at the meeting.
