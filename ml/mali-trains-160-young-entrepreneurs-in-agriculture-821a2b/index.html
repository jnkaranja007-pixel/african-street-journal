Mali trains 160 young entrepreneurs in agriculture

The Institute for Management in Bamako hosted a three-day training for 160 agricultural entrepreneurs.

Source: Mali Actu · 2026-11-07 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

160 young entrepreneurs in Mali completed a three-day training program focused on agricultural value chains. The event, held at the Institute for Management (ISPRIC) in Bamako from November 3 to November 5, 2026, aimed to foster professional opportunities in the agricultural sector. This initiative was coordinated by the Ministry of Agriculture and the Alliance for a Green Revolution in Africa (AGRA).

Participants explored sustainable production, local industrial processing, and regional market commercialization. The training equipped these future business leaders with skills to develop viable business models suited to Africa's climate and economic conditions. The program emphasized innovation in rural development and transforming agricultural value chains into economic drivers.

The Malian government is leveraging its youth's creativity to modernize agriculture and boost the local economy. This program aims to create sustainable jobs and ensure future food security. The agricultural sector is presented as a significant entrepreneurial opportunity for Mali's economic growth.

Why it matters160 young Malian entrepreneurs received training to improve agricultural production, processing, and marketing, boosting their incomes and contributing to food security.
