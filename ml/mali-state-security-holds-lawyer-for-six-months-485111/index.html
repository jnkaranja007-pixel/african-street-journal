Mali: state security holds lawyer for six months

Mountaga Tall, a former minister, was abducted from his home by state security agents.

Source: RFI Afrique · 2026-11-03 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Malian state security agents abducted former minister Mountaga Tall from his home six months ago, on November 2, 2026. The lawyer, known for his pro-democracy advocacy, was taken by hooded individuals. Other pro-democracy activists seized by state security in May have recently reappeared, raising concerns about their health.

Amnesty International is urging the transitional authorities to investigate the disappearance of Mountaga Tall. His abduction occurred six months to the day after he was taken from his residence. The organization is concerned about the well-being of the detained activists.

The current season is harvest time in Mali. While some areas have completed their crop collection, others are still engaged in agricultural activities. The disappearance of Mountaga Tall is a matter of ongoing concern for human rights organizations.
