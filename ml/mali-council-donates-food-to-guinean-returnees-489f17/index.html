Mali Council donates food to Guinean returnees

Five tonnes of rice and other supplies will aid young Malian returnees.

Source: Bamada.net · 2026-11-11 · 1 min read

The Malian Shippers' Council (CMC), through the Ministry of Transport and Infrastructure, donated five tonnes of rice, 200 cartons of sardines, 100 cartons of corned beef, and 100 cartons of Lipton tea to support young Malian returnees from Guinea. The donation was officially handed over on Tuesday, November 10, 2026, to the Minister of Health and Social Development, Colonel-major Assa Badiallo Touré, at her ministry.

These food kits are destined for the reception site at Ouézindougou, aiming to meet the essential needs of the returnees. Minister Touré expressed gratitude for the solidarity shown by the Ministry of Transport and Infrastructure and the CMC, noting that this contribution enhances the reception and assistance conditions for the affected youth while they await their return to their home countries.

The Ministry of Health and Social Development has been coordinating humanitarian efforts, including medical support and supplies, alongside contributions from other partners. The government remains committed to mobilizing necessary resources for the returnees, with the Ministry of Health leading the coordination in collaboration with relevant structures and partners.

Why it mattersMalian youth returning from Guinea are receiving essential food supplies as part of a national solidarity effort.
