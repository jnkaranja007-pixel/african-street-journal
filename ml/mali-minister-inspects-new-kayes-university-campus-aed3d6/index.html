Mali minister inspects new Kayes university campus

Minister Bouréma Kanssaye visited provisional sites for classes starting January 2027.

Source: Studio Tamani · 2026-11-03 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Mali's Minister of Higher Education, Professor Bouréma Kanssaye, visited the future university campus in Kayes on Sunday, November 1, 2026. Accompanied by the governor of Kayes, the minister inspected provisional facilities intended to host the first courses.

These temporary locations include the ANPE jobs workshop, the Massa Makan Diabaté performance hall, and the former district council building. The inspection aimed to assess the readiness for the start of academic activities.

The first classes are scheduled to begin in January 2027 at these provisional sites while the main campus construction progresses.

Why it mattersStudents in Kayes will soon have access to university courses starting in January 2027.
