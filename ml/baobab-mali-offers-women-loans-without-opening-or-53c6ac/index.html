Baobab Mali offers women loans without opening or application fees

Its Crédit Muso loans range from CFA100.000 to CFA1.000.000 over 10 months, with interest still payable.

Source: Journal du Mali · 2026-11-25 · 1 min read

Baobab Mali offers women entrepreneurs Crédit Muso loans from CFA100.000 to CFA1.000.000 over 10 months, with no opening or application fees. The lender says the product is designed to help women finance their businesses.

Borrowers still pay interest and must repay on a schedule suited to their income. A shopkeeper may need money to increase stock, while a workshop owner may use a loan for raw materials and wages before a customer pays. Baobab says its advisers assess clients’ expenses, payment delays and quieter trading periods to determine what they can borrow and repay.

The product was discussed against recommendations from microfinance professionals who met in Bamako from 20 to 23 October 2026. The recommendations, reported on 2 November by the Treasury and Public Accounting Directorate, included a national guarantee mechanism covering women, young entrepreneurs and agriculture. Baobab has operated in Mali since 2013 and has 18 branches, nine outside Bamako.

Why it mattersWomen running shops and workshops can access loans without opening or application fees, but interest and repayments remain due over 10 months.
