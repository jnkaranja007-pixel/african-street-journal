Mali journalist's appeal verdict set for November 16, 2026

Youssouf Sissoko, director of L'Alternance, faces a potential confirmation of his two-year sentence.

Source: Bamada.net · 2026-10-29 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

The Bamako Court of Appeal will deliver its verdict on journalist Youssouf Sissoko's appeal on November 16, 2026. Sissoko, director of the publication "L'Alternance," was sentenced on March 23, 2026, to two years in prison and a fine of one million CFA francs for spreading false information and insulting a foreign head of state.

His arrest on February 5, 2026, followed the publication of an article titled "General Tiani under emotional duress accuses Macron, Ouattara and Talon." The article discussed an attack on Niamey airport and contained critical assessments of Nigerien authorities' statements. Sissoko's lawyer requested his acquittal or a sentence equivalent to his detention time, while the prosecutor sought to uphold the initial ruling.

Sissoko first appeared in court on March 9, 2026, after being charged with diffusing false information, harming the state's reputation, and insulting a foreign head of state. The initial conviction came after the prosecutor requested five years imprisonment.
