Mali detains four pro-democracy activists

Four activists, previously held illegally for six months, have been formally charged and imprisoned.

Source: RFI Afrique · 2026-11-13 · 1 min read

Mali has formally charged four pro-democracy activists, Youssouf Daba Diawara, Aboubacar Soumaré, Moussa « Abba » Djiré, and Ibrahima Tamega, who were released by the State Security services on October 20, 2026. The activists were initially abducted in May 2026 and held in secret state facilities for nearly six months without legal basis.

The four men are described as political and civil society militants. They were placed under a committal order by the judicial pole for anti-cybercriminality. Details regarding the specific charges against them and their current health status have not yet been fully disclosed.

The exact date of their trial has not been announced. Their reappearance and subsequent detention follow a period of illegal confinement by Malian security services.
