N’Tabacoro opens 199 million FCFA health centre

The new facility in N’Tabacoro includes a maternity ward, a clinic, and solar equipment.

Source: Mali Actu · 2026-10-29 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

A new health centre costing nearly 199 million FCFA opened on October 29, 2026, in N’Tabacoro. The facility was funded by the national budget and includes a maternity ward and a clinic. Solar equipment was also installed to power the centre.

The inauguration on October 29, 2026, provides the community of N’Tabacoro with a new health centre. The centre's construction was financed through the national budget.

The new health centre is equipped with a maternity ward to support childbirth services and a clinic for general medical care. Solar equipment was installed to power the centre.

Why it mattersResidents of N’Tabacoro will benefit from improved access to healthcare services, including maternity care and general medical treatment.
