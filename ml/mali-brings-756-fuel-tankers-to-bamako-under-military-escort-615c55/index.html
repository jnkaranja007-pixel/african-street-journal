Mali brings 756 fuel tankers to Bamako under military escort

The convoy takes the total delivered to the capital in November to 2 261, according to Mali's trade and competition directorate.

Source: Bamada.net · 2026-11-22 · 1 min read

A convoy of 756 fuel tankers reached parking areas in Bamako in the night of Friday to Saturday 21 November, escorted by Mali's armed forces. The arrival brought the number of tankers sent to the capital during the first three weeks of November to 2 261, according to the Directorate-General for Trade, Consumption and Competition.

The convoy arrived at about 3 h 05, the directorate said. It was the third delivery this month, after 665 tankers on 7 November and 840 on 14 November. The deliveries are intended to supply fuel to Bamako's service stations.

The armed forces accompanied the latest convoy along its route to the capital, a role the directorate said is important for protecting the convoys and keeping supply moving.

Why it mattersFuel supply to Bamako's service stations depends on these convoys, with 2 261 tankers delivered in the first three weeks of November.
