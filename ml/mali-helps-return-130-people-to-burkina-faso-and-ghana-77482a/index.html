Mali helps return 130 people to Burkina Faso and Ghana

Burkina Faso sent a bus for 123 people on 19 November, while Ghana arranged transport for seven of its citizens.

Source: Bamada.net · 2026-11-22 · 1 min read

Malian authorities helped transport 130 people from gathering sites in Mali to Burkina Faso and Ghana, according to a statement dated 20 November. Burkina Faso sent a bus from the Quinzindougou site on 19 November for 123 people; Ghana arranged transport for seven of its citizens.

The statement said 455 people remained at the Quinzindougou youth centre, down from 564 the previous day. They included 260 Beninese, 190 Togolese, two Burkinabè, two Nigeriens and one Guinean. Another 20 Burkinabè remained at the Kouremalé site.

Mali is consulting the countries concerned to organise returns for those still at the sites. The crisis coordination centre urged communities to help locate and direct people who remain dispersed.

Why it mattersThe 455 people still at Quinzindougou and 20 at Kouremalé depend on Mali coordinating further returns with their countries.
