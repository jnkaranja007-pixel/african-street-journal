Mali aims for 25.25% emissions cut by 2035

The nation's third-generation climate plan targets reductions in energy, agriculture, and waste sectors.

Source: Journal du Mali · 2026-11-07 · 1 min read

Mali has set a goal to reduce greenhouse gas emissions by 25.25% by 2035, compared to a reference scenario. This objective, part of the nation's third-generation climate plan, focuses on the energy, agriculture, and waste sectors. The plan was presented during a validation workshop on October 1.

Gaoussou Berthé, an environmental and climate change expert, noted that political commitments have not always been matched by necessary funding for implementation. Many projects from Mali's previous national climate contribution, which envisioned billions of dollars in investments, are still awaiting their intended resources. The COP31 climate conference is scheduled from November 9 to 20 in Antalya, Turkey.

Farmers in Sikasso and Bougouni experienced disrupted planting seasons in 2026 due to rainfall pauses, leading to water scarcity for crops. September rains later improved conditions in several areas, highlighting the critical role of rain patterns for producers, according to Mali-Météo. The COP30 conference called for tripling adaptation finance by 2035, with developed nations leading the effort.

Why it mattersMalian farmers face challenges with unpredictable rainfall impacting their crops and livelihoods.
