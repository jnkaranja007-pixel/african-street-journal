Mali’s special forces free about 30 soldiers near Algerian border

The operation used combat and transport helicopters, a Sukhoï fighter and drones, according to Bamada.net.

Source: Bamada.net · 2026-11-23 · 1 min read

Mali’s special forces, backed by Russian allies, freed about 30 members of the Malian Armed Forces in a locality near the Algerian border, the outlet Bamada.net reported. The operation was carried out in the north of the country.

The mission used combat and transport helicopters, a Sukhoï fighter and recent-generation drones, the report said. The soldiers have returned to their units and families, and the report described the operation as a success.

The report gave no further details on who held the soldiers or when they were taken. It did not say how many forces took part in the operation.

Why it mattersThe freed soldiers have returned to their units and families, and the armed forces face the task of keeping up this level of operation in the north.
