Mali distributes aid to vulnerable populations

Rice, oil, and school kits were given to people with disabilities in Kadiolo, Bafoulabé, and Mahina.

Source: Studio Tamani · 2026-11-03 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

During the Month of Solidarity and Fight Against Exclusion, aid was distributed in several Malian locations. In Kadiolo, the FELAPH provided 23 sacks of 50kg rice, 23 five-litre oil containers, six tricycles, a wheelchair, and school kits to individuals with disabilities. People with disabilities in Bafoulabé and Mahina received aid kits containing rice, oil, sugar, mosquito nets, and a symbolic cash envelope.

In Gao, 50 households of internally displaced and vulnerable people received food donations. These actions aim to support those facing hardship and exclusion within these communities.

The distribution is part of a national solidarity initiative. Further details on the total number of beneficiaries or the duration of the solidarity month were not provided in the report.

Why it mattersVulnerable people in Kadiolo, Bafoulabé, Mahina, and Gao received essential food items and mobility aids.
