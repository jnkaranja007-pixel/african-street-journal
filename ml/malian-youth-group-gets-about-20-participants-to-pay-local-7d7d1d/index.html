Malian youth group gets about 20 participants to pay local tax

At a Koulikoro awareness day on Tuesday 16 November, participants paid the regional and local development tax in the presence of municipal authorities.

Sources: Studio Tamani and Bamada.net · 2026-11-19 · 1 min read

A local branch of Mali’s Collective of Youth Associations and Movements for Development held an awareness day in Koulikoro on Tuesday 16 November to encourage payment of the Regional and Local Development Tax (TDRL).

About 20 participants paid the tax at the event, which municipal authorities attended. The group said the initiative was intended to encourage young people to help mobilise the population.

The report gave no further details about the tax or any next step.

Why it mattersThe participants who paid at the event have completed their tax payment, while the group is encouraging young people to mobilise residents.
