Insecurity disrupts classes for 3 979 children in Mali

The UNICEF figure covers pupils affected by the crisis, while 639 students displaced from Kidal were counted in Gao after April 2026 violence.

Source: Journal du Mali · 2026-11-25 · 1 min read

Violence in Mali has disrupted classes for 3 979 children, according to UNICEF. In Gao, 639 pupils displaced from Kidal were counted after violence in April 2026, disrupting a school year already under way.

A special session allowed 24 displaced candidates to sit the Diplôme d’études fondamentales examinations. At the October 2026 school-year opening, 2 444 schools were reported non-functional, affecting more than 733 000 children; UNICEF said insecurity was the main cause.

Catch-up classes served 10 252 pupils across several regions in the third quarter, and six schools reopened in Gao with UNICEF and partner support. The organisation said some schools close again when threats persist.

Why it mattersDisplaced pupils and children at non-functional schools face interrupted lessons; 24 displaced candidates needed a special examination session.
