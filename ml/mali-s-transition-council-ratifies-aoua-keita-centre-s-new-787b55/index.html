Mali's transition council ratifies Aoua Keïta centre's new status

The change gives the women's vocational training centre administrative and financial autonomy through a board, directorate and scientific committee.

Sources: Bamada.net and Mali Actu · 2026-11-21 · 1 min read

Mali's National Transition Council (CNT) ratified an order creating a new public-institution status for the Aoua Keïta Vocational Training Centre at a plenary session in Bamako on Friday, 20 November. All 119 members present voted for the bill.

The centre, created in 1999, provides vocational and tailored training, with a mission to advance women's social and economic standing and combat girls' school dropout. The new status provides for administrative and financial autonomy, managed through a board, a directorate and a pedagogical and scientific committee.

The council's committee on women, family, child protection, youth, sports, labour and employment recommended adding courses in food processing, entrepreneurship, digital skills and other promising trades, as well as scholarships for people in need and stronger partnerships with two national training and education directorates.

Why it mattersThe centre's administrators must set up three management bodies to oversee its vocational programmes for women.
