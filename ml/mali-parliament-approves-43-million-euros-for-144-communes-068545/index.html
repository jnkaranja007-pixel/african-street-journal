Mali parliament approves 43 million euros for 144 communes

The funding will support water, health care, education and farming and pastoral activities in six central and northern regions through June 2028.

Source: Mali Actu · 2026-11-21 · 1 min read

Mali’s legislature unanimously approved an additional 43 million euros for the Community Recovery and Stabilization Project for the Sahel, extending its work until 30 June 2028. The vote took place in Bamako on Friday, 20 November, with all 121 lawmakers present voting in favour of ratifying the financing agreement between Mali and the World Bank’s International Development Association.

The funding, equivalent to about CFA28.206 billion, is intended for 144 communes in Mopti, Bandiagara, Douentza, Gao, Ménaka and Timbuktu. Mali’s reconciliation minister, Ismaël Wagué, told lawmakers it would support drinking water, health care, education and agricultural and pastoral activities, with particular attention to the Liptako-Gourma area.

The project gives communities a role in setting priorities, choosing investments and monitoring local infrastructure. A parliamentary committee urged the government to maintain that approach, coordinate ministries and ensure completed facilities are maintained.

Why it mattersResidents in 144 communes are due to receive investments in water, health, education and livelihoods under funding extended through 30 June 2028.
