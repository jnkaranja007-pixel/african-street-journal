three teachers abducted in Mali's San region

School leaders express concern over demands for Arabic instruction or school closures.

Source: Studio Tamani · 2026-10-23 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Three teachers were abducted in Mali's San region on October 20 and 21, 2026. The victims include a school director and two teachers, according to Tiékoura Coulibaly, a regional spokesperson for the education union Synergy. The first abduction occurred on October 20, involving the director of the Fani primary school in Yangasso. Two more teachers were taken on October 21, between 30 and 40 kilometers from San town.

These abductions are reportedly linked to threats against school operations, with demands for Arabic to be taught or for schools to close. Tiékoura Coulibaly stated that union information suggests this ultimatum. Negotiations are reportedly underway for the release of the two teachers abducted on Wednesday. The Synergy union is calling on authorities to enhance school security and protect educators.

The union wants all three abducted colleagues to return safely to their families. The abductions have raised concerns about the functioning of educational institutions in the region. The Synergy union is urging authorities to implement measures to ensure the safety of teachers and schools.

Why it mattersTeachers and school directors in the San region of Mali are directly affected by these abductions and threats.
