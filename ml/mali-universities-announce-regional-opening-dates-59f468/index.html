Mali universities announce regional opening dates

Bandiagara will recruit students in November 2026, and Kayes will start classes in January 2027.

Sources: Mali Actu and Bamada.net · 2026-11-08 · 1 min read

Mali's Council of Ministers, meeting on October 16, 2026, announced plans for regional universities. The University of Bandiagara will open applications for its Materials Science program in November 2026, with classes beginning in the 2026-2027 academic year. The University of Kayes will launch its Mines and Geology department courses in January 2027.

These timelines were presented by the Minister of Higher Education and Scientific Research as part of a gradual rollout for universities in Sikasso, Gao, Timbuktu, Bandiagara, and Kayes. The goal is to provide education and research tailored to local needs. The University of Sikasso's rural development institute has been operational since the 2023-2024 academic year, currently serving 325 students in two programs.

The Council of Ministers, chaired by Transition President Assimi Goïta, also adopted three draft decrees, made appointments, and heard communications on social dialogue, sport, climate, and health. The minister aims for these universities to eventually offer all planned courses and establish research laboratories meeting international standards.

Why it mattersMalian students in Bandiagara and Kayes will have new higher education opportunities starting in late 2026 and early 2027.
