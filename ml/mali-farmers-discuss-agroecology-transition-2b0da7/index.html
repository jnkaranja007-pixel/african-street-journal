Mali farmers discuss agroecology transition

Over 100 farmers and sector actors gathered in Ségou for a three-day national event focused on agroecology and organic farming.

Source: Studio Tamani · 2026-10-22 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

A national week dedicated to peasant agroecology began on October 20 in Ségou, Mali. More than 100 farmers and sector stakeholders from various districts within the region are participating in the event.

For three days, attendees will engage in discussions centered on the transition to agroecology and organic agriculture. The primary objective of these exchanges is to bolster food sovereignty across Mali.

The regional platform for agroecology and organic agriculture is the organizing body behind this national initiative, bringing together key figures in the agricultural sector to share knowledge and strategies.

Why it mattersFarmers are affected by discussions on agroecology and organic farming aimed at strengthening food sovereignty.
