Macina records more than 100 gender-based violence cases in 2026

The local service for women, children and families reported the cases, including an alleged sexual assault on a four-year-old girl.

Sources: Studio Tamani and Bamada.net · 2026-11-20 · 1 min read

Macina recorded more than 100 cases of gender-based violence in 2026, the local service for the promotion of women, children and families said. The reported cases include an alleged sexual assault on a four-year-old girl.

The Local Committee for Orientation and Monitoring of Gender-Based Violence Cases met in Macina on Wednesday, 18 November. Discussions included female genital cutting and violence between spouses, according to Studio Tamani.

Representatives of justice, security forces, women, young people and local elected officials attended, with support from the non-governmental organisation G Force. The report gave no further action or deadline.

Why it mattersMacina's local service and committee must respond to more than 100 recorded cases this year, with discussions covering female genital cutting and violence between spouses.
