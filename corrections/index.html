Corrections

When we get something wrong, we fix it quickly.

  • Every story links to the report it is based on. Where our story and the source disagree, the source stands, and we correct or remove ours.
  • We correct factual errors in published stories as soon as we confirm them, normally within one working day.
  • A story that should not have run, such as a misattributed report or an item that is not news, is taken down.
  • AI models write our stories and software checks them. Errors still get through, and readers catch many of them. Please tell us when you see one.

To report an error, email hello@africanstreetjournal.com with the link to the story and what is wrong.
