U.S. lawmaker links Eritrea trade talks to progress on peace

Representative Jim Baird's proposed Eritrea PILLAR Act would open U.S. farm exports, mining help and port access to talks once peace steps are taken.

Source: Eritrea Focus · 2026-11-23 · 1 min read

Jim Baird, a Republican congressman from Indiana, is set to introduce the Eritrea PILLAR Act and the Ethiopia SPARK Act, which tie formal trade talks with the United States to steps toward lasting peace. The bills were expected to be introduced Thursday and shared with The Hill.

The texts seek to increase Eritrean and Ethiopian access to U.S. corn, soybeans, soybean meal, livestock feed, farm equipment and agricultural research. They also call for U.S. help in mining gold, silver, copper, zinc, iron ore, potash and other minerals, access to port infrastructure, and reduced tariffs or better trade terms. Baird said the plan would make the United States a preferred partner over China, which has deep ties in both countries.

The proposal comes as Ethiopian and Eritrean forces build up along their shared border, Reuters reported, and after fighting broke out in September between Ethiopia's federal government and rebels in Tigray. Secretary of State Marco Rubio spoke with Ethiopian Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed Ali on October 14 and called for a return to the 2018 Ethiopia-Eritrea peace framework.
