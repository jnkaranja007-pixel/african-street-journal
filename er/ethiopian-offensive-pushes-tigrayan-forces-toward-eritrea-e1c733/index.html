Ethiopian offensive pushes Tigrayan forces toward Eritrea border

Clashes are reported in several areas, including near Badme, as analysts warn that the fighting risks a wider conflict involving Eritrea.

Source: Eritrea Focus · 2026-11-26 · 1 min read

Ethiopian forces have resumed an offensive in Tigray, pushing local armed formations toward the border with Eritrea, according to Deutsche Welle, citing military analysts. The fighting threatens to widen into a regional conflict, the report said.

The Armed Conflict Location and Event Data project (ACLED), a monitoring organisation, says clashes are occurring in several areas. Near Badme, a town on the disputed border, heavy artillery fire has reportedly been used. Abdurahman Said, director of the Center for Regional Integration of the Horn of Africa, said troop concentrations along the border point to the danger of a large-scale conflict.

Ethiopia's pursuit of Red Sea access is another source of tension; Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed has previously not ruled out using force to control Eritrea's port of Assab. Ethiopia and Sudan are also trading accusations of interference, while their dispute over the Grand Ethiopian Renaissance Dam is intensifying.

Why it mattersEritrean communities near the border face renewed clashes, with heavy artillery fire reported near Badme and analysts warning of a large-scale conflict.
