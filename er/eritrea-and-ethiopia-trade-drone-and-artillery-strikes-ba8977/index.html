Eritrea and Ethiopia trade drone and artillery strikes

Fighting near the disputed town of Badme has intensified, driving civilians from their homes.

Sources: Eritrea Focus and Anadolu Africa · 2026-11-16 · 1 min read

Eritrean and Ethiopian forces have reportedly exchanged drone and artillery strikes near the disputed border town of Badme. The fighting has intensified over several days, driving civilians from their homes, according to diplomatic sources cited by local outlet Addis Standard on Saturday. Ethiopian forces reportedly deployed drones, while Eritrean troops used heavy artillery.

Two diplomats familiar with intelligence assessments told the Ethiopian outlet that the clashes had escalated. One source stated, “We are aware of drone strikes, particularly from the Ethiopian side, and the use of heavy artillery from the Eritrean side, and we are monitoring the situation.” A second source warned that the exchanges could lead to a major military confrontation between the neighboring countries.

Residents reported nighttime explosions, closed businesses, and families leaving their homes as Ethiopian federal troops increased their presence around Badme. The Armed Conflict Location & Event Data Project (ACLED) reported on November 13 that neither side controlled Badme. The renewed confrontation threatens to deepen instability in the Horn of Africa.
