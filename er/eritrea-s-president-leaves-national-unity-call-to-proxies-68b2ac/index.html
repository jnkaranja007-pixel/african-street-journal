Eritrea's president leaves national unity call to proxies

A newscaster read the appeal on television, while poet Awel Said called for unity in early November.

Source: Eritrea Focus · 2026-11-26 · 1 min read

Eritrea Focus says President Isaias Afwerki has not addressed the nation himself as calls for unity and defence of the country circulate amid fighting on the border. A newscaster read a proclamation on television, and poet-orator Awel Said made a unity appeal in early November.

The article says mothers fear younger children will be conscripted for another war, after earlier generations served in Badme and Tigray. It says some Eritreans were sent to fight in those conflicts, and mothers were not told where their children were; the article says there is no list of the dead or answer for families.

The article also says national service involves forced labour and abuse, and that singer Senait Debessai has been imprisoned without charge or trial since 2003. It questions how citizens can answer a call to defend the country when the president speaks through others.

Why it mattersMothers whose children are conscripted face another possible deployment, while Eritrea's president has not spoken to the nation himself.
