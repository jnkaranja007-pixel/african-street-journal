Ethiopian forces push Tigrayan fighters toward Eritrean border

Clashes have been reported near Badme as artillery was fired toward dwellings close to the Eritrean border town.

Source: DW Africa · 2026-11-18 · 1 min read

Ethiopian forces have pushed Tigrayan fighters toward Eritrea’s border after renewed clashes in the contested area near Badme, a border town in Eritrea. The Ethiopian National Defense Forces (ENDF), backed by pro-government militia, have advanced into Tigray, northern Ethiopia, against forces allied with the Tigray People’s Liberation Front (TPLF).

ACLED, a conflict-monitoring organization, reports skirmishes at several locations as the ENDF moved the Tigrayan Defense Forces toward the border. The Ethiopian troops also fired heavy artillery toward dwellings near Badme. No casualty figure is given in the report.

The TPLF-allied fighters began their offensive on September 23, seeking to overthrow Ethiopia’s government as part of a rebel coalition. Abdurahman Sayed, director of the London-based Horn of Africa Center for Regional Integration, said Ethiopia might see reclaiming Badme as a way to ease its internal conflict. The town was awarded to Eritrea by a boundary commission after the 1998-2000 war.

Why it mattersResidents near Badme face artillery fire, while the report says skirmishes have been recorded at several border locations.
