Eritrea opens National Conference on Eritrean Agricultural Studies

The National Conference on Eritrean Agricultural Studies 2026 opened Aug. 20 at the National Confederation of Eritrean Workers Hall in Asmara. The event focuses on advancing sustainable agri-food systems for national economic growth, according to the state news agency Shabait.

Why it mattersThe conference brings together ministers, experts, and food processors to coordinate strategies for increasing national economic growth through food systems.
