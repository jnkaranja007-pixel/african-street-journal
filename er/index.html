Eritrean Embassy refutes tanker hijacking claims, clarifies flag status

The Eritrean Embassy in London has denied reports of an "Eritrean flagged" oil tanker being hijacked off Yemen, stating the vessel has no ties to Eritrea.

The Embassy of the State of Eritrea in London clarified today that reports from news outlets, including Reuters, AFP, and BBC, about a hijacked "Eritrean flagged" oil tanker on August 20, 2026, are inaccurate. The Embassy stated that the United Kingdom Maritime Trade Operations (UKMTO), cited as the primary source, did not mention the ship being Eritrean flagged in its report, a fact UKMTO reiterated to the Embassy.

Further investigation by the Eritrean Embassy revealed that the ship, identified by IMO Registration 920476, is not owned by or linked to Eritrea. The International Maritime Organization (IMO) categorizes this vessel as part of a "Shadow Fleet" with "indeterminate ownership." Eritrea's Permanent Mission to the IMO confirmed that official records show the ship is "flying a false Eritrean flag; i.e. as not owned or officially registered in Eritrea."

The Embassy criticized what it called a "despicable campaign of disinformation on Eritrea," asserting that the true facts contradict the initial media reports. This clarification aims to correct the record regarding the vessel's nationality and ownership, emphasizing that the ship has no official connection to the State of Eritrea.

Why it mattersThis story affects international shipping and trade by clarifying the ownership and flag status of a vessel involved in a reported hijacking, which can impact insurance rates and maritime security perceptions.

Eritrea celebrates Eid Mewlid Al-Nebi nationwide

The 1448 Hijri year's Eid Mewlid Al-Nebi was celebrated across Eritrea today, with a main ceremony in Asmara attended by government officials and religious leaders.

Eritrea marked Eid Mewlid Al-Nebi, commemorating the 1448 Hijri year, with nationwide celebrations today, August 25. The main ceremony took place at the Al-Khulafa Al-Rashidin Mosque in Asmara, drawing a large congregation of faithful alongside ministers, senior Government and PFDJ officials, members of the diplomatic community, and leaders from various Churches.

Haj Mohammed-Seid Bashir, Chairman of the Islamic Awkaf of Asmara, provided a detailed briefing on the historical importance of the holiday and acknowledged those who contributed to the event. Sheik Salem Ibrahim Almuktar, Mufti of Eritrea and Chairman of the Higher Assembly of Eritrean Iftae and Islamic Affairs, extended congratulations to Eritreans both within the country and abroad.

Sheik Salem Ibrahim Almuktar emphasized the continued importance of adhering to Eritrea’s foundational ethical principles and standards amidst globalization. He also called for the preservation and strengthening of the nation's strong national unity and cohesion, which he stated were secured through the sacrifices of its martyrs. The celebration featured spiritual performances.

Why it mattersThe celebration of Eid Mewlid Al-Nebi affects Eritrean citizens at home and abroad by reinforcing national unity and ethical principles.

Eritrea expands renewable energy supply and use

The Ministry of Energy and Mines is working to increase the availability and adoption of renewable energy sources across Eritrea, including solar, wind, and bioenergy.

Eritrea is expanding its renewable energy infrastructure, with the Ministry of Energy and Mines reporting encouraging results from efforts to introduce and expand various clean energy sources. Mr. Tesfay Gebrehiwet, Director of Renewable Energy at the Ministry, noted that these initiatives cover household, agricultural, and other purposes, encompassing solar, wind, geothermal, green-hydrogen, and bioenergy technologies.

Significant progress has been made in solar energy, with 34 megawatts installed in Dekemhare, 2 megawatts around Asmara, and 2.25 megawatts in Areza and Mai-Dima. The Ministry is also organizing training for producers of smokeless ovens and other household appliances, building on a Ministry of Agriculture initiative from 2000 that has seen approximately 200,000 beneficiaries adopt these ovens.

To support this expansion, theoretical and practical capacity-building training has been provided by both foreign and national experts, with ongoing efforts planned. Mr. Tesfay also called for greater public understanding of solar energy systems and their proper use to maximize the benefits of these renewable energy projects.

Why it mattersEritrean households and agricultural sectors are directly affected by the expansion of renewable energy, which aims to improve energy supplies and efficiency.

Eritrean Officials Conduct Seminar to Eradicate Harmful Practices

A three-day seminar in Assab gathered representatives from justice, police, and social services to address harmful practices like underage marriage and female genital mutilation.

A seminar focused on eradicating harmful practices concluded on August twenty-first in Assab, Southern Red Sea Region. The event, which ran from August nineteenth, brought together partner institutions including the Ministry of Justice, police, social service institutions, and reconciliation committees.

The seminar was organized through a collaboration between the Ministry of Labor and Social Welfare, the Ministry of Health, and the National Union of Eritrean Women branch in the region. Discussions covered women’s and children’s rights, public registration, traditional values, case resolution, legal perspectives on harmful practices, and the consequences of female genital mutilation.

The primary objective was to improve public understanding and participation in efforts to eliminate practices such as underage marriage and female genital mutilation. Ambassador Mohammed Sid Mantai, Governor of the region, urged participants to take a leading role in these eradication efforts.

Why it mattersThis seminar directly affects women and children in the Southern Red Sea Region by aiming to protect their rights and eliminate harmful practices like underage marriage and female genital mutilation.
