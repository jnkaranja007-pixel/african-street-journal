Eritrean Community in Egypt Marks Independence Struggle Anniversary

Nationals in Cairo gathered to commemorate the 65th anniversary of the armed struggle for Eritrean independence with cultural displays.

The Eritrean community residing in Egypt held a patriotic celebration in Cairo to mark the 65th anniversary of the commencement of the armed struggle for Eritrean independence. The event, which took place on September 1, featured cultural programs designed to reflect the significance of Bahti Meskerem for the Eritrean people.

Mr. Aman Abdelwasi’e, the Charge d’Affaires at the Eritrean Embassy, addressed attendees, emphasizing September 1 as a pivotal date in the nation's history. He stated that the day signifies the beginning of the armed struggle for independence and dignity, serving as a reminder for citizens to intensify their efforts in national development initiatives.

The celebration underscored the importance of the armed struggle in Eritrea's journey toward self-determination and highlighted its ongoing relevance to national progress. The gathering provided a platform for the Eritrean diaspora in Egypt to connect with their heritage and reaffirm their commitment to the country's development.

Why it mattersEritrean nationals in Egypt are reminded of their country's history and encouraged to contribute to national development programs.
