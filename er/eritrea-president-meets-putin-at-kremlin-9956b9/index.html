Eritrea president meets Putin at Kremlin

Russian President Vladimir Putin and Eritrean President Isaias Afwerki discussed trade and economic cooperation in Moscow.

Source: Eritrea Focus · 2026-11-10 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Eritrean President Isaias Afwerki met Russian President Vladimir Putin at the Kremlin on the sidelines of the 2026 Russia-Africa Summit. The leaders discussed bilateral relations and ways to increase trade between their countries. Putin highlighted potential sectors for cooperation including energy, mining, infrastructure, agriculture, and fisheries.

Putin stated that Russia is providing assistance to enhance Eritrea's food security. He also expressed gratitude for Eritrea's support of Russian initiatives at the United Nations and other multilateral forums. Afwerki observed Putin's strength and active engagement in summit issues, finding his clarifications inspiring.

The meeting occurred in October 2026. Several Russian officials, including Deputy Prime Ministers Andrei Nikitin and Alexander Novak, attended the discussions. Both leaders acknowledged shared positions on many international issues.
