Expert links Sampa to Facebook page in ECZ polling case

A digital forensics manager told the Lusaka Magistrate's Court that the former Matero MP ran a page alleging an illegal polling station.

Source: Lusaka Times · 2026-11-20 · 1 min read

A digital forensics expert told the Lusaka Magistrate's Court that former Matero Member of Parliament Miles Sampa was responsible for a Facebook page alleging that the Electoral Commission of Zambia (ECZ) had set up an illegal polling station in Chawama Constituency.

Jeff Sitali, manager for digital forensics at the Zambia Information and Communications Technology Authority, gave the evidence, linking Sampa to the page in the case over the allegation against the commission.

The evidence was heard in the Lusaka court.

Why it mattersSampa is now linked by expert testimony to a Facebook page at the centre of a case over an allegation against Zambia's electoral commission.
