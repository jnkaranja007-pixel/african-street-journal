Zambia ends manual passport applications nationwide

The Home Affairs Ministry says all new applications, renewals and related services must now go through its online system.

Sources: Mwebantu and News Diggers · 2026-11-19 · 1 min read

Zambia’s Ministry of Home Affairs and Internal Security has ended manual passport applications nationwide, with immediate effect. New applications, renewals and related services must now be submitted through the Online Passport Application System.

The ministry said applicants can submit forms and track their status online, reducing the need for frequent visits to passport offices. It said the change would also reduce direct contact between applicants and staff, limiting opportunities for corrupt practices.

The system launched in December 2025, and manual and online applications had run alongside each other during the transition. Passport office officers and staff at designated service points will continue to help applicants access and complete online forms.

Why it mattersApplicants who need help moving to online forms can seek assistance from passport office officers and designated service points.
