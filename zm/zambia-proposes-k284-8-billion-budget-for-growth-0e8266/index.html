Zambia proposes K284.8 billion budget for growth

The 2027 budget prioritises production and infrastructure, aiming for increased electricity, agriculture, mining, and tourism.

Sources: Mwebantu and Lusaka Times · 2026-11-08 · 1 min read

Zambia's proposed K284.8 billion budget for 2027 targets accelerated growth by focusing on production and infrastructure expansion. Minister of Finance Situmbeko Musokotwane presented the budget, themed ‘Grow Zambia’, to Parliament, shifting focus from economic stabilisation. Key areas include increased electricity generation, agricultural output, mining production, and tourism arrivals.

Households receiving Social Cash Transfers are set to be the clearest winners, with the programme's allocation rising to K10.4 billion, expanding coverage to over 1.6 million households. Public services like education and health receive K68.5 billion, about 24 percent of the budget. School feeding allocations double to K1 billion, and K6.4 billion is earmarked for drugs and medical supplies.

Farmers may benefit from subsidised inputs and improved feeder roads, while manufacturers of ceramic tiles and sanitary products could see gains from adjusted customs duties and VAT exemptions. Tourism operators may benefit from duty suspensions on safari vehicles and hospitality equipment. Artisanal gold miners will see their mineral royalty fall from 6 to 3 percent when selling to approved aggregators.

Why it mattersZambians will experience varying benefits from the 2027 budget depending on their use of public services, market access, and tax obligations.
