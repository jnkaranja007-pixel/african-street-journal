LAZ vows legal action as youths plan march to scrap term limits

Over 60,000 young people from 10 provinces plan to march in Lusaka on November 25 to demand the removal of presidential term limits.

Sources: Lusaka Times and Mwebantu · 2026-11-17 · 1 min read

The Law Association of Zambia, a body of legal professionals, has restated its support for presidential term limits and warned it may take legal action to defend Article 106(3) of the Constitution. Its statement, dated November 16, comes while petitions to remove the two-term limit are before the National Assembly.

Boas Kanyama, vice chairperson of the Zambia Youth Organisation Consortium, said 63,151 youths from all 10 provinces have confirmed they will march in Lusaka on November 25. The march will start at Northmead Bus Station and end at Parliament Buildings.

The Association welcomed an assurance on the constitutional provisions governing the presidency, according to its statement.

Why it mattersPetitions to change the two-term limit now before lawmakers are the target, and Parliament Buildings is where the youths will end their march.
