Lusaka youths walk 480km for Grow Zambia agenda

Five young people are walking for 12 days to raise awareness about national development.

Source: Lusaka Times · 2026-11-05 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Five young people from Lusaka, three males and two females, have started a 480 km journey called the “Youth Walk.” They are championing the Grow Zambia Agenda and aim to raise awareness about the importance of citizen involvement in national development.

The walk is expected to last 12 days. The youths are walking to encourage everyone to participate in the country's development.

This initiative highlights young people's engagement with the Grow Zambia Agenda.
