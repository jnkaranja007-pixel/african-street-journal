Zambia reaffirms livelihood support for vulnerable households through its Food Security...

The government says the programme offers sustainable livelihood opportunities to improve household food security across the country.

Source: Lusaka Times · 2026-11-22 · 1 min read

Zambia's government has reaffirmed support for vulnerable but viable households through the Food Security Pack (FSP), a programme intended to provide sustainable livelihood opportunities and improve household food security. The Ministry of Community Development and Social Services is delivering the programme.

Micah Simululwe, Lusaka Province's senior community development officer, said the FSP includes an Alternative Livelihood Initiative. The announcement identifies vulnerable households as the intended beneficiaries but gives no number of households, funding amount or details of the initiative's activities.

The government described the programme as part of its commitment to improving food security in Zambia. No further implementation dates or next steps were provided.

Why it mattersHouseholds judged vulnerable but viable are the ones to receive the livelihood support, which the government says is meant to strengthen their food security.
