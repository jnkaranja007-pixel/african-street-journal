Zambia police reject clearance for two Lusaka term-limit marches

The planned processions, one for and one against removing presidential term limits, were set for November 25, 2026.

Sources: Lusaka Times and Mwebantu · 2026-11-18 · 1 min read

Zambia Police Service declined to clear two planned processions and public assemblies in Lusaka over presidential term limits and proposed constitutional changes. The groups had scheduled them for November 25, 2026.

Police said in a press statement that they received notifications from two consortiums of organisations intending to hold peaceful processions. The service said its decision followed a review of the notifications.

The proposed marches take opposing positions: one supports removing presidential term limits, while the other opposes the proposed constitutional changes. Zambia Youth Organisation Consortium Vice Chairperson Boas Kanyama said 63,151 youths from all 10 [the report’s statement is incomplete].

Why it mattersOrganisers of both Lusaka gatherings cannot proceed with their planned public processions on November 25 after police declined clearance.
