Zambian minister declines to say whether Hichilema backs ending term limit

Rival petitions on Article 106(3) of the Constitution are now before the National Assembly, and one quotes the president's own "third term" remarks.

Source: Lusaka Times · 2026-11-15 · 1 min read

Justice Minister Jack Mwiimbu declined to say whether President Hakainde Hichilema would support scrapping the presidential term limit if Parliament approved it. Rival petitions on Article 106(3) of the Constitution are now before the National Assembly.

One of the petitions quotes the president's own past words on a "third term". Mwiimbu's refusal to take a position leaves open where the government stands on the amendment before lawmakers.

The petitions will be considered by the National Assembly, which will decide whether the constitutional provision is changed.

Why it mattersIf lawmakers approve the change, the question of whether the president would back it will shape how Zambians judge the amendment.
