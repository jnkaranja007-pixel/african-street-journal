Bank of Zambia holds policy rate at 10.75%

Annual inflation edged up to 6.4% in November, while actual and projected inflation remain within the bank’s 6 to 8% target band.

Sources: Lusaka Times and Mwebantu · 2026-11-28 · 1 min read

The Bank of Zambia kept its Monetary Policy Rate at 10.75% after annual inflation rose to 6.4% in November, from 6.3% in October. The decision leaves the central bank’s benchmark unchanged as consumer-price growth increased for a second month.

Governor Denny Kalyalya said the bank’s actual and projected inflation remain within its 6 to 8% target band. The Monetary Policy Committee reached the decision after meeting on November 25 and 26 to review recent economic conditions.

The bank disclosed the decision on November 27. It did not announce a change to the rate.

Why it mattersBorrowers, savers and businesses continue to face a benchmark of 10.75% while inflation stands at 6.4%.
