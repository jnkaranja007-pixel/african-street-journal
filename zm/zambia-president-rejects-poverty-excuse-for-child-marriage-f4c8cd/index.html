Zambia president rejects poverty excuse for child marriage

Hakainde Hichilema also warned of action against adult-led "sex parties" involving minors in Lusaka.

Sources: Lusaka Times and Mwebantu · 2026-11-28 · 1 min read

President Hakainde Hichilema stated that poverty is not a valid excuse for child and early marriages, emphasizing that the government has established sufficient empowerment programmes for citizens. Speaking as the African Union Champion on Ending Child Marriage, he expressed concern over perpetrators who continue these practices despite available support.

Hichilema further announced that the government will take action against so-called "sex parties" in Lusaka, where adults allegedly engage with underage children. He described such conduct as delinquent and immoral, stating that as a parent himself, he would not tolerate seeing children involved in these activities.

The president noted receiving reports about these gatherings, reinforcing his stance that existing state programmes provide adequate tools to prevent exploitation. His remarks signal a direct challenge to communities using economic hardship to justify violations of national laws protecting minors.

Why it mattersLocal authorities and families must now adhere to stricter enforcement against child marriage and exploitative gatherings, regardless of economic status.
