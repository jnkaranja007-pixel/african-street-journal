ZCSA intensifies mobile money scam awareness

Scammers use advanced social engineering tactics to bypass security measures and trap users.

Source: Lusaka Times · 2026-11-02 · 1 min read

The Zambia Cyber Security Agency (ZCSA) is increasing public awareness campaigns to combat sophisticated mobile money scams and account hacking. Scammers are employing advanced social engineering tactics that bypass existing safety measures and breach security systems.

ZCSA Director of Research, Education and Awareness, Edgar Mlauzi, stated that these tactics are making it increasingly difficult to protect users. The agency is working to educate the public on these evolving threats.

The ZCSA's efforts aim to equip citizens with knowledge to identify and avoid these scams. Further details on specific scam methods and protective measures are expected from the agency.

Why it mattersZambians using mobile money services are at risk of losing funds due to advanced scam tactics.
