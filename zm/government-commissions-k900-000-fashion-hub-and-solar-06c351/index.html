Government commissions K900,000 fashion hub and solar system at Chilanga school

Mount Makulu Secondary School received a furnished Fashion and Fabric Hub and a 16-kilowatt solar system, with foundation backing.

Source: Lusaka Times · 2026-11-22 · 1 min read

The Zambian government has commissioned a Fashion and Fabric Hub and a 16-kilowatt solar system at Mount Makulu Secondary School in Chilanga district. The two facilities are valued at over K900,000.

The hub was rehabilitated and fully furnished with financial support from the Elsa Milar Foundation, working through its implementing partners, the African Education Programme. The commissioning brings the school a dedicated facility for fashion and fabric alongside the solar installation.

The report identifies no further commissioning details or next steps.
