Lusaka police and council detain 54 over 'Chi Cup' drinking

Police and council officers swept the area near two Lusaka colleges, catching buyers and drinkers of the illicit spirit known as 'Chi Cup'.

Source: Mwebantu · 2026-11-15 · 1 min read

54 people were arrested in Lusaka for buying and drinking alcohol popularly known as “Chi Cup”, in an operation near the National Institute of Public Administration and Evelyn Hone College. The Lusaka City Council said the Zambia Police Service joined it in the NIPA area at about 10:20 p.m. on Friday.

20 of those detained were found drinking the spirit, the council said. Its stated aim was to curb consumption of such drinks in public spaces, particularly close to learning institutions.

The council did not say what action would follow against those arrested, but said it would keep enforcing public order rules.

Why it mattersAnyone buying or selling Chi Cup near Lusaka's colleges now faces the same arrests the council has begun enforcing.
