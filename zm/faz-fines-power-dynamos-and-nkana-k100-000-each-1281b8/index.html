FAZ fines Power Dynamos and Nkana K100,000 each

Football Association of Zambia penalizes clubs for political chants at matches.

Source: Mwebantu · 2026-11-02 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

The Football Association of Zambia (FAZ) has fined Power Dynamos and Nkana Football Club K100,000 each for allowing political slogans at match venues. The disciplinary committee released separate rulings on Saturday, sanctioning the clubs for breaching FAZ's constitution.

Power Dynamos was fined for supporters chanting political slogans during their MTN Super League match against Nchanga Rangers FC at Arthur Davies Stadium on October 11, 2026. Nkana faced similar charges for political chants by supporters during their match against Makeni All Stars FC at Nkana Stadium on October 10, 2026. FAZ stated this conduct is inconsistent with sportsmanship and neutrality.

Both clubs were asked to show cause for their supporters' actions. Power Dynamos acknowledged responsibility for supporter behaviour, describing it as an isolated incident. Nkana expressed regret and outlined remedial measures in their response to FAZ.

Why it mattersFootball clubs and their supporters in Zambia face financial penalties and stadium bans for political chants during matches.
