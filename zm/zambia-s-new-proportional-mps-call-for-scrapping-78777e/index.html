Zambia's new proportional MPs call for scrapping presidential term limits

In their maiden parliamentary speeches, some of the newly seated legislators urged the removal of presidential term limits, a position Lusaka Times says raises doubts.

Source: Lusaka Times · 2026-11-26 · 1 min read

Some of Zambia's new proportional representation MPs called for removing presidential term limits in their maiden speeches to parliament, according to Lusaka Times. Their seats are intended to amplify the voices of women, young people and persons with disabilities.

The outlet said the speeches raised questions about the legislators' understanding of their constitutional mandate, and argued that they should instead lead efforts to hold the executive to account.

The report does not name the MPs or say whether a proposal to change the term limits has been introduced.

Why it mattersThe MPs' speeches put their own accountability to the groups they were seated to represent under scrutiny.
