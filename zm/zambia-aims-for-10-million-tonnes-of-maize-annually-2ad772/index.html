Zambia aims for 10 million tonnes of maize annually

President Hichilema's Grow Zambia plan targets doubling current production for food security and export earnings.

Source: Zambia Daily Mail · 2026-11-08 · 1 min read

President Hakainde Hichilema unveiled the Grow Zambia initiative on September 1, 2026, setting ambitious targets for 2031, with a primary focus on maize production.

The plan aims to increase annual maize output to 10 million tonnes, a significant jump from the current 3-4 million tonnes produced in good years, with the last season reaching a record 5.1 million metric tonnes.

This increased production is intended to ensure food security, reduce reliance on imports, and generate substantial export revenue, potentially earning between US$840 million and US$1.12 billion annually from maize exports.

Why it mattersZambians will have more consistent access to affordable maize meal and the nation will earn significant foreign currency from exports.
