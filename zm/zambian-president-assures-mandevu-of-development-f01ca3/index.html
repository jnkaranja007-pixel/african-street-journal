Zambian president assures Mandevu of development

President Hakainde Hichilema pledged to address water, roads, and security challenges in Mandevu Constituency.

Source: Zambia Daily Mail · 2026-11-06 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

President Hakainde Hichilema thanked Zambians for re-electing him and the United Party for National Development (UPND) on August 13. Speaking at a rally in Mandevu Constituency, Lusaka, he assured residents that the government will tackle the area's challenges, focusing on water supply, road infrastructure, and security. He also introduced Adrian Banda, the new Member of Parliament for Mandevu.

The President stated that his administration's post-election focus is delivering development nationwide, with water, roads, and security identified as key concerns for Mandevu residents. He emphasized water as a basic necessity requiring urgent attention and highlighted how improved roads would boost mobility and economic activity. President Hichilema also directed security forces to address insecurity caused by individuals referred to as 'junkies'.

He suggested that youths involved in criminal activities should be taken through skills training programmes to become productive citizens, noting that the enhanced Constituency Development Fund (CDF) offers opportunities for vocational training. The President urged residents to abandon violence and embrace development, promoting freedom of movement without fear of attack.
