Zambia elected to lead global green growth bodies

The nation will hold vice-president roles in the assembly and council of the Global Green Growth Institute for 2027-2028.

Source: Zambia Daily Mail · 2026-11-04 · 2 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Zambia has secured two leadership roles within the Global Green Growth Institute (GGGI), becoming vice-president of the assembly and vice-chairperson of the council for the 2027–2028 term. These appointments elevate Zambia's influence in guiding the intergovernmental organisation, which aids developing nations in transitioning to sustainable, low-carbon economies. Minister of Green Economy and Environment Gilbert Siame stated that these positions will enhance Zambia's voice in the governance of the 55-member organisation.

The elections took place last Friday in Seoul, South Korea, during the 15th session of the GGGI Assembly and the 19th session of the council. Minister Siame explained that in these capacities, Zambia will assist the President of the assembly in directing the supreme governing body and support the Council chairperson in overseeing strategy, budgets, and performance. He noted that these roles offer a platform to advance international cooperation on sustainable development, climate action, and green investment.

Minister Siame outlined three priority areas for cooperation: investment in clean energy, electric mobility, and battery value chains. He urged partners with technology and capital to invest in Zambia's green economy and called for continued GGGI support in developing bankable projects, particularly in electric mobility, climate-resilient agriculture, and carbon credit generation. Zambia intends to use these positions to promote access to climate finance, technology transfer, and renewable energy investment.

Why it mattersZambia's new leadership roles in the Global Green Growth Institute will allow it to promote international cooperation on sustainable development, climate action, and green investment.
