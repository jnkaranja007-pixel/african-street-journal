President launches free spectacles for 100,000 Zambians

The HH Vision 100,000 programme aims to improve eye care access for vulnerable citizens including learners and the elderly.

Sources: Zambia Daily Mail and News Diggers · 2026-11-08 · 1 min read

President Hakainde Hichilema launched HH Vision 100,000, a programme that will distribute free spectacles to 100,000 vulnerable Zambians. The initiative targets learners, the elderly, military personnel, and people living with albinism. The programme was announced during a virtual address to the Zambia Ophthalmological Society’s annual conference.

President Hichilema stated the initiative is rooted in social justice, noting that over six million Zambians need spectacles. He said the programme has been piloted in schools like New Jack Secondary and Kabanana Secondary, and at the military base in Mikango. The pilot showed improved learning and work performance when vision is corrected.

The President observed that one in five children struggle to see the blackboard and nearly one in three adults have poor vision. Vision problems have long constrained educational opportunities, livelihoods, and national development, according to the President.
