Zambian commentary says president holds too much appointment power

A Lusaka Times commentary examines the extent of presidential appointment powers under the Constitution and various Acts of Parliament.

Source: Lusaka Times · 2026-11-20 · 1 min read

A Lusaka Times commentary argues that the Constitution of Zambia gives the president too much power. The author says the piece looks at the extent of presidential appointment powers under the current Constitution.

The author examines those powers under the Constitution and various Acts of Parliament, a subject the author describes as particularly important. The commentary does not identify a particular appointment or propose a specific change to the rules.

The piece is an examination of the rules, not a report on any decision taken under them.

Why it mattersReaders in Zambia and abroad can see how the Constitution and Acts of Parliament shape appointment authority at the top of government.
