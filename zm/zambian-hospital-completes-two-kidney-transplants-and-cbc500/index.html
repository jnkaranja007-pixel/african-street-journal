Zambian hospital completes two kidney transplants and targets 50 a year

A wife travelled with her husband from Copperbelt Province to Lusaka to donate a kidney, while a 56-year-old man received one from his son.

Source: Mwebantu · 2026-11-20 · 1 min read

University Teaching Hospitals (UTH) Adult Hospital completed two kidney transplants in Lusaka on Nov. 17 and 18, 2026. One recipient received a kidney from his wife, who travelled with him from Copperbelt Province; the other was a 56-year-old man whose donor was his 29-year-old biological son.

The procedures lasted about seven and eight hours. Dr. Victor Mapulanga, the surgical team lead, said the hospital is targeting at least 50 kidney transplants annually. Local specialists and visiting renal surgeons from the United States worked with anaesthetists, nurses and other healthcare professionals on the operations.

The transplant programme began in 2024, and the hospital is expected to have attended to 27 patients by the end of the current renal camp. The target is part of a plan to expand local access to renal treatment and reduce the need for patients to seek specialised care abroad.

Why it mattersPatients in Zambia have a local treatment option as UTH targets at least 50 kidney transplants each year.
