Zambia's state power company says Kariba water cut will not cause deficit

The utility says its 2027 supply plans already account for the Zambezi River Authority's projected reduction in water allocation.

Sources: Lusaka Times and News Diggers · 2026-11-26 · 1 min read

The Zambian state electricity company, ZESCO Limited, says the country has enough electricity resources to sustain power supply and does not expect a national deficit in 2027. It says its plans account for a projected reduction in water allocated to Kariba Dam.

The Zambezi River Authority is set to reduce the dam's water allocation for 2027, according to the utility. It said the change had been factored into its supply plans, but gave no allocation figure or details of how the plans will meet demand.

The utility's assurance covers the national power outlook for 2027. It gave no further details in the available report.

Why it mattersThe state power company must manage its 2027 supply plans around lower projected water allocation at Kariba Dam while keeping power flowing nationwide.
