Zambian football club bars two fans from all its matches over political symbol

The supporters were filmed displaying a symbol associated with Zambia's governing United Party for National Development during a match against Nkana FC.

Source: Lusaka Times · 2026-11-23 · 1 min read

Maestro United Zambia Football Club, known as FC MUZA, has barred two supporters from attending all its matches after they displayed a political symbol at a Week 8 MTN Super League fixture against Nkana FC.

The club's action followed footage of the supporters displaying a symbol associated with the governing United Party for National Development. The report says some fans were filmed with the symbol; it does not state how long the ban lasts.

The club's ban applies to all its matches, rather than only to games against Nkana FC.

Why it mattersThe two supporters can no longer attend any match of the club under the ban.
