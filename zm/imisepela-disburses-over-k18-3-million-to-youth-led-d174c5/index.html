Imisepela disburses over K18.3 million to youth-led businesses

The digital platform says approximately 2,300 young Zambians have secured jobs since its launch in March 2026.

Source: Lusaka Times · 2026-11-26 · 1 min read

Imisepela, a digital platform for jobs and entrepreneurship, has disbursed over K18.3 million to youth-led businesses in Zambia since its official launch in March 2026, according to a press statement about the organisation’s work.

The statement said approximately 2,300 young Zambians had secured employment through the platform. More than 300, 000 users have registered seeking access to jobs and entrepreneurship, it said.

The statement was made available to the media; it gave no further details about the businesses receiving funds or the jobs secured.

Why it mattersYoung Zambians seeking work and entrepreneurship access are among more than 300, 000 registered users, while approximately 2,300 have secured employment.
