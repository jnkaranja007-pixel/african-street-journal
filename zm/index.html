Ashios Melu honoured posthumously for sporting contribution

President Hichilema awarded the late football legend during the 62nd Independence Anniversary investiture ceremony.

Zambia posthumously honoured football legend Ashios Jordan Melu for his contributions to sport and raising the nation's international profile. The award was presented 29 years after his death.

President Hakainde Hichilema bestowed the honour on Melu at State House during an investiture ceremony marking Zambia's 62nd Independence Anniversary. Melu was recognised for his outstanding service.

The ceremony, held under the theme “Zambia@62: Our Journey, Our Growth, Our Future”, also recognised other individuals for their contributions to the nation.

Zambia assures no loadshedding amid maintenance

Vice-President Mutale Nalumango stated that Zambia has enough electricity and that power outages are due to routine maintenance.

Vice-President Mutale Nalumango assured Zambians yesterday that the country has sufficient electricity to prevent a return to loadshedding. She stated that isolated power outages occurring in some areas are a result of routine maintenance work. The Vice-President dismissed concerns about potential power rationing during Vice-President’s Question Time in the National Assembly.

Mrs Nalumango also addressed the arrest of former Petauke Central Member of Parliament Emmanuel Jay Banda, stating the government would not explain the circumstances. She was responding to Sesheke East MP Romeo Kang’ombe, who questioned why civil society organisations were demanding an explanation. The Vice-President urged objective commentary on Mr Banda's case, referencing his past claims of foresight.

In other matters, Mrs Nalumango announced that President Hakainde Hichilema's election as vice president of COMESA would help Zambia expand its export markets. She also assured farmers that outstanding payments for grain supplied to the Food Reserve Agency would soon be settled, with K14.7 billion allocated in a proposed supplementary budget.

Why it mattersZambians are assured of consistent electricity supply and farmers will receive outstanding payments.

Dorcas Society gives UTH 110 wheelchairs

The donation will improve patient mobility and strengthen health care service delivery at the Lusaka hospital.

The University Teaching Hospitals (UTH) in Lusaka received 110 wheelchairs on Saturday from the Dorcas Society of the Woodlands Conference of the Seventh-day Adventist Church. The donation aims to improve patient mobility and strengthen health care service delivery.

Of the 110 wheelchairs, 50 will go to the UTH-Adult Hospital, with the remaining 60 distributed to the Women and Newborn Hospital, the Eye Hospital, the Cancer Diseases Hospital, and the Children’s Hospital. Dr. Humphrey Kunda, head of clinical care at UTH-Adult Hospital, stated the wheelchairs will ease patient movement within the facilities.

Dr. Daniel Chuunga, president of the Woodlands Conference, highlighted the importance of supporting health institutions, while Woodlands Conference Dorcas Society President Melody Mudenda reaffirmed their commitment to community service. Chief Nursing Officer Georgina Chipowe commended the partnership for strengthening patient care.

Why it mattersPatients at the University Teaching Hospitals in Lusaka will have improved mobility within the facilities.

FC Muza beat Power Dynamos to lead Stream A

Solomon Mpasela scored the only goal in the 50th minute at Nakambala Stadium.

FC Muza defeated defending champions Power Dynamos 1-0 at Nakambala Stadium in Mazabuka. The victory propelled FC Muza to the top of Stream A in the MTN Super League Transitional Season.

Solomon Mpasela scored the match's only goal in the 50th minute, securing the win for Muza. The team held on to their lead despite pressure from Power Dynamos.

FC Muza now leads Stream A with 10 points after four games played. The league continues with further matches to determine final standings.

Why it mattersFC Muza and Power Dynamos are Zambian football clubs competing in the MTN Super League.

Veteran journalist Patson Phiri dies

The Media Liaison Committee mourns the loss of Patson Phiri, a key figure in press freedom advocacy.

Veteran journalist Patson Phiri has died in Zambia. The Media Liaison Committee announced his passing and expressed condolences to his family and colleagues.

Henry Kabwe, Chairperson of the Media Liaison Committee, remembered Phiri for his significant role in forming the committee and defending press freedom. Phiri previously worked for the Zambia Daily Mail, Times of Zambia, and Mobi TV.

Phiri was also noted for his contributions to the World Press Freedom Day organizing committee. His work helped unite journalists against media bills that threatened press freedom in the country.

Why it mattersZambian journalists and the public are affected by the death of a prominent figure in press freedom.
