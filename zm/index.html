Politics · 08/21/2026

Mundubile rejects government account of Kafwaya shooting

Opposition leader Brian Mundubile claims former Transport Minister Mutotwe Kafwaya was shot inside his own bedroom. Mundubile stated in a Wednesday announcement that he witnessed the shooting first-hand and denied government allegations of a militia operating from the residence.

Why it mattersThe dispute over the circumstances of the death follows a security raid on the opposition that killed the former minister.

Politics · 08/21/2026

Mundubile challenges presidential poll results in court

Opposition leader Brian Mundubile is challenging the results of the presidential election in court. Mundubile alleges his campaign documented serious irregularities in the counting, transmission, and declaration of results.

Why it mattersThe legal challenge contests the mandate of President-elect Hakainde Hichilema following the August 13 general elections.

Politics · 08/21/2026

UN urges Zambia to end arbitrary arrests of opposition

UN High Commissioner Volker Türk called on Zambia to stop arbitrary arrests and uphold the rights of detainees. The statement follows reports of opposition figures being detained before and after Thursday's election.

Why it mattersThe UN intervention seeks to protect the legal rights of political figures detained during the election cycle.

FRA buys 680,000MT of maize for K3.4bn

The Food Reserve Agency purchased over 680,000 metric tons of maize. The agency paid farmers K3.4 billion for the crop.

Why it mattersThe procurement provides direct liquidity to farmers while increasing national grain reserves.

News · 08/21/2026

Police investigate death of 25-year-old woman

Zambia Police are investigating the death of 25-year-old Lisa Mutambo. Deputy Police Public Relations Officer Chipo Kaitisha said the woman was brought to the hospital dead on August 20 with no physical injuries.

Why it mattersThe investigation aims to determine the cause of death for a citizen who showed no outward signs of trauma.
