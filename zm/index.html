Zambia inaugurates President Hichilema for second term

The nation prepares for President Hakainde Hichilema's second five-year term inauguration on September 1, 2026, with a public holiday declared.

President Hakainde Hichilema will begin his second five-year term on September 1, 2026, with an inauguration ceremony. The Zambian government has declared this date a public holiday to mark the swearing-in of the President-Elect and Vice-President-elect. The ceremony is scheduled to take place at National Heroes Stadium in Lusaka.

Ministry of Information and Media Director Spokesperson, Henry Kapata, stated that the inauguration follows a first term during which Zambia addressed significant challenges. These included debt restructuring, high inflation, energy shortages, and a weak investor climate. The country is now set to reflect on its progress and future direction under Hichilema's continued leadership.

The announcement of the public holiday, signed by Secretary to the Cabinet Patrick K. Kangwa, follows an earlier notice. This event signifies a formal transition and continuation of presidential leadership in Zambia, with national attention focused on the stadium ceremony and the implications for the country's ongoing development agenda.

Why it mattersThe inauguration of President Hichilema for his second term affects the nation's political leadership and its approach to economic challenges.

Opposition Trio Charged With Treason, Moved to Maximum Security

Opposition leader Brian Mundubile, his running mate Makebi Zulu, and businessman Harry Findlay were transferred to Mukobeko Maximum Security Facility.

Opposition leader Brian Mundubile, his running mate Makebi Zulu, and businessman Harry Findlay were flown under armed guard to Mukobeko Maximum Security Facility in Kabwe on Saturday. The trio were charged with treason alongside sixteen others. They were airlifted aboard a Zambia Police Service helicopter between 05:00 and 06:00 hours.

Police have dismissed claims circulating on social media that Central Police Station was sealed following investigations into alleged treason involving Mundubile and Zulu. Police spokesperson Godfrey Chilabi stated that reports of lawyers being denied access to the two suspects are false. Mundubile had previously gone into hiding at a United Nations building in Zambia's capital.

The transfer to Mukobeko, a maximum security facility, signifies a serious escalation in the treason charges. The involvement of a businessman alongside political figures suggests a broad scope to the investigation. The police have maintained that their operations are transparent and have refuted claims of restricted access for legal representation.

Why it mattersOpposition leader Brian Mundubile, his running mate Makebi Zulu, and businessman Harry Findlay are affected by treason charges and their transfer to a maximum security prison.

Lusaka Motor Show Opens with Professional Insurance Corporation Zambia

Zambia’s largest automotive exhibition, powered by Professional Insurance Corporation Zambia, features cars, entertainment, and live music.

The 16th Lusaka Motor Show officially opened its doors on Friday, August 28, 2026, at the Lusaka Polo Club. The event, which runs through Sunday, August 30, is powered by Professional Insurance Corporation Zambia (PICZ) and features Sasol Techno Oil as its official partner. This showcase aims to bring together a variety of vehicles, mobility solutions, live music, and entertainment for attendees.

The show offers a weekend-long experience designed to blend automotive displays with entertainment. Visitors can expect to see a range of cars and experience what is described as the energy of the event, where mobility intersects with music and entertainment. The organizers are encouraging attendance to experience the showcase.

The Lusaka Motor Show is described as Zambia’s biggest mobility showcase, promising a vibrant atmosphere. The event schedule includes specific timings for Friday and Sunday, from 09:00 to 17:00, while Saturday extends until 20:00, featuring a sundowner event with live entertainment. Tickets are available for purchase.

Why it mattersThe Lusaka Motor Show, powered by Professional Insurance Corporation Zambia, offers a platform for businesses and entertainment, potentially attracting investment and consumer spending.

Government Pledges Continued Support for Small-Scale Farmers

The Ministry of Local Government and Rural Development reaffirms its commitment to an agriculture sector vital for rural development and food security.

Government has reiterated its dedication to assisting small-scale farmers, recognizing agriculture's crucial role in fostering rural development, ensuring food security, and promoting economic empowerment. Gabriel Pollen, Permanent Secretary for the Ministry of Local Government and Rural Development, emphasized the sector's importance.

Pollen stated that the government places significant value on the agriculture sector due to its substantial contributions to enhancing the quality of life and stimulating progress within rural communities across the nation. This support aims to bolster the livelihoods of those directly involved in farming.

The commitment underscores the administration's focus on strengthening the backbone of rural economies. By prioritizing small-scale farmers, the government seeks to ensure sustained agricultural output and broader economic benefits for the country.

Why it mattersSmall-scale farmers are directly affected as the government reaffirms its commitment to supporting their operations, which are critical for rural development and food security.

Technology Boosts Zambia's Tourism Storytelling

Secretary to the Cabinet Patrick Kangwa stated that technology can enhance visitor experiences and promote cultural heritage.

Zambia's tourism sector can be promoted through embracing technology and innovation, according to Secretary to the Cabinet Patrick Kangwa. He stated that these advancements can help find new and engaging ways to tell the country’s story to a wider audience.

Kangwa highlighted that integrating technology into tourism can significantly improve the experiences of visitors. This approach is expected to better showcase Zambia’s rich cultural heritage and ultimately contribute to job creation and broader economic development within the nation.

The push for technological integration aims to create more compelling narratives around Zambia's attractions. By leveraging digital tools, the country seeks to attract more tourists, foster cultural understanding, and stimulate economic growth through a revitalized tourism industry.

Why it mattersThe tourism sector, which contributes to economic development and job creation, can be promoted through technology.
