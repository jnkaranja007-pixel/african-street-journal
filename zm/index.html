Sport · 2026-10-22

Copper Princesses lose to Venezuela on late goal

Zambia's U-17 Women's National Team conceded a dramatic winner in stoppage time, falling 2-1 to Venezuela in the FIFA U-17 Women's World Cup.

The Copper Princesses suffered a second consecutive defeat at the FIFA U-17 Women's World Cup in Morocco, losing 2-1 to Venezuela after a last-gasp goal. Andrea Suarez scored on the final kick of the game to secure the victory for a 10-player Venezuelan side in the Group D match. Zambia had taken an early lead through Precious Mwewa, who tapped in a cross from Grace Phiri following a pass from Taonga Mubanga.

Venezuela equalized when Orliany Duran blocked an attempted clearance and scored. The match took a dramatic turn in stoppage time when Venezuela's goalkeeper Valeria Rebanales was sent off, forcing a defender to take her place. Suarez then found the net in the dying moments to seal the win.

This loss places Zambia at the bottom of Group D with no points, having previously lost their opening match 5-0 to Japan. Japan currently leads the group with six points after defeating France. Zambia Coach Carol Kanyemba has not yet commented on the team's performance.

Zambia president to join SADC virtual summit

President Hichilema will attend an extraordinary summit to discuss treaty positions and regional security cooperation.

President Hakainde Hichilema is set to participate in a virtual Extraordinary Summit of the Southern African Development Community (SADC) Heads of State and Government today. The summit aims to address outstanding matters, including the recruitment for treaty positions and the election of the incoming Chairperson of the SADC Organ on Politics, Defence and Security Cooperation.

The meeting follows an Extraordinary Meeting of the SADC Council of Ministers, also held virtually today. Minister of Foreign Affairs and International Cooperation, Mulambo Haimbe, stated that President Hichilema's attendance signifies Zambia's commitment to strengthening the SADC Secretariat and advancing regional integration.

Haimbe further emphasized that Zambia's participation underscores its dedication to promoting peace, security, and stability among member states. The discussions are expected to focus on key decisions that will bolster the effectiveness of the regional body.

Chinsali council seeks business input for 2027 budget

Local authorities met with traders to align proposed development projects with district priorities for the upcoming fiscal year.

Chinsali Municipal Council has begun consulting its business community on the 2027 Output-Based Budget. The aim is to ensure that planned projects and programs align with the district's development priorities. Council Public Relations Manager Lolavye Simukoko stated that this engagement offers a platform for stakeholders to contribute ideas and suggest projects for the 2027 financial year.

The meetings are part of the council's commitment to fostering public participation in the budgeting process. Presentations covered services offered by the local authority, the performance of the 2026 budget, and the role of the Council’s Integrity Committee. Simukoko emphasized that the consultations also aim to ensure proposed projects align with the Chinsali Integrated Development Plan, the Eighth National Development Plan, and the national Grow Zambia Agenda.

During the discussions, business representatives proposed several initiatives. These included the construction of an ablution block and fencing for the Council sports facility, and the placement of waste bins in the Central Business District to improve sanitation. Other suggestions involved building a transit bus station at Nambuluma Junction, constructing additional council housing, and renovating the community hall at the old market.

Kaputa health director urges cholera vigilance

Jonathan Bwalya calls for public cooperation in preventing the spread of the waterborne disease.

Kaputa District Health Director Jonathan Bwalya has urged residents to remain vigilant against cholera and take extra precautions to prevent its spread. He stressed the importance of observing preventive measures and staying alert to the risks posed by the waterborne disease, which can transmit through contaminated water and food. Bwalya confirmed his medical team is actively working on the ground to stop cholera from reaching Kaputa District.

The health director emphasized that public cooperation is crucial for disease prevention, urging residents to prioritize personal hygiene and sanitation. He encouraged practices such as proper hand washing, drinking safe water, consuming properly handled food, and maintaining clean surroundings to protect communities. Residents experiencing symptoms like acute watery diarrhea and vomiting should seek immediate medical attention, as these may indicate cholera.

Bwalya’s call for vigilance comes as health authorities globally highlight the importance of preventive measures against cholera outbreaks. The emphasis on these actions is vital in safeguarding communities from potential epidemics, especially following reports of ongoing cholera outbreaks in various regions.

Why it mattersKaputa residents are affected by the risk of contracting cholera and are urged to take preventative measures.

Nkana beat Nchanga Rangers 2-0 to lead Super League

Coach Elijah Tana’s side secured a third consecutive victory, maintaining their perfect start to the transitional Super League season.

Nkana claimed a 2-0 victory over Nchanga Rangers at Nkana Stadium on Sunday, extending their flawless start to the transitional Super League season. The win places Nkana at the top of Stream A with a maximum nine points from three games. Striker Lazarous Kambole opened the scoring with a goal set up by Kedrick Mumba, while Patrick Gondwe added the second after a rebound from Captain Harrison Chisala’s shot.

Coach Elijah Tana has successfully blended experienced players like Kambole and Chisala with younger, energetic talent, creating a balanced team that has delivered consistent results. Despite the perfect record, Tana acknowledged the need to avoid complacency, stating his team will continue to push until the end of the competitive, short league season. Nkana has a history of strong starts followed by late-season fades, a pattern they aim to break this year.

The loss puts Nchanga Rangers under significant pressure, with Assistant Coach Israel Mwanza admitting to three straight defeats and a worrying habit of conceding early goals. Mwanza maintains faith in the team's system, but the results business of football suggests that conceding early setbacks undermines any tactical approach.

Why it mattersFootball fans in Zambia are affected by Nkana's strong performance as it sets a high standard for competition in the Super League.
