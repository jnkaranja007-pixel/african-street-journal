Zambia eyes K284.8 billion budget financed domestically

Finance Minister Situmbeko Musokotwane presented the 2027 budget with 82.1 percent from local revenue.

Finance Minister Situmbeko Musokotwane presented Zambia's K284.8 billion national budget for 2027, aiming for 82.1 percent domestic revenue financing.

The proposed budget, representing 28.4 percent of GDP, will draw K41.9 billion from borrowing and K9.2 billion from grants, with the minister stating, “There are no donors to pay, we must pay ourselves.”

Revenue measures are expected to raise an additional K10.8 billion, balancing support for productive activities with fair contributions from individuals and businesses.

Why it mattersZambians will contribute more through domestic revenue to finance the national budget.

President launches free spectacles for 100,000 Zambians

The HH Vision 100,000 programme aims to improve eye care access for vulnerable citizens including learners and the elderly.

President Hakainde Hichilema launched HH Vision 100,000, a programme that will distribute free spectacles to 100,000 vulnerable Zambians. The initiative targets learners, the elderly, military personnel, and people living with albinism. The programme was announced during a virtual address to the Zambia Ophthalmological Society’s annual conference.

President Hichilema stated the initiative is rooted in social justice, noting that over six million Zambians need spectacles. He said the programme has been piloted in schools like New Jack Secondary and Kabanana Secondary, and at the military base in Mikango. The pilot showed improved learning and work performance when vision is corrected.

The President observed that one in five children struggle to see the blackboard and nearly one in three adults have poor vision. Vision problems have long constrained educational opportunities, livelihoods, and national development, according to the President.

Agriculture · 2026-11-08

Zambia aims for 10 million tonnes of maize annually

President Hichilema's Grow Zambia plan targets doubling current production for food security and export earnings.

President Hakainde Hichilema unveiled the Grow Zambia initiative on September 1, 2026, setting ambitious targets for 2031, with a primary focus on maize production.

The plan aims to increase annual maize output to 10 million tonnes, a significant jump from the current 3-4 million tonnes produced in good years, with the last season reaching a record 5.1 million metric tonnes.

This increased production is intended to ensure food security, reduce reliance on imports, and generate substantial export revenue, potentially earning between US$840 million and US$1.12 billion annually from maize exports.

Why it mattersZambians will have more consistent access to affordable maize meal and the nation will earn significant foreign currency from exports.

Business · 2026-11-08

Zambia proposes K284.8 billion budget for growth

The 2027 budget prioritises production and infrastructure, aiming for increased electricity, agriculture, mining, and tourism.

Zambia's proposed K284.8 billion budget for 2027 targets accelerated growth by focusing on production and infrastructure expansion. Minister of Finance Situmbeko Musokotwane presented the budget, themed ‘Grow Zambia’, to Parliament, shifting focus from economic stabilisation. Key areas include increased electricity generation, agricultural output, mining production, and tourism arrivals.

Households receiving Social Cash Transfers are set to be the clearest winners, with the programme's allocation rising to K10.4 billion, expanding coverage to over 1.6 million households. Public services like education and health receive K68.5 billion, about 24 percent of the budget. School feeding allocations double to K1 billion, and K6.4 billion is earmarked for drugs and medical supplies.

Farmers may benefit from subsidised inputs and improved feeder roads, while manufacturers of ceramic tiles and sanitary products could see gains from adjusted customs duties and VAT exemptions. Tourism operators may benefit from duty suspensions on safari vehicles and hospitality equipment. Artisanal gold miners will see their mineral royalty fall from 6 to 3 percent when selling to approved aggregators.

Why it mattersZambians will experience varying benefits from the 2027 budget depending on their use of public services, market access, and tax obligations.

Sport · 2026-11-08

Joseph Sitali joins Kitwe Utd as assistant coach

The former Super League star returns to his boyhood club after a 20-year absence to aid the transitional National League season.

Former Super League player Joseph Sitali has joined Kitwe United as an assistant coach, returning to his boyhood club after a 20-year absence. Kitwe United, also known as Chingalika, appointed Sitali to support interim head coach Chisala Mwandama for the 2026 transitional National League season.

Sitali, who began his playing career at Kitwe United before moving to other clubs, will now contribute his experience from the sidelines. The club aims to leverage his past success and knowledge of the game in the upcoming season.

Sitali returns to Garden Park, the venue where he first played. His new role begins as the club prepares for the challenges of the National League.
