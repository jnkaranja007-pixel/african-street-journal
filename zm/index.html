Lions Club donates wheelchairs to UTH

The Lions Club of Sunningdale provided 10 wheelchairs and kitchen supplies to the University Teaching Hospital's emergency ward.

The Lions Club of Sunningdale donated 10 wheelchairs, kitchenware, and cleaning supplies to the University Teaching Hospital (UTH) Emergency Ward on October 23, 2026.

UTH Senior Medical Superintendent Charles Mbewe stated the donation will improve patient mobility and access to emergency care, thanking the Lions Club for supporting government healthcare efforts.

Lions Club President Jonathan Maliki explained the club observed a shortage of equipment and supplies during visits, noting that functional wheelchairs are vital for patient safety.

Why it mattersPatients at UTH's emergency ward will receive improved mobility and care due to the donation of wheelchairs and supplies.

Hichilema urges COMESA to boost intra-regional trade

Zambian President Hakainde Hichilema challenged the 21-member bloc to move beyond commitments and adopt ambitious economic growth targets.

President Hakainde Hichilema urged the Common Market for Eastern and Southern Africa (COMESA) to move beyond commitments and adopt ambitious economic growth targets. Speaking at the 25th COMESA Heads of State and Government Summit, Hichilema called for practical steps to strengthen intra-regional trade, mobilize domestic capital, and invest collectively in production and value addition. He stated that COMESA, with nearly 700 million people, has significant opportunities in minerals and agricultural produce.

Mr. Hichilema, who is the new COMESA vice chairperson, said member states needed to be aggressive in growing their economies and overcome a reluctance to trade internally. He noted that while intra-COMESA transactions appear encouraging, more can be achieved by strengthening commercial relationships and supporting locally produced goods. The President emphasized that Africa must lead its own development, with other partners following.

He called for the removal of trade barriers, faster movement of goods, one-stop border posts, and harmonized policies to reduce the cost of doing business. Vice President Mutale Nalumango stated that President Hichilema’s election to the COMESA vice-chairmanship supports Zambia’s export-based Grow Zambia Agenda.

Why it mattersZambian President Hakainde Hichilema is pushing for increased intra-regional trade and investment within COMESA to foster economic growth and create jobs.

Finsbury Reinsurance donates K400,000 to mental health facility

Health Minister Roma Chilengi urged other companies to invest in upgrading mental health services.

Finsbury Reinsurance Limited donated over K400,000 to refurbish B Ward at Chainama Hills Hospital in Lusaka. The refurbishment aims to improve health service delivery for mental health patients and staff.

Health Minister Roma Chilengi stated that private sector corporate social responsibility is vital for strengthening healthcare. He made these remarks during the handover of the refurbished ward, challenging other companies to emulate Finsbury's contribution.

The minister commended hospital staff for their dedication under difficult conditions and highlighted the government's value for partnerships in development. This initiative follows Finsbury's annual corporate social responsibility donation.

Why it mattersZambian mental health facilities are being upgraded with private sector investment, directly affecting patient care and working conditions.

ZDA signs $122m copper deal with Rong Xing

The agreement aims to boost copper cathode production and create 700 jobs in Kalulushi.

The Zambia Development Agency (ZDA) signed an investment promotion and protection agreement with Rong Xing Investments for a US$122 million copper processing project in Kalulushi. The deal, signed yesterday, involves Rong Xing investing in a project that will process copper ore into high-grade copper cathodes and concentrates.

Rong Xing Investments, a Zambian subsidiary of Jin Xun operating in Kalulushi for 10 years, has already invested US$42 million and is currently investing US$30 million in plant III construction. ZDA director general Albert Halwampa stated the agreement strengthens investor confidence and Zambia's position as an attractive mining destination.

The expansion project will increase copper cathode production capacity from 5,500 tonnes to 10,000 tonnes annually, supporting the government's goal of three million tonnes of copper production. The investment has already created 506 jobs and will add 194 more, bringing the total workforce to 700.

Why it mattersThe investment will create 700 jobs and increase copper production capacity.

Hichilema salutes Zambia's independence heroes

President Hichilema highlighted the nation's journey of peace, unity, and progress since 1964.

President Hakainde Hichilema honored Zambia's independence heroes and heroines on October 23, 2026, ahead of the nation's 62nd anniversary.

He recalled that Zambia gained its sovereign right to self-determination on October 24, 1964, and has since pursued unity, peace, and prosperity under the motto 'One Zambia, One Nation'.

The President stated that Zambia is now focusing on its future through the Grow Zambia Agenda, aiming to increase production of maize, wheat, and copper, while also boosting electricity generation, tourism, and exports.

Why it mattersZambians are reminded of their nation's progress and future economic goals.
