Zambian Judiciary confirms no presidential election petition filed

The Judiciary announced that no formal challenge to Hakainde Hichilema’s re-election was submitted within the constitutional seven-day deadline.

Zambia’s Judiciary confirmed that no petition challenging the presidential election result was filed by the constitutional deadline of 6 p.m. on Sunday, August 24. Chief Administrator Nalishebo Imataa communicated this in a letter dated August 25 to the Secretary to the Cabinet, stating the Constitutional Court Registry had not received any such petition.

The Judiciary’s communications office also addressed an email sent to Chief Justice Dr. Mumba Malila’s private address, which contained documents described as “the people’s presidential petition.” They characterized this method of communication as “highly irregular,” indicating it did not constitute a formal filing.

This confirmation means the seven-day window for challenging the election results has closed without a legally recognized petition. The Judiciary’s statements clarify the official status of any challenges to the presidential re-election, despite public attempts to submit documents outside of established procedures.

Why it mattersThis news clarifies the stability of the presidential election outcome, affecting the political environment for all citizens and businesses in Zambia.

Zambia plans three million tonnes copper production by 2031

Zambia aims to produce three million tonnes of copper annually by 2031, a target that could boost exports and fiscal revenues.

Zambia has set an ambitious goal to increase its annual copper production to three million tonnes by 2031. This target is part of a national strategy to enhance the country's economic standing, according to News Diggers on August twenty-fifth, twenty twenty-six.

Achieving this production level could significantly strengthen Zambia's exports, leading to an expansion of its fiscal revenues. The strategy also aims to deepen local supply chains and support greater value addition within the copper industry.

While the three million tonnes by 2031 is a strategic production target, News Diggers notes that it is not yet a forecast, indicating the challenges and efforts still required to meet this goal.

Why it mattersThis strategy affects Zambian citizens by potentially increasing national revenue and strengthening the economy through copper exports and local supply chain development.

Zambia courts close, blocking election challenge deadline

Zambia's top courts were closed on Monday, preventing the opposition from filing an election petition on the final day to challenge President Hakainde Hichilema's victory.

Zambia's Supreme Court and Constitutional Court in Lusaka were closed on Monday, which authorities cited as being for security reasons. This closure coincided with the deadline for filing an election petition, drawing criticism from opposition figures, lawyers, and civil society groups.

A memorandum from the judiciary advised staff not to report for work, while police were observed inside the closed gates of both courts. Authorities stated that security assessments were ongoing, despite the timing raising concerns about the constitutional right to challenge election results.

President Hakainde Hichilema was declared the winner of the August 13 election with about 60% of the vote. Opposition leader Brian Mundubile had expressed intentions to challenge the results, but members of his camp were arrested, and he was questioned by police over alleged national security offenses.

Why it mattersThe closure of Zambia's courts on the deadline for an election challenge directly impacts the opposition's ability to legally contest the presidential election results, affecting the country's political stability.

John Deere exhibits at 16th Lusaka Motor Show 2026

John Deere will participate as an exhibitor at the 16th Lusaka Motor Show 2026, Zambia's largest mobility showcase, held from August 28th to 30th at the Lusaka Polo Club.

John Deere is confirmed as an exhibitor at the upcoming 16th Lusaka Motor Show 2026, which is scheduled for next weekend, August 28th to 30th. The event, described as Zambia’s biggest mobility showcase, will take place at the Lusaka Polo Club.

The motor show is powered by Professional Insurance and has Sasol Techno Oil as its official partner. Attendees can purchase tickets online to experience what organizers promise will be an event focused on mobility, innovation, and entertainment.

The participation of John Deere highlights the range of vehicles and equipment expected at the show, catering to various sectors. The event provides a platform for companies to display their latest offerings and engage with the public in Lusaka.

Why it mattersBusinesses involved in mobility, agriculture, and related services will benefit from the exposure and networking opportunities at Zambia's largest mobility showcase.

Ngenda Situmbeko pledges development in Chilanga South

Newly elected Chilanga South Member of Parliament Ngenda Situmbeko committed to working with the church and other stakeholders to bring tangible development to the constituency.

Ngenda Situmbeko, the recently elected Member of Parliament for Chilanga South Constituency, has promised to collaborate with the church to deliver development. Mr. Situmbeko assured the faith community of his dedication to working with them and other groups to achieve concrete progress across the constituency.

The MP's pledge was made during an event where he addressed the faith community, emphasizing his commitment to partnership. This approach aims to leverage community organizations in the effort to implement development initiatives.

The commitment highlights a strategy to involve local institutions, such as the church, in the governance and development processes within Chilanga South. This collaboration is intended to ensure that development efforts are tangible and reach various parts of the constituency.
