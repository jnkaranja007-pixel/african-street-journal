Zambia-Lobito railway advances to preferred bidders

The proposed line from Chingola to Luacano has partial African Development Bank funding, but its economic case remains the question.

Source: News Diggers · 2026-11-25 · 1 min read

The Zambia-Lobito Railway project, a proposed link between Chingola in Zambia and Luacano in Angola, has named preferred bidders for engineering, procurement and construction contracts. The announcement advances the project’s contracting process, while a central question remains whether the railway makes economic sense.

The African Development Bank has provided partial funding, according to News Diggers. Media and social media posts have praised the project as it gains momentum, but the report frames its economic viability as an open question.

The railway’s history and Zambia’s need for investment are part of the assessment. The report gives no cost estimate, expected traffic volume or construction schedule.

Why it mattersThe preferred bidders must now pursue the contracts, while the project's economic case remains under examination.
