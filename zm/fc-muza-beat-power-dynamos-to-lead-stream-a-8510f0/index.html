FC Muza beat Power Dynamos to lead Stream A

Solomon Mpasela scored the only goal in the 50th minute at Nakambala Stadium.

Source: Lusaka Times · 2026-10-26 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

FC Muza defeated defending champions Power Dynamos 1-0 at Nakambala Stadium in Mazabuka. The victory propelled FC Muza to the top of Stream A in the MTN Super League Transitional Season.

Solomon Mpasela scored the match's only goal in the 50th minute, securing the win for Muza. The team held on to their lead despite pressure from Power Dynamos.

FC Muza now leads Stream A with 10 points after four games played. The league continues with further matches to determine final standings.

Why it mattersFC Muza and Power Dynamos are Zambian football clubs competing in the MTN Super League.
