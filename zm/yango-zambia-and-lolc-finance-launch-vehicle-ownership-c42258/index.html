Yango Zambia and LOLC Finance Launch Vehicle Ownership Program

The Drive to Own initiative allows eligible drivers to finance vehicles with a low initial contribution.

Yango Zambia, a subsidiary of Yango Group, has partnered with LOLC Finance to introduce the Drive to Own initiative. This program aims to provide vehicle financing solutions for eligible partner drivers in Zambia. The initiative was launched on October 19, 2026, in Lusaka.

Under the Drive to Own program, drivers can access vehicle financing by making a minimal initial contribution of 10%. This scheme is designed to make vehicle ownership more accessible to individuals working with Yango.

More than 200 vehicles have already been financed through this new program since its inception. The partnership between Yango Zambia and LOLC Finance seeks to expand opportunities for drivers within the Yango ecosystem.

Why it mattersThe initiative affects drivers by providing a pathway to vehicle ownership through financing.
