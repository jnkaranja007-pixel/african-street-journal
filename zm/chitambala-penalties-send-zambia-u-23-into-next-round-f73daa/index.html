Chitambala penalties send Zambia U-23 into next round

Zambia overturned a 1-0 first-leg defeat to Burkina Faso, winning the shootout 3-2 after a 1-1 aggregate draw in Lusaka.

Sources: Lusaka Times and News Diggers · 2026-11-16 · 1 min read

Zambia have advanced to the final qualifying round of the TotalEnergies CAF U-23 Africa Cup of Nations Egypt 2027 after beating Burkina Faso. The Junior Chipolopolo overturned a 1-0 first-leg defeat in Lusaka on Sunday, winning 3-2 on penalties after the tie finished 1-1 on aggregate.

Substitute Caleb Musonda scored a late goal to take the second leg to extra time and penalties. Goalkeeper Chitambala saved decisively in the shootout, and the win keeps Zambia's Under-23 side in the continental qualifiers.

The report from News Diggers, published on Monday, names Chitambala as the player whose penalty saves decided the tie. Zambia's next opponent and match date were not given in the reports.

Why it mattersZambia's Under-23 side now plays in the final qualifying round for the continental tournament, which qualifies a team for Egypt 2027.
