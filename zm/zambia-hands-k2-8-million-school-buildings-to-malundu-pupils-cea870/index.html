Zambia hands K2.8 million school buildings to Malundu pupils

Until now, Malundu Community School's lessons were held under a tree and, at times, in neighbouring churches, district commissioner Joel Mboyoma said.

Source: Lusaka Times · 2026-11-19 · 1 min read

Infrastructure worth K2.8 million has been handed over to Malundu Community School in Chisamba district by the government.

Speaking at the handover ceremony, district commissioner Joel Mboyoma credited Build It International with putting up the school. Before this, he said, pupils were taught outdoors beneath a tree and sometimes inside neighbouring churches.

Mboyoma said the new facilities will improve teaching at the school. The report gave no details of the buildings or a date for classes to begin in them.

Why it mattersMalundu's pupils move from outdoor and church lessons into permanent classrooms the government has paid for, according to the district commissioner.
