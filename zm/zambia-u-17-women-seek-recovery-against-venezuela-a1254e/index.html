Zambia U-17 Women Seek Recovery Against Venezuela

The Zambian Under-17 Women's National Team faces Venezuela in a crucial World Cup match after an opening defeat.

Source: Lusaka Times · 2026-10-21 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X

The Zambia Under-17 Women’s National Team aims for recovery in their second Group D match at the FIFA U-17 Women’s World Cup in Morocco, facing Venezuela on Wednesday. The team seeks a turnaround after a 5-0 loss to Japan in their opening game. Venezuela also lost their first match, falling 2-0 to France, placing both teams under pressure to secure a win and keep their last-16 hopes alive.

Despite the significant defeat to Japan, coach Carol Kanyemba expressed confidence in her team's morale, stating that the setback should serve as a turning point. Kanyemba emphasized the importance of the right attitude for the upcoming match, viewing it as an opportunity to advance from the group stage. She anticipates a physical contest, noting Venezuela's playing style is similar to African teams, and believes maximum points are achievable.

Zambia's progression in the tournament follows a Round of 16 appearance last year, a step up from their first-round elimination in 2024. Coach Kanyemba, who has guided the team to three consecutive World Cups, aims to continue this upward trend in performance and development.

Why it mattersThe Zambia U-17 Women's National Team needs a win against Venezuela to advance in the FIFA U-17 Women's World Cup.
