Boakai signs US$5.7 billion Bea Mountain infrastructure deal

The agreement funds four power plants, grid upgrades and 14 road and bridge projects, with new company revenue earmarked for costs from 2027.

Sources: FrontPage Africa, The New Dawn Liberia and GNN Liberia · 2026-11-26 · 1 min read

Liberian President Joseph Nyuma Boakai signed a US$5.7 billion Master Infrastructure Development Agreement with Bea Mountain Mining Corporation at the Executive Mansion in Monrovia on Wednesday, November 25. The five-year programme covers four power plants, new transmission lines and 14 road and bridge projects.

Boakai said the plants are expected to add 270 megawatts, while dams known as VIA and SP-4 would store about 10 billion cubic meters of water to support power generation in the dry season. New revenue streams from Bea Mountain, starting in 2027, are to finance the projects. The company also agreed to provide a US$500 million bridge financing facility, according to The New Dawn Liberia.

The biomass power plant and transmission lines are expected to be completed within two years. Presidential Legal Advisor Bushuben M. Keita said revenues otherwise due to the government would be redirected to the programme.

Why it mattersPower cuts and poor roads are the constraints the deal targets, with dry-season electricity and road links to every region promised within five years.
