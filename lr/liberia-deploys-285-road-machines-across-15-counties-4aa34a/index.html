Liberia deploys 285 road machines across 15 counties

The equipment is stationed at 19 hubs, but the initiative's lasting value will depend on maintenance, budgets and public reporting.

Source: GNN Liberia · 2026-11-27 · 1 min read

Liberia has deployed 285 pieces of earth-moving and support equipment across all 15 counties, using 19 operational hubs to tackle poor roads. The nationwide rollout gives public works authorities machinery to rehabilitate roads, including in rural communities.

The report by journalist Evelyn Kpadeh Seagbeh says the machines need trained operators and mechanics, servicing and spare-parts budgets, secure storage and regular performance checks. It calls for public reporting on where equipment is deployed, which roads are repaired and whether machines remain operational.

The report says results should be judged by passable road kilometres, communities connected, transport costs and access to services, as well as how many machines still work five or 10 years later.

Why it mattersPublic works authorities must maintain the 285 machines and report their deployment, repaired roads and operating status across 19 hubs.
