Liberia task force alleges US$1.2 billion in annual gold smuggling

PORT chairman Abraham Kromah says gold leaves the country outside tax collection, while residents demand records of recoveries and prosecutions.

Source: GNN Liberia · 2026-11-25 · 1 min read

Abraham Kromah, chairman of Liberia’s Protect Our Resources Task Force (PORT), alleged that about US$1.2 billion worth of gold is smuggled out of Liberia each year. He made the claim during a local radio appearance in Monrovia over the weekend, saying the gold bypasses the Liberia Revenue Authority.

Kromah said PORT had identified people allegedly financing and facilitating illegal mining and warned they would face prosecution. He also alleged links between illegal mining and drug cartels, and said the task force was working with the Liberia Immigration Service on immigration violations, including deportation where legally justified. The claims, including the smuggling estimate and criminal links, require independent verification.

Residents in Gbarpolu County are questioning why mineral-rich communities remain poor and lack adequate public services. Citizens are seeking figures on gold and diamonds recovered, equipment seized, suspects arrested, cases prosecuted and revenue returned to government.

Why it mattersLiberians seeking public services and accountability are asking for verified figures on recovered minerals, prosecutions and revenue returned to government.
