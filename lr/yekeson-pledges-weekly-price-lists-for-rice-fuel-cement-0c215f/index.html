Yekeson pledges weekly price lists for rice, fuel, cement

Liberia's commerce minister nominee aims to publish prices across all 15 counties within 100 days.

Source: FrontPage Africa · 2026-10-23 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Stephen M. Yekeson Jr., Liberia's acting Commerce and Industry Minister, told senators on Wednesday that he will publish weekly prices for rice, fuel, and cement in all 15 counties within 100 days. He also pledged to protect consumers, enforce Liberian-only business sectors, and cut the cost of moving goods. Yekeson aims for Liberia to produce more of what it consumes by 2029.

Yekeson admitted that prices have continued to rise despite a stronger Liberian Dollar, which closed September at L$172 to US$1. Small traders report wholesalers still pricing stock at L$190 to the dollar, a gap of nearly 12 percent. A Senate probe found diesel prices rose 47 percent between October 2024 and September 2026.

The minister-designate will review pricing with the Liberia Petroleum Refining Company (LPRC) and promises transparent, rule-based prices reflecting import costs. World rice prices are up about 67 percent on the year, hitting a 26-month high in early October, while the domestic price was last fixed in March.

Why it mattersLiberians will have access to weekly price information for essential goods like rice, fuel, and cement.
