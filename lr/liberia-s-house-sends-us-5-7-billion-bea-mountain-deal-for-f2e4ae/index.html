Liberia's House sends US$5.7 billion Bea Mountain deal for review

The proposed agreement includes 270 megawatts of power projects and road construction, but still needs legislative review and ratification.

Source: The New Dawn Liberia · 2026-11-28 · 1 min read

Liberia's House of Representatives has referred a proposed US$5.7 billion infrastructure agreement with Bea Mountain Mining Corporation to two committees for review. The House acted Thursday after President Joseph Nyuma Boakai submitted the deal for legislative consideration and ratification.

The committees on Investment and Concessions and Public Works are expected to report to the full House within two weeks. The agreement would let Bea Mountain finance infrastructure, with approved spending offset against specified entitlements and corresponding government obligations.

Proposed works include three hydropower plants and a biomass plant, together rated at 270 megawatts, plus transmission lines and upgrades to the national electricity grid. The deal also lists roads in several counties and bridge and road work in Montserrado County; it remains subject to legislative approval.

Why it mattersThe two House committees must report on the proposed deal within two weeks before lawmakers consider ratification.
