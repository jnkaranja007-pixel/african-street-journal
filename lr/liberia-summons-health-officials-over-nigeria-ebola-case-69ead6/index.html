Liberia summons health officials over Nigeria Ebola case

Lawmakers want details on preparedness, screening, and supplies amid regional outbreak.

Source: FrontPage Africa · 2026-10-23 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Liberia's House of Representatives has summoned health authorities to brief them on the nation's readiness for Ebola. The summons follows a confirmed case in Nigeria and an ongoing outbreak in Bundibugyo. Lawmakers voted on Thursday, October 22, 2026, to require the Minister of Health and the NPHIL Director General to appear before the House on Tuesday, October 27.

Montserrado County Representative Dixon Seboe requested the briefing, citing the need to assess Liberia's preparedness, including medical supplies, trained staff, and emergency resources. The Ministry of Health had already directed health facilities and border points to enhance screening and infection control measures.

The health officials must explain the government's Ebola strategy, response coordination, disease surveillance, and laboratory capacity. They will also report on county-level preparedness, isolation facilities, border screening protocols, public awareness campaigns, and resources for enforcing preventive directives.

Why it mattersLiberian citizens' health and well-being are at risk from a potential Ebola outbreak.
