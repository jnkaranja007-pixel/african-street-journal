Liberian economist Jackson seeks travel for medical treatment

Samuel Jackson, facing charges in his wife's death, appealed to court for permission to travel abroad due to declining health.

Source: FrontPage Africa · 2026-10-23 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Liberian economist Samuel Jackson has asked Criminal Court “A” for permission to travel abroad for medical treatment, citing his deteriorating health. Jackson was seen struggling to walk at the Temple of Justice in Monrovia on Thursday, October 22, 2026, as he seeks specialized care outside Liberia. He remains barred from leaving the country while facing charges related to his wife's March 2026 death.

Jackson is accused in connection with the death of his wife, South African attorney Ntombi Khumalo Jackson, who was found in their Monrovia home. He told authorities he discovered her semi-conscious after returning from a television engagement. Reports indicated she suffered severe head injuries, and images allegedly showing bruises circulated on social media.

The economist was released on a US$20,000 bond with travel restrictions and mandatory court check-ins. Jackson has denied responsibility, calling the prosecution politically motivated. A major point of contention is the reported failure of authorities to release the official autopsy and toxicology findings.

Why it mattersEconomist Samuel Jackson is seeking permission to travel abroad for medical treatment due to his declining health while facing charges.
