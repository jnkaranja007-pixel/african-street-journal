China donates over 300 farm machines to Liberia

The equipment includes tractors, tillers, and harvesters to boost food production and reduce reliance on imports.

Source: GNN Liberia · 2026-11-04 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

The People’s Republic of China has donated over 300 modern agricultural machines and equipment to Liberia to increase food security and local production. The donation aims to provide farmers with modern tools to improve productivity, reduce manual labor, and increase the cultivation of crops like rice. The handover ceremony took place Friday at Camp Sandee Ware in Careysburg.

President Joseph Nyuma Boakai thanked China for its support, stating the donation is an important contribution to Liberia’s food self-sufficiency drive. He urged the Ministry of Agriculture to ensure effective distribution and maintenance across all 15 counties. Agriculture Minister Dr. J. Alexander Nuetah announced plans to expand mechanized services, building on 13 existing hubs.

A 40-day training program for 130 Liberians, including 27 women, is underway at the Central Agricultural Research Institute (CARI) in Bong County, with support from six Chinese specialists. The machinery includes tractors, tillers, transplanters, combine harvesters, and irrigation equipment, designed to assist in all farming stages from land preparation to post-harvest processing.

Why it mattersLiberian farmers will receive over 300 modern agricultural machines to improve crop yields and reduce their reliance on imported food.
