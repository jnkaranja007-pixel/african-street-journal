Liberia House committee urges rejection of OeSD ID concession

The lawmakers cite more than 40 alleged legal, procurement, financial and national-security defects in the proposed civil registry deal.

Source: FrontPage Africa · 2026-11-18 · 1 min read

A joint committee of Liberia’s House of Representatives has urged lawmakers to reject a proposed concession with OeSD International GmbH to develop and manage the country’s central digital civil registry and vital statistics system. The committee identified more than 40 alleged legal, procurement, financial and national-security deficiencies.

In a report submitted to the House Plenary, the committee said the government failed to follow procurement directions for open international bidding and delegated too much authority to the private company. It recommended rejecting the agreement in full and directing the executive branch to address statutory breaches before considering another arrangement.

The report says the Public Procurement and Concessions Commission rejected a sole-source request in May 2025 and, on July 7, 2025, instructed the government to hold open international bidding. The committee said that instruction was not implemented before the agreement was signed on April 30, 2026; the House received it for ratification on July 15.

Why it mattersThe House must decide whether to ratify a deal the committee says bypassed procurement rules, after recommending rejection and a review of statutory breaches.
