Liberia records 2,900 new HIV infections and 1,400 AIDS deaths in 2025

National AIDS Commission chair Cecelia Nutah said about 56,000 people are living with HIV, most of them women.

Source: GNN Liberia · 2026-11-19 · 1 min read

Liberia recorded about 2,900 new HIV infections and 1,400 AIDS-related deaths in 2025, according to figures disclosed by Dr. Cecelia Nutah, chairperson of the National AIDS Commission. She said an estimated 56,000 people are currently living with the virus across the country.

Women account for about 35,000 of those living with HIV, compared with 17,400 men and 3,600 children. The report says rural residents face limited access to HIV testing and antiretroviral drugs, while stigma discourages people from getting tested or staying on treatment.

The report urges the National AIDS Commission and the Ministry of Health to expand voluntary testing, awareness campaigns and uninterrupted treatment access. It also calls on the government to disclose how HIV/AIDS funds are spent and which communities they reach.

Why it mattersRural patients and women, who make up the largest share of those living with HIV, face the risk of losing access to testing and antiretroviral drugs if spending is not made public.
