UNDP launches Liberia's law reform plan

The five-year strategy aims to modernize the legal framework and improve access to justice by 2030.

Source: GNN Liberia · 2026-11-10 · 1 min read

The United Nations Development Programme (UNDP) Resident Representative, Aliou Mamadou Dia, launched the Law Reform Commission (LRC) Strategic Plan 2026-2030 in Monrovia on November 6. This five-year plan provides a roadmap for modernizing Liberia’s legal framework and improving access to justice, aligning with the government’s ARREST Agenda. The launch gathered government, legislative, judicial, and civil society representatives.

Dia described law reform as a cornerstone of democratic governance and economic development, essential for inclusive national progress. He praised the LRC's work in reviewing outdated laws, supporting constitutional reform, and developing an online legal portal for public access. UNDP will continue supporting the LRC in areas such as legislative drafting, legal research, and digitalization.

The strategic plan, themed “Better Laws for a More Accountable Society,” outlines priorities including strengthening the rule of law, advancing decentralization, and promoting reforms that meet citizens' needs. It also aligns with UNDP’s Country Programme Document 2026-2030, aiming for a more accessible and effective legal system by 2030.

Why it mattersLiberians will benefit from a modernized legal system and improved access to justice through the LRC's five-year strategic plan.
