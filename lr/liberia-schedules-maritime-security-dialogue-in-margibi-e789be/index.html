Liberia schedules maritime security dialogue in Margibi County

The Liberia Maritime Authority will convene officials and shipping leaders on Thursday, before the country assumes the UN Security Council presidency in December.

Sources: The New Dawn Liberia and GNN Liberia · 2026-11-18 · 1 min read

Liberia will host a maritime safety and security dialogue on Thursday, November 19, at the Liberia Maritime Training Institute in Marshall, Margibi County. The Liberia Maritime Authority (LiMA) scheduled the meeting for 10 a.m., as the country prepares to assume the rotating presidency of the United Nations Security Council in December 2026.

LiMA Commissioner and Chief Executive Officer Neto Zarzar Lighe announced the event at a Ministry of Information briefing on November 17. He said senior government officials, diplomats, United Nations representatives, security experts and shipping industry leaders would discuss threats including piracy, armed conflict, cyberattacks, illegal fishing and trafficking.

The talks are set to cover international shipping routes, seafarer protection, information sharing and coordination. Lighe said Liberia, one of the world’s leading flag states, has a responsibility to promote maritime safety, freedom of navigation and cooperation.

Why it mattersOfficials and shipping leaders are scheduled to discuss threats to international routes and seafarers at the 10 a.m. meeting on November 19.
