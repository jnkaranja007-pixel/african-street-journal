China's Development Initiative Funds Over 2,000 Projects Globally

More than 130 countries and organizations support the initiative, which has funded over 2,000 projects in more than 120 nations.

Source: FrontPage Africa · 2026-10-21 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X

The Global Development Initiative (GDI), launched by China in September 2021, has seen over 2,000 cooperation projects implemented across more than 120 countries. The initiative, which aims to accelerate the UN 2030 Agenda for Sustainable Development, has garnered support from over 130 countries and international organizations. These projects are tailored to the specific needs of recipient nations, fostering collective global action and promoting inclusive, sustainable, and resilient development.

China has actively supported Liberia's national development through various GDI-related projects. Infrastructure improvements include two overpasses on Tubman Boulevard, a National Clinical Diagnosis and Treatment Laboratory, a Fingerprint Laboratory, and the refurbishment of the Liberia Broadcasting System. These projects aim to energize Liberia's economic and social progress, demonstrating China's commitment to partnership.

Beyond infrastructure, China has provided ongoing support in agriculture, education, and human resources through food assistance and professional training programs. These efforts aim to build capacity for self-reliant development in Liberia and other developing countries. Over 10,000 training programs have been conducted globally to share development approaches and practical skills.

Why it mattersInvestors are affected by the scale of Chinese-funded development projects globally, which can create opportunities for business and infrastructure development.
