US investors propose pineapple farms and winery in Liberia

A trade delegation from Maryland, USA, plans to invest in large-scale pineapple farms and a winery, aiming to create over 350 jobs.

Source: The New Dawn Liberia · 2026-11-04 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

A trade delegation from Maryland, United States, visited Liberia and proposed investments in large-scale pineapple farms, agro-processing facilities, and a winery. The plans, disclosed on November 3, 2026, are expected to generate more than 350 jobs in the country's agricultural sector.

The delegation, including representatives from Clyopatra Winery and Libahna Produce, met with Liberia's Agriculture Minister Dr. J. Alexander Nuetah. Minister Nuetah welcomed the private sector participation, emphasizing the government's commitment to strengthening agricultural value chains and supporting smallholder farmers through off-taker arrangements and training.

The proposed investments align with Liberia’s National Agriculture Development Plan, which aims for food self-sufficiency by 2030. The delegation also expressed interest in supporting agribusiness development, with specific plans for modern machinery and processing facilities for export to the United States.

Why it mattersLiberians will benefit from over 350 new jobs created by US investment in pineapple farms and a winery.
