Liberia army discharges two sergeants for domestic violence

The military action follows the circulation of a video showing an alleged assault on a woman in Paynesville.

Source: The New Dawn Liberia · 2026-10-23 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

The Armed Forces of Liberia (AFL) administratively discharged two sergeants, Roberts P. Menyon and Abu S. B. Sheriff, on Wednesday, October 21, 2026. The action followed a social media video allegedly showing domestic violence involving Alfredia Blessing Kpaegai in Paynesville, Montserrado County. The Ministry of National Defense announced the immediate discharge.

AFL Chief of Staff Major General Davidson F. Forleh stated the incident was inconsistent with military standards at a ceremony at the Barclay Training Center in Monrovia. He reaffirmed the military's commitment to discipline and human dignity, emphasizing that conduct abusing or devaluing women would not be tolerated. The discharge, under the National Defense Act of 2008, aims to reinforce institutional standards and public confidence.

Alfredia Blessing Kpaegai is receiving medical care at the 14-Military Hospital. The Ministry of National Defense acknowledged public cooperation and urged citizens to report similar allegations. The discharge occurred amid public attention on the video and calls for accountability in cases of alleged violence against women.
