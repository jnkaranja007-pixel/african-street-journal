NOCAL breaks ground on Sinje hospital trauma ward

The National Oil Company of Liberia is building a trauma ward at Sinje Hospital, due to be finished and dedicated within 16 months.

Source: The New Dawn Liberia · 2026-11-16 · 1 min read

The National Oil Company of Liberia (NOCAL) has begun construction of a modern trauma ward at Sinje Hospital in Grand Cape Mount County. The project, part of the company's Corporate Social Responsibility programme, is meant to strengthen emergency care and specialised treatment for residents.

The Ministry of Health chose Sinje because it has secure land, sits on the Bo Corridor and serves about 10,000 people, compared with roughly 3,000 in Robertsport, according to NOCAL. The groundbreaking was attended by Grand Cape Mount senators Simeon Taylor and Dabah M. Varpilah, and by ministry officials on behalf of Health Minister Dr. Louise M. Kpoto.

The ward will include a triage area, emergency and resuscitation rooms, operating and recovery rooms, a blood bank, a laboratory, a Neonatal Intensive Care Unit and a pharmacy. Once completed, it is expected to widen the hospital's capacity for surgery, maternal care and newborn care.

Why it mattersResidents of Sinje and nearby communities will gain surgical, maternal and newborn services that are currently hard to reach, once the ward opens.
