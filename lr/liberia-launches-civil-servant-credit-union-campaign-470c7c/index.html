Liberia launches civil servant credit union campaign

The Civil Service Agency and CSAL-CU aim to boost savings and credit access for over 60,000 government employees.

Source: GNN Liberia · 2026-11-10 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

The Civil Service Agency (CSA) and the Civil Servants Association of Liberia Credit Union (CSAL-CU) have begun a nationwide awareness campaign for their new institution. The credit union officially launches on November 13, 2026, at the Ministerial Complex in Congo Town, Monrovia. This initiative targets over 60,000 government employees across Liberia, aiming to improve financial empowerment, encourage disciplined savings, and expand access to credit.

CSA Director-General Dr. Josiah F. Joekai Sr. pledged institutional support for the credit union, which kicked off its campaign on Saturday, November 7, with a parade and sports activities at Invincible Park in Sinkor. Dr. Joekai urged leaders to ensure strict accountability and cautioned against fund misappropriation, emphasizing the need to safeguard members’ contributions. He also encouraged civil servants to maintain regular savings through salary deductions.

CSAL-CU Chief Executive Officer Pertaingber Lawrence Noaus stated the campaign aims to unite credit unions and cooperatives to broaden economic opportunities for government employees. CSAL Board of Directors Chairperson Prince Leo Kponbowoe described the credit union as a key tool for improving civil servants' financial well-being. Participating organizations included the Port Authority Credit Union, the University of Liberia Credit Union Cooperative Society, and the Teachers Credit Union.
