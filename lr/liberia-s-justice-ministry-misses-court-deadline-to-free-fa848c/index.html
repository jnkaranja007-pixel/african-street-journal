Liberia's Justice Ministry misses court deadline to free vessel and seven crew

A judge ordered the IB Atlantic IV and its seven crew released by 4 p.m. Wednesday, but they were still in state custody that evening.

Source: FrontPage Africa · 2026-11-19 · 1 min read

Liberia's Justice Ministry did not release the IB Atlantic IV and its seven crew members by a court-ordered 4 p.m. Wednesday deadline. At about 5 p.m., FrontPage Africa observed the vessel and crew still under state custody, with the crew still aboard.

Criminal Court 'A' Judge D. Onesimus Banwon ordered their release after a habeas corpus petition challenged their detention. Lawyers for the crew confirmed their clients had not been freed. Solicitor General Abrahim Sillah declined to explain the delay, referring the newspaper to the court.

Ministry sources said officials were considering releasing the vessel and crew early Thursday, but no release had been confirmed by Wednesday evening. The vessel was first seen off York Island in Grand Cape Mount County in August.

Why it mattersThe crew's continued detention aboard the vessel leaves the Ministry to explain its delay in obeying the court and to decide whether to release them early Thursday.
