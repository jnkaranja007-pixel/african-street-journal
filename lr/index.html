Business · 08/20/2026

Liberia collects US$904.7 million in domestic revenue

The Liberia Revenue Authority reported US$904.7 million in domestic revenue collection as of August 18. The figure brings the government closer to a US$1 billion mark. This allows the government to strengthen domestic resource mobilization.

Why it mattersIncreased domestic revenue reduces the government's reliance on external funding for public spending.

Politics · 08/20/2026

Unity Party backs security probe into Jewel Howard Taylor

The governing Unity Party commended Joint Security for charges against former Vice President Jewel Howard Taylor. The charges relate to a multimillion-dollar cocaine probe. The party urged security agencies to remain professional despite public pressure.

Why it mattersThe party's support signals a commitment to pursuing high-level officials involved in narcotics trafficking.

Liberia signs new Satellite Communications Guidelines

LTA Chairperson Massaquoi and Minister of Posts and Telecommunications Sekou M. Kromah signed new Satellite Communications Guidelines. The officials displayed the signed copy of the document on Tuesday.

Why it mattersThe new guidelines establish the regulatory framework for satellite service providers operating in the country.

Education · 08/20/2026

AMEU and University of Sierra Leone renew partnership

The African Methodist Episcopal University and the Institute of Public Administration and Management at the University of Sierra Leone renewed an academic cooperation agreement on Tuesday. The partnership focuses on joint research and faculty and student exchanges.

Why it mattersStudents and faculty at both institutions gain access to postgraduate opportunities and knowledge transfer across borders.
