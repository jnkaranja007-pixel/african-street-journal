Liberia receives 20 deportees from U.S. under new transfer deal

The first group of 20 deportees arrived at Roberts International Airport on Thursday. The Liberian Government says up to 1,200 people could be transferred from the U.S. over the next year.

Why it mattersThe arrangement increases the number of returnees the government must integrate into the country over the next 12 months.

Ex-VP Jewel Howard Taylor hospitalized during police custody

Former Vice President Jewel Howard Taylor was transported to GIMS Hospital in Monrovia on Thursday evening. FrontPage Africa reports she experienced a medical emergency while in police custody.

Why it mattersThe medical emergency interrupts the detention of a former high-ranking official currently linked to a multimillion-dollar cocaine probe.

LRA exceeds 2026 domestic revenue target

The Liberia Revenue Authority exceeded its domestic revenue target for 2026. The Center for Transparency and Accountability in Liberia welcomed the report on Thursday.

Why it mattersThe surplus provides the government with additional funds that CENTAL argues should be spent on projects benefiting ordinary citizens.

AMEU and University of Sierra Leone renew academic partnership

The African Methodist Episcopal University and the Institute of Public Administration and Management reaffirmed a partnership on Tuesday. The agreement focuses on joint research and faculty and student exchanges.

ECOWAS urges Liberian youth to protect peace

ECOWAS Ambassador to Liberia Mohammed Konneh called on young Liberians to take responsibility for the country's peace on August 20. He warned that gains made since the end of the civil war must not be taken for granted.

Why it mattersThe appeal seeks to prevent political instability by engaging the youth population in maintaining the current security environment.
