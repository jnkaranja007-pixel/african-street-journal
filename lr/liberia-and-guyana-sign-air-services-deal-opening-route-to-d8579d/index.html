Liberia and Guyana sign air services deal, opening route to South America

The agreement creates a legal framework for passenger and cargo flights between the two countries, but no start date for services has been set.

Source: The New Dawn Liberia · 2026-11-21 · 1 min read

Liberia and Guyana have signed a Bilateral Air Services Agreement in Montreal, Canada, on the sidelines of the International Civil Aviation Organization’s Seventh Worldwide Air Transport Conference. Julius D. Dennis Jr., director general of the Liberia Civil Aviation Authority, signed for Liberia, and Guyana’s Minister of Public Utilities and Aviation, Deodat Indar, signed for Guyana.

The deal lets designated airlines introduce direct or code-sharing passenger and cargo services between the two countries, and commits both sides to cooperation on aviation safety and security. Dennis said it would expand Liberia’s international aviation network and link West Africa with the Caribbean and South America.

The announcement did not say when commercial flights between the two countries will begin.
