Singer Hage uses music to fight estate battle

Raquel Hage released a five-track EP to express frustration with the Liberian justice system over her mother's medical care.

Source: FrontPage Africa · 2026-11-17 · 1 min read

Liberian singer Raquel Hage released a new five-track EP titled Justice on November 13, using music to express her family's ongoing struggle with the Liberian court system. The songs address the delays and perceived injustices surrounding the estate of her late father, Milad Hage, which the family needs access to for her mother's medical treatment in New Delhi, India.

Raquel Hage stated that the EP is a "beef album" intended to voice what years of legal filings have not achieved, as her family seeks funds from her father's estate to cover her mother's severe kidney problems, hypertension, and other health conditions. The family blames these health issues on stress experienced since Milad Hage's death in 2010, and they are petitioning the probate court for access to money held in escrow.

The EP's tracks, including "Shame on You" and "Fraudster," target the government, the justice system, and a relative accused of fraud, whom Raquel Hage claims a jury found liable. She expressed hope in exposing those she believes are responsible, referencing a Liberian proverb about a thief's luck eventually running out.

Why it mattersThe Hage family is seeking court permission to access funds from a deceased father's estate to pay for the mother's medical treatment in India.
