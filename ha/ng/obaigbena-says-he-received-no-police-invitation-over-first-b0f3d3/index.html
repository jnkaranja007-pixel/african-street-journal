Obaigbena ya musanta samun gayyatar ‘yan sanda a kan First Bank

Shugaban THISDAY da ARISE ya ce tattaunawar sasanci da First Bank da GHL na cigaba bayan hukuncin Kotun Koli.

Majiyoyi: Vanguard da Nairametrics · 25 ga Nuwamba, 2026 · Karatun minti 1

Nduka Obaigbena, shugaban kamfanonin yada labarai na Najeriya wato THISDAY, da kuma gidan talabijin na ARISE, ya ce bai samu wata gayyata ta 'yan sanda ko wata sanarwa daga hukumomin tsaro ba game da rikicin da ya shafi First Bank da kamfanoninsa na yada labarai. A cikin wata sanarwa da the ofishinsa ya fitar a Ikoyi, Legas, ya musanta rahotannin da ke cewa 'yan sanda sun bayyana shi a matsayin wanda ake nema ruwa a jallo kuma ya musanta barazana ga babban jami'in First Bank Olusegun Alebiosu.

Ofishin Obaigbena ya ce ya binciki duka adireshinsa kuma bai ga wata gayyata da ta shafi bincike ba. Ya danganta rahotannin da wani rikici na kudi da ya shafi First Bank, GHL, THISDAY Newspapers da ARISE Television, kuma ya ce ana tattaunawa domin shawo kan matsalolin da suka rage. Sanarwar ta ce Kotun Koli ta yanke hukunci baki daya, 5-0, domin goyon bayan GHL a shari'arta da First Bank a ranar 3 ga Yuli, 2026.

Sanarwar ta ce First Bank da GHL sun binciko yiwuwar sasanci a wajen kotu tun bayan hukuncin, yayin da kokarin magance basussukan da ake zargin ana bin kamfanonin yada labarai ke ci gaba. Obaigbena ya ce ya yi magana da manyan jami'an ‘yan sanda a ranar 19 ga Nuwamba game da shirin kafa tsaron ‘yan sandan jihohi.

Dalilin da ya sa yake da muhimmanciFirst Bank da GHL na cigaba da tattaunawar sasanci bayan hukuncin da Kotun Koli ta yanke a ranar 3 ga Yuli saboda GHL, yayin da ake ci gaba da tattaunawa kan basussukan da ake zargin ana bin kamfanonin yada labaran.
