Obi ya yi makokin Pete Edochie, dan shekara 79

Tsohon gwamnan jihar Anambra ya yabi tsohon dan wasan kwaikwayon saboda nuna rayuwar Okonkwo a fim din Things Fall Apart na Chinua Achebe.

Majiyoyi: Vanguard, Premium Times da The Punch · 25 ga Nuwamba, 2026 · Karatun minti 1

Peter Obi, dan takarar shugaban kasa na jam'iyyar Nigeria Democratic Congress kuma tsohon gwamnan jihar Anambra, ya yi makokin tsohon dan wasan Nollywood Pete Edochie bayan iyalan sun sanar da rasuwarsa a ranar Laraba. Edochie yana da shekaru 79 a duniya.

A sakon ta'aziyya da ya fitar a shafin X, Obi ya ce Edochie ya taimaka wajen sanya al'adun Najeriya da na Afirka ga masu kallo a fadin duniya, musamman ta hanyar fitowa a matsayin Okonkwo a cikin shirin talabijin na littafin Chinua Achebe mai suna Things Fall Apart. Obi ya kuma yabi kwarewar dan wasan a harshen Turanci da Inyamuranci da kuma gogewarsa a fannin watsa labarai.

Shugaba Bola Tinubu shi ma ya yi makokin rasuwar Edochie, inda ya kira shi a matsayin majagaba a Nollywood wanda ayyukansa suka tallata al'adun Najeriya a duniya, kamar yadda jaridar The Punch ta ruwaito. Gwamnan jihar Enugu Peter Mbah ya bayyana rasuwar a matsayin babban rashi ga jihar, kamar yadda jaridar Premium Times ta rawaito.
