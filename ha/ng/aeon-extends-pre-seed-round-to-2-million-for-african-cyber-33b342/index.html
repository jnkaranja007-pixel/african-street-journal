Aeon ta kara kudin bincike zuwa dala miliyan 2

Kamfanin tsaron yanar gizo na Najeriya ya samu hannun jari daga Launch Africa Ventures da Octerra Capital bayan ya tara dala miliyan 1 a watan Satumba.

Majiya: Nairametrics · 25 ga Nuwamba, 2026 · Karatun minti 1

Aeon, wani kamfanin tsaron yanar gizo na Najeriya, ya kara wa'adin neman kudadensa na farko zuwa dala miliyan 2 yayin da yake gina kayayyakin tsaron yanar gizo don Afirka da kuma Kudancin Duniya.

Kararin kudin ya zo ne tare da sabbin jarin da aka zuba daga Launch Africa Ventures da Octerra Capital.

Wannan karin kudin ya biyo bayan zagayen neman kudi na dala miliyan 1 da Aeon ta yi a watan Satumba na 2026, wanda kamfanin Terra Industries ya jagoranta. Kamfanin na cigaba da gina abin da ya bayyana a matsayin ababen more rayuwa na tsaron yanar gizo ga yankin da kuma Kudancin Duniya.
