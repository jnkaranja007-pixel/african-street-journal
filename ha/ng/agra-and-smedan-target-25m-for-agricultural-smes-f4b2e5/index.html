AGRA da SMEDAN sun sanya gaba da tara dala miliyan 25

Abokan huldar sun kuma kaddamar da wani shiri na raba kudaden tsada, wanda AGRA ta sanya dala 100,000 ciki, domin sauwaka ayyukan kasuwanci.

Majiya: AgroNigeria · 27 ga Nuwamba, 2026 · Karatun minti 1

Kungiyar Alliance for a Green Revolution in Africa (AGRA) da Hukumar Kula da Kananan Masana'antu ta Najeriya wato SMEDAN sun sanya gaba da tara akalla dala miliyan 25 domin ba da tallafin kudi ga kananan masana'antu da na matsakaita na fannin noma. Sun kuma bayyana wani shiri na taimaka wa 'yan kasuwa su samu ayyukan tallafi na kwararru a wani taro da suka yi a Ibadan, Jihar Oyo.

AGRA ta ware dala 100,000 a matsayin farko domin shirin bayar da tallafin kudi na hadin gwiwa wato Multi-Donor Business Development Services Cost-Sharing Facility, wanda zai hada gudummawa daga abokan huldar ci gaba, cibiyoyin kudi da kuma hukumomin gwamnati. Dr Godswill Aguiyi, wanda ya wakilci Daraktan AGRA a Najeriya Rufus Idris, ya ce rashin ingantattun takardun kudi, mulkin kamfanoni, amfani da fasaha da kuma bayanan kasuwa na sanya shi da wahala ga masana'antar noma su jawo hankalin masu zuba jari.

Babban shirin na neman hada 'yan kasuwa da masu saya da kuma cibiyoyin kudi. Manufar shirin ita ce fadada damammaki ga mata da matasa a fannin noma; AGRA ta ce gasar kirkire-kirkire ta matasa a fannin noma wato Youth in Agribusiness Innovation Contest ta samu neman shiga fiye da 10,000, kuma 'yan kasuwa 10 da aka zaba sun raba kyaututtukan kudi tare da kai adadin nairori miliyan 17.

Dalilin da ya sa yake da muhimmanciKananan hukumomi da matsakaitan masana'antu na noma dake neman jari da shawarwari sune wadanda za a amfana, tare da sanya burin kudi a kalla dala miliyan 25 da gudummawar farko ta AGRA ta dala 100,000.
