Najeriya da IFAD sun duba aiwatar da shirin gona na dala miliyan 50

Manzo zai kimanta aiki a Ogun da Kano, inda shirin ke nufin mutane 100,000 kai tsaye da 500,000 a kaikaice.

Majiya: AgroNigeria · 25 ga Nuwamba, 2026 · Karatun minti 1

Gwamnatin Tarayyar Najeriya da Asusun Kula da Cigaban Noma na Duniya (IFAD) sun fara duba haɗin gwiwa na shirin Yankunan Kula da Noma na Musamman (Special Agro-Industrial Processing Zones) a Abuja a ranar 24 ga Nuwamba, inda suke neman saurin aiwatar da zuba jarin dala miliyan 50.

Manzon zai binciki kwangiloli, kashe kuɗi, sakamoko, da kuma cikas a fannin gudanarwa kafin ƙarin kuɗin farko na shirin da kuma bita na tsakiyarwa, in ji Emmanuel Oladipo Akogun, babban darekta a ma'aikatar noma. Shirin na tallafawa fannin shinkafa da rogo a Ogun da Kano, tare da manufar cin gajiyar mutane 100,000 kai tsaye da kuma 500,000 a kaikaice, musamman mata da matasa.

A jihar Kano, wani gwajin 2024 ya tallafawa kananan manoma fiye da 1,000 da ƙungiyoyin manoma da kayan aiki, da alaƙar kasuwa, da horo. An tsara ziyarar aiki zuwa ranar 2 ga Disamba; lokacin farko na lamunin zai kare ne a ranar 30 ga Satumba, 2029.

Dalilin da ya sa yake da muhimmanciManoma a Kano da Ogun suna cikin wani shiri mai wuraren cin gajiyar mutane 100,000 kai tsaye, yayin da jami'ai ke duba aiwatarwa da kuma cikas.
