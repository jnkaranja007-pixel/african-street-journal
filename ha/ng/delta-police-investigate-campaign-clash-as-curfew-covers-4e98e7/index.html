Yan sandan Delta na binciken rikicin siyasa yayin da aka sanya dokar ta baci a Ughelli North

‘Yan sanda sun ba da rahoton mutuwar mutane shida da samun raunuka da dama a wani taron yakin neman zabe, yayin da gwamnatin jihar ta kakaba dokar hana fitowa dare a fadin yankin kananan hukumomin.

Majiyoyi: Vanguard, Premium Times da The Punch · 24 ga Nuwamba, 2026 · Karatun minti 1

Rundunar ‘yan sandan Najeriya ta ce mutane shida ne suka mutu sannan wasu da dama suka jikkata yayin wani taron yakin neman zabe a yankin al'ummar Uduere da ke Karamar Hukumar Ughelli North ta Jihar Delta. Gwamnatin Jihar Delta ta sanya dokar hana fitowa daga karfe 6 na yamma zuwa karfe 6 na safe kullum a fadin yankin har sai an sanar da wani sabon mataki.

Kakakin ‘yan sanda Ani Iniedu ya ce binciken farko ya nuna cewa mutane shida ne suka mutu, yayin da za a gudanar da bincike don tabbatar da adadin wadanda suka jikkata da kuma gano abin da ya faru. Babban Inspector Janar na ‘Yan Sanda Olatunji Disu ya umarci kwamishinan ‘yan sandan Delta da ya binciki yadda lamarin ya faru, da gano wadanda ke da hannu, da kuma duba matakan jami'an tsaro da kuma yadda aka tura su wajen taron.

‘Yan sandan sun ce rundunar jihar na aiwatar da dokar hana fitowa kuma ta kara kaimu wajen daukar matakan tsaro. Binciken ya biyo koke ne daga tawagar yakin neman zaben tsohon mataimakin shugaban majalisar dattawa Ovie Omo-Agege cewa ‘yan bindiga sun kai hari kan tawagarsa; tawagar ta bayar da rahoton mutuwar mutane hudu.

Dalilin da ya sa yake da muhimmanciMazauna Ughelli North dole ne su kiyaye dokar hana fitowa daga karfe 6 na yamma zuwa karfe 6 na safe kullum har sai an sanar da wani sabon mataki yayin da ‘yan sanda ke binciken rikicin.
