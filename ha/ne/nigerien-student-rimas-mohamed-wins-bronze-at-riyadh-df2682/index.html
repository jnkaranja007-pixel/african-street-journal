Dalibar Nijar Rimas Mohamed ta lashe lambar azurfa a Riyadh

Ta lashe kyautar ne saboda wani aikin kare muhalli, inda ta fafata da kungiyoyi sama da 1 200 daga kasashe 55.

Majiya: Agence Nigerienne de Presse · 27 ga Nuwamba, 2026 · Karatun minti 1

Dalibar Nijar Rimas Mohamed ta lashe lambar tagulla a baje kolin kirkire-kirkire na duniya wato SAIF International Innovation Fair da kuma gasar tsaro da kirkire-kirkire ta duniya a Riyadh, kasar Saudiyya, wadda aka gudanar daga 24 zuwa 26 ga Nuwamba 2026. Ta lashe kyautar ne saboda wani sabon aiki na kare muhalli, inda ta wakilci Jamhuriyar Nijar.

Kamfanin dillancin labaran Nijar wato Agence Nigerienne de Presse ne ya bayar da rahoton lambar yabo a ranar 27 ga Nuwamba. Kungiyoyi sama da 1 200 daga kasashe 55 ne suka halarci gasar, inda suka fafata a fannonin kirkire-kirkire 14, wadanda suka hada da dorewar muhalli, makamashi, muhalli, fasahar kere-kere ta zamani wato artificial intelligence, tsaro a intanet da kuma fannin na'urorin robot.

Baje kolin na hada masu kirkire-kirkire, masu bincike da dalibai domin baje kolin ayyuka da kuma karfafa kirkire-kirkiren kimiyya, a cewar rahoton.
