Nijar ta duba aikin tituna inda aka kammala kilomita 189.7

Kwamitin PACNEN ya tantance aikinsa na 2026 tare da nazarin kasafin 2027, yayin da za a ba da ƙarin kuɗi don gyaran wasu tituna.

Majiya: Agence Nigerienne de Presse · 20 ga Nuwamba, 2026 · Karatun minti 1

Ministan kayan aiki da ababen more rayuwa na Nijar, Kanar-Manjo Salissou Mahaman Salissou, ya jagoranci taron kwamitin aikin a Niamey a ranar Juma'a, 20 ga Nuwamba, domin duba Aikin Inganta Sadarwa na Arewa Maso Gabas (PACNEN). Mambobin sun tantance yadda aka aiwatar da shirin ayyuka da kasafin kudin shekarar 2026 tare da nazarin daftarin na 2027.

Aikin ya gyara kilomita 189.7 na titi daga cikin kilomita 225.7 da aka sa a gaba, wanda ke nuna kashi 84.05 na kammala aikin, a cewar ministan. Wani karin kasafin kudi da aka amince da shi bayan gyaran tsari zai ba da kuɗin gyaran sashin Tiguidit zuwa Agadez mai tsawon kilomita 62 da kuma inganta kilomita 200 na titunan karkara.

PACNEN, wanda aka kaddamar a ranar 22 ga Satumba, 2021, ya kuma shafi babbar hanyar kasa ta 11 ta Arewa da kuma samun damar shiga ababen more rayuwa na asali a kan titin Zinder zuwa Agadez. Za a fadada yankin da aikin ke shafa zuwa yankunan Niamey da Tahoua.
