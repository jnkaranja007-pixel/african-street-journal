Five of eight family members die after suspected poisoning in Burundi's south

Health sources said the eight relatives were not taken to hospital until Tuesday, 17 November, days after the incident is believed to have happened.

Source: SOS Medias Burundi · 2026-11-20 · 1 min read

Five members of one family, a man, a woman and three children, died after a suspected poisoning at the Site de la Paix on Musenyi hill in Gitara zone, Nyanza commune, in Burunga province, southern Burundi, according to SOS Médias Burundi. The site mostly houses former Burundian refugees repatriated from neighbouring Tanzania. The fifth death, a man, was recorded on Wednesday evening at Mabanda communal hospital.

Health sources told the outlet that the incident is believed to have happened the previous Friday, but the eight relatives were only taken to Mabanda hospital on the morning of Tuesday, 17 November. The three surviving relatives are still hospitalised and are not in critical condition. The exact causes of death and the nature of the substance have not been officially established.

Other suspected poisoning cases were reported in Makamba, the capital of Burunga province, and in Bujumbura. Medical sources confirmed several cases but did not link the incidents or confirm an organised network. Relatives abroad told the outlet they had fallen ill after eating groundnuts or eggs bought from street vendors in Bujumbura.

Why it mattersThree surviving relatives remain in hospital, and officials have not established what substance caused the deaths.
