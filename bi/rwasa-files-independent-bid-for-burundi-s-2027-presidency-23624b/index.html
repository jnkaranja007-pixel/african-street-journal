Rwasa files independent bid for Burundi’s 2027 presidency

The former National Congress for Liberty leader paid a 100 million-franc deposit and says he cannot obtain a required party-membership certificate.

Sources: SOS Medias Burundi and Iwacu · 2026-11-22 · 1 min read

Agathon Rwasa, a former leader of Burundi’s National Congress for Liberty (CNL) and a longtime opposition figure, filed an independent candidacy for the presidency on Saturday, 21 November. The election is scheduled for 3 May 2027. He submitted his papers to the national electoral commission after paying a deposit of 100 million Burundian francs to the central bank on 20 November.

The commission requires independent candidates to provide proof that they belong to no political party. Rwasa said he cannot obtain that certificate under current circumstances. He said his departure from the CNL leadership was an expulsion, not a voluntary resignation, and argued that this makes him eligible to run independently.

Francis Rohero, president of the FP Ineza party, also filed a candidacy, while Aloys Kamenyero submitted an application missing several documents, SOS Medias Burundi reported.

Why it mattersRwasa’s eligibility now turns on whether election officials accept his explanation in place of the required certificate for independent candidates.
