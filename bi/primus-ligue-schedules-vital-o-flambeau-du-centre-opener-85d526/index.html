Primus Ligue schedules Vital’O-Flambeau du Centre opener

The 14th-round match is set for Friday at 15:00 at Urunani, with three more fixtures scheduled for Saturday.

Source: Iwacu · 2026-11-27 · 1 min read

Burundi’s Primus Ligue is due to open its 14th round on Friday with Vital’O against Flambeau du Centre at 15:00 at Urunani stadium in Bujumbura. The league schedule also lists three matches for Saturday.

Rukinzo FC is scheduled to host Never Give Up at Urunani at 13:00 on Saturday, 28 November. Inter Star is due to play Young Star FC at the same stadium at 15:45. The schedule gives no scores or standings.

The fixtures bring six clubs into action across two days, all at Urunani. The report gives no further matches or results.

Why it mattersThe six clubs named in the schedule have matches at Urunani across Friday and Saturday, including three fixtures on 28 November.
