Bubanza peace torch visit shuts market until 16 h 30

Traders reopened only an hour and a half before the market's usual closing time as schools also stopped lessons for the reception.

Source: SOS Medias Burundi · 2026-11-21 · 1 min read

The Flambeau de la paix caravan’s stop in Bubanza, western Burundi, on 19 November left the central market closed for almost the entire day and interrupted lessons at most schools in the commercial centre. The market reopened at 16 h 30, only an hour and a half before its usual closing time.

SOS Médias Burundi reported that schools shut from 10 heures so pupils and teachers could attend the reception. Pupils were told to wear civilian clothes rather than school uniforms. The outlet said traders’ daily incomes depend on sales, and some complained of repeated disruption to their work.

The national caravan was launched in 2006 by former president Pierre Nkurunziza. Its 2026 edition, the twentieth, began on 13 novembre in Bujumbura under President Évariste Ndayishimiye; the report said some residents also lost time for farm work and other income-generating activities.

Why it mattersBubanza traders who rely on daily sales lost most of a market day, with reopening at 16 h 30 leaving only an hour and a half before usual closing.
