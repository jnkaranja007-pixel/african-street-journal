Burundi police general detained with three officers

General Romuald Ndababishije, deputy migration commissioner, was arrested on October 17 in Bujumbura amid unclear circumstances.

Source: SOS Medias Burundi · 2026-10-23 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Burundi police general Romuald Ndababishije, deputy commissioner general of migrations, was detained on October 17 in Bujumbura. Three other police officers, including his driver, were arrested before the general. The first officer was reportedly arrested on October 14, and the driver in Kayanza province.

Police sources suggest the detentions may be linked to passports and involve a presidential office official, though this remains unverified. A tense atmosphere prevails at the migration headquarters in northern Bujumbura. The police and administrative authorities have not officially commented on the reasons for the arrests or the charges.

The detentions occur as Burundi approaches an election period, with sources indicating rivalry for Ndababishije's strategic position. The conditions of detention and whether family visits have occurred are unknown. No official statement has been released regarding the arrests or potential charges.
