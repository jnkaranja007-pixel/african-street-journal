Rebel FRN retakes Rurambo and Munanira in eastern Congo

Local sources say the movement also took Muhungu hill and Kahiya, as civilians fled the fighting near Lubarika.

Source: SOS Medias Burundi · 2026-11-26 · 1 min read

The Front for the Refoundation of the Nation (FRN), a rebel movement led by former Congolese president Joseph Kabila, has retaken the localities of Rurambo and Munanira, along with Muhungu hill, in the highlands of Uvira territory in South Kivu, eastern Democratic Republic of Congo, after several days of fighting, SOS Médias Burundi reported. Local sources also said FRN fighters seized Kahiya, on the boundary between Uvira and Walungu territories.

Burundian soldiers are fighting alongside the Congolese armed forces (FARDC) against the movement. Kinshasa accuses Rwanda of backing the rebels, which Kigali denies. Reports from Lubarika said civilians were leaving the affected areas, with some heading to Lubarika and others seeking refuge in mountains above the Ruzizi plain.

Congolese activist André Byaduniya has called for the commander of the FARDC regional military command based in Uvira to leave his post. Vital Kamerhe, a former National Assembly president who leads the Union for the Congolese Nation (UNC), has questioned the army's performance given the large funds allocated to it.

Why it mattersBurundian troops fighting beside Congolese forces in Uvira are under pressure as the FRN takes ground, and civilians are fleeing toward Lubarika and the mountains above the Ruzizi plain.
