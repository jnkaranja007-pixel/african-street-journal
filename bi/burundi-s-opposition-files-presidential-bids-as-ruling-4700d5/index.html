Burundi's opposition files presidential bids as ruling party faces little rivalry

Two parties have named candidates for the 3 May 2027 election, but the main opposition forces are absent from the race.

Source: SOS Medias Burundi · 2026-11-19 · 1 min read

Burundi's Alliance for Peace, Democracy and Reconciliation (APDR) filed the candidacy of its president, Gabriel Banzawitonde, with the national electoral commission (CENI) on Monday, 16 November, without having gathered the full 100 million Burundian-franc deposit. The party says it expects to complete the payment by 22 November.

Banzawitonde told reporters the money was being collected in stages, citing difficulty withdrawing such a large sum from banks at once. He said the party would not have filed unless it was confident of meeting the requirement.

The Congress for Freedom (CNL) chose its leader, Nestor Girukwishaka, as its candidate at a congress on Sunday. He told delegates that not fielding a candidate would be regrettable and could be seen as withdrawing from politics. Incumbent Évariste Ndayishimiye is the candidate of the ruling CNDD-FDD.

Why it mattersAPDR must raise the rest of the 100 million-franc deposit by 22 November or its candidate will not be eligible.
