Burundi holds senior RTNB cameraman at intelligence service after fire

Bonaventure Niyongabo, a 60-year-old cameraman known as Gihuha, has been held since November 6 at the SNR in Bujumbura over a fire at the public broadcaster.

Source: SOS Medias Burundi · 2026-11-17 · 1 min read

Bonaventure Niyongabo, a senior cameraman at Radio Télévision nationale du Burundi (RTNB), has been held since 6 November at the premises of the Service national de renseignement (SNR), the national intelligence service, in Bujumbura. He was arrested in a bar on Avenue du Large in the Mukaza district. Human rights organisations have reported arbitrary detention and mistreatment at the agency's cells.

Niyongabo, 60 and known as Gihuha, was reportedly questioned about a fire at the RTNB days earlier, which the broadcaster's managers had blamed on a short circuit. Three other staff members questioned in the same case, Yollande Niyubahwe, Claudette Butoyi and Prince Ntororwe, were released after questioning. His family is demanding his immediate release, and colleagues say he had no link to the fire and helped firefighters put it out.

Colleagues also say Niyongabo had criticised what they describe as discrimination against RTNB staff who are not members of the ruling party. One employee, speaking anonymously, described a hostile working environment for part of the staff. Colleagues say Niyongabo is approaching retirement age.

Why it mattersNiyongabo's family and colleagues are asking for his release so he can return to work before reaching retirement age.
