Burundi’s refugee agency orders urban return ahead of surprise camp checks

ONPRA representatives demanded formal identification from hundreds of city-dwelling refugees, threatening loss of status for those absent during verification.

Source: SOS Medias Burundi · 2026-11-24 · 1 min read

The National Office for the Protection of Refugees and Stateless Persons (ONPRA) is launching a physical verification operation in all Burundian camps starting this week. The agency has warned that refugees living outside designated camps, particularly in urban centers, risk sanctions including the deactivation of their refugee status if they are absent during the count.

Following a tour by coordinator Richard Uwimana, refugee representatives from five camps—Musasa, Kinama, Kavumu, Nyankanda and Bwagiriza—met with ONPRA to protect students, workers and the sick living in cities. The agency responded by requiring formal documentation to justify their presence; without it, they must return to their registration sites.

Moïse, a street vendor in Bujumbura, said he cannot meet demands for business registers, tax IDs and bank accounts while operating on limited capital. He noted that such requirements are impossible for many informal traders who rely on mobile sales to support families.

Why it mattersRefugees like Moïse, who sell juice and water in Bujumbura, now face deregistration unless they present documents such as tax IDs and bank accounts that their informal trade cannot provide.
