Burundi communes face pressure to turn 22 billion FBu into youth jobs

The 2026-27 budget sets aside 22 billion FBu for each commune, while young people describe scarce work and limited prospects.

Source: Yaga Burundi · 2026-11-28 · 1 min read

Burundi’s 2026-27 budget allocates more than 944 billion FBu to provinces and communes, including 22 billion FBu for each commune. The funds come as many young people struggle to find work, according to Yaga Burundi’s report.

People younger than 35 make up more than 70% of Burundi’s population of 12.33 million, based on the 2024 census. Jeanne Nakiteretse, social affairs adviser in Ntahangwa commune, said the commune gives many tax-collector jobs to young people, but some remain unemployed and further activities are planned.

Vianney Ndayisaba, national coordinator of the Association for the Fight Against Unemployment and Torture, urged communes to use state funds and tax revenue for local economic activity. At a September 23, 2026, planning debate in Gitega, Kiganda’s administrator Diane Kaneza said involving young people in project design remained a challenge.

Why it mattersYoung people seeking work depend on communes to turn their allocations of 22 billion FBu each into employment, while Ntahangwa says further activities are planned.
