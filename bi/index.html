Burundi football team faces Zambia in CAN qualifiers

The national team, Intamba Mu Rugamba, will play two matches against Zambia in November 2026.

Burundi's national football team, known as Intamba Mu Rugamba, will compete against Zambia in the Africa Cup of Nations qualifiers in November 2026. The first match is scheduled for November 13 in Ndola, Zambia, at the Levy Mwanawasa Stadium. The second leg will take place on November 17 in Bujumbura, Burundi.

Historically, Burundi and Zambia have met five times, with one win for each side and three draws, scoring four goals each. Burundi's recent performance shows an average of two goals scored per match, while Zambia's defense has conceded an average of 2.2 goals. A draw in Ndola would be considered an exploit for Burundi.

These qualifiers are for the main tournament, not a friendly competition like the CECAFA tournament. The team's performance in these matches will determine their advancement. The atmosphere in Bujumbura is expected to be highly charged if the team returns from Ndola standing.

Why it mattersBurundian football fans will experience the national team's attempt to qualify for a major continental tournament.

Burundi engineer disappears from sugar company

Three SOSUMO managers questioned by prosecutors after the agricultural equipment maintenance chief went missing on October 30.

Ismaël Mugisha, deputy head of agricultural equipment maintenance at the Moso Sugar Company (SOSUMO) in Rutana province, has been missing since October 30. Three SOSUMO managers, Arnaud Nikuze, Jean Claude Ntwari, and Aimé Clovis Irakoze, were questioned by the Makamba Court of Appeal prosecutor on November 3 regarding his disappearance. Mugisha, who had worked at SOSUMO for four years, was last seen leaving his home for work on the morning of October 30.

Colleagues reported seeing Mugisha at his office that morning, while others did not see him, leaving his exact movements unclear. Internal sources suggest disagreements over the maintenance and management of agricultural equipment, with Mugisha reportedly advocating for repairs over new purchases. Some colleagues expressed suspicions of mismanagement and potential embezzlement related to equipment procurement and upkeep, though these claims are unproven. Mugisha had also told colleagues he felt threatened by some individuals.

SOSUMO announced Mugisha's disappearance in local churches on November 1. The company has been investigating the circumstances surrounding his vanishing, with the prosecutor's office now involved in the inquiry. The investigation aims to clarify the events leading up to his disappearance and determine if any foul play was involved.

Why it mattersThe disappearance of an engineer from a state-owned sugar company raises concerns about potential mismanagement and threats within the workplace, affecting his colleagues and family.

Burundi football team loses six home matches

The national team has suffered five defeats at Levy Mwanawasa Stadium this season.

The Burundi national football team has lost six consecutive home matches at Levy Mwanawasa Stadium. For a long time, teams did not lose in Ndola, a law respected by all.

The team, known as the Chipolopolo, has lost five times at home during the 2025-2026 season. These defeats include a 0-2 loss to Morocco in September 2025.

A 0-1 loss to Tanzania in June 2024 during the World Cup 2026 qualifiers was initially dismissed as an accident. The team also drew 0-0 with Chad.

Why it mattersBurundi's national football team is directly affected by these repeated home losses.
