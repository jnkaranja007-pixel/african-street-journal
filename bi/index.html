Coffee Quality Business buys Bukinanyana coffee and employs 110

Raphaël Yaminyibuka bought a local association and its coffee stock after returning to Burundi in 2023 with 25 million Burundian francs.

Raphaël Yaminyibuka returned to his home hill in Burundi in 2023 with 25 million Burundian francs and bought the Ikawa Umwizero Wacu association as its leaders were liquidating assets. He renamed it Coffee Quality Business and bought the coffee stock producers wanted to sell.

Yaminyibuka says his company commits between 700 and 850 million Burundian francs to coffee purchases during a campaign. He says coffee from Bukinanyana commune accounts for half, or as much as a third, of the commune’s harvest arriving at his business. The company employs 110 people: 60 during the coffee campaign and 50 permanently.

He financed the purchase with a loan from a local microfinance institution. Before returning, he moved to Kenya in 2019 after his father died, worked as a mason in Nairobi and then sold eggs on the street; he says an egg sold for 20 shillings.

Why it mattersCoffee producers in Bukinanyana sell stock to the company, which says it buys between 700 and 850 million Burundian francs’ worth during a campaign.

Burundi opposition parties name presidential candidates

The APDR filed Gabriel Banzawitonde’s bid without the full 100 million Burundian francs deposit, while the CNL chose Nestor Girukwishaka.

Burundi’s APDR and CNL have named candidates for the presidential election scheduled for 3 May 2027, with incumbent Évariste Ndayishimiye running for the ruling CNDD-FDD. The APDR filed its president Gabriel Banzawitonde’s candidacy with the National Independent Electoral Commission on 16 November, but had not raised the required 100 million Burundian francs deposit.

Banzawitonde told journalists he expected to gather the full amount by 22 November, citing difficulty withdrawing such a large sum from banks at once. The CNL’s congress on Sunday selected party official Nestor Girukwishaka; he said the party judged it important to field a candidate rather than risk being seen as leaving politics.

The report says the main opposition forces had also stayed out of the legislative elections. Banzawitonde said the APDR had more reason to expect victory than in 2020, when it backed the ruling party’s candidate.

Sport · 2026-11-18

Burundi clubs split eight league matches across the weekend

Musongati beat Vital’O 2-0 away, while Vision FC, Rukinzo and Panthère Noire also won in the twelfth round.

Burundi’s twelfth league round produced eight matches on Saturday and Sunday, with five winners and three draws. Vision FC beat Muzinga FC 2-0 in Bujumbura, and Never Give Up won 1-0 away to Aigle Noir in Ngozi. Rukinzo defeated Bumamuru 3-1.

On Sunday, Inter Stars beat Flambeau du Centre 1-0, while Young Star and Olympic Star drew 0-0 and Kayanza United and Messager Ngozi finished 1-1. Panthère Noire won 3-1 away to Ngozi City.

Musongati completed the round by beating Vital’O FC 2-0 at home to Vital’O. The results included three away wins, two home wins and three draws.

Why it mattersMusongati took all three points at Vital’O’s home ground with a 2-0 win in the round’s final match.
