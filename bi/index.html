Business · 08/21/2026

Beer prices in Gitega double official rates

Primus and Amstel beers are selling for twice the official price in Gitega. Iwacu reports the price spikes follow Brarudi's decision to export products to the DRC to compensate for a lack of foreign currency.

Why it mattersConsumers in Gitega face higher costs for beverages due to widespread market speculation.

Health · 08/21/2026

Gitega installs first operational scanner outside Bujumbura

A medical scanner is now operational in Gitega. Yaga Burundi reports it is the first such device functioning outside of Bujumbura in 2026.

Why it mattersPatients in the Gitega region can now access diagnostic imaging without traveling to the capital.

Politics · 08/21/2026

CENI presents Gitega electoral map for 2027

The Independent National Electoral Commission (CENI) presented the electoral mapping for Gitega province on August 19. SOS Médias Burundi reports the move is part of preparations for the 2027 presidential election.

Why it mattersThe mapping defines the specific voting locations and boundaries for residents of Gitega province.

Sport · 08/21/2026

Burundi wins opening match at Beach Pro Tour Bujumbura

The Burundi men's team defeated Germany in the opening match of the Volleyball World Beach Pro Tour Bujumbura Futures. The tournament runs from August 19 to 23 on the shores of Lake Tanganyika, according to Iwacu.

Why it mattersThe victory marks a successful start for the host nation in an international sporting competition.

Climate · 08/21/2026

Rusizi National Park receives new protection infrastructure

The Rusizi National Park has acquired new equipment and infrastructure to manage biodiversity and tourism. The handover ceremony took place August 13 with representatives from the EU, UNDP, and the Belgian government, Iwacu reports.

Why it mattersThe new installations are intended to increase the park's ability to protect wildlife and attract more tourists.
