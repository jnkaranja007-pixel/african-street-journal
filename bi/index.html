Burundi soldier shoots man smuggling fuel

A man is in critical condition after being shot by a soldier while transporting fuel from the DRC.

A man was critically injured by a gunshot on Thursday evening, October 29, 2026, in Kaburantwa, Bukinanyana commune, Bujumbura province. The victim was riding a motorcycle carrying fuel containers from the Democratic Republic of Congo when a soldier fired at his legs after he refused to stop. He was taken to a health center in Kaburantwa before being transferred to Prince Régent Charles Hospital in Bujumbura.

Local administration stated the soldier shot the man's legs when he did not comply with orders. The fuel enters Burundi from the RDC via the Rusizi River and is collected near the border before being sold illegally. Residents questioned the use of live ammunition, suggesting a warning shot could have been used. This incident occurs amid ongoing tensions between law enforcement and fuel smugglers.

The victim remained in critical condition at the hospital. This event highlights the risks associated with the clandestine fuel trade, which supplies markets in Burundi, including Bujumbura, where prices can be five times the official rate. Burundi has faced fuel shortages for nearly six years, leading some citizens to travel to the RDC for supplies.

Why it mattersA man was critically injured by a gunshot while involved in the illegal fuel trade.

Burundi U15 team finishes top 8 in division

The Burundi U15 team reached the quarter-finals of Division D at the FIFA U15 World Cup Festival in Baku.

The Burundi U15 team competed in the first FIFA U15 World Cup and Festival in Baku, Azerbaijan, on October 30, 2026. The tournament featured 189 nations playing football at eight a side. Burundi was placed in Level D, the fourth tier of the competition, which ranked teams from 97th to 128th globally.

Burundi's journey included a 4-3 victory against Togo in the Level D round of 16 and a 2-1 win over Cape Verde in the round of 8. However, their progress was halted by a 7-0 defeat to Canada in the Level D quarter-finals on October 28. Reaching these quarter-finals placed Burundi in the Top 8 of Division D.

Spain was crowned the overall world champion after winning Level A, defeating the Czech Republic in the final. Eight African nations, including Algeria, Morocco, Angola, Cameroon, Guinea, Egypt, Ghana, and Kenya, finished as group leaders. Guinea achieved the best African performance, finishing 6th overall.

PAM equips Burundi institutions with 74 devices

Computers, printers, and scanners will strengthen public services for school feeding and social protection.

The World Food Programme (WFP) provided 74 pieces of equipment, including laptops, printers, and scanners, to Burundi's National Office for Refugees and Stateless Persons (ONPRA) and the Permanent Executive Secretariat of the National Social Protection Commission (SEP/CNPS) on October 30, 2026. The equipment aims to enhance the work capabilities of public services involved in school feeding and social protection initiatives.

Of the total devices, 27 laptops and 27 printers/scanners are allocated to education services in 27 communes, while 14 laptops and 6 printers/scanners will support the social protection sector. This initiative accompanies the increasing responsibility of communes in organizing school meals, with WFP support and under the Ministry of National Education and Scientific Research.

The national school feeding program, PNASE, prioritizes locally produced food, connecting children's education with economic opportunities for agricultural producers and local processing units. Schools will source supplies from cooperatives, fostering a link between education and local economic development.

Why it mattersBurundian agricultural producers and local processing units will benefit from increased demand for locally sourced food for school meals.
