Burundi president sacks security chief amid corruption probe

Colonel Alfred Innocent Museremu removed from his post as permanent secretary of the National Security Council, replaced by retired Colonel Domitien Kabisa.

Burundi's President Évariste Ndayishimiye dismissed Colonel Alfred Innocent Museremu from his role as permanent secretary of the National Security Council on Saturday, November 14. The presidential decree appointing his successor, retired Colonel Domitien Kabisa, was signed on the same day. The official reasons for Museremu's removal were not stated in the decree.

While the presidential decree offers no explanation, unverified reports from SOS Médias Burundi link Museremu's dismissal to an investigation involving businessman Aloys Ntakarutimana, accused of trading with the M23 rebel group. This investigation has already led to the arrest of the director of Mpimba central prison and two other officials for allegedly granting Ntakarutimana special privileges. Museremu was reportedly interrogated for several days by the National Intelligence Service in Bujumbura.

Human rights organizations have previously implicated Colonel Museremu in arbitrary arrests and torture dating back to the 2015-2016 crisis, when he held positions within the Bujumbura police. His departure occurs amidst ongoing accusations of human rights violations and his alleged connection to the Aloys Ntakarutimana case, though authorities have not provided any official statement on the matter.

Why it mattersColonel Alfred Innocent Museremu was removed from his position as permanent secretary of the National Security Council on November 14, with unverified reports linking his dismissal to an investigation into a businessman accused of trading with rebels.

Burundi police arrest opposition official over widow's house

Elias Kagoma of the UPRONA party was detained by ruling CNDD-FDD officials in Giharo zone, southeast Burundi, over a house built for a widow.

Elias Kagoma, a representative of the UPRONA party in Giharo zone, Musongati commune, in Burunga province, southeast Burundi, was arrested on Tuesday 10 November by several local officials of the ruling CNDD-FDD party. Kagoma is accused of trying to credit his party with the construction of a house for a widow, an act that was in fact a gesture of solidarity.

The arrest was carried out by Alexis Baraguma, known as Ruganzizindi, the CNDD-FDD leader in Giharo zone, accompanied by zone chief Désiré Hakizimana, CNDD-FDD leader Simon Nivyayo and hill chief Claver Mugisha. The dispute concerns a house for Thérèse Ntirampeba, a widow from Nkurye, and officials say the CNDD-FDD claims the initiative. Kagoma denies helping to fund or build the house, saying he only aided the widow and her children after meeting them at Giharo market, where they were sleeping in sheds, and took them into his home on 3 August 2026.

The case has renewed concern about political intolerance in some parts of Burundi, and about administrative officials taking part in arrests of opposition figures. Administrative and judicial authorities are expected to explain the circumstances of the arrest and any legal grounds for detaining Kagoma.

Why it mattersOpposition members in Giharo now face arrest by ruling party officials, and Kagoma's detention is a test of whether local administrators can act against rival politicians.

Vision FC tops Burundi's league table with 27 points from 12 matches

Vision FC has a goal difference of 21, while Muzinga FC sits three points behind in second place with 24 points from 12 games.

Vision FC leads the Burundi football championship with 27 points from 12 matches and a goal difference of 21. Muzinga FC, in second place, has 24 points from the same number of games, a gap of only three points.

Rukinzo FC is third on 20 points, but has played only 10 matches, two fewer than most of the league. Musongati FC, which beat Vital'O, also has 20 points from 12 games and sits just behind them.

Olympic Star has 18 points from 11 matches. Messager Ngozi has 17 points from 10 matches, and Bumamuru FC and Never Give Up each have 17 points from 12 games. Ngozi City has 15 points from 11 matches.
