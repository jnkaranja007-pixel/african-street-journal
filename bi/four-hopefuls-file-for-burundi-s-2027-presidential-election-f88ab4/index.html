Four hopefuls file for Burundi’s 2027 presidential election

The candidates include two independents and nominees of FPN Imboneza and CNL, the Congrès National pour la Liberté party, with one filing missing a 100 million FBu deposit receipt.

Source: Iwacu · 2026-11-23 · 1 min read

Burundi’s electoral commission received candidacy files from four hopefuls for the presidential election scheduled for 3 May 2027: independent Abel Nduwimana, FPN Imboneza candidate Valentin Kavakure, independent Dieudonné Nahimana and Nestor Girukwishaka of the CNL, the Congrès National pour la Liberté party.

Kavakure’s file lacked the receipt for the required 100 million Burundi francs deposit, Iwacu reported. He said the document would be provided and urged the commission to allow the press to follow the election process, especially the vote count and announcement of results.

Nduwimana, a former priest, said after filing that he would answer questions at a forthcoming news conference. Nahimana said his campaign sought to advance the welfare of all Burundians without exception amid social inequalities.

Why it mattersKavakure must provide the receipt for the 100 million FBu deposit, while Nduwimana said he plans to hold a news conference.
