Peace torch visit shuts Bubanza market for most of a day

Traders reopened at 16 h 30, while schools in the town’s commercial centre closed so pupils and teachers could attend the reception.

Source: SOS Medias Burundi · 2026-11-25 · 1 min read

Burundi’s peace torch caravan disrupted trade and schooling in Bubanza on 19 November, when the market remained closed for almost the whole day and most schools in the commercial centre stopped classes. The caravan passed through the commune in western Burundi as residents gathered for its reception.

The central market reopened at 16 h 30, only an hour and a half before its usual closing time, SOS Medias Burundi reported. Traders said repeated disruptions cost them a day’s work, with incomes dependent on daily sales. Most schools shut their doors from 10 o’clock so pupils and teachers could join the welcome; pupils were told to wear civilian clothes instead of uniforms.

The 2026 caravan, its twentieth edition, began on 13 November in Bujumbura and is scheduled to end on 10 December in Gitega. Organisers say it will visit communes for community development work and provide material support to some local projects.

Why it mattersMarket traders lost most of a day’s sales, reopening only at 16 h 30, and some residents also gave up time for farm work.
