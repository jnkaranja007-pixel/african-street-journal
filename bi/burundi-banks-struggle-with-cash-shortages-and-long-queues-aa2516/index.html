Burundi banks struggle with cash shortages and long queues

Customers face rationed withdrawals and delayed transfers, eroding trust in the banking system.

Source: Yaga Burundi · 2026-11-12 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Burundians are experiencing significant difficulties accessing their money from banks, with long queues, rationed withdrawals, and unavailable liquidity becoming common. Customers report waiting for hours, witnessing what they describe as preferential treatment for some clients, while others face refusals to withdraw their own funds due to a lack of cash, even in local currency.

Beyond withdrawal issues, bank transfers are reportedly delayed for weeks due to inadequate systems or processing failures. This situation directly impacts clients, leading to doubts about their solvency, tarnished reputations, and employees waiting for salaries that have already been transferred. The banks' inability to execute basic transactions and return funds to customers undermines their credibility.

While cultural habits of saving at home are often cited as reasons for distrust, the current banking system's failures make this practice a rational risk management strategy. The promises of security, availability, and fair treatment are compromised by endless queues, nepotism, cash shortages, and withdrawal limits, leading many to keep their money at home for certainty, time, peace, and dignity.

Why it mattersBurundians are unable to access their own money reliably, impacting their daily lives and financial stability.
