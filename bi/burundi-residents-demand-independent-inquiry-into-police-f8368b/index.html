Burundi residents demand independent inquiry into police chief's river death

Police in Bugendana say Sébastien Niragira killed himself, but residents of Jenda hill dispute that account and are seeking an independent investigation.

Source: SOS Medias Burundi · 2026-11-25 · 1 min read

Sébastien Niragira, the chief of a police post in Buraza, was found dead in the Ruvyironza River on Sunday, 22 November, at the foot of Jenda hill in Bugendana commune, Gitega province, in central Burundi. Police in Bugendana say he jumped into the river, linking the act to family conflicts over an adultery case.

Residents of Jenda hill reject that explanation. They say he may have been killed elsewhere and his body thrown into the river to make it look like suicide, and they want an independent inquiry to establish the circumstances and bring any responsible people to justice. Stany Nahimana, an elected local official on the hill, said the circumstances of the death had not yet been clearly established. Police have taken the body to the regional hospital mortuary, and investigations are continuing.

SOS Médias Burundi counts more than 100 bodies and people killed in varied circumstances across Gitega province since the start of 2026.

Why it mattersNiragira's family and the Jenda residents now depend on the ongoing police investigation, and residents are asking authorities to open an independent inquiry into how he died.
