Burundi team wins two matches in one day

The Hirondeaux beat Togo 4-3 and then Cape Verde 2-0 in Baku on Wednesday.

Source: Iwacu · 2026-10-30 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Burundi's Hirondeaux team faced Togo in a knockout match on Wednesday at 7:30 GMT, winning 4-3 after a back-and-forth game. Later that day, at 18:15 Baku time, they played Cape Verde at the Hovsan Stadium. The Burundian team adopted a compact and solid defensive strategy against the possession-focused Cape Verdeans.

The Hirondeaux secured a 2-0 victory against Cape Verde with two sharp counter-attacks. This followed a group stage where the team experienced mixed results, including initial losses to Portugal and Wales, followed by wins against Fiji and Bangladesh. Their final group match against Indonesia resulted in a significant 6-1 defeat.

This double victory on Wednesday advances the Hirondeaux in the tournament. The team demonstrated resilience after earlier losses and a heavy defeat in their final group game. Their performance against Togo, a team that had scored 13 goals in the first round, highlights their competitive spirit.

Why it mattersBurundian athletes competed and won two football matches on the same day.
