Burundi football team faces Zambia in CAN qualifiers

The national team, known as Intamba Mu Rugamba, will play two matches in November 2026.

Source: Iwacu · 2026-11-07 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Burundi's national football team, the Intamba Mu Rugamba, is set to face Zambia in two matches for the Africa Cup of Nations qualifiers in November 2026. The first match will be played on November 13 in Ndola, Zambia, at the Levy Mwanawasa Stadium. The return leg is scheduled for November 17 in Bujumbura, Burundi.

Historically, Burundi has a balanced record against Zambia, with one win, three draws, and one loss in five previous encounters, conceding four goals and scoring four. Zambia's defense has recently been conceding an average of 2.2 goals per game, while Burundi's offense averages two goals per match. A draw in Ndola would be considered an exploit fondateur for Burundi.

These qualifiers represent a step up from the CECAFA tournament, marking a more serious challenge for the team. If the Intamba returns from Ndola standing, the November 17 match in Bujumbura will be a communion.

Why it mattersBurundian football fans and the national team will be directly affected by the outcome of these CAN qualifier matches.
