Cholera infects at least 12 Saint-Marc students amid water shortage

The boarding school in Bujumbura has reportedly lacked potable water for nearly two weeks, while parents say students cannot return home.

Source: SOS Medias Burundi · 2026-11-28 · 1 min read

At least 12 students at Lycée Saint-Marc, a boarding school in Bujumbura, have contracted cholera since Wednesday, SOS Médias Burundi reported. Students said the school had been without potable water for nearly two weeks and linked the shortage to the spread of the disease.

Seven students were taken to Prince Régent Charles Hospital on Wednesday, and five more were brought there on Thursday, according to the report. The school’s director confirmed cholera cases but declined to give further details. Students said their parents had not been informed; parents said the director would not allow students to return to their families.

Cases were also reported in Cibitoke and Bwiza, where eight people were said to be affected. Administrative authorities confirmed the reports but gave no further details.
