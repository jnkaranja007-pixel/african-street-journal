Vital'O FC Calls Extraordinary Meeting to Address Club's Future

The historic football club is convening a general assembly to discuss its current challenges and future prospects.

Vital'O Football Club has announced an extraordinary general assembly scheduled for Wednesday, October 21, 2026, at 9 AM. The meeting will take place at Safi Garden, a location chosen for its historical significance, mirroring the club's own long legacy.

The club, established in 1957, likens itself to an old tree whose roots remain strong but whose foliage requires care. This assembly aims to bring together its supporters to openly discuss the club's current situation, the obstacles it faces, and the potential pathways forward.

The organizers emphasize that every voice and constructive idea is valuable in strengthening the club and ensuring its future aligns with its storied past and ambitions. The meeting is intended to foster a transparent exchange on how to fortify the club and secure its continued success.

Why it mattersVital'O FC is holding a meeting to discuss its financial and operational challenges, which could impact its business operations and future viability.
