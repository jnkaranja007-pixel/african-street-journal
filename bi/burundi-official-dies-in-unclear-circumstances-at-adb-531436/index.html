Burundi official dies in unclear circumstances at ADB

Bruce Sebanza was buried without an autopsy amid allegations of torture and calls for investigation.

Source: SOS Medias Burundi · 2026-10-23 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Bruce Sebanza, an official at the Burundi Development Agency (ADB), died on October 13, 2026, at the agency's premises in Bujumbura. He was buried on Wednesday, October 21, without a public autopsy, prompting questions about the circumstances of his death. The National Independent Human Rights Commission (CNIDH) visited the ADB to investigate and requested official inquiries into the matter.

During a service in Bujumbura, Sebanza's mother described his death as "barbaric torture" and praised his professionalism. Relatives who went to the Kamenge military hospital on October 13 reported finding his body transported in a government pickup truck, unclothed except for a soiled singlet and bloody underwear. They noted his body was still warm and showed signs of abrasions consistent with being dragged.

Witnesses reported hearing distress calls from the ADB premises on the night of October 12 to October 13. Some accounts from the morgue staff suggested physical marks on the body could indicate constriction, possibly by a rope. No independent medical or legal conclusions on the cause of death have been made public.

Why it mattersThe death of Bruce Sebanza, an ADB official, is being investigated due to allegations of torture and suspicious circumstances surrounding his burial.
