Club owner offers 300 million for victory

The club owner has offered a 300 million prize to motivate the team to win.

The club owner has offered a 300 million prize, challenging the team to give their all and fight for every moment. This significant sum signals the club's ambition to compete at the highest level, not just participate. The team has been reinforced with new recruits, described as forging an army ready to attack and make history.

Despite the financial backing and the prepared squad, the club is calling for public support. The owner emphasizes that the team needs the energy and voices of the fans to create an intimidating atmosphere. The appeal is for every seat to vibrate and every chant to be heard, aiming to create a roar that resonates widely.

The upcoming match is framed as more than just a game for eleven players; it is presented as a collective defense of Burundi's colors. The club is urging the public to attend and lend their support, emphasizing that the entire nation is behind the team in this crucial contest.

Why it mattersThe team's performance and potential success could bring national pride and recognition to Burundi.
