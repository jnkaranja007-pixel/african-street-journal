Imbonerakure truck crash in Kabanga leaves 31 dead, sources say 75

President Évariste Ndayishimiye put the death toll at 31, while anonymous security sources and local ruling-party officials say at least 75 people were killed.

Sources: SOS Medias Burundi and Iwacu · 2026-11-17 · 1 min read

A truck carrying more than 150 members of the Imbonerakure, the youth league of Burundi's ruling CNDD-FDD party, ran into a crowd gathered at the Kabanga trading centre in Busoni commune, Butanyerera province, on Sunday, 15 November. President Évariste Ndayishimiye said on X on Monday that 31 citizens had died and several were injured, offering condolences to the families.

Anonymous security sources and local CNDD-FDD officials told SOS Médias Burundi that the toll was at least 75, a figure the outlet could not confirm independently. Witnesses accuse authorities and party representatives of rushing burials and preventing photographs, which they say was meant to limit the spread of information about the disaster.

According to local sources, the truck was returning from a paramilitary-style training on Mukinya hill, organised by party secretary-general Révérien Ndikuriyo, with new recruits on board. Some local officials instead say the young people were vulnerable people clearing trees for a government avocado model field, a claim local sources dispute.

Why it mattersFamilies of the dead and injured now face a disputed official toll, and the discrepancy leaves the true number of victims unconfirmed.
