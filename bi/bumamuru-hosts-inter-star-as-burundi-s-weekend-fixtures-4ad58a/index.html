Bumamuru hosts Inter Star as Burundi’s weekend fixtures begin

The teams meet at Stade Urunani at 15:00 on Friday, followed by Muzinga FC against Rukinzo FC at the same venue on Saturday.

Source: Iwacu · 2026-11-20 · 1 min read

Bumamuru FC hosts Inter Star at Stade Urunani on Friday, 20 November, at 15:00, opening a weekend of Burundi football fixtures. The teams are listed for the 13th matchday.

Muzinga FC faces Rukinzo FC at the same stadium on Saturday, 21 November, at 13:00. The fixture listing gives no results or league standings for either match.

Both games are scheduled at Stade Urunani; the report names no other fixtures.

Why it mattersSupporters of the four clubs have two scheduled matches at Stade Urunani, with the second starting at 13:00 on Saturday.
