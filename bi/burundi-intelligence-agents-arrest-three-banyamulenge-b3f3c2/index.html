Burundi intelligence agents arrest three Banyamulenge refugees in Bujumbura

Families say they have heard nothing about the men since their arrest on 20 November, and one was due to marry the next day.

Source: SOS Medias Burundi · 2026-11-23 · 1 min read

Agents of Burundi's National Intelligence Service (SNR) arrested three young Banyamulenge refugees in Bujumbura on Friday, 20 November, according to testimonies gathered by SOS Médias Burundi. The men are Alexis Ndanyuzwe, Patrick Mukeka Ndungutse and Alexis Bagirisoko.

Relatives said the three were at a sauna when agents detained them and drove them to an unknown destination. Their families have received no information about where the men are held or why they were arrested. Bagirisoko was due to marry Chantal Nyahumure, who had travelled from the United States for the ceremony, on Saturday, 21 November.

Community sources said more than 30 refugees from the Banyamulenge community have been arrested and imprisoned in Burundi since September. Some were held at SNR offices before being moved to prisons around the country, sometimes far from their families.

Why it mattersFamilies of the three men, including Nyahumure, who flew in from the United States for the wedding, still have no word on where the men are held or on any charge against them.
