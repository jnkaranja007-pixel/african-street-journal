Two bodies discovered in Gitega province

A 33-year-old woman and a newborn were found dead on November 5 and 6.

Source: SOS Medias Burundi · 2026-11-09 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Two bodies were discovered in Burundi's Gitega province on November 5 and 6. The victims were a 33-year-old woman and a newborn infant. Three people have been arrested in connection with the woman's death, and two others have been summoned by police regarding the infant.

On November 6, the body of Marie Rose Nkurunziza, 33, was found in a bush in Bugendana commune, with her throat cut. A local official confirmed the death, stating that the motive is unclear but a refused abortion is being considered. Tharcisse, Moussa, and Pascal were arrested on the same day. The previous day, November 5, a newborn's body was found in Gishubi commune, believed to have been abandoned by its mother. Daphrose Niyonzima and her daughter Aline Nizigiyimana have been summoned to appear before police on November 9.

These discoveries follow other recent deaths with disputed circumstances in the province. On October 27, a man was found hanged in Gitega, with residents questioning the suicide narrative. Investigations are ongoing to clarify the circumstances of these two recent deaths and establish responsibility.

Why it mattersResidents of Gitega province are affected by recent discoveries of bodies and ongoing investigations into suspicious deaths.
