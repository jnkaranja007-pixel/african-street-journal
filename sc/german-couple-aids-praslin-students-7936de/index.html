German couple aids Praslin students

Conny and Simone Baum visit Seychelles for the fifth time to help develop local students' skills.

Source: Seychelles Broadcasting Corp · 2026-11-04 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

A German couple, Conny and Simone Baum, are visiting Seychelles for their fifth time, focusing on aiding local children's development in arts. They are currently working with students at the Grand Anse Praslin Primary School.

The couple's initiative aims to provide skills development for Seychellois children, going beyond the typical tourist experience. Their engagement with the students at Grand Anse Praslin Primary School marks a continued commitment to the island's youth.

Visitors to Seychelles often focus on what the islands can offer them, but the Baum's visit highlights a reciprocal relationship. Their efforts are directed towards nurturing the artistic talents of young Seychellois.
