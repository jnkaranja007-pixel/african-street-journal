German couple aids Praslin students

Conny and Simone Baum visit Seychelles for the fifth time to help children at Grand Anse Praslin Primary School develop in the arts.

Source: Seychelles Broadcasting Corp · 2026-11-05 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

German visitors Conny and Simone Baum are in Seychelles for their fifth visit, aiming to help local children develop in the arts. They are currently working with students at Grand Anse Praslin Primary School.

The couple's engagement focuses on fostering the development of the children. This initiative shows visitors contributing to the community beyond typical tourist activities.

The Baums' continued presence in Seychelles demonstrates their commitment to supporting the island's youth through educational enrichment.
