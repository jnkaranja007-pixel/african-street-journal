Seychelles commission works on rights report

The TRNUC Enforcement Commission is making efforts to fulfill recommendations from the TRNUC report.

Source: Seychelles Broadcasting Corp · 2026-11-05 · 1 min read

The TRNUC Enforcement Commission reported that despite challenges, it is putting significant effort into fulfilling the recommendations of the TRNUC report. The TRNUC was established in Seychelles to address human rights violations that occurred during the single-party era, aiming to foster national unity.

The commission is working to complete the tasks outlined in the TRNUC report. These efforts are directed at addressing past human rights abuses and promoting a unified national identity.

The TRNUC report was created to provide an opportunity to address human rights violations in Seychelles. The commission's current work focuses on implementing these recommendations to achieve national unity.

Why it mattersSeychellois citizens affected by past human rights violations will see the TRNUC report's recommendations addressed.
