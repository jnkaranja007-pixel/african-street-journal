Seychelles repairs 200 flood-damaged manholes

Public Utilities Company workers are fixing drainage systems damaged by recent flooding on La Digue.

Source: Seychelles Broadcasting Corp · 2026-10-23 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Public Utilities Company (PUC) workers are repairing manholes connected to its drainage system on La Digue island, which were damaged by recent flooding. The work is ongoing in the Anse Reunion and La Passe areas.

Approximately 200 manholes have been repaired so far, with an additional 300 still needing attention. PUC officials and contractors were observed assessing the progress of the repairs.

The repairs aim to restore the functionality of the drainage system following the flood damage.

Why it mattersResidents of La Digue are affected by the ongoing repairs to drainage manholes damaged by flooding.
