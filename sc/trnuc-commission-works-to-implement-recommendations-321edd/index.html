TRNUC commission works to implement recommendations

The TRNUC Enforcement Commission is making significant efforts to fulfill the recommendations of the TRNUC report.

Source: Seychelles Broadcasting Corp · 2026-11-04 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

The TRNUC Enforcement Commission stated that despite facing challenges, it is dedicating considerable effort to complete the recommendations from the TRNUC report. This commission was established to address human rights violations that occurred in Seychelles, particularly during the era of a single political party, with the aim of fostering national unity.

The commission is actively working to implement these recommendations, which stem from the TRNUC's mandate to investigate past human rights abuses. The report itself was a product of the TRNUC's establishment as an opportunity to deal with these violations and promote reconciliation.

The TRNUC report's recommendations are being pursued by the commission as a path toward national unity. The commission's work is ongoing, focusing on the implementation of these findings to address historical issues.

Why it mattersThe TRNUC Enforcement Commission is working to address past human rights violations in Seychelles.
