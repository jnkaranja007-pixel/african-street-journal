Praslin children compete in third 'Art Talent Quest'

Primary and secondary students from Praslin and La Digue created art inspired by local hotels.

The third edition of the 'Art Talent Quest' competition took place on Praslin island. Children from primary and secondary schools on Praslin and La Digue participated in the event.

Students spent a day drawing or painting artworks. Their creations were themed around the local hotels 'Oasis' and 'Le Duc de Praslin'.

This cultural event provided a platform for young artists to showcase their skills and engage with their community's landmarks.

Seychelles licensing official charged over permits

An employee of the Seychelles Licensing Authority faces charges for allegedly distributing driver's permits illegally.

An employee of the Seychelles Licensing Authority has been charged in connection with a suspected case of illegal driver's permit distribution. The authority confirmed the charge against the employee on Saturday.

The Minister responsible for Finance announced that authorities are now only accepting driver's permits in card form, not numerical ones. This change follows the alleged illegal distribution of permits.

The authority is investigating the circumstances surrounding the distribution of permits. Further details on the investigation are expected.
