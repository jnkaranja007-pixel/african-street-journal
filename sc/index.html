Seychelles backs Lana Nusseibeh for UN chief

The island nation supports the candidate in a race with seven contenders for the top UN position.

Seychelles announced its support for Lana Nusseibeh's bid to become the next United Nations Secretary-General. There are a total of seven candidates vying for the position.

This marks a potential historic moment as it is possible for a woman to hold the UN's highest office for the first time. Nusseibeh is one of seven individuals seeking the role.

The announcement comes as the selection process for the next UN Secretary-General continues.

Why it mattersSeychelles is supporting Lana Nusseibeh's bid for the position of United Nations Secretary-General.

Seychelles parliament amends tobacco control law

Lawmakers voted unanimously to update the 2009 Tobacco Control Act for new products like vapes.

The Seychelles National Assembly approved a bill to amend the Tobacco Control Act from 2009. All 28 members present at yesterday's session voted in favour.

These amendments are necessary due to new tobacco products entering the market, such as electronic cigarettes or 'vapes'. The proposed changes will ensure these new products are properly regulated and controlled.

The bill's approval by the assembly is a step towards updating the country's tobacco regulations.

Why it mattersThe National Assembly's vote to amend the Tobacco Control Act will lead to new regulations for products like vapes.

Seychelles commission works on rights report

The TRNUC Enforcement Commission is making efforts to fulfill recommendations from the TRNUC report.

The TRNUC Enforcement Commission reported that despite challenges, it is putting significant effort into fulfilling the recommendations of the TRNUC report. The TRNUC was established in Seychelles to address human rights violations that occurred during the single-party era, aiming to foster national unity.

The commission is working to complete the tasks outlined in the TRNUC report. These efforts are directed at addressing past human rights abuses and promoting a unified national identity.

The TRNUC report was created to provide an opportunity to address human rights violations in Seychelles. The commission's current work focuses on implementing these recommendations to achieve national unity.

Why it mattersSeychellois citizens affected by past human rights violations will see the TRNUC report's recommendations addressed.
