Regina Melanie literary prize honours students

Lenstiti Kreol and the Ministry of Tourism and Culture celebrated young writers at the 6th Regina Melanie Literary Prize.

Lenstiti Kreol, in partnership with the Ministry of Tourism and Culture, honoured students who performed well in the 6th Regina Melanie Literary Prize competition. Several primary schools participated in the event, including Mont Fleuri, Anse Boileau, Bel Ombre, Grand Anse Praslin, Au Cap, Grand Anse Mahe, and Takamaka.

The competition, named the Regina Melanie Literary Prize, aims to encourage literary talent among young Seychellois. The Ministry of Tourism and Culture supports initiatives that foster cultural expression and creativity within the nation's youth.

This year marked the sixth iteration of the literary prize, highlighting a continued commitment to nurturing the next generation of writers in Seychelles. The participating schools represent diverse regions across the islands, showcasing a broad engagement with the arts.

Why it mattersYoung students in Seychelles were recognised for their literary achievements.

Seychelles prepares for December rainy season

Government announces measures to mitigate flooding and reduce impact of heavy rains.

Preparations are underway in Seychelles for the upcoming rainy season in December, with work to reduce the impact of heavy rainfall continuing. Measures implemented over the past year have helped lessen flooding in central regions.

Vice President Sebastien Pillay stated at a press conference yesterday at State House that the government is putting several measures in place for the rainy season.

The work to mitigate the effects of heavy rain needs to continue in preparation for the season which begins next month.

Why it mattersSeychelles residents will be affected by government measures to reduce flooding during the rainy season.
