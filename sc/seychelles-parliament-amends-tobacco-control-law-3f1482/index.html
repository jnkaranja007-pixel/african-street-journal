Seychelles parliament amends tobacco control law

The National Assembly voted to update the 2009 law to regulate new products like vapes.

Source: Seychelles Broadcasting Corp · 2026-11-06 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

The Seychelles National Assembly approved a bill to amend the 2009 Tobacco Control Act. All 28 members present in the session voted in favour of the proposed changes.

These amendments are necessary to address new tobacco products entering the market, such as electronic cigarettes, also known as vapes. The new proposals aim to ensure these products are properly regulated and controlled.

The bill's approval means new regulations will apply to these emerging products.

Why it mattersSeychelles residents consuming new tobacco products like vapes will now be subject to updated regulations.
