Former DRDM Director Knew of Explosives at CCCL Site

Robert Ernesta stated that all relevant partners were aware of explosives stored in containers at the Civil Construction Company Limited facility.

Robert Ernesta, former director general of the disaster risk and management division, confirmed that stakeholders knew about explosives present at the Civil Construction Company Limited (CCCL) site in Providence. The explosives were being kept in containers on the company's premises.

Ernesta's statement indicates a prior awareness among involved parties regarding the hazardous materials. The specific nature of the explosives and their intended use or origin were not detailed in the provided information. The location at CCCL, Providence, was identified as the storage site.

This disclosure raises questions about safety protocols and communication channels concerning potentially dangerous materials stored within commercial facilities. The extent of the 'partners concerned' and their roles in managing or overseeing the explosives remains unclear from the initial report.

Why it mattersInvestors may be concerned about the storage of explosives at a construction company, potentially impacting operational safety and regulatory compliance.
