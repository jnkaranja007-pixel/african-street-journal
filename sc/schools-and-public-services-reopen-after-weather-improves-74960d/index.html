Schools and public services reopen after weather improves

Schools and public services resumed operations today following an improvement in weather conditions, according to the Department of Risk and Disaster Management.

Source: Seychelles Broadcasting Corp · 2026-10-22 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Schools and public services reopened today in Seychelles after a period of adverse weather. The announcement came from Daniel Cetoupe, Principal Secretary for the Department of Risk and Disaster Management (DRDM). Cetoupe stated that the decision to resume normal operations was based on the latest information received from meteorological authorities indicating improved weather.

The DRDM has been monitoring the situation closely, coordinating with the meteorological office to assess the safety and feasibility of reopening public facilities. The department's statement confirmed that the weather has now stabilized, allowing for the resumption of essential services and educational activities across the country.

This reopening signifies a return to normalcy for many residents who rely on these public services and for students and staff returning to educational institutions. The DRDM has assured the public that they will continue to monitor weather patterns to ensure the safety and well-being of the community.

Why it mattersResidents of Seychelles are affected as schools and public services resume normal operations after a period of disruption due to weather.
