Seychelles broadcasters meet on technology adoption

Public television and radio stations from Southern Africa are meeting in Seychelles to discuss technology.

Source: Seychelles Broadcasting Corp · 2026-10-24 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Seychelles Broadcasting Corporation (SBC) faces challenges adopting new technologies while meeting audience needs. This is being discussed at the 10th conference for public television and radio stations in the Southern Africa region. The meeting is taking place at the Avani Barbarons hotel today and tomorrow.

The conference addresses how public broadcasters can adapt to technological changes. Delegates are discussing ways to integrate new platforms and methods to reach audiences effectively. This is part of the ongoing discussions for public broadcasting in the region.

The event gathers representatives from public broadcasting stations. They will share experiences and best practices to ensure broadcasters remain relevant.

Why it mattersSeychellois citizens who consume media will be affected by how their public broadcasters adopt new technologies.
