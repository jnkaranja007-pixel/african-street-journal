Seychelles opposition leader Bernard Georges calls 2027 budget a small-change plan

Georges said the budget made no major announcements and was a small-change plan, cutting into the spending response the government had promised.

Source: Seychelles Broadcasting Corp · 2026-11-18 · 1 min read

Bernard Georges, leader of the opposition in Seychelles' National Assembly, said the 2027 budget was a "small-change" plan that contained no major announcements. He made the comment on Wednesday morning while responding to Finance Minister Pierre Laporte's budget speech.

Laporte presented the budget speech on 22 November. Georges's criticism centred on the absence of major announcements, which he said made the proposal a minor adjustment rather than a shift in policy.

The report gives no spending totals or specific measures in the budget, and it does not say what the government will do next in response to the criticism.
