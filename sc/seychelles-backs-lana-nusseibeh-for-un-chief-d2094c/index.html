Seychelles backs Lana Nusseibeh for UN chief

The island nation supports the candidate in a race with seven contenders for the top UN position.

Source: Seychelles Broadcasting Corp · 2026-11-05 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Seychelles announced its support for Lana Nusseibeh's bid to become the next United Nations Secretary-General. There are a total of seven candidates vying for the position.

This marks a potential historic moment as it is possible for a woman to hold the UN's highest office for the first time. Nusseibeh is one of seven individuals seeking the role.

The announcement comes as the selection process for the next UN Secretary-General continues.
