Seychellois elders and youth unite for Creole Language Day

A group of elders and young Seychellois people met to share experiences and discuss during a workshop.

Source: Seychelles Broadcasting Corp · 2026-11-03 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

A group of elders and young Seychellois people gathered at the International Conference Centre on Wednesday to commemorate International Creole Language and Culture Day. The event focused on sharing experiences and encouraging more active community participation among different age groups.

The workshop provided a platform for intergenerational dialogue, allowing participants to learn from each other and foster a deeper understanding of their shared cultural heritage. This initiative aimed to strengthen community bonds through shared learning and discussion.

The event was held to celebrate the International Creole Language and Culture Day, highlighting the importance of preserving and promoting the Creole language and its associated culture within Seychelles.
