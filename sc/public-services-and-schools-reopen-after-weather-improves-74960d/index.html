Public services and schools reopen after weather improves

Schools and public services resumed operations today following an improvement in weather conditions, according to the Department of Risk and Disaster Management.

Source: Seychelles Broadcasting Corp · 2026-10-21 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X

Public services and schools reopened today after weather conditions improved. Daniel Cetoupe, Principal Secretary for the Department of Risk and Disaster Management (DRDM), announced the resumption in a press conference. He stated that the decision was based on the latest information received from meteorological authorities indicating better weather.

The DRDM, responsible for managing disaster-related issues, confirmed the reopening of essential services and educational institutions. This move signifies a return to normalcy after a period of disruption caused by adverse weather.

The meteorological authorities provided updated information that led to the DRDM's assessment of improved weather. This assessment directly informed the decision to allow schools and public services to resume their normal operations across the country.

Why it mattersThe reopening of public services and schools affects the daily lives of citizens and the functioning of the country.
