Itheku laseKapa lifaka iziteringi eziyi-14 zokuthumela izithelo phesheya

I-Transnet Port Terminals ithi ukuthengwa kwemishini engama-R83 wezigidi kuhloswe ngakho ukwehlisa ukuma kwamakhreyini ngesikhathi kulayishwa imikhumbi.

Umthombo: Food Business Africa · 27 Novemba 2026 · Imizuzu engu-1 yokufunda

Itheku leziqukathi laseKapa litshale izigidi ezingama-R83 (amaRandi ayizigidi eziyizinkulungwane ezi-4.78) kuziteringi zeziqukathi eziyi-14 njengoba isizini yokuthunyelwa kwezithelo zaseNingizimu Afrika iqala. I-Transnet Port Terminals ithe le mishini isetshenziswa emikhunjini, emathangeni nasemakhreyini asendaweni.

Umphathi wetheku uNoxolo Thabatha uthe ama-hydraulic twist-lock eziteringi avikela iziqukathi futhi kuhloswe ngawo ukwehlisa ukulibaziseka kwemishini ngesikhathi kulayishwa imikhumbi. Amayunithi amane azosebenzela itheku laseKapa elinezinhlobonhlobo, elisekela itheku leziqukathi ngezikhathi zokuthumela eziphezulu.

Ethekwini leziqukathi, amahora okusebenza kwemikhumbi athuthuke ngo-15% unyaka wezimali ophele ngoMashi 2026, kuyilapho ukuguqulwa kwamaloli kwehle kusuka kumaminithi angu-52 kwaba yimizuzu engama-35. Imimoya enamandla eningizimu-mpumalanga ibiza itheku izinsuku zokusebenza ezingama-79 ngonyaka odlule; ngesikhathi sokuma kwesimo sezulu, amathekhu amabili azosebenzisa indawo yasendlini yaseBelcon ukuhlela ukunyakaza komhlaba nokwamukela iziqukathi ezibandayo.

Kungani kubalulekileAbathumela izithelo phesheya kanye namakhasimende empahla bathembela emisebenzini yokulayisha yamachweba, neziteringi ezine ezinikezwe itheku elinezinhlobonhlobo ngezikhathi eziphezulu.
