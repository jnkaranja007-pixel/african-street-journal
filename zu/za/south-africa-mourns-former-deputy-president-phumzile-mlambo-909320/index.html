INingizimu Afrika ilila ngoPhumzile Mlambo-Ngcuka owayeyiphini lempilo

UMlambo-Ngcuka, oshone eneminyaka engu-71 emva kwezinkinga zokuhlinzwa, ube yiphini likamongameli kusuka ngo-2005 kuya ku-2008.

Imithombo: The Citizen SA, Daily Maverick kanye Mail & Guardian · 23 Novemba 2026 · Imizuzu engu-1 yokufunda

UPhumzile Mlambo-Ngcuka, oyiphini likaMengameli wesifazane wokuqala eNingizimu Afrika, ushone ngoLwesihlanu eneminyaka engu-71 emva kwezinkinga zenqubo yezokwelapha cishe emasontweni amabili ngaphambi kwalokho. Ungene ephalamende lokuqala lentsandokazi yeningi lezwe ngo-1994 waba ungqongqoshe wezohwebo nezimboni eminyakeni emibili emva kwalokho.

Uthathe umnyango wezemisebenzi yezimandla namandla ngomnyaka ka-1999 wawubamba iminyaka eyisithupha. NgoJuni 2005, waba yiphini likamongameli, waze wakhonza kuze kube uSepthemba 2008, lapho eshiya khona emva kokuhamba kukaMengameli Thabo Mbeki. Okhulumela yena uthe ucashunwe ngezizathu zakhe siqu futhi wayefuna umongameli ozayo aqoke elakhe iphini.

UMlambo-Ngcuka ujoyine iCongress of the People ngoMashi 2009, wabe esebuyela ku-African National Congress. Ngo-Agasti 2013 waba ngumqondisi omkhulu we-UN Women kanye n secrétaire jenerali waphansi weNhlangano yezizwe.
