I-SA yamukela isinqumo se-ICJ sokuthi iMyanmar inecala lokubulala isizwe

Inkantolo iyalele iMyanmar ukuthi inikeze izisulu zaseRohingya isinxephezelo futhi iqinisekise ukuthi lokhu kungenzeki futhi.

Imithombo: The Citizen SA, Daily Maverick kanye Mail & Guardian · 20 Novemba 2026 · Imizuzu engu-1 yokufunda

I-Ningizimu Afrika yamukela isinqumo senkantolo yamazwe ngamazwe i-ICJ mhla ziyi-19 kuNovemba 2026 esithole iMyanmar inecala lokubulala isizwe samaRohingya, okuyiqembu elincane lamaSulumane. Leli cala lathathelwa phezulu yiThe Gambia, kanti isinqumo senkantolo samavoti ayisishiyagalolunye kwabayisishiyagalolunye nesithupha sithinta umkhankaso weMyanmar wowe-2017 esifundeni saseRakhine.

Umnyango Wezobudlelwano Bamazwe Ngamazwe Nokubambisana eNingizimu Afrika uthe lesi sinqumo siyisinyathelo esikhulu esiya phambili ekutholeni ubulungiswa kwezizulu zaseRohingya. Inkantolo iyalele iMyanmar ukuthi inikeze izinxephezelo zomonakamo ongazange ulungiswe, futhi inikeze iziqinisekiso zokuthi lokhu ngeke kuphinde kwenzeke.

I-Ningizimu Afrika ithe izosifunda lesi sinqumo ihlolisise umthelela waso emthethweni wamazwe ngamazwe, kubandakanya namacala aqhubekayo nawesikhathi esizayo angaphansi Kwesivumelwano Sokubulawa Kwezigidi. Iphinde yanxusa iMyanmar ukuthi iphenye, ishushise futhi ijezise labo abanecala.

Kungani kubalulekileUmnyango Wezobudlelwano Bamazwe Ngamazwe eNingizimu Afrika kumele uhlolisise umthelela walesi sinqumo kumacala aqhubekayo nawesikhathi esizayoaphansi Kwesivumelwano Sokubulawa Kwezigidi.
