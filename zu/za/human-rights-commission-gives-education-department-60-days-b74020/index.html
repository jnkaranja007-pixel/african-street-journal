Ikhomishini inika uMnyango weMfundo izinsuku ezingu-60 ukuba ulungise izinkinga ezikoleni

Ikhomishini ikhulume ngokuminyana kwabafundi emakilasini, izakhiwo ezingaphepheni nokungabi bikho kwezindlu zangasese.

Imithombo: Mail & Guardian kanye Daily Maverick · 24 Novemba 2026 · Imizuzu engu-1 yokufunda

IKhomishini Yamalungelo Abantu eNingizimu Afrika inike uMnyango wezMfundo Eyisisekelo izinsuku ezingu-60 ukuthi uphendule ngezinkinga ezingapheli zikahulumeni ezikoleni, yase isho ukuthi iz Thatha izinyathelo zomthetho uma izincomo zayo zingalandelwa. Lokhu kuvela Umbikweni Wokuhlolwa Kwezimo Zezikole ka-2026 weHuman Sciences Research Council owakhishwa ngoMsombuluko, mhla ziyi-23 kuNovemba.

Lo mbiko uqophe abafundi abayi-101 ekilasini likaGrade 8 nabayi-99 likaGrade 10 esikoleni i-Adolf Mhinga Senior Secondary School eLimpopo. Uphinde wathola ukuthi kunezifundazwe eziyisishiyagalolunye ezinekinga yokushoda kwamafaisha, kanti abahloli babone abafundi abathathu babelana ngedeski elilodwa kwezinye izikole KwaZulu-Natal naseKapa Lakeni.

Ikhomishini icele ukuthi kube nohlelo lwezimali lokwakha amakilasi engeziwe nokuthenga amafaisha. UMnyango ubufake imibiko ePhalamende ngezinkinga zengqalasizinda yezikole ekuqaleni kuka-2026.

Kungani kubalulekileAbafundi abasemakilasini agcwele kakhulu kanye nalezo zikole ezingenazo izindlu zangasese ezisebenzayo kuzomele balinde uMnyango, owunikwe izinsuku ezingu-60 ukuthi uphendule.
