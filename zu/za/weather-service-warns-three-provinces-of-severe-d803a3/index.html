Abosibhakabhaka baxwayisa izifundazwe ezintathu ngezulu elibi

Isexwayiso seLevel 4 besiqhubeka kuze kube u-23:59 ngoMsombuluko, kuneziphepho zemvula enkulu, isichotho nemimoya enamandla eGauteng, Limpopo naseMpumalanga.

Imithombo: The South African kanye The Citizen SA · 23 Novemba 2026 · Imizuzu engu-1 yokufunda

Abosibhakabhaka baseNingizimu Afrika bakhiphe isexwayiso seYellow Level 4 sezulu esibi eGauteng, Limpopo naseMpumalanga ngoMsombuluko, mhla ziyi-23 kuNovemba. Siqale ngo-13:00 saze saba semthethweni kuze kube u-23:59.

Isibhakabhaka sixwayise ngokuthi imvula eningi, isichotho nemimoya enamandla kungadala izikhukhula ezindaweni eziphansi, emijondolweni nasemigwaqeni, futhi kulimaze nezakhiwo. Uthe iziphepho kulindeleke ukuthi zibe seNingizimu neNkabankulu yeGauteng kanye naseMpumalanga eningizimu-ntshonalanga, kanti isexwayiso besihlanganisa neLimpopo.

Okhulumela uMnyango Wezinhlekelele eGoli, uRobert Mulaudzi, unxuse abahlali ukuthi bagweme imigwaqo egcwele amanzi, amabhuloho aphansi, imifula namadamu. Esinye isexwayiso seLevel 4 sikhishiwe ngezindawo zaseKwaZulu-Natal, eMpumalanga eningizimu, eFreistata esempumalanga naseNtshonalanga Koloni ngoLwesibili, mhla ziyi-24 kuNovemba.

Kungani kubalulekileAbahlali ezindaweni eziphansi nabashayeli babhekene nengozi yezikhukhula nokuphepha emigwaqeni ngaphambi kokuba isexwayiso siphele ngoMsombuluko.
