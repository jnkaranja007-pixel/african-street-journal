Abasebenzi baseTFG batekwile ezikhungweni ezimbili eGauteng bafuna ukunyuselwa ngama-94%

Amakhulu abasebenzi avale izitolo ezikhungweni zokusabalalisa zaseKempton Park naseMidrand emva kokuba ukuxoxisana ngamaholo kuphazamisekile.

Imithombo: The Citizen SA kanye GroundUp · 23 Novemba 2026 · Imizuzu engu-1 yokufunda

Amakhulu abasebenzi baseThe Foschini Group (TFG) ezikhungweni zokusabalalisa eKempton Park naseMidrand, eGauteng, batekwile ngoMsombuluko, mhla ziyi-23 kuNovemba, befuna ukunyuselwa amaholo okungenani ngama-94%. Bafake isaziso samahora angu-48 ngoLwesine, mhla zingama-20 kuNovemba emva kokuthi ukuxoxisana kuvalekile.

U-Esther Mwasi, umsebenzi nommeli weSimunye Workers Forum, uthe abasebenzi baqoqene ezikhungweni kusukela ekuseni ngoMsombuluko. Ulinganisele ukuthi ababengaphezulu kwabayi-100 abebekhona, yize umnyango uthi abasebenzi bangaba yi-295. I-TFG ithe icele iCCMA ukuthi isize ezingxoxweni, kodwa abasebenzi benqabile, nokho inkampani isavulekile ukuthi kuxoxwe.

Leli fowamu liphinde lifune isikhathi sokusebenza kusukela ngoMsombuluko kuya uLwesihlanu, amahora angu-45 ngesonto, imali yezamahora engeziwe ngempelaviki kanye namaholo acacile esikhundleni ngasinye. Litshela ukuthi abanye abasebenzi bahola kancane njengo-R6 200 ngenyanga.
