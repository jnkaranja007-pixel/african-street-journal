Iningizimu Afrika · Isimo sezulu

Uphiko lwesimo sezulu luxwayisa ngeziphepho ngoMgqibelo kuyo yonke iNingizimu Afrika

Izexwayiso ze-Yellow Level 2 zihlanganisa izingxenye zezifundazwe eziyisishiyagalolunye, kanti kunezingozi zikhukhula kanye nengozi enkulu yomlilo ezindaweni ezintathu.

Imithombo: The South African kanye The Citizen SA · 20 Novemba 2026 · Imizuzu engu-1 yokufunda

Uphiko lwesimo sezulu saseNingizimu Afrika lukhiphe izexwayiso ze-Yellow Level 2 ngezishingishane ezinzima ngoMgqibelo, mhla ziyi-21 kuLwezi, ezintanyeni zezifundazwe eziyisishiyagalolunye. Lezi zexwayiso zithinta iFleyistata, enkabeni yeNorth West, KwaZulu-Natal, eningizimu yeGauteng, ingxenye enkulu yeMpumalanga, enkabeni naseningizimu-mpumalanga yeLimpopo, kanye nasezingenyakatho-mpumalanga yeNtshonalanga Koloni.

Lolu phiko luxwayisa ngokuthi imvula eningi, imimoya eyingozi, isichotho kanye nombani omningi kungakhukhula imigwaqo esengozini, izindawo eziphansi namabhuloho. Ingozi enkulu yomlilo ibikezelwe enkabeni nasempumalanga yeNtshonalanga Kapa, enyakatho-mpumalanga yeNtshonalanga Koloni, nakwezinye izingxenye zenkabeni nenyakatho yeNtshonalanga Koloni.

Isibikezelo futhi sikhuluma ngezimo ezishisayo nezimanzi KwaZulu-Natal esempumalanga, uphiko oluthi luzoba olungakhululeki kakhulu. Imvula ehlakazekile kanye nezulu elimqquba kulindeleke kwezinye izingxenye zezwe ngempelasonto.

Kungani kubalulekileIzakhamizi nabashayeli abasemandleni ezixwayiso babhekene nengozi yezikhukhula emigwaqweni ethintekayo, izindawo eziphansi namabhuloho phakathi neziphepho zangoMgqibelo.
