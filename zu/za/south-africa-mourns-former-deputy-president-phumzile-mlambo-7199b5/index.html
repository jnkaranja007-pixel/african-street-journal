I-South Africa ilila ngokukhothama kwePhini likaMengameli wakudala uPhumzile Mlambo-Ngcuka

Phumzile Mlambo-Ngcuka ushone ngoLwesihlanu eneminyaka engu-71; iNyuvesi yaseGoli nabaphathi bezombangazwe bathume imiyalezo yenduduzo.

Imithombo: The Citizen SA, Mail & Guardian kanye The South African · 21 Novemba 2026 · Imizuzu engu-1 yokufunda

UPhumzile Mlambo-Ngcuka, obeyiphini likamengameli wokuqala wesifazane eNingizimu Afrika, ushone ngoLwesihlanu eneminyaka engu-71 yobudala. Wake waba ngumphatiswa wemayini namandla kanye nephini likangqongqoshe wezohwebo nezimboni ngaphambi kokumela iNingizimu Afrika eZizweni Zihlangene njengenhloko ye-UN Women.

INyuvesi yaseGoli, obeyishansela kuyo, ithe ulwazi lwakhe lobuholi nokuzibophezela kwakhe kwezemfundo kuyohlala kukhunjulwa esikhungweni. I-ANC ithe izosebenzelana nomndeni wakhe ngezinto ezithinta ukuxhumana nezinhlelo ezizayo; umndeni ucele ukuba uyekwe uwedwa ngokwemvelo, kusho iHhovisi likaMengameli.

UMlambo-Ngcuka waqokwa njengephini likamengameli ngo-2005 futhi waba ngumqondisi ophethe we-UN Women kanye nephini likanobhala jikelele weZizwe Zihlangene. Wake wahola nekomidi lezisebenzi zikahulumeni ekuphathweni kokuqala kwangemva kuka-1994 futhi abamba iqhaza ekubhalweni koMthethosisekelo wentando yeningi waseNingizimu Afrika.

Kungani kubalulekileINyuvesi yaseGoli kufanele ibhekane nokulahlekelwa ngushansela wayo, kanti i-ANC ixhumana nomndeni wakhe ngezinhlelo zomngcwabo.
