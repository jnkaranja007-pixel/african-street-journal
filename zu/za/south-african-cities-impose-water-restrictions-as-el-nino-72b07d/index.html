Amadolobha ase-SA abeka imingcele yamanzi njengoba u-El Niño eqina

Isikhungo sase-US sokuBikezelwa Kwesimo Sezulu sithi amathuba okuba nesimo esinamandla kakhulu kuJanuwari-Mashi 2027 angaphezu kuka-79%.

Umthombo: Daily Maverick · 20 Novemba 2026 · Imizuzu engu-1 yokufunda

ISithwane, iJohannesburg kanye neKapa sekubeke imingcele yokusetshenziswa kwamanzi njengoba u-El Niño eqina, kanti iNtshonalanga Kapa nayo ibhekene nemingcele efanayo. Le mingcele ilandela ukunxusa kukaNgqongqoshe Wezolimo uWillie Aucamp ngenyanga edlule ukuthi kuthathwe izinyathelo zokuqala ukubhekana nalesi simo sezulu.

Isikhungo sase-US sokuBikezelwa Kwesimo Sezulu sithe embikweni esiwukhiphe ekuqaleni kwaleli sonto ukuthi u-El Niño onamandla kakhulu kungenzeka uqhubeke kuze kube uJanuwari-Mashi 2027, kanti amathuba ahlala engaphezu kuka-79%. Umbiko uthe lesi simo sizandisa izinkinga zamanzi emadolobheni ezibangelwa ukungagcinwa kahle kwengqalasizinda ebalulekile.

Amazinga amadamu ahlala angaphezu kuka-90% kuzo zonke izifundazwe ngaphandle kweNtshonalanga Kapa naseKwaZulu-Natali, ngokusho kombiko wamasonto onke woMnyango Wezamanzi Nezokuthuthwa Kwezinto; izifundazwe ezinhlanu zingaphezu kwamazinga azo ngesikhathi esifanayo ngonyaka odlule. Ndaba le ithi kukhona uxhaso lwezimali olungangezigidi eziyizinkulungwane eziyi-10 zamarandi olutholakalayo ukubhekana nale nkinga.

Kungani kubalulekileAbasebenzisi bamanzi eNtshonalanga Kapa nasemadolobheni amathathu okukhulunywa ngawo kumele banciphise ukusebenzisa kwabo, kanti umnyango ubika amazinga amadamu angaphansi kuka-90% eNtshonalanga Kapa naseKwaZulu-Natali.
