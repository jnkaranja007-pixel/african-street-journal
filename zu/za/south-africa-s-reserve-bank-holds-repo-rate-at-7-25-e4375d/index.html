Ibhange LaseNingizimu Afrika ligcina i-repo rate ku-7.25%

Lesi sinqumo sigcina inzalo yokuqala isemazingeni ka-10.75%, yize amalungu ayisikhombisa ekomiti amathathu ayefuna ukukhuphuka kwamaphuzu ayi-25.

Imithombo: The South African kanye Daily Maverick · 19 Novemba 2026 · Imizuzu engu-1 yokufunda

Ibhange LaseNingizimu Afrika ligcine i-repo rate yalo ku-7.25% emhlanganweni wokugcina weKomiti Yezinqumo Zezezimali wowe-2026 ngoLwesine, lishiya inzalo yokuqala yeziboleko ku-10.75%. Lesi sinqumo sisho ukuthi akusekho ukukhuphuka kwenzalo kwabalethe ziboleko ezihlobene nezinga lokuqala.

Amalungu amane ekomiti avotele ukugcina amazinga, kanti amathathu afuna ukukhuphuka kwamaphuzu ayi-25. UM governador uLesetja Kganyago uthe ibhange lime kancane “okwamanje” kodwa lihlala lilungele ukukhuphuka kwenzalo uma izinga lokwenyuka kwamanani lizidinga. Ukwenyuka kwamanani kukhuphukile kwaba u-5.2% ngo-Okthoba kusuka ku-4.5% ngoSeptemba, phezu kwelitshe lebhange lika-3%; ibhange lilindela ukuthi lihlale lingaphezulu kuka-5% kuze kube uqala u-2027, ikakhulu ngenxa yezindleko eziphezulu zikaphethroli.

Ukubanjwa nakho akulethi mpumuzo entsha yokunciphiswa kwenzalo. Isinyathelo esilandelayo sebhange sizoxhomeka ekwenyukeni kwamanani, izintengo kaphethroli, irandi kanye nezimo zomnotho ezibanzi.

Kungani kubalulekileImindeni eneziboleko zendlu ezihlobene nenzalo yokuqala, ezezimoto kanye nezivumelwano zesikweletu igcina izinga lika-10.75%, kodwa ayitholi sinciphiso ezinkokhelweni zazo.
