U-Khumalo nabaphathi abaphezulu abayisihlanu bafuna over R112m

Isikhulu seCrime Intelligence nabanye basola ukushushiswa ngokungafanele nokuboshwa ngokungekho emthethweni emva kokuba amacala esusiwe.

Imithombo: The Citizen SA kanye Mail & Guardian · 23 Novemba 2026 · Imizuzu engu-1 yokufunda

UDumisani Khumalo, oyisikhulu seCrime Intelligence eNingizimu Afrika, namaphoyisa aphezulu ayisihlanu bafuna ngaphezu kwezigidi ezingu-112 zamarandi kuhulumeni emva kokuhoxiswa kwamacala enkohliso nenkulumompikiswano abebhekene nawo. Isaziso sabo somthetho, esikhishwe mhla ziyi-19 kuNovemba 2026, sisola abashushisi nabaphenyi ngokushushisa ngokungafanele, ukuboshwa nokugcinwa esitokisini ngokungekho emthethweni.

Izikhulu zisola ukuthi uphenyo lusekelwe encwadini yefika engeyinhle nokuthi umphenyi wadukisa umantshi ukuze athole izincwadi zokuboshwa. Baphinde bathi banqatshelwa ukudla ngenkathi beboshiwe. Icala likahulumeni ladilika emva kwezinyanga ezingaba ngu-14, ngokusho kwesaziso somthetho. U-Khumalo ufuna izigidi ezingu-28 zamarandi, ecaphuna umonakalo empilweni yakhe, izindleko zokwelashwa, izindleko zomthetho kanye nomonakalo wesithunzi sakhe.

Isaziso sisho owayeyiNhloko ye-Investigating Directorate Against Corruption u-Andrea Johnson, umphenyi uBrian Padayachee nezinhlangano zikahulumeni eziningi phakathi kwalabo abathintekayo. Izinsolo ziyizicelo ezisemacaleni.

Kungani kubalulekileUhulumeni kumele aphendule ngesicelo esidlula R112 wezigidi esivela ezikhulwini; esikaKhumalo saba yedwa siyizigidi ezingu-28 zamarandi.
