Izikole zikaHulumeni zilindele ukukhuphula izimali ngo-10% ngo-2027

AbakwaFedsas bathi ezinye izikole zibikezela ukukhuphula ngo-12% kuya ku-15%, njengoba imindeni isinquma ukunciphisa ukuthenga ukudla nokusebenzisa imali yokonga ukuze ikhokhele imfundo.

Umthombo: Daily Maverick · 26 Novemba 2026 · Imizuzu engu-1 yokufunda

Izimali zezikole zikaHulumeni eziningi eNingizimu Afrika kulindeleke ukuthi zenyuke ngo-7% kuya ku-10% ngo-2027, lokhu kubeka ingcindezi enkulu emindenini esivele inciphisa ukuthenga ukudla nokusebenzisa imali yokonga ukuze izingane zihlale ezikoleni. Umfaniswano, izincwadi zokubhala, ezokuthutha kanye nezindleko zokudla nakho kungabakhona ngenyanga efana naleyo yokukhokhela isikole.

URiaan van der Bergh, iphepha laseFedsas, uthe izikole abaxoxe nazo bezilindele ukukhuphuka okungaka, kanti ezinye ezihlukile bezihlela ukukhuphula ngo-12% kuya ku-15%. Ulindele ukuthi abazali abaningi bafune ukuxolelwa ezimalini ngo-2027; abanye bavele basho ngesikhathi sokwamukelwa ukuthi bahlose ukufaka isicelo sosizo.

Izimali zezikole zasezingeni eliphansi zenyuke ngo-6% kusuka ku-2025 kuya ku-2026, kanti izimali zezikole eziphezulu zenyuke ngo-5.7%, ngokusho kweCompetition Commission. AbakwaFedsas bathi izindleko zesikole ezifana nemihlomulo, izintela zikamasipala, ugesi namanzi zenyuka ngokushesha kunezinga lokunyuka kwamanani, zicindezela izimali zezincwadi zokufunda nokulungisa izakhiwo.

Kungani kubalulekileImindeni esivele isebenzisa imali yokonga futhi inciphisa ukudla ukuze ikwazi ukukhokhela izikole ibhekene nokukhuphuka okungafika ku-10% ngo-2027, kanti abazali abaningi kulindeleke ukuba bafake izicelo zokuxolelwa ezimalini.
