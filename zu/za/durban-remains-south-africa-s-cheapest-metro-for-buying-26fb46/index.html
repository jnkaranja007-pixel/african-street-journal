ITheku lihlala liyidolobha elishibhe kakhulu eNingizimu Afrika ukuthenga indlu

Ngaphandle kwedolobha phakathi, indlu ejwayelekile lapho ithengiswa ngo-R920 000, kanti eGoli kuyisilinganiso sika-R1.1 million, ngokusho kombiko wezindlu.

Umthombo: The South African · 26 Novemba 2026 · Imizuzu engu-1 yokufunda

ITheku liyidolobha elishibhe kakhulu eNingizimu Afrika ukuthenga indlu ngaphandle kwedolobha phakathi, lapho intengo yokuthengisa isilinganiso sayo ingamaR920 000, ngokusho kweThe South African. Isilinganiso sokuthengisa saseGoli kulo lonke idolobha singamaR1.1 million, kanti ama-flat phakathi nendawo eKapa abiza ngaphezulu kwamaR1 million.

Phakathi nendawo eThekwini, isilinganiso sokuthengisa singamaR420 000, kanti indlu encane idinga okungenani amaR280 000. IStatistics South Africa ibeka izinga lokungasebenzi eThekwini, umasipala ohlanganisa iTheku, ku-39.2% kusukela kwikota yesithathu. Izintengo zezindlu eThekwini zenyuke ngo-5.1% phakathi kuka-2021 no-2025.

EPitoli, isilinganiso sokuthengisa edolobheni lonke singamaR989 000, okusho ukuthi sikhuphuke ngo-16.8% eminyakeni emihlanu. Umbiko uthi intengo yesilinganiso saseGoli ngaphandle kwedolobha phakathi ayishintshanga kusukela ku-2024.
