UHill-Lewis uqala ihlandla lesibili njengemeya yaseKapa

I-Democratic Alliance igcine iningi layo elibonakalayo kuphela kumasipala waseNingizimu Afrika lapho iqembu linye liwine khona ukulawula, njengoba ebeka phambili izinto ezinhlanu.

Imithombo: The South African kanye Daily Maverick · 19 Novemba 2026 · Imizuzu engu-1 yokufunda

UGeordin Hill-Lewis ufunzwe isifungo sehlandla lesibili njengemeya yaseKapa ngoLwesine, mhla ziyi-19 kuNovemba, emhlanganweni wokuqala womkhandlu kamasipala. I-Democratic Alliance (DA) igcine iningi elibonakalayo okhethweni lukamasipala wowe-2026, okwenza iKapa laba ngumasipala kuphela waseNingizimu Afrika lapho iqembu elilodwa liwine khona.

UHill-Lewis uthe uzogxila emaphoyiseni aqinile, emisebenzi, ezidingweni ezinokwethenjelwa ezingabizi, ezindlini kanye nasemadolobheni ahlanzekile. Izinhlelo zakhe zibandakanya ukwakha iyunithi yophenyo kamasipala, ukutshala imali kwezobuchwepheshe bokulwa nobugebengu, ukunciphisa ukuchitheka kwendle kanye nokukhulula umhlaba kamasipala wezindlu. Uphinde wathembisa ukufuna amandla amakhulu ophenyo amaphoyisa kamasipala.

UXanthea Limberg ukhethelwe ubusemephu wamasekeni, kanti uFelicity Purchase uhlale njengoSomlomo. Amaqembu aphikisayo amukele ukuphinde kukhethwe kukaHill-Lewis kuyilapho esiza ngokuthi kube nesinyathelo sokubhekana nokungalingani, izindlu, ukungasebenzi kwabantu kanye nengqalasizinda, futhi ecela ukuthi abeke phambili iKapa ngaphambi kwezinhloso zezepolitiki kazwelonke.

Kungani kubalulekileIzakhamizi zaseKapa zizothemba abaphathi bamasipala ukuthi baqhubekisele phambili ezamaphoyisa, izindlu kanye nokuthuthukiswa kwezidingo phakathi nalesikhathi seminyaka emihlanu sikaHill-Lewis.
