Iningizimu Afrika · Ezebhizinisi

Ingosi yekhomishini yamanzi iqondise ukulahleka kamasipala njengoba amanzi angenayo imali afinyelela ku-47.4%

Isikhungo sezinsiza sihlanganisa amathuluzi ahloliwe, kuyilapho isici sokwabelana esihleliwe sizovumela imikhandlu ukuba idlulisele izindlela ezisebenze kumanethiwekhi ayo.

Umthombo: Mail & Guardian · 27 Novemba 2026 · Imizuzu engu-1 yokufunda

IWater Research Commission ithuthukisa ingosi yokusiza omasipala baseNingizimu Afrika ukuthi babhekane namanzi angenayo imali, okuyibhalansi yamanzi kazwelonke yomnyango eyibeka ku-47.4%. Ikhomishini ihlonze izinsiza ezingama-84 zendawo yokubuthana, kusho usosayensi wocwaningo uLeonie Vorster kuwebinar eThekwini mhla zingama-25 uNovemba.

UVorster uthe isigaba esilandelayo sizovumela abasebenzisi ababhalisiwe ukuba babelane ngezindlela ezisebenze kumanethiwekhi abo. Ingosi ibisesigabeni sokuthuthukiswa cishe iminyaka emibili, futhi abanye omasipala sebeqale ukuyisebenzisa. Izohlangabezana nemihlahlandlela, amathuluzi kanye nolwazi mayelana nobuchwepheshe obuhloliwe ensimini, kusho uchwepheshe wamanzi uDkt Manjusha Sunil.

Ibhalansi yamanzi kazwelonke, ethatha okuhloliwe koMnyango ka-2023 No Drop, ilinganisele ukulahleka kwamanzi ku-40.8%. Amanzi angenayo imali abuye ahlanganise izimpahla ezingakhiqizi mali; izikhulu zidinga ukwehlukanisa ukuvuza, okudinga ukulungiswa, kusuka ekufundeni okungalungile noma amaphutha okukhokhisa.

Kungani kubalulekileIzikhulu zikamasipala zumele zithole ukuthi ukulahleka kuvela yini ezindongeni noma ezinkingeni zokulinganisa nokukhokhisa, kanti ibhalansi yamanzi kazwelonke ilinganisa ukulahleka kwamanzi ngokomzimba ku-40.8%.
