I-ANC ifuna izivumelwano zokubambisana emadolobheni ngo-27 November

Leli qembu lithole u-31% wamavoti kodwa lathola amavoti amaningi emadolobheni amabili kuphela kweziyisishiyagalolunye zaseNingizimu Afrika.

Umthombo: BBC Africa · 21 Novemba 2026 · Imizuzu engu-1 yokufunda

Amaqembu aseNingizimu Afrika anesikhathi kuze kube mhla ziyi-27 kuNovemba ukuthi axoxisane futhi akhe ohulumeni basekhaya, kanti i-African National Congress (ANC) ifuna izivumelwano zokubambisana ukusebenza amadolobha ngemuva kokulahlekelwa kulolu khetho lwasekhaya lwale nyanga.

Isabelo sayo samavoti sehle saba u-31%, sisuka ku-46% okhethweni lwasendaweni olwedlule, futhi ithole amavoti amaningi emadolobheni amabili kuphela kweziyisishiyagalolunye zezwe, uma kuqhathaniswa nesithupha ngo-2021. I-ANC ithole iningi eLimpopo kuphela, isifundazwe saseNingizimu Afrika esisodwa kwesiyisishiyagalolunye, sehle sisuka ezifundazweni eziyisithupha ngaphambili.

EGauteng, ikhaya laseGoli nasePitoli, iDemocratic Alliance eqondene namabhizinisi ithole amavoti amaningi kuwo womabili amadolobha nase-Ekurhuleni, esinye isifunda esikhulu sesifundazwe. I-ANC isaqhubekile nokuthola amavoti amaningi uwonke kunaliphi elinye qembu, kodwa ilahlekelwe ukulawula imikhandlu engaphezu kwesigamu ebibambe ngayo ngo-2021.

Kungani kubalulekileAmaqembu axoxisana ngohulumeni basekhaya baseNingizimu Afrika kufanele afinyelele ezivumelwaneni mhla ziyi-27 kuNovemba, ngemuva kokuthi i-ANC ilahlekelwe ukulawula emkhandlwini engaphezu kwesigamu saleyo yase-2021.
