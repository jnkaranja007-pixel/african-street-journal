Iningizimu Afrika · Ezebhizinisi

Imali engenayo yePremier FMCG inyuke ngo-38.8% yafinyelela ku-R14.3 bhiliyoni

Imiphumela yalo menzi wokudla waseNingizimu Afrika ibandakanya ibhizinisi lakhe lokupheka, kanti izindleko zenzalo zinyuke ngo-17.1% zaba uR139 wezigidi ngemuva kokuthi ithathe izikweletu.

Umthombo: Food Business Africa · 20 Novemba 2026 · Imizuzu engu-1 yokufunda

Umenzi wokudla waseNingizimu Afrika iPremier FMCG ubike imali engenayo engu-R14.3 bhiliyoni esikhathini esiphelile mhla ziyi-30 kuMandulo 2026, okuyinyuko ka-38.8% ngemuva kokuthi ukuthengwa kwayo kwe-RFG ngo-Indasa, eyayaziwa ngokuthi yi-Rainbow Food Group, kulethe umkhakha wokupheka ebhizinisini layo. Inzuzo nethe yenyuke ngo-62.1% yafinyelela ku-R1.2 bhiliyoni, kanti inzuzo ngaphambi kwenzalo, intela, ukwehla kwenani lempahla kanye nokukhokhelwa kwayo (EBITDA) yenyuke ngo-51.4% yaba uR2.0 bhiliyoni.

IMillbake, eyenze u-66% wemali engenayo yeqembu, yandise ukuthengisa ngayo ngo-1.3% yafinyelela ku-R9.4 bhiliyoni yize amanani ommbila ehla ngo-20% kanye namandla kakolweni ngo-5%. I-EBITDA yayo yenyuke ngo-23.7% yaba uR1.6 bhiliyoni. Imali engenayo yezimpahla zokudla ibiyi-R4.9 bhiliyoni, kubandakanya nezokupheka, kanti i-EBITDA yayo yafinyelela kuR513 wezigidi, ngenzuzo engu-10.4%.

Izindleko zenzalo zinyuke ngo-17.1% zaba uR139 wezigidi kulandela ukufakwa kwezikweletu ezathathwa kuthengiselwano lwe-RFG. IPremier ithe ukwenziwa kwemali kuzoxhasa izindleko zokutshala izimali, ukwehlistwa kwezikweletu kanye nokuthengwa kabusha kwamasheya okungenzeka; ilindele ukuthi ukuhlanganiswa kokusebenza kanye nezinzuzo zokulethwa kwezimpahla zinyuke engxenyeni yesibili yonyaka.
