Inkantolo yaseWestern Cape ichitha umbiko wokukhishwa kukaRamaphosa

Isinqumo esamukelwa bonke siphinde saphumuza izinyathelo zePhalamende ezisekelwe embikweni, ngesikhathi i-African Transformation Movement ibonisana ngesicelo sokudlulisela udaba enkantolo ephakeme.

Imithombo: The South African, Mail & Guardian kanye Africanews · 26 Novemba 2026 · Imizuzu engu-1 yokufunda

Inkantolo Enkhulu yaseWestern Cape eNingizimu Afrika ngoLwesithathu, mhla zingama-25 kuNovemba, ichithe umbiko owawuvule indlela yokuqala inqubo yokukhipha uMengameli Cyril Ramaphosa esikhundleni. Iphaneli ephelele yanquma ngazwi linye ukuthi umbiko wephaneli ezimele wawungekho emthethweni futhi yachitha nezinyathelo ezalandela zePhalamende ezazisekelwe kuwo.

Abaquli bafumene ukuthi iphaneli, eyayiholwa owayeyiPhini leNkosi yezabaMeli uSandile Ngcobo, isebenzise uvivinya olungelona futhi yadlulela ngaphaya kwezinsolo uRamaphosa owayecelwe ukuba aziphendulele kuzo. Umbiko wephaneli kaNovemba 2022 uthe unecala lokuphendula ngama-dollar angu-$580 000 ebiwe efameni lakhe lasePhala Phala ngoFebhuwari 2020. Inkantolo ayizange inqume ngezinsolo zokuziphatha kabi ezisekhasini.

Usihlalo weKomiti yokuKhipha uMakashule Gana uthe ucele iseluleko somthetho sokuthi umhlangano wakhe ohlelelwe mhla zingama-26 kuNovemba ungaqhubeka yini. USomlomo uThoko Didiza uzofunda isinqumo, kanti i-African Transformation Movement ibonisana nabameli ngokuqondene nesicelo sokudlulisela udaba enkantolo ephakeme.
