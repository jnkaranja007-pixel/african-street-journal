Inhlangano yamalungelo yaseNingizimu Afrika ifuna kuphenywe ngokuthengiswa kwezikhali eMyanmar

ISouthern Africa Litigation Centre ifuna abashushisi bahlole ukuthunyelwa okugunyaziwe ngesikhathi ibutho lezempi laseMyanmar lenza unyongwana kumaRohingya.

Imithombo: Daily Maverick kanye Mail & Guardian · 20 Novemba 2026 · Imizuzu engu-1 yokufunda

ISouthern Africa Litigation Centre, inhlangano yamalungelo asemthethweni yaseNingizimu Afrika, icele iNational Prosecuting Authority kanye neDirectorate for Priority Crime Investigation ukuthi ziphenye ukugunyazwa nokuthunyelwa kwezikhali zaseNingizimu Afrika eMyanmar kusuka ku-2017 kuya ku-2021. Le nhlangano iphindife ifuna abaphenyi bahlole iNational Conventional Arms Control Committee, umzimba wongqongqoshe nezikhulu eziphezulu ogunyaze lokhu kuthengisa.

Lolu bizo lulandela isinqumo seNkantolo Yezobulungiswa Yamazwe Ngamazwe sangoLwesine, mhla ziyi-19 kuLwezi, sokuthi uhulumeni wezempi waseMyanmar wenza unyongwana esizweni samaRohingya futhi waphula umthethosisekelo wonyongwana. Isinqumo senkantolo kwaba ngesokuqala ukuthola ukuthi umbuso wenze unyongwana, ngokusho kweMail & Guardian.

Inhlangano yezomthetho ithe lesi sinqumo singathinta icala laseNingizimu Afrika elihlukile lonyongwana elibhekene no-Israel enkantolweni, kubandakanya nendlela okuhlaziywa ngayo izibopho zemibuso nobufakazi. Umholi wayo Wezobulungiswa Ngamazwe Ngamazwe, uAtilla Kisla, uthe isinqumo sibhekane nezindaba ezihlobene nalelo cala.

Kungani kubalulekileAbashushisi baseNingizimu Afrika nabaphenyi bamacala manje babhekene nesicelo sokuhlola ukugunyazwa kwezikhali nokuthunyelwa kwazo eminyakeni emihlanu kusuka ku-2017 kuya ku-2021.
