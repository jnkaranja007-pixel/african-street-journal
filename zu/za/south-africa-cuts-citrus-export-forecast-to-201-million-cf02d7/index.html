SA yehlisa isibikezelo sokuthunyelwa kwamawolintshi

I-Citrus Growers Association ithi izikhukhula kanye nemikhawulo yaseziteshini kuqhube isilinganiso sika-2026 saba ngaphansi ngo-1.5% kunoka-2025 nango-4% ngaphansi kwesibikezelo sika-April.

Umthombo: Food Business Africa · 25 Novemba 2026 · Imizuzu engu-1 yokufunda

I-Citrus Growers Association yaseNingizimu Afrika ibikezele amabhokisi angu-201 million ama-15kg esizinini samawolintshi sika-2026, ngaphansi ngo-1.5% kunoka-2025 nango-4% ngaphansi kwesilinganiso sayo sika-April sezigidi ezingama-209.4. Izikhukhula phakathi kukaMay naJune kwezinye izingxenye zeEastern naWestern Cape, Limpopo naseMpumalanga, kanye nemikhawulo yaseziteshini zasekhaya, kuholele ekubuyekezweni.

Isilinganiso sokuqala seSouth African Table Grape Industry sika-2026-27 singamabhokisi ayizigidi ezingama-78.4 ahloliwe okuthunyelwa kwamanye amazwe, yehle ngo-4% kusuka esizinini esidlule kodwa yaphezulu ngo-1% ngaphezu kwesilinganiso seminyaka emihlanu. Ukuthunyelwa kwamawolintshi namagilebhasi kwenza ama-24% okuthunyelwa kwezolimo kwaseNingizimu Afrika kwezigidigidi ze-US$15.1 ngo-2025; amaawolintshi abiza amaRandi ayizigidi eziyizi-US$2.5 billion kanti amagilebhasi namagilebhasi omisiwe abiza ama-US$1.1 billion.

Amazinga aphezulu amadamu emva kwemvula yasehlobo ka-2025-26 asekela ukukhiqizwa okuniswayo, kodwa ukuhlaziya kuyaxwayisa ngokuthi isomiso se-El Niño singasinda ezitjalweni zasemasisweni nakwezinye izithelo kanye nemifino ngo-2027. Kuphinde kuveze nokushisa kwesimo sezulu kwamanje nokungenzeka kube yingozi yemvula.

Kungani kubalulekileAbalimi bamawolintshi namagilebhasi abhekene nemithamo ephansi yezibikezelo zokuthunyelwa kwamanye amazwe, kuyilapho iziteshini zasekhaya zihlala ziwumkhawulo ngaphambi kwesizini yokuthumela yangowezi-2027.
