INingizimu Afrika imisa ikomidi njengoba isifo sem Macadamia sithinta amahektha angu-34,000

Lesi sifo sithinta cishe ingxenye yendawo etshalwe ngama-macadamia ezweni, kanti ukwanda kwaso kwehlisa isivuno futhi kubeka imisebenzi engcupheni.

Umthombo: Food Business Africa · 21 Novemba 2026 · Imizuzu engu-1 yokufunda

UMnyango Wezolimo eNingizimu Afrika uthi isifo sama-macadamia felted coccid sesisakazeke cishe kumahektha angu-34,000 ama-orchards, cishe ingxenye yendawo etshalwe kuleli. Kuqinisekisiwe e-Ohrigstad, eLimpopo, ngoNovemba, ngemuva kokusakazeka eMpumalanga Koloni kusukela kuqale ukubonakala eBarberton Valley ngo-2017.

Ukuhlolwa kwamapulazi kuthole ukulimala amaqabunga, izihlahla ezifayo namantongomane amancane; ezinye izihlahla bezinomonakalo ongaphezulu kuka-80% ophahleni lwesihlahla. Esimweni sokusakazeka kakhulu, cishe amahektha angu-14,600 eMpumalanga abhekene nokulahlekelwa isivuno esingafika ku-70%, kanti imisebenzi engu-5,950 isengcupheni. I-Macadamias South Africa ithi isichithe ngaphezu kuka-R6.3 wezigidi ocwaningweni kuze kube ngu-2026. Abalimi babika ukuthi izindleko zemithi zenyuka zisuka ku-R60,000-R70,000 ngehektha ngaphandle kwalesi sifo ziya ku-R100,000 uma ukulawula kungezwa.

Ngemuva kokuvakashela amapulazi ngoNovemba, uhulumeni ubize abathintekayo ukuba baxoxisane ngohlelo lokuqina. Ikomidi eliholwa uhulumeni kazwelonke nelwesifundazwe liphakanyisiwe ukuxhumanisa ucwaningo, ukubhaliswa, ukwesekwa kwabalimi kanye nokuvuselelwa kwama-orchards.

Kungani kubalulekileAbalimi bama-macadamia babhekene nezindleko zemithi zika-R100,000 ngehektha ezindaweni ezithintekile, kanti ikomidi eliholwauhulumeni lizohlanganyela ukweseka nokuvuselela.
