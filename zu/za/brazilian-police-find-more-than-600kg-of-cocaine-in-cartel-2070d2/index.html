Amaphoyisa aseBrazil athola u-600kg kokeine ophenyweni lwekhateli

Uphenyo luqale ngemuva kokuthi umshayeli wendiza wase-Australia uTimothy Clark ephahlakele eBrazil ngo-2025 no-202kg wekokeine endizeni esuka eNingizimu Afrika.

Umthombo: Daily Maverick · 22 Novemba 2026 · Imizuzu engu-1 yokufunda

Amaphoyisa aseBrazil nase-Australia athole ngaphezu kuka-600kg wekokeine futhi abamba abantu abaningana eBrazil, ophenyweni oluhlobene nokuphahlakaka kwendiza kuka-2025. Uphenyo luxhumanisa iNingizimu Afrika nekhateli lomhlaba wonke, ngokusho kombiko.

Umshayeli wendiza wase-Australia uTimothy Clark ushone ngenkathi indiza encane ayishayela isuka eNingizimu Afrika iphahlakaka ensimini yomoba eCoruripe, esifundeni sase-Alase Brazil, mhla ziyi-13 kuSepthemba 2025. Iziphathimandla zithole u-202kg wekokeine endaweni yesigameko, enophawu lwe-SpaceX olungelona iqiniso. UClark ubexhunywe ophenyweni oluholele ekuboshweni kwabantu ababili ePerth, omunye enobudlelwane naseNingizimu Afrika.

Amaphoyisa eZwe laseBrazil amemezele ezinye zezinguquko zakamuva mhla ziyi-18 kuNovemba 2026. Umbiko awubanga nabantu ababoshiwe emkhankasweni omusha waseBrazil.

Kungani kubalulekileAbantu abanezingibe zaseNingizimu Afrika bathinteka ophenyweni oluholele ekuboshweni eBrazil nasePerth, kuyilapho amaphoyisa elandela inethiwekhi yekokeine.
