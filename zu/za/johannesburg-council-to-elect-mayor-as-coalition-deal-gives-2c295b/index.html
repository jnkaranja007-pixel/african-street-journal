UMkhandlu waseGoli uzokhetha imeya kanti isivumelwano siletha i-ANC

Kuthiwa kunesivumelwano samaqembu amane okuyi-ANC, i-ActionSA, i-MK kanye ne-EFF, uHerman Mashaba ongase ahole idolobha.

Imithombo: The South African, Daily Maverick kanye The Citizen SA · 23 Novemba 2026 · Imizuzu engu-1 yokufunda

Umkhandlu kamasipala omusha waseGoli uhlelelwe ukuthi ukhethe imeya emhlanganweni wokuqala ngoMsombuluko mhla ziyi-23 kuNovemba, njengoba izingxoxo zemifelandawonye ziphetha. Umholi we-ActionSA uHerman Mashaba kuthiwa usesicongweni sokuthatha lesi sikhundla. Umhlangano, obekelwe ihora le-13:00 e-Connie Bapela Council Chambers, uzophinde ufake izifungo kumacebhalo futhi ukhethe usomlomo nezinye izikhulu.

I-Daily Maverick ibike ukuthi i-African National Congress (ANC), i-ActionSA, i-MK okuwuhuquzu we-uMkhonto weSizwe, kanye ne-Economic Freedom Fighters (EFF) bavumelene ngokubusa iGoli, iTshwane kanye neEkurhuleni. Lungekho iqembu eliwine ngqwambile eGoli. I-Democratic Alliance (DA) ithole amavoti amaningi kule ndawo ngo-34.84%, kodwa kulindeleke ukuthi ahlale esiphikweni.

I-DA icele umphathi wedolobha laseGoli uFloyd Brink ukuthi ahlehlise umhlangano, ngokusho kwe-TimesLIVE, ithi izingxoxo zemifelandawonye ziyaqhubeka.

Kungani kubalulekileIzakhamizi zaseGoli zizoba nemeya entsha nobuholi bomkhandlu obuzoya ngokuthi isivumelwano se-ANC simelana kanjani nomzamo weDA wokuhlehlisa udaba.
