UMashaba ubuyela esikhundleni sokuba ngucwaningi eGoli

Umholi we-ActionSA ukhethwe emhlanganweni wophiko lwezepolitiki, kulandelwa izinkulumo zemifelandawonye phakathi kwe-ANC, i-ActionSA ne-EFF.

Imithombo: The Citizen SA, Mail & Guardian kanye The South African · 27 Novemba 2026 · Imizuzu engu-1 yokufunda

U-Herman Mashaba ubuyele esikhundleni sokuba ngumphathi wosodolophu eDolobheni laseGoli, emva kokuba amakhansela emkhethile emhlanganweni woMkhandlu eGoli Council Chamber ngoLwesihlanu mhla zingama-27 kuNovemba. Unqobe uHelen Zille weDemocratic Alliance (DA), iqembu ayeyimenenja yalo njengesodolophu kusukela ngo-2016 kuya ku-2019. Manje uhola i-ActionSA.

Amakhansela avotele ngemuva kwemibiko yesivumelwano semifelandawonye ebandakanya i-African National Congress (ANC), i-ActionSA ne-Economic Freedom Fighters (EFF). I-DA inqobe izihlalo eziningi okhethweni luhulumeni wesigodi mhla zine kuNovemba, ngezihlalo ezingama-91 kwezingama-270, ilandelwa yi-ANC enezingama-63 ne-ActionSA enezingama-43, kodwa yehlulekile ukuthola iningi eliphelele.

U-Zille wayeseshilo ukuthi izingxoxo zemifelandawonye zemizuzu yokugcina zazisaphithizela ngaphambi kokuba kuvotelwe, futhi wenqabe ukusho ukuthi i-DA yayixoxisana ne-ActionSA noma cha.
