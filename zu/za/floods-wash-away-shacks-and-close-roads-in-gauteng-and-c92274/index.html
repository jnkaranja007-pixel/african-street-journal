Izikhukhula zikhukhula imijondolo zavimba nemigwaqo

Umonakalo wesiphepho ufake amanzi emijondolweni, waphazamisa izinsiza zomphakathi waphinde wonakalisa ibhuloho ku-R542 eduze kwaseBoomplaas.

Imithombo: The South African kanye The Citizen SA · 26 Novemba 2026 · Imizuzu engu-1 yokufunda

Imvula eningi ngoLwesibili nangoLwesithathu ifake amanzi emakhaya, yakhukhula imijondolo yonakalisa nemigwaqo eGauteng naseMpumalanga. EBethal, eMpumalanga, abafana ababili abasha baminze ngezigameko ezihlukene. Amathimba ezimo eziphuthumayo ahlenge abashayeli bezimoto njengoba amanzi ezikhukhula emboze imigwaqo namabhuloho.

UMasipala Wase-Ekurhuleni uqophe izigameko ezingama-20 ezihlobene nesiphepho, kubandakanya ukuhlengwa kwabantu abayi-15 emanzini namacala amahlanu ezihlahla eziwele phansi. Imijondolo eminingi endaweni yaseGugulethu kubikwa ukuthi ikhukhulekile, kanti amakhaya aseSkomplaas, eSprings, angene amanzi. Okhulumela izicishamlilo zaseGoli uRobert Mulaudzi uthe amapayipi amanzi emvula avalekile anegalelo ezikhukhuleni. I-Johannesburg Roads Agency ivaliwe uMgwaqo uNasrec emva kokuba ingqalasizinda iwile.

E-Mpumalanga, izikhukhula zonakalise amahhovisi nemishini esiteshini samaphoyisa saseEvander zaphazamisa ugesi nezinsiza zobuchwepheshe esiteshini saseKwaMhlanga. Ibhuloho ku-R542 eduze kwaseBoomplaas lonakele, futhi iziphathimandla zapambukisa ithrafikhi ngenkathi zisahlola. Imigwaqo yaseMoloto ayasakwazi ukuhanjwa, futhi nesibhedlela sendaweni sithintekile.
