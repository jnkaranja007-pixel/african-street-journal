AmaBhokobhoko afinyelela kowamanqamu naphezu kokuhlulwa yi-Ireland

I-Ireland inqobile eDublin, kodwa iphoyinti leBokobhoko lokulahlekelwa liyazifikisa kowamanqamu eTwickenham.

Imithombo: The Citizen SA, Daily Maverick kanye BBC Africa · 21 Novemba 2026 · Imizuzu engu-1 yokufunda

I-Ireland inqobe iNingizimu Afrika ngo-20-17 eDublin ngoMgqibelo, kodwa amaBhokobhoko athole iphoyinti lokulahlekelwa ngokwehlulwa ngamaphoyinti angaphansi kwayisikhombisa futhi aqhubekela phambili kowamanqamu eNations Championship. Lo mdlalo ubuse-Aviva Stadium, kanti ukunqotshwa bekungokwesibili kulandelana kwamaBhokobhoko ngemuva kokuhlulwa yiFrance ngempelasonto edlule.

I-Ireland ihambe phambili ngo-17-10 ngesikhathi sekhefu futhi ishaye imizamo emithathu kune yaseNingizimu Afrika emibili. I-Ireland inqobe ibhola lama-lineout, yaphatha umdlalo futhi yagcina amaBhokobhoko ecindezelwe ohlangothini lwawo isikhathi esiningi somdlalo. U-Rassie Erasmus, umqeqeshi omisiwe, ubukele eshotela leqembu.

Owamanqamu useTwickenham ngeSonto elizayo, lapho iNingizimu Afrika kulindeleke ukuthi ibhekane neFrance noma i-England. IFrance ibihlelelwe ukudlala ne-Argentina ngokuhamba kwesikhathi ngoMgqibelo, kanti ukunqoba kwe-England kuma-All Blacks ngalolo suku kusho ukuthi bayazibandakanya uma iNingizimu Afrika ingaphumeleli.

Kungani kubalulekileAmaBhokobhoko afanele alungiselele owamanqamu eTwickenham ngeSonto elizayo, ebhekene neFrance noma i-England.
