IJohannesburg yandisausesho lomfana oneminyaka engu-11 okhukhuleke ezikhukhuleni zaseSoweto

Amaqembu asesha kusuka enqulwini yezibi ngamamitha angaba ngu-4 emahleni, namaphoyisa ezinja, abatshuza kanye namalungu omphakathi abamba iqhaza kulo msebenzi.

Imithombo: The Citizen SA, Mail & Guardian kanye The South African · 19 Novemba 2026 · Imizuzu engu-1 yokufunda

Amaqembu okuhlenga aseJohannesburg andise usesho lwawo ngoLwesine luka-Anqobile Jiyela, umfana oneminyaka engu-11 okhukhuleke amanzi ezikhukhula eSoweto ntambama ngoLwesibili. Usesho luqale kabusha eDobsonville Extension 3 emuva kokuthi isimo sezulu esibi simisiwe ngoLwesithathu.

Okhulumela uPhiko Lwezimo Eziphuthumayo Zezokwelapha uRobert Mulaudzi uthe amaqembu ayasebenza kusuka enqulwini yezibi ngamamitha angaba ngu-4 emahleni aye lapho u-Anqobile angene khona emanzini, kanye nokuhlola iziteshi zamanzi eziseduze. Amaqembu amabili amaphoyisa ezinja, amaqembu amabili ochwepheshe bezokuhlenga, abatshuza kanye namalungu omphakathi babambe iqhaza kulo msebenzi, ohlanganisa indawo ephakathi kwamabhuloho amabili.

Indiza enophephela emhlathini yamaphoyisa isize ngomsebenzi wamadrone, kanti amaqembu asuse imihlanga ukuthuthukisa ukungena. UMulaudzi utshele i-eNCA ukuthi amaqembu ahlele ukusebenzisa amaphetshana nemimese ngoLwesihlanu ukususa imihlanga eyengeziwe futhi angene endaweni eqondiwe.

Kungani kubalulekileAmaqembu okuhlenga kanye namavolontiya omphakathi eDobsonville kumele aqhubeke nokusesha phakathi kwamabhuloho amabili, namaqembu esebenza kusuka enqulwini yamamitha angaba ngu-4 emahleni.
