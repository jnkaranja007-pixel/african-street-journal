Ufakazi ubehlwitha iphoyisa laseGoli elimisiwe ngokusiza iqembu likaMolefe

KwiKhomishini kaMadlanga, iphoyisa lithe imiyalezo ikhombisa ukuthi uJohannes Mokgatle ubenezandla okwedlula ukuba umunyu.

Imithombo: The Citizen SA, Daily Maverick kanye Mail & Guardian · 23 Novemba 2026 · Imizuzu engu-1 yokufunda

Iphoyisa le-SAPS lophando ePitoli lithe kwiKhomishini kaMadlanga ngoMsombuluko ukuthi uJohannes Mokgatle oyiphoyisa elimisiwe laseMetro yaseGoli, wayengaphezulu komunyu nje kuphela kumuntu osolwa ngokubulala uKatiso "KT" Molefe nabangane bakhe. Ufakazi C uthe imiyalezo ye-WhatsApp ikhombisa ukuthi uMokgatle wayeyingxenye yeqembu labo futhi kungenzeka waba ingxenye yemisebenzi yabo.

Le miyalezo ethathwe kwiphoni kaMokgatle ngoFebruwari 2025, ichaza leli qembu ngokuthi "umndeni" noma "ubuzalwane", kufakaza uFakazi C. Uthe le miyalezo ikhombisa ukuthi uMokgatle wayesondelene kakhulu noMolefe nabanye abasolwa ngokubulala unjiniyela u-Armand Swart. UMolefe ucalwa kanye noMusa Kekena, Tiego Floyd Mabusela kanye nowayelijaji lasekhaya uMichael Pule Tau.

UFakazi C uphinde wacashela uMokgatle ngokusebenzisa isikhundla sakhe samaphoyisa nokuthola ulwazi oluyimfihlo ukuze asize uMolefe nabangane bakhe. Uthe isimo sasingaphansi kukaTau, owayebika kuMolefe, kanti uMolefe wayebonakala ebikela usaziwayo wamataxi ongasekhona uJotham "King Mswazi" Msibi.
