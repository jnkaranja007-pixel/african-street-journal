Abathengi bezindlu bokuqala baqophe u-48.5% wezicelo

Imininingwane ye-ooba ikhomba ukuthi abathengi bokuqala bakhokha idiphozithi ka-8.5%, kanti izicelo ezivunyiwe zifinyelele ku-83.9% kulo nyaka.

Umthombo: The South African · 22 Novemba 2026 · Imizuzu engu-1 yokufunda

Abathengi bezindlu bokuqala benze u-48.5% wezicelo zemalimboleko yezindlu eNingizimu Afrika ngo-October, ngokusho kwemininingwane ye-ooba Home Loans esetshenziswe yiPam Golding Properties. Isabelo sabo besingu-46.9% ngesilinganiso kulo nyaka kuze kube manje.

Laba bathengi bakhokhe idiphozithi engu-8.5% ngesilinganiso senani lokuthenga, uma iqhathaniswa no-12.8% kubo bonke abathengi bezindlu onyakeni kuze kube ngu-October. Izinga lokuvunywa kwalemalimboleko belingu-83.9% ngesilinganiso kulo nyaka, kanti i-ooba ithole izinga eliyi-prime elingu-minus 0.77% ngo-October, le mininingwane ikhomba njalo.

Izicelo ezihlanganisa izindleko zomthetho, zentela kanye nezokuphatha zenze u-10.1% wezicelo ngoSeptember no-October. IBabhanki Enkulu (SARB) lishiye izinga lenzalo yezikweletu (repo rate) lingashintshile emhlanganweni walo kaNovemba, kanti izinga lenzalo yebhange (prime lending rate) lisezinsukwini ezingu-10.75%.

Kungani kubalulekileAbathengi bokuqala bafaka idiphozithi engu-8.5% ngesilinganiso, uma iqhathaniswa no-12.8% kubo bonke abathengi bezindlu enyangeni ka-October.
