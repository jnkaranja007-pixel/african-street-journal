Women parliamentarians seek Isidore Mvouba's support for parity

A delegation from the Francophonie Parliamentary Assembly's women's network met with the National Assembly president in Brazzaville to discuss increasing female representation.

Source: Journal de Brazza · 2026-11-15 · 1 min read

The Network of Women Parliamentarians of the Francophonie Parliamentary Assembly (APF) met with Isidore Mvouba, president of the National Assembly, on November 11 in Brazzaville to discuss gender parity in politics. The meeting aimed to advance the representation of women in the Republic of Congo's parliament, which remains below legal objectives.

The delegation, in the country for a two-day seminar on women's leadership and local governance, presented conclusions from their work to Mvouba. Discussions focused on legislative measures supporting women, mechanisms for promoting their presence in decision-making bodies, and future prospects for increasing their parliamentary representation.

Laure Baflan Donwahi, a member of the APF women's network steering committee, stated that exchanges covered legislative frameworks for women, parity, and future parliamentary perspectives. The Republic of Congo's law already enshrines gender parity.

Why it mattersWomen parliamentarians are seeking support from the National Assembly president to increase female representation in parliament, a goal enshrined in law.
