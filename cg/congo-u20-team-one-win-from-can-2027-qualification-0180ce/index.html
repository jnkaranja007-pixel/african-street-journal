Congo U20 team one win from CAN 2027 qualification

The junior Red Devils will face the Central African Republic in a semi-final match on October 21, aiming for a spot in the continental championship.

Source: Journal de Brazza · 2026-10-22 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

The Republic of Congo's U20 football team, known as the Red Devils, are 90 minutes away from qualifying for the 2027 Africa U20 Cup of Nations. They will play the Central African Republic in the semi-finals of the Union of Football Federations of Central Africa (UNIFFAC) tournament on October 21 at the Olembé Stadium in Cameroon.

This crucial match represents a significant opportunity for the Congolese youth team to advance in the regional competition and secure a place in the continental finals. The team needs to demonstrate determination and effectiveness to overcome their opponents, the Fauves du Bas-Oubangui.

A victory would not only send the Red Devils to the UNIFFAC final but also earn them a ticket to the CAN U20 finals in Ghana in 2027. This qualification would mark an important milestone for Congolese football, offering the young generation a chance to compete against Africa's best youth teams.

Why it mattersThe U20 football team and Congolese football stand to gain prestige and a platform to compete at a higher level if they qualify for the CAN U20 tournament.
