Congo president meets UN official on climate action

Denis Sassou-N’Guesso and Khalilur Rahman discussed forest preservation and climate change ahead of COP31.

Source: Journal de Brazza · 2026-11-10 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

President Denis Sassou-N’Guesso met with Khalilur Rahman, president of the 81st UN General Assembly and Bangladesh's foreign minister, on Sunday, November 8, 2026, in Brazzaville. The leaders discussed environmental issues, focusing on preserving forest ecosystems and addressing the urgency of climate change. Rahman led a delegation of UN diplomats during their visit to the Republic of Congo.

The meeting emphasized the need for stronger international cooperation to protect the environment, especially as climate change effects intensify. Both officials stressed the importance of turning international commitments into concrete actions before the upcoming COP31 summit in Antalya, Turkey. They called for moving beyond mere declarations to implement effective climate measures.

The Congo Basin forests, vital carbon reservoirs, hold strategic importance for global warming efforts. This discussion occurred as the world prepares for the 31st UN Climate Change Conference. The leaders aimed to accelerate the implementation of effective climate measures.

Why it mattersThe President of the Republic of Congo met with a UN official to discuss climate change and forest preservation, issues directly impacting the nation's environment and its role in global climate efforts.
