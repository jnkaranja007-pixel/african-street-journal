Congo football league expands to 18 clubs

The Fécofoot will adopt a zonal format for the next national championship to cut costs.

Source: Journal de Brazza · 2026-10-30 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

The Congolese Football Federation (Fécofoot) plans to expand its top-tier league from 14 to 18 teams for the upcoming season. The competition will adopt a zonal format, a decision announced on October 28 in Brazzaville. Fécofoot president Éric Alain Mantot stated this change aims to address the economic realities faced by clubs and reduce the financial burden of organizing the championship.

This new structure requires clubs to adapt their operations. Fécofoot will also provide training for club leaders from November 16 to 18 in Brazzaville, with support from FIFA. Topics will include financial management, leadership, and marketing. Clubs signing agreements for exceptional subsidies will undergo fund usage checks.

Mantot clarified that Fécofoot will not directly fund clubs but will encourage them to seek sponsors. The federation aims to enhance management capabilities and financial transparency within sports structures. The success of this new organization hinges on clubs' ability to adapt to these financial and operational changes.

Why it mattersCongolese football clubs will face operational and financial adjustments due to the league's expansion and new zonal format.
