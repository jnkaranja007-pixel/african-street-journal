Congo children’s parliament urges adults to include young people in decisions

Its president said children want their views to count on choices affecting their future, including access to school, health care and safety.

Source: Vox.cg · 2026-11-22 · 1 min read

Lucia Bouenitelamio, president of the Republic of Congo’s children’s parliament, called on adults in Brazzaville on 20 November to give greater weight to young people’s needs and concerns in decisions affecting them. She spoke at the country’s celebration of World Children’s Day.

Bouenitelamio said children want their views to count in decisions about their future, including whether they can attend school, receive medical care and live safely. Gilles Figninou, the United Nations Children’s Fund’s regional director for West and Central Africa, praised the children’s determination and said children’s rights were not negotiable.

Figninou called for coordinated action to defend those rights amid crises. The celebration took place against a worrying health backdrop for children, including flooding in some northern regions, Vox.cg reported.

Why it mattersChildren in Congo’s flood-affected northern regions face a worrying health context, while the children’s parliament is asking adults to include their needs in decisions.
