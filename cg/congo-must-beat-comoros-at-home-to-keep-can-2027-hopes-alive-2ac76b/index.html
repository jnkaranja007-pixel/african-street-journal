Congo must beat Comoros at home to keep CAN 2027 hopes alive

The Red Devils drew 0-0 in Moroni and now host the Comoros in Brazzaville on Tuesday, needing a win to lift their total to five points.

Source: Journal de Brazza · 2026-11-17 · 1 min read

The Republic of Congo's national football team, the Red Devils, drew 0-0 with the Comoros in Moroni on Friday, 13 November, in the third round of the Africa Cup of Nations (CAN) 2027 qualifiers. The result leaves Congo bottom of Group G with two points from three matches, behind Cameroon on 7, the Comoros on 4 and Namibia on 3.

Coach Claude Le Roy's side held firm at the back, with goalkeeper Melvin Zinga repelling several attempts. In the 85th minute, the crossbar saved Congo from a shot by Idris Mohamed. The attack, however, failed to convert its chances, and the team missed chances in the final moment.

The return match is scheduled for Tuesday, 17 November, at the Alphonse-Massamba-Débat stadium in Brazzaville. Congo must win to reach five points and overtake the Comoros in the table. Before the double fixture, Le Roy had said he wanted at least four points from the six available.
