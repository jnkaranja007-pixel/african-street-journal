Congo Officials Map Electoral Districts for 2027 Polls

Election officials met with Pointe-Noire mayors to review electoral maps and inventory materials for upcoming legislative and local elections.

Preparations for the July 2027 legislative and local elections are underway in Pointe-Noire, Republic of Congo. Jean-Claude Etoumbakoundou, the Director General of Electoral Affairs, met with Pointe-Noire's district mayors on October 16 to discuss electoral mapping and material inventory. Accompanied by local authorities, including the prefect, Etoumbakoundou focused on defining electoral boundaries and identifying potential voting centers and offices.

The meeting addressed the framework for electoral operations and the necessary material for the upcoming polls in the Pointe-Noire department. Etoumbakoundou emphasized the critical role of electoral mapping in the preparation process. This involves precisely determining constituency limits and pinpointing suitable locations for polling stations, which is a foundational step for logistical planning and material deployment.

This electoral mapping exercise is one of the initial stages for organizing the consultations. It aims to anticipate logistical needs and ensure efficient distribution of electoral materials across the region. The discussions with local administrators are crucial for a smooth electoral process in Pointe-Noire.
