Congo regulator presents Wapi-Yo to postal operators

The digital platform is intended to support postal services, while the regulator says physical transport infrastructure is still needed to deliver mail.

Source: Vox.cg · 2026-11-20 · 1 min read

The Republic of Congo’s postal and electronic communications regulator presented its Wapi-Yo digital platform to postal operators in Brazzaville on 19 November, during Postal Regulation Day. The regulator also cited Landayango as a digital tool developed for the country’s postal sector.

Rudy Massamba, the regulator’s director of projects and planning, said digital tools could support postal services, but were not enough on their own. He said transport infrastructure was needed to move letters between locations. Benjamin Mouandza, the regulator’s director of networks and electronic communications services, urged operators to use the tools and comply with requirements ranging from approval to regular data submissions.

Mouandza said regulation should protect users, secure investment and ensure equal conditions for operators. The event included a panel on digital technology’s impact on postal services, followed by commitments between the regulator and postal operators.

Why it mattersPostal operators are expected to adopt the regulator’s digital tools and provide data regularly, alongside meeting approval requirements.
