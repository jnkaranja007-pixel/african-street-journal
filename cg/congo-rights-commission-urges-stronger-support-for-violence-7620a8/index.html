Congo rights commission urges stronger support for violence survivors

In a statement in Brazzaville, the commission called for better prevention and protection and an end to impunity for violence against women and girls.

Source: Journal de Brazza · 2026-11-28 · 1 min read

The Republic of Congo’s National Human Rights Commission urged the government to strengthen prevention and support for women and girls facing violence. It made the call in a statement released in Brazzaville on 25 November.

Gickelle Mpika, head of the commission’s subcommittee on economic, social and cultural rights, presented the statement. The commission cited the 2022 law against violence towards women, a national programme, the Mouébara centre and hotline 1444 as measures already in place.

The commission urged the government to build on those measures to improve care for survivors and combat impunity.

Why it mattersThe government is being asked to improve survivor care through existing services, including the Mouébara centre and hotline 1444.
