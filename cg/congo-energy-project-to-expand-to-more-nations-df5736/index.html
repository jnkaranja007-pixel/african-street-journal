Congo energy project to expand to more nations

The Pool of Central African Energy aims to boost regional electricity trade and grid interconnection.

Source: Journal de Brazza · 2026-11-07 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

The Pool of Central African Energy (PEAC) plans to expand its Energy Friendship Loop project to include more countries in the sub-region. This initiative, discussed on November 5 in Brazzaville between the Republic of Congo's Minister of Energy Bruno Jean Richard Itoua and PEAC officials, aims to strengthen energy exchanges across Central Africa. Initially connecting the Republic of Congo, Angola, and the Democratic Republic of Congo, the project could now incorporate new states to enhance cross-border electricity trade and grid interconnections.

PEAC's ambition is to accelerate regional energy integration amidst ongoing challenges with electricity access and supply security in several sub-Saharan nations. Discussions also covered the proposed Chollet hydroelectric dam, which has an estimated production capacity of 600 MW. This infrastructure is seen as a key project to increase electricity generation and support trade among participating countries.

The PEAC seeks to promote better sharing of energy resources and speed up the development of common infrastructure. The ultimate goal is to leverage grid interconnection as a tool for energy security and economic development throughout Central Africa.

Why it mattersThe expansion of the Energy Friendship Loop project will affect citizens in the Republic of Congo and potentially other Central African nations by improving electricity access and supply security.
