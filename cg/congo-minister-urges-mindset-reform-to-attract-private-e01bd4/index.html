Congo minister urges mindset reform to attract private investors

Denis Christel Sassou Nguesso says administrative conduct is driving investors to seek more favourable conditions in other countries.

Source: Vox.cg · 2026-11-28 · 1 min read

Republic of Congo’s minister for public-private partnerships, Denis Christel Sassou Nguesso, called for changes in attitudes to improve the business climate, speaking in Brazzaville on 24 November at the 5th Vox Eco Forum. He said reform was needed in public administration, government directorates and ministerial offices.

The minister said investors facing what he described as improper conduct by officials were turning to other countries in the sub-region for more favourable conditions. He said the private sector was expected to provide nearly 70% of the funding for the new national development plan. The 2022-2026 plan had identified a financing gap of nearly 8 CFA000 billion to be filled by private investment, he said.

Sassou Nguesso said reforms were already under way but the government might not have communicated or publicised them sufficiently. He said changing conduct was now the work required.

Why it mattersPrivate investors expected to fund nearly 70% of the new national development plan face administrative conduct the minister says sends them to other countries.
