Congo inspectors seize 488 cartons of expired beauty lotion

Market-control brigades in Poto-Poto sanctioned the establishment’s manager and will oversee the seized goods’ destruction by incineration.

Source: Journal de Brazza · 2026-11-19 · 1 min read

Market-control brigades in Poto-Poto, a district in Brazzaville, seized 488 cartons of expired beauty lotion from distribution in the Republic of Congo. The products were unfit for sale because their expiry dates had passed.

The departmental directorate for competition and commercial fraud said the establishment’s responsible manager faced sanctions under current rules. The seized cartons are to be destroyed by incineration under the supervision of authorised services.

The control services said they intend to strengthen market surveillance. Consumers were urged to check expiry dates and storage conditions before buying products.

Why it mattersThe establishment’s manager has faced sanctions, while authorised services must supervise the incineration of all 488 seized cartons.
