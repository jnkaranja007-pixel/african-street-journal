Congo opens 20-hectare Madingo-Kayes farming zone

The site has solar-powered pumping for irrigation, mechanisation support and four Kavaki vehicles, with vegetables intended for markets in three cities.

Source: Vox.cg · 2026-11-23 · 1 min read

The Republic of Congo has opened a 20-hectare protected agricultural zone for vegetable farming at Madingo-Kayes, in Kouilou department. Agriculture and Livestock Minister Paul Valentin Ngobo said it was the government’s largest investment in infrastructure for such zones in the country.

The site has a solar-powered pumping station supplying 12 irrigation units. It also received mechanisation support and four Kavaki vehicles, Ngobo said. Local authorities in Kouilou have promised to monitor activities. Arthur Tamane Mavoungou, the zone’s president, said growers plan to supply markets in Madingo-Kayes, Pointe-Noire and Brazzaville.

The zone is the first protected agricultural zone in Kouilou. Mavoungou also said producers hoped to sell at the Congo agricultural fair.

Why it mattersGrowers at the site have irrigation and mechanisation support, while Kouilou’s local authorities have promised to monitor their activities.
