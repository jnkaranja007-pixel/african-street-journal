Congo resumes government evaluation seminars after two-year pause

The eighth seminar, opened in Brazzaville on 13 November by Prime Minister Anatole Collinet Makosso, reviews three planning documents that set priorities to 2031 and beyond.

Source: Journal de Brazza · 2026-11-17 · 1 min read

After a pause of more than two years, the Congolese government has resumed its evaluation seminars. The eighth seminar opened on 13 November at the Palais des congrès in Brazzaville, with Prime Minister Anatole Collinet Makosso presiding. It is the first seminar of the new five-year term.

The seminar is examining three documents: an evaluation of the 2022-2026 National Development Plan (PND), the draft Congo-Vision 2063 strategic document, and the draft PND for 2027-2031. The prime minister said the evaluation must identify progress as well as difficulties, shortfalls and the factors that slowed results, not serve only as a report.

The 2022-2026 plan was adopted by law no. 3-2022 of 14 January 2022 and aimed at a strong, diversified and resilient economy. During its implementation an acceleration programme for 2024-2026 was adopted to speed up delivery, including youth aspirations.

Why it mattersMinistries and public bodies will use the outcome of these reviews to set the national development priorities for 2027 to 2031.
