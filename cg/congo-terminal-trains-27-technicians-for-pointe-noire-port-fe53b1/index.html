Congo Terminal trains 27 technicians for Pointe-Noire port cranes

The 9–14 November course covered maintenance of ship-to-shore cranes planned for the port’s future East Mole.

Source: Journal de Brazza · 2026-11-24 · 1 min read

Congo Terminal trained 27 technicians in maintenance of ship-to-shore (STS) quay cranes from 9 to 14 November 2026, ahead of the equipment’s planned arrival at the East Mole of Pointe-Noire port. The specialised course covered cranes with advanced control systems and technologies.

The company said the training is intended to equip local teams to operate and maintain the cranes. Aristide Ndjawe, Congo Terminal’s human resources director, said it would strengthen the company’s technical autonomy and its ability to maintain equipment to international standards.

The course follows other technical training organised abroad in recent months: in Ghana and Finland for electric tugs, China for STS and electric yard cranes, and France for automated port-equipment systems.

Why it mattersThe 27 technicians are being prepared to maintain the new cranes, while Congo Terminal must equip local teams to operate them.
