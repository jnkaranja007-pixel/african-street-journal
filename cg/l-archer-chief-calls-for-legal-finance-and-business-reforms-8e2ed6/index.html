L’Archer chief calls for legal, finance and business reforms in Congo

Gilles Tchamba says clear state rules, bolder entrepreneurs and finance that channels savings to businesses are needed for a healthier business climate.

Source: Vox.cg · 2026-11-28 · 1 min read

Gilles Tchamba, chief executive of Congo’s Groupe L’Archer, said the business climate depends on three things: a legal framework set by the state, entrepreneurs willing to take risks and financial institutions that direct funds to businesses. He made the case in an interview at the Vox Eco Forum.

Tchamba said banks in Central Africa still rely on guarantees to approve loans, a model he said traps savings instead of moving them to borrowers. He pointed to rules from the Central African Financial Market Supervisory Commission, which have enabled crowdfunding and private-equity investment funds.

He said the financial system should provide businesses with money for investment and daily operations, while entrepreneurs also need to be willing to act. Tchamba argued that improving the business climate requires a collective response from the state, finance providers and entrepreneurs.

Why it mattersBusinesses seeking investment and operating funds depend on financial institutions to channel savings, while entrepreneurs and the state also have roles, Tchamba said.
