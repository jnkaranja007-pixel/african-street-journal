Congo minister reaffirms human rights commitment

Aimé Ange Wilfrid Bininga attended the African Commission on Human and Peoples' Rights 89th session in Dakar.

Source: Journal de Brazza · 2026-10-23 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Republic of Congo's Justice Minister Aimé Ange Wilfrid Bininga attended the 89th ordinary session of the African Commission on Human and Peoples' Rights in Dakar, Senegal, from October 12 to 21, 2026. He led an official delegation to discuss the promotion and protection of human rights across Africa.

Minister Bininga stated that while the Republic of Congo has made progress in human rights, continuous efforts are necessary. He emphasized that effective human rights protection requires constant commitment from public authorities and all stakeholders.

The Congo's participation in the session allowed for discussions on fundamental rights challenges, strengthening protection mechanisms, and implementing African states' commitments. The Commission, an African Union body, works to promote and protect human rights on the continent.
