MaishaPay launches merchant payment and stock platform in Congo

The service links sales tracking and mobile-money or card payments with Linkia, which lets wholesalers and retailers order stock and pay suppliers.

Source: Vox.cg · 2026-11-22 · 1 min read

MaishaPay, an African fintech company, began operating in Brazzaville on 21 November with a payment terminal and Linkia, a platform connecting merchants and suppliers. Founder Landry Ngoya Kasim said the service is designed to help merchants manage sales and stock.

The terminal accepts Mobile Money and card payments, depending on which services are activated, while Linkia lets wholesalers order stock and pay for goods from their shops or sales points. A Vival corporate sales manager said the platform centralises transactions and allows the wholesaler to collect recorded payments at the end of the day. Merchant Jolie Moly said she has used MaishaPay for three months and can pay suppliers electronically or in cash; customers can pay through Mobile Money or Airtel Money and receive invoices tracking their purchases.

Founded in the Democratic Republic of Congo in 2018, MaishaPay also operates in Ethiopia, Nigeria and Côte d’Ivoire.

Why it mattersMerchants can record sales and stock, take customer payments through Mobile Money or Airtel Money, and receive invoices that track purchases.
