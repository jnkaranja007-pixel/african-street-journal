Congo validates preschool education standards guide

A five-day workshop in Brazzaville aims to refine a new guide for preschools to ensure child development.

Source: Journal de Brazza · 2026-11-05 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

A guide for preschool education standards is being validated in Brazzaville from November 2 to 6. The workshop, opened by Minister of Preschool, Primary, Secondary Education and Literacy Jean-Luc Mouthou, aims to improve the functioning of preschools. The document defines conditions for the well-being and development of young children, focusing on safe and hygienic learning environments.

Participants will review the guide's content over five days, offering feedback to create a unified and relevant document for the Congolese education system. The process involves examining four complementary areas to strengthen preschool regulations. Authorities intend to establish a better framework for early childhood education and promote supportive learning conditions from the earliest years.

The guide's application standards will define necessary conditions for child development and learning environments. This initiative seeks to ensure safety, hygiene, and protection for young children in preschool settings.

Why it mattersPreschool children in the Republic of Congo will benefit from improved learning environments and safety standards.
