Congo holds aquaculture forum as local fish output falls short of demand

The country needs about 163 000 tonnes of fish a year, while local production reaches nearly 60 000 tonnes, leaving a gap of more than 100 000 tonnes.

The Republic of Congo held its first Forum on Investment in Aquaculture at the Grande foire agricole du Congo in Bambou Mingali, Ignié district, over the weekend. National fish needs are estimated at about 163 000 tonnes a year, while local production is nearly 60 000 tonnes.

The shortfall of more than 100 000 tonnes is met through imports, Journal de Brazza reported. Nearly 120 experts and sector participants gathered for two days to discuss barriers to aquaculture growth and hear findings from an international organisation’s study of the country’s aquaculture potential.

The forum received financial support from the World Bank and Proclimat and was organised ahead of the planned launch of the Congo Agriculture and Aquaculture Development Project. The initiative is intended to attract investment and increase production capacity.

Why it mattersFish consumers rely on imports to cover a shortfall of more than 100 000 tonnes against annual national needs.

Business · 2026-11-18

Fire damages shops and stalls at Dolisie Grand Market

The blaze spread through part of the market near the MUCODEC roundabout on Monday evening, prompting firefighters to call in reinforcements.

A fire broke out on the evening of Monday 16 November in part of the Grand Market in Dolisie, near the MUCODEC roundabout, and spread to several shops and stalls. The flames caused material damage, but the extent of the losses has not been established.

Firefighters responded to contain the blaze and stop it spreading to other businesses. Witness accounts shared on social media said the first fire truck to arrive had no water, complicating the initial response; reinforcements were later mobilised to extinguish the fire. Some traders tried to move their goods to safety as panic spread among merchants and nearby residents.

The cause of the fire remains unknown, and no official account of property losses or casualties has been released. Several traders may have lost a substantial share of their goods and property, according to Journal de Brazza.

Sport · 2026-11-18

Congo loses to Comoros as Claude Le Roy announces resignation

The 1-0 defeat in Brazzaville leaves the national team last in Group G with two points after four matches in the 2027 Africa Cup of Nations qualifiers.

Congo lost 1-0 to Comoros in Brazzaville on Tuesday, 17 November, in the fourth round of qualifying for the 2027 Africa Cup of Nations. Ben Chayeel Hamada scored the only goal in the 32nd minute.

After the defeat, Congo coach Claude Le Roy said he intended to resign, saying he had failed in his task. Congo have two points from four Group G matches, with one goal scored and three conceded; second-placed Comoros have seven points.

Congo drew 0-0 away to Comoros in Moroni on 13 November. The team has two qualifying matches left, and Le Roy said a miracle would be needed for Congo to reach the finals.

Why it mattersCongo have two qualifying matches left, with the team five points behind second-placed Comoros in Group G.

Business · 2026-11-18

Congo Terminal installs simulator to train port-equipment operators

The 3D system lets workers practise emergencies without taking cranes, terminal vehicles or tugboats out of service.

Congo Terminal received and put into operation a 3D training simulator on 13 November at its Operations Directorate. The equipment recreates conditions for using STS gantry cranes, RTG cranes, mobile cranes and port tugboats.

The company said the simulator lets operators practise routine work and rare emergencies without tying up the terminal’s working equipment. Training manager Ngala Sanda said it is expected to cut training hours by 30 to 40 % and let experienced operators refresh their skills more often, at lower cost.

Technical director Kehindé Salami said the system allows teams to repeat varied exercises without interrupting operations. Congo Terminal said the simulator is also intended to support training across the AGL network and ports of medium size.

Why it mattersCongo Terminal expects the simulator to reduce operators’ training hours by 30 to 40 % while keeping equipment available for terminal operations.

Politics · 2026-11-18

Congo presents three priorities at Francophonie meeting in Phnom Penh

Foreign Minister Constant Serge Bounda urged member states to back a forest decade, Kébé-Kébé heritage status and Congo’s bid for a UN Security Council seat.

The Republic of Congo presented three priorities for member-state support at the 48th Ministerial Conference of La Francophonie in Phnom Penh, Cambodia, on 14 November. Foreign Minister Constant Serge Bounda, who led the Congolese delegation, called for the Francophone community to support the United Nations Decade for Afforestation and Reforestation, covering 2027-2036.

Brazzaville also sought backing for Kébé-Kébé’s nomination to UNESCO’s list of intangible cultural heritage. Its third priority is a bid for a non-permanent seat on the United Nations Security Council for 2030-2031; Congo cited its experience in mediation and seeking political solutions to crises.

The ministerial session finalised strategic documents for heads of state and government, who met in Phnom Penh on 15 and 16 November. The gathering also addressed the designation of the organisation’s next secretary-general.

Why it mattersCongo’s bid for a non-permanent Security Council seat covers 2030-2031, while its forest proposal seeks Francophone support for a decade beginning in 2027.
