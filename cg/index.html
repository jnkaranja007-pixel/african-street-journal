AGL Congo raises breast cancer awareness

Companies including AGL Congo, Congo Terminal, and SAGA Congo held events in Brazzaville and Pointe-Noire from October 15-17, 2026.

AGL Congo, Congo Terminal, SAGA Congo, and TBC organized events between October 15 and 17, 2026, for the international breast cancer awareness campaign, Octobre Rose. Under the slogan "Breast cancer must no longer silence hope," these initiatives aimed to boost prevention awareness, encourage early detection, and prioritize employee well-being in Brazzaville and Pointe-Noire.

On October 15, a webinar with 108 participants, led by the company's medical advisor, discussed risk factors, early detection, and prevention. A practical self-examination workshop and quiz followed. On October 17, AGL Congo, Congo Terminal, and SAGA Congo teams joined a 6.5-kilometer Pink Ribbon solidarity walk in Pointe-Noire with partners MCL and Urban Sport, including sports activities and discussions on breast cancer awareness.

In Brazzaville, female employees participated in a well-being morning that included a discussion with a psychologist on mental health and daily well-being, concluding with a breakfast. Erlette Raïssa DEKAMBI, Quality Compliance and CSR Service Chief, stated that participating in the Pink Ribbon was a "beautiful experience of solidarity and sharing" that reminds them to care for their health and encourage early screening. Roberta Mieret, an AGL Brazzaville administrative agent, described the well-being morning as a "true moment of relaxation and exchange."

Why it mattersEmployees of AGL Congo and its subsidiaries in Brazzaville and Pointe-Noire participated in events to raise awareness and promote early detection of breast cancer.

Congo Terminal installs 65 dock fenders at Môle Est

The rubber and steel buffers protect ships from impact during docking operations at Pointe-Noire port.

Congo Terminal has begun installing 65 dock fenders along a 750-meter stretch of the Môle Est in Pointe-Noire. The first phase will see 35 fenders installed by year-end on 400 meters of the quay, with the remaining 30 to be fitted on the next 350 meters in early 2027.

These high-resistance rubber and steel fenders act as giant shock absorbers, safeguarding vessels from collision damage upon docking. Jérôme MOUILLE, QHSE Manager for the Môle Est Project at Congo Terminal, stated that these fenders are the primary protection for every ship calling at the quay, emphasizing that operational safety is the company's standard.

This installation is a concrete step for the Môle Est project, aimed at enhancing Congo Terminal's capacity and supporting the growth of port activities in Pointe-Noire and the wider sub-region. Congo Terminal, a subsidiary of Africa Global Logistics, is the exclusive operator for container and roll-on/roll-off vessel handling at the port.

Why it mattersThe installation of dock fenders at Pointe-Noire port ensures safer operations for ships and supports the development of port activities.

Business · 2026-11-03

MTN customers report unjustified MoMo withdrawals

Congolese clients of the South African telecom giant MTN claim their mobile money accounts are being debited without consent.

MTN, a South African telecommunications multinational founded in 1994, faces accusations from Congolese customers regarding its Mobile Money (MoMo) service. Clients report unjustified withdrawals from their accounts, with MTN allegedly citing family members as the cause for transactions even when the account holder lives alone.

Customers also complain that MTN claims refunds are impossible for blocked transactions due to expired deadlines, raising questions about where the money goes. Lambert E., a customer, expressed frustration with these practices, suggesting they are linked to MTN's IT services.

MTN Mobile Money offers services such as bill payments, money transfers, deposits, withdrawals, airtime purchases, and sports betting. The company operates in 22 countries across Africa and the Middle East and reported a turnover of 10 billion euros in 2021.

Why it mattersCongolese customers of MTN are experiencing financial losses due to alleged fraudulent activities on their mobile money accounts.

Politics · 2026-11-03

Republic of Congo seeks UN Security Council seat

President Sassou N'Guesso asked African leaders to back the country's bid for a non-permanent seat on the UN Security Council for 2030-2031.

President Denis Sassou N’Guesso has asked fellow African leaders to support the Republic of Congo's bid for a non-permanent seat on the United Nations Security Council for the 2030-2031 term. He made this appeal during the third Russia-Africa summit in Moscow on October 28 and 29.

Sassou N’Guesso stated that African support would strengthen the continent's voice within the UN body responsible for international peace and security. He highlighted global challenges such as geopolitical tensions, terrorism, climate change, and armed conflicts as reasons for increased African representation.

The Congolese president emphasized his country's commitment to dialogue, mediation, and peaceful dispute resolution as its diplomatic stance for the Security Council seat. Brazzaville aims to leverage this approach if successful in its bid.
