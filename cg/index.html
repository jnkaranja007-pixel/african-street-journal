Congo maintains cement prices at 4,900 FCFA in Brazzaville

Cement prices for a 50kg bag remain unchanged at 4,900 FCFA in Brazzaville, 4,700 FCFA in Pointe-Noire, and 5,500 FCFA in Ouesso. The Ministry of Trade, Supplies and Consumption and producers Dangote, Sonocc, and Forspak agreed on Wednesday to keep the 2024 rates. This follows requests from producers to increase tariffs.

Why it mattersConstruction costs for builders and homeowners remain stable despite producer demands for price hikes.

Business · 2026-08-21

Congo sets $90 per barrel oil contract threshold

The Council of Ministers approved four bills to adjust oil sector contracts, including a single threshold of $90 per barrel. The measures include a redevelopment project for the Banga Kayo permit and amendments for permits Nanga II Bis, Nanga IV, Nangua V, and Marine XXIX. The changes involve the National Petroleum Company of Congo and various contractors.

Why it mattersThe adjusted contractual framework aims to increase production and attract further investment in the oil sector.

Congo police seize six firearms from poaching suspect

Police and eco-guards from the Lésio-Luna nature reserve seized six firearms and hundreds of rounds of ammunition on Aug. 13. The weapons were found during a search of a suspect's home in Yono village. Authorities identified the suspect as the alleged leader of a poaching network.

Why it mattersThe operation removes illegal weaponry from the Djoué-Léfini region to protect wildlife in the nature reserve.

Congo proposes National Order of Dental Surgeons

Health Minister Jean Rosaire Ibara presented a bill to create a national professional order for dental surgeons. The government intends to use the structure to regulate the profession and modernize health services. The project aims to address patient demands for safety and competence.
