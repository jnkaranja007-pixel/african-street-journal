Education · 2026-11-08

Republic of Congo adopts preschool standards

A national guide aims to harmonize teaching practices and improve care for children aged 3 to 5.

The Republic of Congo adopted a national guide on November 6 in Brazzaville to standardize preschool teaching and enhance the quality of care for children aged 3 to 5. This technical framework outlines six domains and 16 minimum standards for early childhood facilities.

Régine Tchicaya Oboa, cabinet director for the Minister of Preschool, Primary, Secondary Education, and Literacy, stressed the need for collective action to implement the new standards. The guide is intended to help facilities assess their compliance, identify shortcomings, and apply corrective measures.

The guide also offers specific pedagogical orientations for preschools to better support young children's development and learning needs. The next challenge is ensuring the guide's effective application on the ground to create suitable and secure learning environments.

Why it mattersPreschool children in the Republic of Congo will benefit from improved learning environments and care due to new national standards.

Sport · 2026-11-08

Congo minister discusses binationals with French MPs

Sports minister Hugues Ngouélondélé met a French delegation in Brazzaville on November 5 to boost youth and sports cooperation.

Republic of Congo's Minister of Sports Hugues Ngouélondélé met a French parliamentary delegation on November 5 in Brazzaville. The discussions focused on youth development and strengthening sports cooperation between France and Congo, aiming to enhance relations after a 13-year gap. The delegation was led by Nadège Abomangoli, president of the France-Congo friendship group of the French National Assembly.

Young people, representing over 70% of the Congolese population, are a major focus for public policy, and their support, training, and integration were key discussion points. The meeting also addressed the situation of binationals, particularly ahead of the Congo vs. Comoros match on November 12, which is expected to feature over 20 binationals. Minister Ngouélondélé emphasized the need to continue outreach to the diaspora to mobilize Congolese talents abroad for national teams.

The French parliamentary mission aims to reinforce ties in sports and youth sectors. The minister highlighted the importance of engaging Congolese talents living abroad to play for national selections.

Politics · 2026-11-08

Brazzaville city union suspends strike action

Municipal workers will receive their June 2026 salaries after negotiations with the city administration.

The union coalition for Brazzaville City Hall suspended its strike notice on November 6, which was set to begin on November 9. The union had initially announced the strike on November 3.

This decision followed talks between the municipal administration, led by Mayor Dieudonné Bantsimba, and the union coalition. The agreement secured the payment of June 2026 salaries for municipal agents.

This compromise addresses the socio-professional demands of municipal employees, as carried by the union coalition for Brazzaville City Hall.

Business · 2026-11-08

Republic of Congo forum to discuss business climate

The fifth Vox Éco Forum on November 24 will focus on private investment and economic reform.

The fifth Vox Éco Forum will take place in Brazzaville on November 24, focusing on "Business Climate: The Lever for Private Investment." Organized by Groupe Vox Congo, the event will bring together public officials, entrepreneurs, investors, experts, and financiers to discuss the country's economic challenges and propose concrete actions for reform.

This forum addresses the critical need for economic diversification and private sector growth in the Republic of Congo. Despite ongoing reforms and existing entrepreneurial potential, challenges such as administrative burdens, taxation, legal security, and difficulties in mobilizing capital continue to hinder investment and affect business confidence.

The forum aims to identify obstacles, review current reforms, and foster structured public-private dialogue for pragmatic solutions. Three panels will cover reforms and attractiveness, catalyzing investment through expertise and financing, and the expectations and blockages faced by the private sector. Participation is by invitation or prior registration.

Why it mattersEntrepreneurs and investors in the Republic of Congo face administrative, fiscal, and legal hurdles that the forum aims to address.
