UBA Congo clients must update accounts by December 2026

The bank requires updated personal and business documents to ensure security and regulatory compliance.

UBA Congo is asking all individual and business clients to update their account files by December 31, 2026. This action is part of the bank's commitment to secure banking operations and meet regulatory requirements. A complete and current client file helps the bank maintain reliable information, secure banking relationships, and prevent fraud.

Individual clients need a valid ID and updated personal and professional information, with recent proof of address if necessary. Business clients must provide up-to-date legal and administrative documents, details on directors and beneficial owners, and proof of business activity. Clients can visit their UBA branch or contact their account manager to complete these updates.

Updating client files strengthens account security, streamlines operations, and ensures transparent banking relationships. The bank urges clients not to wait until the last moment to verify and update their information before the 2026 deadline.

Congo football league expands to 18 clubs

The Fécofoot will adopt a zonal format for the next national championship to cut costs.

The Congolese Football Federation (Fécofoot) plans to expand its top-tier league from 14 to 18 teams for the upcoming season. The competition will adopt a zonal format, a decision announced on October 28 in Brazzaville. Fécofoot president Éric Alain Mantot stated this change aims to address the economic realities faced by clubs and reduce the financial burden of organizing the championship.

This new structure requires clubs to adapt their operations. Fécofoot will also provide training for club leaders from November 16 to 18 in Brazzaville, with support from FIFA. Topics will include financial management, leadership, and marketing. Clubs signing agreements for exceptional subsidies will undergo fund usage checks.

Mantot clarified that Fécofoot will not directly fund clubs but will encourage them to seek sponsors. The federation aims to enhance management capabilities and financial transparency within sports structures. The success of this new organization hinges on clubs' ability to adapt to these financial and operational changes.

Why it mattersCongolese football clubs will face operational and financial adjustments due to the league's expansion and new zonal format.

Health · 2026-10-30

Congo pharmacists' order seeks Propharma permit review

The pharmacists' council cited alleged procedural irregularities and unauthorized activities by Propharma.

The National Council of the Order of Pharmacists of Congo has asked the Ministry of Health to review Propharma's operating permit. President Dr. MPANDZO Djagger stated on October 27, 2026, that Propharma allegedly conducted activities, including importation, without prior authorization, violating law n° 012-92.

Dr. MPANDZO Djagger reported that the Order previously contacted the General Inspectorate of Health on March 30, 2026, and the Ministry of Health on June 1, 2026, seeking clarification on Propharma's authorizations, but received no response.

The Order is urging the Ministry of Health to formally address its appeal to ensure proper application of regulations for drug importation and sales, aiming to prevent illicit trafficking and protect public health.

Why it mattersThe health of citizens is at risk due to potential issues with the quality and safety of medicines available.
