Senate Approves Oil Contract Amendments

Lawmakers in Brazzaville passed four bills to modify production sharing agreements, aiming to adapt to sector realities and encourage continued investment.

The Senate in Brazzaville approved four bills on Wednesday, August 26, 2026, that amend oil production sharing contracts. These legislative changes, adopted during the third extraordinary session of the Senate under President Pierre Ngolo, concern the Nanga II bis, Nanga IV, Nanga V, and Marine XXIX-A permits.

Two amendments, for Nanga II bis and Nanga IV, were signed on November 23, 2023, between the Republic of Congo, the national oil company (SNPC), and China's Dingheng Mining Co. Ltd. The third amendment relates to Nanga V, and the fourth to Marine XXIX-A, signed on April 25, 2025, with Oriental Energy SAU. All four amendments focus on technical mechanisms tied to "high price" clauses within the oil contracts.

The stated objective of these adjustments is to provide greater clarity for operators and foster conditions conducive to ongoing exploration, exploitation, and oil production activities. Lawmakers were urged to consider the economic, financial, industrial, budgetary, and social consequences of these modifications, which extend beyond purely legal frameworks.

Why it mattersInvestors are affected by the Senate's approval of oil contract amendments that aim to adapt to sector realities and encourage continued investment.

Belgium Offers Waste Management Expertise to Congo

Belgian Ambassador Emmanuelle Plissart De Foy met with Congo's Minister of Urban Sanitation to discuss technical assistance in waste management and urban development.

Belgium has offered to share its expertise in sanitation and urban development with the Republic of Congo, aiming to strengthen cooperation between the two nations. Ambassador Emmanuelle Plissart De Foy met with Minister Juste Désiré Mondelé on August 25 in Brazzaville to explore potential collaborations, with a particular focus on waste management and urban planning.

Discussions centered on improving waste collection and valorization, including strategies for a circular economy to give waste a second life and reduce environmental impact. The talks also addressed the recovery of waste from both land and aquatic environments, such as rivers and the sea. The Belgian diplomat sought to identify specific areas where her country could provide technical support.

While no precise projects have been finalized, both parties identified several sectors for enhanced cooperation. This collaboration could lead to improved waste management practices and support the Congolese authorities' efforts to create cleaner and better-organized cities. The initiative aims to bolster urban sanitation and local development initiatives.

Why it mattersThis initiative offers potential for improved waste management and urban development, which could indirectly benefit farmers through cleaner environments and better resource utilization.

Coach Details Red Devils' Africa Cup Preparations

National team coach Claude le Roy outlined the team's strategy for upcoming Africa Cup of Nations qualifiers during a press conference on August 26.

The Republic of Congo's national football team, the Red Devils, are preparing for their first two Africa Cup of Nations qualifiers, scheduled for late September and early October. Coach Claude le Roy held a press conference on August 26 at the Congolese Football Federation headquarters to discuss the team's strategy. This initial training camp focuses on local players, providing the technical staff an opportunity to assess domestic talent and identify potential candidates for the national squad.

Le Roy detailed the team's approach to the crucial opening matches against Namibia and Cameroon. He emphasized that these games are vital for a strong start to the qualification campaign and for securing early points. Beyond the competitive aspect, the coaching staff plans to use this preparation period to foster team cohesion, develop tactical understanding, and refine playing strategies. This camp serves as a critical evaluation phase before finalizing the squad for international duties.

The technical team has several weeks to make final decisions and build a competitive team for the upcoming fixtures. The focus is on integrating local players into the team's structure and assessing their suitability for international competition. The coach aims to establish strong team dynamics and tactical discipline as they prepare to face formidable opponents in their quest for qualification.

Why it mattersThe national football team's preparation for the Africa Cup of Nations qualifiers affects local players by providing them opportunities to be selected for the national squad.

Congo Forces Clash Near Makotipoko; Leaders Urge Calm

Naval and gendarmerie elements from the Republic of Congo and the Democratic Republic of Congo engaged in an incident near Makotipoko, prompting calls for restraint from both.

An incident involving naval and gendarmerie forces from the Republic of Congo and the Democratic Republic of Congo occurred near Makotipoko in the Nkéni-Alima department. Authorities from both nations are actively working to determine the exact circumstances surrounding the confrontation, which has created a confused situation in the border region.

The Democratic Republic of Congo's Minister of Foreign Affairs, Thérèse Kayikwamba Wagner, stated that ongoing discussions between the two countries' defense ministers are focused on triangulating information to clarify the event. She emphasized that the incident does not reflect the strong bilateral relations and urged all parties to exercise restraint.

Minister Wagner stressed the importance of reinforcing existing dialogue and coordination mechanisms between the two states to better anticipate and prevent future incidents. Both governments aim to shed full light on the event and quickly restore calm in the Makotipoko area.

Why it mattersThe incident near Makotipoko could potentially disrupt cross-border movement and communication for people living in the Nkéni-Alima department and along the shared border.
