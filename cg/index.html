Health · 2026-11-14

Republic of Congo to vaccinate health workers against Ebola

The preventive campaign, which starts on November 16, targets health professionals and other staff most exposed to the virus.

The Republic of Congo will begin vaccinating health workers against Ebola on November 16. Health Minister Jean Rosaire Ibara announced the campaign on November 13 in Brazzaville, saying it will prioritise health professionals and other staff likely to be exposed to the virus in their duties.

Ibara said the aim is to protect these workers before any possible outbreak and to strengthen the national health system's ability to respond to Ebola threats. He stressed that the campaign is preventive and does not mean Ebola has been declared in the country, even though the virus is already near the national territory.

The vaccination adds to measures already under way, including stronger epidemiological surveillance, infection prevention and control, preparation of health facilities and screening at entry points. Ibara called on health facility managers to make every arrangement for the operation to succeed and urged the public to stay vigilant and follow prevention guidelines.

Why it mattersHealth workers who treat patients will be the first to be protected, with vaccinations beginning in the coming days.

Business · 2026-11-14

Congo's $100 million power upgrade nears completion

The World Bank-funded Pasel project, which aims to cut losses on the grid and improve supply, is due to finish before the end of the year.

Work on the Project for the Improvement of Electricity Services (Pasel) in the Republic of Congo is moving toward its final stage. The World Bank is financing the $100 million programme, which targets technical and commercial problems in the transport and distribution of electricity. Energy Minister Bruno Jean Richard Itoua met a World Bank delegation in Brazzaville on November 10 to review progress.

The delegation was led by Cheick Fantamady Kanté, the bank's division director for Central Africa, who said the project is advancing normally and that the work could be completed before the end of the year. The country has significant generating capacity, but poor delivery of power has hurt households and businesses.

Talks covered progress on work on electrical installations, the purchase of technical equipment, and steps to speed up interventions on the transport and distribution networks.

Why it mattersHouseholds and businesses that have suffered poor power delivery would see the network improvements the World Bank is funding, and the project's completion date is set for the end of this year.

Politics · 2026-11-14

Congo minister meets Moroccan envoy in Brazzaville

Luc Joseph Okio and Najoua El Berrak discussed strengthening bilateral ties and Okio's upcoming trip to Marrakech.

The Republic of Congo's Minister of State Reform and Relations with Parliament, Luc Joseph Okio, met with Morocco's Ambassador to Congo, Najoua El Berrak, on November 11 in Brazzaville. They discussed the current state of bilateral cooperation and ways to enhance it. Both officials reaffirmed their commitment to boosting friendship and cooperation between Brazzaville and Rabat, aligning with the directives of Congolese President Denis Sassou-N’Guesso and Moroccan King Mohammed VI.

The meeting also covered Minister Okio's upcoming participation in the 12th African Evaluation Association (AfrEA) Conference. This conference is scheduled to take place in Marrakech, Morocco, from November 23 to 27, 2026. Ambassador El Berrak welcomed Okio's planned attendance and assured him that her services would be available to ensure his visit proceeds smoothly.

Minister Okio's attendance at the AfrEA conference in Marrakech signifies a step in strengthening diplomatic and professional ties between the two nations. The discussions in Brazzaville highlighted a shared desire to deepen these relationships, with specific attention given to facilitating the minister's travel and participation in the upcoming event.

Why it mattersMinister Luc Joseph Okio will attend a conference in Marrakech from November 23 to 27, 2026, following discussions on strengthening bilateral relations.
