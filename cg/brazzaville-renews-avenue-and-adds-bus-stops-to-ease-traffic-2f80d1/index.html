Brazzaville renews avenue and adds bus stops to ease traffic

Road work on Avenue des 1ers Jeux Africains is nearly finished, with new stops beside the examinations and competitions directorate.

Source: Vox.cg · 2026-11-26 · 1 min read

Brazzaville’s municipality is renewing Avenue des 1ers Jeux Africains, in the city’s second arrondissement, with road-boundary and earthworks nearly complete and workers now laying out sidewalks. Work has been under way for more than two weeks on the stretch from the Rectorate to the junction by the technical and general education ministries.

Jean Henry Lobah-Sebette, a passer-by, said potholes had made the road difficult for vehicles to use and welcomed the rehabilitation. Resident Samy Mifoundou said the municipality was also building bus stops beside the examinations and competitions directorate; previously, buses stopped on the main road and caused traffic jams.

Vox.cg reported that residents welcomed the work. The municipality is also rehabilitating roads elsewhere in the urban area, while demand remains for repairs on avenues Jacques Opangault in Moukondo, Marien Ngouabi in Moungali and Avenue de l’Abattoir at Château d’eau in Makélékélé.

Why it mattersBus passengers gain designated stops beside the examinations and competitions directorate, where buses had previously stopped on the main road and caused traffic jams.
