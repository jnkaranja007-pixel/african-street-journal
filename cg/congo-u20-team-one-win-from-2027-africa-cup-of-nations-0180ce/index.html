Congo U20 team one win from 2027 Africa Cup of Nations qualification

The Congolese U20 football team faces the Central African Republic in a crucial semi-final match on October 21.

Source: Journal de Brazza · 2026-10-21 · 1 min read

The Congolese U20 national football team, known as the Red Devils, are on the cusp of qualifying for the 2027 Africa Cup of Nations. They will play the Central African Republic in the semi-finals of the Union of Central African Football Federations (UNIFFAC) tournament on October 21 at Olembé Stadium in Cameroon. This match is a significant opportunity for the young Congolese squad to advance in the regional competition and secure a spot in the continental championship.

A victory against the Central African Republic, nicknamed the "Fauves du Bas-Oubangui," would not only propel the Red Devils into the UNIFFAC final but also grant them a ticket to the U20 Africa Cup of Nations finals in Ghana in 2027. The team needs to demonstrate determination and effectiveness to overcome their opponents in this decisive encounter.

Qualifying for the CAN U20 would mark an important milestone for Congolese football, providing this emerging generation of players with a platform to compete against the continent's top youth teams. The players are urged to seize this critical opportunity in Cameroon.

Why it mattersFarmers are not directly affected by this football match.
