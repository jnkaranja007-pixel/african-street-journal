Congo Government Seeks to Lower Industrial Energy Costs

Officials met with business leaders to discuss fuel import options and fiscal support to maintain industrial competitiveness.

The Congolese government is exploring solutions to reduce fuel supply costs for businesses and safeguard the national industrial sector. Minister Michel Djombo met with economic operators to address challenges in fuel and electricity access that hinder economic activities. Discussions included the possibility of large consumers directly importing fuel, pending regulatory approval, and potential fiscal and logistical support measures.

These energy supply issues significantly impact production costs, profitability, and job preservation within the industrial landscape. The government aims to strengthen dialogue with the private sector through these consultations, fostering concrete measures to ensure the continuity of industrial operations and enhance the competitiveness of the Congolese economy.

The decision to maintain the price of diesel at 625 FCFA per liter is part of the authorities' strategy to protect household purchasing power. The government's objective is to implement practical solutions that support industrial activities and improve the overall economic standing of the Republic of Congo.

Why it mattersIndustrial businesses face higher production costs and reduced profitability due to energy supply challenges.
