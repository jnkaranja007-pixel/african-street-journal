Congo hosts accord for diplomatic ties between Kenya and DR Congo

The two presidents agreed to pursue diplomatic channels, work toward the return of their ambassadors and expand regional cooperation.

Source: Journal de Brazza · 2026-11-24 · 1 min read

The presidents of Congo, the Democratic Republic of Congo and Kenya agreed on November 22 to use diplomacy to ease tensions between Kinshasa and Nairobi. The talks took place in Oyo, in Congo’s Cuvette department, at a four-country summit hosted by Congo’s President Denis Sassou N’Guesso.

The two presidents reaffirmed respect for sovereignty, territorial integrity and peaceful dispute resolution, Journal de Brazza reported. They agreed to work toward the return of their respective ambassadors and to deepen cooperation through the International Conference on the Great Lakes Region and the East African Community.

Togo’s President Faure Gnassingbé, the African Union mediator in the security crisis in eastern DR Congo, also took part. The leaders agreed to cooperate against Ebola and pursue stronger economic ties.

Why it mattersCongo’s government must keep diplomatic efforts moving toward the return of the Kenyan and DR Congo ambassadors.
