Ivory Coast, Benin and Togo sign pact to study shared gas supply

The agreement, backed by the World Bank, also provides for gas exchanges between the three countries to support electricity production.

Source: Linfodrome · 2026-11-20 · 1 min read

Ivory Coast, Benin and Togo have signed an agreement to coordinate efforts to secure natural gas supplies, reduce costs and make electricity production more reliable. The pact covers cooperation among the three countries.

With support from the World Bank, the agreement provides for a study of a shared supply of liquefied natural gas (LNG) and the creation of mechanisms for gas exchanges among the partners. The study and exchanges are intended to support the countries’ efforts to secure supply.

The agreement does not set out a timetable or volumes for the proposed supply or exchanges.

Why it mattersThe three governments must study a shared LNG supply and establish mechanisms for gas exchanges under the agreement.
