Two men charged with murder near Korhogo

The victim's cousin allegedly planned the killing due to jealousy over a relationship.

Source: Ivoire Matin · 2026-11-12 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Two men, including the victim's cousin, face charges for the suspected premeditated murder of S. S. near Korhogo. The victim's body was found on September 30, 2026, near the village of Waraniéné with a suspicious mark on his neck.

Investigations by the Korhogo Brigade de recherche et d’intervention (BRI) revealed prior tensions between the cousins. An anonymous tip on November 4 led to the arrest of a second suspect in Kafonon, who reportedly confessed and implicated the victim's cousin as the mastermind.

The victim's cousin allegedly admitted to planning the murder, citing jealousy and frustration over the victim's relationship with his own partner. Both suspects have been presented to judicial authorities for prosecution.
