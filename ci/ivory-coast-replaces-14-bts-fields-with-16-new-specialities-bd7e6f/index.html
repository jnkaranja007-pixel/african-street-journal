Ivory Coast replaces 14 BTS fields with 16 new specialities

The cabinet approved the overhaul in Abidjan, adding courses including cybersecurity and energy efficiency while modernising 19 existing fields.

Sources: Afrique Sur 7, Linfodrome and Ivoire Matin · 2026-11-27 · 1 min read

Ivory Coast’s cabinet approved a reform of the Higher Technician Certificate (BTS) on Wednesday, 25 November, in Abidjan. The changes remove 14 of the diploma’s 33 fields, modernise 19 and add 16 specialities, bringing the total to 35.

The higher-education ministry said the discontinued fields no longer offered job prospects. The new options include cybersecurity, energy efficiency, digital systems maintenance and metal-structure construction. Employers’ representatives from 13 professional branches approved the programmes, which will place more emphasis on practical training.

The ministry has not set a start date or explained what happens to students already enrolled in fields being removed. It is also expected to provide an adaptation timetable for colleges offering the diploma.

Why it mattersStudents already enrolled in discontinued programmes await the ministry’s decision on their status, with no implementation date set.
