SYNAP-CI demands names of beneficiaries of CFA291 billion cocoa operation

The agricultural union is seeking disclosure of who received state funds for a programme to remove 100 000 tonnes of cocoa.

Sources: Linfodrome and Ivoire Matin · 2026-11-27 · 1 min read

Koné Moussa, president of the National Agricultural Union for the Progress of Côte d’Ivoire (SYNAP-CI), called for the full list of beneficiaries of CFA291 billion in state funds for a cocoa removal operation. He made the demand at a press conference on Wednesday 25 November 2026 in Plateau, Abidjan, and criticised the operation’s management.

The programme, run by the Coffee-Cocoa Council, covered 100 000 tonnes of cocoa, according to Linfodrome. Moussa called for the identities of everyone who received money to be made public.

Unsold cocoa stocks from the 2025-2026 main season remain unresolved, Ivoire Matin reported. Cocoa buyers are seeking an audit of stocks and said CFA28,8 billion is tied up.

Why it mattersCocoa buyers say CFA28,8 billion is tied up in unsold stocks and are seeking an audit.
