Eurochocolate considers first chocolate plant in Ivory Coast’s Haut-Sassandra

The festival organiser raised the proposal with Ivorian partners in Abidjan, saying it would process local cocoa into finished goods and create jobs.

Sources: Afrique Sur 7 and Ivoire Matin · 2026-11-20 · 1 min read

Eurochocolate is considering a first industrial chocolate-processing plant in Haut-Sassandra, the region around Daloa, with Ivorian and Italian partners. Festival president Eugenio Guarducci announced the proposal at a meeting in Abidjan on November 7, ahead of the event’s opening in Perugia, Italy. No budget or timetable was provided.

Guarducci said the proposed facility would turn some locally produced cocoa into finished goods, create jobs and transfer skills and technology. Ivory Coast’s government has set a target of processing 80% of national cocoa production by 2030, according to the report.

At Eurochocolate, which runs through November 22, Ivorian chocolatiers are selling products and seeking European buyers and distributors. The Coffee and Cocoa Council also presented the national traceability system, effective September 1, and the rollout of the Africa-wide ARS 1000 sustainability standard for cocoa, a certification scheme, across cocoa-growing areas.

Why it mattersProcessors in Haut-Sassandra are the proposed partners for a plant with no budget or timetable; the stated plan includes jobs and skills transfer.
