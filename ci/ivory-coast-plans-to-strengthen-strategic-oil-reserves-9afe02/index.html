Ivory Coast plans to strengthen strategic oil reserves

The country raised reserve capacity at an Abidjan energy and extractives forum, citing the role of stocks during supply disruptions.

Sources: RFI Afrique and Afrique Sur 7 · 2026-11-24 · 1 min read

Ivory Coast said it intends to strengthen its strategic oil reserves, a subject discussed at the International Extractive Resources and Energy Salon in Abidjan, which closed on Sunday 22 November. The reserves are intended to cover demand for a period when supply is disrupted.

The forum addressed how states can build stocks to respond to interruptions in oil supply. The report said Ivory Coast considers stronger capacity important amid an uncertain international context, but gave no target volume or timetable.

The salon ran from 18 to 22 November, according to a second report, which said its investment discussions covered mining, oil and energy. It did not report a reserve-building decision or funding commitment.

Why it mattersThe Ivorian government must strengthen its oil reserve capacity, with no target volume or timetable yet reported.
