Ivory Coast suspends influencer’s driving licence for four years

The influencer admitted speeding at 120 km/h on a road limited to 100 km/h, and must return to driving school before regaining his licence.

Sources: Agence Ivoirienne de Presse, Linfodrome and Ivoire Matin · 2026-11-27 · 1 min read

Ivory Coast’s driving-licence commission suspended influencer and web comedian Amany Kouakou Florent, known as Observateur Ébène, for four years at a hearing in Abidjan on Thursday, 26 November 2026. The decision followed several alleged traffic offences, including speeding on the Abidjan-Mondoukou road.

The commission said Florent admitted driving at 120 km/h where the limit was 100 km/h. His record also showed unpaid fines totalling more than 600 CFA000. After the suspension, he must complete training at a driving school before he can obtain a licence again. As he lives in France, authorities will inform him of the ruling through France’s embassy.

The commission reviewed 43 cases in the session and imposed 32 licence sanctions. Oumar Sacko, director general of land transport and traffic, said enforcement must follow procedure and protect road users.

Why it mattersFlorent must complete driving-school training after the four-year suspension before he can obtain a licence again.
