FIFA clears Oumar Solet to play for Ivory Coast

Football's world body has made the defender eligible for the Elephants with immediate effect, after the Ivorian Football Federation (FIF) asked for his association to be changed.

Sources: Linfodrome and Ivoire Matin · 2026-11-15 · 1 min read

FIFA, the world football governing body, officially authorised Oumar Solet to represent Ivory Coast on Friday, 13 November 2026. The body approved a request from the Ivorian Football Federation (FIF) to change his association, and said the defender is eligible to join the Elephants with immediate effect.

The decision means Solet can take part in the return leg against The Gambia, scheduled for Tuesday, 17 November. The Ivorian outlet Ivoire Matin described the move as a new defensive option for the national team.

The match is the second leg of the Africa Cup of Nations 2027 qualifier between the two sides, according to the report's title.

Why it mattersSolet can now be selected for the Elephants' return match against The Gambia on Tuesday, 17 November.
