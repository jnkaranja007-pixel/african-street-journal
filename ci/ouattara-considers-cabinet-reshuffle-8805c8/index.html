Ouattara Considers Cabinet Reshuffle

President Alassane Ouattara is reportedly contemplating a government overhaul due to dissatisfaction with several key issues.

Sources: Afrique Sur 7 and Linfodrome · 2026-10-21 · 1 min read

President Alassane Ouattara is reportedly considering a reshuffling of his government, which has been in place since late January 2026. This potential change comes as the head of state expresses dissatisfaction with the handling of various governmental dossiers. The current administration includes Téné Birahima Ouattara, the 70-year-old younger brother of the president, who serves as Vice Prime Minister and Minister of Defense, a position he has held since March 2021.

Téné Birahima Ouattara, also known by the nickname "Photocopie" due to his striking resemblance to the president, has a background in banking and security before entering politics. He held significant roles in banking institutions, including Société Générale de Banques en Côte d'Ivoire and Banque Atlantique Côte d'Ivoire, before leading the SNG company and the International Institute for Africa. He co-founded the RDR party with his brother.

The potential cabinet reshuffle follows a period where several ministers, including Robert Beugré Mambé and Téné Birahima Ouattara, have been assigned specific missions. The President's dissatisfaction suggests a desire for new approaches or improved performance in critical areas of governance. The exact timing and scope of any changes remain uncertain, but the review indicates a focus on governmental effectiveness.
