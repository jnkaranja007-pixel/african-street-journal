Agriculture · 2026-11-03

Ivory Coast opens digital farming school

The Digital Farming School in Yamoussoukro will train 50 students annually in tech for agriculture.

A new school focused on digital technologies for agriculture, the Digital Farming School (DFS), opened on October 31, 2026, at the INP-HB in Yamoussoukro. The school aims to modernize Ivory Coast's farming sector by training students in areas like sensors, satellite imagery, and artificial intelligence.

The DFS is a partnership between Ivory Coast's INP-HB and Morocco's UM6P, with funding from OCP Group and initial operational support from the FIRSI foundation. It will offer a two-year program, starting with 50 students per year, to develop solutions for better water and energy management, soil preservation, and climate change adaptation.

Minister of Agriculture Bruno Koné stated that digital tools can improve weather forecasts and fertilizer use, helping farmers reduce costs and increase profits. The school also includes a 25-hectare orchard for practical learning and experimentation.

Why it mattersFarmers in Ivory Coast can benefit from improved farming practices and increased income through new digital technologies taught at the DFS.

Culture · 2026-11-03

Moroccan film week opens in Abidjan to boost coproduction

Ivory Coast and Morocco aim to move from film screenings to joint creation and investment.

The sixth Moroccan film week opened on Monday, November 2, 2026, at the Majestic cinema in Abidjan. The event aims to strengthen partnerships between Ivory Coast and Morocco through joint creation, production, and financing of film projects.

Representing the Minister of Culture, Pr Hien Sié called for a shift from cooperative representation to cooperative creation, production, and investment. He emphasized leveraging the complementary strengths of both film industries and proposed a portfolio of jointly conceived Ivorian-Moroccan film projects with shared financing.

Morocco's Ambassador to Ivory Coast, Othmane El Ferdaous, reaffirmed the embassy's support for artist and technician mobility between the two nations. The director general of Côte d’Ivoire Cinéma, Johnson Lison Fall, stressed the need to implement the 2011 coproduction agreement into permanent collaboration mechanisms.

Why it mattersThe event seeks to foster collaboration and shared investment in film production between Ivory Coast and Morocco.

Politics · 2026-11-03

Cédric Diarra's age remains undisclosed

The vice-president of Ivory Coast's National Assembly, Cédric Tidiane Diarra, is 44 or 45 years old.

Cédric Tidiane Diarra, the vice-president of Ivory Coast's National Assembly, is 44 or 45 years old as of November 2026. His exact birth date has not been publicly revealed, though he was 39 in August 2021, indicating a birth year of 1981 or 1982.

Diarra, a RHDP deputy for Bako, Bougousso, and Dioulatiédougou, assumed his role on February 5, 2026. He is the son of former Prime Minister Seydou Diarra, who led the government during periods of tension in 2000 and from 2003 to 2005. Cédric Diarra previously worked in communications and private investment before entering state service at age 35.

His education includes the European Communication School in Brussels, the New York Institute of Technology, and a program at Harvard Business School in 2019. In the private sector, he was an associate director at Johnson Investments, involved in renewable energy projects. He served as a technical advisor to the Minister of Defense and later as chief of staff to Hamed Bakayoko before joining Patrick Achi's office.

Why it mattersThe age and background of a key figure in the National Assembly are not fully disclosed to the public.

Ouattara wins 89.77% of votes in Ivory Coast election

Ivory Coast President Alassane Ouattara secured a fourth term with 89.77% of votes on October 25, 2025, but turnout was 50.10%.

Alassane Ouattara, born January 1, 1942, secured 89.77% of votes in Ivory Coast's presidential election on October 25, 2025. The constitutional council confirmed these results on November 4, 2025. Ouattara, an economist who previously led the BCEAO, has governed Ivory Coast since 2011.

Voter participation for the October 25, 2025 election stood at 50.10%. This election saw Ouattara win a fourth mandate. Ibrahim Traoré, born March 14, 1988, leads Burkina Faso through a transition extended to 60 months from July 2024.

Burkina Faso's transition charter was replaced on March 27, 2026, with a "Charter of the Revolution." The government is now called the "Government of the People." On February 9, 2026, the transition legislative assembly unanimously repealed the charter of parties and laws on party financing.

Health · 2026-11-03

Ivory Coast intensifies animal disease surveillance

Health, animal and environmental sectors train in Grand-Bassam on zoonotic disease detection.

Ivory Coast is strengthening its defenses against diseases that can spread from animals to humans. From November 2 to 4, 2026, health, animal, and environmental professionals are attending a training workshop in Grand-Bassam. Dr. Kallo Vessaly, Director of Veterinary Services, emphasized the need for faster detection of zoonotic diseases and better cooperation between sectors.

The initiative uses the "One Health" approach, recognizing the links between people, animals, and the environment. Diseases like Ebola, avian flu, rabies, Mpox, Rift Valley fever, Marburg virus, and bovine tuberculosis are being targeted. Recent health events in Ivory Coast's wildlife highlight the importance of constant monitoring. Participants will learn data collection, transmission, and reporting techniques, as well as early warning systems.

The training is part of the EU-funded ZOOSURSY project, supported by the World Organisation for Animal Health (OMSA). The program includes using OMSA's WildEpi tool and practical exercises. Ivory Coast aims to improve its health surveillance system to anticipate epidemics, protect its population, and conserve biodiversity.

Why it mattersIvory Coast residents face increased risk from diseases like Ebola and rabies transmitted from animals.
