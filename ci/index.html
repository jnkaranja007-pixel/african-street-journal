Agriculture · 2026-10-28

ivory coast removes vat on animal feed

The minister of animal resources announced a total VAT exemption on animal inputs and feed starting in 2027.

Ivory Coast will remove value-added tax (VAT) on animal feed and inputs starting in 2027. The announcement came after a difficult 2026 for the livestock sector, which faced new VAT charges on industrial feed. Minister Sidi Tiémoko Touré made the declaration on Monday, October 26, 2026.

The total VAT exemption aims to relieve the livestock industry, according to the minister of animal resources and fisheries. Sidi Tiémoko Touré revealed this measure during an appearance on the program "Les Grands rendez-vous" on the Ivorian press agency. This decision follows a challenging year for farmers who had to contend with the introduction of VAT on their supplies.

The exemption is set to take effect in 2027, providing a potential financial respite for livestock producers. This move by the Ivorian government addresses concerns raised by the sector following the VAT implementation in 2026.

Why it mattersFarmers in Ivory Coast will benefit from lower costs for animal feed and inputs.

Ivory Coast actors launch film festival and school

Isaach de Bankolé and Philippe Lacôte aim to establish a new acting school and a film festival in Abidjan.

Actors Isaach de Bankolé and filmmaker Philippe Lacôte launched a project on Monday, October 26, to support cinema in Ivory Coast. The initiative includes plans to create an acting school and a film festival in Abidjan, with the first festival edition expected by the end of 2027.

The Retechci, a network of cinema technicians, also launched its own event, "Ombres et Lumières," on Friday, October 23, at the Goethe Institute. Jamel Basiru, Retechci's executive director, stated the project aims to recognize technicians' contributions, celebrate their expertise, foster professional cohesion, and pass knowledge to future generations.

The "Premier Continent" project's film festival is slated for late 2027.

Why it mattersThe "Premier Continent" project will establish a new acting school and film festival in Abidjan, Ivory Coast.
