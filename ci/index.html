Mayor's office audit yields 24 recommendations for improved management

Adiaké municipal council reviewed 24 audit recommendations aimed at enhancing administrative operations and financial oversight.

Twenty-four recommendations from a July 2026 audit of the Adiaké mayor's office management were presented to the municipal council on August 28, 2026. The audit, conducted by an evaluation unit from the Ministry of Budget and Finance, aims to improve the commune's administrative management. Key recommendations include better archiving of important documents, annual reports on the mayor's management, establishing a procedures manual, and adhering to a three-year plan's deadlines.

The first deputy mayor, Colonel Atchet Bilé Jean, stated that the council has decided to implement these recommendations for more effective administration. He noted that copies of the audit report are available to council members and interested parties. These points will now be a focus for the municipal administration to improve its functioning.

In a separate development, Ivorian singer A'Salfo, leader of the group Magic System, has acquired Racing Club de Koumassi, a third-division club. This move, announced in late August 2026, marks the artist's official entry into Ivorian football ownership, according to Jeune Afrique. He joins other Ivorian personalities investing in local football.

Why it mattersThe audit recommendations affect the administrative and financial management of the Adiaké municipality, while A'Salfo's acquisition impacts the ownership structure of a third-division football club.

Agency Trains Tchologo Businesses on Competitiveness Programs

The Agency for the Development and Competitiveness of Industries in Côte d’Ivoire held an workshop in Ferkessédougou to inform local businesses about support mechanisms.

An agency workshop in Ferkessédougou on August 26, 2026, aimed to educate small and medium-sized enterprises and industrial firms in the Tchologo region about support programs designed to boost their competitiveness. The event, organized by the Agency for the Development and Competitiveness of Industries of Côte d’Ivoire, gathered prefectural representatives, administrative authorities, professional organizations, and economic actors.

Yannick Sanga, the agency's Director of Training and Partnership, explained that businesses operate in a competitive environment requiring quality, productivity, and compliance. He stated that the agency's mission is to strengthen the capabilities and performance of Ivorian companies. The workshop presented the agency's programs, industrial firm upgrade schemes, eligibility criteria, support conditions, service costs, and expected contributions from beneficiary companies.

The secretary general of the Ferkessédougou prefecture, Émile Koffi, encouraged local economic actors to utilize the available support systems to enhance their national and international market performance. The agency, established in 2014, has already assisted over 200 Ivorian companies in improving their performance. Business leaders and project holders also shared their needs and constraints regarding upgrades, financing, productivity, quality, and market access.

Why it mattersBusinesses in the Tchologo region are affected by being informed about programs designed to improve their competitiveness and performance in national and international markets.

Forestry Project Drills 17 Wells for Rural Water Access

The second phase of the Forest Investment Project will fund 17 new boreholes across seven regions to improve water access for communities near protected forests.

Seventeen new boreholes are planned across seven regions and 11 departments in Ivory Coast as part of the Forest Investment Project, phase 2. The initiative aims to improve water access for rural communities living near protected areas and classified forests. This was announced during a meeting in Soubré concerning the simplified environmental and social impact study for the work.

The project, which seeks to preserve and increase forest stock while boosting rural incomes, will operate around 16 classified forests, three agroforestry zones, and four national parks. It includes components for participatory development, national park management, reforestation, and project monitoring. Two boreholes are specifically planned for the Buyo department, intended to enhance water access and strengthen the operational capabilities of SODEFOR and OIPR bases.

Beyond water access, the project anticipates job creation, local capacity building, and the use of local expertise, particularly in civil engineering. Awareness campaigns on gender-based violence and sexual harassment are also scheduled. While the project aims to reduce waterborne diseases, the environmental impact study noted potential negative effects from construction activities, with mitigation measures proposed, including regular equipment maintenance.

Why it mattersRural communities near protected forests will gain improved access to water, potentially reducing waterborne diseases and creating local employment opportunities.

Minister Launches 2025-2026 Technical Education Statistics Campaign

The campaign, supported by UNICEF, aims to improve data collection for technical and vocational training programs across 702 institutions.

Jean Louis Moulod, the Minister Delegate for Technical Education, officially launched the 2025-2026 statistical campaign on August 28, 2026. This initiative, undertaken with support from UNICEF and sector partners, follows the presentation of the 2024-2025 yearbook for Technical and Vocational Education and Training (ETFP). The yearbook highlights structural trends influencing training supply and demand, including industrial and technological enrollment, qualification and apprenticeship programs, female participation, gender inclusion, and the gap between economic needs and training offerings.

The campaign seeks to achieve several key objectives by 2030: increasing ETFP student numbers from approximately 5% in 2025 to 9% of secondary school enrollment, boosting graduate employment rates from 46% in 2025 to 70%, and raising the proportion of girls in industrial fields from 18% in 2025 to 30%. It also plans to construct and equip 136 new technical and professional training facilities and strengthen partnerships with the private sector.

UNICEF's representative in Ivory Coast, Jean François Basse, stated that enhancing data collection will help identify vulnerable youth, reduce inequalities, and make public decisions more effective. The 2025-2026 statistical campaign will cover 702 institutions, with data collection running from September 2026 to April 2027 and results expected by May 20, 2027. This effort reaffirms the commitment to strengthening ETFP management and promoting a more inclusive system for better professional prospects.

Why it mattersStudents and educators in technical and vocational training programs are directly affected by the data collection and planned improvements to enrollment, employment rates, and facilities.

Ivory Coast Basketball Team Defeats Mali in World Cup Qualifier

The Elephants secured a 83-77 victory against Mali in the opening match of the second round of the 2027 Basketball World Cup qualifiers.

Ivory Coast began the second round of the 2027 Basketball World Cup qualifiers with a win, defeating Mali 83-77. The match took place on Thursday, August 27, 2026, at the Dakar Arena in Senegal. Despite trailing early in the game, the Ivorian team, known as the Elephants, managed to turn the tide and secure a hard-fought victory.

This win marks a successful start for Ivory Coast in the fourth window of the 2027 World Cup qualification campaign. The team's performance demonstrated resilience as they overcame an initial deficit to claim the win in a closely contested match.

The Elephants of Ivory Coast have now positioned themselves well in the ongoing qualification process. Their ability to come back from behind in the opening game suggests a strong competitive spirit as they advance through the tournament stages.

Why it mattersIvory Coast's basketball team is affected by this win as it marks a successful start to their 2027 World Cup qualification campaign.
