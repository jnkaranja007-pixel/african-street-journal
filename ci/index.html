Ivorian MP pledges support for Kouadiotékro development

Aka Desquith Ambroise will help secure an ambulance and improve village road access.

The Member of Parliament for Tiémélékro, Aka Desquith Ambroise, pledged to support Kouadiotékro's development projects, including acquiring an ambulance for the health centre and improving the village's access road. He made this commitment on Sunday, November 8, 2026, in Abidjan during the ordinary general assembly of the Mutual for the Development of Kouadiotékro (MUDEK). Ambroise stated that steps have already been taken with the Ministry of Health to obtain the ambulance.

Ambroise also intends to contact the Moronou Regional Council regarding the road improvements, leveraging his position as a regional councillor. MUDEK president Mardochée Kouassi presented the mutual's achievements, including restructuring and updating its statutes. Kouassi also detailed plans for a "Maison du peuple" costing nearly 107 million CFA Francs, intended to house chieftaincy offices and community spaces.

Kouassi explained the need for the "Maison du peuple" due to the lack of a suitable venue for village gatherings. He mentioned that contributions from residents are being collected and that public and private partners will be approached in 2027. The MUDEK president also highlighted efforts to ease youth tensions and announced upcoming elections for the youth bureau on November 22.

Why it mattersResidents of Kouadiotékro will benefit from improved healthcare access and better infrastructure.

Botswana president to attend Abidjan resources expo

Duma Boko will visit Ivory Coast for the SIREXE 2026 event, focusing on mining and energy sectors.

Botswana's President Duma Gideon Boko will attend the SIREXE 2026 exhibition in Abidjan, Ivory Coast, from November 18 to 22. His visit follows a meeting with Ivorian Mines Minister Mamadou Sangafowa-Coulibaly, confirming diplomatic significance for the international extractive resources and energy fair. The two nations have an existing cooperation agreement covering mining and energy.

The precise schedule for President Boko's state visit, which was confirmed on July 30, 2026, in Gaborone, remains undisclosed. Minister Sangafowa-Coulibaly travelled to Botswana on behalf of Ivorian President Alassane Ouattara. SIREXE 2026, the event's second edition, aims to attract public officials, companies, and foreign delegations to discuss the mining, oil, and energy sectors.

Organizers are introducing "SIREXE Invest" and "SIREXE Awards" to boost investment and promote excellence in the strategic mining, oil, and energy industries. Botswana is the guest of honor at the exhibition, which will be held at the Parc des Expositions d’Abidjan. The event seeks to stimulate investment and highlight best practices within these sectors.

Ivory Coast trains 31 new conflict resolution specialists

New mediators and arbitrators will help resolve disputes outside of traditional court proceedings.

31 new specialists in mediation and arbitration have completed training in Ivory Coast, preparing them to help resolve conflicts. The training equips them with tools to prevent disputes and facilitate resolutions outside of formal court procedures.

Vice-president of the National Assembly, Ouattara Brahima, sponsored the ceremony and highlighted the importance of these mechanisms for peace and social cohesion. He urged the graduates to perform their duties with professionalism, impartiality, integrity, and discretion.

The graduates, represented by Becanthy Coulibaly, thanked trainers and officials from the Centre for Research and Professional Training in Mediation and Negotiation (CERFOPMAN). The UCAO and its partners aim to foster a culture of dialogue and peaceful dispute resolution across Ivory Coast through this new cohort.

FISH4ACP advances tilapia sector in Ivory Coast

The program reports increased aquaculture production, better access to finance, and improved governance after five years.

The FISH4ACP program has completed five years of work in Ivory Coast, reporting progress in tilapia aquaculture production, improved access to financing, and advancements in governance and animal health.

These achievements and future development prospects for the sector were discussed at a results-sharing workshop held on Friday, November 6, 2026, at the Capitol Hotel in Abidjan.

The program's support has strengthened the Ivorian tilapia sector, stimulating investment and preparing for the future of aquaculture.
