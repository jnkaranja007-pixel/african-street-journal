Politics · 2026-11-15

Tiassalé residents asked to give views on new Bandama bridge

A new bridge over the Bandama River will be built as part of a five-kilometre road, and the environmental study is now open to public comment.

Gisèle Ehouman, secretary general of the Tiassalé prefecture, has invited residents to record their opinions and recommendations in a register at the prefecture on the socio-environmental impact of the new bridge at the entrance to the town. She made the call on Thursday, 12 November 2026, when the environmental and social impact study (IES) was opened.

The bridge will cross the Bandama River and be linked to a five-kilometre road with two carriageways of two lanes each, seven metres wide, separated by a two-metre central strip. Éric Zéan, of the consultancy Centunion, which completed the technical study of the route, said a 315-metre bridge will also cross the rice-growing lowland between the detention centre and the drinking water plant to join national road A2 towards Divo. The old 94-year-old bridge will be rehabilitated and kept.

Marie Prudence Gnoan of the National Environment Agency (ANDE) said the views of residents are "very much awaited" to complete the preliminary studies. Onésime Koffi, head of the prefecture's cabinet, has been named investigating commissioner for 10 working days, from 12 November to 25 November 2026, during working hours.

Why it mattersResidents have until 25 November 2026 to file their views in the prefecture register before the study is completed.

Health · 2026-11-15

Kokari residents briefed on road safety, gender violence and HIV risks

The awareness drive is part of social measures accompanying road works on lot 6 of a World Bank and AIIB-backed rural connectivity project.

Residents of Kokari, in the Tengrela department of northern Ivory Coast, were briefed on Wednesday 11 November 2026 on road safety, gender-based violence (GBV) and sexually transmitted infections, including HIV/AIDS. The session was held at the public square in Kokari and organised by the Interafricaine pour la promotion de la santé et des droits humains (IPSDH), a non-governmental organisation.

The campaign forms part of the social measures accompanying road works carried out by the contractor EGETRAV under phase 1 bis of the Inclusive Rural Connectivity and Infrastructure Project in Ivory Coast (PCR-CI), on lot 6 in the Bagoué region. The project is co-financed by the World Bank and the Asian Infrastructure Investment Bank (AIIB), and aims to improve rural connectivity across the country's 11 northern regions. EGETRAV's site manager, Assi Amoncho Judas, said awareness work is an essential part of the project, aimed at preventing traffic accidents, fighting GBV and curbing HIV transmission.

Discussions were led by Kouadio N'Guessan, a representative of the Tengrela social centre, and Koné Paulin Martial, on safe road practices, prevention and care for GBV survivors, and ways to prevent STIs and HIV. A representative of the sub-prefect of Papara, Ouehi Droh Jacques, stressed the importance of dialogue between authorities, communities and project actors to manage social risks linked to the works.

Why it mattersResidents living along the lot 6 road works in the Bagoué region are the people the awareness drive is meant to reach, with its aim of preventing accidents and protecting them from GBV and HIV during construction.

Sport · 2026-11-15

FIFA clears Oumar Solet to play for Ivory Coast

Football's world body has made the defender eligible for the Elephants with immediate effect, after the Ivorian Football Federation (FIF) asked for his association to be changed.

FIFA, the world football governing body, officially authorised Oumar Solet to represent Ivory Coast on Friday, 13 November 2026. The body approved a request from the Ivorian Football Federation (FIF) to change his association, and said the defender is eligible to join the Elephants with immediate effect.

The decision means Solet can take part in the return leg against The Gambia, scheduled for Tuesday, 17 November. The Ivorian outlet Ivoire Matin described the move as a new defensive option for the national team.

The match is the second leg of the Africa Cup of Nations 2027 qualifier between the two sides, according to the report's title.

Why it mattersSolet can now be selected for the Elephants' return match against The Gambia on Tuesday, 17 November.

Security · 2026-11-15

Ivory Coast, France, Gabon armies end joint security drill

Operation Fangha 2026 tested interoperability and regional threat response in Yamoussoukro.

The multinational military operation "Fangha 2026" concluded on Thursday, November 12, 2026, in Yamoussoukro. The drill, which officially began on November 7, 2026, involved the armed forces of Ivory Coast, France, and Gabon.

This inter-army maneuver was part of the Grand Exercise Multinational (GEX 26) and aimed to strengthen the interoperability of the participating nations. The objective was to enhance their collective capacity to counter security threats affecting the region.

The joint operations demonstrated the combined forces' ability to conduct coordinated actions. The exercise concluded with a display of power by the three armies in Yamoussoukro.

Why it mattersThe armed forces of Ivory Coast, France, and Gabon demonstrated their ability to conduct joint operations on November 12, 2026.
