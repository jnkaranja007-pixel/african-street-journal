Climate · 2026-11-11

Ivory Coast seeks 984 billion FCFA for climate-sensitive disease projects

The delegation at COP 31 in Antalya, Turkey, is requesting funds for six projects to make the health system more resilient to climate risks.

Ivory Coast is seeking 984 billion FCFA to finance six projects aimed at improving the resilience of its health system to climate risks. The request is being made by its delegation at the 31st Conference of the Parties (COP 31), held in Antalya, Turkey.

Vice-President Tiémoko Meyliet Koné is representing President Alassane Ouattara at the conference, which runs from November 9 to 20, 2026. The projects focus on adapting to and preventing health problems linked to climate change.

The figure of 984 billion FCFA is the total the delegation is looking for across the six projects. The packet does not say which funders have been approached or when a decision on the funding is expected.

Why it mattersThe projects would strengthen the health system against climate risks, which affects the population Ivory Coast's health services serve.

Politics · 2026-11-11

PPA-CI demands full light on deaths of six detainees

Laurent Gbagbo's party says six militants have died in custody without trial and wants the State held responsible.

Ivorian newspapers on Tuesday, November 10, 2026, carried the PPA-CI's outrage over the death in detention of a sixth militant. Le Temps quoted the party saying "six human lives lost, it is too much!", while Le Quotidien d'Abidjan reported "cascading deaths" and a demand for "full light" on their causes.

La Voie originale said the six detainees died "in prison without trial" and that the party is calling on the State to answer for its responsibility. Dernière Heure listed the party's demands: the release of political detainees and a review of provisional detentions.

The packet reports that Le Quotidien d'Abidjan announced Charles Rodel Dosso before the judges on November 23, and that La Voie originale said the trial of Blaise Lasm was postponed to November 16 after three adjournments.

Why it mattersFamilies and supporters of the six dead militants now depend on the authorities opening an inquiry into how they died.

Sport · 2026-11-11

Ivory Coast recalls Oumar Diakité after Ange-Yoan Bonny is ruled out

Diakité, left out of the 25-man squad, returns for the Gambia double-header after scoring twice for Sturm Graz on Sunday.

Ivorian striker Oumar Diakité scored twice for Sturm Graz in a 4-3 defeat to Red Bull Salzburg on Sunday, November 8, 2026, in the Austrian league's 12th round. Coach Hervé Renard had left him out of the 25-player squad named three days earlier for the two-legged Gambia qualifiers.

The Ivorian Football Federation said on Monday, November 9, that Ange-Yoan Bonny of Inter Milan is out of the third and fourth qualifying rounds for medical reasons, and that Diakité has been called up in his place. Renard had earlier explained his omission by the level of the Austrian championship, a view the striker's recent scoring has challenged.

The first leg is on Thursday, November 12, at the Stade de la Paix in Bouaké, with the return on Tuesday, November 17, in Bakau, Gambia. Ivory Coast have two wins from two matches so far, a 2-0 win over Ghana in Bouaké on September 24 and a narrow win over Somalia.

Why it mattersDiakité, a Sturm Graz player on loan from Stade de Reims, has four days to press his case for a starting place before the Bouaké match against The Gambia.
