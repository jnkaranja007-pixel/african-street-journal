Kafoumba Diabaté wins Man of Africa Universe 2026

Kafoumba Diabaté secured Ivory Coast's first title in the Man of Africa Universe competition held in Ghana.

Kafoumba Diabaté has won the 2026 Man of Africa Universe title, bringing the first crown to Ivory Coast. The competition took place in Ghana from October 18 to October 25, 2026.

The 'Grand retour de la diaspora' forum also took place in Ivory Coast, aiming to encourage diaspora investment. Approximately 50 participants from abroad met with Ivorian stakeholders during the economic day on Saturday, October 24. This event is one of several forums designed to help diaspora members return or settle in Africa.

Separately, in Abidjan, four leaders from the PDCI-RDA and PPA-CI opposition parties were placed under judicial supervision on Friday, October 23. They were summoned by the anti-terrorism section of the Abidjan court. This follows similar legal actions against other opposition figures the previous week.

Why it mattersIvorian citizens and diaspora members are affected by cultural achievements, investment opportunities, and political developments.

Himra wins 30 million FCFA at Primud awards

The Ivorian rapper received five trophies including best rap francophone at the October 11, 2025 ceremony in Abidjan.

Ivorian rapper Himra received 30 million FCFA and five awards, including best rap francophone, at the 10th Primud urban music awards ceremony on October 11, 2025, in Abidjan. His album "Jeune & Riche," released July 25, 2024, achieved platinum certification in Ivory Coast within six months and diamond status less than a year later.

Himra's song "Number One," featuring Minz, earned gold certification from Snep in France on February 9, 2026, for over 15 million streams. He also performed at the Zénith de Paris on January 28, 2026, and won the Flamme for best African music track at La Seine Musicale on April 23, 2026.

Rapper Didi B, who founded Kiff No Beat in 2009, is scheduled to perform at the Ebimpé stadium on December 26, 2026. His album "Mojotrône II," released in 2022, was certified diamond in May 2025, and "Bazarhoff & Diyilem" went platinum in under five months.

Why it mattersIvorian musicians Himra and Didi B are achieving significant commercial success and performing at major venues.

Eli Kroupi chooses France over Ivory Coast

The 20-year-old Bournemouth attacker confirmed his international future lies with the French national team.

19-year-old attacker Eli Kroupi has chosen to represent France internationally, rejecting offers from Ivory Coast and Portugal. Kroupi, who was born in Lorient, France, stated his preference for the French national team due to his upbringing and familiarity with the culture.

Kroupi, whose father played for Ivory Coast, has represented France at various youth levels, from under-16s to the Espoirs. He has scored 13 goals in 35 matches for Bournemouth but has been sidelined for three to four months following foot surgery in July.

The Ivory Coast national team, known as the Elephants, will not have Kroupi available for their upcoming 2027 Africa Cup of Nations qualifiers against Gambia on November 12 and 17. Coach Hervé Renard will announce his squad for these matches on November 5.

Why it mattersIvory Coast will not have a promising young attacker, Eli Kroupi, available for their upcoming football matches.
