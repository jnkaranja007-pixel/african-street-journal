Ouattara inaugurates Koné gold mine in Kani

Téné Birahima Ouattara opened the Koné gold mine in Kani, which several newspapers describe as the country's largest.

Téné Birahima Ouattara inaugurated the Koné gold mine in Kani, in the north-west of Ivory Coast, on Friday, November 13, 2026. Several Ivorian newspapers, including Soir Info, L'Inter, Le Jour Plus and Le Matin, describe it as the largest gold mine in the country.

Fraternité Matin calls it the largest gold mining project, while L'Avenir describes it as a mine of all records. The Ivorian mining sector is taking a new step with the opening, and the government is pointing to youth employment as one of its aims.

The same day's front pages were dominated by other stories, including the opposition's concerns over detentions and the sponsorship process ahead of elections.

Why it mattersYoung people in Kani and the wider north-west are the ones who stand to gain from the jobs the mine is expected to bring.

FIFA clears defender Oumar Solet to play for Ivory Coast

The Ivorian Football Federation's request to change Solet's international association has been approved, with immediate effect.

FIFA has officially authorised Oumar Solet to represent Ivory Coast, the world football body announced on Friday, 13 November 2026. It approved the request for a change of association submitted by the Ivorian Football Federation (FIF), making the defender eligible to join the Elephants with immediate effect.

The clearance means Solet can take part in the return match against Gambia, scheduled for Tuesday, 17 November. The authorisation adds a defensive option to the squad for that fixture.

Gambia's return match is the next step in the national side's qualifying campaign for the 2027 Africa Cup of Nations.

Why it mattersSolet becomes available for the Elephants' return match against Gambia on 17 November, giving the coach a new defensive option.

Amad Diallo wins man-of-the-match on Ivory Coast debut under Renard

The Elephants beat Gambia 2-1 in the third round of CAN 2027 qualifying at the Stade de la Paix in Bouaké.

Ivory Coast beat Gambia 2-1 on Thursday, 12 November 2026, at the Stade de la Paix in Bouaké, in the third round of qualifying for the 2027 Africa Cup of Nations. Amad Diallo, the Manchester United winger, made his first appearance under coach Hervé Renard and was named man of the match with a rating of 7 out of 10.

Renard said he was pleased with the three points but told his players to stay alert in the remaining matches. The win moves the Elephants forward in a group that also includes Gambia, the team they beat at home in the same match.

The match was the third of the qualifiers for the tournament, which Ivory Coast will host in 2027 as part of the African football calendar.

Why it mattersDiallo's first start under Renard gives the coach a new option on the wing before the next qualifiers.

Politics · 2026-11-14

Achi urges Canada economic partnership and skills exchange

Ivory Coast's National Assembly president asked Canadian parliamentarians to encourage private investors to explore the Ivorian market.

Patrick Achi, president of Ivory Coast's National Assembly, called in Abidjan on Thursday, November 13, 2026, for a stronger economic partnership with Canada, built on private investment, training and skills exchange. He presented investment opportunities under the 2026-2030 National Development Plan (PND), including local processing of farm commodities, food security, industry, infrastructure and new technologies.

Achi said wealth creation rests above all on human skills, and he pressed for more Ivorian students to gain access to Canadian higher education, with mechanisms to bring skills back home. He asked the visiting Canadian parliamentary delegation, led by Senator Amina Gerba, to urge Canadian private investors to look further at the Ivorian market.

Gerba called the exchanges "very fruitful" and said her association wanted to deepen ties between the two countries. Ivory Coast is among 10 priority countries in Canada's Africa strategy announced in 2025. The Canada-Africa Parliamentary Association, founded in 2003, is making only its second visit to Ivory Coast, after one in 2013.
