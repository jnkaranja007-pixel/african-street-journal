Ivory Coast rubber exports surpass cocoa in volume

Natural rubber became the country's top agricultural export by volume in 2025, surpassing cocoa.

Ivory Coast exported nearly two million tonnes of natural rubber in 2025, making it the country's leading agricultural export by volume. This volume surpasses that of cocoa, which previously held the top position.

The performance was presented by the Minister of Agriculture, Rural Development and Food Production, Bruno Nabagné Koné, at an event on November 5-6. He represented the Vice-Prime Minister and Minister of Defence, Téné Birahima OUATTARA, who was the patron of the natural rubber event.

Natural rubber now ranks as the country's primary agricultural export by volume and has moved to second place in terms of value. The nation is confirming its growth in the heveaculture sector.

Business · 2026-11-08

Ivory Coast opens 390 MW Atinkou thermal plant

The new combined cycle power station in Jacqueville will boost national electricity production and West African energy hub status.

Prime Minister Robert Beugré Mambé inaugurated the Atinkou combined cycle thermal power plant on Friday, November 6, 2026. The facility is in Taboth, Jacqueville department.

This energy infrastructure supports the national policy to develop the electricity sector. It consolidates national production and reinforces Ivory Coast's position as a West African energy hub.

The Atinkou plant in Jacqueville has a capacity of over 390 MW. Its commissioning officially enhances Ivory Coast's energy potential.
