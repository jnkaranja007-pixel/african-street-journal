US, Ivory Coast End Border Security Program

A three-year U.S.-funded initiative has concluded, enhancing security along Ivory Coast's northern frontiers against terrorism.

The United States Embassy in Ivory Coast and Ivorian security officials marked the end of a three-year counter-terrorism assistance program on September 3, 2026, in Abidjan. The initiative, funded by the U.S. Department of State's Bureau of Counterterrorism and implemented by the Diplomatic Security Service, aimed to strengthen security along the country's northern borders.

The program provided training, modernized infrastructure, and supplied tactical equipment, including surveillance drones, vehicles, assault weapons, communication gear, and protective equipment, to the national Police and Gendarmerie. U.S. Embassy Chargé d'Affaires Junaid Munir stated the project successfully equipped border areas with permanent, professional law enforcement capabilities. National Police Controller General Chérif Souleymane praised the strategic partnership, noting its direct benefit to northern detachments and requesting further immersion training in the U.S. for unit commanders.

Brigadier General Bassanté Aly of the Gendarmerie national's specialized units highlighted the deteriorating regional security context that prompted the creation of a dedicated border patrol, operational first aid, and anti-drug unit. He announced that the trained Gendarmerie unit will become fully autonomous by the end of September, signifying the sustainable adoption of acquired skills.

Why it mattersIvorian security forces are better equipped and trained to secure northern borders against terrorism, enhancing national stability.

Think Tanks Debate Africa's Future Freedom and Development

Abidjan hosted discussions on rethinking liberty and development based on the continent's human, social, and cultural realities.

Discussions on how to rethink liberty and development in Africa, grounded in the continent's human, social, and cultural realities, took place on Thursday, September 3, 2026, in Cocody Riviera 4. The event, titled "African Encounters of Freedom," was organized by Audace Institut Afrique. Key partners in this initiative included Atlas Network, the Friedrich Naumann Foundation for Freedom, and the Centre for Research and Action for Peace (Cerap).

The Salon International du Livre d’Abidjan (SILA) also served as a platform for major political reflection on the same day. This event focused on reinventing democracy in Africa, drawing insights from an essay by Ousmane Ndiaye. The discussions moved beyond the act of voting to explore deeper democratic principles.

These gatherings brought together various organizations and thinkers to engage in critical dialogue about the continent's trajectory. The focus was on practical and cultural considerations, aiming to foster new perspectives on governance and societal progress within Africa.

Why it mattersAfrican leaders and intellectuals are affected by discussions on how to rethink liberty and development, which could influence future governance and societal structures.

Prix d’Excellence Mobilizes Over 258 Million CFA Francs for Student Merit

The 8th annual Ehouo Jacques Gabriel Excellence Award ceremony honored top students and educators in Abidjan's Plateau commune.

The Plateau town hall courtyard hosted the 8th edition of the Ehouo Jacques Gabriel Excellence Award on Thursday, September 3, 2026. This event, now a fixture on the commune's calendar, annually recognizes outstanding students, teachers, and other individuals contributing to academic success. Over 258 million CFA francs were mobilized to support this initiative.

The school year 2026-2027 is set to officially commence on Monday, September 14, 2026, and will conclude on Friday, July 30, 2027. The Prix d’Excellence event aims to foster a culture of achievement and provide tangible support for educational endeavors within the community.

This award ceremony highlights the importance placed on academic achievement and the dedication of educators. The significant funds raised underscore a commitment to rewarding merit and encouraging continued excellence in education within the Plateau commune.

Why it mattersStudents and educators in the Plateau commune are directly impacted by the recognition and financial support provided through the Prix d’Excellence awards.
