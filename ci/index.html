Ivory Coast health budget to rise 21% in 2027

The health ministry plans to spend 954.7 billion FCFA next year, an increase of 21% from 2026.

The Ministry of Health, Public Hygiene and Universal Health Coverage in Ivory Coast plans to allocate 954.7 billion FCFA to the health sector for the 2027 fiscal year. This proposed budget was presented to the deputies of the National Assembly's Economic and Financial Affairs Commission on Wednesday, November 11, 2026.

This budget represents a significant increase of approximately 21% compared to the allocation for 2026. The funds are intended to support various health initiatives and services across the country.

In related economic news, the gambling sector in Ivory Coast generated over 922 billion FCFA in revenue by the end of December 2025.

Politics · 2026-11-13

Ivory Coast frees more than 200 PPA-CI militants under judicial supervision

The supporters of Laurent Gbagbo's party were held since the October 2025 presidential election and left Abidjan's former Maca prison overnight.

More than 200 militants of the PPA-CI, the party of former president Laurent Gbagbo, left the ex-Maca prison in Abidjan on the night of Tuesday, November 10, 2026. They had been detained since the October 2025 presidential election. They are free but remain under judicial supervision, and the release was not announced officially.

Sources close to the party told the KOACI news site that every released militant is still subject to judicial control. The prosecutor's office has not said how many people were freed or what obligations they must meet. Lawyer Roseline Aka-Sérikpa, of the Abidjan bar, called the release a "major procedural easing" and said other releases are under way.

The same week, the party reported the sixth death of a detainee in custody. Atcho Kraidi Arnaud, a member of the Grand-Bassam branch, died on November 6 at the Abidjan penitentiary complex, and the party has demanded that the circumstances be made clear. On November 11 Gbagbo, back from Brussels, also had 14 executive vice-presidents named.

Why it mattersFamilies of the detained supporters now face an open question about when and whether those still in prison will be released, since no public count has been given.

Sport · 2026-11-13

Ivory Coast beats Gambia 2-1 in CAN 2027 qualifier in Bouaké

The Elephants move level on six points with Gambia at the top of Group C, with a return leg set for Bakau on Tuesday, 17 November.

Ivory Coast beat Gambia 2-1 on Thursday, 12 November, in Bouaké, in the third round of qualifiers for the 2027 Africa Cup of Nations (CAN). The Elephants and Gambia are both unbeaten on six points in Group C, separated only by goal difference, +4 for Ivory Coast and +3 for Gambia.

Gambia's coach, Johnathan McKinstry, said on Wednesday that his side were not afraid of Ivory Coast and had come to take the three points. Ivory Coast coach Hervé Renard was without Inter Milan forward Ange-Yoan Bonny, who was ruled out for medical reasons according to the Ivorian Football Federation, and without Ghislain Konan, who is missing the November window with a knee ligament injury. Oumar Diakité returned to the squad.

The two sides meet again on Tuesday, 17 November, in Bakau, Gambia. Over the two legs, the winner would take a serious step towards first place in the group and a place at the CAN 2027, which Kenya, Tanzania and Uganda will host in June and July. In Group G, Cameroon beat Namibia 2-0.

Why it mattersThe winner of the two-leg series in Bouaké and Bakau will hold a six-point lead over third place and a strong claim to top spot in Group C and a place at CAN 2027.

Business · 2026-11-13

Ivory Coast inaugurates Koné gold mine

The new mine, built in under two years, is now the largest in the country and one of Africa's most significant.

Téné Birahima Ouattara, the Vice-Prime Minister and Minister of Defence, inaugurated the Koné gold mine in Fadiadougou, Kani department, on Thursday, November 12, 2026. The mine is operated by Montage Gold Corporation.

Constructed in less than two years, the mine represents an investment exceeding 580 billion FCFA. It is now recognized as the largest gold mine in Ivory Coast and ranks among the most important on the African continent.

The inauguration ceremony was presided over by Téné Birahima Ouattara, highlighting the national significance of this major mining development.

Why it mattersThe Koné gold mine, the largest in Ivory Coast, has been inaugurated, representing an investment of over 580 billion FCFA.

Abidjan hotel manager fined for illegal sewage link to storm drains

The manager of Hotel Askala in Marcory Zone 4 was sanctioned for connecting the hotel's septic tank to public rainwater drains without authorisation.

The manager of Hotel Askala, in Marcory Zone 4 in southern Abidjan, has been sanctioned for illegally discharging water from the hotel's septic tank into public storm-water drains. He was presented to the Public Prosecutor on Tuesday, November 10, 2026, after a judicial procedure.

The fraudulent connection ran from the hotel's septic tank to the public rainwater drainage network on Boulevard de Marseille. He appeared before the Public Prosecutor at the Plateau Court of First Instance with a group of lawyers, and after several hesitations admitted the facts and pledged not to repeat them. A heavy fine was imposed through a judicial settlement, though its amount was not disclosed.

The Ministry of Hydraulics, Sanitation and Public Health said the case shows its determination to fight uncivil acts that disrupt sanitation infrastructure. It urged residents and businesses, whatever their origin or status, to respect public drainage works built for rainwater.

Why it mattersHotels and other businesses connected to public drainage now face prosecution and fines, and the ministry has told operators to keep public rainwater works clear of waste.
