Ivory Coast Launches Cocoa Season with Mandatory Producer ID Cards

The country begins its cocoa marketing season with new traceability rules and the announcement of farmgate prices for the upcoming harvest.

The Ivory Coast officially opened its major cocoa marketing season on Tuesday, September 1, 2026. This launch coincides with the implementation of a national traceability system, requiring all cocoa producers to obtain identification cards. This measure aims to better identify farmers, enhance control over cocoa bean flows, and secure transactions within the sector.

The new farmgate prices for the 2026-2027 cocoa season are set to be officially revealed today. This announcement is a key event for the country's cocoa industry, which is a significant global supplier of the commodity. The introduction of producer identification cards is a notable change for the sector's operational framework.

In parallel, the Directorate General of Hydrocarbons has confirmed fuel prices for September 2026. Super unleaded gasoline will remain at 905 FCFA per liter, diesel at 725 FCFA per liter, and kerosene at 780 FCFA per liter. These fuel prices are separate from the cocoa sector's new pricing announcements.

Why it mattersCocoa farmers are directly affected by the new mandatory producer identification cards and the announcement of farmgate prices for the 2026-2027 season.

Ivory Coast Basketball Team Loses to Angola

The Elephants suffered their second loss in nine qualifying matches for the 2027 World Cup.

The Ivory Coast men's basketball team lost to Angola with a score of 70-91 on Sunday, August 30, 2026. This defeat marks their second loss in the 2027 World Cup qualifiers. The match took place in Dakar, Senegal, as part of the fourth window's third encounter.

Despite the loss, the Elephants maintain their position at the top of Group F. They have accumulated 16 points in the standings. This result follows a previous loss in the qualifying campaign, indicating a challenging path toward the global tournament.

The team's performance in this qualifying series is crucial for their advancement. While they lead their group, the recent defeat highlights areas needing improvement as they continue their pursuit of a spot in the 2027 World Cup.

Why it mattersThe Ivory Coast basketball team's performance in the 2027 World Cup qualifiers affects national pride and potential future sporting investments.

Radio Côte d’Ivoire Launches 'Mon Allié' Program for Social Cohesion

The new radio show aims to promote interethnic alliances as a tool for social cohesion and development, officially launching on August 29, 2026.

Radio Côte d’Ivoire has introduced a new program called "Mon Allié," designed to highlight interethnic alliances as a means to foster social cohesion and development. The official launch event took place on Saturday, August 29, 2026, at the Ficgayo space in Yopougon, marked by a festive atmosphere.

This initiative seeks to leverage traditional relationships and partnerships between different ethnic groups within Ivory Coast to strengthen national unity and encourage collaborative progress. The program's debut underscores a commitment to using media for positive social impact and community building.

Separately, the Abissa 2026 festival, a significant cultural event for the N'zima Kôtôkô people, was also officially launched on the same day, August 29, 2026, in Grand-Bassam. The ceremony, held in a warm atmosphere, focused on themes of cohesion, culture, and sustainable development.

Why it mattersThe launch of 'Mon Allié' by Radio Côte d’Ivoire aims to build social cohesion by promoting interethnic alliances, potentially impacting community relations and development efforts.

Gendarmerie Dismantles Illegal Gold Mining Sites in Ivory Coast

National Gendarmerie operations between August 16 and 27 destroyed equipment used in clandestine gold extraction across the Indénié-Djuablin region.

The National Gendarmerie has intensified its crackdown on illegal gold mining in the Indénié-Djuablin region. Between August 16 and August 27, 2026, multiple operations were conducted in various locations, leading to the dismantling of numerous clandestine sites. Significant quantities of equipment used for illegal gold extraction were destroyed during these actions.

These operations are part of ongoing efforts by the Brigade Spéciale de Surveillance et d’Intervention (BSSI) to protect the environment in Ivory Coast. The BSSI continues its environmental protection activities, targeting illegal mining operations that harm natural resources and ecosystems.

The intensified fight against illegal gold mining aims to curb environmental degradation and assert state control over resource extraction. The actions taken demonstrate a commitment to enforcing regulations against unauthorized mining activities, which often operate without regard for environmental impact or legal frameworks.

Why it mattersIllegal gold mining operations are being dismantled, affecting the environment and potentially disrupting illicit economic activities.

Ministry Equips Gontougo Health Region With 650 Devices

The Ministry of Health received 650 computer devices valued at 106 million CFA francs to expand the mHealth digital platform in eastern Ivory Coast.

The Ministry of Health, Public Hygiene, and Universal Coverage received 650 computer devices on Friday, August 28, 2026, in Abidjan. The equipment, valued at 106 million CFA francs, is intended to expand the mHealth digital platform within the Gontougo health region.

This initiative is supported by UNICEF, with funding from Canada. The goal is to advance the digitalization of the community health system in eastern Ivory Coast, aiming to improve healthcare delivery and accessibility.

The introduction of these digital tools is expected to enhance the efficiency of health services in the Gontougo region. This move signifies a commitment to modernizing healthcare infrastructure and integrating technology into community health programs.

Why it mattersThe Gontougo health region is directly affected by the introduction of 650 new digital devices to expand the mHealth platform.
