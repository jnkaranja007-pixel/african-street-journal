Ivory Coast says African energy bank will start on Jan. 2 in Abuja

Mamadou Sangafowa-Coulibaly said the APPO and Afreximbank project will mobilise funding for Africa’s energy sector and infrastructure.

Ivory Coast’s mines, petroleum and energy minister, Mamadou Sangafowa-Coulibaly, said the African Energy Bank will begin operations on Jan. 2, 2027, in Abuja, Nigeria. He announced the date on Tuesday, Nov. 17, in Abidjan, at the opening of the African Petroleum Producers’ Organization’s 50th ministerial session.

The bank is a joint project of the African Petroleum Producers’ Organization (APPO) and the African Export-Import Bank (Afreximbank). Sangafowa-Coulibaly said its first general assembly was held in Nigeria on Oct. 31, electing directors and appointing the institution’s first president and auditor. He described the bank as a way to mobilise financing for energy development amid limited access to capital and infrastructure needs.

The session is also considering APPO’s 2026 budget execution and proposed 2027 budget, its long-term strategy and nominations to the organisation’s bodies for 2027. The bank’s planned launch follows the assembly’s appointments.

Why it mattersThe bank’s first president, directors and auditor have been appointed, with operations scheduled to start on Jan. 2, 2027.

Prosecutor seeks five years in prison for Blaise Lasm

The Ivory Coast opposition figure is charged with plotting against state authority, but prosecutors could not date videos presented at trial.

Prosecutors in Abidjan on Monday 16 November requested five years in prison for Blaise Lasm, a senior figure in Laurent Gbagbo’s PPA-CI party, on a charge of plotting against state authority without an act. The court is due to deliver its ruling on 30 November.

The prosecution presented videos at trial but could not establish when they were recorded, Afrique Sur 7 reported. Lasm’s lawyer said the defence had not received the footage during the investigation or instruction. Lasm said one video was recorded in 2019 or 2020, rather than between August and September 2025, as prosecutors argued.

A September 7, 2026 order had already dropped charges of inciting insurrection and undermining territorial integrity, leaving the charge on which Lasm is being tried. Prosecutors also requested five years’ loss of civic rights and a five-year ban on leaving his home department. Three years in prison were sought for fellow PPA-CI deputy secretary-general Dahi Nestor Douhouré.

Why it mattersLasm’s trial is scheduled for a ruling on 30 November, while he has already spent more than a year in detention, according to his lawyer.

BSSI destroys 18 illegal gold-mining dredges on Bagoué River

The operation at M’Basso in Tengréla department was carried out at dawn on Monday, 16 November, as surveillance of areas exposed to illegal mining was stepped up.

A detachment of Ivory Coast’s Special Surveillance and Intervention Brigade (BSSI) destroyed 18 dredges used for illegal gold mining on the Bagoué River, near M’Basso in Tengréla department, on Monday, 16 November. The operation began at dawn.

The dredges were used in clandestine gold extraction, according to reports by Linfodrome and Ivoire Matin. The operation formed part of stepped-up surveillance of areas exposed to illegal mining in the north of the country.

The reports gave no further details about arrests or the next stage of the surveillance effort.

Why it mattersThe destruction removes the equipment illegal miners on the Bagoué were using to extract gold, leaving them without their dredges.

Abidjan District awards 300 million FCFA to 60 young entrepreneurs

The 2026 Alassane Ouattara young entrepreneur prize recipients received funding cheques at a ceremony in Cocody on Monday.

60 young entrepreneurs received funding cheques from the Autonomous District of Abidjan on Monday, 16 November, at the Sofitel Hôtel Ivoire ice rink in Cocody. The district allocated 300 million FCFA in total to recipients of the 2026 Alassane Ouattara Emerging Young Entrepreneur Prize.

The funding is intended to support the recipients in putting their projects into practice and help them enter the economy. The report did not give the amount allocated to each entrepreneur or identify their projects.

The award ceremony took place in Abidjan, where the district distributed the cheques to the 60 beneficiaries.

Why it mattersThe 60 recipients are to use the district funding to develop their projects and enter the economy.

Culture · 2026-11-18

Dehima Mission holds peace prayer in Tiassalé

The ceremony on 15 November formed part of the 31st annual peace celebrations organised by the AD3T association in the town.

The Dehima Mission held a prayer for peace at Tiassalé town hall on 15 November, as part of Côte d’Ivoire’s National Peace Day. The ceremony was initiated by Anouanzè Dehima of Tiassalé, Taabo and Toumodi, known as AD3T.

Mermoz Koffi, the event’s organising committee president, said the mission had maintained a tradition of peace prayers since 1988. Preacher Oka Kouadio urged worshippers and Ivorians to practise forgiveness and tolerance in daily life. Tiassalé Mayor Assalé Tiémoko, also a mission member, called on residents to adopt peaceful attitudes.

The AD3T celebrations ran from 13 to 15 November and marked their 31st edition. They also recognised 50 years of priesthood for Etien Koffi Mathieu, the Dehima national council president and AD3T founder. Koffi said the mission prayed for peace before the national observance was established by decree in 1996.

Why it mattersThe AD3T’s 31st peace celebration ran through 15 November in Tiassalé, with organisers urging residents to practise tolerance and forgiveness.
