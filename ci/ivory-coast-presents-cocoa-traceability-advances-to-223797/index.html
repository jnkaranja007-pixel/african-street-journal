Ivory Coast presents cocoa traceability advances to European buyers

At Eurochocolate in Italy, the Coffee-Cocoa Council outlined the country’s work on sustainable production as EU rules take effect on 1 January 2027.

Sources: Linfodrome and Ivoire Matin · 2026-11-19 · 1 min read

Ivory Coast’s Coffee-Cocoa Council (CCC) presented the country’s progress on cocoa traceability and sustainable production at Eurochocolate 2026 in Italy, as the country seeks to meet new European market requirements.

The presentation came ahead of an EU regulation on products linked to deforestation, scheduled to take effect on 1 January 2027. The CCC’s presentation was intended to reassure European buyers about the origin and sustainability of Ivorian cocoa.

Ivory Coast wants to preserve its access to the European market by providing guarantees on traceability and sustainability, according to Ivoire Matin.

Why it mattersEuropean buyers will face the new requirements from 1 January 2027, and Ivory Coast is providing traceability and sustainability guarantees to preserve its market access.
