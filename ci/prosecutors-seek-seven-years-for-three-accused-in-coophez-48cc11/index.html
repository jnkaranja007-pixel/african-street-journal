Prosecutors seek seven years for three accused in Coophez case

The economic and financial crimes prosecutor made the request on 19 November; the court is due to deliver its ruling on 7 December.

Sources: Ivoire Matin and Linfodrome · 2026-11-20 · 1 min read

Ivory Coast’s economic and financial crimes prosecutor has requested seven years in prison for Marie-Paule Adjé’s husband and two co-accused in the Coophez case. The prosecutor presented the request on 19 November, according to Linfodrome.

The case involves two cooperatives whose complainants say they received neither the expected goods nor a return of their invested money months after the transaction. The file also covered commercial relations between the Ivorian Rubber Company, led by Tah Kobina Thomas, and Prime Prestige, led by Mohamed Adnane Achirou, including an agency contract to buy products.

The court postponed the case from 5 November to 19 November so the prosecution could present its requests and the parties could make their arguments. The three accused are due to learn the court’s decision on 7 December 2026.

Why it mattersThe three accused now face the court’s decision on 7 December after prosecutors requested prison terms.
