Ouattara and Steinmeier agree to promote trade exceeding CFA918 billion

The presidents made the commitment in Abidjan, where Ouattara urged German investors to consider projects under Ivory Coast's 2026-2030 development plan.

Sources: Ivoire Matin and Linfodrome · 2026-11-25 · 1 min read

Ivory Coast's President Alassane Ouattara and German President Frank-Walter Steinmeier agreed to promote trade between their countries during talks in Abidjan on Monday 23 November 2026. Annual trade averaged CFA918.3 billion over the last three years, Ouattara said.

Ouattara said German cooperation through KfW, Germany's state development bank, and GIZ, Germany's state development agency, stood at 968.62 million euros, or about CFA635.5 billion. He invited German investors to consider the 2026-2030 national development plan, which covers infrastructure, mining, agro-industry, energy, health and education.

The sixth round of intergovernmental negotiations is scheduled for 26 and 27 November 2026. The two presidents also discussed cooperation on renewable energy, biodiversity, forest restoration, vocational training and private-sector development.

Why it mattersTraders and investors in six priority sectors now have a stated invitation to bid on projects under the plan, with the next round of talks due on 26 and 27 November 2026.
