Songon Plateau buyers challenge rival land claims over one approval number

More than 1,000 buyers say a 76-hectare dispute involves contested administrative documents and destruction of property.

Sources: Linfodrome and Ivoire Matin · 2026-11-26 · 1 min read

More than 1,000 land buyers in Songon Agban (Plateau), in Ivory Coast, say their plots are caught in a dispute over the same approval number. Village leaders and a buyers’ collective held a press conference in Songon Plateau on Tuesday, 24 November 2026, to denounce what they described as the seizure of their land by Songon Kassemblé.

The dispute concerns 76 hectares, according to Ivoire Matin. The buyers say they have been wronged amid customary claims and contested administrative documents; the outlet also reported destruction of property.

The land dispute follows a similar conflict in Songon Ayéwahi, Linfodrome reported. The reports give no official resolution or next step.

Why it mattersThe buyers’ collective and village leaders must pursue a resolution to competing claims over 76 hectares and one approval number.
