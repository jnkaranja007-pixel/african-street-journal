Ivory Coast's Félix Houphouët-Boigny University launches nuclear medicine specialist di...

The programme was launched alongside a health sciences section for Ivorian women researchers and academics.

Source: Linfodrome · 2026-11-25 · 1 min read

The Félix Houphouët-Boigny University (UFHB) launched a specialist diploma in nuclear medicine in Abidjan-Cocody on Tuesday, 24 November. The programme is intended to strengthen medical skills and scientific research in Côte d’Ivoire.

The university introduced the diploma at the same time as a health sciences section of the Association of Women Researchers and Teacher-Researchers of Côte d’Ivoire (AFEMC-CI). The source describes both initiatives as aimed at building medical expertise and research.

The announcement gives no details on course length, admissions or the number of trainees.

Why it mattersDoctors and patients in Côte d’Ivoire will get a locally based training route in nuclear medicine, a field the source ties to the country’s shortage of specialists.
