Ivory Coast farmers push for national livestock traceability to curb cattle theft

Breeders at the Abidjan livestock fair want a nationwide system to identify animals, which they say would better address cattle theft and the spread of disease.

Source: RFI Afrique · 2026-11-23 · 1 min read

The livestock fair in Abidjan closed on Sunday, 22 November 2026, with the identification and traceability of farm animals at the centre of its discussions. Breeders gathered there took up the problem of stolen cattle and the spread of animal disease.

The government has announced an initiative on animal identification and traceability. Breeders are hoping it will grow into a system covering the whole country, one that would let authorities respond more effectively to cattle theft and to the spread of disease.

The report does not give a timetable, budget or set of measures for the government's initiative, so the details of any national system remain unannounced.

Why it mattersHerders and cattle owners who have lost animals to theft or disease would benefit if the announced government initiative becomes a nationwide identification system.
