Ivory Coast NGO reports dozens harmed by skin-lightening creams

An Ivorian non-governmental organization has documented dozens of victims suffering lasting health damage from unregulated skin-lightening cosmetic products.

Source: RFI Afrique · 2026-10-22 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

An Ivorian non-governmental organization has raised concerns over the health impacts of skin-lightening creams, soaps, and lotions. The group reported identifying dozens of individuals who have suffered severe and sometimes permanent health consequences from using these products in recent months.

The NGO stated that while many cosmetic products are part of daily life, some do not meet safety standards and can lead to serious health issues. The organization is now calling for stricter regulatory oversight and increased controls on the sale and distribution of such cosmetics within the country.

The victims' ailments range from skin conditions to other lasting effects, highlighting a significant public health challenge. The NGO's alert underscores the hidden dangers associated with beauty products that promise lighter skin but deliver detrimental health outcomes.

Why it mattersDozens of people in Ivory Coast are suffering lasting health damage from using unregulated skin-lightening cosmetic products.
