African Union Head Praises Ouattara's Sahel Peace Efforts

Mahmoud Ali Youssouf commended President Alassane Ouattara's leadership in fostering regional stability and economic integration.

Source: Agence Ivoirienne de Presse · 2026-10-21 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

The President of the African Union Commission, Mahmoud Ali Youssouf, lauded Ivorian President Alassane Ouattara's vision and commitment to peace and security in the Sahel region. Youssouf specifically highlighted Ouattara's dedication to economic integration and resolving regional crises during a meeting in Abidjan on Tuesday, October 20, 2026.

The praise, according to a statement from the Ivorian Presidency, underscored Ouattara's significant role in promoting regional stability and strengthening cooperation among African nations. The discussions occurred amidst ongoing security challenges in the Sahel and critical economic integration issues facing the continent, which are key concerns for regional and continental bodies.

The African Union Commission, as the executive arm of the pan-African organization, is responsible for implementing AU decisions and fostering collaboration among member states. Youssouf's remarks emphasized the importance of leadership in addressing these complex challenges.
