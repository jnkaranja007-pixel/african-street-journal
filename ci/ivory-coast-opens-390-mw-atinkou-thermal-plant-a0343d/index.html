Ivory Coast opens 390 MW Atinkou thermal plant

The new combined cycle power station in Jacqueville will boost national electricity production and West African energy hub status.

Sources: Linfodrome and Ivoire Matin · 2026-11-08 · 1 min read

Prime Minister Robert Beugré Mambé inaugurated the Atinkou combined cycle thermal power plant on Friday, November 6, 2026. The facility is in Taboth, Jacqueville department.

This energy infrastructure supports the national policy to develop the electricity sector. It consolidates national production and reinforces Ivory Coast's position as a West African energy hub.

The Atinkou plant in Jacqueville has a capacity of over 390 MW. Its commissioning officially enhances Ivory Coast's energy potential.
