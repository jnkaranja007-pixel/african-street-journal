Smart Energy and CTCI sign financing-backed solar deal for Alépé factory

The project combines nearly 1.5 megawatt-peak of solar panels with 2 megawatt-hours of storage to address power cuts and voltage drops.

Sources: Agence Ivoirienne de Presse and Linfodrome · 2026-11-21 · 1 min read

Smart Energy and the Compagnie de transformation du caoutchouc ivoirien (CTCI), an Ivorian rubber processor, signed a contract in Abidjan on Friday 20 November 2026 to build a solar plant with storage at CTCI’s factory in Alépé. The project includes nearly 1.5 megawatt-peak (MWc) of panels and 2 megawatt-hours (MWh) of storage, Smart Energy chief executive Kpan Refus said.

CTCI chief executive Karim Diarra said the factory, located at the end of the power line, faces electricity cuts and voltage drops and has relied on costly gas generators. He said solar power would replace or reduce some generator use, helping control production costs and maintain operations. Smart Energy will finance the project based on its results, the companies said.

Smart Energy’s offer combines engineering, procurement, construction and financing, with predictable energy costs over 20 to 25 years, Kpan said. Deployment at the site is scheduled for Monday.

Why it mattersCTCI’s Alépé factory can reduce its costly reliance on gas generators and improve control of production costs as site deployment is scheduled for Monday.
