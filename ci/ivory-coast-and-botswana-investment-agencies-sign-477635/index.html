Ivory Coast and Botswana investment agencies sign cooperation agreement

The deal is intended to ease trade, connect business communities and open investment opportunities between the two countries.

Sources: Ivoire Matin and Linfodrome · 2026-11-19 · 1 min read

Ivory Coast’s investment-promotion agency and the Botswana Investment and Trade Centre signed a cooperation agreement on Tuesday, 17 November, at the opening of the Côte d’Ivoire-Botswana Economic Forum in Abidjan. Vice-President Tiémoko Meyliet Koné urged Botswana’s business operators to invest in Ivory Coast’s 2026-2030 National Development Plan.

The agreement is intended to facilitate trade, bring the countries’ business communities closer and encourage new investment opportunities, according to Ivoire Matin. Koné cited mining, energy, agriculture, livestock and fisheries, tourism, and financial services as areas for cooperation. Botswana President Duma Gideon Boko urged businesses from both countries to build lasting partnerships.

Koné said private-sector investment should support Ivory Coast’s economic transformation. The forum was held at the headquarters of the Ivorian employers’ organisation in Abidjan’s Plateau district.

Why it mattersIvorian and Botswana businesses are expected to turn the signed cooperation agreement into investment projects across the sectors named by Koné.
