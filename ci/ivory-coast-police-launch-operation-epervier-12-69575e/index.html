Ivory Coast police launch operation Epervier 12

The operation aims to dismantle criminal networks across the nation.

Source: Linfodrome · 2026-10-30 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Operation Epervier 12 was launched in Bouaké on Thursday, October 29, 2026, by the Director General of National Police, General Youssouf Kouyaté. The Minister of Interior and Security was present at the launch. This operation involves the National Police, Gendarmerie, FACI, Waters and Forests, Customs, and Civil Protection working together.

The coordinated effort is designed to dismantle criminal networks throughout the country. General Kouyaté leads the national police force in this initiative. The participating agencies will combine their resources for a unified approach against crime.

Separately, in Yamoussoukro, about 30 female agents from the Ministry of Waters and Forests began a leadership training on October 27, 2026. This training aims to strengthen female leadership within the ministry.

Why it mattersOperation Epervier 12 aims to combat criminal networks, impacting public safety and security in Ivory Coast.
