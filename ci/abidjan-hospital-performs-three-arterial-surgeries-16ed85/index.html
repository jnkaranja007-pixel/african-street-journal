Abidjan hospital performs three arterial surgeries

Institut Cœur de Grâce uses a hybrid operating room and French specialists to treat vascular conditions and train local doctors.

Source: Ivoire Matin · 2026-10-30 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

The Institut Cœur de Grâce (ICG) in Abidjan successfully operated on three patients for arterial blockages between October 26 and 28, 2026. Procedures included replacing an aortic valve, performing a coronary bypass, and treating a coarctation of the ascending aorta. The hospital utilized its latest-generation hybrid operating room for these interventions.

French specialists collaborated with ICG to conduct the surgeries and train Ivorian doctors in vascular procedures. Hospital officials stated this mission advances cardiovascular surgery and local medical capabilities. They also stressed the importance of early detection and regular cardiology check-ups for the population.

The ICG aims to improve access to cardiovascular care in Ivory Coast and the sub-region through its facilities and specialized equipment. The development of such interventions is a major challenge for the health system, as cardiovascular diseases require rapid diagnosis and appropriate management.
