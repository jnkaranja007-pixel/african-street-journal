FISH4ACP advances tilapia sector in Ivory Coast

The program reports increased aquaculture production, better access to finance, and improved governance after five years.

Source: Linfodrome · 2026-11-09 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

The FISH4ACP program has completed five years of work in Ivory Coast, reporting progress in tilapia aquaculture production, improved access to financing, and advancements in governance and animal health.

These achievements and future development prospects for the sector were discussed at a results-sharing workshop held on Friday, November 6, 2026, at the Capitol Hotel in Abidjan.

The program's support has strengthened the Ivorian tilapia sector, stimulating investment and preparing for the future of aquaculture.
