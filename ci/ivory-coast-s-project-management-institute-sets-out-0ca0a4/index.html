Ivory Coast's project management institute sets out priorities for the next decade

The PMI Côte d'Ivoire marked its 10th anniversary in Abidjan on 20 and 21 November 2026, with its president setting out a focus on certification, partnerships with the state.

Source: Linfodrome · 2026-11-23 · 1 min read

The Project Management Institute (PMI) Côte d'Ivoire celebrated its 10th anniversary in Abidjan on 20 and 21 November 2026, under the theme of the next decade of project management. Its president, Emmanuelle Adiabouah, used the event to set out the institute's priorities for the coming years.

Those priorities centre on professional certifications, partnerships with the Ivorian state, and passing on know-how to young people as artificial intelligence changes the way projects are run. The institute presented these as its roadmap for the next 10 years.

The report gives no further figures, targets or timelines for the programme, and no detail on which partnerships or certifications are planned.

Why it mattersFirms and professionals who bid for or manage state projects in Ivory Coast now have a stated focus on certification and public partnerships to plan around.
