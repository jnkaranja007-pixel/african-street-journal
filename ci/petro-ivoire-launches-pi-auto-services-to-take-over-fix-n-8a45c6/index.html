Petro Ivoire launches PI Auto Services to take over Fix N’Go work

The new subsidiary follows Petro Ivoire’s acquisition of Point S and will provide vehicle servicing and maintenance in Côte d’Ivoire.

Sources: Agence Ivoirienne de Presse and Linfodrome · 2026-11-21 · 1 min read

Petro Ivoire, an oil-products distributor, launched PI Auto Services in Abidjan on Friday, 20 November, after acquiring Point S. The new subsidiary takes over the car-mechanics activities previously operated under the Fix N’Go name.

Chief executive Sébastien Kadio-Morokro said the holding company wants to build a vehicle-maintenance business in Côte d’Ivoire and across Africa. Point S International chief executive Fabien Bouquet said the agreement also focuses on training workshop operators and upgrading equipment, including electronic diagnostics.

Bouquet said drivers’ maintenance habits in Côte d’Ivoire still focus largely on fixing mechanical failures, and argued that regular preventive servicing will require stronger workshop skills. Point S International and local partners are willing to support government ministries developing mechanical-training programmes, he said.

Why it mattersPI Auto Services now takes over the Fix N’Go mechanics business, while Point S International and local partners are prepared to support ministries developing mechanic training.
