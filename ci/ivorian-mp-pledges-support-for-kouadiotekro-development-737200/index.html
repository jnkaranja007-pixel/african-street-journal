Ivorian MP pledges support for Kouadiotékro development

Aka Desquith Ambroise will help secure an ambulance and improve village road access.

Source: Agence Ivoirienne de Presse · 2026-11-09 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

The Member of Parliament for Tiémélékro, Aka Desquith Ambroise, pledged to support Kouadiotékro's development projects, including acquiring an ambulance for the health centre and improving the village's access road. He made this commitment on Sunday, November 8, 2026, in Abidjan during the ordinary general assembly of the Mutual for the Development of Kouadiotékro (MUDEK). Ambroise stated that steps have already been taken with the Ministry of Health to obtain the ambulance.

Ambroise also intends to contact the Moronou Regional Council regarding the road improvements, leveraging his position as a regional councillor. MUDEK president Mardochée Kouassi presented the mutual's achievements, including restructuring and updating its statutes. Kouassi also detailed plans for a "Maison du peuple" costing nearly 107 million CFA Francs, intended to house chieftaincy offices and community spaces.

Kouassi explained the need for the "Maison du peuple" due to the lack of a suitable venue for village gatherings. He mentioned that contributions from residents are being collected and that public and private partners will be approached in 2027. The MUDEK president also highlighted efforts to ease youth tensions and announced upcoming elections for the youth bureau on November 22.

Why it mattersResidents of Kouadiotékro will benefit from improved healthcare access and better infrastructure.
