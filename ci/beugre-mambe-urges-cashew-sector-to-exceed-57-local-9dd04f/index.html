Beugré Mambé urges cashew sector to exceed 57% local processing

The prime minister made the appeal at the opening of the cashew-processing equipment and technology fair in Treichville on 26 November.

Sources: Afrique Sur 7 and Linfodrome · 2026-11-28 · 1 min read

Ivory Coast Prime Minister Robert Beugré Mambé urged the cashew sector to raise local processing above 57% at the opening of the International Fair for Cashew Processing Equipment and Technologies, known in French as the Salon international des équipements et des technologies de transformation de l'anacarde (SIETTA), in Treichville on Thursday, 26 November.

The appeal came at the fifth edition of the fair, held at the Palais de la Culture. Linfodrome reported that Mambé called for the sector to surpass its current local-processing performance.

The report gave no target beyond exceeding 57%, and named no deadline for reaching a higher rate.

Why it mattersCashew growers and processors are being pressed to lift local processing above the sector's current 57% rate, with no deadline stated.
