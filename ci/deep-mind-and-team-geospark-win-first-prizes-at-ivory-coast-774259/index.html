Deep Mind and Team Geospark win first prizes at Ivory Coast's extractive industries hac...

Deep Mind's modular biorefinery turns organic waste into biomethane, carbon dioxide and biofertilisers, while Team Geospark built a filter for acidic mine water.

Sources: Agence Ivoirienne de Presse, Linfodrome and Afrique Sur 7 · 2026-11-23 · 1 min read

Deep Mind and Team Geospark won the first prizes at the second SIREXE Hackathon, the competition held alongside the international fair for extractive resources and energy in Abidjan. Results were announced on Sunday 22 November, at the close of the fair held at the Abidjan exhibition centre. Deep Mind took the thematic category with a modular biorefinery built around the Ivorian Biorefinery Company (SIB). Team Geospark took the open category with its Cacao-Mine circular biofilter, designed to treat acidic mine water.

Each first-place team receives CFA10 million, while second-place teams YOVICOM and HEXAVENOM receive five million CFA francs each and third-place teams BIOPOWER and SOFT ENGINEER receive 2.5 million each. Winners also receive trophies, medals, certificates and post-hackathon support. YOVICOM's ISOBIO unit converts industrial organic waste into biogas and then electricity at production sites.

The competition, which asked how infrastructure can support economic development in Africa, drew 20 shortlisted teams in its first phase. Six were chosen for technical assessments and jury interviews held from 12 to 15 October.

Why it mattersWinning teams receive cash awards of 2.5 million to CFA10 million, with post-hackathon support for the projects.
