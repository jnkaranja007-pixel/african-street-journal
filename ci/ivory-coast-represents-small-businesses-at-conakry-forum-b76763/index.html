Ivory Coast represents small businesses at Conakry forum

Minister Kalil Konaté represents the guest-of-honour country at the 12th edition, which brings together 20 countries and 100 exhibitors.

Source: Linfodrome · 2026-11-22 · 1 min read

Ivory Coast sent Minister Kalil Konaté to represent its small and medium-sized enterprises at the 12th edition of the SBPME, a forum in Conakry, Guinea, running from 20 to 23 November 2026. Ivory Coast is the event’s guest of honour.

The forum has drawn 20 countries and 100 exhibitors, according to the event details reported by Linfodrome. Konaté is attending on behalf of Prime Minister Robert Beugré Mambé.

This edition is the first to be held outside the West African Economic and Monetary Union (UEMOA) zone, the report said. The forum is scheduled to run through 23 November.
