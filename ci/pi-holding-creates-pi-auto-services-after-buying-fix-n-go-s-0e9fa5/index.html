PI Holding creates PI Auto Services after buying Fix N’Go’s mechanical business

The new POINT S-branded subsidiary will handle vehicle servicing and repairs, while Fix N’Go is expected to support a transfer of expertise.

Sources: Ivoire Matin and Linfodrome · 2026-11-22 · 1 min read

PI Holding launched PI Auto Services in Abidjan on Friday 20 November 2026, after signing an agreement to buy the mechanical-work business operated by Fix N’Go. The new subsidiary will provide vehicle maintenance and repairs under the POINT S brand, including mechanical work, oil changes, tyres, bodywork and paint.

Sébastien Kadio-Morokro, Petro Ivoire’s chief executive and a PI Holding leader, said Fix N’Go would help transfer expertise to the new company. PI Auto Services takes over an activity Petro Ivoire and Fix N’Go had developed under the POINT S name since 2020. Kadio-Morokro said the group wanted the subsidiary to become a reference for vehicle maintenance in Ivory Coast in the medium term and across Africa in the long term.

Petro Ivoire deputy managing director Maud Kadio-Morokro said the fuel distributor’s products had grown by about 21% through the end of September 2026, three times faster than the market. She also said Petro Ivoire operates half of the national electric-vehicle charging network.
