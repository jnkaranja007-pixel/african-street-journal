Mozambique's Supreme Court delays testimony in Mondlane trial until Friday

Prosecutors, the defence and a participant in the case sought further questions for the former presidential candidate before witnesses testify.

Source: Carta de Mocambique · 2026-11-26 · 1 min read

Mozambique’s Supreme Court has postponed witness testimony in the trial of former presidential candidate Venâncio Bila Mondlane until Friday, after further questions about his interrogation changed the court’s schedule. The testimony had been planned for Wednesday.

The prosecution and the case’s assistant, Rede Viária de Moçambique, sought another round of questioning based on Mondlane’s answers to his lawyers on Tuesday; the defence also requested more questions. The court is expected to hear 36 witnesses, with 12 called by prosecutors, four by the assistant and 20 by the defence. Testimony is expected to begin in the afternoon and continue on Monday.

The court also accepted new evidence from prosecutors and the assistant on Wednesday, despite a defence objection that it had not previously seen the material. Three experts are due to testify, including two from mobile operators Tmcel and Vodacom.

Why it mattersThe prosecution, the case’s assistant and Mondlane’s defence must prepare for further questioning before 36 witnesses begin testifying Friday afternoon.
