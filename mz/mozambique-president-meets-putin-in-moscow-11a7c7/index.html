Mozambique president meets Putin in Moscow

Daniel Chapo and Vladimir Putin discussed strengthening ties and potential new agreements.

Source: Club of Mozambique · 2026-10-28 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Mozambican President Daniel Chapo met with Russian President Vladimir Putin in Moscow on October 28, 2026, to discuss strengthening bilateral relations.

Putin noted that Mozambique was represented at a high level in previous Russia-Africa forums, which he believes laid a foundation for developing ties with the continent. He also mentioned that trade between the two countries nearly doubled from January to August, and an intergovernmental commission had its fourth meeting on October 1, signing a protocol to promote cooperation in areas including geology, energy, education, and agriculture.

Mozambique's Foreign Minister Maria Manuela Lucas stated that negotiations are underway for strategic agreements, with transport and economic cooperation being key areas. This engagement aims to secure economic and military benefits for Mozambique, while Russia seeks to maintain diplomatic connections amid international isolation.
