Maputo hosts population and development conference

The Ministry of Planning and Development and UNFPA are holding a high-level meeting on November 4-5.

Source: O Pais · 2026-11-04 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Mozambique's Ministry of Planning and Development (MPD), in partnership with the United Nations Population Fund (UNFPA), will host a Conference on Population and Development in Mozambique on November 4 and 5. The event will take place at the Mozambique-China Cultural Centre in Maputo.

This high-level meeting aims to foster dialogue and define strategies concerning population and development within the country. Officials from the MPD and UNFPA are organizing the conference.

The conference is scheduled to run for two days, bringing together stakeholders to discuss critical issues related to population dynamics and their impact on national development.
