Mozambique’s 100 largest companies record 615 billion meticais in turnover

President Daniel Chapo announced the 2025 figure and called for reforms to improve the business environment.

Source: Club of Mozambique · 2026-11-28 · 1 min read

Mozambique’s 100 largest companies recorded approximately 615 billion meticais (€8.7 billion) in turnover in 2025, President Daniel Chapo announced on Thursday. He also called for reforms to improve the business environment.

Chapo said the figures showed the strength and potential of Mozambique’s business sector, as well as the challenges it faces. The announcement covered the combined turnover of the 100 companies; it did not identify individual firms.

The president’s call for reform was the next step he set out, but no specific changes or timetable were given.

Why it mattersThe 100 companies whose combined turnover was reported are the businesses at the centre of Chapo’s call to improve Mozambique’s business environment.
