Roadworks disrupt N7 traffic in Moatize

Temporary restrictions will be in place from 7:30 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. daily.

Source: Club of Mozambique · 2026-11-08 · 1 min read

Roadworks on the N7 in Moatize, Mozambique, will cause temporary traffic disruptions. The National Roads Administration (ANE) is undertaking these works as part of the Water Services and Institutional Support Project II (WASIS II).

Traffic will be managed with a STOP AND GO system between 7:30 a.m. and 5:00 p.m. daily because diversions are not possible. ANE will use warning signs, barriers, and marshals with flags to guide vehicles through the construction zones.

ANE has asked road users for understanding and cooperation, advising them to reduce speed, follow signs and marshals, maintain distance, avoid overtaking, and expect delays. These works aim to rehabilitate and construct water supply networks in Tete and Moatize.

Why it mattersRoad users in Moatize will experience traffic delays and need to follow new traffic management systems.
