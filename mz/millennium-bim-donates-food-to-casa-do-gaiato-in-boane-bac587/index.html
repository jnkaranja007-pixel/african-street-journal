Millennium bim donates food to Casa do Gaiato in Boane

The bank's donation supports a Maputo province institution that serves more than 300 meals every day to children, young people and staff.

Source: Club of Mozambique · 2026-11-16 · 1 min read

Millennium bim has donated essential food supplies to Casa do Gaiato, which is located in Boane district, Maputo province. The institution cares for vulnerable children and young people and supports their education and training.

Rui Pedro, Chairperson of Millennium bim's Executive Committee, said the bank wanted to make a practical contribution to the institution's needs and to thank those who look after the children every day. Casa do Gaiato serves more than 300 meals a day to residents, pupils at its school and staff.

The donation is part of the bank's corporate social responsibility programme, "Mais Moçambique Pra Mim", which covers education, health, the environment, financial literacy, youth sport and social solidarity. In 2021, the bank also donated an irrigation system for the institution's farm.

Why it mattersResidents, pupils and staff at Casa do Gaiato receive food support for their daily meals.
