Chapo seeks Paris visit as Macron's term nears its end

Three dates have been proposed for a Mozambique-France meeting, but Maputo has struggled to fit them in its schedule.

Source: Zitamar News · 2026-11-28 · 1 min read

Mozambican President Daniel Chapo has not yet made an official visit to Paris, and Africa Intelligence reports he is growing impatient over difficulties arranging one before French President Emmanuel Macron leaves office. Zitamar News understands that three dates have been proposed, but they have proved hard to accommodate on the Mozambican side.

The talks would cover a dispute over about $4.5 billion in additional costs that TotalEnergies has claimed after the suspension of Mozambique LNG in 2021. The Mozambican government has resisted the company's demands, and France is one of the country's most important investment partners, principally through TotalEnergies, which leads the $25 billion-plus project in Cabo Delgado.

Africa Intelligence suggests Macron's intervention could ease tensions between Chapo and TotalEnergies chief executive Patrick Pouyanné. Zitamar News notes that the dispute is fundamentally commercial, and a presidential meeting would not guarantee a resolution. The two countries also share interests in maritime security, counterterrorism and stability in the Mozambique Channel.

Why it mattersThe Mozambican government has resisted TotalEnergies' claim for about $4.5 billion in added costs on Mozambique LNG, a claim that the company says must be settled as the project proceeds.
