Mozambique signs $500 million development financing deal with UAE fund

The Abu Dhabi Fund for Development will finance priority projects that Mozambique's government identifies, and Mozambique becomes its 109th partner country.

Source: Club of Mozambique · 2026-11-16 · 1 min read

Mozambique and the Abu Dhabi Fund for Development (ADFD) signed a US$500 million agreement in Abu Dhabi, formalised at Emirates Palace during a UAE–Mozambique Business Roundtable. The signing coincided with the conclusion of negotiations on a Comprehensive Economic Partnership Agreement (CEPA) between the two countries.

Mohammed Saif Al Suwaidi, the fund's Director-General, and Basílio Zefanias Muhate, Mozambique's Minister of Economy, signed the deal. President Daniel Chapo and UAE Minister of Foreign Trade Thani bin Ahmed Al Zeyoudi attended the ceremony. Al Suwaidi said the financing will support Mozambique's priorities and strengthen key sectors.

Muhate called the agreement an important milestone in bilateral cooperation, opening access to the fund's financing for national priorities and development projects. ADFD said it directs its financing towards projects identified by partner governments.

Why it mattersProjects in Mozambique's priority sectors can now draw on the fund's financing, with the terms and timing of that money still to be set.
