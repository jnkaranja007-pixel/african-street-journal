Chapo pledges over three billion meticais to rebuild damaged public infrastructure

The investment is for public facilities destroyed during demonstrations that followed Mozambique’s 2024 general elections.

Source: Club of Mozambique · 2026-11-21 · 1 min read

Mozambican President Daniel Chapo has announced over three billion meticais for rebuilding public infrastructure destroyed during demonstrations after Mozambique’s 2024 general elections. The sum is about US$47 million at the current exchange rate.

Chapo announced the investment on Thursday during a visit to vandalised public facilities in Namacurra district. The money is earmarked for reconstruction of the damaged infrastructure, according to the president.

The announcement follows mass demonstrations linked to the 2024 elections. The reported investment covers public infrastructure, rather than a stated programme for private property.
