Mambinhas draw 2-2 with Australia, close to World Cup knockout stage

Mozambique's under-17 side took a two-goal first-half lead in Qatar and now stands virtually assured of a Round of 32 place.

Source: Club of Mozambique · 2026-11-26 · 1 min read

Australia and Mozambique shared four goals on Wednesday in the final Group C match of the FIFA U-17 World Cup in Qatar, a 2-2 draw that leaves the Mambinhas virtually assured of reaching the Round of 32.

Mozambique's side put itself two goals up in the first half before Australia pulled level, according to Club of Mozambique. The draw completed the team's group-stage fixtures.

The report did not give a formal confirmation of qualification, so the team's place in the knockout stage remains to be officially confirmed.

Why it mattersThe Mambinhas must await official confirmation of a Round of 32 place after completing Group C with a 2-2 draw.
