Supreme Court allows Graça Machel to be called in Mondlane trial

The defence of former presidential candidate Venâncio Mondlane sought her testimony in the case over violence during post-election protests.

Sources: Club of Mozambique, Carta de Mocambique and Zitamar News · 2026-11-25 · 1 min read

Mozambique’s Supreme Court on Tuesday granted the defence of former presidential candidate Venâncio Mondlane’s request to call social activist Graça Machel as a declarant in his ongoing trial over violence during protests.

The defence sought Machel’s appearance in the case concerning post-election demonstrations. Machel made public comments about the protests, including that their initial intent was legitimate, according to reporting by Carta de Mocambique.

Mondlane is also being questioned in court about alleged links between his calls for civil disobedience and violence. He has denied encouraging violence, Zitamar News reported.

Why it mattersMondlane’s defence must now prepare for Machel to appear in court as a declarant in the trial.
