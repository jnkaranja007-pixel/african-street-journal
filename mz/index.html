ExxonMobil awards $1.1 billion in Rovuma LNG equipment contracts

ExxonMobil Mozambique awarded approximately $1.1 billion in pre-investment contracts for long-term critical equipment for Phase 1 of the Rovuma LNG Project in Cabo Delgado. The company issued the contracts on behalf of Area 4 partners.

Why it mattersThe investment secures the long-lead equipment necessary to advance the development of the natural gas project.

BCI first-half profit falls to 3.3 billion meticais

Profit for Banco Comercial e de Investimentos fell to 3.3 billion meticais from 3.7 billion meticais in the first half of 2026 compared to the same period in 2025. The bank reported a spike in reserves due to high credit risk from non-performing loans.

Why it mattersIncreased reserves for bad loans have reduced the bank's net earnings for the first six months of the year.

Mozambique collects $43.83 million from Rovuma gas in first half

The Mozambican state collected $43.83 million from the production and export of liquefied natural gas from the Rovuma basin between January and June 2026. The figure is based on the Economic and Social Plan and State Budget (PESOE) balance.

Why it mattersThese funds increase the total state revenue from the extractive industry to $296.65 million.

Over 9,000 Mozambicans find work abroad since January

More than 9,000 citizens were placed in mining and farming sectors in South Africa since January. National Directorate of Migrant Labor Director Alice Brito reported that new recruitment is underway for Portugal and the United Arab Emirates.
