ExxonMobil awards $1.1 billion in Rovuma LNG equipment contracts

ExxonMobil Mozambique awarded approximately $1.1 billion in pre-investment contracts for long-term critical equipment. The contracts support Phase 1 of the Rovuma LNG project in Cabo Delgado province. The company acted on behalf of Area 4 partners.

Why it mattersThe procurement allows the project to move toward the development of the first phase of liquefied natural gas production.

Business · 08/21/2026

BCI first-half profit falls to 3.3 billion meticais

Profit for Banco Comercial e de Investimentos fell to 3.3 billion meticais from 3.7 billion meticais in the same period of 2025. The bank reported a spike in reserves due to high credit risk from non-performing loans. Figures cover January to June 2026.

Why it mattersIncreased reserves for bad loans have reduced the bank's overall profitability compared to last year.

Mozambique collects $43.83 million from Rovuma gas in first half

The state collected $43.83 million from the production and export of Rovuma liquefied natural gas between January and June 2026. Total collections rose to $296.65 million. Data comes from the Economic and Social Plan and State Budget (PESOE) balance.

Why it mattersThese funds provide direct revenue to the state budget from the extractive industry.

Over 9,000 Mozambicans find work abroad since January

More than 9,000 citizens were placed in the mining and farming sectors of South Africa since January. National Directorate of Migratory Labor head Alice Brito reported that new recruitment is underway for the UAE and Portugal.

Why it mattersThe program increases employment opportunities for Mozambicans in foreign labor markets.

News · 08/21/2026

INATRO removes intermediaries to speed up driver's license issuance

The National Institute of Road Transport introduced a new biometric license production mechanism on August 8. The change eliminates the intermediate platform that previously linked the systems. The agency integrated the involved computer systems to facilitate the process.

Why it mattersThe removal of the middle platform reduces the time required for citizens to receive their biometric licenses.
