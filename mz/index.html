Agriculture · 2026-11-10

Chapo launches 2026-27 agricultural campaign

President Daniel Chapo launched PROSPERA to connect producers with services, climate alerts, and markets.

President Daniel Chapo launched Mozambique's 2026-27 agricultural campaign on Monday in Lichinga, Niassa province, under the slogan “Let’s Produce to Feed Mozambique”. The campaign aims to boost production, enhance food security, and reduce import reliance. He also presented a National Seed Traceability and Labelling System to verify agricultural input authenticity.

During the event, seven cotton and oilseed producers received cheques totalling seven million meticais, with each producer getting one million meticais. The PROSPERA initiative was also launched to link producers with technical assistance, financing information, climate alerts, and market access. President Chapo stated PROSPERA is an inclusion instrument, not a financing programme.

The government initiative aims to increase domestic production, improve farming family incomes, and strengthen food security. President Chapo reaffirmed Niassa's potential as Mozambique's agricultural capital, highlighting its strengths in bean, maize, peanut, and macadamia cultivation.

Why it mattersMozambican farmers will benefit from increased production support, access to technical assistance, climate information, and markets through the PROSPERA initiative.

UK and UNU-WIDER fund climate finance programme in Mozambique

The FORÇA programme will strengthen Mozambique's ability to access and manage climate funds.

The United Kingdom and UNU-WIDER have signed a Memorandum of Understanding to launch the FORÇA programme in Mozambique. This initiative, led by Mozambique's Government through the Ministry of Planning and Development, aims to bolster the nation's capacity to mobilize and access climate finance. The UK's Foreign, Commonwealth & Development Office is providing funding for the programme, with UNU-WIDER acting as fund manager and implementation partner.

Mozambique faces significant risks from climate change, with cyclones, floods, and droughts impacting its economy by several percentage points of GDP annually. The FORÇA programme will support the National Directorate for Climate Finance by strengthening institutional capacity, providing technical expertise, and improving data systems. Louise Walker, Deputy High Commissioner at the British High Commission in Maputo, stated that the UK is backing Mozambique to build a reliable climate finance system.

The programme seeks to enhance Mozambique's ability to identify financing opportunities, coordinate stakeholders, and improve climate finance planning and implementation. UNU-WIDER's role includes providing financial and operational oversight, and working with the National Directorate for Climate Finance to embed new skills and systems. This initiative aims to build lasting national capacity for climate action and attract green investment by leveraging Mozambique's solar, wind, and water resources.

Why it mattersMozambique's institutions will gain skills and systems to access and manage climate finance, helping the country address climate change impacts and attract investment.

Business · 2026-11-10

Mozambique fuel retailers launch digital platform

The Association of Fuel Retailers of Mozambique (ARCOMOC) introduced a new digital system to connect operators across all provinces.

The Commercial and Investment Bank (BCI) hosted the official launch of ARCOMOC's new digital platform in Maputo on Monday, November 2, 2026. This initiative, developed with the Confederation of Economic Associations of Mozambique (CTA), aims to modernize Mozambique's fuel sector. The platform will link fuel operators in real-time from all provinces, simplifying information access and reducing bureaucracy.

BCI's Director of Public Relations, Heisler Castelo David, stated that digital transformation drives economic development and aligns with the bank's vision for Mozambique's progress. ARCOMOC President Luís Pereira highlighted the platform as a strategic tool to strengthen the association and improve communication among operators, focusing on innovation, commercial margins, supply security, and economic sustainability.

The Deputy National Director of Hydrocarbons and Fuels, José Muhai, affirmed the authorities' commitment to improving the regulatory environment through ongoing legal reforms. CTA Vice-President Amâncio Gume praised ARCOMOC's strategic vision and organizational capacity in adopting digitalization for business competitiveness.

Why it mattersFuel retailers and consumers in Mozambique will benefit from a more integrated, efficient, and competitive fuel sector due to the new digital platform.

Mozambique uses AI to boost child vaccinations

A platform called PAAD analyzes health data to identify areas needing more vaccines, aiming to reach children who have never been vaccinated.

A new artificial intelligence platform, PAAD, is helping Mozambique identify areas with low vaccination coverage to improve immunization rates. The Advanced Data Analytics Platform, developed by Zenysis Technologies with the Ministry of Health and Gavi, is expanding to over 80 districts across six provinces. This tool integrates data from more than 10 health systems, providing a unified view of vaccination indicators, vaccine availability, and service performance.

This initiative addresses the fact that 24% of Mozambican children are "zero dose," meaning they have never received routine vaccines, according to 2022-2023 data. Gabriel Risk Martins, director of partnerships at Zenysis, stated that PAAD offers teams a clearer picture of vaccination activity in one place. In Zambézia province, immunization official Alberto Jacenau found the platform simplified data analysis, allowing him to complete tasks in a fraction of the time previously needed with Excel.

Technological advancements coincide with improved vaccination indicators, with DTP3 vaccine coverage rising from 55% in 2022 to 70% in 2023, according to Unicef and WHO data. The PAAD technology was initially used in the response to Cyclone Idai in 2019, helping reduce cholera cases in Sofala province from about 400 daily to zero in under a month. Mozambique announced in September the expansion of PAAD from 15 to over 80 districts.

Politics · 2026-11-10

Parliament authorises government to revise Road Code

The assembly approved a legislative authorisation proposal to update the 2011 road code for modernisation and driver accountability.

Mozambique's Assembly of the Republic on Friday authorised the government to revise the Road Code, which has been in effect since 2011. The update aims to modernise the legal framework for road traffic, enhance safety, and increase driver accountability. The proposal was approved by consensus.

Minister of Transport and Logistics, João Matlhombe, stated the revision will update norms to match national and international traffic dynamics, technological advancements, and sustainable mobility needs. Proposed changes include stronger enforcement mechanisms, the introduction of surveillance cameras for traffic control, and specific regulations for electric, hybrid, and gas-powered vehicles.

The revision also plans to introduce a points-based driving license system and improve driver control, particularly for passenger and goods transport. Matlhombe emphasised the changes focus on safety, modernisation, and accountability, with stricter penalties for high-risk behaviour like drunk driving and mobile phone use while driving. Technology will be used to optimise safety and efficiency.

Why it mattersThe government is revising the Road Code to improve road safety and driver accountability, affecting all road users in Mozambique.
