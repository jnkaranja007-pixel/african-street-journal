ExxonMobil awards $1.1 billion in Rovuma LNG equipment contracts

ExxonMobil Mozambique awarded approximately $1.1 billion in pre-investment contracts for long-term critical equipment. The contracts support the development of Phase 1 of the Rovuma LNG Project in Cabo Delgado province.

Why it mattersThe investment advances the construction of the gas project, which aims to develop energy resources in the northern province.

BCI first-half profit falls to 3.3 billion meticais

Profit for Banco Comercial e de Investimentos fell to 3.3 billion meticais from 3.7 billion meticais in the same period in 2025. The bank reported that reserves increased due to high risks of bad credit.

Why it mattersHigher loan default risks have forced the bank to increase its reserves, reducing its overall profitability.

Over 9,000 Mozambicans find work abroad since January

More than 9,000 citizens were placed in the mining and farming sectors in South Africa since January. National Directorate of Migrant Labor Director Alice Brito said recruitment is also underway for Portugal and the UAE.

Why it mattersThe program increases the number of Mozambican workers accessing foreign wages in the agricultural and mining industries.

Sunderland offers €40 million for Geny Catamo

British club Sunderland offered a fixed sum of €30 million plus €10 million in variables for Geny Catamo. Sporting rejected the offer, though the British club indicated it would increase the bid.

Police commander and two foreigners detained for illegal mining

The police commander of Chinde district, a Portuguese citizen, and a Chinese citizen were detained Monday. Authorities suspect the three of the illegal exploitation of tantalite in Zambézia province.

Why it mattersThe arrests target the illegal extraction of minerals and involve a high-ranking local law enforcement official.
