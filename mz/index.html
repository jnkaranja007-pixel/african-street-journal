Insurgents kill 22 in Cabo Delgado farmland

Islamic State claimed 14 Christian victims in Muidumbe and Mueda districts over two weeks.

At least 22 people have died in the past two weeks in the farmland of Cabo Delgado's Muidumbe and Mueda districts. Islamic State claimed responsibility for 14 of these deaths, stating their victims were Christians. This follows a pattern where insurgents have exploited perceived passivity from defence forces since June, moving into new areas.

Insurgent groups have operated in districts like Montepuez and Niassa province, even attacking a hunting camp in September. Proximity to military positions offered no protection, as insurgents struck near Chai and Litamanda in Macomia, blocking the N380 road. The Local Force militia, integrated into the armed forces by a 2023 decree, faces ammunition shortages and delayed allowances, hindering their ability to counter enemies using drones.

Four women released by insurgents near Muatide reported that their captors used binoculars and a drone to evade Local Force patrols. The militia, equipped with rifles, is struggling to combat this advanced enemy.

Why it mattersCivilians in Cabo Delgado's Muidumbe and Mueda districts are directly affected by killings and the inability of local militias to provide security.

Mozambique police arrest sixth suspect in bishop's murder

A former soldier expelled for crimes is accused of carrying out the killing of Bishop Osório Citora Afonso.

Mozambican police presented a sixth suspect on November 12 in connection with the June 6 murder of Bishop Osório Citora Afonso. The man, a former member of the Defence and Security Forces expelled for crimes, was arrested on October 29 in Zambézia province. Authorities stated he is believed to be the individual who physically carried out the assassination using an AKM firearm.

Domingos Barone, head of SERNIC's Criminalistics Department in Zambézia, said the suspect, also a former border police officer, was monitored after evidence linked him to the crime. The police presented an AKM firearm, a magazine, and 23 rounds of ammunition, which they believe will help clarify the case. The suspect allegedly buried the weapon after the murder and fled to Zambia, returning two months later.

Barone also indicated that other suspects are at large, and efforts are underway to locate them. The arrested individual reportedly maintained friendly relations with another suspect believed to be the intellectual instigator of the crime. Investigations suggest financial transfers occurred between them in May and June 2026.

Why it mattersThe arrest of a sixth suspect, believed to be the direct perpetrator, brings Mozambican authorities closer to solving the murder of Bishop Osório Citora Afonso.

World Bank backs more flexible project financing in Mozambique

The bank says Mozambique must loosen its funding mechanisms so natural resources can turn into development, 42 years after ties began.

The World Bank has acknowledged that Mozambique needs to make its project financing mechanisms more flexible. The aim, the bank says, is to ensure the country's natural resources are converted into development for Mozambique.

The relationship between Mozambique and the World Bank officially began in 1984. 42 years later, the bank says many challenges remain, one of them being delays in the approval and release of project funds.

The packet does not give further figures, dates or reactions to the bank's position.

Why it mattersInvestors in Mozambique's natural resource projects are directly affected, because the World Bank is calling for changes to how those projects are financed.

Merec Industries builds first pasta plant in northern Mozambique

The Nacala plant will give Merec pasta production in all three regions of Mozambique, north, central and south, once it is complete.

Merec Industries, a subsidiary of Invictus Investment Company PLC, is building the first pasta manufacturing plant in northern Mozambique, in Nacala. Once it is complete, Merec will be the only company in Mozambique with pasta factories in the north, central and south of the country.

Together with an expansion of its Machava industrial facility in the south, the new plant will take Merec's total pasta capacity beyond national demand, so the country can meet its pasta needs from local production and export the surplus. Nampula Province Governor Eduardo Abdula visited the facilities to view construction progress.

Nacala has a deep-water port and is the gateway to the Nacala Logistics Corridor, which links Mozambique to Malawi and Zambia. Invictus Investment said Helios Fund V has agreed to acquire up to a 49% interest in Merec's holding company, with the deal expected to close by the end of 2026.

Why it mattersMerec's pasta production will reach all three regions of Mozambique, and Helios Fund V's agreement to acquire up to a 49% interest in Merec's holding company is expected to close by the end of 2026.

Maputo hosts 7th FNB Maputo 10K race

The annual race celebrates Maputo's 139th anniversary with a new route through city avenues.

Maputo will host the 7th edition of the FNB Maputo 10K- Corrida das Acácias on November 15. This annual sporting event is part of the city's anniversary celebrations.

This year, participants will run along major avenues for the first time, offering a new perspective of the city. Dennis Mbingo, FNB Moçambique's Administrator-Delegate, stated the race is an invitation to rediscover Maputo and feel its energy. Osvaldo Faquir, Maputo City Council's Councillor for Education, Culture and Sport, added that the race promotes healthy lifestyles and city pride.

The event, also known as the Corrida das Acácias, is a celebration of the city, community, and healthy living. Partners include the Maputo City Council and the Maputo City Athletics Association, with Coca-Cola Moçambique and Hollard Moçambique as official sponsors.

Why it mattersMaputo residents and visitors will participate in a city-wide running event celebrating the capital's anniversary and promoting healthy lifestyles.
