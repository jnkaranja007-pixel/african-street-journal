Mozambican boy, 15, brutally murdered; friend missing

The Sant’Egidio Community reported the discovery of Juvêncio's body with signs of extreme violence, raising fears of ritualistic practices.

The body of 15-year-old Juvêncio was found in the Matola River on Monday, November 9, bearing evident signs of extreme violence, according to Father Rolando Curzi of the Sant’Egidio Community. Juvêncio and his 12-year-old friend Ilton, who attended the Sant’Egidio Community’s Nutrition Centre and School of Peace in Matola, disappeared on Saturday, November 7. Fishermen discovered Juvêncio's body, and the nature of the mutilations has led to suspicions of magical-superstitious practices.

Ilton remains missing, and a search is ongoing in Matola, Father Curzi told Lusa. The Sant’Egidio Community is urging authorities to investigate the crime swiftly. This is the first such case involving children from the Community’s centre, though similar incidents have occurred involving minors, particularly boys with albinism. The Community runs the BRAVO! programme to register children at birth, making them visible and offering protection.

The Matola Nutrition Centre provides lunch to approximately 500 disadvantaged minors weekly, with over 100 receiving educational support at the School of Peace on Saturdays. Father Curzi stated the Community aims to raise civil society awareness to create a protection network for children. Similar cases involving minors, especially boys with albinism, have been reported previously.

Why it mattersThe Sant'Egidio Community is calling for swift investigation into the brutal murder of a 15-year-old boy and the ongoing disappearance of his 12-year-old friend.

Mozambique needs US$35 million to rebuild cancer services after fire

Maputo Central Hospital's oncology unit was damaged by a major fire on 1 November, and its radiotherapy director says the rebuild needs a digitalised facility and new equipment.

Mozambique needs about US$35 million to rebuild oncology services at Maputo Central Hospital, the country's largest healthcare facility, after a major fire on 1 November damaged them. Elias Guto, Director of the Radiotherapy Service, presented the preliminary figures in Maputo at a solidarity conference to raise financial, material and technical resources.

Guto said the largest costs will be infrastructure and equipment, since radiotherapy and nuclear medicine machines carry high initial prices. The estimate covers reconstruction, hospital security, and laboratory and diagnostic capacity in a new digitalised facility. The rebuild will also add two linear accelerators to meet demand for treatment.

The proposal includes clinical oncology, haematology, radiotherapy, paediatric oncology and nuclear medicine services. Guto said further training for staff is also needed so the hospital can provide more comprehensive care.

Why it mattersCancer patients who rely on Maputo Central Hospital will have to wait for the rebuilt services, and the hospital needs the funds and staff training to restore treatment capacity.

Mozambique Supreme Court trial uses new audio-visual recording

The Supreme Court in Maputo will begin the trial of Venâncio Mondlane on Tuesday, November 17, using new technology to record hearings.

The Supreme Court in Maputo will begin the trial of Venâncio Mondlane on Tuesday, November 17, using new audio and video recording technology for hearings. This system, which transcribes recordings into written documents, aims to significantly reduce trial length, according to Supreme Court judge-counsellor Pedro Nhatitima. The trial will take place in the criminal section of the Supreme Court, with daily sessions scheduled from 10:00 to 15:30 local time.

The case involves charges brought by the Public Prosecutor’s Office against Mondlane, a former presidential candidate who has not recognized the election results. The proceedings relate to post-election demonstrations between October 2024 and March 2025, during which over 400 people died in clashes with police and hundreds of businesses were vandalized. Mondlane faces charges including public glorification of crime and incitement to terrorism. The trial will hear from 12 prosecution witnesses, 20 defence witnesses, and two experts from mobile operators Vodacom and Tmcel.

The courtroom has a maximum capacity of 45 people and will include five Supreme Court judge-counsellors, two deputy attorneys-general, and legal representatives. Mondlane is standing trial before the Supreme Court because he is a member of the Council of State, which lifted his immunity.

Why it mattersVenâncio Mondlane faces charges of incitement to terrorism and public glorification of crime in a trial starting November 17.

Chapo seeks UAE investment during Abu Dhabi visit

President Daniel Chapo is in the United Arab Emirates on a three-day working visit from 13 to 15 November to strengthen ties and draw new investment.

Mozambique's President Daniel Chapo is in Abu Dhabi, in the United Arab Emirates, for a three-day working visit that runs from 13 to 15 November. The trip is aimed at strengthening bilateral cooperation and attracting new investment to Mozambique.

Three legal instruments in the economic sphere are to be signed during the visit. The agreements are meant to deepen economic partnership between the two countries.

The visit is expected to shape the terms of future Emirati investment in Mozambique.

Politics · 2026-11-15

Frelimo lays first stone for Mavalane water supply system

The ruling party's local branch in Maputo's KaMavota district has begun building a system expected to serve more than 60 families across four neighbourhoods.

The Frelimo party, at the level of the Mavalane zone in KaMavota municipal district in Maputo city, laid the first stone on Saturday for a water supply system. The project is expected to benefit more than 60 families in four neighbourhoods.

The launch comes as residents in the area struggle to get safe drinking water. The party's local branch organised the event, which marks the start of construction.

The report does not give a completion date or cost for the system.

Why it mattersFamilies in four neighbourhoods of Mavalane are expected to receive a piped water connection once the system is built.
