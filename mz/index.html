Mozambique used car prices rise again in October

Food and non-alcoholic drinks also saw price increases for the second month running.

Used car prices in Mozambique rose by 5.4% in October, marking the second consecutive monthly increase. This contributed to a broader inflation rate of 0.48% for the month across eight major cities and Inhambane province. The National Institute of Statistics (INE) reported that fresh fish, restaurant meals, onions, lettuce, and tomatoes also saw price hikes.

The INE noted that while most prices increased, charcoal and pumpkin leaves decreased, slightly offsetting the overall rise. From January to October, the general price level in the country increased by 5.50%, with transport and food and non-alcoholic beverages being the biggest contributors. Across all collection centres, prices increased in October, with Inhambane province seeing the highest rise at 1.18%.

Year-on-year, October prices were 6.32% higher than in the previous year. The transport division saw the largest annual increase at 15.06%, followed by food and non-alcoholic beverages at 8.56%. Tete city recorded the highest year-on-year price increase at 9.20%.

Why it mattersConsumers in Mozambique face higher prices for essential goods and second-hand vehicles, with transport costs rising significantly.

LAM warns of flight changes and cancellations over fuel shortage

The national airline says temporary limits on supplies of Jet A-1 aviation fuel could change its timetables and reduce its flights in the coming days.

Linhas Aéreas de Moçambique (LAM), Mozambique's national airline, has warned passengers that its schedules may change, that it may run fewer flights and that some flights may be cancelled in the coming days.

The warning follows temporary constraints in the supply of Jet A-1, the aviation fuel the airline uses. LAM set out the situation in a statement addressed to passengers, partners and the general public.

The airline's statement, as reported, gives no date for when the fuel supply is expected to return to normal.

Why it mattersPassengers booked on LAM flights will face schedule changes, fewer flights and possible cancellations while the Jet A-1 supply is constrained.

Mozambique signs $500 million development financing deal with UAE fund

The Abu Dhabi Fund for Development will finance priority projects that Mozambique's government identifies, and Mozambique becomes its 109th partner country.

Mozambique and the Abu Dhabi Fund for Development (ADFD) signed a US$500 million agreement in Abu Dhabi, formalised at Emirates Palace during a UAE–Mozambique Business Roundtable. The signing coincided with the conclusion of negotiations on a Comprehensive Economic Partnership Agreement (CEPA) between the two countries.

Mohammed Saif Al Suwaidi, the fund's Director-General, and Basílio Zefanias Muhate, Mozambique's Minister of Economy, signed the deal. President Daniel Chapo and UAE Minister of Foreign Trade Thani bin Ahmed Al Zeyoudi attended the ceremony. Al Suwaidi said the financing will support Mozambique's priorities and strengthen key sectors.

Muhate called the agreement an important milestone in bilateral cooperation, opening access to the fund's financing for national priorities and development projects. ADFD said it directs its financing towards projects identified by partner governments.

Why it mattersProjects in Mozambique's priority sectors can now draw on the fund's financing, with the terms and timing of that money still to be set.

Mondlane treason trial begins Tuesday

Supreme Court sets aside seats for press and civil society in the treason trial of opposition leader Venâncio Mondlane.

The Supreme Court in Mozambique will begin the treason trial of opposition leader Venâncio Mondlane on Tuesday. Supreme Court judge Pedro Nhatitima announced on Friday that press and civil society will have guaranteed seats in the 45-person courtroom. The Centre for Public Integrity (CIP) and the Centre for Democracy and Human Rights (CDD) will each have seats, alongside 10 media organisations including LUSA. Mondlane will have 15 seats for his supporters, and the Bar Association will have two.

A new recording and transcription system will be used to speed up the trial, Nhatitima stated, aiming to reduce its length. Image and sound will be recorded and transcribed into written documents, a departure from the manual recording of previous large trials. However, the trial will not be broadcast live, and recording or photographing by the press or public will not be permitted. 10 seats will be available to the general public, requiring prior registration.

President Daniel Chapo has been named the Frelimo presidential candidate for 2029, an early announcement indicating party backing. This decision was made by the Frelimo Central Committee on August 26 and announced by Secretary General Chakil Aboobacar on October 18, following a meeting of the powerful Frelimo Political Commission. Chapo faces three tasks: maintaining Frelimo's political control, combating corruption, and securing international support for Mozambique.

Why it mattersOpposition leader Venâncio Mondlane faces a treason trial starting Tuesday, with limited public access to the courtroom.

Millennium bim donates food to Casa do Gaiato in Boane

The bank's donation supports a Maputo province institution that serves more than 300 meals every day to children, young people and staff.

Millennium bim has donated essential food supplies to Casa do Gaiato, which is located in Boane district, Maputo province. The institution cares for vulnerable children and young people and supports their education and training.

Rui Pedro, Chairperson of Millennium bim's Executive Committee, said the bank wanted to make a practical contribution to the institution's needs and to thank those who look after the children every day. Casa do Gaiato serves more than 300 meals a day to residents, pupils at its school and staff.

The donation is part of the bank's corporate social responsibility programme, "Mais Moçambique Pra Mim", which covers education, health, the environment, financial literacy, youth sport and social solidarity. In 2021, the bank also donated an irrigation system for the institution's farm.

Why it mattersResidents, pupils and staff at Casa do Gaiato receive food support for their daily meals.
