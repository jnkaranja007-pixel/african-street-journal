Mozambique Supreme Court begins questioning Venâncio Mondlane

The former presidential candidate faces five charges, including incitement to terrorism, in the court’s evidentiary stage.

Sources: Club of Mozambique and Noticias Lusofonas · 2026-11-19 · 1 min read

Venâncio Mondlane, a former presidential candidate in Mozambique, began answering questions on Wednesday afternoon as the evidentiary stage of his trial opened at the Criminal Section of the Supreme Court. The court is Mozambique’s highest judicial body.

The stage requires Mondlane to respond to questions from the court and prosecution, according to the report. He is charged with five crimes, including incitement to terrorism.

The trial’s first stage began on Wednesday; the report identifies no further hearing date.
