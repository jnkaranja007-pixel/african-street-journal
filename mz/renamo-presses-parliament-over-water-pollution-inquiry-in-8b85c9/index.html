Renamo presses parliament over water pollution inquiry in Moma and Larde

The opposition movement renewed its call for a parliamentary commission after a request filed about 14 days ago received no response.

Source: Club of Mozambique · 2026-11-20 · 1 min read

Renamo, formerly Mozambique's main opposition movement, has demanded that parliament respond to its request for a Parliamentary Commission of Inquiry into suspected water pollution in Moma and Larde districts, in northern Mozambique's Nampula province.

The request was submitted about 14 days ago, and the party has renewed its demand for a reply. The report does not say that parliament has approved the commission or set a date for a decision.

The party's renewed demand is the latest step in its effort to have parliament take up the matter.

Why it mattersParliament's answer decides whether an official inquiry into the water in Moma and Larde goes ahead.
