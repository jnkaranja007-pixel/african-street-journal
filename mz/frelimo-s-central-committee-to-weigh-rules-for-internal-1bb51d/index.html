Frelimo's Central Committee to weigh rules for internal elections and member conduct

The ruling party's Central Committee meets in Maputo province to review proposals for its 13th Congress, ahead of a code of conduct for members.

Source: O Pais · 2026-11-26 · 1 min read

Mozambique's ruling Frelimo party will hold an extraordinary session of its Central Committee on Friday and Saturday in Maputo province. The meeting will review proposals for the party's 13th Congress.

The committee is due to examine proposed theses for the congress, a directive on internal party elections and a code of conduct and ethics for party members. Party spokesperson Pedro […] announced the session in Maputo on Thursday.

The announcement did not give further details on the proposals or a date for the congress.

Why it mattersParty members will be bound by a new code of conduct and elect their internal leaders under the rules the committee approves this weekend.
