Mondlane tells court “take power” was meant to mobilise public

The Mozambican politician said the phrase referred to popular power and people’s ability to influence decisions.

Source: Club of Mozambique · 2026-11-22 · 1 min read

Mozambican politician Venâncio Mondlane defended his use of the expression “take power” in court on Friday, saying he intended it to mobilise the public and use its ability to influence decisions.

Mondlane said the phrase, “the people must take power”, was meant to justify the strength of popular power and show that people could change fundamental decisions.

The account of his court explanation did not identify a ruling or further step in the case.

Why it mattersMondlane has to defend the wording of his public statements before a court, which puts his political speech itself under legal scrutiny.
