Mozambique's criminal investigation service seizes 334kg of methamphetamine in Vilankulo

Two Mozambican nationals were arrested after the drugs were found packed in 257 plastic boxes in a Mahindra vehicle in Inhambane province.

Source: Club of Mozambique · 2026-11-26 · 1 min read

The National Criminal Investigation Service, known as SERNIC, seized 334 kilogrammes of methamphetamine in the early hours of Wednesday in Phambara, in Vilankulo district of Inhambane province. Two Mozambican nationals were arrested in the operation.

The drugs were packed in 257 tigelas, or plastic boxes, and were being transported in a Mahindra vehicle when officers intercepted them. The seizure is one of the larger methamphetamine hauls recorded by the service in the province.

Investigators are expected to question the two suspects and trace where the consignment was headed. No further details about the route or buyers were reported in the initial account.

Why it mattersThe arrests and the seizure mean the two suspects now face criminal charges in Inhambane province, and investigators must establish where the 334 kilogrammes were bound.
