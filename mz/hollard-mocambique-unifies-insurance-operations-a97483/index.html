Hollard Moçambique unifies insurance operations

The company now operates as a single entity, aiming to expand financial protection to more Mozambicans.

Sources: Carta de Mocambique and O Pais · 2026-11-06 · 1 min read

Hollard Moçambique has completed its merger of Hollard Seguros, Hollard Vida, and Global Alliance, consolidating operations under one brand. This unification allows the insurer to offer all non-life and life insurance branches, alongside pension fund management services, under a single identity.

The company's CEO, Henri Mittermayer, stated the ambition is to strengthen its presence and extend insurance protection to more Mozambicans. Hollard Moçambique plans to develop inclusive solutions, including agricultural insurance, education plans, and digital motor liability insurance, to reach individuals and small businesses with limited financial protection.

Hollard Moçambique will combine local market knowledge with an international network, supported by Tokio Marine. The strategy includes enhancing customer experience and investing in digital innovation to improve efficiency and claims processes, aiming to increase insurance sector participation in Mozambique.

Why it mattersMozambicans will have broader access to insurance products and financial services following the merger.
