Mozambique police urge calm before Mondlane trial

The trial of Venâncio Mondlane, a political figure, is due to open on Tuesday at the Supreme Court in Maputo.

Source: Club of Mozambique · 2026-11-17 · 1 min read

Mozambican police on Monday asked the public to follow the trial of Venâncio Mondlane and to remain calm. The General Command of the Police issued a statement urging respect for public order and the legal limits on the right to protest.

The trial was due to open on Tuesday at the Supreme Court in Maputo, the country's highest court. The police statement set out the legal limits on protest and asked citizens to avoid anything that could disrupt the proceedings.

The statement did not say what measures the police would take if protests occurred during the trial.

Why it mattersProtesters and spectators planning to attend or demonstrate outside the court now face police warnings about the legal limits on protest.
