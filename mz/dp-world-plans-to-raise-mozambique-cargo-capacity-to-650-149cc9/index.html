DP World plans to raise Mozambique cargo capacity to 650,000 TEU

The company told President Daniel Chapo it wants to lift handling capacity from 225,000 to approximately 650,000 TEU by the second quarter of 2027.

Source: O Pais · 2026-11-18 · 1 min read

DP World plans to increase its cargo-handling capacity in Mozambique from 225,000 to approximately 650,000 TEU by the second quarter of 2027. The company presented the plan to President Daniel Chapo at a meeting in Maputo on Tuesday.

The proposed expansion would raise capacity by more than doubling it, according to the figures presented to Chapo. The report identifies the meeting’s location and participants but gives no further details about the investment or how the additional capacity would be delivered.

The meeting also addressed another subject, but no details were provided. The capacity target is scheduled for the second quarter of 2027.
