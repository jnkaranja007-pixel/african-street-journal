Mozambique needs US$35 million to rebuild cancer services after fire

Maputo Central Hospital's oncology unit was damaged by a major fire on 1 November, and its radiotherapy director says the rebuild needs a digitalised facility and new equipment.

Source: Club of Mozambique · 2026-11-15 · 1 min read

Mozambique needs about US$35 million to rebuild oncology services at Maputo Central Hospital, the country's largest healthcare facility, after a major fire on 1 November damaged them. Elias Guto, Director of the Radiotherapy Service, presented the preliminary figures in Maputo at a solidarity conference to raise financial, material and technical resources.

Guto said the largest costs will be infrastructure and equipment, since radiotherapy and nuclear medicine machines carry high initial prices. The estimate covers reconstruction, hospital security, and laboratory and diagnostic capacity in a new digitalised facility. The rebuild will also add two linear accelerators to meet demand for treatment.

The proposal includes clinical oncology, haematology, radiotherapy, paediatric oncology and nuclear medicine services. Guto said further training for staff is also needed so the hospital can provide more comprehensive care.

Why it mattersCancer patients who rely on Maputo Central Hospital will have to wait for the rebuilt services, and the hospital needs the funds and staff training to restore treatment capacity.
