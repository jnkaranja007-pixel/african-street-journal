Frelimo lays first stone for Mavalane water supply system

The ruling party's local branch in Maputo's KaMavota district has begun building a system expected to serve more than 60 families across four neighbourhoods.

Source: O Pais · 2026-11-15 · 1 min read

The Frelimo party, at the level of the Mavalane zone in KaMavota municipal district in Maputo city, laid the first stone on Saturday for a water supply system. The project is expected to benefit more than 60 families in four neighbourhoods.

The launch comes as residents in the area struggle to get safe drinking water. The party's local branch organised the event, which marks the start of construction.

The report does not give a completion date or cost for the system.

Why it mattersFamilies in four neighbourhoods of Mavalane are expected to receive a piped water connection once the system is built.
