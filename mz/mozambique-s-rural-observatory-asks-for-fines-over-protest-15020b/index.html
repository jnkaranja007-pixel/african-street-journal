Mozambique's rural observatory asks for fines over protest film to be dropped

The OMR says the penalties, set by the state culture institute, are equal to 200 public-service minimum wages.

Source: O Pais · 2026-11-24 · 1 min read

The Observatório do Meio Rural (OMR), a Mozambican rural research group, asked on Monday for the revocation of fines equal to 200 public-service minimum wages. The penalties were imposed over a documentary about post-election protests, which the authorities say was filmed without authorisation.

The fines were applied by the National Institute of Cultural and Creative Industries (INICC), according to the Portuguese news agency Lusa. The OMR is contesting them.

The report did not give the amount in meticais or say what the institute will do next.

Why it mattersFilmmakers and the observatory must now fight penalties equal to 200 public-service minimum wages, and the OMR is asking for them to be revoked.
