Chapo seeks UAE investment during Abu Dhabi visit

President Daniel Chapo is in the United Arab Emirates on a three-day working visit from 13 to 15 November to strengthen ties and draw new investment.

Source: O Pais · 2026-11-15 · 1 min read

Mozambique's President Daniel Chapo is in Abu Dhabi, in the United Arab Emirates, for a three-day working visit that runs from 13 to 15 November. The trip is aimed at strengthening bilateral cooperation and attracting new investment to Mozambique.

Three legal instruments in the economic sphere are to be signed during the visit. The agreements are meant to deepen economic partnership between the two countries.

The visit is expected to shape the terms of future Emirati investment in Mozambique.
