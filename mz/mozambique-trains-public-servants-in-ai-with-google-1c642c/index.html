Mozambique trains public servants in AI with Google

The U.S. and Mozambican governments partnered with Google for a one-day training on Super Intelligence and cloud computing.

Source: Club of Mozambique · 2026-10-29 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Mozambican public servants received training in Super Intelligence (SI) and cloud computing in Maputo on October 29, 2026. The U.S. and Mozambican governments collaborated with Google for the one-day program, which aimed to enhance digital skills within the public sector.

Chargé d’Affaires Abigail L. Dressel and Minister Américo Muchanga attended the opening of the training, emphasizing U.S.-Mozambique cooperation. Joylynn Kirui, UpSkill Universe’s lead trainer and Super Intelligence specialist, led sessions covering SI opportunities in Africa, cloud infrastructure, data policies, and public administration applications.

The training included case studies, discussions, and hands-on exercises to explore SI applications, alongside addressing data protection, privacy, and necessary safeguards for responsible technology use.

Why it mattersMozambican public servants are being trained to use new technologies like Super Intelligence and cloud computing in their work.
