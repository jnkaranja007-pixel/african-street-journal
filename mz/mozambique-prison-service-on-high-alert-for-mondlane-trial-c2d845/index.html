Mozambique prison service on high alert for Mondlane trial

The state is preparing for unrest on November 16, the day before Venâncio Mondlane's trial begins.

Source: Zitamar News · 2026-10-23 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Mozambique's prison service, SERNAP, has ordered maximum combat readiness across all facilities from November 16, the day before Venâncio Mondlane's trial starts. This directive specifically addresses prisons affected by incursions and escapes during the 2024 unrest, with guards redeployed from administrative duties and external patrols strengthened. SERNAP will maintain coordination with police and military authorities to protect prisoners, staff, and neighbouring communities, while also requiring proportionality and respect for human rights.

Venâncio Mondlane stated in an interview on October 18 that he would not call his supporters to the streets if convicted. The Council of State suspended Mondlane’s immunity on June 10 to allow the criminal case to proceed at the Supreme Court. Mondlane argues this decision is null, as the Council can suspend a member for criminal proceedings but cannot lift immunity.

Amnesty International's April 2025 report, Protest Under Attack, documented reckless and unlawful force, mass arbitrary arrests, and deliberate restrictions on information during the post-election crackdown. The civil society platform DECIDE counted around 315 deaths between October 2024 and January 2025.

Why it mattersThe state is preparing for potential unrest and taking security measures around Venâncio Mondlane's trial.
