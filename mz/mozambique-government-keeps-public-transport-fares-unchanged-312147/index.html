Mozambique government keeps public transport fares unchanged

The government and FEMATRO agreed on a temporary compensation mechanism for transport operators.

Source: Club of Mozambique · 2026-11-08 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Public transport fares in Greater Maputo will remain unchanged after the government and FEMATRO reached an agreement. National Director of Transport and Safety Alberto Matusse gave the assurance that passengers can continue using services at current rates.

To maintain fares, the government will implement an extraordinary compensation mechanism for transport operators. Payments will range from 25,000 to 90,000 meticais, varying by vehicle type and operational needs, Matusse explained.

This measure is temporary, lasting for three months, during which the government and operators will monitor its implementation. They will assess results and analyze conditions for the sustainability of public transport services in Maputo City, Matola, Boane, and Marracuene.

Why it mattersResidents of Greater Maputo will not face increased public transport costs.
