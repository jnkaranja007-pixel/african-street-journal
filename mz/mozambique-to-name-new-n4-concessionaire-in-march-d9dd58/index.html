Mozambique to name new N4 concessionaire in March

Transport Minister Matlombe says three companies are competing, while the terms for Maputo's toll remain unresolved.

Source: Carta de Mocambique · 2026-11-23 · 1 min read

Mozambique’s government plans to announce the new concessionaire for the N4, linking Maputo and Witbank, in March of next year. Transport Minister Matlombe said three companies are competing for the contract, including the current operator, Trans African Concessions (TRAC).

Matlombe did not name the other two bidders or disclose their offers or the proposed concession model, according to Carta de Moçambique. The outlet’s report asks what will happen to the Maputo toll, including whether it will remain under the new concession and how its charge will be set.

TRAC says it has invested 65 biliões de randes since 1997, with 40% on Mozambique’s side. The report says the company has not disclosed its profit; it also asks how much the Maputo toll has collected and whether it has repaid the investment in the Ceres/EN1 section.

Why it mattersN4 users at the Maputo toll still lack disclosed terms for the charge they pay, while the government is due to name the concessionaire in March of next year.
