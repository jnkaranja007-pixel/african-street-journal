Nampula court suspends Praça do Metical construction

Civil society groups opposed the project's use of public parkland, prompting a legal review.

Source: Club of Mozambique · 2026-10-30 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

A court in Nampula has suspended construction work on Praça do Metical. The suspension came after civil society organisations opposed the project's location in the Jardim-Parque Popular. These groups argued that public spaces should remain accessible for residents' enjoyment.

The court's decision aims to halt work while it examines the project's compliance with urban space regulations. This review seeks to ensure the restoration of legality and the safeguarding of public interest. The construction had faced opposition shortly after the foundation stone was laid.

The legal examination will focus on the occupation and use of urban spaces. The court's ruling prevents further work until these issues are resolved.

Why it mattersThe construction of Praça do Metical in Nampula has been halted by a court order due to public opposition over the use of a park.
