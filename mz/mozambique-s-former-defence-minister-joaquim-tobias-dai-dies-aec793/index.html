Mozambique's former defence minister Joaquim Tobias Dai dies

Dai served as minister from 2000 to 2008 and was a veteran of the National Liberation Struggle.

Source: Club of Mozambique · 2026-11-03 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Former Mozambique Defence Minister Joaquim Tobias Dai died on Monday at the age of 75, reportedly from illness. Dai, a veteran of the National Liberation Struggle, served as Minister of National Defence from January 2000 to March 2008.

He held the defence portfolio under Presidents Joaquim Chissano and Armando Guebuza, who was his brother-in-law. Dai also served as a member of parliament during his career in public service. The President of the Republic issued a condolence message.

President Daniel Chapo highlighted Dai’s contributions to national sovereignty and independence in his message. The presidency stated that Dai dedicated part of his life to serving the nation. Condolences were extended to his family, friends, and comrades.

Why it mattersThe death of Joaquim Tobias Dai affects his family, friends, and former colleagues who are offered condolences.
