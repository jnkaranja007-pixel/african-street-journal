Mozambique launches mining company with 850 million meticais

The state-owned ENM is tasked with managing strategic minerals and taking part in their exploration, processing and sale.

Source: Club of Mozambique · 2026-11-21 · 1 min read

Mozambique is launching its National Mining Company, known by its Portuguese initials ENM, with 850 million meticais in public capital, according to the document setting out the company’s statutes. The funding is equivalent to around €11.5 million.

The statutes define ENM’s role as managing strategic minerals and ensuring the state participates directly in the exploration, processing, marketing and development of mineral resources. The company’s launch gives the government an institution with a stated mandate across those stages.

The statutes are the source of the capital and mandate details; the report provides no further operating timetable or named mineral projects.

Why it mattersThe state must deploy 850 million meticais of public capital through ENM, whose mandate covers exploration, processing, marketing and mineral development.
