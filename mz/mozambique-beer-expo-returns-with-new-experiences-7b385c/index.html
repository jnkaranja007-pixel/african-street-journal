Mozambique Beer Expo returns with new experiences

Maputo will host the annual beer festival on October 24, featuring gastronomy, music, and industry insights.

Source: Carta de Mocambique · 2026-10-22 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

The Mozambique Beer Expo is returning to Maputo on October 24 at Repinga, starting at 12:00 PM. The event promises new experiences, surprises, and a celebration of beer culture, blending entertainment, knowledge, gastronomy, and music. This year's expo aligns with the global trend of daytime events, offering a relaxed outdoor atmosphere for attendees to enjoy lunch and an afternoon of discovery.

The festival begins on October 23 with an Industry Day at the JFS Corporate Tower in downtown Maputo, part of the Festival Maputo Fast Forward. This session will feature a panel discussion with industry representatives addressing challenges, innovation, and growth opportunities in Mozambique's brewing sector. The main expo on October 24 will include special beer tastings, interactive experiences, lounge areas, and diverse food options, with music provided by DJs and a samba group.

Organized jointly by the Maputo Municipal Council, ANDITUR, Revista Xonguila, Malta do Vinho, and Connoisseur Prestige Club, with production by Arena Eventos, the expo aims to promote tourism and boost the local economy. The brewing industry is significant for Mozambique, with two major production units in the Maputo region creating jobs and contributing to state revenue.

Why it mattersThe beer expo affects local businesses by promoting tourism and the brewing industry, creating jobs and revenue.
