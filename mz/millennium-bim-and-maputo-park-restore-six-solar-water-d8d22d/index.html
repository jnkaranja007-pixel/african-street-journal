Millennium bim and Maputo park restore six solar water systems

The systems serve 800 to 930 people in six communities and support vegetable growing on about 1.75 hectares.

Source: Carta de Mocambique · 2026-11-19 · 1 min read

Millennium bim and Maputo National Park have rehabilitated six solar-powered water systems serving between 800 and 930 people in communities around the park in Mozambique. The partnership was established in 2025.

The park says most communities’ water-collection distance has fallen from more than two kilometres to less than 500 metres. The time needed to obtain water has dropped from two to three hours to about 20 to 30 minutes. In Massoane and Tchia, the supply also supports vegetable production on about 1.75 hectares combined.

Six community water-management committees have 74 members, including 40 men and 34 women. Their training in management, system operation, hygiene and accountability is nearing completion; each committee has elected leaders, statutes and internal rules.

Why it mattersFamilies in six communities spend about 20 to 30 minutes collecting water instead of two to three hours, and 74 committee members are completing management training.
