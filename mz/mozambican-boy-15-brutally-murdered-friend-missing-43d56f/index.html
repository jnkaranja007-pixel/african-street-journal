Mozambican boy, 15, brutally murdered; friend missing

The Sant’Egidio Community reported the discovery of Juvêncio's body with signs of extreme violence, raising fears of ritualistic practices.

Source: Club of Mozambique · 2026-11-15 · 1 min read

The body of 15-year-old Juvêncio was found in the Matola River on Monday, November 9, bearing evident signs of extreme violence, according to Father Rolando Curzi of the Sant’Egidio Community. Juvêncio and his 12-year-old friend Ilton, who attended the Sant’Egidio Community’s Nutrition Centre and School of Peace in Matola, disappeared on Saturday, November 7. Fishermen discovered Juvêncio's body, and the nature of the mutilations has led to suspicions of magical-superstitious practices.

Ilton remains missing, and a search is ongoing in Matola, Father Curzi told Lusa. The Sant’Egidio Community is urging authorities to investigate the crime swiftly. This is the first such case involving children from the Community’s centre, though similar incidents have occurred involving minors, particularly boys with albinism. The Community runs the BRAVO! programme to register children at birth, making them visible and offering protection.

The Matola Nutrition Centre provides lunch to approximately 500 disadvantaged minors weekly, with over 100 receiving educational support at the School of Peace on Saturdays. Father Curzi stated the Community aims to raise civil society awareness to create a protection network for children. Similar cases involving minors, especially boys with albinism, have been reported previously.

Why it mattersThe Sant'Egidio Community is calling for swift investigation into the brutal murder of a 15-year-old boy and the ongoing disappearance of his 12-year-old friend.
