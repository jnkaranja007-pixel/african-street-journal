Guro opens 25 de Junho primary school and a supermarket

Mayor Latifo Vinho opened both facilities, with the school costing 5.8 million meticais to put up.

Source: Club of Mozambique · 2026-11-18 · 1 min read

Mayor Latifo Vinho has inaugurated a new primary school and a supermarket in the municipality of Guro, Mozambique, adding both to its local infrastructure. The moves are being presented as the delivery of campaign pledges the mayor made just over two years ago.

The 25 de Junho Primary School was raised from the ground up for 5.8 million meticais, according to Club of Mozambique. The report names the supermarket as the second new facility but gives no cost for it.

The report does not give the school's student capacity, an opening date or any further details about the supermarket.
