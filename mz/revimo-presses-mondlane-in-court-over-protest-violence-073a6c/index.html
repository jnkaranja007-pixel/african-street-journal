Revimo presses Mondlane in court over protest violence

The toll-road operator's lawyer challenged the opposition politician over infrastructure damage and police killings during the post-election unrest.

Source: Zitamar News · 2026-11-24 · 1 min read

On the fifth day of his Supreme Court trial on Monday, Mozambique's toll-road operator Revimo pressed Venâncio Mondlane on whether his calls for civil disobedience were tied to violence during the post-election protests.

Rodrigo Rocha, a lawyer for Revimo, which has joined the case in support of the prosecution, asked Mondlane about destroyed infrastructure and the killing of police officers. Mondlane denied encouraging violence, saying his statements were political warnings and calls for peaceful resistance, not instructions to commit crimes.

Rocha said at least 59 mobile telecommunications towers were destroyed during the unrest, giving a measure of damage to Mozambique's communications infrastructure.

Why it mattersRevimo's lawyer has put the destruction of at least 59 mobile towers before the court as Mondlane answers questions about the unrest.
