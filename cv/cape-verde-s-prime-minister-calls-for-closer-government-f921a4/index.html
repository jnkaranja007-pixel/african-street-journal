Cape Verde’s prime minister calls for closer government-business ties

Francisco Carvalho said the government is reviewing industrial policy and trade laws to support local production and make Cape Verdean goods more competitive.

Source: Mindel Insite · 2026-11-20 · 1 min read

Cape Verde’s Prime Minister Francisco Carvalho called for closer cooperation between government and businesses at the opening of the Cape Verde International Fair (FIC) in São Vicente on Thursday. He said companies need better access to energy, water and finance, simpler licensing and links between universities and businesses to grow and compete abroad.

Carvalho said the government is reviewing industrial policy and trade legislation to support domestic production, industrial processing and the competitiveness of Cape Verdean products. He also urged faster resolution of business disputes, saying investors need clear rules and respected rights. FIC managing director Angélica Fortes said the fair promotes Cape Verdean products and services and creates business opportunities.

Carvalho said the government wanted to hear from entrepreneurs and work with them on solutions. He called for a more diversified economy that produces, processes and exports goods.

Why it mattersBusinesses seeking to invest or export need simpler licensing and clearer rules as the government reviews industrial policy and trade legislation.
