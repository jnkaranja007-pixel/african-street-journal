Cape Verde’s sea institute takes up fishing-device rules and emergency plan

IMar will present proposed rules for fish-aggregating devices and seek feedback on a maritime alert plan for artisanal fishing in Praia.

Source: Mindel Insite · 2026-11-27 · 1 min read

Cape Verde’s Institute of the Sea (IMar) will present proposed regulations for fish-aggregating devices at a workshop in Praia on 4 December. The institute will also discuss an emergency alert plan for rough seas affecting artisanal fishing.

IMar says experience with the devices has shown a need for clear rules to reduce disputes among fishing operators and manage marine resources. The plan is intended to improve prevention, alerts and risk management during extreme sea conditions and climate change, protecting fishers, coastal infrastructure and fishing communities.

The meeting at Hotel Vista, in Praia’s Achada Santo António area, will bring together public bodies, fishing groups, development partners and researchers to debate the proposals and offer changes. The work is part of a project implemented through an agreement between LuxDev and IMar.

Why it mattersArtisanal fishing operators are being asked to contribute to device rules intended to reduce disputes, while fishers and coastal communities are the plan’s intended beneficiaries.
