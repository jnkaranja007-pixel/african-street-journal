Dino D’Santiago wins Latin Grammy for Brazil-Africa music project

The Cape Verdean musician accepted the award for Best Brazilian Popular Music/Afro-Portuguese Music Album with Brazilian artists Criolo and Amaro Freitas.

Sources: Mindel Insite and A Nacao · 2026-11-15 · 1 min read

Musician Dino D’Santiago won a Latin Grammy on Thursday, November 12, for Best Brazilian Popular Music/Afro-Portuguese Music Album. He shared the award for his project with Brazilian artists Criolo and Amaro Freitas, accepting the prize in Las Vegas, USA. The collaboration aims to connect cultures and sounds across the Atlantic.

During his acceptance speech, D’Santiago highlighted his role in bringing music from Lusophone countries like Cape Verde, Angola, Mozambique, Guinea-Bissau, São Tomé and Príncipe, and Portugal to a global audience. The project originated from the 2024 song “Esperança,” a collaboration with Criolo, Amaro Freitas, and producer Nave, which also earned him a Grammy nomination that year. The album features 12 tracks blending genres such as rap, Brazilian popular music, jazz, morna, and funaná.

The trio's collaboration has been showcased internationally, including performances at festivals like Primavera Sound Porto, MEO Kalorama, and Rock in Rio during the summer of 2026. Further performances are scheduled for 2027, including a slot at Lollapalooza in Brazil in March. This Grammy win further cements D’Santiago's international recognition and the ability of Cape Verdean and Lusophone music to engage diverse audiences and regions.

Why it mattersDino D’Santiago, a Cape Verdean musician, won a Latin Grammy for an album that blends music from Lusophone countries and Brazil, showcasing international cultural exchange.
