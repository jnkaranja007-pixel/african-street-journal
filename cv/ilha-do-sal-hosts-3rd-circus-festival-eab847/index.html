Ilha do Sal hosts 3rd circus festival

The festival aims to bring circus art to the public through free shows in public spaces.

Sources: Expresso das Ilhas and Noticias Lusofonas · 2026-11-03 · 1 min read

The island of Sal in Cape Verde is hosting the 3rd edition of the International Circus Festival of Cape Verde. The festival's objective is to present circus art to the entire population with free performances in public areas. This initiative promotes access to culture, artistic diversity, and exchange between artists from different nations.

A notable highlight of this year's festival is the participation of the Brazilian company Cia. Corpo na Contramão, traveling from Brazil to be part of the event. The organization stated that the festival's return offers a chance for cultural exchange between Brazil and Cape Verde, enhancing the event's artistic offerings and exposing Cape Verdean audiences to varied circus art forms.

The organization described the return of Sal & Circo as an important moment for culture on Ilha do Sal. They emphasized the role of street art in fostering inclusion, social interaction, and community connection.

Why it mattersResidents of Ilha do Sal will have access to free circus performances in public spaces, promoting cultural engagement.
