Cape Verde pledges FIC venues in Mindelo and Praia

Prime Minister Francisco Tavares said the next editions will use sites owned by the fair, which has struggled with space in both cities.

Source: Mindel Insite · 2026-11-19 · 1 min read

Cape Verde Prime Minister Francisco Tavares pledged at the opening of the 2026 Cape Verde International Fair in Mindelo to provide the event with dedicated venues in Mindelo and Praia for future editions.

Angélica Fortes, the fair’s managing director, said the Mindelo exhibitions have moved between warehouses since the Lajinha warehouses were handed over, relying on premises lent by public and private bodies. In Praia, she said, the covered area is spacious but no longer meets demand and still needs adaptation work.

Tavares said the government would work on a suitable site in São Vicente, where Mindelo is located. Fortes said the fair’s two-city format had produced positive financial results and helped business activity.

Why it mattersFIC organisers must find suitable venues for the next editions in both cities, ending years of moving Mindelo exhibitions between borrowed warehouses.
