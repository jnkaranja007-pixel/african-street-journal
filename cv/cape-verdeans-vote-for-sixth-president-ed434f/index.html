Cape Verdeans vote for sixth president

Six candidates are vying for the presidency in Sunday's election, presenting diverse visions for the nation's future.

Source: A Nacao · 2026-11-15 · 1 min read

Cape Verdeans went to the polls on Sunday, November 15, to elect their sixth President since independence in 1975. Six candidates, including Antero Tavares, Casimiro de Pina, José Maria Neves, Gilson Alves, Joana Rosa, and Loisito Tavares, are competing for the position at the Palácio do Platô.

Antero Tavares, a 58-year-old judge from Praia, is running on a platform of social inclusion and sustainable development, aiming for a presidency closer to citizens and focused on reducing regional disparities. Casimiro de Pina, a 52-year-old lawyer from Fogo, emphasizes legality and transparency in his campaign.

The candidates present different approaches to the role of head of state, with their campaigns ranging from institutional continuity to political renewal. The National Elections Commission (CNE) has officially admitted all six candidacies.

Why it mattersVoters are choosing their sixth president, a decision that will shape the nation's institutional direction and political renewal.
