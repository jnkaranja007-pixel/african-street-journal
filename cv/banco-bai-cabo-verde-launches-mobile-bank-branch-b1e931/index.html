Banco BAI Cabo Verde launches mobile bank branch

The first mobile banking agency in Cape Verde will bring services to different locations.

Source: A Nacao · 2026-11-01 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Banco BAI Cabo Verde (BAICV) launched the country's first mobile banking agency on Friday, October 30. The inaugural ceremony took place at the parking lot of the Bank of Cape Verde (BCV) in Praia.

BAICV stated the mobile agency demonstrates its commitment to financial inclusion and customer proximity, aiming to extend services to various localities for a more accessible banking experience.

The Governor of the Bank of Cape Verde, Óscar Santos, presided over the opening ceremony.

Why it mattersThe new mobile agency will extend banking services to different localities, enhancing financial inclusion and customer proximity.
