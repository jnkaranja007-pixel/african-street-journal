Banco BAI Cabo Verde launches mobile bank branch

The first mobile banking unit in Cape Verde will bring services to different locations.

Source: A Nacao · 2026-10-30 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Banco BAI Cabo Verde (BAICV) launched the country's first mobile bank branch on Friday, October 30.

The mobile branch aims to increase financial inclusion and customer proximity by bringing banking services to various locations, according to a BAICV statement.

The inauguration ceremony took place at the parking lot of the Banco Central de Cabo Verde in Praia and was presided over by the Governor of the Central Bank, Óscar Santos.
