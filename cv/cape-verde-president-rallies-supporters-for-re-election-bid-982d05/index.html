Cape Verde president rallies supporters for re-election bid

Jose Maria Neves seeks a second term in the November 15 presidential election, promising salary increases and reforms.

Source: Africanews · 2026-11-02 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Hundreds of supporters cheered President Jose Maria Neves at a rally in Sao Vicente as campaigning began for the November 15 presidential election. Neves, backed by the African Party for the Independence of Cape Verde (PAICV), is seeking a second term in office. Six candidates are competing for the presidency ahead of the official end of campaigning on November 13, according to the National Elections Commission.

At the rally, Neves stated, "It is essential that we begin working to increase salary levels in Cape Verde. No country grows or competes on the basis of low wages." He added that implementing reforms is necessary to improve wages and enhance household income. His main rival is expected to be Joana Rosa, supported by the Movement for Democracy (MpD).

Cape Verde, an archipelago of 520,000 inhabitants, has been viewed as a model of democracy in Africa since its independence from Portugal in 1975. The election will determine the country's leadership for the next term.

Why it mattersCape Verdean citizens will vote in a presidential election on November 15, with the incumbent president promising reforms to increase salaries.
