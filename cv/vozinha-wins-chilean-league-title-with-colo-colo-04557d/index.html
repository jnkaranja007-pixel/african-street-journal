Vozinha wins Chilean league title with Colo-Colo

The Cape Verdean goalkeeper secured his team's 35th national title with a crucial late save.

Source: A Nacao · 2026-11-10 · 1 min read

Cape Verdean goalkeeper Vozinha became a champion in Chile on Sunday, November 8, winning the national league title with his club Colo-Colo. The team secured their 35th league championship with a 2-1 victory over Ñublense, with Vozinha playing a vital role in the win. He made a key save in the 90+7th minute to preserve the victory.

The 40-year-old international, who described the win as the culmination of his life and career, was instrumental in securing the title with a 10-point lead over Universidad de Chile. Vozinha's contract with Colo-Colo ends on December 31, and his future with the club, including potential participation in the Copa Libertadores, remains uncertain.

Vozinha joined Colo-Colo in August on a six-month deal with an option for an automatic one-year extension. To trigger this extension, he must play at least 50% of the team's minutes until the end of the year. If his contract is renewed, he could participate in the 2027 Copa Libertadores, as Colo-Colo has already qualified for the tournament with this league title.
