Cape Verde nominated for four CAF Awards

The national football teams, coach Pedro Leitão, and goalkeeper Vozinha received nominations for the CAF Awards ceremony in Lagos.

Source: Expresso das Ilhas · 2026-11-05 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Cape Verde has received four nominations for the Confederation of African Football (CAF) Awards. The national women's and men's teams are contenders for African National Team of the Year. Coach Pedro Leitão, also known as Bubista, is nominated for African Coach of the Year, and goalkeeper Vozinha is a candidate for Goalkeeper of the Year.

The Cape Verdean Football Federation (FCF) stated that these nominations recognize the hard work, dedication, and talent of players, coaches, technical staff, officials, and clubs, alongside the support from Cape Verdeans at home and abroad. The awards ceremony will take place in Lagos, Nigeria, on Monday, December 7, 2026.

CAF announced the nominations following a significant year for African football, which included the TotalEnergies CAF Africa Cup of Nations Morocco 2025, a record continental presence at the FIFA World Cup 2026, and intense CAF inter-club competitions.

Why it mattersCape Verdean football teams and individuals are recognized for their achievements in African football.
