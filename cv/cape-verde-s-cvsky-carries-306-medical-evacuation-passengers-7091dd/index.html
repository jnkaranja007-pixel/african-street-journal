Cape Verde’s CVsky carries 306 medical evacuation passengers

The airline has taken over a service among the Coast Guard King Air’s planned missions since the aircraft’s operation was suspended.

Source: Expresso das Ilhas · 2026-11-25 · 1 min read

Cape Verde’s CVsky has carried 306 passengers on medical evacuations since 29 March, taking over a service among the planned missions of the Coast Guard’s King Air aircraft. The government suspended the plane’s operation.

Colonel Domingos Correia, who previously led the military aviation project, told Expresso das Ilhas that the suspension reduced the country’s capacity for medical evacuations and maritime security. He said the aircraft was designed for several duties, including transporting patients, and disputed the government’s economic justification for stopping its use.

The King Air’s suspension has prompted debate over how Cape Verde provides medical evacuations, while CVsky continues to carry patients.

Why it mattersPeople needing medical evacuations now rely on CVsky, which has carried 306 passengers since 29 March.
