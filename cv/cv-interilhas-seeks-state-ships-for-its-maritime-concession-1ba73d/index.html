CV Interilhas seeks state ships for its maritime concession

The company says the 2023 contract amendment already provides for new vessels and requires changes to operating and financial terms.

Source: Expresso das Ilhas · 2026-11-26 · 1 min read

Cape Verdean ferry concessionaire CV Interilhas has asked the government to bring newly acquired state ships into its public maritime transport concession. In a letter to Transport and Sea Minister João do Carmo Brito Soares, it said any handover to other private operators must follow public procurement rules and rights set out in the current contract.

The company said an amendment approved in 2023 expressly allows new ships to enter the concession, whether at the state’s initiative or its own. It says integration would require adjustments to operating, economic and financial models, as well as to the benchmarks used to calculate compensation.

CV Interilhas argues that assigning public ships to other operators for services covered by the concession could distort competition and breach its contractual rights. It wants a legal, economic and operational assessment, with the company consulted, before the government chooses an operating model. The company said it is willing to work with the government.

Why it mattersCV Interilhas must be consulted and the legal, economic and operational model assessed before the government decides how to operate the new vessels.
