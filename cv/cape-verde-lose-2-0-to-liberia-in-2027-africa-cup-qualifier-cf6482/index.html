Cape Verde lose 2-0 to Liberia in 2027 Africa Cup qualifier

Liberia scored in the 47th and 86th minutes, leaving the Blue Sharks level with Liberia on three points in Group K.

Source: A Nacao · 2026-11-18 · 1 min read

Cape Verde lost 2-0 to Liberia in the fourth round of Group K qualifying for the 2027 Africa Cup of Nations, a match played in Liberia, according to A Nação.

Liberia scored in the 47th and 86th minutes. Mali lead the group on 12 points, followed by Rwanda on six, while Cape Verde sit third on three points.

Cape Verde and Liberia now have three points each, with Liberia fourth in the standings.

Why it mattersThe defeat leaves Cape Verde needing results from its remaining group matches to stay in contention for a place at the tournament.
