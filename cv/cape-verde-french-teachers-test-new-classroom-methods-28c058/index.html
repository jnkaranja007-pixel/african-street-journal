Cape Verde French teachers test new classroom methods

A two-day colloquium in Mindelo brought 70 teachers together to share approaches to grammar, digital tools and teaching children.

Source: Mindel Insite · 2026-11-25 · 1 min read

A two-day national colloquium for French teachers from São Vicente and Santo Antão ended on 24 November at the Mindelo Technology Park, with 70 teachers attending. The event focused on teaching methods, classroom tools and ways to make French lessons more engaging.

Maria Helena dos Santos, a trainer and São Vicente representative of the Cape Verde Association of French Teachers, said the training was intended to equip teachers for changing demands in foreign-language education. Participants took part in four workshops on topics including teaching French to children, digital tools, the TV5MONDE platform, a French-language television network, and more interactive grammar lessons.

The association organised the colloquium with the French Embassy. It followed a July training in France attended by 16 teachers from different islands, who were expected to share what they learned with colleagues. Dos Santos said students have few opportunities to encounter French outside class.

Why it mattersThe 70 teachers attending are expected to share classroom methods, while students have few chances to encounter French outside lessons.
