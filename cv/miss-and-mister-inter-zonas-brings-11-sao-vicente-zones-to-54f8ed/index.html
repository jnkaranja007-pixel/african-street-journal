Miss and Mister Inter Zonas brings 11 São Vicente zones to stage

The 19 December show at Mindelo’s technical-school auditorium will use performances based on the four colours of Cape Verde’s flag.

Source: Mindel Insite · 2026-11-24 · 1 min read

The third Miss and Mister Inter Zonas SV contest is scheduled for 19 December, with contestants representing 11 zones of São Vicente at the auditorium of the Mindelo Technical School. The event will feature four main presentations inspired by the four colours of Cape Verde’s flag.

Sindjy Cawinny, the contest instructor, said the show is intended to put neighbourhoods on the outskirts of Mindelo at the centre of a celebration of culture, youth and national identity. She said rehearsals have been intense, with contestants working on stage presentation, confidence and individual expression.

Cawinny said the organisers want each presentation to reflect the island’s diversity and give its neighbourhoods visibility. The contest is in its third edition.
