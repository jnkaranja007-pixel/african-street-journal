Barbosa Elected MpD Parliamentary Group Leader

Emanuel Barbosa will lead the MpD parliamentary group with a focus on internal cohesion and constructive opposition.

Emanuel Barbosa has been elected as the new president of the MpD Parliamentary Group. He takes over from Luís Carlos Silva, who held the position since June. Barbosa, a deputy for the Europe and Rest of the World constituency, stated his intention to strengthen internal cohesion and value the experience of fellow deputies.

The new leadership's commitment includes fostering a responsible, dialogical, and proactive opposition. Barbosa emphasized the importance of dialogue, proximity, and listening to citizens, aiming to translate their concerns into concrete proposals for national development. He described the opposition's role as one that criticizes when necessary but also offers solutions.

The MpD Parliamentary Group's new leadership reaffirms its dedication to constructive opposition, maintaining a firm stance in overseeing government actions while remaining open to dialogue for the national interest. Barbosa's election follows the National Convention that appointed Paulo Veiga as the president of the MpD party.
