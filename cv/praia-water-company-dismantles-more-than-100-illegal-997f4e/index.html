Praia water company dismantles more than 100 illegal connections

Águas de Santiago says some connections supplied water for resale, crop irrigation and construction, while others bypassed meters or followed debt suspensions.

Source: A Nacao · 2026-11-25 · 1 min read

Águas de Santiago, the company responsible for water supply in Santiago, has dismantled more than 100 illegal connections in Praia over the past three months, it said. The work is part of inspections aimed at irregularities in the public water system.

The company said some connections were used to sell water to third parties, irrigate vegetable plots on the city’s outskirts or supply construction sites, housing complexes and rental buildings. Other methods included reconnecting service after suspension for unpaid bills, tapping the network without a meter, removing meters and using bypass devices.

Águas de Santiago said the practices increase water losses and affect the sustainability and fairness of the service. It did not provide a figure for the water lost or set out a date for further inspections.

Why it mattersThe company must continue inspections of Praia’s network after identifying more than 100 illegal connections in three months.
