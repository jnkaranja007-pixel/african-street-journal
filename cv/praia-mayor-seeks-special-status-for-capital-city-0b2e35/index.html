Praia mayor seeks special status for capital city

Fernando Pinto argues the capital needs differentiated treatment due to its national responsibilities.

Source: Expresso das Ilhas · 2026-11-01 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Fernando Pinto, mayor of Praia, is calling for the capital city to receive "differentiated treatment" to reflect its status as the nation's capital. He stated that Praia should be seen and treated as the capital, not just as a municipality, and actively participate in national solutions.

Pinto believes investment should be spread across all municipalities to prevent over-concentration of people and services in Praia. He highlighted that Praia receives a large influx from rural exodus, African coastal migration, cruise ships, and hosts the presidency, government, major companies, universities, and embassies, necessitating balanced national investment.

The mayor also emphasized the urgent need to invest in inter-island connectivity, allowing citizens to travel and work safely between islands like Praia and Maio. This initiative coincides with World Cities Day and aims to foster dialogue on urban development challenges in Cape Verde.

Why it mattersThe mayor's proposals for balanced national investment and improved inter-island connectivity directly affect all Cape Verdean citizens.
