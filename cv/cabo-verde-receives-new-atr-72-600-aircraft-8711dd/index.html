Cabo Verde receives new ATR 72-600 aircraft

The new plane will improve inter-island travel and operational stability for the airline.

Sources: Expresso das Ilhas and A Nacao · 2026-11-08 · 1 min read

Cabo Verde received a new ATR 72-600 aircraft, presented at the Nelson Mandela International Airport in Praia. The new plane, acquired new, is intended to strengthen inter-island air links.

Manuel Lima, president of LACV, stated that the new aircraft is part of a contract for two units, with a fourth expected in December. He explained that operating with four aircraft will lead to greater operational stability and reduce service interruptions.

The new ATR 72-600 features improvements in maintenance, performance, fuel consumption, and baggage capacity compared to the previous model. The airline expects the aircraft to begin operations soon, pending administrative procedures.
