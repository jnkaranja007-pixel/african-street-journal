Barbosa elected leader of opposition parliamentary group

Emanuel Barbosa will lead the Movement for Democracy's parliamentary group, aiming to foster internal cohesion and present a responsible opposition.

Source: A Nacao · 2026-10-22 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Emanuel Barbosa was elected on Monday, October 20, in Praia, as the new leader of the parliamentary group for the Movement for Democracy (MpD), the main opposition party. Celso Ribeiro, Sandra Galina, Liver Gomes, and Ivone Cardoso were appointed as vice-presidents. The new leadership aims to strengthen internal unity, leverage the experience of its deputies, and establish a responsible, dialoguing, and proactive opposition.

Barbosa stated that the group will offer qualified opposition, criticizing when necessary but also presenting concrete proposals. This leadership transition follows the national convention that elected Paulo Veiga as the MpD's national president. The elected leader emphasized the importance of dialogue, proximity, and listening to citizens, ensuring parliamentary actions reflect the concerns of Cape Verdeans.

The new leadership reaffirmed its commitment to constructive opposition, maintaining oversight of government actions and openness to dialogue in matters of national interest. The previous leader, Luís Carlos Silva, resigned following changes to party statutes that mandated a renewal of the parliamentary group's leadership within 15 days of the national convention.

Why it mattersCape Verdean citizens are affected as the new leadership of the main opposition party's parliamentary group aims to represent their concerns through constructive criticism and policy proposals.
