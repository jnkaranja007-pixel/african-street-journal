Cape Verde police detain 423 people, seize 63 firearms in October

National Police reported 21,568 people approached and 6,039 searched during crime prevention operations.

Source: Mindel Insite · 2026-11-12 · 1 min read

Cape Verde's National Police apprehended 423 individuals and confiscated 63 firearms across the nation during October. The police also seized 172 rounds of ammunition and 76 bladed weapons as part of their crime prevention and combat operations.

During the month, 423 people were arrested for crimes committed in flagrante delicto, while 649 were brought to police stations for identification, and 25 preventive detention cases were recorded. Authorities also inspected 6,039 individuals and 11,029 vehicles as part of wider control measures.

These results stem from a series of operations including preventive policing, inspections, criminal investigations, public order maintenance, and customs control. The institution stated the data reflects its commitment to fighting crime and reinforcing community safety.

Why it mattersThe operations directly affect the safety and security of Cape Verdean communities.
