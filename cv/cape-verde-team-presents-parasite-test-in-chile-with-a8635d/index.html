Cape Verde team presents parasite test in Chile, with Portugal trial ahead

The Bioanalítica and University Jean Piaget reagent has been used in schistosomiasis outbreaks in Cape Verde and Mozambique.

Source: Mindel Insite · 2026-11-24 · 1 min read

A Cape Verdean research team presented Parasimax Multicolor, a method for detecting Schistosoma haematobium in clinical samples, at an event in Chile. Bioanalítica developed the reagent with the University Jean Piaget, and the gathering brought together scientists and students from Latin American countries.

Researcher Maximiliano Fernandes told Mindel Insite that the reagent helped identify a schistosomiasis outbreak in Ribeira Principal, Cape Verde, and is being used to diagnose an outbreak in Mozambique. He said it also detected a rare parasite in a laboratory in Afghanistan’s Kunduz province.

Fernandes said the team represented Brazil at the Chile event because it had lacked funding to attend international events for the last decade. The reagent is expected to be tested in Portugal soon; Fernandes said it was attracting interest from other investors.

Why it mattersThe research team must prepare for testing in Portugal, the next step Fernandes says is expected soon.
