Cape Verde reports meeting more than 90% of climate adaptation targets

The government says work under its national plan is ahead of its 2030 horizon, with investment in water security and coastal protection next.

Source: A Nacao · 2026-11-21 · 1 min read

Cape Verde met more than 90% of the targets in its National Climate Change Adaptation Plan in 2026, State Secretary for Finance Maria José Lopes said on Thursday, 19 November. She announced the progress at a climate action event held alongside the United Nations climate conference (COP31) in Antalya, Turkey.

The plan runs through 2030. Lopes said municipalities, communities and partners had helped deliver the targets. The government presented cliMAPAS CV, which maps climate risks and vulnerabilities across all 22 municipalities, as well as a climate information portal and local action plans tailored to each territory.

The next stage, Lopes said, is to turn planning and information into investments and projects, particularly in water security, clean energy, coastal protection and resilient infrastructure. She said delivery depends on cooperation among governments, municipalities, financial institutions, companies, universities and civil society.

Why it mattersMunicipalities, communities and project partners now face the task of converting adaptation plans into projects, particularly for water security and coastal protection.
