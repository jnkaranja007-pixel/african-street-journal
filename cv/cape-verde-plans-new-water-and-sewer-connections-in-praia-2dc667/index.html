Cape Verde plans new water and sewer connections in Praia for 2027

The 2027 state budget proposal allocates about 1,3 billion escudos to water and sanitation as the minister cites losses above 60% in the public network.

Source: Expresso das Ilhas · 2026-11-28 · 1 min read

Cape Verde’s Environment, Climate Action and Energy Minister Carlos Varela said Praia is expected to gain around 1,000 water and sewer network connections in 2027. He announced the plan during debate on the 2027 state budget proposal in the National Assembly.

Varela said losses in the public network exceed 60%, a burden he described as unsustainable for water-supply companies. He also said the country lacks enough water storage to keep supplies going if production stops, and cited continuing supply problems on Maio and Boa Vista.

The budget proposal sets aside about 1,3 billion escudos for water and sanitation within a package of approximately 5,2 billion escudos for energy, water, sanitation, environment, biodiversity and climate action.
