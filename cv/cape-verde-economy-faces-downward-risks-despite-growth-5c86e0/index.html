Cape Verde Economy Faces Downward Risks Despite Growth Forecast

The Public Finance Council finds the 2027 economic growth forecast plausible but warns of significant downward risks.

The Public Finance Council (CFP) has assessed the State Budget proposal for 2027, deeming the projected 5.6% economic growth plausible for Cape Verde. However, the council issued a cautionary note, highlighting that risks to this forecast are predominantly downward. This vulnerability is attributed to the nation's reliance on tourism, fluctuations in international commodity prices, and the broader global economic uncertainty.

In its report on the macroeconomic forecasts underpinning the 2027 budget, the CFP recommended that the government provide more robust substantiation for its underlying assumptions. Additionally, the council advised a more thorough analysis of the potential impacts of unforeseen shocks on public finances. The council's findings were presented in the context of the upcoming state budget, which outlines the nation's economic direction.

The CFP's analysis underscores the delicate balance Cape Verde must maintain to achieve its economic objectives. While the projected growth indicates potential progress, the identified risks necessitate careful monitoring and strategic planning to mitigate potential negative impacts on the national economy and public finances.
