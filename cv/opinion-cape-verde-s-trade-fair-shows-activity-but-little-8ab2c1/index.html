Opinion: Cape Verde's trade fair shows activity but little urgency for change

Hélio Africano Varela, writing for Mindel Insite, says visitors to São Vicente's international trade fair found businesses at work but few new products or ways of working.

Source: Mindel Insite · 2026-11-22 · 1 min read

Hélio Africano Varela, writing in the Cape Verdean outlet Mindel Insite, describes visiting every stand at the Feira Internacional de Cabo Verde (FIC), a trade fair held on São Vicente, from technology to grogue. He found businesses, workers, products and services at work, and said the event reflects the country's economic pulse.

Varela says he left with a sense of sameness. He reports no sign of urgent change, no innovation driving new ideas, products or ways of working, and not enough evidence of a country preparing to compete in a rapidly changing world. He calls the problem a "bubble of lethargy", in which old models are applied to the present.

He points to the new political cycle, which has announced a commitment to the primary sector and industrialisation, and asks where the fair's response to that agenda was. The column does not name specific projects or commitments announced at the fair.

Why it mattersBusinesses and workers exhibiting at the FIC are being measured against the new government's announced focus on primary industries and industrialisation.
