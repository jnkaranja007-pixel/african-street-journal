Rain bands approach Cape Verde southern islands

The National Institute of Meteorology and Geophysics forecasts variable rain for Maio, Santiago, Fogo, and Brava.

Source: Expresso das Ilhas · 2026-11-03 · 1 min read

Rain cloud bands are approaching Cape Verde, with the National Institute of Meteorology and Geophysics (INMG) predicting cloudy to overcast skies and possible rain for Maio, Santiago, Fogo, and Brava islands between today and Tuesday.

The INMG also forecasts light to moderate rain in Boa Vista on Tuesday afternoon, and drizzle in Santo Antão. Winds will be light to moderate from the north-northeast, and sea waves are expected to reach 2.5 meters, potentially affecting inter-island travel.

NGA AgroClimate Tech Ltd advised residents and agricultural communities to monitor weather changes as the rain bands approach the southern islands, potentially increasing cloudiness and causing localized showers.

Why it mattersResidents and agricultural communities on the southern islands face increased cloudiness and localized showers.
