Cape Verde's parliament receives budget proposal worth 101.7 billion escudos

The draft projects 5.7% growth and 1.8% inflation, while a technical analysis questions the quality and resilience of those forecasts.

Source: Expresso das Ilhas · 2026-11-23 · 1 min read

Cape Verde's government submitted its proposed state budget to the National Assembly on 1 October, with a total value announced at about 101.7 billion escudos. The proposal is now under parliamentary review.

An analysis published by Expresso das Ilhas says the projected 5.7% economic growth and 1.8% inflation do not by themselves show whether the budget will deliver broad gains. It calls for scrutiny of revenue and spending forecasts, public debt, investment execution and how fiscal and social measures distribute resources.

The analysis also flags the island economy's dependence on imported food, fuel, equipment and other goods as a vulnerability behind the inflation outlook. It says the budget's official projections and tables must be checked, and cautions that authorised spending is not the same as expenditure carried out.

Why it mattersNational Assembly members must assess the proposed spending, revenue forecasts and public debt before the budget can proceed.
