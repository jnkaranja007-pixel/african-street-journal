Escola de Samba Tropical plans 1,200-person Isaura Gomes tribute

The São Vicente group will divide its Carnaval 2027 parade into three sections and estimates preparation costs at 11,900 contos.

Sources: Expresso das Ilhas and Mindel Insite · 2026-11-23 · 1 min read

Escola de Samba Tropical, a carnival group in São Vicente, plans to honour former mayor Isaura Gomes in its Carnaval 2027 parade, with 1,200 participants and an estimated budget of 11,900 contos. Its president, David Leite, said the group intends to propose an honorary doctorate for Gomes and invite the crowd to sing happy birthday to her during the parade.

The parade will trace Gomes’s childhood, academic life and political career, while also recognising her work as a social activist and businesswoman. Leite said the group faces rising costs and a shortage of skilled workers, and is turning its workshop into an arts training space under carnival artist Fernando de Morais.

The group plans to present its official song on 20 December. It also expects to sign an agreement with Falcões do Norte, a club in Chã de Alecrim, to use the neighbourhood’s sports hall for rehearsals starting 8 January.

Why it mattersThe group’s training effort is intended to prepare skilled workers to continue its carnival productions in coming years, amid an estimated 11,900-contos budget.
