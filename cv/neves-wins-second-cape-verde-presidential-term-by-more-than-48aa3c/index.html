Neves wins second Cape Verde presidential term by more than 40,000 votes

The incumbent widened his 2021 lead over his nearest rival, while abstention remained above 60% in Sunday's vote.

Source: Expresso das Ilhas · 2026-11-23 · 1 min read

José Maria Neves won a second term as Cape Verde’s president in the election on Sunday, 15 November, finishing more than 40,000 votes ahead of challenger Joana Rosa Amado. The election commentary published by Expresso das Ilhas said abstention and Neves each registered a score above 60%.

Neves had won the 2021 election by just over 12,000 votes against Carlos Wahnon Veiga. The commentary said he spent five years preparing for re-election, while Amado had about 100 days to prepare after her party lost May’s legislative election. It said she had also lost in local elections and had to stand in the Santiago-South constituency to enter parliament.

The commentary argued that Amado’s campaign did not draw abstaining voters to the polls, while Neves had support from the party that won the most recent legislative election. It described his second term as the outcome of a sustained re-election effort.

Why it mattersAmado's presidential campaign had about 100 days to prepare, compared with five years of preparation by Neves.
