Cape Verde government pays overdue carnival grants to São Vicente groups

Each group receives two million escudos, the overdue amount plus an advance on the December instalment.

Sources: Expresso das Ilhas and Mindel Insite · 2026-11-18 · 1 min read

Cape Verde's government has transferred two million escudos to each official carnival group in São Vicente, doubling the grant, Culture Minister António Sequeira Duarte said. The payment covers an overdue tranche and an advance on the instalment originally due in December.

Duarte said the delay came from circumstances and processing at the Finance Ministry, and that the problem has now been resolved. He said support also reaches carnival festivities in other municipalities, but the national total for the 2027 carnival is not yet available.

Support for the remaining municipalities will be arranged between December and early January with association leaders and municipal councils. Separately, São Vicente's municipal council deposited 1,000 contos for each group ahead of the 2027 contest, scheduled for 9 February.

Why it mattersGroups preparing parade costumes and floats now have the funds to keep work on schedule for the contest on 9 February.
