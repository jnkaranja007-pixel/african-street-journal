Cape Verde drops three places to 71st in FIFA ranking

The team remains the highest-ranked Portuguese-speaking country, with 1,360.08 points in the latest update.

Source: Expresso das Ilhas · 2026-11-19 · 1 min read

Cape Verde fell three places to 71st in the latest FIFA ranking, with 1,360.08 points. The update was released in November, following the previous ranking at the start of October.

Expresso das Ilhas reported that Cape Verde remained the highest-ranked among Portuguese-speaking countries. Guinea-Bissau was the only one in that group to rise, moving from 124th to 120th; Angola stayed 83rd and Mozambique 98th.

São Tomé and Príncipe held 194th place and Timor-Leste 202nd. Spain stayed first despite losing 3-2 to England, while Portugal dropped one place to sixth.

Why it mattersCape Verde’s national team now sits 71st in the ranking, three places below its previous position.
