Banco de Cabo Verde seeks mechanical maintenance firm

The central bank is launching a public tender for a 12-month contract to service its mechanical installations.

The Banco de Cabo Verde (BCV) has announced a national public tender, number 14/2026, to hire a specialized company for the maintenance of its mechanical installations. The contract, to be performed at the bank's headquarters in Praia, Santiago, will initially run for 12 months.

The tender documents can be requested via email at contratacaopublicabcv@bcv.cv, specifying 'Solicitação documentos CP 14/2026_Manutenção Instalações Mecânicas' in the subject line. The BCV's Board of Directors is the competent authority for awarding the contract and authorizing expenditure.

The contract may be renewed for up to three additional years, based on a positive evaluation of the services provided. The Department of Heritage, Security and Administration is responsible for conducting the procurement process.

Why it mattersCape Verdean businesses can bid for a 12-month contract to maintain mechanical installations at the central bank.

ELECTRA warns of power cuts due to lack of investment

The new ELECTRA board cites underinvestment since 2015 and broken equipment as causes for ongoing power outages.

The new board of ELECTRA Sociedades has identified a lack of essential investments since 2015 and breakdowns in three production units at the Palmarejo plant in Praia as the main reasons for recent power cuts affecting customers. The team, led by Pedro Lima da Rocha, also warned of a significant financial imbalance but pledged to restore operational capacity.

The board found that all thermal production capacity at Palmarejo was installed by 2015, with no sufficient investments made since then to renew, modernise, or expand it. Of the 70,800 kilowatts (kW) of installed thermal power, only about 45,500 kW, or 64.3%, is currently available due to three broken production units and deteriorating equipment.

This situation leaves the electricity system vulnerable, especially during peak consumption periods. The immediate priority is to ensure available production meets demand, which requires approximately 43,500 kW for Santiago during peak hours. The board also noted degraded working conditions and employee demotivation.

Why it mattersCustomers in Praia are experiencing power cuts because of insufficient investment in electricity infrastructure and equipment failures.

Cabo Verde athletes win five powerlifting medals

Evilson Tavares secured gold in the 120kg bench press at the African Powerlifting Championship in South Africa.

Three athletes from Cabo Verde competed in the 2026 African Powerlifting Championship in Potchefstroom, South Africa, securing five medals: one gold and four silver. Evilson Tavares became the first Cape Verdean athlete to win an international gold medal in powerlifting.

The Cape Verdean team, which participated from October 30 to November 7, included Jeisson Carvalho (+120kg), Evilson Tavares (120kg), and Ledna da Silva (63kg). Tavares also won silver in the 120kg powerlifting event, da Silva took silver in the 63kg category, and Carvalho earned silver in the +120kg powerlifting and bench press events.

The Cape Verdean Weightlifting Federation (FECAH) congratulated the athletes on their historic participation. FECAH stated on Facebook that the three athletes from the Santiago Norte region demonstrated talent and determination, highlighting Cabo Verde on the continental stage.

Why it mattersThree athletes from Cabo Verde achieved international success in powerlifting and bench press at a continental championship.
