Cabo Verde to restart arms control commission

A national commission for light and small arms control will be reactivated to improve coordination and training.

Cabo Verde will restart its national commission for the control of light and small arms and ammunition, a body that had ceased functioning. The recommendation came after a national assessment by the Economic Community of West African States (ECOWAS) which found the lack of such a structure a gap in national coordination. The commission will ensure articulation between institutions responsible for arms management.

Abayomi Adiomi, head of the ECOWAS assessment team, stated that Cabo Verde needs this national commission to coordinate entities involved in arms control. He also highlighted the need to train customs, police, and other relevant professionals, and to provide them with necessary equipment. Camilo da Graça from the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and Defence confirmed the reactivation, adding that the National Police will lead the interministerial and interdisciplinary body.

The commission will monitor arms and ammunition management and produce reports for comparison with other ECOWAS member states. The United Nations Institute for Disarmament Research (UNIDIR) representative, Mohamed Coulibaly, stressed the importance of improved information sharing and operational coordination. Measures identified include awareness campaigns, voluntary arms collection, and destruction of obsolete or seized weapons, building on the 2023 law on arms and ammunition.

Cape Verde hosts national gymnastics championship

18 athletes from Santiago Sul, Santiago Norte, and São Vicente compete for national titles.

Praia will host the 26th National Gymnastics Championship on Saturday, October 24, at the Vavá Duarte Sports Pavilion. 18 gymnasts aged 12 and older, representing regional associations from Santiago Sul, Santiago Norte, and São Vicente, will compete. The event is organized by the Cape Verdean Gymnastics Federation (FCG).

The competition follows the All-Around classification format, using the International Gymnastics Federation's scoring code for 2025-2028. A jury will evaluate the athletes, who qualified through their respective regional championships. The championship aims to assess technical progress and identify potential national team members for international competitions.

Wânia Monteiro, president of the FCG, stated that the championship is a moment to showcase the year's work from clubs across different islands. She emphasized its importance in celebrating national gymnastics and encouraging more young people to participate in the sport. Awards will be presented on Saturday evening.

Praia students march for road safety after pupil's death

Centro Educativo Miraflores organizes a march on October 24 to raise awareness about road safety following a fatal accident.

Students from Centro Educativo Miraflores (CEM) in Praia will march on Saturday, October 24, to raise awareness about road safety. The march is in memory of CEM student Aylla Danielle, who died on October 14 after being hit by a vehicle.

The march begins at 8:30 AM at CEM in Palmarejo Grande and will pass through several areas of Praia, including Monte Vermelho, Praça Center, Terra Branca, and Chã de Areia, ending at the National Library. The school stated, "It is urgent to change behaviors and make our roads safer. A child's life cannot be cut short by the recklessness of a driver."

The driver involved in the accident, a young man, was detained by the National Police and is awaiting the outcome of the process in preventive detention. The incident occurred as the child was crossing a pedestrian crossing and was struck by a vehicle traveling at high speed.

Why it mattersStudents and the school community in Praia are affected by a fatal road accident and are taking action to improve road safety.
