Cape Verde presidential campaign begins

Four candidates are vying for the presidency in the election scheduled for November 15.

The election campaign for the presidential elections, scheduled for November 15, began today, Thursday, October 29. For two weeks, candidates will hold rallies, debates, and direct engagement events to win voter confidence before election day.

The National Elections Commission (CNE) urged a civic and transparent campaign focused on constructive debate, emphasizing adherence to electoral law regarding campaign finance, propaganda placement, and equal media access. President José Maria Neves is seeking re-election with a platform focused on consolidating democracy, while Joana Rosa, Casimiro de Pina, and Gilson Alves present platforms centered on justice modernization, legality and transparency, and radical socio-economic transformation, respectively.

Voters will choose from diverse visions for governance, with analysts suggesting undecided voters will be decisive. The CNE called for mutual respect and elevated political discourse, avoiding personal attacks and misinformation during this democratic celebration.

Why it mattersCape Verdeans will vote for their next president on November 15, choosing between four candidates with different visions for the nation.
