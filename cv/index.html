Cape Verde government pays overdue carnival grants to São Vicente groups

Each group receives two million escudos, the overdue amount plus an advance on the December instalment.

Cape Verde's government has transferred two million escudos to each official carnival group in São Vicente, doubling the grant, Culture Minister António Sequeira Duarte said. The payment covers an overdue tranche and an advance on the instalment originally due in December.

Duarte said the delay came from circumstances and processing at the Finance Ministry, and that the problem has now been resolved. He said support also reaches carnival festivities in other municipalities, but the national total for the 2027 carnival is not yet available.

Support for the remaining municipalities will be arranged between December and early January with association leaders and municipal councils. Separately, São Vicente's municipal council deposited 1,000 contos for each group ahead of the 2027 contest, scheduled for 9 February.

Why it mattersGroups preparing parade costumes and floats now have the funds to keep work on schedule for the contest on 9 February.

Cape Verde’s central bank seeks maintenance firm for data centre and UPS

The Banco de Cabo Verde is offering a 12-month contract covering its Praia data centres, a three-phase UPS and required consumables.

The Banco de Cabo Verde has opened national public tender No. 10/2026 to hire a specialist company to maintain its data-centre infrastructure and the three-phase uninterruptible power supply (UPS) at the bank’s headquarters in Praia, Santiago.

The work covers the bank’s integrated APC data-centre infrastructure, technical assistance and all consumables needed for the service. The contract is set for 12 months, with renewal subject to a positive assessment of the work.

The bank may renew the agreement for up to three years beyond the initial term. Companies can request the tender documents by email from the bank’s public procurement unit, stating the tender number and maintenance service in the subject line.

Why it mattersThe selected company will provide maintenance and consumables at the central bank’s data centres and headquarters for an initial 12 months.

Cape Verde records 372 prostate cancer cases over three years

Carla Barbosa said diagnoses rose most between 2024 and 2025, but better detection and cancer registration may explain part of the increase.

Cape Verde recorded 372 prostate cancer cases over three years, Carla Barbosa of the national programme for cancer prevention and control said at a men’s health workshop. Diagnoses rose most between 2024 and 2025, she said, but the increase does not necessarily mean the disease became more common.

The cancer registry shows the most diagnoses among men aged 50 to 79. Barbosa said 50 people died from prostate cancer in 2022, making it the cancer that contributed most to cancer deaths in the country. She said late detection contributes to mortality, while early diagnosis is associated with better treatment and survival prospects.

Barbosa cited limited access to diagnostic tests across the islands, shortages of specialists and the absence of multiparametric magnetic resonance imaging and PET scanning in the public system. She also said Cape Verde lacks radiotherapy and called for stronger imaging and pathology services, decentralised care and closer links between health centres and hospitals.

Why it mattersMen across the islands face unequal access to diagnostic tests, while the public system lacks radiotherapy and two advanced imaging services.

Politics · 2026-11-18

Lula meets Neves in Praia to discuss new Brazil-Cape Verde cooperation

Brazil's president congratulated Neves on his re-election and discussed cooperation in digital transformation, energy, agriculture and health.

Brazilian President Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva met Cape Verdean President José Maria Neves in Praia on Monday, 16 November, during a technical stop on his return from COP31 in Turkey. Lula congratulated Neves, who had been re-elected on Sunday, 15 November, for a second presidential term.

The meeting took place in the VIP room at Nelson Mandela International Airport, with Cape Verde's foreign affairs and communities minister, Manuel Amante da Rosa, also present. The two presidents discussed expanding bilateral ties and said they wanted to advance new cooperation initiatives soon.

They identified digital transformation, the energy transition, agriculture, agro-food industry and health as priority areas. Education, higher education and science were also discussed as established fields of cooperation between the two countries.

Why it mattersOfficials in both countries are to advance cooperation initiatives in agriculture, energy, health and other named sectors.

Sport · 2026-11-18

Cape Verde lose 2-0 to Liberia in 2027 Africa Cup qualifier

Liberia scored in the 47th and 86th minutes, leaving the Blue Sharks level with Liberia on three points in Group K.

Cape Verde lost 2-0 to Liberia in the fourth round of Group K qualifying for the 2027 Africa Cup of Nations, a match played in Liberia, according to A Nação.

Liberia scored in the 47th and 86th minutes. Mali lead the group on 12 points, followed by Rwanda on six, while Cape Verde sit third on three points.

Cape Verde and Liberia now have three points each, with Liberia fourth in the standings.

Why it mattersThe defeat leaves Cape Verde needing results from its remaining group matches to stay in contention for a place at the tournament.
