CV Telecom restores services on São Vicente after Mindelo station fire

Internet, telephone and television went down for nearly 24 hours after a fire at the Mindelo station on Tuesday night.

Telecoms services returned to São Vicente on Wednesday evening, 11 November, after a fire at the Mindelo station on Tuesday night left the island almost cut off from communications for nearly 24 hours. Internet, telephone and television services were all affected.

CV Telecom, the country's telecoms operator, said in a statement that the fire disrupted services on several islands. Customers queued at its counters, mainly at its head office on Praça Nova in Mindelo, seeking explanations and a timetable for restoration. The company said it had sent technical teams to repair the damage.

Before São Vicente was restored, services had come back on Fogo and Brava, and fixed and mobile internet on Sal and Boa Vista. The Judicial Police are investigating the circumstances behind the fire. CV Telecom said its teams remain deployed to restore the services still affected and has promised further updates.

Cape Verde upgrades weather system at COP31

The nation is transmitting hourly data to the World Meteorological Organization to improve forecasts and early warnings.

Cabo Verde is enhancing its weather observation system by sending hourly data to the World Meteorological Organization (WMO) network from stations on Sal and Praia islands. Minister of Environment, Climate Action and Energy Carlos Alberto Ramos Varela announced these advancements on November 10 during the UN's COP31 climate conference in Turkey. The meteorological station in Mindelo, São Vicente, has also been restored and is in an experimental phase of data transmission.

These improvements are supported by the Systematic Observations Financing Facility (SOFF) and international partners, aiming to refine weather forecasts and strengthen early warning systems. The minister also advocated for increased investment in maritime meteorological observation, emphasizing its importance for navigation safety, fishing, and blue economy activities. Cabo Verde's goal is to better protect its population and improve preparedness for climate change-related risks.

The COP31 conference, running from November 9 to 20, gathers global leaders to discuss the planet's climate situation. Cabo Verde seeks to bolster its capacity to respond to climate change impacts through these enhanced meteorological observations.

Almada launches women entrepreneurs' five-year strategic plan in Praia

The plan for 2026-2031 is meant to strengthen female entrepreneurship and women's role in Cape Verde's economy.

Interim President Janira Hopffer Almada presided over the opening of the presentation of the 2026-2031 Strategic Plan of the Association of Women Entrepreneurs of Cape Verde & Diaspora (AMES&CD) on Thursday, 12 November, at the Nice Kriola restaurant in Achada Santo António, Praia. The plan sets out the association's priorities and lines of action for the next five years.

The event included the signing of a framework protocol of institutional partnership and cooperation to support the plan's implementation, according to a statement from the Presidency. The programme also featured the Network of Women in Sustainable Tourism and a document of demands from women entrepreneurs and businesswomen in Cape Verde, plus the association's new institutional image and website.

The meeting was also meant to bring together entrepreneurs, institutions and development partners to form new partnerships and forms of collaboration. The presidency's statement described the event as a milestone for women's economic leadership in the country.

Why it mattersWomen entrepreneurs in Cape Verde and the diaspora now have a published five-year agenda and a new website to join and follow the association.
