Business · 08/21/2026

Cape Verde adds 55,719 payment accounts in 2025

The country recorded 439,973 resident individual accounts by the end of 2025. According to a reported document, 85.6% of the resident population now holds at least one bank account. The number of bank branches fell by two to 127 during the year.

Why it mattersThe decline in physical branches reflects a shift toward the use of banking agents to provide financial services.

US State Department lists Cape Verde among 73 nations for fiscal transparency

Cape Verde has maintained its status as a nation meeting minimum requirements for budget integrity and public account management. The US State Department detailed the finding in its latest Fiscal Transparency Report.

Why it mattersThis status is intended to improve the country's reputation with multilateral creditors and foreign investors.

Cape Verde and Gambia sign business cooperation protocol

The Sotavento Chamber of Commerce and the Gambia Chamber of Commerce and Industry signed a cooperation agreement on Aug. 20 in Praia. The protocol aims to strengthen economic and business ties between the two nations.

Why it mattersThe agreement is part of a strategy to increase trade and corporate proximity between the two countries.

Health · 08/21/2026

São Vicente implements monthly mosquito studies

Health authorities have established monthly entomological surveillance at ports, airports, and hospitals. Health official Celso Martins told Expresso das Ilhas that no cases of dengue have been recorded in São Vicente.

Why it mattersThe monitoring system allows authorities to identify and act on imported malaria cases from endemic zones.

Cape Verde Football Federation wins Team Player 11+ award

The Cape Verdean Football Federation and President Mário Semedo will receive the international prize on Aug. 21. The award recognizes the application of innovative approaches to football development.

Why it mattersThe distinction marks the federation's success in implementing new promotional and developmental methods for the sport.
