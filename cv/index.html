Fretilin declines presidential bid amid crime concerns

The Fretilin party will not field a candidate in the 2027 presidential elections, citing a need to prioritize the nation and address rising organized crime.

The Fretilin party announced it will not present its own candidate for the 2027 presidential elections, stating a commitment to prioritize the party and the Timorese people. This decision was formalized during a central committee meeting where political conditions and significant concerns about organized crime were discussed. The party expressed alarm over the recent detentions of hundreds, primarily Chinese, Cambodian, and Indonesian nationals, suspected of online illegal activities like gambling and fraud.

A United Nations Office on Drugs and Crime report highlighted that these developments indicate a shift in Southeast Asia's scam centers. Fretilin emphasized the urgent need to collectively raise awareness and act to prevent organized crime syndicates from infiltrating or dominating state institutions. The party's stance comes as the opposition Movimento para a Democracia (MpD) announced its support for Joana Rosa, a former Justice Minister, in the upcoming November 15 presidential elections.

The MpD's National Directorate officially declared its backing for Rosa's candidacy during an online meeting. This move sets a clear political alignment for the November elections, contrasting with Fretilin's decision to abstain from presenting a candidate. The party's focus remains on national stability and combating the perceived threat of organized crime taking root within the government structures.

Why it mattersThe Fretilin party's decision not to contest the presidential elections is driven by concerns over organized crime and its potential to destabilize the state.

Bino Branco's Voice Silenced, Funaná Loses Pioneer

The co-founder of Ferro Gaita, known for his distinctive voice and mastery of the traditional 'ferrinho' instrument, has died at age 56.

Bino Branco, a pivotal figure in contemporary funaná music, has died at the age of 56. He had been undergoing medical treatment for cancer since 2025 in the United States. Branco was a founding member of the influential group Ferro Gaita, established in 1996 with the aim of reviving traditional funaná sounds, emphasizing the 'ferrinho' and 'gaita' instruments amidst a keyboard-dominated scene.

Branco, whose real name was Carlos Alberto Pereira Lopes, was celebrated for his powerful vocals and his skillful playing of the 'ferrinho,' a traditional percussion instrument. His image became synonymous with the 'ferrinho,' often serving as a reference point for the instrument in Cape Verdean music. He began his musical journey in youth groups, notably Grupo Djassy, before moving to Brava island and later co-founding Ferro Gaita.

Ferro Gaita's debut album, 'Fundu Baxu,' released in 1997, achieved significant success in Cape Verde and among the diaspora, spearheading a revival of traditional funaná. Over a career spanning more than three decades, Branco contributed to Ferro Gaita's status as a leading Cape Verdean band, undertaking international tours and promoting the nation's cultural identity through its traditional rhythms.

Why it mattersThe death of Bino Branco affects Cape Verdean culture by removing a key reference figure in the revival and international promotion of traditional funaná music.

Cape Verde Basketball Team Suffers Third Straight Loss in World Cup Qualifiers

The national basketball team concluded its 2027 World Cup qualifying campaign with a 100-84 defeat against Nigeria in Tunisia.

Cape Verde's national basketball team ended its participation in the fourth window of the FIBA 2027 World Cup African qualifiers with a loss to Nigeria. The match, held in Radès, Tunisia, saw the Nigerian team dominate for most of the game, ultimately winning 100-84. Cape Verde's 'Blue Sharks' trailed in the first three quarters, with scores of 23-9, 29-26, and 24-19. Although they won the final quarter 30-24, the comeback was not enough to secure a victory.

This latest defeat marks Cape Verde's third consecutive loss in this qualifying phase. Prior to facing Nigeria, the team had already been defeated by Tunisia with a score of 60-56 in their first game and by Guinea-Conakri 81-61 in the second. The fourth window of qualification for the 2027 World Cup took place from August 27 to 30 in Tunisia, bringing together national teams competing for a spot in the global basketball tournament.

The team's performance in this qualifying window resulted in three losses from three games played. The matches were part of the ongoing effort by African nations to secure a place in the 2027 FIBA Basketball World Cup. The results indicate a challenging campaign for the Cape Verdean squad as they aimed to advance in the tournament.

Why it mattersCape Verdean basketball players are affected by losing three consecutive games in the 2027 World Cup qualifiers, impacting their chances of advancing.

Tribunal Overturns 45-Day Suspension for TCV Director

A Praia court ruled the 45-day suspension with loss of pay for the TCV director excessive, reducing one penalty to a fine.

The Judicial Court of Praia found the 45-day suspension with loss of pay imposed by Rádio e Televisão de Cabo Verde (RTC) on Televisão de Cabo Verde (TCV) director Bernardina Ferreira to be excessive. The court partially upheld the director's action against the RTC administration, nullifying sanctions related to two of the charges against her.

For a third charge concerning the "Show da Manhã" program in Tarrafal do Santiago, the tribunal acknowledged an irregularity but deemed the suspension disproportionate. This penalty was replaced by a fine, graduated up to ten days of her base salary. The disciplinary process, initiated by the RTC Board of Directors on August 14, 2025, also examined the sending of a contract between RTC and Agência Cabo-Verdiana de Imagens (ACI) to various parties and the publication of a TCV Management Note on the RTC website.

The court's sentence report stated that, after reviewing each fact individually, only the "Show da Manhã" negotiation involved conduct serious enough to warrant disciplinary action. While functional irregularities were admitted, the 45-day suspension with lost wages was deemed "disproportionate and inappropriate," being excessive given the analyzed facts.

Why it mattersThe director of TCV, Bernardina Ferreira, faced a disciplinary process that resulted in a 45-day suspension with loss of pay, which was later reduced by a court.

Volunteers Clean Cape Verde Neighborhoods

Around 120 volunteers participated in a community cleanup campaign in Boa Esperança and João Questão, targeting areas with significant waste accumulation.

The Seventh-day Adventist Church's community service initiative brought together approximately 120 volunteers for a cleanup campaign in Boa Esperança and João Questão. This effort is part of the I Campori dos Desbravadores of the 4th Region, which runs until August 31st in Ribeira d’Água under the theme “Anchored in Christ.” The event includes nearly 200 participants from Santiago and São Nicolau.

Pastor Rafael Fernandes, national director of the Adventist Youth Ministry, stated that the chosen locations were critical due to high waste levels, aiming to raise public awareness about maintaining cleaner environments. Local residents, like Elisangela Vieira from Boa Esperança, also joined the effort, appealing to neighbors to improve public health by keeping areas around their homes clean and not solely relying on municipal services or organized campaigns.

The cleanup operation required masks and protective gear due to the condition of some sites. This social impact action is a key component of the regional Campori, which also features practical training in basic rescue, water safety, outdoor cooking, and investitures. The Desbravadores Club is an Adventist ministry focused on the holistic development of children and adolescents aged 10 to 15.

Why it mattersResidents of Boa Esperança and João Questão benefit from improved public health and a cleaner community environment due to the cleanup campaign.
