Praia Traffic Jams Worsen as Vehicle Imports Surge

Cape Verde's capital faces daily gridlock due to a rising number of vehicles and insufficient road infrastructure, impacting commuters and businesses.

Cidade da Praia is experiencing severe traffic congestion, exacerbated by the arrival of over 500 new vehicles monthly. This surge, noted by former minister Paulo Rocha, strains the island's road network, particularly on Santiago, which exhausted its initial license plate series in November 2024, prompting a new registration system. Commuters like Cristina, who travels from Palmarejo to Achada Grande Frente, now leave home thirty minutes before their 8:00 AM start time to avoid delays, illustrating the daily struggle.

The lack of alternative routes means any incident on major roads causes city-wide disruptions. Adriano Monteiro of the Praia Taxi Drivers Association highlighted how an accident on Avenida Marginal can create a domino effect, pushing traffic onto already crowded areas like Plateau. Congestion is further intensified by simultaneous accidents, which divide police resources and leave stranded vehicles blocking lanes, prolonging delays across the capital.

Driving schools report a significant increase in demand for licenses, mirroring the growing vehicle population. Arlindo Cardoso from Escola de Condução Cedência de Passagem observed a higher volume of individuals seeking driving permits compared to 2015. This rising number of new drivers, coupled with the influx of vehicles, contributes to the already challenging traffic conditions on Praia's roads.

Why it mattersCommuters and taxi drivers in Praia face longer travel times and increased stress due to persistent traffic congestion.

MpD backs Joana Rosa for presidential election

The opposition party's national leadership endorsed the former justice minister with 85% of the vote.

The opposition Movement for Democracy (MpD) party officially announced its support for Joana Rosa, the former Minister of Justice, in the upcoming presidential elections scheduled for November 15. The decision was made during an online meeting of the party's National Directorate, which focused solely on the presidential contest. The endorsement followed a thorough discussion of the current political landscape and an evaluation of candidates seeking the party's backing.

Joana Rosa secured the support of 85% of the National Directorate members, indicating a strong consensus within the party's leadership. This endorsement formally establishes the MpD's strategy for the presidential race, uniting its members behind Rosa's candidacy for the November elections. The party aims to align its base around her figure for the electoral contest.

Several individuals have publicly expressed their intention to run for president, including Casimiro de Pina, Milton Paiva, Péricles Tavares, engineer Fernando Delgado, former Mayor of Ribeira Grande de Santiago Manuel de Pina, and doctor Gilson Alves. Former minister Janine Lélis and Hélio Sanches had previously announced their candidacies but have since withdrawn.

Why it mattersThe MpD party's decision affects its members and potential voters by formally selecting a candidate for the presidential election.

Cape Verde Debuts at AI Olympiad with Distinction

A school's initiative led to the country's first participation in the International Olympiad on Informatics, selecting four students after a national competition.

Cape Verde has made its debut at the International Olympiad on Informatics (IOAI) in Astana, with the StellarCV team achieving distinction. The journey began with a robotics competition, the FIRST Global Challenge, where student Pedro da Cruz dos Santos connected with peers who introduced him to the IOAI. He then proposed the idea to his school, the Escola Industrial e Comercial do Mindelo (EICM), and his teacher, Jorge Michael Rocha. Despite initial doubts about the ambition and logistics, the school's administration supported the initiative.

The first step was accrediting Cape Verde as a participating nation, proving EICM's technical capacity. This was followed by a national selection process coordinated by EduSpace, involving nearly 500 students from across the islands. The competition, held in phases, started with a theoretical test to gauge interest in artificial intelligence and gradually increased in difficulty. Finalists underwent further tests and interviews in English, the competition's official language.

Four students were ultimately selected to form Team StellarCV: Alexandre Nascimento, José Aguiar, and Pedro da Cruz dos Santos from EICM, and Nilson Leite from Escola Secundária Polivalente Cesaltina Ramos in Praia. They trained intensively for about a month under the mentorship of Professor Jorge Michael Rocha, facing the challenge of preparing for a competition in which the country had no prior experience, with limited time before the event.

Why it mattersCape Verdean students are affected by gaining international exposure and experience in artificial intelligence through this competition.

29 Athletes Compete in Cape Verde Bodybuilding Championship

The 5th annual Taça Cabo Verde de Culturismo e Fitness is being held in Mindelo, honoring bodybuilding pioneer Jorge Duarte.

Mindelo is hosting the 5th Taça Cabo Verde de Culturismo e Fitness today, Saturday, August 29th, at the Mindel Hotel. A total of 29 athletes from seven sporting regions across the country are registered to compete. The event is organized by the Cape Verdean Weightlifting Federation (FECAH).

Participants hail from São Vicente, Santiago Sul, Sal, São Nicolau, Boa Vista, Santiago Norte, and Santo Antão. São Vicente leads with 15 registered athletes, followed by Santiago Sul with four, Sal with three, São Nicolau and Boa Vista with two each, and Santiago Norte and Santo Antão with one athlete apiece. The competition includes categories for Men's Bodybuilding, Men's Classic Physique, Men's Physique, Women's Bikini, and Women's Wellness.

This year's competition also serves as a tribute to Jorge Duarte, known as “Bana,” a significant figure in Cape Verdean bodybuilding. Bana holds the distinction of being the first athlete to earn an IFBB Elite Pro Card in a competition held within Cape Verde.

Why it mattersThe event showcases local talent and cultural heritage in bodybuilding, potentially attracting tourism and local engagement.

Cape Verde President Calls for African Union Reform

President José Maria Neves advocates for a more efficient African Union capable of implementing its decisions and redistributing tasks.

Cape Verde's President, José Maria Neves, reiterated the urgent need for a profound reform of the African Union during an interview in Luanda. He emphasized that the organization must become more efficient in implementing its decisions to effectively address continental challenges, including conflict prevention and constitutional ruptures. Neves stressed the importance of establishing a multilevel governance system that clearly defines roles for the AU, sub-regional bodies, and individual states.

Speaking on the sidelines of an extraordinary summit in Luanda focused on conflict prevention, Neves highlighted the difficulties the AU faces in translating resolutions into action. He argued that reforms are essential for the AU to become a relevant political actor with greater influence, requiring sufficient mobilization of material, financial, and human resources for concrete on-the-ground action. The president acknowledged that such reforms involve complex changes to structures, norms, rules, and mindsets.

Neves also met with Angolan President João Lourenço, discussing bilateral cooperation in tourism, transport, and national defense. He welcomed the announcement of TAAG Angola Airlines resuming flights to Cape Verde, connecting Luanda with Dakar and Praia, which will enhance regional connectivity. The AU's Executive Council met in Luanda to prepare documents for the summit, which will feature opening remarks from the host, the AU Commission chair, and the current AU Chairperson.

Why it mattersCape Verde's President is advocating for reforms within the African Union to improve its effectiveness in decision-making and conflict resolution, impacting governance structures across the continent.
