Cape Verde nominated for four CAF Awards

The national football teams, coach Pedro Leitão, and goalkeeper Vozinha received nominations for the CAF Awards ceremony in Lagos.

Cape Verde has received four nominations for the Confederation of African Football (CAF) Awards. The national women's and men's teams are contenders for African National Team of the Year. Coach Pedro Leitão, also known as Bubista, is nominated for African Coach of the Year, and goalkeeper Vozinha is a candidate for Goalkeeper of the Year.

The Cape Verdean Football Federation (FCF) stated that these nominations recognize the hard work, dedication, and talent of players, coaches, technical staff, officials, and clubs, alongside the support from Cape Verdeans at home and abroad. The awards ceremony will take place in Lagos, Nigeria, on Monday, December 7, 2026.

CAF announced the nominations following a significant year for African football, which included the TotalEnergies CAF Africa Cup of Nations Morocco 2025, a record continental presence at the FIFA World Cup 2026, and intense CAF inter-club competitions.

Why it mattersCape Verdean football teams and individuals are recognized for their achievements in African football.

Cape Verde extends voter registration for accessible transport

The National Electoral Commission (CNE) has extended voter registration for its accessible transport program until November 10.

Cape Verde's National Electoral Commission (CNE) has extended voter registration for its accessible transport program until November 10. This program provides free transport for voters with mobility challenges, including the elderly, disabled, and homebound, to reach their polling stations.

The CNE first launched the accessible voting initiative in 2019, aiming to remove barriers for citizens with disabilities and reduced mobility. Previous measures included Braille ballots, sign language in audiovisual materials, and accessibility training for electoral agents. The free transport service was piloted during the May 17, 2026 legislative elections in Praia.

For the upcoming presidential elections on November 15, the CNE plans to expand the free transport service to all municipalities nationwide. This is the second edition of the program, building on the pilot's success in partnership with civil society organizations.

Why it mattersCape Verdean voters with mobility issues will have easier access to polling stations for the November 15 presidential elections.

Simenti launches instrumental album Minimal in Praia

The six-member group Simenti will present their new album, Minimal, in its entirety at a free concert in Praia.

Cape Verdean group Simenti has released a new instrumental album titled "Minimal" in Praia. The album's title track inspired its name, reflecting a musical approach rooted in Cape Verdean traditions while incorporating urban rhythms and contemporary influences. The group, comprising Ndu Carlos, Gilmar, Elias, Nene, Alex Figueiredo, and Dino, consists of musicians with established individual careers who have collaborated with prominent Cape Verdean artists.

According to InSulada, "Minimal" offers an innovative take on Cape Verdean instrumental music, positioning it as a space for experimentation and artistic expression. The group will perform the entire album at a concert, followed by a Q&A session about the music and creative process. Entry to the event is free with a ticket, available in advance at the Palácio da Cultura Ildo Lobo.

Simenti, a project of the Xintidu label, has also prepared a limited edition of merchandise, including T-shirts and tote bags designed with artist Lua Oliveira. This collection aims to build the group's visual identity and connect their music with other art forms. The group has further performances planned across other islands in the archipelago.

Cape Verde police arrest 20 in drug trafficking sting

Operation Shark nets suspects including police and prison officers in Praia.

Cape Verdean police arrested 20 individuals, including police officers, prison guards, and a public official, on Tuesday, April 3rd, as part of the ongoing Operation Shark. The arrests, conducted by the Central Directorate of Criminal Investigation, target alleged high-risk drug trafficking, money laundering, and criminal association.

The suspects, aged between 20 and 60, are accused of co-perpetrating drug trafficking, conversion, concealment, money laundering, and criminal association. During searches, authorities seized a shotgun, a 6.35mm pistol, ammunition, cannabis, cocaine, cutting agents, and drug preparation paraphernalia. Two vehicles and electronic equipment were also confiscated.

This latest phase follows an initial crackdown on July 16th in Praia, which led to 12 arrests and the seizure of 1.1627 kilograms of cocaine, 2,048,269 Cape Verdean escudos, and 770 euros. The operation aims to dismantle a criminal network believed to have been active for 15 years.

Why it matters20 individuals, including law enforcement and public officials, face charges related to drug trafficking and money laundering.
