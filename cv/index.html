Neves and Lula agree to speed up cooperation in Praia stopover

Cape Verde and Brazil will work quickly to strengthen ties in digital transformation, energy transition, agriculture, agro-industry and health.

Cape Verde's President José Maria Neves met Brazil's President Lula da Silva in Praia during a technical stopover on his return flight to Brazil. Lula came to congratulate Neves personally on his election victory, and Neves called the visit an honour and a source of pride for the country.

In his remarks to journalists, Neves said the two governments agreed to work as soon as possible to strengthen bilateral relations. The priority areas include digital transformation, the energy transition, agriculture, agro-industry and health, which are meant to complement existing cooperation in education, higher education and science. Lula instructed both governments to start the necessary work quickly.

Neves invited Lula to attend his inauguration, with a date still to be announced. Lula invited Neves to his own inauguration in Brazil on 5 January, and Neves said efforts are under way to ensure both leaders attend each other's ceremonies.

Banco de Cabo Verde seeks data center maintenance

The central bank is hiring a firm to service its data center and UPS systems for 12 months.

The Banco de Cabo Verde (BCV) has launched a national public tender, number 10/2026, to find a specialized company for the maintenance of its data center infrastructure and uninterruptible power supply (UPS) systems.

The services will cover the BCV's main building in Praia, Santiago, and are contracted for an initial period of 12 months, with the possibility of renewal for up to three years based on performance. The tender documents can be requested via email at contratacaopublicabcv@bcv.cv.

The Banco de Cabo Verde's Board of Directors is the competent authority for contracting and authorizing the expenditure for this service. The Department of Heritage, Security, and Administration is responsible for conducting the tender process.

Why it mattersBusinesses seeking to provide maintenance services for data center and UPS infrastructure can bid on a 12-month contract with the Banco de Cabo Verde.

ENAPOR to fund air conditioning at Mindelo Cultural Centre

The port company will spend about three million escudos on cooling equipment for the São Vicente venue's auditorium.

ENAPOR – Portos de Cabo Verde has agreed to pay for cooling equipment for the Mindelo Cultural Centre, under a partnership agreement with the Ministry of Culture and Creative Industries. The company will spend about three million escudos on acquiring and installing the equipment, which will be bought from a supplier now mobilising to start work.

The auditorium lost its thermal comfort after rehabilitation works, a problem Zenaida Alfama, who handles finances for the Mindelact festival, called shameful during the 32nd edition's opening on November 3. She said the 200-capacity hall had gone without air conditioning for more than a month despite repeated promises. Hamir Inocêncio, chairman of ENAPOR's board, said the company saw the spending as part of its social responsibility policy.

Culture Minister António Duarte said the intervention would resolve the problem permanently and that the government intends to seek similar partners for other cultural facilities, including municipal ones. He said he had already discussed the situation of other spaces with São Vicente's mayor, including the Jota Monte Music Academy.

Why it mattersAudiences at the Mindelo Cultural Centre's auditorium are expected to return to a venue with working cooling, with the work starting as soon as the supplier is ready.

Cape Verde trade fair attracts 200 companies

The 29th International Fair of Cape Verde in Mindelo features over 300 stands and participants from four European countries.

The 29th International Fair of Cape Verde opened in Mindelo with approximately 200 companies and more than 300 stands. The event, which runs for an unspecified duration, includes businesses from Cape Verde, Portugal, Spain, Italy, and France, alongside delegations from the European Union and the Economic Community of West African States (CEDEAO). Angélica Fortes, president of the administrative council of FIC, described the fair as multisectorial, representing nearly all economic sectors.

Fortes stated that the fair is a step in a 29-year journey to promote business and attract investment to Cape Verde. This year's event faces a logistical challenge as it is held at a temporary, off-center location at Cavibel in the Lazareto Industrial Zone, requiring coordination with transport operators. FIC is working to adapt the space, which is not its own property, to meet event needs.

The organization has invested significant financial and logistical effort to host the fair in Mindelo, as it is São Vicente's turn to host. Fortes highlighted FIC's ongoing difficulties due to the lack of a permanent physical space. She expressed hope that the construction of a dedicated venue will be prioritized by the new government, following initial positive discussions with Prime Minister Francisco Carvalho.

Why it mattersThe International Fair of Cape Verde aims to promote business and attract investment, with this year's event featuring 200 companies and over 300 stands.
