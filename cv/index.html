Brazil, Portugal Eye Cape Verde as West Africa Business Hub

A business forum in Praia aims to identify investment and economic cooperation opportunities between Brazil, Cape Verde, and Portugal.

A business forum focused on "Cape Verde as a Strategic Platform for Investment and Expansion into West Africa" began on September 3 in Praia. Organized by Teletur Brasil in partnership with Cape Verde's Sovereign Fund for Private Investment Guarantee, the event seeks to foster business, investment, and economic cooperation among Brazil, Cape Verde, and Portugal. Discussions will cover the digital economy, technology, transport, logistics, construction, real estate, renewable energy, tourism, hospitality, and health sectors.

The forum includes discussions on investment opportunities in Cape Verde, business partnerships, company internationalization, private sector financing, and high-growth sectors. Gildo Batista from Brazil, Pedroso Leal from Portugal, and Indira Campos from the World Bank are scheduled to participate. Cape Verdean companies Loyde Engenharia and Duergema will share their growth experiences, challenges, and partnership opportunities.

The event features an open dialogue session for participants, companies, and institutions to present projects and investment interests, followed by a networking session. Brazilian and Portuguese business leaders view Cape Verde as a gateway to West Africa, highlighting opportunities in technology, tourism, and energy, and advocating for stronger business ties between the three nations.

Why it mattersBrazilian and Portuguese business leaders see Cape Verde as a platform for West African expansion, particularly in technology, tourism, and energy.

Moussaoui seeks transfer from US to France

The convicted plotter in the September 11 attacks has requested a move to France, citing exceptional circumstances and injustice.

Zacarias Moussaoui, who is serving a life sentence in a Colorado maximum-security prison, has formally requested a transfer to France. His lawyer, Romain Ruiz, submitted the plea to the Ministry of Justice, invoking a 1983 bilateral agreement between France and the United States that permits the transfer of convicted individuals. Moussaoui, 58, was detained shortly before the September 11, 2001 attacks and convicted in 2006 for complicity in the events orchestrated by Al-Qaida, which resulted in nearly three thousand deaths.

The transfer request, accessed by Agence France-Presse (AFP), is based on "exceptional circumstances related to the injustice of the trial, the current detention conditions, and the family situation." Ruiz argued that the declassification of September 11 documents in 2021 violated Moussaoui's rights and obstructed his defense. He also contends that Moussaoui, previously identified as the alleged 20th hijacker, was intended for a subsequent operation, not the September 11 attacks themselves.

Lawyer Ruiz highlighted Moussaoui's "family rights" and "inhumane living conditions," noting the prisoner has been in isolation since his arrest. Moussaoui's mother, who has visited him only three times in 25 years, cannot travel to Colorado due to financial and health issues. A judicial source speaking to AFP expressed doubt that the U.S. would extradite the sole individual convicted for the attacks, while French judicial authorities declined to comment.

Why it mattersThe convicted plotter in the September 11 attacks is affected by his request to be transferred to France due to alleged trial injustice and inhumane detention conditions.

Trump envoys travel to Moscow and Kyiv for peace talks

US negotiators Witkoff and Kushner aim to meet Russian and Ukrainian officials in Moscow and Kyiv on September 5 and 6.

Envoys for U.S. President Donald Trump are scheduled to visit Moscow and Kyiv on September 5 and 6 for discussions aimed at ending the conflict in Ukraine. Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky confirmed that the negotiators, Witkoff and Kushner, had agreed to visit Kyiv for the first time, following repeated visits to Moscow. Ukraine has been requesting a Kyiv visit from the negotiators for months.

The Kremlin, however, has tempered expectations for breakthroughs, with spokesperson Dmitri Peskov stating that conditions for concrete progress are not yet in place. Peskov indicated that direct contact with Kyiv would not occur before the visit and that Ukrainian attacks on Russian territory must cease, drawing a parallel to a previous visit by the CIA director. He suggested that the U.S. might demand an end to these actions.

Russian President Vladimir Putin acknowledged that possibilities for constructive peace negotiations still exist and thanked the U.S. and China for advocating a political solution. Putin emphasized that the primary responsibility for resolving the conflict lies with Russia and Ukraine. He also noted that while intelligence services maintain contact, recent Ukrainian threats against civilian aviation hinder peace talks.

Why it mattersInvestors are affected by the potential for a peaceful resolution to the conflict, which could stabilize regional trade and economic conditions.

Cape Verde Seeks Investment to Boost Jobs and Economy

The government aims to leverage historical ties with Brazil and Portugal to attract investment and create employment opportunities.

Cape Verde's government is actively seeking to transform its long-standing affinities with Brazil and Portugal into tangible economic benefits, focusing on investment and job creation. Minister stated that despite its small domestic market, the nation's strategic location between Africa, Europe, and the Americas positions it as a gateway to broader African markets. The goal is to convert this historical connection into a robust ecosystem of investment, businesses, shared knowledge, technology, and employment.

Opportunities are identified in key sectors including water, agriculture, renewable energy, the blue and digital economies, tourism, industry, transport, logistics, and training. The emphasis is on attracting capital, technology, and expertise that will strengthen local companies, generate wealth, and provide better employment prospects for the youth. This initiative views shared legal and institutional frameworks as practical business cost-reducers, not just symbolic ties.

The Chairman of the Private Investment Guarantee Sovereign Fund echoed this sentiment, emphasizing Cape Verde's potential as a platform for West Africa. He highlighted the need for capital to be accompanied by technology, knowledge, and business acumen to transform natural resources into value chains, industry, and wealth. The recent Brazil-Cape Verde Business Forum in Praia aimed to foster new partnerships and identify investment prospects within the country.

Why it mattersThe government's initiative aims to attract investment and technology, which could lead to stronger companies, better jobs, and increased wealth for the country's youth.

Cape Verde Films Debut at Mozambique Festival

Documentary and animated shorts, alongside a director's lab, showcase Cape Verdean talent at the 16th Kugoma film forum.

Cape Verdean cinema is being showcased at the 16th Kugoma film forum in Maputo, Mozambique. The documentary short "Guentis Fonte Lima," directed by Ricardo Leote, will have its official premiere on Friday at the Centro Cultural Franco-Moçambicano. This film, a production by Nos Raiz in partnership with Insular Films, highlights the identity and strength of the women potters from Santiago Island.

"Guentis Fonte Lima" originated from the third edition of the Concurso Bodji, an initiative supported by the Lidera Rural project and funded by Spanish Cooperation. The film also received institutional backing from the Ministry of Culture, Creative Industries, Youth and Sports, the Institute of Cultural Heritage, and the Fonte Lima Association. The event also features "Devagarim," a short film by Grace Ribeiro, and four one-minute animated shorts created by Cape Verdean student collectives.

Beyond film screenings, Cape Verde is participating in the FilmLab Moçambique, a training program for emerging directors from Portuguese-speaking African countries and Timor-Leste, coordinated by filmmaker Samira Vera Cruz. Production companies Ekran Eventos and Parallax Filmes are also representing the country at the forum, underscoring a broader engagement with regional filmmaking.

Why it mattersCape Verdean filmmakers and production companies are gaining international exposure and networking opportunities through their participation in the Kugoma film forum.
