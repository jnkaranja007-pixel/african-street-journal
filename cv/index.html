CV Telecom restores São Vicente services after fire

Internet, phone, and television services were down for nearly 24 hours following a fire at the Mindelo Station.

Telecommunication services on the island of São Vicente were restored on Wednesday, November 11, after a fire at the Mindelo Station on Tuesday night caused nearly 24 hours of isolation.

The outage affected internet, telephone, and television services, causing significant disruption to residents and businesses. CV Telecom confirmed the fire caused widespread disruptions and mobilized technical teams to repair the damage and progressively restore services.

Before services returned to São Vicente, CV Telecom announced their restoration on the islands of Fogo and Brava, as well as fixed and mobile internet on Sal and Boa Vista. The Judicial Police are investigating the circumstances of the fire.

Why it mattersResidents and businesses on São Vicente experienced a near 24-hour disruption to essential communication services.

Cape Verde police detain 423 people, seize 63 firearms in October

National Police reported 21,568 people approached and 6,039 searched during crime prevention operations.

Cape Verde's National Police apprehended 423 individuals and confiscated 63 firearms across the nation during October. The police also seized 172 rounds of ammunition and 76 bladed weapons as part of their crime prevention and combat operations.

During the month, 423 people were arrested for crimes committed in flagrante delicto, while 649 were brought to police stations for identification, and 25 preventive detention cases were recorded. Authorities also inspected 6,039 individuals and 11,029 vehicles as part of wider control measures.

These results stem from a series of operations including preventive policing, inspections, criminal investigations, public order maintenance, and customs control. The institution stated the data reflects its commitment to fighting crime and reinforcing community safety.

Why it mattersThe operations directly affect the safety and security of Cape Verdean communities.

Cape Verde and China mark 50 years of ties with new cooperation plans

Prime Minister Francisco Carvalho says the partnership will focus on the blue economy, renewable energy and connectivity.

Cape Verde and China marked 50 years of diplomatic relations in 2026, and Prime Minister Francisco Carvalho called for a new phase of the bilateral partnership. He named the blue economy, renewable energy, connectivity, the primary sector and creative industries as priority areas for the coming years.

Speaking in Praia at a ceremony held by the Chinese Embassy in Cape Verde, Carvalho described the five decades of relations as a "solid, continuous and promising" path. He cited Chinese help in building and improving infrastructure and equipment linked to public administration, culture and citizenship, and he credited the Chinese community in the country with boosting economic activity and creating jobs.

Carvalho pointed to the elevation of relations to a Strategic Partnership in 2024 and the recent signing of an Economic Partnership Agreement as steps that open the way for the next phase. Training and skills development remain central to the cooperation agenda. Cape Verde reaffirmed its support for the "one China" principle and its commitment to mutual respect and non-interference, and the ceremony also marked the 77th anniversary of the founding of the People's Republic of China.

Why it mattersCape Verde's government plans to deepen cooperation with China in named sectors, and a recently signed Economic Partnership Agreement sets the framework for that work.

Two old friends share grogue at local bar

Txiku Iaia and Ntoni Txéka, friends for over 50 years, met at their usual bar for drinks.

Two old friends, Txiku Iaia and Ntoni Txéka, met at their favorite neighborhood bar on a Sunday afternoon around 4 p.m. The friends, who grew up together and are both over 50 years old, regularly visited the bar for drinks.

Ntoni Txéka ordered two glasses of grogue for himself and Txiku Iaia. The bar owner, Txutxa, happily served them, asking how they were doing. Ntoni Txéka complimented Txutxa on his appearance, noting he looked happy and healthy.

Txiku Iaia then expressed that he had slept poorly, experiencing chest pain. Ntoni Txéka dismissed this, stating Txiku Iaia had the health of an iron man and had never been sick since childhood.

Why it mattersThe story describes a social interaction between two elderly friends in Cape Verde.
