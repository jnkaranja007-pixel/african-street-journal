Neves wins Cape Verde presidential vote with nearly 64%

Preliminary counts from 98% of ballots give the incumbent José Maria Neves a first-round lead over Joana Rosa, who has 31.5%.

José Maria Neves, the incumbent president standing for re-election, has won Cape Verde's presidential election held on Sunday, 15 November, according to preliminary results. With 98% of ballots counted, Neves has nearly 64% of the vote, while his opponent Joana Rosa has 31.5%.

Neves voted at the Catholic University School of Cape Verde in Praia. He said he was proud of the country and its democracy, and urged citizens to vote, saying that only those who vote decide the outcome. He described the campaign as one of democratic debate and said it had shown the president's role as arbiter and moderator of the political system.

Neves said his campaign had aimed to win outright in the first round. He planned to visit his late mother's home in Pedra Barro, Santa Catarina, on Santiago island, then join his family for lunch before returning to Praia to follow the results.

Why it mattersA first-round win would keep Neves in office without a runoff, giving the country a known head of state for the next term.

Police destroy three cannabis plantations in Picos Acima

Judicial police destroyed the cannabis plants on three agricultural plots in Picos Acima on Thursday, November 12.

The Polícia Judiciária (PJ) destroyed three cannabis plantations in Picos Acima on Thursday, November 12. The operation was carried out by the Criminal Investigation Department of Assomada, working with the Santa Catarina Public Prosecutor's Office.

According to a PJ statement, cited by Inforpress, the operation targeted three agricultural plots used to grow cannabis. The plants were destroyed on the spot.

The operation is one of a series of actions by authorities against the cultivation and trafficking of narcotics.

Why it mattersOwners of the three plots lose their crops, and the plants were destroyed on the spot rather than removed.

Morna Fest expands to seven Cape Verde municipalities this year

The 13th edition opens on November 20 in Assomada and ends on December 12 in Mindelo, with 15 artists confirmed so far.

The 13th edition of the Morna Fest will visit seven municipalities, starting on November 20 in Assomada and closing on December 12 in Mindelo. Sal hosts it on November 27, São Nicolau on November 28, Boa Vista on December 5, São Filipe on Fogo on December 7 and Praia on December 11. Last year's 12th edition reached only four municipalities.

Júlio do Rosário, managing partner of Boa Música and the event's organiser, told Expresso das Ilhas that islanders are already waiting for the festival and that many young people want to sing morna. He said far more people have asked to take part than the festival can accommodate. The edition will honour the musician Mário Marta, who took part in three previous editions.

Rosário said organisers plan 10 municipalities in 2027 and have a request from the Ribeira Grande council on Santo Antão. Porto Novo already hosted the festival in June. The councils of São Domingos, Tarrafal de Santiago and the island of Maio asked to postpone their shows to next year.

Why it mattersIslanders in seven municipalities will see Morna Fest concerts on dates from November 20 to December 12, and the organiser plans 10 municipalities in 2027.
