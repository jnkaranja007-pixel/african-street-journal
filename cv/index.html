Cape Verde to install five mammography machines by 2027

The government plans to increase diagnostic capacity for stomach cancer and other diseases.

Cape Verde plans to significantly boost early cancer detection by 2027, aiming to install at least five mammography machines across the country. Currently, only one mammograph is functional at Hospital Agostinho Neto, hindering national screening efforts for breast cancer. Health Minister Lúcio Fernandes announced the initiative on October 30, 2026, during the inauguration of the Oncology Unit at Hospital Baptista de Sousa in São Vicente.

The new Oncology Unit at Hospital Baptista de Sousa will initially serve approximately three breast cancer patients. This facility aims to bring cancer treatments closer to patients in the Barlavento region, reducing the need for costly and emotionally taxing evacuations to Santiago. The government also intends to increase the number of CT scan machines from two to eight by next year and enhance stomach cancer diagnosis through more endoscopy and colonoscopy procedures.

To support these efforts, two Cuban gastroenterologists are expected to arrive soon to conduct diagnostic procedures on various islands. The Ministry of Health also plans to introduce a radiotherapy service within the current legislative term. 28 Cuban specialist doctors, including nephrologists, will join the workforce, alongside training for 50 Cape Verdean doctors in Brazil.

Why it mattersCape Verdeans will benefit from earlier cancer diagnoses and more accessible treatment, reducing the need for travel for medical care.

Health · 2026-10-30

Cape Verde radiation levels within safe limits

ARME reports non-ionizing radiation from radiofrequency stations in Praia poses no public health danger.

The multi-sector economic regulator ARME has confirmed that non-ionizing radiation (RNI) levels in Cape Verde's capital, Praia, are within internationally recommended limits. A monitoring campaign across 77 points in the city during 2026 found exposure levels from radiofrequency stations to be safe for public health.

ARME's report states that all 77 measured locations in Praia met the exposure limits, which range from 28 V/m to 61 V/m depending on frequency, with most sites showing values significantly below these thresholds. The agency also monitored other islands, with results expected later this year.

This initiative, part of ARME's role in managing the radioelectric spectrum, aims to track population exposure from radiofrequency stations used for mobile communications, radio, and television. The findings align with guidelines from the International Commission on Non-Ionizing Radiation Protection (ICNIRP) and the World Health Organization (WHO).

Why it mattersResidents of Praia are assured that radiation from common communication technologies does not pose a health risk.

Business · 2026-10-30

Banco BAI Cabo Verde launches mobile bank branch

The first mobile banking unit in Cape Verde will bring services to different locations.

Banco BAI Cabo Verde (BAICV) launched the country's first mobile bank branch on Friday, October 30.

The mobile branch aims to increase financial inclusion and customer proximity by bringing banking services to various locations, according to a BAICV statement.

The inauguration ceremony took place at the parking lot of the Banco Central de Cabo Verde in Praia and was presided over by the Governor of the Central Bank, Óscar Santos.

Politics · 2026-10-30

Praia mayor seeks special status for capital city

Fernando Pinto argues for differentiated treatment of Praia due to the costs of its capital status.

Fernando Pinto, mayor of Praia, is calling for the capital city to receive "differentiated treatment" to acknowledge its status. He stated that Praia should be seen and treated as the capital, not just as a municipality, and be an active part of national solutions. Pinto advocates for balanced investment across all of Cape Verde's municipalities to prevent excessive migration to Praia.

Pinto explained that investing in other municipalities would reduce the "avalanche of rural exodus" to Praia. He also noted that Praia currently hosts the Presidency, the Government, major companies, universities, and all embassies. He believes that investing in inter-island connectivity, such as enabling travel between Praia and Maio for work, is a priority for balanced national development.

The mayor emphasized the need to reinforce collaboration with the National Institute for Territorial Management. This initiative is part of the World Cities Day celebrations, aiming to foster dialogue on urban development challenges in Cape Verde. Pinto stressed that improved connectivity would resolve many urban issues and lead to more equitable development.

Culture · 2026-10-30

Cape Verde hosts weekend of music and arts

Events include magazine launch, concerts, and a festival in Praia and São Domingos.

Cape Verde will host a weekend of cultural events across Praia and São Domingos.

Friday in Praia features the launch of Revista Magazine honoring José Luís Hopffer Almada at SOCA at 18h00, a concert by Manuel de Candinho at the Centro Cultural Português at 19h00, and multiple music performances including Rabecada, Batuque, Triu di Fogu, and Nataniel Simas at various venues starting at 20h30.

Saturday includes Tocatina music in São Domingos at 20h00 and live music at Nice Kriola and Seven Beach Club in Praia from 20h30, with more artists at Quintal da Música at 21h00. Sunday features the IV Festival di Midju – Rui Vaz 2026 in São Domingos at 11h00, followed by an afternoon of Tocatina with Zé Viola at 16h00.
