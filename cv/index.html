Prime Minister proposes national education pact for stability

Prime Minister Francisco Carvalho advocates for a national education pact to ensure stability and prevent policy changes with each new government or minister.

Prime Minister Francisco Carvalho emphasized education as the foundation for resolving social and economic crises during the official inauguration of new leadership bodies across the country. Speaking at the expanded Council of the Ministry of Education, Professional Training, Higher Education, Science, and Innovation, Carvalho highlighted the need for a system that prepares citizens for personal fulfillment and common development.

Carvalho stressed that education must be a lifelong process that solidifies knowledge and the capacity to learn and evolve. He stated that schools must fully play their role, requiring dialogue and openness, and that the plan demands listening to teachers, students, families, universities, businesses, and unions, along with the courage to change what does not serve the country.

The Prime Minister announced reforms focusing on pre-school, higher education, and professional training, including the regulation of ending public higher education fees and creating new models for private education scholarships. He promised that the success of the Ministry of Education would be measured by concrete results and changes in citizens' lives, aiming for education to break cycles of poverty and exclusion.

Why it mattersCitizens will be affected by proposed changes to education policy, including the elimination of public higher education fees and new scholarship models for private education.

Cape Verde Basketball Team Faces Three Qualifiers for FIBA World Cup 2027

The Cape Verdean national basketball team is set to compete in three demanding qualification matches from August 27 to August 30, aiming for a spot in the FIBA World Cup Qatar.

The Cape Verdean Basketball Federation (FCBB) announced that the national team will play three high-stakes games in the qualification window. The first match is scheduled for August 27 against Tunisia, followed by a game on August 28 against Guinea-Conakry, and concluding on August 30 against Nigeria.

According to the FCBB, this qualification phase is particularly important for the national team's aspirations to advance to the next stage of the competition. The federation emphasized that the team's objective is to keep alive their goal of reaching the FIBA World Cup Qatar 2027.

National coach Emanuel Trovoada has called up a squad for this mission. The FCBB expressed confidence in the selected group and the national technical staff, highlighting the commitment, quality, and sense of mission among the athletes representing Cape Verde in this international competition.

Why it mattersCape Verdean basketball fans and athletes are directly affected by these qualification games, as their outcome determines the team's path to the FIBA World Cup 2027.

ABC da Praia wins Cape Verde men's senior basketball championship

The ABC da Praia team from Santiago Sul secured the national title by defeating Académica do Mindelo 82-66 in the final held at Pavilhão Vavá Duarte.

ABC da Praia, representing Santiago Sul, claimed the Cape Verde men's senior basketball championship for the 2025/2026 season on Sunday, August 23. They triumphed over Académica do Mindelo from São Vicente with a final score of 82-66 in the championship game played in Praia. This victory marks ABC da Praia as the successor to Real Sociedade, the previous national champion.

The home team established an early lead, winning the first period 26-14, which proved crucial for the final outcome. They maintained their performance throughout the match, also securing wins in the third (23-22) and fourth (18-15) periods, with a 15-15 tie in the second period.

The championship, organized by the Cape Verdean Basketball Federation, took place from August 17 to August 23 at Pavilhão Vavá Duarte in Praia. Seven teams participated, divided into two groups, with ABC da Praia advancing through the semi-finals by defeating Pantera Negra 101-61.

Why it mattersBasketball fans and local residents in Praia and Santiago Sul are directly affected by this win, as their team has brought home the national championship title.

António Costa urges EU leaders to agree on budget by year-end

António Costa, President of the European Council, began a tour of European capitals to push for a political agreement on the EU's long-term budget for 2028-2034 by the end of 2026.

António Costa called on European leaders to approach the final stages of the budget debate with an open mind and a spirit of collective responsibility. He emphasized that the budget, covering 2028-2037, is a political choice about the Europe they want to build together, not just numbers. This autumn is considered decisive for the EU, with the approval of the European budget being one of the most important decisions of this political cycle.

The former Portuguese Prime Minister stated that the next Multiannual Financial Framework for 2028-2034 should prioritize competitiveness, industrial transformation, defense, security, and strategic resilience, reflecting the demands of difficult times. He also stressed the need for effective use of funds for cohesion policy and common agricultural policy, alongside reasonable contributions from member states.

Costa warned against interruptions in EU funding in 2028, advocating for a balanced and ambitious package of new own resources, or new EU revenues. He is visiting all member states except Sweden until September seventeen, following similar tours in 2024 and 2025, to gather input from heads of government and state on key challenges for the upcoming budget negotiations.

Why it mattersThis story affects European citizens, including those in Cape Verde, as it outlines the financial framework and policy priorities that will shape the European Union's direction and funding for the next decade.

Cape Verde considers China partnership for electric vehicle transition

Cape Verdean officials are urged to explore a strategic partnership with China to accelerate the country's shift to electric mobility, leveraging China's competitive production.

Cape Verde is considering a strategic partnership with China to advance its transition to electric mobility, according to A Nacao. The proposal suggests that Cape Verde could benefit from China's position as a leading global producer of electric vehicles, which offers a wide range of models at competitive prices.

This collaboration could help Cape Verde, a nation that imports most of its consumables and spends significant resources on fuel imports, to meet its environmental commitments. The move would transform electric mobility from a distant promise into a concrete policy, starting with state fleets, public companies, taxis, rental cars, and collective transport.

The partnership would involve not only importing vehicles but also establishing charging stations, training technicians, providing maintenance, and facilitating knowledge transfer. This initiative is seen as a way to integrate electric mobility into Cape Verde's sustainable development strategy, alongside increased solar and wind energy production.

Why it mattersCape Verdean citizens and businesses would benefit from reduced fuel costs and a more sustainable transportation system if the country transitions to electric mobility.
