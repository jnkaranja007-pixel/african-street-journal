Cabo Verde receives new ATR 72-600 aircraft

The new plane will improve inter-island travel and operational stability for the airline.

Cabo Verde received a new ATR 72-600 aircraft, presented at the Nelson Mandela International Airport in Praia. The new plane, acquired new, is intended to strengthen inter-island air links.

Manuel Lima, president of LACV, stated that the new aircraft is part of a contract for two units, with a fourth expected in December. He explained that operating with four aircraft will lead to greater operational stability and reduce service interruptions.

The new ATR 72-600 features improvements in maintenance, performance, fuel consumption, and baggage capacity compared to the previous model. The airline expects the aircraft to begin operations soon, pending administrative procedures.

Why it mattersThe new aircraft aims to ensure that the archipelago's insularity does not hinder development, family unity, and socio-economic cohesion.

Banco de Cabo Verde seeks mechanical maintenance firm

The central bank is launching a national tender for a 12-month contract to service its mechanical installations.

The Banco de Cabo Verde (BCV) is seeking a specialized company to provide mechanical maintenance services for its facilities in Praia. The national public tender, number 14/2026, was announced by the central bank, located at Avenida OUA, n.º 2, in Achada de Santo António.

The contract duration is set for 12 months, with the possibility of renewal for up to three years based on positive service evaluations. The BCV's Board of Directors will make the final decision on awarding the contract, with the Department of Property, Security, and Administration managing the procurement process.

Companies interested in bidding can obtain the tender documents by emailing contratacaopublicabcv@bcv.cv, referencing 'Solicitação documentos CP 14/2026_Manutenção Instalações Mecânicas' in the subject line. The services will be performed at the BCV headquarters in Praia.

Why it mattersLocal businesses have an opportunity to secure a contract for mechanical maintenance services at the Banco de Cabo Verde.

Electra faces power cuts due to lack of investment

The new Electra board cites a significant financial imbalance and broken equipment as causes for ongoing energy outages.

The new board of Sociedades ELECTRA has identified a lack of essential investments since 2015 and breakdowns in three production units at the Palmarejo plant in Praia as the main reasons for the ongoing power cuts affecting customers. The team, led by Pedro Lima da Rocha, also warned of a significant financial imbalance but pledged to progressively restore ELECTRA's operational capacity.

Beyond the technical, operational, commercial, and financial issues, the board also noted deteriorating working conditions and staff demotivation. They stated that under normal circumstances, the installed thermal capacity would be enough to meet peak demand, even without renewable energy contributions.

However, the board found that all thermal capacity installed in Palmarejo was incorporated by 2015, with insufficient investments since then to renew, modernize, or expand production. Currently, only 45,500 kW of the 70,800 kW installed thermal capacity is available, with three production units offline due to breakdowns and equipment degradation.

Why it mattersCustomers in Praia are experiencing power cuts due to the company's inability to meet demand because of a lack of investment and equipment failures.

Two youths held for alleged theft in Santa Catarina

Police arrested two men, aged 20 and 23, on November 5 for suspected qualified theft.

Two young men from Santa Catarina de Santiago are in preventive detention following an investigation into alleged qualified theft. The Public Prosecutor's Office announced on Saturday that the two individuals, aged 20 and 23, were arrested by the National Police on November 5. They were detained under arrest warrants issued by the Public Ministry.

The arrests were made outside of flagrante delicto, based on warrants issued by the Public Ministry. Following their initial judicial interrogation, the court ordered preventive detention for both men. This decision was made at the request of the Public Ministry.

The investigation into the case remains ongoing at the Public Prosecutor's Office of the Santa Catarina de Santiago District. The judicial process is currently under seal, meaning details are not publicly available.

Why it mattersTwo young men in Santa Catarina de Santiago face preventive detention for alleged qualified theft.
