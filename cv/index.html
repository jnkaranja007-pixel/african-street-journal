Authorities suspend three Praia businesses over hygiene and safety violations

Three retail establishments in Praia were suspended following inspections that uncovered health risks, expired products, and illegal alcohol sales.

Three retail businesses in Praia have been suspended due to poor hygiene and other irregularities identified during an inspection operation by the Regional Command Santiago Sul and Maio. Authorities stated that conditions in some of the inspected spaces posed risks to consumer health. The operation also led to the seizure of expired products, alcoholic beverages without fiscal seals, and illegally produced aguardente known as “garrafinha.”

Further irregularities included the absence of price displays and operating hours, lack of business licenses, and missing health cards for employees. Inspectors also found improper food handling, inadequate labeling, pest infestations, poor ventilation, overcrowding, and accumulated waste. Additionally, some staff lacked proper training, and prohibited products were being sold.

The Inspectorate General of Economic Activities (IGAE) urged economic operators to comply with legal requirements for their activities. They warned that non-compliance could endanger consumer health and result in legal penalties, following the inspection of about two dozen retail establishments that concluded in the early evening.

Why it mattersConsumers in Praia are directly affected by the suspension of businesses and seizure of unsafe products, which aims to protect their health and ensure fair trade practices.

Cape Verde basketball team begins World Cup qualifiers without two key players

The national senior basketball team faces Tunisia today in Radès, Tunisia, for the first of three matches in the fourth qualification window for the 2027 World Cup in Qatar.

Cape Verde's national basketball team starts its penultimate qualification window for the 2027 World Cup today, missing key players Edy Tavares due to a prolonged injury and Sander, a last-minute absence. The team, which recently completed an intensive training period in Praia, is now in Tunisia for these crucial games.

National coach Emanuel Mané Trovoada stated the team is mentally strong and prepared, despite the pressure. The team understands the responsibility these three games hold for their World Cup aspirations. The Cape Verdean Basketball Federation (FCBB) president, José Semedo, acknowledged the difficulty but believes qualification is achievable.

Following today's match against Tunisia, Cape Verde will play Guinea-Conakry tomorrow, Friday, the twenty-eighth, and conclude the fourth window on August thirtieth against Nigeria. Positive results in these matches are essential for Cape Verde to remain in contention for a spot in the FIBA World Cup 2027.

Why it mattersCape Verdean basketball fans and the national federation are directly affected by the team's performance in these qualification games, as their success determines the country's participation in the 2027 World Cup.

MpD challenges prime minister's intelligence service delegation

The opposition MpD party declared the prime minister's decision to delegate powers over the Republic Information Service (SIR) to the Minister of Internal Administration.

Cape Verde's opposition party, MpD, has voiced strong opposition to Prime Minister Francisco Carvalho's delegation of powers concerning the Republic Information Service (SIR) to Carlos Miguel Sena Castro Teixeira, the Minister of Internal Administration. The MpD stated in a communiqué that this decision is "ostensibly illegal and unconstitutional," arguing it alters the established political-institutional model for the intelligence service.

According to the MpD, Law No. 70/VI/2005 and Decree-Law No. 55/2009 both stipulate that the SIR operates under the direct and exclusive authority of the prime minister. The party contends that this arrangement is crucial for democratic control over a service that operates in an area sensitive to citizens' rights and freedoms. The MpD also highlighted that the SIR lacks investigative powers, such as questioning or detaining suspects.

The opposition party views the transfer of SIR leadership to the Minister of Internal Administration as problematic, as this minister also oversees internal security and criminal police. The MpD believes this creates a "dangerous proximity" between the intelligence service and entities with powers to intervene in citizens' private lives, posing a risk to institutional balance and democratic legality. The party also disputes the legality of the delegation itself, citing a lack of sufficient legal authorization.

Why it mattersThis political dispute directly affects citizens by potentially altering the democratic oversight of an intelligence service that operates in sensitive areas related to their rights and freedoms.

Cabo Verde Minister Secures Vice-Presidency of African Health Fund

Cabo Verde's Minister of Health, Lúcio Fernandes, was elected vice-president of the African Public Health Emergency Fund's Monitoring Committee during a recent meeting in Addis.

Cabo Verde has gained a new role in African public health, with its Minister of Health, Lúcio Fernandes, elected vice-president of the Monitoring Committee for the African Public Health Emergency Fund (APHEF). This election occurred during a meeting organized by the World Health Organization for the African Region (WHO Africa) in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia.

In this new capacity, Cabo Verde will oversee the implementation and operation of the fund, working to strengthen its governance, transparency, and effectiveness in collaboration with other member states and the WHO. The presidency of the Committee will be held by John Mbadi, Secretary of the National Treasury and Economic Planning of the Republic of Kenya.

While the vice-presidency does not involve direct management of the fund or automatic financing for Cabo Verde, the Ministry of Health states it offers an opportunity to amplify the nation's voice in African decision-making forums. It also aims to promote the sharing of national best practices and deepen regional cooperation in preparing for, preventing, and responding to health emergencies.

Why it mattersThis election means Cabo Verde will have a stronger voice in African health policy and resource mobilization for health emergencies, potentially improving its own preparedness and response capabilities.

Corda hosts first Apple and Quince Festival to boost local produce

The inaugural Apple and Quince Festival in Corda aims to celebrate local products, encourage farmers to increase production, ensure fairer prices, and reduce waste.

Corda is hosting its first Apple and Quince Festival, an initiative stemming from the community's work in agricultural production and agri-food transformation, according to Maria do Rosário, president of the Ponta d’Cinta Community Association. The festival seeks to value local products and producers, with the apple being a key symbol for the Corda community.

The event intends to motivate farmers to produce more by offering unified prices for their products, thereby strengthening the agricultural activity and reducing waste. Rosário noted that the association faces financial challenges in organizing the festival and that some producers are unable to exhibit due to a lack of products at this time of year, partly due to insufficient rain.

The festival program includes guided visits to apple trees for children and adults, donkey rides for children, and a round table discussion on innovation and entrepreneurship in fruit transformation. A product fair and tastings are also planned, despite the challenge of some exhibitors prioritizing the current tourist season over participation.

Why it mattersThe festival aims to improve the livelihoods of local farmers by providing a platform to sell their produce at unified prices and encouraging increased production.
