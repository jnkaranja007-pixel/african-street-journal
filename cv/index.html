Health · 08/26/2026

Health Minister attends WHO meeting, seeks partnerships for national health system

Cape Verde's Minister of Health is in Ethiopia representing the country at the 76th Session of the WHO Regional Committee for Africa, aiming to strengthen international.

Cape Verde's Minister of Health is participating in the 76th Session of the WHO Regional Committee for Africa in Ethiopia, a move the government states is part of its strategy to enhance international cooperation and consolidate partnerships. The goal is to promote a more modern, efficient, and equitable national health system, according to a note sent to Inforpress.

During the session, Cape Verde is engaging in high-level debates on strengthening national health systems, emergency preparedness, universal health coverage, digital transformation, sustainable financing, and health security. The country's presence also allows for sharing national experiences and best practices, aiming to boost regional cooperation and project Cape Verde as an active partner in health promotion on the continent.

The Minister's agenda includes bilateral meetings with representatives from various countries and institutions to deepen cooperation and identify new partnership opportunities for health sector development in Cape Verde. The Minister will also attend the Inaugural Meeting of the Reconstituted Monitoring Committee of the African Public Health Emergency Fund and a Ministerial Dialogue, initiatives focused on reinforcing regional coordination and resource mobilization for health emergencies.

Why it mattersThe participation of the Health Minister in this regional forum directly affects Cape Verdeans by aiming to improve the national health system, emergency response, and access to healthcare.

Education · 08/26/2026

Prime Minister proposes national education pact for stability

Prime Minister Francisco Carvalho advocates for a national education pact to ensure stability and prevent policy changes with each new government or minister.

Prime Minister Francisco Carvalho emphasized education as the foundation for resolving social and economic crises in Cape Verde. Speaking at the expanded Council of the Ministry of Education, Professional Training, Higher Education, Science, and Innovation, Carvalho highlighted the need for a system that prepares citizens for personal fulfillment and common development.

Carvalho stated that the success of the Ministry of Education will be measured by concrete results and changes in citizens' lives. He announced that eliminating tuition fees in public higher education and expanding support for private and professional education are key priorities for the new political cycle.

The Prime Minister stressed that this process will be realized through announced reforms, focusing on pre-school, higher education, and professional training. This includes regulating the end of public tuition fees and establishing new models for scholarship allocation in private education.

Why it mattersStudents will benefit from the proposed elimination of public university tuition fees and expanded support for private and professional education, potentially reducing financial barriers to their studies.

News · 08/26/2026

Cape Verde and China sign agreement to boost media cooperation

The new partnership will deepen collaboration in social communication, focusing on content sharing and professional exchange in radio, television, and online audiovisual platforms.

Cape Verde and China have signed an agreement to enhance cooperation in the social communication sector, as announced on Tuesday, August twenty-fifth, by the Ministry of Justice, Presidency of the Council of Ministers, Parliamentary Affairs and Social Communication. The agreement establishes new possibilities for exchange, particularly in sharing content and programs across radio, television, and online audiovisual platforms.

The accord was signed by Clóvis Silva, the Minister of Justice, Presidency of the Council of Ministers, Parliamentary Affairs and Social Communication, during his participation in the Seventh Forum on China-Africa Media Cooperation. This event took place in Beijing from August nineteenth to twenty-first, under the theme “Sharing development opportunities, shaping the audiovisual future.”

Beyond content sharing, the partnership includes the exchange of experiences and knowledge among professionals, as well as training and capacity-building initiatives in social communication. It also covers technological innovation and digital transformation, reflecting the rapid evolution of media and communication platforms, aiming to create new collaboration opportunities.

Why it mattersThe agreement will affect media professionals and consumers in Cape Verde by increasing the availability of shared content and training opportunities.

Sport · 08/26/2026

Cabo Verde basketball team begins World Cup qualifiers

Cabo Verde's national basketball team will compete in three qualification games this week, aiming for a spot in the FIBA World Cup Qatar 2027.

The Cabo Verde national basketball team is set to play three challenging qualification games this week as part of their bid for the FIBA World Cup Qatar 2027. The team will face Tunisia on August 27, Guinea-Conakry on August 28, and Nigeria on August 30, according to the Cabo Verde Basketball Federation (FCBB).

The FCBB emphasized the importance of this qualification phase for the national team's aspirations to advance in the competition. National coach Emanuel Trovoada has called up a squad for this mission, with the federation expressing confidence in the selected players and technical staff.

The federation highlighted the commitment, quality, and sense of mission among the athletes representing Cabo Verde in this international competition. These games are crucial for the team's objective of reaching the World Cup.

Why it mattersCabo Verdean sports fans are directly affected as their national team competes in crucial World Cup qualification matches, potentially bringing national pride and international recognition.

Politics · 08/26/2026

António Costa urges EU budget agreement by year-end

António Costa, president of the institution that unites European leaders, called for a political agreement on the EU's long-term budget for 2028-2034 by the end of 2026.

António Costa declared that this autumn will be decisive for the EU, as leaders work towards approving the European budget for the period 2028-2037. Speaking in Bratislava alongside Slovakia's Prime Minister Robert Fico, Costa emphasized that the budget is a political choice about the future of Europe. He urged European leaders to approach the final stages of this debate with open minds and a spirit of collective responsibility.

Costa, on a tour of European capitals to discuss the community budget, highlighted that the next Multiannual Financial Framework for 2028-2034 should prioritize competitiveness, industrial transformation, defense, security, and strategic resilience. He also stressed the need for effective use of funds for cohesion policy and the common agricultural policy, while ensuring reasonable contributions from member states.

The European Commission proposed an EU budget for 2028-2034 of two billion euros in July 2025. The Cypriot presidency of the Council of the EU, which ended in June, proposed a budget of one billion, nine hundred and forty billion euros. The European Parliament advocates for a budget of two billion, fourteen billion euros. Negotiations are ongoing, with a revised proposal expected from the current Irish presidency in October.

Why it mattersThe ongoing negotiations for the EU's long-term budget directly affect member states and their access to funds for various policies, impacting their economic and social development.
