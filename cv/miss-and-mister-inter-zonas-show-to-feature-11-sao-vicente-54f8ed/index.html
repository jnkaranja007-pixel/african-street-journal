Miss and Mister Inter Zonas show to feature 11 São Vicente areas

The third contest, set for 19 December at Mindelo Technical School's auditorium, will build its four presentations around the colours of Cape Verde's flag.

Source: Mindel Insite · 2026-11-23 · 1 min read

The third Miss and Mister Inter Zonas SV contest will bring candidates from 11 zones of São Vicente together for a show on 19 December at the auditorium of Mindelo Technical School. The four main presentations of the evening will explore the colours of the national flag, organiser Sindjy Cawinny said.

Cawinny said the event is meant to place neighbourhoods on the outskirts of Mindelo at the centre of a celebration of culture, youth and national pride. She said rehearsals have been intense, demanding commitment, discipline and the ability to overcome challenges from the candidates.

The preparation also aims to build the confidence and individual expression of the participants, with the representation of each neighbourhood intended as a chance for encounter and recognition of young people.

Why it mattersYoung people from the 11 zones will represent their neighbourhoods on stage before a public audience in Mindelo.
