Cape Verde sets five new priorities to protect children

Family Minister Adélsia Almeida announced measures on violence, digital exploitation, case management, children's participation and marriage before 18.

Source: Expresso das Ilhas · 2026-11-21 · 1 min read

Cape Verde’s family minister, Adélsia Almeida, announced five new priorities to strengthen child protection at a ministerial conference in the Philippines on 18 and 19 November. The commitments cover action against violence, abuse and sexual exploitation of children and adolescents, as well as protection from violence and exploitation online.

The government also plans to put an integrated case and information management system into operation, ensure children take part in decisions affecting their lives and strengthen the legal framework to prevent marriage before 18. Almeida presented progress on commitments Cape Verde made in Bogotá in 2024, according to Expresso das Ilhas.

The conference was jointly organised by the Philippine government and the United Nations Children’s Fund. Almeida also met the fund’s deputy executive director and counterparts from Portuguese-speaking countries on cooperation in child protection.

Why it mattersCape Verde's government must strengthen its legal framework to prevent marriage before 18 and operationalise its child case-management system.
