Cape Verde import prices rise 2.9% in October

Intermediate goods rose 5.8% and fuels 4.2%, while prices for construction-related processed goods climbed 15.6%.

Source: Expresso das Ilhas · 2026-11-20 · 1 min read

Cape Verde’s import-price index rose 2.9% in October to 166 points, from 161.3 in September, according to the National Statistics Institute (INE). Intermediate goods prices increased 5.8%, fuel prices rose 4.2% and consumer goods edged up 0.2%.

Within intermediate goods, processed products for construction recorded a 15.6% price increase. Primary food products drove the rise in consumer goods, with prices up 3.4%. Capital goods moved the other way, falling 5.2%, mainly because machinery prices dropped 5%.

Export prices increased 2.2% from September to 155.4 points, while the terms-of-trade index fell 0.6% to 93.6. Compared with October 2025, that index was down 17.5%.

Why it mattersImporters buying construction materials, fuel and primary food products face the reported monthly price increases, including 15.6% for processed construction goods.
