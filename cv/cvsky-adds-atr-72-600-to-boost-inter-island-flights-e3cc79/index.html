CVsky adds ATR 72-600 to boost inter-island flights

The airline's operational fleet grows to three aircraft with the new plane, with plans for a fourth by January 2027.

Source: A Nacao · 2026-11-07 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Cape Verdean airline CVsky received a new ATR 72-600 aircraft on November 6 at Nelson Mandela International Airport in Praia. The new plane will increase the company's operational capacity and improve air travel connections between the archipelago's islands.

The ATR 72-600, serial number MSN 1829, is undergoing final certification with the Civil Aviation Authority (AAC) and is expected to be integrated into commercial operations within days. This addition brings CVsky's operational fleet to three aircraft, with a fourth planned for January 2027.

The airline stated the expansion aims to ensure a reserve aircraft for greater protection and continuity of national air links. The new model is suited for regional aviation and island markets, enhancing the regularity, reliability, and efficiency of domestic flights.

Why it mattersCape Verdean residents and businesses relying on inter-island travel will experience improved connectivity and reliability.
