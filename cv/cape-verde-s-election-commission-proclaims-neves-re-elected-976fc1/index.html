Cape Verde's election commission proclaims Neves re-elected president

José Maria Neves won 63.96% of valid votes in the 15 November election, while turnout was the lowest on record for a presidential vote.

Sources: Expresso das Ilhas and A Nacao · 2026-11-22 · 1 min read

Cape Verde’s National Election Commission proclaimed José Maria Neves re-elected president after he won 82,621 votes, or 63.96% of valid ballots, in the 15 November election. The commission announced the final results in Praia on Saturday, 21 November, after completing the general count on Friday.

Joana Rosa placed second with 40,568 votes, or 31.40%, according to the results. Abstention reached 68.33%, the highest recorded in the country’s presidential elections. It stood at 65% in Cape Verde and 81% among voters abroad: of 76,452 registered overseas voters, 14,266 cast ballots.

Neves, who ran for re-election, will serve his second and final term from 2026 to 2031. His first term ran from 2021 to 2026.

Why it mattersNeves must serve his final presidential term from 2026 to 2031 after the commission proclaimed his re-election.
