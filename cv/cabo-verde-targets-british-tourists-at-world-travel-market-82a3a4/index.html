Cabo Verde targets British tourists at World Travel Market

The archipelago aims to boost its presence in the UK market, a key source of visitors.

Source: Expresso das Ilhas · 2026-11-03 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Cabo Verde is participating in the World Travel Market 2026 in London to promote the island nation as a tourist destination to international operators. The Institute of Tourism of Cabo Verde is leading the effort to strengthen the country's presence in the British market, which is currently its largest source of tourists.

The World Travel Market is a major global platform for the tourism sector, bringing together destinations, tour operators, airlines, hotels, and technology providers from over 180 countries. Cabo Verde's participation seeks to foster business relationships, create new opportunities, and increase international visibility.

This engagement is part of Cabo Verde's broader tourism promotion strategy, with a specific focus on attracting more visitors from the United Kingdom. The event provides a space for negotiation and contact between buyers and sellers in the tourism industry.

Why it mattersCabo Verde's tourism sector is seeking to attract more visitors from the United Kingdom, a key source of international tourists.
