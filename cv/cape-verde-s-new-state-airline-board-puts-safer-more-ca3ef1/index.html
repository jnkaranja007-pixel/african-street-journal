Cape Verde's new state airline board puts safer, more regular flights first

Chairman Mario Almeida says the team will assess the airline and hear workers before announcing specific measures.

Sources: Mindel Insite and A Nacao · 2026-11-26 · 1 min read

The new board of the Transportadora Aérea Cabo-Verdiana (TACV), Cape Verde's state airline, led by Mario Almeida, took office in Praia on Tuesday with priorities of safer, more regular operations, better passenger service and tighter use of resources.

Almeida said the team would first assess the airline, hear workers and identify priorities, and would not announce solutions before completing that review. He said financial sustainability depended on operational quality and reliability, with commercial and financial decisions to be assessed carefully.

Transport Minister João do Carmo said TACV needed to regain confidence among passengers, workers and partners. Almeida also promised transparent dealings with suppliers and accountability to shareholders and the government on results, difficulties and decisions.

Why it mattersTACV workers and suppliers are to be heard as the board assesses the company before it announces measures.
