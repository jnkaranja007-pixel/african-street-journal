Lusófona University awards Germano Almeida its first honorary doctorate

The Cape Verdean writer, who won the Camões Prize in 2018, is to receive the title in Mindelo on Friday at 16:00.

Source: A Nacao · 2026-11-20 · 1 min read

The University of Lusófona in Cape Verde is awarding writer Germano Almeida an honorary doctorate in Mindelo on Friday, 20 November, at 16:00. Almeida won the Camões Prize in 2018.

The university said the honour recognises contributions to Cape Verdean society and knowledge. Almeida told the Cape Verdean News Agency that he accepted it gladly, viewing it as recognition of his career rather than a prize. He said it is the first distinction of this kind he has received.

The ceremony is the university’s first honorary doctorate since it was founded in 2006. Almeida has also received state honours and literary awards, including Portugal’s Grand Officer of the Order of Prince Henry in 2019.

Why it mattersAlmeida receives his first distinction of this kind at the university’s ceremony, which is its first honorary doctorate since 2006.
