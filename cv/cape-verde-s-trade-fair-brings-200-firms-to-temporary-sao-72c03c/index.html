Cape Verde's trade fair brings 200 firms to temporary São Vicente site

The 29th Feira Internacional de Cabo Verde (FIC), or International Fair of Cape Verde, runs through 21 November, with companies from several countries spread across 300 stands.

Source: Mindel Insite · 2026-11-19 · 1 min read

Cape Verde's 29th International Fair, known by its Portuguese initials FIC, opened on 18 November at the Lazareto Industrial Zone on São Vicente, bringing together about 200 domestic and international companies across 300 stands. The fair runs through 21 November at a temporary site provided by the company Cavibel.

FIC's managing director, Angélica Fortes, told the state broadcaster RTC that organisers had worked to adapt the venue and were arranging transport for visitors. The site is farther from the centre of Mindelo than the fair's usual location, creating a logistical challenge.

Fortes said FIC had held an initial discussion with the new government about its long-standing request for permanent premises, which she said would ease difficulties in the institution's activities. Companies from Portugal, Spain, Italy and France are attending, alongside representatives of the European Union and other international organisations.

Why it mattersFIC organisers are arranging visitor transport to a venue farther from Mindelo's centre, and the fair's permanent-premises request is back with the new government.
