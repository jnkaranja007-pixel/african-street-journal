Angola cuts main rate to 14%, lowest in more than a decade

The central bank's fourth consecutive reduction followed October inflation of 8.16%, its lowest reading since March 2015.

Source: Africanews · 2026-11-19 · 1 min read

Angola’s central bank cut its main interest rate to 14% from 14.75% on Tuesday, taking it to its lowest level in more than a decade. The National Bank of Angola said the decision in a statement.

Governor Manuel Tiago Dias said slowing inflation and a favourable short- and medium-term economic outlook drove the cut. Inflation was 8.16% in October, down from 15.7% at the end of last year and the lowest since March 2015.

The cut was the fourth in a row: the bank lowered the rate by 100 basis points in September, 125 in July and 50 in May, according to Reuters. Angola’s move contrasts with many central banks that have chosen to raise rates.

Why it mattersBorrowers and businesses face a lower benchmark rate of 14% as October inflation falls to 8.16%.
