Rui Verde says China-linked reconstruction left Angola with debt and misused funds

The author’s new book traces more than two decades of ties built through private deals, Chinese companies and public money.

Source: Folha 8 · 2026-11-22 · 1 min read

Rui Verde’s new book says Angola’s postwar partnership with China brought reconstruction loans and Chinese companies, but also diverted funds and debt the country is still paying. The book examines more than two decades of relations after the civil war ended in 2002.

In an interview with Lusa, Verde said the relationship was conducted through individuals rather than states. He identified businessman Sam Pa, his partner Lu Fong, former Sonangol chairman Manuel Vicente and former state minister Manuel Hélder Vieira Dias “Kopelipa” among the central figures. Verde said funds were diverted, many through state oil company Sonangol, and loans were poorly applied.

The network came under greater scrutiny after José Eduardo dos Santos left power in 2017 and João Lourenço became president, Verde said. His book is titled “Redefining the China-Angola Relationship: Privatization, Development and Disenchantment.”

Why it mattersAngolans are still paying debt incurred through the reconstruction model, while Verde says some funds were diverted through Sonangol.
