Mbalundu king criticised for party affiliation

A traditional leader's historical legitimacy is being undermined by involvement in partisan politics, a community member stated.

Source: Folha 8 · 2026-11-09 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

The traditional Kingdom of Mbalundu, a centuries-old institution in Angola, is facing criticism over its current king's public affiliation with the ruling party.

A community member, whose grandfather was a deacon and respected figure, stated that the king's role transcends political parties and should represent all citizens, regardless of their political or religious beliefs.

The kingdom's historical continuity as a symbol of identity and culture predates Angola's political parties, which emerged in the late 1950s and early 1960s.
