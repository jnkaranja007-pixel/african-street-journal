Angola blocks foreign technicians needed for factories

Government invites international capital but creates obstacles for essential foreign professionals.

Source: Folha 8 · 2026-11-02 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Angola actively seeks foreign investment to build factories and create jobs, aiming to diversify its oil-dependent economy. However, companies face significant hurdles when trying to secure visas and work permits for their essential foreign technicians.

Some investors have already spent large sums on factory construction but are unable to bring in the necessary technical staff on time. This delay can halt equipment installation, disrupt production trials, and lead to fines from immigration authorities.

The issue highlights a lack of coordination between government departments responsible for investment promotion, immigration, and labour. Without clear, stable, and legal pathways for essential professionals, Angola risks attracting investment that never translates into concrete industrial results.

Why it mattersForeign investors and their essential technical staff face difficulties entering Angola, potentially delaying or preventing the launch of industrial projects.
