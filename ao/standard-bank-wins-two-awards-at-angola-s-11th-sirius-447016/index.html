Standard Bank wins two awards at Angola's 11th SIRIUS business awards

Grupo Naval took the Company of the Year prize, while winners were recognised across seven categories covering performance, innovation, technology and social responsibility.

Source: Mercado · 2026-11-20 · 1 min read

Standard Bank won awards in the Human Capital Development and Financial Sector categories at the 11th Prémios SIRIUS (SIRIUS awards) in Luanda. Grupo Naval received the Company of the Year distinction, and Luís Lélis was named Manager of the Year.

The awards, organised by Deloitte, recognised companies, projects and leaders across seven categories. Banco Angolano de Investimentos (BAI) won in the Technology Programme category, Anda Angola in Innovation and Science, and Omatapalo in Social Responsibility.

The jury also gave nine honourable mentions in Social Responsibility, including to Catoca, Pumangol, BFA, Sonangol, Casais, Azule Energy, Endiama, Refriango and Standard Bank. The ceremony also marked 30 years of Deloitte's presence in Angola.

Why it mattersCompanies recognised for social responsibility include nine named organisations, with the jury also awarding Omatapalo the category's top prize.
