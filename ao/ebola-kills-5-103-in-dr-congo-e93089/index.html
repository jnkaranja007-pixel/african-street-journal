Ebola kills 5,103 in DR Congo

The Democratic Republic of Congo's health ministry reports 10,467 total cases and 980 patients currently hospitalized.

Source: Folha 8 · 2026-11-04 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

The Democratic Republic of Congo's National Institute of Public Health (INSP) confirmed 5,103 deaths and 10,467 infections from Ebola as of March 11, 2026. In the last 24 hours, 45 deaths and 53 new cases were recorded, maintaining a mortality rate of 48.8%.

Currently, 980 patients are hospitalized or in isolation across eight affected provinces, with Ituri province being the epicenter, accounting for 70.8% of all infections. A total of 2,824 individuals have recovered and been discharged.

This outbreak is the deadliest in DR Congo's history, surpassing the 2018-2020 epidemic. It also projects to exceed the 2014-2016 West Africa outbreak which saw 28,616 cases and 11,325 deaths. Cases linked to the DR Congo situation have also appeared in Kenya and Nigeria.

Why it mattersAngola's neighbor, the Democratic Republic of Congo, is experiencing its deadliest Ebola outbreak, with over 5,100 deaths and a significant number of ongoing hospitalizations.
