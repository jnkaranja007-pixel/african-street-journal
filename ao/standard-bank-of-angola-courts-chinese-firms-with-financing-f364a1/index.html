Standard Bank of Angola courts Chinese firms with financing pitch in Luanda

At a China Day event in Luanda, the bank brought clients and partners together to discuss funding options and Angola's economic conditions.

Source: Mercado · 2026-11-26 · 1 min read

Standard Bank of Angola held its China Day in Luanda on Wednesday, 25 November, bringing together clients and partners from the Chinese business community to explore trade ties and business opportunities.

Discussion covered the macroeconomic situation in Angola, business environment challenges and financing instruments for expanding companies, undertaking new projects and raising investment capacity. China's embassy in Angola sent Liu Dajiang, a minister-counsellor, while the China Chamber of Commerce in Angola and the Industrial and Commercial Bank of China also took part.

The bank describes Chinese companies as a significant segment of its client base, citing their growing presence in construction, energy, industry, trade and infrastructure. It said it aims to offer financial solutions adapted to each business.

Why it mattersChinese firms in construction, energy, industry and trade in Angola now have a bank offering financing tailored to their projects and expansion plans.
