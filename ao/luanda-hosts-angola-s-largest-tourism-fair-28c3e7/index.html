Luanda hosts Angola's largest tourism fair

BITUR 2026 aims to attract investment and promote Angola's natural and cultural attractions to global markets.

Source: Mercado · 2026-11-17 · 1 min read

Luanda will host the International Tourism Fair of Angola (BITUR 2026) from Friday, November 20, to Sunday, November 22, at the Tendas do Grupo Arena. The event, organized by the Institute for Tourism Promotion (INFOTUR) under the Ministry of Tourism (MINTUR), will focus on internationalizing Angola's tourism sector under the theme “Angola Like Never Before”.

BITUR 2026 is designed to foster direct dialogue between the public and private sectors to attract national and foreign investment for projects in hospitality, transport, and services. The fair also serves as a platform to showcase Angola's natural, cultural, and landscape riches to regional and international markets, seeking strategic alliances with blocs like the Southern African Development Community (SADC) and the Community of Portuguese Language Countries (CPLP).

A key part of the event is the International Tourism Forum (FIT 2026), themed “Connecting Angola to the World,” which will feature discussions on regional integration, visa facilitation, hospitality, gastronomy, territorial marketing, digital innovation, and new financing models. Individual tickets cost 2,500.00 Kz, with free entry for children under 12.
