Angola opens gold refinery with capacity for 25 kilograms a day

The Endiama-financed plant in Viana includes a laboratory and the country’s first public office for certifying precious metals.

Sources: Mercado and Folha 8 · 2026-11-20 · 1 min read

Angola opened its first gold refinery on Friday at the Viana Industrial Development Hub in Luanda, with capacity to process up to 25 kilograms of gold a day. The plant adds refining to the stages of the gold business that can be carried out in the country.

Endiama, the group that built and financed the facility, said it includes a refinery, a specialist laboratory and Angola’s first public assay office. The office will test the purity of gold, silver and platinum and issue official marks of quality. The project is expected to create 35 direct jobs in its first phase, mainly for young Angolans with technical training.

The country has 49 gold-prospecting projects and four gold-production ventures, according to the Ministry of Mineral Resources, Petroleum and Gas. From 2019 to 2025, 346.63 kilograms of gold were assayed in Angola, with an average purity of 93.2%.
