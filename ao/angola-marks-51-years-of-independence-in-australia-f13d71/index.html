Angola marks 51 years of independence in Australia

The Angolan embassy in Australia hosted a reception in Canberra to celebrate the nation's 51st anniversary of independence.

Sources: Mercado and Noticias Lusofonas · 2026-11-16 · 1 min read

The Embassy of the Republic of Angola in Australia organised an official reception in Canberra on November 11, 2026, to mark 51 years of National Independence. Ambassador António Luvualu de Carvalho hosted the event, which included local authorities, foreign diplomats, business people, academics, civil society representatives, and members of the Angolan community in Australia. The celebration began with the singing of national anthems.

The Australian government's representative, Ambassador Jonathan Muir, Director General of State Protocol, proposed a toast to Angolan President João Manuel Gonçalves Lourenço and the Angolan people. Ambassador Carvalho then proposed a toast to King Charles III and the Commonwealth of Australia. In his speech, Ambassador Carvalho highlighted the strong relationship between Angola and Australia, noting that 2026 also marked 50 years of bilateral relations established on April 28, 1976.

Ambassador Carvalho also pointed to Angola's economic growth, stating that the country has become the 6th largest economy in Africa and the 3rd largest in Sub-Saharan Africa. He also mentioned the embassy's consular work and Angola's participation in the Africa Down Under conference, a major mining sector event held outside the continent.

Why it mattersAngolan citizens living in Australia celebrated their nation's 51st independence anniversary with diplomatic and community leaders.
