Angola registers over five million voters

The electoral registration process, which began on June 15, 2026, has seen more than five million eligible citizens update their details.

Source: Folha 8 · 2026-11-10 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Over five million Angolans have updated their voter registration details since the process began on June 15, 2026. The national director of Official Electoral Registration, Pascoal Santana, announced the figures following a meeting with the Minister of Territorial Administration, Daniel Neto, and the National Electoral Commission (CNE). This update is part of preparations for Angola's sixth general elections, expected in 2027.

The registration drive is being conducted through 648 service points in municipal and communal administrations, supplemented by over 600 mobile brigades. Santana noted that these mobile units are crucial for reaching citizens in remote areas. However, the onset of the rainy season has created access challenges in some regions, prompting the government to deploy special transport like Unimog vehicles and helicopters to ensure continued registration.

The registration process for citizens living abroad is scheduled to commence on January 5, 2027, and conclude on March 31, 2027. Santana stated that the technical and material conditions for this international phase are being addressed. The electoral update aims to confirm citizens' presence and update their personal information, serving as proof of life for the electoral database.

Why it mattersOver five million eligible Angolan citizens are directly affected by the electoral registration update, which is a prerequisite for participating in the upcoming 2027 general elections.
