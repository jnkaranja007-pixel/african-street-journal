Kiluanji Kia Henda film premieres at Rotterdam festival

Angolan artist Kiluanji Kia Henda's new film "Karl Marx, Luanda" blends African sci-fi with spaghetti western.

Source: Folha 8 · 2026-11-02 · 1 min read

Angolan artist Kiluanji Kia Henda's new film, "Karl Marx, Luanda," will premiere at the 56th Rotterdam Film Festival from January 28 to February 7, 2027.

The film, a co-production between Angola and Portugal, imagines a country renewing its commitment to communism and features a love story with time travel, as described by the festival.

Kia Henda, born in 1979, is a multidisciplinary artist known for exploring collective memory, public spaces, and post-colonialism, and he previously presented work at Rotterdam in 2021.

Why it mattersAngolan culture and art are showcased internationally through Kiluanji Kia Henda's film.
