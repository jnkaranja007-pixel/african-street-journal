Angola tourism secretary urges stronger domestic market

Augusto Laurindo Kalikemala says tourism depends on local spending and links hotels to farm suppliers, transport firms and other businesses.

Source: Folha 8 · 2026-11-24 · 1 min read

Angola's Secretary of State for Tourism, Augusto Laurindo Kalikemala, called for a stronger domestic tourism market at the 9th Angola International Tourism Fair (BITUR 2026). He said the country should not rely only on international visitors to develop the industry.

Kalikemala said hotels need regular supplies of farm produce, meat, fish, furniture, transport and maintenance services, creating business opportunities for local companies and producers. He also said tourism can generate jobs and revenue across agriculture, industry, construction and services.

He identified stronger household purchasing power, especially among the middle class, and measures to raise productivity and formal employment as conditions for growing domestic demand.

Why it mattersAngola's tourism secretary says stronger purchasing power, particularly among the middle class, and more formal jobs are needed to grow local demand.
