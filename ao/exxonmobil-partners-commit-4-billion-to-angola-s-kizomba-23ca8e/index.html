ExxonMobil partners commit $4 billion to Angola's Kizomba oil redevelopment

The investment decisions complete the Block 15 life-extension project, after the Kizomba C redevelopment was approved in 2025.

Source: Folha 8 · 2026-11-21 · 1 min read

ExxonMobil and its partners in Angola's Block 15 have formalised final investment decisions worth an estimated $4 billion to redevelop the Kizomba A and Kizomba B fields. The National Agency for Petroleum, Gas and Biofuels (ANPG) announced the decisions in a statement issued on Friday.

The decisions complete the Block 15 life-extension project, following approval of the Kizomba C redevelopment in 2025. The agency said the project is expected to use existing infrastructure and develop additional resources across the Kizomba hub. ANPG president Paulino Jerónimo called the decision an important milestone for the national oil industry.

The agency and the ExxonMobil-operated group also reported a new discovery at the Tchihumba West-01ST1 exploration well, the block's 21st and its second this year. The well found 65.4 metres of sandstone reservoir with hydrocarbons, about 350 kilometres northwest of Luanda.

Why it mattersContractors and suppliers on the Kizomba fields will see the $4 billion spent on the redevelopment, keeping work flowing on existing infrastructure.
