Banco Sol follows up with 12 technology firms after Lisbon summit

The Angolan bank is assessing options for core banking, payments, fraud prevention and artificial intelligence with potential partners in Luanda.

Source: Mercado · 2026-11-20 · 1 min read

Banco Sol, an Angolan bank, is holding follow-up meetings in Luanda with potential technology partners after making contacts with 12 companies at the Web Summit in Lisbon, Portugal. The event ran from 09 to 12 November.

The bank said its delegation met firms from Portugal, Albania, Spain, India, the United Kingdom and China. It is assessing solutions in areas including core banking, customer identification, fraud prevention, payment systems, cybersecurity and artificial intelligence for financial services.

Four Banco Sol representatives attended sessions on digital payments, the digital euro, artificial intelligence and customer loyalty. The bank said the Luanda meetings are intended to evaluate options for updating internal processes and improving service to customers.

Why it mattersBanco Sol's technology team and potential partners must now assess solutions in Luanda across six areas, including payments and fraud prevention.
