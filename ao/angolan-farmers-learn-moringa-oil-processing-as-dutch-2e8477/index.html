Angolan farmers learn moringa oil processing as Dutch agronomist eyes Huambo factories

A training course covered everything from seed selection and leaf treatment to pressing, certification and international buyers for the plant's oil.

Source: Folha 8 · 2026-11-26 · 1 min read

Angolan farmers and entrepreneurs took part in a training course on moringa, a fast-growing plant, covering its nutritional, medicinal and economic value. The course, titled “Moringa — from Plant to Nutrition,” was held under an agricultural cooperation strategy with the Netherlands and led by Dutch agronomist Andreas Wesselmann, according to the Angolan news agency ANGOP.

The lessons covered crop health measures, seed selection for oil production, harvesting, washing and several drying methods. They also covered grinding leaves, pressing oil, filtration, packaging, labelling, organic certification, hygiene systems and an overview of the main international customers.

Wesselmann said he was interested in supporting the installation of factories in Huambo to process moringa oil and avocado oil. Moringa is widely grown and used in food, herbal medicine and natural supplements in Angola, especially in the provinces of Huíla and Huambo.
