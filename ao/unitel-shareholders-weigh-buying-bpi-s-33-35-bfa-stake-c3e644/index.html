Unitel shareholders weigh buying BPI’s 33,35% BFA stake

The 26 de Novembro meeting will consider a first-refusal right over the Portuguese bank’s shares, which are agreed for sale to a company linked to Grupo Carrinho.

Source: Mercado · 2026-11-21 · 1 min read

Unitel shareholders will meet in Luanda on 26 de Novembro to consider exercising their right of first refusal over Banco de Fomento Angola (BFA) shares held by Portuguese bank BPI. The stake is 33,35% of BFA, and BPI agreed on 3 de Setembro de 2026 to sell it to Congolian Financial Services Angola, a company linked to Grupo Carrinho.

BPI valued the deal at 388,5 milhões de euros, including a fixed 345 milhões de euros and 43,5 milhões due in Maio de 2027, plus a variable payment tied to half the 2026 dividends attributable to the shares. Unitel owns 36,9% of BFA; buying the stake would take it to 70,25%. Expansão reported that exceeding half of the voting rights could require Unitel to make a public takeover offer. The purchase also needs approvals and procedures involving Banco Nacional de Angola and relevant regulators.

The extraordinary meeting will also consider Unitel’s dividend policy, changes to 12 articles of its statutes, executive pay and dealings in its own shares. State entities hold 85% of Unitel, while private investors hold the remaining 15%.

Why it mattersUnitel shareholders must decide whether to take up the 33,35% stake, while private investors holding 15% of the operator also have matters on the meeting agenda.
