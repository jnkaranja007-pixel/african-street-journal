TAAG launches flights to Cape Verde on December 12

Angola aims to boost air connectivity within Portuguese-speaking countries with the new route.

Source: Folha 8 · 2026-10-28 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Angolan airline TAAG will begin flights to Cape Verde on December 12, connecting Luanda to the capital city.

Minister Ricardo de Abreu stated the new route is part of Angola's effort to strengthen air connections within the Community of Portuguese Language Countries (CPLP). He highlighted the need for mechanisms to share the costs of strategic routes.

TAAG already flies to other Lusophone destinations like Maputo and São Tomé, as well as Portugal and Brazil. Angola seeks to expand its network to facilitate the movement of people, goods, and business opportunities.
