Angola clears 595,3 milhões de dólares Eximbank debt

The payment clears Angola’s oil-backed debt to the Chinese bank, but the country still owes China Development Bank about 5.790 milhões de dólares.

Source: Mercado · 2026-11-21 · 1 min read

Angola has completed payment of the 595,3 milhões de dólares it still owed to the Export-Import Bank of China (Eximbank), according to a report by Angola’s Valor Económico, citing Zitamar News and a source familiar with the operation. The loans were guaranteed by oil revenues.

The payment was not yet recorded in official public-debt statistics available when the report was published. It is expected to appear in data for the fourth quarter. At the end of September, Angola’s oil-backed debt to China was estimated at about 6.380 milhões de dólares, including approximately 5.790 milhões owed to China Development Bank.

The payment removes Angola’s oil-backed debt to Eximbank, while obligations to China Development Bank remain.

Why it mattersAngola’s remaining Eximbank balance has been paid and is expected to appear in fourth-quarter public-debt figures.
