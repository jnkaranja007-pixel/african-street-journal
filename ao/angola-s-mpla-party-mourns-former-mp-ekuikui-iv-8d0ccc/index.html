Angola's MPLA party mourns former MP Ekuikui IV

The ruling party praised the traditional leader for his resistance against colonialism and devotion to national unity.

Source: Folha 8 · 2026-11-10 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

The MPLA party announced the death of Ekuikui IV, also known as the king of Bailundo, who served as a Member of Parliament for the party. The announcement came via a statement from the MPLA's political bureau.

In April 2008, then-deputy campaign coordinator João Lourenço presented Ekuikui IV with a Land Rover vehicle in Huambo province. The king thanked José Eduardo dos Santos and expressed satisfaction with road improvements and living conditions in Bailundo.

The MPLA statement recognized Ekuikui IV as an example of resistance against Portuguese colonialism, patriotism, and dedication to peace, national reconciliation, freedom, and development.
