MPLA committee endorses Lourenço's re-candidacy

The party's central committee approved the report on candidacies for the IX Congress.

Source: Folha 8 · 2026-11-07 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

The MPLA's highest body between congresses met in Luanda to approve the report for the upcoming IX Congress. Two pre-candidates were excluded, leaving only the current President, João Lourenço, as the sole candidate for party leader. The meeting, presided over by Lourenço himself, also approved the report from the preparatory commission regarding candidacies.

The Central Committee expressed its recognition and support for Lourenço's leadership, highlighting his efforts in consolidating party unity and addressing political and organizational challenges. The final communiqué encouraged the leader to continue with determination to overcome future challenges and congratulated him on the approval of his candidacy.

The committee's deliberations are considered important for the regular progress of the IX Ordinary Congress preparation. The final document also emphasized strengthening unity, cohesion, and political responsibility among members for the congress and the new political cycle.

Why it mattersAngolan citizens who are members of the MPLA party will have João Lourenço as their only choice for party president.
