Angola promotes trade at China expo

Angolan officials attend a major Chinese trade platform to attract foreign investment and find new business opportunities.

Source: Folha 8 · 2026-11-09 · 1 min read

Angola is participating in a key international trade platform in China to showcase its economic potential and attract foreign investment. The event aims to identify new business and cooperation opportunities for Angolan companies. Minister Rui Miguêns de Oliveira's presence lends institutional weight to Angola's engagement, reinforcing economic ties with China.

The trade fair serves as a space for market convergence and strategic partnerships, allowing participants to present products and services. Angola seeks to increase its involvement in international trade dynamics, promote economic diversification, and strengthen the presence of its national products and services abroad.

Separately, Angolan citizens and Macau residents will soon be able to enter both territories without a visa, following an agreement signed on September 4, 2026. Macau's Secretary for Administration and Justice, Wong Sio Chak, stated this agreement will significantly ease travel for tourism, academic exchange, and trade.
