Ebola kills 5,103 in DRCongo

The Democratic Republic of Congo's National Institute of Public Health reported 10,467 total cases.

Source: Folha 8 · 2026-11-05 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

The Democratic Republic of Congo's National Institute of Public Health (INSP) reported 5,103 deaths and 10,467 infections as of March 11, 2026. In the last 24 hours, 45 deaths and 53 new cases were documented. The current mortality rate stands at 48.8% for the neighboring nation.

Currently, 980 patients are hospitalized or in isolation, while 2,824 individuals have recovered and been discharged. The contact tracing rate is 86.6%. Eight provinces have been affected, with Ituri province remaining the epicenter, accounting for 70.8% of all infections across 67 of 178 health zones.

This epidemic is the deadliest in DRCongo's history, surpassing the 2018-2020 outbreak. Projections suggest it could exceed the 2014-2016 West Africa epidemic, which saw 28,616 cases and 11,325 deaths in Guinea, Liberia, and Sierra Leone. Cases linked to the DRCongo situation have also been confirmed in Kenya and Nigeria.

Why it mattersAngola shares a border with the DRCongo, and the spread of Ebola poses a direct health risk to its citizens.
