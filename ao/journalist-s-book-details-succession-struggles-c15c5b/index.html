Journalist's book details succession struggles

Luís Costa's new book explores the complex relationship between former Angolan President José Eduardo dos Santos and his successor, João Lourenço.

Source: Folha 8 · 2026-11-13 · 1 min read

Journalist Luís Costa has authored a book titled “A Sucessão, Bicefalia e Outras Histórias” which examines the power dynamics between former Angolan President José Eduardo dos Santos and his successor, João Lourenço. The book, set to be presented in Luanda, focuses on the period before and after the 2017 general elections when Lourenço took over as president and MPLA leader.

Costa's narrative highlights the nuanced cohabitation between dos Santos and Lourenço, describing it as at times harmonious, cold, and competitive. He recounts instances, such as in December 2017, where both leaders acted with strategic intent, revealing underlying motivations to assert political authority. The book also touches on the controversial succession process that led to Lourenço succeeding dos Santos, who died in Spain in July 2022.

Angola is expected to hold general elections in 2027, with President João Lourenço reportedly in the final year of his second term and constitutionally barred from seeking re-election. However, Lourenço is the sole candidate for the MPLA leadership at the upcoming December congress.

Why it mattersPresident João Lourenço is the sole candidate for MPLA leadership in December, ahead of expected 2027 general elections where he cannot seek re-election.
