MPLA committee voids Higino Carneiro leadership bid

The ruling party's Central Committee declared Carneiro's candidacy null and void, citing alleged document falsification.

Source: Folha 8 · 2026-10-26 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

The MPLA's Central Committee declared Higino Carneiro's candidacy for party president null and void on Wednesday. Jurist Rui Verde stated that Carneiro could appeal this decision to the Constitutional Court, which might order a repeat of the entire process.

The committee's decision stems from accusations that Higino Carneiro, a former governor and retired general, allegedly falsified documents. The MPLA also decided to initiate disciplinary proceedings against Carneiro for violating ethical and moral duties.

The facts of the alleged document falsification will be communicated to the Attorney General's Office for potential criminal charges. The MPLA's decision aligns with directives from MPLA President General João Lourenço.

Why it mattersHigino Carneiro's leadership bid for the MPLA party has been voided due to alleged document falsification.
