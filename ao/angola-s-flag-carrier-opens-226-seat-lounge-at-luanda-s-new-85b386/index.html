Angola's flag carrier opens 226-seat lounge at Luanda's new airport

The 1,600-square-metre Kalandula lounge is the national airline's third at the airport, where 29% of its passengers were in transit in the first half of 2026.

Source: Mercado · 2026-11-26 · 1 min read

Angola's state flag carrier, TAAG (Transportadora Aérea Angolana), has opened the Kalandula Lounge at Luanda's Dr. António Agostinho Neto International Airport. The premium space covers about 1,600 square metres and can accommodate 226 passengers.

The lounge has areas for rest, meals, work and meetings, and its decor draws on Angolan culture. It is named after the Kalandula Falls in Malanje province. Transport minister Ricardo Viegas d'Abreu and the airline's chairman, Clóvis Lara Rosa, attended the opening, held the day after the country's 51st independence anniversary.

In the first half of 2026, 29% of passengers carried by the airline were in transit in Luanda, the airline said. The airline's Imbondeiro lounge at the same airport was named Africa's best lounge at the Aviation Week Africa 2026 Awards in April.

Why it mattersTravellers connecting through Luanda, which handled 29% of the airline's first-half traffic in transit, now have access to a 226-seat premium lounge.
