Angola hosts AU summit to adopt new conflict prevention strategy

Angola will host the African Union's first extraordinary summit on conflict prevention and resolution, aiming to shift from reactive to proactive crisis management.

The African Union will hold its first extraordinary summit on conflict prevention, management, and resolution in Luanda, Angola, on August twenty-ninth and thirtieth. This marks the first time since the AU's founding that heads of state will convene specifically on this issue, according to Miguel Bembe, Angola's ambassador to Ethiopia and spokesperson for the conference.

The summit's central objective is to transition from a reactive stance to a proactive, structural strategy for crisis prevention across the continent. Bembe stated that the meeting will conclude with the adoption of the Luanda Decision, complemented by Action Plans and an Implementation Matrix, which will clearly define objectives, targets, indicators, responsible parties, budgeting, and deadlines.

Bembe highlighted that these three documents represent an innovation, as previous extraordinary conferences typically adopted only a Political Declaration and a Decision. Angola proposed this new approach to ensure concrete results, with specific deadlines and monitoring mechanisms for implementation, addressing current challenges like weak coordination and underutilized operational structures.

Why it mattersAfrican leaders are affected by this summit as it aims to provide new mechanisms and a proactive strategy for preventing and resolving conflicts across the continent.
