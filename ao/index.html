Unitel restores national services after three-week cyberattack

Unitel has completed the restoration of mobile voice, messaging, data, and internet services across Angola. The company announced the recovery in a market statement following a total blackout caused by a cyberattack. Call-waiting services remain offline until a later date.

Why it mattersThe restoration returns digital communication and internet access to users nationwide after more than three weeks of total service loss.

Angola expels more than 53,000 irregular foreigners in seven months

Angolan police deported over 53,000 foreign citizens in irregular situations over the last seven months. Commissioner Francisco Ribas told reporters the operation targeted individuals involved in crimes including illegal diamond mining. The actions followed an operational plan directed by President João Lourenço.

Why it mattersThe crackdown removes thousands of undocumented migrants and disrupts the illicit diamond trade.

General Higino Carneiro stated he will not withdraw from the race for the leadership of the MPLA. Carneiro spoke to journalists at the Supreme Court in Luanda during a hearing regarding charges of embezzlement and money laundering. He is currently contesting these accusations.

News · 2026-08-21

William Tonet targeted in robbery in Morro Bento

Director William Tonet was the victim of a qualified robbery on a public road on Aug. 19. The incident occurred at 9:20 p.m. while Tonet's vehicle was parked in Morro Bento. Reports suggest the attackers may have been system agents reacting to Tonet's rejection of CNE police escorts.

Why it mattersThe attack highlights security risks for officials who opt out of state-provided police protection.
