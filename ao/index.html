Angola hosts international art triennial

ART CUT'26 in Luanda explores empathy and dialogue through contemporary art.

The first contemporary art triennial hosted by Catholic Universities, ART CUT'26, is underway in Angola until December 20. The event, held in collaboration with the Catholic University of Angola (UCAN), brings together universities, artists, and curators globally under the theme “Exercices in Empaty.”

The triennial aims to reflect on art's power to connect people and cultures, promoting dialogue and diversity. Cardinal José Tolentino de Mendonça highlighted art as a space for learning empathy and interdisciplinary conversation, linking aesthetics and ethics. UCAN rector Maria da Assunção noted the event's role in exploring art's emancipatory power.

Exhibitions will take place at three Luanda locations: UCAN for the opening, Hotel Globo for a second display, and FAS Project Space featuring a video work by Sierra Leonean artist Julianknxx. The event explores the African philosophy of “ubuntu,” emphasizing mutual respect.

Why it mattersAngolan citizens and the academic community in Luanda will engage with international artists and global perspectives on empathy and art.

Angola proposes 10% public servant pay rise for 2027

The government also plans to launch a new health mutual product for all public workers from January.

Angolan public servants may receive a 10% salary increase in 2027, following a proposal presented by the government to trade unions in Luanda. The meeting also discussed the operationalization of a new health mutual product for workers, set to begin in January.

Minister of Public Administration, Labour and Social Security, Teresa Dias, stated that the health insurance, facilitated through the Public Service Provident Fund, will benefit all public employees, prioritizing those without current coverage. The fund, previously inactive, requires capitalization from the General State Budget.

Union representatives found the government's health insurance initiative welcome, acknowledging its potential impact on individual budgets. They requested the government consider increasing the proposed 10% pay increment, deferring a final decision on the salary proposal to their internal bodies. The Triennial Agreement for worker valorization is reported to be 80% implemented.

Why it mattersPublic servants in Angola will be directly affected by a proposed 10% salary increase and the introduction of a new health insurance plan.

Angola president congratulates Lula on Brazil election win

João Lourenço expressed confidence that bilateral cooperation will strengthen economies and progress.

Angolan President João Lourenço sent a written message to Brazil's President-elect Lula da Silva on Sunday, congratulating him on his election victory. Lourenço stated that the Brazilian people's choice ensures the continuation of policies focused on their fundamental interests and Brazil's international role.

Lourenço expressed his conviction that Angola and Brazil will maintain and seek to achieve objectives outlined in their bilateral cooperation during Lula da Silva's new term. This cooperation aims to strengthen both nations' economies and build a foundation for prosperity and progress.

The Angolan president wished Lula da Silva success in his mission, which was once again entrusted to him by the Brazilian people. Brazil, the first country to recognize Angola's independence, is looking to reinforce agro-livestock relations with Luanda.

Why it mattersAngolan President João Lourenço congratulated Brazil's President-elect Lula da Silva on his election, aiming to strengthen bilateral cooperation.

TAAG launches flights to Cape Verde on December 12

Angola aims to boost air connectivity within Portuguese-speaking countries with the new route.

Angolan airline TAAG will begin flights to Cape Verde on December 12, connecting Luanda to the capital city.

Minister Ricardo de Abreu stated the new route is part of Angola's effort to strengthen air connections within the Community of Portuguese Language Countries (CPLP). He highlighted the need for mechanisms to share the costs of strategic routes.

TAAG already flies to other Lusophone destinations like Maputo and São Tomé, as well as Portugal and Brazil. Angola seeks to expand its network to facilitate the movement of people, goods, and business opportunities.

Why it mattersAngola's new flight route to Cape Verde aims to facilitate the circulation of people, goods, and business opportunities.
