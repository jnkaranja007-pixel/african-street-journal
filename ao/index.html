Angola expels 53,000 irregular foreigners in seven months

Angolan police deported more than 53,000 foreign citizens in irregular situations over the last seven months. Commissioner Francisco Ribas announced the figure following a National Security Council meeting in Luanda. The operation targeted individuals involved in crimes including the illicit exploitation of diamonds.

Why it mattersThe crackdown targets foreign nationals engaged in the illegal extraction of diamonds to secure the country's mineral resources.
