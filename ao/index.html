Angola's inflation cools to 10.9% in May, three-year low

Consumer prices in Angola rose 10.9% year-on-year in May, down from an 11.6% increase in April, marking the softest reading since 2015 according to FocusEconomics. The slowdown follows a string of interest-rate cuts and a period of relative kwanza stability that has eased pressure on household budgets.

Why it mattersCooling inflation gives the central bank more room to keep easing policy without reigniting currency pressure.

Angola's cholera cases fall 23% in May to 1,742

Angola reported 1,742 new cholera cases and 34 deaths in May 2026, a 23% drop in cases and 13% fall in deaths from April, according to a World Health Organization update. Benguela, Luanda and Icolo e Bengo provinces accounted for the bulk of the roughly 4,852 cases and 96 deaths logged nationwide between January and May 2026.

Why it mattersThe decline suggests outbreak-response measures are working, but Angola remains among Africa's hardest-hit countries this year.

Lobito Corridor railway closes $753 million financing deal

The Lobito Atlantic Railway consortium achieved financial close on a $753 million financing package in July 2026 to upgrade the 1,300-km rail line linking Angola's Lobito port to the DRC border, according to Engineering News. The investment covers 1,555 wagons and 35 locomotives and is expected to lift the corridor's freight capacity roughly tenfold, to about 4.6 million tonnes a year, while cutting mineral-transport costs by an estimated 30%.

Why it mattersMore capacity on the corridor strengthens Angola's bid to become the main Atlantic gateway for Congolese copper and cobalt exports.
