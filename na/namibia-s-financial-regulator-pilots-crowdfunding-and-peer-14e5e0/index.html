Namibia's financial regulator pilots crowdfunding and peer-to-peer lending platforms

Two firms will test digital funding services under supervisory limits as Namibia develops rules for financing models it does not yet regulate.

Sources: The Brief and Namibia Economist · 2026-11-28 · 1 min read

Namibia's financial regulator has signed testing agreements with CrowdConnect Financial Services and Bellatrix Investment Managers to pilot crowdfunding and peer-to-peer lending platforms. The agreements were signed on 24 November, placing the firms in the second cohort of the Namibia Financial Institutions Supervisory Authority (NAMFISA), the regulator's Regulatory Sandbox, which lets financial products be tested under its supervision.

The trials will assess digital services that connect businesses seeking capital directly with potential investors and lenders. The agreements set operational limits, consumer and investor protections, and measures for judging platform performance, NAMFISA said.

Namibia has no dedicated rules for either financing model. The regulator said results from the pilots would inform a framework covering both, with attention to investor protection and financial inclusion.

Why it mattersThe two firms must operate within agreed limits and protection measures while NAMFISA assesses their platforms to shape rules for both financing models.
