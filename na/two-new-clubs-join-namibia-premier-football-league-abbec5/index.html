Two new clubs join Namibia Premier Football League

Onambula United and KK United will compete against 14 established teams in the 16-club league.

Source: New Era Live · 2026-11-07 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

The 2026/2027 Namport Namibia Premier Football League season kicks off with two new clubs, Onambula United and KK United, entering the 16-team competition. These newcomers face established football heavyweights, aiming to prove their place among Namibia's elite teams.

Onambula United's head coach Festus Paulus described their promotion as a significant milestone, crediting the players, technical team, management, and local community. He believes top-flight status will bring national exposure and potential sponsorship, while KK United will host Millennium Julinho in their opening match.

Coach Paulus's immediate priority for Onambula United is securing top-flight survival and avoiding relegation. The club is struggling with financial difficulties, raising questions about its ability to sustain participation at the highest level without adequate resources and administrative structures.

Why it mattersNamibian football fans will experience new teams and challenges in the country's top-flight league.
