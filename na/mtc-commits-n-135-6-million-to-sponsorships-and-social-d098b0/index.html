MTC commits N$135.6 million to sponsorships and social investment over three years

The telecoms operator says its cumulative contribution to sponsorships and corporate social investment now stands at about N$420 million.

Sources: New Era Live, Namibia Economist and The Brief · 2026-11-27 · 1 min read

MTC, Namibia's telecommunications operator, has committed N$135.6 million over the next three years to sponsorships and corporate social investment (CSI). The commitment works out to N$45.2 million a year, of which N$30.38 million goes to sponsorship programmes, mostly in sport, and N$14.82 million to CSI funding.

The CSI money targets rural education, health, entrepreneurship, creative industries, environmental sustainability and community development. Managing Director Licky Erastus said the company's cumulative contribution to sponsorships and CSI now stands at about N$420 million, and that the aim is long-term change in communities across the country, not only compliance.

Erastus pointed to MTC's inaugural CSI Mukopano as a platform bringing together government, the private sector, civil society and community groups. Sophy Mouton of Hope Village thanked the company on behalf of sponsorship and CSI beneficiaries.

Why it mattersSports bodies and community groups that seek sponsorship or CSI funding from MTC will draw on an annual pool of N$45.2 million over the next three years.
