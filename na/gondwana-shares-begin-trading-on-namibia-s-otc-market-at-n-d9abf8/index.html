Gondwana shares begin trading on Namibia’s OTC market at N$9.60

The tourism group’s shares are available through four stockbrokers, but its OTC quotation does not raise new capital and trades can be infrequent.

Sources: The Brief and Namibia Economist · 2026-11-18 · 1 min read

Gondwana Holdings shares began trading on the Namibia Securities Exchange’s Over-the-Counter Market on 13 November, allowing investors to buy or sell through four participating stockbrokers. The last recorded trade was on 19 October at N$9.60 a share.

The OTC quotation makes existing shares easier to trade through brokers, but Gondwana is not raising new money and its shares trade less often than those on the exchange’s main board. The company says buying N$10,000 of shares costs N$75 and selling the same amount costs N$55; broker fees may differ.

Gondwana reported revenue of N$625.3 million for the six months to August 2026, up 14%, while bed occupancy remained at 50.3%. Management said occupancy was below expectations, citing geopolitical tensions, higher aviation fuel costs and Namibia’s visa regime.

Why it mattersInvestors pay N$75 to buy N$10,000 of shares under Gondwana’s example, while infrequent trading can delay a sale.
