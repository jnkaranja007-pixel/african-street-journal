Three Namibian women earn 50MIA sports nominations

Journalists Limba Mupetami and Geneva De Jager and Netball Namibia representative Carol Garoes were nominated across the continental honours.

Source: New Era Live · 2026-11-23 · 1 min read

Three Namibian women have been nominated for this year’s 50 Most Influential African Women in Sports honours. Sports journalists Limba Mupetami and Geneva De Jager, and Carol Garoes of Netball Namibia, represent sports journalism, coaching and athlete development, and sports administration.

De Jager is the sole Namibian nominee in the Coaching Excellence and Athlete Development category. She said the recognition reflected the demands of balancing motherhood with coaching and media work. She also wants to use the platform to advocate for grassroots development, including martial arts and combat sports in school physical education programmes.

Mupetami, a journalist at New Era, said the nomination recognised dedication to her profession and the value of African sports stories. The 50MIA initiative recognises women advancing sport across Africa; organisers have yet to confirm the summit and awards ceremony’s date and venue.

Why it mattersThe three nominees gain continental recognition, while organisers still have to confirm when and where the awards ceremony will take place.
