UN says over 250 000 displaced in Yemen

The United Nations refugee agency reported a sharp increase in internal displacement since fighting reignited in Yemen in July.

Source: New Era Live · 2026-10-22 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

More than 250 000 people have been internally displaced in Yemen since the civil war reignited, the United Nations said. The conflict surged back to life and intensified when the Houthis seized strategic territory along the Red Sea coast last month. Displacement is rising sharply across the country, which was already suffering a vast humanitarian crisis. Matthew Saltmarsh, spokesman for the UN refugee agency, stated that the numbers displaced have reached over 250 000 between August 1 and October 17.

Around half of those newly displaced are being hosted in Taiz, Yemen’s third largest city. Houthi fighters have encircled Taiz since early October, blocking the road that links it to Aden, the interim capital of the Saudi-backed government. The most immediate needs identified are shelter, food, and multi-purpose cash assistance, according to Saltmarsh.

The spokesman also noted that as of Saturday, some 3 836 new arrivals had been recorded in Djibouti, and a further 242 in Somalia, including refugees and returnees. Following a boat capsizing on the night of October 8-9, 139 bodies have been recovered, including 109 identified, while 82 survivors were receiving support.

Why it mattersOver 250 000 Yemenis are displaced and in need of shelter, food, and cash assistance due to renewed fighting.
