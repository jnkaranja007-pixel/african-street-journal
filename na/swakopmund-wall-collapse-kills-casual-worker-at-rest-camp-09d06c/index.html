Swakopmund wall collapse kills casual worker at rest camp

Stephanus Hamutenya died after a section of a concrete wall fell on him during demolition work at the Municipal Rest Camp in Swakopmund.

Source: New Era Live · 2026-11-14 · 1 min read

Stephanus Hamutenya, a casual worker engaged by the contractor Frampton Investment CC, was fatally injured on Wednesday, 11 November 2026, when a concrete wall collapsed on him at the Municipal Rest Camp in Swakopmund. He was working with other employees on the demolition of existing bungalows at the Fish Bungalows site when the wall fell unexpectedly.

Fellow workers pulled him from the rubble and emergency medical services attended to him before taking him to hospital, where he died of his injuries. The Municipality of Swakopmund confirmed the death in a media statement, extending condolences to his family, friends and colleagues.

All construction and demolition activities at the site have been temporarily suspended while investigations and safety assessments are carried out. The municipality said it is working with the contractor and the relevant authorities to establish the full circumstances of the incident.

Why it mattersWork at the Fish Bungalows site has stopped while investigators and safety assessors examine what caused the wall to fall.
