King Tee Dee plans businesses and community projects to outlast his music

The Namibian musician, once known as The Dogg, says his shift in stage name reflects more experience and a wider vision.

Source: The Namibian · 2026-11-22 · 1 min read

Namibian musician and businessman Martin Morocky, who performs as King Tee Dee and was formerly known as The Dogg, says he is building businesses and community projects he expects to last beyond him. He describes the change in name as a move from a young artist proving himself to a businessman focused on long-term growth.

Across two decades in Namibia's kwaito and hip-hop scene, Morocky has released many albums and headlined large festivals across southern Africa. He says sustaining a career that long meant adjusting to shifts in the music market and in his own priorities.

Morocky says fans still call him The Dogg because they grew up with those songs, and that he does not object. He says he is proud of where he started.
