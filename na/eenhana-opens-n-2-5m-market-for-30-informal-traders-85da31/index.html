Eenhana opens N$2.5m market for 30 informal traders

The stalls beside Eenhana District Hospital were funded by the Social Security Commission and the Ministry of Urban and Rural Development.

Sources: New Era Live and Namibia Economist · 2026-11-19 · 1 min read

Ohangwena governor Kadiva Hamutumwa handed over a N$2.5 million open market in Eenhana, providing 30 stalls for informal traders operating beside the district hospital. All stalls have been allocated to identified beneficiaries.

The traders will pay N$150 a month towards municipal services and are exempt from trading fees, according to the report. The project received N$1.2 million from the Social Security Commission and N$1.3 million from the Ministry of Urban and Rural Development, including money for a perimeter fence.

Last month, vendors demonstrated peacefully and petitioned Hamutumwa to intervene after resisting the town council's efforts to relocate traders from streets and open spaces.
