Windhoek approves nearly N$1 billion in building plans over four months

The city cleared nearly 600 plans from July to October, with October’s 182 approvals the most in any month.

Sources: The Brief and Namibia Economist · 2026-11-20 · 1 min read

The City of Windhoek approved nearly 600 building plans valued at close to N$1 billion between July and October 2026, clearing proposed construction projects in Namibia’s capital. The value covers projects approved, not construction already completed.

The municipality said September had the highest value of approvals, exceeding N$365 million, while October had the largest monthly count. About 85% of standard permit applications were processed within the city’s 28-working-day target during October, and more than 700 building inspections that month were completed within two working days of requests.

The city said it was working to improve approval and inspection services as Windhoek develops.

Why it mattersBuilding firms and property developers have nearly N$1 billion in proposed projects cleared, while standard applications are measured against a 28-working-day processing target.
