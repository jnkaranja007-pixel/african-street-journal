Namibian banks face cash access challenges

Customers struggle with unavailable cash and non-functional ATMs during busy holiday shopping periods.

Source: The Namibian · 2026-11-08 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

August and December are busy months in Namibia, especially in northern towns like Omafo, as people travel and shop, increasing demand for banking services. Customers face long queues and find ATMs without cash or out of order, despite cash remaining essential for daily transactions.

Hidipo Hamata, who has personally experienced ATM failures, stated that banks should use transaction data to anticipate demand and replenish machines before problems arise. He noted that unreliable banking affects taxi operators, informal traders, and small businesses that depend on customer payments.

Hamata emphasized that banks must ensure cash availability and protect customer privacy at ATMs, stating that trust in banking services comes with responsibility. He believes digital payments should not excuse poor cash availability.

Why it mattersNamibians face difficulties accessing their money during peak shopping seasons, impacting their ability to pay for essential goods and services.
