Miss Namibia unveils bee-eater costume for Miss Universe

The national costume is inspired by the Southern Carmine Bee-eater, a bird found along Namibia’s Zambezi River, and features crimson, teal and black colours.

Source: New Era Live · 2026-11-22 · 1 min read

The Miss Namibia Organisation has unveiled the national costume that Charene Labuschagne, Miss Universe Namibia 2026, will wear at the 75th Miss Universe competition in Puerto Rico on 24 November.

The design takes its inspiration from the Southern Carmine Bee-eater, a bird found along Namibia’s Zambezi River. The organisation said the costume reflects the country’s natural beauty, resilience and cultural heritage, and its crimson, teal and black colours match the bird’s striking appearance.

A netted headdress and flowing train symbolise the threat of poaching and the need to protect the species, the organisation said.

Why it mattersThe costume carries a conservation message about poaching of the Southern Carmine Bee-eater into an international beauty competition watched abroad.
