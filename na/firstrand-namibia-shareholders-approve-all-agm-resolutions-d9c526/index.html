FirstRand Namibia Shareholders Approve All AGM Resolutions

Shareholders overwhelmingly backed financial statements, dividends, and director appointments at the bank's annual general meeting.

Source: The Windhoek Observer · 2026-10-21 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X

FirstRand Namibia shareholders have overwhelmingly approved all ordinary resolutions presented at the bank's annual general meeting on October 16, 2026. The resolutions included the adoption of the annual financial statements for the year ending June 30, 2026, and the confirmation of dividends, both receiving support from 88.27% of the shares voted. No votes were cast against these key financial approvals.

The meeting also saw the re-election of non-executive director Samantha Balsdon and independent non-executive director Markus Johannes Lubbe, alongside the appointment of Conville Raymond Britz to fill a vacancy. While most director appointments and the reappointment of external auditors garnered strong support, the appointment of Audit and Risk Committee members showed some division, with Lubbe's committee appointment receiving 83.38% approval and 4.89% against.

Further resolutions passed included director remuneration for the 2026 and 2027 financial years, each receiving 88.18% support, and the approval of the Reward Philosophy and Principles Policy with 88.25% backing. The final resolution, authorizing the signing of documents, was also approved by 88.27% of the shares voted, indicating broad shareholder consensus on the bank's governance and operational matters.

Why it mattersInvestors are affected by the approval of financial statements, director appointments, and remuneration policies which signal stability and governance.
