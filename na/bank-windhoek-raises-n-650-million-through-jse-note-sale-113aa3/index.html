Bank Windhoek raises N$650 million through JSE note sale

The three-year unsecured notes drew more than N$3.3 billion in bids and were priced 0.66 percentage points tighter than the bank’s previous bond listing.

Sources: The Windhoek Observer and Namibia Economist · 2026-11-27 · 1 min read

Bank Windhoek raised N$650 million through a three-year senior unsecured note issuance on the Johannesburg Stock Exchange (JSE), after investors submitted more than N$3.3 billion in bids. The offer began at ZAR500 million, with an option to increase it to ZAR750 million.

The bank said demand within and below its price guidance allowed it to raise the funds at a lower cost than its previous bond listing. The notes were priced at the South African Rand Overnight Index Average (ZARONIA) plus 1.20%, 0.66 percentage points tighter than that earlier listing, according to the bank.

Managing director James Chapman said the bank would use diversified funding sources to support growth and manage funding costs.

Why it mattersBank Windhoek now has to service a three-year N$650 million borrowing priced at ZARONIA plus 1.20%.
