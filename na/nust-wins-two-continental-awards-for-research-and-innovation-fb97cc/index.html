NUST wins two continental awards for research and innovation

The Namibia University of Science and Technology received honours at the 2026 SARIMA Excellence Awards and the 2026 Africa Startup Ecosystem Builders Awards.

Source: Namibia Economist · 2026-11-10 · 1 min read

The Namibia University of Science and Technology (NUST) has received dual continental recognition for its work in research management and entrepreneurial support. NUST was honoured at the 2026 Southern African Research and Innovation Management Association (SARIMA) Excellence Awards and the 2026 Africa Startup Ecosystem Builders (ASEB) Awards.

The university was celebrated for its leadership in advancing research management and for supporting entrepreneurial ecosystems. These awards highlight NUST's commitment to fostering innovation and development within the region.

NUST's achievements at these continental events underscore its growing influence in research and innovation management across Africa.

Why it mattersNamibian students and researchers at NUST will benefit from enhanced support for their innovative ideas and entrepreneurial ventures.
