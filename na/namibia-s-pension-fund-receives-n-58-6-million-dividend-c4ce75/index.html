Namibia's pension fund receives N$58.6 million dividend from infrastructure fund

The payout takes distributions from its N$280 million investment above N$208 million since February 2020, as the fund moves into its final phase.

Sources: Namibia Economist and The Brief · 2026-11-21 · 1 min read

Namibia's Government Institutions Pension Fund (GIPF) received a N$58.6 million dividend from the Mergence Namibia Infrastructure Fund Trust at a presentation in Windhoek on Friday. The payment was the latest distribution from GIPF's N$280 million investment in the trust.

Anselma Haushiku, GIPF's acting chief executive, said the returns had gone to pension-fund members, while the money had also paid for renewable-energy and land-servicing projects. According to The Brief, total distributions have now passed N$208 million since February 2020.

The trust was set up in 2017 and is now winding down, so further payouts are expected as its remaining holdings are sold off. Projects it financed include solar plants and residential land servicing in Namibia.

Why it mattersGIPF members have received more than N$208 million in distributions since February 2020, with further payments expected as the trust realises its remaining investments.
