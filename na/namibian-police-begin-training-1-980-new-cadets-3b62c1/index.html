Namibian police begin training 1 980 new cadets

The recruits started on 1 November at three training centres after 2 050 cadet constables graduated in May and June.

Source: New Era Live · 2026-11-27 · 1 min read

The Namibian Police began training 1 980 new cadet constables on 1 November at three police training centres, Acting Inspector-General Major General Anne-Marie Nainda said. She spoke at the commemoration of the 36th anniversary of Police Day.

Nainda said the recruitment followed the graduation of 2 050 cadet constables in May and June this year. She said the new trainees were part of efforts to meet the force’s staffing needs and improve its ability to prevent and combat crime.

The force is also building or upgrading police facilities, while construction continues at Police Village in Babylon, Katutura, Epako Police Station in the Omaheke region and Greenwell Matango Police Station in the Zambezi region.
