Namibia producer price inflation slows to 14.7% in Q3

The index rose 1.3% from the previous quarter, while manufacturing prices climbed 18.2% over the year.

Source: The Windhoek Observer · 2026-11-23 · 1 min read

Namibia’s National Producer Price Index rose 14.7% year-on-year in the third quarter of 2026, easing from the previous quarter, and increased 1.3% quarter-on-quarter. The measure tracks prices producers receive for goods and services before they reach consumers.

Manufacturing prices rose 1.8% during the quarter and 18.2% over the year. Mining and quarrying prices fell 0.4% quarter-on-quarter as uranium prices dropped 7.1% and gold fell 4.8%, but the sector’s annual index was 10.0% higher than in the third quarter of 2025.

Electricity prices increased 1.7% quarter-on-quarter but were down 5.1% year-on-year. Water prices rose 0.4% during the quarter and fell 0.4% over the year; the index stood at 100.4, against 100.1 in the previous quarter.

Why it mattersManufacturers and mining producers are facing higher prices received for output, with the annual producer index at 14.7% in Q3.
