Namibia wrestlers seek N$334 600 for Commonwealth championships

The federation needs the funds by 27 November for accommodation, meals and a training camp in Polokwane, South Africa.

Source: New Era Live · 2026-11-25 · 1 min read

Namibia’s elite wrestlers are seeking financial support to compete at the Commonwealth Wrestling Championships in Polokwane, South Africa, from 3 to 7 December. The Namibia Wrestling Federation needs N$334 600 to cover accommodation, meals and a three-day international training camp for a 16-member delegation.

The team comprises 12 wrestlers, two coaches, a team leader and one official, competing in under-17, under-20 and senior categories. The federation said the money is needed by 27 November to meet payment deadlines set by the host federation. About 80% of the squad comes from the Ministry of Gender Equality and Child Welfare’s Afterschool Centre wrestling club.

The federation is offering sponsorship packages from N$1 500, which covers one wrestler’s tournament meals, up to the full delegation cost. The championships are part of the team’s preparation for the 2027 African Continental Championships and African Games.

Why it mattersThe federation must raise N$334 600 by 27 November to cover costs for the 16 people travelling to Polokwane.
