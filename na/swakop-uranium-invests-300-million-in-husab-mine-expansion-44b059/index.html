Swakop Uranium invests $300 million in Husab Mine expansion

The company's Industrial Heap Leach Project will add a new processing method to recover value from lower-grade uranium resources.

Source: New Era Live · 2026-11-17 · 1 min read

Swakop Uranium is expanding its Husab Mine with an Industrial Heap Leach Project, a US$300 million investment to improve resource use and boost long-term production. This project introduces an additional processing method to recover value from lower-grade uranium deposits, strengthening Namibia's position in the global industry.

The project will create over 800 temporary jobs during construction and approximately 200 permanent positions once operational. Swakop Uranium Chief Executive Officer Luo Wei stated the initiative reflects the company's commitment to innovation, responsible resource management, and long-term investment in Namibia.

This heap leach project is the result of over a decade of research, with initial work beginning in 2013 and a semi-industrial trial successfully completed in August 2025. The project was presented at the 2025 Namibia Public-Private Forum, chaired by President Netumbo Nandi-Ndaitwah, highlighting private sector investment's role in national development.

Why it mattersThe expansion will create over 800 temporary jobs and 200 permanent positions, contributing to Namibia's economic development.
