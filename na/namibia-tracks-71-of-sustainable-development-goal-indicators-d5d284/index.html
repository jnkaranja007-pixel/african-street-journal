Namibia tracks 71% of Sustainable Development Goal indicators

The statistics agency’s 2025 framework covers 176 of 248 measures, up from 66.4% in 2019, but leaves gaps in three goals.

Sources: The Windhoek Observer and The Brief · 2026-11-19 · 1 min read

Namibia now tracks and reports on 71% of the global Sustainable Development Goal indicators, up from 66.4% in 2019, according to a 2025 framework released by the Namibia Statistics Agency (NSA). The framework covers 176 of 248 indicators, against 162 of 244 in 2019.

Statistician-General and NSA chief executive Alex Shimuafeni said an internal audit lowered the 2021 coverage figure from 85.8% to 66.8%, correcting earlier overestimates. Namibia has eliminated its Tier III indicators, which lack internationally established methods, while Tier I indicators with established methods and regular data rose from 86 to 135. Nationally sourced data now makes up 60.8% of the framework.

Reporting is thinnest for Goal 11 on sustainable cities and communities, at 40%. Goal 12 on responsible consumption and production stands at 53.8%, and Goal 16 on peace, justice and strong institutions at 54.2%. Across 131 measured indicators, 47% improved from baseline, though the framework measures reporting, not whether the goals have been achieved.

Why it mattersGovernment agencies responsible for development statistics must close reporting gaps that leave Goal 11 coverage at 40%.
