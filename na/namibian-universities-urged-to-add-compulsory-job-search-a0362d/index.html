Namibian universities urged to add compulsory job-search module

A proposal would require every degree and diploma programme to teach CVs, cover letters and interview skills.

Source: The Namibian · 2026-11-15 · 1 min read

Namibia's universities and colleges should ask whether they are preparing students for jobs or only for graduation, The Namibian reports in an opinion piece. Students spend three or four years and their own money on diplomas and degrees, but a qualification alone does not show them how to present themselves to employers.

The writer proposes that all universities and colleges in Namibia introduce a compulsory module on job search and career development across every academic programme. The module would teach practical skills such as writing a professional CV, a convincing cover letter and answering questions confidently in a job interview.

Citing the Namibia Statistics Agency's 2023 Labour Force Survey, the piece says overall unemployment stood at 36.9% and youth unemployment at 44.4%. It adds that some graduates have the technical knowledge a job requires but struggle to communicate it to employers.

Why it mattersGraduates would have to complete a career development module in every academic programme if the proposal is adopted.
