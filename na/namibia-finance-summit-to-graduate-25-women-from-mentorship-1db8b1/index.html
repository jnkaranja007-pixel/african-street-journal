Namibia finance summit to graduate 25 women from mentorship programme

The fifth Women in Finance and Insurance Summit will bring banking, insurance and investment stakeholders together to discuss women’s participation and leadership.

Sources: The Brief and Namibia Economist · 2026-11-20 · 1 min read

Namibia will host the fifth Women in Finance and Insurance Summit on 24 November 2026, with 25 women set to graduate from its mentorship programme. The event will bring together stakeholders from banking, insurance and investment to discuss women’s participation in financial services.

The programme supports the professional development of women working in finance and insurance. Hertha !Gontes, co-founder of Sisedi Investment Group, said financial institutions should better support women as customers, entrepreneurs, investors and business leaders. She said she hoped the summit would produce clear commitments that institutions could act on and measure.

Organising committee representative Almarie Bartsch said the summit would examine barriers to women’s advancement and opportunities for greater participation in financial decision-making. Its agenda includes financial inclusion, entrepreneurship, investment and innovation.

Why it mattersThe 25 women completing the mentorship programme gain a formal graduation at the 24 November summit, which is focused on their professional development in finance and insurance.
