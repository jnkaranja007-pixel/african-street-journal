Niger receives 53 fuel tankers from Algeria

The shipment aims to alleviate global fuel supply tensions and bolster national capacity.

Sources: Agence Nigerienne de Presse, Air Info Agadez and RFI Afrique · 2026-11-02 · 1 min read

Niger's Minister of Petroleum, Hamadou Tini, welcomed 53 fuel tanker trucks carrying petroleum products from Algeria to Agadez on Sunday, November 1, 2026. The delivery, a Sonidep order, arrived under the escort of joint Nigerien and Algerian forces.

The Minister, accompanied by the Algerian ambassador and the Director General of Sonidep, inspected the trucks at the Sonidep Agadez site. Niger expressed gratitude to Algeria for facilitating the logistics amidst global fuel supply challenges, including conflicts and disrupted supply chains.

Niger's government established a crisis committee, chaired by the Prime Minister, to coordinate responses to fuel shortages. This initiative aims to build national capacity for anticipation, monitoring, and coordination to meet citizens' needs.

Why it mattersNigeriens will benefit from improved fuel availability and distribution following this delivery.
