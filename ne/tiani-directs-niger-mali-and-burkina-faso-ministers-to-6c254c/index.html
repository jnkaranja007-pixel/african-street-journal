Tiani directs Niger, Mali and Burkina Faso ministers to advance investment work

The three countries’ trade and industry ministers briefed Niger’s president on the Invest in Niger forum, which opened 48 hours earlier.

Source: Agence Nigerienne de Presse · 2026-11-26 · 1 min read

Niger’s President Abdourahamane Tiani met trade and industry ministers from Mali and Burkina Faso in Niamey on Wednesday, 25 November, during the first International Invest in Niger Forum. The ministers were Moussa Alassane Diallo of Mali and Serge Gnaniodem Poda of Burkina Faso.

Poda said the delegation briefed Tiani on the forum’s work, 48 hours after its official opening. He said Tiani gave the ministers guidance and instructions to translate the three countries’ shared development vision into practical work. That vision, Poda said, centres on investment by economic actors from Niger, Mali and Burkina Faso, using local resources and skills.

Poda also said the ministers offered Tiani condolences over the death of a relative; the report did not identify the person.

Why it mattersThe trade and industry ministers from Niger, Mali and Burkina Faso must now act on Tiani’s instructions to turn their investment vision into practical work.
