Algeria's parliament to vote on Niger troop deployment

Algerian lawmakers will convene on Sunday, October 25, to consider sending troops to Niger.

Source: Jeune Afrique · 2026-10-24 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Algerian lawmakers are being urgently summoned to parliament on Sunday, October 25, to potentially approve the deployment of troops to Niger. This move follows a recent military cooperation agreement signed between the two nations on October 21, aimed at strengthening security ties.

The Algerian Minister of Defense met with his Nigerien counterpart in Algiers on October 21, signing a military protocol to enhance cooperation. This agreement comes two months after a mutiny in Niger, with Algiers providing military support to the current Niamey government led by Abdourahamane Tiani.

The Algerian constitution requires parliamentary approval for troop deployments abroad. The specific agenda for the October 25 session has not been officially stated, but sources suggest it concerns validating the dispatch of soldiers to Niger.

Why it mattersAlgeria's parliament will vote on sending troops to Niger, directly involving Niger's military and government.
