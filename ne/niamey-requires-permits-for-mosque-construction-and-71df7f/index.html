Niamey requires permits for mosque construction and expansion

A November 17 order covers worship sites across all five municipal districts and gives existing sites six months to have their status reviewed.

Source: Air Info Agadez · 2026-11-27 · 1 min read

Niamey's delegated city administrator, Col. Boubacar Soumana Garanké, signed an order on November 17 regulating the construction, expansion, renovation and occupation of land around mosques and other places of worship in the capital. It applies to mosques, prayer rooms, religious schools attached to worship sites and religious gathering spaces in all five municipal districts.

New construction, expansion or major changes require prior approval. Applicants must document land rights, submit technical plans and meet building, planning and safety rules. Permanent installations on sidewalks and roads are banned, as are obstructions to drains, traffic routes and property access. The order also requires a census of existing worship sites; managers have six months from publication to have their sites reviewed by the relevant services.

Violations can lead to work being suspended, unauthorised installations removed, public land cleared and fines imposed under existing rules.

Why it mattersManagers of existing worship sites must arrange an official review within six months of the order's publication.
