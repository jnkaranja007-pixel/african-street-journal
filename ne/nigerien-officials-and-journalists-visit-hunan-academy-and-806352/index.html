Nigerien officials and journalists visit Hunan academy and media park

The group toured Yuelu Academy and Malanshan’s audiovisual and cultural industry park in Changsha during a seminar in China.

Source: Agence Nigerienne de Presse · 2026-11-19 · 1 min read

Nigerien information officials and journalists attending a seminar in China visited Yuelu Academy at Hunan University and the Malanshan audiovisual and cultural industry park in Changsha on 18 November 2026.

Yuelu Academy hosts about 36,000 students, including nearly 2,000 international students, according to Agence Nigerienne de Presse. The academy offers degree programmes in English and has halal dining on campus. Malanshan, created in December 2017, covers 15,75 square kilometres and focuses on digital video, artificial intelligence for audiovisual work, television production and related financial services.

The park brings together several thousand companies and tens of thousands of young professionals, the report said. The seminar was organised for Nigerien officials and journalists to promote cooperation with China and support human-resource development in developing countries.

Why it mattersNigerien information officials and journalists took part in visits during a seminar intended to promote cooperation and develop human resources.
