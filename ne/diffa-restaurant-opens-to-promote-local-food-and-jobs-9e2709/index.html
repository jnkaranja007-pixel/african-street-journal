Diffa restaurant opens to promote local food and jobs

Hajia Oumma launched a new eatery on November 1, 2026, to showcase regional cuisine and create employment.

Source: Agence Nigerienne de Presse · 2026-11-02 · 1 min read

A new restaurant opened in Diffa on Sunday, November 1, 2026, aiming to promote local food products and culinary heritage. Local entrepreneur Hajia Oumma established the eatery within the artisanal village, intending to offer a welcoming space for residents to enjoy diverse dishes inspired by regional traditions.

The restaurant also serves as a platform to highlight local produce and preserve gastronomic skills, according to Oumma. She expressed satisfaction with the project's completion and thanked supporters, including the Diffa Regional Director of Tourism and Handicrafts, Mr. Idrissa Chaibou, who praised the initiative.

This new venture in Diffa's artisanal village combines dining with the promotion of local products and regional culinary heritage. The initiative is expected to contribute to the local economy by generating income and creating jobs for young people.

Why it mattersThe opening of Hajia Oumma's restaurant in Diffa creates employment opportunities for local youth and promotes regional food products.
