Niger shows investors sites for two proposed industrial zones

Yawaré covers 250 hectares and Bougoum 2421 hectares; the government is discussing investment with forum participants and Turkey.

Source: Agence Nigerienne de Presse · 2026-11-27 · 1 min read

Niger’s commerce and industry minister, Abdoulaye Seydou, led investors to proposed industrial sites at Yawaré and Bougoum near Niamey on 26 November. The visit took place during the International Forum “Invest in Niger,” held in Niamey from 24 to 26 November.

Seydou said Yawaré covers 250 hectares and Bougoum 2421 hectares. The government has listed both developments among 30 priority projects presented to domestic and foreign investors, and is discussing them with forum participants who expressed interest in setting up industrial units, as well as with Turkey at an institutional level.

The government is also pursuing a process for Bougoum to qualify for an industrialisation facilitation mechanism for least developed countries. Seydou said the special economic zone is intended to produce goods for export as well as domestic production.

Why it mattersForum participants who expressed interest in setting up industrial units are now in discussions with the government over the two proposed sites.
