Diffa governor opens entrance exams for 78 health and social action candidates

The internal and professional contests began on 21 November at Diffa’s Idriss Alaoma High School, with candidates divided among three categories.

Source: Agence Nigerienne de Presse · 2026-11-23 · 1 min read

Diffa Governor Mahamadou Ibrahim Bagadoma opened written entrance exams for 78 candidates at Idriss Alaoma High School on 21 November. The contests are for internal and professional entry to public health and social action schools.

The examination centre’s tally lists 56 candidates in category A1, 19 in A2 and three in B2. Bagadoma urged candidates to follow the rules and warned against fraud, saying selection must be transparent and based on each person’s own work.

He said the health and social action sectors need qualified, competent and honest staff to improve services for the public. The report gave no date for the next stage of the contests.

Why it mattersThe 78 candidates must complete a rule-bound selection process, with places contested across three categories.
