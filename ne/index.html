Türkiye seeks deeper Niger partnership

Ankara wants to boost cooperation in defense, health, education, energy, and mining.

Türkiye's ambassador to Niger, Özgür Arslan, stated on October 29, 2026, in Niamey that his country wants to strengthen cooperation with Niger. The ambassador spoke at a reception in Niamey celebrating the 103rd anniversary of the founding of the Republic of Türkiye. He highlighted that Türkiye's Africa policy, led by President Recep Tayyip Erdoğan, is based on solidarity, mutual benefit, and respect for sovereignty.

Ambassador Arslan noted that ties with Niger have grown in defense, health, education, energy, mines, and professional training. He recalled high-level visits, including Niger's Prime Minister Ali Mahamane Lamine Zeine to Türkiye in 2024, and a Turkish delegation's visit to Niamey the same year. Niger's President Abdourahamane Tiani officially visited Ankara and Istanbul in June 2026.

Türkiye aims to deepen its political and economic relations with Niger through sustainable investments. The ambassador assured that Türkiye closely monitors Niger's security challenges and humanitarian needs. He stated that Türkiye intends to continue strengthening its ties with Niger, respecting national sensitivities and strategic priorities outlined in Niger's "Programme de Refondation de la République."

Why it mattersNigeriens will benefit from strengthened cooperation in areas like health, education, and energy.

Niger leaders pray for peace

Maradi mosque held collective prayers for national stability and protection of authorities.

A collective Friday prayer followed by supplications for peace and stability in Niger took place on October 30, 2026, at the Taawaki mosque in Maradi. The event was attended by the region's Secretary General, Abdou Karim Mahamadou, who conveyed greetings from the Governor.

Officials, including Amadou Labo Maraya, a special advisor to the President, praised the ulemas for their efforts in praying for the nation's peace and the protection of its leaders and security forces. The mosque's imam, Bachir, stated that the Qunut prayers are regularly recited in daily services.

These prayers are an appeal for the country to be preserved from harm and threats. The initiative aims to implore divine intervention for continued peace and cohesion among Niger's population.

Why it mattersNiger's population is directly affected by prayers for peace and stability.

Niger journalist Moussa Kaka detained for two months

Reporters Without Borders reports that Moussa Kaka has been held incommunicado since August 31.

Journalist Moussa Kaka has been held in secret detention in Niger for two months, according to Reporters Without Borders (RSF). Kaka, who reported on Nigerien news for RFI Afrique, was detained on August 31 after leaving his media outlet in Niamey and has not been able to see his family or lawyer.

Niger's Justice Minister Alio Daouda confirmed in early October that Kaka was alive and in detention, RSF reported. Sadibou Marong, RSF's director for sub-Saharan Africa, stated that the organization is demanding information and calling for Kaka's immediate release, asserting that Niger is violating its international commitments.

RSF is calling for information and Kaka's immediate release, stating Niger is violating its international commitments.

Zinder hosts military training competition closing ceremony

12 delegations from defense and security training schools competed in the first edition of the Minister of State for National Defense Cup.

The first edition of the Minister of State for National Defense Cup, a competition for military and security training schools, concluded on Thursday, October 29, in Zinder. The event, held at the Lawan Didi Manzo Stadium, brought together 12 delegations from various institutions, including the Agadez National School for Active Non-Commissioned Officers and the Air Force Technical School.

Colonel Saminou Mani, Director of Information, Public Relations, and Sports, stated that the 10-day tournament fostered cohesion and demonstrated a shared commitment to building stronger, more united, and better-prepared defense and security forces. He highlighted that the competition celebrated discipline, self-improvement, cohesion, and fraternity among future national servants.

The closing ceremony, presided over by M. Mourtala Ibrahim, Deputy Secretary General of the Zinder region, celebrated not only sporting performances but also the qualities essential for national defense. Colonel Mani encouraged participants, noting that defeat in sport, as in military life, can be a lesson for progress.

Why it mattersFuture members of Niger's defense and security forces participated in a sports competition to foster cohesion and discipline.

Niger transfers NITRA oversight to finance ministry

A council of ministers on October 29, 2026, modified an ordinance to move technical oversight of Niger-Transit to the finance minister.

Niger's Council of Ministers moved oversight of the state-owned company Niger-Transit (NITRA) to the Ministry of Finance on October 29, 2026. The council modified ordinance n° 2026-02 from January 23, 2026, which had nationalized the company.

This change transfers the technical tutelage of NITRA to the minister in charge of finance. The council also abrogated previous transitional provisions related to the company's nationalization.

The decision was made during a Council of Ministers meeting held on Thursday, October 29, 2026, in Niamey.

Why it mattersNiger's government has changed the administrative oversight of the nationalized company Niger-Transit.
