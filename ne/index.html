Niger summons Chad ambassador after drone strike

Niger's interim foreign minister met Chad's envoy after a drone strike killed two civilians in Agadez region.

Niger summoned Chad's ambassador to Niamey on October 27, 2026, following a drone strike in the Agadez region. The strike, which occurred around 1 a.m. on October 24, 2026, in Emiloulou, reportedly originated from Chadian territory with foreign support. Two civilians were killed and several others injured in the attack.

Niger's government strongly condemned the act as a serious violation of national sovereignty and international law. The interim Minister of Justice, Alio Daouda, conveyed Niger's position, demanding explanations for the deadly strike. Niger intends to report the incident to the UN Security Council and the African Union's Peace and Security Council, seeking an independent international investigation.

The Nigerien authorities reaffirmed their commitment to defending national interests and protecting civilian populations, while also stressing their dedication to peace and good neighborly relations. This incident has heightened tensions between the two neighboring countries.

Why it mattersNiger's sovereignty was violated by a drone strike from Chad, resulting in civilian deaths and injuries.

Niger minister discusses national refoundation at university

Minister Abdourahamane Oumarou spoke on changing mindsets and patriotic values at Tahoua University on October 26, 2026.

Minister of Refoundation and Promotion of Social Values, Abdourahamane Oumarou, led a public conference at Tahoua University on October 26, 2026. The event focused on "Refoundation and Changing Mentalities in Niger: What Values to Build a Patriotic, Responsible, and Engaged Youth?" The conference gathered the university community, regional authorities, and security officials.

Oumarou defined refoundation as a matter of conscience, behavior, and values, emphasizing the need to rebuild both citizens and the state. He highlighted the roles of artists and universities in addressing contemporary issues and stressed that successful revolutions require a shift in mindsets, a focus on work ethic, food sovereignty, and solidarity.

The minister outlined five cardinal values for refoundation: excellence, protection of property, solidarity, and promoting dialogue. He urged the youth to embrace refoundation and take action for a better future, stating that youth represent not only the future but also the present.

Why it mattersNigerien youth are called to action and to embrace patriotic values for national development.

Algerian experts help Niger modernize oil data bank

A delegation from Algeria's ALNAFT agency will spend five days in Niamey assessing national oil and gas data systems.

Niger's Ministry of Petroleum is hosting Algerian experts from the National Agency for the Valorization of Hydrocarbon Resources (ALNAFT) from October 26 to 30, 2026. The mission aims to modernize Niger's national oil and gas data bank. This collaboration strengthens strategic ties between Niger and Algeria, aligning with President Abdourahamane Tiani's vision for national resource management.

The Algerian team will conduct a detailed assessment of Niger's data management infrastructure, equipment, software, personnel, and security systems. Niger's Minister of Petroleum, Hamadou Tini, welcomed the initiative, thanking Algerian authorities for their support. He emphasized the importance of this cooperation in the hydrocarbon sector, reflecting strong bilateral relations.

Following their assessment, the experts will provide recommendations and a technical and financial roadmap. This plan will guide the modernization of the national data bank and enhance the skills of Nigerien oil sector professionals. The initiative is part of a broader cooperation based on expertise sharing and resource development.

Why it mattersAlgerian experts are in Niger to modernize the national oil and gas data bank, which will help Nigerien professionals develop their skills in the hydrocarbon sector.

Niger onion day focuses on youth and e-commerce

The 6th National Onion Day in Niamey aims to integrate young people into agri-food processing and boost online sales.

Niger held its 6th National Onion Day in Niamey from October 25-27, 2026, focusing on involving youth in agri-food processing and developing e-commerce to enhance national production value. The event's theme highlighted youth integration in processing and electronic trade to better valorize the onion sector.

Ali Samba Diabiri, Secretary General of the Ministry of Commerce and Industry, stated that selling raw onions limits profits due to conservation issues, post-harvest losses, and market access constraints. He emphasized that true value comes from better conservation, processing, packaging, and distribution, calling agri-food transformation a key to economic sovereignty.

Diabiri stressed the need for training and job creation for youth in agriculture and agri-food sectors, noting e-commerce potential to connect producers and consumers and expand markets. The ministry is developing a national e-commerce strategy, with a validation workshop planned in Bangoula.

Why it mattersNiger's onion producers and processors are directly affected by initiatives to improve conservation, processing, and market access through e-commerce.

Malian press agency staff protest unpaid wages

Workers at the Agence Malienne de Presse et de Publicité have not received salaries for over two months.

Agents of the Malian Press and Advertising Agency (AMAP) began a sit-in on Monday, October 26, 2026, demanding better working conditions. The protest was organized by the union committee to express dissatisfaction with the current situation.

The workers have not been paid for more than two months, a situation described as intolerable, leading to the mobilization. They are also demanding the reinstatement of health insurance, which has been cut for several years. The union committee called for solidarity among all workers to ensure the success of their fight for rights and dignity.

Support for the protest was shown by the general secretaries of the ORTM and AGETIC unions, who attended to demonstrate their backing for their colleagues. This action, affecting numerous employees, is being closely monitored for potential repercussions across the sector and for worker rights in Mali.
