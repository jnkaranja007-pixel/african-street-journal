Türkiye seeks deeper Niger partnership

Ankara wants to boost cooperation in defense, health, education, energy, and mining.

Türkiye's ambassador to Niger, Özgür Arslan, stated on October 29, 2026, in Niamey that his country wants to strengthen cooperation with Niger. The ambassador spoke at a reception in Niamey celebrating the 103rd anniversary of the founding of the Republic of Türkiye. He highlighted that Türkiye's Africa policy, led by President Recep Tayyip Erdoğan, is based on solidarity, mutual benefit, and respect for sovereignty.

Ambassador Arslan noted that ties with Niger have grown in defense, health, education, energy, mines, and professional training. He recalled high-level visits, including Niger's Prime Minister Ali Mahamane Lamine Zeine to Türkiye in 2024, and a Turkish delegation's visit to Niamey the same year. Niger's President Abdourahamane Tiani officially visited Ankara and Istanbul in June 2026.

Türkiye aims to deepen its political and economic relations with Niger through sustainable investments. The ambassador assured that Türkiye closely monitors Niger's security challenges and humanitarian needs. He stated that Türkiye intends to continue strengthening its ties with Niger, respecting national sensitivities and strategic priorities outlined in Niger's "Programme de Refondation de la République."

Why it mattersNigeriens will benefit from strengthened cooperation in areas like health, education, and energy.

Niger officials attend peace prayer in Maradi

Maradi's regional secretary general and a presidential advisor joined prayers for stability.

Maradi, Niger – Regional officials and presidential advisors gathered on Thursday, October 29, 2026, for a collective prayer session at the Zone de Défense n°6 mosque. The prayers, including Du’a, were specifically for peace and stability within Niger.

Abdou Karim Mahamadou, Secretary General of the Maradi region, attended the Zuhr prayer and urged religious leaders and traditional chiefs to continue praying for the country's peace and its leaders. Amadou Labo Maraya, a special advisor to the President, echoed the call for prayers, extending them to the defense and security forces.

The prayer meeting also included the Administrator Delegate of Maradi city and security officials. The initiative aimed to implore divine intervention for the preservation of Niger from insecurity and the strengthening of social cohesion.

Why it mattersNiger's regional and presidential officials participated in prayers for peace and stability, directly affecting the country's governance and security.

Niger launches 2027 Bac and BTS pre-registration

Online platforms open October 29 for students seeking state diplomas, with new subject integrations.

Pre-registration for the 2027 Baccalauréat and State Brevet de Technicien Supérieur (BTS) exams began on October 29, 2026, in Niamey. The official launch ceremony took place at the Francophonie site, with representatives from parent associations, teacher unions, and student organizations attending.

The OBEECS Director General, Pr Alhou Bassirou, announced that digital pre-registration platforms opened at 23:59 on October 29. Baccalauréat pre-registration runs until December 31, 2026, at 23:59 on www.officebacniger.com, while BTS pre-registration continues until February 28, 2027, at 23:59 on https://bts.obeecs.com.

Innovations for the 2027 session include the integration of Islamic studies into the A8 series with a coefficient of 2, and Physical and Sports Education (EPS) becoming a standalone subject with a coefficient of 1 for all candidates.

Algeria to send troops to Niger

Algerian parliament approved the deployment of troops to Niger on October 25.

Algeria will deploy troops to Niger, marking the first time since the 1973 "Kippour war" that Algerian soldiers will operate outside their national borders.

The Algerian parliament approved the troop deployment project on October 25. This decision follows assurances regarding the security of the Trans-Saharan gas pipeline.

Hasni Abidi, director of the Center for Studies and Research on the Arab and Mediterranean World, discussed the shift in Algerian military doctrine and the potential establishment of an Algerian military base in Niger.

Why it mattersAlgerian troops will be stationed in Niger to secure the Trans-Saharan gas pipeline.

Algeria sends troops to Niger to protect energy interests

President Tebboune announced the deployment after parliament approved intervention on October 25.

Algeria will send military units to Niger to protect its energy interests and enact a military cooperation agreement. President Abdelmadjid Tebboune announced the deployment following parliamentary approval on October 25.

The move aims to secure Algerian economic interests south of the border and support the Niamey regime. This follows the signing of a military and security cooperation accord on October 21.

The deployment comes after both chambers of the Algerian Parliament voted to authorize the army's intervention in Niger.

Why it mattersAlgerian troops are being sent to Niger to safeguard the country's energy interests.
