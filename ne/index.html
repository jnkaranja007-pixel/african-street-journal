Niger Digital Day trains 140 youth in digital skills in Tahoua

The initiative, part of a national tour, focuses on AI and digital skills for youth, aiming to boost employment and entrepreneurship in the region.

The Niger Digital Day program launched a training session for 140 young people in Tahoua, focusing on digital skills. Governor Colonel-Major Souleymane Amadou Moussa presided over the launch, highlighting the program's alignment with national priorities to equip youth for future careers and self-sufficiency.

The training covers artificial intelligence, web development, cybersecurity, content creation, and digital finance. Governor Moussa emphasized that these skills are crucial for participants to create their own businesses, generate income, and build their futures within the region.

The Governor also noted the Ministry of Communication and New Information Technologies' sponsorship, indicating state support for this initiative. He expressed particular satisfaction with the inclusion of young girls and women, stating that true development requires the full involvement of all regional forces.

Why it mattersOne hundred forty young people in Tahoua are directly affected by gaining new digital skills that can lead to employment and entrepreneurship.

President Tiani urges AES parliamentarians to unite for confederation challenges

General Abdourahamane Tiani met with leaders of the Alliance of Sahel States (AES) parliamentary bodies, emphasizing cohesion to address regional concerns.

Niger's President, General Abdourahamane Tiani, called on parliamentarians from the Alliance of Sahel States (AES) to remain united to tackle the confederation's challenges. He received a delegation of presidents from the AES parliamentary bodies, led by Dr. Ousmane Bougouma, President of the Legislative Assembly of Burkina Faso, on August 25, 2026, in Niamey.

Dr. Bougouma stated that the delegation thanked President Tiani and the AES authorities for establishing the confederation's architecture, including the inaugural session and installation of confederal deputies. He noted that the AES Parliament is now operational, with an internal regulation adopted and three working commissions formed: Defense and Security, Diplomacy, and Development.

President Tiani reminded the parliamentarians of the context surrounding the AES's creation and urged them to remain pragmatic, work to address the populations' concerns, and foster fraternity within the AES to build a space of security, development, and well-being for their people.

Why it mattersThis event directly affects citizens of the AES member states by shaping the legislative and developmental direction of their confederation, influencing their security and well-being.

MSF expands pediatric malaria unit in Dan Issa to relieve Madarounfa district

Médecins Sans Frontières (MSF) has opened a new seasonal pediatric unit, "Unité Palu," at the Dan Issa Integrated Health Center to treat children aged one to fifty-nine months.

The new unit aims to decongest the pediatric services at the Madarounfa District Hospital, which frequently exceeds its capacity, and bring adequate care closer to patients in Dan Issa. The Prefect of Madarounfa, Battalion Chief DJIBEROU Mahamadou, stated that the unit "saves lives by taking care of thousands of patients each year."

The "Unité Palu" operates annually from August to November, coinciding with the peak malaria season. In 2025, the unit admitted 4,713 urgent patients, achieving a death rate of 2.6%, well below the national standard of up to 10% mortality. Additionally, 1,575 transfusions were performed, supported by a technical platform.

MSF provides 100% of the support for the unit, which in 2025 was staffed by thirteen doctors, forty-three nurses, forty nursing assistants, eleven surface agents, and five laboratory technicians. The unit has expanded significantly since its 2009 inception with twenty beds, reaching a capacity of one hundred twenty beds in 2026.

Why it mattersChildren aged one to fifty-nine months in Dan Issa and surrounding areas will have closer access to specialized care for severe malaria, reducing the burden on the Madarounfa District Hospital.

Business · 08/25/2026

OCRTIS seizes 37 cannabis balls, arrests one suspect in Niamey

Niger's anti-drug agency, OCRTIS, confiscated 37 cannabis balls and a Toyota Avensis vehicle, leading to the arrest of a Nigerien national in Niamey.

The Central Office for the Repression of Illicit Drug Trafficking (OCRTIS) announced the seizure of 37 cannabis balls in Niamey's Dan Gao neighborhood. This operation follows investigations initiated after a previous seizure of 18 cannabis balls in Abalak on August twenty-first, 2026.

Further investigation by the regional branch of OCRTIS in Niamey led to the arrest of a Nigerien national. A search of the individual's home resulted in the confiscation of a Toyota Avensis vehicle, which police claim was used to transport the drugs.

National Police are urging the public to increase vigilance and cooperate with security services to combat drug trafficking and maintain public safety. They remind citizens that useful information can be shared via toll-free numbers 8383 and 17.

Why it mattersThis event affects citizens by disrupting illicit drug trade networks that can impact community safety and public health.
