Business · 2026-08-21

Niger signs 126.1 billion FCFA deal for 200 MWc solar plant

The state of Niger signed a public-private partnership contract worth 126.1 billion FCFA with Niger Electricity Power Production. The agreement covers the construction of a 200 MWc photovoltaic solar plant.

Why it mattersThe project increases the national power grid capacity through the addition of a 200 MWc energy source.

News · 2026-08-21

Niamey court records 420 infractions including terrorism cases

The Niamey High Court (TGI) recorded 420 total infractions during its seventh prosecutor's meeting of 2026. The Prosecutor of the Republic reported that a large portion of these cases were terrorist in nature.

Why it mattersThe high volume of terrorism-related cases increases the judicial workload for the Niamey prosecutor's office.

Education · 2026-08-21

Abdou Moumouni University expects 20,000 new students for 2026-2027

Universite Abdou Moumouni Dioffo expects 20,000 new graduates for the 2026-2027 academic year. Recteur Moussa Baragé stated the institution currently has over 42,000 students, with women making up 31.4% of the 2025-2026 population.

Why it mattersThe influx of 20,000 new students will increase the demand for capacity across the university's 200 programs.
