Security · 2026-11-14

Attack near Téra in Niger's Tillabéri region kills at least 37, sources say

Local sources told AFP the attack took place near Téra, close to the borders with Burkina Faso and Mali.

An attack took place on Thursday, November 12, in the Tillabéri region of western Niger, an area that has been the epicentre of terrorist violence. It happened near the locality of Téra, close to the borders with Burkina Faso and Mali.

Local sources cited by the Agence France-Presse news agency put the death toll at at least 37. Niger's authorities have not issued any official statement or death toll so far.

The figure has not been confirmed by officials, and no further details about the attackers or the victims were reported.

Why it mattersFamilies and residents near Téra now face the question of how many dead the official count will eventually confirm, since authorities have yet to publish one.

Niger bans all peanut exports to build local oil processing

Customs officers and security forces may seize any peanuts caught leaving the country, and the state keeps them.

Niger's Minister of Commerce and Industry, Abdoulaye Seydou, announced at a press briefing on Friday 13 November 2026 that peanut exports are banned across the whole country under decree 05-18, dated 11 November 2026. The aim, he said, is to strengthen the processing and consumption of local peanut products, especially oil.

The minister said Niger imports cooking oil from distant countries even though it has the raw material to produce enough good-quality oil from peanuts at home. The ban adds to existing bans on exports of millet, polished rice, paddy and sorghum. Exceptional export permits may be granted to countries of the AES confederation under a set procedure.

Ministry of Commerce inspectors, customs agents and the defence and security forces are authorised to enforce the ban. Any peanuts found in an attempted export will be seized systematically and handed to the state through the usual judicial procedure.

Why it mattersPeanut farmers and oil processors now have no legal export outlet, so any harvest surplus must be sold inside Niger.

Business · 2026-11-14

World Bank pledges $700 million more for Niger over five years

The new partnership strategy, launched by Niger's finance minister, is meant to create more and better jobs, especially for young people and women.

Niger's President, General Abdourahamane Tiani, received Ousmane Diagana, the World Bank's vice-president for West and Central Africa, in Niamey on Friday, 13 November, leading a large delegation. The talks covered the Kandadji Programme, a new partnership framework between the Nigerien state and the bank, agriculture and energy.

Diagana acknowledged that implementation of the Kandadji Project had been delayed for various reasons. A round-table with all partners, held on 11 November under the prime minister's leadership, produced agreements that he said should allow the project to restart quickly. He said the bank would mobilise about $700 million in additional public-sector resources during the strategy, on top of a portfolio of more than $4 billion already in Niger.

The five-year strategy is to fund structural programmes, including a major bank-backed scheme that gives young people access to credit to start small and medium enterprises. Diagana said electricity, agriculture and water were the three sectors that matter most for development, and cited the Mission 300 electricity initiative.

Why it mattersYoung people and women seeking to start small businesses in Niger will have access to bank-backed credit under the programme.

Health · 2026-11-14

Niger hands solar equipment worth 86 million FCFA to Poudrière hospital

The energy and health ministries say uninterrupted power protects mothers and newborns at the Niamey regional hospital, which treats hundreds of patients daily.

Niger's Minister of Energy, Colonel Seyni Alzouma, handed solar power equipment worth about 86 million FCFA to his health counterpart, Colonel Major Garba Hakimi, on Friday, 12 November 2026. The equipment, financed by Istithmar West Africa, is for the Poudrière Regional Hospital in Niamey.

Alzouma said electricity is a basic condition for safe care in a hospital, especially in maternity and operating theatres. He listed the functions it supports: biomedical equipment, lighting in delivery and operating rooms, the storage of vaccines and medicines, and emergency treatment.

The hospital receives several hundred patients a day, mainly in gynaecology and paediatrics. Alzouma said the government plans to electrify 80% of health centres by 2030 to cope with power cuts and the high cost of running generators.

Why it mattersPatients in maternity and paediatric wards at the Poudrière hospital gain a secure power supply for delivery rooms, operating theatres and vaccine storage.

Sport · 2026-11-14

Morocco beats Niger 5-1 in CAN 2027 qualifier, with Hakimi scoring twice

Mali came from behind to beat Rwanda 3-2 in Group K, moving closer to a place at the 2027 Africa Cup of Nations.

Morocco beat Niger 5-1 at home on Friday in a qualifier for the 2027 Africa Cup of Nations (CAN), and is now close to securing its place in the tournament. Achraf Hakimi scored twice in the win.

In Group K, Mali also moved a step closer to qualifying, beating Rwanda 3-2 after a match that went badly for them early on before they turned it around.

Niger's defeat leaves its qualifying campaign in difficulty, and the group table will be decided by the remaining matches.

Why it mattersNiger, which lost 5-1 to Morocco, now needs results in its remaining qualifiers to keep its place in the race for CAN 2027.
