News · 2026-10-22

Austrian and Swiss hostages freed in Libya

Eva Gretzmacher and Claudia Abbt, kidnapped in Niger in 2025, arrived in Benghazi after more than a year in captivity.

Austrian Eva Gretzmacher and Swiss national Claudia Abbt, who were abducted separately in Agadez in 2025, have been released and arrived in Benghazi, eastern Libya, on Wednesday, October 21. Their handover to Austrian and Swiss authorities is expected after necessary formalities are completed.

According to reports, a plane was scheduled to depart Benghazi on Thursday, October 22, to stop in Vienna to deliver Eva Gretzmacher to Austrian officials before continuing to Switzerland with Claudia Abbt. The exact circumstances of their release have not been made public.

Eva Gretzmacher was taken from her home in Agadez on January 11, 2025, and Claudia Abbt was kidnapped in the same city in April of that year. Several sources have attributed the kidnappings to the Islamic State in the Sahel (EIS), with some information suggesting a significant role played by Saddam Haftar, son of Libyan Marshal Khalifa Haftar, in their liberation, though this has not been officially confirmed.

Why it mattersThe two women, Eva Gretzmacher and Claudia Abbt, were held for over a year and a half before their release.

Senegalese artist reaffirms Niger development support

Singer Coumba Gawlo Seck met President Tiani to pledge her commitment to Niger's growth and cultural promotion.

Senegalese artist Coumba Gawlo Seck met Niger's President Abdourahamane Tiani on October 21, 2026, to express her dedication to the country's development efforts. Seck, a renowned singer and composer, stated her intention to support Niger's growth and national sovereignty. She emphasized the importance of promoting Niger's image internationally, describing it as a vibrant and welcoming nation.

During the meeting, Seck highlighted the role of culture and music in fostering communication, education, and combating poverty and violence. She expressed her belief that art is a powerful tool for national promotion and development. Seck also conveyed her desire to contribute to the President's vision for peace, security, and social cohesion within Niger.

The artist announced plans to mobilize other African artists to rally support for Niger, stating that the country's development is integral to the progress of the entire African continent. Seck, known internationally for her rendition of "Pata Pata," has a history of engagement with Niger, citing strong personal ties and affection for the nation and its people.

Why it mattersNigerians are affected by the artist's pledge to promote their country's image and mobilize support for its development.

Nigerien military schools compete in first tournament

12 delegations from Niger's defense and security training institutions are participating in a 10-day sports competition in Zinder.

The first-ever national competition for military schools and training structures in Niger kicked off on Tuesday, October 20, at the Stade lawan Didi Manzo in Zinder. The event, named the Cup of the Minister of State, Minister of National Defense, brings together 12 delegations from various national training centers for 10 days. Participants include students from the Agadez non-commissioned officers' school, the Air Force technicians' school, and training centers in Zinder, Dosso, Tondibia, and the fire brigade.

The competition also features national gendarmerie schools, a unit from the Niger National Guard, the National Police school, and a team from the Customs school. Colonel Saminou Mani, Director of Information, Public Relations, and Sports, stated that approximately 500 future officers, non-commissioned officers, and trainees from across the defense and security apparatus are gathered for the event. He emphasized that the tournament promotes preparation, cohesion, and excellence, highlighting the fundamental role of sports in building uniformed personnel.

Colonel Toudjani Malam Rabé, Commander of Zone of Defense No. 3 in Zinder, expressed pride in hosting the championship, calling it a tradition that will unite forces through discipline, cohesion, and self-improvement. The opening ceremony, which included the ceremonial kickoff by Colonel-Major Kadago Mohamed, marked the beginning of a competition designed to foster camaraderie and honor among participants as they represent their respective institutions.

Why it mattersFuture military and security personnel are affected by this competition which aims to build cohesion and excellence through sports.

Niger communication chief visits Algerian news agency

Niger's National Communication Observatory president met with APS officials to strengthen information and communication ties.

Niger's National Communication Observatory (ONC) president, Ibrahim Manzo Diallo, visited the Algerian Press Service (APS) headquarters in Algiers on October 20, 2026. Diallo led a delegation on a working visit to Algeria aimed at reinforcing bilateral cooperation in information and communication sectors.

The visit underscores the existing relationship between Algeria and Niger, which share a common border and collaborate on various fronts including hydrocarbons, training, and security. The ONC's engagement with APS signals a focus on enhancing the flow and quality of information between the two nations.

This exchange is expected to foster greater understanding and collaboration in media practices and communication strategies. The ONC's initiative highlights a commitment to developing the media landscape in Niger through international partnerships.

Why it mattersNigerians may benefit from improved information flow and communication strategies developed through this partnership.

News · 2026-10-22

Gunmen kill 12 in Niger bus attack

An armed group attacked two buses carrying gold, killing passengers and injuring several others on the Arlit-Agadez road.

12 people, including women and children, were killed and several others injured in an armed attack on two SONITRAV buses on the Arlit-Agadez road on October 20, 2026. The governor of Agadez region, General Ibra Boulama Issa, visited the injured at the regional hospital and expressed condolences to the families of the deceased. Some of the seriously wounded were transferred to the CHR for better medical care after initial stabilization at the SONICHAR hospital in Tchirozérine.

The attack is believed to be linked to the transport of gold on board one of the buses. Governor Issa stated that the perpetrators of the criminal acts should be sought, prosecuted, and brought before the competent courts. He emphasized the need for discussions with transport company officials to establish secure transport mechanisms, in line with regulations, to prevent further endangerment of lives.

The governor conveyed the deep compassion of the state's highest authorities and the government to the victims and their families. He reaffirmed solidarity with the injured and wished them a speedy recovery. Assurances were given that necessary measures would be taken for the respectful and appropriate burial of the deceased.

Why it mattersPassengers on buses transporting gold were killed and injured in an armed attack.
