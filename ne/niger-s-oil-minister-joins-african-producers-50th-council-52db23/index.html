Niger's oil minister joins African producers' 50th council session in Abidjan

Hamadou Tini is attending talks on local content, technology transfer, refining and regional gas infrastructure, with the African Energy Bank also on the agenda.

Source: Agence Nigerienne de Presse · 2026-11-19 · 1 min read

Niger's Oil Minister Hamadou Tini has attended the 50th ordinary ministerial council session of the African Petroleum Producers' Organization (APPO) in Abidjan, Côte d'Ivoire, since 17 November. The meeting is led by Ivorian Hydrocarbons Minister Mamadou Sangafowa-Coulibaly and aims to harmonise energy policies and strengthen African oil and gas sovereignty.

Ministers are discussing local content, technology transfer, refining and regional gas infrastructure, according to Agence Nigerienne de Presse. They have also stressed unifying national strategies to make better use of resources and secure financing.

Earlier statutory meetings, which began on 14 November, examined the organisation's internal audit and budgets, and the executive council met on 15 and 16 November. The African Energy Bank is due to be officially launched in Abuja on 2 January 2027. Niger's delegation is due to take part in the SIREXE 2026 extractives and energy exhibition, a Pan-African fair for mining, oil and energy, at the Abidjan Exhibition Park until 22 November.

Why it mattersNiger's oil minister is involved in discussions on regional energy policy and the settlement of APPO member states' contributions.
