Niger minister visits AGRHYMET centre

Colonel Bylaly Elhadj Gambobo explored the climate centre's work supporting farmers and pastoralists.

Source: Agence Nigerienne de Presse · 2026-11-05 · 1 min read

Niger's Minister of Agriculture and Livestock, Colonel Bylaly Elhadj Gambobo, visited the AGRHYMET climate centre in Niamey on Tuesday, November 3, 2026. He aimed to understand the institution's activities and discuss agricultural and pastoral challenges with its leaders.

During the visit, Colonel Gambobo learned about AGRHYMET's training, research, and climate monitoring systems designed to anticipate climate risks, assist producers, and enhance food security. Discussions also covered strengthening collaboration between the ministry and AGRHYMET on data sharing, capacity building, and agricultural policy support.

Colonel Bylaly Elhadj Gambobo praised AGRHYMET's expertise, stressing the need to connect scientific knowledge with the practical needs of farmers and herders. AGRHYMET, established in 1974 and based in Niamey, is a specialized institution of the CILSS, comprising 13 member states.

Why it mattersNigerien farmers and herders will benefit from improved climate risk anticipation and support for their needs.
