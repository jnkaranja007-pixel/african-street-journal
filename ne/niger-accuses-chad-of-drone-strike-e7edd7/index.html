Niger accuses Chad of drone strike

Niamey claims a Chadian drone strike on October 24 killed two civilians and injured others.

Source: Jeune Afrique · 2026-11-10 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Niger's government accused Chad of conducting a drone strike on its territory on October 24, which it claims killed two civilians and injured several others. The incident, which occurred around 1 AM, also reportedly damaged property. This accusation followed a sharp diplomatic escalation between the two neighboring nations on October 26.

Niger's President Abdourahamane Tiani is described as unpredictable, with his government issuing a strongly worded statement against Chad after the alleged strike. Despite attempts by Niamey and N’Djamena to de-escalate tensions following the diplomatic row, security and geopolitical disagreements between the leaders make a swift return to normal relations unlikely.

The diplomatic dispute between Niger and Chad highlights ongoing security and geopolitical divergences between the two countries. The packet does not provide further details on the immediate next steps or reactions from either government following the accusations.
