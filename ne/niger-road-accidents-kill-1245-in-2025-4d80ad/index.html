Niger road accidents kill 1245 in 2025

Human behaviour caused 99.24% of crashes, with speeding and ignoring right-of-way as top factors.

Source: Agence Nigerienne de Presse · 2026-11-03 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Road accidents in Niger claimed 1,245 lives in 2025, a 12.56% increase from the previous year, according to Dr. Abou Mountari, Director General of the Road Safety Agency (ANISER). While the total number of accidents decreased by 11.26% to 7,116, the severity of incidents rose, with serious injuries up by 3.9% to 4,439.

Dr. Mountari stated that 99.24% of these accidents were attributed to human behaviour, including excessive speed (40.76%), failure to yield (26.03%), and imprudence (18.28%). Other factors like road conditions, vehicle maintenance, and environmental issues contributed marginally. ANISER conducts awareness campaigns and joint road controls with security forces to enforce traffic laws and improve road safety.

ANISER is working towards global road safety indicators outlined in the WHO's 2021-2030 action plan, focusing on safe infrastructure, vehicles, users, and victim care. The agency, established by law, operates as a public administrative body with financial autonomy under the Ministry of Transport and Civil Aviation.

Why it mattersNigerians faced a higher risk of death on the roads in 2025 due to preventable human errors.
