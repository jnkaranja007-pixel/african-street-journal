Zinder women's groups launch drive for Niger's solidarity fund

The Mata Massu Doubara ambassadors held the launch on Friday, answering the president's call for CFA100 per person.

Source: Agence Nigerienne de Presse · 2026-11-21 · 1 min read

Women's groups in Zinder gathered on Friday at the Abdoussalam Adam cultural centre to raise money for Niger's Fund for Solidarity for the Safeguarding of the Homeland (FSSP). The launch was organised by the Mata Massu Doubara (MMD) ambassadors, a women's network, and was attended by regional and customary authorities, religious leaders and representatives of several women's groups.

Aïssa Amadou Moudiéli, representing the Zinder regional governor, said the campaign answered President Abdourahamane Tiani's call for each person to contribute CFA100 to the fund. MMD president Hadjia Mariama Maman called the ambassadors' approach a new reform by women's organisations to raise resources for the fund.

Maman urged the groups to take part, saying their contributions could help fund security and food sovereignty.

Why it mattersEach woman in the groups is asked to pay CFA100 into the FSSP fund.
