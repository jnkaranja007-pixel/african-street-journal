Diffa officials confirm two wholesale markets are complete

The markets in Goudoumaria and Mainé-Soroa have received provisional acceptance, while supervisors also inspected two roads toward Nigeria.

Source: Agence Nigerienne de Presse · 2026-11-26 · 1 min read

Mamane Gouzaé, deputy secretary-general of Niger’s Diffa region, inspected projects funded by the Lake Chad Region Recovery and Development Project (PROLAC-Niger) on Wednesday, 25 November, in Goudoumaria and Mainé-Soroa. The two wholesale markets are complete and have received provisional acceptance.

Gouzaé said the businesses contracted to build the markets had met their commitments. The facilities are intended to help producers and traders sell agricultural goods and other merchandise and improve local distribution. The inspection also covered two laterite roads: a 45.898-kilometre route linking Goudoumaria with Kodjiméri and Abuja at the Nigerian border, and the 36.620-kilometre Djajéri-Chéri-Boula Toura road toward Nigeria.

Regional and local officials, PROLAC-Niger’s national coordinator, technical services, inspectors and contractors joined the visits. The report gave no completion status for either road.

Why it mattersProducers and traders in the two departments gain completed wholesale markets intended to improve sales and distribution of agricultural goods and other merchandise.
