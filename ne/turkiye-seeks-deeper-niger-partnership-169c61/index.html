Türkiye seeks deeper Niger partnership

Ankara wants to boost cooperation in defense, health, education, energy, and mining.

Source: Agence Nigerienne de Presse · 2026-10-30 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Türkiye's ambassador to Niger, Özgür Arslan, stated on October 29, 2026, in Niamey that his country wants to strengthen cooperation with Niger. The ambassador spoke at a reception in Niamey celebrating the 103rd anniversary of the founding of the Republic of Türkiye. He highlighted that Türkiye's Africa policy, led by President Recep Tayyip Erdoğan, is based on solidarity, mutual benefit, and respect for sovereignty.

Ambassador Arslan noted that ties with Niger have grown in defense, health, education, energy, mines, and professional training. He recalled high-level visits, including Niger's Prime Minister Ali Mahamane Lamine Zeine to Türkiye in 2024, and a Turkish delegation's visit to Niamey the same year. Niger's President Abdourahamane Tiani officially visited Ankara and Istanbul in June 2026.

Türkiye aims to deepen its political and economic relations with Niger through sustainable investments. The ambassador assured that Türkiye closely monitors Niger's security challenges and humanitarian needs. He stated that Türkiye intends to continue strengthening its ties with Niger, respecting national sensitivities and strategic priorities outlined in Niger's "Programme de Refondation de la République."

Why it mattersNigeriens will benefit from strengthened cooperation in areas like health, education, and energy.
