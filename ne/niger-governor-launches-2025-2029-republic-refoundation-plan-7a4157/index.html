Niger governor launches 2025-2029 republic refoundation plan

Colonel Maïna Boukar presented the regional plan to local leaders and civil society in Tillabéri on November 5, 2026.

Source: Agence Nigerienne de Presse · 2026-11-06 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Tillabéri Governor Colonel Maïna Boukar opened a regional workshop on November 5, 2026, to explain the 2025-2029 Republic Refoundation Programme. The meeting in his office included regional secretaries, local officials, traditional leaders, security chiefs, and civil society representatives.

The workshop aims to present the programme's goals to regional development actors and encourage their commitment. Governor Boukar stated the plan, based on President Tiani's vision, is particularly important for Tillabéri due to its unique geographical, economic, social, and security challenges. He stressed that refoundation must lead to concrete changes in daily life, improving state authority, governance, social justice, local economies, and community cohesion.

Colonel Boukar urged participants to focus on the workshop's discussions to adapt national orientations to local realities. He also called for continuous efforts to popularize the programme through outreach and local explanations across the region. The programme focuses on security, governance, economic sovereignty, and social reforms.

Why it mattersNigeriens in the Tillabéri region will be directly affected by the implementation of the 2025-2029 Republic Refoundation Programme, which aims to improve security, governance, and economic development.
