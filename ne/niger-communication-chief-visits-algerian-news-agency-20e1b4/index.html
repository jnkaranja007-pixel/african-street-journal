Niger communication chief visits Algerian news agency

Niger's National Communication Observatory president met with APS officials to strengthen information and communication ties.

Source: Agence Nigerienne de Presse · 2026-10-22 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Niger's National Communication Observatory (ONC) president, Ibrahim Manzo Diallo, visited the Algerian Press Service (APS) headquarters in Algiers on October 20, 2026. Diallo led a delegation on a working visit to Algeria aimed at reinforcing bilateral cooperation in information and communication sectors.

The visit underscores the existing relationship between Algeria and Niger, which share a common border and collaborate on various fronts including hydrocarbons, training, and security. The ONC's engagement with APS signals a focus on enhancing the flow and quality of information between the two nations.

This exchange is expected to foster greater understanding and collaboration in media practices and communication strategies. The ONC's initiative highlights a commitment to developing the media landscape in Niger through international partnerships.

Why it mattersNigerians may benefit from improved information flow and communication strategies developed through this partnership.
