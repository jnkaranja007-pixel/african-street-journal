Senegalese artist reaffirms Niger development support

Singer Coumba Gawlo Seck met President Tiani to pledge her commitment to Niger's growth and cultural promotion.

Source: Agence Nigerienne de Presse · 2026-10-22 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Senegalese artist Coumba Gawlo Seck met Niger's President Abdourahamane Tiani on October 21, 2026, to express her dedication to the country's development efforts. Seck, a renowned singer and composer, stated her intention to support Niger's growth and national sovereignty. She emphasized the importance of promoting Niger's image internationally, describing it as a vibrant and welcoming nation.

During the meeting, Seck highlighted the role of culture and music in fostering communication, education, and combating poverty and violence. She expressed her belief that art is a powerful tool for national promotion and development. Seck also conveyed her desire to contribute to the President's vision for peace, security, and social cohesion within Niger.

The artist announced plans to mobilize other African artists to rally support for Niger, stating that the country's development is integral to the progress of the entire African continent. Seck, known internationally for her rendition of "Pata Pata," has a history of engagement with Niger, citing strong personal ties and affection for the nation and its people.

Why it mattersNigerians are affected by the artist's pledge to promote their country's image and mobilize support for its development.
