Diffa governor explains Niger’s decision to expel Mohamid community

Mahamadou Ibrahim Bagadoma distinguished the group from Nigerien Arabs and urged clear communication to protect social cohesion.

Source: Agence Nigerienne de Presse · 2026-11-24 · 1 min read

Diffa region Governor Mahamadou Ibrahim Bagadoma held a public information meeting on Tuesday, 24 November, about the government’s process to expel the Mohamid community from Niger. The meeting took place in Diffa and brought together representatives of regional professions and public services.

Bagadoma said the session was intended to clarify the authorities’ process and address contradictory information circulating on social media. He distinguished the Mohamid community, which he described as foreign, from Nigerien Arabs, whom he said had long been established in the country and were not covered by the decision.

The governor said his briefing addressed the Mohamid community’s identity, its arrival in Niger, grievances against it, measures taken by national authorities and the government decision’s meaning. He called for responsible communication to prevent confusion and tensions in the region.

Why it mattersMohamid residents in Diffa are the group covered by the government’s expulsion process, which Bagadoma discussed at the public meeting.
