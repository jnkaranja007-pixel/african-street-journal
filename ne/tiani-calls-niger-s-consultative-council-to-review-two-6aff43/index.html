Tiani calls Niger's consultative council to review two draft laws

The council is also due to hear government communications on the expulsion of Mohamids and administrative governance.

Source: Agence Nigerienne de Presse · 2026-11-25 · 1 min read

Niger's President Abdourahamane Tiani signed a decree on 24 November convening the second ordinary session of the Consultative Council for Refoundation in 2026. The session is scheduled to open on 1 December in Niamey.

The council's agenda includes draft ordinances on rules for demonstrations and meetings in Niger and on a social security code, according to the decree. Members are also to examine government communications, including discussions about expelling Mohamids from the country and administrative governance.

The decree gives the council those matters to consider at its final ordinary session of the year.

Why it mattersCouncil members must consider draft rules for demonstrations and meetings, a social security code and government communications when the session opens on 1 December.
