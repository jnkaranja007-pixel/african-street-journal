Zinder women's groups mobilise contributions for Niger solidarity fund

The campaign backs President Abdourahamane Tiani's call for each person to contribute CFA100 to the Fund for the Safeguarding of the Homeland.

Source: Agence Nigerienne de Presse · 2026-11-22 · 1 min read

Women's groups in Zinder, Niger, mobilised resources for the Fund for the Safeguarding of the Homeland at an event on Friday, 20 November, organised by the Mata Massu Doubara ambassadors. The campaign responds to President Abdourahamane Tiani's call for a contribution of CFA100 per person.

Aïssa Amadou Moudiéli, representing the Zinder regional governor, praised the campaign and said it followed the president's appeal. Hadjia Mariama Maman, president of the ambassadors, urged women's groups to contribute. She said the citizen mobilisation could help fund security and food sovereignty; no amount raised was reported.

The launch took place at the Abdoussalam Adam Cultural Centre, with regional and traditional authorities, religious leaders and representatives of women's groups present. Maman thanked the development partners and non-governmental organisations attending, including CARE, formerly the Cooperative for Assistance and Relief Everywhere, and AFB, a partner organisation named in the report.

Why it mattersWomen's groups are being asked to raise a CFA100 contribution per person for the fund, with no total reported for the Zinder campaign.
