Niger minister calls youth to energy jobs

Colonel Alzouma Seyni urged young Nigeriens to pursue careers in energy research, innovation, and entrepreneurship.

Source: Agence Nigerienne de Presse · 2026-10-23 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Niger's Minister of Energy, Colonel Alzouma Seyni, called on the nation's youth on October 22, 2026, to engage more in energy sector careers, scientific research, technological innovation, and entrepreneurship.

Speaking during World Energy Day, Seyni highlighted energy's role in economic and social development and national sovereignty. He noted the government's priority on energy sovereignty under President Abdourahamane Tiani and Prime Minister Ali Mahamane Lamine Zeine.

The Ministry of Energy's 2024-2027 action plan aims to strengthen national electricity production, define an energy transition model, and improve energy access. Seyni expressed a desire for a resilient energy sector supporting national development and sovereignty.

Why it mattersNiger's youth are called to invest in the energy sector to bolster national energy independence and economic development.
