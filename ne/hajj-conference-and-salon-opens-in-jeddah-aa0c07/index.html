Hajj conference and salon opens in Jeddah

More than 520 entities showcase services for transport, housing, food, and health.

Source: Agence Nigerienne de Presse · 2026-11-09 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

The 6th Hajj Conference and Salon began Sunday, November 8, 2026, in Jeddah, Saudi Arabia, with over 520 public, private, and non-profit entities participating. These organizations are exhibiting services and solutions for transport, housing, catering, healthcare, and technology, detailing preparations made months before pilgrims arrive.

The event highlights the complex chain of planning, contracts, and logistics involving multiple bodies and specialties that occur before pilgrims travel, find accommodation, or eat. Services like travel, air transport, lodging, and health are presented alongside infrastructure, telecommunications, digital services, and training, showing interconnected links impacting all Hajj phases.

The conference facilitates mutual needs assessment and partnership exploration among participants. Business meetings, contract finalizations, and agreement signings are taking place. Innovation and technology spaces connect solution providers with pilgrim journey challenges, complementing training programs for field agents.

Why it mattersNigeriens participating in the Hajj will benefit from improved services in transport, housing, and health, as a result of partnerships and solutions showcased at the Jeddah conference.
