Niger launches 13th modern music competition

The Prix Dan Gourmou, themed 'The musician at the heart of Niger's refoundation,' officially began on October 24, 2026.

Source: Agence Nigerienne de Presse · 2026-10-26 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Niger's Minister of Youth, Sports, and Culture, Sidi Mohamed AlMahmoud, officially launched the 13th National Modern Music Competition, known as the 'Prix Dan Gourmou,' on October 24, 2026, in Tahoua. The event, held at the Maison des Jeunes et de la Culture, focused on the theme 'The musician at the heart of Niger's refoundation.' The minister conveyed greetings from President Abdourahamane Tiani and Prime Minister Ali Mahamane Lamine Zeine to the regional authorities and people of Tahoua for their support of culture.

Minister AlMahmoud highlighted the importance of musicians in fostering societal awareness, promoting national identity, consolidating peace, and preserving cultural heritage. He stated that music accompanies significant life events and serves as a tool for communication and mobilization on matters of general interest. The competition, established in 1987, aims to promote competitive musical creativity inspired by Niger's rich cultural heritage.

The theme for this 13th edition reflects a conviction that the refoundation of Niger is a collective citizen engagement for sustainable development. The minister expressed his belief that this vision aligns with the political will of the country's highest authorities to build a sovereign and prosperous Niger. The competition is widely followed within Niger and beyond.

Why it mattersNigerien musicians and cultural stakeholders are directly involved in the Prix Dan Gourmou competition.
