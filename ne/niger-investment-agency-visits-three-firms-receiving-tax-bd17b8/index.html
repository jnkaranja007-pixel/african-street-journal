Niger investment agency visits three firms receiving tax-code benefits

The Niamey delegation reviewed production and jobs at Murex, Nataro Agro and Bénie, which benefit from the investment code.

Source: Agence Nigerienne de Presse · 2026-11-18 · 1 min read

A delegation from Niger’s Agency for the Promotion of Private Investment and Strategic Projects (ANPIPS) visited three industrial firms in Niamey on 17 November to review operations, production capacity and their contribution to the economy. The firms—Murex SARLU, Nataro Agro SARL and Bénie SARLU—receive benefits under the investment code.

Murex produces A4 paper and employs 16 people, with capacity to make 800 cartons a day, equivalent to half a container of imported paper. Its director, Hachem Ibrahim Khalil, said the agency had advised and supported the factory. The company reported an investment of 641 894 848 FCFA and a tax exemption of 142 233 722 FCFA.

Nataro Agro, a natural-juice producer created by the Belvie group, reported an investment of 1 535 821 223 FCFA and an exemption of 417 166 514 FCFA. Company managers presented investments, production capacity and jobs during the visits; the report gave no figures for Bénie.

Why it mattersMurex employs 16 people and makes 800 cartons of A4 paper a day, while its reported tax exemption is 142 233 722 FCFA.
