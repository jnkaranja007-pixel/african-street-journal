Alger accuses France over EU statement on Niger troop deployment

The European Union's foreign affairs spokesperson addressed troop movements in Niger on October 30.

Source: Jeune Afrique · 2026-11-10 · 1 min read

Algeria has accused France of influencing a European Union statement regarding the deployment of Algerian troops in Niger. The EU's foreign affairs spokesperson, Anouar El Anouni, read a statement on October 30 concerning the situation. This diplomatic exchange intensifies tensions between Algeria and France.

Anouar El Anouni, who handles foreign affairs and security policy for the European Commission, was questioned by the press in Brussels. He read a prepared one-minute statement that appeared carefully worded. The EU's position was shared during a daily press briefing.

The diplomatic dispute follows reports of Algerian troop movements within Niger. This incident further strains relations between Algeria and France, dimming hopes for improved diplomatic ties.
