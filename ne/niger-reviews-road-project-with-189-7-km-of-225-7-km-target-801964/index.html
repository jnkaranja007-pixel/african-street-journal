Niger reviews road project with 189.7 km of 225.7 km target completed

The PACNEN committee assessed its 2026 work and examined a 2027 budget, while added financing is set to fund more road rehabilitation.

Source: Agence Nigerienne de Presse · 2026-11-21 · 1 min read

Niger’s equipment and infrastructure minister, Colonel-Major Salissou Mahaman Salissou, chaired a project committee meeting in Niamey on Friday, 20 November, to review the Northeast Connectivity Improvement Project (PACNEN). Members assessed implementation of the 2026 annual work programme and budget and examined the draft for 2027.

The project has rehabilitated 189.7 km of road against a 225.7 km target, an 84.05% completion rate, according to the minister. An additional financing package secured after a restructuring is to fund rehabilitation of the 62 km Tiguidit-Agadez section and improvements to 200 km of rural roads.

PACNEN, launched on 22 September 2021, also covers national road 11 North and access to basic infrastructure along the Zinder-Agadez corridor. Its intervention area is to expand to the regions of Niamey and Tahoua.

Why it mattersRoad users and communities along the Zinder-Agadez corridor are served by a project that has rehabilitated 189.7 km of its 225.7 km target.
