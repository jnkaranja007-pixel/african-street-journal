Algerian military escort brings first fuel convoy into Niger

The convoy was headed to Agadez after Algeria's parliament voted to send military units to protect energy interests.

Source: RFI Afrique · 2026-11-20 · 1 min read

A first fuel convoy, accompanied by a military escort from Algeria, entered Niger and was headed for Agadez in the north, according to Radio France Internationale (RFI), a French public broadcaster, which reported on the movement. The convoy's arrival came days after Algeria's parliament voted on 25 October 2026 to send military units to Niger to secure energy interests.

RFI said it had traced the convoy's route and confirmed that Algerian military personnel were present in Niger for several days. False claims spread on social media about the troops, including rumours that they had settled in the country after completing the escort mission.

The report did not establish a continuing Algerian military presence in Niger after the escort. The parliamentary vote concerned sending units to protect energy interests.

Why it mattersAlgerian troops were in Niger for several days to escort the fuel convoy, after which rumours circulated about whether they would remain.
