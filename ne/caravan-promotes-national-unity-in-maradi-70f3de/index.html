Caravan promotes national unity in Maradi

A national awareness and mobilization campaign visited Maradi to foster unity and patriotism among civilians and security forces.

A national awareness and mobilization caravan, featuring the Chœur de la Garde Nationale du Niger and the Groupe Yan Maza Na Tigre, arrived in Maradi on September 5, 2026. The event, themed 'Civilians and FDS: Hand in Hand Against the Threats of Niger's Enemies,' aimed to strengthen social cohesion and national unity. Performances by renowned artists focused on general mobilization, love of country, and tribute to the Defense and Security Forces.

The caravan's visit to Maradi, following stops in Diffa and Zinder, was presided over by regional and local officials. The Administrator Delegate of Maradi, Colonel-major Abdoulaye Moussa Garba, emphasized that general mobilization should be understood as citizen engagement for Niger's superior interests and encouraged youth to reject hate speech. The organizing committee highlighted Maradi's economic importance and its role as a bulwark of national sovereignty.

The caravan conveyed a message of unity and fraternity, seeking to align it with the dynamism of the Maradi region's population. Organizers reaffirmed their support for the National Council for the Safeguarding of the Homeland (CNSP) and the transitional government. The Deputy Secretary General of the region stated that the general mobilization, decreed by the President, is ongoing and exemplified by this caravan, which is part of a series of such initiatives.

Why it mattersThe caravan's message of unity and patriotism is intended to strengthen social cohesion and national identity among the population.
