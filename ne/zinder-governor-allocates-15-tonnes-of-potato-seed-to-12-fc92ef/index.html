Zinder governor allocates 15 tonnes of potato seed to 12 communes

The early-maturing variety takes 80 to 90 days to mature, and the regional agriculture director says it yields 30 tonnes per hectare.

Source: Agence Nigerienne de Presse · 2026-11-26 · 1 min read

Zinder’s governor, Colonel Massalatchi Mahaman Sani, presided over the handover of 15 tonnes of potato seed to the regional agriculture directorate on Tuesday, 25 November. The 600 sacks are for producers in 12 communes in the region.

Regional Agriculture Director Ibro Mahamadou Boubacar said the state’s seed support would ease producers’ difficulties ahead of the flood-recession farming season. He said the variety matures in 80 to 90 days and yields 30 tonnes per hectare.

Mahaman Sani said the support contributes to the state’s food self-sufficiency efforts and thanked national authorities for providing it to Zinder’s producers.
