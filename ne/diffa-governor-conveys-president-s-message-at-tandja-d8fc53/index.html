Diffa governor conveys president’s message at Tandja remembrance

Authorities and residents gathered in Mainé-Soroa for prayers marking six years since the former Nigerien president’s death.

Source: Agence Nigerienne de Presse · 2026-11-24 · 1 min read

Authorities and residents gathered at former Nigerien President Mamadou Tandja’s residence in Mainé-Soroa, Diffa region, on Tuesday for prayers marking six years since his death. The ceremony was initiated by Mainé-Soroa’s canton chief, Abdou Katiellou Katiella Gasso.

Diffa Governor Mahamadou Ibrahim Bagadoma attended on behalf of President Abdourahamane Tiani and conveyed greetings and solidarity to Tandja’s family and the town’s residents. Participants read the Quran in full, recited the Fatiha and prayed for Tandja, social cohesion and peace in Niger, Burkina Faso and Mali, as well as for soldiers killed while defending the country.

After the prayers, officials gathered at Tandja’s grave. Bagadoma said the ceremony reflected a duty to remember figures who shaped Niger’s political and institutional history.
