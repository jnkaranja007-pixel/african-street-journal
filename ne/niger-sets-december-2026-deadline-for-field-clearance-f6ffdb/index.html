Niger sets December 2026 deadline for field clearance

Tillabéri governor Maina Boukar announced the date to prevent farmer-herder conflicts and protect harvests.

Source: Agence Nigerienne de Presse · 2026-11-10 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

The governor of Niger's Tillabéri region, Colonel Maina Boukar, set December 15, 2026, as the deadline for clearing fields. This decision aims to prevent conflicts between farmers and herders and ensure peaceful coexistence. The meeting included administrative, customary, and religious authorities, along with security officials.

Governor Boukar emphasized that clearing fields is a sensitive step in the agro-pastoral calendar, crucial for livestock movement and access to agricultural residue. He noted that Tillabéri's economy relies heavily on farming and herding, contributing to food security and local income. The deadline protects farmers' harvests and interests while allowing herders access to pastures.

The governor urged traditional chiefs to mediate conflicts and called on communal authorities, technical services, farmers, and herders to respect the agreed-upon rules. Mechanisms for surveillance, early warning, and dispute resolution will be strengthened to maintain peace and security in the region.

Why it mattersFarmers and herders in the Tillabéri region will have a clear date to manage their activities and avoid disputes over land use.
