Niger orders Mahamid Arabs to leave by 31 December

Several thousand members of the community, many settled in the south-east for decades, have been told to leave the country by the end of 2026.

Sources: RFI Afrique and Jeune Afrique · 2026-11-18 · 1 min read

Nigerien authorities have ordered the Mahamid Arab community to leave the country before the end of 2026, according to RFI Afrique. Local authorities announced that several thousand nomadic Mahamid Arabs living in south-east Niger must depart by 31 December.

Many Mahamid have lived in Niger for decades, and the first arrived from Chad and Sudan in the 1970s. Authorities have taken measures against the community for several months, and voices in the country have denounced serious discrimination.

The deadline applies to a community concentrated in the south-east, where it has lived for generations. Its members now have less than six weeks to decide whether and where to go.

Why it mattersMahamid families who have lived in south-east Niger for decades must leave the country by 31 December or face the consequences of the order.
