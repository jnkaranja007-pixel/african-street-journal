Tiani appoints Aboubacar Issaka Oumarou to lead Niger’s national police

The former head of the country’s narcotics-trafficking office was named police director general by a decree signed on 13 November.

Source: Agence Nigerienne de Presse · 2026-11-18 · 1 min read

Niger’s President, Gen. Abdourahamane Tiani, appointed police commissioner general Aboubacar Issaka Oumarou as director general of the National Police in a decree signed on 13 November. Oumarou previously led the Central Office for the Suppression of Illicit Drug Trafficking.

Oumarou has held several senior police posts in Niger, including regional police director in Agadez and deputy director of public security. He also served in United Nations peacekeeping missions in Timor-Leste, Burundi and the Democratic Republic of Congo, where he coordinated UN police in the east from Goma.

In Congo, he directed a project to train six rapid-intervention police battalions ahead of the 2011 general elections and led the UN police electoral group. The appointment puts him in charge of Niger’s national police as public security and citizen protection remain challenges, according to Agence Nigerienne de Presse.
