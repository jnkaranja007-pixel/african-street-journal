Niger court processed 322 cases in September

Niamey's public prosecutor's office reported 80 judgments and 322 case files handled last month.

Source: Agence Nigerienne de Presse · 2026-11-06 · 1 min read

Niamey's public prosecutor's office handled 322 case files and delivered 80 judgments in September 2026. The ninth meeting of the prosecutor's office for the year, held on October 30, 2026, reviewed these figures. The prosecutor, Ousmane Beydo, presented the monthly activity report to the attendees.

The discussions focused on the processing of received case files, criminal hearings held, and the main reasons for case referrals. The office also reviewed the status of individuals placed in detention and those released during September. These outcomes were presented at the cabinet of the public prosecutor.

The prosecutor's office also examined the number of people detained and released in the past month. The meeting on October 30, 2026, was the ninth such session in 2026.

Why it mattersNigerians in Niamey faced legal proceedings and judgments in September 2026.
