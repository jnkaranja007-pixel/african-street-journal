Niger opens investor forum with 30 project proposals

Prime Minister Ali Mahamane Lamine Zeine said the projects cover agriculture, mining, energy, infrastructure and other sectors.

Source: Agence Nigerienne de Presse · 2026-11-25 · 1 min read

Niger opened its first International Forum to Invest in Niger in Niamey on Tuesday, 24 November 2026, with Prime Minister Ali Mahamane Lamine Zeine presenting about 30 investment dossiers to national and international investors. The proposals cover agriculture and agro-industry, mines, oil and gas, energy, transport and communications infrastructure, and groundwater.

Zeine said the country had revised its investment code in February 2025. He said it provides equal treatment regardless of investors’ nationality, protects private property and allows the transfer of income, profits and sale proceeds. The code also says later legislation cannot reduce benefits granted under an investment approval.

Zeine identified insecurity as the main constraint on investment and said a national force had been established specifically to protect major investments and supply corridors. He said the force offers tailored security plans to investors.

Why it mattersInvestors considering the 30 proposals receive equal-treatment, property and transfer protections under the revised code, according to Zeine.
