Niger reports 81 flood deaths and 131 injured by 31 October

Floods also affected 97 334 people and destroyed 12 985 homes, according to the population ministry’s representative.

Source: Agence Nigerienne de Presse · 2026-11-25 · 1 min read

Niger recorded 81 deaths and 131 people injured in floods by 31 October 2026, Sani Douda, representing the minister of Population, Social Action and National Solidarity, said on 24 November in Maradi. The floods affected 97 334 people across 13 976 households.

The toll also includes 12 985 houses that collapsed and 1 366 head of livestock killed, Douda said. Officials and partners met in Maradi to review the 2026 response, identify shortcomings and recommend ways to improve flood prevention and management in 2027.

Douda said the year’s work included strengthening early-warning systems, operational preparedness and community mobilisation. Gaspard Cirhalwirwa, the World Food Programme’s deputy head of office in Maradi, said the agency would continue supporting Niger’s disaster-prevention and management efforts.

Why it mattersThe 13 976 affected households face damage to homes and livestock, while officials are to recommend prevention measures for 2027.
