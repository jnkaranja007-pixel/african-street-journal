Niger Fuel Shortages Strand Drivers in Capital

Motorists in Niamey face long queues and disrupted daily life due to an ongoing gasoline shortage, with no clear end in sight.

Fuel shortages are increasingly affecting Niger, with the capital city Niamey experiencing long lines at petrol stations. Residents report that the lack of gasoline is hindering their ability to commute to work and perform essential daily tasks. Some drivers have spent days searching for fuel, despite official assurances that the scarcity has been resolved.

Frustration is mounting among Niamey residents who are urging authorities to implement a lasting solution to the persistent fuel problem. "For a week now we've been struggling, we can't even find fuel to get to work," said Harouna Chérif, a resident. Abdoulaye Zakari, another resident, added, "We've been told the shortage is over, yet we still don't see the light at the end of the tunnel."

The exact cause of the ongoing fuel shortages and the timeline for the full restoration of supplies remain unclear. This uncertainty leaves motorists in Niamey with no definite indication of when they will be able to refuel their vehicles without significant delays.

Why it mattersNigerien drivers are affected by being unable to find fuel, disrupting their commutes and daily activities.
