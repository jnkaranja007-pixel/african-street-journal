Niger hostages Eva Gretzmacher and Claudia Abbt freed

The Austrian and Swiss women, kidnapped in Agadez in 2025, arrived in Benghazi after over a year and a half in captivity.

Source: Air Info Agadez · 2026-10-23 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Austrian Eva Gretzmacher and Swiss Claudia Abbt, who were abducted separately in Agadez in 2025, were released and arrived in Benghazi, Libya, on Wednesday, October 21, 2026. Their handover to Austrian and Swiss authorities is scheduled after necessary formalities are completed.

Gretzmacher was taken from her Agadez home on January 11, 2025, and Abbt was kidnapped in the same city in April 2025. Both women were held by the Islamic State in the Sahel for over 18 months. Saddam Haftar, son of Libyan marshal Khalifa Haftar, reportedly played a role in their liberation, though this has not been officially confirmed.

A plane was expected to depart Benghazi on Thursday, October 22, 2026, to take Gretzmacher to Vienna and then continue to Switzerland with Abbt. The exact circumstances of their release remain undisclosed.

Why it mattersEva Gretzmacher and Claudia Abbt were held for over a year and a half in Niger by the Islamic State in the Sahel.
