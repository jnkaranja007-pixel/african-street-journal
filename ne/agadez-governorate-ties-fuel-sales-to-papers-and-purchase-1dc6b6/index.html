Agadez governorate ties fuel sales to papers and purchase caps

From 1 December, buyers must show road documents; car purchases are capped at 30 litres, while tricycles and motorcycles face cash limits.

Sources: Air Info Agadez and Agence Nigerienne de Presse · 2026-11-24 · 1 min read

Agadez’s governorate will require drivers to show road documents to buy petrol or diesel at service stations from 1 December 2026, and will cap purchases by vehicle type. Buyers must present a driving licence, vehicle registration, insurance and road tax sticker.

For cars making trips within the city, the limit is 30 litres, worth a maximum of 15 CFA000. Drivers of Adaidaita tricycles are limited to 3 CFA000 of fuel, and motorcyclists to 2 CFA000, according to the governorate’s 23 November announcement. It told road users to bring their documents into compliance by 30 November; security forces are instructed to enforce the measures, and violations expose drivers to regulatory sanctions.

The rules take effect amid continuing fuel supply and distribution problems in the region. The governorate said the measures are intended to help improve supply and distribution.

Why it mattersAgadez drivers must bring their road papers into compliance by 30 November to keep access to stations under the new rules.
