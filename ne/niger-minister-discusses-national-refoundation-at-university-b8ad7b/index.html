Niger minister discusses national refoundation at university

Minister Abdourahamane Oumarou spoke on changing mindsets and patriotic values at Tahoua University on October 26, 2026.

Source: Agence Nigerienne de Presse · 2026-10-28 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Minister of Refoundation and Promotion of Social Values, Abdourahamane Oumarou, led a public conference at Tahoua University on October 26, 2026. The event focused on "Refoundation and Changing Mentalities in Niger: What Values to Build a Patriotic, Responsible, and Engaged Youth?" The conference gathered the university community, regional authorities, and security officials.

Oumarou defined refoundation as a matter of conscience, behavior, and values, emphasizing the need to rebuild both citizens and the state. He highlighted the roles of artists and universities in addressing contemporary issues and stressed that successful revolutions require a shift in mindsets, a focus on work ethic, food sovereignty, and solidarity.

The minister outlined five cardinal values for refoundation: excellence, protection of property, solidarity, and promoting dialogue. He urged the youth to embrace refoundation and take action for a better future, stating that youth represent not only the future but also the present.

Why it mattersNigerien youth are called to action and to embrace patriotic values for national development.
