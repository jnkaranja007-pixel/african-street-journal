Niger PM signs cooperation deal with Haftar camp

Prime Minister Ali Mahamane Lamine Zeine visited Benghazi on November 2, 2026, to sign an accord with the Haftar camp.

Source: RFI Afrique · 2026-11-04 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Niger's Prime Minister Ali Mahamane Lamine Zeine visited Benghazi, Libya, on Monday, November 2, 2026. He signed a memorandum of understanding to boost cooperation with the Haftar camp, which controls eastern Libya. Niamey also maintains ties with the government in Tripoli.

The agreement was signed during Zeine's visit to Benghazi, where he met General Saddam Haftar. The stated aim was to strengthen bilateral relations and expand cooperation across various sectors. This development follows Algeria's recent announcement of sending troops to Niger.

The accord aims to enhance security cooperation between Niger and the Haftar-controlled region of Libya.
