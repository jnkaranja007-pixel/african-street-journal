Niger president opens judicial year

President Abdourahamane Tiani presided over the 2026-2027 judicial opening ceremony in Niamey.

Source: Agence Nigerienne de Presse · 2026-10-23 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Nigerien President Abdourahamane Tiani opened the 2026-2027 judicial year in Niamey on Thursday, October 22, 2026. The ceremony focused on the theme 'What roles should justice play in affirming national sovereignty?'

State Court President Abdou Dan Galadima stated that the judicial opening is a time for assessment and projection, allowing justice to review progress and reaffirm its commitment to the Republic. He emphasized that national sovereignty includes the ability to create and enforce one's own legal order, with justice rendered by local judges according to national laws and values.

Bar Association President Fati Kountché Adji noted that national sovereignty is a legitimate aspiration for controlling destiny and development. She added that sovereignty must be reflected in daily institutional operations, heritage protection, public security, law enforcement, and public trust in the state.

Why it mattersNigeriens will experience how their justice system operates under the theme of national sovereignty.
