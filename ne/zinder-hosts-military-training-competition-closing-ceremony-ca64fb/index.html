Zinder hosts military training competition closing ceremony

12 delegations from defense and security training schools competed in the first edition of the Minister of State for National Defense Cup.

Source: Agence Nigerienne de Presse · 2026-10-31 · 1 min read

The first edition of the Minister of State for National Defense Cup, a competition for military and security training schools, concluded on Thursday, October 29, in Zinder. The event, held at the Lawan Didi Manzo Stadium, brought together 12 delegations from various institutions, including the Agadez National School for Active Non-Commissioned Officers and the Air Force Technical School.

Colonel Saminou Mani, Director of Information, Public Relations, and Sports, stated that the 10-day tournament fostered cohesion and demonstrated a shared commitment to building stronger, more united, and better-prepared defense and security forces. He highlighted that the competition celebrated discipline, self-improvement, cohesion, and fraternity among future national servants.

The closing ceremony, presided over by M. Mourtala Ibrahim, Deputy Secretary General of the Zinder region, celebrated not only sporting performances but also the qualities essential for national defense. Colonel Mani encouraged participants, noting that defeat in sport, as in military life, can be a lesson for progress.
