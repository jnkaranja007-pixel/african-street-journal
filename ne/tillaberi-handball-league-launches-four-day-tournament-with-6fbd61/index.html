Tillabéri handball league launches four-day tournament with five teams

The regional league opened the competition on 16 November, with the youth council president linking its peace theme to insecurity in the region.

Source: Agence Nigerienne de Presse · 2026-11-18 · 1 min read

The Tillabéri regional handball league opened a tournament at the regional stadium on 16 November, bringing together five teams from the city. The competition, sponsored by Abdoul Aziz Maliki, president of the Tillabéri communal youth council, is scheduled to run through 19 November.

Maliki said insecurity had weakened ties between communities and argued that sport could bring young people together. The tournament is intended to showcase local talent and give handball greater visibility in the region. Municipal and traditional authorities attended the opening.

Two matches were scheduled for the first day. In the men’s game reported, AS Dogoney beat AS Le Mafé 16-6; officials urged the teams to show discipline and responsibility throughout the competition.

Why it mattersFive Tillabéri teams are competing through 19 November, with local players seeking greater visibility for handball in the region.
