Qualikeeper wins Qualcomm's US$75,000 livestock technology grant

The Ghanaian company will use the equity-free award and technical support to develop and expand its livestock intelligence platform.

Source: Ghanaian Times · 2026-11-24 · 1 min read

Qualikeeper Investments Ltd., a Ghanaian startup, won Qualcomm's 2026 for Good Social Impact Fund grant of US$75,000 to develop and expand StocKeeper, its livestock intelligence platform. The award was announced after the fourth Qualcomm Make in Africa Startup Mentorship Programme ended in Cape Town, South Africa.

Qualcomm selected Qualikeeper from 10 startups in the programme, which supported businesses from nine African countries. The company will also receive tailored technical support. Its platform uses artificial intelligence and Internet of Things technology to improve livestock management and support farmers.

The programme offered business coaching, engineering support and intellectual-property guidance without taking equity. Participants who complete it are due to receive a US$5,000 stipend and reimbursement of up to US$5,000 for one full utility patent application.

Why it mattersThe grant pays for Qualikeeper to measure the platform's impact and extend it to more farms, and the company receives technical support alongside the money.
