Ghana reaches first Amputee World Cup final after Turkey shootout win

The Black Challenge beat defending champions Turkey 4-2 on penalties and will meet Morocco in an all-African final in Mexico.

Sources: MyJoyOnline and Ghanaian Times · 2026-11-22 · 1 min read

Ghana’s Black Challenge reached the 2026 Amputee Football World Cup final in Mexico after beating defending champions Turkey 4-2 on penalties on Saturday. The semi-final ended 1-1 after extra time, and the shootout secured Ghana’s first place in the tournament final.

The African Paralympic Committee, which oversees African Paralympic sport, congratulated Ghana and Morocco after both teams qualified. The committee said their performances also challenged perceptions of disability. The teams will meet at 1:30am Monday, each seeking its first World Cup title.

Ghana began the tournament with a 2-0 loss to Angola and a 1-1 draw with Colombia, then beat Iraq 6-0 to advance from its group. In the knockout rounds, it defeated the United States 3-0 and Egypt 2-0 before the Turkey match.

Why it mattersThe Black Challenge will play Morocco at 1:30am Monday, with Ghana seeking its first Amputee Football World Cup title.
