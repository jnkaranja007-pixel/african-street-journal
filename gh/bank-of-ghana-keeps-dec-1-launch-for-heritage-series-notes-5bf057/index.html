Bank of Ghana keeps Dec. 1 launch for Heritage Series notes

Governor Johnson Asiama met representatives of the families whose relatives appear on the cedi notes after public debate over a portrait.

Sources: MyJoyOnline and 3News · 2026-11-26 · 1 min read

Bank of Ghana Governor Johnson Asiama met representatives of families whose relatives appear on the new Ghana Cedi Heritage Series banknotes on Wednesday, November 25. The central bank says the notes will enter circulation on December 1, 2026.

Asiama said in a Facebook post that the meeting recognised the families’ contributions and thanked them for allowing the bank to honour their relatives. The series includes a GH¢50 note bearing a portrait of Theodosia Salome Okoh, designer of Ghana’s national flag.

Public debate followed the notes’ unveiling on November 12, with some questioning whether the portrait showed Okoh or her sister, Letitia Obeng. The Bank of Ghana said on November 13 that it had created the image from photographs of Okoh, adapting it for currency printing.
