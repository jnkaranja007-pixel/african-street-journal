Hearts pip Holy Stars to go top of Ghana Premier League

Martin Karikari scored the decisive goal in the 82nd minute, securing a third consecutive win for Hearts.

Source: MyJoyOnline · 2026-10-24 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Accra Hearts of Oak defeated Holy Stars 1-0 at the University of Ghana Stadium on Friday, with Martin Karikari scoring the only goal.

The victory marks Hearts' third consecutive league win and sends them to the top of the Ghana Premier League table with 17 points.

Hearts are now unbeaten in their last four matches, while Holy Stars have recorded two wins and two defeats in their recent four games.
