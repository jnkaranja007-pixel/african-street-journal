Ghana banks face pressure to make digital services work reliably

The Bank of Ghana reported rising mobile-money transaction values and volumes in the first quarter of 2025 compared with a year earlier.

Source: Ghanaian Times · 2026-11-18 · 1 min read

Ghanaian banks are handling more payments, transfers and account services through digital channels, while customers and businesses increasingly depend on them for everyday transactions. The shift is raising expectations for reliable, secure services that work across payment platforms.

The Bank of Ghana’s 2025 Payment Systems Oversight Annual Report said new technologies, changing customer behaviour and cooperation across the financial sector helped make payment channels more efficient, secure and accessible. Its FinTech Sector Report for the first quarter of 2025 recorded higher mobile-money transaction values and volumes than in the corresponding period of 2024.

Banks are being urged to judge digital services by completed customer journeys, not just how many people start an online process. The measures cited include transaction success rates, service availability, complaint volumes, resolution times and continued customer use; branches and service centres remain important for complex issues and disputed transactions.

Why it mattersCustomers and businesses relying on digital banking need transactions to complete; banks are advised to track success rates, complaints and resolution times.
