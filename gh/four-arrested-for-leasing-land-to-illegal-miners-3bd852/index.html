Four arrested for leasing land to illegal miners

Unit committee members allegedly took money from galamsey operators who degraded land near Abesua bridge.

Source: MyJoyOnline · 2026-10-31 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Four people, including two Unit Committee members, were arrested in Ghana's Ashanti Region for their role in illegal mining that caused the Abesua bridge to collapse. The bridge, the sole access for several communities, fell after galamsey operators excavated land around the River Kukubu, leading to its overflow. The arrests followed public outcry after a JoyNews report exposed the damage.

District Chief Executive Ibrahim Issahak confirmed that preliminary investigations link local leaders to the illegal miners, stating the Unit Committee members allegedly collected money and leased land for mining near the bridge and river buffer zone. The four suspects are in police custody and will appear in court on November 3, 2026, facing charges for illegal mining and damaging public infrastructure.

The bridge collapse has halted economic activities, preventing farmers from reaching markets and disrupting healthcare access. Schoolchildren struggle to attend classes as the swollen river makes crossing impossible. Emergency repair work is underway to make the road motorable while permanent reconstruction plans are considered.

Why it mattersResidents of Abesua and surrounding communities are cut off from markets and essential services due to the collapsed bridge.
