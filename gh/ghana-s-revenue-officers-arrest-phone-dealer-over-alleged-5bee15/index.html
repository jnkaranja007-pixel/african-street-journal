Ghana's revenue officers arrest phone dealer over alleged GH¢1,000 mobile money bribe

The arrest followed a tax sweep at Kwame Nkrumah Circle that led officials to close 48 shops over non-compliance.

Sources: MyJoyOnline and 3News · 2026-11-25 · 1 min read

Police arrested a mobile phone dealer at Kwame Nkrumah Circle in Accra on Tuesday afternoon after he allegedly sent GH¢1,000 by mobile money to a tax official during a tax enforcement exercise. The operation also resulted in 48 shops being closed over tax non-compliance.

Joseph Annan, the Accra Area Enforcement Manager of the Ghana Revenue Authority (GRA), told Joy Business that the trader claimed he had paid tax through an unidentified officer at the authority's Adabraka office. After questioning cast doubt on that claim, Annan ordered the shop shut; the trader then allegedly sent him the money. Customs Division officers of the GRA arrested the dealer, who is to give a statement while Annan also writes one, he said.

The GRA set up a temporary registration and payment stand for businesses under the Modified Taxation Scheme. The exercise was conducted with the Union of Mobile Phone Dealers of Ghana.

Why it mattersThe 48 shop operators shut during the sweep must address their tax non-compliance, while the arrested dealer is to give a statement.
