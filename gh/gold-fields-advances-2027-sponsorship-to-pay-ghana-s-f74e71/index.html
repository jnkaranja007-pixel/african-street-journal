Gold Fields advances 2027 sponsorship to pay Ghana's amputee team

Each Black Challenge player and delegation member will receive US$8,000 in outstanding per diems and match bonuses.

Sources: Ghanaian Times, MyJoyOnline and 3News · 2026-11-21 · 1 min read

Ghana's Black Challenge amputee football team was due to face Turkey in the semi-final of the 2026 Amputee Football World Cup in Mexico on Saturday, November 21, at 1:30am. The team reached the last four after beating Egypt 2-0 in the quarter-final.

Gold Fields Ghana Limited, the Ghana Football Association and the National Paralympic Committee of Ghana agreed the payments after player appeals over outstanding per diems and bonuses. NPC Ghana president Samson Deen confirmed the arrangement, under which Gold Fields will bring forward part of its 2027 sponsorship allocation.

Ghana opened the tournament with a 2-0 loss to Angola and a 1-1 draw with Colombia, then beat Iraq 6-0. It went on to defeat the United States 3-0 in the round of 16 and Egypt in the quarter-final.

Why it mattersPlayers and delegation members receive the payments now, rather than waiting on the 2027 sponsorship cycle, so they can focus on the remaining matches.
