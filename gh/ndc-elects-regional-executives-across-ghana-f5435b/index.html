NDC elects regional executives across Ghana

The governing party held elections supervised by the Electoral Commission to select new regional leaders for the next four years.

Sources: Ghanaian Times, MyJoyOnline and 3News · 2026-11-16 · 1 min read

Delegates of the National Democratic Congress (NDC) on Saturday elected new regional executive officers across Ghana to lead the party for the next four years. The polls, overseen by the Electoral Commission (EC) with significant security, saw competitive races for various positions in multiple regions.

In the Greater Accra Region, Benjamin Danso defeated incumbent Emmanuel Nii Ashie Moore by over 500 votes to become the new regional chairman. Felicia Mekpoi Bortey secured her fourth term as Regional Women’s Organiser with 65 votes, while Mohammed Abdul Muqmin Juda was elected Regional Youth Organiser. The Ashanti Region re-elected its incumbent Chairman, Augustus Andrews, who received 814 votes against Isham Alhassan's 622.

The NDC's reorganisation at the grassroots level now concludes with these regional elections, preparing the party for national officer elections in December. The Bole-Bamboi MP, Yusif Sulemana, congratulated the new Savannah Region executives, urging unity for the 2028 general elections, a region described as the home of President John Dramani Mahama.

Why it mattersThe governing National Democratic Congress has completed its regional executive elections, setting the stage for national officer elections in December as the party prepares for the 2028 general elections.
