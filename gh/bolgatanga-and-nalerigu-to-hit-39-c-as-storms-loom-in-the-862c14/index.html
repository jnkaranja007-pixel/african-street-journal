Bolgatanga and Nalerigu to hit 39°C as storms loom in the south

Localised afternoon and evening thunderstorms are forecast for coastal and inland southern areas after a misty start.

Source: 3News · 2026-11-27 · 1 min read

Bolgatanga and Nalerigu are forecast by Ghana's weather service, GMet, to peak at 39°C on Friday afternoon, while Tamale, Wa and Jirapa reach 38°C under dry, hazy skies. Across the south, sunny periods are expected before localised thunderstorms or rain move across coastal and inland areas.

Akim Oda and Tarkwa carry a 40% afternoon thunderstorm chance, and Kwahu Tafo 33°C with a 40% evening storm chance at 27°C. Kumasi is due to reach 34°C before a 40% evening storm chance, and GMet's hourly model places rain in nearby Ejisu and Mamponteng between 6pm and 8pm.

Dawn mist carried a 40% chance across 17 coastal, forest and upland stations, while the forecast sheet showed no morning rain.

Why it mattersResidents of Ejisu and Mamponteng are forecast to face rain between 6pm and 8pm on Friday.
