GRA named PR Organisation of the Year

The Ghana Revenue Authority received the award at the 2026 National PR Summit in Koforidua.

Source: Ghanaian Times · 2026-10-30 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

The Ghana Revenue Authority (GRA) was named Public Relations (PR) Organisation of the Year 2025 in the Public Sector Category at the Institute of Public Relations, Ghana (IPR Ghana) Excellence Awards. The award was presented at the 2026 National PR Summit, held in Koforidua from October 22 to 24.

The award recognises the Authority’s commitment to professional public relations and strategic communication in its work. Deputy Commissioner of Communication and Public Affairs, Mrs Florence Asante, stated that public relations is a strategic management function that builds trust and strengthens stakeholder relationships.

The GRA sees professional communication as a tool for advancing its mandate and strengthening relationships with taxpayers, the media, stakeholders, and the general public. Several staff members from the Authority’s Communication and Public Affairs Department are active members of the professional body.

Why it mattersThe Ghana Revenue Authority's recognition for public relations work may enhance its credibility with taxpayers and stakeholders.
