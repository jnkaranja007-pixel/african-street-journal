GMet forecasts 39°C heat in northern Ghana and southern storms

Bolgatanga and Nalerigu are expected to reach 39°C, while dawn mist and fog could cut visibility across coastal, forest and mountain areas.

Source: 3News · 2026-11-25 · 1 min read

Ghana's Meteorological Agency (GMet) forecasts highs of 39°C in Bolgatanga and Nalerigu on Wednesday, with dry, hazy conditions in the north. Tamale, Wa and Jirapa are forecast to reach 38°C.

In the south, GMet's Tuesday evening fog alert warned that dawn mist and fog could bring visibility down to 200 metres in coastal, forest and mountainous areas, and advised drivers to use fog lights where necessary. Sunny weather with occasional cloud is forecast after the mist, followed by afternoon or evening thunderstorms or rain in parts of the south.

Accra is forecast to reach 33°C at noon, while Kumasi is expected to hit 34°C with a 40% chance of an evening storm. Across the south, Takoradi and Axim face a 30% chance of morning rain, reaching 31°C by afternoon, when thunderstorms are forecast.

Why it mattersDrivers in coastal, forest and mountainous areas face visibility as low as 200 metres at dawn, and GMet advises using fog lights.
