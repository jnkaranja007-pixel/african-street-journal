Ghana's Black Challenge to bring home first amputee football World Cup

The squad flies back to Accra on November 26 after a 1-0 final win over Morocco in Mexico.

Sources: MyJoyOnline and 3News · 2026-11-24 · 1 min read

Ghana's Black Challenge are expected in Accra on Thursday, November 26, carrying the country's first Amputee Football World Cup trophy. Captain Richard Arthur Opentil scored the only goal as they beat Morocco in Sunday's final in San Juan de los Lagos, Mexico.

The National Paralympic Committee of Ghana confirmed the return. In a virtual meeting on Monday evening, Sports and Recreation Minister Kofi Adams told the players and technical team that each would receive a US$10,000 bonus alongside their agreed allowances. The ministry is working with partners on a national heroes' welcome.

Forward Mubarik Mohammed won the tournament's Golden Boot with seven goals. Ghana reached the final by beating defending champions Türkiye on penalties in the semi-finals, after a 1-1 draw following extra time.

Why it mattersThe returning players are due to receive US$10,000 each on top of their agreed allowances.
