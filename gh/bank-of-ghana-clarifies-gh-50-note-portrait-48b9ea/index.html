Bank of Ghana clarifies GH¢50 note portrait

The central bank stated the image of Theodosia Okoh was artistically adjusted for security, not her sister's photo.

Sources: MyJoyOnline and 3News · 2026-11-14 · 1 min read

The Bank of Ghana (BoG) has clarified that the portrait on the new GH¢50 Heritage Series banknote is of Theodosia Okoh, the designer of Ghana’s national flag, and not her sister, Dr Letitia Obeng. The central bank issued a statement on Friday, November 13, 2026, explaining that the image was developed from different photographs of Mrs Okoh but underwent artistic adjustments for currency production.

According to the BoG statement, changes made during the engraving process, such as altering Mrs Okoh’s head tie to a hairstyle common for the period, led to differences in her appearance that caused public concern. The bank acknowledged the close resemblance to Dr Obeng but confirmed the portrait was solely based on Mrs Okoh's images. The clarification aims to recognise Mrs Okoh’s contribution to Ghana’s national identity.

The Heritage Series banknotes are scheduled to enter circulation on December 1, 2026. The Bank of Ghana stated it appreciated the constructive engagement from the Okoh family and the public's interest in preserving national heritage. It pledged to ensure distinguished personalities on currency are represented with dignity and respect.

Why it mattersThe Bank of Ghana confirmed the portrait on the new GH¢50 note is Theodosia Okoh, which will enter circulation on December 1, 2026.
