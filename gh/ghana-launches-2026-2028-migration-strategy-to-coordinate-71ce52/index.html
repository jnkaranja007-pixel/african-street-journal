Ghana launches 2026-2028 migration strategy to coordinate agencies

Interior Minister Muntaka Mohammed Mubarak says the plan will strengthen migrant protection, border management and action against trafficking and smuggling.

Source: MyJoyOnline · 2026-11-18 · 1 min read

Ghana has launched the International Organisation for Migration (IOM) Strategy for 2026-2028, a plan to coordinate state agencies and strengthen collaboration with society on migration governance. Interior Minister Muntaka Mohammed Mubarak launched the revised strategy with IOM, the Ghana Immigration Service and other state institutions.

Mubarak said the plan would improve systems to identify, refer and protect migrants, support voluntary return and help migrants reintegrate, particularly vulnerable people. He said stronger national referral mechanisms would help ensure vulnerable migrants receive support. The strategy also calls for better migration data, contingency planning and risk monitoring to respond to insecurity, climate vulnerability, displacement and public health emergencies.

IOM Ghana led development of the plan. IOM Chief of Mission Fatou Diallo Ndiaye said the organisation would support Ghana’s efforts and promote policy coordination at national, regional and international levels. By 2028, the strategy is intended to deliver more coordinated border management, stronger action against trafficking and smuggling, improved reintegration support and more reliable migration data.

Why it mattersThe strategy sets out stronger referral and reintegration support for vulnerable migrants, with progress expected by 2028.
