Ghana’s Black Challenge beat USA 3-1 to reach Amputee World Cup quarter-finals

The national team scored once before half-time and twice after the break, setting up a match with Egypt on Thursday in Mexico.

Sources: MyJoyOnline and 3News · 2026-11-19 · 1 min read

Ghana’s Black Challenge beat the United States 3-1 in the Round of 16 in Mexico to advance to the Amputee Football World Cup quarter-finals. The national amputee football team took the lead in the first half and added two goals after the break.

Coach Richard Obeng said the team stayed composed and followed its plan. Ghana entered the knockout match after beating Iraq 6-0 in its final Group D game. Obeng said Ghana would prepare a strategy for Egypt, which he described as a strong team.

Ghana was scheduled to face Egypt on Thursday, November 19, for a place in the semi-finals.

Why it mattersThe Black Challenge must prepare for Egypt’s quarter-final, with victory needed to progress to the semi-finals.
