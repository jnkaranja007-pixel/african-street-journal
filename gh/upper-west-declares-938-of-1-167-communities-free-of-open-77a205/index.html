Upper West declares 938 of 1,167 communities free of open defecation

The regional share has risen from about 29% in 2011 to 79%, while Nandom's chief promises education across 88 communities.

Sources: MyJoyOnline and 3News · 2026-11-21 · 1 min read

The Upper West Region has declared 938 of its 1,167 communities free of open defecation, up from about 29% in 2011 to 79% now. The figures were reported at a World Toilet Day event in Nandom, where officials described the region as leading Ghana in rural sanitation.

Freda Naatu, the region's environmental health director, said safe toilets protect women's and girls' health, safety and dignity. Theresa Swanzy Baffoe, project manager of the Healthy Future for All project run by the Dutch development organisation SNV, said households without toilets in Nandom had fallen from 37.31% to 6.92%. The project, run with the Nandom Municipal and Lambussie District assemblies, has reached 88 communities.

Ko Naa Thomas Gyireh Domapie, the chief who chaired the event, pledged to intensify sanitation education across Nandom's 88 communities. He said he expected all of them to be declared free of open defecation by about this time in 2027.

Why it mattersNandom's 88 communities are due to receive intensified sanitation education, with their chief pledging open-defecation-free status across all of them by about this time in 2027.
