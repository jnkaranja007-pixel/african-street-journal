UN advocate calls for financial health council in Ghana

Queen Máxima urged a coordinated approach to boost financial well-being and socio-economic development for Ghanaians.

Source: Ghanaian Times · 2026-11-07 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Queen Máxima of the Netherlands, the UN Secretary-General’s Special Advocate for Financial Health, has called for Ghana to establish a Financial Health Council. This council would coordinate government and private sector actions to improve the financial well-being of Ghanaians. She spoke in Accra at the joint inauguration of the Working Committee on the National Financial Inclusion and Development Strategy.

The committee will develop a strategy for implementation between 2027 and 2031. Queen Máxima stated that access to financial services like bank accounts, insurance, and pensions does not automatically ensure financial security. She outlined four components of financial health: managing daily finances, withstanding shocks, achieving long-term goals, and maintaining financial confidence.

Queen Máxima emphasized that financial inclusion is a means of achieving financial health and broader socio-economic development. The strategy document is intended for implementation over the next five years, from 2027 to 2031.

Why it mattersGhanaians will be directly affected by a new council and strategy aimed at improving their financial well-being.
