Ghana's food imports reached GH¢36.46 billion in 2025

An opinion article says investment in irrigation, technology, finance, storage, processing and skilled workers is needed to expand domestic production.

Source: Ghanaian Times · 2026-11-26 · 1 min read

Ghana imported food products worth approximately GH¢36.46 billion in 2025, the Ghana Statistical Service (GSS), the country's official statistics agency, reported. An opinion article in the Ghanaian Times says the country imports more food than it should despite its land, water, climate and human resources.

The article argues that Ghana has not fully used agriculture to drive growth, industrialisation and employment. It says closing the gap between the country's agricultural potential and domestic production requires investment in irrigation, modern technology, agricultural finance, storage, processing and skilled workers.

The article presents agriculture as a pillar of Ghana's New Economy policy, but gives no timetable or funding commitment for the proposed investment.

Why it mattersFarm producers face a domestic supply gap, with food imports valued at approximately GH¢36.46 billion in 2025.
