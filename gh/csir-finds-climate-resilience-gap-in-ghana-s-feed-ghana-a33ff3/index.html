CSIR finds climate resilience gap in Ghana’s Feed Ghana Programme

The assessment says the programme is not aligned with the climate-resilience pillar of the African agriculture strategy as sector policy is revised.

Source: Ghanaian Times · 2026-11-28 · 1 min read

Ghana’s Council for Scientific and Industrial Research (CSIR) found that the Feed Ghana Programme is not aligned with the climate-resilience pillar of the Comprehensive Africa Agriculture Development Programme’s Kampala Strategy. The assessment was conducted by CSIR’s Science and Technology Policy Research Institute as the government revises its Food and Agriculture Sector Development Policy.

Researcher Justina Onumah said the programme has production targets but needs greater attention to climate resilience. Her team put forward four options: revise the sector policy while keeping Feed Ghana, align the programme directly with the continental framework, revise both together, or make no immediate changes. The options were assessed for policy coherence, feasibility and timing, programme continuity and cost.

The sector policy dates to 2008. CSIR’s research institute is on the technical committee for the new policy and is expected to contribute evidence, Onumah said.

Why it mattersThe government’s technical committee is weighing four policy options, and CSIR’s research institute is expected to contribute evidence to the sector-policy revision.
