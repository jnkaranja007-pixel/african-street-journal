Hearts of Oak beat Heart of Lions 3-0

Ransford Mensah and John Antwi scored for Hearts of Oak in their Ghana Premier League victory.

Source: 3News · 2026-11-09 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Accra giants Hearts of Oak secured a decisive 3-0 win against Heart of Lions in a Ghana Premier League Matchweek 10 fixture at the University of Ghana Stadium in Legon on Sunday.

Ransford Mensah opened the scoring in the second minute, with John Antwi adding a brace in the 25th and 48th minutes to seal the victory. Abdul Karim was named the NASCO Man of the Match for his performance.

The win moves Hearts of Oak to 20 points after 10 matches, strengthening their pursuit of league leaders, and provides a boost for coach Nebojsa Kapor's team before their next match against FC Samartex 1996.
