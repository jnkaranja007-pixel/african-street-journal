Ghana's Black Challenge wins first amputee football world title

The team beat Morocco 1–0 in Mexico in the first all-African final of the tournament.

Sources: MyJoyOnline and 3News · 2026-11-23 · 1 min read

Ghana's Black Challenge beat Morocco 1–0 on Sunday in San Juan de los Lagos, Mexico, to win the 2026 Amputee Football World Cup and the team's first world title. It is the second time an African nation has won the tournament, after Angola in 2018.

The final was the first all-African title match in the competition. Ghana had also beaten Morocco in the 2023 African Para Games and the 2024 African Amputee Cup of Nations finals. Forward Mubarik Mohammed won the Golden Boot with seven goals.

On the way to the final, Ghana beat the United States 3–1 in the Round of 16 and Egypt 2–0 in the quarter-finals. In the semi-final, they defeated defending champions Türkiye 4–3 on penalties after a 1–1 draw following extra time.

Why it mattersMubarik Mohammed's seven goals earned him the tournament's Golden Boot, the first individual award for the team at this World Cup.
