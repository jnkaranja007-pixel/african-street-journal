Ghana and Poland explore investment and security cooperation

Deputy Minister James Gyakye Quayson met Poland’s Undersecretary of State Wojciech Zajaczkowski in Accra to discuss trade, investment, and industrial development.

Source: MyJoyOnline · 2026-10-29 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Ghana and Poland are exploring deeper cooperation in trade, investment, industrial development, technology, education, and security. Deputy Minister for Foreign Affairs, James Gyakye Quayson, met Poland’s Undersecretary of State for Foreign Affairs, Wojciech Zajaczkowski, in Accra on Thursday, October 29, 2026, to review bilateral relations.

Mr. Zajaczkowski expressed Poland’s renewed interest in Ghana, citing its political stability and economic potential as attractive for Polish businesses. He proposed Polish experts could visit Ghana to share technologies in border management and critical infrastructure protection. Mr. Quayson encouraged Polish investment in sectors like cocoa and gold processing, agribusiness, IT, and manufacturing.

Discussions also covered the African Continental Free Trade Area (AfCFTA), headquartered in Accra, as a platform for businesses in Ghana to access continental markets. Both officials expressed confidence in expanding cooperation in trade, investment, and technology transfer based on historical ties and mutual benefit.

Why it mattersGhanaian and Polish officials met to discuss investment opportunities and security cooperation, aiming to strengthen bilateral ties and economic development.
