Wa West reaches 92% yellow fever vaccination coverage

Ghana Health Service Director-General Dr. Samuel Kaba Akoriyea announced the vaccine is safe and internationally approved, with only one mild side effect recorded.

Source: MyJoyOnline · 2026-10-23 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Yellow fever vaccination in Ghana's Wa West District has reached 92% of the target population. Director-General of the Ghana Health Service, Dr. Samuel Kaba Akoriyea, stated the vaccine is free, dry, safe, and internationally approved. Out of 225,446 people vaccinated, only one mild headache has been reported as a side effect.

The campaign, targeting individuals aged 9 months to 60 years, has vaccinated 79,238 males and 92,768 females. Dr. Akoriyea expressed concern about lower male participation and announced a house-to-house mop-up under Free Primary Health Care to achieve 100% coverage. He also deployed 100 motorbikes to the Upper West Region to support the vaccination efforts.

To further boost coverage, health services will collaborate with Veterinary Services to vaccinate nomadic Fulani herdsmen and report sick individuals from neighbouring countries. Dr. Akoriyea stressed his commitment to achieving full vaccination, stating, "If we don’t cover 100% yellow fever vaccination, I cannot go to sleep."

Why it mattersResidents of Wa West District are receiving free, safe, and internationally approved yellow fever vaccinations, with efforts underway to reach 100% coverage.
