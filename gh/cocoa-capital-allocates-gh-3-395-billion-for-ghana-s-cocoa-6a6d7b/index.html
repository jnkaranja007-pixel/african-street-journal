Cocoa Capital allocates GH¢3.395 billion for Ghana's cocoa purchases

The commercial paper, listed in Accra, drew about GH¢3.59 billion in investor demand and will fund the Ghana Cocoa Board's 2026/27 bean buying.

Source: MyJoyOnline · 2026-11-23 · 1 min read

Cocoa Capital PLC accepted GH¢3.395 billion from its inaugural commercial paper issuance to finance Ghana's cocoa bean purchases for the 2026/27 crop season. The paper was listed on the Ghana Fixed Income Market on October 14, 2026, at an annual interest rate of 11%.

The issuance drew approximately GH¢3.59 billion in investor demand and was the first under the company's GH¢16.3 billion note programme. Kwamina Asomaning, president of the Ghana Association of Banks and chief executive of Stanbic Bank Ghana, said timely funding would help move beans from farmers through licensed buyers and marketers to international markets, supporting exports and foreign-exchange earnings.

Asomaning said the programme's success would depend on prudent use of the proceeds, transparent administration and repayment on time. He also urged full and consistent implementation of announced cocoa-sector reforms to give investors and businesses across the value chain greater certainty.

Why it mattersThe accepted proceeds will finance the Ghana Cocoa Board's 2026/27 bean purchases, supporting the buying chain that moves cocoa from farmers to international markets.
