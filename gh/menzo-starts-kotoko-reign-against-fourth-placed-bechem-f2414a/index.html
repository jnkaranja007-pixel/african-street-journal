Menzo starts Kotoko reign against fourth-placed Bechem United

The new coach takes charge on Sunday with Kotoko 11th on 14 points after 11 matches in the Ghana Premier League.

Source: Ghanaian Times · 2026-11-20 · 1 min read

Stanley Purl Menzo will begin his tenure as Kumasi Asante Kotoko coach on Sunday, leading the Ghana Premier League side against Bechem United at Nana Fosu Gyeabour Park in Bechem. Kotoko enter the Matchday 12 fixture 11th in the table with 14 points from 11 games.

Menzo, also the club’s director of football, takes over a side that has won three, drawn five and lost three matches. Bechem United are fourth with 19 points and will host the game.

The round began on Saturday with Tema-based Vision FC scheduled to host debutants Debibi United at the Nii Adjei Kraku II Sports Complex in Tema New Town. Sunday’s other fixtures include second-placed Accra Hearts of Oak against Aduana FC and league leaders Medeama SC against FC Ashantigold 04.

Why it mattersKotoko supporters have a new coach tasked with guiding a side on 14 points from 11 games, beginning with Sunday's match at Bechem United.
