Ghanaian man stabbed to death in London

Metropolitan Police have launched a murder investigation into the death of 21-year-old Thomas Oduro.

Sources: MyJoyOnline and 3News · 2026-11-15 · 1 min read

A 21-year-old Ghanaian man, Thomas Oduro, was stabbed to death on Friday, November 13, in north London. Emergency services were called to Lordship Lane in Haringey around 6 pm after reports of an attack. Paramedics attended the scene but could not save Oduro, who was pronounced dead.

The Metropolitan Police identified the victim as Thomas Oduro from Tottenham and have informed his next of kin. Detective Chief Inspector Phil Clarke is leading the murder investigation and appealed for witnesses who were near Lordship Recreation Ground at the time of the incident to come forward. Increased police presence is expected in the area.

The killing of Oduro follows a series of recent deaths of young Ghanaians abroad, including incidents in the United States and Canada. Investigations into Oduro's death are ongoing, and police are asking anyone with information to contact them via 101 or Crimestoppers.

Why it mattersThe death of Thomas Oduro in London is being investigated by police, with his family receiving support.
