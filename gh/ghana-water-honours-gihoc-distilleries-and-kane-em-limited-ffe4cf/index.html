Ghana Water honours GIHOC Distilleries and KANE-EM Limited

The water utility recognised two major commercial clients for their prompt bill payments at an event in Accra.

Source: Ghanaian Times · 2026-10-31 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Ghana Water Limited's Accra West Region recognised GIHOC Distilleries and KANE-EM Limited yesterday for consistently paying their water bills on time. The event, held in Accra as part of Customer Service Month, aimed to strengthen relationships between the utility and its commercial clients.

Ing. Francis Lamptey, Regional Chief Manager, stated that timely payments enable Ghana Water to meet operational expenses, including electricity bills, treatment chemicals, repairs, and staff salaries. GIHOC Distilleries CEO, Mr Jones Borteye Applerh, highlighted water's essential role in beverage production and affirmed the company's commitment to their partnership.

The Accra West Region serves approximately 67,000 customers across five districts. As tokens of appreciation, Ghana Water presented GIHOC Distilleries and KANE-EM Limited with G-Water products and souvenirs. The utility plans similar appreciation visits for other commercial customers to foster stronger business ties.

Why it mattersPrompt bill payments by commercial customers like GIHOC Distilleries and KANE-EM Limited help Ghana Water Limited cover operational expenses and sustain its services to 67,000 customers.
