Julius Nainoo elected NDC Berekum West chairman

Delegates elected Julius Nainoo with 334 votes, unseating incumbent James Ntim who received 302 votes.

Source: MyJoyOnline · 2026-10-26 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Julius Nainoo has been elected the new National Democratic Congress (NDC) chairman for the Berekum West Constituency in the Bono Region. He secured 334 valid votes, defeating the incumbent chairman, James Ntim, who received 302 votes. The election was supervised by the Electoral Commission at Koraso.

Other elected officials include Simeon Sakyi as Vice Chairman with 336 votes, and Regina Ferka as Women Organiser with 43 votes. Several other positions, including Communications Officer and Youth Organiser, were filled by acclamation. Newly elected chairman Nainoo pledged to work towards making the NDC more appealing in the constituency.

The newly elected executives aim to work for the NDC to retain the Berekum West parliamentary seat and political power in the 2028 elections. Madam Esther Mmra secured 39 votes for Deputy Women Organiser, and Sampson Adjei polled 445 votes for the Zongo Caucus position.
