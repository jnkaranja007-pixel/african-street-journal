Ghana prepares second intake of 8,000 health workers

MP Titus Beyuo says the planned hires would follow an earlier intake and include pharmacists, nutritionists and dietitians.

Sources: MyJoyOnline and 3News · 2026-11-20 · 1 min read

Ghana is preparing to recruit another 8,000 health professionals into the public system after bringing a similar number on board earlier this year, Prof. Titus Beyuo, the MP for Lambussie, told Parliament on Thursday, November 19. He said the second intake was intended to ease staffing shortages and create jobs for professionals awaiting public-service posts.

Beyuo said the first 8,000 were hired with financial clearance, and that the earlier intake included pharmacists, nutritionists and dietitians, some of whom had waited almost five years for recruitment. He said arrangements were under way for the next intake and rejected the view that the 2027 Budget’s lack of an explicit reference to clearance meant the plan had been dropped.

The 2027 Budget reports clearance for 16,000 health professionals, with 8,600 already on the government payroll. In May, the Health Ministry said more than 7,200 professionals had secured placements in the earlier exercise.
