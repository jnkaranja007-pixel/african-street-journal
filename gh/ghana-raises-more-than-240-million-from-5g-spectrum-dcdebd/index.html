Ghana raises more than $240 million from 5G spectrum allocation

The revised licensing framework opens three frequency bands to competing operators, with consumers expected to access 5G early next year.

Source: MyJoyOnline · 2026-11-25 · 1 min read

Ghana has secured more than $240 million by allocating spectrum for fifth-generation (5G) mobile services, Communications Minister Samuel Nartey George told Parliament during debate on the 2027 Budget. The allocation covers the 700 megahertz, 2.3 gigahertz and 3 gigahertz bands.

The government changed the licensing framework after the National Communications Authority removed exclusivity from the wholesale 5G infrastructure licence held by Next Gen Infraco Limited in July 2026. The authority invited applications, and MTN Ghana, Telecel Ghana and Goal Telecommunications qualified to advance in the licensing process.

George said President John Dramani Mahama was expected to announce the network’s launch before the end of 2026. Consumers are expected to begin using the service early next year.
