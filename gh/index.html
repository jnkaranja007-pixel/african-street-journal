Northshore Apparel Hub Opens, Promises 10,000 Jobs in Northern Ghana

The newly commissioned garment factory in Savelugu aims to stimulate economic activity and provide significant employment opportunities, according to the GEXIM CEO.

The Northshore Apparel Hub, a wholly Ghanaian-owned facility, has been commissioned in Savelugu, with the Ghana Export-Import Bank (GEXIM) CEO, Sylvester Adinam Mensah, stating it will create up to 10,000 jobs when fully operational. The factory is expected to generate 3,000 jobs in its initial phase and aims to boost economic activity across the Northern Region. This project, financed through a blended model including GEXIM and Germany's KfW Development Bank, is seen as a major step in empowering local businesses and competing globally.

Mensah highlighted that the factory's impact extends beyond direct employment on the production floor, creating additional opportunities in transportation, food services, accommodation, maintenance, packaging, and logistics. He emphasized that wages earned by employees will improve livelihoods, support children's education, increase access to healthcare, and strengthen local businesses, thereby extending the factory's economic value throughout the community.

The GEXIM CEO described the facility as a flagship investment under the bank's five-year strategic plan, prioritizing garment and apparel manufacturing for job creation, industrialization, and export growth. The establishment of the hub in Savelugu is intended to demonstrate confidence in the skills and potential of the Northern Region's population and prove that indigenous businesses can undertake large-scale industrial projects.

Why it mattersThe Northshore Apparel Hub will affect farmers by potentially increasing demand for local food services and creating jobs that could reduce rural-to-urban migration.

Ghana Government Amends Procurement Law to Favor Local Goods

New legislation will require state institutions to prioritize Ghanaian-made products, including uniforms and equipment, by listing licensed local manufacturers.

The Ghanaian government is amending the Public Procurement Act to ensure state institutions purchase locally manufactured goods. President John Mahama announced the change, which will introduce a schedule of licensed Ghanaian products and their producers, compiled by the Ghana Standards Authority. Government agencies, schools, and hospitals will be compelled to buy from these approved local companies when their required items are available.

This policy aims to directly support domestic manufacturing and create a guaranteed market for Ghanaian producers, according to President Mahama. He cited the production of uniforms and equipment as key areas where local capacity exists but has been undermined by a preference for imports, referencing the past struggles of the Kumasi Shoe Factory. The amendment intends to bypass competitive tendering for listed Ghanaian products.

The president challenged Northshore Apparel to expand its capacity to produce uniforms and protective gear for security services. He stated that Ghanaian manufacturers already possess the capability to produce items currently imported, such as uniforms, boots, and industrial protective gear, emphasizing the need to support these local industries.

Why it mattersGhanaian businesses producing listed goods will benefit from guaranteed government contracts, bypassing competitive tendering and securing a market for their products.

Security Forces Seize Mining Equipment Near Black Volta River

Ten individuals arrested and heavy machinery confiscated during a raid on illegal mining camps that threatened the Black Volta River in Ghana's Savannah Region.

A joint operation by the National Anti-Illegal Mining Operations Secretariat (NAIMOS), the Ghana Armed Forces, and the Ghana Police Service has resulted in the arrest of ten individuals and the seizure of significant mining equipment. The raid targeted illegal mining camps operating near the Black Volta River in the Kpali area of the Savannah Region, an operation aimed at curbing environmental degradation. The operation uncovered active mining sites and extensive land disturbance.

During the raid, authorities arrested three Chinese nationals and seven Ghanaians, seizing two excavators, three excavator monitors, a Toyota Hilux, a fuel bowser, a generator, batteries, and communication devices. Three firearms, 25 live cartridges, nine communication radios, and 12 mobile phones were also recovered, along with cash in local and foreign currencies. Fifteen makeshift structures and a generator were destroyed to prevent further illegal operations.

The arrested suspects and seized items have been transferred to the Savannah Regional Police Command for further investigation and legal proceedings. NAIMOS stated that protecting the Black Volta and its catchment area is crucial for safeguarding Ghana's water resources from the impacts of illegal mining activities. The seized excavators and heavy equipment are being held at designated sites.

Why it mattersIllegal mining operations threaten the Black Volta River through land degradation and pollution, impacting water resources vital for communities and ecosystems.

Committee Formed to Implement Ghana Constitution Review

An 11-member committee has been inaugurated to oversee the implementation of proposals for reviewing Ghana's 1992 Constitution, aiming to strengthen democratic governance.

Attorney General and Minister of Justice, Dr Dominic Ayine, inaugurated an 11-member committee tasked with implementing proposals from Ghana's 1992 Constitution review. Chaired by Presidential Legal Counsel Marietta Brew Appiah-Oppong, the committee includes representatives from legal, parliamentary, and local government sectors. This body will examine the government's position paper and the recommendations of a previous review committee to create an implementation framework.

The committee's formation follows the government's official response to the findings of the Constitution Review Committee, which was chaired by Professor H. Kwesi Prempeh. The newly formed group's mandate is to translate the review's recommendations into actionable steps. The government has stated that the process will prioritize inclusiveness and transparency.

The broader objective of this constitutional review implementation process is to enhance Ghana's democratic governance. The committee's membership reflects a range of expertise, bringing together diverse perspectives to support the complex task of constitutional reform and its practical application across the nation.

Why it mattersThis committee's work on constitutional review implementation could affect the legal and regulatory framework within which businesses operate, potentially impacting investors.

University of Ghana Expands Online and Distance Learning Programs

The University of Ghana is increasing access to higher education by offering more fully online courses and specialized academic programs, including a new Educational Technology.

The University of Ghana's College of Education is broadening access to higher education by expanding its distance learning and fully online programs. Professor Samuel Amponsah, Dean of Continuing and Distance Education, stated on JoyFM that these initiatives, alongside new specialized offerings, are designed to meet the evolving needs of students and professionals across the country.

The School of Continuing and Distance Education currently operates through 11 centers, offering five diploma and three degree programs. Innovative courses like Educational Technology, which is fully online, have been introduced, alongside programs in Adult Education, Human Resource Development, Professional Development, and Development Education, extending up to PhD levels. Seven new programs, including Youth Development and Leadership, are also being rolled out.

Further postgraduate offerings in Educational Leadership and Management, Educational Innovations, and Educational Pedagogy are available through the School of Education and Leadership. Teacher Education provides approximately 20 degree programs developed in partnership with various university departments, covering disciplines from dance and archaeology to engineering. A sports management program is also attracting significant interest.

Why it mattersProfessionals and students seeking flexible higher education options can now access a wider range of online and distance learning programs, including specialized fields like Educational Technology and Sports Management.
