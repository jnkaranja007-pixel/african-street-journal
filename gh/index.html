Ghana ambassador condoles family of teen shot in New York

Javits Badu Nkansah Frimpong, 15, was shot dead in the Bronx after moving to the US for a better future.

Ghana’s Ambassador to the United States expressed condolences to the family of 15-year-old Javits Badu Nkansah Frimpong, who was shot dead in the Bronx, New York. The Ambassador conveyed the nation's solidarity with the bereaved family in a message on behalf of President John Dramani Mahama.

The message, addressed to the victim’s father, Thomas Frimpong, described Javits’s death as tragic and untimely, noting he had moved to the US less than a year prior for education. The Ambassador called for a thorough investigation by law enforcement to establish facts and ensure justice, stressing the need to protect children from bullying and racial discrimination.

The Government of Ghana assured Thomas Frimpong of its full support in the pursuit of justice for his son. The Ambassador prayed for strength and comfort for the family and for the soul of the deceased to rest in peace.

Why it mattersA 15-year-old Ghanaian teenager was shot and killed in New York, prompting a message of condolence and a call for justice from Ghana's ambassador.

Four arrested for leasing land to illegal miners

Unit committee members allegedly collected money from galamsey operators for land near a collapsed bridge.

Four people, including two Unit Committee members, were arrested in Ghana's Ashanti Region for their role in illegal mining that caused the Abesua bridge collapse. The suspects allegedly leased land to galamsey operators along the River Kukubu in the Atwima Mponua District. The bridge's collapse has cut off five communities, including Abesua No.1 and No.2, from main roads.

District Chief Executive Ibrahim Issahak confirmed the arrests, stating preliminary investigations link local leaders to the illegal miners. He said the unit committee members are accused of taking money to lease land near the bridge and river buffer zone. The four are in police custody and will appear in court on November 3, 2026, facing charges for illegal mining and damaging public infrastructure.

The collapse has halted economic activities, preventing farmers from reaching markets and disrupting health services. Schoolchildren struggle to attend Atwima Asamang Basic School, often missing classes due to the swollen river. Emergency repair works, including clearing the culvert and reinforcing the road, are underway while permanent reconstruction plans are considered.

Why it mattersResidents of five communities are cut off from markets, health facilities, and schools due to the collapse of the Abesua bridge, caused by illegal mining activities.

National Theatre hosts Funworld 2026 for breast cancer awareness

The event combines education, entertainment, and competition to raise awareness of mental well-being and breast cancer among schoolchildren.

The National Theatre of Ghana will host Funworld 2026, an event using performing arts to promote mental health awareness and breast cancer education. This initiative aims to raise awareness among schoolchildren, teachers, parents, and the wider community through education, entertainment, and competition.

Funworld has been a family edutainment platform for over three decades, helping young people develop creative abilities and showcase talents, contributing to figures like Shatta Wale and Martha Ankomah. This year's event will feature traditional and contemporary dances, drama, poetry, and music, celebrating Ghanaian culture while nurturing confidence in pupils. Springfield School and Gloryhill International will participate in a debate competition.

The programme will also highlight mental health, emotional well-being, and the importance of supportive environments for children. Educational messages and artistic presentations will encourage pupils to understand breast cancer awareness and health consciousness. Promasidor Ghana, through its Twisco product, is sponsoring the event, while Kiddafest 2026 is scheduled for December 17 and 18.

Why it mattersOver 300 residents of Asanko Gold Ghana Ltd's host communities benefited from breast cancer screening, indicating a direct health impact on the population.

Police hunt suspect after two shot dead in Sakumono

George and Irene Atuguba were killed following an alleged GH¢500,000 debt dispute.

The Ghana Police Service is searching for Samuel Christopher Osel Anto after he allegedly shot and killed George Atuguba, 57, and his niece Irene Atuguba, 47, at their Sakumono residence on Friday, October 30, 2026. Officers found the victims with fatal gunshot wounds after responding to a report of a financial dispute.

Preliminary investigations suggest the killings stemmed from a GH¢500,000 loan George Atuguba had allegedly failed to repay to the suspect. Anto, accompanied by Beatrice Mumuni, reportedly went to the home to demand the money, leading to a confrontation and the fatal shootings. The bodies were taken to the Police Hospital Mortuary.

The Accra Regional Police Command has appealed to the public for any information that could help apprehend Anto. The police are working to bring the suspect to justice.

GRA named PR Organisation of the Year

The Ghana Revenue Authority received the award at the 2026 National PR Summit in Koforidua.

The Ghana Revenue Authority (GRA) was named Public Relations (PR) Organisation of the Year 2025 in the Public Sector Category at the Institute of Public Relations, Ghana (IPR Ghana) Excellence Awards. The award was presented at the 2026 National PR Summit, held in Koforidua from October 22 to 24.

The award recognises the Authority’s commitment to professional public relations and strategic communication in its work. Deputy Commissioner of Communication and Public Affairs, Mrs Florence Asante, stated that public relations is a strategic management function that builds trust and strengthens stakeholder relationships.

The GRA sees professional communication as a tool for advancing its mandate and strengthening relationships with taxpayers, the media, stakeholders, and the general public. Several staff members from the Authority’s Communication and Public Affairs Department are active members of the professional body.

Why it mattersThe Ghana Revenue Authority's recognition for public relations work may enhance its credibility with taxpayers and stakeholders.
