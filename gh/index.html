Ghana Air Force evacuates injured immigration officer after Hamile blast

An explosion at Hamile killed three people and injured an immigration officer who is now being airlifted to Accra.

The Ghana Air Force deployed a medical team to airlift an injured immigration officer from Nandom Hospital to Accra on Tuesday, November 3, following an explosion at Hamile. The blast occurred during a border patrol operation along an unauthorised route, killing three people including an immigration officer and two civilians. The injured officer was receiving emergency care at Nandom Hospital before the transfer to the capital for specialist treatment.

Interior Minister Mohammed Muntaka Mubarak announced the evacuation in Parliament, stating the Air Force team was en route to Accra with the officer. He expressed the government's condolences to the families of the deceased and the Ghana Immigration Service. The minister noted the incident highlights the dangers faced by immigration personnel protecting the nation's borders, particularly near unauthorised crossing points.

The circumstances of the explosion, including the nature and origin of the suspected explosives on the tricycle, were not detailed by the minister. The government is working to contact the relatives of the affected officers. The minister assured Parliament of the government's commitment to the welfare and safety of officers in border communities.

Why it mattersAn immigration officer was injured and three people died in an explosion at Hamile, requiring an emergency medical evacuation.

Tenerife striker De Miguel dies after collision

Jesus de Miguel, 30, collapsed after a clash with goalkeeper Jesus Owono during a Spanish second division match.

Tenerife striker Jesus de Miguel died yesterday at age 30 after a collision during a Spanish second-tier match against Andorra. The game was abandoned seven minutes into stoppage time after De Miguel collapsed following a clash with away goalkeeper Jesus Owono. Medical staff treated De Miguel on the pitch for over five minutes before he was taken to hospital by ambulance.

Police in Spain have opened proceedings for possible involuntary manslaughter following the death of De Miguel, a source from the National Police said. Judicial authorities in the Canary Islands stated that the investigation is a routine procedure for any non-natural death. Owono was shown a straight red card and has since deactivated his Instagram account due to online abuse.

The Spanish Football Federation postponed all matches scheduled for yesterday, including Copa del Rey and Copa de la Reina fixtures. UEFA announced that a period of silence would be observed before all European games this week. De Miguel joined Tenerife from Castellon ahead of the 2025-26 campaign, scoring 11 goals last season.

Why it mattersFootball fans and players across Europe are mourning the death of Jesus de Miguel, a player who died after a match collision.

Ghana arrests three in Lebanon cocaine bust

Authorities in Ghana apprehended a Syrian, a Tunisian, and a Lebanese national following a 46.7 kilogramme cocaine seizure in Beirut.

Ghanaian authorities arrested three foreign nationals, a Syrian, a Tunisian, and a Lebanese, in connection with the seizure of 46.7 kilogrammes of cocaine at Beirut’s Rafic Hariri International Airport. The arrests occurred on Tuesday, November 3, as part of an investigation into a drug trafficking network that moved the cocaine from Accra to Beirut. Two suspects were detained in Accra earlier in the day, with the third apprehended around 5 p.m. by the Narcotics Control Commission (NACOC).

The operation followed intelligence shared by Lebanese law enforcement after a Syrian national was arrested in Beirut on Saturday, October 31, arriving from Accra with the cocaine hidden in luggage. Lebanese Customs officers found 40 packages containing the drugs, concealed with sophisticated materials and electronic tracking devices. NACOC stated the arrest in Lebanon resulted from a coordinated controlled-delivery operation involving both Ghanaian and Lebanese agencies to identify those in the supply chain.

Lebanese authorities described the seizure as the largest drug bust in the airport's history. Preliminary findings suggest the suspect travelled from Syria to Accra before returning with the consignment. Investigations are ongoing to identify accomplices and dismantle the transnational criminal network. NACOC confirmed it is pursuing suspected collaborators within Ghana.

Why it mattersThree foreign nationals were arrested in Ghana as part of an international drug trafficking investigation involving a large cocaine seizure in Lebanon.

Politics · 2026-11-04

NPP targets minister for exposing wrongdoing, says NDC

Fifi Kwetey claims the ruling party is retaliating against Samuel Okudzeto Ablakwa for revealing alleged theft and abuse.

NDC General Secretary Fifi Fiavi Kwetey accused the New Patriotic Party (NPP) of targeting Foreign Affairs Minister Samuel Okudzeto Ablakwa. Kwetey stated on JoyNews that the NPP is retaliating against Ablakwa for exposing alleged wrongdoing during its administration. He challenged the NPP to produce evidence that the minister stole public funds, dismissing protests in Accra as a campaign against Ablakwa for holding the NPP accountable.

The NPP youth wing demonstrated in Accra, demanding the recovery of GH¢16.4 million they allege was unaccounted for during the evacuation of 1,964 Ghanaians. Protesters requested flight invoices, payment records, a full beneficiary list, and an independent audit. Kwetey dismissed concerns about signatures and alleged duplications in ministry documents as peripheral, asserting Ablakwa provided "hardcore evidence" of stealing and abuse with authentic documentation.

Kwetey warned the NPP that their current path of "low-level politicking" would hinder their political recovery. He recalled his own decision to leave a role after the 2016 election defeat due to pressure to find scandals, stating he would only speak on genuine wrongdoing. The NDC General Secretary maintained that any shortfalls should have been reported at the airport by recipients, not months later.

Why it mattersGhanaian citizens, particularly those involved in or observing political accountability, are affected by accusations of corruption and political retaliation.

Ghana opens two new health compounds in Keta

The facilities at Seva and Tengeykope-Anyrawase were built in seven months and include outpatient departments and delivery rooms.

The Volta Regional Minister, Mr James Gunu, inaugurated two new Community-based Health Planning and Services (CHPS) compounds in Keta Municipality on Monday. The facilities at Seva and Tengeykope-Anyrawase were constructed within seven months using funds from the District Assemblies Common Fund (DACF). Sparshey Construction Company Limited and CityMax Ghana Limited built the compounds.

The new health centres feature outpatient departments, consulting rooms, delivery rooms, admission wards, and records units. They also include nurses’ quarters with essential amenities to support health personnel. The Regional Minister stated that the nurses’ quarters would allow staff to live closer to the facilities and respond to emergencies promptly. The Municipal Health Officer assured residents of the health directorate’s commitment to working with communities for effective use.

Minister Gunu noted that 253 more CHPS compounds are needed nationally to reach the target of 460 facilities supporting the Free Primary Healthcare programme. He also mentioned that water challenges in Anyako and Seva have been addressed, and a new transformer was installed for stable electricity. In a related development, a six-unit classroom block was handed over to the Anlo Afiadenyigba Roman Catholic Church and Basic School.

Why it mattersResidents of Keta Municipality will have improved access to healthcare services with the opening of two new CHPS compounds.
