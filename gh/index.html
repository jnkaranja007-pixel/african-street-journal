UENR launches Ghana Battery Network to boost local production

The university urges a shift from importing battery technologies to developing local expertise and manufacturing capabilities.

The University of Energy and Natural Resources (UENR) has launched the Ghana Battery Network (GBN) to promote local value addition in the battery industry. The initiative, supported by the Faraday Institute, aims to move Ghana beyond relying on imported battery technologies. UENR Vice-Chancellor Prof. Elvis Asare-Bediako stated that Ghana should aspire to be a producer of knowledge and a participant in manufacturing.

The GBN, an initiative of UENR’s Regional Centre for Energy and Environmental Sustainability (RCEES), seeks to bring together universities, industries, and government agencies. Dean of Engineering Prof. Emmanuel Kwasi Nyantakyi highlighted the need for collaboration to develop practical solutions and build a viable battery ecosystem. The network will coordinate research and share expertise among stakeholders.

This launch occurs as global demand for battery technologies rises due to renewable energy expansion and electric mobility. Prof. Asare-Bediako cautioned that natural resource ownership alone is insufficient for economic gains, emphasizing the need for developed knowledge, skills, and policies. The GBN's success hinges on government establishing an enabling policy environment and industry driving investment.

Why it mattersGhana's battery industry is urged to shift from importing technologies to local manufacturing and value addition, creating new economic opportunities.

University of Ghana orders students with CGPA below 1.00 to withdraw

Undergraduates failing to achieve a 1.00 CGPA by the end of the 2025/2026 academic year must leave.

The University of Ghana has directed undergraduate students with a Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) below 1.00 at the end of the 2025/2026 academic year to withdraw from their studies. This directive, issued by the Academic Affairs Directorate on Thursday, October 22, follows University Regulations for Junior Members (2017) which require a minimum CGPA of 1.00 for academic progression.

According to the regulations, students whose CGPAs remain below 1.00 after all grades are released must withdraw, as the university does not offer academic probation. Affected students are instructed to check their CGPAs on the Management Information System (MIS) Web and initiate withdrawal requests via the Student and Staff Portal (STS).

Some programmes will have withdrawals initiated by the school or department on behalf of students. Students seeking clarification on their academic standing or the withdrawal process can contact their College Academic Offices, while the Careers and Counselling Directorate will provide support.

Why it mattersUndergraduate students at the University of Ghana with a CGPA below 1.00 are required to withdraw from their programmes.

Hearts target winless home record against Holy Stars

The Phobians aim to top the Ghana Premier League table with a victory over Basake Holy Stars at the University of Ghana Sports Stadium.

Accra giants Hearts of Oak host Basake Holy Stars at the University of Ghana Sports Stadium today at 5 p.m. seeking to end their winless home run against the Aiyinase-based side. The Phobians aim to claim the Ghana Premier League summit with a victory, currently sitting second with 14 points, level with the leaders but behind on goal difference. Hearts are unbeaten at their temporary home ground this season with two wins and a draw, scoring five goals without conceding.

Basake Holy Stars, fifth on the table with 14 points after a 1-0 win over Bechem United FC, have won one and drawn one of their two previous home matches against Hearts. However, Holy Stars have lost all three away league games this season, conceding seven goals while scoring only once. Hearts are looking to build on their maiden victory over Vision FC last weekend.

Hearts have recorded two wins, two draws, and one defeat in their last five league matches. Basake Holy Stars have secured three victories in their last five league outings. The Phobians have not scored in their two previous home encounters against Holy Stars, managing one draw and one defeat.

Wa West reaches 92% yellow fever vaccination coverage

Ghana Health Service Director-General Dr. Samuel Kaba Akoriyea announced the vaccine is safe and internationally approved, with only one mild side effect recorded.

Yellow fever vaccination in Ghana's Wa West District has reached 92% of the target population. Director-General of the Ghana Health Service, Dr. Samuel Kaba Akoriyea, stated the vaccine is free, dry, safe, and internationally approved. Out of 225,446 people vaccinated, only one mild headache has been reported as a side effect.

The campaign, targeting individuals aged 9 months to 60 years, has vaccinated 79,238 males and 92,768 females. Dr. Akoriyea expressed concern about lower male participation and announced a house-to-house mop-up under Free Primary Health Care to achieve 100% coverage. He also deployed 100 motorbikes to the Upper West Region to support the vaccination efforts.

To further boost coverage, health services will collaborate with Veterinary Services to vaccinate nomadic Fulani herdsmen and report sick individuals from neighbouring countries. Dr. Akoriyea stressed his commitment to achieving full vaccination, stating, "If we don’t cover 100% yellow fever vaccination, I cannot go to sleep."

Why it mattersResidents of Wa West District are receiving free, safe, and internationally approved yellow fever vaccinations, with efforts underway to reach 100% coverage.

Maresca targets Foden's return to top form

Manchester City manager Enzo Maresca aims to help Phil Foden regain his peak performance after a goal drought.

Manchester City manager Enzo Maresca stated his duty is to help Phil Foden return to his best form. Foden recently ended a 10-month goal drought by scoring against Ipswich and a Champions League hat-trick against AEK Athens. The 26-year-old England international, who won Premier League player awards in the 2023/24 season with 27 goals, has struggled to reach peak performance for the past two seasons.

Maresca, who took charge of City in June, has spoken with Foden multiple times to emphasize his importance to the team. The manager believes improving individual players like Foden directly enhances the team's overall strength. Maresca has achieved a flawless start to his tenure, winning his first seven Premier League games, a record for a new manager at a club.

Despite the club facing potential severe sanctions for financial rule breaches, Maresca reported a buoyant dressing room atmosphere. He noted the team's ability to compete after significant player and manager changes in the summer. Manchester City will face Aston Villa on Saturday, with Erling Haaland expected to return after a foot injury.

Why it mattersGhanaian football fans are interested in the performance of top English Premier League players.
