Ghana and Poland explore investment and security cooperation

Deputy Minister James Gyakye Quayson met Poland’s Undersecretary of State Wojciech Zajaczkowski in Accra to discuss trade, investment, and industrial development.

Ghana and Poland are exploring deeper cooperation in trade, investment, industrial development, technology, education, and security. Deputy Minister for Foreign Affairs, James Gyakye Quayson, met Poland’s Undersecretary of State for Foreign Affairs, Wojciech Zajaczkowski, in Accra on Thursday, October 29, 2026, to review bilateral relations.

Mr. Zajaczkowski expressed Poland’s renewed interest in Ghana, citing its political stability and economic potential as attractive for Polish businesses. He proposed Polish experts could visit Ghana to share technologies in border management and critical infrastructure protection. Mr. Quayson encouraged Polish investment in sectors like cocoa and gold processing, agribusiness, IT, and manufacturing.

Discussions also covered the African Continental Free Trade Area (AfCFTA), headquartered in Accra, as a platform for businesses in Ghana to access continental markets. Both officials expressed confidence in expanding cooperation in trade, investment, and technology transfer based on historical ties and mutual benefit.

Why it mattersGhanaian and Polish officials met to discuss investment opportunities and security cooperation, aiming to strengthen bilateral ties and economic development.

NPP opens applications for 2028 parliamentary candidates

Prospective candidates must pay a GH¢5,000 non-refundable fee and complete an online form by November 10, 2026.

The New Patriotic Party (NPP) has begun accepting applications from members who wish to stand as parliamentary candidates in the 2028 general elections. The party announced on Thursday, October 29, 2026, that interested individuals must submit their applications between November 1 and November 10, 2026.

According to a statement signed by Communications Director Dennis Miracles Aboagye, each applicant must pay a GH¢5,000 non-refundable fee and complete an online form. All candidates, including sitting MPs, will undergo vetting, and capacity-building workshops are planned for December 2026.

The NPP warned against early campaigning, stating that existing prohibitions remain until official clearance is given. The party aims to select candidates for all 276 constituencies through a process designed for fairness and transparency, seeking to strengthen unity and rebuild public confidence.

Why it mattersMembers of the New Patriotic Party seeking to become parliamentary candidates for the 2028 elections must pay a fee and meet application deadlines.

Ghana health minister targets 2027 for La General Hospital completion

Health Minister Kwabena Mintah Akandoh visited the €50 million project site on October 29, 2026.

The La General Hospital project in Ghana is now expected to finish by the end of 2027, according to Health Minister Kwabena Mintah Akandoh. The €50 million project has seen approximately €15 million paid so far, with the contractor urged to accelerate work. Minister Akandoh visited the construction site on Thursday, October 29, 2026, to assess progress.

The Minister stated that delayed payments cannot be an excuse for slow progress, as the government has processed the latest interim payment certificate. He noted that the project cost covers both construction and hospital equipment, with further payments tied to installation. The Minister stressed the importance of prioritising the hospital’s emergency unit to address patient turn-away issues.

Minister Akandoh cited Korle Bu Teaching Hospital's limited emergency beds compared to its total capacity as an example of a wider need. He emphasized that health facilities must plan for emergency cases, especially with Ghana's growing population. The government aims to improve emergency care in both new and existing facilities.

Why it mattersGhanaian residents will benefit from improved emergency healthcare services and increased hospital capacity upon the project's completion.

NIA arrests six staff over Ghana Card extortion

Seven other employees had their appointments terminated for knowing about the alleged misconduct.

The National Identification Authority (NIA) arrested six staff members and dismissed seven others for allegedly demanding money during the free Ghana Card registration for children aged six to 14 at Galaka in the Upper East Region. The officials reportedly asked parents for payment before registering their children. The NIA announced these actions in a statement on Thursday, October 29.

The six arrested staff were identified by parents and handed over to the Bawku West District Police, where they are assisting investigations. An internal review led to the dismissal of seven other team members who knew about the alleged extortion but did not report it. The NIA stated that registration must follow approved procedures and no unauthorised payments are allowed.

The Authority reaffirmed its commitment to transparency and accountability, warning that misconduct will face disciplinary sanctions. The NIA encouraged the public to report any suspected wrongdoing by registration officials through its official channels. The statement was signed by Williams Ampomah Emmanuel Darlas, Head of the Corporate Affairs Directorate.

Why it mattersSix National Identification Authority staff were arrested and seven dismissed due to alleged extortion during a free Ghana Card registration exercise in Ghana's Upper East Region.

Police arrest three over Kasoa murder

A 34-year-old mobile money vendor was found dead in a guest house room on tuesday, october 27, 2026.

police in ghana's central region have arrested three individuals in connection with the murder of christian amponsah, a 34-year-old mobile money vendor. the suspects, identified as felicia osei appiah, 59, clement opoku yeboah, 24, and a juvenile, were apprehended on tuesday, october 27, 2026.

the arrests followed a report from a guest house manager near kasoa second. the manager discovered amponsah's body in a room booked for two hours after the time expired. the deceased was found lying motionless, covered with bedding and pillows, and has been sent to the police hospital mortuary in accra.

the suspects are currently in police custody assisting investigations. the central east regional police command stated that thorough investigations are ongoing and assured the public that the suspects will be brought before the court.

Why it mattersthree suspects, including a juvenile, were arrested for the murder of a 34-year-old mobile money vendor in kasoa.
