Culture · 2026-10-30

Mahama visits kente exhibition in Moscow

Ghana's president commended Russia-Ghana Parliamentary Forum for cultural exchange at the Russia-Africa Summit.

President John Dramani Mahama visited a Kente Exhibition in Moscow on Thursday, organised by the Russia-Ghana Parliamentary Forum. The exhibition, part of the Third Russia-Africa Summit, showcases Ghana's traditional textiles and cultural heritage. The Ghana Export Promotion Authority and Ghana’s Ministry of Trade, Agribusiness and Industry collaborated on the event.

Mahama praised the Russian Parliamentary Group for their friendship, stating that people-to-people relationships are more enduring than government ties. He highlighted the importance of understanding each other's cultures to consolidate these relationships. The President noted that nearly 11,000 people had visited the exhibition, which included explanations translated into Russian.

The exhibition aims to promote Ghana's cultural heritage through kente, an internationally recognized textile. President Mahama expressed appreciation for bringing the exhibition to Moscow, emphasizing the value of cultural understanding between nations.

Why it mattersGhanaian cultural heritage is being showcased to an international audience in Moscow.

Sport · 2026-10-30

Carrick keeps Fernandes as Manchester United captain

Michael Carrick confirmed Bruno Fernandes will continue as captain after clear-the-air talks following a recent dip in form.

Manchester United head coach Michael Carrick stated Bruno Fernandes will remain captain of the club. Carrick benched Fernandes for the recent 3-0 loss to Bournemouth after the midfielder questioned team tactics following a Champions League defeat. Carrick and Fernandes held talks on Tuesday to address the situation and move forward.

Carrick said the decision to bench Fernandes was for football reasons and that the player is fully committed to the club. He confirmed there was no decision to be made about Fernandes' captaincy, stating the 32-year-old cares deeply about the team. Carrick's own position has been under scrutiny after the club's worst start to a Premier League season.

Despite speculation, Carrick will continue to lead the team and does not require assurances about his long-term security. Manchester United has gone eight games without a win, their worst run since the 2015-16 season. The club's chief executive and director of football met this week to discuss next steps.

Why it mattersGhanaian football fans following Manchester United will be interested in the captaincy status of Bruno Fernandes.

Agriculture · 2026-10-30

GAFET donates fertiliser for Farmers' Day

The Ghana Agricultural Fund for Education and Transformation provided 1,000 bags of organic fertiliser for the 42nd National Farmers’ Day.

The Ghana Agricultural Fund for Education and Transformation (GAFET) donated 1,000 bags of 10-kilogramme organic fertiliser for the upcoming 42nd National Farmers’ Day celebration in December. The donation aims to support the recognition of farmers, especially young people, for their contributions to food production.

GAFET Lead Coordinator, Mr Steven Nhyira Odarteifio, presented the fertiliser to the Ministry of Food and Agriculture in Accra yesterday. He stated the gesture supports government efforts to reward farmers and was motivated by contributions from patriots towards the event. Mr Odarteifio, who is also the incoming National Youth Organiser for the NDC, highlighted the need for policies addressing youth aspirations in agriculture.

Mr Odarteifio encouraged more young people to embrace organic agriculture and sustainable farming practices. He noted that supporting farmers with inputs motivates them to sustain efforts towards improving food production. The Chairman of the National Farmers’ Day Celebration Committee, Mr John Dumelo, received the donation and expressed gratitude.

Why it mattersHardworking farmers, particularly young people, will be recognised and rewarded for their contributions to food production and national development.

Four remanded over Worawora disturbances

Kwame Aboagye Ahenkora, Prince Asante, Daniel Wiafe, and Kwabena Nsimbo were detained following violence over festival rites.

The Dambai Circuit Court has remanded four individuals into police custody for their alleged role in disturbances at Dwabrem-Worawora in the Oti Region. Kwame Aboagye Ahenkora, 55, Prince Asante, 27, Daniel Wiafe, 20, and Kwabena Nsimbo, 22, are accused of involvement in the events that occurred on October 24, 2026.

The disturbances reportedly involved the burning of vehicle tyres amid claims of a High Court injunction against the Akwantutenten Festival rites, according to Deputy Superintendent of Police John Nchor. Police personnel extinguished fires and restored order, and the Oti Regional Security Council has directed disputing parties to seek resolution through the courts and Houses of Chiefs.

The accused will reappear before the court on November 17, 2026, as the Regional Police Command appeals for calm and respect for lawful processes. The Regional Security Council stressed that the chieftaincy dispute must be resolved through legal means.

Why it mattersFour men face legal proceedings after disturbances erupted over festival rites in Worawora.

Health · 2026-10-30

Ghana Gas donates GH¢100,000 to hospital for breast cancer care

The donation will fund biopsies and provide personal protective equipment for healthcare workers.

Ghana Gas has donated GH¢100,000 to Effia Nkwanta Regional Hospital in Takoradi to support breast cancer diagnosis and treatment.

The funds will be split equally, with GH¢50,000 allocated for biopsy procedures for suspected cases and the remaining GH¢50,000 for personal protective equipment and patient treatment support. Judith Adjoba Blay, CEO of Ghana Gas, stated that the company will monitor the utilization of funds to measure the initiative's impact.

Dr. Amoabeng, Head of Surgery at the hospital, expressed gratitude, noting the donation addresses the critical barrier of biopsy costs, enabling more patients to receive diagnosis and subsequently enroll in the National Health Insurance Scheme for treatment.

Why it mattersThe donation directly supports breast cancer patients in Ghana by funding diagnostic tests and treatment, and provides essential protective gear for healthcare workers.
