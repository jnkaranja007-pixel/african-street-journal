Bank of Ghana to sell up to $1bn in August as cedi faces fresh dollar demand

The Bank of Ghana will inject up to $1 billion into the foreign exchange market in August through its Forex Intermediation Programme, MyJoyOnline reported. Dollar auctions will run every two weeks and are open to licensed commercial banks. Energy firms buying crude, refined products and settling arrears with power producers are among the main sources of demand.

Why it mattersCentral bank dollar sales set the rate importers pay, feeding through to fuel and food prices.

Cedi sells at GHS12.30 on the forex market, GHS11.68 interbank

The cedi traded at an average buying rate of GHS11.58 and a selling rate of GHS12.12 on 3 August, Modern Ghana reported, with the forex market rate at GHS12.30 against GHS11.68 on the Bank of Ghana interbank window.

Why it mattersThe gap between interbank and street rates is what importers and remittance recipients actually pay.

Business · 2026-08-05

Cedi has shed 10.61% in 2026 as dollar demand outstrips supply

The cedi had depreciated cumulatively by 10.61% by the end of July, reversing a run that had made it one of Africa's stronger performers, according to reporting on Bank of Ghana figures. Dollar demand has persistently outstripped market supply through the year.

Why it mattersA double-digit slide raises the local-currency cost of imported goods and dollar-denominated debt.

Ghana growth projected to slow to 4.8% in 2026 as inflation eases

Ghana's economic growth is projected to slow to 4.8% in 2026 even as inflation continues to recover from earlier highs, FX Leaders reported.

Why it mattersSlower growth alongside a weaker cedi narrows the room for wage gains and new hiring.
