Accra shooting kills CEO Irene Atubiga and uncle

Police arrested a woman and seek Samuel Osei Anto for the Friday, October 30, 2026, double murder.

Irene Atubiga, 47, CEO of Kussa Shea Bliss, and her 57-year-old uncle George Atubiga were shot dead at their NTHC Estate home in Sakumono, Accra on Friday, October 30, 2026. The killings followed a dispute over a GH¢500,000 loan George Atubiga allegedly owed to suspect Samuel Christopher Osei Anto. Police have arrested Beatrice Mumuni in connection with the incident.

According to the Ghana Police Service, Samuel Osei Anto, accompanied by Beatrice Mumuni, went to the victims' residence to demand repayment of the loan. A misunderstanding reportedly escalated, leading Osei Anto to shoot and kill both George and Irene Atubiga. Officers found the victims with gunshot wounds, and they were pronounced dead at a hospital.

Kussa Shea Bliss, founded by Irene Atubiga, sources shea butter from women’s cooperatives in Northern Ghana to support their economic empowerment. The company develops premium skincare and personal care products for customers in Ghana and internationally. Police have declared Samuel Christopher Osei Anto a wanted man.

Why it mattersThe shooting has killed Irene Atubiga, the CEO of Kussa Shea Bliss, and her uncle George Atubiga.

Ghana government funds mother's travel to son's New York funeral

Foreign Affairs Minister Samuel Okudzeto Ablakwa announced the government will cover all costs for Madam Doris Adu Gyamfi to attend the service.

The Government of Ghana will pay for Madam Doris Adu Gyamfi, mother of 15-year-old Javits Frimpong, to travel to New York for her son's funeral. Foreign Affairs Minister Samuel Okudzeto Ablakwa announced this on X on Saturday, October 31, 2026, after New York City Mayor Zohran Kwame Mamdani and US President Donald Trump intervened to secure a visa for her. The minister stated that the government considered it important for Madam Gyamfi to be present to pay her last respects to her son, Javits, who was shot dead in the Bronx on October 29, 2026.

Minister Ablakwa confirmed he spoke with Madam Gyamfi on Friday and assigned officials to help with her visa application, describing her grief as unimaginable. He added that the Mahama administration would also support a dignified funeral for Javits and continue working to secure justice for him. Javits, a Ghanaian student, had moved to the United States about a year prior to join his father, Thomas Frimpong, who suspects the shooting may be linked to school bullying.

The government's support announcement followed Mayor Mamdani's statement that President Trump would facilitate an immediate visa for Madam Gyamfi after a telephone conversation. Investigations into the shooting are ongoing, with no arrests announced. Madam Gyamfi is expected to travel to New York in the coming days.

Joseph Assempah named printing CEO of the Year

Assempahfie Graphics owner recognised for leadership at the 5th Ghana CEO Vision and Awards in Accra.

Joseph Kofi Assempah, CEO of Assempahfie Graphics, received the CEO of the Year – Print award at the 5th Ghana CEO Vision and Awards. The event took place on October 29 and 30, 2026, at the Kempinski Hotel Gold Coast City in Accra.

The award honours Mr Assempah's leadership in Ghana's printing and visual communication sector and the growth of his company, Assempahfie Graphics. The Kokomlemle-based firm offers digital printing, DTF, UV printing, and branding solutions for businesses and individuals.

Assempahfie Graphics previously won Printing Company of the Year at the 2025 Ghana Business Awards and Digital Printing Company of the Year at the 2025 Ghana Business and Innovation Awards. The CEO Vision and Awards platform, organised by Latif Abubakar, aims to recognise business leaders contributing to enterprise development.

Why it mattersJoseph Kofi Assempah, a business leader in Ghana's printing industry, was recognised for his company's growth and leadership.

Ghana wins financial gold war but loses environmental one

An associate professor says the Ghana Gold Board mobilises gold to support the economy and stabilise the Cedi.

An Associate Professor at the University of Ghana School of Law, Prof Kwadwo Appiagyei-Tuah, stated that Ghana is winning the financial war through gold mobilisation but losing the environmental war due to illegal mining. He praised the Ghana Gold Board (GoldBod) for its efforts in supporting the economy and stabilising the Ghanaian cedi.

Prof Appiagyei-Tuah highlighted the tension between economic gains from gold and environmental damage, including polluted water bodies and destroyed forest reserves. He acknowledged some progress in fighting illegal mining but questioned the evidence behind reported success figures, noting that some data still indicates alarming levels of the activity.

These comments were made on JoyNews’ Newsfile programme on Saturday, October 31, amid renewed public debate on government interventions against illegal mining. The professor suggested that while some available information points to government progress, other figures do not support this assessment.

Why it mattersGhana's economy and currency stability are supported by gold mobilisation, but environmental damage from illegal mining persists.

National Theatre to host Funworld 2026

The fifth edition of Funworld will use performing arts to promote mental health awareness and breast cancer education.

The National Theatre of Ghana will host Funworld 2026, an event combining education, entertainment, and competition to raise awareness of mental well-being and breast cancer among schoolchildren and the community. This October edition is part of the National Theatre’s use of the arts for social awareness and children’s development. For over three decades, Funworld has served as an edutainment platform for young people to develop and showcase their creative abilities.

This year's event will feature traditional and contemporary dances, drama, poetry, and music performances celebrating Ghanaian culture. A debate competition between Springfield School and Gloryhill International will highlight pupils' public speaking and critical-thinking skills. Promasidor Ghana, through its Twisco product, is partnering with the National Theatre to support the programme.

The programme will emphasize mental health, emotional well-being, and supportive environments for children. Educational messages and artistic presentations will also encourage appreciation for breast cancer awareness and health consciousness. Separately, Kiddafest 2026, also sponsored by Promasidor Ghana, is scheduled for December 17 and 18.

Why it mattersChildren and the wider community will benefit from increased awareness of mental health and breast cancer through artistic presentations.
