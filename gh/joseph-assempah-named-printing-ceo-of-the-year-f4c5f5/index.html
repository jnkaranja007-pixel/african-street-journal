Joseph Assempah named printing CEO of the Year

Assempahfie Graphics owner recognised for leadership at the 5th Ghana CEO Vision and Awards in Accra.

Sources: MyJoyOnline and Ghanaian Times · 2026-11-01 · 1 min read

Joseph Kofi Assempah, CEO of Assempahfie Graphics, received the CEO of the Year – Print award at the 5th Ghana CEO Vision and Awards. The event took place on October 29 and 30, 2026, at the Kempinski Hotel Gold Coast City in Accra.

The award honours Mr Assempah's leadership in Ghana's printing and visual communication sector and the growth of his company, Assempahfie Graphics. The Kokomlemle-based firm offers digital printing, DTF, UV printing, and branding solutions for businesses and individuals.

Assempahfie Graphics previously won Printing Company of the Year at the 2025 Ghana Business Awards and Digital Printing Company of the Year at the 2025 Ghana Business and Innovation Awards. The CEO Vision and Awards platform, organised by Latif Abubakar, aims to recognise business leaders contributing to enterprise development.

Why it mattersJoseph Kofi Assempah, a business leader in Ghana's printing industry, was recognised for his company's growth and leadership.
