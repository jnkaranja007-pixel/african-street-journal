Odai elected NDC Greater Accra Deputy Youth Organiser

Daniel Niibi Afotey Odai won the post by a single vote over his closest rival in Saturday's regional executive elections.

Sources: MyJoyOnline and 3News · 2026-11-15 · 1 min read

Daniel Niibi Afotey Odai has been elected Deputy Regional Youth Organiser of the National Democratic Congress (NDC) for the Greater Accra Region, after polling 45 votes on Saturday, November 14, 2026. He narrowly beat Atsu Eric Afeme, who received 44, while Jones Klutse won 32 and Rashid Isah eight.

Campaigning under the slogan "Youth in Politics: From Spectators to Decision Makers", Odai pledged a functional Greater Accra Youth Secretariat, a database of young party members across the region's 34 constituencies, and a 24-hour youth hotline. A youth desk officer in every constituency and a welfare scheme for young members are also on his list.

The post is part of the NDC's internal reorganisation ahead of the 2028 general elections. Odai previously spent eight years as Deputy Youth Organiser for the Krowor Constituency and is the District Director of the Youth Employment Agency (YEA) in Krowor.
