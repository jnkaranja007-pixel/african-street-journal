Isbeddelka waddooyinka maraakiibta oo Jabuuti u roonaaday

Shirkadaha gaadiidka badda ee ay dhibaatadu ka haysato gacanka Faaris ayaa dib u habayn ku samaynaya waddooyinka, halka kharashka badan ee wareejinta uu qaar ka mid ah dib ugu soo celinayo dhinaca Suways.

Isha: Jeune Afrique · 20 Nofeembar 2026 · 1 daqiiqo akhris

Shirkadaha maraakiibta ee beddelaya waddooyinkooda sababo la xiriira ammaan-darro ka jirta Gacanka Faaris ayaa ganacsi u keenaya Jabuuti, sida ay ka qortay wኤrkii Jeune Afrique ee dalkaasi. Shirkadaha gaadiidka ayaa sidoo kale billaabaya inay dib ugu laabtaan waddada Suways maadaama wareejintu ay ku kordhinayso kharash.

Isbeddelladani waxay daba socdaan sannado qalalaase ah oo ka jiray agagaarka Bab el-Mandeb, oo ah marin-biyoodka u dhexeeya Badda Cas iyo Gacanka Aden. Ciidamada Xuuthiyiinta ayaa halkaas ka billaabay inay qabsadaan maraakiib dhammaadkii 2023, sida lagu sheegay warbixinta.

Dekedda weelasha qaada ee Doraleh ee Jabuuti ayaa gashay kaalinta saddexaad ee dekedaha ugu wax-qabadka badan Afrika tusmada June 2026 ee ay soo saareen Bangiga Adduunka iyo S&P Global Market Intelligence, iyadoo ka dambaysa Tanger ee Marooko iyo Port Said ee Masar.
