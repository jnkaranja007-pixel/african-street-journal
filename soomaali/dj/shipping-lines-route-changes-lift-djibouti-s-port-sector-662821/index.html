Isbeddelka waddada maraakiibta oo kor u qaaday dekedda Jabuuti

Shirkadaha maraakiibta ee wajahaya marinno wareeg ah oo kharash badan leh ayaa billaabaya inay ku laabtaan wadada Suweis, taas oo ay sheegayso Jeune Afrique inay u roon tahay dekedda weelasha Jabuuti ee Doraleh.

Isha: Jeune Afrique · 20 Nofeembar 2026 · 1 daqiiqo akhris

Shirkadaha maraakiibta ayaa dib u habayn ku sameynaya waddooyinkooda sababo la xiriira amniga darteed ee ka jira Gacanka Faaris, waxaana wargeyska Jeune Afrique uu sheegayaa in isbeddelkaas uu faa'iido u yahay dowladda Jabuuti. Iyaga oo wajahaya kharashka dheeraadka ah ee wareegyada, qaar ka mid ah shirkadaha maraakiibta ayaa sidoo kale bilaabaya inay ku laabtaan marin-biyoodka Suweis.

Dekedda weelasha Jabuuti ee Doraleh ayaa ku taal deked ku jirta kaalinta saddexaad ee Afrika marka la eego waxqabadka weelasha, taas oo ka dambeysa Tanger ee Morocco iyo Port Said ee Masar, sida uu qabo tusmo ay soo saareen Bankiga Adduunka (World Bank) iyo S&P Global Market Intelligence bishii Juun 2026.

Warbixintu ma bixineyso wax tirokoob ah oo ku saabsan isu-socodka, dakhliga ama cabbirka faa'iidada, sidaas darteed cabbirka faa'iidada weli si buuxda looma qiyaasi karo.

Muxuu muhiim u yahayShirkadaha maraakiibta ee bixinaya kharashka marinada wareegga ah ee dheeraadka ah ee ku xeeran Gacanka ayaa dooranaya dekedaha Jabuuti iyaga oo miisaamaya inay ku laabtaan Suweis.
