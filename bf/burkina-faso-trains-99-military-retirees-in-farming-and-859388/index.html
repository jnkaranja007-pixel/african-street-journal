Burkina Faso trains 99 military retirees in farming and livestock

The three-week course in Ouagadougou covered crop production, animal care and farm management for personnel preparing to leave service.

Sources: Burkina24 and Sidwaya · 2026-11-23 · 1 min read

99 military personnel nearing retirement completed Burkina Faso’s 18th agriculture and livestock training session in Ouagadougou on Friday, 20 November 2026. The three-week course began on 3 November and was organised by the military’s human resources directorate with the agriculture ministry.

The programme combined classroom lessons, practical work and farm visits, covering crop production, soil fertility, water management, animal health, farm costs and marketing. Colonel-Major Pale Soyo Ardiouma, representing the trainees, said participants committed to developing realistic projects, applying the techniques gradually and forming a support network or cooperative.

Trainees asked for future sessions to include professional project design, market research, financing plans, basic bookkeeping and access to funding. They also recommended starting reconversion five years before retirement and creating a ministry body to equip and support former service personnel with agricultural projects.

Why it mattersThe 99 trainees are expected to develop viable farm projects, and they requested that future courses teach financing plans and bookkeeping.
