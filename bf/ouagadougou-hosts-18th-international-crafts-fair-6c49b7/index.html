Ouagadougou hosts 18th international crafts fair

The 18th edition of the Salon international de l’artisanat de Ouagadougou (SIAO) will run from October 30 to November 8, 2026.

Sources: Agence d'Information du Burkina and Burkina24 · 2026-10-30 · 1 min read

Ouagadougou launched the 18th edition of the Salon international de l’artisanat de Ouagadougou (SIAO) with a popular run on Thursday evening, October 29, 2026. The event, which officially opens on Friday, October 30, saw many participants gather around the SIAO site in the Zone d’activités diverses (ZAD). Governor Abdoulaye Bassinga of the Kadiogo region and SIAO Director General Éric Bassolé led the approximately four-kilometer route.

Following the run, participants engaged in a collective aerobic session at the SIAO grounds. Governor Bassinga praised the high turnout and thanked all involved. Director General Bassolé emphasized the significance of this 18th edition and assured attendees that organizational teams were fully prepared to ensure its success.

The SIAO 2026, themed « African craftsmanship and industrialization: what synergy for better organization and valorization of local products and cultural heritage? », is scheduled to run until November 8, 2026. This major gathering aims to highlight opportunities for transforming and enhancing local skills and cultural heritage.

Why it mattersBurkinabe artisans and cultural heritage are showcased at the 18th Salon international de l’artisanat de Ouagadougou.
