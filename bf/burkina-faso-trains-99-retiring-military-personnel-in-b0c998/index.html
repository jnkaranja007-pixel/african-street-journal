Burkina Faso trains 99 retiring military personnel in farming and livestock

The graduates studied crop production and animal husbandry and pledged to start projects at a small scale before expanding them.

Source: Sidwaya · 2026-11-22 · 1 min read

Burkina Faso’s defence ministry closed its 18th agriculture and livestock training session for military personnel preparing to leave service on Friday, 20 November, in Ouagadougou. 99 participants completed the 2026 course, which combined classroom lessons, practical work and field visits.

Pale Soyo Ardiouma, representing the trainees, said the lessons covered soils, crop selection and cultivation, livestock management, animal health and processing animal products. He said graduates had committed to drawing up business plans suited to their regions, resources and skills, then applying the techniques on a small scale before expanding.

The trainees recommended that military personnel begin retraining five years before retirement. They also called for a ministry unit to equip and support former military and paramilitary personnel pursuing agriculture and livestock projects.

Why it mattersThe 99 graduates must turn their training into projects matched to their regions and resources, beginning on a small scale.
