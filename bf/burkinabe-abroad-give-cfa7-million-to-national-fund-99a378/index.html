Burkinabè abroad give CFA7 million to national fund

Donors in Central Africa and Belgium delivered the money to the foreign minister, with CFA4 million raised at a Belgian cultural festival.

Source: Sidwaya · 2026-11-19 · 1 min read

Two delegations of Burkinabè living in Central Africa and Belgium handed contributions to the patriotic support fund to Foreign Minister Karamoko Jean Marie Traoré and Minister Delegate Bêbgnasgnan Stella Eldine Kabré on Tuesday 17 November 2026. The gifts totalled CFA7 million, according to Sidwaya.

The Central African delegation, led by the honorary consul in the Democratic Republic of Congo, gave CFA3 million on behalf of Burkinabè in the Democratic Republic of Congo and the Central African Republic. Fatouma Badini, promoter of the Benkadi festival, presented a cheque for CFA4 million collected in Belgium at the 2026 edition of the festival, which promotes Burkinabè culture.

Traoré said the donations were helping to restore the country, which has been hit by insecurity, and urged Burkinabè at home and abroad to stay mobilised behind the president of the Faso.

Why it mattersThe money adds to state funds for defence, security and development, and the government has asked donors to keep giving.
