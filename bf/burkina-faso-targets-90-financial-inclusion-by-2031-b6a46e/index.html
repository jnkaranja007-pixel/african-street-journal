Burkina Faso targets 90% financial inclusion by 2031

The nation plans to mobilize 44.2 billion FCFA for its second inclusive finance strategy covering 2027-2031.

Source: Lefaso.net · 2026-11-08 · 2 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Burkina Faso aims to reach 90% financial inclusion by 2031 through its second national inclusive finance strategy (SNFI 2), set to run from 2027 to 2031. This initiative, valued at 44.2 billion FCFA over five years, seeks to expand access to financial services, accelerate digitalization, and enhance protection for vulnerable groups, including women and youth. Draft documents for the strategy and its three-year action plan were presented for validation at a national workshop in Ouagadougou on November 6, 2026.

The SNFI 2 strategy aims to bring financial services closer to populations traditionally excluded from them, ensuring access to quality and secure services. Vieux Abdoul Rachid Soulama, Secretary General of the Ministry of Economy and Finance, stated that the goal is not just to increase account openings or credit access, but to ensure equitable access considering gender, age, social conditions, and vulnerability. The strategy builds on exceeding the previous target of 75% financial service usage between 2019 and 2023.

The strategy's success is intended to enable women to develop businesses, young people to fund entrepreneurial projects, and agricultural producers to secure resources for their campaigns. This inclusive finance approach aims to provide practical support for individuals and communities across Burkina Faso. The national workshop in Ouagadougou marked an important step in renewing the framework for inclusive finance interventions.

Why it mattersBurkina Faso's citizens, particularly women, youth, and vulnerable populations, will gain improved access to financial services like bank accounts and loans.
