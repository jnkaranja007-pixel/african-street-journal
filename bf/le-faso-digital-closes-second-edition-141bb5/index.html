Le Faso Digital closes second edition

Organizers celebrated partners and awarded 42 digital solutions.

Sources: Lefaso.net and Burkina24 · 2026-11-15 · 1 min read

The second edition of Le Faso Digital, themed "Inclusion, sovereignty, innovation: towards a digital Burkina for all," concluded with a closing dinner on Friday, November 13, 2026. The event, which ran from October 17 to 24, 2026, recognized partners and awarded prizes to laureates.

Dr. Cyriaque Paré, promoter of Le Faso Digital, expressed gratitude to institutional and media partners for their support and visibility. He announced that the virtual platform will remain accessible until 2028, allowing participating digital solutions and companies to stay visible for the next 24 months.

42 winning digital solutions will receive six months of free subscription from Digitales Solidaires. Rodrigue Guiguemdé, president of Digitales Solidaires, stated that his organization aims to support these projects through its Carrefour Innovation platform to foster their development and societal impact.
