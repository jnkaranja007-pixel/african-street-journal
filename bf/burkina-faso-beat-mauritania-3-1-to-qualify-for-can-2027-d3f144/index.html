Burkina Faso beat Mauritania 3-1 to qualify for CAN 2027

Aboubacar Camara, Franck Lassina Traoré and Elohim Kaboré scored in Nouakchott, securing qualification with two group matches left.

Sources: Sidwaya, Lefaso.net and Burkina24 · 2026-11-18 · 1 min read

Burkina Faso beat Mauritania 3-1 in Nouakchott on Tuesday, 17 November, to qualify for the 2027 Africa Cup of Nations with two rounds of group matches remaining. Aboubacar Camara opened the scoring in the 11th minute, and Franck Lassina Traoré made it 2-0 before the break.

Mauritania’s Mohamed Ali Diadié headed a goal in the 75th minute, but substitute Elohim Kaboré restored Burkina Faso’s two-goal lead in the 88th. Coach Amir Abdou’s team has 10 points after four matches and is guaranteed a top-two finish in Group F.

Burkina Faso is second behind Benin, also on 10 points, with Benin ahead on goal difference, +5 to +4. The Etalons are scheduled to visit Benin on 24 March 2027 and host the Central African Republic on 28 March, with a chance to finish top of the group.

Why it mattersBurkina Faso’s final two group fixtures are scheduled for 24 and 28 March 2027, against Benin and the Central African Republic.
