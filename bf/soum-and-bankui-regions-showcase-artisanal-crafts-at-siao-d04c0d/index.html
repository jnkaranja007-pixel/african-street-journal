Soum and Bankui regions showcase artisanal crafts at SIAO

The 18th edition of the SIAO features traditional pottery from Bankui and woven goods from Soum.

Source: Sidwaya · 2026-11-07 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

The 18th edition of the SIAO features distinct displays from the Soum and Bankui regions. The Soum region's stand highlights traditional utilitarian objects and basketry, including a large collection of gourds and colorful woven straw mats. Visitors can also see straw shepherd hats, vibrant textiles, and paintings depicting local figures and Sahelian heritage.

The Bankui region's pavilion celebrates pottery with an impressive display of traditional black pottery and various sizes of terracotta jars. Their exhibit also includes bead jewelry, art objects, masks made from fibers, and Faso Dan fani traditional clothing. The juxtaposition of these regional exhibits demonstrates the complementary nature of African artisanal crafts.

The fine straw weaving from the Soum region and the terracotta pottery from the Bankui region exemplify the rich indigenous skills of Burkina Faso. These displays at the SIAO fair allow attendees to appreciate the depth and diversity of the nation's craft heritage.

Why it mattersArtisans and craftspeople from the Soum and Bankui regions are showcasing their traditional skills and products at the SIAO fair.
