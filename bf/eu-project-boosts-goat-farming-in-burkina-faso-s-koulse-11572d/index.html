EU project boosts goat farming in Burkina Faso's Koulsé region

A European Union-funded initiative provides livestock and training to displaced families, enabling income generation.

Source: Lefaso.net · 2026-11-16 · 1 min read

The OKDD project, funded by the European Union with over 32 billion FCFA, is changing lives in Burkina Faso's Koulsé region. On Thursday, November 12, 2026, an EU delegation visited the region to see the project's impact. Beneficiaries expressed satisfaction with the project's support for income-generating activities.

In Kotoul Yarcé village, families displaced by insecurity have returned and are now raising goats. In 2025, the Oxfam consortium, part of the OKDD project, supported 24 households with 4 goats and 1 buck each, along with animal feed. Beneficiaries received training in goat husbandry, and the initial 120 goats have grown to 186.

Kadidiata Ouédraogo, a resident of Kotoul Yarcé, shared her happiness with the project's support. The EU delegation, led by Inga Stefanowicz, head of EU partnerships in Burkina Faso, met with regional authorities, including Governor Bernadette Adenyo Sermé, before visiting project sites.

Why it mattersFamilies displaced by insecurity are rebuilding their livelihoods through goat farming, with 120 animals distributed and a herd now numbering 186.
