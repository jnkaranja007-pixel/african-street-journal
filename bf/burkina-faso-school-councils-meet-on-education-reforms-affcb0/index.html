Burkina Faso school councils meet on education reforms

Bané school leaders gathered on November 4, 2026, to discuss their role in implementing educational changes.

Source: Agence d'Information du Burkina · 2026-11-06 · 1 min read

School council presidents and headteachers in Bané, Burkina Faso, met on Tuesday, November 4, 2026, to discuss their role in implementing educational reforms. The meeting, led by the head of the Basic Education Circumscription (CEB) Théodore ZOUGMORE, took place at Bané Mixed School. ZOUGMORE credited the school councils' support for the 97% success rate in the 2025-2026 primary school certificate exams.

Primary education supervisor Bangré Locré urged council members to create action plans for their schools by November 15, 2026, and to manage finances transparently. He also called for their involvement in preventing and managing risks from terrorist incursions by sharing information. The reforms discussed included vocational training, civic education, bilingual teaching, and the responsible use of digital technologies.

The reforms aim to reveal students' aptitudes for future employment through practical activities like school gardens and livestock. Civic education will focus on respect for laws and national values, including wearing helmets and respecting national symbols. Bilingual education, using national languages, is intended to improve learning and preserve linguistic heritage, with councils encouraged to promote traditional attire.

Why it mattersSchool leaders and students in Bané are affected by new educational reforms focused on vocational training, civic education, and bilingual instruction.
