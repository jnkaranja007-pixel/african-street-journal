Burkina Faso hosts China-focused forum on cultural and human exchange

The 26-27 November gathering at the University of Burkina brings together students, Chinese diplomats, experts and businesspeople, alongside participants from Gabon, Cameroon.

Sources: Sidwaya and Lefaso.net · 2026-11-27 · 1 min read

Burkina Faso’s Club of Young People for the Promotion of Sino-Burkinabè Cooperation opened its seventh international forum on Thursday, 26 November, at the University of Burkina in Ouagadougou. The two-day event focuses on cultural and human exchange between China and Burkina Faso.

The organisers say students, staff from China’s embassy, experts and businesspeople are taking part in discussions on human, commercial and cultural ties. Abdoul Razahagou Dénié, the club’s national coordinator, said participants would consider China’s revolution as a source of ideas for Burkina Faso’s development. Delegations from Gabon, Cameroon and Senegal are also attending.

Jean-Noël Bonkoungou, representing the culture minister, urged Burkinabè students who study in China to return and apply their expertise at home. China’s ambassador, Zhao Deyong, said the embassy had conducted human and cultural activities with Burkinabè government bodies and associations during 2026.

Why it mattersBurkinabè students studying in China are being urged by the culture minister’s representative to return and use their expertise in the country’s development.
