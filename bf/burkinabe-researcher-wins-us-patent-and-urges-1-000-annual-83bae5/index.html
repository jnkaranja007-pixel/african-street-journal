Burkinabè researcher wins US patent and urges 1 000 annual applications

Moïse Convolbo’s predictive artificial-intelligence system was developed in Japan at Rakuten after nearly six years and five provisional refusals.

Source: Lefaso.net · 2026-11-27 · 1 min read

Burkinabè researcher Dr Moïse Convolbo received a US patent on 24 November 2026 for an artificial-intelligence system that predicts users’ decisions on digital services. Developed in Japan within the Rakuten group, it is designed to identify a visitor’s behaviour while they are still using a service, without removing their freedom to choose.

Convolbo told Lefaso.net that the system learned from many visitors’ online journeys, including pages viewed, click order and returns to earlier pages, alongside how each visit ended. He said the invention began with online shoppers who browse and compare products but leave without completing a purchase; conventional tools analyse such departures only afterward.

The patent followed nearly six years of proceedings and five provisional refusals, according to the report. Convolbo called for Burkina Faso to set a target of 1 000 patent applications a year and for closer ties between African research and markets.

Why it mattersConvolbo’s proposed annual target gives Burkina Faso’s research system a benchmark of 1 000 patent applications.
