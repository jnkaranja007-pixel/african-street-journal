Burkina Faso charges four over 577.48 grams of missing cocaine

A court clerk and a prison security officer are among those accused of taking and selling cocaine kept as evidence for destruction after trial.

Source: Lefaso.net · 2026-11-24 · 1 min read

Burkina Faso prosecutors have charged four people over the removal and sale of 577.48 grams of cocaine held as judicial evidence in Ouagadougou. The case came to light on 17 November 2026, after two people were arrested on suspicion of selling drugs in the Kouba neighbourhood.

The Justice and Human Rights Ministry said investigators traced the cocaine to a court clerk, who allegedly took it while the Manga courthouse was emptied for construction work, then passed it to the two sellers. A prison security officer is accused of introducing them to the clerk. The four were placed in pretrial detention on 20 November 2026.

Prosecutors filed charges including theft and breaking of sealed evidence, and transfer of narcotics. The case is listed for a hearing on Wednesday 25 November 2026. Penalties depend on each person’s alleged role and the offence charged.

Why it mattersThe four accused must answer the filed charges at a hearing scheduled for Wednesday 25 November 2026.
