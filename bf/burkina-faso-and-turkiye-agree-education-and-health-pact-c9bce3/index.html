Burkina Faso and Türkiye agree education and health pact

Ankara talks between the two nations also explored economic and security cooperation.

Source: Agence d'Information du Burkina · 2026-10-23 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Burkina Faso and Türkiye explored new avenues for cooperation in education, health, and the economy during high-level talks in Ankara on October 20 and 21, 2026. A Burkinabè delegation, led by Foreign Affairs Ministry Secretary General Hermann Yirigouin Toé, met with Turkish officials from institutions including the Maarif Foundation, TIKA, and the Ministry of Health. The discussions aimed to strengthen bilateral ties and expand existing partnerships.

The Maarif Foundation agreed to increase scholarships for Burkinabè students and support vocational and technical training, building on its existing schools in Ouagadougou and Bobo-Dioulasso. TIKA committed to supporting emergency medical training and re-examining a project for emergency medicine development. Both sides also agreed to renew a 2024 academic cooperation accord, with the Turkish Higher Education Council promising more master's and doctoral scholarships for Burkinabè students.

Security cooperation was also a key focus, with Burkina Faso inviting Türkiye to partner with the Sahel States Confederation (AES) in combating terrorism. Türkiye reaffirmed its commitment to supporting Burkina Faso's security and territorial integrity, expressing a desire for a defense and security agreement. The two nations also plan to hold their fourth joint cooperation commission in Ouagadougou in 2027.

Why it mattersBurkinabè students will benefit from increased scholarships and expanded vocational training opportunities in Türkiye.
