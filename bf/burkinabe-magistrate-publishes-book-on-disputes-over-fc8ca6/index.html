Burkinabè magistrate publishes book on disputes over terminated public contracts

Ambroise Nébié's 100-page book sets out the remedies open to contractors and how administrative judges decide cases after a public contract is ended.

Sources: Lefaso.net and Burkina24 · 2026-11-21 · 1 min read

Ambroise Nébié, a magistrate, presented his book on disputes over terminated public contracts in Ouagadougou on Thursday, 19 November. The 100-page work, divided into two chapters, examines the legal mechanisms and remedies that follow the termination of public contracts.

The book reviews the remedies open to a contractor whose contract has been terminated, the claims made by each party and the arguments put before the administrative judge. Drawing mainly on case law, it takes both a descriptive and a critical approach. Nébié said he had handled several public-contract termination cases while serving at the administrative court in Bobo-Dioulasso.

He said public contracts meet the administration's needs, so ending one can interrupt work the state depends on. The book was published in April 2026 by Éditions Nouvelle Édition Burkinabè.

Why it mattersA contractor whose public contract is terminated now has a set of remedies and court procedures that the book maps out for the administrative judge.
