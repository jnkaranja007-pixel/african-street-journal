Burkina Faso army marks 66 years amid anti-terror fight

Minister Célestin Simporé stated that the army has gained the upper hand against terrorists.

Source: Lefaso.net · 2026-11-03 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

The Burkina Faso National Armed Forces (FAN) celebrated their 66th anniversary on Sunday, November 1, 2026, with a ceremony in Ouagadougou. The event included a military parade and the awarding of honors to 56 soldiers and volunteers. Since its creation on November 1, 1960, the FAN have served to defend Burkina Faso's sovereignty.

Minister of War Célestin Simporé praised the dedication of military personnel and remembered those who died fighting for territorial integrity. He declared the army's loyalty to the president and expressed confidence in overcoming terrorism, which he described as an imperialist-imposed war. Simporé stated that the armed forces are now prevailing over the enemy.

Among those honored was VDP Issaka Guira, who received a medal for his service since June 2023. Guira lost a leg, his left arm, and was disfigured after encountering an improvised explosive device during a mission to clear a village for returning residents. He had actively participated in counter-terrorism operations in the Yaadga region.

Why it mattersThe 66th anniversary celebration of the Burkina Faso National Armed Forces highlights the ongoing struggle against terrorism and the sacrifices made by soldiers and volunteers.
