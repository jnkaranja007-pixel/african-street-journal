AES ministers review second-year roadmap in Ouagadougou

Ministers from Burkina Faso, Mali and Niger are reviewing progress on the confederation's roadmap at a two-day session that ends with a synthesis note.

Source: Lefaso.net · 2026-11-12 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Ministers of the Alliance of Sahel States (AES) confederation opened a two-day ministerial session in Ouagadougou on Wednesday, November 11, 2026, to review how the second-year roadmap is being carried out. Delegations from Burkina Faso, Mali and Niger are taking part, and the session is chaired by Burkina Faso's Minister of War and Patriotic Defence, General Célestin Simporé.

Simporé, who read the opening speech, said the meeting will assess activities across the three pillars of the confederation: defence and security, diplomacy and development. He called for better sharing of intelligence and stronger strategic autonomy, and for the three countries to speak with one voice internationally. The roadmap was finalised on February 26, 2026, in Ouagadougou.

The ministers are expected to produce a synthesis note on progress made during the confederation's second year, and to make recommendations ahead of the next meeting of the heads of state college. The session also addressed Burkinabè gold miners expelled from Guinea, and Simporé thanked the Malian authorities for their handling of the case.

Why it mattersBurkinabè readers will see whether the confederation's joint security, trade and resource plans move from paper to practice before the heads of state meet.
