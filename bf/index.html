Burkinabè exiles in Ivory Coast answer military regime's charges

Former officials, soldiers and journalists living abroad are accused of apology for terrorism and incitement to rebellion, and some face extradition requests.

Former Burkinabè politicians, military officers and journalists are now in the sights of the justice system in Burkina Faso, according to RFI. Some face arrest warrants and extradition requests from the military government in Ouagadougou.

The exiles are being prosecuted officially on charges of apology for terrorism and incitement to rebellion. Several of them live in Ivory Coast. The report asks how they respond to the accusations against them.

The report does not say what answers the exiles have given or whether any extradition request has been granted.

Burkina Faso opens terror-related cases against 45

Legal proceedings target individuals and entities for terrorism financing, complicity, and incitement, with international arrest warrants issued.

Burkina Faso's anti-terrorism prosecutor has initiated six legal proceedings against 45 individuals and entities for terrorism-related offenses and public order violations. The cases, announced on Friday, November 13, 2026, include charges of complicity in terrorist conspiracies, glorifying terrorism, and disseminating terrorist content. Many of those targeted reside outside Burkina Faso, prompting the issuance of international arrest warrants and extradition requests to countries including Côte d'Ivoire, the United States, and France.

The prosecutor general, Désiré Sawadogo, detailed that the investigations cover a range of serious crimes, such as complicity in terrorist financing, possession of illegal firearms for terrorist purposes, and demoralizing national forces. Prominent figures named include former transitional leader Paul Henri Sandaogo Damiba and businessman François Compaoré, alongside Ivorian activist Camille Makosso. These individuals face charges that could lead to their extradition and prosecution within Burkina Faso.

Several other individuals and entities are also under judicial investigation, with specific charges including defamation and contempt of public authority. Among those facing ongoing instructions are Yacouba Ladji Bama, Aminata Rachow, Henri Sebgo, Maixent Somé, and the media outlet France 24. The legal actions signal a broad crackdown on activities deemed detrimental to national security and public order.

Le Faso Digital closes second edition

Organizers celebrated partners and awarded 42 digital solutions.

The second edition of Le Faso Digital, themed "Inclusion, sovereignty, innovation: towards a digital Burkina for all," concluded with a closing dinner on Friday, November 13, 2026. The event, which ran from October 17 to 24, 2026, recognized partners and awarded prizes to laureates.

Dr. Cyriaque Paré, promoter of Le Faso Digital, expressed gratitude to institutional and media partners for their support and visibility. He announced that the virtual platform will remain accessible until 2028, allowing participating digital solutions and companies to stay visible for the next 24 months.

42 winning digital solutions will receive six months of free subscription from Digitales Solidaires. Rodrigue Guiguemdé, president of Digitales Solidaires, stated that his organization aims to support these projects through its Carrefour Innovation platform to foster their development and societal impact.

Culture · 2026-11-15

Burkina Faso launches fourth Sino-Burkinabè film week

14 films, seven from China and seven from Burkina Faso, will screen for free in Ouagadougou.

The fourth Sino-Burkinabè Film Week officially opened on Thursday, November 12, 2026, at the Centre culturel du Faso in Ouagadougou. The event, themed "Sino-African Cultural and Human Exchanges," features 14 films, seven produced in Burkina Faso and seven in China. The opening ceremony included the screening of a Chinese film and was attended by the Chinese ambassador to Burkina Faso and several Burkinabè officials.

Ambassador Zhao Deyong of China highlighted the strong public turnout and the event's role in strengthening cultural cooperation and friendship. He noted Ouagadougou's status as the capital of African cinema, suggesting the film week would promote civilizational diversity. Pazouknam Jean-Baptiste Ouédraogo, representing the minister of culture, also praised the initiative, encouraging further public engagement with the free screenings.

Ouédraogo suggested that Burkina Faso could learn from China's experience in developing its film industry, emphasizing strategic vision, investment, training, infrastructure, and promotion of national productions. He stated this aligns with the Burkinabè government's vision for building a stronger national cinema through knowledge sharing and professional collaboration.

Why it mattersThe fourth Sino-Burkinabè Film Week in Ouagadougou will feature 14 free film screenings, promoting cultural exchange between the two nations.

Sport · 2026-11-15

Burkina Faso wins team air badminton title in Benin

The team title was decided in a final against Benin's A team at the Daho Beach Club in Akpakpa, Benin.

Burkina Faso won the team trophy at an international air badminton competition held in Benin from 12 to 14 November 2026. The team beat Togo on the way to the final, then defeated Benin's A team in the decision on Friday, 13 November, at the Daho Beach Club in Akpakpa.

Abdoul Fatao Tapsoba and Fayçal Kafando were the players credited with securing the team victory. Benin's B team finished third in the first team edition of the competition. Benin, the host, entered two teams, alongside Burkina Faso, Côte d'Ivoire, Nigeria and Togo.

The individual events follow on Saturday, 14 November, with men's doubles, women's doubles, mixed doubles, men's triples and women's triples. Air badminton is played on sand, usually on a beach, with natural or plastic shuttlecocks and rules similar to tennis.

Why it mattersPlayers and clubs in the five participating countries now go into the individual doubles and triples events that run from Saturday, 14 November.
