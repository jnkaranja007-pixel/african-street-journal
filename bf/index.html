Burkina Faso minister praises animal vaccination campaign

Minister Amadou Dicko oversaw a national animal vaccination and artificial insemination drive in Nazinon, Oubri, and Yaadga regions.

The Minister Delegate for Animal Resources, Amadou Dicko, led a mission from November 10 to 12, 2026, to supervise the national animal vaccination and artificial insemination campaign in the Nazinon, Oubri, and Yaadga regions. The Ministry of Agriculture, Water, Animal and Fisheries Resources aims to assess the progress of this dual campaign against priority animal diseases and for artificial insemination for the 2026-2027 period.

Following the tour, Minister Dicko reported a satisfactory outcome in the visited localities, noting this is the fourth large-scale vaccination campaign by the government. He stated that the ambition is to control most animal pathologies by 2030, with many vaccines heavily subsidized or entirely free. A mid-term review workshop is scheduled for November 27, 2026, to formulate recommendations for the campaign's success.

Minister Dicko emphasized the importance of artificial insemination for improving the national herd's genetic potential and increasing meat and milk production, aligning with the 2026-2028 agro-pastoral offensive. He also highlighted ongoing investments in animal health, including a national vaccine manufacturing unit at Samandéni and the operationalization of a veterinary medicine purchasing center.

Why it mattersLivestock producers in Nazinon, Oubri, and Yaadga regions must ensure their animals are vaccinated, as the minister warned of sanctions for non-compliance.

ACEP Burkina opens two service points near Ouagadougou markets

The microfinance institution opened the counters at Sankar-Yaaré and 10 Yaar on Monday, 16 November 2026, to bring its services closer to market users.

ACEP Burkina, a microfinance institution, opened two new counters on Monday, 16 November 2026, near markets in Ouagadougou. One is at Sankar-Yaaré, on the east side of the market entrance beside the Petrofa station. The other is at the north-east entrance of the 10 Yaar market.

The counters serve traders and other users of the two markets, who can now carry out cash transactions and open accounts without travelling as far. The institution said the move is part of its strategy to strengthen its presence close to its customers.

ACEP Burkina says the two points add to its growing local network in Ouagadougou. The institution has pledged to make its financial services easier to reach for people going about their daily business.

Why it mattersTraders and other market users at Sankar-Yaaré and 10 Yaar can open accounts and do cash transactions at the new counters without long trips.

Bobo-Dioulasso to host AFoodPack 5 packaging salon from November 27

The fifth edition of the African agro-food packaging salon will focus on ecological packaging for cashew, mango, sesame and shea producers.

Bobo-Dioulasso will host the fifth edition of the African agro-food packaging salon, AFoodPack, from November 27 to 29, 2026, at the Maison de la Culture. The theme is « Ecological packaging: catalysts for innovation in the agro-food sectors of Guiriko », and the salon will look at packaging needs for cashew, mango, sesame and shea. A day called « 3S: Sya without Sachets » is planned for November 26.

The organising committee, led by its president Salimata Sori Coulibaly, presented the programme to the press on Monday, November 16, at RTB 2 Guiriko in Bobo-Dioulasso. She said packaging now affects preservation, health safety, traceability, transport and access to new markets. Organisers say the fifth edition moves from general packaging questions to the specific needs of each of the four sectors, with the aim of identifying concrete solutions.

The salon is under the patronage of the Minister of State for Agriculture, the Minister of Communication, Culture, Arts and Tourism, and the Minister of Industry, Commerce and Handicrafts. Roger Baro, special adviser to the president, and the mayor of Bobo-Dioulasso are guests of honour. The governor of the Guiriko region and the president of the regional chamber of commerce and industry are also associated with the event.

Why it mattersShea, cashew, mango and sesame processors in the region will see which packaging solutions are discussed for their sectors at the salon from November 27.

Burkina Faso, Nicaragua agree visa exemption

Diplomats from Burkina Faso and Nicaragua met on Monday to boost cooperation in agriculture, culture, and parliamentary exchanges.

Burkina Faso's Minister of Foreign Affairs, Karamoko Jean Marie Traoré, met with Nicaragua's Chargé d’affaires, Sandy Anabell Dávila Sandoval, in Ouagadougou on Monday, November 16. The meeting was a first contact for the new Nicaraguan diplomat, who arrived recently in Burkina Faso. Sandoval affirmed her country's commitment to strengthening friendship and cooperation with Burkina Faso, aiming to continue the work of her predecessor.

Sandoval pledged to work towards enhancing the partnership between Nicaragua and Burkina Faso in parliamentary, agricultural, university, and cultural sectors. Minister Traoré welcomed Sandoval, noting the strong relationship between the two nations and encouraging her to build on existing ties. He assured her of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs' support during her mission in Burkina Faso.

The Burkinabè foreign minister and the Nicaraguan Chargé d’affaires agreed to advance on specific issues, including the accreditation of ambassadors and a visa exemption agreement between the two countries. They also highlighted the dynamic agricultural cooperation, particularly in sharing expertise on coffee production.

Why it mattersBurkina Faso and Nicaragua have agreed to work towards a visa exemption, which will affect travel for citizens of both nations.

Dr Adama Sawadogo becomes Burkina Faso's first cardiovascular surgeon with agrégé rank

He won the rank of Maître de conférences agrégé at a continental competition held in Bangui, Central African Republic.

Dr Adama Sawadogo has been admitted to the rank of Maître de conférences agrégé (MCA) in cardiovascular surgery, making him the first Burkinabè to hold this rank in the specialty. He passed the examination at the 23rd health sciences aggregation competition of the Conseil africain et malgache pour l'enseignement supérieur (CAMES), the African and Malagasy council for higher education, held in Bangui.

He passed all three stages: presenting his scientific and academic credentials, delivering a lecture to test his teaching ability, and a clinical examination called the patient lesson, which assesses clinical skills. The rank allows him to take a larger part in training and supervising future cardiovascular specialists.

Lefaso.net interviewed Dr Sawadogo in 2024 for a report on treating children with heart disease at the Tengandogo University Hospital Centre in Ouagadougou.

Why it mattersDr Sawadogo can now supervise and train future cardiovascular surgeons, adding to the country's own capacity in the specialty.
