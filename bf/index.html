Belgium reaffirms unconditional cooperation with Burkina Faso

Belgian Vice-Prime Minister Maxime Prévot stated his country's commitment to continue its cooperation with Burkina Faso without conditions after meeting with Burkinabè Foreign.

Belgium's Vice-Prime Minister, Maxime Prévot, reaffirmed his country's commitment to continue cooperation with Burkina Faso unconditionally during a 72-hour visit to Ouagadougou. This declaration followed discussions with Burkinabè Foreign Minister Karamoko Jean Marie Traoré, where Prévot emphasized the goal to "continue cooperation, not condition it."

The Belgian official explained that his visit aimed to gather firsthand information from Burkinabè authorities, comparing it with reports from Brussels. Minister Traoré welcomed this approach, highlighting the need for direct dialogue to understand African realities and presenting the context of the security crisis in Burkina Faso and the government's efforts to address it.

Discussions also covered the dynamics of the Sahel States Confederation (AES) and future cooperation between Burkina Faso and the European Union. Prévot stated Belgium's intent to invest "in Burkina Faso, with Burkina Faso, but not in place of Burkina Faso," underscoring a bilateral relationship spanning over 50 years, primarily through the Belgian development agency Enabel, across various sectors including economic development, agriculture, health, and education.

Why it mattersThis development affects Burkinabè citizens by ensuring continued Belgian support in sectors like health, education, and economic development, which directly impacts their daily lives and access to essential services.

BMCRF sells 100 tonnes of seized rice at social prices starting September 1, 2026

The Mobile Brigade for Economic Control and Fraud Repression (BMCRF) announced it will sell 100 tonnes of rice, confiscated due to clandestine storage, at social prices.

The BMCRF will begin selling 100 tonnes of seized rice at social prices on September 1, 2026, according to a communiqué published on August 27, 2026. This initiative follows the confiscation of the rice due to clandestine storage. Interested individuals can subscribe in advance at the BMCRF offices, located opposite the former Primature, from 9 hours to 15 hours daily until stocks are depleted.

The proceeds from this sale will be fully transferred to the Patriotic Support Fund (FSP). The BMCRF also stated that controls are ongoing, and any offenders will face sanctions in accordance with current regulations. The public is encouraged to report suspicious cases using the green numbers 80 00 11 84/85/86.

This action comes as regional authorities, such as the Tannounyan regional directorate of industry, commerce, and crafts, are conducting price and quality controls in Banfora. On August 24, 2026, officials visited over a dozen businesses to ensure compliance with state-fixed prices and hygiene standards, warning that non-compliant merchants face fines and community service.

Why it mattersConsumers will benefit from access to rice at social prices, while merchants face increased scrutiny and potential penalties for non-compliance with regulations.

Burkina Faso trains 2,246 youth in national identity and sovereignty

The second edition of the Compulsory Patriotic Immersion program concluded on Thursday, August twenty-seventh, 2026, after a month of civic and patriotic training for new high.

Two thousand two hundred forty-six high school graduates from the Goulmou, Sirba, and Tapoa regions completed their compulsory patriotic immersion in Fada N’Gourma. The program, which ran from July twenty-seventh to August twenty-sixth, 2026, focused on national identity, citizenship, and sovereignty, with one thousand seven hundred seventy-three participants from the Goulmou region alone.

The closing ceremony was presided over by the Governor of the Goulmou region, Ram Joseph Kafando, alongside administrative, military, paramilitary, religious, and customary authorities. Participants demonstrated knowledge and values acquired during the month-long training, which included discipline, love of country, Burkinabè history, and military and civic instruction.

Military instructor Lieutenant Boureima Ouédraogo stated the training covered discipline, weapons handling, and social cohesion, as well as civics, road safety, health, and hygiene. Governor Kafando urged the graduates to become “ambassadors of peace and love of country” and models of conduct in their future endeavors.

Why it mattersThis program directly affects the two thousand two hundred forty-six high school graduates by shaping their understanding of national identity and civic responsibility.

Burkinabe Handball Team Seeks Blessings from Mogho-Naba for Upcoming Tournament

The Burkina Faso women's national handball team, the Étalons dames, visited Mogho-Naba Baongho to receive his blessings before hosting the IHF Nation Trophy Zone 3 tournament.

The Étalons dames, Burkina Faso's national women's handball team, met with His Majesty Mogho-Naba Baongho at his royal palace in Ouagadougou on August twenty-sixth. The team sought his blessings ahead of the IHF Nation Trophy Seniors Zone 3 tournament, which Burkina Faso will host from September two to six. This visit is a customary practice for regional or international competitions, according to Boubakar Sanfo, president of the Burkinabe Handball Federation (FBH).

The upcoming tournament will feature eight nations from Zone 3, including Ghana, Côte d'Ivoire, Benin, Niger, Nigeria, Togo, Liberia, and Burkina Faso. The Burkinabe players are currently in residential training, aiming to win the trophy and qualify for the Africa Cup of Nations (CAN) in handball. Sanfo stated that winning this first edition of the tournament is their objective.

During the audience, Mogho-Naba Baongho offered advice to the players and staff, urging them to play without worry and maintain team cohesion. He emphasized the importance of defending the national colors with honor. Captain and goalkeeper Orokia Kazony expressed gratitude for the blessings and advice, stating the team is prepared and confident to compete.

Why it mattersThe national women's handball team is directly affected as they seek traditional blessings and prepare to compete in an international tournament hosted in their country.

SONABEL opens Bagré dam gates early, warns of flood risks

Burkina Faso's national electricity company, SONABEL, will open the Bagré hydroelectric dam's floodgates on Friday, August twenty-eighth, 2026, due to critical water levels.

SONABEL announced the early opening of the Bagré hydroelectric dam's floodgates, starting at nine hours on Friday, August twenty-eighth, 2026. This measure is necessary because recent heavy rainfall has caused the dam's water level to reach a critical threshold, requiring a faster evacuation of surplus water.

The company is urging communities living downstream from the dam to remain vigilant and take all necessary precautions. This warning comes as there is a risk of flooding resulting from the gate opening.

The decision to open the gates is a direct response to the elevated water levels in the reservoir. SONABEL's communication emphasizes the need for preparedness among the affected riverside populations.

Why it mattersRiverside populations downstream from the Bagré dam are affected by potential flooding due to the early opening of the dam's floodgates.
