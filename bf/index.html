Over 340 Recruits Pledge Loyalty to Burkina Faso

Young recruits completed mandatory patriotic immersion training, vowing to uphold national integrity and contribute to peace and social cohesion.

More than 340 young recruits in the Bam province have pledged to never betray Burkina Faso following their mandatory patriotic immersion training. The official ceremony in Kongoussi saw the recruits reaffirm their commitment to their homeland after a period of training focused on strengthening patriotism, civic duty, and responsible citizenship. The recruits received instruction in patriotism, civics, living together, and social cohesion, alongside self-defense and first aid techniques.

The recruits' spokesperson, Abdoul Razak Zougouri, expressed gratitude for the military and civilian guidance received during the month-long program. He highlighted that the immersion provided valuable knowledge and fostered a sense of unity. Provincial high commissioner Karim Ouédraogo urged the graduates to translate their patriotic commitment into daily actions, emphasizing that true patriotism is demonstrated through behavior, not just words or symbols.

Ouédraogo advised the recruits to be disciplined, honest, and to protect public property, contributing to peace and social unity. He also cautioned them to exercise discernment on social media and avoid spreading hate or misinformation. The ceremony concluded with demonstrations of self-defense and first aid, followed by a parade of the recruits.

Why it mattersThis event affects young Burkinabè citizens by reinforcing their commitment to national values and civic responsibility through mandatory training.

ESPOIR Project Boosts Livelihoods in Burkina Faso's Kuilsé Region

The Lutheran World Relief project has supported gardening, carpentry, and small businesses, enhancing economic opportunities for households in Kongoussi and Sabcé.

The Lutheran World Relief (LWR) NGO has concluded a review of its ESPOIR project in Burkina Faso's Kuilsé region, focusing on investments made to strengthen household livelihoods. Provincial authorities and LWR officials visited communes including Kongoussi and Sabcé from August 24 to 25, 2026, to assess the project's impact. Activities supported ranged from community gardening and agriculture to carpentry, commerce, livestock, and nutrition initiatives, all funded by the United States.

In Goungla village, a community garden on one hectare, established in 2025, was showcased, where approximately fifty individuals cultivate vegetables like okra, eggplant, and tomatoes. Beneficiaries reported that the garden provides both work and income. Similarly, a carpenter in Sabcé received tools and materials, not direct cash, enabling him and his seven employees to produce furniture for sale. Other beneficiaries received support for shops, pigsties, and chicken coops.

The project's reach extended to Kongoussi, where a displaced person's restaurant business was visited, demonstrating increased autonomy for the owner. The overall assessment by visiting officials in both Kongoussi and Sabcé was positive, highlighting the project's tangible contributions to local economies and individual self-sufficiency.

Why it mattersFarmers can benefit from the project's support for community gardens and agricultural activities, while investors may see opportunities in the development of local businesses and improved economic conditions.

Le Faso Digital Signs Two Partnerships to Boost Digital Ecosystem

The initiative aims to better mobilize, value, connect, and support digital businesses, startups, and innovators in Burkina Faso through new agreements.

Le Faso Digital, an initiative by Lefaso.net, has signed two partnership agreements in Ouagadougou to strengthen Burkina Faso's digital sector. The first agreement is with the Fédération du Numérique du Burkina Faso (FED Numérique), recognizing it as a strategic partner to enhance professional integration within the national digital ecosystem. The second partnership is with DIGITALES SOLIDAIRES, through Carrefour Innovations, aligning with the vision of "Inclusion, sovereignty, innovation: Towards a digital Burkina for all."

The collaboration with FED Numérique is expected to bolster the credibility and adoption of Le Faso Digital by mobilizing its network of member organizations and associations. FED Numérique will encourage businesses and professionals to participate in the virtual fair, promote calls for exhibitors and candidates, and relay information about conferences and competitions. They will also facilitate connections between the organizing committee and key players in the digital sector.

FED Numérique will also contribute expertise by proposing speakers for panels and masterclasses, identifying priority themes for businesses, and assisting in the selection of innovative startups and companies. The federation's involvement may extend to participating in the Le Faso Digital 2026 digital innovation competition, further integrating professional expertise into the initiative's development.

Why it mattersDigital businesses and innovators in Burkina Faso will benefit from increased visibility, networking opportunities, and support through these new partnerships.

Belgian Minister Visits Burkina Faso to Strengthen Ties

Maxime Prévot arrived in Ouagadougou for a 72-hour visit aimed at reinforcing bilateral cooperation and understanding the country's security context.

Belgian Deputy Prime Minister and Minister of Foreign Affairs, Maxime Prévot, arrived in Ouagadougou on August 26, 2026, for a three-day working visit. He was met by his Burkinabe counterpart, Karamoko Jean Marie Traoré, and held initial discussions with collaborators. The visit focuses on strengthening the long-standing bilateral cooperation between Belgium and Burkina Faso, which has been active for over 50 years, primarily through the Belgian development agency Enabel.

Minister Prévot stated that the visit aims to reinforce cooperation in a spirit of maturity and sincerity, emphasizing a desire to listen directly to local realities rather than relying solely on reports from Brussels. Burkinabe Foreign Minister Traoré welcomed this approach, using the opportunity to detail the security crisis in Burkina Faso and the government's efforts to address it through endogenous dynamics and sincere partnerships. He expressed that the visit would provide a clear understanding of the actual situation on the ground.

Discussions also covered the dynamics of the AES Confederation and future cooperation prospects with the European Union, whose headquarters are in Brussels. At the end of the meetings, Minister Prévot expressed his satisfaction and assured that Belgium remains committed to investing in Burkina Faso, working collaboratively with the country, but not in its place. The Belgian minister's visit underscores a commitment to direct engagement and understanding.

Why it mattersBelgian Minister Maxime Prévot's visit to Burkina Faso aims to strengthen bilateral cooperation and understand the country's security situation, impacting diplomatic relations and development partnerships.

AES Youth Urged to Promote Peace and Patriotism

Dr. Ousmane Bougouma called on young people in the Alliance of Sahel States to champion peace, discipline, and solidarity at a forum in Ouagadougou.

The president of the Confederation Sessions of the Parliaments of the Alliance of Sahel States (AES), Dr. Ousmane Bougouma, urged youth within the confederation to foster peace, discipline, and patriotism. Speaking at the opening of the 2026 Confederation Youth and Volunteer Forum in Ouagadougou, Bougouma emphasized the responsibility of young people to uphold values that form the bedrock of their social and cultural realities.

Bougouma also encouraged the youth to actively combat hate speech, disinformation, and divisive tactics that could weaken their societies. He called for concrete initiatives in local development, volunteering, and social cohesion, stressing that the forum should generate innovative ideas and actionable proposals to inform government decisions. The minister of Sports, Youth and Employment of Burkina Faso, Annick Pikbougoum Zingué/Ouattara, echoed these sentiments, highlighting the forum's importance for political and civic development.

The minister added that youth engagement is crucial for consolidating inter-ethnic cohesion through dialogue, promoting peace, and strengthening community resilience against violence and misinformation. She also called for support for innovation, entrepreneurship, and employment, advocating for a shared vision based on common values, with youth serving as a vital social, civic, and citizen force.

Why it mattersYoung people in the AES confederation are being called upon to actively promote peace, discipline, and patriotism, and to combat disinformation, which could affect their social cohesion and civic engagement.
