Politics · 2026-08-21

Burkina Faso Foreign Minister meets UN Special Envoy

Foreign Minister Karamoko Jean Marie Traore received UN Special Envoy Sahle-Work Zewde on Thursday, Aug. 20. Zewde is conducting a work visit to evaluate actions of the UN Regional Office for West Africa and the Sahel (UNOWAS).

Why it mattersThe mission assesses the effectiveness of UN regional operations in West Africa and the Sahel.

Sport · 2026-08-21

Tour du Faso 2026 sets budget at 600 million FCFA

The 37th edition of the Tour du Faso will take place from Oct. 30 to Nov. 8 with a projected budget of 600 million FCFA. Sports Minister Annick Pikbougoum Zingué Ouattara announced the figures during a meeting with sponsors in Ouagadougou on Aug. 20.

Why it mattersThe budget determines the scale of the cycling competition and the level of sponsorship required for the event.

Police intercept seven minors headed for gold mines

The Regional Brigade for Child Protection (BRPE) intercepted seven minors on Tuesday, Aug. 18, in Koudougou. The eldest of the children was 12 years old.

Why it mattersThe operation prevents children from entering hazardous artisanal gold mining sites.

Health · 2026-08-21

CHU Sourô Sanou to close services for rehabilitation

Certain clinical and medico-technical services at the Sourô Sanou University Hospital (CHUSS) will close starting Monday, Aug. 24. The closure is for the rehabilitation and extension of the health facility in Bobo-Dioulasso.

Why it mattersPatients will lose access to specific medical services at the hospital during the construction period.
