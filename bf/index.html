Education · 2026-10-29

Ouagadougou honours education and health workers

Teachers and health workers received financial rewards, motorcycles, and certificates for their achievements.

Ouagadougou municipality honoured outstanding individuals in education and health sectors on October 28, 2026, in the capital. The ceremony, held at the public primary school Château A, recognized top students, deserving teachers, and high-performing health structures. Awards included cash envelopes, bicycles, motorcycles, and certificates of recognition.

Rihanata Ouédraogo, a teacher, received a motorcycle for achieving a 100% success rate in the primary school leaving certificate. She attributed the success to a team effort involving students and parents, emphasizing a rigorous follow-up system for all students. Dorcas Dembélé, a student at Prytanée militaire de Kadiogo, was awarded a tablet and cash for her academic excellence.

The event, organized by the Ouagadougou commune, aimed to acknowledge and encourage continued efforts to improve education and healthcare quality. El Hadj Sayouba Traoré, the ceremony's patron, contributed financially to the prizes, including the motorcycles for laureates, and pledged a RAV4 vehicle for the next edition.

Why it mattersTeachers and students in Ouagadougou were recognized for their academic achievements, with some receiving motorcycles and tablets.

Culture · 2026-10-29

Récréâtrales festival transforms Ouagadougou neighbourhood

68 artists and technicians from 12 countries created a 550-metre urban stage for the 14th edition.

The Récréâtrales theatre festival is animating a neighbourhood in Ouagadougou, Burkina Faso, from October 24 to 31. Residents of Bougsemtenga are hosting performances in their courtyards for the festival's 14th edition, a role they have played since its creation.

Choreographer Djibril Ouattara has been collaborating with local women and youth since February on an opening performance exploring humanity's capacity for response and beauty. Agathe Serré, an amateur actress with the Bougsemtenga Women’s Association, described the demanding but rewarding rehearsals with Ouattara, noting the participants' commitment to giving their all.

The festival transforms an alleyway into a 550-metre urban stage, featuring 68 technicians and artists from 12 countries. Professional actor Claude Ilboudo, who grew up with the festival, stated that Récréâtrales employs many young people as volunteers or through their involvement in dance, theatre, and technical production.

Why it mattersThe festival directly involves residents of Ouagadougou, including women and youth, in artistic co-production and performance.

Sport · 2026-10-29

Burkina Faso to award 21 football honours

The fourth annual Night of Women's Football will celebrate local and diaspora players on November 27.

Burkina Faso will host the fourth Night of Women's Football on November 27, 2026, in Ouagadougou. The event, organised by Aïsport, aims to recognise the achievements of female footballers, coaches, and administrators. 21 awards, including honours for best player, best goalkeeper, and top scorer, will be presented.

Bal Aïcha Zerbo, the event's promoter, stated the night aims to energise those promoting women's football and challenge past reluctance from parents to let daughters play the sport. A jury will select the award recipients, and preparations are currently 85% complete, with partnerships still being finalised.

Last year, Pascaline Sawadogo, goalkeeper for USFA, received the "golden player" award among 19 distinctions. Sawadogo noted that hard work by stakeholders is changing perceptions, enabling players to pursue professional careers abroad and earn a living from the sport, similar to men.

Why it mattersFemale footballers in Burkina Faso and those playing abroad will be recognised for their contributions to the sport.
