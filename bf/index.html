Burkina Faso launches LAAFIAKO health complaint platform

The digital tool allows citizens to submit complaints, claims, suggestions, and information requests to health services.

Burkina Faso's Minister of Health, Robert Lucien Jean-Claude Kargougou, launched "LAAFIAKO" on Monday, November 2, 2026, in Ouagadougou. This digital platform is designed to collect and process complaints, claims, suggestions, and information requests from users of health services, with a stated processing time of 10 days.

The LAAFIAKO platform enables citizens to submit four types of requests: reports of alleged ethical or regulatory breaches, claims of dissatisfaction, requests for information, and suggestions for improvement. Access is available online at lafiaco.minsante.bf, via the toll-free number 3535, WhatsApp at 52 25 30 30, and through 108 reception and information services staffed by 233 personnel.

Minister Kargougou stated that the platform aims to make user feedback a tool for improving health service quality and strengthening accountability. The initiative applies to both public and private health facilities, with a commitment to providing feedback to requesters on the actions taken regarding their concerns.

Why it mattersBurkina Faso citizens can now formally report issues and seek information regarding their healthcare experiences.

Burkina Faso launches digital university research project

The four-year Digit OACS project, funded by Germany, will analyze digital practices in universities across Burkina Faso, Kenya, and Nigeria.

A four-year research project called Digit OACS was launched on Monday, November 2, 2026, in Ouagadougou to analyze digital transition in higher education. The project, funded by German development cooperation with approximately 200 million FCFA, will examine how digital tools are used by academics and administrative staff in universities in Burkina Faso, Kenya, and Nigeria.

The Digit OACS project aims to understand the actual uses of digital technologies by university actors, not just their presence, according to project director Dr. Natéwindé Sawadogo. Researchers will identify which digital tools academics choose to integrate into their teaching and research and which they prefer to manage traditionally, also studying administrative staff practices.

The comparative study is expected to highlight common practices and country-specific particularities, potentially guiding digital transformation policies in higher education and research institutions. Dr. Sawadogo noted that current digital transformation policies can be too generalized, failing to account for the different realities of various academic professions.

Why it mattersAcademics and administrative staff in Burkina Faso's universities will have their digital practices studied to inform future digital transformation policies.

Politics · 2026-11-03

Burkina Faso army marks 66 years amid anti-terror fight

Minister Célestin Simporé stated that the army has gained the upper hand against terrorists.

The Burkina Faso National Armed Forces (FAN) celebrated their 66th anniversary on Sunday, November 1, 2026, with a ceremony in Ouagadougou. The event included a military parade and the awarding of honors to 56 soldiers and volunteers. Since its creation on November 1, 1960, the FAN have served to defend Burkina Faso's sovereignty.

Minister of War Célestin Simporé praised the dedication of military personnel and remembered those who died fighting for territorial integrity. He declared the army's loyalty to the president and expressed confidence in overcoming terrorism, which he described as an imperialist-imposed war. Simporé stated that the armed forces are now prevailing over the enemy.

Among those honored was VDP Issaka Guira, who received a medal for his service since June 2023. Guira lost a leg, his left arm, and was disfigured after encountering an improvised explosive device during a mission to clear a village for returning residents. He had actively participated in counter-terrorism operations in the Yaadga region.

Why it mattersThe 66th anniversary celebration of the Burkina Faso National Armed Forces highlights the ongoing struggle against terrorism and the sacrifices made by soldiers and volunteers.
