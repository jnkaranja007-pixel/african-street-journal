Belgian Minister Visits Koulsé Region Projects

Maxime Prévot toured agricultural and social initiatives funded by Belgian cooperation, reaffirming long-term commitment.

Belgian Deputy Prime Minister and Minister of Foreign Affairs, Maxime Prévot, concluded a 72-hour working visit to Burkina Faso on August 28, 2026. During his stay, he visited the Koulsé region, including the Tengressené agro-ecological farm. There, he observed various income-generating activities such as poultry, beekeeping, sheep and cattle farming, and fish farming, supported by water towers for market gardening and grain production. This farm benefits 19 households and is part of a larger FAO project financed by Belgium to enhance resilience to climate change.

Prévot also visited a nutritional support center in Kaya, funded by Belgium and overseen by UNICEF, which aids children suffering from acute malnutrition and educates mothers on enriched porridge preparation. His tour extended to a center in Tiwèga, Kaya, focused on the socio-economic reintegration of vulnerable women and girls through vocational training in weaving, beading, and sewing. Over 250 women have been assisted by this center, enabling them to become self-sufficient.

The Belgian minister's visit, which included meetings with high-level Burkinabe officials, aimed to foster a "cooperation of respect" amid strained relations between the EU and Burkina Faso. Prévot reaffirmed Belgium's commitment to its over 50-year-old partnership with the country, highlighting the tangible results of Belgian-funded projects in agriculture, health, and women's empowerment.

Why it mattersBelgian Minister Maxime Prévot's visit highlights ongoing cooperation projects that directly benefit vulnerable households, children, and women through agricultural support, nutritional aid, and vocational training.

Over 340 Youths Pledge Loyalty to Burkina Faso

Newly trained recruits commit to national service after completing a month of patriotic immersion and self-defense training.

More than 340 young recruits in the Bam province have pledged to never betray Burkina Faso during their official graduation ceremony in Kongoussi. The recruits, who underwent mandatory patriotic immersion, reaffirmed their commitment to their homeland in the presence of provincial authorities and military officials. This pledge follows a month-long training focused on strengthening patriotism, civic duty, and social responsibility.

The recruits received training in patriotism, civic engagement, and living together, alongside self-defense and first aid skills. Abdoul Razak Zougouri, the promotion's spokesperson, expressed gratitude for the military and civilian guidance, highlighting the knowledge gained in fostering national unity. Provincial high commissioner Karim Ouédraogo urged the graduates to translate their patriotic commitment into daily actions, emphasizing that true patriotism is demonstrated through behavior, not just words.

Ouédraogo advised the new patriots to be disciplined, honest, and to contribute to peace and social cohesion, urging them to be discerning on social media and avoid spreading hate or disinformation. The ceremony concluded with demonstrations of self-defense and first aid, followed by a parade of the recruits, signifying their readiness to serve the nation.

Why it mattersOver 340 young recruits are affected by this mandatory patriotic immersion training, which aims to instill loyalty and civic duty.

Burkinabe journalists witness China's robotic advancements

Journalists from Burkina Faso observed the world robot games in Beijing, highlighting China's dominance in humanoid robot exports.

Burkinabe media professionals attending the second World Humanoid Robot Games in Beijing have expressed admiration for China's burgeoning robotics industry. The event, held from August twenty-second to twenty-sixth, featured over two thousand fifty-six robots from six hundred sixty-six teams across sixteen nations competing in various sports. This competition showcased significant progress by Chinese robotics firms, underscoring China's leading role in sector innovation.

During the first half of twenty twenty-six alone, China exported more than forty thousand humanoid robots, capturing ninety-seven percent of global deliveries. Souleymane Diallo, director of communication for the government information service, noted that the games signify a pivotal moment for robotics, moving beyond industrial and service applications into new dimensions. He anticipates a future of increasing human-machine complementarity, where robots handle repetitive or dangerous tasks, freeing humans for decision-making and creativity.

Sié Simplice Hien, editor-in-chief of Sidwaya Sport, described the rapid development of humanoid robots in China as a major technological turning point. He observed that the games demonstrate robots interacting with the real world and performing complex activities, including sports. Hien highlighted the shift from industrial robotics to applications closer to daily life, indicating a significant evolution in the field.

Why it mattersBurkinabe journalists are observing China's advanced robotics, which could influence future technological development and adoption in their country.

Burkina Faso seizes 100 tonnes of rice for social sale

The economic control brigade will sell confiscated rice at reduced prices starting September 1, 2026, requiring prior registration.

The Brigade mobile de contrôle économique et de la répression des fraudes (BMCRF) announced the sale of one hundred tonnes of seized and confiscated rice. This operation, aimed at combating clandestine storage, will offer rice at social prices across different categories. Interested individuals must register with the BMCRF starting Tuesday, September 1, 2026, and continue until the stock is depleted, with sales occurring daily between nine in the morning and three in the afternoon.

Participants are required to present valid identification documents at the BMCRF offices, located opposite the former Prime Minister's office. All proceeds from this sale will be fully remitted to the Fonds de soutien patriotique (FSP). The BMCRF also stated that ongoing controls will lead to sanctions for any violations of current regulations, and the public is encouraged to report suspicious activities via hotline numbers 80 00 11 84/85/86.

Separately, the Minister Delegate to the Minister of State, Minister of Agriculture, Amadou Dicko, launched an initiative in Bobo-Dioulasso to provide fifteen thousand tonnes of cereals at subsidized prices to animal feed producers. This aims to lower production costs for livestock and poultry feed, thereby increasing the availability of avian products nationally as part of the 2026-2028 agro-pastoral offensive.

Why it mattersThe BMCRF is selling seized rice at social prices to combat clandestine storage and fund a patriotic support fund, while a separate initiative provides subsidized cereals to animal feed producers to lower costs.

AES Youth Urged to Promote Peace and Patriotism

Leaders at a Ouagadougou forum called on young people across the Sahel Alliance states to champion discipline, solidarity, and national interest.

Dr. Ousmane Bougouma, president of the Confederation Parliaments of the Alliance of Sahel States (AES), urged youth to foster peace, discipline, and patriotism at the opening of the 2026 Confederation Youth and Volunteer Forum. He stressed the importance of these values as the foundation of their social and cultural realities, and called for combating hate speech and disinformation that could weaken their societies.

Bougouma encouraged young attendees to translate their commitment into concrete actions for local development, volunteering, and social cohesion. He expressed his expectation that the forum would generate innovative ideas and actionable proposals to inform government decisions within the AES confederation, aiming to strengthen cooperation, promote youth mobility, and accelerate common policies.

Burkinabe Minister of Sports, Youth, and Employment, Annick Pikbougoum Zingué/Ouattara, highlighted the forum as a crucial step in the political and civic construction of the confederation. She emphasized the youth's role in consolidating inter-ethnic cohesion through dialogue, promoting peace, building community resilience, and supporting innovation and entrepreneurship.

Why it mattersYoung people in the Sahel Alliance states are called upon to actively promote peace, discipline, and patriotism to strengthen their societies and contribute to development.
