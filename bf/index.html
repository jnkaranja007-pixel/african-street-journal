Business · 08/21/2026

ANEVE cuts environmental permit processing from 126 to 26 days

The National Agency for Environmental Evaluations (ANEVE) has reduced dossier processing times from 126 days to 26 days. The agency announced the reform during a meeting with accredited consultancy firms on Aug. 20.

Why it mattersThe shorter timeline allows businesses and developers to obtain environmental clearances faster to begin construction or operations.

Nassoumbou commune gives 400,000 CFA to Djibo volunteers

The commune of Nassoumbou provided 400,000 francs CFA to the commune of Djibo on Aug. 17. The funds are designated to support Volunteers for the Defense of the Homeland (VDP).

Why it mattersThe payment provides financial resources for local paramilitary forces engaged in security operations.

Sport · 08/21/2026

AS Police trains 110 female players on empowerment

The AS Police sports association is conducting a training workshop for 110 young female players from various clubs from Aug. 20 to 21. The event is being held in Ouagadougou.

Why it mattersThe program uses football as a tool to increase the social and economic autonomy of young women.

Politics · 08/21/2026

Burkina Faso Foreign Minister meets UN special envoy

Foreign Minister Karamoko Jean Marie Traoré received UN Special Envoy Sahle-Work Zewde on Aug. 20. Zewde is visiting to evaluate the actions of the UN Regional Office for West Africa and the Sahel (UNOWAS).

Why it mattersThe mission assesses the effectiveness of UN regional diplomatic efforts in the Sahel.

Culture · 08/21/2026

Burkina Faso minister presents media vision in Beijing

Communication Minister Pingdwendé Gilbert Ouédraogo delivered a presentation on content innovation in Beijing on Aug. 20. The appearance took place during the 7th Sino-African Media Cooperation Forum.

Why it mattersThe initiative seeks to use digital media innovation to strengthen diplomatic and cultural ties between Burkina Faso and China.
