Health · 2026-10-28

Burkina Faso launches health communication month

Minister Robert Kargougou announced the initiative will use national languages to reach more citizens.

Burkina Faso's Minister of Health, Robert Kargougou, launched a month-long health communication initiative on Tuesday, October 27, 2026, in Ouagadougou. The campaign, running from November 2 to November 30, 2026, aims to improve health awareness and practices by using national languages to communicate vital information.

Minister Kargougou stated that understandable health information can save lives, emphasizing the need to reach populations in their own languages. The initiative will involve partner radio stations broadcasting interactive programs, expert advice, and short messages to address health issues and encourage positive behaviors. The first week will focus on accountability for health sector actions since September 2022.

The communication month will dedicate the following three weeks to health problems specific to different regions across the country. This effort is supported by departments responsible for Communication and national languages, aiming to increase citizen participation in preserving their health and to ensure they become active agents in their own well-being.

Why it mattersThe health communication month aims to help more Burkinabè understand health information and participate in preserving their well-being.

Burkina Faso PM travels to Moscow for Russia-Africa summit

Prime Minister Rimtalba Jean Emmanuel Ouédraogo will attend the third Russia-Africa summit and economic forum on October 28-29.

Prime Minister Rimtalba Jean Emmanuel Ouédraogo departed Ouagadougou on Monday, October 26, 2026, for Moscow. He will participate in the third Russia-Africa Summit and the Economic and Humanitarian Forum on October 28 and 29. The visit aims to strengthen bilateral cooperation in security, investment, local resource transformation, and technology transfer.

This summit, the third after Sochi in 2019 and Saint Petersburg in 2023, will bring together African leaders with Russian President Vladimir Putin. Discussions will focus on trade, investment, food and energy security, and state sovereignty. The Economic and Humanitarian Forum will also cover industrial development, technology, digital transformation, AI, peaceful nuclear energy, health, education, and training.

On the sidelines of the summit, the Prime Minister will hold bilateral meetings to deepen partnerships for Burkina Faso's security and development. This engagement follows the February 12, 2026, agreement on the foundations of relations between Burkina Faso and Russia, reflecting President Ibrahim Traoré's desire to diversify partnerships.

Why it mattersThe Prime Minister's visit to Moscow aims to strengthen cooperation in key areas for Burkina Faso's development and security.

Tech · 2026-10-28

five burkinabè digital innovations win social awards

Faso-Parking, Jinkassan, MU-POL, Alerte Contravention, and EduBF were recognized for improving daily life.

Five Burkinabè digital innovations were awarded for their social and economic utility during the second edition of Le Faso Digital, held from October 17 to 24, 2026. The winning projects, including Faso-Parking for parking management and Jinkassan for inclusive financial innovation, address everyday concerns for Burkinabè citizens.

Faso-Parking, developed by NAWA START-UP SARL, aims to digitize parking operations in Ouagadougou using mobile money payments and QR codes to improve transaction ease and revenue tracking. The solution has already seen pilot deployments, registering 566 users and 490 transactions at Université Aube Nouvelle.

The Le Faso Digital event, themed 'Inclusion, sovereignty, innovation: towards a digital Burkina for all,' showcased local digital talents and encouraged the adoption of their solutions by citizens, businesses, and institutions.

Why it mattersThese digital innovations aim to solve daily problems for Burkinabè citizens in areas like parking, finance, health access, road safety, and education.

Climate · 2026-10-28

Burkina Faso researchers win OMM award for runway monitoring

A team from Burkina Faso received the 11th Professor Dr Vilho Väisälä Award for their work on real-time runway monitoring during rain.

A team of Burkinabe researchers has won the eleventh Professor Dr Vilho Väisälä Award from the World Meteorological Organization (OMM). They were recognized for their work on real-time monitoring of runway conditions during rainy periods. The award was presented on October 7, 2026, in Amsterdam, Netherlands, during the OMM Technical Conference (TECO-2026) and the Meteorological Technology World Expo 2026.

The distinction rewards a technological innovation designed to improve runway monitoring and enhance air traffic safety during the rainy season. Dr. Mariam Tidiga, Director General of Burkina Faso's National Meteorology Agency, attended the ceremony. Dr. Dieudonné Sama presented the winning research and accepted the diploma and medal on behalf of the team. The award includes a financial prize of 10,000 United States dollars.

This award highlights Burkina Faso's scientific contributions. 10 researchers affiliated with Burkina Faso institutions were also listed in the 2026 global ranking of the world's most cited scientists, according to TOPSCINET data. This presence shows the influence of several researchers and scientific teams associated with the country's research and higher education institutions.

Why it mattersBurkinabe researchers received international recognition and a financial award for innovation in meteorological technology.

Business · 2026-10-28

45 women's groups trained in financial literacy in Yako

The training aims to boost rural women's economic empowerment through better financial management skills.

The Network for the Promotion and Empowerment of Rural Women (REPAFER) in Burkina Faso launched a financial literacy training for 45 women's cooperatives and associations in Yako on October 26, 2026. The training, supported by the Union des Baoré-Tradition d’épargne et de crédit (UBTEC), will run until October 29, 2026.

This initiative targets rural women, including agricultural producers, local product processors, traders, and members of savings and credit associations, to enhance their financial management skills. Mamounata Ki/Ouédraogo, REPAFER Burkina's executive director, stated that women are pillars of rural development but face challenges in managing their financial resources.

The training is part of the 7th National Rural Women's Week and focuses on strategies for building a rural economy led by women, incorporating climate adaptation and technology. The opening ceremony was attended by Boukaré Zomodo, President of the special delegation of Yako, and Hafissatou Zakané from the provincial family and solidarity department.

Why it mattersRural women in Burkina Faso's Passoré province are receiving training to improve their financial management and economic autonomy.
