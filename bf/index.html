Burkina Faso vows support for gold miners expelled from Guinea

Thousands of Burkinabe miners expelled from Guinea for illegal gold mining are arriving home, with the government promising education, training and reintegration help.

Thousands of Burkinabe gold miners expelled from Guinea over illegal gold mining activities are returning home, reaching the transit hub of Bobo-Dioulasso before heading to their communities. Gold miner Omar Bandé told Africanews that the miners did not object to being expelled but said their gold and money were taken from them, and that some Guinean citizens lost their lives in the process.

Bandé said the miners received no prior warning and were given a 24-hour ultimatum to leave. Some of the returning miners could join the Volunteers for the Defence of the Homeland, while others could take up livestock farming, and he asked the authorities to support these plans. The Burkinabe government has announced access to education, vocational training and professional reintegration programmes for the returnees.

Members of the civic monitoring movement have mobilised to receive their fellow citizens and have welcomed the government's measures.

Why it mattersMiners who lost their gold and savings now depend on state training and job programmes to rebuild their livelihoods at home.

Coris Bank opens Cash Point for digital banking and money transfers in Ouagadougou

The bank's new Cash Point Rue des Étoiles combines digital banking and rapid money transfer services in its former headquarters.

Coris Bank International SA officially opened its Cash Point Rue des Étoiles in Ouagadougou on Friday, 13 November 2026. The specialised space, set up in the bank's former headquarters, brings together its digital banking and rapid money transfer activities under one roof.

Jean Théodore Sandwidi, deputy general manager in charge of the support division, said the aim is to make operations easier, widen access to services and give customers and partners more effective support. He said the centre already handled more than 25% of Mobile Money operations across the bank's branch network and more than 10% of rapid money transfers by the end of September 2026.

Souleymane Korgo, head of the rapid money transfer division, listed the transfer services available there, including Western Union, MoneyGram, Ria, SikaCash, Juba Express and RapidEx New. The space also serves electronic money distributors and sub-agents, who can bring their operational concerns to the centre.

Why it mattersCustomers sending or receiving money through these services can now do so at one dedicated counter, and partner distributors have a place to resolve their operational problems.

Burkina Faso wins CAN 2027 qualifier on Friday

Gabon also won its match, while Tunisia lost at home to Libya.

Burkina Faso secured a narrow victory against Lesotho in a CAN 2027 qualifier match played on Friday. The win keeps Burkina Faso's hopes alive in the continental tournament.

The match was part of ongoing qualifiers for the Africa Cup of Nations 2027. Gabon also achieved an important win in their fixture against Mauritania.

Meanwhile, Tunisia experienced a setback, losing 2-0 at home to Libya. This result signals difficulties for the Tunisian team in their qualification campaign.

Why it mattersBurkina Faso's national football team won its qualifier match on Friday, advancing its campaign for the Africa Cup of Nations 2027.
