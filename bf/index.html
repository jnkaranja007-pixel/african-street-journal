News · 08/20/2026

Police intercept 7 minors headed for gold mining sites

The Regional Brigade for the Protection of Children intercepted seven minors on Tuesday, Aug. 18, in Koudougou. The eldest of the group was 12 years old. The children were reportedly traveling to artisanal gold mining sites.

Why it mattersThe operation prevents children under the legal age from working in hazardous mining conditions.

UEMOA reports solid 2026 growth amid inflation risks

The West African Economic and Monetary Union (UEMOA) maintains strong economic momentum in 2026. Growth is driven by the construction, financial services, and trade sectors. Lefaso.net reports that insecurity and distribution difficulties in certain zones may increase price pressures.

Why it mattersDistribution failures in insecure areas could raise the cost of essential goods for residents in those regions.
