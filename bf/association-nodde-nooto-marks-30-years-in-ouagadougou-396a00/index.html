Association Nodde Nooto marks 30 years in Ouagadougou

The organisation honoured five partners for their contributions to development and humanitarian aid.

Sources: Lefaso.net and Agence d'Information du Burkina · 2026-11-09 · 1 min read

The Association Nodde Nooto (A2N) celebrated 30 years of its existence on Saturday, November 7, 2026, in Ouagadougou. The event recognised the organisation's pioneers and presented awards to five partners for their support of development and humanitarian actions benefiting communities.

A2N, founded in Dori, brought together partners, former collaborators, staff, and officials to commemorate its journey and achievements. The five distinguished partners are Action de Carême Suisse, Vétérinaires Sans Frontières Belgique, the CCBE-BF, the SPONG, and the Danish Refugee Council, all acknowledged for their assistance in local development, food security, education, and humanitarian aid.

A film showcasing A2N's 30-year activities was screened, and former collaborators shared memories of the association's founding and challenges. Clarisse Honadia Kambou, president of A2N's board, paid tribute to all who contributed to the organisation's growth, including founders, administrators, staff, and partners.
