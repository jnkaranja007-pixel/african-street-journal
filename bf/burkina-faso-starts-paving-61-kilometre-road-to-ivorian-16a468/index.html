Burkina Faso starts paving 61-kilometre road to Ivorian border

The RN11 extension will link Batié and Kpuéré to the border, with state funding and work divided between a public agency and private firms.

Source: Lefaso.net · 2026-11-23 · 1 min read

Burkina Faso’s construction minister, Mikailou Sidibé, launched work on 21 November on a 61-kilometre stretch of National Road 11 from Batié through Kpuéré to the Côte d’Ivoire border. The paved section extends the existing Gaoua-Batié road and will serve 11 villages.

The project will replace a dirt track with a seven-metre-wide rural road and an eight-metre-wide road through built-up areas. The ministry’s communications directorate said the works also include 51 culverts, removal of 26 existing structures, drainage, signs, safety features and street lighting. The state is paying for the project.

The public agency Faso Mêbo will handle earthworks, pavement construction and surfacing, while domestic private companies will build structures and drainage. Sidibé urged those involved to work rigorously and quickly. The route is expected to ease agricultural shipments and trade with Côte d’Ivoire and Ghana.

Why it mattersFarmers in the 11 villages served by the route are expected to have an easier way to move agricultural produce, while trade links extend to Côte d’Ivoire and Ghana.
