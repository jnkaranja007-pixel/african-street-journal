ADCT funds 32 shops in Koudougou for 71 500 CFA358

The market project includes electrical meter rooms and latrines; a tenant pays 20 000 francs CFA a month in rent.

Source: Lefaso.net · 2026-11-26 · 1 min read

Burkina Faso’s Agency for the Support of Territorial Development (ADCT) funded 32 street shops in Koudougou, in the Nando region, at a cost of 71 500 CFA358. The agency’s delegation visited the shops on 24 and 25 November 2026 during a tour of projects it financed for local authorities.

Built in 2025, the site also has two electrical meter rooms and two blocks of four-stall latrines. Issouf Siemdé, a tenant for nearly a year, said he had previously worked from a small shelter and now pays 20 000 francs CFA monthly rent. He received his shop through a draw organised by municipal authorities.

Koudougou’s special delegation president, Amédée Paré, said the investment addressed difficulties faced by traders. The ADCT also visited a three-hectare market garden in Guirgo village, in rural Sourgou commune.

Why it mattersKoudougou tenants pay 20 000 francs CFA monthly for shop space, while the municipality’s project cost 71 500 CFA358.
