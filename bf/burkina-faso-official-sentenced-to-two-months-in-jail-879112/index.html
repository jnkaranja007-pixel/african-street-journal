Burkina Faso official sentenced to two months in jail

Lebinoua Nikièma received a 12-month sentence, with two months suspended, and a fine for inciting unrest.

Source: Lefaso.net · 2026-11-01 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

A court in Ouaga I on October 27, 2026, sentenced Lebinoua Nikièma to 12 months in prison, with two months to be served immediately, and a one million CFA franc fine. Nikièma, a financial services administrator, was found guilty of inciting demoralization of the armed forces and outraging public officials. He was acquitted of charges related to undermining state security.

The public prosecutor immediately appealed the verdict, having previously requested a 36-month sentence and a one million CFA franc fine. Nikièma admitted to Facebook posts that led to his arrest by the cybercrime unit but stated he did not intend to cause authorities to flee. He asked for leniency and pledged support for the Popular Progressive Revolution.

Nikièma's publications in early September 2026 were deemed damaging to the army's image. The Council of Ministers officially dismissed him from the National Human Rights Commission on September 24, 2026. The court's decision followed a hearing on October 15, 2026, where his defense argued for a suspended sentence or a fine.

Why it mattersLebinoua Nikièma, a financial services administrator, faces immediate jail time and a fine for online posts deemed harmful to state security and the military.
