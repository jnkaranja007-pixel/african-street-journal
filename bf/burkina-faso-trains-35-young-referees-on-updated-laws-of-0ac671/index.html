Burkina Faso trains 35 young referees on updated laws of the game

The three-day session at the Focus Sport academy, which ends Monday, covers rule changes trialled at the last World Cup.

Source: Burkina24 · 2026-11-16 · 1 min read

35 young referees in Burkina Faso, 25 referees and 10 assistants, are taking a three-day refresher course on the laws of the game at the Focus Sport academy. The session runs from November 14 to 16, 2026, and was organised by Suduu Andal Sport, with the central referees commission taking part.

Losseni Paré, president of the Commission centrale des arbitres (CCA), the body that oversees referees, said the ambassador for sport had offered the training and that the commission used the chance to select its young referees. Rosalie Tempa Ndah François, the course trainer, said changes tested at the most recent World Cup had sped up matches and improved the handling of time-wasting and feigned injuries.

The final day, on Monday, November 16, 2026, is open to everyone involved in the sport, including journalists and coaches.

Why it mattersReferees who complete the course will apply the updated rules in matches, and the organisers hope this will reduce disputes over decisions.
