Vista Bank launches French subsidiary

The pan-African bank, led by Burkinabè Simon Tiemtoré, opens its first European branch with 50 million euros capital.

Source: Burkina24 · 2026-10-23 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Vista Bank, the pan-African banking group chaired by Burkinabè national Simon Tiemtoré, has officially launched Vista Bank France. This new entity, announced in Bangkok during International Monetary Fund and World Bank meetings, marks the group's first banking presence outside of Africa. The French subsidiary begins operations with an approved capital of 50 million euros.

Vista Bank France aims to facilitate financial exchanges between the group's African subsidiaries and international markets. Services offered will include foreign trade financing, corporate banking, correspondent banking, foreign exchange and treasury operations, factoring, and advisory services. The group intends to reduce transaction times and costs for its African clients.

Simon Tiemtoré stated that Vista Bank France expresses the vision of building a pan-African group that sustainably connects Africa to the world. The European expansion is part of Vista Bank's growth strategy, which has previously involved acquiring banking assets from international groups in Africa.

Why it mattersBurkinabè national Simon Tiemtoré's banking group has expanded its international reach by opening a new subsidiary in France.
