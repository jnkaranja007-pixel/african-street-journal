Burkina Faso employment agency guides more than 200 Ziniaré students

The first-year students learned about career assessments, job-search advice and internship placement through the agency’s university outreach.

Source: Sidwaya · 2026-11-20 · 1 min read

Burkina Faso’s employment agency, known as Faso Tonlo Zi, held a career guidance session on Thursday 12 November 2026 for more than 200 first-year students at the Centre universitaire de Ziniaré. The students are studying territorial planning and sustainable development.

The session introduced the agency’s skills assessment and support offices, which help students identify strengths, interests and skills to develop, according to Soumaïla Daboné, the agency’s regional director for Kadiogo. He said each assessment produces a professional project and an action plan. Services also include job-search advice, internship placement, financial education and guidance on business management and entrepreneurship.

The agency said the programme is intended to bring its services closer to universities and help students plan their careers before graduating. Teacher-researcher Médina Karambiri said the initiative addressed a need for support in presenting students’ skills beyond their academic knowledge.

Why it mattersStudents assessed by the agency receive an individual career plan and action steps, alongside advice on internships, job searches and entrepreneurship.
