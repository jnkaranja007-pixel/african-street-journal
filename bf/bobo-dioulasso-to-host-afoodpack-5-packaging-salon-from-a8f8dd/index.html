Bobo-Dioulasso to host AFoodPack 5 packaging salon from November 27

The fifth edition of the African agro-food packaging salon will focus on ecological packaging for cashew, mango, sesame and shea producers.

Source: Lefaso.net · 2026-11-17 · 2 min read

Bobo-Dioulasso will host the fifth edition of the African agro-food packaging salon, AFoodPack, from November 27 to 29, 2026, at the Maison de la Culture. The theme is « Ecological packaging: catalysts for innovation in the agro-food sectors of Guiriko », and the salon will look at packaging needs for cashew, mango, sesame and shea. A day called « 3S: Sya without Sachets » is planned for November 26.

The organising committee, led by its president Salimata Sori Coulibaly, presented the programme to the press on Monday, November 16, at RTB 2 Guiriko in Bobo-Dioulasso. She said packaging now affects preservation, health safety, traceability, transport and access to new markets. Organisers say the fifth edition moves from general packaging questions to the specific needs of each of the four sectors, with the aim of identifying concrete solutions.

The salon is under the patronage of the Minister of State for Agriculture, the Minister of Communication, Culture, Arts and Tourism, and the Minister of Industry, Commerce and Handicrafts. Roger Baro, special adviser to the president, and the mayor of Bobo-Dioulasso are guests of honour. The governor of the Guiriko region and the president of the regional chamber of commerce and industry are also associated with the event.

Why it mattersShea, cashew, mango and sesame processors in the region will see which packaging solutions are discussed for their sectors at the salon from November 27.
