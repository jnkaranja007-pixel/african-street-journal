Burkina Faso receives 200 tractors for farming

The tractors, with ploughs and harrows, will support agricultural volunteers and other producers.

Source: Lefaso.net · 2026-11-09 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

The National Bureau of Major Projects of Burkina (BN-GPB) handed over 200 tractors to the Ministry of Agriculture on Friday, November 6, 2026, in Ouagadougou. The initiative, part of the Presidential Initiative for Agricultural Production and Food Self-Sufficiency (IP-P3A), aims to boost farm mechanization and national production capacity. The tractors are 40 horsepower and include ploughs and harrows.

These 200 tractors are primarily designated for agricultural Volunteers for the Defense of the Fatherland (VDP) and will also be available to other producers across Burkina Faso. BN-GPB Executive Director Pr Hamidou Sawadogo urged beneficiaries to use the equipment responsibly and asked mechanization brigades to support the VDP. Minister of Agriculture, Water, and Animal and Fisheries Resources, Ismaël Sombié, assured the prompt deployment of the machinery.

Under Captain Ibrahim Traoré, Burkina Faso is implementing initiatives like the Presidential Initiative for Food Self-Sufficiency and the 2023-2028 Agro-Pastoral, Hydraulic, and Fisheries Offensive. The minister stated that these combined efforts helped Burkina Faso reach a cereal needs coverage rate of 124.6 in 2025, an unprecedented figure for the country, moving towards food security.

Why it mattersFarmers in Burkina Faso will benefit from new tractors to improve their work and increase cultivated areas.
