Two buses collide, killing six in Burkina Faso

A head-on collision between two passenger buses on the national road 1 near Ipendo killed six people.

Source: Lefaso.net · 2026-11-10 · 1 min read

A collision between two passenger buses on the national road 1 (RN1) in Burkina Faso resulted in six fatalities. The accident occurred in Ipendo, approximately seven kilometers from Sabou, during the night of Sunday, November 8, to Monday, November 9, 2026. One bus was traveling from Ouagadougou and the other from Bobo-Dioulasso when they crashed.

Six people died instantly in the crash, according to preliminary reports. Several other passengers sustained severe injuries and were rescued by firefighters from Koudougou. These injured individuals were then transported to health centers in Koudougou and Sabou for medical attention.

The accident took place on the RN1, a major transport artery. The specific cause of the collision was not detailed in the initial report.
