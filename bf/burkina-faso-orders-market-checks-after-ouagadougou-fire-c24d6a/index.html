Burkina Faso orders market checks after Ouagadougou fire

Security Minister Mahamadou Sana said the 19 November blaze was linked to insecticides that may have been poorly packaged, not explosives.

Sources: Lefaso.net and Sidwaya · 2026-11-23 · 1 min read

Burkina Faso’s Security Minister, Mahamadou Sana, ordered stronger field checks and public awareness campaigns after a fire at the commercial and administrative zone in Ouagadougou on 19 November. The fire was reported at about 19 hours and caused property damage, with no deaths recorded.

The ministry’s communications directorate said initial findings linked the blaze to insecticides that were improperly packaged, rather than explosives. Sana instructed officials to issue communication clips on safe handling and storage of dangerous goods, and said the Laabal Brigade would join checks in markets.

Sana called on residents and economic operators to follow rules for packaging, storing and transporting dangerous goods. The fire affected goods and other property, according to Sidwaya.

Why it mattersMarket sellers and transporters will face checks by the Laabal Brigade, while officials are to issue public guidance on handling dangerous goods.
