Burkina Faso, Turkey agree to boost education cooperation

Ankara talks between the two nations also covered increased scholarships for Burkinabe students.

Source: Sidwaya · 2026-10-24 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Burkina Faso and Turkey held political consultations in Ankara on October 20-21, 2026, to strengthen cooperation. The Burkinabe delegation, led by Ambassador Hermann Yirigouin TOE, met with Turkish officials from various state structures, including the Maarif Foundation, TIKA, the Council of Higher Education, and the Ministry of Health.

Discussions with the Maarif Foundation focused on signing an agreement for school establishment and management, as the foundation already operates schools in Ouagadougou and Bobo-Dioulasso. The foundation agreed to increase scholarships for Burkinabe students and provide support for vocational and technical training.

TIKA, the Turkish development agency, committed to supporting Burkina Faso in vocational and technical training in 2027 and is considering opening an office in Ouagadougou. The Council of Higher Education also agreed to renew an academic cooperation accord and increase Master's and Doctorate scholarships for Burkinabe students.

Why it mattersBurkinabe students will benefit from increased scholarships and expanded vocational training opportunities in Turkey.
