Germany reaffirms aid to Burkina Faso

German ambassador highlights shared values and cooperation in Ouagadougou on reunification anniversary.

Source: Burkina24 · 2026-11-08 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Germany celebrated the 36th anniversary of its reunification on Friday, November 6, 2026, at the German ambassador's residence in Ouagadougou. The event brought together political figures, partners, and friends of Germany to commemorate the historic occasion.

German Ambassador Dietrich Becker stated that reunification teaches essential lessons about peaceful coexistence and respect for international law, which are vital for peace, progress, and freedom. He also acknowledged Burkina Faso's efforts to strengthen its sovereignty and the cooperation within the Alliance of Sahel States (AES).

Ambassador Becker reiterated Germany's commitment to supporting Burkina Faso through the RELANCE program, focusing on water supply, agricultural processing, and sustainable industrialization. He also announced continued efforts in cultural and scientific exchanges, including training opportunities for young Burkinabè in Germany and expanding the Goethe-Institut's presence.

Why it mattersBurkina Faso will receive German support in water supply, agricultural processing, and industrialization through the RELANCE program.
