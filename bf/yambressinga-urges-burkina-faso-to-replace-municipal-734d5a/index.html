Yambressinga urges Burkina Faso to replace municipal special delegations with revolutio...

The governance specialist says the appointed bodies have put routine administration ahead of investment and local development in the communes.

Source: Lefaso.net · 2026-11-22 · 1 min read

Guilga François de Paule Yambressinga, a doctor of economic and social history and a specialist in territorial governance, has called for the special delegations running Burkina Faso's municipalities to be replaced by municipal revolutionary councils. In an opinion article published on Lefaso.net, he argues for reform of local government across the country.

Yambressinga says all Burkinabe communes have been administered by special delegations since 2022. He argues the bodies tend to favour the management of day-to-day affairs over structural investment and local development initiatives, and says their functioning does not match a decree dated 16 March 2026.

He also argues that the Popular Progressive Revolution should be reflected in how local authorities are governed. The article is an argument by an individual and reports no government decision on the proposal.
