Burkina Faso villagers repair Saye-Gourcy road

Residents of Saye in Zondoma province used shovels and wheelbarrows to fill potholes on a vital route.

Source: Agence d'Information du Burkina · 2026-11-09 · 1 min read

Residents of Saye village in Burkina Faso's Zondoma province, specifically women and the local citizen watch, worked on November 6, 2026, to manually repair the road connecting their village to Gourcy. This second phase of community work followed an initial effort on October 23, aiming to restore the road's usability for agricultural transport and access to Gourcy's social and health facilities.

Equipped with tools like hoes, shovels, and wheelbarrows, the women and men collected earth to fill potholes, leveled the road surface, and cleared the verges. Safiata Ouédraogo, the women's leader, stated the initiative provides relief, especially for emergency medical evacuations. Alassane Zallé, representing the citizen watch, noted it will improve year-round traffic and economic exchanges.

The village development councilor, Amadé Ouédraogo, praised the solidarity, saying it will improve travel safety and speed up rescue responses. This community action demonstrates Saye's commitment to improving their living conditions through self-effort.

Why it mattersVillagers in Saye, Zondoma province, repaired a road to improve access for agricultural products and emergency services.
