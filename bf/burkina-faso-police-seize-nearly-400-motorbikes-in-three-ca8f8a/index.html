Burkina Faso police seize nearly 400 motorbikes in three hours

Ouagadougou police confiscated motorbikes during a night operation targeting mandatory helmet use.

Source: Lefaso.net · 2026-10-26 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Burkina Faso's national police seized nearly 400 motorbikes in Ouagadougou during a three-hour night operation on Friday, October 23, 2026. The action targeted mandatory helmet use, with police confiscating vehicles from riders not wearing the required safety gear after dark.

The operation, led by Kadiogo regional police director Aguima Abdoulaziz Yonli, aimed to enforce road safety and protect riders, especially at night when helmet use reportedly declines. Police officials stated that the seizures highlight continued non-compliance with safety regulations despite awareness campaigns.

Director Yonli urged motorcyclists to make helmet use a permanent habit for their own safety and their families' well-being. The police initiative, named "Bouclier tête durcie" (Hardened Head Shield), aims to reinforce that wearing a helmet is essential for preserving lives on the road.

Why it mattersMotorcyclists in Ouagadougou face motorbike seizures if they do not wear helmets.
