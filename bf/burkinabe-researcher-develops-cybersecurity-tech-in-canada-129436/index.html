Burkinabè researcher develops cybersecurity tech in Canada

Dr Karim Ganamé, a Burkinabè computer scientist, is creating advanced cybersecurity solutions from Montreal, Canada.

Source: Lefaso.net · 2026-10-24 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Dr Karim Ganamé, a Burkinabè researcher based in Montreal, Canada, is developing advanced cybersecurity technologies. He works on detecting threats before they impact systems and also helps limit damage after attacks. Ganamé's work aims to protect sensitive digital systems from cyberattacks like ransomware.

Ganamé, who began his IT career at Burkina Faso's Office National des Télécommunications in 1999, holds a doctorate in computer security from the University of Franche-Comté in France. His expertise combines scientific research with entrepreneurship to create protective digital solutions.

His journey includes studies in communication networks and computer networks in France, followed by a master's in management and entrepreneurship. Ganamé's company develops solutions for environments where data and infrastructure protection are critical.

Why it mattersBurkinabè citizens are affected by the development of advanced cybersecurity technologies created by a fellow national working abroad.
