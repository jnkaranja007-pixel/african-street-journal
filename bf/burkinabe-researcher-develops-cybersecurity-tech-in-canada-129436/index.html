Burkinabè researcher develops cybersecurity tech in Canada

Dr Karim Ganamé, a Burkinabè scientist based in Montreal, Canada, is creating advanced cybersecurity solutions.

Source: Lefaso.net · 2026-10-23 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Dr Karim Ganamé, a Burkinabè researcher, is developing advanced cybersecurity technologies from his base in Montreal, Canada. He holds a doctorate in computer security and founded a company that creates solutions for sensitive data protection environments. Ganamé's work aims to detect threats before they cause damage, addressing the growing risk of cyberattacks on organizations.

His career began in Burkina Faso in 1999 with an analyst-programmer qualification from the ESI. Ganamé then pursued higher education in France, earning degrees in communication systems, computer networks, and management from institutions including INSA Lyon and the University of Clermont-Ferrand. He completed his cybersecurity doctorate at the University of Franche-Comté in 2007.

Ganamé's expertise combines scientific research with entrepreneurial ventures, focusing on protecting digital systems from threats like ransomware. His company's solutions are designed for environments where data and infrastructure security are critical, helping organizations avoid disruptions and data loss.

Why it mattersBurkinabè citizens living abroad are contributing to technological advancements that protect digital systems.
