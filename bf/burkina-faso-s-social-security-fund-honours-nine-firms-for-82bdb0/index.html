Burkina Faso’s social security fund honours nine firms for workplace safety

The first national occupational health and safety prize recognised companies in mining, construction, industry and services at Kaya’s three-day event.

Source: Burkina24 · 2026-11-28 · 1 min read

Burkina Faso’s National Social Security Fund (CNSS) honoured nine companies with its first National Occupational Health and Safety Prize in Kaya on Friday, 27 November. The awards closed the 16th edition of the fund’s three-day workplace risk-prevention event, which began on 25 November.

CNSS Director General Herman Yacouba Nacambo said the companies were selected after an evaluation and recognised across mining, construction and public works, industry, and services. More than 150 participants from different regions of Burkina Faso attended discussions on working conditions and quality of life at work.

Nacambo said the prize was created because workplace safety practices at some companies were little known and rarely shared. The event also produced recommendations on handling workplace conflict, training managers and expanding workplace health committees’ remit.

Why it mattersThe nine recognised companies receive public recognition through a new prize intended to make their workplace safety practices better known and shared.
