Three Burkina Faso communes involve residents in budget oversight

Citizen committees track local investments as simplified budgets help residents follow planned revenue and spending.

Source: Lefaso.net · 2026-11-25 · 1 min read

Residents in Korsimoro, Ziniaré and Nagréongo, in Burkina Faso’s Oubri region, now help shape municipal budgets and monitor public investments, with support from the Centre for Applied Studies and Research in Public Finance (CERA-FP). The budgets in the three communes rose from 2022 to 2026, Lefaso.net reported.

Residents can submit concerns during budget preparation, while local citizen dialogue and monitoring committees follow investments in health, education, water and sanitation. CERA-FP helps the three special delegations prepare simplified budgets detailing expected revenue and planned investments. Korsimoro’s first vice-president, Naba Sanem, said on 16 November that the support had helped investments for residents move forward.

About 10 media outlets visited the communes from 15 to 19 November. The work is part of a project supported by Denmark’s foreign ministry and Oxfam in Burkina Faso.

Why it mattersResidents can raise budget concerns and track planned spending on health, education, water and sanitation in their communes.
