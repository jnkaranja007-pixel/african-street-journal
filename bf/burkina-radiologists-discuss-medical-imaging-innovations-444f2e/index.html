Burkina radiologists discuss medical imaging innovations

Professionals met in Ouagadougou on November 6-7, 2026, to explore technological advancements for patient care and radiation safety.

Source: Agence d'Information du Burkina · 2026-11-10 · 1 min read

Radiology professionals in Burkina Faso met in Ouagadougou on November 6 and 7, 2026, for scientific days focused on technological innovations. The Association of Professionals of Radiological Sciences and Medical Imaging of Burkina Faso (APSTRIM/BF) organized the event to discuss improving medical diagnosis, patient management, and controlling ionizing radiation doses.

Issaka Kaboré, vice-president of APSTRIM/BF, stated that medical imaging development relies on technological innovation, equipment quality, and radioprotection. He urged professionals to adopt new solutions for better diagnostics and patient care, emphasizing that modern equipment requires regular maintenance and control for reliable results. Kaboré also highlighted the health risks of ionizing radiation, calling for adherence to justification and optimization principles.

APSTRIM/BF President Alassane Guiro noted that the scientific days provided a platform to discuss challenges related to ionizing radiation and safety mechanisms for professionals. Discussions included artificial intelligence in radiology, dosimetry innovation, remote work, imaging's role in treating terrorism victims, and radiobiology, presented through three international scientific conferences and nine scientific communications.

Why it mattersBurkina Faso's health sector professionals are exploring new technologies to enhance patient diagnosis and safety in medical imaging procedures.
