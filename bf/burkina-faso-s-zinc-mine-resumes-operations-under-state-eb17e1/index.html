Burkina Faso’s zinc mine resumes operations under state subsidiary

The mine had been shut since 2022 floods that killed eight people; the transition president is due to attend its restart.

Source: RFI Afrique · 2026-11-27 · 1 min read

A zinc mine in Burkina Faso is scheduled to resume operations on Friday, 27 November, after being closed since deadly floods in 2022. The mine will now be operated by a subsidiary of the country’s mining participation company.

The floods killed eight people, according to RFI Afrique. The restart is due to take place in the presence of Burkina Faso’s transition president, the report said.

The report identifies the new operator as a subsidiary of the state mining participation company, but gives no production target or other operating details.

Why it mattersThe mine’s new state-linked operator is due to restart operations on 27 November, after the site’s closure following floods that killed eight people.
