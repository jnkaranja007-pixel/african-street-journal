Burkina Faso and Türkiye discuss expanding partnership

The Turkish ambassador highlighted cooperation in health, defense, and education during a celebration in Ouagadougou.

Source: Lefaso.net · 2026-11-04 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Burkina Faso and Türkiye are strengthening their bilateral ties, with Ambassador Feriba Hokkaci Esirgen celebrating the 103rd anniversary of the Turkish Republic in Ouagadougou on November 2, 2026. The ambassador noted advancements in areas including political consultations, kidney transplant operations, defense, education, and investments, expressing a desire for further consolidation of this cooperation.

Ambassador Esirgen praised Burkina Faso's efforts towards economic and social sovereignty, including initiatives for energy independence and value addition. She affirmed Türkiye's commitment to supporting Burkina Faso's development and its fight against terrorism, aligning with shared goals for peace and prosperity.

The first political consultations between the two nations took place in Ankara on October 20, 2026. In the health sector, three kidney transplant operations have been successfully completed through collaboration between medical professionals from both countries, with a fourth mission planned before the end of 2026.

Why it mattersBurkina Faso's citizens will benefit from expanded cooperation in health services and potentially economic development initiatives.
