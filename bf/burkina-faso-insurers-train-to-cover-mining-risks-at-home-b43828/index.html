Burkina Faso insurers train to cover mining risks at home

Faso Réassurances, AVENI-RE and the insurers’ association are reviewing mining policies as many risks remain insured outside the national market.

Sources: Lefaso.net and Burkina24 · 2026-11-26 · 1 min read

Faso Réassurances, Burkina Faso’s state-created reinsurer, opened a three-day training seminar on mining insurance in Ouagadougou on Wednesday, 25 November. The programme, organised with AVENI-RE and the Association of Insurance Companies of Burkina Faso, runs through 27 November and focuses on reviewing and improving policies for the mining industry.

Insurance Director Djénéba Dao said a large share of mining risks is covered outside Burkina Faso, sending premiums abroad and reducing value retained in the economy. She said local insurers need the expertise and financial capacity to meet commitments to policyholders, and urged them to price risks on a technical basis and manage their exposure carefully.

Faso Réassurances chief executive Drissa Traoré said the state-created reinsurer had a duty to support the mining sector by covering its risks. Participants are due to study ways to cover mining activities and strengthen the national insurance market’s expertise.

Why it mattersBurkina Faso’s insurers must strengthen their expertise and financial capacity to meet policy commitments as the training runs through 27 November.
