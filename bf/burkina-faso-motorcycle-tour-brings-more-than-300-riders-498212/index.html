Burkina Faso motorcycle tour brings more than 300 riders across eight regions

The second edition ended in Ouagadougou on Saturday, 21 November 2026, after riders covered more than 1 800 kilometres in eight days.

Source: Burkina24 · 2026-11-23 · 1 min read

More than 300 riders from 11 delegations completed the second Tour Motard du Burkina on Saturday, 21 November 2026, arriving in Ouagadougou after eight days and more than 1 800 kilometres across eight regions. The organisers said they were satisfied with the event despite difficulties along the route.

Organising committee president Mahamadi Sawadogo said riders learned to handle gravel, sand and potholes. Three accidents involved six riders; the resulting injuries were not considered particularly worrying. Sawadogo also praised women who completed the tour.

West Coast Adventure club president Adama Ouédraogo said lessons from the edition would inform improvements to future tours. Organisers want to attract more participants from abroad and make them ambassadors for Burkina Faso.

Why it mattersParticipants from 11 delegations now have lessons for future tours, while organisers plan to seek more riders from abroad.
