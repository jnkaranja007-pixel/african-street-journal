Maroc Telecom finds outdated PHP on 66 sites in cyber scan

The operator watches its subsidiaries through central security and incident-response teams, with artificial-intelligence agents filtering out false alarms.

Source: TelQuel · 2026-11-21 · 1 min read

Maroc Telecom's group-wide scan found 66 sites running outdated PHP, a web programming language, according to TelQuel's account of the company's cyber-security response. The sites are exposed to PHP2Shell flaws rated 9.2 out of 10.

The operator monitors its subsidiaries from a central security operations centre and a central computer emergency response team, the group's main cyber-defence units. Artificial-intelligence agents form the first layer of screening, filtering false positives before the security teams act.

The report gives no timetable for updating the 66 sites and does not say whether any had been compromised.
