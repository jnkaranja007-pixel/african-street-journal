Morocco coach Ouahbi names squad on November 4

Mohamed Ouahbi will announce the list of players for the 2027 Africa Cup of Nations qualifiers against Niger.

Source: Hespress English · 2026-10-31 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Morocco national team coach Mohamed Ouahbi will announce the squad list for upcoming matches on Wednesday, November 4. The announcement will take place at an 11:00 AM press conference at the Mohammed VI Football Complex.

The selected players will participate in a training camp starting November 9, preparing for two matches against Niger. These games are part of the 2027 Africa Cup of Nations qualifiers.

Morocco will host Niger on November 13 at the Mohammed V Complex in Casablanca. The second match between the two teams is scheduled for November 17 at the Prince Moulay Abdellah Stadium in Rabat.

Why it mattersMoroccan football fans will be interested in the selection of players for the national team's upcoming matches.
