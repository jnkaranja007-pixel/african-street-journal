Morocco faces European auto investment challenge

European countries aim to rebuild domestic car production, potentially diverting projects from Morocco.

Source: Hespress English · 2026-11-11 · 1 min read

Morocco faces increased competition for automotive investment as European nations work to rebuild their own car manufacturing industries, potentially redirecting projects previously planned for the Kingdom, according to Industry and Trade Minister Ryad Mezzour. He stated that Europe's industrial policy shift could encourage manufacturers to move production closer to home, weakening a key factor that made Morocco attractive: lower production costs.

Mezzour highlighted discussions around the European Parliament's proposed Industrial Acceleration Act, which targets having 80% of car production within Europe. This development, along with Europe's transition to electric vehicles, could affect Morocco's ability to attract industrial investment. He noted that discussions on these European policy changes began about 18 months ago, coinciding with the redirection of several expected investments away from Morocco.

The minister urged Morocco to prepare for these changes immediately by accelerating the adoption of robotics, industrial automation, and artificial intelligence. Strengthening innovation and expanding into more international markets, as well as supporting Moroccan brands, are also crucial steps. Mezzour described the shift as a major test for the national economy, requiring a coordinated effort from economic and political stakeholders.

Why it mattersMorocco's automotive sector, a significant source of investment and jobs, faces potential disruption as European countries prioritize domestic manufacturing.
