Crédit du Maroc reports 19,1 % rise in consolidated net profit

The Moroccan bank’s consolidated banking net income increased 8,3 % to 2 900 MDH in the nine months to September.

Sources: Les Inspirations ECO and TelQuel · 2026-11-28 · 1 min read

Crédit du Maroc reported a 19,1 % increase in consolidated net profit for the nine months ended 30 September 2026, the bank said after its supervisory board met on 26 November. Its outstanding loans rose 12 % over 12 months to 65 021 MDH at end-September.

Business lending increased 6,2 % to 52 972 MDH, with equipment loans up 30,5 %, leasing up 28,4 % and property-development loans up 12,8 %. Household lending grew 4 % to 22 889 MDH, including an 11,4 % increase in consumer loans and a 2,2 % rise in housing loans.

Consolidated banking net income rose 8,3 % to 2 900 MDH, as interest margin and commissions offset weaker market operations. Net interest margin increased 11,4 % to 2 220 MDH, while commission income rose 3,5 % to 392 MDH.

Why it mattersThe bank’s borrowers are part of a loan book that expanded 12 % over 12 months to 65 021 MDH at end-September.
