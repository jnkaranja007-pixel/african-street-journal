Morocco’s unions demand higher wages and pensions from El Mansouri

The three most representative union confederations raised pay and retirement benefits at meetings chaired by the head of government in Rabat.

Sources: TelQuel and Maroc Diplomatique · 2026-11-26 · 1 min read

Morocco’s three most representative union confederations asked for higher wages and retirement pensions at meetings with head of government Fatima Ezzahra El Mansouri in Rabat on Tuesday, 24 November. The meetings were consultations chaired by El Mansouri.

The unions were the Moroccan Labour Union (UMT), the General Union of Workers of Morocco (UGTM) and the Democratic Confederation of Labour (CDT), Maroc Diplomatique reported. The UGTM and CDT also called on the government to honour commitments made in earlier social agreements.

The meetings were held with the employment and dialogue minister present, according to Maroc Diplomatique. The reports give no amount for the requested increases.

Why it mattersThe government must address union demands for higher wages and pensions and calls by the UGTM and CDT to honour earlier social agreements.
