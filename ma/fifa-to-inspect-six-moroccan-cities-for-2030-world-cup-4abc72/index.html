FIFA to inspect six Moroccan cities for 2030 World Cup preparations

The delegation will assess sports infrastructure, transport networks and accommodation capacity as Morocco prepares to co-host the tournament with Spain and Portugal.

Sources: Maroc Diplomatique and TelQuel · 2026-11-22 · 1 min read

A FIFA delegation is due to begin inspecting six Moroccan cities on 28 November as part of preparations for the 2030 World Cup, which Morocco will co-host with Spain and Portugal. The tour will cover Rabat, Casablanca, Tangier, Marrakech, Agadir and Fez.

The international football federation’s delegation will assess the progress of sports infrastructure, transport networks and accommodation capacity. A separate report says its technical team will examine stadiums, transport and hotel capacity in the six cities being considered to host matches.

Morocco plans to propose the future Grand Stade Hassan II as the venue for the final. FIFA’s inspection tour in Morocco is scheduled to follow visits to Portugal and Spain.

Why it mattersThe six cities being considered for matches must show FIFA the state of their stadium, transport and hotel preparations from 28 November.
