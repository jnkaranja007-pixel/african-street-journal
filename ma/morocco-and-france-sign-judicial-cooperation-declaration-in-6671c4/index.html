Morocco and France sign judicial cooperation declaration in Rabat

Hicham Balaoui, Morocco's public prosecution chief, and French Justice Minister Gérald Darmanin signed the declaration during Darmanin's working visit.

Sources: TelQuel and Maroc Diplomatique · 2026-11-20 · 1 min read

Morocco's public prosecution and France's Justice Ministry signed a declaration of intent on legal and judicial cooperation in Rabat on Thursday, 19 November. Hicham Balaoui, procurator general at the Court of Cassation and head of Morocco's public prosecution, signed for Morocco, and French Justice Minister Gérald Darmanin was in Morocco on a working visit.

The meeting took place in the presence of the French ambassador to Morocco, according to TelQuel. Maroc Diplomatique reported that the declaration covers cooperation in legal and judicial matters.

TelQuel reported that the two officials also discussed cross-border crime. The reports gave no further terms or implementation date for the declaration.

Why it mattersThe declaration gives Morocco's prosecutors and France's Justice Ministry a formal framework for legal and judicial cooperation.
