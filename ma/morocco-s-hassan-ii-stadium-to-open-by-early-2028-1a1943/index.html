Morocco's Hassan II Stadium to open by early 2028

Economy Minister Fouzi Lekjaa stated construction is progressing well for the stadium set to host major clubs and events.

Source: Hespress English · 2026-10-29 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Morocco's Hassan II Stadium in Benslimane is scheduled for completion by early 2028, according to Economy and Finance Minister Fouzi Lekjaa. Construction is progressing well on the facility, which is expected to significantly influence urban development in Greater Casablanca. The stadium aims to be ready for use by the national team and Casablanca clubs Raja and Wydad before the 2030 World Cup.

Minister Lekjaa, who also heads the Royal Moroccan Football Federation, indicated the stadium could host other events prior to the 2030 tournament. He noted that FIFA regulations require the World Cup final to be held at the largest capacity stadium, and Hassan II Stadium currently holds that distinction. Lekjaa emphasized the 2030 World Cup's vision extends beyond football to highlight shared Mediterranean history and promote intercultural dialogue.

The stadium's completion by early 2028 positions it to be a key venue for Morocco's preparations for the 2030 World Cup. The tournament, which marks 100 years since the first World Cup, is a joint bid involving Morocco, Portugal, and Spain. Minister Lekjaa highlighted the goal of creating opportunities for young people and promoting dialogue between civilizations.

Why it mattersMoroccan football clubs Raja and Wydad, along with the national team, will have a new venue for matches before the 2030 World Cup.
