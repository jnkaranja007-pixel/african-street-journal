Morocco's lower house passes 2027 Finance Bill 179 to 126

The bill now goes to the House of Councillors, with MAD 44.2 billion set aside for social protection in 2027.

Morocco's House of Representatives adopted the 2027 Finance Bill on Saturday by majority vote, with 179 in favour and 126 against and no abstentions. The bill now moves to the House of Councillors for examination and a vote.

The bill is built around five priorities: restoring purchasing power and strengthening the social state, promoting youth integration into education and employment, accelerating advanced regionalisation, reinforcing strategic sovereignty in water, energy, food and digital sectors, and keeping macroeconomic and fiscal stability. During the committee stage, 291 amendments were submitted and 59 received government approval.

Key measures include MAD 210 billion over eight years for territorial development programmes and a target of 52% renewable energy in installed electricity capacity by 2030. Dam storage capacity is to exceed 24 billion cubic metres by the end of 2027.

Why it mattersHouseholds receiving social protection will draw on MAD 44.2 billion in 2027, and the bill's 52% renewable energy target for 2030 sets the path for power generation.

US military to test pilotless medical aircraft in Morocco

The US Africa Command will trial an autonomous aircraft that can fly to remote areas and evacuate wounded people without a pilot on board.

The U.S. military plans to test an autonomous aircraft designed to evacuate wounded people from remote areas, with no pilot on board and no continuous link to a ground control station. U.S. Africa Command (AFRICOM) awarded a $500,000 contract to Merlin, an American aviation technology company, to run the demonstration during the 2027 U.S. fiscal year.

The trial will use a Cessna 208B Grand Caravan fitted with Merlin's autonomous flight technology, known as Merlin Pilot. The aircraft will plan its own route, fly to a simulated injury site, load a stretcher, and return to a medical facility for an automated landing. Merlin says the system can adjust its route when it detects obstacles or threats, and can communicate with air traffic controllers using natural language processing.

One of AFRICOM's main objectives is to assess whether the aircraft can keep operating safely if communication with ground stations is lost. Merlin will also install an air ambulance unit with oxygen supplies, power sources and mounts for patient-monitoring equipment. Merlin founder and CEO Matt George said medical evacuations often require getting injured people to surgical facilities as quickly as possible.

Why it mattersMerlin, the company receiving the $500,000 AFRICOM contract, will carry out the demonstration using its own Merlin Pilot system on a Cessna 208B Grand Caravan.

Sport · 2026-11-16

Morocco beats Niger 5-1 in AFCON qualifiers

Achraf Hakimi scored twice as the Atlas Lions secured a dominant victory in Casablanca.

Morocco defeated Niger 5-1 in a Group A matchday 3 game of the Africa Cup of Nations (AFCON 2027) qualifiers. The match took place Friday evening at the Mohammed V Sports Complex in Casablanca.

The Atlas Lions' goals were scored by Yassir Zabiri in the 4th minute, Achraf Hakimi in the 27th and 45th minutes, Youssef En-Nesyri in the 77th minute, and Ayoub Oufkir in the 90th minute.

This victory places Morocco in a strong position in their group as the qualifiers continue.

Why it mattersMoroccan football fans will follow the team's progress in the Africa Cup of Nations qualifiers.

Rabat hosts ECOWAS women cybersecurity week

The initiative aims to promote African women's leadership and skills in cybersecurity.

Rabat is hosting the ECOWAS Women in Cybersecurity week from November 16-20, a regional initiative organized by the General Directorate for Information Systems Security (DGSSI), Smart Africa, and the World Bank. The event aims to promote African women's leadership and strengthen their skills in cybersecurity.

Brigadier General Abdellah Boutrig, Vice President of the African Network of Cybersecurity Authorities (ANCA) and Director General of the DGSSI, will open the five-day event. The program includes conferences, technical sessions, simulations, and discussions on critical infrastructure protection, cybercrime, and artificial intelligence.

The week will conclude with the launch of the ECOWAS Women in Cybersecurity network, featuring mentoring programs and a knowledge-sharing platform. This network seeks to foster female leaders who can enhance the continent's digital resilience and influence cybersecurity policies.

Why it mattersThe cybersecurity week in Rabat will focus on developing skills and leadership for women in the field, with a launch of a new network to foster digital resilience.

Politics · 2026-11-16

Morocco opens cultural pavilion at Francophonie Village in Phnom Penh

The Moroccan space invites visitors to explore its heritage during the 20th Francophonie Summit in Cambodia.

Morocco's national pavilion at the Village de la Francophonie in Phnom Penh is inviting Cambodian visitors and summit participants to discover the Kingdom's heritage, historical traditions, cultural expressions and craftsmanship.

The pavilion is designed as a meeting place for exchange in a friendly setting, and it presents Morocco's cultural identity and the variety of its influences among the national pavilions on display.

Head of Government Fatima Ezzahra El Mansouri arrived in Phnom Penh on Saturday, 14 November, to represent King Mohammed VI at the 20th Francophonie Summit, which runs from 15 to 16 November.
