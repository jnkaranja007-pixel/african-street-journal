Politics · 2026-10-31

Morocco envoy calls Algeria's Sahara-Palestine comparison shameful

Omar Hilale accused Algiers of exploiting humanitarian arguments and restricting refugee rights in Tindouf camps.

Morocco's UN envoy Omar Hilale on Friday called Algeria's comparison of the Sahara dispute with the Palestinian cause "shameful" at the UN Security Council. Hilale accused Algiers of exploiting humanitarian arguments while restricting the rights of refugees in the Tindouf camps. He challenged Algeria's ambassador, questioning why Algiers intervened as an observer and not a directly concerned party.

Hilale defended Morocco's humanitarian engagement, asking who sent assistance and worked toward a peaceful solution, contrasting it with political speeches. He focused on the Tindouf camps, accusing Algeria of refusing to register residents despite UN requests, which he stated deprived them of protection. Hilale alleged Algeria transferred authority to the Polisario Front, restricted movement, and allowed children in military exercises.

The Moroccan diplomat called for residents to be allowed to return voluntarily to Morocco or travel elsewhere. He accused Algeria of making camp residents "hostages to an Algerian political agenda" and an armed separatist group linked to terrorism. This exchange followed Russia's veto of a US-drafted Sahara resolution that would have altered MINURSO's mandate.

Why it mattersMoroccan citizens and residents of the Tindouf camps are directly affected by the political dispute and accusations of human rights violations.

Morocco coach Ouahbi names squad on November 4

Mohamed Ouahbi will announce the list of players for the 2027 Africa Cup of Nations qualifiers against Niger.

Morocco national team coach Mohamed Ouahbi will announce the squad list for upcoming matches on Wednesday, November 4. The announcement will take place at an 11:00 AM press conference at the Mohammed VI Football Complex.

The selected players will participate in a training camp starting November 9, preparing for two matches against Niger. These games are part of the 2027 Africa Cup of Nations qualifiers.

Morocco will host Niger on November 13 at the Mohammed V Complex in Casablanca. The second match between the two teams is scheduled for November 17 at the Prince Moulay Abdellah Stadium in Rabat.

Why it mattersMoroccan football fans will be interested in the selection of players for the national team's upcoming matches.

Health · 2026-10-31

Morocco allocates 45.4 billion dirhams to health in 2027 budget

The 2027 health budget includes investments in medicines and staffing to improve healthcare access.

Morocco's government has allocated 45.4 billion dirhams to the health sector for the 2027 fiscal year. This significant budget confirms the state's substantial financial commitment to healthcare services.

The allocated funds will cover various areas, including investments in medicines, staffing, and the autonomy of management structures for health facilities. The aim is to ensure these resources translate into accessible healthcare for citizens.

The Ministry of Health and Social Protection is responsible for implementing the health provisions outlined in the 2027 finance bill. The budget's success will be measured by the actual improvement in healthcare accessibility and quality for the population.

Why it mattersMoroccan citizens will experience changes in healthcare access and the availability of medicines due to the new health budget.

Business · 2026-10-31

Managem's weight in MASI index falls

The mining company's weighting in Morocco's stock market index dropped from 10.5% to 6.9% after annual adjustments.

Managem's influence on the MASI stock market index has decreased significantly. The company's weighting fell from 10.5% to approximately 6.9% following the annual review of free-float factors. This substantial adjustment took effect on October 30.

Market sources indicated that this change could prompt some investors to reduce their holdings in Managem. The company's stock has been suspended from trading since October 30, adding to the uncertainty surrounding its market position.

The MASI index tracks the performance of the largest publicly traded companies in Morocco. Managem is a prominent mining company whose share performance directly impacts the overall market.

Why it mattersInvestors in Morocco's stock market may need to adjust their portfolios due to the reduced weighting of Managem in the MASI index.

Education · 2026-10-31

Moroccan professor reinvents teaching with AI

Safaa Makati, a professor at ISGA, is adapting her methods to integrate generative artificial intelligence into her classroom.

Generative artificial intelligence is changing university practices and forcing teachers to rethink their methods. Safaa Makati, a professor at ISGA in Morocco, has changed her teaching approach, moving away from traditional assessments. She found that AI tools did not eliminate her classroom but instead compelled her to innovate.

Makati explained that AI does not replace the professor but rather highlights the instructor's role more than ever. She is exploring new pedagogical possibilities while addressing concerns about the loss of meaning in evaluations. Her experience shows a shift in how educators are engaging with new technologies.

The professor's approach involves a continuous evolution of her teaching style in response to AI. This adaptation aims to maintain the relevance and effectiveness of academic evaluations in an increasingly digital environment.

Why it mattersMoroccan university students and professors are directly affected by changes in teaching methods due to artificial intelligence.
