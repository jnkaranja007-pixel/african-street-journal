Moroccan finance committee records 20.76% absence during 2027 budget talks

Of 287 amendments proposed to the 2027 Finance Bill, the government accepted only 59 and rejected 116 after eight working days of review.

Morocco's Finance and Economic Development Committee recorded a 20.76% absence rate during its meetings on the 2027 Finance Bill, with overall attendance at 70.24%. Only 9% of the absences were excused.

The committee held 11 meetings over eight working days, spending 55 hours and 25 minutes reviewing the bill. The government received 287 amendment proposals to the first part of Draft Finance Bill No. 60.26, accepting 59, rejecting 116, and seeing sponsors withdraw another 112.

The majority groups of PAM, Istiqlal, PPS and USFP had the most amendments accepted, with the government approving 33 of their 42 proposals. The PJD submitted 82 amendments but only four were accepted, while the Left Alliance's 67 yielded one. In the final vote, 23 members voted for the bill, 17 against and none abstained.

Why it mattersOnly 59 of 287 proposed amendments were accepted, so changes sought by opposition groups such as the PJD and the Left Alliance largely did not reach the bill.

Princess Lalla Oumkeltoum visits new Kénitra psychiatric hospital

Her foundation and the health ministry signed a partnership to expand mental health care under the 2030 plan.

Princess Lalla Oumkeltoum, who chairs the Lalla Oumkeltoum Foundation for Mental Health, visited the new psychiatric hospital in Kénitra on Friday, 13 November. She also presided over the signing of a partnership agreement between the Ministry of Health and her foundation.

The agreement forms part of the Mental Health Plan 2030, launched by the health ministry, and of the foundation's Strategic Action Plan. Officials described it as a framework to promote mental health care across Morocco.

The agreement was signed on the same day, according to North Africa Post, which reported that the framework is meant to promote mental health care in Morocco.

Why it mattersPatients and families in Morocco gain a framework for mental health care, with the foundation working alongside the health ministry under the 2030 plan.

Morocco sends Head of Government to 20th Francophonie Summit

Fatima Ezzahra El Mansouri arrived in Phnom Penh on Saturday to represent King Mohammed VI at the two-day summit.

Morocco's King Mohammed VI is represented by Head of Government Fatima Ezzahra El Mansouri at the 20th Francophonie Summit in Phnom Penh, Cambodia. Mrs. El Mansouri arrived in the Cambodian capital on Saturday, and the summit runs from November 15 to 16.

The summit, themed "Reinvesting in Peace for Shared Prosperity and Lasting Stability," gathers leaders from the 90 member states and governments of the International Organization of La Francophonie (OIF). Leaders will set the organisation's strategic priorities and discuss Francophone cooperation.

The summit is also due to elect the next Secretary-General for a four-year term covering 2027 to 2030. Heads of state and government were expected to adopt the Phnom Penh Declaration and decide the venue for the 21st summit, scheduled for 2028.

Why it mattersMorocco's position in the choice of the next Secretary-General and the planning of the 2028 summit will be shaped by the decisions taken in Phnom Penh.

Hakimi stars as Morocco beats Niger 5-1 in AFCON qualifiers

Spanish daily AS names Achraf Hakimi the world's best fullback after his two goals and assist in the victory.

Moroccan international Achraf Hakimi has been praised by Spanish sports daily AS as the world's best fullback. The Paris Saint-Germain defender scored two goals and provided an assist in Morocco's 5-1 victory over Niger on Friday night in Africa Cup of Nations qualifiers. The Atlas Lions secured their win at the Mohammed V Sports Complex in Casablanca.

AS highlighted Hakimi's decisive influence, stating "Achraf is the one calling the shots" and that "Morocco has been dancing to his tune." The publication described his first goal as a "powerful shot from outside the box" and his second as a free kick taken "to perfection." The win solidified Morocco's first-place position in Group A with nine points, putting them on track for AFCON qualification.

The match also marked the 100th appearance for national team goalkeeper Yassine Bounou. Other Moroccan goals were scored by Yassir Zabiri, Youssef En-Nesyri, and Ayoub Oufkir. The AS article also noted the offensive play of Brahim Díaz, Abde, and Zabiri, comparing their style to La Liga teams.

Why it mattersMoroccan players and the national team are on track to qualify for the next Africa Cup of Nations after a decisive 5-1 victory.

Sport · 2026-11-15

Morocco learns its beach soccer schedule for the CAN in Senegal

The Confederation of African Football released the official calendar for the 2026 beach soccer Cup of Nations, which runs from November 28 to December 6 in Saly, Senegal.

The Confederation of African Football (CAF) unveiled the official schedule for the 2026 beach soccer Cup of Nations on Friday. The tournament runs from November 28 to December 6 in Saly, Senegal, with eight national teams and 18 matches, including Morocco.

Morocco is in Group B with Mauritania, Malawi and Angola. Its opening match is against Malawi on Sunday, November 29, at 16:30 GMT. The competition carries the continental title and two places at the 2027 FIFA Beach Soccer World Cup.

Host nation and defending champions Senegal open against Ghana on November 28 at 16:30 GMT, after Mozambique meets Uganda at 15:00 GMT in the opening match.

Why it mattersMoroccan fans abroad who want to watch the team's first match against Malawi need the Sunday, November 29 kickoff at 16:30 GMT.
