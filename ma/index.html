Health · 2026-11-08

Morocco reports over 400 leishmaniasis cases in Zagora

Civil society groups call for urgent medical intervention as children are among the infected.

Over 400 cases of cutaneous leishmaniasis have been reported in Morocco’s Zagora province, with infections also noted in El Jorf, Errachidia province. The outbreak has particularly affected Fezouata and Tamekroute in Zagora, and El Jorf in Errachidia. Leishmaniasis is transmitted by infected sand flies, causing skin sores and ulcers that can lead to permanent scarring.

The Friends of the Environment association warned that children are among those infected and that a shortage of doctors could worsen the situation. Jamal Akchbab, head of the association, stated that the region experienced a similar outbreak in the mid-2000s which was contained by coordinated efforts. He linked the current spread to pools and lakes formed after heavy summer rainfall, and noted that some residents sleep on rooftops.

Civil society groups are calling for an emergency response plan including medical teams to diagnose and treat patients, and field campaigns to control rodent and sand fly populations. Younes Kadiri, of the Moroccan Association for Human Rights, held local authorities responsible for allowing the disease to spread and called on the Ministry of Health to intervene urgently.

Why it mattersOver 400 people in Morocco's Zagora and Errachidia provinces are affected by leishmaniasis, a disease that causes skin sores and can leave permanent scars.

Morocco launches 2026-2027 farm season targeting higher output

Minister Khalid Hatimi announced plans to boost production, create jobs, and strengthen food security during the season's launch in Béni Mellal-Khénifra.

Morocco officially began its 2026-2027 agricultural season on Saturday in the Béni Mellal-Khénifra region. Agriculture Minister Khalid Hatimi announced plans to increase production, generate jobs, and enhance national food security during the launch event.

The season's kickoff included a visit to agricultural projects in Béni Mellal and Fquih Ben Salah provinces. Hatimi stated that the new season aims for higher productivity and added value for farmers and consumers, while also creating sustainable employment opportunities. He acknowledged that climate change and rising input costs necessitate modernized approaches to sector management.

The ministry has implemented measures to secure agricultural inputs, develop production chains, and modernize irrigation. The delegation visited a women's cooperative processing spices and cereals, and a center for distributing certified seeds to farmers.

Why it mattersMoroccan farmers will benefit from measures to secure inputs, access certified seeds, and modernize irrigation systems for the new agricultural season.

Sport · 2026-11-08

Morocco futsal team routs Solomon Islands to reach Youth Olympics semifinals

Hamza El Ahmadi scored five goals as Morocco secured a 15-1 victory in Dakar on Saturday.

Morocco's men's futsal team thrashed the Solomon Islands 15-1 on Saturday in Dakar to qualify for the semifinals of the 2026 Youth Olympic Games. Hamza El Ahmadi led the scoring with five goals, supported by Mohammed Karouk El Bahi with three, Alae Aït Challa with three, Brahim Houdane with two, Zakariya Sarghine with one, and Mohammed Serrar with one.

The Moroccan team established a 5-0 lead by halftime, with El Ahmadi opening the scoring in the third minute. After the Solomon Islands scored their only goal in the second half, Morocco resumed their dominant attack to secure the decisive win. This victory followed Morocco's earlier group stage results: a 6-3 win against Paraguay and a 1-4 loss to Portugal.

With six points from three matches in Group B, Morocco has secured their place in the semifinals. The top two teams from each group advance to the final four of the men's futsal tournament.

Why it mattersMoroccan youth athletes have advanced to the semifinals of a major international futsal tournament.

Casablanca authorities investigate foul port odors

Residents complain of smells linked to imported livestock held at the port.

Residents of Casablanca’s Old Medina are complaining of strong, foul odors near the city’s port, prompting local authorities to launch an investigation. The smell, which residents say is increasingly disruptive, is believed by some to be connected to livestock imported from Latin America that remains on ships for extended periods.

Casablanca-Settat regional authorities, acting on instructions from Governor Mohamed Mhidia and with the National Office for Food Safety (ONSSA), have begun field investigations. Deputy mayor Moulay Ahmed Afilal stated the city council is also investigating whether the odors stem from the reception and holding of imported livestock at the port, examining hygiene and health-safety measures.

Afilal visited Casablanca Port to inspect the situation and has not ruled out that waste from imported calves and other livestock held there is the source of the smell. Affected residents are demanding that the source be addressed to ensure a clean and healthy living environment.

Why it mattersCasablanca residents are affected by foul odors originating from the port, impacting their living environment.

Health · 2026-11-08

Morocco health budget rises to 45.36 billion dirhams

The 2027 health budget increases by nearly 3 billion dirhams, with 8,000 new positions allocated.

Morocco's Ministry of Health budget for 2027 has been set at 45.36 billion dirhams, a rise of almost 3 billion dirhams compared to 2026. This increased funding includes the creation of 8,000 new budget posts. The budget prioritizes investment in public hospitals, with a specific allocation of 6.14 billion dirhams for hospital infrastructure.

Minister Youns Bjijou defended the budget, emphasizing its focus on execution and improved funding for the GST (General Social Protection) system. The plan aims to significantly boost hospital investments. The ministry plans to fill the 8,000 new positions to support these initiatives.

The budget aims to enhance public healthcare services across the country. The increased allocation for hospitals is intended to improve facilities and patient care. Further details on the execution of this budget are expected as the year progresses.

Why it mattersMoroccan citizens will benefit from increased investment in public hospitals and the creation of new healthcare positions.
