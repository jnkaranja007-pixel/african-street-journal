Morocco to host home matches for nine African national teams

Morocco will host home fixtures for nine African teams during the opening window of the 2027 Africa Cup of Nations qualifiers. The teams include Niger, Lesotho, Central African Republic, Liberia, Benin, Togo, Guinea, Madagascar and Somalia. The move follows a determination that these nations' domestic stadiums do not meet Confederation of African Football standards.

Why it mattersThe arrangement allows these nine teams to compete in qualifiers despite lacking approved domestic infrastructure.

Morocco investigates money laundering at three casinos

The National Financial Intelligence Authority is investigating suspected money laundering through poker games at three casinos in Marrakech and Agadir. Sources told Hespress that gamblers are suspected of presenting funds of unknown origin as winnings to deposit into bank accounts.

Why it mattersInvestigators are comparing declared winnings with game records to identify the origin of the funds.

Health · 08/20/2026

Gharb municipalities report no mosquito surge after floods

Municipal authorities in the Gharb region reported no unusual increase in mosquitoes during the first summer following recent floods. Officials attributed the result to early drainage and pest-control operations.

Why it mattersThe measures prevented stagnant flood waters from becoming breeding grounds for insects.
