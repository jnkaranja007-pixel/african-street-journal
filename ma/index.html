Moroccan officers seize 75 kilograms of cocaine at Guerguerat crossing

Customs officers discovered the drugs concealed within a truck's fuel tank during a routine inspection at the border.

Moroccan customs officers seized over 75 kilograms of cocaine from a truck at the Guerguerat border crossing on Sunday. The drugs, weighing 75 kilograms and 132 grams, were found hidden in the fuel tank of a trailer with Moroccan plates during a routine scan. The operation was conducted in coordination with security services.

Authorities have detained the truck driver on suspicion of smuggling and transferred him to judicial police for further investigation. Investigators are working to determine the origin and intended destination of the cocaine, as well as to identify any potential accomplices or links to larger trafficking networks. No formal charges have been announced.

Guerguerat, situated on the border with Mauritania, serves as the primary overland trade route connecting Morocco with West Africa. The seizure occurred in the import inspection zone as the truck underwent examination. The Customs and Indirect Tax Administration and national police have not yet issued a detailed public statement on the incident.

Why it mattersThe seizure of a large quantity of cocaine at a key trade crossing impacts the flow of illicit goods and highlights security challenges for businesses operating in the region.

Morocco Men's U20 Team Secures Bronze Medal in Mediterranean Games

The Atlas Lions defeated host Italy 4-1 to claim third place in the 20th edition of the Mediterranean Games held in Taranto, Italy.

Morocco's Men's U20 National Team has won the bronze medal at the 20th Mediterranean Games in Taranto, Italy. Coach Ludovic Batelli's squad secured third place after a decisive 4-1 victory against the host nation, Italy, on Sunday at Iacovone Stadium.

The winning goals for the Atlas Lions were scored by Mohammed Mounssef in the ninth minute of the first half, followed by second-half strikes from Abdelali Eddaoudi in the forty-seventh minute, Karim Essaadi in the fiftieth minute, and Anass Benomar in the seventy-fifth minute.

This bronze medal marks the fourth time a Moroccan soccer team has achieved this distinction in the Mediterranean Games. Previous bronze medals were won in the 1991 Athens, 2018 Tarragona, and 2022 Oran tournaments.

Why it mattersMoroccan football fans and the diaspora will be interested in this achievement as it adds to the nation's soccer history.

Masterflex launches Moroccan aerospace plant in fourth quarter

The German industrial-hose maker will begin production at its new Casablanca facility, creating 77 jobs and investing 50 million dirhams.

German industrial-hose maker Masterflex will start production at its new aerospace components plant near Casablanca in the fourth quarter of 2026. Initial customer deliveries are planned before the year ends. The facility, located in Nouaceur’s Midparc industrial zone, will be operated by Masterduct Morocco, a subsidiary of Masterflex’s aviation-focused Matzen & Timm business.

Masterflex plans to invest approximately 50 million dirhams ($5.5 million) in the operation over five years and create about 77 direct jobs. The 4,000-square-metre plant will manufacture technical hoses, bellows, moulded components, and connection systems from high-performance elastomers and technical textiles. The company has reserved an additional 4,000-square-metre plot for potential expansion.

The company expects the Moroccan operation to reach full capacity by 2030. Construction of the plant was a primary driver for Masterflex’s investment spending increase to 3.3 million euros in the first half of 2026, up from 1.3 million euros a year earlier. Group revenue rose 1.7% to 54.3 million euros, with operating earnings before interest and tax increasing to 8 million euros.

Why it mattersThis new aerospace plant will create jobs and boost investment in Morocco's industrial sector.

Zagora Date Growers Forecast 15% Harvest Increase

Producers anticipate a stronger yield for the 2026-2027 season, signaling a recovery from drought-affected harvests.

Date producers in Zagora expect their output to rise by 10% to 15% for the 2026-2027 season, marking a recovery from several drought-affected harvests. Majid Abdessalam, who runs a date-processing unit in the province, noted that early crop conditions indicate stronger production across varieties like Jihel, Bousthammi, and Medjool. This improvement is partly attributed to a significant reduction in palm mite infestations and other diseases that had impacted oasis farms during the drought period.

Growers in Figuig also reported an improved outlook, with early varieties already available and Feggous and Medjool dates expected to ripen in September. However, producer Fouad Ajdir cautioned that labor shortages could potentially increase harvesting costs and influence market prices. These forecasts are assessments from local producers, as the Agriculture Ministry has not yet released a national estimate for the upcoming crop.

Morocco's previous harvest was estimated at 160,000 metric tons, a substantial increase of 55% from the 103,000 tons harvested in the 2024-2025 season. The Drâa-Tafilalet region was the largest contributor, supplying 76% of the national output last season, followed by Souss-Massa and the Oriental region, each contributing 11%.

Why it mattersFarmers in Zagora and Figuig are affected by the expected increase in date harvest, which could lead to higher yields but also potential increases in harvesting costs.

Akhannouch Highlights Morocco's Conflict Prevention Strategy at AU Summit

Morocco's Head of Government, Aziz Akhannouch, stated that King Mohammed VI views peace and stability as a strategic project for the African continent.

Aziz Akhannouch, representing King Mohammed VI at an African Union summit in Luanda, outlined Morocco's approach to conflict prevention. He stated that the King has designated security and stability as a strategic project for Africa. Akhannouch emphasized Morocco's commitment to a preventive and lasting strategy, focusing on addressing economic and social issues as root causes of instability.

Speaking on the sidelines of the AU's extraordinary session on strengthening conflict prevention and resolution mechanisms, Akhannouch told reporters that Morocco champions South-South cooperation. This cooperation is framed within a participatory and partnership model among African nations, guided by the King's leadership. The Moroccan delegation included Foreign Minister Nasser Bourita and ambassadors Mohamed Arrouchi and Saadia Alaoui.

The summit aimed to enhance mechanisms for preventing and resolving conflicts across the continent. Morocco's participation underscores its active role in continental diplomacy and its focus on addressing the underlying socio-economic factors that contribute to instability and conflict.

Why it mattersMoroccan officials are promoting a conflict prevention strategy that prioritizes economic and social solutions, potentially impacting regional stability and cooperation.
