Politics · 08/21/2026

Morocco enacts Law 66.23 regulating legal profession

The new law regulating the legal profession took effect Thursday following its publication in the Official Gazette. The measure was enacted by Royal Decree 1.26.75 on Aug. 18, according to the Official Gazette. A nationwide lawyers' strike against the law has been ongoing since June 15.

Why it mattersThe enactment proceeds despite an unresolved constitutional challenge to the regulation.

King Mohammed VI pardons 667 convicted individuals

The Ministry of Justice announced that 667 people convicted by various courts were granted a royal pardon for Youth Day. The group includes 526 inmates currently in detention.

Why it mattersThe pardon allows 526 prisoners to be released from detention.

Business · 08/21/2026

Lithuanian plant shutdown may increase Moroccan fertilizer demand

Morocco could see higher European demand for fertilizer after EuroChem’s Lifosa plant in Lithuania suspended all production. Argus Media attributed the shutdown to high raw-material costs.

Why it mattersThe production halt combined with global shipping disruptions tightens the supply of phosphate products in Europe.

Business · 08/21/2026

Global Moroccan-style lantern market projected to hit $879 million by 2034

Worldwide sales of Moroccan-style lanterns are forecast to reach $879.38 million by 2034, up from an estimated $514.16 million in 2025. The projection was distributed by Japan’s Global Information.

Why it mattersThe forecast tracks the growth of products marketed as Moroccan lanterns globally rather than the domestic manufacturing industry.

Health · 08/21/2026

Gharb municipalities report no mosquito surge after floods

Authorities in the flood-affected Gharb region reported no unusual increase in mosquitoes during the first summer following the floods. Officials cited early drainage and pest-control operations as the reason for the lack of breeding grounds.

Why it mattersThe result serves as the first public-health test of the environmental effects following the exceptional rainfall.
