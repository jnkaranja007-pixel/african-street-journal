Morocco security chief orders promotion for injured officer

An officer in Agadir was struck by a vehicle and will receive an exceptional promotion and full medical support.

Morocco's national security chief Abdellatif Hammouchi ordered an exceptional promotion and full medical support for a police officer seriously injured in Agadir. The officer was struck by a vehicle after three occupants refused to comply with police instructions. The three suspects were arrested and are under judicial investigation.

A senior security delegation visited the injured officer at Mohammed VI University Hospital in Agadir. Hammouchi, director-general of National Security and Territorial Surveillance, instructed departments to cover all medical treatment and provide additional support. Medical staff reported the officer's condition was stable and he was responding positively to treatment.

The officer was granted an exceptional promotion to the rank of police officer. The delegation included Agadir’s police prefect and representatives from the Mohammed VI Foundation for Social Works of National Security Personnel.

Moroccan jewelers slow to cut gold prices amid global drop

Local gold prices fell 1.64% to MAD 1,020 per gram, far less than the global plunge below $4,000 an ounce.

Gold prices in Morocco have fallen more slowly than international markets following a sharp global selloff that pushed bullion below $4,000 an ounce. Spot gold dropped 3.5% to $3,880.97 an ounce, its lowest level since October 2025, while US December futures declined 3.3% to $3,898.90. In Morocco, the indicative price of raw 18-carat gold fell to approximately MAD 1,020 per gram, down MAD 17, or 1.64%, from Friday’s closing level.

Industry professionals told Hespress that the domestic decline remained considerably smaller than the international price movement. Sofiane, a gold and jewelry trader in Rabat, attributed the initial MAD 17 decline partly to sellers seeking immediate liquidity. Some gold holders accepted approximately MAD 20 less per gram rather than risk larger losses if prices continued dropping. Conventional cash purchases have slowed sharply, with customers exchanging older jewelry for newer designs.

Traders predict that the global decline could become more visible in Moroccan markets within two or three days. Domestic gold prices could fall by another MAD 20 to MAD 30 per gram by midweek if international weakness persists. Traders expect some seasonal demand for lightweight jewelry ahead of New Year’s celebrations, although they said any recovery would likely remain limited against the broader slowdown in sales.

Why it mattersMoroccan jewelers and buyers are affected by the slow adjustment of gold prices to global declines, impacting purchasing power and inventory values.

Morocco ends UN Sahara wait after veto

Rabat will no longer await Security Council resolutions on the Sahara dispute, experts say.

Morocco will stop waiting for new United Nations Security Council resolutions on the Sahara dispute following a diplomatic deadlock on Friday. Experts state that Russia's veto did not alter the framework set by Resolution 2797, which established Morocco's autonomy proposal as the negotiation basis. The Royal Cabinet's October 30 statement marked a decisive shift in Rabat’s approach, moving away from decades of reliance on the Council's periodic reviews.

The deadlock occurred when Russia vetoed an American draft aimed at making Morocco's autonomy plan the sole negotiation basis and restructuring the UN mission, MINURSO. A competing Russian proposal to extend MINURSO's mandate for one year failed to pass. Abdelfattah Belamchi, president of the Moroccan Center for Parallel Diplomacy, said Morocco's decision means it will not await further UN decisions. He noted that the kingdom could seek to terminate MINURSO's role and maintain security over its southern provinces.

Professor of international law Driss Lekrini stated that the Russian veto could not invalidate Resolution 2797, which recognized genuine autonomy as a feasible outcome. He argued that the Council's failure to adopt either proposal highlights its inability to advance the dispute. Lekrini believes Morocco's case is strengthened for removing the issue from the Council's agenda as international support for its autonomy initiative grows.

Why it mattersMorocco's government has decided to stop waiting for UN Security Council resolutions on the Sahara, potentially altering its approach to the long-standing dispute.

Morocco's Saibari undergoes ankle surgery

The Bayern Munich midfielder Saibari will miss an estimated three months after surgery on Sunday.

Morocco international Ismael Saibari underwent surgery on his right ankle on Sunday, his club Bayern Munich announced. Saibari was injured during Saturday's 2-2 draw with Borussia Dortmund after a collision with the opposing goalkeeper. He was substituted in the 29th minute of the match.

Bayern stated that Saibari and teammate Serge Gnabry will be sidelined for an unspecified period. German newspaper Bild reported that the Moroccan midfielder is expected to be out for approximately three months. The club did not provide an update on Michael Olise, who also sustained an injury during the game.

Saibari's absence adds to Bayern's injury concerns, with Jamal Musiala and Luis Diaz already unavailable. Coach Vincent Kompany is also taking time away from the dugout due to meningitis. Bayern are scheduled to play Atletico Madrid in the Champions League on Tuesday.

Why it mattersMoroccan footballer Ismael Saibari will be out of action for an estimated three months due to ankle surgery.

Four killed in Casablanca-Agadir highway crash

A bus struck stopped vehicles after a truck tire failure, injuring 32 others.

Four people died and 32 were injured in a multi-vehicle crash on the Casablanca-Agadir highway early Sunday. The collision involved a bus, two cars, and a truck.

Morocco’s National Motorway Company (ADM) reported that a truck stopped in the emergency lane due to a tire failure. Two cars slowed behind it before a bus collided with them. Four people suffered serious injuries, and 28 had minor ones.

Emergency services and authorities responded to the scene, concluding operations by 7:34 a.m. Traffic was briefly suspended and diverted. Authorities are investigating the crash circumstances.

Why it mattersFour people died and 32 were injured in a highway crash in Morocco.
