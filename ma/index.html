Morocco prepares to loosen the dirham peg and move to a flexible exchange rate

Morocco is preparing to shift the dirham from its peg to a euro and dollar basket toward a flexible exchange rate regime, Morocco World News reported. The authorities have framed the change as a way to strengthen economic resilience, attract foreign investment and integrate further into global financial markets.

Why it mattersA floating dirham changes import costs, remittance value and hedging for every business holding foreign currency.

Moroccan economy projected to grow 5% in 2026 with inflation near 1.3%

Morocco's economy is projected to expand by about 5% in 2026, up from an estimated 4.6% in 2025, with inflation running near 1.3%.

Why it mattersLow inflation alongside faster growth is a favourable backdrop for borrowing and investment.
