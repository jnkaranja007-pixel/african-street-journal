Spain sends Morocco 170 files to return unaccompanied minors from Ceuta

Spanish police say the families of the children have been identified and have agreed to their return, but Rabat has not responded to any of the files.

Source: Hespress English · 2026-11-22 · 1 min read

Spanish police have submitted 170 case files to Moroccan authorities seeking the return of unaccompanied Moroccan minors in Ceuta so they can rejoin their families. The files were sent through Spanish consulates in recent months after officers identified the families and obtained their consent.

Citing police sources, the Spanish outlet The Objective reported that Moroccan authorities had not responded to any of the files despite repeated follow-ups. It also said Spain's Ministry of Children and Youth had taken over the handling of the files, a claim a ministry spokesperson denied, saying the police commissariat remains responsible under Spanish regulations.

Spanish authorities had sent 189 files to Moroccan diplomatic missions as of Tuesday, including 19 reportedly forwarded by the ministry. Ceuta is hosting about 3,767 migrant minors in 23 reception centres, while about 500 others were reportedly sleeping on the streets.

Why it mattersCeuta's authorities want every minor returned, so the unanswered files leave about 3,767 children in reception centres with no firm date for reunification.
