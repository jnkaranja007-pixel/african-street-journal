IM Alliance to distribute SonoScape endoscopy systems in Morocco

The T2S Group subsidiary expands its healthcare offerings with a new agreement for advanced diagnostic and treatment devices.

Sources: Maroc Diplomatique and TelQuel · 2026-11-17 · 1 min read

IM Alliance, a subsidiary of T2S Group, has become an authorized distributor in Morocco for SonoScape Medical Corp.'s video-endoscopy systems. This partnership allows IM Alliance to offer an integrated range of endoscopy equipment, from columns and scopes to diagnostic and treatment devices, to healthcare facilities across the country. The agreement aims to strengthen IM Alliance's market position in digestive endoscopy.

The new distribution agreement covers SonoScape's full portfolio for digestive and pulmonary endoscopy, including gastroscopes, colonoscopes, duodenoscopes, bronchoscopes, and high-definition systems. Notably, the HD-650 4K system, featuring advanced LED lighting and virtual chromoendoscopy modes like SFI, VIST, and HCI, will be available to improve lesion detection and characterization.

These SonoScape devices complement IM Alliance's existing offerings of interventional endoscopy solutions from Boston Scientific, such as the SpyGlass™ DS cholangioscopy system and the Hot AXIOS™ stent. This consolidated approach provides a single point of contact for gastroenterology services, covering routine examinations to advanced interventional procedures.

Why it mattersMoroccan healthcare facilities can now access a wider range of advanced diagnostic and treatment endoscopy equipment through IM Alliance.
