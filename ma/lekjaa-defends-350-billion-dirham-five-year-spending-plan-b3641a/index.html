Lekjaa defends 350 billion dirham, five-year spending plan

Morocco’s finance minister says the 2027 finance bill preserves Parliament’s control over each annual budget vote.

Sources: TelQuel and Maroc Diplomatique · 2026-11-21 · 1 min read

Fouzi Lekjaa, Morocco’s economy and finance minister, defended a government plan to mobilise nearly 350 billion dirhams over its five-year mandate for five priorities. The plan allocates 63 billion dirhams in its first year.

Speaking before the House of Councillors on Friday, Lekjaa said Parliament would retain authority to vote on each annual budget. He said the 2027 finance bill respects the principle of annual budgeting and does not weaken parliamentary oversight.

Lekjaa said the bill complies with the organic law governing the finance law. The government’s five-priority programme is to be funded across its mandate.

Why it mattersParliament retains the vote on every annual budget, including the first-year allocation of 63 billion dirhams.
