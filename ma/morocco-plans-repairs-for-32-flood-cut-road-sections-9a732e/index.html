Morocco plans repairs for 32 flood-cut road sections

The regional equipment director says contracts for flood-damaged roads are expected before December ends, with awarded contractors given up to three months.

Source: Hespress English · 2026-11-23 · 1 min read

Morocco’s Ministry of Equipment and Water plans repairs after flooding cut off 32 road sections in the Tangier-Tetouan-Al Hoceima region. The damage affects routes in Larache, Ouezzane, Chefchaouen and Tangier, where residents say poor roads disrupt traffic and make travel difficult.

Ali Hamimou, the ministry’s regional director, told Hespress AR that technical studies of roads damaged in the previous winter were complete and repair contracts were expected to launch before the end of December. Assessments identified 300 damaged locations; landslides and soil erosion prompted further studies to determine repair methods and materials. Contractors will have no more than three months to complete work.

Hamimou said regional and provincial directorates were on alert for the coming winter rains and snowfall. Equipment had been sent to Chefchaouen and Al Hoceima, while machinery elsewhere was being serviced and tested.

Why it mattersResidents and road users in the four named provinces face disrupted journeys, while contractors must complete awarded repairs within three months.
