Capital Economics sees Morocco growth accelerating to 4.8% in 2027

The British research firm expects growth to rise from 4% in 2026 as energy-related pressures ease, while other forecasters are less optimistic.

Sources: North Africa Post and Les Inspirations ECO · 2026-11-20 · 1 min read

Morocco’s economy is forecast to grow 4% in 2026 and 4.8% in 2027, according to Capital Economics, a British research firm. Its analysis expects the recovery to continue through 2028 and says easing energy-related pressures will support next year’s acceleration.

The 4.8% projection is above analyst consensus. Other forecasts are lower: Morocco’s High Commission for Planning projected 3% growth in 2027, while the Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development forecast 3.9%. The World Bank expects 4%.

Morocco imports about 90% of its energy needs, according to the OECD. It says high energy prices raise businesses’ production costs and household expenses; it also forecasts inflation rising from 0.7% in 2025 to 3.2% in 2026.

Why it mattersBusinesses face higher production costs when energy prices rise, while Morocco imports about 90% of its energy needs.
