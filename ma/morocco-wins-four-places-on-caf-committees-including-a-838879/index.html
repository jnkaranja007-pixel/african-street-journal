Morocco wins four places on CAF committees, including a chairmanship

CAF approved the appointments at its assembly in Abuja, while Fouzi Lekjaa left the finance committee and kept his first vice-president role.

Source: North Africa Post · 2026-11-22 · 1 min read

Morocco secured four positions on the Confederation of African Football’s (CAF) restructured standing committees at the body’s assembly in Abuja, Nigeria, on Saturday, November 21. Taha Mansouri was named chairman of the Futsal and Beach Soccer Development Committee, while Abdellatif Moktarid joined the Club Competitions Committee.

Former Moroccan footballer and coach Fathi Jamal was appointed to the Technical Development Committee, and Ezzedine Yacoubi joined the Youth Competitions Committee. CAF approved the appointments as it renewed eight standing committees. Fouzi Lekjaa left the finance committee but retained his position as CAF’s first vice-president.

The youth committee oversees tournaments for under-17, under-20 and under-23 players. The club committee handles continental tournaments and related organization, while the technical committee works on training and football development across African federations.

Why it mattersMorocco’s four appointees now hold roles overseeing CAF tournaments and development work, including the chairmanship of its futsal and beach soccer committee.
