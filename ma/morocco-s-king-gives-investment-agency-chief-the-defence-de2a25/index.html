Morocco's king gives investment-agency chief the defence-industry roadmap

Mohammed VI tasked Ali Seddiki with operationalising the plan as two framework agreements were signed to finance the sector.

Sources: TelQuel and Les Inspirations ECO · 2026-11-26 · 1 min read

Morocco signed two framework agreements in Rabat on Wednesday, 25 November, to finance its defence and security industry. King Mohammed VI presided over the signing and tasked Ali Seddiki, director general of the Moroccan Agency for Investment and Export Development (AMDIE), with preparing the roadmap's implementation.

Seddiki is to oversee the operational planning while taking account of the roles of the institutions involved, Les Inspirations ECO reported. He will continue as AMDIE's director general during the interim period.

The assignment lasts until the creation of the Strategic Agency, known as DIFAA, according to TelQuel. The reports did not give the agreements' funding amounts.

Why it mattersSeddiki must develop the roadmap while retaining his AMDIE post until DIFAA is created.
