Morocco construction boom strains coastline

Sand extraction for building projects is damaging coastal areas, prompting calls for alternatives.

Source: TelQuel · 2026-10-23 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Morocco's construction boom is depleting sand resources, threatening coastal erosion in some areas. The intensive extraction of sand, a key material for building, has raised environmental concerns. This practice has become a significant threat to the nation's coastlines.

The United Nations issued a warning on May 12 regarding the planet's most extracted resource. The UN report highlights the unsustainable nature of current sand mining practices. Officials are exploring alternatives to lessen the construction sector's dependence on this finite resource.

The search for replaceable materials is underway as the construction sector faces pressure to adopt more sustainable practices. The long-term impact on coastal ecosystems and infrastructure remains a concern for environmentalists and policymakers.

Why it mattersMorocco's construction sector's reliance on sand extraction is causing coastal erosion.
