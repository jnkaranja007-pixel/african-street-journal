Morocco wins Arab beach soccer cup for first time

The Moroccan national team defeated Mauritania 6-5 in a dramatic final match held in Port-Said, Egypt.

Source: TelQuel · 2026-11-10 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Morocco's national beach soccer team secured their first-ever Arab Cup title on Monday, defeating Mauritania 6-5 in the final. The match took place in Port-Saïd, Egypt.

Morocco trailed 4-2 during the game but staged a remarkable comeback to clinch the victory. This win marks a significant achievement for the team in the history of the competition.

The team's historic win brings the cup to Morocco for the first time. Further details on the tournament's reception in Morocco were not provided in the report.

Why it mattersMoroccan athletes have won a major regional sporting competition.
