French firm Colas Rail wins €29 million Moroccan rail contract

The railway operator ONCF has awarded Colas Rail work on track, catenary and civil engineering in the Casablanca area.

Sources: North Africa Post and TelQuel · 2026-11-13 · 1 min read

Morocco's National Railways Office (ONCF) has awarded French firm Colas Rail a contract worth 29 million euros, roughly 316 million dirhams, as part of the Kenitra–Marrakesh high-speed rail project. The work covers track, catenary and civil engineering in the Casablanca area, between Roches-Noires station and the Nouaceur Technicentre.

The deal includes railway rehabilitation, new rail tracks, catenary installation and related civil engineering. Hervé Le Joliff, Executive Vice President of Railways at Colas Rail, said Morocco is a strategic market and that the contract reaffirms the company's commitment to high-speed rail in the Kingdom. Colas Rail, which has operated in Morocco for more than 17 years, employs around 550 staff.

The company has carried out more than 40 projects across Morocco, including work on the high-speed line. It is already building the Kenitra–Marrakesh line under two contracts secured in January 2025, worth 250 million euros in total.

Why it mattersMorocco's rail network gains another 29 million euros of track and electrification work, with Colas Rail's crews adding to the 550 people it already employs in the country.
