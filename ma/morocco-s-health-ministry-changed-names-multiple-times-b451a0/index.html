Morocco's health ministry changed names multiple times

Since independence, the department responsible for health has evolved its name and missions.

Source: TelQuel · 2026-10-24 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Morocco's ministry responsible for health has undergone several name changes since the country gained independence. These shifts reflect the evolving health needs of the population and the changing scope of the ministry's responsibilities. Najib Diouri, an international health economics expert, has examined this evolution.

Diouri's analysis notes that the ministry's leadership has also seen frequent changes, with ministers serving terms ranging from seven days to eight years. This frequent turnover in leadership has accompanied the department's name alterations over time.

The ministry's name changes and evolving missions have occurred as it has adapted to the country's sanitary requirements. The department has consistently worked to meet the health demands of the Moroccan people throughout its history.

Why it mattersThe frequent changes in the name and leadership of the health ministry affect the continuity of public health policy for Moroccans.
