Morocco tenders MAD 9.516 million for cloud-seeding equipment

The meteorology directorate plans systems and cartridges at six sites, but neither contract has been awarded.

Source: Hespress English · 2026-11-23 · 1 min read

Morocco’s General Directorate of Meteorology (DGM), the country’s weather agency, has opened two tenders worth a combined estimated MAD 9.516 million for cloud-seeding equipment. The planned purchases cover cartridges and automated systems for six locations.

One tender, valued at MAD 2.82 million including taxes, is for cartridges that release substances into clouds. The other, estimated at MAD 6.696 million, covers installation and maintenance of automated systems in Azilal, El Hajeb, Sefrou, Ouezzane, Khenifra and Hay Hassani in Casablanca. Neither contract has been awarded, and final spending depends on the bids selected, according to Hespress English.

Bids for the cartridge contract were due Nov. 23; the second tender closes Dec. 7. The equipment is intended to strengthen Al Ghaith, Morocco’s cloud-seeding program, which operates under suitable weather conditions and has been running for decades.

Why it mattersThe DGM must assess bids for the automated-systems contract by Dec. 7, while the cartridge tender’s bids were due Nov. 23.
