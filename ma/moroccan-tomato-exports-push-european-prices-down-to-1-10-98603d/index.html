Moroccan tomato exports push European prices down to €1.10 per kilogram

Prices fell from €1.80 to €2 per kilogram as slower sales pressured traders to discount their produce.

Source: Food Business Africa · 2026-11-25 · 1 min read

Moroccan tomato exports to European markets have increased in recent weeks, cutting prices from €1.80 to €2 per kilogram to €1.10 to €1.30, according to a source at France’s Rungis International Market. The earlier range was equivalent to US$2.07 to US$2.30 per kilogram; the new range is US$1.27 to US$1.50.

Slower sales have squeezed traders’ expected profit margins, the source said, as some cut prices to avoid losses on perishable stock. More shipments are expected over the next two weeks as new crops ripen in Morocco’s Souss-Massa region.

French and Dutch tomatoes have remained available longer amid higher temperatures, increasing competition for Moroccan and Spanish supplies. Morocco’s Agriculture Minister Khalid Hatimi said authorities are tightening restrictions on exports to African markets until they assess domestic food needs.

Why it mattersMoroccan exporters selling into Europe are receiving lower prices per kilogram, while new Souss-Massa shipments are expected over the next two weeks.
