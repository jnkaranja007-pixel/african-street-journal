Morocco and Japan mark 70 years of diplomatic relations

At a Tokyo reception, Princess Takamado recalled exchanges between the Moroccan royal family and Japan’s imperial family and urged closer cooperation.

Source: Maroc Diplomatique · 2026-11-28 · 1 min read

Princess Takamado of Japan highlighted the close historical ties between Morocco’s royal family and Japan’s imperial family at a reception in Tokyo on Thursday, as the countries marked 70 years of diplomatic relations. Morocco’s embassy in Japan organised the event.

In her speech, Takamado recalled visits and meetings that have shaped exchanges between the two families. She said their relationship reflected friendship and mutual esteem, and expressed hope that their ties of friendship and cooperation would grow stronger in the years ahead.

The reception focused on relations between the royal and imperial families, alongside the diplomatic anniversary.

Why it mattersMorocco’s embassy and the two families were represented at a Tokyo reception marking 70 years of diplomatic relations.
