Morocco and Madagascar sign nine cooperation agreements in Rabat

The countries also backed Morocco’s autonomy plan, while Rabat will raise scholarship places for Malagasy students from 90 to 200.

Sources: TelQuel and Les Inspirations ECO · 2026-11-25 · 1 min read

Morocco and Madagascar signed nine cooperation agreements in Rabat on Monday, 23 November, after the second session of their joint commission. The agreements widen cooperation between the two countries.

The talks covered trade, agriculture and training, according to TelQuel. Morocco will increase places for young Malagasy students in its scholarship programme from 90 to 200 from the next university intake.

Madagascar also reaffirmed its support for Morocco’s autonomy plan. The commission was co-chaired by Morocco’s foreign minister, Nasser Bourita, and his Malagasy counterpart, Alice N’Diaye.
