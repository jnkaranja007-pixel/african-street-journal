Morocco risks losing EU aid for electric cars made by Renault and Stellantis

Three European Parliament committees are due to vote on a text defining the rules on December 1.

Source: TelQuel · 2026-11-14 · 1 min read

Electric cars made in Morocco by Renault and Stellantis could lose European Union aid, according to TelQuel. The report says the car industry would bear the collateral damage of a European effort to protect its market from Chinese competition.

Three committees of the European Parliament are due to vote on a text that defines which vehicles qualify for the aid. The report says Morocco risks being left outside the European door even though the cars are built inside the kingdom.

The vote on the text is scheduled for December 1, the date the report gives for the committees' decision on the rules.
