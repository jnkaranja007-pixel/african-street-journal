Morocco mobilizes 2.5 billion dirhams for startups and AI

Minister Ahmed Rahhou also outlined plans for two public AI assistants, first to be tested in administrations and public institutions.

Source: North Africa Post · 2026-11-19 · 1 min read

Morocco will mobilize 2.5 billion dirhams for startups through an accelerated mechanism, Minister Ahmed Rahhou told the House of Representatives. The government also plans to help very small, small and medium-sized enterprises adopt artificial intelligence to improve productivity and competitiveness.

Two AI assistants are being developed: one to guide businesses through administrative procedures and compliance, and another to help officials consult laws and regulations and draft bills and decrees. They will be tested first in administrations and public institutions, alongside training for civil servants, before proven tools are expanded.

The plans sit under the “AI Made in Morocco” roadmap, alongside “Digital Morocco 2030,” and aim to develop Moroccan AI solutions. The ministry also plans to identify occupations most exposed to AI and establish continuing education and retraining programs with relevant sectors.
