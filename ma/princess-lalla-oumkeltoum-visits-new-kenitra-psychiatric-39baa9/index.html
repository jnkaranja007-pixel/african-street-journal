Princess Lalla Oumkeltoum visits new Kénitra psychiatric hospital

Her foundation and the health ministry signed a partnership to expand mental health care under the 2030 plan.

Sources: TelQuel and North Africa Post · 2026-11-15 · 1 min read

Princess Lalla Oumkeltoum, who chairs the Lalla Oumkeltoum Foundation for Mental Health, visited the new psychiatric hospital in Kénitra on Friday, 13 November. She also presided over the signing of a partnership agreement between the Ministry of Health and her foundation.

The agreement forms part of the Mental Health Plan 2030, launched by the health ministry, and of the foundation's Strategic Action Plan. Officials described it as a framework to promote mental health care across Morocco.

The agreement was signed on the same day, according to North Africa Post, which reported that the framework is meant to promote mental health care in Morocco.

Why it mattersPatients and families in Morocco gain a framework for mental health care, with the foundation working alongside the health ministry under the 2030 plan.
