Morocco's Saibari undergoes ankle surgery

The Bayern Munich midfielder Saibari will miss an estimated three months after surgery on Sunday.

Source: Hespress English · 2026-11-02 · 1 min read

Morocco international Ismael Saibari underwent surgery on his right ankle on Sunday, his club Bayern Munich announced. Saibari was injured during Saturday's 2-2 draw with Borussia Dortmund after a collision with the opposing goalkeeper. He was substituted in the 29th minute of the match.

Bayern stated that Saibari and teammate Serge Gnabry will be sidelined for an unspecified period. German newspaper Bild reported that the Moroccan midfielder is expected to be out for approximately three months. The club did not provide an update on Michael Olise, who also sustained an injury during the game.

Saibari's absence adds to Bayern's injury concerns, with Jamal Musiala and Luis Diaz already unavailable. Coach Vincent Kompany is also taking time away from the dugout due to meningitis. Bayern are scheduled to play Atletico Madrid in the Champions League on Tuesday.
