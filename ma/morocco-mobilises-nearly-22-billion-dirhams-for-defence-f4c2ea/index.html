Morocco mobilises nearly 22 billion dirhams for defence industry

Two agreements bring public funders, banks and market bodies into a plan targeting 50 billion dirhams in annual revenue, mostly from exports.

Sources: Les Inspirations ECO and TelQuel · 2026-11-28 · 1 min read

Morocco has mobilised nearly 22 billion dirhams from public partners to develop its defence and security industry, officials said at a signing ceremony in Rabat on Wednesday, 25 November. King Mohammed VI presided over the event, where two framework agreements were signed.

Ali Seddiki, director general of the Moroccan Agency for Investment and Export Development (AMDIE), announced the funding. One agreement finances the "Morocco Offer" roadmap; the other brings in domestic private and financial institutions. The programme aims eventually to generate at least 50 billion dirhams in annual revenue, with more than 65% from exports.

The second agreement includes Bank Al-Maghrib, the Moroccan banks' association, the Casablanca Stock Exchange and other financial bodies. A future strategic agency, named DIFAA (Defence Industry Strategic Agency), was also announced; the distribution of public funding among projects and the terms of private financing remain to be set.

Why it mattersMoroccan industrial companies and financiers must shape projects and financing terms; the public funding has not yet been allocated among projects.
