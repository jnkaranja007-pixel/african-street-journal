Nexans Maroc opens innovation challenge applications through 31 December

The third edition sets four electrification challenges and accepts submissions from ideas to mature solutions.

Source: Les Inspirations ECO · 2026-11-25 · 1 min read

Nexans Maroc has opened applications for the third Nexans Innovation Challenge, with submissions accepted from 23 November to 31 December 2026. The programme sets four industrial challenges under the theme of smarter, more sustainable and resilient electrification.

Nexans Maroc said the challenge is intended to connect industrial needs with solutions from startups, universities, research laboratories and entrepreneurs. Applicants can put forward an idea, prototype, pilot or more developed solution.

The company is running the initiative with Morocco’s industry and energy ministries, Mohammed VI Polytechnic University and the Renewable Energy Cluster. The challenges address electrification, including network digitalisation, renewable energy, artificial intelligence, decarbonisation, circular economy and infrastructure resilience.

Why it mattersStartups, researchers and entrepreneurs have until 31 December 2026 to submit ideas, prototypes, pilots or developed solutions for the four industrial challenges.
