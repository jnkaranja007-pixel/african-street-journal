Morocco interior ministry sets November 15 deadline for election law changes

Political parties must propose amendments for the 2027 local, regional and professional elections by mid-November.

Source: Hespress English · 2026-10-29 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Morocco's Interior Ministry has set a November 15 deadline for political parties to submit proposals for changes to electoral laws ahead of the 2027 local, regional and professional elections. Preparations for these elections follow September's parliamentary contests.

The deadline was announced Thursday during a meeting between the interior minister and party leaders, part of consultations under royal instructions. Discussions focused on extending measures from the September 23, 2026, elections, which aimed to strengthen electoral integrity and prevent misconduct.

The proposed changes will enter the legislative process as the government aims to complete legal preparations for the 2027 electoral cycle, which includes communal, provincial, and regional councils, as well as professional chambers and the House of Councillors.
