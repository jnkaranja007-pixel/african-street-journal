Russian embassy displays silk scarves in Conakry

An exhibition of Nina Routchkina's scarf designs opened at Guinea's National Museum on November 10, 2026.

Source: Guinee Matin · 2026-11-12 · 1 min read

The Russian Embassy in Guinea hosted an exhibition titled "Russia Woven in Silk" at the National Museum of Guinea on Tuesday, November 10, 2026. The event showcased silk scarves created by Russian artist Nina Routchkina, inspired by various regions of the Russian Federation. This cultural exchange aimed to foster closer ties between Conakry and Moscow.

The opening ceremony was attended by Guinea's Minister of Culture, Moussa Moïse Sylla, and the Minister of Higher Education, Djami Diallo, among other dignitaries. Mohamed Amirou Conté, Director General of the National Museum, highlighted the museum's role as a space for cultural dialogue and heritage preservation. He praised Routchkina's artistry for offering a new perspective on Russian cultural heritage.

Conté stated that the exhibition uses silk, color, and motifs to express Russian identity and artistic richness. He emphasized that art transcends borders, serving as a universal language for mutual understanding and cultural connection between peoples. The exhibition seeks to use art as an instrument for dialogue.

Why it mattersGuineans can experience Russian culture through an art exhibition featuring silk scarves.
