1xBet Guinea runs breast-cancer awareness campaign across October

The company held staff information activities and a 30 October expert forum in Conakry through its 1xImpact social programme.

Source: Africaguinee · 2026-11-19 · 1 min read

1xBet Guinea ran breast-cancer awareness activities throughout October 2026 under its 1xImpact social programme, focusing on prevention and early diagnosis. The campaign included information sessions for company staff and discussions involving health professionals and civil-society organisations.

The campaign closed with a forum on 30 October at the 1XHUB digital space in Hamdallaye, Conakry. 1xBet Guinea marketing director Alpha Bah said the company had carried out awareness activities with its employees through 1xImpact. A themed Pink Day also gave staff an opportunity to discuss breast cancer and share health information.

The programme addressed cancer-related diseases during October, with particular attention to women’s health. The company said 1xImpact supports projects serving the public interest and collaboration with local communities.

Why it matters1xBet Guinea’s staff were the direct audience for the information sessions, including the Pink Day discussion on breast cancer.
