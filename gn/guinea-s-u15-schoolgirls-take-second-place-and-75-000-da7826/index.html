Guinea’s U15 schoolgirls take second place and 75 000 dollars US

The team earned 5 points in Monrovia, won the tournament’s fair-play award and missed the only qualifying place for the continental finals.

Source: Guinee Matin · 2026-11-28 · 1 min read

Guinea’s girls’ U15 school team finished second in the West African Football Union’s fifth zonal school tournament in Monrovia, Liberia, on Friday 27 November 2026, earning 75 000 dollars US. The three-team competition was played home and away, and only the winner qualified for the continental finals.

Guinea ended with 5 points, behind Senegal, and also received the tournament’s fair-play award. The team beat Gambia 2-1 and lost to Senegal 0-1 in the first round. In the return matches, it drew 1-1 with Gambia and 0-0 with Senegal, remaining unbeaten in that phase.

Senegal took the qualifying place for the U15 School Africa Cup of Nations. Guinea’s team left Liberia with the prize money and fair-play trophy.

Why it mattersGuinea’s players and school team receive 75 000 dollars US, while second place leaves them without the continental finals place.
