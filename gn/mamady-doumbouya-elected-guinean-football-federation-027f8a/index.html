Mamady Doumbouya elected Guinean football federation president

The new president promised transparency in federation finances and club subsidies within 100 days.

Source: Africaguinee · 2026-10-28 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Mamady Doumbouya was elected president of the Guinean Football Federation (FGF) for a four-year term on Tuesday, October 27, 2026. He pledged to focus on unity, transparency, and accountability in his leadership.

Doumbouya announced that he will conduct a review of the federation's operations and ensure transparent subsidy distribution to clubs. He committed to reporting on his initial actions within 100 days of taking office.

The new president called for an end to electoral divisions, stating his intention to be the president for all members of the Guinean football family. His mandate also includes strengthening club support, training, and competition development.

Why it mattersMamady Doumbouya's election as president of the Guinean Football Federation affects football stakeholders in Guinea.
