China and Guinea launch Simandou 2040 project

The integrated iron ore project involves a mine, railway, and port, with total infrastructure investments exceeding $20 billion.

Source: Africaguinee · 2026-11-16 · 1 min read

Chinese and Guinean companies are jointly developing the Simandou iron ore project in Guinea's mountains. The project includes a mine, a trans-Guinean railway, and the Morébaya port, with total infrastructure investments expected to surpass $20 billion.

Once fully operational, the Simandou project aims for an annual capacity of 120 million tonnes. Baowu Resources, a subsidiary of China Baowu, is integrating environmental, social, and governance standards into the development and operation of the northern Simandou mining chain.

On November 6, Baowu Resources released "The Sustainability of the Simandou Project" in Shanghai, the first public ESG report focused on Simandou and the first thematic ESG report on an overseas integrated iron ore and infrastructure project involving a Chinese company.

Why it mattersThe Simandou 2040 program aims to boost Guinea's economic transformation through mining, with total infrastructure investments exceeding $20 billion.
