Rio Tinto SimFer shows Simandou mine operations

Media professionals toured mining facilities in Beyla, Guinea, on October 28-29, 2026, to see ore processing.

Source: Guinee Matin · 2026-10-30 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Rio Tinto SimFer hosted a two-day media tour of its Simandou mining blocks 3 and 4 in Beyla, Guinea, from October 28 to 29, 2026. Reporters observed the ore crushing, storage, and transport processes. The visit aimed to showcase the operational realities of the large-scale mining project.

During the immersion, journalists visited the training center, nursery, crushing facilities, and ore storage areas. A training center official explained the practical risk demonstration zone, designed to bridge theory and practice for workers with limited formal education. This facility includes areas for risk identification, health and safety demonstrations, tire assembly simulations, and traffic management.

The tour covered various stages of ore processing, from crushing to transport towards the port. Media delegates interacted with technical managers overseeing the operations. The company highlighted its training and risk prevention measures to enhance worker skills and promote safety.

Why it mattersMedia professionals gained firsthand insight into the operational processes and safety training at Rio Tinto SimFer's Simandou mine in Beyla.
