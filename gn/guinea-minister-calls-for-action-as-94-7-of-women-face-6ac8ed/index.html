Guinea minister calls for action as 94.7% of women face genital mutilation

Aminata Kaba urged families and institutions to protect survivors and end impunity during the national campaign running from 25 November to 10 December.

Sources: Guinee Matin and Africaguinee · 2026-11-27 · 1 min read

Guinea’s minister for women, family and solidarity, Aminata Kaba, called for national action against violence toward women and girls as a 16-day campaign began on 25 November. It runs through 10 December and sets out priorities of access to justice, survivor protection and ending impunity.

National data cited by the ministry show that 94.7% of women aged 15 to 49 have experienced female genital mutilation. The figures also say 80.7% of women aged 15 to 69 have faced at least one form of violence in their lives, while about 46% of girls marry before age 18.

Kaba called on parents and families to listen to children and protect women and girls, and urged families to keep girls in school. She said state bodies, communities, religious and traditional leaders, media, civil society and the private sector all share responsibility for addressing the violence.

Why it mattersFamilies are being urged to protect girls from early marriage and keep them in school, with about 46% married before age 18.
