Guinea weather alert warns of heat through Nov. 25

Moderate to locally strong heat is forecast in Basse-Guinée and Forest Guinea, with high temperatures expected to persist at night.

Source: Mosaiqueguinee · 2026-11-21 · 1 min read

Guinea’s meteorological services issued a moderate-severity alert on Friday, Nov. 20, for heat expected from Saturday, Nov. 21, through Wednesday, Nov. 25, mainly in Basse-Guinée and Forest Guinea. The forecast runs until 11 p.m. on Nov. 25 and carries a probable level of certainty.

The services and civil protection officials warn that sustained daytime and nighttime heat raises the risk of heatstroke and dehydration, especially for older people, infants, young children and people with chronic illnesses. Residents in the affected areas, including Greater Conakry, are advised to drink regularly, avoid strenuous activity during the hottest hours and check on vulnerable people.

Officials also expect greater use of fans and air conditioners to increase electricity demand in the affected zones. The alert describes the heat as moderate to locally strong.

Why it mattersOlder people, young children and those with chronic illnesses in the affected regions face heatstroke and dehydration risks through Nov. 25.
