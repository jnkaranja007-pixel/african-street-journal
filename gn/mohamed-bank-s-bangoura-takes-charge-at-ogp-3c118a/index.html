Mohamed Bank’s Bangoura takes charge at OGP

New director general Mohamed Bank’s Bangoura aims to restore stability and improve performance at the Guinean Office of Advertising.

Source: Guinee Matin · 2026-10-23 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Mohamed Bank’s Bangoura officially took over as the new Director General of the Guinean Office of Advertising (OGP) on Tuesday, October 20, 2026, in Conakry. His deputy, Alya Camara, also assumed his new role. The new leadership faces challenges including restoring stability, strengthening governance, and improving the institution's performance.

The handover ceremony was attended by interim Minister of Communication, Digital Economy and Innovation Souleymane Bah, and Presidential Advisor and OGP Board Chairman Thierno Mamadou Bah. Alya Camara, who served as interim director for three months, pledged his support to the new team, emphasizing collaboration and continuity of public action.

Bangoura stated his mandate will focus on listening, responsibility, and performance. He aims to understand the institution's realities, build on existing achievements, and make necessary decisions for its development. The OGP is tasked with serving the nation through modernization and enhanced performance.

Why it mattersMohamed Bank’s Bangoura and Alya Camara have taken new leadership roles at the Guinean Office of Advertising, impacting the institution's governance and performance.
