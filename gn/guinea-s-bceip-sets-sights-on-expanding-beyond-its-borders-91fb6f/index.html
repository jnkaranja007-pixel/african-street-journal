Guinea's BCEIP sets sights on expanding beyond its borders

The 30-year-old company says it wants to deepen its contribution to Guinea's economy while extending its activities abroad.

Source: Guinee Matin · 2026-11-22 · 1 min read

BCEIP, a Guinean company providing recruitment, outsourced human-resources management and other services, marked 30 years in Conakry on Saturday, 21 November. The company was founded in 1996 and also works in security, property management, home assistance and agribusiness.

Managing director Mohamed Yiremba Sidibé said BCEIP helps companies recruit staff and secure their operations in Guinea. At the anniversary ceremony, the company presented its record and future plans, and Sidibé said its strategy includes continued training for workers, both inside and outside the company.

Sidibé said BCEIP had faced regulatory and social difficulties, including challenges in meeting labour law requirements. He said the company intends to strengthen its contribution to Guinea’s economy and expand its activities beyond the country.

Why it mattersBCEIP’s workers and business partners face a company planning to extend its activities beyond Guinea after reporting regulatory and labour-law challenges.
