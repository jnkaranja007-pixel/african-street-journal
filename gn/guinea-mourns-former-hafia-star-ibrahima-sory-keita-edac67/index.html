Guinea mourns former Hafia star Ibrahima Sory Keita

Known as Petit Sory, the former Hafia 77 player was remembered by a parliamentarian who had urged national recognition for the club’s former players.

Sources: Guinee Matin, Africaguinee and Mosaiqueguinee · 2026-11-23 · 1 min read

Guinean footballer Ibrahima Sory Keita, known as Petit Sory, has died, according to a tribute published by Guinee Matin. Keita was a former star of Hafia 77, the tribute said.

Dr Fodé Oussou Fofana, a parliamentarian and former president of the Liberals Democrats parliamentary group, said he had spoken in parliament in favour of dignified treatment and national recognition for former Hafia players. Keita attended the session and thanked him afterwards, Fofana said.

Fofana said he had known Keita while the footballer lived in Landréah, in Dixinn commune. A symposium in Keita’s honour was held at the Palais des Sports at the 28-Septembre stadium on Sunday, 22 November, according to Africaguinee.

Why it mattersFormer Hafia players are the focus of Fofana’s call for dignified treatment and national recognition, which he made in parliament while Keita was alive.
