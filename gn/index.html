Sport · 2026-10-28

Mamady Doumbouya elected Guinean football federation president

The new president promised transparency in federation finances and club subsidies within 100 days.

Mamady Doumbouya was elected president of the Guinean Football Federation (FGF) for a four-year term on Tuesday, October 27, 2026. He pledged to focus on unity, transparency, and accountability in his leadership.

Doumbouya announced that he will conduct a review of the federation's operations and ensure transparent subsidy distribution to clubs. He committed to reporting on his initial actions within 100 days of taking office.

The new president called for an end to electoral divisions, stating his intention to be the president for all members of the Guinean football family. His mandate also includes strengthening club support, training, and competition development.

Business · 2026-10-28

Guinea court hears bank mismanagement case

Former BNIG director Alpha Mohamed Kallo faces charges including embezzlement and money laundering.

The Crief court on Tuesday, October 27, 2026, examined the case of Alpha Mohamed Kallo, former director general of the Banque nationale d’investissement de Guinée (BNIG). Kallo, detained since July 2022, faces charges including embezzlement of public funds, corruption, and money laundering. Also appearing were Ibrahima Tounkara, Maurice Eugène Alecaut, and companies Afriland First Bank, Egico SARL, and Djoliba Pêche.

Current BNIG director Moussa Kamissoko reported governance issues and financial irregularities upon taking office, noting a 7 billion Guinean franc disbursement to Djoliba Pêche without a clear contract. Djoliba Pêche representative Sory Doumbouya stated the company fulfilled its contractual obligations for the funds, which were intended for fishing equipment and fish sales. The court heard of alleged fund outflows totaling 180 billion Guinean francs.

The trial also involves accusations of corruption of public officials, forgery, and breaches in public procurement access. Some defendants contested the claims, offering explanations to the court. The case continues with further examination of financial movements and contractual agreements.

Why it mattersFormer BNIG director Alpha Mohamed Kallo and others face trial for alleged financial crimes including embezzlement and money laundering.

News · 2026-10-28

two-year-old boy drowns in well in Labé

Mamadou Aliou Bah fell into an open well in his mother's garden on Tuesday morning.

A two-year-old boy, Mamadou Aliou Bah, died after falling into a well in the Hôré Hollâdhé neighborhood of Labé on Tuesday morning, October 27, 2026. His mother, Mariama Bobo Diallo, stated the child had been sleeping in the garden while she harvested tomatoes. She discovered he was missing when she went to check on him.

The child's father was called to the scene and confirmed the boy was not where his mother had left him. A search of the garden led to the discovery of Mamadou Aliou Bah's body in the well. The neighborhood chief, Abdoulaye Bademba Barry, alerted emergency services, and the body was taken to Labé Regional Hospital for an autopsy.

This incident has reignited concerns about the safety of open wells and excavations in Labé. According to the neighborhood chief, similar accidents have occurred in Hôré Hollâdhé before. The community is awaiting the hospital's decision regarding the release of the body for burial.

Education · 2026-10-28

university of labé plans student reception

the university has established a mixed commission to address accommodation and integration for new students.

The University of Labé in Guinea has developed a strategic plan to welcome and integrate students for the 2026-2027 academic year. Rector Dr. Mohamed Chérif Sow announced the initiative on October 26, outlining measures to improve the learning environment and modernize the higher education system.

A mixed commission, comprising university officials, dormitory managers, local authorities, and student representatives, has been formed to find sustainable solutions for student reception and accommodation. Dr. Sow stated that the university will work diligently to ensure the best learning conditions, noting the expansion of academic programs from 10 to over 30.

This plan aligns with the Ministry of Higher Education's roadmap and aims to inform the university community and the public about the new support systems. The university also highlighted the Guinean state's attention to the sector's needs and its efforts to improve quality.

Why it mattersStudents arriving at the University of Labé will benefit from improved reception and accommodation services.

News · 2026-10-28

child drowns in construction site pit in Sonfonia

Five-year-old Abdoul Gadiry Ly died after falling into a water-filled excavation behind General Lansana Conté University.

A five-year-old boy, Abdoul Gadiry Ly, drowned in a construction site pit in Sonfonia on Tuesday, October 27. His body was recovered from the excavation behind General Lansana Conté University after he went missing while playing with friends.

The child's grandmother, Odia Sanoh, reported finding his shoes before discovering him submerged in the pit. She stated that children have previously drowned in these same holes, with two prior incidents where children were rescued but exhausted. Sanoh expressed deep concern, noting that the construction site owner remains unreachable.

The child's father confirmed that this is the third such incident at the location, with previous victims being saved. The family is awaiting the results of an investigation before burying the child, who was pronounced dead at the hospital.

Why it mattersA five-year-old child died after drowning in an open, water-filled construction pit.
