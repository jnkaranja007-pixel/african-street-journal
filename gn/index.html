Guinea sets dates for regional and senatorial election campaigns

President Mamadi Doumbouya announced campaign periods for November 29 regional and December 20 senatorial votes.

President Mamadi Doumbouya has set the campaign dates for Guinea's upcoming regional and senatorial elections. The regional elections are scheduled for November 29, 2026, with campaigning running from November 9 to November 26, 2026. The senatorial elections will take place on December 20, 2026, and their campaign period will be from November 20 to December 17, 2026.

These dates were announced in decrees read on Guinean state television on Monday, November 9, 2026. The High Authority for Communication (HAC) has also established rules for equitable access to media for candidates during the regional election campaign, according to decision N°0045/HAC/P/2026 dated November 6, 2026.

Several ministers, including those for Territorial Administration, Justice, Security, Defense, and Economy, are responsible for implementing these decrees. The officials of electoral institutions and competent services are also tasked with executing these provisions for the senatorial elections.

Why it mattersGuinean citizens will participate in regional and senatorial elections in late 2026, with specific dates set for their election campaigns.

SOGEAG launches hospitality week at Conakry airport

The airport management company aims to improve passenger experience with personalized attention and cultural events.

The Société de gestion des aéroports de Guinée (SOGEAG) launched its first Hospitality Week on Monday, November 9, 2026, at the Ahmed Sékou Touré International Airport in Conakry. For seven days, travelers, frontline staff, and airport partners will experience personalized attention, cultural events, and opportunities for exchange, according to airport authorities. This initiative is part of SOGEAG's broader plan to modernize infrastructure and services to meet international standards.

Airport officials stated the week aims to honor passengers for their trust with enhanced welcome services and cultural activities. It also seeks to highlight the contributions of airport staff, including security, maintenance, cleaning, and retail personnel, whose daily work ensures passenger safety and comfort. The SOGEAG also plans to conduct satisfaction surveys and recognize engaged employees.

The SOGEAG intends to improve traveler listening through satisfaction surveys and direct interactions to better understand and meet passenger expectations. The initiative also aims to promote Guinea's image by offering travelers an experience that reflects Guinean hospitality and warmth.

Why it mattersPassengers and airport staff in Conakry will experience enhanced services and recognition during the week-long hospitality event.

Guinea closes Dar-es-Salam dump site

Conakry's waste management shifts to Baritodé, increasing transport costs and collection challenges.

Conakry's main waste dump, Dar-es-Salam, has been closed as part of an effort to improve city cleanliness. For years, the site received most of the capital's refuse, becoming a hazardous and saturated location. Following its closure on September 3, waste is now being directed to the Baritodé site, a move that increases transport distances and operational expenses for waste collection services.

The shift to Baritodé, which is further from the city, means fewer truck rotations per day and higher running costs. This has led to a reappearance of illegal dumping and saturation at some transit points. The number of collection vehicles and transport capacities have not kept pace with the growing waste production from Conakry's population and economic activities.

The ministry is considering a global management approach, potentially with a private operator, to better coordinate operations and improve collection regularity. The daily cost of managing waste in Greater Conakry is estimated at 660 million Guinean Francs, totaling approximately 19.8 billion Guinean Francs for 30 days, highlighting the significant resources needed for sanitation.

Séquence FC takes second place in Ligue 2

The Dixinn club defeated Flamme Olympique 2-1 to climb the league standings.

Séquence FC secured a 2-1 victory over Flamme Olympique on Monday, November 9, 2026, at the Petit Sory de Nongo stadium. Mohamed Kaba scored in the second minute, and Mohamed Sidy added another goal in the fifth minute, giving Séquence FC a commanding 2-0 lead. Flamme Olympique managed to score a consolation goal in the 90th+4 minute through Harouna Camara.

This win propelled Séquence FC to second place in the Ligue 2 standings with 20 points. Flamme Olympique remains at the bottom of the league with 7 points. The match saw an aggressive start from Séquence FC, which managed to control the game after establishing an early lead.

The victory marks a significant achievement for Séquence FC in their pursuit of promotion. Flamme Olympique faces increasing pressure to improve its performance and avoid relegation. The league continues with further matches to determine final standings.

Why it mattersFootball fans in Guinea will follow the league standings as Séquence FC aims for promotion and Flamme Olympique fights relegation.
