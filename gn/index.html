President Doumbouya Welcomes New Daughter

The Guinean head of state announced the birth of his daughter in the early hours of September 2, 2026, with both mother and child reported to be in good health.

President Mamadi Doumbouya of Guinea announced the birth of his daughter on the morning of September 2, 2026. The announcement came from the Presidential Palace, marking a moment of joy for the presidential family. The President shared that the delivery occurred at 2 a.m., and both the First Lady, Laurianne Doumbouya, and the newborn are doing well.

In a statement, President Doumbouya expressed his gratitude to God for the new arrival and extended thanks to the medical staff who assisted during the delivery. He also conveyed his appreciation to the Guinean population for their prayers and support during this time. The President concluded by wishing for blessings upon the homes of his people, mirroring the joy in his own.

The birth occurred during a period when Guinea is also hosting Gambian President Adama Barrow, who arrived in Conakry for discussions on strengthening bilateral relations. The Gambian leader was received by Prime Minister Amadou Oury Bah upon his arrival, signaling ongoing diplomatic activities alongside the personal news from the presidential residence.

Why it mattersThe birth of a child to the head of state is a significant personal event for the presidential family and a moment of national celebration for the Guinean people.

Former US Ambassador Joins Ivanhoe Atlantic Board

Troy Fitrell's appointment as a non-executive director strengthens the American leadership as the company prepares to start construction on its Kon Kweni iron ore project.

Ivanhoe Atlantic Inc., an American mining and strategic minerals company, has appointed former U.S. Ambassador Troy Fitrell as a non-executive director. This move is intended to bolster the company's American leadership as it moves towards the construction phase of its flagship Kon Kweni ultra-high-grade iron ore project in Guinea. The project is currently in the final stages of obtaining necessary authorizations in the country.

Ambassador Fitrell, who served as the U.S. Ambassador to Guinea from 2022 to 2025 and most recently led the U.S. State Department's Bureau of African Affairs in 2025, brings extensive regional knowledge and strategic experience. J. Peter Pham, Executive Chairman of Ivanhoe Atlantic, noted Fitrell's understanding of Africa's economic potential and the importance of sustained American commercial leadership.

Fitrell's appointment aligns with Ivanhoe Atlantic's strategy to position itself as a leading American investment platform in Africa, focusing on strategic resources that support U.S. national security policy. He joins two other former U.S. Ambassadors to Guinea on the board of Ivanhoe Atlantic's Guinean subsidiary, Société des Mines de Fer de Guinée (SMFG). Fitrell stated his aim to support the company's mission of strengthening U.S.-Africa economic ties and securing industrial supply chains.

Why it mattersThe appointment of former U.S. Ambassador Troy Fitrell to Ivanhoe Atlantic's board directly impacts investors by reinforcing American leadership and strategic partnerships for the Kon Kweni iron ore project in Guinea.

UBA Guinée simplifies customer service with new single contact number

The bank introduces 8210 as its sole customer service line to streamline client interactions and improve accessibility for information, assistance, and complaints.

UBA Guinée has launched 8210 as its new, single customer service number, aiming to simplify how clients access banking support. This initiative is part of the bank's ongoing efforts to enhance customer experience by providing a memorable and easily accessible point of contact for all inquiries.

The introduction of the 8210 number means customers no longer need to search for different contacts depending on their specific needs, whether it's for information, assistance, or to lodge a complaint. UBA Guinée stated that this change is intended to make client relations more straightforward and offer a clearly identifiable channel for support.

This move by UBA Guinée is designed to make its telephone support system more accessible and to guide customers more effectively when they seek information or report issues. The bank hopes that the single number will become the primary and most convenient way for clients to connect with its customer service.

Why it mattersThe new single customer service number 8210 makes it easier for UBA Guinée clients to access information and assistance.

Central Bank Suspends Twelve Employees for Two Months

Guinea's central bank governor has ordered the immediate suspension of twelve staff members for a period of two months without stating the reasons.

Governor Dr. Karamo Kaba of the Central Bank of the Republic of Guinea (BCRG) announced on Wednesday, September 2, the suspension of twelve employees. The affected staff will be on leave from September 2 until November 1, 2026, as per a service note detailing the disciplinary action.

The note specifies the names and employee identification numbers of the twelve individuals facing suspension. Implementation of this decision has been assigned to several BCRG department heads, including the Directors General of Administration and Legal Services, Finance and IT, Permanent Control, and the Director of Human Resources.

While the service note outlines the disciplinary measure and its execution, it does not provide any information regarding the specific reasons or alleged misconduct that led to the suspension of these twelve agents. The note was communicated by Mosaiqueguinee.com.

Why it mattersTwelve employees of Guinea's central bank are directly affected by a two-month suspension from their duties.
