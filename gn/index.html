Guinea U20 team returns to Conakry after UFOA-A championship win

The Syli National junior team was welcomed by enthusiastic supporters at Ahmed-Sékou-Touré airport following their victory in Bamako.

The Syli National junior team returned to Conakry on Monday, November 2, 2026, after winning the UFOA-A U20 tournament in Bamako. Numerous supporters gathered at the Ahmed-Sékou-Touré international airport to celebrate the young champions' victory. The team was also congratulated by Mamady Doumbouya, the new president of the Guinean Football Federation.

Outside the airport, fans created a festive atmosphere as the players boarded a bus. This reception highlighted the pride and joy felt by Guineans for the achievement of their youth team in Bamako. The victory also qualifies the team for the next African Nations Championship.

Meanwhile, Syli National coach Paulo Duarte announced a 25-player squad on November 2, 2026, for upcoming 2027 Africa Cup of Nations qualifiers against Eritrea on November 12 and November 16. Only one player from the Guinean league, Yacouba Gnagna Barry of Horoya AC, was selected.

Why it mattersThe Syli National junior football team's victory in the UFOA-A U20 tournament has brought national pride and recognition to the young players.

Guinea president promotes army officer to major general

General of brigade Séna Camara was elevated to the rank of major general on November 2, 2026.

President Mamadi Doumbouya promoted General of brigade Séna Camara to the rank of major general on Monday, November 2, 2026. The decree announcing Camara's elevation was read on national television (RTG). Camara serves as the Inspector General of the Guinean armed forces.

This promotion signifies a renewed trust from President Doumbouya in Sennah Camara, who holds a key position within the Guinean Armed Forces. The decision was officially communicated through a decree broadcast on national television.

Separately, at the CRIEF court, the public prosecutor requested the acquittal of court bailiffs Aboubacar Camara and Alhassane Bah. They are accused of corruption and other offenses related to their duties. The prosecution's request followed their testimonies before the court.

Why it mattersPresident Mamadi Doumbouya has promoted a high-ranking military official, indicating shifts in military leadership.

Mohamed Samoura takes over Siguiri prosecutor's office

Dominique Loua officially handed over duties after 10 months leading the Siguiri court's prosecution office.

Mohamed Samoura has officially taken over as prosecutor at the Siguiri Court of First Instance (TPI), succeeding Dominique Loua. The handover ceremony occurred on Monday, November 2, 2026, at the court premises.

Dominique Loua described his 10-month tenure as an enriching professional and human experience before moving to Labé. The new prosecutor, Mohamed Samoura, stated his priorities will be accessibility, speed, and impartiality in justice.

Boubacar Barry, president of the Siguiri TPI, praised Loua's contributions to the court's functioning and the defense of societal interests. Barry urged Samoura to reinforce collaboration between the court's branches while respecting judicial independence.

Culture · 2026-11-03

Guinea state to fund hip-hop duo's medical care

The government will provide emergency health support, including overseas treatment, for Two Marley and Magica.

The Guinean state will provide emergency health support for Two Marley and Magica, two prominent members of the hip-hop group Mifagueya. The Ministry of Culture, Tourism and Handicrafts announced this commitment on Monday, November 2, 2026, following a meeting with the artists.

The two artists, recognized as pioneers of Guinean hip-hop, are facing serious health issues. Minister Moussa Moïse Sylla stated that the government must ensure their dignity is maintained. An immediate action plan includes a medical diagnosis within seven days, dedicated accommodation, and a planned short-term transfer abroad for specialized treatment.

The initiative is part of President Mamadi Doumbouya's cultural policy. The artists are now covered by health insurance and are being monitored in consultation with FODAC, BGDA, and their families.

Culture · 2026-11-03

Beyla identifies 33 tourist sites

Access difficulties and poor road conditions hinder the development of natural and historical attractions.

Beyla, Guinea has identified 33 tourist sites, including the Famoila and Samiadou waterfalls and the historic Samory Touré house. The region's diverse heritage includes natural sites, historical locations, and memorial spaces. However, the development and promotion of these attractions face significant challenges.

Mamady Kassia Doumbouya, Beyla's prefectural director for Culture, Tourism, and Handicrafts, stated that 33 sites have been cataloged. These include the Famoila falls in Moussadou and Samiadou falls in Beyla's urban commune, as well as Bembéya spring and the Place des Martyrs. Doumbouya noted that the round house at the Place des Martyrs is a historic structure where Almamy Samory Touré stayed.

The prefectural services have begun developing the Place des Martyrs, transforming it into an open-air space next to the historic round house. Further projects are planned for Bembéya spring, but their realization depends on technical preparation and resource mobilization. Access issues and the condition of roads, especially during the rainy season, remain major obstacles to tourism development.

Why it mattersThe natural and historical tourist sites in Beyla are underdeveloped due to poor road access, preventing their full potential from being realized.
