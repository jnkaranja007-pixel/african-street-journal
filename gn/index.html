Politics · 08/21/2026

Guinea creates 2 new administrative regions

President Mamadi Doumbouya established Beyla and Siguiri as new administrative regions on Aug. 20. The move was announced via an official act restructuring the country's territorial divisions.

Why it mattersThe change elevates Beyla and Siguiri from their previous status as prefectures to regional centers of administration.

Politics · 08/21/2026

Guinea creates 11 new prefectures

President Mamadi Doumbouya created 11 new prefectures on Aug. 20. A regulatory act from the Ministry of Territorial Administration and Decentralization will define their organization and functioning.

Why it mattersThe decree grants these 11 localities the status of full-exercise administrative entities.

Politics · 08/21/2026

Guinea revokes magistrate Algassimou Diallo

The Higher Council of the Judiciary permanently revoked magistrate Algassimou Diallo from the judiciary. Diallo had been suspended on Aug. 18 for failing to meet statutory obligations.

Why it mattersThe removal follows the leak of an audio recording of Diallo speaking with exiled former Prime Minister Cellou Dalein Diallo.

Business · 08/21/2026

Central Bank of Guinea warns against unauthorized trading

The Central Bank of the Republic of Guinea issued a warning on Aug. 19 regarding unauthorized crypto-asset and online financial trading platforms. The bank urged citizens to remain vigilant against these services.

Why it mattersThe alert follows a rise in pyramid-style scams that have led to major financial losses and blocked withdrawals for investors.

Sport · 08/21/2026

Guinea bids to host 2032 and 2036 Africa Cup of Nations

Prime Minister Amadou Oury Bah signed the official candidacy dossiers for Guinea to host the 2032 and 2036 editions of the competition. The signature followed a working meeting dedicated to the bid.

Why it mattersThe move formalizes the country's ambition to host the continental tournament for the first time in those years.
