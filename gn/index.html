Hilton opens first hotel in Guinea

The 196-room DoubleTree by Hilton Conakry Kaloum is set to open in 2029.

Falibra Group and SCF SA signed an agreement with Hilton to launch the DoubleTree by Hilton Conakry Kaloum hotel in Guinea's capital. The 196-room hotel, scheduled to open in 2029, marks Hilton's entry into Guinea and will be developed on the site of the Grand Hôtel de l’Indépendance in the Kaloum commune.

Located on the Atlantic waterfront near government ministries and the Central Bank, the hotel aims to host official delegations and business travelers. Falilou Diallo, General Manager of Falibra Group, stated the project will provide quality infrastructure and create jobs for Guinean hospitality talent.

The hotel will feature multiple dining options, a pool, a gym, a spa, and tennis courts. It will also offer flexible spaces for corporate meetings and events. Carlos Khneisser, Hilton's Chief Development Officer for the Middle East and Africa, noted Conakry's economic potential for the brand's expansion in Africa.

Why it mattersThe new hotel will offer international hospitality standards and create jobs in Guinea's hospitality sector.

Guinea minister suspends Siguiri governor and 21 officials

Minister Djenabou Touré suspended the interim governor, four prefects, and 17 sub-prefects on October 30, 2026.

Minister of Territorial Administration and Decentralization Djenabou Touré suspended 22 administrative officials in Guinea's Siguiri region on Friday, October 30, 2026. The suspended officials include Colonel Fayimba Mara, the interim governor and prefect of Siguiri, along with four prefects and 17 sub-prefects.

The decision, announced via national television, also named interim prefects for Doko, Dialakoro, Kintinian, and Siguirini, and listed 17 sub-prefects by name. Their duties will be temporarily handled by designated administrative secretaries and deputy sub-prefects.

These suspensions do not preclude further administrative or disciplinary actions, and any potential criminal offenses will be reported to judicial authorities. The minister's decision aims to ensure the continuity of public services in the affected areas.

Why it mattersThe suspension of administrative officials in the Siguiri region affects the governance and public services for residents there.

Maïmouna Diakhaby appointed head of Port of Conakry

President Mamadi Doumbouya names strategy advisor to lead the autonomous port authority.

President Mamadi Doumbouya appointed Maïmouna Diakhaby as the new Chairperson of the Board of Directors for the Port Autonome de Conakry (PAC). Diakhaby, currently a Presidential Advisor on strategy for growth and economic development, will lead the port's strategic direction. The Port Autonome de Conakry is one of Guinea's main economic infrastructures.

This appointment aims to strengthen governance in strategic public establishments and accelerate reforms to improve economic competitiveness. The Port of Conakry handles the majority of Guinea's trade with other countries and is essential for national and sub-regional logistics. Diakhaby's expertise in economic development and strategic planning is expected to support the modernization of infrastructure and services.

The Port Autonome de Conakry's performance is closely linked to the country's economic attractiveness and investment dynamics. As head of the Board, Diakhaby will oversee the institution's strategic orientation, ensure good governance, and contribute to modernizing infrastructure and improving port services.

Why it mattersMaïmouna Diakhaby's appointment to lead the Port of Conakry aims to improve the country's economic competitiveness and investment dynamics.

Sport · 2026-10-31

Guinea's Syli Junior wins UFOA-A U20 title

Aboubacar Keïta scored the only goal in the 40th minute to secure a 1-0 victory over Gambia.

Guinea's U20 football team, the Syli Junior, won the UFOA-A U20 tournament on Friday, October 30, 2026, by defeating Gambia 1-0 in the final. The match took place at the Mamadou-Konaté stadium in Bamako. This victory marks the first time Guinea has won this regional trophy.

Captain Ibrahima Tawel N’Diaye's powerful free kick was saved by the Gambian goalkeeper in the 26th minute. Aboubacar Keïta scored the decisive goal in the 40th minute from a corner kick. Coach Mohamed Lamine Sylla, known as Leandro, led the team to this historic win.

The Syli Junior also secured qualification for the next U20 Africa Cup of Nations. The team showed solidarity and determination throughout the second half when Gambia increased pressure. This triumph adds a regional title to Guinea's football achievements.

Why it mattersYoung Guinean footballers have won a regional championship and qualified for a continental tournament.

Kaloum FC held to draw by SOAR Académie

The team drew 0-0 for the sixth consecutive match, remaining third in Ligue 2.

Kaloum FC and SOAR Académie drew 0-0 on Friday, October 30, 2026, at the Coléah stadium during the 9th round of Ligue 2 matches. Kaloum FC had multiple scoring chances but failed to convert, while SOAR Académie grew more dangerous after 30 minutes. Both teams struggled to find the net throughout the match.

This draw marks Kaloum FC's sixth consecutive tie, a streak that impacts their championship ambitions. Despite the result, the club moves to 3rd place with 15 points. SOAR Académie secured a point away from home, climbing to 7th place with 11 points.

Kaloum FC urgently needs a win to end its frustrating series of results and stay in contention for top positions. The club's next match is important for their season.

Why it mattersKaloum FC fans are affected by their team's inability to secure a win, extending a streak of draws.
