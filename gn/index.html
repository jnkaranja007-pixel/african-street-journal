Satellite FC wins 2-0 to extend league lead

The Conakry club remains undefeated in Ligue 2 Pro after beating Tembou FC on Sunday.

Satellite FC defeated Tembou FC 2-0 at the Stade de l’Amitié in Kamsar on Sunday, November 8, 2026. Ibrahima Khaly Camara scored in the second minute from a free kick, and Alhassane Baldé added a second goal in the 34th minute for the visiting Conakry club.

This victory on the 11th matchday of Ligue 2 Pro consolidates Satellite FC's top position in the league standings with 23 points. The team is now the only undefeated club in the championship. Tembou FC remains in fifth place with 15 points.

Satellite FC now holds a five-point lead over its closest pursuers. The win sends a strong message to competitors in the race for promotion.

Why it mattersSatellite FC's victory extends their lead in Guinea's Ligue 2 Pro football championship, impacting their promotion chances.

Amadou Damaro Camara returns to Conakry after medical trip

The former National Assembly president arrived back in Guinea on November 8, 2026, concluding a weeks-long medical stay in Atlanta.

Amadou Damaro Camara, former president of Guinea's National Assembly, returned to Conakry on the night of Sunday, November 8, 2026. His arrival marked the end of a medical stay in Atlanta, United States, that lasted several weeks. Camara had departed Conakry on September 16, 2026, for specialized treatment.

The former parliamentary leader had been granted permission by competent jurisdictions to travel for medical care as part of arrangements for individuals facing charges at the Court of Repression of Economic and Financial Infractions (CRIEF). Before his departure, Camara expressed gratitude to Guinean authorities for the authorization and assistance he received.

No official communication has been made regarding the results of his treatment or any prescribed medical recommendations. Camara had stated before leaving that he would return in early November, a timeline his return on November 8 aligns with. He is expected to continue monitoring his medical situation in Conakry.
