Doumbouya names Diakité head of Nimba Mining Company

Djiba Diakité appointed chairman of Nimba Mining Company for three years, renewable.

President Mamadi Doumbouya has expanded the authority of Djiba Diakité, the current head of the Simandou strategic committee. Diakité was named chairman of Nimba Mining Company (NMC) for a three-year term, renewable, in a decree read on national television on October 23, 2026.

Nimba Mining Company is a new public entity created after the termination of the Guinea Alumina Corporation agreement, focusing on bauxite processing. Diakité, who also serves as minister director of the Presidency's cabinet, is recognized for his role in the Simandou mining megaproject. His appointment to NMC was proposed by the Ministry of Mines and Geology.

Diakité's new role is for a renewable three-year mandate. He is known as a key figure in realizing the Simandou mining project.

Why it mattersDjiba Diakité's expanded role in Nimba Mining Company affects Guinea's bauxite processing sector.

Rio Tinto SimFer trains suppliers on ethics

The mining company held a five-hour session on October 22, 2026, for partners in Coléah, Matam.

Rio Tinto SimFer conducted a training session on ethics, compliance, and service management for its suppliers on October 22, 2026. The five-hour meeting, held at the company's Coléah premises in Matam, aimed to improve professional relationships by clarifying Rio Tinto SimFer's requirements and helping partners identify and prevent risks to contractual commitments.

The training covered topics including ethical business practices, risk management for trafficking operations, application requirements, and service invoice management. Trainers emphasized Rio Tinto's core values of kindness, courage, and curiosity as guides for action, stating adherence prevents material, financial, and reputational harm for both the company and its partners. Detailed explanations were also provided on the order process from issuance to delivery.

This event provided a direct exchange forum for Rio Tinto SimFer representatives and suppliers to address concerns and clarify requirements. Participants expressed satisfaction with the initiative, appreciating the direct dialogue and improved understanding of procedures. Rio Tinto SimFer intends to build a partnership framework based on transparency, responsibility, and rule adherence.

Why it mattersSuppliers in Guinea are affected by Rio Tinto SimFer's new training on ethics and compliance for their business relationships.

Dubreka arts institute official dies in road accident

Amadou Bah, deputy director general for studies at ISAMK, died Friday after his tricycle collided with a military convoy.

Amadou Bah, the deputy director general for studies at the Mory Kanté Higher Institute of Arts (ISAMK) in Dubreka, died on Friday, October 23, 2026. The accident occurred in Kénendé, on the road between Dubreka and Kagbelen, when the tricycle he was travelling in collided with a military convoy.

Bah was on his way to Conakry for a funeral when the crash happened. He sustained severe injuries, including fractures and internal bleeding, and was taken to the Dubreka prefectural hospital. Medical staff attempted to save him, but he succumbed to his injuries.

His colleagues expressed shock, having seen him participating in institute activities just hours before. Bah was considered a key figure in the ISAMK administration, and his death is a significant loss for the university community in Dubreka and Guinea's higher education sector.

Why it mattersAmadou Bah, a senior official at a Guinean higher education institution, died in a traffic accident.

Sport · 2026-10-24

Aigles Verts de Ziama beat Tembou FC in Kankan

Abdoulaye Sylla scored the only goal in the 47th minute to give the Macenta club a win.

The Aigles Verts de Ziama secured a 1-0 victory over Tembou FC of Kamsar on Friday, October 23, 2026. The match, played at the M’Balou Mady Diakité stadium in Kankan as the opening fixture of the 8th Ligue 2 round, saw Abdoulaye Sylla score the decisive goal shortly after halftime.

Abdoulaye Sylla found the net in the 47th minute, capitalizing on a defensive lapse from Tembou FC. Despite Tembou FC's efforts to equalize in the final 30 minutes, the Aigles Verts' defense held firm until the final whistle. This win marks the third victory of the season for the Macenta club.

The victory lifts the Aigles Verts de Ziama to provisional sixth place in the league standings with 10 points. Tembou FC of Kamsar remains in fourth place with 11 points, missing an opportunity to move closer to the league leaders.

Why it mattersThe Aigles Verts de Ziama and Tembou FC are football clubs competing in Guinea's Ligue 2.
