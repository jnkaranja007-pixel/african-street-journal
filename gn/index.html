CBG outlines alumina refinery plan at Conakry mining summit

The Guinean bauxite producer said construction work has begun and it expects to invite guests to a possible opening three years from next year.

Compagnie des Bauxites de Guinée (CBG), a Guinean bauxite producer, presented its industrial plans at the first Simandou Mining Summit, held in Conakry from 11 to 14 November 2026. Chief Executive Karifa Condé said the company had started work on an alumina refinery and anticipated an opening in three years, counting from next year.

Condé made the remarks at the summit’s opening ceremony on Kassa Island. CBG’s refinery project is intended to process bauxite in Guinea and add value locally, according to the company. The summit brought together public officials, investors, mining operators and technical partners to discuss local processing, responsible production and community relations.

CBG also described its voluntary adoption of the Aluminium Stewardship Initiative’s performance standard. Pierre Dominique Traoré, the company’s group director for strategy, development and quality, said the standard addresses environmental, social and governance requirements across the aluminium value chain, from bauxite extraction to processing.

Why it mattersCBG’s refinery plan puts local bauxite processing on its development agenda, with the company anticipating a possible opening three years from next year.

Doumbouya appoints senior officials to Guinea’s labour ministry

A decree read on national television on 17 November names officials for employment, workplace safety and labour-market agencies.

Guinean President Mamadi Doumbouya appointed senior officials to the Ministry of Employment, Labour and Social Protection in a decree read on national television on 17 November 2026.

The appointments include Moustapha Diané as secretary-general and Aminata Kouyaté as principal adviser. Elhadj Mamadou Diallo was named director-general of the Guinean Agency for the Promotion of Employment and Entrepreneurship, while Alhousseine Yansané was appointed director-general of the workplace safety and health directorate.

The decree also names leaders for the National Labour Market Observatory and the National Directorate of Labour and Social Welfare. The announcement lists officials’ roles but gives no further details about their plans.

Why it mattersThe newly appointed agency director-general, Elhadj Mamadou Diallo, takes charge of Guinea’s employment and entrepreneurship promotion agency.

Soutra Money earns top-tier payment security certification

The Guinean fintech says its PCI DSS level 1 status follows a full audit and requires annual reviews of its payment systems and data safeguards.

Soutra Money, a Guinean digital financial services platform, said on 17 September it had earned level 1 certification under the Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard (PCI DSS), the highest tier of the payment-data security framework. The company is based in Conakry.

The standard applies to organisations that store, process or transmit payment data. Level 1 requires an annual full audit of system security, data protection, access management, continuous monitoring and regular penetration tests, according to the announcement.

Soutra Money said the certification can serve as a prerequisite for large-scale payment-service integrations with commercial, financial and technology partners. It described Guinea’s digital payments market as growing, with security becoming a factor for consumers, merchants, companies and banks.

Why it mattersCommercial, financial and technology partners assessing large-scale payment integrations can use the certification as a prerequisite, while Soutra must undergo a full audit each year.

Guinean commentator urges Burkina Faso to address its own crises

Amadou Tidiane Bah says Burkina Faso’s security and humanitarian challenges should command attention amid media criticism of Guinea.

Amadou Tidiane Bah, a commentator writing in Guinea, has criticised coverage of the country on Burkina Faso’s public broadcaster RTB and urged Ouagadougou to focus on its own security and humanitarian challenges. In an opinion piece published by Mosaiqueguinee, he said some broadcasts and media commentary had portrayed Guinea in a tendentious, caricatured way.

Bah said accusations about Guinea should be documented and verified, and argued that the country has the right to set its public policies and govern its mineral resources under its laws. He also said no clandestine network should replace the Guinean state’s authority on its territory.

He said Burkina Faso faces challenges in protecting its population, controlling territory and restoring stability. Bah added that Guinea remains committed to cooperation with peoples in the subregion, including Burkinabè people, while insisting that this should not be treated as disrespect for its institutions.

Why it mattersBah says Burkina Faso’s authorities face security and humanitarian challenges in protecting people and controlling territory.

Guinea beats Botswana 7-0 to reach final qualifying round

The under-23 side advances to the last round of qualifying for the 2027 Africa Cup of Nations after a 1-0 first-leg win.

Guinea’s under-23 team beat Botswana 7-0 at Conakry’s 28-Septembre Stadium on Tuesday, 17 November, to advance from the first round of qualifying for the 2027 Africa Cup of Nations. Guinea had won the first leg 1-0.

Botswana’s goalkeeper was sent off in the 17th minute after striking a Guinean player outside the penalty area, leaving the visitors with 10 players. Abdoulaye Kolev Camara opened the scoring in the 23rd minute; Mohamed Kolev Camara added a second before half-time. Abdoul Karim Camara, Aboubacar Bah and Mohamed Bangoura scored between the 50th and 56th minutes, with Bah and Bangoura adding two more late goals.

Guinea will face the Republic of the Congo in the final qualifying round for a place at the tournament. Coach Morlaye Cissé’s team needed only a draw to progress after its first-leg victory.

Why it mattersGuinea’s under-23 team must beat the Republic of the Congo in the final qualifying round to reach the 2027 tournament.
