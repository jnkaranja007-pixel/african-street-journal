Guinea U-15 crushes American Samoa 17-0

The Guinean U-15 team secured their second win in the Azerbaijan tournament with a dominant performance.

Guinea's U-15 national team achieved a resounding 17-0 victory against American Samoa on Sunday, October 25, 2026, in their second match of the U-15 World Tournament held in Azerbaijan. The young Syli national players displayed superior speed, technical skill, and finishing, overwhelming their opponents from the start.

By halftime, the match was decided, but the Guinean players continued to press, scoring 17 goals without conceding any. This performance marks one of the most significant wins in the competition so far, showcasing the team's collective fluidity and strong defense, having conceded only one goal in two games.

This second consecutive win reinforces Guinea's ambitions in the global tournament. The team's talent suggests a bright future for Guinean school football, sending a clear message to future opponents about their formidable attacking prowess.

Guinea minister launches ENA selection phase

Minister Faya François Bourouno oversaw written exams for 570 candidates seeking entry into the first promotion of the ENA.

Guinea's Minister of Public Administration Modernization and Civil Service, Faya François Bourouno, launched the third selection phase for the first promotion of the National School of Administration (ENA) on Sunday, October 25, 2026. The written examination, held at Gamal Abdel Nasser University in Conakry, involved 570 candidates vying for a spot in the new public administration diploma program.

This stage aims to evaluate the written skills of future civil servants, following an initial application review and multiple-choice questions. Minister Bourouno expressed satisfaction with the organizational setup, ensuring equal opportunities for all participants. A total of 240 candidates will advance to the next step in the selection process.

The ENA selection is built on principles of rigor, merit, and excellence, according to the minister. The selection process includes four stages, with the current phase focusing on assessing candidates' written abilities.

Bah Sadio's 50-year absence marked in Guinea

Elhadj Mamadou Bano Diallo shared memories of the iconic Guinean artist 50 years after his death in Paris.

October 25, 2026, marks 50 years since Guinean artist Bah Sadio died at Bichat-Claude-Bernard Hospital in Paris. His music, including the song 'Jeere-Leele', continues to be remembered. To commemorate this anniversary, Africaguinee.com spoke with Elhadj Mamadou Bano Diallo, aged 81, who was Bah Sadio's exile companion and guardian in Dakar.

Diallo recounted Bah Sadio's journey from clandestine escape from Guinea in 1968 to his final moments, immortalized in the song '56'. He shared intimate details about the man, the poet, and the brother he knew, covering his time in Dakar and his secret departure for Mauritania before his death.

The report details Bah Sadio's elegance and his status as a giant of Peul culture. Diallo's account offers an exceptional narrative of a fraternal friendship, spanning from the streets of Dakar to the announcement of the artist's death.

Why it mattersGuinean citizens remember the iconic artist Bah Sadio 50 years after his death.

FIFA representative arrives for Guinean football election

Fatou Camara will monitor the election of the new president and executive committee of the Guinean Football Federation.

FIFA representative Fatou Camara arrived in Conakry on October 24 to oversee the Guinean Football Federation's (FGF) general assembly and elections. The assembly, scheduled for October 27, will elect a new president and executive committee members. This follows the dissolution of the previous bureau led by Sory Doumbouya.

Camara, FIFA's Senior Member Association Development Manager for Africa, was met at Ahmed Sékou Touré International Airport by Ibrahima Blasco Barry, the FGF's Secretary General. Her presence signals FIFA's close attention to the electoral process and its importance for the governance of Guinean football.

The election aims to establish new leadership for the FGF. The international football body's involvement underscores the significance of this transition for the sport's administration in Guinea.

Why it mattersThe election will determine the new leadership of Guinean football.
