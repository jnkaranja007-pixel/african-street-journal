Guinea president calls elections for November 29 and December 20

President Mamadi Doumbouya decreed voter turnout for regional and senatorial polls, marking the final stage of general elections.

Guinea's President Mamadi Doumbouya has called voters to the polls for regional elections on November 29, 2026, and senatorial elections on December 20, 2026. The decrees were announced via the national television broadcast on Tuesday, November 3, 2026. These elections represent the final phase of the general elections planned under the country's constitution.

The presidential decrees were made public on Tuesday, November 3, 2026. These upcoming votes are intended to complete the general electoral process outlined in the constitution. The specific dates were confirmed through official channels.

In separate decrees on the same day, President Doumbouya also admitted 144 military personnel to retirement and dismissed 22 territorial administrators in Siguiri. The administrators were removed due to involvement in illegal gold mining and environmental degradation.

Why it mattersGuinean citizens will participate in regional and senatorial elections, completing the general election cycle.

Lambanyi commune targets 100 billion francs by 2028

Mayor Baba Alimou Barry aims for 30 billion Guinean francs in 2027 and 100 billion by 2028.

The commune of Lambanyi held an extraordinary session on November 3, 2026, to discuss financial resource mobilization and service modernization. Mayor Baba Alimou Barry announced ambitious revenue targets of 30 billion Guinean francs for 2027 and 100 billion for 2028.

These initiatives include a comprehensive taxpayer census, enhanced revenue collection capacity, and the digitalization of revenue and sanitation services. The mayor stated that financial leakage is a significant problem, and a proper census will help understand the commune's fiscal potential. All proposals were unanimously adopted by the councilors.

The commune seeks to improve administrative efficiency and contribute to local development through these modernization efforts. The mayor expressed satisfaction with the unanimous approval, emphasizing unity and collaboration with councilors to improve living conditions for households and working conditions for traders.

Why it mattersResidents of Lambanyi will experience improved local services and potentially better living conditions due to increased commune revenue.

COBAD distributes school supplies in Boké

The mining company also inaugurated four water wells in the Kourawel and Dougoula areas.

The Compagnie de bauxites et d’alumine de Dian-Dian (COBAD) distributed school kits to students in Dougoula and Kourawel on October 27 and 28, 2026. The company also inaugurated four water wells, including one at the Kourawel primary school.

COBAD's general director, M. Pochikaev, stated that investing in youth education is investing in Guinea's future and that the company's responsibility extends beyond mining to community well-being. The interim prefect of Kamsar, Lieutenant-Colonel Diaka Doumbouya, thanked COBAD for supporting students and parents.

Lieutenant-Colonel Doumbouya encouraged other mining companies to follow COBAD's example in improving local living conditions. COBAD aims to continue its investments in Guinea and strengthen cooperation with authorities.

Why it mattersStudents in Dougoula and Kourawel received school supplies, and communities gained access to new water sources.

Boubacar Barry found dead after alleged kidnapping

The body of a Conakry businessman, allegedly abducted from his home, was returned to his family for burial.

The body of Boubacar Barry, who was allegedly kidnapped from his Lambanyi home, was returned to his family on Tuesday, November 3, 2026. The exact circumstances of his death remain unclear as his grieving family travels to Siguiri for burial on Wednesday.

An associate confirmed the family received Barry's body and is en route to Siguiri for the funeral, scheduled for Wednesday, November 4, 2026, barring any last-minute changes. Barry, a merchant from Dalaba, was reportedly abducted on the night of Thursday, October 30, 2026.

The precise details of the abduction and the conditions under which Barry's body was discovered are yet to be clarified.

Why it mattersBoubacar Barry's family is undertaking a journey to Siguiri for his burial following his alleged kidnapping and death.

Kankan police seize 60 cartons of shisha products

Police discovered 15 bags of charcoal and flavouring near the Farako 2 bus station.

Police in Kankan, Guinea, seized 60 cartons of shisha products, including charcoal and flavouring, on Tuesday, November 3, 2026. The items were found in abandoned bags near the Farako 2 bus station.

Central Police Commissioner Nanamoudou Magassouba stated the operation followed a requisition from the Kankan public prosecutor to enforce a January 5, 2021 ban on shisha. The seized goods included 50 cartons of Black Tiger charcoal and 10 cartons of JBR flavouring.

Investigations are underway to identify the owners of the 15 bags, each weighing 150 kg, which were found by the Special Police Intervention Brigade. No one has yet claimed the abandoned parcels or provided information on their origin or destination.

Why it matters60 cartons of shisha products were seized by police in Kankan, Guinea, as part of an ongoing ban on the substance.
