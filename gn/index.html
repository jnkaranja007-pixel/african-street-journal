Clashes at Balato leave 10 dead and 13 injured in Guinea

The government says the violence broke out during a patrol enforcing a ban on illegal gold panning in the Siguiri region.

Violent clashes broke out on Wednesday, 11 November, at Sètiguiyah in the Balato district of Kintinian prefecture, in Guinea's Siguiri region. The government says at least 10 people were killed and 13 were injured, and that significant property damage was caused.

The clashes started during a control operation meant to enforce a ban on illegal gold panning, according to a communiqué from the Ministry of Territorial Administration and Decentralisation issued on Thursday, 12 November. Officials said the figures were provisional, and condemned the violence while calling on residents to stay calm.

The authorities have referred the matter to the courts to establish responsibility for the violence. The communiqué did not give a timetable for the judicial process.

Cellcom Guinée workers start indefinite strike

Union members refuse to work, even without minimum service, over planned technical leave for 45 employees.

Workers at Cellcom Guinée began an indefinite general strike on Thursday, November 12, 2026, refusing to go to their workplaces.

Alexandre Niambalamou, the union's general secretary, stated the decision follows the CEO's plan to place 45 out of 87 employees on technical leave without proper compensation discussions. He added that the workers' access to their computers and user accounts was deactivated.

The union is demanding that the company negotiate with the General Labour Inspectorate and is calling on the president to intervene to save Cellcom, a company that has faced financial difficulties since 2023.

Politics · 2026-11-13

Guinea's special prosecutor clears former minister Zénab Nabaya Dramé

Alphonse Charles Wright says the embezzlement accusations against her were not backed by any evidence.

The Special Prosecutor of the Court for the Repression of Economic and Financial Offences (CRIEF), Alphonse Charles Wright, has cleared Zénab Nabaya Dramé, Guinea's former minister of technical education and vocational training, on Wednesday, 12 November 2026. Dramé had been accused of embezzling more than 200 billion Guinean francs.

Speaking to Télé 24, Wright said he had read the case file piece by piece and that the accusations were not based on any solid material evidence. He said the money allegedly embezzled had not disappeared and was still there, and he criticised the journalist who first reported the claim, Youssouf Boundou, for putting it in conditional terms.

Wright warned that Boundou could face legal action, saying he must either explain the basis of his report or answer for obstructing justice or acting under corruption. He also said a judge may charge someone only where there are serious and consistent clues.

Why it mattersDramé's name is now cleared of the 200 billion franc charge, and the journalist who reported it faces possible prosecution.

Business · 2026-11-13

Super Sécurité professionalises private security in Guinea

The Conakry-based firm has secured over 100 clients and operates at more than 150 sites nationwide.

Super Sécurité, a Guinean company founded in November 2022 in Conakry, is transforming private security practices by moving beyond simple agent presence to a comprehensive approach for protecting people and facilities. The firm aims to professionalise the sector by offering services including risk identification, tailored instructions, agent training, and regular supervision, alongside electronic security and digitalisation.

The company, based in Lambanyi, Conakry, has expanded its operations to key economic zones such as Boké, Sangaredji, Kamsar, Forécariah, and Boffa. Super Sécurité reported deploying agents to the Ashapura mining site in Houda, Sangaredji, in October 2026. It has also conducted over 35 specialised training sessions, focusing on first aid and fire prevention and fighting, to maintain agent skills.

Super Sécurité integrates technology like video surveillance and access control, and offers a client application for real-time monitoring and anomaly reporting. Thierno Sita Diallo, the company's director general, discussed the firm's creation, development, and ambitions in an interview, highlighting its focus on enhancing team efficiency through technology without replacing human presence.

Why it mattersSuper Sécurité's expansion to over 150 sites nationwide impacts businesses and infrastructure across Guinea by enhancing their security measures.

Sport · 2026-11-13

Guinea beats Eritrea 2-1 at home in CAN 2027 qualifier

Aguibou Camara captained the Syli national team, which trailed South Africa in Group D before the win, while only one qualifying place is available in the group.

Guinea's Syli national team beat Eritrea 2-1 on Thursday, 12 November 2026, at the 28-Septembre stadium in Conakry, in the third round of qualifiers for the 2027 Africa Cup of Nations (CAN). Coach Paulo Duarte, a Portuguese, fielded Mohamed Camara Richard in goal, with Aboubacar K. Keita, Mohamed Soumah, Ibrahim Brèze Fofana and Sékou Oumar Sylla in defence.

Aguibou Camara wore the captain's armband and played in midfield alongside Ousmane Diabaté and Morlaye Sylla. Aliou Baldé, Yadaly Diaby and Kerfala Dada Cissoko led the attack. Before the match Guinea were second in Group D, behind South Africa.

Only one qualifying place is open in Group D, because Kenya, a co-host, has already qualified automatically for the final phase.

Why it mattersOnly one qualifying place is open in Group D, so Guinea's win keeps them in contention for that single ticket against South Africa.
