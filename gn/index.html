CAF approves Guinea's 28 Septembre Stadium

GUICOPRES BTP completed renovations allowing national teams and clubs to play at home.

The Confederation of African Football (CAF) has confirmed the approval of Guinea's 28 Septembre Stadium in Conakry on October 27, 2026. This follows a provisional approval granted on August 15, 2026, which was valid only for September and required specific corrections. GUICOPRES BTP, a subsidiary of Groupe GUICOPRES, undertook the renovation work to meet international standards.

The stadium's natural pitch, stands, VIP boxes, and media facilities have been upgraded. New features include a Media Center, a press conference room, and a mixed zone. The improvements also encompass access ramps, parking, and elevators, ensuring compliance with CAF's requirements. This approval allows Guinea's national team and clubs to host matches in Conakry.

The Syli National played its first home match since June 2022 against Kenya on October 1, 2026, in CAN 2027 qualifiers. This renovation project, completed by a Guinean company, aims to restore the stadium's status as a major venue for Guinean football after years of teams playing abroad.

Why it mattersThe national football team and Guinean clubs can now host matches in Conakry, ending years of playing abroad.

Guinean business group seeks president's help for foreign investment

GOHA International president Chérif Mohamed Abdallah Haïdara wants Egyptian, Moroccan, Turkish, and Arab investors to start projects in Guinea.

Chérif Mohamed Abdallah Haïdara, president of GOHA International, has asked Guinea's President Mamadi Doumbouya to support foreign investment in the country. Haïdara wants investors from Egypt, Morocco, Turkey, and Arab nations to begin projects in sectors like agriculture, agro-industry, infrastructure, and health.

Haïdara stated that his organization has made significant contacts and signed partnership agreements with entities including the Moroccan Chamber of Commerce, the Federation of Egyptian Industries, and the Arab Union of Women Investors. These partnerships create considerable opportunities for Guinea, with investors ready to commit financial resources.

The GOHA International president emphasized that these investments could significantly boost job creation, reduce poverty, and strengthen the national private sector. He is requesting the Guinean authorities' support to facilitate these partners' arrival and project realization in Guinea.

Why it mattersGuinean citizens and entrepreneurs stand to benefit from job creation and skills transfer through these foreign investments.

Business · 2026-10-29

Rio Tinto SimFer shows Simandou mine operations

Media visited blocks 3 and 4 in Beyla, Guinea, to see ore processing and worker training.

Rio Tinto SimFer hosted a two-day media tour of its Simandou mining blocks 3 and 4 in Beyla, Guinea, from October 28 to 29, 2026. Reporters observed the ore crushing, storage, and transport processes. The visit aimed to showcase the operational realities of the large-scale mining project.

During the immersion, journalists toured key facilities including a training center, a nursery, crushing installations, and storage areas. They also saw how the company trains workers and implements safety measures. A training center official explained the practical risk demonstration zone, designed to bridge theory and practice for workers with limited formal education.

The tour covered the entire ore treatment process, from crushing to transport towards the port. Media saw practical training zones for risk identification, health and safety, tire mounting, and road safety. The visit also included demonstrations on fatigue and traffic management.

Why it mattersMedia professionals visited Rio Tinto SimFer's Simandou mining operations in Beyla to observe ore processing and worker training.
