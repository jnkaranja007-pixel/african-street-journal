Politics · 2026-08-20

CSM revokes Supreme Court prosecutor Algassimou Diallo

The Superior Council of the Magistracy revoked Algassimou Diallo, the general prosecutor at the Supreme Court, on Aug. 20. The disciplinary action followed a referral by Justice Minister Ibrahima Sory 2 Tounkara regarding failures to meet statutory obligations.

Why it mattersThe decision removes the general prosecutor from his post and may lead to his permanent disbarment.

Rio Tinto SimFer delivers 5 schools in Beyla

The SimFer joint venture delivered five new schools to educational authorities in Beyla. According to the company, the facilities provide education access for more than 1,700 students from communities near the Simandou Project.

Why it mattersThe new infrastructure increases classroom capacity for 1,700 children in the riverine communities of the mining project.

Business · 2026-08-20

BCRG warns against unauthorized crypto-asset platforms

The Central Bank of the Republic of Guinea (BCRG) issued a warning against the use of illegal trading and crypto-asset investment platforms. The bank stated in an official communique that it has not issued any legal authorizations for these financial services.

Why it mattersThe alert warns the public that investments in these unauthorized online platforms lack legal protection from the central bank.

Fire in Simambossya kills 2 children

Two children died in a house fire in the Cantine sector of the Simambossya neighborhood on Aug. 19. The blaze occurred around 1 p.m. in a family residence in the Lambanyi urban commune.

Why it mattersThe incident resulted in the deaths of two children and affected residents of the Simambossya neighborhood.
