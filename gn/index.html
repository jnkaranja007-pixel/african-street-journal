Guinean advertising association seeks unity

The Association des Professionnels de la Publicité (APP) met an end to internal divisions to boost economic development.

The Association des Professionnels de la Publicité en Guinée (APP) met on Saturday, October 30, 2026, in Conakry to unify and restructure the advertising sector. Members of the new executive bureau and industry actors gathered to end internal divisions and build a strong, influential corporation for economic development.

Adama Diané, the new APP president, stated that despite past disagreements, the association is now talking again and aims to become a force for proposals. He emphasized that shared goals of defending interests, strengthening solidarity, and promoting the advertising sector in Guinea are transforming individual ambitions into a shared vision.

Diané highlighted the strategic role of advertising in Guinea's socio-economic fabric, calling for the APP to become a major institutional partner. The association's mission includes defending members' interests, promoting professions, and improving the business environment, contributing to national development efforts.

Why it mattersThe Association des Professionnels de la Publicité (APP) aims to strengthen its members' economic interests and contribute to Guinea's socio-economic development.

Mouctar Bah feted for 30 years at RFI, 38 at AFP

A surprise party in Conakry celebrated the veteran journalist's long career with Radio France internationale and Agence France-Presse.

Veteran journalist Mouctar Bah was surprised on Friday, October 30, 2026, in Conakry with a celebration marking his 30 years with Radio France internationale (RFI) and 38 years with Agence France-Presse (AFP). Dr. Safiatou Diallo orchestrated the event at the Centre international de recherche et documentation (CIRD), initially deceiving Bah into believing he was helping plan a party for her husband.

Bah arrived at the CIRD unaware he was the guest of honor, only to find family, friends, colleagues, and collaborators gathered. Video testimonials and speeches praised his experience, humility, helpfulness, and humor. Dr. Diallo, the event's initiator, expressed her delight at Bah's surprise and the heartfelt tributes.

Overwhelmed, Bah shared his emotion and gratitude, recalling significant events in Guinea he covered, including elections since 1998, rebel attacks on borders, and political demonstrations for freedom of expression and democracy. He described the tributes as touching and a profound reflection on his career.

Why it mattersMouctar Bah, a journalist covering Guinea for decades, was honored for his extensive professional career.

Yimbaya alumni clean up former school

Former students of Yimbaya High School organized a cleanup day to improve learning conditions and hygiene.

Alumni of Yimbaya High School in Conakry held a cleanup day on Saturday, October 31, 2026, to improve their former school. The Amicale des anciens élèves du lycée Yimbaya, with the school's administration and parent association, led the effort to clean, weed, and promote hygiene. This initiative is part of the alumni association's annual action plan to enhance the learning environment.

Mohamed Kaba, president of the alumni association, stated the cleanup was an act of gratitude and essential for learning. Activities included cleaning courtyards, classrooms, and school surroundings, maintaining water points, and installing waste bins. The day also featured hygiene awareness sessions for students. Other alumni groups, like Amicale Kötö Barry, joined the effort, emphasizing their shared connection to Yimbaya High School.

School principal Sékouba Oularé praised the alumni's commitment, calling it a concrete act of solidarity and responsibility. Matoto's deputy mayor, Aïssatou Barry, officially launched the event, encouraging students to maintain cleanliness daily. The initiative aims to foster a cleaner and healthier environment conducive to academic success.

Why it mattersFormer students of Yimbaya High School in Conakry are directly involved in improving their former educational institution.
