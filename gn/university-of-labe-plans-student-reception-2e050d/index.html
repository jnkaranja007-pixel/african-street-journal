university of labé plans student reception

the university has established a mixed commission to address accommodation and integration for new students.

Source: Africaguinee · 2026-10-28 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

The University of Labé in Guinea has developed a strategic plan to welcome and integrate students for the 2026-2027 academic year. Rector Dr. Mohamed Chérif Sow announced the initiative on October 26, outlining measures to improve the learning environment and modernize the higher education system.

A mixed commission, comprising university officials, dormitory managers, local authorities, and student representatives, has been formed to find sustainable solutions for student reception and accommodation. Dr. Sow stated that the university will work diligently to ensure the best learning conditions, noting the expansion of academic programs from 10 to over 30.

This plan aligns with the Ministry of Higher Education's roadmap and aims to inform the university community and the public about the new support systems. The university also highlighted the Guinean state's attention to the sector's needs and its efforts to improve quality.

Why it mattersStudents arriving at the University of Labé will benefit from improved reception and accommodation services.
