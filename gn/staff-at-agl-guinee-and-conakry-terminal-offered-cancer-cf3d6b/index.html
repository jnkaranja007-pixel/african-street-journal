Staff at AGL Guinée and Conakry Terminal offered cancer screening through 9 December

More than 500 employees joined prevention sessions from 16 to 18 November, with urologists answering questions anonymously.

Source: Africaguinee · 2026-11-26 · 1 min read

AGL Guinée, part of the AGL group, and Conakry Terminal held sessions on male cancers across their sites in Guinea from 16 to 18 November 2026, bringing together more than 500 employees. The companies said the campaign covered prevention, warning signs and early detection.

Two urologists joined staff from human resources, medical services and communications for sessions at sites including the general management office, operations and technical departments, Burkina park and the Kagbelen logistics platform. Employees could also submit questions anonymously to urologists. A screening campaign for staff runs from 23 November to 9 December.

Ibrahima Diallo, the group's country managing director in Guinea, said the screening campaign was open to employees through 9 December.

Why it mattersEmployees at both companies can book screening through 9 December, with the campaign described as the first of its kind for men at the group in Guinea.
