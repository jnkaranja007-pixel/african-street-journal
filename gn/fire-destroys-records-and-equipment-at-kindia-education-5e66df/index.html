Fire destroys records and equipment at Kindia education office

The fire damaged the prefectural director’s office after reported electricity-supply disruptions, and investigations are pending.

Source: Guinee Matin · 2026-11-19 · 1 min read

A fire damaged the office of the prefectural education director in Kindia, Guinea, during the night of Tuesday to Wednesday, 18 November 2026. It destroyed furniture, curtains, work equipment and several administrative documents, according to Guinee Matin’s correspondent in the city.

Arabé Condé, the prefectural education director, said staff had spent the day without electricity until she left the office on Tuesday. A section chief reported a smell of smoke the following morning, and the office was found largely consumed by fire. Condé said earlier electrical problems had damaged a reprography machine and a printer, and she suspects voltage fluctuations may be linked to the blaze.

The cause has not been established; investigations are pending. Condé said her desk and small work area were spared.

Why it mattersKindia’s prefectural education office must account for damaged administrative records and equipment while investigators determine the cause.
