Guinea suspends Infosbruts and director for three months

The media regulator said the site published electoral propaganda for a Lélouma Senate candidate before the Supreme Court issued its final candidate list.

Sources: Africaguinee, Mosaiqueguinee and Guinee Matin · 2026-11-28 · 1 min read

Guinea’s High Authority for Communication (HAC), the media regulator, suspended the Infosbruts news site and its publishing director, journalist Idrissa Sampiring Diallo, for three months on Friday, 27 November. The order took effect on the date it was signed.

The HAC said Infosbruts had carried content it considered electoral propaganda in favour of a Senate candidate in Lélouma prefecture. Its decision said the publication violated election rules because it appeared before the Supreme Court published the final list of candidates.

During the suspension, the site is barred from publishing and Diallo from journalistic activity anywhere in Guinea, the regulator said. In a letter reported by Guinee Matin, Diallo apologised for a publication he said was made inadvertently and accepted responsibility.

Why it mattersInfosbruts and Diallo must stop publishing and journalistic work throughout Guinea for three months under the regulator’s order.
