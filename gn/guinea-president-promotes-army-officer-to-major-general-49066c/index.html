Guinea president promotes army officer to major general

General of brigade Séna Camara was elevated to the rank of major general on November 2, 2026.

Source: Africaguinee · 2026-11-03 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

President Mamadi Doumbouya promoted General of brigade Séna Camara to the rank of major general on Monday, November 2, 2026. The decree announcing Camara's elevation was read on national television (RTG). Camara serves as the Inspector General of the Guinean armed forces.

This promotion signifies a renewed trust from President Doumbouya in Sennah Camara, who holds a key position within the Guinean Armed Forces. The decision was officially communicated through a decree broadcast on national television.

Separately, at the CRIEF court, the public prosecutor requested the acquittal of court bailiffs Aboubacar Camara and Alhassane Bah. They are accused of corruption and other offenses related to their duties. The prosecution's request followed their testimonies before the court.

Why it mattersPresident Mamadi Doumbouya has promoted a high-ranking military official, indicating shifts in military leadership.
