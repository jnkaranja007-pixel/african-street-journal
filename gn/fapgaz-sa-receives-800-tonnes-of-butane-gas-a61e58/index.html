FAPGAZ-SA receives 800 tonnes of butane gas

The arrival of the gas at Conakry Port aims to meet rising domestic demand and ensure regular supply.

Source: Mosaiqueguinee · 2026-11-08 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

FAPGAZ-SA announced the arrival of a ship carrying 800 tonnes of butane gas at the Autonomous Port of Conakry on Saturday, November 7, 2026. This delivery is part of the company's strategy to ensure a steady supply of domestic gas to the national market.

The significant cargo is intended to address a growing demand for domestic gas. The company stated this shipment will help guarantee a regular supply for consumers.

The arrival of the butane gas is a direct response to increasing consumer needs in Guinea.

Why it mattersGuinean households will benefit from a more regular supply of domestic gas.
