Guinea court adjourns trial of prison guard over hospital inmate escape

The Dixinn court postponed the case to 30 November after the prosecution asked for more time to investigate the escape.

Source: Guinee Matin · 2026-11-17 · 1 min read

The trial of two prison guards and a state nurse, charged over the escape of an inmate from the Sino-Guinean Hospital in Conakry, was adjourned on Monday, 16 November 2026, at the Dixinn Court of First Instance. Two of the three accused were present; the nurse, Yacouba Condé, was absent.

After a judicial visit to the escape site, prosecutors said they had reviewed surveillance footage that set the inmate's movements in motion, but said gaps remained in the file. They asked for more time to question the Telecommunications Regulatory Authority (ARPT) about the inmate.

Guard Lancinet Traoré, who is in detention, asked for provisional release, citing poor health. Prosecutors opposed it because of the seriousness of the charges, and the court refused. The case resumes on 30 November, when Condé is due to appear.

Why it mattersLancinet Traoré remains in detention while the court waits on the ARPT inquiry and the absent nurse's appearance.
