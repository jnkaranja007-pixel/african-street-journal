Diaka Camara Foundation trains students at three Conakry schools on abuse

The sessions cover physical, psychological and sexual violence, harassment and early marriage, with Guinea’s child-protection office supplying trainers.

Source: Mosaiqueguinee · 2026-11-26 · 1 min read

The Diaka Camara Foundation has started training students at three schools in Conakry on gender-based violence, as part of an international campaign running from 25 November to 10 December. The schools are Lycée 2-Octobre and Collège Château d’Eau in Kaloum, and Lycée-Collège 1er-Mars in Matam.

Lamine Kaba Barry, a project officer at the foundation, said the sessions explain forms and consequences of abuse and how students can seek help. The Office for the Protection of Gender, Children and Morals (OPROGEM) joined the programme and provided the female trainers working in the schools. Topics include physical, psychological and sexual violence, harassment and early marriage.

OPROGEM director Marie Gomez urged the foundation to extend its work to other parts of Guinea. She also cited a library the foundation installed at a school.

Why it mattersStudents at the three schools are being taught how to recognise abuse and where to seek help, with OPROGEM trainers supporting the sessions.
