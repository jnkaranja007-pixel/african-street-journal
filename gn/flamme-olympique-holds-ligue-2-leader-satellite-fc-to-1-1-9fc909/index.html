Flamme Olympique holds Ligue 2 leader Satellite FC to 1-1

Mohamed Yattara equalised in the 59th minute, leaving Satellite level with Séquence FC on 24 points at the halfway stage.

Source: Guinee Matin · 2026-11-21 · 1 min read

Flamme Olympique de Guinée drew 1-1 with Satellite FC at Petit Sory de Nongo stadium on Friday, 20 November, in the final match of Guinea’s Ligue 2 first half. Satellite, the league leader, went ahead through Ibrahima Khaly Camara’s header in the 34th minute. Mohamed Yattara equalised with a header for Flamme in the 59th.

Satellite finished the first half level with Séquence FC on 24 points, but stayed top with a goal difference of +7. Flamme, which began the match in last place, has 8 points after the draw.

The result leaves Flamme at the bottom of the table as the return phase approaches; the draw did not immediately resolve the club’s position in the fight to avoid relegation.

Why it mattersFlamme Olympique remains last on 8 points, and the draw leaves the club’s fight to avoid relegation unresolved before the return phase.
