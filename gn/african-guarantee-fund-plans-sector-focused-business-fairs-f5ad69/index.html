African guarantee fund plans sector-focused business fairs in Guinea

The fund says it will discuss further editions with Guinean authorities after the first event outside the West African monetary union.

Sources: Guinee Matin and Africaguinee · 2026-11-22 · 1 min read

The 12th Banks and SMEs Fair for the West African Economic and Monetary Union (UEMOA) ended in Conakry on Saturday, 21 November, after two days of discussions on financing small businesses. Guinea, which is not a UEMOA member, hosted the event for the first time at the initiative of the African Fund for Guarantee and Economic Cooperation (FAGACE).

FAGACE president Ngueto Tiraïna Yambaye said the fair brought banks, microfinance institutions and businesses together to build confidence and speed up financing. Atlantic Microfinance network officer Adama Yaya Diallo said her institution made contacts with companies that could qualify for funding.

Yambaye said FAGACE would discuss special editions with Guinean authorities and its administrators, potentially holding several fairs each year. He said they could focus on financing agriculture and livestock.

Why it mattersGuinean businesses seeking agricultural and livestock finance will be the focus of proposed special fairs that FAGACE plans to discuss with authorities.
