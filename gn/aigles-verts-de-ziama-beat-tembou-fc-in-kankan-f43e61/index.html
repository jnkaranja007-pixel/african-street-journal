Aigles Verts de Ziama beat Tembou FC in Kankan

Abdoulaye Sylla scored the only goal in the 47th minute to give the Macenta club a win.

Source: Guinee Matin · 2026-10-24 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

The Aigles Verts de Ziama secured a 1-0 victory over Tembou FC of Kamsar on Friday, October 23, 2026. The match, played at the M’Balou Mady Diakité stadium in Kankan as the opening fixture of the 8th Ligue 2 round, saw Abdoulaye Sylla score the decisive goal shortly after halftime.

Abdoulaye Sylla found the net in the 47th minute, capitalizing on a defensive lapse from Tembou FC. Despite Tembou FC's efforts to equalize in the final 30 minutes, the Aigles Verts' defense held firm until the final whistle. This win marks the third victory of the season for the Macenta club.

The victory lifts the Aigles Verts de Ziama to provisional sixth place in the league standings with 10 points. Tembou FC of Kamsar remains in fourth place with 11 points, missing an opportunity to move closer to the league leaders.

Why it mattersThe Aigles Verts de Ziama and Tembou FC are football clubs competing in Guinea's Ligue 2.
