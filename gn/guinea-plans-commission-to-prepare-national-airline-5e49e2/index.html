Guinea plans commission to prepare national airline

Transport Minister Ousmane Gaoual Diallo says the body will work with the finance ministry on plans to establish a carrier within two years.

Source: Mosaiqueguinee · 2026-11-20 · 1 min read

Guinea is preparing to establish a national airline within the next two years, Transport Minister Ousmane Gaoual Diallo said in a televised statement on Wednesday, 18 November. The plan follows an ambition set by President Mamadi Doumbouya.

Diallo said his ministry and the Ministry of Economy and Finance are preparing a commission to turn the president’s instruction into an operational plan. The commission is also expected to address the airport infrastructure needed for a Guinean carrier to resume flying.

The minister said the government would work towards getting the country’s flag carrier back in the air as soon as possible; no launch date was announced.

Why it mattersThe transport and finance ministries must organise the commission and plan the airport infrastructure needed for a carrier within the president’s two-year ambition.
