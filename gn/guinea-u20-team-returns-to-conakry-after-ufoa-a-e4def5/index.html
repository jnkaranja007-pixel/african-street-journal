Guinea U20 team returns to Conakry after UFOA-A championship win

The Syli National junior team was welcomed by enthusiastic supporters at Ahmed-Sékou-Touré airport following their victory in Bamako.

Source: Africaguinee · 2026-11-03 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

The Syli National junior team returned to Conakry on Monday, November 2, 2026, after winning the UFOA-A U20 tournament in Bamako. Numerous supporters gathered at the Ahmed-Sékou-Touré international airport to celebrate the young champions' victory. The team was also congratulated by Mamady Doumbouya, the new president of the Guinean Football Federation.

Outside the airport, fans created a festive atmosphere as the players boarded a bus. This reception highlighted the pride and joy felt by Guineans for the achievement of their youth team in Bamako. The victory also qualifies the team for the next African Nations Championship.

Meanwhile, Syli National coach Paulo Duarte announced a 25-player squad on November 2, 2026, for upcoming 2027 Africa Cup of Nations qualifiers against Eritrea on November 12 and November 16. Only one player from the Guinean league, Yacouba Gnagna Barry of Horoya AC, was selected.

Why it mattersThe Syli National junior football team's victory in the UFOA-A U20 tournament has brought national pride and recognition to the young players.
