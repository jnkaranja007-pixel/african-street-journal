Guinea hosts Simandou Mining Summit for investors

Conakry will connect global investors with Guinean businesses from November 11 to 14, 2026.

Source: Guinee Matin · 2026-11-08 · 2 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Guinea will host the first Simandou Mining Summit in Conakry from November 11 to 14, 2026, aiming to attract global investors and highlight national businesses. The summit intends to position Guinea as a major investment destination, focusing on the Simandou mining project and fostering economic partnerships. Ibrahima Dieng, founder of Easycom, stated that the event's success will be measured by its ability to make national entrepreneurship a key player in the country's economic transformation.

The summit will bring together mining operators, investors, financial institutions, and economic partners in Conakry. This event follows the recent operation of the Simandou project, including the first passenger train circulation on October 12. Guinea aims to convert international attention into structured investments, contracts, jobs, and opportunities for national companies, as part of its Simandou 2040 program for infrastructure modernization and economic diversification.

Conakry will serve as a platform for negotiations, potentially accelerating partnerships that would normally take months to explore. Guinean businesses in sectors like industrial maintenance, logistics, public works, security, and technical supplies can find new opportunities. Dieng emphasized that the summit should showcase Guinea's local skills and entrepreneurial fabric as capable of supporting major development projects, ensuring national companies benefit from Simandou's generated investments.

Why it mattersThe Simandou Mining Summit in Conakry aims to create investment and job opportunities for Guinean businesses and citizens.
