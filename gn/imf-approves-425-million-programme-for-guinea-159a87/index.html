IMF approves $425 million programme for Guinea

The 2026-2029 agreement backs reforms to public finances, domestic revenue collection and budget transparency, and supports the Simandou 2040 vision.

Source: Africaguinee · 2026-11-24 · 1 min read

The International Monetary Fund’s board approved a 2026-2029 economic programme for Guinea on Monday, 23 November, worth 310.59 million special drawing rights, or about $425 million. The agreement is supported by the IMF’s Extended Credit Facility and Extended Fund Facility.

Guinea’s economy and finance minister said the programme backs reforms to public financial management, domestic revenue collection and budget transparency. Prime Minister Amadou Oury Bah said the agreement followed difficult negotiations and would accompany the Simandou mining project and the Simandou 2040 development programme.

Bah said IMF backing helps the government build credibility with technical and financial partners and makes lower-cost financing more accessible. The government is seeking to reassure investors and lenders about its commitment to sounder public finances and modern economic management.

Why it mattersGuinea’s government must carry out the programme’s public-finance and budget-transparency reforms while seeking financing for Simandou 2040.
