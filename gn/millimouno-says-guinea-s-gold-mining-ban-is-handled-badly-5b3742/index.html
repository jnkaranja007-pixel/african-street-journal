Millimouno says Guinea's gold mining ban is handled badly

The lawmaker says the state's method has strained relations with neighbouring gold-producing countries, while the ban itself is not disputed.

Sources: Africaguinee and Mosaiqueguinee · 2026-11-15 · 1 min read

Guinea's government has banned illegal and unregulated gold mining in Haute-Guinée to halt environmental damage. Faya Lansana Millimouno, a deputy and politician, told Africaguinee.com on Saturday, 14 November 2026, that the ban itself is not disputed, but that the government is going about it the wrong way.

Millimouno said the machine-driven mining that has ruined the environment is mostly run by officials based in Conakry, not by artisanal miners working with hand tools. He said the first step of the ban has put Guinea in difficulty with neighbours, noting that Ghana, Burkina Faso and Mali also have gold mines, where Guineans work too.

Mamadou Bhoye Sall, president of the Nouvelles Forces Démocratiques (NFD), told Mosaiqueguinee.com this weekend that he backs the ban but not the violence. Clashes between security forces and residents in Siguiri over the ban have caused deaths, the outlet reported.

Why it mattersTraders and miners in Siguiri and Haute-Guinée face a crackdown whose methods are now being disputed in parliament and by party leaders.
