Doumbouya appoints senior officials to Guinea’s labour ministry

A decree read on national television on 17 November names officials for employment, workplace safety and labour-market agencies.

Sources: Guinee Matin, Africaguinee and Mosaiqueguinee · 2026-11-18 · 1 min read

Guinean President Mamadi Doumbouya appointed senior officials to the Ministry of Employment, Labour and Social Protection in a decree read on national television on 17 November 2026.

The appointments include Moustapha Diané as secretary-general and Aminata Kouyaté as principal adviser. Elhadj Mamadou Diallo was named director-general of the Guinean Agency for the Promotion of Employment and Entrepreneurship, while Alhousseine Yansané was appointed director-general of the workplace safety and health directorate.

The decree also names leaders for the National Labour Market Observatory and the National Directorate of Labour and Social Welfare. The announcement lists officials’ roles but gives no further details about their plans.

Why it mattersThe newly appointed agency director-general, Elhadj Mamadou Diallo, takes charge of Guinea’s employment and entrepreneurship promotion agency.
