Urbaine Découverte names young Guinean talents

The third edition of the talent competition featured 50 candidates in music, slam, and modeling.

Source: Guinee Matin · 2026-11-05 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

The third edition of Urbaine Découverte concluded on Friday, October 30, 2026, at the Kofi Annan University of Guinea. Organized by M-Events, the competition showcased 50 young talents across four categories: Miss, Mister, slam, and music.

Moustapha Diallo, CEO of M-Events, expressed satisfaction with the revealed talents and aims to expand the competition nationally next year. He called for support from sponsors and the government to nurture these young artists.

The organizers provided three produced music videos for the top three music contestants and prizes for Miss, Mister, and slam participants. M-Events used its own funds this year, seeking future partnerships to offer more opportunities.

Why it mattersYoung Guineans with talents in music, modeling, and slam are given a platform to showcase their abilities and launch careers.
