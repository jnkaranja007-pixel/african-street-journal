Guinea orders distributors to remove Burkina Faso’s RTB channel

The communications regulator says the public broadcaster aired an editorial it judged hostile to Guinea and a caricature targeting the president.

Sources: Africaguinee and Mosaiqueguinee · 2026-11-19 · 1 min read

Guinea’s High Authority for Communication (HAC) on Thursday, 19 November, banned Burkina Faso’s public broadcaster, Radiodiffusion Télévision du Burkina (RTB), across Guinea until further notice. The order took effect when signed in Conakry, the regulator said.

The HAC said RTB had broadcast an editorial comment it considered hostile to Guinea, illustrated by a caricature that it said harmed the president’s honour, dignity and image. The decision directs Canal+ Guinée and all image distributors and audiovisual package operators in the country to remove RTB from their commercial offers immediately upon notification.

Distributors that fail to comply face sanctions under Guinean laws and regulations. The regulator said the decision must be registered and published in Guinea’s official journal.
