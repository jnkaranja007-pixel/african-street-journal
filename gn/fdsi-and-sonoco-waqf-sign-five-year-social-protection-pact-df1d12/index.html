FDSI and SONOCO WAQF Sign Five-Year Social Protection Pact

The two institutions will collaborate on health and education programs to aid vulnerable populations in Guinea.

The General Directorate of the Social Development and Indigence Fund (FDSI) and SONOCO WAQF have formalized a five-year partnership agreement in Conakry to bolster social protection efforts across Guinea. The agreement, signed by FDSI Director General Lansana Diawara and the Executive Director of SONOCO WAQF, establishes a framework for joint initiatives aimed at improving the lives of those facing socio-economic challenges.

This collaboration prioritizes health and education sectors, focusing on the empowerment of vulnerable individuals. The implementation will leverage Guinea's Unified Social Register (RSU) to ensure precise targeting of beneficiaries and enhance the coordination of services. This initiative aligns with the government's broader agenda to modernize the nation's social protection system.

The five-year convention underscores a commitment to sustained cooperation and structured action in key social areas. FDSI and SONOCO WAQF aim for this partnership to serve as a catalyst for human development, social inclusion, and stability within Guinea.

Why it mattersThe FDSI and SONOCO WAQF partnership will focus on health and education programs, directly impacting vulnerable populations by improving access to essential services.
