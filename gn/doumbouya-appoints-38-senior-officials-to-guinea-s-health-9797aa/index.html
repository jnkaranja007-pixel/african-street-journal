Doumbouya appoints 38 senior officials to Guinea’s health ministry

The appointments cover public health oversight, hospitals, nutrition, laboratories and the fight against disease.

Sources: Africaguinee, Mosaiqueguinee and Guinee Matin · 2026-11-24 · 1 min read

Guinean President Mamadi Doumbouya appointed 38 senior officials to the Ministry of Health and Public Hygiene, in a decree made public on state television on Monday, 23 November. The appointments include Professor Falaye Traoré as inspector general and Dr. Pépé Bilivogui as deputy inspector general.

The decree also names Dr. Boubacar Sidiki Magassouba to lead the ministry’s strategy and development bureau, and Dr. Diénè Fadima Kaba as national director for family health and nutrition. Dr. Félémou Niakoye, an emergency physician and health-services manager, was named national director of public and private hospitals.

Other posts include national directors for laboratories, public hygiene, community health and medicine, and pharmacy and medicines. The ministry’s full list was published by Guinee Matin.

Why it mattersThe 38 appointees must now take up senior ministry posts, including oversight of hospitals, laboratories and public health.
