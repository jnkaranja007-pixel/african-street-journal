Mamady Doumbouya's RF224 hosts afterwork in Conakry

The Renouveau Football 224 list presented its four-year plan for Guinean football development at the event.

Sources: Mosaiqueguinee and Africaguinee · 2026-10-23 · 1 min read

The campaign for the presidency of the Guinean Football Federation (FGF) intensified on Thursday, October 22, 2026, with Mamady Doumbouya's Renouveau Football 224 (RF224) list hosting an afterwork event in Conakry. The gathering served to introduce the RF224 team and discuss their project for developing Guinean football, aiming to break from past practices.

Sega Diallo, a member of the RF224 list, explained that the group's pragmatic four-year program intends to restore hope for Guinean youth, revive football, and address the sector's difficulties. He noted the strong presence of business leaders and statutory members at the event, who expressed confidence in the need for change and new dynamics to help young Guineans reach the national team.

Kamil Zayatt, former captain of the Syli national team, voiced his support for RF224, believing the team's new vision will improve Guinean football, which has faced challenges for over 20 years. Ousmane Boly Traoré, CEO of Orange Guinea and an FGF partner, also attended, affirming his support for the list and its project.

Why it mattersThe RF224 list's project aims to develop Guinean football, affecting players, coaches, and administrators.
