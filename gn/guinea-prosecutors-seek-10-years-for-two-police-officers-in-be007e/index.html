Guinea prosecutors seek 10 years for two police officers in theft case

The prosecution also wants each of the two officers to pay a 5 million Guinean franc fine, with a ruling due on 30 November.

Source: Guinee Matin · 2026-11-24 · 1 min read

Guinea’s prosecution has asked a court in Kaloum, Conakry, to sentence two police officers to 10 years in prison each for aggravated theft. Prosecutors made the requests on 23 November 2026 against Colonel Said Beye and Captain Fodé Momo Touré, and also sought a 5 million Guinean franc fine for each.

The six defendants, held since 3 November, are accused of taking several million Guinean francs from Moussa Lamah. Prosecutors requested five-year prison terms and 20 million franc fines for Captain Aly Bangoura, Chief Warrant Officer Morlaye Cissé and Ibrahima Naby Boffa, who face receiving stolen goods charges. They asked the court to acquit Alsény 1 Sylla for insufficient evidence. Five defendants admitted the charges; Sylla pleaded not guilty.

The court is due to deliver its ruling on 30 November. All six defendants remain in preventive detention.

Why it mattersThe six defendants remain in detention pending the 30 November ruling, while prosecutors seek prison terms and fines for five of them.
