Guinea fonio project sets processing standard and supports six firms

The programme, backed by the Netherlands and running since 2023, trained businesses and linked them with investors and international markets.

Source: Guinee Matin · 2026-11-27 · 1 min read

A Guinea fonio programme supported by the Dutch government formally closed in Conakry on Thursday, 26 November, ahead of its planned end in December 2026. Since 2023, it has worked with six small and medium-sized businesses and national institutions to improve production and processing.

Project lead Femke Dekker said the programme produced good-practice manuals, established a standard for precooked fonio and helped the six firms prepare food-safety and social-responsibility action plans. The businesses also received training in food safety, organic certification and packaging, and introductions to investors and international markets.

Sira Bayo, head of Guinea’s fonio industry body, said the support helped producers and processors adopt standards and better production practices. She said stronger equipment is needed to increase processing capacity.

Why it mattersThe six supported firms must maintain their new standards and practices after the programme ends in December 2026.
