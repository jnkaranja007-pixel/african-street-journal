Bel Air Mining barge collision leaves two Guinean fishermen dead off Boffa

The bodies of the two fishermen missing after their canoe was caught in the barge's nets were recovered after almost 48 hours of search.

Sources: Guinee Matin and Mosaiqueguinee · 2026-11-22 · 1 min read

Two artisanal fishermen who went missing after a bauxite barge operated by Bel Air Mining collided with their canoe off Boffa were found dead, the national artisanal fishing federation said on Saturday. The accident happened on the night of 18 to 19 November off the port of Cap Verga, in Boffa prefecture, when the barge became entangled in the fishermen's nets and dragged their canoe towards the vessel.

Fodé Idrissa Kallo, communications officer of the Fédération nationale de la pêche artisanale de Guinée (FENAPAG), said one fisherman jumped into the water and was rescued, while the other two were pulled under the barge. One body was found on Friday and the second on Saturday morning, after several hours of searching each day.

Kallo said a barge of the same company had run over a fishing boat on 13 February 2026, killing a fisherman, and that the then ministers of fisheries and mines visited the site afterwards. He called for the maritime rules to be enforced, for the channel the mining firms are creating to be marked with buoys, and for each company's navigation plan to be shared with the fishing community.

Why it mattersFishermen working the Cap Verga waters must now operate alongside bauxite barges on a channel that is not yet marked, and their federation wants navigation plans from each mining company shared with them.
