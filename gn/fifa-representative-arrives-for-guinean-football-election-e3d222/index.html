FIFA representative arrives for Guinean football election

Fatou Camara will monitor the election of the new president and executive committee of the Guinean Football Federation.

Source: Mosaiqueguinee · 2026-10-26 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

FIFA representative Fatou Camara arrived in Conakry on October 24 to oversee the Guinean Football Federation's (FGF) general assembly and elections. The assembly, scheduled for October 27, will elect a new president and executive committee members. This follows the dissolution of the previous bureau led by Sory Doumbouya.

Camara, FIFA's Senior Member Association Development Manager for Africa, was met at Ahmed Sékou Touré International Airport by Ibrahima Blasco Barry, the FGF's Secretary General. Her presence signals FIFA's close attention to the electoral process and its importance for the governance of Guinean football.

The election aims to establish new leadership for the FGF. The international football body's involvement underscores the significance of this transition for the sport's administration in Guinea.

Why it mattersThe election will determine the new leadership of Guinean football.
