Conakry Terminal secures €82.6 million loan to extend port quays

AFG Bank and Ecobank will finance the next phase of Quai 14 at the Port of Conakry, part of a €341 million investment in quay capacity.

Source: Africaguinee · 2026-11-27 · 1 min read

Conakry Terminal, a subsidiary of the AGL group, signed a €82.6 million financing agreement with AFG Bank and Ecobank on 26 November to develop additional quays at the Port of Conakry. The funding goes to the Quai 14 project.

The terminal said €85.2 million of the project's €341 million total had already been mobilised and pre-financed from its own funds. The new agreement finances the next phase, which the terminal says will expand operating capacity and improve the flow of port operations.

The terminal has also started studies for Quai 16, which would add 400 metres of quay and require nearly €400 million in further investment. It said the expansion is meant to prepare for volumes from projects under the Simandou 2040 programme.
