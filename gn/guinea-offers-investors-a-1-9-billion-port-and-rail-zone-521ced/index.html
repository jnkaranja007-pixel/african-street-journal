Guinea offers investors a $1.9 billion port and rail-zone projects in Morocco

The government says the country drew $7.8 billion in foreign direct investment in 2025, the largest sum in West Africa.

Sources: Guinee Matin and Mosaiqueguinee · 2026-11-26 · 1 min read

Guinea's development agency presented a $1.9 billion port and other projects to potential investors at the MEDays forum in Tangier, Morocco, on Wednesday, November 25. Namory Camara, the agency's director general, spoke on behalf of Prime Minister Amadou Oury Bah and invited companies to form joint ventures with Guinean firms so that value stays in the country.

Camara cited figures from the UN Conference on Trade and Development showing Guinea received $7.8 billion in foreign direct investment in 2025, the largest amount in West Africa. The projects open to private investment include the port of M'Bottiny, a second undersea cable and economic zones along the Simandou railway.

The Simandou 2040 programme envisages more than $200 billion in investment by 2040, including $65 billion between 2025 and 2030, with the private sector expected to supply 40% of that first phase. The agency, wholly owned by the state and attached to the presidency, is offering investors a single point of contact from first approach through to follow-up after installation.

Why it mattersPrivate companies that partner with Guinean firms would fund 40% of the first-phase Simandou investment, which the government puts at $65 billion over 2025 to 2030.
