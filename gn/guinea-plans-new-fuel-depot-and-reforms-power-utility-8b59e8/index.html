Guinea plans new fuel depot and reforms power utility

Economy Minister Mariama Ciré Sylla says studies for the depot are complete as the government works to improve the utility’s governance.

Source: Mosaiqueguinee · 2026-11-28 · 1 min read

Guinea has completed studies for a new fuel depot and put a reform plan in place for state power utility EDG, Economy Minister Mariama Ciré Sylla said at a news conference on the 2026-2029 economic programme with the International Monetary Fund. The country has faced daytime electricity cuts in Conakry and difficulties securing fuel since an explosion at its hydrocarbons depot two years ago.

Sylla said the EDG plan is intended to improve governance and efficiency, while construction of the depot would give the country more flexibility in fuel supply. She said the government wants to move quickly on the project. The government has kept the pump price of fuel at 12 000 GNF, she said.

Sylla said fuel prices in 2027 would be assessed in light of changes in international oil prices. She also said the IMF programme would help the government rationalise subsidies and direct support towards vulnerable people, while protecting household purchasing power.

Why it mattersGuinean households facing power cuts and fuel-supply difficulties are the intended beneficiaries of subsidy changes designed to protect purchasing power; fuel prices will be assessed in 2027.
