AS Kalombada beats Alu Star with late goal

Kabiné Sakho scored in added time to give AS Kalombada a 1-0 victory over Alu Star.

Source: Guinee Matin · 2026-10-30 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

AS Kalombada defeated Alu Star 1-0 in a Ligue 2 match on Thursday, October 29, 2026, at the Yorokoguia stadium. Kabiné Sakho scored the winning goal in the 90th+3 minute, securing a late victory for the Siguiri club.

The match, part of the 9th round of the Ligue 2 championship, was tense with few clear chances in the first half, ending 0-0. AS Kalombada intensified their pressure after halftime, and Sakho's goal came during a moment of confusion in the Alu Star defense.

This win is AS Kalombada's second of the season, bringing them to 9 points and moving them out of the relegation zone to 12th place. Alu Star, with 12 points, dropped to 5th after their third loss.

Why it mattersFootball fans in Guinea watched AS Kalombada secure a win to move up the league standings.
