Comoros loses prominent radio journalist Mselem Ali

Mselem Ali, a veteran journalist known for his work at Radio Comores and Radio Ngazidja, has passed away.

Source: Comores Infos · 2026-11-04 · 1 min read

Mselem Ali, a significant voice in Comorian radio journalism, has died. He was a journalist for Radio Comores and later served as editor-in-chief at Radio Ngazidja in the early 2000s. His career spanned decades, accompanying the evolution of Comorian media.

In August 2003, Ali was arrested with the director of Radio Ngazidja for an editorial criticizing the justice system and alleging corruption. He was detained for nearly two months, with Reporters Without Borders documenting the incident in their 2004 report. The Committee to Protect Journalists also recorded his arrest and detention.

Ali, who had been living in France in his later years, was also involved in discussions about the media's role in Comoros in 2011. His passing marks the loss of a figure who experienced considerable political and judicial pressures during his career.
