Comoros Consul Presents Credentials in Kenya

Mouigni Abdou officially begins his term as the first Comorian Consul General in Nairobi, aiming to strengthen bilateral ties.

Nairobi, Kenya - September 3, 2026 marked a significant diplomatic step as Mouigni Abdou, appointed on June 11, 2026, presented his Letters of Mission to Dr. Musalia Mudavadi, Kenya's First Cabinet Secretary for Foreign Affairs and Diaspora.

This formal handover signifies the official commencement of Abdou's duties as the inaugural Comorian Consul General in Kenya. He expressed his ambition to revitalize the partnership between the two nations through enhanced trade, cultural and academic exchanges, and by focusing on tourism and investment sectors.

The meeting underscored a shared aspiration by both governments to deepen connections between their peoples, united by their Indian Ocean heritage. Abdou committed to injecting new momentum into bilateral relations throughout his mandate.

Why it mattersComorian citizens and businesses may benefit from strengthened trade and investment links between Comoros and Kenya.
