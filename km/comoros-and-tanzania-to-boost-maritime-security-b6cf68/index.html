Comoros and Tanzania to boost maritime security

The two nations will coordinate efforts against irregular migration and enhance maritime surveillance.

Source: Comores Infos · 2026-11-14 · 1 min read

Comoros and Tanzania plan to strengthen their cooperation to secure maritime spaces and combat irregular migration. Interior Minister Mohamed Ahmed Assoumani met with Tanzanian Ambassador Salim Othman Hamad in Moroni on Friday, November 13, 2026, to discuss these priorities.

The officials, accompanied by their teams, committed to coordinating actions against migration flows transiting through Tanzania towards the Comorian archipelago. Maritime surveillance is identified as a key area for this security partnership. A memorandum of understanding, developed under the International Organization for Migration (IOM), is expected to be finalized soon.

Following technical validation by relevant ministries, the memorandum will be signed. Future steps include increased joint patrols and operations aimed at curbing irregular sea crossings between the two countries.

Why it mattersComorian and Tanzanian officials will intensify joint patrols and operations to curb irregular sea crossings.
