Second convicted murderer dies in Naïcha case

Fahami Moustoifa, sentenced to death, died after being admitted to El Maarouf hospital.

Source: Comores Infos · 2026-11-11 · 1 min read

Fahami Moustoifa, who was sentenced to death as the main perpetrator in the murder of Naïcha M’Madi, has died. He passed away after being admitted to El Maarouf hospital in critical condition.

Moustoifa's death follows that of Athoumane Abdourahmane, who was sentenced to 25 years in prison for the same crime. Abdourahmane died at the military health center one week prior, with a heart attack reportedly cited as the cause, though this remained unconfirmed.

The successive deaths of the two convicted men add a new dimension to the case. While religious interpretations suggest divine justice, the circumstances of their deaths remain unclear, and no medical link has been established between them. The crime and the pain of Naïcha's family endure despite these developments.

Why it mattersThe deaths of two men convicted in the murder of Naïcha M'Madi mean the crime and the victim's memory remain central.
