Comorian researcher Faïdat Mzé Hamadi defends doctoral thesis on legume foods in Nantes

Her thesis examined how the structure of legume-based model foods relates to their properties and the processes used to make them.

Source: Comores Infos · 2026-11-18 · 1 min read

Faïdat Mzé Hamadi, from Moroni, defended her doctoral thesis in Nantes on Tuesday, 17 November 2026, completing research on foods made from legumes.

The thesis, titled Détermination des relations entre structures, propriétés et procédés d'aliment-modèle à base de légumineuses, was carried out at the BIA unit of France's National Research Institute for Agriculture, Food and the Environment (INRAE) in Nantes, in partnership with the GRAPPE unit of the Higher School of Agriculture (ESA) in Angers.

The thesis was supervised by Guy Della Valle, with Sophie Le Gall and Marie Dufrechou as co-supervisors. A jury of several specialists heard the defence.

Why it mattersThe result gives a Comorian researcher a doctorate after years of work spanning two French research institutions.
