Politics · 2026-10-30

Comoros drafts 2035 migration policy

The plan aims to unify migration governance, protect rights, and leverage diaspora resources for development.

The Union of Comoros is developing a national migration policy, with a first draft presented on October 29, 2026. Minister of Interior Mohamed Ahmed Assoumani led a meeting where the document, created with the International Organization for Migration (IOM), was discussed. The policy aims to balance national sovereignty with migrant protection and economic growth.

The project, presented by national consultant Moctar Zouboudou, outlines eight objectives for unified and sustainable migration governance by 2035. Key goals include strengthening institutions and legal frameworks, enhancing inter-agency coordination, and protecting migrants' fundamental rights. It also focuses on preventing irregular migration and combating human trafficking.

The policy also seeks to organize returns and promote safe, regular mobility channels. A significant component is mobilizing the Comorian diaspora's financial resources and skills for national development. Other planned orientations include adapting to climate crises, improving migration data, and fostering international partnerships.

Why it mattersThe Comorian government is creating a new migration policy by 2035 that will affect migrants, the diaspora, and national development.

Health · 2026-10-30

Comoros plans national cancer strategy

Health officials met in Moroni to draft a strategic plan to combat cancer, aiming for improved care by December.

Comoros health officials met in Moroni from October 20-22, 2026, to develop a national cancer control strategy with support from the WHO and UNICEF. The meeting aimed to assess current capabilities and prepare a strategic plan expected in December. This initiative addresses the lack of a national cancer registry, which prevents precise tracking of patients, diagnoses, treatments, and outcomes.

Estimates from Globocan suggest 619 new cancer cases and 418 deaths in 2022, with uterine, prostate, and breast cancers being most frequent. The country currently lacks an operational radiotherapy service, on-site pathologists, and standardized chemotherapy protocols, often necessitating costly medical evacuations for patients. Questions remain about the selection process, state and family costs, and care for those unable to travel.

The planned strategy prioritizes better organization of medical evacuations, strengthening island-based care, and establishing a national cancer registry. Monthly remote medical consultations are proposed for Comorian doctors to consult with foreign specialists on complex cases. Dr. Saïd Mohamed Soulé noted that agreements with specialized foreign hospitals will be sought, with a pilot project planned within six months and a first oncology unit hoped for within a year.

Why it mattersComoros residents affected by cancer face challenges due to the absence of a national registry, specialized services like radiotherapy, and standardized treatment protocols, leading to reliance on expensive medical evacuations.

Comorian man died one hour after TikTok live

Kassim, known as Mkataba, was killed in a car accident in Marseille on October 15.

Kassim, known online as Mkataba, finished a TikTok live stream at 23:34 on October 14, 2026. An hour and six minutes later, around 00:40 on October 15, he was involved in a fatal accident on the A7 motorway in Marseille. His family confirmed his death on October 23.

The TikTok live, broadcast from the account @mkatabao, began at 22:59 and lasted 35 minutes. Mkataba was struck by an Audi RS3 in the Plombières area, travelling at high speed towards Aix-en-Provence. Witnesses reported the vehicle was moving very fast, and the pedestrian died at the scene.

Previous reports suggested Mkataba was alive after the accident, but no verified testimony supports these claims. While Mkataba had received death threats on social media, no evidence links these threats to his death or implicates members of the Comorian community.

Why it mattersA Comorian national died in a traffic accident in France.
