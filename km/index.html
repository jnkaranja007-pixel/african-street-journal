Comoros and China agree to deepen strategic partnership

President Azali Assoumani and Chinese Ambassador Huang Zheng met on November 3 to discuss enhanced bilateral cooperation.

Comoros President Azali Assoumani and Chinese Ambassador Huang Zheng met on November 3 to reaffirm their commitment to deepening the strategic partnership between their nations. The meeting highlighted agreement on bilateral cooperation and international issues.

Ambassador Huang Zheng presented China's vision of a shared future for humanity and Beijing's proposals for resolving African crises. He stated China's readiness to translate these directions into cooperation and strengthen ties with Comoros.

President Assoumani welcomed the Chinese president's ideas and initiatives, expressing a desire to increase exchanges in all areas. He aims to make the relationship an international example, with diplomatic advisor Djae Ahamada Chanfi and embassy counselors Xia Yin and Meng Yuhong also attending.

Why it mattersThe Comorian president and the Chinese ambassador met to strengthen their countries' strategic partnership, indicating a focus on diplomatic relations.

Comoros prisoner dies, another reportedly hospitalized

Athoumane Abdourahmane, convicted in the Naïcha M’Madi murder, died at the military health center.

Athoumane Abdourahmane, who was sentenced to 25 years in prison for the murder of Naïcha M’Madi, has died at the military health center. Unconfirmed reports also state that Fahami, another individual imprisoned in the same case, has been hospitalized.

A heart attack is being suggested as the cause of Athoumane Abdourahmane's death, though this explanation awaits official confirmation from the public prosecutor. Fahami's admission to the military health center is also unverified at this time, with no details on his condition available.

The death of Athoumane Abdourahmane is confirmed, while Fahami's alleged hospitalization requires verification. No link has been established between these two separate events.

Why it mattersTwo men convicted in the Naïcha M’Madi murder case are involved in separate health events, one confirmed death and one unconfirmed hospitalization.

Comoros notaries train to fight money laundering

A national training session on detecting suspicious funds precedes professional immersion for notaries in Reunion and Mayotte.

The Comoros National Chamber of Notaries held a training session on October 31 focused on combating money laundering and terrorist financing. This event prepares notaries for upcoming professional immersion programs in La Réunion and Mayotte. The training covered identifying beneficial owners, vigilance towards politically exposed persons, detecting suspicious operations, and reporting procedures.

This initiative, organized with the Chamber of Notaries of La Réunion, aims to align Comorian notarial practices with Financial Action Task Force (GAFI) standards. The session was co-led by Anne-Françoise Zattara, a professor at the University of La Réunion, and Ait Ahmed Mohamed Djalim, secretary general of the Comorian Financial Intelligence Service. Notaries are involved in real estate and asset transactions that could be used for funds of dubious origin.

Following the training, the presidents of the two chambers signed agreements on November 2 for the upcoming immersion stages. Notaries will soon join practices in La Réunion and Mayotte to enhance their practical skills. The training lasted three hours and focused on four key areas.

Why it mattersComorian notaries are receiving training to combat money laundering and terrorist financing, which affects their professional practice in real estate and asset transactions.
