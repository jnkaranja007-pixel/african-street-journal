Comoros police trained in crime scene management

20 officers completed a week-long course on preserving evidence and coordinating interventions.

20 Comorian security agents, including 10 police officers and 10 gendarmes, completed a week-long training on crime scene management. The course, held at the Ministry of Interior, concluded on Friday, October 23, 2026, focusing on securing locations and preserving evidence.

The training was organized by the security service of the French Embassy in collaboration with the Comorian Ministry of Interior. Participants practiced methods for securing crime scenes and preserving evidence to aid investigations, aiming to improve coordination between the police and gendarmerie.

Upon completion, the 20 agents received certificates recognizing their acquired knowledge. The training emphasized applying these new skills to enhance citizen security through improved daily interventions.

Why it matters20 police and gendarme officers in Comoros received training in crime scene management.

Comorian man found dead on Marseille highway

The family of Kassim, known as Mkataba on TikTok, confirmed his death on October 23.

The family of Kassim, known on TikTok as Mkataba, confirmed his death on Friday, October 23, 2026. The Comorian man, in his thirties and living in Marseille, had been missing since October 16. His body was reportedly found on the A7 highway in Marseille.

The exact circumstances of his death and how he came to be on the highway remain unclear. Questions linger about the timeline between his disappearance and the announcement of his death, whether this delay relates to the discovery of his body, its identification, or informing the family. Mkataba had reportedly received death threats on TikTok due to disputes, but no link to his death has been established.

The precise details surrounding his demise are still under investigation.

Why it mattersA Comorian national living in Marseille has died, and his family confirmed his death.
