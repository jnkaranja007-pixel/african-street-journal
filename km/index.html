Health · 2026-10-29

Comoros announces national cancer plan

Health officials met from October 20-22 to draft a strategic plan for cancer care, expected in December.

Comoros plans to establish a national strategy against cancer, with a draft plan due in December. From October 20 to 22, 2026, health officials met in Moroni to assess the country's current capabilities with support from the WHO and UNICEF.

The nation currently lacks a national registry to track cancer patients, diagnoses, and treatments. In 2022, an estimated 619 new cancer cases and 418 deaths were recorded, with uterine, prostate, and breast cancers being the most frequent, according to Globocan estimates.

The strategy aims to improve medical evacuations, strengthen island healthcare, and create a national cancer registry. A pilot project is planned within six months, potentially leading to the first oncology unit within a year.

Why it mattersComoros residents face challenges accessing cancer care due to a lack of national infrastructure and specialized services.

Comoros drafts 2035 migration policy

The plan aims to unify migration governance, protect migrants, and leverage diaspora resources for development.

The Comoros government, led by Interior Minister Mohamed Ahmed Assoumani, has presented a draft national migration policy aimed at organizing the country's approach to migration until 2035. This initiative, developed with the International Organization for Migration (IOM), seeks to balance national sovereignty with migrant protection and economic growth.

The policy document, presented by national consultant Moctar Zouboudou, outlines eight objectives including strengthening institutions, legal frameworks, and inter-agency coordination. Key priorities also include protecting migrants' fundamental rights, preventing irregular migration, and combating human trafficking and smuggling, while facilitating safe and regular mobility and organizing returns.

A significant aspect of the policy focuses on the Comorian diaspora, with the government intending to better mobilize their financial resources and skills for national development. Other planned orientations include adapting to climate crises, improving migration data, and enhancing international partnerships.

Why it mattersThe Comoros government is developing a new migration policy to better manage migration, protect migrants, and utilize the diaspora's potential for development by 2035.

News · 2026-10-29

Comorian man died one hour after TikTok live

Kassim, known as Mkataba, was killed in a car accident in Marseille on October 15.

Kassim, known online as Mkataba, finished a TikTok live stream at 23:34 on October 14, 2026. An hour and six minutes later, around 00:40 on October 15, he was involved in a fatal accident on the A7 motorway in Marseille. His family confirmed his death on October 23.

The TikTok live, broadcast from the account @mkatabao, began at 22:59 and lasted 35 minutes. Mkataba was struck by an Audi RS3 in the Plombières area, travelling at high speed towards Aix-en-Provence. Witnesses reported the vehicle was moving very fast, and the pedestrian died at the scene.

Previous reports suggested Mkataba was alive after the accident, but no verified testimony supports these claims. While Mkataba had received death threats on social media, no evidence links these threats to his death or implicates members of the Comorian community.

Why it mattersA Comorian national died in a traffic accident in France.

News · 2026-10-29

Young man's body found on Comoros beach

Harouna, in his twenties, was discovered on Chavani beach in Domoni on October 29, 2026.

The body of Harouna, a young man in his twenties from Koni residing in Domoni, was found on Thursday morning, October 29, 2026, on Chavani beach in Anjouan.

Beachgoers extracting marine sand discovered the body washed ashore on the Domoni coast. The national gendarmerie was alerted and contacted the prefect of the Domoni region.

Harouna was reportedly seen swimming at Chikrini beach hours before his body was found. The remains were transferred to Koni, his home village.

Why it mattersHarouna, a young man from Koni, was found dead on a beach in Domoni.
