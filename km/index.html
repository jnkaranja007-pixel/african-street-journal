Suspect Arrested After Critical Sexual Assault of 9-Year-Old

A 30-to-40-year-old man, identified as Ismaël, was apprehended by gendarmes following the severe assault of a child.

A 9-year-old boy remains in critical condition at the Moroni military health center after a severe sexual assault. The child, who underwent surgery, is currently unable to sit or perform natural bodily functions. The alleged perpetrator, a man between 30 and 40 years old named Ismaël, fled the scene on Wednesday but was arrested by the gendarmerie on Thursday.

The investigation has taken a concerning turn as reports indicate that members of the suspect's family attempted to negotiate a financial settlement with the victim's relatives. They reportedly offered a significant sum to prevent the case from proceeding further. The gendarmerie has also detained an uncle of the suspect, though the exact reasons for his arrest and his role in the events remain officially unconfirmed.

The incident has raised serious questions about justice and potential interference in the legal process. The critical state of the young victim underscores the gravity of the alleged crime. Authorities are continuing their investigation into the assault and the alleged attempts to obstruct justice.

Why it mattersA 9-year-old boy is in critical condition after a sexual assault, and the suspect's family allegedly attempted to pay off the victim's family to drop the case.
