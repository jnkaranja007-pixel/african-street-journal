Comoros and Tanzania to boost maritime security

The two nations will coordinate efforts against irregular migration and enhance maritime surveillance.

Comoros and Tanzania plan to strengthen their cooperation to secure maritime spaces and combat irregular migration. Interior Minister Mohamed Ahmed Assoumani met with Tanzanian Ambassador Salim Othman Hamad in Moroni on Friday, November 13, 2026, to discuss these priorities.

The officials, accompanied by their teams, committed to coordinating actions against migration flows transiting through Tanzania towards the Comorian archipelago. Maritime surveillance is identified as a key area for this security partnership. A memorandum of understanding, developed under the International Organization for Migration (IOM), is expected to be finalized soon.

Following technical validation by relevant ministries, the memorandum will be signed. Future steps include increased joint patrols and operations aimed at curbing irregular sea crossings between the two countries.

Why it mattersComorian and Tanzanian officials will intensify joint patrols and operations to curb irregular sea crossings.

Sport · 2026-11-14

Comoros held to goalless draw by Congo in AFCON qualifier

The Coelacanths missed a chance to strengthen their 2027 Africa Cup of Nations qualification bid after a 0-0 draw at home.

The Comoros national football team, known as the Coelacanths, drew 0-0 with Congo's Red Devils on Friday, November 13, at the Malouzini stadium. This result was a missed opportunity for the Comorian team, which had hoped to use its home advantage to improve its standing in the 2027 Africa Cup of Nations qualification race.

After a 1-0 victory against Namibia in September, the Comoros entered this third qualifying match aiming for a second consecutive home win. Congo, seeking its first win of the campaign, could not find the net either. The draw means Comoros earns one point, reaching four points from three matches, while Congo has two points.

The draw leaves the qualification race in Group G open. Before this match, Comoros was second in the group with three points, behind Cameroon. Congo had previously lost to Namibia 1-0 and drawn 1-1 with Cameroon.

Why it mattersComoros' football team earned one point in their 2027 Africa Cup of Nations qualification match, leaving their chances of advancing open.

Politics · 2026-11-14

Comorians mark Maoré Day, reaffirming unity and claim to Mayotte

Ceremonies in Moroni and France on Thursday reasserted the archipelago's unity and its sovereignty claim over Mayotte, a French-administered island.

The national Maoré Day was marked on Thursday, 12 November 2026, in Comoros and in France. In Moroni, several hundred people, including many schoolchildren from the capital and from Bambao and Itsandra, gathered at the Palais du Peuple for the official ceremony, organised by the Comité Maoré with the Comorian government.

The programme drew members of national institutions, diplomats, civil society figures and delegations from the archipelago's islands. Milissane Hamdia, vice-president of the Union Assembly, and Monjoin Mohamed, president of the Comité Maoré, both spoke of the need to preserve national unity, with Comorian flags, patriotic songs and speeches marking the day.

Celebrations began on 11 November with a poetry vigil and traditional performances. In La Courneuve, near Paris, the town hall held its own ceremony, attended by mayor Aly Diouara, Comorian ambassador to France Ahamada Hamadi and the Comoros' permanent representative to UNESCO, Kassim Mohamed-Soyir Bajrafil.

Why it mattersComorian families abroad, who keep close ties to the four islands, saw the cause of Mayotte restated by officials in France itself on the day.
