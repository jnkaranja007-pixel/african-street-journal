Azali says preparations under way for Sambi's medical evacuation

The president said discussions have begun and the steps needed for an evacuation abroad are being prepared for the jailed former president.

President Azali Assoumani said preparations are under way for a possible medical evacuation abroad of former president Ahmed Abdallah Mohamed Sambi, who is currently imprisoned. Speaking to TV5MONDE on the sidelines of the Francophonie Summit in Phnom Penh, Cambodia, he said discussions are ongoing and the necessary procedures are being prepared.

Asked by the journalist why an evacuation for medical reasons had apparently been refused, Azali said Sambi had personally written to him about his situation. He did not give a date for the transfer.

Sambi has been in detention for several years. The president's comments were the first indication in the report that the process has moved beyond a request.

Why it mattersSambi's health and any transfer abroad now depend on the government completing the procedures the president described.

Russian ambassador Vadim Barabanov arrives in Moroni

The envoy's arrival follows President Azali Assoumani's presentation of Comoros' priorities at the Russia-Africa summit in Moscow in October.

Vadim Barabanov, Russia's ambassador accredited to the Union of the Comoros, arrived in Moroni on Sunday, 15 November 2026. The arrival comes a few weeks after President Azali Assoumani attended the third Russia-Africa summit in Moscow.

On 29 October, Assoumani presented the archipelago's priorities at the summit, including partnership prospects with Moscow, investment opportunities and questions of sovereignty and security. He also asked partners for support in hosting the Indian Ocean Island Games in 2027.

Barabanov was received at the airport by Alexey Buryak, the interim chargé d'affaires of the Russian embassy in Madagascar, and Nassir Houssen Zahir Houssen, president of COMRUSS, an association that promotes cultural, human and economic exchanges between Comorians and Russians.

Why it mattersThe ambassador gives Comorian officials a direct contact to turn the investment and security discussions from Moscow into concrete projects.

Sport · 2026-11-17

Ngaya Club wins 3-0 in Ngazidja second division

Alizé Fort and US Mbeni lead the league with 10 points each after four matches.

Ngaya Club de Mde defeated Asim Club d’Itsandraya-Mdjini 3-0 on Sunday, November 15, in the fourth round of the Ngazidja second division championship. The victory at Moroni stadium brings Ngaya Club's points total to nine, placing them fourth in the tightly contested league.

The league leaders, Alizé Fort of Salimani and US Mbeni, both have 10 points after winning their respective matches against Dynamo Club de Shamle (2-0) and AJS Dzwadjuu (1-0). Petit Harlem de Shindini is third with nine points after beating FC Mdjwaezi 2-0.

This round saw 26 goals scored across nine games, highlighting the league's offensive dynamism. US Selea won 5-2 against Avenir Club, JACM de Mitsudje beat Twamaya de Nvuni 4-0, and Enfants des Comores secured a 2-1 victory over Coin Nord, while Kaz Club and FC Mvumbari drew 1-1.

Why it mattersFootball fans in Ngazidja are following a league where no team has yet established a clear lead after four rounds.
