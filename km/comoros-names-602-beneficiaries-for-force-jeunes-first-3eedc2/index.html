Comoros names 602 beneficiaries for Force jeunes’ first cohort

Agriculture Minister Daniel Ali Bandar said 483 beneficiaries are in Ngazidja and 119 in Ndzuani, while applications in Mwali remain under review.

Source: Comores Infos · 2026-11-21 · 1 min read

Comoros’ Force jeunes project has selected 602 people for its first cohort, Agriculture Minister Daniel Ali Bandar said at a cabinet meeting on Wednesday, 18 November. The beneficiaries include 483 in Ngazidja and 119 in Ndzuani.

The review of applications in Mwali is still under way, Bandar said. He also announced that 100 dairy cows donated through a project of the International Fund for Agricultural Development had been received. The animals are to be distributed to livestock farmers to expand local milk production, and Bandar said he would ensure veterinary follow-up.

The cabinet also approved talks with Saudi Arabia, the United Arab Emirates and Morocco on visa exemptions for holders of diplomatic and service passports. It requested clarification on liabilities between Comores Télécom and Comores Câbles during a review of the latter’s statutes.

Why it mattersApplicants in Mwali are still waiting for the review of their Force jeunes applications to finish.
