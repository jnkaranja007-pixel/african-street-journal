Omani delegation meets Comorian police chiefs

Officials from Oman's interior ministry visited Moroni to discuss security cooperation and capacity building.

Source: Comores Infos · 2026-11-10 · 1 min read

A three-person delegation from Oman's Ministry of Interior arrived in Comoros on November 4 and met with national police authorities on November 5 to discuss security cooperation. The delegation, led by General Jamal Habib Abdullah Al Quraishi, director general of Judicial Police, met with Comoros' Minister of Interior Mohamed Ahmed Assoumani.

During a working session, Comorian police officials, including Controller General Nassif Kaissane and his deputy, Commissioner Mohamed Achikali Yahaya, discussed strengthening institutional capacities with the Omani visitors. The delegation also visited the central police station in Moroni and the Judicial Police premises at Dar Sanda to observe operational structures.

The mission aims to consolidate relations and explore new collaborations between Comoros and Oman. The specific commitments and their implementation timeline were not detailed in the police national communiqué.

Why it mattersComorian citizens are affected by strengthened security cooperation and institutional capacity building within their national police force.
