Comorian youth learn Chinese history

Delegations attended lectures on the Chinese People's Political Consultative Conference and visited the Communist Party Museum.

Source: Comores Infos · 2026-10-31 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Young Comorians participating in the 10th Sino-African Youth Festival engaged with China's political history on October 30, 2026. The second day of the event featured a conference on the Chinese People's Political Consultative Conference (CPPCC), an institution central to political consultation and cooperation in China.

Following the conference, the delegates toured the National Museum of the Chinese Communist Party (CCP). The museum's exhibits, including historical documents and artifacts, detailed the founding of the CCP, the Chinese revolution, and the development of modern China. Sefrick Abdou was noted as a participant.

The day concluded with a visit to the former residence of Soong Ching Ling, a significant historical figure and revolutionary activist. She actively supported revolutionary movements and promoted international peace and cooperation.

Why it mattersComorian youth gained insight into Chinese political history and revolutionary figures through festival events.
