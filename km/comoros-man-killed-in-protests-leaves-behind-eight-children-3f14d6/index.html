Comoros Man Killed in Protests Leaves Behind Eight Children

A citizen-led initiative is building a house for the widow and eight children of Attoumani Saïd, who died during fuel price protests.

Source: Comores Infos · 2026-10-21 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X

Five months after protests against fuel price hikes in Comoros, a citizen initiative is organizing support for the family of Attoumani Saïd. Saïd, 39, died during clashes in Anjouan in May, leaving behind a wife and eight children. The initiative aims to build a house for his widow and children to provide concrete aid.

In May 2026, widespread protests erupted across the Comoros following an announcement of significant fuel price increases. The demonstrations escalated into confrontations with law enforcement, particularly on Anjouan island. Two individuals lost their lives, including Attoumani Saïd from Pagé. These events prompted the government to temporarily suspend the fuel price increase.

Behind the official reports and political decisions, families continue to grapple with the aftermath of those tragic days. The construction of a house for Saïd's family is intended to offer hope to his eight children, addressing the immediate consequences of the unrest.

Why it mattersThe family of Attoumani Saïd, who was killed during protests, is receiving a house to help them cope with their loss.
