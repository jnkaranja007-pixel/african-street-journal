Comoros prisoner dies, another reportedly hospitalized

Athoumane Abdourahmane, convicted in the Naïcha M’Madi murder, died at the military health center.

Source: Comores Infos · 2026-11-05 · 1 min read

Athoumane Abdourahmane, who was sentenced to 25 years in prison for the murder of Naïcha M’Madi, has died at the military health center. Unconfirmed reports also state that Fahami, another individual imprisoned in the same case, has been hospitalized.

A heart attack is being suggested as the cause of Athoumane Abdourahmane's death, though this explanation awaits official confirmation from the public prosecutor. Fahami's admission to the military health center is also unverified at this time, with no details on his condition available.

The death of Athoumane Abdourahmane is confirmed, while Fahami's alleged hospitalization requires verification. No link has been established between these two separate events.

Why it mattersTwo men convicted in the Naïcha M’Madi murder case are involved in separate health events, one confirmed death and one unconfirmed hospitalization.
