Comoros and China reaffirm strategic partnership

President Azali Assoumani and Chinese Ambassador Huang Zheng met on November 3 to deepen bilateral ties.

Source: Comores Infos · 2026-11-06 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Comorian President Azali Assoumani and Chinese Ambassador Huang Zheng met on November 3 to reaffirm their commitment to strengthening the strategic partnership between their nations. The meeting highlighted agreement on bilateral cooperation and international issues.

Ambassador Huang Zheng presented China's vision of a shared future for humanity and Beijing's proposals for resolving African crises. He also conveyed China's readiness to translate these directives into practical cooperation with Comoros, aiming to consolidate existing links.

President Assoumani welcomed the Chinese initiatives and expressed a desire to increase exchanges across all sectors. He aims to establish the Comoros-China relationship as an international model, with diplomatic advisors from both sides attending the discussion.

Why it mattersComoros and China are working to deepen their strategic partnership through increased exchanges in all areas.
