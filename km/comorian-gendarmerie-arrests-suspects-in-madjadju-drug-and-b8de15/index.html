Comorian Gendarmerie Arrests Suspects in Madjadju Drug and Counterfeiting Raids

National Gendarmerie operations over the weekend in Madjadju resulted in multiple arrests for drug offenses, assault, and currency counterfeiting.

Source: Comores Infos · 2026-10-21 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X

The Comorian National Gendarmerie reported multiple arrests in Madjadju over the weekend following security operations. The actions targeted several criminal activities, including drug trafficking, physical assaults, and the counterfeiting of bank notes, according to a gendarmerie statement.

The investigations involved allegations of criminal association, possession and sale of narcotics, intentional assault and battery, and currency forgery. Authorities have not specified whether all apprehended individuals are implicated in the same offenses, nor have they provided details on the assault cases, their victims, or the circumstances.

Among those detained are individuals sought for physical assaults. The gendarmerie confirmed these arrests but offered no further information regarding these specific incidents or the individuals involved.

Why it mattersThe arrests in Madjadju affect residents through increased security presence and potential disruption from law enforcement operations.
