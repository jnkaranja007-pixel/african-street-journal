Comoros shows its pilgrim services at Hajj exhibition in Jeddah

The national Hajj agency is presenting its support for pilgrims at a Saudi congress that brings together more than 520 organisations from over 150 countries.

Source: Comores Infos · 2026-11-13 · 1 min read

From Sunday to Wednesday, the Comorian National Agency for Hajj and Omra (ANHO) is taking part in the sixth Hajj Congress and Exhibition in Jeddah, alongside delegations from more than 150 countries. Its pavilion at the Superdome shows how it looks after pilgrims from preparation to their return home, covering accommodation, transport, meals, health monitoring and religious guidance.

The agency points to a gold medal in the Labayt prize, won by the Comorian pilgrims' affairs office during the 1447H Hajj for the quality of its services. The director general, Oustath Mohamed Elfatihou Alfadhli Djamalilayri, said the award calls for further effort, and the exhibition is also meant to uncover new solutions and build cooperation that benefits Comorian pilgrims.

The event supports Saudi Arabia's plans to modernise the Hajj. The Comorian aim is to turn these exchanges into better services before the 1448H season, with particular attention to the quality of what pilgrims receive.

Why it mattersComorian pilgrims are expected to see changes in service quality in the coming 1448H season, depending on what the agency takes from these exchanges.
