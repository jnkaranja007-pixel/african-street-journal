Comoros drafts 2035 migration policy

The plan aims to unify migration governance, protect rights, and leverage diaspora resources for development.

Source: Comores Infos · 2026-10-30 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

The Union of Comoros is developing a national migration policy, with a first draft presented on October 29, 2026. Minister of Interior Mohamed Ahmed Assoumani led a meeting where the document, created with the International Organization for Migration (IOM), was discussed. The policy aims to balance national sovereignty with migrant protection and economic growth.

The project, presented by national consultant Moctar Zouboudou, outlines eight objectives for unified and sustainable migration governance by 2035. Key goals include strengthening institutions and legal frameworks, enhancing inter-agency coordination, and protecting migrants' fundamental rights. It also focuses on preventing irregular migration and combating human trafficking.

The policy also seeks to organize returns and promote safe, regular mobility channels. A significant component is mobilizing the Comorian diaspora's financial resources and skills for national development. Other planned orientations include adapting to climate crises, improving migration data, and fostering international partnerships.

Why it mattersThe Comorian government is creating a new migration policy by 2035 that will affect migrants, the diaspora, and national development.
