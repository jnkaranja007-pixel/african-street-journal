Senegal police present response to renewed migrant boat departures

The country has seen a serious rise in boats heading for Spain, with dozens of would-be migrants dying in two wrecks over two weeks.

Source: RFI Afrique · 2026-11-23 · 1 min read

Senegal’s police and gendarmerie have presented measures to address a renewed wave of boats carrying irregular migrants toward Spain, according to RFI Afrique. The country is facing what the report describes as a serious increase in departures.

Dozens of people seeking to migrate irregularly died in two boat wrecks over two weeks, one at Lompoul and the other at Yenne. The police and gendarmerie outlined the measures they have put in place in response to the departures.

The report did not detail the measures or say how they would be enforced.

Why it mattersPolice and gendarmes must now carry out the measures they presented after two wrecks killed dozens of people in two weeks.
