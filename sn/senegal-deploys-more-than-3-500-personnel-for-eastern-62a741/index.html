Senegal deploys more than 3 500 personnel for eastern security exercise

Boundou 2026 runs in Tambacounda and Kédougou from 28 November to 6 December, with free medical consultations also planned in some localities.

Sources: Seneweb and Dakaractu · 2026-11-28 · 1 min read

Senegal’s armed forces are holding Boundou 2026, a national exercise involving more than 3 500 military and paramilitary personnel in the eastern regions of Tambacounda and Kédougou from 28 November to 6 December 2026.

The exercise brings together the military, gendarmerie, police, firefighters, customs, prison administration and environment services to assess and strengthen coordination between units, according to the armed forces’ information and public relations directorate. The army health service plans free consultations for residents of some localities in the exercise area.

A press briefing by the army chief of staff is scheduled for 10 a.m. on Monday, 30 November, at the Mamadou Lamine Dramé military camp in Tambacounda. A VIP day is planned in Goudiry on the morning of 4 December.

Why it mattersResidents in some localities within the exercise area are due to receive free medical consultations from the army health service during the operations.
