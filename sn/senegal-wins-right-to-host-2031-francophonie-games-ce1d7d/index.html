Senegal wins right to host 2031 Francophonie Games

The Permanent Council of La Francophonie picked Senegal on 13 October 2026, a first for the country, after Dakar hosted the Youth Olympic Games.

Source: Le Soleil · 2026-11-16 · 1 min read

Senegal will host the 11th Francophonie Games in 2031, after the Permanent Council of La Francophonie chose it on 13 October 2026 in Paris, during the Youth Olympic Games. The Games combine sport with culture, bringing together athletes and artists from French-speaking countries.

The International Olympic Committee's president, Kirsty Coventry, called Dakar 2026 a "100% success" at Diamniadio on 12 November, and said Dakar had changed the image of Africa. The Youth Olympic Games closed with the flame going out at Diamniadio on 13 November.

Senegal plans to reuse existing venues, including the Dakar Arena, the Léopold Sédar Senghor stadium, the Abdoulaye Wade stadium, the Olympic Village and the Train Express Régional. The article says the Games should also support vocational training, job creation, entrepreneurship and tourism.

Why it mattersDakar must now reuse its Youth Olympic venues and add cultural facilities for the 2031 Games, with five years to prepare.
