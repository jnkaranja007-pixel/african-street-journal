Senegal arrests former bodyguard over CFA235 million plot fraud

Investigators in Dakar say a colonel and several acquaintances paid deposits for residential land whose allocation certificates now appear to be fictitious.

Sources: Dakaractu and Seneweb · 2026-11-20 · 1 min read

Senegal's Criminal Investigation Division has arrested Lamine Faye, a former bodyguard and nephew of lawyer Abdoulaye Wade, and Yaya Ameth Diagne, an accountant and property agent, over alleged sales of fictitious plots in Dakar. The case began with a complaint from Colonel M. Guèye.

According to the newspaper Libération, Guèye and several acquaintances paid a total of CFA235 million in deposits for land in the Mamelles, Almadies, Mermoz and Maristes areas. The allocation certificates they received, presented as signed by the property directorate, have been called into question, as has the identity of the man presented as the owner.

Libération reports that Faye and Diagne are charged with criminal association and land fraud, and that other suspects were being sought.

Why it mattersBuyers who paid deposits for the advertised plots are left without valid title documents while investigators examine the seller's identity and the papers they were given.
