Senegal reports 548 cases of acute digestive syndrome

The Ministry of Health and Public Hygiene stated that patient conditions are stable with no severe cases or deaths recorded.

Source: Le Soleil · 2026-11-07 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

The Kébémer health district in Senegal's Louga region has recorded 548 cases of acute digestive syndrome, with 65 new cases reported between November 5 and 6, 2026. These infections were identified across nine sites within the district.

According to the Ministry of Health and Public Hygiene (MSHP), all patients are in stable condition, with three currently under observation and no severe cases or deaths reported to date. Joint investigation teams are working to identify a potential common source of contamination.

The MSHP is ensuring continued patient care and monitoring, especially in the most utilized health facilities. Awareness campaigns on good food and water hygiene practices are also underway. The ministry urges vigilance and adherence to preventive measures.

Why it mattersResidents of the Kébémer health district are affected by a widespread outbreak of acute digestive syndrome, with the Ministry of Health monitoring their conditions.
