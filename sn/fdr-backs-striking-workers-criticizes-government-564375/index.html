FDR backs striking workers, criticizes government

The Front for the Defense of Democracy and the Republic demands immediate social dialogue and respect for union commitments.

Source: Dakaractu · 2026-10-23 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

The Front for the Defense of Democracy and the Republic (FDR) declared solidarity with mobilized workers on October 21, 2026, denouncing the government's handling of social conflict. The organization called for immediate negotiations and criticized parliamentary inquiry commissions, urging national forces to unite.

The FDR supports the Union Front for the Defense of Labor, citing two general strike calls in three months: a 24-hour strike in July 2026 and a 72-hour strike in October. The group stated that government responses to the social stability pact demands are insufficient and that consensus from tripartite consultations on labor and social security codes was not respected.

The FDR also condemned the government's use of security forces to drive buses and requisition workers, viewing these as coercive measures. The organization demands structured social dialogue, respect for union commitments, and an end to intimidation tactics hindering the right to strike. The FDR expressed concern over tensions between legislative and executive powers regarding finance bills.

Why it mattersSenegalese workers face government pressure and unfulfilled commitments, impacting their right to strike and social dialogue.
