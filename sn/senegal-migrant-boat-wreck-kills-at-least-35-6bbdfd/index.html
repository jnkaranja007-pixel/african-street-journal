Senegal migrant boat wreck kills at least 35

The death toll from a capsized pirogue off the coast of Kébémer has risen significantly, with searches ongoing.

Source: RFI Afrique · 2026-11-09 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

At least 35 people have died after a migrant boat capsized off the coast of Kébémer in northwestern Senegal. Rescuers are continuing to search for survivors following the incident.

The provisional death toll rose from 21 on Saturday to at least 34 on Sunday, according to Dakaractu. RFI Afrique reported the toll had climbed to at least 35. Searches are ongoing in the Kébémer department for any additional victims.

This tragedy occurs on the Atlantic route used by thousands of young people attempting to reach Europe.

Why it mattersThe dangerous journey to Europe claimed at least 35 lives when a migrant boat capsized off Senegal's coast.
