Senegal's national assembly opens review of 2027 finance bill

The examination of the 2027 finance bill opens against a deteriorating climate in parliament, with the ruling party Pastef facing an internal purge.

Source: Jeune Afrique · 2026-11-22 · 1 min read

Senegal's national assembly has opened its examination of the 2027 finance bill, Jeune Afrique reported in its weekly news brief. The review begins against a backdrop of a purge within Pastef, the ruling party, and what the outlet described as a deteriorating climate in the assembly.

The report did not give details of the lawmakers affected by the purge, the measures under review in the bill or its figures. It also did not say who is carrying out the purge.

The brief also covered the Francophonie summit in Phnom Penh and diplomatic tensions over expulsions of gold miners in the Sahel.

Why it mattersLawmakers must now work through the 2027 finance bill while the internal row inside Pastef unfolds, shaping how the government's budget is handled in parliament.
