Senegal awaits sports court ruling on stripped Afcon title

The Court of Arbitration for Sport is deciding Senegal’s appeal against the decision awarding the 2025 championship to Morocco.

Sources: BBC Africa and Anadolu Africa · 2026-11-24 · 1 min read

Senegal’s appeal over the 2025 Africa Cup of Nations title is due for a ruling from the Court of Arbitration for Sport on Tuesday afternoon. The case challenges a decision to strip Senegal of the title and award it to Morocco after the January final.

The court heard the case in early October. The final in Rabat was goalless deep into second-half stoppage time when referee Jean Jacques Ndala ruled for a Morocco free-kick after Senegal thought Ismaila Sarr had scored. Senegal’s players briefly walked off the pitch, Anadolu Africa reported.

Morocco were seeking their first Afcon title since 1976 in front of 66,000 fans at Prince Moulay Abdellah Stadium. Sadio Mane was named the tournament’s best player.

Why it mattersSenegal’s appeal remains before the sports court, which is due to decide whether the country regains the title or Morocco keeps the 2025 championship.
