Senegal investigators refer two Omega Pro-linked people over fund collection

A complainant says she handed over four cheques totalling CFA31.56 million after being promised returns of up to 300%.

Sources: Dakaractu and Seneweb · 2026-11-19 · 1 min read

Senegal’s Criminal Investigation Division is examining two people linked to Omega Pro recruitment and fund collection after a complaint in Dakar. Aminata Satigui Sy and her brother, Hamady Mosatigui Sy, were arrested and referred to investigators on Wednesday, 18 November, Libération reported.

A complainant, N. F. Diop, told investigators she handed over four cheques between 16 August and 16 September 2022, totalling CFA31.56 million, payable to Hamady on his sister’s instructions. She said the proposed investment promised dividends of up to 300% after 18 months, but she could not verify where her money went. Hamady said he collected and transferred funds on his sister’s instructions and did not know Omega Pro’s internal workings; Aminata said she believed in the programme and lost nearly CFA100 million.

Another participant, K. Diop, said she transferred CFA3.164 million to E-Pocket Suarl and is seeking repayment. Investigators examined that company’s bank account: from 2 August 2022 to 29 February 2024, it recorded more than CFA4.53 billion in credits and more than 4.75 billion in debits, according to Libération.
