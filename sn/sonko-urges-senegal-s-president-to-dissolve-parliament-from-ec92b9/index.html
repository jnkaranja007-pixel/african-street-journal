Sonko urges Senegal’s president to dissolve Parliament from 2 December

The former prime minister says Bassirou Diomaye Faye can dissolve the legislature once the constitutional date arrives.

Sources: RFI Afrique and Seneweb · 2026-11-28 · 1 min read

Ousmane Sonko, Senegal’s former prime minister and leader of the parliamentary majority party, urged President Bassirou Diomaye Faye to dissolve Parliament in an address posted to social media on the evening of Thursday, 26 November. The Constitution allows dissolution from 2 December.

Sonko’s call comes during a dispute between the legislature and executive that has been under way since June, according to RFI Afrique. The president has not been reported as responding to the appeal.

Separately, the National Assembly’s bureau, chaired by Sonko on Friday, 27 November, adopted four proposals to create parliamentary inquiry commissions. The inquiries will cover debt, Banco Santander and special funds.

Why it mattersThe president must decide whether to dissolve Parliament once the constitutional window opens on 2 December, as the executive-legislative dispute continues.
