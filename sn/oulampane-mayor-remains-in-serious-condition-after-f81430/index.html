Oulampane mayor remains in serious condition after suspected arson

Sagar Coly was burned at his home in Diacounda on Thursday and transferred to Ziguinchor for specialised treatment.

Sources: Dakaractu, Seneweb and Le Soleil · 2026-11-27 · 1 min read

Sagar Coly, mayor of Oulampane in Senegal’s Bignona department, was badly burned in a fire at his home in Diacounda early Thursday, 26 November. He was taken to Ziguinchor for specialised treatment, and his condition remained serious on Friday, Dakaractu reported.

Investigators suspect Coly’s first wife started the fire after locking him in a room. Neighbours heard his cries and helped get him out. She was detained at Bounkiling and placed in police custody; accounts differ on where she was held afterward.

The prosecutor at Ziguinchor’s regional court has asked investigators to hear witnesses and establish whether the fire was planned. Reports say Coly had recently married a Senegalese woman living in France; the alleged role of that marriage is under investigation.

Why it mattersColy remains in serious condition and is receiving specialised treatment in Ziguinchor while the prosecutor has ordered witness interviews.
