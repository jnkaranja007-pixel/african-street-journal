Health union And Gueusseum calls 48-hour strike over housing allowances

The union says contract staff are owed less than CFA32 billion and wants a meeting with the prime minister.

Sources: Dakaractu and Seneweb · 2026-11-23 · 1 min read

And Gueusseum, Senegal's health workers' union, has called a 48-hour strike for Thursday 26 and Friday 27 November, its twentieth action plan. The union says contract staff in public health facilities, and local-government workers assigned to health, are still waiting for housing allowances tied to agreements signed in March and May 2022.

Coordinator Mballo Dia Thiam put the outstanding liability for contract staff at less than CFA32 billion and proposed paying about 8 billion a year over four years. He said the union may repeat the strike every 15 days and has asked to be received by the prime minister, because the health ministry depends on the finance ministry for some allowance decisions.

Thiam also cited delays in training and promotion, including nursing assistants waiting for places in training cohorts and requests dating back to 2018. He said the union was not a signatory to the economic and social stability pact and refused to add its name to it.

Why it mattersAbout CFA8 billion a year over four years is the union's proposed schedule for clearing the contract staff allowance debt.
