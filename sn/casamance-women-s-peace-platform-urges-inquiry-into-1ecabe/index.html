Casamance women’s peace platform urges inquiry into Oulampane mayor attack

The group called for calm and said authorities must establish responsibility after the mayor was seriously burned while asleep on 26 November.

Sources: Dakaractu and Le Soleil · 2026-11-28 · 1 min read

The Platform of Women for Peace in Casamance has called on Senegalese authorities to investigate an attack that seriously burned the mayor of Oulampane, in the Ziguinchor region, while he slept on the night of 26 November. The organisation issued its statement on 27 November.

The platform condemned the violence without regard to the attacker’s identity or motives, and urged residents to remain calm while administrative and judicial authorities and security forces investigate. Le Soleil reported that the mayor was taken to Ziguinchor and admitted to intensive care at the Peace Hospital; his condition was stable, according to the outlet.

The group said defending women’s rights should not excuse violence committed by women, during the 16 days of activism against gender-based violence. It urged families and communities to use dialogue and mediation to resolve disputes.

Why it mattersThe administrative and judicial authorities and security forces are being asked to investigate and establish responsibility for the attack.
