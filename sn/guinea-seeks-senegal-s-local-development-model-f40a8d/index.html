Guinea seeks Senegal's local development model

A Guinean delegation visits Senegal this week to learn from its community development program.

Source: Le Soleil · 2026-11-16 · 2 min read

A Guinean government delegation is in Senegal from November 16 to 21, 2026, to study the country's Programme d’urgence de développement communautaire (PUDC). The visit, supported by the United Nations Development Programme (UNDP), aims to help Guinea design its own local development program. Senegal is sharing its experience in community development with Guinea.

Led by Ambassador Kabelé Soumah, secretary general of Guinea's ministry of Planning and International Cooperation, the delegation will examine the PUDC's planning, financing, execution, and monitoring. This mission occurs as Guinea prepares a new local development program to support its Simandou 2040 plan, which seeks to improve territorial governance, reduce inequality, and enhance access to basic services. The future Guinean program will focus on vulnerable communes and villages, addressing social infrastructure, income-generating activities, climate resilience, and local capacity building.

Senegalese officials will discuss the PUDC's origins, institutional and financial structures, beneficiary targeting, procurement, project monitoring, and investment sustainability. The delegation will also meet with the Minister of Infrastructure in Diamniadio and visit rural electrification sites, a rural road, post-harvest facilities, and a health post in the Kaolack and Fatick regions. They will also hold working sessions with several Senegalese public bodies, including the Directorate General for Planning and Economic Policies (DGPPE) and the National Agency for Statistics and Demography (ANSD).
