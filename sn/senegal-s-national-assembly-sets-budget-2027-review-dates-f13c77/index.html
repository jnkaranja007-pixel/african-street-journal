Senegal's National Assembly sets budget 2027 review dates

The finance commission will scrutinize all ministries between November 7 and November 16, 2026.

Sources: Dakaractu, Seneweb and Le Soleil · 2026-11-06 · 1 min read

Senegal's National Assembly will examine the 2027 finance bill from November 7 to November 16, 2026. The finance commission will review all government departments during this period, with intense sessions scheduled daily. The process begins on November 7 with general considerations, including public revenue and debt, followed by specific ministries each day.

Alphonse Mané Sambou, president of the finance commission, defended the procedure after disagreements with the Ministry of Economy, Finance, and Planning. He stated the review of the 2027 budget is based on the rejected 2026 rectificative finance bill, making the figures legally inconsistent. Sambou aims for institutional dialogue to facilitate the bill's adoption.

The opposition, led by Takku Wallu Sénégal, has criticized the government's budget sincerity following the rejection of the 2026 rectificative finance bill on November 3. The National Assembly's decision to proceed with the 2027 budget review, despite these tensions, signals an intent to move forward. The opposition may use this forum to challenge the budget figures.

Why it mattersSenegalese lawmakers are reviewing the 2027 national budget, affecting government spending and national finances.
