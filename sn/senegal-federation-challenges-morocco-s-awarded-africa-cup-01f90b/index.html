Senegal federation challenges Morocco’s awarded Africa Cup title at sports court

The African football confederation gave Morocco a 3-0 forfeit win after Senegal had beaten it 1-0 in the 18 January final.

Sources: Le Soleil and Seneweb · 2026-11-24 · 1 min read

Senegal’s football federation has taken its dispute with Morocco over the Africa Cup of Nations title to the Court of Arbitration for Sport in Lausanne, Switzerland. Senegal beat Morocco 1-0 after extra time in the 18 January 2026 final, but the African football confederation’s appeal panel awarded Morocco a 3-0 forfeit victory on 17 March.

The Senegalese federation is challenging that ruling, which followed a temporary withdrawal by Senegal’s players after a penalty was awarded to Morocco, according to Le Soleil. The case asks the sports court to decide between the match result and the confederation’s application of its rules.

The court has handled disputes involving athletes, clubs and sports bodies, including cases concerning South African runner Caster Semenya and Manchester City. Le Soleil said the Senegal-Morocco case concerns the title itself, rather than an individual suspension or a club’s exclusion.

Why it mattersThe Senegalese football federation must pursue its challenge against the confederation’s ruling, which transferred the continental title to Morocco.
