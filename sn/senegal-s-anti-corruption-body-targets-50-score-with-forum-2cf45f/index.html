Senegal’s anti-corruption body targets 50% score with Forum Civil

The partners are beginning a self-assessment after Senegal remained in the red zone of Transparency International’s corruption index since 1998.

Sources: Dakaractu, Seneweb and Le Soleil · 2026-11-28 · 1 min read

Senegal’s National Office for the Fight Against Fraud and Corruption (OFNAC) and civil society group Forum Civil held a workshop in Saly on November 27 and 28 to develop steps to improve the country’s score on Transparency International’s Corruption Perceptions Index. OFNAC president Moustapha Ka said the partners aim to reach 50%, which he described as the threshold for leaving the index’s red zone.

Ka said the workshop follows a partnership agreement with Forum Civil and is intended to start a national self-assessment rather than leave Senegal to respond only to international rankings. He said corruption is present across public administrations, including the justice system, but OFNAC has no precise figures to rank the most vulnerable sectors.

OFNAC has begun studies with the environment and transport ministries and held a meeting with the justice ministry in Saly the previous week. Ka said reaching the 50% target requires efforts beyond his office.

Why it mattersOFNAC and Forum Civil must carry out the self-assessment and coordinate wider efforts to reach the 50% threshold.
