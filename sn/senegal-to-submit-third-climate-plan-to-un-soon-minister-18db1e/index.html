Senegal to submit third climate plan to UN soon, minister says

Environment Minister Aliou Gori Diouf said the validated NDC 3.0 will go in very shortly, ahead of COP32 in Ethiopia in 2027.

Source: Dakaractu · 2026-11-17 · 1 min read

Senegal unveiled its climate ambitions on Monday, November 16, 2026, at its national day at COP31 in Antalya, Turkey. Environment and Ecological Transition Minister Dr Aliou Gori Diouf said the country's third-generation Nationally Determined Contribution, known as NDC 3.0, has been validated and will be submitted very soon.

Speaking at the Senegal Pavilion under the theme "Senegal / Vision 2050", Diouf said climate change is already a lived reality, pointing to a retreating coastline, unpredictable rains and uncertain harvests. He said developing countries need accessible and predictable financing, shared technologies and stronger capacities, and that they are ready to prosper with low-carbon models.

Diouf called COP31 the "COP of implementation" and urged a move from pledges to investment. He also cited the validated Green Taxonomy, which is meant to steer financing toward sustainable projects, and said three high-level panels on industrial transition, loss and damage, and climate finance were on the programme.

Why it mattersFarmers and coastal communities face the impacts he described, and the validated Green Taxonomy is meant to direct financing toward sustainable projects in the coming years.
