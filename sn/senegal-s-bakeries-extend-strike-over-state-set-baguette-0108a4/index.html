Senegal’s bakeries extend strike over state-set baguette price

Three of four employer unions called for production to stop; 35% of bakeries were closed nationwide on Monday, according to the unions.

Source: RFI Afrique · 2026-11-24 · 1 min read

Senegal’s bread strike entered its second day on Tuesday, with production stoppages planned through Wednesday. Three of the country’s four employer unions in the baking sector called on bakeries to halt production as they seek a higher price for the 190-gram baguette, which the state sets.

The unions said an average of 35% of bakeries across Senegal closed on Monday. In the Dakar region, the closure rate was close to 40%, they said. Bakers say their operating costs have surged and are demanding an increase in the baguette’s regulated price.

The stoppage is scheduled to continue through Wednesday, the unions said. Their demand is for the state-set price to rise; the unions gave no proposed new price.

Why it mattersBakeries taking part in the stoppage are seeking a higher state-set price for the 190-gram baguette, with closures averaging 35% on Monday.
