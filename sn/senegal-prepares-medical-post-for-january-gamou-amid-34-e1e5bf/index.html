Senegal prepares medical post for January Gamou amid 34 diphtheria cases

State services also committed to water and electricity supplies for the religious gathering in Médina Niassène de Keur Madiabel.

Sources: Le Soleil and Dakaractu · 2026-11-27 · 1 min read

Senegal’s state services are preparing a medical post, water and electricity supplies for the Gamou religious gathering in Médina Niassène de Keur Madiabel, scheduled for 9 January 2027. The preparations were discussed at a meeting chaired on 26 November by Ibrahima Fall, the prefect of Nioro du Rip department.

Fall said the department had recorded 34 diphtheria cases and called for greater vigilance to prevent the disease from spreading. Health measures for the gathering will include public-awareness work, while the hygiene service plans disinfection operations. The event’s organisers also raised security and urged pilgrims to follow the instructions set by the religious leader.

The gathering will be held under the theme “Mercy is the foundation of Islam.”

Why it mattersHealth services must prepare an advanced medical post and awareness work for pilgrims while responding to 34 diphtheria cases in the department.
