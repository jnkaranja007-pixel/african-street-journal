CAF names Mané and Gueye among five African player finalists

Senegal has two contenders, while Morocco leads with three; the winner will be announced at the CAF Awards in Lagos on 2 December.

Sources: Le Soleil and Seneweb · 2026-11-25 · 1 min read

The Confederation of African Football (CAF) named Senegal’s Sadio Mané and Pape Gueye among five finalists for its 2026 African Player of the Year award on Wednesday, 25 November. Morocco has three contenders: Achraf Hakimi, Brahim Diaz and Ismaïl Saibari.

Mané, who won the award in 2019 and 2022, is seeking a third title. Gueye scored the extra-time winner against Morocco in the Africa Cup of Nations final in Rabat and was named in the tournament’s team of the tournament.

Senegal forward Ismaïla Sarr was left off the list despite winning the Africa Cup of Nations and the Conference League with Crystal Palace. The winner will be announced at the CAF Awards in Lagos, Nigeria, on Wednesday, 2 December, from 19:00 local time.

Why it mattersMané and Gueye must wait until 2 December to learn whether either will win the award, while Sarr has no place among the finalists.
