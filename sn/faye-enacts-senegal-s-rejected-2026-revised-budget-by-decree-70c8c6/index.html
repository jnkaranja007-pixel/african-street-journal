Faye enacts Senegal's rejected 2026 revised budget by decree

The president acted after Pastef, which holds a large majority in parliament, rejected the revised budget and the 2027 budget.

Sources: RFI Afrique, Seneweb and Le Soleil · 2026-11-20 · 1 min read

Senegalese President Bassirou Diomaye Faye has put the 2026 revised finance bill into force by decree, the presidency announced on 19 November. The move followed its rejection by Pastef, the African Patriots of Senegal for Work, Ethics and Fraternity, which holds a large majority in the National Assembly.

The authorities say the decree is constitutionally and legally grounded and is intended to ensure continuity of the state budget, according to a note reported by Le Soleil. Parliament rejected the revised bill on 3 November.

Pastef also rejected the 2027 budget proposal on 18 November.

Why it mattersThe government must keep the state budget operating under the decree after lawmakers rejected the revised bill.
