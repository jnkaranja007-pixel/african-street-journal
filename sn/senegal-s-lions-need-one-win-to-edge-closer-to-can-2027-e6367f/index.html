Senegal's Lions need one win to edge closer to CAN 2027

Senegal, leaders of Group J with 7 points, face Sudan in Juba on Monday, needing a win and a Mozambique loss to Ethiopia to qualify directly.

Sources: Le Soleil and Seneweb · 2026-11-16 · 1 min read

Senegal beat Sudan 4-1 in Dakar last Thursday and now face the Nile Crocodiles in Juba, South Sudan, on Monday at 13:00. Coach Patrick Vieira's side have won two and drawn one of their three matches, and are the only unbeaten team in Group J.

Vieira warned that Juba's playing conditions and the match context could make the task harder than the first leg suggested. He said Senegal must match Sudan tactically and physically and win their duels. Mozambique's surprise 1-0 home defeat to Ethiopia has also opened a route to qualification.

Senegal must still win the return match to qualify directly, and Mozambique would need to win in Addis Ababa. The final two qualifying days are scheduled for March 2027, and the tournament will be hosted by Uganda, Kenya and Tanzania.

Why it mattersA win in Juba would let Senegal book their place at the 2027 Africa Cup of Nations before the final two qualifying days in March 2027.
