Senegal's PUMA invests CFA560 million in Casamance schools

The new facilities are for pupils in villages where displaced families have returned, and form the programme's first phase of support.

Sources: Le Soleil and Seneweb · 2026-11-27 · 1 min read

Senegal's Programme for the Emergency Modernisation of Border Roads and Territories (PUMA) has invested CFA560 million in school infrastructure for villages in Casamance where displaced families have returned. The facilities were inaugurated during an evaluation tour across Ziguinchor, Sédhiou and Kolda regions.

PUMA national coordinator Ndèye Marieme Samb said the schools were an initial stage of support for returning residents. The programme has also built health posts in some localities. Samb said PUMA had completed 75% of a project valued at CFA6.4 billion.

Further planned work includes village electrification, improving access to isolated villages and economic activities for women and young people. PUMA also plans to continue helping residents obtain civil-status documents, working with the relevant services.

Why it mattersReturning families in Casamance are the intended beneficiaries of planned electrification, village-access work and economic activities, alongside the school and health facilities.
