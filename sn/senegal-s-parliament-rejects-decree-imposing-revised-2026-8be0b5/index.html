Senegal’s parliament rejects decree imposing revised 2026 budget

The assembly adopted its resolution by 128 votes to nine, warning officials against implementing a budget it considers unconstitutional and illegal.

Sources: RFI Afrique, Seneweb and Dakaractu · 2026-11-26 · 1 min read

Senegal’s National Assembly voted on Wednesday, 25 November, to oppose the government’s decision to impose the revised 2026 budget by decree after parliament rejected the bill. The resolution passed by 128 votes to nine, with two abstentions, among 139 voters.

The assembly, controlled by the Pastef party, said the decree was unconstitutional and illegal and warned public officials who implement it. President Bassirou Diomaye Faye, Senegal’s head of state, had instructed his cabinet on Wednesday to put the revised budget into effect without delay.

The resolution demands that the decree be withdrawn. The dispute pits the assembly’s opposition to the measure against the president’s instruction to implement it.

Why it mattersPublic officials face a parliamentary warning as the president’s order requires them to implement the revised budget without delay.
