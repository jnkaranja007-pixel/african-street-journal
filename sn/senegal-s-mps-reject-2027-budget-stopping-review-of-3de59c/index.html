Senegal's MPs reject 2027 budget, stopping review of remaining sections

The National Assembly voted 126 to 18 against the first section, covering revenue and debt, leaving the rest of the budget unexamined.

Sources: RFI Afrique, Seneweb and Dakaractu · 2026-11-19 · 1 min read

Senegal's National Assembly rejected the government's 2027 budget proposal on Wednesday evening, November 18, after MPs voted down its first section, covering revenue and debt. The vote was 126 against and 18 in favour, and the remaining sections will not be examined.

Ousmane Sonko, the Assembly president, asked the government to engage its responsibility after the vote. Most MPs are members of Pastef, the party that holds the majority in the Assembly. Finance and Budget Minister Cheikh Diba warned during the session that prolonged uncertainty over the budget could complicate efforts to mobilise a planned $2.2 billion programme with the International Monetary Fund (IMF).

Sonko said the Assembly was open to continued dialogue and supported an agreement with the IMF on clear and sincere terms. He objected to the Assembly being excluded from discussions with the Fund.
