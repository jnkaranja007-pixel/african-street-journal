Senegal's ENAMC drama students win prizes at festivals in Algiers and Rabat

The troupe took a Special Jury Prize in Rabat in October 2026 and another in Algiers in July, with one student winning the best male performance award.

Sources: Le Soleil and Seneweb · 2026-11-15 · 1 min read

Students of the National School of Arts and Crafts of Culture (ENAMC) in Senegal have won prizes abroad for their stage production "La Graine de la discorde". The troupe received a Special Jury Prize at the International Festival of Higher Education Schools in Dramatic Art in Rabat, Morocco, in October 2026, and student Abdel Sow won the Best Male Performance award for his role in the play.

The production was directed by Abdel Kader Diarra, a trainer and coordinator of the school's drama division. Earlier, in July 2026, the same troupe won a Special Jury Prize at the Algeria-Africa University Theatre Festival in Algiers, Algeria.

The school's new director general, Aboubekr Thiam, has said he wants to give the institution fresh momentum and raise its profile. The prizes come after a period of slower creative activity that observers had noted at the school.

Why it mattersThe awards give the school's students recognition on the international festival circuit, which the director general says he wants to build on.
