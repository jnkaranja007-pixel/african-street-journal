Sports tribunal confirms Senegal’s 2025 African title

The tribunal accepted Senegal’s football federation’s appeal and overturned the African confederation’s appeal-board decision on 24 November.

Sources: Seneweb and Jeune Afrique · 2026-11-27 · 1 min read

The Court of Arbitration for Sport (CAS) accepted Senegal’s football federation’s appeal and overturned an earlier ruling by the Confederation of African Football’s appeal board, confirming Senegal as African champions for 2025. The decision was issued on 24 November 2026.

Senegal’s federation appealed to CAS on 24 March after the confederation’s appeal board had ruled against it. The title confirms the result of Senegal’s match against Morocco in Rabat on 18 January 2026, according to an account by Seneweb.

The federation’s president, Abdoulaye Fall, discussed the legal victory in an interview with Jeune Afrique. The ruling also confirmed Senegal’s second continental star, Seneweb reported.

Why it mattersSenegal’s football federation can now count the team’s 2025 African championship as confirmed after the tribunal overturned the confederation’s ruling.
