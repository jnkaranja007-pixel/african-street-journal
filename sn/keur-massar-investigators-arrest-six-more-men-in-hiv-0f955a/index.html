Keur Massar investigators arrest six more men in HIV-transmission inquiry

The men were detained on 17 and 18 November after contacts on two suspects’ phones led gendarmes to new people in the case.

Sources: Dakaractu and Seneweb · 2026-11-20 · 1 min read

Gendarmes in Keur Massar arrested six men on 17 and 18 November in an inquiry into suspected deliberate HIV transmission, according to Senegalese newspaper L’Observateur. Investigators traced contacts through phones belonging to two people already under investigation, including O. Diop, who said he acted in the television series “Jeux de dames.”

The searches covered homes and tailoring workshops in Parcelles Assainies, Castors, Dalifort and Pikine, where officers seized boxes of antiretroviral medicines. L’Observateur reported that investigators were checking messages about meetings in private apartments and seeking to establish what those involved knew about their HIV status. The prosecutor ordered the suspects’ police custody extended.

The men are also suspected of criminal association and endangering others’ lives, the newspaper reported. Investigators continued examining seized phones to identify other contacts and clarify each person’s role.

Why it mattersThe six arrested men remain in extended police custody while investigators check their phones for other contacts and each suspect’s role.
