Senegal hosts African and diasporic arts events

Dakar is a venue for November exhibitions and festivals exploring colonial violence and Black identities.

Source: RFI Afrique · 2026-11-04 · 1 min read

African and diasporic creation is taking over museums, festivals, and galleries in Dakar this November. The events aim to examine colonial violence, Black identities, Senegalese heritage, and cosmological imaginaries. These gatherings are essential appointments for Afro and African cultures.

The cultural events span from Berlin to Tunis and Antananarivo, with Dakar being a key location. Organizers invite the public to send in their upcoming cultural events for future features.

These cultural rendezvous are part of a broader exploration of African and diasporic artistic expression throughout November.

Why it mattersSenegalese heritage and identity are being explored through art exhibitions and festivals in Dakar.
