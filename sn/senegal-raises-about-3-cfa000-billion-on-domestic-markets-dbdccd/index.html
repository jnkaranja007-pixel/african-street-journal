Senegal raises about 3 CFA000 billion on domestic markets

Public debt director Alioune Diouf said government securities supplied about 2 CFA500 billion, while regional public offerings brought in about 640 billion.

Sources: Dakaractu and Seneweb · 2026-11-26 · 1 min read

Senegal raised about 3 CFA000 billion on domestic markets from the start of 2026, public debt director Alioune Diouf said at a regional financial-market panel on Wednesday. The funding came through government securities and public offerings on the regional financial market.

Government securities accounted for about 2 CFA500 billion, while public offerings raised about 640 billion, Diouf said. He said domestic borrowing helps the state limit exchange-rate risk and cover financing needs using regional savings.

The state raised more than 4 CFA000 billion in 2025, against total financing needs of 6 CFA000 billion. Diouf said three-month operations also help the government meet obligations when revenue arrives at uneven intervals.
