FDR calls protest over budget and youth unemployment

The coalition demands a budget to address youth joblessness and poverty after a deadly pirogue sinking.

Sources: Dakaractu and Seneweb · 2026-11-12 · 1 min read

The Front for the defense of democracy and the Republic (FDR) is calling for a large demonstration on November 11, 2026, to demand the passage of a budget. The coalition wants the budget to permanently solve the problems of unemployed youth and increasingly impoverished populations. This call follows the capsizing of a pirogue off Lompoul, where 42 bodies were recovered.

The FDR links the public affairs management, leadership choices, and economic conditions to collective destiny, citing the pirogue disaster as evidence that young people risk death to leave the country. The coalition directly blames PASTEF and KIRAAY for the current crisis, stating their unrealistic proposals for youth issues have led to sterile debates in the National Assembly. The FDR accuses the ruling parties of disrespect for Senegalese citizens and criticizes their perceived incompetence and irresponsibility.

The FDR addresses the youth, acknowledging their doubts and weariness, and encourages them to strive for a better future in Senegal through effort and hard work. The coalition pledges to be at the forefront of this effort and offers condolences to the bereaved families.

Why it mattersThe FDR's call for a demonstration and its criticism of the government's economic policies directly affect Senegalese citizens, particularly the unemployed youth and impoverished populations.
