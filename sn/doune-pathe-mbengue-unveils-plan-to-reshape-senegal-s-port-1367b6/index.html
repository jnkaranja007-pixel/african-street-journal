Doune Pathé Mbengue unveils plan to reshape Senegal's port network

The Port Autonome de Dakar plans to reconfigure the Dakar port, convert Ndayane into a multi-terminal platform and link ports from Saint-Louis to Ziguinchor.

Sources: Dakaractu and Seneweb · 2026-11-16 · 1 min read

Doune Pathé Mbengue, director general of the Port autonome de Dakar (PAD), has set out a plan to reposition Senegal in African maritime and logistics competition. The plan covers the reconfiguration of the Dakar port, the diversification of the Ndayane terminals, the repositioning of Bargny-Sendou and a port network running from Saint-Louis to Ziguinchor.

Speaking at the Tourism Investment Forum, Mbengue said the aim is to make the Port autonome de Dakar the main gateway into Africa. He said Ndayane must move beyond a container terminal to host several types of terminals. The programme is aligned with the Senegal 2050 Transformation Agenda, and technical teams are still finalising its investment details.

Mbengue said the plan is meant to connect Senegal's ports to global trade routes and to the markets of its hinterland. No timetable for the investment programme was given at the forum.

Why it mattersShipping lines, cargo owners and inland traders will depend on which terminals receive the investment and when the details are fixed.
