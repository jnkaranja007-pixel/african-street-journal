Senegalese young reporters convene African media conference in Dakar

The 23-24 November gathering will examine how news organisations can fund journalism while preserving editorial independence.

Sources: Dakaractu, Seneweb and Le Soleil · 2026-11-22 · 1 min read

The Convention of Young Reporters of Senegal (CJRS) and the Konrad Adenauer Foundation will hold the Conference of Young African Journalists in Dakar on 23 and 24 November 2026. Media professionals from across Africa are due to discuss economic models that can sustain independent journalism.

The organisers say the agenda will address financial difficulties facing press companies, digital transformation, falling advertising revenue, changing news consumption and the arrival of new players. Participants will consider how media organisations can secure durable, diverse funding while protecting editorial independence, credibility and their role in democratic life.

The organisers also plan networking and exchanges of practices and experience among young journalists. They say the conference will explore cooperation and possible initiatives for a more resilient media sector.

Why it mattersThe CJRS and Konrad Adenauer Foundation must bring African media professionals together in Dakar for two days of discussions on financing independent journalism.
