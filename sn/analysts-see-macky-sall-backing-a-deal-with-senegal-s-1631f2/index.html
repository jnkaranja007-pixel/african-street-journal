Analysts see Macky Sall backing a deal with Senegal's president

Two political analysts say the former president is more likely to back Faye's coalition than to return to lead his party.

Sources: Seneweb and Dakaractu · 2026-11-23 · 1 min read

Several officials of the Alliance pour la République (APR) have called for former president Macky Sall to return and take control of the party to prepare its return to power. Two analysts interviewed by L'Observateur, Demba Guèye and Abdou Khadre Sanoko, instead point to a rapprochement with President Bassirou Diomaye Faye.

Guèye said Sall could join a broader coalition to support Faye's second term, arguing that an anti-Pastef coalition is needed and will exist. Sanoko said Sall could seek to strengthen the APR's presence in the National Assembly and make it a reliable ally of the presidential camp, Kiraay, before the next legislative elections.

Sanoko also credited Sall with a strategic role in the Front for the Defence of the Republic and said he had likely discussed Idrissa Seck's return to public politics with him. Sall, who withdrew from the race for UN secretary-general, has continued to appear at international forums.
