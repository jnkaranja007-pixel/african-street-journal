Marie Sarr reaches taekwondo final, guaranteeing Senegal another Dakar 2026 medal

The 55 kg taekwondo fighter beat American Karlee Vogel 2-0 at the Iba Mar Diop Complex, lifting Senegal into the top 10 medal-winning nations.

On Tuesday, November 10, 2026, Senegalese taekwondo athlete Marie Sarr won her women's -55 kg semi-final against American Karlee Vogel at the Iba Mar Diop Complex during the Dakar 2026 Youth Olympic Games. She won the match 2 rounds to 0, which guarantees Senegal a new medal.

Sarr took the first round 21 to 4 with eight kicks to the body and one to the head. Vogel tried to respond in the second round, but Sarr's defence and seven successful body kicks secured a 15 to 5 win and ended the fight in two rounds.

The same day, Aïda Touré lost her -63 kg semi-final to Iran's Asal Goltapeh but took bronze after Goltapeh did not appear for the third-place match. Senegal now sits in the top 10 medal-winning countries at the Games.

Why it mattersSenegal's medal tally rises, so its taekwondo athletes now compete for gold in the final.

Migrant boat sinks off Senegal, at least 35 dead

The provisional toll for a capsized migrant boat off northwestern Senegal has climbed to at least 35, with one survivor recovered so far.

A small boat carrying migrants sank off northwestern Senegal on Saturday, according to media reports. The pirogue-type flat-bottom boat capsized off the Loumpoul dunes in Kebemer department, between Dakar and the northern city of Saint-Louis.

Emergency services said on Sunday night that at least 35 people had died, with one survivor. The Senegalese interior ministry first reported 21 bodies and one survivor, and the Senegalese Press Agency later put the toll at 34. An interior ministry source said the toll would unfortunately rise, and an indeterminate number of people were reported missing.

The sinking is the latest on the dangerous Atlantic route from west Africa to Spain's Canary Islands, where thousands of people have died in recent years. The figures remain provisional as the search continues.

Why it mattersFamilies of the missing and the survivor now face an uncertain count, with an unknown number of people still unaccounted for.

Health · 2026-11-11

Senegal reports 649 food poisoning cases in Kébémer

No new cases of acute digestive syndrome have been reported in the last 24 hours.

Health authorities in Senegal reported 649 cases of collective food poisoning in the Kébémer health district between November 2 and November 9, 2026. The Ministry of Health and Public Hygiene stated on Tuesday that no new cases of acute digestive syndrome have been reported in the past 24 hours. All affected individuals are receiving outpatient care, with no hospitalizations or deaths recorded.

Investigations have identified Salmonella sp. in five analyzed cases at the Pasteur Institute of Dakar, though further serotyping and antibiogram results are pending. Six stool samples analyzed at Louga Regional Hospital did not detect Salmonella, Shigella, or Vibrio cholerae. Water and food samples have been sent for analysis, and sanitary inspections of food preparation sites are ongoing.

The Ministry of Health has urged the public to remain vigilant and seek immediate medical attention for symptoms like diarrhea, vomiting, abdominal pain, or fever. A multidisciplinary research team will be established to document the facts, identify causes, and pinpoint the source of contamination.

Why it mattersThe outbreak of food poisoning affects 649 people in the Kébémer district, with ongoing investigations into its cause.

Babacar Gaye accuses PASTEF of using budget as bargaining chip in Senegal

Former minister Babacar Gaye says budget talks are being used to negotiate the return of Ousmane Sonko and PASTEF to government.

Babacar Gaye, a former minister of state and leader of the Mankoo Mucc party, has written in the Dakaractu outlet that Senegal's national budget appears to be becoming a bargaining chip in talks over the return of Ousmane Sonko and his PASTEF party to government. He argues that if this reading is confirmed, the country would face not a simple political crisis but a hostage-taking of its institutions, with PASTEF using its parliamentary majority as a form of blackmail.

Gaye says the budget governs the lives of millions of Senegalese for 12 months, the running of public services and the state's financial credibility with the IMF and investors. He also criticises the entry into the talks of religious-influence networks and conflict brokers acting as mediators, and says that any paid promotion of a reconciliation narrative in the press would amount to manipulation of information. He calls for constitutional mechanisms and, if needed, voters to settle the institutional disagreement.

Gaye's article is an opinion column rather than a report of a confirmed agreement, and the piece itself does not establish that any deal has been struck. Its claims about payments to commentators remain unproven, as the column presents them only as suspicions.

Why it mattersMillions of Senegalese depend on a 12-month budget whose passage is now tied to talks over PASTEF's return to government, according to the column.

Transitaire inculpé dans l'affaire de faux billets de Reug Reug

Sidy Ndaw a été inculpé après que le marabout Diéguane Faye l'a accusé de lui avoir remis de faux billets, des accusations qu'il conteste.

Le juge du 3e cabinet du tribunal de Dakar a inculpé le transitaire Sidy Ndaw dans l'affaire de faux monnayage impliquant le lutteur Oumar Kane, alias « Reug Reug ». Ndaw a été cité par le marabout Diéguane Faye, qui affirme qu'il lui a remis les faux billets et l'a hébergé. Absent du pays au moment de l'éclatement de l'affaire, le transitaire a été placé sous contrôle judiciaire à son retour.

Lors de l'audition au fond, Diéguane Faye, sous mandat de dépôt, a maintenu ses accusations. Ndaw a reconnu l'avoir hébergé mais conteste lui avoir remis de faux billets, affirmant qu'il s'agissait de prières pour ses chevaux engagés dans une course hippique. Une confrontation entre les deux hommes n'a débouché sur aucun rapprochement. Reug Reug, également sous contrôle judiciaire, nie les faits et affirme avoir seulement transporté une caisse qu'il croyait contenir des bains mystiques.

L'affaire remonte au 24 septembre 2026, date à laquelle Reug Reug, son oncle Ahmadou Bamba Ndiaye et le marabout Diéguane Faye ont été interpellés par la Brigade de recherches de Faidherbe. Ils sont soupçonnés de détention de billets noirs de 100 dollars, dont la contre-valeur est estimée à 830 millions de francs CFA. L'agent immobilier Amady Diop, quatrième personne interpellée, a reconnu avoir effectué des transactions avec le marabout Faye.

Why it mattersLe transitaire Sidy Ndaw est désormais inculpé dans une affaire de billets noirs d'une valeur estimée à 830 millions de francs CFA.
