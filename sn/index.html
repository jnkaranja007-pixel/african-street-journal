Senegal expresses concern over U.S. sanctions on ICC official

The Senegalese government expressed deep concern on Thursday regarding U.S. sanctions announced Aug. 18. The measures target International Criminal Court officials, including Senegalese national Abdoulaye Seye. Seye is a member of the Court's Office of the Prosecutor and a candidate for a judge position.

Why it mattersThe sanctions affect the diplomatic standing and judicial candidacy of a Senegalese official at the international level.

Linguère police arrest 4 for drug trafficking

The Linguère urban police station arrested four individuals for association of criminals and drug trafficking. The suspects were detained for the possession and use of narcotics, according to reports from Seneweb and Le Soleil.

Why it mattersThe arrests remove four suspected drug traffickers and users from the local community.

Deputy Sagna challenges telecom credit and network practices

Deputy Guy Marius Sagna called for the application of indemnities for digital agents during the review of Bill No. 25/2026. He criticized the practice of expiring telephone credit and the use of 'Dalal Tones' in the National Assembly. Sagna also questioned network coverage across several regions.

Why it mattersChanges to credit expiration and network coverage would directly affect the cost and reliability of mobile services for telephone users.
