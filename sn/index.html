Dakar governor suspends popular football matches

The governor of Dakar, Ousmane Kane, banned local football games citing security needs for the Youth Olympic Games.

Popular football matches, known as Navétanes, have been suspended across the Dakar region starting Monday, October 26, 2026. Governor Ousmane Kane issued an order on October 23, 2026, halting these widely followed neighborhood games and related activities until further notice.

The suspension follows a request from the Dakar Gendarmerie Legion, which cited security imperatives for the upcoming Dakar 2026 Youth Olympic Games (YOG). The governor's order highlighted the significant deployment of security forces for the YOG and the risk of public disorder and property damage associated with the football matches.

No date has been set for the resumption of the Navétanes, leaving the duration of the suspension indefinite. The YOG are scheduled to take place from October 31 to November 13, 2026.

Why it mattersPopular football matches in Dakar are suspended due to security preparations for the Youth Olympic Games.

Senegal postpones budget review to October 27

The National Assembly delayed its review of the 2026 supplementary finance law, originally set for October 23.

The Senegalese National Assembly postponed its plenary session to examine the 2026 supplementary finance bill from October 23 to October 27 at 10 AM. This adjustment was announced via a press release from the Assembly's communication department. The proposed law, numbered 40/2026, allows the government to modify the state budget during the fiscal year if revenue or expenditure forecasts change.

The parliamentary group Takku Wallu Sénégal abstained from the vote on the 2026 supplementary finance bill in a previous plenary session. This supplementary budget allows for adjustments to state spending and revenue to new economic realities within parliamentary procedures.

In separate operations, the Fatick gendarmerie arrested 378 individuals attempting irregular emigration between October 20 and 22, 2026. Seized items included boats, outboard motors, and significant amounts of fuel. Investigations into these emigration attempts are ongoing.

Why it mattersSenegalese citizens attempting irregular emigration were arrested, and the government's budget review was postponed.

Macron cancels Senegal visit amid French social unrest

French President Emmanuel Macron was scheduled to visit Dakar on October 30-31, his first since President Faye's election.

French President Emmanuel Macron has cancelled a planned trip to West Africa, which included a stop in Dakar, Senegal, at the end of October. The decision was reportedly made due to unprecedented social unrest in France. Macron was expected in Dakar on October 30 and 31 for what would have been his first official visit since President Bassirou Diomaye Faye was elected.

The visit, which also included a stop in Abuja, Nigeria on October 28, was aimed at reviving France's relations in West Africa. Preparations for the trip were advanced, though it had not been officially announced by the Élysée Palace. The trip was also set against the backdrop of Senegal hosting the Youth Olympic Games.

Paris sought to relaunch its relations in West Africa with this planned visit. The cancellation comes as France grapples with significant internal social challenges.

Why it mattersPresident Macron's visit to Senegal, his first since President Faye's election, was cancelled due to social unrest in France.

Senegal union ends three-day strike

The Front syndical pour la défense du travail suspended negotiations with the government after a national strike.

Senegal's three-day general strike concluded on Friday, October 23, 2026, with union leaders reporting a massive turnout across the country and in Dakar.

The Front syndical pour la défense du travail (FSDT) has suspended its industrial action and is awaiting the outcome of ongoing negotiations with the Senegalese government.

Union leaders expressed satisfaction with the broad participation in the strike, which took place on October 21, 22, and 23, 2026.

Why it mattersWorkers in Senegal participated in a three-day strike affecting the country and its capital.

Sport · 2026-10-24

Mamelodi Sundowns eliminated from CAF Champions League

The defending champions drew 1-1 with Angola's Wiliete SC, exiting the competition on away goals.

Defending CAF Champions League champions Mamelodi Sundowns were eliminated from the 2026-2027 competition after a 1-1 draw with Angola's Wiliete SC on Friday. The match, held at Loftus Versfeld Stadium in Pretoria, was the second leg of the preliminary round.

After a 0-0 draw in the first leg in Angola, Mamelodi Sundowns needed a home win to continue defending their title. Tashreeq Matthews scored for the South African side in the 11th minute, but Angolan forward Mabululu equalized in the 49th minute. This result meant the aggregate score was 1-1.

Wiliete SC advances to the group stage on the away goals rule, while Mamelodi Sundowns exit the tournament in the second preliminary round. The Senegalese club had previously lost the first leg 0-2 in Dakar and needed a comeback to qualify for the group stage.

Why it mattersSenegalese football fans will not see their club compete further in the CAF Champions League.
