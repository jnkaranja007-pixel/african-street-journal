Pastef urges government to respect 2027 local election timeline

The majority party in the Assembly, Pastef, has issued a statement reminding the government that local elections must occur by Sunday, January 17, 2027.

Pastef, the majority party in Senegal's Assembly, has called on the government to adhere to the electoral calendar for the upcoming local elections. The party stated that these elections should take place no later than Sunday, January 17, 2027. This reminder comes as the decrees setting the election date and the amount of the electoral deposit have not yet been published by the executive.

The party emphasized that respecting the electoral calendar is a legal obligation for all. This statement highlights a crucial political test for Senegal, with local elections anticipated in January 2027. The absence of key decrees has prompted Pastef to publicly press the government on the matter.

The government has not yet responded to Pastef's communiqué. The upcoming local elections are a significant event in the country's political landscape, and the timely publication of the necessary decrees is essential for their proper organization and execution.

Why it mattersCitizens are affected by the uncertainty surrounding the local elections, as the delay in publishing key decrees could impact their participation and representation.

Keur Massar police arrest alleged gang leader after assaults

Police in Keur Massar arrested an individual on August 25, 2026, identified as the presumed leader of a gang linked to multiple assaults and threats against security forces.

The Keur Massar Research Brigade apprehended the suspect following complaints from two victims assaulted on the night of August 24 to 25, 2026. One victim sustained a knife wound to the forearm while attempting to assist another person near the Shell station in Keur Massar, and the second victim suffered injuries and had their motorcycle damaged.

Investigators state the individual allegedly led a group that committed assaults, often after wrestling matches, and used the Mbao forest as a retreat. The suspect also reportedly posted threats against security forces on TikTok. A search of his home uncovered two machetes, empty diluent containers, and cannabis residue.

The suspect faces charges including criminal association, armed robbery, intentional assault and battery, rebellion, possession of a bladed weapon, and cannabis trafficking. He is currently in police custody as the investigation continues.

Why it mattersResidents of Keur Massar and surrounding areas are directly affected by the arrest, which addresses concerns about local safety and criminal activity.

Senegal basketball team hosts Egypt in FIBA World Cup qualifier

Senegal's national men's basketball team, the Lions, will play Egypt today at Dakar Arena at 18 h 30 in their first game of the fourth window of the FIBA 2027 World Cup African.

The Lions, currently leading their group with a 5-1 record, aim to qualify for the World Cup. This match is part of 24 games taking place from Thursday, August 27, to Sunday, August 30, in Radès, Tunisia, and Dakar, Senegal. Dakar Arena is hosting World Cup qualifiers for the first time.

Senegal is in Group F alongside Côte d’Ivoire (5-1), Mali (5-1), Angola (4-2), Egypt (3-3), and the Democratic Republic of Congo (2-4). The team seeks to replicate their performance from window three, where they secured three victories in as many outings. The Lions have won five of their six matches at Marius Ndiaye stadium.

Egypt, known as the Pharaohs, showed progress by beating Angola away and winning two of their three matches in Luanda. The Senegalese team, led by Brancou Badio, will need to counter Egypt's tenacity. Senegal has dominated the paint in this campaign, averaging 52 rebounds and 7,8 blocks per game, the best in the competition.

Why it mattersBasketball fans in Senegal and across Africa are directly affected as their national teams compete for a spot in the FIBA 2027 World Cup.

Aston Villa nears £65m deal for Chelsea's Jackson

Aston Villa is close to agreeing to sign striker Nicolas Jackson from Chelsea for up to £65m, a move that could facilitate Ollie Watkins' transfer to Al-Hilal.

Aston Villa is nearing an agreement to acquire Senegalese international Nicolas Jackson from Chelsea, with a deal valued at up to £65m. This potential transfer would see Chelsea receive an initial sum of about £50m, with an additional £15m in add-ons. The move is contingent on Jackson agreeing to personal terms and completing the switch to Villa.

Jackson, who has eight goals in 37 caps for Senegal and played in the World Cup, is expected to reunite with Unai Emery, his former coach at Villarreal. His arrival at Villa is anticipated to trigger the departure of England international Ollie Watkins to Saudi Pro League side Al-Hilal in a deal worth about £51m.

Watkins, who became Villa's record Premier League goalscorer with 91 goals, missed Sunday's 4-0 defeat at Brighton. Villa had been preparing to sell Watkins but needed to secure a replacement before sanctioning his move. The club is also ready to allow Tammy Abraham to join Ipswich Town on loan.

Why it mattersThis event directly affects Nicolas Jackson's career trajectory and financial standing, as well as the strategic planning and financial outlay of Aston Villa and Chelsea.

Nepal, Tibet floods leave over one thousand missing, 270 dead

Rescue efforts are underway in Nepal and Tibet to locate more than one thousand missing individuals, including hundreds of foreign tourists, after a devastating flood on Wednesday.

A devastating flood on Wednesday at the Nepal-Tibet border has left at least 270 people dead and over one thousand missing, according to Nepalese authorities and Chinese state media. Nepalese authorities reported 823 people unaccounted for, including 517 foreign nationals, while Chinese state media stated 558 people, including 260 foreigners, are missing after mudslides buried Gyirong Port.

The floods swept through the Trishuli River valley in Nepal's Nuwakot district and the Bhotekoshi valley east of Kathmandu. Among the missing foreign nationals are 178 Indians, 65 Americans, 53 Ukrainians, 51 Malaysians, 33 Britons, 25 Canadians, 15 Australians, 12 Israelis, and four French citizens, as detailed by the Nepalese tourism bureau.

Nepalese Prime Minister Balendra Shah urged national unity, stating the state would provide full support. Chinese President Xi Jinping emphasized prioritizing search and rescue operations, evacuating residents, treating the injured, and minimizing casualties. Nearly 800 rescuers, 150 vehicles, sniffer dogs, and boats have been deployed for the operations.

Why it mattersThe floods have directly impacted hundreds of foreign tourists and local residents, causing deaths and widespread disappearances, leading to significant disruption and loss of life.
