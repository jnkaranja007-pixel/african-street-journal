Senegal's parliament rejects government's revised budget

The National Assembly, controlled by the opposition, voted against the 2026 supplementary finance bill on Tuesday, November 3.

Senegal's National Assembly, dominated by the opposition, rejected the government's revised 2026 budget on Tuesday, November 3. This decision leaves the executive with limited options for adopting a budget essential for settling debts with private companies and securing International Monetary Fund financing.

The Ministry of Economy, Finance and Planning disputes a request to withdraw the 2027 finance bill, made by the head of the National Assembly's Finance and Budgetary Control Committee. The ministry stated on November 4 that the rejection of the 2026 revised budget does not justify withdrawing the following year's budget, calling the parliamentary approach contradictory.

The Finance Committee chairman had requested the withdrawal of the 2027 draft finance bill on November 3, arguing that the rejection of the 2026 revised budget undermines its foundational assumptions. He proposed presenting a new version based on the 2026 initial finance law, a stance the ministry contests while questioning adherence to parliamentary procedures.

Why it mattersThe opposition-controlled National Assembly rejected the government's revised 2026 budget, impacting the executive's ability to manage national finances and secure international aid.

Dakar hosts culinary fair during Youth Olympics

A food fair showcasing African culture is running alongside the Dakar 2026 Youth Olympics until November 13.

Dakar is hosting the Youth Olympic Games from October 31 to November 13, 2026. The city and the Municipal Development and Solidarity Fund (FODEM) are using the event to highlight culture and gastronomy. A culinary fair opened on November 2 at the city's esplanade, aiming to display Africa's cultural diversity.

Vendors like Fodé Seydi of Tawfekh service are selling traditional drinks such as 'wass' and 'café Touba'. Maman Sané, CEO of MS Global, partnered with caterer Fa Zeyna service for a 250,000 CFA franc spot to offer diverse meals from breakfast to dinner. Sané also works in digital marketing.

The fair, which lasts for 15 days, has seen visitors attending, particularly in the evenings. Organizers are optimistic about the event's outcomes, with initial sales described as satisfying. The fair features stands with colorful banners and thematic posters, alongside Olympic branding and the mascot Ayo.

Why it mattersSenegalese residents and visitors in Dakar can experience diverse African culinary traditions and cultural displays during the Youth Olympic Games.

Health · 2026-11-05

Senegal launches national diabetes awareness campaign

The Association Sénégalaise de Soutien aux Diabétiques (ASSAD) is urging early screening and better management of the disease.

The Association Sénégalaise de Soutien aux Diabétiques (ASSAD) launched a national campaign on November 4, 2026, to raise awareness and combat diabetes. Endocrinologist Professor Abdoulaye Leye stressed the importance of early detection, stating that a simple screening can cost less than 500 CFA francs.

Professor Leye highlighted that early diagnosis and lifestyle changes can help individuals live healthily for longer, even up to 100 years. He noted that 87% of ASSAD patients come from impoverished families, indicating a significant financial burden for care.

The campaign aims to foster dialogue, raise public awareness, and mobilize efforts for prevention and early detection. Dr. Moustapha Diop from the Ministry of Health and Public Hygiene confirmed that diabetes is a priority for health authorities due to its significant medical, social, and economic consequences.

Why it mattersDiabetes is a major public health challenge in Senegal, impacting individuals' health, social lives, and the economy.

Sport · 2026-11-05

Senegal futsal team wins first JOJ match

Mamadou Diagne scored the winning goal in the final minute against Panama.

Senegal secured their first victory at the Youth Olympic Games (JOJ) on November 4, 2026, defeating Panama in a thrilling futsal match at the Iba-Mar-Diop Complex. Diakhaté opened the scoring in the 8th minute and quickly added a second goal. Mamadou Diagne extended the lead to 3-0 early in the second half.

Panama fought back, scoring twice to narrow the deficit and then equalizing in the 33rd minute. Senegal regained the lead with a fourth goal, but Panama equalized again. In the closing moments, Mamadou Diagne scored the decisive goal, securing the 4-3 victory and sparking celebrations among the large crowd.

The Senegalese team, known as the Lionceaux, are now third in their group. They will face Afghanistan on Saturday at 17:30 GMT in a match that will determine a place in the semi-finals.

Why it mattersSenegalese youth athletes have secured a victory in the Youth Olympic Games futsal tournament.

Health · 2026-11-05

Senegal increases insulin subsidy to 520 million CFA francs

The Ministry of Health aims to promote annual health check-ups for all citizens to prevent costly diseases.

The Senegalese government has raised its subsidy for insulin from 300 million to 520 million CFA francs to improve diabetes care. This increase supports the availability of antidiabetic medications through the national pharmacy.

Mamadou Moustapha Diop, director of disease control, stated that non-communicable diseases, especially diabetes, are a top priority for the Ministry of Health. The ministry plans to implement a Targeted Annual Check-up (BAC) program to encourage citizens to adopt regular health screenings.

The BAC initiative aims to have every Senegalese person undergo a check-up based on their age, health background, and sex. This preventative measure is intended to anticipate and manage diseases that represent a significant financial burden for the state.

Why it mattersSenegalese citizens with diabetes will benefit from increased insulin subsidies and improved access to medication.
