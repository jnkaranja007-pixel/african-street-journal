Senegal mobilises 600 health workers for Dakar 2026 Youth Olympics

Over 30 ambulances and reinforced hospital facilities will support the health coverage for the upcoming youth games.

Senegal's Ministry of Health and Public Hygiene has mobilised more than 600 health professionals and 30 ambulances to ensure health coverage for the Dakar 2026 Youth Olympic Games (YOG). This health provision includes medical posts at competition sites, emergency services, ambulances, and reference hospitals. The Hôpital d’Enfants de Diamniadio has been designated as the official YOG hospital and received upgraded technical equipment to improve diagnostic and patient care capabilities.

The health plan involves coordinated efforts between medical teams across various sites, intervention squads, and care facilities, with professionals distributed to competition venues and support teams. The public health facility in Mbour also underwent upgrades to meet standards. Epidemiological surveillance has been intensified at border posts and entry points, with regular bulletins shared and delegation medical teams trained on the national alert system. Simulation exercises were conducted at the Hôpital d’Enfants de Diamniadio.

The National Supply Pharmacy (SEN-PNA) is managing the distribution of medicines and medical supplies, with regular stock monitoring to prevent shortages. A dedicated digital patient file system for the YOG will enhance care traceability and medical information flow. The ministry aims to provide appropriate care for athletes, delegations, spectators, and the public while strengthening prevention and emergency management during the games.

Why it mattersOver 600 health professionals and 30 ambulances are mobilised to ensure health coverage for the Dakar 2026 Youth Olympic Games, affecting athletes, delegations, and the public.

Senegal seeks global energy dialogue role in Moscow

Energy Minister Abdourahmane Diouf met Russian and Azerbaijani counterparts to discuss partnerships.

Senegal aims to play a key role in global energy discussions, according to Minister of Energy and Petroleum Abdourahmane Diouf. He attended the 8th summit of the Gas Exporting Countries Forum (GECF) in Moscow on October 26-27, 2026. Diouf led Senegal's delegation at the meetings chaired by Russian President Vladimir Putin.

Discussions focused on global gas market strategies, energy security, market stabilization, and gas's role in the energy transition. Diouf also held bilateral meetings with ministers from Russia, Azerbaijan, Iran, and Qatar. These talks aimed to establish strategic partnerships for Senegal's hydrocarbon projects, including gas valorization and the Gas-to-Power strategy.

The minister stated that Senegal's active participation reaffirms its determination to play a key role in global energy dialogue and secure economic sovereignty, aligning with the Vision Senegal 2050 energy goals. He also discussed potential collaborations between Russia's Gazprom and Senegal's Petrosen.

Why it mattersSenegalese citizens are affected by the government's pursuit of strategic partnerships for hydrocarbon projects and energy security.

Colonel Abdoulaye Mbengue takes command of military zone 3

The new commander will focus on anticipation, discipline, and collaboration with local populations to address security threats.

Colonel Abdoulaye Mbengue was officially installed on Tuesday, October 27, 2026, as the new commander of Military Zone N°3, which covers the Kaolack, Kaffrine, and Fatick regions. The ceremony was presided over by the Chief of General Staff of the Armed Forces, Vice Admiral Oumar Wade, and included a military parade.

Colonel Mbengue stated his priorities include improving existing prevention activities and enhancing troop readiness, emphasizing discipline. He also highlighted the need for anticipation and frank collaboration with administrative authorities and populations to counter new threats, particularly those emerging from the Sahel and border areas.

The new commander stressed the importance of information gathering and reaction time to address threats before they reach the population. He called for public cooperation, noting that the population's security and property protection are paramount.

Why it mattersColonel Abdoulaye Mbengue's appointment as commander of Military Zone N°3 affects the security of populations in the Kaolack, Kaffrine, and Fatick regions.

Senegal parliament sends budget bill back to committee

The proposed 2026 supplementary finance law was returned for further discussion on October 27, 2026.

Senegal's parliament sent the proposed 2026 supplementary finance law back to committee on October 27, 2026. The move followed objections from the parliamentary majority and the Pastef party. This decision aims to find a compromise between the government and the opposition.

Minister of Economy, Finance and Planning Cheikh Diba requested the bill's return to the finance and budget control committee. He stated this would allow for continued discussions between the government and deputies. The request came after the general discussion opened on Tuesday.

The bill's referral to committee was anticipated by some observers. It follows the reading of the committee's report and formal observations. The minister's advisors reportedly suggested this course of action.

Why it mattersThe parliamentary majority and the Pastef party are directly affected by the proposed finance law.

Politics · 2026-10-28

Senegal forces arrest 28 at MFDC political wing site

Security forces seized phones, documents, and flags during the operation in Ziguinchor.

Senegalese army and gendarmerie forces arrested 28 people, including nine women, in Ziguinchor on October 27, 2026. The operation targeted a compound identified as the headquarters of the political wing of the Movement of Democratic Forces of Casamance (MFDC) in the Lyndiane Coboda neighborhood. Seized items included 17 mobile phones, documents, and MFDC insignia.

The raid occurred on the anniversary of the death of Abdou Elinkine Diatta, a significant figure in the Casamance rebellion, according to a source close to the case. The gathering was reportedly marked by the display of the MFDC flag, which authorities removed. The arrested individuals and seized items were handed over to the Ziguinchor gendarmerie research section for further investigation.

The investigation will aim to clarify the circumstances of the meeting and determine responsibilities. The site, known as 'Mangoukouro,' is a symbolic location for the MFDC's political wing, where its flag and anthem were developed. This action follows the August 2025 arrest and subsequent provisional release of MFDC spokesperson Amidou Djiba.

Why it mattersThe operation in Ziguinchor directly affects 28 individuals arrested and the political wing of the MFDC.
