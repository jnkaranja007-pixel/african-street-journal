Politics · 2026-11-18

Faye meets Macron and investors on Paris leg of diplomatic tour

Senegal's president also held talks with Rothschild & Co and Vinci on energy, health and airport projects before the Francophonie summit in Cambodia.

President Bassirou Diomaye Faye completed a diplomatic tour of Paris and Phnom Penh on 16 November, after a trip from 9 to 16 November. In Paris on 9 November he met French President Emmanuel Macron at the Élysée Palace, and the two discussed bilateral issues including preparations for an intergovernmental seminar and trade exchanges.

On 11 November Faye received leaders of industrial and financial groups, including Rothschild & Co and Vinci. Talks covered projects in energy, health and airport infrastructure, and how they would be financed. Faye presented Senegal's priorities and reforms aimed at improving investment security, according to Le Soleil.

The second leg took Faye to Phnom Penh on 15 and 16 November for the 20th Francophonie Summit, where discussions focused on choosing the next secretary-general of the International Organisation of La Francophonie (OIF), the organisation of French-speaking countries. Faye had also thanked France for its support for the Dakar 2026 Youth Olympic Games.

Security · 2026-11-18

Senegal police arrest man accused of 19.2 million CFA job scam

Investigators allege the suspect took the money by promising a director's post at the Port autonome de Dakar at 2.5 million CFA francs a month.

The Division des investigations criminelles arrested D. Fall, a 30-year-old who presented himself as a geotechnician, over an alleged fraud and forgery involving 19,203,650 CFA francs. He is accused of promising a complainant that his wife would be hired as a director at the Port autonome de Dakar, with a monthly salary of 2.5 million CFA francs.

According to Libération, the alleged dealings began in March 2025. Fall allegedly collected 12,163,650 CFA francs as application fees, handed over a technician's diploma that was later found to be false, and promised a 15-day internship at a mining company in Ghana. He then allegedly demanded another 7 million CFA francs to unblock the file at the education ministry.

When the complainant checked, he reportedly found the diploma was false and no documents had been registered in his wife's name. He asked for his money back, but Fall allegedly refused and became unreachable. He has been arrested on suspicion of fraud and forgery; the allegations have not been proven.

Why it mattersThe complainant lost nearly 19.2 million CFA francs, and the suspect faces charges of fraud and forgery over the alleged fake hiring offer.

Sport · 2026-11-18

Six teams qualify for 2027 Africa Cup of Nations

Mali, Burkina Faso, Benin, Cameroon and Morocco joined Senegal after Tuesday’s qualifying matches, with the final places still undecided.

Mali, Burkina Faso, Benin, Cameroon and Morocco qualified for the 2027 Africa Cup of Nations on Tuesday, 17 November, joining Senegal as the first six teams confirmed for the tournament. The results came in the fourth round of qualifying matches.

Mali beat Rwanda 2-0 in Kigali, while Burkina Faso won 3-1 away to Mauritania. Burkina Faso’s victory also secured Benin’s place through its position in Group F; Mauritania and the Central African Republic are out of contention. Cameroon qualified without playing after Comoros beat Congo-Brazzaville 1-0, leaving Cameroon’s 10 points enough to guarantee a top-two finish in Group G.

Morocco secured qualification with a 1-0 win over Niger, its fourth consecutive victory. The remaining qualifying rounds will decide the other places in the 2027 tournament; Rwanda remains in contention for second in Group K.

Why it mattersRwanda remains in contention for the second qualifying place in Group K, while Mauritania and the Central African Republic are eliminated.

Security · 2026-11-18

Senegal arrests 26 in suspected irregular migration attempt at Abéné

The group included 24 Senegalese and two Gambians; investigators suspect two or three alleged organisers were posing as would-be migrants.

Senegalese gendarmes arrested 26 people on Abéné beach in the Kafountine area during the night of 16 to 17 November 2026, at about 4 a.m., after receiving information about a planned irregular migration departure. The group included 24 Senegalese and two Gambian nationals.

A security source said patrols intercepted two groups near the Kalisai hotel and Atlantique camp. The Senegalese group included two minors. Authorities took those arrested to the Kafountine brigade for identification and an investigation; initial findings suggest two or three suspected organisers were among them.

In the Kafountine-Diouloulou area, authorities have foiled 11 departures since the start of 2026 and arrested 183 would-be migrants and 13 alleged organisers or boat operators, according to Le Soleil. Seven departures were stopped by Kafountine’s local brigade and four by Diouloulou’s territorial brigade.

Why it mattersThe 26 people arrested, including two minors and two Gambian nationals, were taken to the Kafountine brigade for identification and investigation.

Education · 2026-11-18

UCAD launches inclusion directorate and plans campus access work

The new office will assess community needs and oversee work on facilities for students with disabilities and psychological support.

Cheikh Anta Diop University of Dakar (UCAD) launched a Directorate of Inclusion, Well-being and Human Development on Tuesday, 17 November 2026. The office is built around inclusion and equity, cohesion and solidarity, well-being and mental health, and human development.

Its director, Amy Kébé Mané, said the office would first map university units and external partners working on inclusion and equality, then conduct a full assessment of the community’s needs. Three divisions will cover inclusion and changing practices, social action and solidarity, and well-being and psychological support for students and staff.

Mané said making the directorate operational was the immediate priority. Work is under way to refurbish a room for students with disabilities, renovate toilets and improve campus accessibility. The student association president said students were seeking rights, including access so no one is left behind.

Why it mattersStudents with disabilities are the direct beneficiaries of planned room refurbishment, toilet renovation and improved campus accessibility.
