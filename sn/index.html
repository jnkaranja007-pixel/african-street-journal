Senegal PM defends 2050 vision amid budget constraints

Prime Minister Ahmadou Alhaminou Mohamed Lô stated that the method for achieving Senegal's 2050 goals will adapt, not the ambition.

Prime Minister Ahmadou Alhaminou Mohamed Lô defended Senegal's "Vision 2050" goals on October 25 in Dakar, stating that while the country faces budget constraints, the ambition for economic sovereignty and social justice remains unchanged. He called for institutional differences to be set aside to prioritize the interests of Senegalese citizens, emphasizing that a nation's strength is measured by its ability to overcome difficulties without losing sight of its priorities.

During a press conference on the revised finance law (LFR) for 2026, Prime Minister Lô stressed the need to distinguish between democratic debate and obstruction of government work, noting a "profound nuance" between amendments and hindering governmental action. He also addressed the management of special funds, urging for calm discussions and dialogue to prevent the issue from becoming a public controversy.

The Prime Minister outlined the state's simultaneous imperatives: honoring commitments, ensuring public service continuity, and maintaining essential investments for the country's future. Opposition leader Ousmane Sonko had invited Prime Minister Lô to renew dialogue regarding the LFR 2026.

Why it mattersSenegalese citizens are directly affected by the government's budget constraints and its ability to maintain public services and investments.

Senegalese footballers score in European leagues

Nicolas Jackson, Boulaye Dia, and five other players found the net in weekend matches.

Several Senegalese footballers scored goals in European leagues over the weekend. Nicolas Jackson, Boulaye Dia, Mouhamed Ndiaye, Saliou Faye, Ibrahima Cissé, and Massiré Sylla all scored. Ibrahim Mbaye and Iliman Ndiaye provided assists in their respective matches.

Pape Gueye was sent off during his game, while Pathé Ciss also faced difficulties. The players' performances took place across European and Saudi Arabian football pitches.

These matches occurred during the weekend of October 25-26, 2026, as reported by the Agence de Presse Senegalaise and Seneweb.

Why it mattersSenegalese footballers playing abroad were involved in scoring and disciplinary actions in European and Saudi leagues.

Senegal's Kiiraay party mobilises in Kolda

Younoussa Mballo urges support for President Faye's development plans and criticises opposition inaction.

Younoussa Mballo, a founding member of the Kiiraay party, is leading mobilisations in the Fouladou region of Kolda. He is presenting President Bassirou Diomaye Faye's development projects to local populations. Mballo stressed the importance of informing people about the party's goals for regional and national progress, including initiatives for women's empowerment, youth employment, infrastructure, health, and education.

Mballo criticised the opposition for what he called their 'irresponsible' failure to approve the LFR, stating it hinders the population's well-being. Speaking to militants and supporters in Sinthiang Tountouroung, he argued that strengthening the presidential movement is essential to counter an opposition lacking a credible program for the people.

The Kiiraay party, also known as the patriots-republicans, continues its expansion across Fouladou through significant public gatherings. This outreach aims to consolidate support for the current administration's agenda.

Why it mattersSenegalese citizens in Kolda are being mobilised by Younoussa Mballo to support President Bassirou Diomaye Faye's development plans.

Senegal U20 team eliminated from UFOA-A tournament

The Lionceaux lost 3-2 to Guinea in the semi-finals, failing to qualify for the Africa Cup of Nations.

Senegal's under-20 men's national football team was eliminated from the UFOA-A tournament after a 3-2 semi-final loss to Guinea. Moussa Gueye scored both of Senegal's goals in the 45th minute plus stoppage time and the 70th minute. The team, known as the Lionceaux, had led by two goals before Guinea's comeback.

The defeat represents a significant disappointment for the Senegalese U20 squad. They were unable to secure qualification for the Africa Cup of Nations following the semi-final match in Dakar. The loss came despite an initial two-goal advantage.

The Lionceaux's elimination means they will not participate in the upcoming Africa Cup of Nations. The semi-final match took place on October 25, 2026. Guinea advanced to the next stage of the competition.

Why it mattersSenegal's U20 football team failed to qualify for the Africa Cup of Nations after losing a semi-final match.

Mbappé double gives Real Madrid victory over Barcelona

Kylian Mbappé scored twice to secure a 2-1 win for Real Madrid against FC Barcelona in La Liga on Sunday.

Real Madrid defeated FC Barcelona 2-1 on Sunday at the Camp Nou in the 10th round of La Liga. Kylian Mbappé scored both goals for Real Madrid, with Raphinha scoring for Barcelona. The victory for Real Madrid ends Barcelona's nine-game winning streak in the league.

Kylian Mbappé opened the scoring for Real Madrid in the 41st minute, assisted by Diomandé. Raphinha equalized for FC Barcelona in the 61st minute from a corner by Pedri. Mbappé then scored the winning goal in the 88th minute after a run by Eduardo Camavinga.

This win brings Real Madrid to 24 points, three points behind league leaders FC Barcelona, who have 27 points. Atlético de Madrid is second with 25 points. The match was Mbappé's second brace for Real Madrid.

Why it mattersSenegalese football fans follow La Liga and Kylian Mbappé's performance in this major match.
