Senegal wins first Olympic gold medals

Élisabeth Diatta and Maguette Diop secured two beach wrestling golds at Dakar 2026 Youth Olympics.

Senegal achieved a historic sporting moment on Saturday, November 7, 2026, at the Dakar 2026 Youth Olympic Games. Élisabeth Diatta and Maguette Diop won two gold medals in beach wrestling in Saly, adding to Matar Diop's earlier bronze in judo.

Élisabeth Diatta won gold in the under-55kg category by defeating Japan's Reisa Kojima, and Maguette Diop followed with a victory in the under-80kg category against Azerbaijan's Nihad Suleymanli. President Bassirou Diomaye Faye acknowledged these achievements on social media, congratulating the athletes, their coaches, and families.

The president stated that these three young athletes have made Senegal history in Olympic sports. Matar Diop's bronze in judo on November 1 was Senegal's first medal of the games, which are being held for the first time on the African continent.

Why it mattersSenegalese athletes Élisabeth Diatta, Maguette Diop, and Matar Diop have won Olympic medals, bringing national pride and historical achievements to the country.

Senegal's finance committee rejects budget proposals

The National Assembly's finance commission voted against all budget projects submitted for review on Saturday, November 7, 2026.

The finance and budgetary control commission of Senegal's National Assembly rejected all budget projects on Saturday, November 7, 2026. The commission did not approve revenue and public debt forecasts, nor the budgets for institutions including the Presidency and the Supreme Court. Commissioners raised concerns about the consistency of figures and the sincerity of budget projections.

Alphonse Sambou, the commission's president, presided over the session attended by Economy Minister Cheikh Diba and budget officials. The Presidency's budget was set at 223.86 billion FCFA, an increase of 19.32 billion from the previous year, while the National Assembly's budget was proposed at 21.11 billion FCFA, a decrease from 22.30 billion FCFA. The Constitutional Council was allocated 1.50 billion FCFA, the Supreme Court 4.69 billion FCFA, and the Court of Auditors 13.07 billion FCFA.

Debates focused on the reliability of budget documents, with several commissioners noting inconsistencies and questioning the government's sincerity in its forecasts. The absence of supporting documents, such as the 2025 settlement law, also drew criticism. Despite discussions with government representatives, the reservations were not resolved, leading to the commission's vote against the submitted projects.

Why it mattersThe National Assembly's finance commission rejected budget proposals for key state institutions, affecting government spending plans for 2027.

Senegal assembly rejects budget law

The rejection of the revised finance law by the National Assembly revives fears of a dissolution.

Senegal's National Assembly rejected the revised finance law. This action has revived fears of a potential dissolution of the assembly.

The weekly Brief of Jeune Afrique reported on the rejection of the finance law. The publication noted that the event raises concerns about the political future of the assembly.

The rejection of the budget law by the assembly is a significant political development in Senegal. The specific date of the rejection was not provided in the report.

Why it mattersSenegalese citizens face uncertainty regarding the future of their National Assembly following the budget law's rejection.

101 migrants rescued after boat fire off Gambia

The Gambian immigration department reported 101 irregular migrants, including 72 Senegalese, were saved on November 7, 2026, after their boat caught fire.

A boat carrying 101 irregular migrants caught fire off the coasts of Sika and Albreda in Gambia on November 7, 2026. The Gambian Department of Immigration (GID) reported that Barra border patrol units, the Gambian fire and rescue services, and the Gambian navy conducted the rescue. The group included 72 Senegalese, 27 Gambians, and 2 Guineans, all men, with five minors among them.

Following the rescue, 14 migrants received care at the Essau health center. Two others were treated and released from the Albreda health center. These individuals are now available to investigators.

The rescued migrants are in the custody of Gambian authorities as investigations into the incident continue.
