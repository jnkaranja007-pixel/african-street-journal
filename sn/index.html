Senegal budget rejection blocks $2.2 billion IMF loan

The National Assembly's finance commission rejected budget proposals, halting progress on a critical IMF loan.

Senegal's 2027 budget remains blocked after the National Assembly's finance commission rejected proposals from various ministries and state revenues on Saturday. The ruling Pastef party cited a "lack of budgetary sincerity" for the rejections.

The International Monetary Fund (IMF) on November 12 reiterated that adopting a budget is essential for Senegal to access a $2.2 billion loan program. The country faces a complicated financial situation, with this blockage impacting its ability to secure vital funding.

Abdou Lahad Ndiaye, the Minister of Land and Air Transport, publicly reacted to the rejection of his ministry's 2027 budget project by the finance commission.

Why it mattersThe adoption of the 2027 budget is essential for Senegal to unlock a $2.2 billion loan from the IMF.

Thiès destroys 48 tonnes of unsafe goods seized in six months

Officials burned the stock by incineration, with the seized goods valued at 26 million CFA francs.

Over six months, the regional commerce service in Thiès seized 48.306 tonnes of products unfit for consumption, and officials had them incinerated on Friday, November 13, 2026. The stock was valued at about 26 million CFA francs.

The ceremony was presided over by Ababacar Sadikh Niang, the deputy governor in charge of development, who said food products made up 80% of the stock, insecticides 10%, and pharmaceuticals, cosmetics and hardware the remaining 10%. He called on residents to stay vigilant about using such products and to report suspicious goods to the competent services.

The destroyed goods included 42 tonnes of food such as drinks, biscuits, milk and mustard, along with 5.306 tonnes of counterfeit insecticides. The operation was carried out by the fire brigade in the presence of the gendarmerie company commander and consumer association representatives.

Why it mattersConsumers in Thiès who buy food, insecticides or cosmetics are told to watch for unsafe products and report them to the authorities.

Senegal president attends Francophonie summit in Cambodia

President Bassirou Diomaye Faye arrived in Phnom Penh on November 13, 2026, for the 20th Francophonie Summit.

President Bassirou Diomaye Faye arrived in Phnom Penh, Cambodia, on Friday, November 13, 2026, to participate in the 20th Summit of Heads of State and Government of the Francophonie. The summit is held under the theme: “Reinvesting in Peace for Shared Prosperity and Lasting Stability.” This meeting is particularly historic as Senegal and Cambodia are among the four founding members of the institutional Francophonie, established in Niamey, Niger, in 1970.

The summit brings together nations to define new directions for the Francophone space amidst international geopolitical tensions and conflicts. Discussions are expected to focus on strengthening dialogue, consolidating peace, and promoting shared prosperity. Senegal's participation continues its diplomatic commitment to peace and multilateral dialogue, following its involvement in the Paris Peace Forum.

President Faye will engage in discussions on the future of the Francophonie and collective responses to contemporary challenges. The summit marks a reunion of founding nations 56 years after the organization's inception, aiming to contribute to global stability and cooperation.

Why it mattersPresident Bassirou Diomaye Faye will discuss collective responses to contemporary challenges at the Francophonie Summit.

Sport · 2026-11-14

Dakar hosts breaking finals at Youth Olympic Games

12 breakers aged 14 to 17, from around the world, fought duels on November 12 to qualify for the finals.

Breaking, also called break dance, returned to the Olympic programme at the Youth Olympic Games (JOJ 2026) in Dakar, four years after the Paris 2024 Games. On November 12, 12 breakers aged 14 to 17, from countries around the world, competed in fast head-to-head duels to qualify for the final.

Crowds in Dakar turned out for the contest, drawn to the acrobatic style. The discipline is not on the programme for the Los Angeles 2028 Olympics, which makes its place in Dakar a rare chance for young dancers to compete at this level.

Aminata Touré, the High Representative of President Bassirou Diomaye Faye, congratulated the president on the success of the Games in a social media post and praised Senegalese hospitality, known as Téranga.

Why it mattersYoung dancers aged 14 to 17 who qualify for the finals in Dakar gain a rare Olympic-level stage, since breaking is not on the Los Angeles 2028 programme.
