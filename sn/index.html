Business · 2026-11-17

Expert warns Dakar port wheat dispute could lift bread prices

A blockade of a wheat ship at Dakar's berth 4 has been in place since October 26, 2026, between Grands Moulins de Dakar and the new concessionaire Jambaar SA.

Expert miller Mamadou Niang warned in an interview with L'Observateur that the blockade of a wheat ship's unloading at berth 4 of the Port of Dakar, which began on October 26, 2026, has raised the risk of higher bread prices. He said tensions between Grands Moulins de Dakar (GMD) and the new concessionaire Jambaar SA could disrupt supplies to flour mills and raise production costs.

Niang said unloading delays and a possible rise in port tariffs could affect the price of flour and bread, saying: "An increase in the price of flour and bread is conceivable." To avoid this, he recommended letting GMD continue its operations, citing its more than 50 years of experience, and urged Jambaar SA to work with existing operators.

He called on the state and port authorities to act quickly, saying "the priority must remain the security and regularity of the country's wheat supply." The warning comes as bakers demand a uniform bread price of 200 FCFA.

Security · 2026-11-17

Senegal minister urges regional security cooperation

West African army chiefs meet in Dakar to address escalating security threats.

Senegalese Minister of Armed Forces Yankhoba Diémè called for stronger cooperation and faster implementation of collective security mechanisms among Economic Community of West African States (ECOWAS) member states. He spoke Monday at the opening of the 45th session of the ECOWAS Chiefs of Staff Committee in Dakar.

The ECOWAS army chiefs are meeting in Dakar from November 16 to 18, 2026, to discuss the security challenges facing West Africa. Minister Diémè urged participants to adapt cooperation and act with more coherence and determination against evolving threats, according to the Directorate of Information and Public Relations of the Armed Forces (Dirpa). He emphasized accelerating the operationalization of the regional counter-terrorism force and increasing financial autonomy for the community.

The meeting aims to assess existing military cooperation mechanisms and propose recommendations for collective security and regional stability. Dr. Bakary Sambe, director of the Timbuktu Institute, noted that the region faces a continuous front of threats from the Sahel to coastal countries, with groups like JNIM and the Islamic State in the Sahel exploiting divisions between security architectures.

Why it mattersArmy chiefs from ECOWAS nations are meeting in Dakar to address escalating security threats across the region, including terrorism.

Climate · 2026-11-17

Senegal to submit third climate plan to UN soon, minister says

Environment Minister Aliou Gori Diouf said the validated NDC 3.0 will go in very shortly, ahead of COP32 in Ethiopia in 2027.

Senegal unveiled its climate ambitions on Monday, November 16, 2026, at its national day at COP31 in Antalya, Turkey. Environment and Ecological Transition Minister Dr Aliou Gori Diouf said the country's third-generation Nationally Determined Contribution, known as NDC 3.0, has been validated and will be submitted very soon.

Speaking at the Senegal Pavilion under the theme "Senegal / Vision 2050", Diouf said climate change is already a lived reality, pointing to a retreating coastline, unpredictable rains and uncertain harvests. He said developing countries need accessible and predictable financing, shared technologies and stronger capacities, and that they are ready to prosper with low-carbon models.

Diouf called COP31 the "COP of implementation" and urged a move from pledges to investment. He also cited the validated Green Taxonomy, which is meant to steer financing toward sustainable projects, and said three high-level panels on industrial transition, loss and damage, and climate finance were on the programme.

Why it mattersFarmers and coastal communities face the impacts he described, and the validated Green Taxonomy is meant to direct financing toward sustainable projects in the coming years.

Sport · 2026-11-17

Senegal and Cameroon close in on CAN 2027 finals spots

Both sides won their matches on Monday, the fourth round of qualifiers for the Africa Cup of Nations, and are strong favourites to reach the final phase.

Senegal and Cameroon both dominated their opponents on Monday, November 16, in the fourth round of qualifiers for the Africa Cup of Nations (CAN) 2027. Each side has a strong chance of booking a place in the final phase after four matchdays.

Neither team will know its status until Tuesday, when the other qualifying results are confirmed. Their wins on Monday have put both in a strong position to advance to the tournament proper.

Senegal and Cameroon are among the favourites to reach the finals, and the rest of their group fixtures will decide whether they secure qualification.

Why it mattersSenegal's and Cameroon's fans can expect their teams' qualification to be confirmed when Tuesday's results are published.
