FDR calls protest over budget and youth unemployment

The coalition demands a budget to address youth joblessness and poverty after a deadly pirogue sinking.

The Front for the defense of democracy and the Republic (FDR) is calling for a large demonstration on November 11, 2026, to demand the passage of a budget. The coalition wants the budget to permanently solve the problems of unemployed youth and increasingly impoverished populations. This call follows the capsizing of a pirogue off Lompoul, where 42 bodies were recovered.

The FDR links the public affairs management, leadership choices, and economic conditions to collective destiny, citing the pirogue disaster as evidence that young people risk death to leave the country. The coalition directly blames PASTEF and KIRAAY for the current crisis, stating their unrealistic proposals for youth issues have led to sterile debates in the National Assembly. The FDR accuses the ruling parties of disrespect for Senegalese citizens and criticizes their perceived incompetence and irresponsibility.

The FDR addresses the youth, acknowledging their doubts and weariness, and encourages them to strive for a better future in Senegal through effort and hard work. The coalition pledges to be at the forefront of this effort and offers condolences to the bereaved families.

Why it mattersThe FDR's call for a demonstration and its criticism of the government's economic policies directly affect Senegalese citizens, particularly the unemployed youth and impoverished populations.

Institut Pasteur Dakar advances African health sovereignty

The institute is developing rapid diagnostics and resuming vaccine production to combat epidemics.

The Institut Pasteur de Dakar is preparing to host the World Health Forum from November 17 to 19, 2026, in Senegal, focusing on African health sovereignty. Dr. Ibrahima Socé Fall, the institute's administrator general, stated on November 11 that this sovereignty requires Africa to anticipate epidemics, develop its own diagnostics, and produce vaccines.

The institute is deploying teams to the Democratic Republic of Congo and the Central African Republic to strengthen surveillance and response to nearly 70 ongoing epidemics across the continent. They have also trained representatives from 16 countries and provided diagnostic equipment. Dr. Socé Fall presented a rapid measles diagnostic test that yields results in 20 minutes, compared to three weeks for traditional lab analyses.

Institut Pasteur de Dakar is also resuming production of the yellow fever vaccine and researching candidate vaccines for Rift Valley fever and Marburg virus disease. Sophie Diouf, director of the administrator general's cabinet, emphasized that the forum aims to move beyond discussions to secure concrete decisions, collaborations, and commitments for health security.

Why it mattersSenegalese citizens will benefit from improved disease surveillance, faster diagnostics, and increased local production of essential vaccines.

Senegal: 24 irregular migrants intercepted in Gambia

Gambian immigration services stopped 104 people, including 24 Senegalese nationals, in Mayamba and Batokunku.

Gambian immigration officers intercepted 104 individuals attempting irregular migration during a nighttime operation on November 11, 2026. The operation took place in the localities of Mayamba and Batokunku. Among those apprehended, 24 were identified as Senegalese nationals.

The group also included 61 Gambians, 8 Guineans, 7 Sierra-Leoneans, 3 Malians, and 1 Ivorian. The intercepted individuals, comprising 9 women and 7 minors, were transferred to the Tanji and Barra detention centers for identification and profiling.

In a separate but related operation in Ziguinchor between November 8 and 9, Senegalese gendarmes arrested 17 suspected smugglers and seized three pirogues, five outboard motors, fuel, GPS devices, and provisions. This operation aimed to prevent departures towards Spain, with routes also planned via Gambia and Thiaroye.

Why it mattersSenegalese citizens are directly affected by the interception of 24 nationals attempting irregular migration in Gambia and the arrest of 17 smugglers in Ziguinchor.

Fake officer demands ID from real police

A 27-year-old man faces charges for impersonating an officer and threatening to arrest police.

A 27-year-old man, identified as A. Ndiaye, has been sent to the Dakar prosecutor for impersonating an officer and carrying a baton. He allegedly demanded identification from plainclothes police officers and threatened to detain them. The incident occurred around two in the morning on November 9 near a bar.

Agents from the vice squad were on a foot patrol when Ndiaye approached them, claiming to be a police officer from the central Dakar station. He reportedly demanded their identification and threatened to take them to the station if they did not comply. When asked to prove his identity, he showed a card labeled "Leadership sécurité," according to reports.

During questioning, Ndiaye presented himself as a close protection agent. He admitted to the actions and offered his apologies. The case has now been forwarded to the Dakar prosecutor's office.

Why it mattersA man impersonating a police officer in Dakar has been arrested and faces charges, affecting public trust in law enforcement.

Sport · 2026-11-12

Senegal beach handball teams reach JOJ finals

Senegal's athletes are competing for 15 titles on the penultimate day of the Dakar 2026 Youth Olympic Games.

Senegal's national beach handball teams will compete for a place in the final on Thursday, November 12, at the Dakar 2026 Youth Olympic Games. The men's and women's selections aim to secure medals for the country on the penultimate day of the competition. In taekwondo, the mixed team event will see Senegal face Jordan in the quarter-finals at 11 AM.

Marie Sarr previously won Olympic gold in taekwondo's under-55kg category, and Aïda Touré secured a bronze medal in the under-63kg category. Ousmane Thioune will participate in the final windsurfing races, with the 15th race scheduled for 12:47 PM in Saly. Tening Diallo is also competing in the women's individual sabre fencing, starting pool phases at 10:30 AM.

Breaking, a new discipline, debuts today, while boxing will award 10 titles. The Youth Olympic Games, in their first African edition, conclude with a closing ceremony on Friday. 15 titles are available in total on this penultimate day of competition.
