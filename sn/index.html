Senegal expresses concern over U.S. sanctions on ICC official

The Senegalese government expressed deep concern Thursday regarding U.S. sanctions announced Aug. 18 against International Criminal Court officials. The sanctions target Abdoulaye Seye, a member of the ICC Prosecutor's Office and a Senegalese candidate for judge.
