Senegal Lifts Onion Import Ban After Seven Months

The government has officially ended the freeze on onion imports, allowing trade to resume and aiming to ensure market supply and price stability.

Senegal has lifted the seven-month ban on onion imports, effective August 31, 2026. The government's Ministry of Industry and Commerce announced the decision, which allows importers to resume commercial activities. This measure, initially imposed on January 16, 2026, was intended to support local production and protect small farmers. The ministry stated that the evolving local supply now justifies reopening the market to ensure consistent availability and stable prices for consumers.

The annual suspension of onion imports, a practice since 2006, typically runs from February to July or August. Its purpose has been to facilitate the sale of local harvests, stabilize prices, and move towards self-sufficiency. While the reopening aims to secure supply until the next harvest, some local producers, represented by the UNACOIS youth president Aly Ndiaye, had requested a five-day extension to sell their stored stock.

The Ministry of Industry and Commerce is urging importers to coordinate with the Market Regulation Agency (ARM) for necessary arrangements. The government seeks to balance supporting national production when sufficient, protecting local producers' markets, ensuring onion availability, and preventing price hikes for consumers. The decision aims to manage these competing interests as the onion market enters a new phase under regulatory supervision.

Why it mattersThe lifting of the onion import ban affects consumers by aiming to ensure a steady supply and stable prices, while also impacting local producers who may face increased competition.

Senegal doctors announce 48-hour strike over working conditions

The autonomous union of doctors, pharmacists, and dentists is demanding better pay, career progression, and improved healthcare infrastructure.

The Syndicat autonome des médecins, pharmaciens et chirurgiens-dentistes du Sénégal (SAMES) will stage a 48-hour strike on September 8 and 9, 2026, to protest unsatisfactory responses from authorities regarding their demands. Dr. Diabèle Dramé, the union's national secretary-general, stated that despite months of dialogue, commitments on the special status of medical professionals, recruitment, career advancement, and retirement pensions remain unfulfilled.

The union's grievances extend to the payment of internship bonuses and a recent circular from the Ministry of Health concerning professional premiums. SAMES also highlighted deficiencies in infrastructure, equipment, and human resources, calling for a public health facility in every department, scanners and MRIs in regions, and functional laboratories. They argue that improving working conditions for healthcare professionals directly translates to better quality and equitable care for the population.

Following strategic meetings and general assemblies in early September, the SAMES announced the planned strike, emphasizing that emergency services and minimum services will be maintained. Union members will wear red armbands during the strike, with a mobilization assessment scheduled for September 10. While maintaining open dialogue, SAMES stated they are now awaiting concrete actions from the government.

Why it mattersDoctors, pharmacists, and dentists are affected by the strike due to unmet demands regarding their working conditions, pay, and career progression.

Khadre Community Mourns 96-Year-Old Spiritual Leader

Cheikh Saad Bouh Ould Cheikh Mouhamed Takhiyoullah, the ninth khalife of the Khadre community, died yesterday in Dakar and will be buried in Nimzatt, Mauritania.

The Khadre community is in mourning following the death of Cheikh Saad Bouh Ould Cheikh Mouhamed Takhiyoullah, the ninth spiritual leader of the Cheikhna Cheikh Saad Bouh family. Known as 'Yaguene' to his followers, the 96-year-old died yesterday in Dakar. He had served as the general khalife since 2022, succeeding Cheikh Aya Aïdara, and was known for promoting unity within the community.

Cheikh Takhiyoullah, born August 30, 1930, was described as a merciful father and a good educator. He studied with Serigne Mountakha Mbacké, the current general khalife of the Mourides, in Mauritania. During his time as khalife, he worked to unite the dispersed branches of the Cheikhna Cheikh Saad Bouh family, urging them to remain together and dedicated to the Lord and the Prophet Muhammad.

Senegalese President Bassirou Diomaye Faye expressed his deep sadness and offered condolences to the family and the Khadre community in Senegal and Mauritania. Cheikh Atkhana Aïdara, the family's spokesperson, confirmed the death and announced the burial in Nimzatt, Mauritania. The leadership of the Khadre community now passes to the eldest member of the Cheikhna Cheikh Saad Bouh family, Cheikh Sidy Bouya Ould Cheikh Hadrame Cheikh Bounana ould Cheikh Saad Bouh.

Why it mattersThe death of a major spiritual leader affects the religious community and its followers, impacting their spiritual guidance and community cohesion.

Messi Retires from International Football After 20 Years

The 39-year-old Argentine forward announced his retirement from international football on Monday, citing personal reasons and the emergence of new talent.

Lionel Messi has announced his retirement from international football with Argentina, concluding a 20-year career with the national team. The 39-year-old forward made the announcement on Instagram, stating that the recent death of his father, Jorge Messi, on August 8th in Rosario, solidified his decision. Messi explained that he had drafted the message on July 21st, two days after Argentina's World Cup 2026 final loss in extra time to Spain, but delayed its publication.

Messi described his decision as painful but necessary, emphasizing that he has always given his all for the Argentine jersey, even during his early years. He also noted the arrival of talented young players who deserve opportunities in the national squad. The captain expressed gratitude to his family, teammates, coaches, and fans, promising to continue supporting the team from the outside. Messi's international career began in 2005.

During his tenure, Lionel Messi earned a record 207 caps and scored 125 goals for Argentina, making him the national team's all-time leading scorer. Under his captaincy, Argentina achieved significant success, including winning the 2022 World Cup and two Copa América titles. His departure marks the end of a remarkable era for Argentine football.

Why it mattersFootball fans worldwide, particularly those who follow Argentine football, are affected by the retirement of their star player, Lionel Messi.

Minister Cissé Directs File Autonomy Unit for Efficiency

Interior Minister Mouhamadou Makhtar Cissé visited the Direction de l’Autonomisation du Fichier, issuing directives to enhance operational effectiveness and service quality.

Minister of Interior and Public Security Mouhamadou Makhtar Cissé visited the Direction de l’Autonomisation du Fichier (DAF) on Monday, August 31, 2026. The visit aimed to understand the working conditions of the staff and address their concerns. During the visit, the minister provided several directives focused on improving the operational efficiency and service quality of the DAF.

Following the meeting at the DAF, Minister Cissé toured various departments within the central ministry building. He engaged directly with personnel, encouraging them to continue their dedicated service to the state. The ministry itself reported on the minister's visit and the orientations he provided.

The Direction de l’Autonomisation du Fichier is responsible for administrative functions within the ministry. The minister's visit and subsequent directives signal a focus on optimizing the performance and output of this key governmental department.

Why it mattersThe Ministry of Interior and Public Security's directive to enhance operational efficiency and service quality at the Direction de l’Autonomisation du Fichier affects government administrative processes.
