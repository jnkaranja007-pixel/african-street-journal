Senegal assembly rejects revised budget law

Lawmakers voted 109 to 20 to block the finance bill without a full debate on public spending.

Senegal's National Assembly rejected the government's revised finance bill on Tuesday, voting 109 to 20 with three abstentions. The vote used a procedural motion to end debate before discussing the bill's substance. This action prevents a public examination of the government's proposed adjustments to state revenue and spending for 2026.

The Ministry of Economy, Finance and Plan stated it provided details on energy subsidies and public investments, but lawmakers cited insufficient documentation on investment execution and debt. The bill had previously been rejected twice by the finance committee before being sent back to the assembly. The assembly's first vice-president, Malick Ndiaye, led the session in the absence of President Ousmane Sonko.

The rejected bill risked blocking a $2.2 billion loan from the International Monetary Fund. The finance committee's report faced controversy during the session, with the finance minister contesting its contents and some deputies alleging possible alterations. The assembly's internal rules allow for the first vice-president to preside over sessions when the president is absent.

Why it mattersSenegalese citizens will not have a public debate on the government's revised budget, affecting public spending and investments.

Senegal's PM meets China's envoy over Dakar 2026 Youth Olympics

Prime Minister LO discussed the ongoing Youth Olympic Games with Gao Zhidan, China's special envoy and Olympic Committee president.

Senegalese Prime Minister Ahmadou Alhaminou Mouhamed LO met China's special envoy Gao Zhidan on Tuesday, November 3, 2026, in Dakar. The discussions focused on the Dakar 2026 Youth Olympic Games, which are currently underway in the Senegalese capital. Gao Zhidan, who is also the President of the Chinese Olympic Committee, reaffirmed China's interest in the international sporting event.

China is participating in the Youth Olympic Games with a large delegation of athletes, demonstrating its support for the first edition of the games held on the African continent. The games are taking place in Dakar, Diamniadio, and Saly until November 13, 2026, bringing together young athletes from around the world.

The opening ceremony, led by Khady Niang, was described as "profoundly human" and a gesture of African pride, focusing on history and youth energy rather than technology. Niang, who has a background in finance and film makeup, led the ceremonies for the JOJ Dakar 2026.

Why it mattersSenegal is hosting the Dakar 2026 Youth Olympic Games, an event involving international athletes and significant diplomatic engagement.

Senegal MPs block finance law

Pastef lawmakers refused to vote for the revised finance law, impacting social projects and salaries.

Members of the Pastef party in Senegal's parliament blocked the revised finance law on March 11, 2026. This refusal means projects for hospitals, social policies, and internal debt payments will be halted.

The lawmakers' decision affects salary payments and other state functions, while they continue to receive their own salaries and travel allowances. The action is described as a betrayal of the people who trusted Pastef to support President Bassirou Diomaye Faye's economic and social agenda.

The report criticizes Pastef's leader, Ousmane Sonko, for allegedly betraying President Faye after being appointed Prime Minister and given significant state positions. The author claims Sonko attempted to undermine the President to hasten his departure from office.

Why it mattersSenegalese citizens will face disruptions in social services and salary payments due to the parliament's rejection of the finance law.

Senegal police arrest two over theft of Belarusian delegation's camera

A driver from the Ministry of Health and a market vendor were detained after a Nikon camera was stolen from the Dakar 2026 Youth Olympic Games hotel.

Two members of the Belarusian delegation at the Dakar 2026 Youth Olympic Games had a Nikon camera stolen from the parking lot of the Pullman Hotel on November 2. Security footage showed a suspect driving a Toyota Land Cruiser taking the camera during a moment of inattention from guards.

An investigation led to the Ministry of Health and Public Hygiene, with a request sent to the DMTA. Driver S. Thiongane was arrested and identified O. Thiané, a vendor at the Nguélaw market, as the receiver of the stolen goods. The camera was recovered from Thiané's shop.

Both suspects were taken into custody for theft with breaking and entering and receiving stolen property, according to Libération. The incident occurred while the delegation was in Dakar for the JOJ 2026 events.

Why it mattersTwo individuals, a ministry driver and a market vendor, were arrested and charged with theft and receiving stolen property following the incident.

Senegal hosts African and diasporic arts events

Dakar is a venue for November exhibitions and festivals exploring colonial violence and Black identities.

African and diasporic creation is taking over museums, festivals, and galleries in Dakar this November. The events aim to examine colonial violence, Black identities, Senegalese heritage, and cosmological imaginaries. These gatherings are essential appointments for Afro and African cultures.

The cultural events span from Berlin to Tunis and Antananarivo, with Dakar being a key location. Organizers invite the public to send in their upcoming cultural events for future features.

These cultural rendezvous are part of a broader exploration of African and diasporic artistic expression throughout November.

Why it mattersSenegalese heritage and identity are being explored through art exhibitions and festivals in Dakar.
