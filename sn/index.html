Senegal launches national cloud infrastructure with Alibaba

The technology, developed for Dakar 2026 Youth Olympic Games, will become a national platform after the event.

Senegal has launched a major cloud infrastructure, a national digital legacy from the Dakar 2026 Youth Olympic Games (YOG). The system was developed through a partnership involving the International Olympic Committee (IOC), the Dakar 2026 Organizing Committee (COJOJ), the Senegalese state, and China's Alibaba group.

Ibrahima Wade, COJOJ's general coordinator, stated on Sunday that this cloud is one of Africa's largest, supporting ticketing, results tracking, and data security for the Games. Senegal Numérique (SENUM SA), the state's digital services company, will be a primary beneficiary of this technology.

This partnership also aims to transfer skills and enhance national expertise in digital technologies. The IOC and Alibaba highlighted the infrastructure's capabilities to support Senegal's digital transformation beyond the sporting event.

Why it mattersSenegal gains a significant digital infrastructure and enhanced technological capabilities through this partnership.

Senegal seeks suspect's return from Mauritania

Alioune Badara Gaye, sought in Astou Pierre Ndiaye's acid attack death, arrested in Mauritania.

Senegalese authorities are arranging the return of Alioune Badara Gaye from Mauritania. Gaye is wanted in connection with the death of Astou Pierre Ndiaye, who died on October 1 after being doused with acid at her workplace. Interpol's National Central Bureau in Dakar is coordinating the operation to bring the suspect back to Senegal.

Astou Pierre Ndiaye was attacked in June at the restaurant "Le Fougon" and succumbed to her injuries months later at Fann hospital in Dakar. Her younger sister, Awa Pierre, expressed relief at Gaye's arrest in Mauritania on Friday, facilitated by police cooperation between the two nations. She hopes the judicial process will now proceed to establish responsibility.

The suspect, identified as Ndiaye's former partner, had been on the run since the fatal assault. His flight had complicated the investigation into the circumstances of the attack. The arrest, confirmed by Senegalese judicial police, allows investigators to proceed with uncovering the truth and assigning accountability.

Why it mattersThe family of Astou Pierre Ndiaye, who died after an acid attack, can now pursue justice following the suspect's arrest and planned repatriation.

Senegal hosts Islamic Peace Forum in Thiès

The 24th edition of the Islamic Peace Forum opened on November 1, 2026, in Thiès, Senegal.

The 24th Islamic Peace Forum began on Sunday, November 1, 2026, in Thiès, Senegal. The event, themed "Together for a Senegal of Peace and Development," gathered about 20 Islamic associations and brotherhoods from different currents.

Cheikh Ahmed Saloum Dieng led the forum, which annually convenes in Thiès for two days to discuss current issues. This year's sub-themes included the contribution of Senegalese social heritage to stability and the role of Sufi leaders in development.

Dieng emphasized the importance of peace for development and offered prayers for the head of state and for Senegal's prosperity. The forum's discussions also touched upon the "Senegalese exception" in relation to continuity and threats.

Why it mattersThe Islamic Peace Forum in Thiès brought together Senegalese Islamic associations and brotherhoods to discuss peace and development.

Sport · 2026-11-02

Senegal lioncels thrash Fiji 8-1 in youth games futsal opener

The Senegalese women's futsal team leads group A after their dominant victory, with Marème Niang scoring four goals.

The Senegalese women's futsal team, the Lioncelles, began their campaign at the Dakar 2026 Youth Olympic Games with a decisive 8-1 victory over Fiji. The team displayed strong offensive efficiency and collective control in their opening match.

This commanding win places Senegal at the top of Group A in the women's futsal tournament. Marème Niang was a standout player, scoring four goals in the match. The Lioncelles will next face Venezuela on November 3.

Specialist Souleymane Amar noted that the Youth Olympic Games offer Senegal a historic chance to showcase its territories and skills, aiming to convert global exposure into lasting economic attractiveness and employment opportunities.

Why it mattersSenegalese athletes secured a significant win in a major international sporting event hosted in their country.

Two men die in car and motorcycle crash

A 33-year-old mechanic is in police custody after a collision near Auchan Castors on Sunday, November 1, 2026.

Two men died in a collision between a car and a motorcycle on Sunday, November 1, 2026, near Auchan Castors in Senegal. One man died instantly, while the second died later from his injuries at the hospital. The driver of the car was taken into custody.

Police were alerted to the accident around 4:40 AM and found a car and a motorcycle involved. Two men, estimated to be between 20 and 30 years old, were on the motorcycle. The driver of the car, identified as 33-year-old mechanic O. Ndiaye, is being held for involuntary manslaughter.

Autopsies have been ordered for the two unidentified victims. Investigators at the Dieuppeul precinct are working to establish the exact circumstances of the crash and identify the deceased. The prosecutor's office has been informed of the incident.

Why it mattersTwo men have died and a third is in police custody following a traffic accident in Senegal.
