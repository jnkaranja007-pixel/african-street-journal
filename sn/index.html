Ablaye Diouf wins bronze in beach wrestling

Senegal's Ablaye Diouf secured a bronze medal in the under-90kg beach wrestling at the Dakar 2026 Youth Olympic Games.

Senegalese wrestler Ablaye Diouf won the bronze medal in the under-90kg beach wrestling event at the Dakar 2026 Youth Olympic Games on Sunday. Diouf won his first two matches against Ly Quy Bao Ha of Vietnam and Ali Zaman of Pakistan. This medal adds to Senegal's tally in beach wrestling at the home games.

Diouf's victories placed him favorably in Group C, contributing to the host nation's success in the discipline. His bronze medal follows earlier wins by Elisabeth Diatta and Maguette Diop in beach wrestling. Senegal has now secured a podium finish in this event at the Youth Olympic Games.

The day also saw strong performances from Senegalese athletes in basketball 3x3. Senegal's delegation continues to collect medals at the Dakar 2026 Youth Olympic Games.

Why it mattersSenegalese athletes are competing and winning medals at the Youth Olympic Games hosted in Dakar.

Senegal pirogue capsizes off Lompoul, over 30 dead

A pirogue carrying migrants to the Canary Islands capsized on November 7, with 11 bodies recovered on November 8.

A pirogue that departed Saint-Louis three days prior for the Canary Islands capsized on Saturday, November 7, off Lompoul. 11 bodies were recovered on Sunday morning, November 8, raising the death toll to over 30. The vessel capsized in rough seas near Léona commune in Kébémer department. Most victims were from Léona and Thiéppe, with only one survivor reported.

Member of Parliament Abdou Mbow stated that the incident is a consequence of the executive and legislative branches arguing over the Revised Finance Law. He claimed the state and the National Assembly failed the youth, who embark on such perilous journeys due to unfulfilled promises of jobs and dignity. Mbow criticized the authorities' response as a mere statement of regret, noting the Interior Minister's planned visit to Lompoul on Monday.

Mbow linked the Lompoul tragedy to a separate incident on Saturday, November 7, where 142 migrants, including women and children, were intercepted near Dakar heading for Spain. He argued that the youth choose to risk death at sea over a slow death of despair, accusing the government of indifference and prioritizing political calculations for 2029. Mbow warned of more such tragedies if the current political climate persists.

Why it mattersOver 30 Senegalese migrants died when their pirogue capsized off Lompoul while attempting to reach the Canary Islands.

Four in five Senegal diabetes patients unaware

Nearly four in five diabetic people in Senegal do not know they have the disease, according to Assad.

Nearly four out of five diabetic people in Senegal are unaware they have the disease, according to the Senegalese Association for Diabetes Support (Assad). This lack of awareness delays care and complicates the prevention of complications. Specialist Pr Abdoulaye Lèye raised this alert on November 4, 2026, ahead of World Diabetes Day.

Globally, Pr Abdoulaye Lèye estimates 600 million people live with diabetes, with 30 million in Africa. In Senegal, Ministry of Health surveys cited by the specialist indicate one in 24 adults has diabetes, leading to an estimated 1,150 adult deaths annually. The Ministry of Health's Director of Disease Occurrence, Dr. Mamadou Moustapha Diop, stated that 27 integrated diabetes care centers are operational, and 520 million FCfa has been allocated for insulin purchases.

Pr Abdoulaye Lèye advocates for early preventive interventions, including addressing risk factors before birth and managing weight and obesity, noting that 28.8% of the Senegalese population is overweight. He also stressed that preventing complications is far less expensive than treating them. Another support measure is expected in 2027.

Why it mattersThe large number of undiagnosed diabetes cases in Senegal means many people are not receiving care, increasing their risk of serious health complications.

Senegal migrant boat wreck kills at least 35

The death toll from a capsized pirogue off the coast of Kébémer has risen significantly, with searches ongoing.

At least 35 people have died after a migrant boat capsized off the coast of Kébémer in northwestern Senegal. Rescuers are continuing to search for survivors following the incident.

The provisional death toll rose from 21 on Saturday to at least 34 on Sunday, according to Dakaractu. RFI Afrique reported the toll had climbed to at least 35. Searches are ongoing in the Kébémer department for any additional victims.

This tragedy occurs on the Atlantic route used by thousands of young people attempting to reach Europe.

Why it mattersThe dangerous journey to Europe claimed at least 35 lives when a migrant boat capsized off Senegal's coast.

Senegalese artist Boubou uses children's handprints for art

Artist Boubou incorporated children's handprints into a painting at Dakar's Amadou Diagne Wore elementary school.

Senegalese artist Boubou transformed a classroom at Dakar's Amadou Diagne Wore elementary school into an art workshop on Saturday, November 7. He led a live painting session for students, teachers, and youth as part of the Dakar 2026 Youth Olympic Games. The initiative was supported by ONE Academy, a free platform from ONE Campaign focused on developing African youth leadership and advocacy skills.

Boubou, known for painting portraits upside down and using everyday objects, demonstrated his technique, with close-ups shown on a large screen. He invited students to add their handprints to a canvas bearing the message "THESE HANDS WILL CHANGE THE WORLD," integrating their marks into his artwork. Boubou explained that art gave him freedom to see things differently and that he wanted each child to know their ideas matter and can make a difference.

The activity aimed to raise awareness among young people about their ability to effect change in their communities. Serah Makka, ONE Campaign's Executive Director for Africa, stated that African youth have the energy for change but need resources and knowledge. ONE Academy offers free certified training to Africans aged 18-35 to understand issues, gain advocacy and leadership skills, and apply them in their communities.

Why it mattersChildren in Dakar participated in an art workshop that aimed to empower them with the belief that their ideas and actions can create change.
