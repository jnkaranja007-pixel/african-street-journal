Colonel Abdoulaye Mbengue takes command of military zone 3

The new commander will focus on anticipation, discipline, and collaboration with local populations to address security threats.

Source: Dakaractu · 2026-10-28 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Colonel Abdoulaye Mbengue was officially installed on Tuesday, October 27, 2026, as the new commander of Military Zone N°3, which covers the Kaolack, Kaffrine, and Fatick regions. The ceremony was presided over by the Chief of General Staff of the Armed Forces, Vice Admiral Oumar Wade, and included a military parade.

Colonel Mbengue stated his priorities include improving existing prevention activities and enhancing troop readiness, emphasizing discipline. He also highlighted the need for anticipation and frank collaboration with administrative authorities and populations to counter new threats, particularly those emerging from the Sahel and border areas.

The new commander stressed the importance of information gathering and reaction time to address threats before they reach the population. He called for public cooperation, noting that the population's security and property protection are paramount.
