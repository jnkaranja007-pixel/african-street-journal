CAS restores Senegal as 2025 Africa Cup of Nations champions

The court overturned the African football body’s 3-0 forfeit ruling after Senegal’s players walked off during the final against Morocco.

Sources: BBC Africa and Anadolu Africa · 2026-11-26 · 1 min read

Senegal are again the 2025 Africa Cup of Nations champions after the Court of Arbitration for Sport (CAS) overturned the African football body’s decision to strip them of the title. The ruling restores Senegal’s 1-0 win over Morocco in January’s final.

A three-member CAS panel unanimously overturned the Confederation of African Football appeals board’s 3-0 forfeit decision, which followed Senegal players leaving the pitch in protest at a penalty while the match was goalless. The panel ruled that referee Jean-Jacques Ndala had final authority to restart the match and play it to a conclusion. Senegal’s federation president, Abdoulaye Fall, celebrated the decision on social media with a photograph of the team lifting the trophy.

Morocco’s football federation said it would pursue legal avenues to challenge the verdict. It could take its case to the Swiss Federal Court.

Why it mattersSenegal’s football federation regains the title, while Morocco’s federation must decide whether to pursue a challenge at the Swiss Federal Court.
