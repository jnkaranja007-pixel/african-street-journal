Senegal wins gold in men's 3x3 basketball

The host nation's young men secured the gold medal at the Dakar 2026 Youth Olympic Games.

Source: Le Soleil · 2026-11-10 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Senegal's men's 3x3 basketball team won the gold medal at the Dakar 2026 Youth Olympic Games. The Lions secured the victory on Monday, adding to the excitement of the host nation's performance.

The young Senegalese players triumphed in a closely contested tournament final. Their victory brings the gold medal home for the host country.

In the women's 3x3 basketball final, Poland defeated China 12-11 to claim the gold medal. The women's tournament concluded with this intense match.

Why it mattersSenegalese youth athletes have won a gold medal in basketball at the Youth Olympic Games held in their country.
