Senegal parliament debates budget correction

Lawmakers are set to vote on a revised 2026 budget, with opposition lawmakers expressing concerns.

Source: RFI Afrique · 2026-11-03 · 1 min read

Senegalese lawmakers will reconvene to vote on a revised 2026 budget, known as the loi de finances rectificative. The finance committee has reviewed the budget, but the opposition Pastef party, holding 130 of the 165 parliamentary seats, remains unconvinced.

The Pastef party's opposition to the revised budget could potentially block the approval of a $2.2 billion loan from the International Monetary Fund. The party's stance has not changed despite a new review by the finance committee.

The revised budget aims to adjust the country's financial plan mid-year. The outcome of the parliamentary vote will determine the next steps for the proposed IMF loan and the government's financial strategy for the remainder of 2026.
