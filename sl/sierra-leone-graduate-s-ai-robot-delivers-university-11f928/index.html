Sierra Leone graduate’s AI robot delivers university graduation address

Gibrilla Conteh, a visual arts graduate, built the robot, which gave the vote of thanks at Milton Margai Technical University’s ceremony.

Source: Sierraloaded · 2026-11-23 · 1 min read

A robot powered by artificial intelligence, developed by Milton Margai Technical University graduate Gibrilla Conteh, delivered the vote of thanks at the university’s graduation ceremony in Sierra Leone. Conteh graduated with a degree in Visual Arts from the Faculty of Education.

Chief Minister Moinina David Sengeh identified Conteh in a Facebook post and said the two first met after Conteh presented him with artwork. Sengeh said he encouraged Conteh to apply to university; the principal, Dr Philip Kanu, supported his admission and provided scholarships. Conteh received further mentorship from the Visual Arts Department head, Dr Coker. The robot wore academic regalia, sat in a wheelchair and featured a robotic prosthetic device.

Sengeh described Conteh, also known as Abu G. White, as an artist, coder, robotics engineer and community leader. He said the achievement might be the first of its kind in Sierra Leone; a claim that it is Africa’s first has not been independently established.

Why it mattersConteh’s education was supported by scholarships from the university principal after he had faced financial difficulties continuing his studies.
