UNDP trains Sierra Leone innovators on plastic solutions

20 entrepreneurs will develop scalable businesses to tackle plastic pollution and create jobs.

Source: Sierra Leone Telegraph · 2026-11-04 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

The United Nations Development Programme (UNDP) has launched a three-day training for 20 Sierra Leonean innovators in Freetown. The programme aims to help them scale solutions for plastic pollution into viable businesses. This initiative is part of the Global Plastics Innovation Programme (GPIP), funded by the UN Peace and Development Trust Fund.

The training combines expert sessions, practical exercises, and networking to address challenges like accessing finance and markets. Participants will receive ongoing technical support to strengthen their business models. Innovators are developing solutions for waste collection, recycling, reuse systems, and circular business models across Sierra Leone.

Kevin Petrini, UNDP Deputy Resident Representative, stated that the fight against plastic pollution is a story of innovation and entrepreneurship. Binta Barrie, UniPod Lead, noted the programme equips innovators with knowledge and tools to increase their impact and turn local solutions into sustainable enterprises.

Why it matters20 Sierra Leonean innovators will gain skills to build businesses addressing plastic pollution, creating jobs and improving livelihoods.
