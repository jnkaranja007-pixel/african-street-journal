Engage Salone finds Sierra Leone councils score 29.8 out of 100

A survey of 1,489 people across six council areas found 60% said their council never consults communities.

Source: Sierraloaded · 2026-11-27 · 1 min read

Engage Salone, a group that researched decentralisation, reported that Sierra Leone’s local councils scored an average of 29.8 out of 100. Its study surveyed 1,489 people across six council areas and found that decentralisation remains incomplete more than two decades after elected councils returned in 2004.

60% of respondents said their councils never consult communities, and only 17% had attended a council meeting. The report identified inadequate funding, weak coordination with central government, limited institutional capacity and political interference as obstacles to local governance.

Despite those concerns, 55% of respondents supported giving councils more powers and increased funding. The report also raised concerns about women’s participation in community governance and council responses during emergencies, including flooding.

Why it mattersResidents in the six surveyed council areas report little consultation, while 55% support giving councils more powers and funding.
