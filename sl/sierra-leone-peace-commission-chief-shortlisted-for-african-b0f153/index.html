Sierra Leone peace commission chief shortlisted for African leadership award

Hawa Sally Samai is a finalist in Category 9, one of four Sierra Leone nominations in the 2026 awards.

Source: The Calabash · 2026-11-26 · 1 min read

Hawa Sally Samai, executive secretary of Sierra Leone’s Independent Commission for Peace and National Cohesion, has been shortlisted for African Public Sector Leader of the Year in the 16th African Leadership Magazine Persons of the Year Awards 2026. She is a finalist in Category 9.

The magazine’s Editorial Board approved a shortlist covering 18 categories after an open call for nominations from Africa and the diaspora. Sierra Leone has four nominees. The commission supported dialogue after political tensions around the 2023 general elections and processes linked to the Agreement for National Unity, signed by the government and the All People’s Congress in October 2023.

In October 2026, the commission convened a conference in Freetown that adopted a declaration calling for an Elders’ Forum, stronger inter-party dialogue and conflict-prevention mechanisms.

Why it mattersSamai and the commission are among Sierra Leone’s four nominees, with the award shortlist spanning 18 categories.
