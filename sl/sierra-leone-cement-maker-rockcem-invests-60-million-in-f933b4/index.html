Sierra Leone cement maker ROCKCEM invests $60 million in processing and distribution

The company is building a warehouse in Songo and a grinding mill designed to produce more than 60,000 bags a day.

Source: The Calabash · 2026-11-24 · 1 min read

ROCKCEM (SL) Ltd, a Sierra Leonean cement company, is investing an estimated US$60 million in cement processing, production facilities, warehousing and distribution. The company says the expansion will improve supply of its RC BRAND (a cement brand) CEM II 42.5 R (a grade of Portland-limestone cement) to contractors and builders.

A warehouse at Sumbuya in Songo was scheduled to become operational in November 2026. ROCKCEM says provincial wholesalers and retailers will be able to buy cement there instead of travelling to Freetown. The company has assembled about 50 vehicles for deliveries to contractors, dealers and individual customers.

ROCKCEM is also developing a grinding mill designed to produce more than 60,000 bags of 50 kilograms each daily. Actual output will depend on operational readiness, production schedules and market conditions, the company said.

Why it mattersProvincial wholesalers and retailers were due to gain a cement buying point in Songo in November 2026, rather than travelling to Freetown.
