Sierra Leone finance minister faces civil-society call for dismissal

The CSOs Consortium on Public Accountability says Sheku Bangura should be removed, while a guest writer defends his record on inflation and debt.

Source: Politico SL · 2026-11-21 · 1 min read

A group calling itself the CSOs Consortium on Public Accountability has demanded the immediate dismissal of Sierra Leone's Finance Minister, Sheku Ahmed Fantamadi Bangura. Its call, which Politico SL carried on Saturday, is answered in a guest article by Foday M. Daboh, who argues the consortium confuses economic hardship with mismanagement.

Daboh says inflation peaked after global food and fuel shocks and then declined as fiscal and monetary tightening took hold and central-bank financing of deficits was curtailed. He cites International Monetary Fund reports that link improved fiscal discipline and reduced central-bank financing to moderating inflationary pressures.

On debt, Daboh says Sierra Leone remains at high risk of debt distress but borrows mostly on concessional terms and has restrained commercial loans. The article reports no government response to the consortium's demand.
