Sierra Leone regulator reports NLE 7.4 million in 2024 revenue

The electricity and water watchdog collected the money from levies and licenses, up from NLE 2.3 million in 2023.

Source: Politico SL · 2026-11-25 · 1 min read

Sierra Leone's Electricity and Water Regulatory Commission (SLEWRC), the state body that licenses and oversees electricity and water providers, recorded NLE 7.4 million in internal revenue from levies and licenses in 2024, compared with NLE 2.3 million in 2023. The New Leone (NLE) is the country's currency.

Deputy Director Sama Sandi told the Ministry of Finance's budget discussions that the commission received no ministry funding for its recurrent spending in the 2023 and 2024 financial years, even though it had budgeted for it. In 2023, it took enforcement action against 64 non-compliant water companies, and its reports showed 80% of complaints were resolved within a week.

Sandi said the commission had developed a solar mini-grid tariff model that was close to completion. He submitted a budget of NLE 5,590.29 for the 2025 to 2027 financial years, and the commission's stated plans include monitoring tariff rates and expanding access.

Why it mattersThe commission's budget request for 2025 to 2027 now sets out the funding it says it needs for service and tariff oversight.
