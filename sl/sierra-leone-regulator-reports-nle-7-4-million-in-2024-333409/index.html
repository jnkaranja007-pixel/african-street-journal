Sierra Leone regulator reports NLE 7.4 million in 2024 revenue

The electricity and water commission said revenue from levies and licenses rose from NLE 2.3 million in 2023.

Source: Politico SL · 2026-11-28 · 1 min read

Sierra Leone's Electricity and Water Regulatory Commission (SLEWRC), which regulates electricity and water providers, recorded NLE 7.4 million in internal revenue from levies and licenses in 2024, up from NLE 2.3 million in 2023. NLE stands for New Leone, the country's currency.

Deputy Director Sama Sandi told Ministry of Finance budget discussions on 21 August 2024 that the commission received nothing from the ministry in 2023, despite NLE 954 thousand being designated against NLE 2.037 million budgeted for recurrent spending. In 2024 it again received nothing, after NLE 911 thousand was allocated. Sandi said the commission resolved an average of 80% of complaints within a week and took enforcement action against 64 water companies it described as non-compliant.

For 2025 to 2027, Sandi submitted a budget of NLE 5,590.29. The commission's plans include completing a tariff model for solar mini-grids and monitoring electricity and water rates.

Why it mattersElectricity and water providers face continued rate monitoring, and the commission must deliver its 2025-2027 plans on the budget it has submitted.
