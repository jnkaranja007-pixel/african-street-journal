Sierra Leone regulator reports NLE 7.4 million in 2024 revenue

The Sierra Leone Electricity and Water Regulatory Commission said levies and licences brought in the income, up from NLE 2.3 million in 2023.

Source: Politico SL · 2026-11-22 · 1 min read

The Sierra Leone Electricity and Water Regulatory Commission (SLEWRC), which licenses and regulates electricity and water providers, recorded internal revenue of NLE 7.4 million in 2024, up from NLE 2.3 million in 2023. The money came from levies and licences.

Deputy Director Sama Sandi told a Ministry of Finance budget discussion on 21 August 2024 that the commission had been allocated NLE 954 thousand for recurrent spending in the 2023 financial year but received nothing from the ministry. The commission said it resolved an average of 80% of complaints within a week and took enforcement action against 64 non-compliant water companies.

For 2024, the commission was allocated NLE 911 thousand in recurrent spending and again received none, Sandi said. He submitted a budget of NLE 5,590.29 for the 2025 to 2027 period.

Why it mattersElectricity and water providers must hold a SLEWRC licence and follow its rate guidelines, and the regulator has asked for NLE 5,590.29 for 2025 to 2027.
