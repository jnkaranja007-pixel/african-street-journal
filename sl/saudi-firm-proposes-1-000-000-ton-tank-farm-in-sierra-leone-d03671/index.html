Saudi firm proposes 1,000,000-ton tank farm in Sierra Leone

AHQ says ARAMCO and ADNOC would supply feedstock, while Sierra Leone’s regulator has offered licensing and technical guidance.

Sources: Sierraloaded and The Calabash · 2026-11-21 · 1 min read

AHQ, a Saudi energy firm, has proposed building a 1,000,000-metric-ton tank farm in Sierra Leone, after discussions at the West Africa Integration and Investment Summit in Lungi on November 19. The project would use a build-operate-transfer model, under which the firm would build and operate the infrastructure before transferring it.

Brima Baluwa Koroma, director-general of Sierra Leone’s National Petroleum Regulatory Authority, said the plan aligns with national energy security goals. The authority offered regulatory support, licensing guidance and technical assistance as the project moves through feasibility and development. AHQ has requested industry data to advance its work.

AHQ representatives said partnerships with Saudi Aramco (Saudi Arabia’s state oil company) and the United Arab Emirates’ Abu Dhabi National Oil Company (ADNOC) would provide feedstock. The delegation also visited Freetown’s Eco-Energy Terminal to assess existing facilities and how petroleum supplies reach Guinea and Liberia.

Why it mattersThe National Petroleum Regulatory Authority must provide AHQ with industry data and guide its feasibility and project-development work.
