Sierra Leone passes 11 MCC scorecard indicators for 2027

The country scored strongly on girls’ primary education and governance as Vice President Mohamed Juldeh Jalloh oversees a US$480 million energy compact.

Source: The Calabash · 2026-11-19 · 1 min read

Sierra Leone passed 11 of the 22 indicators on the United States Millennium Challenge Corporation’s (MCC) 2027 scorecard, according to results on the corporation’s website. Vice President Mohamed Juldeh Jalloh is overseeing implementation of a US$480 million energy compact.

The scorecard records percentile rankings of 97% for girls’ primary education completion, 88% for women in the economy and 77% for government accountability. Sierra Leone also passed the inflation indicator, with a score of 7.6 against a maximum threshold of 15.0. The MCC said information received after the data were finalised confirmed a pass on one additional indicator after the relevant data source acknowledged a correction.

The country’s weaker areas include access to credit, property and land rights, regulatory quality, health expenditures and child health. Government effectiveness had a percentile ranking of 41%.

Why it mattersThe government must continue implementing the US$480 million energy compact while the scorecard identifies access to credit and regulatory quality as reform gaps.
