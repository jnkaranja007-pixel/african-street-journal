Sierra Leone teachers threaten December 16 strike over pay

Union executive member Foday Kuyateh rejects the Government’s proposed 50% salary increase spread over three years as inadequate.

Source: Sierraloaded · 2026-11-26 · 1 min read

The Sierra Leone Teachers’ Union has warned the Government that teachers will withdraw their services on December 16, 2026, if their pay and conditions demands are not addressed. Executive member Foday Kuyateh said the union had issued a 21-day strike notice.

Speaking on Truth Media’s Morning Devotion programme, Kuyateh said teachers with bachelor’s degrees earn approximately NLe1,800 monthly and those with master’s degrees around NLe2,500. He rejected the proposed 50% salary increase spread over three years and called for salary reductions for the President, Ministers, Electoral Commissioners and the Anti-Corruption Commissioner, with resources redirected to teachers’ welfare.

Kuyateh said workers with similar qualifications in other public-sector institutions earn between NLe20,000 and NLe30,000 monthly. He also claimed Sierra Leonean teachers are among the lowest-paid in the sub-region.

Why it mattersThe Government must address teachers’ pay and conditions before the union’s December 16, 2026 strike deadline.
