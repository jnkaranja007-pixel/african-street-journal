Sierra Leone president warns kush sellers to stop

President Bio visits a drug rehabilitation facility and urges parents to find honest work.

Source: Politico SL · 2026-10-30 · 2 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

President Julius Maada Bio has ordered sellers of the synthetic drug Kush to cease their illicit trade, which is causing widespread harm in Sierra Leone. During a visit to the Government’s Harmful Drug Rehabilitation Facility in Hastings on October 30, 2025, he assured recovering users of his administration’s commitment to stopping the sale of dangerous substances. He specifically implored parents involved in selling Kush to stop immediately and seek legitimate livelihoods.

The president addressed drug users at the rehab, stating that their future is essential for Sierra Leone’s development and that the government is working to break the drug supply chain. He condemned those producing and selling Kush, a drug linked to the deaths of many young people since 2019. Bio highlighted the establishment of rehabilitation centers in Hastings, Gondama, and Daru, with plans for another in Teko, Makeni.

Parents shared personal struggles, including Elizabeth Ansumana whose lawyer son became addicted to Kush and required government treatment. A former user, Margaret Jawara, now an ambassador against drug use, credited the Hastings facility and the Ministry of Social Welfare for her recovery. Minister of Social Welfare, Melrose Karminty, reported successful family reintegration for many victims. President Bio also launched a Presidential Task Force on Drugs and Substance Abuse.

Why it mattersYoung people and their families in Sierra Leone are directly affected by the pervasive use and sale of the dangerous drug Kush.
