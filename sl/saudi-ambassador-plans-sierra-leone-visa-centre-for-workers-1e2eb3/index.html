Saudi ambassador plans Sierra Leone visa centre for workers

The proposed centre would handle visa applications and biometric services, while a training facility would prepare prospective workers for Saudi jobs.

Source: Sierraloaded · 2026-11-25 · 1 min read

Saudi Arabia plans to open a visa centre in Sierra Leone to process applications and provide biometric services, Ambassador Saud Al Musaed said during a meeting in Freetown on November 24. He also expressed interest in establishing a training centre to prepare prospective workers for jobs in Saudi Arabia.

Al Musaed said 50 labour visas had been issued to Sierra Leoneans in the previous month. The proposed training facility would assess and screen applicants and equip them with skills before travel. Employment Minister Umaru Napoleon Koroma said recruitment must be transparent and agencies properly vetted, with clear contracts and protections for workers abroad.

Koroma urged people seeking Saudi jobs to understand their visa categories, working conditions and legal rights before travelling. The countries have more than 15 memoranda of understanding and are discussing further agreements.

Why it mattersSierra Leoneans pursuing Saudi jobs are being advised to check visa categories, working conditions and legal rights before they travel.
