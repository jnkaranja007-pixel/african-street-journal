Sierra Leone Judiciary seeks public help to locate murder case accused

The bench warrant for Mohamed Haroun Conteh remains in force after the accused reportedly failed to return to custody during a 2023 prison break.

Source: Sierraloaded · 2026-11-24 · 1 min read

Sierra Leone’s Judiciary has asked the public for credible information to help locate and arrest Mohamed Haroun Conteh, accused of murdering Providence International High School teacher Gillie Edward Ola Johnson. The Judiciary said in a November 23 press release that Conteh was reportedly 17 and Johnson’s pupil when Johnson was found dead at his Freetown home on March 11, 2023.

Conteh was charged with murder, and the case moved from Pademba Road Magistrate Court No. 1 to the High Court on August 2, 2023. The trial began on November 8 but was disrupted by the attempted coup and prison break on November 26. The Judiciary said Conteh was among inmates believed to have escaped after failing to return to Pademba Road Correctional Centre.

The presiding judge issued a bench warrant, which remains active. The Judiciary urged anyone with credible information on Conteh’s whereabouts to contact the authorities so the case can proceed.

Why it mattersThe judge’s active warrant leaves authorities seeking information from the public before Conteh’s murder case can proceed.
