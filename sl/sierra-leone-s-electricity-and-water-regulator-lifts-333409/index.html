Sierra Leone's electricity and water regulator lifts revenue to NLE 7.4 million

The regulator, which licenses power and water firms, collected NLE 7.4 million from levies and licenses in 2024, up from NLE 2.3 million in 2023.

Source: Politico SL · 2026-11-27 · 1 min read

Sierra Leone's Electricity and Water Regulatory Commission (SLEWRC) recorded NLE 7.4 million in internal revenue from levies and licenses in 2024, up from NLE 2.3 million in 2023. The commission regulates electricity and water service providers, issuing, renewing, suspending and revoking their licenses, and sets the rates they may charge.

Deputy Director Sama Sandi told the Ministry of Finance's bilateral budget discussions that the commission received no ministry funding in either 2023 or 2024, despite budget allocations. In 2023 it carried out enforcement against 64 non-compliant water companies and reported that 80% of complaints were resolved within a week of receipt. For 2024 it developed a multi-year tariff model for solar mini-grids, which was about to be completed.

For 2025, Sandi submitted a budget of NLE 5,590.29 for 2025 to 2027. The commission's plans include strengthening its organisational capacity and rolling out a safety and warranty system for water products.

Why it mattersElectricity and water providers must hold licenses and meet tariff rules set by the commission, whose 2025 to 2027 budget is NLE 5,590.29.
