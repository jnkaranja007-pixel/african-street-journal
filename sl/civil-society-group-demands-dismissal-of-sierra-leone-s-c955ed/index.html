Civil society group demands dismissal of Sierra Leone's finance minister

A coalition calling itself the CSOs Consortium on Public Accountability says Finance Minister Sheku Ahmed Fantamadi Bangura should be removed immediately.

Source: Politico SL · 2026-11-15 · 1 min read

The CSOs Consortium on Public Accountability has called for the immediate dismissal of Sierra Leone's Minister of Finance, Sheku Ahmed Fantamadi Bangura. Foday M. Daboh, writing as a guest columnist for Politico SL, argues the call confuses economic hardship with economic mismanagement.

Daboh points to overlapping global shocks between 2020 and 2024, including COVID-19 supply-chain disruptions and food and fuel inflation linked to the Russia-Ukraine war. He says Sierra Leone did not default on its external debt, unlike Ghana, while Zambia entered debt restructuring and Nigeria's naira depreciated sharply.

On inflation, Daboh says the Consortium ignores the trend in Bank of Sierra Leone monetary policy statements, which show a decline after coordinated fiscal and monetary tightening. He cites the IMF's view that reduced central bank financing of deficits has moderated inflation in an economy importing over 70 percent of its consumption basket.

Why it mattersIf the minister were removed, the debt, inflation and financing choices he is credited with would pass to a new team during a period when the source says the country remains at high risk of debt distress.
