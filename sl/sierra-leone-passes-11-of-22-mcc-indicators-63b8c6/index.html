Sierra Leone passes 11 of 22 MCC indicators

The country improved its performance in economic management, governance, education, and business development.

Source: The Calabash · 2026-11-10 · 2 min read

Sierra Leone passed 11 of 22 indicators on the United States Government’s Millennium Challenge Corporation (MCC) Fiscal Year 2027 Scorecard, an increase from nine indicators in the previous assessment. The Ministry of Information and Civic Education announced the results on Friday, 6 November 2026, noting improvements in economic management, democratic governance, education, business development, and women’s economic participation. These results reflect ongoing reforms under President Dr. Julius Maada Bio aimed at strengthening institutions and promoting development.

The country satisfied mandatory MCC requirements for Personal Freedom and Control of Corruption within the Ruling Justly category. Sierra Leone also made progress in the Economic Freedom category, with inflation falling to 7.6 per cent from over 28 per cent previously, and passing the new Business Start-Up indicator. However, the packet notes further improvements are needed in access to finance, infrastructure, taxation, and the cost of doing business.

The MCC Scorecard assesses countries on Ruling Justly, Investing in People, and Economic Freedom to measure commitment to democratic governance, human development, and policies supporting economic growth. While Sierra Leone performed strongly in Ruling Justly, passing five of six indicators, Government Effectiveness remained below the passing threshold, indicating a need for continued reforms in public institutions and service delivery.

Why it mattersThe government's performance on the MCC scorecard affects its ability to attract investment and promote economic growth.
