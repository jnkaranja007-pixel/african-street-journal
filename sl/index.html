Agriculture · 2026-08-21

Njala University opens agricultural lab with 1 million euro EU grant

Njala University has opened a new living lab for aquaculture and fisheries management. Coordinator Dr. Olufemi Julius Olapade said the project received 1 million euros in funding from the European Union. The facility uses circular technologies for sustainable food systems.

Why it mattersThe new technology allows for the production of agricultural goods in enclosed spaces to improve regional food security.

Business · 2026-08-21

Sungrow commissions first national-scale power project in Sierra Leone

Sungrow has completed the RESPITE Solar-plus-Storage Project and connected it to the national grid. The company stated this is the country's first major national-scale power generation project completed in nearly a decade.

Why it mattersThe project increases the national grid's capacity to provide electricity for businesses and residents.

Business · 2026-08-21

Sierra Leone to wind up Mineral Wealth Fund

The government announced in early January 2026 that it will wind up the Mineral Wealth Fund Sierra Leone Limited. The administration intends to transition to a new sovereign wealth framework.

Why it mattersThe abrupt scrapping of the fund may discourage foreign investors from participating in national projects.

Njala University launches online MBA program

Njala University has introduced a full online Master of Business Administration program. The initiative was developed in collaboration with the Association of African Universities' eLearnAfricaLMS and eLearnLXD.

Why it mattersThe online format provides flexible access to higher education for business professionals who cannot attend in-person classes.

Health · 2026-08-21

President Bio warns against sale of Kush drug

President Julius Maada Bio called on dealers to stop selling the synthetic drug known as Kush. The president made the statement on October 30, 2025, during a visit to the Government’s Harmful Drug Rehabilitation Facility in Hastings.

Why it mattersThe illicit trade of the synthetic drug is causing health crises and social instability across the country.
