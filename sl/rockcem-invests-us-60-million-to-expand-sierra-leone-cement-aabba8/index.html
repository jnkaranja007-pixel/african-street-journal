ROCKCEM invests US$60 million to expand Sierra Leone cement supply

The manufacturer is building a grinding mill and a new warehouse in Songo to boost distribution nationwide.

Source: The Calabash · 2026-11-17 · 1 min read

ROCKCEM (SL) Ltd, a cement manufacturer, is undertaking a planned investment of approximately US$60 million to strengthen processing, warehousing and nationwide distribution of RC BRAND CEM II 42.5 R Cement in Sierra Leone. The company stated that rising equipment costs, inflation, changing global economic conditions and an expanding scope pushed the investment to that total.

Central to the industrial expansion is a modern cement grinding mill with a projected daily production capacity exceeding 60,000 bags, with each bag weighing 50 kilograms. According to the company, the facility is designed to improve supply consistency for residential, commercial and infrastructure projects.

The firm is also establishing a modern warehouse at Sumbuya in Songo that is scheduled to commence operations in November 2026. The new distribution hub is intended to serve wholesalers, retailers, contractors and construction companies outside Freetown.

Why it mattersConstruction companies and cement dealers outside Freetown can purchase supplies directly from the new Songo warehouse, reducing logistical expenses on fuel, vehicle maintenance and toll-gate charges.
