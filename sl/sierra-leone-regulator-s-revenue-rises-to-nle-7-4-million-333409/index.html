Sierra Leone regulator's revenue rises to NLE 7.4 million

The Sierra Leone Electricity and Water Regulatory Commission (SLEWRC) saw internal revenue climb from NLE 2.3 million in 2023 to NLE 7.4 million in 2024.

Source: Politico SL · 2026-10-22 · 2 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

The Sierra Leone Electricity and Water Regulatory Commission (SLEWRC) generated NLE 7.4 million in internal revenue from levies and licenses in 2024, a significant increase from the NLE 2.3 million collected in 2023. Despite receiving no funds from the Ministry of Finance for recurrent expenditures in both 2023 and 2024, the commission successfully implemented key initiatives. These included sanitizing the packaged water industry through monitoring and enforcement, leading to more licenses and improved service quality, and resolving approximately 80% of complaints within a week.

SLEWRC also conducted enforcement actions against 64 non-compliant water companies, which encouraged increased licensing. The commission expanded its operational network and developed a Multi-Year Tariff Order (MYTO) model for solar mini-grids, which is nearing completion. For the upcoming fiscal years, SLEWRC plans to strengthen its organizational capacity, facilitate market entry and universal access, ensure international quality standards, and implement a safety and warranty batching system for water products.

The commission's strategic plan for 2025 includes monitoring tariff rate implementation to promote sustainability. The Deputy Director of SLEWRC presented a budget of NLE 5,590.29 for FY 2025 to 2027 during bilateral discussions with the Ministry of Finance. SLEWRC is responsible for regulating electricity and water service providers, including issuing and renewing licenses and setting guidelines for service rates.

Why it mattersBusinesses in the electricity and water sectors are affected by SLEWRC's increased enforcement actions and licensing requirements.
