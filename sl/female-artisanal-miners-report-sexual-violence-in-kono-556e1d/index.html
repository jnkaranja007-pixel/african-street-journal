Female artisanal miners report sexual violence in Kono district

Women working in the diamond-rich Jaiama Nimikor area describe systemic rape and harassment while scavenging for gold and diamonds in abandoned pits.

Source: Politico SL · 2026-10-31 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Women working in the artisanal mining sector in Jaiama Nimikor, Kono district, report frequent instances of rape and sexual harassment. Janet, a 32-year-old miner, stated she was raped at age 15 while scavenging for gold and diamonds in abandoned pits located 8 miles from Koidu. She reported the incident to her village head at the time but could not identify her attacker.

The violence has forced women to abandon their homes and families due to social stigma and a lack of official recourse. Haja, a 48-year-old widow, said she fled to the village of Guitor after being raped while working in the mines. She claimed the camp master dismissed her report by suggesting the attacker was her boyfriend, an assertion she denies.

Many women turned to mining as a primary source of income following the end of the civil war. These miners continue to face risks while working in isolated areas to support their families. Haja eventually returned to her original village after her attacker voluntarily left the area.

Why it mattersThe report details the physical and social dangers faced by women working in the artisanal mining sector in Sierra Leone's Kono district.
