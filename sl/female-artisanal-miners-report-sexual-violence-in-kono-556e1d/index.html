Female artisanal miners report sexual violence in Kono district

Women working in diamond and gold pits in Jaiama Nimikor describe a pattern of rape and harassment that goes unpunished by local authorities.

Source: Politico SL · 2026-10-28 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Women working in the diamond-rich district of Kono in eastern Sierra Leone are reporting incidents of rape and sexual harassment while scavenging for minerals. Janet, a 32-year-old miner, stated she was raped at age 15 while working alone in a pit in Jaiama Nimikor, located 8 miles from Koidu. She reported the assault to her village head, but the perpetrator remained unidentified.

The violence often occurs in abandoned mining sites where women seek income to support their families. Haja, a 48-year-old widow, reported that she was raped by a man in her village while working as a gold miner. She stated that the camp master dismissed her complaint, claiming the assailant was her boyfriend, an allegation she denied. Haja fled to another village due to the resulting shame before eventually returning.

These women turned to artisanal mining as a primary livelihood following the end of the civil war. Many report that their complaints to local village leaders and camp masters have yielded no action against the perpetrators. The victims continue to face social stigma and long-term consequences, including unwanted pregnancies and the displacement of their children.

Why it mattersThe story details the experiences of women in Kono district who face sexual violence while working in artisanal diamond and gold mines.
