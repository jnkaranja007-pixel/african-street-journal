Female artisanal miners report sexual violence in Kono district

Women working in the diamond-rich mines of Jaiama Nimikor face persistent threats of rape and harassment while scavenging for gold and diamonds.

Source: Politico SL · 2026-10-30 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Women working in the diamond-rich mines of Jaiama Nimikor in Sierra Leone's Kono district report frequent sexual violence while scavenging for minerals. Janet, a 32-year-old miner, recounted being raped at age 15 while working alone in the pits. She stated that she reported the assault to her village head at the time, but the perpetrator remained unidentified.

The violence has forced many women to flee their homes or face social stigma. Haja, a 48-year-old widow, said she was raped while working in the mines and subsequently fled to the village of Guitor. She reported the incident to a camp master, who dismissed her claim by suggesting the attacker was her partner. Haja stated that the man who assaulted her later left the village voluntarily.

Many women turned to artisanal mining as a primary source of income following the civil war. Janet, who dropped out of school as a teenager, continues to work in the sector to support her child. Other female miners in the area confirmed experiencing similar incidents of sexual harassment and assault while working in the pits.

Why it mattersWomen in the Kono district face physical danger and sexual violence while working in abandoned mining pits to support their families.
