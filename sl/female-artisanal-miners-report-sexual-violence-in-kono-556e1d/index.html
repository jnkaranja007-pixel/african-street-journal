Female artisanal miners report sexual violence in Kono district mines

Women working in diamond-rich pits in Jaiama Nimikor face systemic rape and harassment while struggling to earn a living in Sierra Leone.

Source: Politico SL · 2026-11-01 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Women scavenging for gold and diamonds in the Jaiama Nimikor mining area of Kono district report frequent incidents of rape and sexual harassment. Janet, a 32-year-old miner, stated she was raped at age 15 while working alone in a pit eight miles from Koidu. She became pregnant following the assault and faced community stigmatization, eventually losing custody of her child to a relative.

Haja, a 48-year-old widow and mother of three, said she was also raped while working in the mines. She reported the assault to a camp master, who dismissed her claim by alleging the attacker was her boyfriend. Haja fled to the village of Guitor to escape the shame of the incident, leaving her children behind before eventually returning to her community.

Many women turned to artisanal mining to support their families following the end of the civil war in Sierra Leone. These miners often work in abandoned pits to find gold or diamonds after dropping out of school or losing their spouses. Despite reporting these crimes to village authorities and camp officials, victims say they have received little to no support or justice for the violence they endure.

Why it mattersThe report details the specific risks of sexual violence faced by women working in the artisanal mining sector in Kono district.
