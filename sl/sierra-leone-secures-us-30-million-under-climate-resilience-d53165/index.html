Sierra Leone secures US$30 million under climate resilience programme

The Climate Investment Funds' ARISE programme announced the support at COP31 in Antalya, with financing arrangements and implementation still to be set out.

Source: The Calabash · 2026-11-18 · 1 min read

Sierra Leone secured US$30 million in climate investment support under the Climate Investment Funds' Accelerating Resilience Investments and Innovations for Sustainable Economies (ARISE) programme, announced on 12 November at the 31st Conference of the Parties in Antalya, Türkiye.

Environment and Climate Change Minister Jiwoh Emmanuel Abdulai said the recognition of Sierra Leone as a front-runner country under ARISE affirmed the government's commitment to resilience and adaptation. The announcement does not mean the funds have already entered government accounts; financing terms, project components, allocations and timelines will determine how they are deployed.

The government is expected to provide information on financing conditions, implementing institutions and intended beneficiaries. The article identifies coastal protection, urban drainage, climate-smart agriculture, water infrastructure, disaster preparedness and early-warning systems as possible priorities, with local communities involved in setting them.

Why it mattersThe government must disclose financing conditions, implementing institutions and intended beneficiaries before the US$30 million support is deployed.
