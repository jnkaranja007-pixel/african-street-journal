Sierra Leone assigns 28,838 personnel to December census

Statistics Sierra Leone says the nationwide count starts Dec. 1, after 30,085 applicants sat the final recruitment examination.

Source: Sierraloaded · 2026-11-27 · 1 min read

Statistics Sierra Leone plans to deploy 28,838 personnel for Sierra Leone’s 2026 Population and Housing Census, which begins Dec. 1. Statistician-General and Chief Census Officer Andrew Bob Johnny announced the figure at a press conference in Freetown on Nov. 25.

Deputy Statistician-General Lansana Kpewolo Kanneh said 31,352 applicants were shortlisted, 30,652 attended training and 30,085 took the final examination. Johnny said the agency had received 3,000 additional tablets after some training centres faced shortages, and was reviewing equipment allocations and field assignments. Supervisors will monitor progress and identify areas needing support.

The agency says digital tools will track enumerators in real time and use GPS to check their assigned locations. United Nations Population Fund representative Kunle Adeniyi said the census falls within the UN’s 2030 round, covering 2025 to 2034.

Why it mattersThe census agency must equip and support 28,838 field workers for deployment from Dec. 1, with supervisors monitoring their work.
