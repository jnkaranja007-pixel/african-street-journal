Sierra Leone vice presidents rarely become president

No vice president in Sierra Leone has ever been elected president, raising questions about the current vice president's future.

Source: Politico SL · 2026-11-14 · 1 min read

The office of Vice President in Sierra Leone has historically been a political dead end, with no holder ever ascending to the presidency. Since independence, vice presidents have often been appointed to balance political considerations rather than to groom successors.

Past vice presidents like Sorie Ibrahim Koroma and Francis Minah under Siaka Stevens were powerful but not groomed for succession, with Minah facing execution after an alleged coup. Solomon Ekuma Berewa, considered well-prepared, lost the 2007 presidential election despite his experience, indicating that competence alone is insufficient for electoral success.

Samuel Sam-Sumana's experience as a vice president who was later estranged from the president, expelled from his party, and left politically isolated, further illustrates the precariousness of the position. Victor Bockarie Foh's short tenure also lacked succession ambition.

Why it mattersThe current Vice President of Sierra Leone faces a historical pattern where the office does not lead to the presidency, impacting future political ambitions.
