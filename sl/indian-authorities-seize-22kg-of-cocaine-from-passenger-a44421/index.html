Indian authorities seize 22kg of cocaine from passenger arriving from Sierra Leone

The passenger was arrested in New Delhi after officials said she posed as a diplomatic courier and presented documents linked to the United Nations.

Source: Sierraloaded · 2026-11-20 · 1 min read

Indian authorities arrested a foreign woman arriving in New Delhi from Sierra Leone after finding 22 kilogrammes of cocaine in her luggage, India’s Directorate of Revenue Intelligence (DRI) said in an announcement on November 12, 2026.

The DRI said the passenger presented a briefcase marked “Diplomatic Mail” and documents purporting to link the consignment to a United Nations diplomatic courier. Officers searched it after acting on intelligence and found 22 packets; field tests identified the substance as cocaine. The drugs were seized under India’s narcotics law, and the woman was brought before a court.

India’s Ministry of Finance said the cocaine’s estimated illicit-market value was several crores of Indian rupees. Authorities did not disclose the passenger’s identity or nationality, or say whether further investigations into the route were under way.
