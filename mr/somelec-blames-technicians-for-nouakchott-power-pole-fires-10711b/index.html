Somelec blames technicians for Nouakchott power pole fires

Mauritania's electricity company cites unqualified climbers and illegal connections for recurring fires in the capital.

Source: Sahara Medias · 2026-11-02 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

The Mauritanian electricity company Somelec stated that unqualified external technicians, poor quality cables, and illegal connections are causing recurring fires on electric poles. These practices lead to short circuits when network load and pressure increase, exceeding the capacity of poles and transformers.

Somelec announced a field campaign to remove illegal connections and regularize hookups on poles and the electrical network in the capital, Nouakchott. The company urged citizens to help protect the electrical network and warned of risks to lives, property, and neighborhood safety.

Nouakchott has recently experienced repeated fires in electrical installations, including distribution posts and technical rooms. Somelec attributed these incidents to electrical overload and rising temperatures.

Why it mattersRecurring fires in Nouakchott's electrical installations threaten the safety of residents and their property.
