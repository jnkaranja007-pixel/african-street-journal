Adrar governor launches cleaning campaign in Atar

Governor Abdellahi Ould Mohamed Mahmoud initiated a sanitation drive in Atar's sector 5, targeting Adabaye neighborhood and the western administrative area.

Source: Agence Mauritanienne d'Information · 2026-10-26 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Adrar Governor Abdellahi Ould Mohamed Mahmoud launched a cleaning campaign in sector 5 of Atar city on Saturday. The initiative is part of a regional sanitation drive, following directives from the President.

The governor visited the area, which includes the Adabaye neighborhood and the western administrative zone, to observe the sanitation work. He praised the efforts of the Tourism and Hajj Federation section and urged residents to cooperate in eliminating waste and random dumps.

The head of the Tourism and Hajj Federation section in Adrar, Aslamou Ould Cherki, stated the campaign aims to improve the urban landscape and public health. He called on citizens to actively participate in the cleaning efforts.

Why it mattersThe governor of Adrar and local officials launched a cleaning campaign in Atar city, affecting residents and urban areas.
