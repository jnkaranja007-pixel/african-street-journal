Mauritanian parliament approves government programme timeline

The Conference of Presidents spent over seven hours reviewing the government's agenda before setting dates for its presentation and vote.

Source: Sahara Medias · 2026-11-07 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Mauritania's National Assembly Conference of Presidents spent seven hours and 20 minutes reviewing the government's programme. The conference approved the timeline by a majority vote. The programme will be presented on November 7 and debated and voted on November 9.

The parliamentary body also scheduled the examination and vote for the 2027 finance bill for December 28. This review follows the bill's referral to the finance commission. The meeting was chaired by first vice-president Sidney Sokhona, with Minister of Culture Mohamed Ould Babana representing the government.

The Conference of Presidents is a body within the National Assembly. Its decisions on the government's programme and finance bill directly impact national policy and budget allocation for the coming year.

Why it mattersThe government's programme and the 2027 finance bill will be debated and voted on by the Mauritanian National Assembly, affecting national policy and budget.
