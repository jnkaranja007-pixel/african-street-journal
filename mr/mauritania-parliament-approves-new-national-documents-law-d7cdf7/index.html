Mauritania parliament approves new national documents law

The new legislation replaces a 1968 text and aims to unify collection, preservation, and access to national records.

Source: Sahara Medias · 2026-11-03 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Mauritania's National Assembly approved a new law governing national documents on Monday. The legislation, presented by the Minister of the General Secretariat of the Government, Mokhtar Abdallahi, aims to create a unified legal framework for managing national documents in all formats.

Minister Abdallahi stated the new law addresses the obsolescence of the 1968 archives text and aligns legal practices with international standards. He explained it defines national document types, confirms public and heritage status for public archives, and strengthens the National Archives' role in conservation and guidance.

The law also regulates private documents of national interest and sets conditions for their accessibility. It covers electronic and audiovisual documents, and includes incentives and sanctions to ensure proper management and protection of public records, modernizing administration and preserving national memory.

Why it mattersThe new law aims to modernize administration, preserve national memory, enhance transparency, protect citizen rights, and support scientific and historical research in Mauritania.
