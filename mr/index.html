Mauritanian parliament to vote on government programme November 9

The Conference of Presidents spent over seven hours reviewing the government's programme before approving it for a vote.

Mauritania's National Assembly Conference of Presidents spent seven hours and 20 minutes reviewing the government's programme. The conference approved the programme by majority vote and scheduled its presentation for November 7, with examination and voting set for November 9.

The meeting, presided over by first vice-president Sidney Sokhona, included Minister of Culture Mohamed Ould Babana representing the government. The parliament announced the decision in a communiqué released on Thursday.

The Conference also set December 28 for the examination and vote on the 2027 finance bill, which has been sent to the finance commission. The finance bill's review follows the government programme's approval process.

Why it mattersThe government's programme and the finance bill for 2027 will be debated and voted on by the National Assembly.

Mauritania beach soccer team beats Iraq 8-1

The Mauritanian national beach soccer team secured a dominant 8-1 victory against Iraq in their opening match of the Arab Cup.

Mauritania's national beach soccer team began their Arab Cup campaign with a commanding 8-1 win over Iraq. The match took place this morning in Port Said, Egypt.

The Mauritanian team, known as the "Mourabitounes," delivered a strong performance, demonstrating superiority throughout the game. They converted their dominance into an avalanche of goals, earning their first three points in the tournament.

This victory marks a successful start for the Mauritanian side in the competition.

Three Koumé family members to face Nouakchott court

Family members protest forced eviction and arrest, announcing a sit-in for November 5.

Three young members of the Koumé family will appear before the Nouakchott west prosecutor on Thursday, November 5, 2026, following their arrest during a controversial eviction from their home on Îlot L in the capital. The family claims the eviction on October 21 involved nine police vehicles and disputes the land documents used for the operation.

The family's relatives are demanding the immediate release of the three youths and have called for a peaceful sit-in outside the Nouakchott west Palace of Justice if they are not freed before their court appearance. The Koumé family states that women and children are still living on the street opposite their former residence and reports death threats against some members on November 3.

The organizers of the planned sit-in are calling for the family's return to their home, an independent investigation into the eviction and arrests, and accountability for those responsible. They are inviting human rights organizations, lawyers, civil society associations, media, and citizens to join the gathering to demand justice and protection for the Koumé family.

Why it mattersThe Koumé family, including women and children, are left homeless and face death threats following a forced eviction and subsequent arrests.

Politics · 2026-11-06

Dar Naim mayor reports infrastructure improvements

The mayor of Dar Naim municipality stated that the area has seen significant upgrades to its infrastructure and basic services.

The mayor of Dar Naim municipality, Amem Ould Ghet Ould Amem, confirmed that the area has benefited from the first phase of an emergency program launched by the president. He noted tangible changes in infrastructure, education, and essential services, including new roads and the construction of schools.

Amem Ould Ghet Ould Amem highlighted the visible transformation, particularly the modern road network built across neighborhoods, which he described as a qualitative leap for urban infrastructure. He also mentioned that dozens of schools were built in previously underserved areas, helping to reduce classroom overcrowding.

While acknowledging progress in road construction, school building, green spaces, and street lighting, the mayor pointed out that the issue of sewage systems remains a significant challenge for residents. He expressed hope for special attention to resolve this problem and keep pace with the developmental transformation in Dar Naim.

Why it mattersResidents of Dar Naim municipality are experiencing improved infrastructure and services, though sewage system issues persist.
