Somelec denies employee death in Nouakchott electrocution incident

Mauritania's national electricity company, Somelec, confirmed a person died from electrocution on an electrical pole in Dar El Beïda, Nouakchott, but stated the victim was not.

Somelec clarified that the fatal accident occurred in the Dar El Beïda neighborhood of El Mina moughataa, not Kandahar as some social media reports indicated. The company stated the victim attempted an unauthorized and illegal intervention on the electrical network without safety measures or company knowledge.

The utility emphasized that any work on its electrical installations requires technical qualification and direct coordination with its specialized teams. Somelec warned that climbing poles or making illegal connections can lead to fatal accidents and legal prosecution for those involved.

Somelec urged citizens to avoid using non-company personnel for electrical network issues, especially during the rainy season, due to risks from improper handling of cables, poles, and installations. The company also advised illegal network users to refrain from tampering with facilities to protect lives and property, while offering condolences to the victim's family.

Why it mattersResidents in Nouakchott are affected by the warning against unauthorized electrical work, which could lead to fatal accidents and legal consequences for individuals attempting such interventions.

Mauritania President Thanks OIC Leaders for Secretary-General Election

President Mohamed Ould Cheikh El Ghazouani expressed gratitude to member states for their unanimous support in electing Mauritania's candidate as the new OIC Secretary-General.

President Mohamed Ould Cheikh El Ghazouani of Mauritania extended his sincere thanks to the leaders of the Organization of Islamic Cooperation (OIC) member states. This appreciation follows the unanimous election of Mauritania's candidate, Mr. Ismail Ould Cheikh Ahmed, as the new Secretary-General of the OIC.

The election took place during the twenty-third extraordinary session of the Council of Foreign Ministers, held on Thursday, August twenty-seventh, 2026, in Jeddah, Saudi Arabia. President El Ghazouani acknowledged the support, trust, and assistance from the leaders, which he stated contributed to this significant diplomatic success.

The President also voiced his hope that this tenure will strengthen the OIC's unity and effectiveness, serving the issues and interests of the Islamic nation and fulfilling the aspirations of its peoples.

Why it mattersThe election of Mauritania's candidate to a prominent international role elevates the country's diplomatic standing and could influence regional and international policy affecting its citizens.

OMVS Sends Technical Reinforcements to Nouakchott Power Plants

Equipment and three technical experts, including a Mauritanian, arrived in Nouakchott from Rosso to address the aftermath of two power plant fires.

Technical reinforcements and equipment from the Organization for the Development of the Senegal River (OMVS) were transported to Nouakchott this Tuesday from Rosso. These resources, accompanied by three technical experts, including one Mauritanian working for OMVS, are part of ongoing efforts to mitigate the consequences of two fires that damaged power plants in the capital.

The material was transported under escort by the National Gendarmerie for the second consecutive day, according to a source who spoke to Sahara Media. The source did not specify the exact nature or technical purpose of the equipment, nor whether it would be used directly for repairs or to support recommissioning operations.

These reinforcements arrive as Mauritanian authorities continue to address the significant power outages caused by fires at the East and North electrical transformer stations last Friday and Saturday. The Prime Minister announced that the President ordered an independent inquiry commission and that financial resources have been released for plant rehabilitation.

Why it mattersResidents and businesses in Nouakchott are directly affected by the power outages and the efforts to restore electricity following the fires.

Brakna Governor Inspects Allak Flood Response After 90mm Rainfall

The Governor of Brakna, Mr. Tayeb Ould Mohamed Mahmoud, visited Allak to assess operations draining rainwater following 90 millimeters of rain on Monday night.

Governor Tayeb Ould Mohamed Mahmoud of Brakna, accompanied by the regional emergency committee, inspected several neighborhoods in Allak today, Thursday. The visit aimed to monitor the progress of rainwater pumping operations after the city received 90 millimeters of rain on Monday night, which caused significant accumulation in parts of the city.

Officials from the National Sanitation Office and the Regional Delegation for Civil Security and Crisis Management briefed the Governor on the methods used to pump water and the interventions implemented to mitigate the effects of the rainfall. The Governor stated that the field assessment showed the situation was normal due to ongoing efforts by these institutions to maintain and operate the drainage network.

The Governor praised the human and logistical resources provided by the National Sanitation Office and the Regional Delegation for Civil Security and Crisis Management to manage the rainfall's impact and protect citizens and property. He also noted that current improvements to the sanitation network would soon enhance rainwater drainage and reduce accumulation within the city, urging residents to help protect the network.

Why it mattersResidents of Allak are directly affected by the efforts to manage floodwaters, which impacts their safety and property after heavy rainfall.

Brakna border commission reviews security challenges

The regional border management commission in Brakna province met to assess border security challenges and combat "phenomena foreign to society."

The Brakna regional border management commission convened on Tuesday to examine issues related to securing borders and addressing "phenomena foreign to society." The meeting, a regular session presided over by Wali Tayeb Ould Mohamed Mahmoud, aimed to evaluate the execution of assigned missions and review key challenges.

The Wali stated that the commission is working to strengthen coordination among various stakeholders involved in border management. This effort focuses on fragile areas and involves relevant actors in developing border policies. The commission also monitors the work of village committees.

The commission establishes periodic reports on the results of village committee activities to transmit them to relevant bodies. This process aims to enhance monitoring and improve the performance of actors involved in border management. Future meetings are planned for constituencies along the river as part of decentralization efforts.

Why it mattersThis event affects residents in Brakna province by influencing border security policies and the operations of village committees that impact their daily lives.
