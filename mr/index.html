Mauritanian Prime minister vows judicial reform

Mokhtar Ould Diay also pledged to fight corruption and boost private sector investment.

Mauritanian Prime Minister Mokhtar Ould Diay pledged to reform the judicial system, intensify the fight against corruption, and develop public administration. He also aims to implement economic reforms to stimulate investment and expand the private sector's role in wealth and job creation. Ould Diay announced these commitments on Saturday while presenting the government's general policy to the National Assembly.

The Prime Minister stated that his government's action plan aligns with President Mohamed Ould Cheikh El Ghazouani's program, "My Ambitions for the Homeland." The plan focuses on five key areas, including building an institutional state, strengthening the economy, and developing human capital. Ould Diay emphasized the government's direction towards developing the democratic system, reforming the judiciary, and enhancing human rights and freedom of expression.

Economically, the government intends to undertake structural reforms to liberalize the economy and encourage investment, supporting the private sector's growth contribution. Plans also include developing infrastructure programs and mobilizing necessary funding. The government will also focus on developing sectors with economic potential, considering environmental factors in projects. Human capital development, prioritizing youth qualification in education, training, employment, health, culture, and sports, is considered essential for economic and social policy success.

Why it mattersMauritanian citizens will experience reforms in the justice system, increased efforts against corruption, and potential economic growth through private sector investment.

Trarza team wins traditional shooting contest

The team from Trarza scored 43 hits to secure first place in the Mauritanian traditional shooting competition.

The governor of Hodh El Gharbi, Mohamed Ould Ahmed Mouloud, oversaw the conclusion of the regional traditional shooting competition in Leguey on Sunday. The event brought together teams from various regions to compete in the heritage sport.

The Trarza team emerged victorious with 43 hits, followed by Adrar with 42 hits and Inchiri with 40 hits. Cheikhna Abdoulaye from the Gorgol team was awarded best shooter.

The governor praised the event for fostering brotherhood and national unity, highlighting President Mohamed Ould Cheikh El Ghazouani's support for national heritage. The head of the Mauritanian Traditional Shooting Office, Mohamed Mohamed Znagui, thanked the president and the culture minister for their patronage.

Why it mattersThe traditional shooting competition in Leguey affected participants from Mauritania's regions, including the winning Trarza team.

Mauritania joins international disaster body

The General Delegation for Civil Security will share risk data and early warning systems.

Mauritania's General Delegation for Civil Security and Crisis Management has joined the International Mechanism for Cooperation on Disaster Prevention and Emergency Management. The decision followed a conference on November 6 in Beijing, China, under the "Belt and Road" initiative.

Colonel Mohamed Ould Henany, Director of Prevention and Control, represented Mauritania at the meeting, which included delegates from 25 countries and eight international organizations. Participants acknowledged the mechanism's achievements and China's role in fostering disaster cooperation.

The membership enables the exchange of risk databases for events like earthquakes and floods, and the deployment of updated early warning technologies for vulnerable populations. It also facilitates the integration of strict safety standards into major infrastructure projects.

Why it mattersMauritania will gain access to shared disaster risk data and improved early warning systems for its citizens.

Mauritania beach soccer team reaches Arab Cup final

The national team defeated Lebanon 5-3 in the semi-final to secure their first-ever final appearance.

Mauritania's national beach soccer team has reached the final of the 2026 Arab Cup after beating Lebanon 5-3 in their semi-final match. This victory secures their place in the final for the first time in the team's history in the tournament.

Coach Baqa Yogo Moussa's players delivered a strong performance to clinch the qualification spot. The team's success marks a significant achievement for Mauritanian beach soccer.

The team will now compete in the final match of the Arab Cup. Further details on the final opponent and date were not immediately available in the report.

Why it mattersThe Mauritanian national beach soccer team has reached the final of the Arab Cup, a significant sporting achievement for the country.

Mauritania's prime minister calls for national unity

Prime Minister Ould Guig emerged from parliament on Saturday to outline a vision for national progress.

Prime Minister Ould Guig stated that building nations is a long-term endeavor requiring cumulative effort and intergenerational work, not a short race for immediate gains. He presented the government's policy statement to parliament on Saturday, emphasizing that each phase should build upon the last.

He recalled the president's assertion that governments should continue from where their predecessors left off, adding to achievements and addressing shortcomings. Ould Guig described Mauritania as having a real chance to start a new, broader national phase based on present confidence, future optimism, clear vision, serious work, reform courage, and ambitious achievement.

The Prime Minister urged Mauritanians to unite and work with hope, viewing differences of opinion as enriching rather than divisive. He stressed the importance of employing youth energy, strengthening institutions, and fostering solidarity as foundations for a renewed national launch.

Why it mattersThe Prime Minister's speech outlines a vision for national development and unity that directly affects all Mauritanian citizens.
