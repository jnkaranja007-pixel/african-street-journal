Mauritanian navy saves 134 migrants near Nouadhibou

The rescued migrants, including 118 men, 14 women, and two children, were heading for the Canary Islands.

Mauritania's navy rescued 134 migrants from a boat in the Nouadhibou port channel on Saturday. The vessel was attempting to reach the Canary Islands when the operation took place.

The military announced the rescue, stating the group comprised 118 men, 14 women, and two children. This operation is part of the navy's efforts to save lives at sea and combat illegal immigration.

The navy also aims to disrupt migrant trafficking networks and enhance maritime security. The operation occurred as the boat was en route to the Canary Islands.

Somelec blames technicians for Nouakchott power pole fires

Mauritania's electricity company cites unqualified climbers, poor cables, and illegal connections for recurrent fires.

The Mauritanian electricity company, Somelec, stated that unqualified external technicians, low-quality cables, and illegal connections caused repeated fires on power poles in Nouakchott. These practices lead to short circuits when network load and pressure increase, exceeding the capacity of poles and transformers.

Somelec announced a campaign to remove illegal connections and regularize those on the capital's electricity network. The company urged citizens to help protect the network and warned of risks to lives, property, and residential security.

Nouakchott has recently experienced frequent fires in electrical installations, including distribution posts and technical rooms. Somelec attributed these incidents to electrical overload and rising temperatures.

Why it mattersResidents of Nouakchott face risks to their lives, property, and security due to recurrent fires on electrical installations.

Mauritania president returns from Dakar summit

President Mohamed Ould Ghazouani attended the Dakar Summit on Sport and Sustainable Development and the Youth Olympic Games opening.

President Mohamed Ould Ghazouani returned to Nouakchott on Saturday evening after participating in the Dakar Summit on Sport and Sustainable Development in Senegal's capital. He also attended the opening ceremony of the "Dakar 2026" Summer Youth Olympic Games.

Upon arrival at Nouakchott International Airport, the president was welcomed by Prime Minister Mohamed Ould Béchir and several other ministers, including the Minister of Justice and the Minister of Foreign Affairs. A delegation including the First Lady, Dr. Mariam Fadil Dah, and the Minister of Youth, Employment, Sports, and Civil Service accompanied him.

The president's participation in the summit highlights Mauritania's engagement with continental initiatives aimed at leveraging sports for broader societal progress.

Mauritanian minister orders Zouerate hospital work accelerated

Mamoudou Mamadou Niang inspected ongoing construction at the regional hospital and schools in Zouerate.

Mamoudou Mamadou Niang, Mauritania's minister of state property and land reform, ordered work on Zouerate's regional hospital to speed up on Saturday. He visited ongoing construction sites as part of an emergency program to expand access to essential services.

The minister inspected school construction at "Tarhil 2" and "deputy Saïdou Wane" schools, where work was completed. At Hadrami Ould Moma school, he reviewed progress on four classrooms and received explanations on remaining steps.

Niang's visit aimed to assess the progress of construction and the readiness of completed facilities, ensuring adherence to technical specifications and required standards for the hospital and schools.

Why it mattersResidents of Zouerate will benefit from the accelerated completion of the regional hospital and new school classrooms.

Education · 2026-11-01

Mauritania centre holds seminar on teacher training reform

The Educational Research and Studies Centre organised a seminar to discuss the reality and prospects of initial teacher training.

The Educational Research and Studies Centre, in cooperation with the National Federation of Parents' Associations and the Municipality of Toujounine, held a scientific seminar on Saturday evening. The event, titled “Reform of the Initial Training System in Teacher Training Schools: Reality and Prospects,” aimed to enhance discussion on the current state and future development of teacher training.

The seminar is part of the centre’s monthly programme, “Discussions on Education in Mauritania,” and was held to celebrate World Teachers' Day. Mr. Ba Hammady Hussein, the centre's vice president, stated that the seminar aimed to open public discussion on education issues in Mauritania, highlighting the importance of initial teacher training as a cornerstone for successful educational reform.

Lecturer Mohamed Elamine Ould Elban presented an overview of teacher training schools, focusing on the specificities of the teacher training school in Nouakchott. He discussed the challenges these institutions face and the opportunities available for their development and performance improvement.

Why it mattersThe seminar addresses the quality of education in Mauritania by focusing on the reform of teacher training schools.
