Mauritania Launches Recruitment Drive for 1,132 Educators

A nationwide competition aims to fill 1,132 teaching positions, including 241 college professors and 891 general teachers, to bolster educational staff.

Mauritania has initiated a recruitment competition to hire 1,132 new educators, comprising 241 college professors and 891 teachers. The initiative seeks to strengthen pedagogical teams and support schools across all regions of the country. Sadvi Sidi Mohamed, the Secretary General of the Ministry of Education and Educational System Reform, emphasized the importance of adhering to established procedures with responsibility and discipline.

During a visit to several examination centers, the Secretary General observed the organizational and technical processes, as well as the conditions for candidates and the conduct of the tests. He received detailed explanations from center officials regarding the measures in place to ensure the smooth running of the competition and equal opportunities for all applicants, stressing the importance of fairness and procedural integrity.

Mohamed urged candidates to recognize the significant responsibility inherent in the teaching profession and its vital role in serving students. He highlighted the profession's contribution to ongoing reforms within the educational system, underscoring the commitment to enhancing the quality of education nationwide through these new appointments.

Why it mattersThe recruitment of 1,132 new educators will directly impact students and the educational system by strengthening teaching staff and supporting school operations.

Mauritania and IMF Review Public Financial Data Modernization

Finance Minister Codioro Moussa N’Guenore met with an IMF delegation to enhance public institution financial data collection and verification.

Mauritanian Finance Minister Codioro Moussa N’Guenore met with an International Monetary Fund (IMF) delegation to discuss modernizing financial data for public institutions. The meeting focused on improving mechanisms for collecting and verifying this data as part of ongoing cooperation between the country and the IMF.

The Ministry of Finance stated that the session, led by Francis Gahizi, the IMF’s regional advisor for public finance statistics, reviewed progress in modernizing financial data collection and ensuring its accuracy. This initiative aims to strengthen the quality of public finance statistics within Mauritania.

Minister N’Guenore emphasized the critical need to enhance the quality of public finance statistics and bolster coordination among the bodies responsible for gathering and processing financial information. The discussions highlighted the importance of reliable financial data for effective governance and economic management.

Why it mattersThis event affects investors by potentially improving the transparency and reliability of public financial data, which is crucial for assessing economic stability and investment risk.

Adrar Water Committee Studies Regional Water Resources

A new regional water committee met for the first time to discuss the state of water resources and sustainable exploitation methods.

The Adrar regional water committee convened its inaugural meeting to examine the condition of water resources within the province and explore strategies for their optimal utilization and sustainability. This initiative aligns with national directives aimed at conserving water and enhancing its use to meet population needs.

The meeting, held in Adrar, also highlighted a partnership between the Adrar region and the Korail organization for development, focusing on water source preservation and supporting regional development efforts. A technical study, conducted over three months of fieldwork, was presented to diagnose the water resource situation.

The study involved field surveys, measuring groundwater levels and salinity, and analyzing soil interaction with rainfall. It also monitored water usage patterns, particularly in oasis agriculture, with 14 groundwater monitoring points checked every 15 days to track changes. The findings are intended to improve water availability and rational use.

Why it mattersThe Adrar regional water committee's study on water resources directly affects farmers by assessing water availability and usage patterns, particularly in oasis agriculture.

Egyptian Minister Honors Mauritanian Scholar

Osama Al-Azhari praised the late Mohamed Al-Hassan Ould Ahmed Al-Khadim, a prominent religious scholar and educator.

Egyptian Minister of Religious Affairs, Osama Al-Azhari, paid tribute to Mauritanian scholar Mohamed Al-Hassan Ould Ahmed Al-Khadim, the sheikh of Mahadra Taysir, who passed away at nearly 90 years old. The minister described Al-Khadim as one of Mauritania's most distinguished contemporary ulema, highlighting his lifelong dedication to teaching, writing, and training students in religious sciences.

In a statement, Al-Azhari emphasized the deceased's significant contributions to the study of the Quran, hadith, Islamic jurisprudence (fiqh), and its principles (usul al-fiqh). He also noted Al-Khadim's published works and the many students who benefited from his instruction. The minister recalled a previous visit to Al-Khadim's Mahadra, where he observed the scholar's extensive knowledge and noble character firsthand.

The Egyptian minister extended his condolences to Al-Khadim's family, students, admirers, and Mauritanian ulema. He prayed for divine mercy for the departed scholar. Al-Khadim, based in the Trarza region, was recognized for his profound impact on religious education and scholarship within Mauritania.

Why it mattersThe event honors a prominent religious scholar, highlighting the cultural and educational ties between Egypt and Mauritania through religious studies.

Minister Meets Italian Delegation to Boost Cooperation

Mauritanian Minister of Higher Education and Scientific Research met an Italian delegation in Nouakchott to discuss enhancing bilateral ties in education and research.

Minister of Higher Education and Scientific Research, Mr. Yacoub Ould Amin, received an Italian delegation in his Nouakchott office on Friday. The meeting focused on exploring existing cooperation between Mauritania and Italy and identifying avenues for its expansion, particularly within the higher education and scientific research sectors.

The discussions aimed to strengthen the partnership between the two nations. Present at the meeting were Mr. Yerban El Kharrachi, advisor for international cooperation, Mr. Hamada Ould Didi Ould Sid Ahmed, Director General of the National Company for Rural Development (Sonader), and Mr. Chamakh Ould Mbark, Director of the Higher School of Agricultural and Veterinary Sciences.

This engagement signifies a commitment to fostering international collaboration in academic and scientific fields. The exchange of ideas and exploration of joint initiatives are expected to lead to tangible developments in Mauritania's higher education and research landscape.

Why it mattersThe meeting between the Mauritanian minister and the Italian delegation aims to enhance cooperation in higher education and scientific research, potentially benefiting students and researchers.
