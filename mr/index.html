Education · 2026-08-21

Mauritania to recruit 1,132 education agents

The Ministry of Education and Reform of the Educational System will hold a recruitment competition for 1,132 agents starting Sept. 3. The intake includes 891 teachers and 241 middle school professors, according to the ministry.

Why it mattersThe hiring process is open to Mauritanian citizens aged 20 and above to fill vacancies in the school system.

Rainfall hits Gorgol and Adrar regions

Rain gauges recorded precipitation across the Gorgol and Adrar regions over a 24-hour period. The Ministry of the Interior and Decentralization reported 20mm of rain in Jenkiri and 15mm in Aymnira and Ahl Boukali.

Why it mattersThe rainfall affects water levels and soil moisture in these specific rural districts.
