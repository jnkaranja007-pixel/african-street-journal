Dar Naim mayor reports infrastructure improvements

The mayor of Dar Naim municipality stated that the area has seen significant upgrades to its infrastructure and basic services.

Source: Agence Mauritanienne d'Information · 2026-11-06 · 1 min read

The mayor of Dar Naim municipality, Amem Ould Ghet Ould Amem, confirmed that the area has benefited from the first phase of an emergency program launched by the president. He noted tangible changes in infrastructure, education, and essential services, including new roads and the construction of schools.

Amem Ould Ghet Ould Amem highlighted the visible transformation, particularly the modern road network built across neighborhoods, which he described as a qualitative leap for urban infrastructure. He also mentioned that dozens of schools were built in previously underserved areas, helping to reduce classroom overcrowding.

While acknowledging progress in road construction, school building, green spaces, and street lighting, the mayor pointed out that the issue of sewage systems remains a significant challenge for residents. He expressed hope for special attention to resolve this problem and keep pace with the developmental transformation in Dar Naim.

Why it mattersResidents of Dar Naim municipality are experiencing improved infrastructure and services, though sewage system issues persist.
