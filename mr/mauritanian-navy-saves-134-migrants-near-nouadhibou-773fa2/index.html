Mauritanian navy saves 134 migrants near Nouadhibou

The migrants, including women and children, were en route to the Canary Islands when intercepted.

Source: Sahara Medias · 2026-11-02 · 1 min read

Mauritania's national navy rescued 134 migrants from a boat in the Nouadhibou port channel on Saturday. The migrants, of various nationalities, were attempting to reach the Canary Islands when the navy intercepted their vessel.

The rescued group comprised 118 men, 14 women, and two children, according to an army announcement. This operation was part of the navy's efforts to save lives at sea and combat illegal immigration and trafficking networks.

The navy also aims to enhance maritime security and safety. The army stated these actions are taken to save lives at sea.
