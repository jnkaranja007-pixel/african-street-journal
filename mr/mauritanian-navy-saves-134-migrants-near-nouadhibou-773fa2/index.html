Mauritanian navy saves 134 migrants near Nouadhibou

The rescued migrants, including 118 men, 14 women, and two children, were heading for the Canary Islands.

Source: Sahara Medias · 2026-11-01 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Mauritania's navy rescued 134 migrants from a boat in the Nouadhibou port channel on Saturday. The vessel was attempting to reach the Canary Islands when the operation took place.

The military announced the rescue, stating the group comprised 118 men, 14 women, and two children. This operation is part of the navy's efforts to save lives at sea and combat illegal immigration.

The navy also aims to disrupt migrant trafficking networks and enhance maritime security. The operation occurred as the boat was en route to the Canary Islands.
