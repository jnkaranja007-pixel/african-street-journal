Mauritanian filmmaker honored at Zagora festival

Mohamed Ould Idoumou received recognition for his career in film production, writing, and directing.

Source: Sahara Medias · 2026-11-04 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

The 14th Zagora International Documentary Film Festival in Morocco honored Mauritanian filmmaker Mohamed Ould Idoumou on Monday. The award recognized his career in cinema, production, writing, and directing. Ould Idoumou's contributions to Mauritanian cinema, including leading "la maison du cinéma" and his seven-year presidency of the Nouakchott Film Festival, were highlighted.

The festival also acknowledged Ould Idoumou's role in promoting joint Maghreb cinematic action and his frequent participation in Arab, African, and Maghreb film events. He has served as a speaker, guest of honor, and jury member at international festivals. Iraqi filmmaker Nouzad Cheikhani was also honored.

The festival activities continue in Zagora until November 6, 2026, with participation from filmmakers and artists from multiple countries. This edition also paid tribute to Iraqi director Nouzad Cheikhani.
