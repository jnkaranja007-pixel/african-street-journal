Mauritania press group seeks withdrawal of media eligibility list

The Coordination of Professional Press Bodies says the regulator’s process excluded organisations and lacked consultation and transparency.

Source: Sahara Medias · 2026-11-19 · 1 min read

The Coordination of Professional Press Bodies in Mauritania has asked the High Authority for the Press and Audiovisual (HAPA) to cancel its decision listing private media outlets that meet minimum professional standards. HAPA published the preliminary list on Friday.

In a statement published Tuesday, the Coordination said the selection process involved exclusion, no consultation and insufficient transparency. It called for the list to be withdrawn and for any material, reputational or administrative privileges tied to it to be frozen. The group also questioned whether criteria had been applied equally.

The Coordination urged HAPA to open talks with press organisations and publish a final list based on unified, public legal criteria. HAPA’s list covers television and radio, online and print outlets, and identifies others still working to meet the standards.

Why it mattersPress organisations outside the preliminary list face any material or administrative privileges being frozen while the Coordination seeks withdrawal and public criteria.
