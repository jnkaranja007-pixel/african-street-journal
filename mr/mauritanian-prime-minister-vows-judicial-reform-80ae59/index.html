Mauritanian Prime minister vows judicial reform

Mokhtar Ould Diay also pledged to fight corruption and boost private sector investment.

Source: Sahara Medias · 2026-11-08 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Mauritanian Prime Minister Mokhtar Ould Diay pledged to reform the judicial system, intensify the fight against corruption, and develop public administration. He also aims to implement economic reforms to stimulate investment and expand the private sector's role in wealth and job creation. Ould Diay announced these commitments on Saturday while presenting the government's general policy to the National Assembly.

The Prime Minister stated that his government's action plan aligns with President Mohamed Ould Cheikh El Ghazouani's program, "My Ambitions for the Homeland." The plan focuses on five key areas, including building an institutional state, strengthening the economy, and developing human capital. Ould Diay emphasized the government's direction towards developing the democratic system, reforming the judiciary, and enhancing human rights and freedom of expression.

Economically, the government intends to undertake structural reforms to liberalize the economy and encourage investment, supporting the private sector's growth contribution. Plans also include developing infrastructure programs and mobilizing necessary funding. The government will also focus on developing sectors with economic potential, considering environmental factors in projects. Human capital development, prioritizing youth qualification in education, training, employment, health, culture, and sports, is considered essential for economic and social policy success.

Why it mattersMauritanian citizens will experience reforms in the justice system, increased efforts against corruption, and potential economic growth through private sector investment.
