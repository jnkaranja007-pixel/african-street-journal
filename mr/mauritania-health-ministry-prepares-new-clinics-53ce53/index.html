Mauritania health ministry prepares new clinics

Health Minister Naha Hamdi Mouknass reviewed preparations for opening new health centers in Nouakchott.

Source: Sahara Medias · 2026-10-30 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Mauritania's Ministry of Health is preparing to open new health centers in Nouakchott, focusing on supply needs and team readiness. Minister Naha Hamdi Mouknass led a meeting with ministry officials, regional health directors, and representatives from the medical supply agency and digital transformation sector. The minister directed the medical supply agency to ensure all new centers are stocked, with a review of the supply system planned three months after launch.

A daily monitoring mechanism has been established, assigning a ministry official to each center to track progress and report to a supervision committee. This system aims to address any shortcomings before the centers become operational. Human resources are also being addressed, with training for new center staff set to begin on Thursday, combining theoretical and practical instruction to prepare them for their roles.

The training will equip staff with the skills needed for the new facilities. The supply system will be evaluated for effectiveness and necessary improvements. Daily reports will help identify and fix issues before the centers open.

Why it mattersNew health centers are being prepared for opening in Nouakchott, which will affect the local population's access to healthcare.
