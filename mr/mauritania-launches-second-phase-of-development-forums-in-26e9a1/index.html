Mauritania launches second phase of development forums in Kiffa

Minister Yaqoub Ould Salem Vall oversaw the launch in Sélibabi, focusing on regional development priorities.

Source: Agence Mauritanienne d'Information · 2026-10-29 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Minister Delegate to the Minister of Interior, Decentralization and Local Development, Yaqoub Ould Salem Vall, launched the second phase of regional development forums in Sélibabi on Thursday evening. He met with regional service heads to assess achievements and identify development needs in Kiffa region.

During the visit, the delegation inspected recently built schools in Ajar Ahl Salem and Knik Oum Dekhel villages, and a new health center in Kouri administrative center. They also visited the city hospital to learn about its operations and efforts to improve citizen access to health services.

The delegation included the governor of Kiffa, the mayors of Sélibabi and Gabou districts, and regional directors for health and education. The forums aim to define development priorities for the region.

Why it mattersThe launch of regional development forums directly impacts the planning and execution of local development projects in Mauritania's Kiffa region.
