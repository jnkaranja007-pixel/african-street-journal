Mauritania raises pensions and public-sector pay from January

The changes include a monthly 10 000 old-ouguiya supplement for all civilian and military retirees, with a minimum pension of 50 000.

Sources: Sahara Medias and Le Calame · 2026-11-28 · 1 min read

President Mohamed Ould Cheikh El Ghazouani announced pension and salary increases in Mauritania for civil servants, military personnel and other public workers during the country’s independence anniversary. The pension reform takes effect on 1 January 2027, alongside a monthly supplement of 10 000 old ouguiyas for all civilian and military retirees.

Retirees whose pensions remain below 50 000 old ouguiyas after the supplement will receive an additional increase, taking the minimum pension to 50 000. Workers in education and training, health, the armed forces and security services will also receive salary increases, as will public employees not covered by those measures.

The announced increases include 10 000 old ouguiyas for education and health workers, 15 000 for armed forces and security personnel, and 5 000 more for paramedical staff’s risk allowance. Education workers will also receive an extra 10 000 old ouguiyas in chalk allowance.
