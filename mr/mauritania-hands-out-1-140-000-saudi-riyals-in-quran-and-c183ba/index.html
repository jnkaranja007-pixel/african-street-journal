Mauritania hands out 1 140 000 Saudi riyals in Quran and Sunna contest

President Mohamed Ould Cheikh El Ghazouani chaired the awards in Nouakchott for the contest's second edition, with 160 candidates from 16 countries.

Source: Sahara Medias · 2026-11-20 · 1 min read

Mauritanian President Mohamed Ould Cheikh El Ghazouani chaired the awards ceremony in Nouakchott on Thursday for the second edition of a West African contest on memorising the Quran and Sunna. Winners and participants received cash prizes and encouragement rewards totalling 1 140 000 Saudi riyals.

The 160 candidates from 16 West African countries competed in 10 categories, five on the Quran and five on the Sunna, covering memorisation, recitation, exegesis and understanding of hadiths. Saudi Arabia's Ministry of Islamic Affairs, Da'wa and Guidance organised the contest with Mauritania's Ministry of Islamic Affairs and Original Education.

Organisers said the contest aims to encourage young people to study the Quran and Sunna and to promote moderation and tolerance. Saudi Arabia's ambassador to Mauritania, Abdelaziz ben Abdallah Al-Raqabi, said relations between the two countries had developed notably in recent years and were moving towards a strategic partnership.

Why it mattersMauritania now hosts the contest's prize-giving, and the winners' places in the 16 national delegations are the next outcome for participating countries.
