Military doctors perform first brain surgery in Mauritania

Military surgeons treated a cerebral aneurysm using advanced angiography techniques at the Nouakchott military cardiology center.

Source: Sahara Medias · 2026-10-29 · 1 min read

Military medical teams in Nouakchott performed cerebral catheterization and cerebral vascular malformation treatment procedures on Tuesday and Wednesday. The operations took place at the military cardiology center.

The first procedure treated a cerebral aneurysm using advanced angiography techniques, according to an army statement. Colonel Pharmacist Mohamed Mahmoud Taleb Jidou stated that performing high-precision operations on Mauritanian citizens domestically is an advancement for specialized medical services.

The army statement described the initiative as unprecedented in Mauritania.

Why it mattersMauritanian citizens with cerebral aneurysms and vascular malformations can now receive specialized treatment within the country.
