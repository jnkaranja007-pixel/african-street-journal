Mauritania's former president returns to prison after poisoning treatment

Mohamed Ould Abdel Aziz was treated at a private clinic in Nouakchott after eating a meal brought from home, medical sources said.

Source: Sahara Medias · 2026-11-24 · 1 min read

Mauritania’s former president Mohamed Ould Abdel Aziz returned to prison after receiving treatment at a private clinic in Nouakchott for food poisoning, Sahara Medias reported Monday, citing sources close to him and a medical source. The meal that caused the poisoning had been brought from his home, the sources said.

Medical sources said tests confirmed the suspected poisoning and that he received treatment before returning to prison. The prison administration said Sunday that doctors he had chosen examined him and that the state paid for care at a private clinic. His defence had said Saturday evening that he had a high fever, breathing difficulty, chest and abdominal pain, and repeated vomiting, and sought an urgent transfer abroad.

Ould Abdel Aziz is serving a 15-year prison sentence in the case known as the “decade file”; the Supreme Court upheld the conviction, which became final on 4 November 2025. Judicial and medical sources say treatment is available in Mauritania and his condition does not warrant transfer abroad.
