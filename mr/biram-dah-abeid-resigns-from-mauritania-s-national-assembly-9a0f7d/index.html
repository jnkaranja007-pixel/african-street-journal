Biram Dah Abeid resigns from Mauritania’s National Assembly

The opposition deputy announced his departure during a session on the government’s policy statement, after returning from Europe and the United States.

Source: Le Calame · 2026-11-25 · 1 min read

Biram Dah Abeid, a Mauritanian opposition deputy, resigned from the National Assembly during a session on the government’s policy statement. He said he had returned hurriedly from Europe and the United States after being summoned to attend and threatened with sanctions over repeated absences.

Prime Minister El Moctar Ould Djay was due to present the government’s general policy statement in plenary. Dah Abeid said he would use peaceful means to shake up national politics; the resignation prompted discussion in Nouakchott and elsewhere in the country.

In a later interview with Le Calame, Dah Abeid sought to explain his decision. The outlet’s commentary said the interview did not clarify his reasons, while reporting that people had since emerged seeking to link him to alleged financial and land-related wrongdoing.

Why it mattersDah Abeid must now explain his resignation to supporters and officials, while people are seeking to implicate him in alleged financial and land-related wrongdoing.
