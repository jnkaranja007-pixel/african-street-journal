Mauritania launches Somelec power-network overhaul with French partners

EDF experts are due to assess Nouakchott’s distribution network in December, after August fires prompted authorities to seek a technical review.

Source: Sahara Medias · 2026-11-25 · 1 min read

Mauritania’s government has launched a development programme for state electricity company Somelec, drawing on expertise from France’s Électricité de France (EDF) and working with the French Development Agency. The first phase is a technical assessment of the electricity distribution network in Nouakchott.

EDF International Networks experts are expected to begin fieldwork in December, Energy and Petroleum Minister Mohamed Ould Abdel Fattah said at a ministry meeting on Monday, 23 November. The minister said the programme followed fires in August and would involve national teams and partners. Later phases will provide operational and network-management support with EDF International and RTE International, then develop a network-modernisation programme.

A technical committee will monitor implementation, prepare periodic progress reports and send them to the minister. The cooperation is also intended to improve network maintenance and strengthen Somelec’s technical skills.

Why it mattersSomelec’s teams must take part in the Nouakchott network assessment, with EDF experts due to start fieldwork in December.
