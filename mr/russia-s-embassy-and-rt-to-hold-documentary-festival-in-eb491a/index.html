Russia's embassy and RT to hold documentary festival in Nouakchott

The 1-2 December programme includes screenings, workshops and round-table discussions for filmmakers and documentary specialists.

Source: Sahara Medias · 2026-11-27 · 1 min read

RT, a Russian television channel, and the Russian embassy in Nouakchott will hold the documentary film festival "The Age of Heroes" in Mauritania's capital on 1 and 2 December. Filmmakers, directors and documentary specialists are due to take part.

The organisers say the programme will include screenings, workshops and round-table discussions. Participants will exchange their experience, learn different working methods and discuss the challenges facing filmmakers and ways to expand documentary production.

The Nouakchott event is part of RT.Doc: The Time of Heroes, an international documentary festival launched three years ago. Its screenings now reach 65 Russian regions and more than 30 countries, including Egypt, Algeria and Tunisia.

Why it mattersMauritanian filmmakers and documentary specialists get a two-day forum in Nouakchott on methods, challenges and production.
