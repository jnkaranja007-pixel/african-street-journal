Mauritania joins OECD Development Centre

The membership was formalised at a high-level meeting in Paris as the government cited dialogue on development and international experience.

Source: Sahara Medias · 2026-11-28 · 1 min read

Mauritania joined the Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development’s Development Centre on Thursday, with the membership formalised at a high-level meeting of the centre’s governing board in Paris.

Economic and Development Minister Mohamed Ahmedou M’Haimid said the move reflected Mauritania’s wish to strengthen dialogue on development and draw on international experience while respecting national priorities. He also pointed to the country’s mineral, fisheries and natural-gas resources, and its potential in renewable energy and green hydrogen.

Created in 1962, the centre helps developing countries and emerging economies devise policies and solutions for sustainable economic and social development.

Why it mattersMauritania’s government now has a place in a centre that supports policy-making in developing countries and emerging economies.
