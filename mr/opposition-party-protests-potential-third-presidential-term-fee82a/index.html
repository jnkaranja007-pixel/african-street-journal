Opposition party protests potential third presidential term

The Tewassoul party organized simultaneous rallies across Mauritania to reject a third presidential term and protest corruption and rising prices.

Source: Sahara Medias · 2026-10-22 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

The opposition party Tewassoul organized simultaneous protest rallies on Wednesday in Nouakchott and other wilaya capitals. The demonstrations aimed to reject a potential third presidential term for the current leader and to denounce what the party described as corruption, rising prices, and restrictions on public freedoms.

According to a party statement, these rallies are part of a broader action plan to express the party's stance on the political and economic situation. Tewassoul is demanding action against corruption, improvements in living conditions, and the preservation of public liberties. The party had previously announced a schedule of actions including federation and section rallies from October 23 to 25.

Further actions planned by Tewassoul include internal travel and festivals in wilaya capitals between November 1 and 15, followed by regional festivals from November 15 to 30. These events are being coordinated with the party's parliamentary group as part of their ongoing campaign.

Why it mattersThe opposition party Tewassoul is protesting against a potential third presidential term and economic issues, affecting the political landscape.
