Mauritel opens centre to monitor telecom network and fix faults

The national control centre will track service performance, detect outages and provide data to guide network improvements and coverage expansion.

Source: Sahara Medias · 2026-11-27 · 1 min read

Mauritanian telecom company Mauritel opened a national network supervision centre on Thursday to monitor service performance, detect technical faults and coordinate repairs. The centre is designed to keep watch on the network continuously and supply data for improving its efficiency and extending coverage.

Mauritel said the facility would help improve the quality and continuity of telecommunications and internet services. Ahmed Salem Ould Bedda, the minister for digital transformation and public administration modernisation, attended the opening with Mauritel chief executive Mohamed Baba Ahmed and board chairman Mohamed Ould Habib Al-Rahman.

The company said it would continue investing in infrastructure and technical and operational capacity. The minister said his ministry was coordinating with relevant sectors to develop telecommunications services and improve performance.

Why it mattersMauritel’s network users rely on the company to detect disruptions and intervene, while the centre will also provide data for coverage expansion.
