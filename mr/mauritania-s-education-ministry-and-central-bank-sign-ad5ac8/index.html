Mauritania’s education ministry and central bank sign financial-literacy deal

Joint programmes will bring financial education to pupils at different school levels, while the minister says teachers will also benefit.

Source: Sahara Medias · 2026-11-27 · 1 min read

Mauritania’s Ministry of Education and Reform of the Education System and the Central Bank of Mauritania signed a partnership agreement to add financial literacy to national education programmes. Education Minister Houda Babah and central bank Governor Mohamed Lemine Ould Dhehbi signed for their institutions.

The official announcement says the two bodies plan to develop education and awareness programmes tailored to pupils’ ages and school levels. Babah said the initiative would also reach teachers, who she said make up more than 67% of state employees.

Babah said schools should help prepare students to make sound financial decisions and manage resources, as well as transmit knowledge.

Why it mattersTeachers, who Babah says make up more than 67% of state employees, are also intended to benefit from the planned programmes.
