Mauritanian military delegation visits Moroccan naval facilities

The visit to Casablanca aimed to strengthen military cooperation and exchange expertise in training.

Source: Sahara Medias · 2026-11-08 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

A Mauritanian military delegation visited Moroccan Royal Navy facilities in Casablanca from Monday to Thursday. The delegation, led by Colonel Mohand Baba Hamdi, commander of the Naval Academy, toured the Royal Naval School and the Royal Navy Training Center. This exchange is part of the implementation of the mixed military commission program between Mauritania and Morocco.

The Mauritanian army announced on Friday that the visit focused on enhancing military cooperation and sharing expertise in skills development. The program included presentations and site tours of several naval installations and specialized centers. Discussions and experience-sharing sessions were held between the two naval forces.

The reciprocal visit also saw a delegation from the Moroccan Royal Navy visit the Mauritanian Navy's staff headquarters during the same period. This initiative seeks to foster mutual learning and improve naval training capabilities.
