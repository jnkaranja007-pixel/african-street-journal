Tiris-Zemour governor orders gold miners out of 10-kilometre border zone

Idriss Demba Corera gave prospectors up to two weeks to leave sites inside Mauritania, citing border security and protection for miners.

Source: Sahara Medias · 2026-11-19 · 1 min read

Idriss Demba Corera, governor of Mauritania’s Tiris-Zemour region, has ordered gold prospectors at sites within 10 kilometres of the border to leave within two weeks. The order covers the strip inside Mauritanian territory.

The governor said the move would strengthen border security, let the armed forces carry out their protection duties and keep prospectors away from frontier areas, according to the state news agency. He issued the order during a visit to gold-mining areas, where he also urged miners to follow rules for artisanal mining and safety measures.

Prospectors had reported that about 15 shells fell west of exploration sites during an exchange of fire near the separation wall, with no casualties, according to their account to Sahara Medias. The report also said the Polisario Front targeted areas near the wall and the Moroccan army responded with bombardment outside it.

Why it mattersProspectors in the border strip must leave their mining sites within two weeks, while the armed forces are to carry out border-protection duties.
