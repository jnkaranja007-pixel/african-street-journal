Atar stages 5-kilometre cycling race for Independence anniversary

20 cyclists raced 5 kilometres in Atar on Friday, with the five leading finishers awarded prizes.

Source: Agence Mauritanienne d'Information · 2026-11-28 · 1 min read

A 5-kilometre cycling race started in Atar on Friday as part of events marking the 66th anniversary of Mauritania's Independence Day. Abdallahi Ould Mohamed Mahmoud, the governor of Adrar region, presided over the start.

20 cyclists took part, and the five leading finishers were crowned. Organisers also honoured the late cyclist Al-Maghfari Ould Salem Ould Bilal for his contribution to the sport. The race was organised by the Atar City Renaissance Association and Atar municipality, with the Mauritanian Olympic Committee and the national cycling federation.

The governor said Atar residents had long used bicycles in daily life and that the event would help preserve the city's local heritage. Mayor Ibrahim Ould Abdebbah said it was part of efforts to protect local culture in the region.

Why it mattersAtar's cycling heritage and local culture are being promoted through the race, which local officials say will help preserve the city's identity.
