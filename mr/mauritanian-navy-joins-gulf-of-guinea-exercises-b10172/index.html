Mauritanian navy joins Gulf of Guinea exercises

Naval forces from Mauritania participated in the annual 'Grand African NEMO 2026' drills from October 29 to November 6.

Source: Sahara Medias · 2026-11-10 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Mauritania's navy participated in regional naval exercises in the Gulf of Guinea during the annual 'Grand African NEMO 2026' drills. The exercise, which ran from October 29 to November 6, involved naval forces from multiple countries aiming to combat illegal immigration, drug trafficking, and piracy.

The Mauritanian army announced the participation via its official Facebook page, stating the navy deployed the vessel 'Timbédra' and a special forces team. The drills also included search and rescue, medical evacuation, and efforts against illegal fishing, with Mauritanian liaison officers present at regional maritime coordination centers.

The stated goals of Mauritania's participation were to enhance maritime military cooperation and coordination, share experiences, and develop the operational capabilities of its navy. These exercises are designed to improve maritime security in the region.

Why it mattersMauritania's navy participated in maritime security exercises to enhance its operational capabilities and cooperation with other nations.
