Mauritania presents civil service pension reform options to unions

Prime Minister Moktar Ould Diay said the study diagnosed the scheme and set out choices balancing financial sustainability with social fairness.

Source: Sahara Medias · 2026-11-22 · 1 min read

Mauritania’s Prime Minister Moktar Ould Diay presented findings from a study of civil servants’ pension reform to union leaders and representatives of independent unions on Friday. The meeting focused on the study’s proposed options for changing the scheme.

Ould Diay said the study had produced a full diagnosis of the pension system. He said its scenarios would allow officials to choose a formula balancing financial sustainability with social fairness. The prime minister’s office said participants received a detailed presentation of each option’s financial and administrative effects.

The report does not specify which option the government will choose or when a decision will be made.

Why it mattersCivil servants and their unions must weigh reform choices assessed for financial and administrative effects, with social fairness also cited as a criterion.
