Opposition party chief faults government over rising food prices

Mokhtar Ould Cheikh also condemned alleged privacy breaches and detainee-rights violations in a case involving 22 suspects sent to judicial investigation.

Source: Sahara Medias · 2026-11-26 · 1 min read

Mokhtar Ould Cheikh, head of Mauritania’s Union of Forces for Change party, accused the government of failing to contain rising prices for locally produced meat, dairy products and fish. He made the criticism at a party news conference in Nouakchott on Tuesday, saying the country was facing an unprecedented cost-of-living crisis.

Ould Cheikh said the government had not shifted from an economy reliant on raw-material exports to one that adds value to national resources, creates jobs and strengthens self-sufficiency. He also condemned what he called the disclosure of citizens’ personal data and violations of detainees’ rights in a case involving bloggers.

The public prosecutor sent 22 suspects for judicial investigation and sought pretrial detention for 15, on accusations including blackmail, money laundering and spreading false information. The prosecutor said the alleged acts threatened state security, national unity and civil peace.

Why it mattersConsumers buying locally produced meat, dairy and fish face higher prices, while the government is under pressure to present an effective and lasting response.
