Saudi eye mission offers 400 cataract operations in Atar

The four-day campaign also plans 4,000 free consultations and 950 pairs of glasses at the regional care centre.

Source: Sahara Medias · 2026-11-23 · 1 min read

A Saudi medical mission began on Saturday morning at Atar’s regional care centre in Mauritania, offering free eye treatment and surgery. The campaign plans 4,000 consultations, 400 cataract operations and the distribution of 950 pairs of glasses.

Mission coordinator Abou Obaida Mokhtar said the four-day effort was organised by the Noor Al-Saudia programme of the King Salman Humanitarian Aid and Relief Centre, with Mauritania’s health ministry national blindness-control programme. The 21-member team includes doctors and auxiliary medical staff, and will provide medicines and treatments to patients.

The team’s work also includes fitting intraocular lenses, treating glaucoma and consultations for other eye diseases, according to Mokhtar’s statement relayed by the official Mauritanian News Agency.

Why it mattersPatients attending the Atar centre can receive free consultations, medicines and treatments during the four-day mission, with 950 pairs of glasses planned for distribution.
