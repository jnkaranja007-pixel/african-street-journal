Lesotho to chair SADC security organ

The nation takes on leadership of the Southern African Development Community Organ on Politics, Defence and Security Cooperation.

Source: Lesotho Times · 2026-11-05 · 1 min read

Lesotho will lead the Southern African Development Community (SADC) Organ on Politics, Defence and Security Cooperation starting in 2025. Prime Minister Sam Matekane was elected to the position by SADC heads of state and government. This role signifies a major shift for Lesotho, which was under regional scrutiny for political instability for over a decade.

The country was removed from the Organ’s agenda in August 2025 after years of observation following events like an attempted military coup in 2014 and the assassinations of two army commanders in 2015 and 2017. Foreign Affairs Minister Limpho Tau called the appointment an honour for all Basotho.

This leadership role requires Lesotho to continue its reform efforts, including independent institutions and security agencies operating within the law. The country's constitutional and institutional reforms, though slow, must become a lived reality to credibly address regional challenges.

Why it mattersLesotho's election as chair of the SADC security organ means the country must continue its own reforms to address political and security challenges.
