Lesotho elected chair of SADC security organ

Prime Minister Sam Matekane took the helm of the regional body's Organ on Politics, Defence and Security Cooperation.

Source: Lesotho Times · 2026-10-28 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Lesotho has been appointed the incoming chair of the Southern African Development Community (SADC) Organ on Politics, Defence and Security Cooperation. Prime Minister Sam Matekane was elected to the position during a virtual summit yesterday, chaired by South African President Cyril Ramaphosa. This appointment follows a period where Lesotho was previously on the SADC agenda until 2025.

Foreign Affairs Minister Limpho Tau stated the appointment reflects regional confidence in Lesotho's political stability. Mr Matekane will serve on the Organ Troika alongside the current chairperson from Eswatini and the outgoing chairperson from Malawi. President Ramaphosa urged continued regional cooperation for peace and development.

The summit addressed ongoing instability in the eastern Democratic Republic of Congo and supported dialogue in Madagascar. Efforts to combat terrorism in Mozambique were also recognized. Minister Tau, speaking from Germany, highlighted this role as an honour for Basotho, demonstrating trust in the country's ability to help resolve regional security challenges.

Why it mattersLesotho's government will now lead regional efforts on politics, defence, and security cooperation.
