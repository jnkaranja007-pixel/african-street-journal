Lesotho elected chair of SADC security organ

Prime Minister Sam Matekane will lead the regional body's politics, defence, and security cooperation.

Source: Lesotho Times · 2026-10-24 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Lesotho has been elected the incoming chair of the Southern African Development Community’s Organ on Politics, Defence and Security Cooperation. Prime Minister Sam Matekane was elected to the position during a virtual summit on October 21, 2026, ending a deadlock with Angola. This appointment places Lesotho in a leadership role within the regional bloc's most influential security structure.

Foreign Affairs Minister Limpho Tau stated the appointment reflects the region's confidence in Lesotho's political stability, noting the country was removed from the SADC agenda in 2025 after years of monitoring its domestic instability. The Organ Troika will now include Lesotho, alongside Eswatini and Malawi.

The organ is responsible for supporting democratic governance, conflict prevention, and regional security cooperation. Lesotho's new role means it will directly engage in discussions on regional challenges such as the conflict in eastern Democratic Republic of Congo and violent extremism in northern Mozambique.

Why it mattersLesotho's election to lead the SADC Organ on Politics, Defence and Security Cooperation gives the country a direct role in addressing regional security challenges.
