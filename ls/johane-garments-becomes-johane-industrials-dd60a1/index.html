Johane Garments becomes Johane Industrials

Mosotho entrepreneur Tšepo Johane celebrated his factory's transition from SME to industrial operation in Ha Thetsane.

Source: Lesotho Times · 2026-11-08 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Johane Garments, a textile factory owned by Tšepo Johane, has officially become Johane Industrials, marking its transition from a small and medium-sized enterprise to a full-scale industrial operation. The factory, which began at a skills development centre, celebrated this milestone in Ha Thetsane after years of overcoming debt and compliance issues.

Trade, Industry and Business Development Minister Motlatsi Maqelepo attended the launch, calling it a testament to Mosotho entrepreneurial determination. He noted that Johane Industrials had faced significant challenges, including outstanding debt that nearly led to closure, but acknowledged Mr. Johane's resilience. The minister stated the government is committed to supporting business growth through funding and infrastructure.

Mr. Maqelepo emphasized manufacturing's central role in Lesotho's economic development, calling for greater respect for the sector and highlighting the need for homegrown industrialists. Mr. Johane expressed gratitude for the journey, which started at the Station skills development centre, and pledged to double his efforts for the enterprise's lasting success, focusing on local job creation and sustainability.

Why it mattersThe transition of Johane Garments to Johane Industrials signifies a step towards industrialisation and local economic development in Lesotho.
