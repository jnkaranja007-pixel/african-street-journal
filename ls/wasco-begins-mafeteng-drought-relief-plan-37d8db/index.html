WASCO begins Mafeteng drought relief plan

The water company will provide 29 tanks, drill boreholes and dredge Rasebale Dam.

Source: Sunday Express Lesotho · 2026-11-04 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

The Water and Sewage Company (WASCO) has started a drought response plan in Mafeteng to secure water supplies. The plan includes distributing 29 water tanks and drilling boreholes as emergency measures. A key part of the strategy is dredging Rasebale Dam to increase its storage capacity, which has been a problem since 2012.

WASCO Project manager Lehlohonolo Ncheke stated the tanks will supplement supplies when dam levels fall, while boreholes offer alternative sources if existing supplies run dry. Dredging Rasebale Dam, a first for the utility, presents a significant engineering challenge, according to CEO Lebohang Peke Makara. He noted that population growth has increased demand and that sand accumulation in December 2024 worsened the situation.

Natural Resources Minister Mohlomi Moleko confirmed the dredging project fulfills a 2024 commitment by Prime Minister Sam Matekane. Traditional leaders, however, warned that dredging alone is insufficient without addressing erosion in the dam's catchment area, according to Chief Fako Leshoro Seiso of Tšane Tala.

Why it mattersMafeteng communities face water shortages due to drought and dam capacity issues.
