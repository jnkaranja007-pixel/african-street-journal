Lesotho communications authority trains local cybersecurity professionals

The three-day programme, run through the authority’s interim incident-response team, aims to reduce reliance on international expertise.

Source: Public Eye News · 2026-11-20 · 1 min read

Lesotho’s communications regulator ran a three-day cybersecurity capacity-building programme in Maseru through its Interim National Computer Security Incident Response Team. The training brought together Basotho professionals to develop local expertise in protecting digital systems.

LCA Chief Executive Nizam Goolam said sectors increasingly rely on digital services and Lesotho needs sustainable local skills rather than depending mainly on international experts for cybersecurity solutions and training. The authority said the programme combined training with knowledge-sharing among participants.

Cybersecurity Capacity Centre for Southern Africa Research Director Tendani Chimboza called for practical action, while Goolam said he hoped the centre would continue supporting the authority’s future work.

Why it mattersBasotho professionals are being trained to meet cybersecurity needs as more sectors rely on digital services.
