Khutlisi elected Maseru Mayor

Councillor ’Malerato Khutlisi defeated her predecessor Thabo Sefuthi in a vote at the Maseru City Council Chambers.

Source: Sunday Express Lesotho · 2026-11-17 · 1 min read

’Malerato Khutlisi, a councillor for the Revolution for Prosperity (RFP) party, has been elected the new Mayor of Maseru. She secured 11 votes against her predecessor Thabo Sefuthi’s eight in Friday’s election held at the Maseru City Council (MCC) Chambers. Khutlisi, who won her council seat in 2023, becomes the third woman to lead the capital’s council since September 2020.

Mahlohola Phalatsi, the councillor for Majoe-a-li-tšene, was elected deputy mayor unopposed. Following the election, Ha Leqele Chief Tšosane Mputlane appealed to councillors to unite and focus on improving service delivery. He urged them to put public interests above personal gain.

The new mayor acknowledged the significant responsibilities of her position and expressed readiness to tackle the challenges ahead. Deputy Mayor Phalatsi pledged his full support to Khutlisi and encouraged councillors to move past previous disagreements. Both leaders emphasized the importance of working together for the benefit of the people.
