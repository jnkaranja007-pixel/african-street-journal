Lesotho launches Tebellong bridge and road project

The government has unveiled Unik Construction to build a bridge over the Senqu River and tar eight kilometres of road.

Source: Sunday Express Lesotho · 2026-10-28 · 2 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

The Lesotho government has launched a major infrastructure project in Qacha’s Nek to build a bridge across the Senqu River at Tebellong and tar eight kilometres of road linking Sehlabathebe to Thamathu. The Tebellong bridge project is expected to take 24 months to complete, addressing long-standing safety concerns where the river has claimed lives. The road project will be completed over an 18-month construction period.

Roads Directorate Director General Linehella Ntšere stated that the bridge project marks an important step in addressing community concerns, introducing Unik Construction as the appointed contractor with Zutari as the supervising consultant. Minister of Public Works and Transport Matjato Moteane expressed delight that the project is finally underway after years of promises, noting the government has invested more than M3 billion in Qacha’s Nek over the past four years. Recruitment information for the project is expected to be published next week, according to Principal Secretary Tšepang Koele.

Minister of Tourism, Sports, Arts and Culture Stephen Mputi welcomed the development, stating the bridge will improve access and create opportunities for tourism and local attractions. Area Chief Makhaola Makhaola conveyed the community's happiness and gratitude for the development, which he said comes after a long wait for assistance.

Why it mattersResidents of Tebellong and surrounding areas will experience safer travel and improved access to services and tourism opportunities.
