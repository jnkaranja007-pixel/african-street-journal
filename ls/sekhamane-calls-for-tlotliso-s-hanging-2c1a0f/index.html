Sekhamane calls for Tlotliso's hanging

The Speaker of Lesotho's National Assembly spoke at the funeral of two women murdered in their home.

Source: Sunday Express Lesotho · 2026-11-16 · 1 min read

Tlohang Sekhamane, Speaker of the National Assembly, called for the death penalty for Tlotliso Mohapeloa, 23, accused of murdering his aunt Hlompho Matsoso and her daughter Liteboho. Speaking at the funeral at Thetsane West, Mr Sekhamane said Lesotho must not show mercy to people who prey on vulnerable members of society.

Mr Sekhamane described the violence as particularly disturbing because it was allegedly inflicted by someone raised within the same family. He questioned whether the women's cries for mercy had moved the suspect at all, and said Basotho had not repealed the law on hanging because people like Tlotliso exist.

The two women were fatally attacked on 3 November 2026, shortly after Hlompho had retired from public service. The suspect is said to have targeted her pension, which he believed was kept as cash in the house. Mr Sekhamane urged Basotho to unite against murder and violence.

Why it mattersThe Speaker of the National Assembly has called for the death penalty for a man accused of murder, stating Lesotho's law on hanging people exists for such cases.
