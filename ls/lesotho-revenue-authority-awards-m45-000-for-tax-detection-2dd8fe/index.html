Lesotho revenue authority awards M45,000 for tax-detection idea

Team Dynamic 3.1 won the multinational-tax challenge with a system designed to detect schemes companies use to avoid paying tax in Lesotho.

Source: Sunday Express Lesotho · 2026-11-24 · 1 min read

Revenue Services Lesotho (RSL) awarded M45,000 to Team Dynamic 3.1 after the team won the multinational-tax challenge at a two-day Taxathon held at Thaba-Bosiu Cultural Village on 19 and 20 November. The competition asked local innovators to develop ways to improve informal-sector tax compliance and detect multinational tax evasion, abusive tax planning and refund risks.

Team representative Katleho Nko said the winning system drew on research into previously identified tax-avoidance schemes and was designed to detect other potential schemes. RSL Commissioner of Operations Support Mathlonolofatso Mosaase said the authority wanted to hear ideas from outside its own structures. Bana Ba Monna placed second and received M30,000; TaxGuard Innovators came third and received M15,000.

In the informal-sector challenge, Khoebo won, followed by Headway and Code Kolective. RSL Acting Commissioner General’s representative, Manneheng Mopeli, said the authority wanted solutions that addressed taxpayers’ needs.

Why it mattersThe two winning teams’ ideas address informal-sector compliance and multinational tax avoidance, the tax-administration problems RSL asked them to tackle.
