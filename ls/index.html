China reaffirms ties and multilateralism with Lesotho

Ambassador Yang Xiaokun urged local media to respect China's sovereignty claims over Taiwan.

Chinese Ambassador to Lesotho, Yang Xiaokun, met with local media in Maseru this week to reaffirm China's commitment to multilateralism and strengthening relations with Lesotho. The meeting marked the 55th anniversary of the restoration of the People’s Republic of China’s (PRC) seat at the United Nations (UN) and Commemoration Day of Taiwan’s Restoration.

Ambassador Yang stated that history shapes international relations and offers lessons for safeguarding peace and nations' legitimate rights. He recalled that the UN General Assembly adopted Resolution 2758 on 25 October 1971, which restored the PRC's rights at the UN and recognised its representatives as the sole legitimate representatives of China.

The ambassador also highlighted China's contributions to international development and peace efforts, including its Global Development Initiative which has mobilised over US$23 billion. China has implemented zero-tariff treatment for 53 African countries, a measure from which Lesotho is expected to benefit.

Why it mattersLesotho is expected to benefit from China's implementation of zero-tariff treatment for 53 African countries with which it maintains diplomatic relations.

Econet raises M971 000 for education fund

The annual Econet Golf Day and Expo gala dinner secured funds for vulnerable students in Lesotho.

Econet Telecom Lesotho (ETL) raised M971 000 for its HigherLife Foundation Lesotho through its annual golf day and gala dinner on Friday at Maseru Mall. The funds were generated from pledges and a matching contribution from ETL. The event also marked the conclusion of the fourth Econet Expo, themed “Think AI, Think Econet”.

ETL Chief Executive Officer Dennis Plaatjies stated that the golf day, established in 2018, supports the foundation's mission to uplift young Basotho through education. The foundation currently assists 141 tertiary students and 114 secondary students. Plaatjies explained that ETL allocates two percent of its annual revenue, approximately M13 million to M15 million, to the foundation, but additional fundraising is necessary due to growing needs.

A total of 84 golfers participated in the tournament. The winning team, comprising Tlotliso Khabo, Tsebo Lerotholi, Matšela Matšela, and Moeketsi Mafereka, finished with 58 points. The event's synergy with the ETL Expo allowed international partners to engage in business and personal networking.

Why it mattersThe HigherLife Foundation Lesotho uses the funds to support vulnerable children with their educational needs.

Lesotho cricket team ends Nigeria campaign with defeat

The national side lost to Gambia by three runs, finishing fourth in the tournament standings.

Lesotho's campaign in the Africa Cricket Association regional qualifying tournament in Abuja concluded with a narrow three-run defeat against Gambia. The national team was bowled out for 109 in 19.5 overs while chasing Gambia's total of 112 for nine.

This result placed Lesotho fourth in the final standings, securing one win from five matches and accumulating two points. According to the Lesotho Cricket Association (LCA), Nigeria and Uganda advanced to the Africa Regional Final.

Coach Dzikamai Mavhiko highlighted the team's limitations, including the absence of a dedicated cricket ground and the challenge of competing against full-time players, stating that sustained investment in facilities and player development is necessary.

Why it mattersLesotho's national cricket team finished fourth in a regional tournament after losing their final match.

Lesotho government allocates M50 million for Royal Palace

The Senate's Committee on Government Assurances reports on the stalled M355 million palace project and overpayments.

The Lesotho government has allocated M50 million to restart construction of the new Royal Palace in Maseru. This project, initiated in 2011, has faced delays and cost overruns, now standing at M355 million.

A Senate Committee on Government Assurances report, based on the Auditor-General’s May 2025 audit, revealed overpayments exceeding M74 million to consultants and contractors. The Ministry of Public Works and Transport has developed a new procurement strategy to prevent future issues.

The ministry is preparing to resume construction soon, assuring the committee that corrective measures are in place. The initial project cost was M136.7 million, with completion expected in May 2013.

Why it mattersLesotho taxpayers will fund the resumption of the Royal Palace construction, which has already incurred significant costs and overpayments.

Lesotho names four players for AUSC Region 5 Games

Phuthi Leshoele, Lindiwe Nkanda, Thabo Chimombe, and Kamohelo Mathibela will compete in lawn tennis in Maputo, Mozambique.

The Lesotho Lawn Tennis Association (LLTA) has selected a four-member team for the upcoming African Union Sports Council (AUSC) Region 5 Games in Maputo, Mozambique, from December 4 to 13.

Phuthi Leshoele and Lindiwe Nkanda will play in the women's category, while Thabo Chimombe and Kamohelo Mathibela will compete in the men's category, coached by Bishop Mosebi and ‘Mankopane Nkuatsana. LLTA Public Relations Officer Mathealira Chaka confirmed the team's selection and ongoing preparations.

Lesotho will participate in only eight sporting codes due to financial constraints, missing disciplines like basketball and swimming, though visually impaired and Special Olympics athletes will compete in athletics.

Why it mattersFinancial constraints limit Lesotho's participation to eight sporting codes at the AUSC Region 5 Games.
