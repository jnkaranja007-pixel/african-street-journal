Lesotho court orders revenue authority to reinstate fired commissioner

The Court of Appeal has ordered the Revenue Services Lesotho board to reinstate its Commissioner-General, Adv 'Mathabo Mokoko, or face imprisonment for contempt.

The Court of Appeal ruled on Friday that the Revenue Services Lesotho (RSL) board must reinstate Advocate 'Mathabo Mokoko as Commissioner-General, or its members risk jail for contempt of court. The court upheld her appeal with costs and restored a Labour Court judgment in her favour. Mokoko had been dismissed after the board accused her of incompetence, including failing to resolve mounting tax refund claims.

Court of Appeal President Justice Kananelo Mosito announced the order, saying the full judgment would be made available later. Speaking to the Sunday Express, Mokoko said the order was simple: the board is in contempt and must purge it by putting her back to work. She became emotional as she spoke, describing the months since her dismissal as extremely difficult.

Mokoko said her commitment remained to serve the nation with integrity, professionalism and dignity, and that she had never failed the country. The board must now decide whether to comply with the order or face the consequences of contempt.

Why it mattersTax refund backlogs and the authority's leadership are now in the hands of a board that must either reinstate the commissioner or face jail.

Lesotho prisons hold twice their capacity as court delays build up

The Lesotho Correctional Service says inmate numbers have doubled facility capacity, with more than 100 new prisoners admitted in one week.

The Lesotho Correctional Service (LCS) says the number of inmates in its facilities is now twice their holding capacity, with more than 100 new prisoners admitted in the past week. LCS spokesperson Assistant Superintendent Pheko Ntobane said the service usually receives between 20 and 30 inmates a day.

Ntobane and others point to cases that repeatedly fail to proceed in the Magistrates Courts, leaving accused people in custody for long periods while trials are postponed. Police have in some instances brought cases to court before investigations were complete. A source told the Sunday Express that some cases were allegedly being stalled until the detention period under the Speedy Court Trials Act No. 9 of 2002 expires.

Under Section 4 of that Act, remand beyond 60 days requires compelling reasons, and the prosecution must justify continued detention. The allegations come amid claims of corruption in the magistracy, including missing court files and the unlawful release of suspects, which the Directorate on Corruption and Economic Offences and Chief Justice Sakoane Sakoane publicly acknowledged in July.

Why it mattersAccused people held on remand face longer custody while their trials are postponed, and the prison system is operating at double its capacity.

Khutlisi elected Maseru mayor after closely contested vote

Councillor 'Malerato Khutlisi won 11 votes to eight against former mayor Thabo Sefuthi at the Maseru City Council chambers.

Councillor 'Malerato Khutlisi has been elected the new mayor of Maseru, defeating her predecessor, Thabo Sefuthi, in a closely contested election on Friday. Ms Khutlisi, who won the Thame Council under the Revolution for Prosperity (RFP) banner in 2023, secured 11 votes against Mr Sefuthi's eight at the Maseru City Council (MCC) chambers.

Mr Sefuthi had been elected mayor on 4 August 2026 after snap elections that followed a motion of no confidence against Relebohile Sechache, whom councillors accused of abusing office resources. Majoe-a-li-tšene councillor Mahlomola Phalatsi was elected deputy mayor unopposed, and Ms Khutlisi becomes the third woman to lead the capital's council since September 2020.

Chief Tšosane Mputlane, a councillor, urged members to put aside their differences and restore unity and dignity at the council. Mr Phalatsi pledged to support the new mayor, and Ms Khutlisi said she had accepted the responsibility and would listen to advice.

Why it mattersCouncillors and MCC staff now work under a new mayor who won by three votes, and the council must restore confidence after the no-confidence vote that removed Mr Sechache.

Politics · 2026-11-16

Sekhamane calls for Tlotliso's hanging

The Speaker of Lesotho's National Assembly spoke at the funeral of two women murdered in their home.

Tlohang Sekhamane, Speaker of the National Assembly, called for the death penalty for Tlotliso Mohapeloa, 23, accused of murdering his aunt Hlompho Matsoso and her daughter Liteboho. Speaking at the funeral at Thetsane West, Mr Sekhamane said Lesotho must not show mercy to people who prey on vulnerable members of society.

Mr Sekhamane described the violence as particularly disturbing because it was allegedly inflicted by someone raised within the same family. He questioned whether the women's cries for mercy had moved the suspect at all, and said Basotho had not repealed the law on hanging because people like Tlotliso exist.

The two women were fatally attacked on 3 November 2026, shortly after Hlompho had retired from public service. The suspect is said to have targeted her pension, which he believed was kept as cash in the house. Mr Sekhamane urged Basotho to unite against murder and violence.

Why it mattersThe Speaker of the National Assembly has called for the death penalty for a man accused of murder, stating Lesotho's law on hanging people exists for such cases.

Sport · 2026-11-16

Molapo wins November Golf Corporate League tournament

Mathaleha Molapo secured first place on countback with 39 points in the Maseru Golf Club Corporate League tournament.

Mathaleha Molapo won the Maseru Golf Club Corporate League November tournament on Friday, scoring 39 points. She finished tied with Mphaphathi Moshoeshoe, who also scored 39 points, but Molapo won on countback because her handicap of 22 was lower than Moshoeshoe's 28. Setšabe Lefosa came third with 37 points.

The tournament was sponsored by Momentum Health Lesotho, which has recently transitioned from Metropolitan Health Lesotho. Finance Manager Semethe Lephosa described the event as a success and pledged more sponsorships in the future. He said it gave the company an opportunity to showcase its new identity.

Momentum Health Lesotho provides healthcare and insurance solutions in Lesotho and South Africa. Lephosa encouraged the public to take out insurance to safeguard their lives.

Why it mattersGolfer Mathaleha Molapo won the November tournament with 39 points, sponsored by Momentum Health Lesotho.
