China reaffirms ties and multilateralism with Lesotho

Ambassador Yang Xiaokun urged local media to respect China's sovereignty claims over Taiwan.

Source: Lesotho Times · 2026-11-04 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Chinese Ambassador to Lesotho, Yang Xiaokun, met with local media in Maseru this week to reaffirm China's commitment to multilateralism and strengthening relations with Lesotho. The meeting marked the 55th anniversary of the restoration of the People’s Republic of China’s (PRC) seat at the United Nations (UN) and Commemoration Day of Taiwan’s Restoration.

Ambassador Yang stated that history shapes international relations and offers lessons for safeguarding peace and nations' legitimate rights. He recalled that the UN General Assembly adopted Resolution 2758 on 25 October 1971, which restored the PRC's rights at the UN and recognised its representatives as the sole legitimate representatives of China.

The ambassador also highlighted China's contributions to international development and peace efforts, including its Global Development Initiative which has mobilised over US$23 billion. China has implemented zero-tariff treatment for 53 African countries, a measure from which Lesotho is expected to benefit.

Why it mattersLesotho is expected to benefit from China's implementation of zero-tariff treatment for 53 African countries with which it maintains diplomatic relations.
