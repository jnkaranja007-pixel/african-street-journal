Leribe man to serve one year for culpable homicide

A High Court in Lesotho sentenced a man to one year in prison for the 2020 fatal shooting of a livestock herder, ordering him to compensate the victim's family.

Source: Sunday Express Lesotho · 2026-10-22 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Kabelo Masoabi KOAKOA Sefale has been sentenced to one year in prison for the culpable homicide of livestock herder Fihlang Nkopane. The High Court at Tšifa-li-Mali handed down a 15-year sentence, with 14 years suspended, for the 2020 killing. Sefale must also pay Nkopane's family M104,000 within a year of his release and provide two cows within one month.

Nkopane, 24, was shot dead at a cattle post in Ha Nkopa, Leribe, on Christmas Day in 2020. The Crown argued that the use of a firearm and the nature of the two wounds to the deceased's back demonstrated a serious disregard for human life and a high degree of cruelty. The defence highlighted Sefale's youth, his role as a sole breadwinner, and his guilty plea as mitigating factors.

The court considered that Sefale was 22 at the time of the offence and is the sole provider for his unemployed mother. His defence lawyer argued that his guilty plea showed remorse and that he was willing to pay compensation, offering 10 cattle. The court ordered monthly payments of M8,600 towards the M104,000 compensation after Sefale's release.

Why it mattersA Leribe man will serve one year in prison and pay compensation for the fatal shooting of a livestock herder, impacting his family and the victim's family.
