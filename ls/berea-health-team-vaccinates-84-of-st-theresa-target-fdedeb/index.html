Berea health team vaccinates 84% of St Theresa target children

The week-long measles drive followed four confirmed cases and included contact tracing for children aged nine months to 15 years.

Source: Sunday Express Lesotho · 2026-11-26 · 1 min read

Health workers in Berea vaccinated 84% of the target children in the St Theresa catchment during a campaign run from Monday to Friday. The District Health Management Team (DHMT) began the drive after four confirmed measles cases prompted a public-health alert.

District Child Health Officer Malehano Ramatutu said some parents refused vaccination. Children with possible measles symptoms were not vaccinated and remain under close monitoring. The DHMT did not publish the target population’s total, so the 84% is not a district-wide coverage rate.

The latest outbreak figures cited by the DHMT were dated 16 November and pre-date the campaign result: they recorded 19 confirmed cases in Berea, 345 in Leribe and 69 in Botha-Bothe. At that point, 395 people had recovered, with no deaths or hospital admissions recorded.

Why it mattersParents of eligible children in St Theresa’s catchment must weigh vaccination while health workers monitor symptomatic children and some families have refused doses.
