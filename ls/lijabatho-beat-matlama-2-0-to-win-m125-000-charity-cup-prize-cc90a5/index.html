Lijabatho beat Matlama 2-0 to win M125 000 Charity Cup prize

Tšepang Qhena and Bokang Sello scored in the final at Setsoto Stadium, where Maluti Mountain Cement sponsored the tournament with M800 000.

Source: Sunday Express Lesotho · 2026-11-23 · 1 min read

Lijabatho beat Matlama 2-0 in the final at Setsoto Stadium to win the 2026 Maluti Mountain Cement Charity Cup and its M125 000 prize. Tšepang Qhena and Bokang Sello scored for the Morija-based club.

Sponsor Maluti Mountain Cement put M800 000 into the tournament. Runners-up Matlama received M75 000, while semi-finalists Bantu and Lifofane each took M50 000. Lijabatho had reached the final with a 3-0 semi-final win over Lifofane; Matlama advanced after a 3-2 penalty shootout against Bantu, following a 2-2 draw.

Lijabatho coach Mark Harrison said the cup was secondary to the club’s aim of winning the league, and that he used the competition to give most players game time. Matlama coach Thabile Secker and Lijabatho assistant Kabelo Malapane were sent off after a near-fight early in the second half.

Why it mattersLijabatho’s players and the three other prize-winning clubs received tournament payouts ranging from M50 000 to M125 000.
