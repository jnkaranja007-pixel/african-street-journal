Harrison targets CAF group stage for Lijabatho

New coach Mark Harrison aims to make Lijabatho the first Lesotho club to reach the Confederation of African Football Champions League group stages.

Source: Lesotho Times · 2026-11-08 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Lijabatho's new head coach, Mark Harrison, has set winning the league title and advancing to the CAF Champions League group stages as his primary goals. Harrison has guided the team to two wins in his first two matches since taking over from Kabelo Malapane. Lijabatho currently leads the league standings with 23 points after defeating LCS 3-0 on Sunday.

Harrison stated his immediate aim is to defend the league title and secure a return to continental competition, with a specific ambition to reach the CAF Champions League group stages. This achievement would make the club the first Lesotho side to reach this stage. Lijabatho previously exited the competition in the preliminary round after a loss and a draw against CFFA of Madagascar.

The coach expressed satisfaction with the squad's progress and his intention to implement a passing and possession-based style of play. Harrison noted that while Lesotho has football talent, infrastructure, particularly playing surfaces, presents a challenge, a common issue across Africa. He believes the team is currently at 70-75 percent of its potential.

Why it mattersLesotho football fans will be affected by the potential historic achievement of their club reaching the CAF Champions League group stages.
