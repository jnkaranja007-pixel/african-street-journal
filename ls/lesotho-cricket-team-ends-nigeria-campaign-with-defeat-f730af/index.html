Lesotho cricket team ends Nigeria campaign with defeat

The national side lost to Gambia by three runs, finishing fourth in the tournament standings.

Source: Lesotho Times · 2026-11-01 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Lesotho's campaign in the Africa Cricket Association regional qualifying tournament in Abuja concluded with a narrow three-run defeat against Gambia. The national team was bowled out for 109 in 19.5 overs while chasing Gambia's total of 112 for nine.

This result placed Lesotho fourth in the final standings, securing one win from five matches and accumulating two points. According to the Lesotho Cricket Association (LCA), Nigeria and Uganda advanced to the Africa Regional Final.

Coach Dzikamai Mavhiko highlighted the team's limitations, including the absence of a dedicated cricket ground and the challenge of competing against full-time players, stating that sustained investment in facilities and player development is necessary.
