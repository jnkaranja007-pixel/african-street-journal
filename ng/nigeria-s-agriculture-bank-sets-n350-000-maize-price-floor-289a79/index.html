Nigeria's agriculture bank sets N350,000 maize price floor

The Bank of Agriculture says its N200 billion intervention is part of a wider programme that has mobilised nearly N500 billion for farmers.

Source: AgroNigeria · 2026-11-24 · 1 min read

Farmers hit by steep falls in crop prices are set to benefit from a N200 billion intervention by the Bank of Agriculture (BOA), its managing director, Ayodeji Sontinrin, said at the 2026 National Growth and Development Forum in Abuja on Thursday. Maize has fallen from about N680,000 to N140,000 per tonne, and the bank's price support is meant to establish a floor of N350,000 per tonne.

Sontinrin said the bank has mobilised nearly N500 billion to support more than 500,000 smallholder farmers across 16 states, including over 70,000 women reached by its N250 billion Smallholder Farm Support facility. The facility has distributed over 2.5 million bags of fertiliser and 300,000 bags of certified seeds, which he said could lift grain yields from about one tonne to five tonnes per hectare under suitable conditions.

Farmers are also being offered 2,000 tractors at a 40% discount, along with solar-powered irrigation pumps and electric cargo tricycles. The bank is further backing dry-season rice and wheat production and targeted financing for women and young people.

Why it mattersMaize growers who saw prices drop to N140,000 per tonne will be paid against a N350,000 floor, and the bank's facility has already reached over 500,000 farmers.
