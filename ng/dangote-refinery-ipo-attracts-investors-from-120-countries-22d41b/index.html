Dangote Refinery IPO attracts investors from 120 countries

The Lagos-based refinery's initial public offering exceeded its $1.6 billion target, drawing participation from all 54 African nations.

Source: Vanguard · 2026-10-28 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Dangote Petroleum Refinery, based in Lagos, has concluded its initial public share offering, reporting investment from 120 countries. The offering, initially set to close on October 13, was extended by two weeks to encourage African participation. While the total funds raised were not disclosed, Aliko Dangote stated the target of $1.6 billion was surpassed by "more than three, four times".

Aliko Dangote described the IPO as a "historic achievement for Africa," demonstrating continental self-sufficiency in financing and industrial development. Investors from all 54 African countries and five other continents participated, with Dangote noting the extraordinary international interest and confidence in the refinery's future. The company had previously secured $2.5 billion in private investment in July.

The refinery, with a current capacity of 700,000 barrels per day, aims to double its output to 1.4 million barrels per day, potentially becoming the world's largest. Africa currently imports over 70 percent of its refined fuel, according to an Africa Finance Corporation report.
