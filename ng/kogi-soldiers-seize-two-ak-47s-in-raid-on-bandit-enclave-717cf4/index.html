Kogi soldiers seize two AK-47s in raid on bandit enclave

The army says its intelligence-led operation in Okehi was aimed at kidnap gangs behind recent abductions along the Kabba–Okene Road.

Sources: Vanguard and Premium Times · 2026-11-22 · 1 min read

Troops of the Nigerian Army have driven suspected armed bandits out of a hideout in Okehi Local Government Area of Kogi State, seizing two AK-47 rifles, three magazines and 65 rounds of ammunition. The operation took place on Saturday, November 21, 2026.

Acting Director of Army Public Relations Colonel Appolonia Anele said Patrol Base Kabba launched the raid with other security personnel. In the Osara, Gada and Ohakiti forest areas, soldiers met about six armed men at an abandoned settlement and forced them to flee, the army said. Bags, torchlights, food and a power bank believed to belong to the fleeing men were also recovered.

The army said the raid aimed to dismantle hideouts tied to recent kidnappings on the Kabba–Okene Road and neighbouring communities. It urged the public to keep passing timely and credible information to security agencies.

Why it mattersTravellers and villagers along the Kabba–Okene Road face continued kidnap risk until the hideouts used by the gangs are cleared.
