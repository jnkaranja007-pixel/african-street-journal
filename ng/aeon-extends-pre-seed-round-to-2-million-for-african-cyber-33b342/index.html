Aeon extends pre-seed round to $2 million for African cyber defence

The Nigerian cybersecurity company added investment from Launch Africa Ventures and Octerra Capital after raising $1 million in September.

Source: Nairametrics · 2026-11-25 · 1 min read

Aeon, a Nigerian cybersecurity company, has extended its pre-seed funding round to $2 million as it builds cyber defence infrastructure for Africa and the Global South. The extension brings in new investment from Launch Africa Ventures and Octerra Capital.

The additional funding follows Aeon’s $1 million pre-seed round in September 2026, which was led by Terra Industries. The company is developing what it describes as sovereign cybersecurity infrastructure for the region and the Global South.

The new investors join Terra Industries in the round. The report does not give the size of their investments or a deployment schedule for the expanded funding.

Why it mattersAeon’s expanded funding is for building sovereign cyber defence infrastructure across Africa and the Global South.
