Mbah proposes renaming ESUT after founder Jim Nwobodo

The Enugu governor says he will begin consultations and pursue the legal process needed to change the university's name.

Sources: Vanguard and Premium Times · 2026-11-20 · 1 min read

Enugu State Governor Peter Mbah has proposed renaming Enugu State University of Science and Technology (ESUT) after Jim Nwobodo, who founded the institution in 1980. Mbah announced the plan on Thursday at a university lecture in Enugu, saying it would require stakeholder engagement and legislative approval.

Mbah said the proposal came first from ESUT’s alumni association and that he supported it. He said he intended to begin consultations and work with the institutions concerned to advance the change through the legal process, because ESUT was established by law.

Nwobodo, a former governor of old Anambra State, delivered the lecture. Mbah said the university began as Anambra State University of Technology in July 1980 and that generations of young people had gained educational opportunities through it.

Why it mattersESUT alumni and others affected by the university's name must take part in consultations before the proposed change goes through legislative steps.
