Nigeria opposition candidate pledges N200 petrol and free healthcare in 2027

Adewole Adebayo, candidate of the Social Democratic Party (SDP), says his government would cut the price without subsidy and provide free medical consultations and medication.

Sources: Vanguard and The Punch · 2026-11-28 · 1 min read

Adewole Adebayo, presidential candidate of the Social Democratic Party (SDP), has promised to sell petrol for N200 per litre without subsidy and provide free healthcare if elected president in 2027. He made the pledges at a party town hall meeting in Nasarawa State.

Adebayo said improved efficiency and action against corruption in the petroleum sector would make the fuel price possible. His health proposals include a Federal Medical Centre in every federal constituency, compulsory check-ups four times a year, and free consultations and medication; he gave no details of how these services would be funded.

He also promised free, compulsory education, more food production and jobs, including for people with disabilities. Adebayo urged voters to judge candidates by their records, character and policies in the 2027 election.

Why it mattersPetrol buyers and patients would receive the promised lower fuel price and free medical care only under an Adebayo-led government after the 2027 election.
