Tinubu points to waivers for 75 drug makers as he builds health economy

Between January 2025 and June 2026, more than ₦24 billion in tax and tariff relief was approved for the manufacturers.

Source: Nairametrics · 2026-11-28 · 1 min read

Across January 2025 and June 2026, more than ₦24 billion in tax and tariff waivers were approved for 75 manufacturers, President Bola Ahmed Tinubu said. He framed the sums as progress in building a healthcare economy.

10 factories making priority health products are under way, Dr Oluwaseun Abiola reported in an account published by Nairametrics. The account treats buying medicines as a single market.

The piece does not say how that joint purchasing would operate or when it would start.

Why it mattersThe 75 manufacturers received more than ₦24 billion in tax and tariff waivers, and 10 factories for priority health products are under way.
