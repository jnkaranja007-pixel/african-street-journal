Dangote lifts daily petrol supply to 31.89 million litres as imports climb

Nigeria brought in 15 million litres of petrol a day in October, even as the country's largest refinery raised its own output by 12%.

Sources: The Punch and Nairametrics · 2026-11-21 · 1 min read

In October, Dangote Refinery pushed its daily petrol supply to Nigeria up 12%, to 31.89 million litres, according to Nairametrics. Over the same month, the country's petrol imports grew 47%, reaching 15 million litres a day.

Both flows were moving into the market at the same time, which means local output and foreign-sourced fuel were supplying Nigerians together through October. The report measures daily volumes only and does not name the importers behind the rise.

The figures cover October 2026 alone, and the report offers no comparison with earlier months or any outlook for the coming period.

Why it mattersPetrol buyers now draw on two sources at once, with imports adding 15 million litres a day beside the refinery's 31.89 million.
