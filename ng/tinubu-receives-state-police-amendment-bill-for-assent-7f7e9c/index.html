Tinubu receives state police amendment bill for assent

The Justice Ministry is validating operational guidelines, which a presidential working group will review before presenting them to the president.

Sources: Vanguard, Premium Times and The Punch · 2026-11-19 · 1 min read

Nigeria’s National Assembly transmitted a constitutional amendment bill establishing state police to President Bola Tinubu for assent on Wednesday, after approval by all 36 state Houses of Assembly. The Senate also asked Tinubu to send lawmakers a National Policing Bill setting out how state police would operate.

Femi Gbajabiamila, the president’s chief of staff, said the operational guidelines bill had gone to the Federal Ministry of Justice for validation and legislative drafting. After receiving the validated version, a presidential working group chaired by Gbajabiamila will review it and submit it with a report to Tinubu for consideration.

The Senate directed National Assembly Clerk Kamoru Ogunlana to transmit the constitutional amendment under the Acts Authentication Act, 2004. The amendment is the Sixth Alteration Bill, 2026, sponsored by Senate Leader Opeyemi Bamidele.

Why it mattersThe presidential working group must review the validated guidelines and submit its report to Tinubu for consideration.
