Nigeria's headline inflation falls to 14.06% in October

Food inflation also eased to 19.13 percent, though prices rose 0.43 percent month on month.

Sources: Vanguard and The Punch · 2026-11-17 · 1 min read

The National Bureau of Statistics said Nigeria's headline inflation rate dropped to 14.06 percent in October 2026, down from 14.63 percent in September. The fall marks the fifth consecutive monthly decline, according to The Punch.

Food inflation also slowed, to 19.13 percent year on year from 19.59 percent in September, the bureau said in its latest Consumer Price Index report. On a month-on-month basis, however, headline inflation rose 0.43 percent in October, up from 0.06 percent the previous month, meaning average prices climbed faster than in September.

Year on year, food inflation was highest in Ondo at 31.74 percent and Lagos at 31.43 percent, and lowest in Nasarawa at 3.7 percent. Month on month, food prices rose fastest in Yobe, at 18.41 percent, and fell most in Lagos, by 5.93 percent. Items driving the monthly movement included vegetable oil, sardines, rice and catfish.

Why it mattersHouseholds buying staples such as rice, cooking oil and fish face a month-on-month price rise even as the annual rate eases.
