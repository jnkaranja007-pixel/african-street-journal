Lagos driver kills okada rider fleeing police

A driver in Lagos killed a motorcycle taxi rider and injured two others while evading police.

Sources: The Punch and Premium Times · 2026-10-28 · 1 min read

A driver in Lagos, Nigeria, killed a motorcycle taxi rider and injured two other people on October 28, 2026, while attempting to flee from the police.

The incident occurred as the driver was being pursued by law enforcement officers. The identities of the injured individuals and the specific circumstances leading to the police pursuit were not immediately detailed.

Separately, in Anambra State, an intercommunal clash between Amaeze Aguleri and Umuoba Anam in Anambra East Local Government Area resulted in five deaths and the destruction of homes in early October.
