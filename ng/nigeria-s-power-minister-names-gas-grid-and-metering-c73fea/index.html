Nigeria's power minister names gas, grid and metering failures

Joseph Tegbe told senior editors in Lagos that gas supply, ageing equipment, vandalism and poor metering are holding back the electricity sector.

Source: Nairametrics · 2026-11-16 · 1 min read

Nigeria's Minister of Power, Joseph Tegbe, has named gas supply constraints, ageing infrastructure, vandalism, transmission limits, poor metering and commercial problems as systemic faults in the country's electricity sector. Speaking at an interactive luncheon with senior editors in Lagos on Friday, November 13, 2026, he listed them as the underlying causes of the sector's weakness.

Tegbe sought media partnership to build public trust in the sector and said the problems together hold back its performance and stability. He argued that fixing them needs government, private operators and the public to work together.

The minister did not outline specific government plans for tackling the problems at the luncheon. Power supply has been inconsistent for households and businesses across the country.

Why it mattersHouseholds and businesses that already face unreliable power now have the minister's own list of the faults behind it, and no timetable for fixing them.
