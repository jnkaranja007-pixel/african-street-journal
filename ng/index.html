PDP Candidate Archibong Accepts Governor Otu's Debate Challenge for 2027 Election

Arthur Archibong, the Peoples Democratic Party (PDP) governorship candidate in Cross River, has agreed to debate Governor Bassey Otu ahead of the 2027 election.

Archibong stated his readiness to debate Governor Otu at any time and venue the governor chooses, provided Otu attends personally. This acceptance comes in response to a challenge issued by the state Commissioner for Information, Ekpenyong Cobham, who had called for a public debate on the Otu administration's policies and performance.

The PDP candidate emphasized that the public deserves to hear directly from those seeking to lead them, not from proxies. He declined to engage Cobham in a war of words, noting that the commissioner is not on the ballot. Archibong also defended Arthur Jarvis University, which he founded, against what he described as politically motivated criticisms.

Archibong raised concerns about environmental sanitation, blocked drainages, and the condition of Watt Market, urging critics to focus on these issues. He also alleged inadequate resources for emergency response, citing a lack of diesel for fire trucks and water for firefighters, and questioned the economic benefits of the administration's foreign investment trips.

Why it mattersThis event directly impacts citizens of Cross River State by offering a potential public discussion on governance and policy ahead of the 2027 elections.

Refineries increase crude imports by 151.5% in July

Domestic refineries imported 5.13 million barrels of crude oil in July 2026, a significant rise from 2.04 million barrels in June, according to the NMDPRA.

Crude oil imports by domestic refineries increased by 151.5 per cent to 5.13 million barrels in July 2026, up from 2.04 million barrels in June. This increase occurred as the domestic supply of crude to refineries declined during the same period, according to data from the Nigerian Midstream and Downstream Petroleum Regulatory Authority (NMDPRA).

The NMDPRA’s July 2026 Midstream and Downstream Statistics report indicated that domestic refineries received a total of 17.88 million barrels of crude in July. Of this, 12.75 million barrels were supplied locally, while 5.13 million barrels were imported. Imported crude accounted for 28.7 per cent of total crude receipts, with domestic supplies making up 71.3 per cent.

The 5.13 million barrels imported in July marked a substantial increase from the 2.04 million barrels in June and 2.08 million barrels in May. However, this volume remained below the 9.43 million barrels recorded in March, which was the highest monthly import volume in 2026. The Dangote Refinery operated at an average capacity utilisation of 71.09 per cent in July.

Why it mattersDomestic refineries and the petroleum sector are directly affected by the shift in crude oil sourcing, impacting their operational strategies and supply chains.

Dolly Parton dies at 80, ending six-decade career

American country music star Dolly Parton, whose career spanned nearly six decades across music, film, television, and business, died Tuesday in Nashville, Tennessee.

Dolly Parton, the renowned American country music star, has died at the age of 80, her family announced. Her passing on Tuesday in Nashville, Tennessee, marks the end of a career that encompassed music, film, television, and business over nearly six decades.

The family did not disclose the cause of death, although the singer had previously spoken publicly about health issues. Parton was a prominent figure in the entertainment industry, known for her extensive contributions to various fields.

Her death was reported by Nairametrics and Premium Times, confirming the loss of an iconic figure in American culture. Parton's influence extended beyond music, touching multiple aspects of media and enterprise throughout her long career.

Former Squash No. 1 Olubo completes transcontinental motorcycle journey to Nigeria

Moses Olubo, ex-Nigerian squash champion, is expected to arrive in Lagos today after riding a Can-Am Spyder motorcycle across three continents, including the Sahara Desert.

Former Nigerian squash No. 1 Moses Olubo is anticipated to arrive in Nigeria today, completing an extensive motorcycle journey that began in the United States. His route included Canada, Iceland, Scandinavia, Western Europe, and a challenging crossing of the Sahara and Fouta Jallon Highlands.

Olubo's arrival has been delayed by challenges encountered while exiting Ghana, as his motorcycle is not registered under the ECOWAS system. Despite this, the final stretch to Lagos is expected to be straightforward, with a meet-and-greet scheduled for 2 PM today at the National Stadium, Surulere, Lagos.

Friends, sports enthusiasts, and supporters are expected to welcome Olubo, who organizers have dubbed a “3-Continent Adventurer.” The event will allow sports lovers to celebrate his journey, which combined sport, endurance, and exploration.

Why it mattersNigerians, particularly sports fans, are affected by the arrival of a former national sports figure after a significant personal achievement, offering a chance for public celebration.

Nigeria's new health policy improves doctor working conditions and patient care

The National Health Sector Reform Initiative II (NHSRII) aims to ensure doctors are rested after shifts and locum employees receive clear contracts and regular pay.

Nigeria's new health policy, the NHSRII, intends to improve health outcomes by ensuring doctors can finish a call, hand over to a rested colleague, and go home. This initiative addresses the working conditions of medical professionals, aiming for a more sustainable and effective healthcare system.

Under the new policy, locum employees are expected to have clear contracts, receive regular pay, and transition away from temporary status. This change seeks to provide greater stability and fairness for doctors working in temporary capacities, ensuring they are properly integrated into the healthcare workforce.

The ultimate goal of the NHSRII is for patients to be treated by properly staffed teams, rather than overworked individuals. This focus on adequate staffing and rested medical personnel is designed to enhance the quality and safety of patient care across the country, according to the Premium Times.

Why it mattersThis policy directly affects doctors by improving their working conditions and contract stability, and patients by ensuring they are treated by well-rested and properly staffed medical teams.
