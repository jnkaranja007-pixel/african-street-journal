NESG forecasts Nigeria oil output to exceed 1.7mbpd

Nigeria's crude oil production is expected to top 1.7 million barrels per day in the second half of 2026. The Nigerian Economic Summit Group reports that higher crude oil prices could improve the country's fiscal outlook.

Why it mattersIncreased production and pricing would provide the federal government with more revenue to fund public spending.

Ebola fatality rate climbs above 46%

The fatality rate for the Ebola outbreak in Africa has risen above 46%. Nairametrics reports that health systems in affected areas are under increasing pressure as the outbreak nears pandemic-emergency status.

Why it mattersThe rising death rate and system strain increase the risk of the disease spreading beyond current containment zones.
