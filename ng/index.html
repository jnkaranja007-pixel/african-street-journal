Nigeria confirms imported Ebola case in Abuja

A woman with recent travel history to Uganda tested positive for Ebola Bundibugyo virus disease on October 20.

Nigeria confirmed an imported case of Ebola Bundibugyo virus disease in Abuja on October 20, 2026. The Coordinating Minister of Health and Social Welfare announced the case after laboratory testing identified the virus in a woman who had recently traveled from Uganda. She developed symptoms on October 7 and sought care at a private facility on October 11.

The patient has been isolated and is receiving medical care, while health officials investigate the infection's source and trace her movements. 44 contacts have been identified and are being monitored. This confirmation on the 12th anniversary of Nigeria being declared Ebola-free by the WHO raises questions about the nation's preparedness for a resurgence of the virus.

The 2014 Ebola outbreak in Nigeria resulted in 20 cases and eight deaths before containment. The World Health Organisation declared Nigeria Ebola-free on October 20, 2014, after 42 days without new cases. Swiss authorities have denied claims that the current patient visited the Democratic Republic of the Congo before falling ill.

Why it mattersA new case of Ebola Bundibugyo virus disease has been confirmed in Abuja, Nigeria, affecting the health and safety of the population.

Butcher stabs colleague to death in Ogun over sweeping chore

Police arrested Selim Ayodele, 21, after a disagreement with Ibrahim Wasiu, 26, at their workplace on Sunday, October 18, 2026.

Ogun State Police have arrested 21-year-old butcher Selim Ayodele for allegedly stabbing his 26-year-old colleague, Ibrahim Wasiu, to death. The fatal incident occurred on Sunday, October 18, 2026, after work at their cow meat-selling outlet in Alagbole, Ogun State. A disagreement over who should sweep the floor led to the altercation.

Police Public Relations Officer DSP Oluseyi Babaseyi stated that the Ajuwon Police Station received the report at 8 p.m. Ayodele allegedly stabbed Wasiu in the chest and twisted the knife. Wasiu was confirmed dead on arrival at Life Hospital and his body was taken to Life Channel Private Mortuary pending an autopsy.

The suspect, Ayodele, is in custody as investigations continue for prosecution. Commissioner of Police Bode Ojajuni urged residents to resolve disputes peacefully and report criminal incidents. The command stressed the importance of avoiding violence over minor disagreements.

Why it mattersA 21-year-old butcher was arrested for fatally stabbing his 26-year-old colleague after a workplace dispute.

Veteran Nollywood actor Francis Duru dies at 57

President Bola Tinubu and Nollywood practitioners mourn the passing of the versatile performer, known for his role in 'Rattlesnake'.

Veteran Nollywood actor and filmmaker Francis Duru has died at the age of 57. His family announced his passing on Friday, October 23, 2026, following a brief illness. Duru was a recognisable face in the Nigerian film industry, with a career spanning several decades.

President Bola Tinubu expressed deep sorrow over the death, calling it a painful loss to Nollywood and Nigeria’s creative community. Colleagues, including actors Kanayo Kanayo and Kate Henshaw, paid tribute to Duru’s versatility and contributions. The Actors Guild of Nigeria also mourned the loss of the "great thespian."

Duru studied Theatre Arts at the University of Port Harcourt and was known for his role as Ahanna in the 1995 film 'Rattlesnake'. He also featured in other productions such as 'Sound and Rhythm of Love' and 'Blood Money'. He was also regarded as a mentor to emerging talents.

Why it mattersVeteran actor Francis Duru has died at 57, prompting mourning from President Bola Tinubu and the Nigerian film industry.

Nigeria plans framework for farmer climate finance

A new system aims to guide investments for adaptation and resilience against drought and flooding.

Nigeria is developing a framework to improve access to climate finance for small-scale farmers, guiding investments in agricultural adaptation and protecting rural livelihoods. The initiative will provide financial institutions with clear guidelines for supporting farming activities resilient to climate challenges like drought and flooding. This framework was discussed at a validation workshop in Abuja organised by the Presidential Implementation Committee on Technology Transfer (PICTT) and YAPU Solutions.

PICTT Chairman Dahiru Muhammed stated the framework aims to translate Nigeria’s national climate taxonomy into a practical system for accessing and channeling funds. He explained that while the Federal Ministry of Finance and the UN Development Programme developed the taxonomy, PICTT and partners are establishing implementation mechanisms for rural communities. Muhammed stressed that defining eligible climate adaptation activities is essential for farmers and agricultural enterprises to benefit from financing.

Country Representative of WorldFish in Nigeria, Charles Nyangbe, noted the need for financing solutions tailored to varying regional environmental conditions, such as drought in the north and flooding in the south. YAPU Solutions Managing Director Christoph Junghleisch estimated smallholder farmers could need up to $30 billion annually to respond to climate risks, describing climate adaptation as a significant investment opportunity.

Why it mattersSmall-scale farmers in Nigeria will be directly affected by a new framework designed to improve their access to climate finance for adaptation.

Nigeria convicts 653 terrorism suspects

Attorney-General Lateef Fagbemi announced the convictions in the 11th phase of mass terrorism trials.

Nigerian courts have convicted 653 suspects accused of terrorism-related offenses. The Federal Government's mass trial process has reached this figure after convicting individuals for belonging to Boko Haram, concealing information, or financing terrorism.

Attorney-General of the Federation, Lateef Fagbemi, stated that these convictions are part of the 11th phase of mass terrorism trials. The ongoing trials aim to process a large number of defendants indicted for terrorism.

The convictions were secured as part of the ongoing mass trials.
