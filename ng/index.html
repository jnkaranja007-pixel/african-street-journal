Obi, Baba-Ahmed Criticize Tinubu Over Withheld US Records

President Bola Tinubu faces criticism from opposition leaders Peter Obi and Hakeem Baba-Ahmed regarding alleged attempts to block the release of his past records from the FBI.

Presidential candidate Peter Obi and PRP Chairman Hakeem Baba-Ahmed have publicly criticized President Bola Tinubu for alleged efforts to prevent the FBI from releasing his past records in the United States. Baba-Ahmed questioned the simultaneous absence of the President and Vice President from the country, asking who is currently leading the government.

Speaking at a summit for opposition leaders in Abuja, Baba-Ahmed suggested that Tinubu’s overseas trip was an attempt to stop the FBI from releasing documents related to him. He highlighted this as a unique situation where both the president and vice president are out of the country at the same time. Obi, writing on his X account, called the situation embarrassing and expressed sadness over the nation's direction.

Obi stated that it is deeply embarrassing for a sitting president to seek a foreign court’s intervention to withhold records concerning his past, especially when these records are of public interest. He emphasized that leadership requires confronting one's history with humility and accountability, noting that transparency strengthens authority. Obi also stressed that issues regarding foundational schooling should be clarified with verifiable explanations for Nigerians, particularly the youth.

Why it mattersOpposition leaders Peter Obi and Hakeem Baba-Ahmed are affected by President Tinubu's alleged actions as it raises questions about transparency and accountability in governance.

Gang Leader Found Guilty of Tupac Shakur Murder

Duane Davis, 63, was convicted of first-degree murder with a deadly weapon for the 1996 killing of the rapper.

A Las Vegas jury found Duane “Keffe D” Davis guilty of murdering rapper Tupac Shakur on Monday. Davis, a former Los Angeles gang member, was accused of ordering the September 7, 1996 killing after his nephew was beaten by Shakur’s entourage. Prosecutors stated Davis acquired a firearm and led his group to hunt for Shakur, who was shot in a white Cadillac at a red light. Davis, who faces possible life in prison without parole, intends to appeal the verdict.

The case, one of America’s most celebrated cold cases, was revived by Davis’s memoirs published in 2019, in which he repeatedly boasted of his involvement. His defense attorney argued the confessions were fictional and designed to promote his book, stating there was no evidence implicating Davis other than his own statements. Davis is the only person still alive from the Cadillac that night. Shakur died six days after the shooting.

Sentencing for Davis is scheduled for October 13. The investigation into Shakur’s murder had stalled for decades due to a lack of evidence. Davis, the one-time leader of the South Side Compton Crips, is the only person charged in connection with the shooting. Shakur was in Las Vegas for a Mike Tyson fight and was shot about two hours after a confrontation at a casino.

Why it mattersDuane Davis was found guilty of murdering Tupac Shakur, impacting fans and the cultural legacy of the influential rapper.

Military Rescues 21 Kidnapped Victims, Including 15 NYSC Members in Kogi

The Nigerian military successfully freed 21 individuals, 15 of whom were National Youth Service Corps members, from a forest in Kogi State after a week-long abduction.

The Nigerian military has confirmed the rescue of 21 kidnapped victims, including 15 National Youth Service Corps (NYSC) members, from the Egume-Ochaja forest in Kogi State. The victims, comprising eight males and 13 females, were abducted last week while traveling from Abuja to Bayelsa State. Director of Defence Media Operations, Maj. Gen. Michael Onoja, stated that the military is committed to sustaining operations against criminal elements across the country.

One rescued corps member, David Ayodele, recounted the harrowing experience, detailing threats from the abductors to kill the victims after one month in captivity and warnings against any unauthorized movement. The victims endured physical abuse and were forced to trek through the forest before security operatives, including the Nigerian Army, police, DSS, and local hunters, reached them. The rescued NYSC members were believed to be part of the 2026 Batch ‘B’ Stream II, having concluded their orientation camp.

This rescue operation follows intensified military efforts against terrorists and criminal elements, including the recovery of 308 abducted persons from Kainji Lake National Park on August 5. In August alone, troops neutralized over 332 terrorists, arrested 373, and rescued 610 victims, alongside recovering significant arms and ammunition. The military also recovered 686,800 liters of stolen crude oil and destroyed 21 illegal refining sites.

Why it mattersFifteen National Youth Service Corps members and six other individuals were rescued by the military after being kidnapped and held for a week, ending their ordeal.

Voltaa Mobility Launches Electric Mobility App in Nigeria

The Lagos-based company integrated electric rides, parcel delivery, and truck rentals into a single application.

Voltaa Mobility has launched a new integrated mobility and logistics application in Nigeria. The platform combines chauffeur-driven electric passenger rides, parcel dispatch services, and interstate commercial truck rentals.

The company stated that the app was developed to simplify how individuals and businesses access transportation and delivery services. This launch introduces a unified digital solution for various mobility needs within the country.

This move by Voltaa Mobility aims to streamline operations for both consumers and businesses by offering a comprehensive suite of services through a single, accessible app.

Why it mattersIndividuals and businesses can now access electric rides, parcel delivery, and truck rentals through a single integrated app.

Noor Takaful, Noor Health Distribute N427.96m Surplus to Participants

Nigeria's leading composite Takaful insurance firm and its health subsidiary returned surplus funds to policyholders who did not file claims.

Noor Takaful Insurance Limited and its subsidiary, Noor Health Ltd., have distributed a combined surplus of N427,963,044.68 to participants and enrollees. The distribution is for those who did not make claims on their insurance policies during the period.

Noor Takaful, described as the pioneer and leading composite Takaful insurance firm in Nigeria, operates under Islamic finance principles. The surplus represents profits generated from the participants' fund that were not used for claims or operational expenses.

This distribution reflects the Takaful model, where participants contribute to a fund, and any surplus is typically shared among them. The companies did not specify the exact number of beneficiaries or the period covered by the surplus distribution.

Why it mattersInvestors may see this surplus distribution as a sign of financial health and responsible management within the insurance sector, potentially influencing investment decisions.
