Businessman Dies After Penis Enlargement Procedure in Thailand

A 43-year-old Nigerian-British businessman died from a pulmonary embolism following a cosmetic procedure in Bangkok.

Igho Ubiribo, a 43-year-old British-Nigerian businessman and social media influencer, died on March 6 after receiving 40 millilitres of hyaluronic acid and lidocaine at a clinic in Bangkok. An inquest at Inner West London Coroners’ Court heard that Ubiribo experienced chest pain and lost consciousness twice while having a massage with his wife after the procedure. He was transported to Sukhumvit Hospital, where doctors diagnosed a pulmonary embolism, a blood clot in the lungs.

Doctors attempted resuscitation for over 100 minutes before Ubiribo passed away. A postmortem conducted in the UK found cellular material in his lungs that matched the hyaluronic acid used in the penile filler injection, supporting the pulmonary embolism diagnosis. Coroner Jean Harkin concluded that Ubiribo died as a result of a cosmetic procedure abroad, with the cause of death officially recorded as a pulmonary embolism stemming from the injection.

Ubiribo, who had approximately 185,000 Instagram followers, was buried in London in May. His funeral was attended by prominent figures, including Nigerian singer Davido. Medical professionals, such as NHS andrology specialist Professor Vaibhav Modgil, have previously warned of the potentially devastating consequences of penile filler procedures.

Why it mattersThe death of a prominent Nigerian-British businessman after a cosmetic procedure abroad may deter others from similar medical tourism, impacting the personal finances and well-being of individuals seeking such treatments.

Enugu Police Arrest 6,240 Suspects, Rescue 211 Kidnap Victims

The Enugu State Police Command reported arresting 6,240 suspects and recovering 156 firearms in 17 months of operations.

Enugu State Police Command announced the arrest of 6,240 suspects and the rescue of 211 kidnapped victims over a 17-month period. Commissioner of Police Mamman Giwa stated that operations also led to the recovery of 156 firearms and 1,486 rounds of ammunition. The figures cover Giwa's tenure since March 11, 2025, and include arrests for offenses such as kidnapping, armed robbery, murder, rape, cultism, and unlawful possession of firearms.

During the security operations, police recovered 43 AK-47 rifles and one G-3 assault rifle, among other firearms. Additionally, 209 vehicles, including cars, tricycles, and motorcycles, were recovered. The Commissioner attributed these successes to sustained intelligence-led operations, proactive policing, and collaboration with neighboring states. He highlighted the deployment of specialized units like the Violent Crime Response Unit and intensified border patrols.

The command's efforts were supported by the Inspector-General of Police’s Handshake Patrol initiative and improved emergency response systems. Assistant Inspector-General of Police Miller Dantawaye, visiting the command, urged officers to maintain professionalism, discipline, and accountability while respecting citizens' rights. He emphasized the importance of building public confidence and assured the command of his support in enhancing security within Enugu State.

Why it mattersResidents and businesses in Enugu State are directly affected by the police's efforts to reduce crime and rescue kidnapped victims.

Petrol Theft Fumes Kill 37 in Nigeria

An environmental group reported at least 37 deaths from toxic fumes during an alleged petrol theft from a pipeline in Rivers State.

At least thirty-seven people have died after inhaling toxic fumes while allegedly stealing petrol from a pipeline in Nigeria's Rivers State, an environmental group in the oil-rich region stated. Many are still missing after more than one hundred people descended on Okari Jetty in Okrika in small boats to collect the refined fuel under cover of darkness, according to the Youths and Environmental Advocacy Centre (YEAC).

The police told the BBC an investigation is ongoing to determine the figures of those affected after people reportedly collapsed. Local reports indicated some fell into the Bonny River while others were taken to hospital. Theft from pipelines has been a problem for decades in Nigeria's oil hub, a vast area of swamps and wetlands along the coast.

Criminal groups have tapped pipelines for years, siphoning crude oil or other petroleum products into boats and storage tanks to be sold on the black market or taken to illegal refineries. Rivers State is one of several states that make up the southern Niger Delta region.

Why it mattersAt least 37 people died from toxic fumes while allegedly stealing petrol, affecting local communities and prompting a police investigation.

Boss Mustapha Marks Seven Decades of Service

A prayer for continued health, wisdom, and impact marks the milestone birthday of a Nigerian public servant.

Boss Mustapha, a prominent figure in Nigerian public service, has reached the age of seventy. His career, marked by faithful service, is being celebrated with wishes for continued good health and strength.

The accumulated wisdom of seven decades is seen as a valuable asset that will continue to benefit the nation and future generations. This milestone is accompanied by hopes that the foundations he has laid will yield positive outcomes.

The sentiment expressed is one of gratitude for his dedication and a forward-looking perspective on his ongoing contributions to the country. The celebration focuses on his legacy and the potential for future impact.

Why it mattersBoss Mustapha's seventy years of service and accumulated wisdom are seen as beneficial to the country and coming generations.
