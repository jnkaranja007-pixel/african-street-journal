Naira holds at N1,364.83 official as parallel rate steadies at N1,405

The naira was broadly stable against the dollar on 14 August in both the official Nigerian Foreign Exchange Market and the parallel market. The official NFEM rate stood at N1,364.83 per dollar while the parallel market traded near N1,405 in Lagos and other major cities, leaving a spread of about N40.

Why it mattersThe gap between official and street rates is what importers and anyone receiving remittances actually pay.

Nigeria's external reserves reach 17-year high of $52.19bn, up 28.4%

External reserves rose to $52.19 billion as of 12 August, a 17-year high, according to Central Bank figures. That is 28.39% above the $40.65 billion held in the same period of 2025.

Why it mattersDeeper reserves give the central bank more room to defend the naira and clear foreign exchange backlogs.

Naira rises after CBN lifts FX and securities limits on discount window

The naira strengthened after the Central Bank of Nigeria removed foreign exchange and securities restrictions on its discount window, BusinessDay reported. Analysts said near-term performance would turn on foreign exchange inflows, crude earnings, diaspora remittances and central bank liquidity management.

Why it mattersEasier access to the discount window affects bank liquidity and, through it, lending rates.
