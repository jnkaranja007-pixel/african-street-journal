Nigerian manufacturers urged to deploy AI to cut costs

Industry leaders met in Lagos to discuss technology and financing for energy efficiency and competitiveness.

Manufacturers Association of Nigeria (MAN) president Ifeanyi Okoye urged companies to use Artificial Intelligence (AI) and energy-efficient technologies to combat rising production expenses. The call came during a 2026 summit on energy security and efficiency in Lagos, a meeting organised by MPDCL, GIZ, and NESP. Leaders discussed technology, sustainable energy, and financing to address manufacturers' energy challenges.

Okoye stated that manufacturers spend approximately 40 per cent of their profits on energy issues, citing high electricity tariffs and unreliable grid supply. MAN Director-General Segun Ajayi-Kadir added that AI and smart systems can optimise energy use and lower operating costs. John Aluya, Chairman of MPDCL, noted that high self-generation costs reduce profit margins.

The summit aimed to facilitate access to climate funds and green investments for manufacturers transitioning to efficient energy systems. Ulf Brackmann, CEO of IKAPITAL Family Office & Friends, suggested AI could boost productivity and create jobs, but only where factories have dependable electricity and digital foundations. Industrial goods companies on the Nigerian Exchange Limited spent N820.7 billion on energy in the first nine months of 2026.

Why it mattersNigerian manufacturers are facing significant financial pressure due to high energy costs, impacting their profits and competitiveness.

Tinubu targets prosperity from economic stability

Nigeria's gross external reserves reached $55.07 billion in October 2026, the highest in 18 years.

President Bola Tinubu stated that Nigeria's next economic priority is to convert current reform gains into prosperity through enterprise, industrial growth, and expanded trade. He spoke at the 40th Lagos International Trade Fair, held at Tafawa Balewa Square in Lagos over the weekend. The government has made progress in stabilising the economy and will now focus on stimulating production, creating jobs, and expanding business opportunities.

Tinubu, represented by Minister of State for Industry Senator John Enoh, reported that Nigeria's gross external reserves rose to approximately $55.07 billion in October 2026, the highest level in 18 years, with net reserves exceeding $40 billion. Inflation declined to 14.63 per cent in September 2026, and the economy grew by 4.43 per cent in the second quarter. The administration supported over 115,000 MSMEs in 2025.

Non-oil exports exceeded $6.1 billion in 2025, while capital importation reached about $21 billion, with Nigerian businesses encouraged to leverage the African Continental Free Trade Area (AfCFTA). The National Industrial Policy aims to strengthen manufacturing and deepen local value chains. Nigeria will host the Intra-African Trade Fair in 2027.

Why it mattersNigerian businesses and citizens will be affected by efforts to translate economic stability into prosperity through job creation and expanded trade opportunities.

Rangers extend NPFL lead to 24 points

Enugu Rangers secured a 1-0 win against Kwara United on Sunday, while Enyimba lost 2-1 to Ranchers Bees.

Enugu Rangers solidified their position at the top of the Nigeria Premier Football League with a 1-0 victory over Kwara United on Sunday. Victor Chimezie scored the decisive goal in the 40th minute. This win follows their 1-0 rescheduled fixture victory against Bendel Insurance on Wednesday.

The three points propelled Rangers to 24 points from 11 matches, extending their lead over closest challengers. Meanwhile, Enyimba suffered a 2-1 defeat to Ranchers Bees in Minna, continuing their difficult season. Nine-time champions Enyimba face pressure to improve results and move away from the relegation zone.

In other matches, Nasarawa United defeated Plateau United 3-1, with Plateau United playing with 10 men after Sabbath Mancha's red card. Abia Warriors drew 2-2 with Doma United, with Emeka Obioma scoring twice to rescue a point for the Umuahia-based side, who remain under pressure to secure top-flight status.

Why it mattersFootball fans in Nigeria are affected by the shifting positions in the Nigeria Premier Football League, with Enugu Rangers extending their lead and Enyimba facing relegation concerns.

G-100 chieftain says opposition consensus candidate plan dead

A chieftain of the G-100 group stated that the plan for a consensus opposition candidate has failed.

A chieftain of the G-100 group announced that the opposition's plan to field a single consensus candidate has collapsed. The G-100 is a political group that has been working to unite opposition parties.

The chieftain, who was not named in the report, stated that the consensus candidate plan is now dead. This development comes after discussions among opposition figures, including Peter Obi, Bola Tinubu, and Atiku Abubakar, who were mentioned in relation to the political landscape.

The failure of this plan means that opposition parties may proceed with fielding their own individual candidates in future elections. The G-100 group's objective was to present a unified front against the ruling party.

Why it mattersNigerians seeking a unified opposition voice in upcoming elections will not see a single candidate.

Nigeria's Flamingos win U-17 Women's World Cup

The team became the first African side to secure the FIFA U-17 Women's title after defeating Japan.

Nigeria's U-17 women's national team, the Flamingos, have won the FIFA U-17 Women's World Cup. This victory marks the first time an African team has claimed this title. The Flamingos achieved this historic feat after defeating Japan in the final match.

Akeem Ishola Busari, who was appointed coach just seven months ago, led the Flamingos to this triumph. The Minister of Sports has hailed the win as a landmark achievement for women's football across Africa.

This is the first time Nigeria has won the U-17 Women's World Cup. The team's success follows Busari's appointment seven months prior to the tournament.

Why it mattersNigerian women's football has achieved a historic first by winning the U-17 World Cup.
