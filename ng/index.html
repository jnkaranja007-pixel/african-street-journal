Tinubu Support Group Mobilizes Northwest for 2027 Re-election

A group called 'Relax, Tinubu Is Fixing Nigeria' has begun grassroots mobilization across seven northern states to secure President Tinubu's re-election in 2027.

A support group, 'Relax, Tinubu Is Fixing Nigeria,' has launched a grassroots mobilization campaign across the seven states of the North-West geopolitical zone to support President Bola Ahmed Tinubu's re-election bid in 2027. The group inaugurated coordinators and members from Kaduna, Kano, Katsina, Kebbi, Jigawa, Sokoto, and Zamfara states in Kaduna. They urged patience with the administration, stating Nigeria is undergoing rebuilding and restructuring.

Abubakar Muhammad Mamadi, Special Adviser on Politics to Kaduna State Governor Uba Sani, stated that the North-West has significantly benefited from the Tinubu administration, citing interventions in agriculture, youth and women empowerment, and education. He highlighted financial support programs for women, youths, and traders, as well as funds for disadvantaged students, which he claimed are addressing socio-economic conditions linked to insecurity.

The group's convener, Mike Auta, charged members to reach rural and hard-to-reach areas for aggressive mobilization, aiming to ensure Tinubu's victory. Women leader Karima Abdullai reported that millions of women have benefited from cash assistance and vocational training in areas like tailoring and bakery, contributing to self-reliance and growing support for the administration in the region.

Why it mattersThe 'Relax, Tinubu Is Fixing Nigeria' group is mobilizing voters in the North-West region to support President Tinubu's re-election, highlighting government programs benefiting farmers, women, and students.

Tinubu's lawyers block US court from releasing drug probe records

President Bola Tinubu's legal team filed a 16-page submission arguing that releasing FBI and DEA records would violate his privacy.

President Bola Tinubu is seeking to prevent the release of records related to drug trafficking allegations by filing a challenge in a U.S. court. His legal team, including Christopher W. Carmichael, Victor P. Henderson, and Oluwole O. Afolabi, submitted a 16-page document arguing that the disclosure of these records could infringe upon his privacy rights.

The legal team contends that the submission does not reveal details of a criminal investigation or its results, and that the plaintiff has not pointed to existing public records containing such findings. They argue that the limited disclosures made in connection with a forfeiture proceeding do not negate the privacy interests involved, citing Supreme Court precedent that individuals retain privacy in entire files even with scattered disclosures.

The filing further asserts that the intervenor retains a privacy interest in potential disclosure of the investigative files, regardless of prior information made public during the forfeiture proceedings. The legal team's submission also critiques the FBI's search efforts, the DEA's Vaughn Index, and the applicability of exemptions to deceased individuals and confidential sources, all within the context of obtaining documents bearing the intervenor's name.

Why it mattersPresident Bola Tinubu is affected by this event as his legal team is actively working to prevent the release of sensitive records from U.S. agencies.

Soldiers Injured Repelling Terror Attack on Borno Base

Joint task force troops repelled an assault on a military base in Kukawa, Borno State, resulting in four soldiers sustaining injuries.

Troops of the Operation Hadin Kai successfully repelled a terror attack on a military base in Kukawa, Borno State. During the engagement, four soldiers were injured. The operation involved coordinated ground and air efforts.

The joint task force reported that approximately 26 terrorists were killed during the confrontation. This action is part of ongoing efforts by the military to counter insurgent activities in the North-east region.

The incident highlights the continued security challenges in Borno State. The military's operation aimed to neutralize terrorist threats and secure the base from the attack.

Why it mattersFour soldiers were injured during a terror attack on a military base in Borno State, Nigeria.

Cholera Cases Exceed 65,000 Across 35 Nigerian States

The National Centre for Disease Control reports a significant number of suspected cholera cases, highlighting a widespread public health concern.

Over 65,000 suspected cholera cases have been recorded across 35 states in Nigeria, according to the National Centre for Disease Control (NCDC). The NCDC Director-General, Dr. Jide Idris, provided this figure, indicating the broad reach of the outbreak within the country.

The scale of the reported cases suggests a substantial public health challenge facing the nation. While specific details on the number of confirmed cases or fatalities were not provided in this update, the sheer volume of suspected infections points to a widespread issue requiring attention.

This widespread occurrence of suspected cholera cases across numerous states underscores the need for continued vigilance and public health interventions. The NCDC's data serves as a critical indicator of the current health landscape and potential risks to the population.

Why it mattersOver 65,000 suspected cholera cases affect the general population, posing a significant health risk across 35 Nigerian states.

Nigerians Earn Dollars From Premium Domains

Professionals can acquire premium domains for $3,000 and profit between $15,000 and $35,000, with payment options in Naira or US Dollars.

Nigerians now have the opportunity to earn significant income in US Dollars through the acquisition of premium domain names. This venture allows professionals, including entrepreneurs, doctors, civil servants, IT experts, and accountants, to invest as little as $3,000 for a domain and potentially realize profits ranging from $15,000 to $35,000.

The earnings can be received either in Nigerian Naira or US Dollars, offering flexibility to participants. This initiative presents a new avenue for income generation, particularly for those seeking to operate in foreign currency markets. The opportunity is presented as accessible and beneficial for a wide range of skilled individuals.

Details on how to start and evidence of individuals already benefiting from this domain acquisition scheme are available. The program aims to provide a clear path to earning foreign currency, leveraging digital assets for financial gain within the Nigerian economic landscape.
