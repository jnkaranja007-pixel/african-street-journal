Nigeria's Remittances Reach Record $947 Million in July

Inflows through formal channels neared the $1 billion monthly target, marking a significant increase driven by Central Bank of Nigeria reforms.

Nigeria recorded $947 million in remittance inflows through International Money Transfer Operators (IMTOs) in July 2026, the highest monthly inflow ever through formal channels. This figure approaches the $1 billion monthly target set by the Central Bank of Nigeria (CBN). Inflows for the first seven months of 2026 reached $3.8 billion, a 50.2% increase compared to the same period in 2025, indicating a substantial strengthening of formal remittance flows.

The Central Bank of Nigeria attributes this growth to a series of reforms aimed at enhancing the competitiveness, transparency, and accessibility of formal remittance channels. These initiatives include adopting a more market-determined exchange rate, reforming the regulatory framework for IMTOs, introducing the Non-Resident Bank Verification Number (NRBVN), and fostering closer engagement with IMTOs, banks, and Nigerian diaspora communities. Recent measures have also strengthened requirements for remittance transactions to be routed through designated settlement accounts.

Governor Olayemi Cardoso stated that while July's figure is an important marker, the focus remains on creating conditions for sustained growth in formal remittances. He expressed confidence that Nigeria can reach and sustain monthly inflows above $1 billion. The CBN plans to deepen engagement with diaspora communities and financial partners to reduce friction, widen access, and increase the share of remittances flowing through formal channels.

Why it mattersNigerian diaspora communities are directly affected as their remittances are now flowing through more competitive, transparent, and accessible formal channels, boosting foreign-exchange liquidity and strengthening the country's external financing position.

Nigerian Delegation Explores Hangzhou's Smart Life Innovations

A Nigerian delegation concluded an enriching tour of Hangzhou, China, experiencing lectures and field visits that offered new insights into smart living.

The Nigerian delegation's visit to Hangzhou, China, proved to be an enlightening experience, extending beyond academic lectures to include practical field visits. This immersive approach allowed participants to witness firsthand the innovations and unique aspects of Hangzhou's development, particularly in the realm of 'smart life'. The program, which included twelve lectures, was designed to provide a comprehensive understanding of the city's advancements.

The tour aimed to expose the delegation to new perspectives and methodologies, moving beyond theoretical knowledge gained in classrooms. Each field visit presented distinct opportunities to observe and learn from real-world applications of technology and urban planning. This hands-on exploration was central to the enriching and revealing nature of the Hangzhou experience.

This initiative facilitated a deeper appreciation for the practical implementation of smart city concepts and their impact on daily life. The combination of structured learning and direct observation provided a robust framework for understanding the advancements showcased in Hangzhou.

Why it mattersNigerian business professionals and policymakers gain insights into technological advancements and urban development strategies from Hangzhou's smart life initiatives.

First Lady Launches $1 Billion Prosperity Program

A $1 billion Household Prosperity and Empowerment program has been launched in Abuja, signaling a new economic initiative.

The Nigerian First Lady has launched a $1 billion Household Prosperity and Empowerment program in Abuja. The event was marked by an atmosphere of triumph and jubilation, suggesting significant expectations for the initiative.

The program, named Household Prosperity and Empowerment, aims to stimulate economic activity and improve livelihoods across the nation. Details on its specific mechanisms and target beneficiaries were not immediately available, but the scale of the investment indicates a broad scope.

This launch represents a major economic undertaking by the government. The substantial funding allocated signals a commitment to addressing prosperity and empowerment challenges within Nigeria. The program's success will likely be measured by its tangible impact on households nationwide.

Why it mattersThe $1 billion Household Prosperity and Empowerment program is intended to affect Nigerian households by improving their prosperity and empowerment.
