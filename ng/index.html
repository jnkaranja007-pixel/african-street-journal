Nigeria oil output expected to exceed 1.7mbpd

Crude oil production is projected to top 1.7 million barrels per day in the second half of 2026. The Nigerian Economic Summit Group (NESG) stated that higher crude prices could improve the country's fiscal outlook.

Why it mattersIncreased production and pricing provide the federal government with more revenue for national spending.

Atiku pledges to restore petrol subsidy in 2027

ADC presidential candidate Atiku has vowed to restore the petrol subsidy if elected president in 2027. He stated that individuals who diverted public funds from the previous scheme should be held accountable.

Why it mattersA restoration of the subsidy would lower the pump price of fuel for Nigerian motorists and businesses.
