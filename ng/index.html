Naira falls to N1,405 per dollar in parallel market

The Naira depreciated to N1,405 per dollar in the parallel market on Thursday. The rate was N1,400 per dollar on Wednesday, according to Vanguard.

Why it mattersThe currency drop increases the cost of imported goods for Nigerian consumers.

Business · 08/21/2026

World Bank targets 32 million Nigerians for electricity by 2032

A new World Bank framework aims to connect 32 million Nigerians to electricity and 58 million to broadband internet by 2032. The initiative is designed to boost private sector growth, according to The Punch.

Why it mattersIncreased power and internet access allows small businesses to operate digitally and expand their service hours.

Ebola fatality rate tops 46% in worsening outbreak

The fatality rate for the current Ebola outbreak has climbed above 46%. Health systems in affected areas are under increasing pressure, according to Nairametrics.

Why it mattersThe rising death rate may trigger an escalation of the outbreak to pandemic-emergency status.

Climate · 08/21/2026

NSEMA warns of flooding from distressed Kaduna dam

The Niger State Emergency Management Agency reported structural distress and cracks at the Kangimi dam in Kaduna State. Director General Abdullahi Baba-Arah stated the defective dam could cause serious flooding, according to The Punch.

Why it mattersFailure of the dam structure threatens to displace residents in downstream areas through flash flooding.
