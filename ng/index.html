FG suspends broadcast licence fee reviews

The Federal Government halts all reviews of broadcast licence fees indefinitely due to rising operational costs.

The Federal Government has indefinitely suspended all reviews of broadcast licence fees. This decision follows concerns raised by broadcast operators about increasing operational costs and financial pressures within Nigeria's broadcasting industry. Minister of Information and National Orientation, Mohammed Idris, announced the suspension on Monday in Abuja.

The suspension was announced during a meeting with the Broadcasting Organisations of Nigeria (BON), led by Chairman Senior High Chief Dr Tony Akiotu. BON had raised these concerns at their 82nd General Assembly in Calabar. Minister Idris stated the suspension aims to create a more conducive operating environment for broadcasters, directing the National Broadcasting Commission (NBC) to comply.

The Minister also directed the NBC Director-General to speed up a review of the National Broadcasting Code to align with current industry realities. He promised a stakeholders' meeting to address issues with the Digital Switch-Over programme. The Advertising Regulatory Council of Nigeria (ARCON) was also directed to enforce a 45-day payment period for advertising services.

Why it mattersBroadcast operators in Nigeria face financial pressures and rising costs that have led the Federal Government to suspend reviews of their licence fees.

Nigeria's FX turnover falls 51% to $1.81bn

Dollar transactions in Nigeria's foreign exchange market dropped by $1.90bn in the week ending November 6.

Nigeria’s foreign exchange market saw dollar transactions fall by 51.29 per cent to $1.81bn in the week ended November 6, down from $3.71bn the previous week. This $1.90bn reduction in weekly trading value occurred alongside a slight depreciation of the naira. Data from FMDQ shows average daily FX turnover dropped to $361.67m from $742.51m.

Spot transactions, involving immediate currency delivery, accounted for most of the decrease, falling 50.91 per cent to $1.79bn. Forward transactions also weakened, dropping 69.93 per cent to $22.20m. The one-month forward rate depreciated by 0.2 per cent to N1,370.19 per dollar, while the one-year rate fell by 0.6 per cent to N1,557.65 per dollar.

Analysts expect the naira to remain stable in the short term, supported by FX reserves and Nigeria’s current account surplus. The domestic financial market anticipates N148.57bn from Treasury bills maturities and N774.53bn from Open Market Operations maturities this week. This combined N923.10bn inflow could affect liquidity and investor demand for FX.

Why it mattersNigeria's foreign exchange market experienced a significant drop in dollar trading, impacting the availability and cost of foreign currency.

Midea launches Space Master refrigerator in Nigeria

The appliance aims to solve common household challenges of limited food storage space.

Midea has introduced its Space Master Refrigerator to Nigerian consumers as part of its “More Room for Nigeria” campaign. This launch addresses the everyday challenge of insufficient refrigerator space, particularly after major grocery shopping, hosting events, or during family celebrations.

The Space Master refrigerator is designed to provide practical food storage solutions for Nigerian households. Midea's campaign highlights the appliance's ability to manage the demands of a full fridge, a common issue for families and individuals alike.

This product introduction focuses on enhancing everyday living through improved food storage capacity. The campaign emphasizes the practical benefits of the Space Master for Nigerian consumers facing common kitchen space constraints.

Why it mattersNigerian consumers will benefit from increased food storage capacity in their homes.

Atiku's son joins APC campaign

Atiku junior collapsed his 'Haske' group structure into the ruling party to support President Tinubu's re-election.

Atiku Abubakar’s son has merged his political group, ‘Haske’, with the ruling All Progressives Congress (APC). This move occurred in Jigawa state and signals support for President Bola Tinubu’s re-election campaign.

The announcement came as Atiku junior declared the dissolution of his group’s structure into the APC. This action is intended to bolster the ruling party’s efforts ahead of President Tinubu’s upcoming visit to Jigawa.

Meanwhile, in Plateau State, Oyo Governor Seyi Makinde attacked the APC government during a campaign rally. Makinde, who is the APM presidential candidate, criticised the Tinubu-led administration.

Why it mattersThe political alignment shifts affect the upcoming presidential election campaign for President Tinubu.
