Nigeria's central bank to decide on interest rates on November 23

The Monetary Policy Committee meets on November 23 and 24 to set the Monetary Policy Rate, now 23% after a cut from 26.5% in September.

Source: Nairametrics · 2026-11-15 · 1 min read

The Central Bank of Nigeria's Monetary Policy Committee meets on November 23 and 24 to decide whether to raise, hold or cut interest rates. The Monetary Policy Rate, the benchmark the bank sets, currently stands at 23%, after the bank reset it from 26.5% in September.

The decision turns on more than that one figure, according to the Nairametrics explainer that sets out the process. The committee weighs a range of economic indicators before choosing a direction for the rate.

The meeting falls in the final days of November, and its outcome will determine the rate that applies from the announcement onward.

Why it mattersBanks and businesses that borrow at rates tied to the Monetary Policy Rate will face whatever the committee sets when it announces its decision after the November 24 meeting.
