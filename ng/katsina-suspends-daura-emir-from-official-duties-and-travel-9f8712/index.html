Katsina suspends Daura emir from official duties and travel

The state government acted after a Polish tourist alleged that Umar Farouk Umar grabbed her chest during a tourism encounter.

Source: BBC Africa · 2026-11-25 · 1 min read

Katsina state government has suspended Umar Farouk Umar, the Emir of Daura, from official engagements and travel after a Polish tourist accused him of sexual assault. The visitor said the 92-year-old monarch grabbed her chest during a tourism encounter.

Umar has not commented on the allegation or his suspension. The state acted after calls on social media for the emir to be suspended and investigated, according to BBC Africa.

Umar has held the throne since 2007, when he succeeded his brother, who reigned for 41 years. Emirs retain cultural and religious influence in northern Nigeria, though they have lost political power.

Why it mattersThe emir must forgo official engagements and travel while the Katsina state government responds to the allegation.
