I-invest Wins BAFI Award for Capital Markets Access

The digital investment platform was recognized for its technological innovations that simplify access to investment opportunities for individuals and businesses.

Source: Nairametrics · 2026-10-21 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

i-invest, a digital investment platform operated by Parthian Financial Services, has received the Capital Markets Access Innovator of the Year award at the 14th BusinessDay Banks and Other Financial Institutions (BAFI) Awards. The award celebrates the company's successful use of technology to make investment opportunities more accessible to both individuals and businesses.

This recognition highlights i-invest's role in expanding access within Nigeria's capital markets. The platform aims to streamline the investment process, making it more straightforward for a wider range of participants to engage with financial opportunities.

The BAFI Awards are a significant event in the financial sector, acknowledging excellence and innovation among financial institutions. i-invest's win underscores its contribution to the growth and accessibility of Nigeria's financial landscape through digital solutions.

Why it mattersInvestors are affected by i-invest's award as it signifies innovation in expanding access to Nigeria's capital markets through technology.
