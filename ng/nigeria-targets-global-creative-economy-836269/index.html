Nigeria targets global creative economy

Vice President Kashim Shettima urged investors to fund African creative businesses for future growth.

Source: BusinessDay · 2026-11-06 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Nigeria aims to boost its standing in the global creative economy by increasing trade, investment, and research. The nation is focused on developing this sector for future economic expansion.

Vice President Kashim Shettima called on investors and financial institutions to provide early funding for African creative businesses. He described the sector as a significant source of future global companies and employment.

The creative sector is identified as a major driver for economic growth across the continent. Nigeria's strategy involves strengthening its participation through enhanced trade and investment initiatives.

Why it mattersNigerian creative businesses and investors stand to benefit from increased funding and trade opportunities.
