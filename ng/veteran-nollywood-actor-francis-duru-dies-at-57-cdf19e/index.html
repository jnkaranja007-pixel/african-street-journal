Veteran Nollywood actor Francis Duru dies at 57

President Bola Tinubu and Nollywood practitioners mourn the passing of the versatile performer, known for his role in 'Rattlesnake'.

Source: The Punch · 2026-10-24 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Veteran Nollywood actor and filmmaker Francis Duru has died at the age of 57. His family announced his passing on Friday, October 23, 2026, following a brief illness. Duru was a recognisable face in the Nigerian film industry, with a career spanning several decades.

President Bola Tinubu expressed deep sorrow over the death, calling it a painful loss to Nollywood and Nigeria’s creative community. Colleagues, including actors Kanayo Kanayo and Kate Henshaw, paid tribute to Duru’s versatility and contributions. The Actors Guild of Nigeria also mourned the loss of the "great thespian."

Duru studied Theatre Arts at the University of Port Harcourt and was known for his role as Ahanna in the 1995 film 'Rattlesnake'. He also featured in other productions such as 'Sound and Rhythm of Love' and 'Blood Money'. He was also regarded as a mentor to emerging talents.
