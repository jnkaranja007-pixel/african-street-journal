Nigerian companies generate more operating cash as profits lag

An analysis of 19 non-bank members of the Nigerian Exchange's NGX 30 index found operating cash flow outpaced profit growth in the first nine months of 2026.

Source: Nairametrics · 2026-11-23 · 1 min read

19 non-bank companies in the Nigerian Exchange's NGX 30 index generated operating cash flow faster than their reported profits grew in the first nine months of 2026, according to an analysis of their financial statements. Their combined net cash from operating activities increased substantially over the period.

The comparison shows cash generated from operations outpacing the rise in reported profits. The analysis did not give figures for either increase in the available report. Net cash from operating activities measures the cash a company's operations produce, rather than its reported profit.

The analysis does not identify the companies or give a combined cash total.

Why it mattersInvestors holding these 19 companies can see that their operations are producing cash faster than their reported earnings are rising.
