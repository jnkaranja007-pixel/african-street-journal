Nigerian stocks gain N351bn on Monday

The Nigerian Exchange Limited's All-Share Index rose 0.21 per cent to 252,809.78 points, with industrial, consumer goods and insurance stocks leading the gains.

Source: The Punch · 2026-11-17 · 1 min read

Investors on the Nigerian Exchange Limited gained N351.06bn on Monday as buying interest returned to industrial, consumer goods, and insurance stocks. The All-Share Index closed at 252,809.78 points, a 0.21 per cent increase from Friday's close. Total market capitalisation also rose by 0.21 per cent, reaching N164.15tn.

The market breadth was positive, with 34 stocks advancing against 27 declining. Nigerian Exchange Group Plc led the gainers, surging 10.00 per cent to N182.60. LivingTrust Mortgage Bank Plc followed with a 9.87 per cent gain to N2.56. On the losing side, R. T. Briscoe (Nigeria) Plc dropped 9.84 per cent to N8.25.

Sectoral performance was mostly bullish, with the NGX Consumer Goods Index up 0.80 per cent and the NGX Industrial Index up 0.60 per cent. The NGX Banking Index dipped 0.26 per cent, and the NGX Oil and Gas Index declined 0.10 per cent. Trading activity involved 673.59 million shares exchanged across 38,218 deals.
