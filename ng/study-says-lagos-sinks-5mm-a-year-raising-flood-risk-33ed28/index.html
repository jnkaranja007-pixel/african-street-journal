Study says Lagos sinks 5mm a year, raising flood risk

The study links the city's annual sinking rate with increased exposure to flooding, but the report provides no further details.

Source: The Punch · 2026-11-28 · 1 min read

A study says Lagos is sinking by 5mm annually and faces rising flood risk. The report, published by The Punch, identifies the sinking rate as a threat to the Nigerian city.

The Punch's report describes the finding as coming from a study but provides no details about the researchers, the areas affected or how they measured the annual change. It also gives no estimate of how many residents or properties face increased flood risk.

The report names no proposed response, timeline or further figures for the risk.

Why it mattersLagos residents and property owners face a rising flood risk as the city sinks by 5mm each year.
