Rights group sues Nigeria's data regulator and electoral commission over AI-altered vot...

The Socio-Economic Rights and Accountability Project seeks a court order compelling an investigation into suspected AI-generated photographs in the voters' register.

Sources: Vanguard, Premium Times and The Punch · 2026-11-22 · 1 min read

The Socio-Economic Rights and Accountability Project (SERAP), a rights group, has sued Nigeria's National Data Protection Commission (NDPC) and the Independent National Electoral Commission (INEC) at the Federal High Court in Abuja. The suit, numbered FHC/ABJ/CS/2863/2026, asks the court to compel the NDPC to decide whether to investigate suspected AI-generated photographs, altered personal data and unverifiable records in INEC's preliminary Register of Voters.

INEC said on 1 November that its Automated Biometric Identification System had flagged 268 records for review, with some individuals allegedly exploiting official registration credentials. SERAP wants the NDPC to obtain and examine INEC's records and data, and to exercise its enforcement powers under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, including corrective orders, fines and compensation for affected voters.

SERAP argues that the irregularities raise serious concerns about the security of Nigerians' personal data and the reliability of the register ahead of the 2027 general elections.
