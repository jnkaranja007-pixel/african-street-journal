Sokoto ultimatum issued over attack on ADC candidate

Ekiti police arrest 11 after Omoyele Sowore abduction; conflicting relief payment terms emerge.

Sources: Premium Times and Anadolu Africa · 2026-11-01 · 1 min read

The African Democratic Congress (ADC) has given Sokoto security authorities 24 hours to respond to an alleged attack on its governorship candidate. In Ekiti, police announced 11 arrests following an incident involving Omoyele Sowore, who was forcibly taken from a restaurant in Ado-Ekiti by armed men. Meanwhile, two government publications presented different terms for the ₦50,000 relief programme.

Sowore's official social media accounts confirmed his abduction. Abba Atiku also pledged to provide buses for President Tinubu. The Independent National Electoral Commission (INEC) has outlined its preparations for upcoming elections.

The ADC's ultimatum highlights ongoing political tensions in Sokoto. The conflicting relief payment terms could affect citizens awaiting financial assistance. INEC's preparations signal the electoral body's readiness for future electoral processes.

Why it mattersPolitical actors and security incidents in Sokoto and Ekiti, alongside government relief programs and electoral preparations, directly affect Nigerian citizens.
