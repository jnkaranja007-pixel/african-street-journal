Nigeria proposes N50 billion fund for seed businesses

Agriculture Minister Abubakar Kyari said the fund would attract investment and expand the production and distribution of quality seeds.

Source: AgroNigeria · 2026-11-26 · 1 min read

Nigeria’s Federal Government has proposed a N50 billion fund to attract investment and support seed businesses. Agriculture and Food Security Minister Abubakar Kyari announced the proposal at the Seed Connect Africa Conference in Abuja, according to AgroNigeria.

Kyari, represented by ministry Permanent Secretary Marcus Ogunbiyi, said limited access to affordable finance was a major challenge, particularly for small and medium-sized seed enterprises. He said the fund would support viable businesses and increase production and distribution of quality seeds, with transparent financing arrangements intended to encourage private investment.

Kyari urged banks, insurers, development partners and private investors to consider blended finance, credit guarantees and other risk-sharing tools. He also said the government would strengthen seed certification and market surveillance, and introduce digital systems for farmers to verify seeds.

Why it mattersSeed businesses, especially small and medium-sized firms, face limited access to affordable finance; the proposed N50 billion fund has no announced launch date or terms.
