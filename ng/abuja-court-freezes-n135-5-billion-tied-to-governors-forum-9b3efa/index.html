Abuja court freezes N135.5 billion tied to Governors’ Forum

The order bars the forum and 29 banks from moving the funds while the court considers a lawyer’s claim under a 2018 indemnity.

Sources: Vanguard, Premium Times and The Punch · 2026-11-24 · 1 min read

An Abuja High Court has frozen N135.5 billion linked to the Nigerian Governors’ Forum (NGF) in a dispute over Paris Club refund fees. Justice O. C. Agbaza issued the interim order on Nov. 19, barring the forum from withdrawing or transferring the funds and 29 commercial banks from dealing with NGF money in their custody.

The order followed an application by lawyer Ted Iseghohi-Edwards, who says a 2018 indemnity requires the NGF to pay him if the Federal Government is found liable for his fees. The judge directed the banks to disclose within 48 hours of service whether they hold accounts containing the disputed funds, including balances and currencies.

Iseghohi-Edwards says he demanded payment from the forum in a letter dated Oct. 8, 2026. The sum is equivalent to $100 million at the exchange rate stated in the court report.

Why it mattersThe NGF must keep the funds untouched, and 29 banks must report account details and balances within 48 hours of receiving the order.
