CAF returns to profit with $31.2 million net surplus

Africa's football governing body books a $31.2 million net gain on $284 million in revenue, its first profit after years of financial strain.

Sources: Premium Times and Nairametrics · 2026-11-22 · 1 min read

Africa's football governing body, the Confederation of African Football (CAF), has posted a net profit of $31.2 million for the financial year ended June 2026, on revenue of $284 million. The results were presented at CAF's 48th Ordinary General Assembly in Abuja on 21 November.

The figures show a return to profitability for a body that had spent years in financial difficulty, and the assembly was the setting for the presentation. CAF's leadership has credited its reforms under president Patrice Motsepe for the turnaround.

The reported figures do not include a breakdown of where the revenue came from or how the surplus will be used.

Why it mattersMember federations and sponsors now see the body back in the black, a shift that bears on how CAF spends its money.
