FG suspends Lagos-Ibadan bridge work after 20-hour gridlock

The federal government halted the work following a traffic jam on the expressway that lasted 20 hours.

Source: The Punch · 2026-11-20 · 1 min read

Nigeria’s federal government has suspended work on a bridge along the Lagos-Ibadan expressway after traffic gridlock there lasted 20 hours, The Punch reported on Friday.

The report identifies the roadwork as bridge work and the disruption as gridlock, but gives no details on when the suspension began, which bridge section is affected or how many motorists were caught in traffic.

The report provides no timetable for restarting the work or further details of the suspension.

Why it mattersMotorists using the Lagos-Ibadan expressway face suspended bridge work after a 20-hour gridlock, with no restart timetable reported.
