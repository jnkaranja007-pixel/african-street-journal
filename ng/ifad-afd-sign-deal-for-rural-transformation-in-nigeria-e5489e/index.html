IFAD, AFD sign deal for rural transformation in Nigeria

The International Fund for Agricultural Development and Agence Française de Développement will co-finance a programme supporting nine northern states.

Source: AgroNigeria · 2026-11-16 · 1 min read

The International Fund for Agricultural Development (IFAD) and the Agence Française de Développement (AFD) signed a co-financing agreement in Abuja to support the Value Chain Programme in Northern Nigeria (VCN). IFAD Country Director Mohamed El-Ghazaly and AFD Country Director Jacky Amprou signed the deal, which aims to reduce poverty and improve nutrition in rural communities.

The VCN programme, a flagship initiative of the Nigerian Federal Government, will be co-financed by IFAD, AFD, and the governments of Bauchi, Borno, Jigawa, Kano, Katsina, Kebbi, Sokoto, Yobe, and Zamfara states. IFAD will act as the lead co-financier, providing technical and support services for AFD's contribution, establishing a coordinated framework for joint financing and implementation.

IFAD and AFD urged the federal and state governments to accelerate remaining start-up activities, including preparations for a programme workshop scheduled for early December 2026. Both institutions reaffirmed their commitment to inclusive agricultural growth and resilient food systems in northern Nigeria.

Why it mattersIFAD and AFD have committed to co-financing a programme in nine northern Nigerian states to boost rural incomes and food security.
