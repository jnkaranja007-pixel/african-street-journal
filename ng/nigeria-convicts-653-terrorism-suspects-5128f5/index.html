Nigeria convicts 653 terrorism suspects

Attorney-General Lateef Fagbemi announced the convictions in the 11th phase of mass terrorism trials.

Source: Nairametrics · 2026-10-24 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Nigerian courts have convicted 653 suspects accused of terrorism-related offenses. The Federal Government's mass trial process has reached this figure after convicting individuals for belonging to Boko Haram, concealing information, or financing terrorism.

Attorney-General of the Federation, Lateef Fagbemi, stated that these convictions are part of the 11th phase of mass terrorism trials. The ongoing trials aim to process a large number of defendants indicted for terrorism.

The convictions were secured as part of the ongoing mass trials.
