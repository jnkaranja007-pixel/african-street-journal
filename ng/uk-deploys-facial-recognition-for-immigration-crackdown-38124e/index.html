UK deploys facial recognition for immigration crackdown

The United Kingdom plans to use live facial recognition technology at transport hubs and public events.

Source: The Punch · 2026-11-05 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

The United Kingdom government will expand the use of live facial recognition technology at transport hubs and public events. This measure is part of a major crackdown on immigration violations.

This new technology will be deployed to identify individuals violating immigration laws. The government aims to enhance its enforcement capabilities through this advanced system.

The UK's plan involves increasing surveillance at key transit points and public gatherings to detect and address immigration infringements more effectively.

Why it mattersNigerians seeking to enter or remain in the UK may face increased scrutiny and potential detention due to enhanced immigration enforcement.
