US basketball player arrested with 34kg cannabis in Lagos

Greene Da'monte Khiree was detained at the airport after abandoning luggage containing a potent strain of cannabis.

Source: BBC Africa · 2026-11-16 · 1 min read

US basketball player Greene Da'monte Khiree, 28, was arrested at Lagos airport on Wednesday with 33.95kg of cannabis.

The National Drug Law Enforcement Agency (NDLEA) said Khiree arrived from Thailand via the United Arab Emirates and was found with 32 packs of a potent cannabis strain known as "Loud" in his checked bags.

The agency stated that Khiree, who played professionally in Thailand, claimed he was visiting Nigeria to build career networks and that his phone data indicated his involvement in drug trafficking.
