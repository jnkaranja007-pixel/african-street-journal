Oyedele urges Nigerian firms to compete under AfCFTA

The finance minister says access to African markets will bring prosperity only if businesses produce competitively and diversify exports.

Sources: Vanguard and Nairametrics · 2026-11-27 · 1 min read

Nigeria’s Finance Minister Taiwo Oyedele called using the African Continental Free Trade Area (AfCFTA) to boost exports and diversify the economy an urgent national task at a forum in Abuja on Thursday. He said Nigeria must produce more competitively, earn more from its output and create better opportunities for Nigerians.

Abba Bello, head of the Nigeria Export-Import Bank, said the country must process more of its products to compete and earn more foreign exchange. He said many exports remain raw while Nigeria imports finished goods, citing cocoa and chocolate, cotton and textiles, and hide and skin and leather products.

Bello called for more manufactured and value-added exports and stronger value chains in agriculture and solid minerals. Tope Fashua, an economic adviser to the president, said Africa could no longer serve as a raw-materials warehouse for the rest of the world.

Why it mattersNigerian producers and exporters must process more goods and compete on production quality to earn more from trade under AfCFTA.
