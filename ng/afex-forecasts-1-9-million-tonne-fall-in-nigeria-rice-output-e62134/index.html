AFEX forecasts 1.9 million-tonne fall in Nigeria rice output

The exchange also projects a 1.5 million-tonne decline in maize production during the 2026/2027 market season, based on a survey of about 41,000 farmers.

Source: AgroNigeria · 2026-11-18 · 1 min read

AFEX Commodities Exchange forecasts Nigeria’s rice production will fall by 1.9 million metric tonnes in the 2026/2027 market season, according to its 2026 Wet Season Crop Production Report. The exchange also projects maize output will decline by 1.5 million metric tonnes.

The report draws on surveys of approximately 41,000 randomly selected farmers nationwide, alongside data from AFEX and other agricultural research bodies. AFEX said improved fertiliser availability during the 2026 wet season was partly linked to subsidy interventions by federal and state governments.

AFEX chief executive Akinyinka Akintunde called for stronger agricultural data systems and reforms to lift production capacity. The exchange said improved access to finance, agricultural infrastructure and targeted interventions were needed to address the projected declines.

Why it mattersRice and maize farmers face projected output losses, while AFEX calls for improved access to finance and agricultural infrastructure.
