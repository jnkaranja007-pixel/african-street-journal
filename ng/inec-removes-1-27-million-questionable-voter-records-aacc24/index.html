INEC removes 1.27 million questionable voter records

Nigeria's electoral commission aims to prevent a repeat of 2023 presidential poll failures ahead of 2027.

Sources: BusinessDay, The Punch and Vanguard · 2026-11-06 · 1 min read

Nigeria’s electoral commission has removed over 1.27 million questionable voter records from its register. The Independent National Electoral Commission (INEC) stated this action is part of efforts to address challenges that affected the 2023 presidential election. These measures are intended to improve processes for the 2027 general elections.

INEC Chairman Prof. Joash Ojo Amupitan met with traditional rulers in Kogi State on Thursday to seek their support for peaceful and credible elections. He visited Lokoja to assess the commission’s personnel and infrastructure preparedness for the 2027 general elections and an upcoming off-cycle governorship election. Amupitan also commissioned a storage facility for Bimodal Voter Accreditation System (BVAS) devices at the INEC state headquarters.

The commission is committed to strengthening its operational capacity through infrastructure upgrades and technological tools for seamless electoral processes. Traditional rulers in Kogi State pledged to communicate the INEC chairman’s message to their subjects, assuring the commission of their dedication to peaceful, transparent, and credible elections in the state.

Why it mattersNigerian voters will experience improved electoral safeguards in the upcoming 2027 general elections.
