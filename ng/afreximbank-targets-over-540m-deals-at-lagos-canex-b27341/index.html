Afreximbank targets over $540m deals at Lagos CANEX

The African Export-Import Bank aims to finance African music, film, and fashion businesses.

Source: The Punch · 2026-11-06 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

The African Export-Import Bank (Afreximbank) aims to secure creative industry deals exceeding $540m at the Creative Africa Nexus Weekend 2026 in Lagos. The bank seeks to expand financing for African music, film, fashion, and other cultural businesses.

Nigeria's creative industry is set to receive a financing boost from Afreximbank and Nigerian development finance. This initiative aims to unlock the potential of Nigeria’s creative economy.

The Creative Africa Nexus Weekend 2026 is scheduled to take place in Lagos. Afreximbank is committed to supporting the growth of cultural enterprises across the continent.

Why it mattersNigerian creative businesses will benefit from increased financing opportunities.
