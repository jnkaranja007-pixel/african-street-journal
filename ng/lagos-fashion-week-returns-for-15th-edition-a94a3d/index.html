Lagos Fashion Week returns for 15th edition

Africa's largest fashion event showcases dozens of Nigerian and African designers with a focus on sustainability.

Source: Africanews · 2026-10-31 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Lagos Fashion Week returned this week for its 15th edition, showcasing dozens of Nigerian and African designers. Models displayed unique African fashion sensibilities on the runway.

Cameroonian fashion designer Queen Tumenta stated that African creatives have not had a stage for a long time and are now sharing their existing abundance of talent. This year's theme, 'Crafted,' highlights the heritage and skill in the finished products.

The event, Africa's largest fashion gathering, comes as the world increasingly focuses on African fashion. Designers are using this spotlight to share their work and express their creativity.
