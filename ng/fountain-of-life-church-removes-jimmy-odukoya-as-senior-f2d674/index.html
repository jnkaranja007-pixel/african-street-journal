Fountain of Life Church removes Jimmy Odukoya as senior pastor

Trustees say they are enforcing the succession plan left by the late founder, Taiwo Odukoya, who died in 2023.

Sources: Vanguard, Premium Times and The Punch · 2026-11-17 · 1 min read

The Board of Trustees of the Fountain of Life Church in Lagos has removed Pastor Jimmy Odukoya as its senior pastor, with the decision taking immediate effect. The board announced it in a statement on Tuesday.

The trustees said the move followed a review of the church's constitution and the recorded instructions of its late founder, Pastor Taiwo Odukoya, who died in 2023. They described it as an institutional decision, but did not disclose the succession arrangements or name who will take over.

Odukoya and the church have rejected the decision. They maintain that the trustees acted outside the church's constitution and that his 2023 appointment remains valid.

Why it mattersOdukoya's congregation now has no recognised senior pastor, and the trustees have not yet said who will lead it.
