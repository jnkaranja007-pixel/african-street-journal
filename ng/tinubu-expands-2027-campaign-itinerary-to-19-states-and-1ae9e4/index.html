Tinubu expands 2027 campaign itinerary to 19 states and Abuja

The revised schedule adds several locations to the president's original campaign plan, according to Premium Times.

Sources: The Punch and Premium Times · 2026-11-19 · 1 min read

President Bola Tinubu has expanded his planned 2027 campaign itinerary to include visits to 19 states and the Federal Capital Territory (FCT), the capital region that contains Abuja, according to Premium Times. The revised schedule adds several locations to his original campaign plan.

The report gives no dates for the visits and does not name the newly added locations.

The report did not provide further details about the campaign schedule.

Why it mattersTinubu's campaign team must plan visits across 19 states and the FCT under the revised itinerary.
