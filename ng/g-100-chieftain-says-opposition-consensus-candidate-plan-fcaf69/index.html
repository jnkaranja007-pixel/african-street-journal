G-100 chieftain says opposition consensus candidate plan dead

A chieftain of the G-100 group stated that the plan for a consensus opposition candidate has failed.

Source: The Punch · 2026-11-09 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

A chieftain of the G-100 group announced that the opposition's plan to field a single consensus candidate has collapsed. The G-100 is a political group that has been working to unite opposition parties.

The chieftain, who was not named in the report, stated that the consensus candidate plan is now dead. This development comes after discussions among opposition figures, including Peter Obi, Bola Tinubu, and Atiku Abubakar, who were mentioned in relation to the political landscape.

The failure of this plan means that opposition parties may proceed with fielding their own individual candidates in future elections. The G-100 group's objective was to present a unified front against the ruling party.

Why it mattersNigerians seeking a unified opposition voice in upcoming elections will not see a single candidate.
