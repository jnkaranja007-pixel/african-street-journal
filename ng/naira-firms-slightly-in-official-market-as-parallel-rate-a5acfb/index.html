Naira firms slightly in official market as parallel rate stays above N1,440

The official interbank rate stood at N1,350.50 to the dollar early on Thursday, while street dealers in Lagos and Abuja quoted between N1,445 and N1,468.

Sources: Vanguard and Nairametrics · 2026-11-19 · 1 min read

The naira gained 0.10% in the official interbank market early on Thursday, November 19, 2026, opening at N1,351.81 and trading at N1,350.50 to the dollar on the Nigerian Foreign Exchange Market (NFEM). The gain follows the Central Bank of Nigeria's volume-weighted closing benchmark of N1,363.23 per dollar on Wednesday.

In the parallel market, Bureau de Change operators and street dealers in Lagos and Abuja quoted the dollar within a selling band of N1,445 to N1,468, with buying rates between N1,435 and N1,455. The spread between the official benchmark and the informal market stands between N90 and N105 per dollar.

Currency analysts attribute the gap to moderating autonomous foreign exchange inflows, heavier seasonal import demand and corporate obligations. Weekly inflows recently eased to $807.6 million as offshore portfolio investors turned cautious. Financial institutions expect the central bank to keep official rates within N1,350 to N1,365 through targeted liquidity support.

Why it mattersTraders, importers and families paying abroad face a spread of N90 to N105 per dollar between the official and street rates, so the rate they can get depends on the channel they use.
