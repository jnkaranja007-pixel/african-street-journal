28 state assemblies approve state police bill

President Bola Tinubu will soon receive the State Police Bill for his assent after legislative approval from 28 state houses of assembly.

Sources: Vanguard and The Punch · 2026-11-04 · 1 min read

The National Assembly is preparing to send the State Police Bill to President Bola Tinubu for his assent. This action follows the approval of the constitutional amendment by 28 state houses of assembly across Nigeria.

Omoboyede Olusanya, Chairman of the Nigerian Economic Summit Group (NESG), urged a focus on tangible improvements in jobs and incomes rather than just headline economic growth figures. Meanwhile, the African Action Congress (AAC) called for opposition parties to consider boycotting campaigns if safety is not guaranteed, citing concerns over restrictions and violence ahead of the 2027 elections.

The African Democratic Congress (ADC) is keeping its Sokoto rally venue secret due to security worries, with allegations that the Department of State Services (DSS) plans to disrupt the event. Separately, the Economic and Financial Crimes Commission (EFCC) discovered an illegal oil refinery operated by Chinese nationals in Ogun State.

Why it mattersNigerians will experience changes in policing and economic focus, while political parties face security challenges ahead of the 2027 elections.
