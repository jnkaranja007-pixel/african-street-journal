Nigeria's House suspends debate on farmers' loan waiver pending probe

Lawmakers want an inquiry into alleged diversion of nearly N2 trillion in government-backed farm financing before deciding who qualifies for relief.

Source: AgroNigeria · 2026-11-20 · 1 min read

Nigeria’s House of Representatives has suspended debate on cancelling farmers’ outstanding agricultural loans until an investigative report examines allegations that nearly N2 trillion in government-backed farm financing was diverted. The proposed waiver, brought forward by Rep. Inuwa Garba, would have eased debts owed by farmers.

Bamidele Salam, chairman of the House Committee on Public Accounts, cited findings attributed to the Auditor-General alleging that about N1.1 trillion from the Central Bank of Nigeria’s Anchor Borrowers’ Programme was diverted. Salam said some borrowers used farm loans for real estate and other businesses. He also linked the Bank of Agriculture’s financial difficulties partly to alleged diversion of N795 billion in loans.

Lawmakers said a blanket waiver should distinguish farmers who suffered genuine losses from borrowers who misused funds. Deputy Chief Whip Ayokunle Isiaka proposed that the agriculture and finance ministries, the central bank and the Bank of Agriculture develop rules to identify eligible beneficiaries and improve future loan management.

Why it mattersFarmers who lost crops to disasters and other emergencies must wait while lawmakers seek rules to separate eligible borrowers from those accused of misusing loans.
