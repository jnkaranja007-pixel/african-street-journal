Ex-Lagos civil servant found dead in UK home

Authorities are investigating the death of a former Lagos civil servant discovered in his United Kingdom residence.

Source: The Punch · 2026-11-14 · 1 min read

A former civil servant from Lagos, Nigeria, was discovered deceased in his home in the United Kingdom. The circumstances surrounding his death are currently under investigation by UK authorities.

Details regarding the identity of the former civil servant and the exact location of his residence within the UK have not yet been released. Investigations are ongoing to determine the cause of death and any potential contributing factors.

The discovery of the former civil servant's body prompts further inquiry into the welfare of Nigerians residing abroad and the processes in place for such incidents.
