Nigeria Governors’ Forum distances itself from Paris Club refund dispute

The dispute centres on $100m, though the available report does not say what that figure covers, who is seeking the refunds, or which governments are involved in the matter.

Source: The Punch · 2026-11-25 · 1 min read

The Nigeria Governors’ Forum has distanced itself from a dispute over Paris Club refunds, according to a headline published by The Punch on Wednesday. The headline describes the dispute as involving $100m.

The report’s available text gives no details of what the forum said, who is seeking the refunds or which governments are involved. It also does not explain what the $100m figure covers.

The Punch published the headline on 11/25/2026 at 07:03:16. Its report provides no further information about the dispute or any next step.
