Soludo approves N50,000 December bonus for Anambra workers

The governor said the payment is expected in December 2026 salaries and ordered secretariat road repairs, an ICT centre and tree pruning.

Sources: Vanguard and The Punch · 2026-11-21 · 1 min read

Anambra State Governor Chukwuma Soludo approved a N50,000 December bonus for the state’s civil servants, with payment expected in their December 2026 salaries. He announced it at the 2026 Civil Service Day celebration in Awka.

Soludo acknowledged that workers’ current pay was inadequate amid economic conditions. He said the state would make only commitments it could fulfil. In response to three requests from workers, he directed the rehabilitation of roads at the state secretariat, approved an Information and Communication Technology centre and authorised pruning overgrown trees.

Anambra’s Head of Service, Ngozi Iwouno, said workers could attend regularly without fear after disruption from Monday sit-at-home orders had been overcome.

Why it mattersAnambra civil servants are due an extra N50,000 in their December 2026 salaries, while secretariat workers will see roads repaired and an ICT centre established.
