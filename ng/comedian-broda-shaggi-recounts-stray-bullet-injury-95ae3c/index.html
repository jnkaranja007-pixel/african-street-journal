Comedian Broda Shaggi recounts stray bullet injury

Nollywood actor Samuel Perry, known as Broda Shaggi, described his fear and uncertainty after being shot by a stray bullet while filming a skit.

Source: The Punch · 2026-10-22 · 2 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Nollywood actor and comedian Samuel Perry, known as Broda Shaggi, has detailed his recovery from a stray bullet injury that halted his career for months. The incident occurred while he was filming a comedy skit under the Sango-Ota bridge. Perry recounted being overwhelmed by fear, seeing blood, and praying for survival until he received first aid at a hospital. He expressed concern about his ability to continue performing, as dancing and physical activity are integral to his work.

The injury led Perry to worry about his future in entertainment and whether fans would forget him during his absence. He underwent surgery in Nigeria and later sought further treatment and physiotherapy in Dubai, where his recovery progressed rapidly. Perry stated he could stand with support two days after the Dubai operation and walk with crutches by the third day, describing his survival as a second chance.

Perry, who had maintained a demanding work schedule since 2018, feared his prolonged absence would lead to public amnesia. He is now able to stand without crutches but still uses them for support. The comedian expressed gratitude for being alive and promised fans new projects, urging them to anticipate his return to their screens with exciting content.

Why it mattersThe comedian and his fans are affected as the injury disrupted his career and left him uncertain about his ability to perform.
