Lagos trains public school teachers in disaster preparedness

The state says teachers learned to identify risks, assess vulnerabilities and prepare schools for emergencies.

Source: The Punch · 2026-11-26 · 1 min read

The Lagos State Government trained public school teachers in risk assessment and disaster preparedness through its Resilience Office, which operates under the Ministry of Economic Planning and Budget. The session was themed “Building Resilient Teachers for Safe, Adaptive Schools.”

Lagos State Resilience Office Chief Resilience Officer Folayinka Dania said the training covered identifying risks, assessing vulnerabilities and preparing for emergencies in schools. Dania urged teachers to use what they learned, including by identifying risks early and taking preventive measures. Abisola Dokunmu-Adegbite, permanent secretary in the Ministry of Basic and Secondary Education, said teachers help identify risks and protect learners.

The state said interactive sessions were used to reinforce learning and develop practical understanding of resilience. It gave no number of participating teachers.

Why it mattersLagos public school teachers are expected to identify risks early and use preventive measures in their schools after the training.
