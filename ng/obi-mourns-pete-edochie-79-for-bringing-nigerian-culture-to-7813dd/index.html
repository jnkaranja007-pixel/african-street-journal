Obi mourns Pete Edochie, 79, for bringing Nigerian culture to global audiences

The former Anambra governor praised the veteran actor’s portrayal of Okonkwo in the television adaptation of Chinua Achebe’s Things Fall Apart.

Sources: Vanguard, Premium Times and The Punch · 2026-11-25 · 1 min read

Peter Obi, a presidential candidate of the Nigeria Democratic Congress and former Anambra State governor, mourned veteran Nollywood actor Pete Edochie after his family announced his death on Wednesday. Edochie was 79.

In a condolence message on X, Obi said Edochie helped bring Nigerian and African traditions to audiences around the world, particularly through his portrayal of Okonkwo in the television adaptation of Chinua Achebe’s Things Fall Apart. Obi also praised the actor’s command of English and Igbo and his experience in broadcasting.

President Bola Tinubu also mourned Edochie, calling him a Nollywood pioneer whose roles promoted Nigerian culture internationally, according to The Punch. Enugu State Governor Peter Mbah described the death as a personal loss to the state, Premium Times reported.

Why it mattersNigerian audiences and cultural institutions lose an actor whose performances carried local traditions to viewers around the world.
