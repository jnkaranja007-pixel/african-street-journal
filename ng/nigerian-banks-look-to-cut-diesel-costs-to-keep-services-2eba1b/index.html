Nigerian banks look to cut diesel costs to keep services running

Power interruptions can halt transactions, and banks are seeking ways to reduce their reliance on diesel generators.

Source: Nairametrics · 2026-11-24 · 1 min read

Nigerian banks depend on uninterrupted power to run automated teller machines, branches, digital banking systems and data centres, according to a Nairametrics report published on 24 November 2026. The report says diesel exposure is a cost banks can reduce while keeping those services running.

It says a power interruption can cause transaction downtime, operational disruption and reputational risk across the sector. Banks have relied on diesel for years to keep critical infrastructure operating, the report says, but it gives no savings figure and names no specific bank's plans.

The report does not identify a new measure or timetable for reducing diesel use.

Why it mattersBanks that reduce diesel reliance must still keep ATMs and digital systems running through power cuts.
