Team Gambia flag bearer pledges five-year term

Dr Lamin J. Darboe says his government will focus on rebuilding and reforms, not personal gain.

Source: Foroyaa · 2026-11-09 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Dr Lamin J. Darboe, flag bearer for the Team Gambia Grand Coalition 2026, has promised a five-year transitional government focused on national rebuilding, economic growth, and institutional reform if elected in December. Darboe stated on 5 November 2025 that he would not contest the 2031 presidential election, ensuring an open contest without incumbency advantage.

Darboe described his candidacy as a national duty to bring about desired change, emphasizing that the government would not replace one political establishment with another. He outlined aspirations for dignity, opportunity, justice, prosperity, and accountability, aiming to reduce poverty and dependence on external aid through support for mechanised farming, industry, business, investment, and skills training.

The coalition plans to reform education for practical skills and make quality healthcare affordable. Constitutional and institutional reforms will strengthen the rule of law, protect rights, and separate government powers. Darboe also pledged to prevent governments from using public resources for political advantage, citing concerns about political influence at Gambia Radio and Television Services.

Why it mattersGambians will experience a government focused on national rebuilding, economic growth, and institutional reform, with pledges for better education, healthcare, and fairer political contests.
