UDP diaspora representatives return to campaign for Darboe

The campaigners are accompanying the opposition candidate on a nationwide tour as he seeks to defeat President Adama Barrow on December 5.

Sources: The Alkamba Times and Kerr Fatou · 2026-11-23 · 1 min read

Dozens of United Democratic Party (UDP) diaspora representatives have returned to The Gambia to campaign for Ousainou Darboe, the opposition Gambia First Coalition’s presidential candidate, ahead of the December 5 election. They are accompanying Darboe on a nationwide tour as he challenges incumbent President Adama Barrow.

The campaigners said worsening living conditions prompted them to leave their homes abroad and return. Imam Lamin Jatta, one of those taking part, said the dalasi’s decline against major foreign currencies puts pressure on Gambians abroad who send money to support their families. Fatou Saine Sabally, a UDP supporter living in Helsinki, said conditions facing women, including farmers and entrepreneurs, motivated her return.

Sabally urged women to vote against Barrow and support Darboe on December 5, citing children who have taken dangerous irregular migration routes known locally as the “backway.”

Why it mattersGambians abroad who send money home face added pressure from the dalasi’s depreciation against major foreign currencies, Jatta said.
