Gambia urges Myanmar to comply with binding genocide ruling

The International Court of Justice found Myanmar responsible for genocide against the Rohingya and ordered it to make reparation.

Source: The Point · 2026-11-21 · 1 min read

The Gambia welcomed the International Court of Justice’s ruling that Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya, and said the court ordered Myanmar to make reparation. The government announced its response in a statement from Banjul dated 19 November.

The Gambian government said the judgment is final and binding and urged Myanmar to comply fully and without delay. It also called on the United Nations, the Organisation of Islamic Cooperation and the wider international community to support implementation.

The Gambia brought the case in November 2019, with support from the Organisation of Islamic Cooperation. President Adama Barrow credited Rohingya survivors and witnesses whose testimony, he said, helped make the outcome possible.

Why it mattersGambia’s government must press Myanmar and international bodies to carry out the court’s binding order for reparation to Rohingya survivors.
