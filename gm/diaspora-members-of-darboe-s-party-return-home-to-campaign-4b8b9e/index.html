Diaspora members of Darboe's party return home to campaign before December 5 vote

Dozens of diaspora members of the United Democratic Party (UDP) have come back to The Gambia to join the nationwide tour of opposition candidate Ousainou Darboe against President.

Sources: The Alkamba Times, The Point and Kerr Fatou · 2026-11-24 · 1 min read

Dozens of diaspora members of the United Democratic Party (UDP), an opposition party in The Gambia, have returned home to campaign for Ousainou Darboe ahead of the December 5 presidential election. They are travelling with Darboe, the flagbearer of the Gambia First Coalition, a UDP-led alliance, on his nationwide tour as he seeks to unseat President Adama Barrow.

The participants told The Alkamba Times that worsening living conditions compelled them to leave their homes abroad. Imam Lamin Jatta said the continuous fall of the dalasi against major foreign currencies places severe pressure on diaspora Gambians who send remittances to support families. Fatou Saine Sabally, a critic of the Barrow administration who lives in Helsinki, Finland, said the government had neglected women farmers and entrepreneurs.

The campaigners urged voters to back Darboe. Separately, Lamin J. Darboe, presidential candidate for the Team Gambia Grand Coalition, pledged on November 18 that, if elected, his government would give Gambians living abroad the right to vote.

Why it mattersGambians abroad who send money home face pressure from the dalasi's fall, and voters must decide on December 5 whether to back a change of government.
