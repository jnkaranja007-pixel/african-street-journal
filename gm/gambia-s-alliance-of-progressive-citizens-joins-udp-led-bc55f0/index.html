Gambia's Alliance of Progressive Citizens joins UDP-led coalition before presidential vote

Lamin Sima signed a memorandum of understanding in Jarra Soma, aligning his party with the United Democratic Party (UDP) alliance behind Ousainou Darboe.

Sources: The Alkamba Times and Kerr Fatou · 2026-11-27 · 1 min read

The Alliance of Progressive Citizens (APC), led by Lamin Sima, joined the Gambia First Coalition (GFC) at a signing ceremony at the UDP bureau in Jarra Soma on 26 November. The UDP-led alliance has endorsed lawyer Ousainou Darboe as its flagbearer for the 5 December presidential election.

Sima, who withdrew from the presidential race, is the second cleared candidate to leave the contest and join the coalition. He said the APC's decision was unanimous and followed consultations that included Gambians in the diaspora, and that party supporters would campaign alongside GFC and UDP partners. The coalition now comprises 11 political parties and seven political movements.

Darboe welcomed APC's entry and urged alliance partners to work collectively and strengthen outreach. Recent polls indicate that incumbent President Adama Barrow holds only a narrow lead.

Why it mattersAPC supporters are expected to campaign alongside GFC and UDP partners in the final stretch before the 5 December vote.
