Gambia poll finds lowest voting intention in Kanifing and Banjul

Gambia Participates found 89% of Kanifing respondents and 90% in Banjul intend to vote in the Dec. 5 presidential election.

Sources: The Point and Kerr Fatou · 2026-11-20 · 1 min read

A third-round opinion poll by Gambia Participates found that 89% of respondents in Kanifing and 90% in Banjul said they intended to vote in Gambia’s Dec. 5 presidential election. Those were the lowest figures among the country’s eight local government areas.

In both areas, 10% said they were undecided or unsure about voting, the highest shares in the poll. Janjanbureh and Kuntaur each recorded 98% intending to vote, while Basse, Kerewan and Brikama each recorded 97%; Mansakonko recorded 96%.

Nationally, 95% said they intended to vote and about 4% were undecided. Gambia Participates said the figures reflect stated intention at the time of the survey, not actual turnout; national intention rose from 92% in its second round.

Why it mattersVoters in Kanifing and Banjul have the highest reported uncertainty about participating, with 10% in each area undecided or unsure ahead of Dec. 5.
