Touray pledges cost-of-living taskforce within 100 days if elected

The independent candidate says the taskforce would review measures affecting essential-goods prices, alongside proposed constitutional and public-sector reforms.

Sources: The Point and Kerr Fatou · 2026-11-26 · 1 min read

Independent presidential candidate and former Vice President Isatou Touray has pledged a National Cost of Living Taskforce within the first 100 days of her administration if elected in Gambia's December 5 presidential election. Her campaign platform also includes constitutional reforms, youth employment and more reliable electricity.

Touray said the taskforce would review measures affecting the prices of essential commodities. She has also proposed enforcing asset declarations for senior public officials and increasing transparency in public contracts, as well as investing in electricity infrastructure and agricultural production.

At a campaign address in Sotokoi on November 19, Touray promised a new constitution, jobs for young people and stable electricity. Her proposals also cover healthcare, technical and vocational training, and school infrastructure.
