ActionAid urges peaceful campaigning ahead of Gambia’s Dec. 5 vote

The non-partisan group called on candidates, parties and supporters to obey electoral laws and keep political competition free of intimidation and violence.

Sources: The Alkamba Times and Kerr Fatou · 2026-11-18 · 1 min read

ActionAid International The Gambia has urged political parties, presidential candidates, supporters and the public to campaign peacefully and follow electoral laws as campaigning begins for the Dec. 5, 2026, presidential election. In a statement issued Monday, the non-partisan organisation said political competition must respect peace and tolerance.

The organisation called on party leaders and candidates to honour the Peace Pact they signed through their conduct during the campaign, on election day and after results are announced. It urged the Independent Electoral Commission to act independently and fairly, and security agencies to protect citizens and electoral participants while exercising restraint.

ActionAid also asked citizens, media and social media users to respect differing views and verify information before sharing it. It said voters must be able to cast ballots without intimidation, while electoral complaints should be resolved through legal and institutional processes.

Why it mattersVoters must be able to cast ballots without intimidation on Dec. 5, while complaints are to be handled through legal and institutional processes.
