Lamin J. Darboe files nomination for Gambia presidential election

Team Gambia aspirant pledges rule of law and transparency if elected president on December 5.

Source: Kerr Fatou · 2026-11-12 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Lamin J. Darboe, presidential aspirant for the Team Gambia Grand Coalition, submitted his nomination papers to the Independent Electoral Commission (IEC) on Wednesday for the December 5 election. He pledged to establish a government founded on the rule of law, transparency, and respect for citizens’ dignity if elected.

Darboe stated that good governance is his foremost priority, arguing that meaningful national development cannot occur without accountable and transparent leadership. He believes The Gambia’s persistent governance challenges since independence in 1965 have contributed to the country’s stagnation.

Lawmaker Almameh Gibba, contesting under the APRC No To Alliance, also submitted his papers, outlining priorities including national unity, improved health outcomes, and food self-sufficiency. Gibba also criticized the slow pace of security sector legislative action and called for a full accounting of assets linked to the former president.

Why it mattersGambia's presidential election on December 5 will determine the country's future leadership and governance approach.
