Minister cites mathematics teacher shortage as major challenge

Dr. Drammeh identified a shortage of mathematics teachers as a significant factor in poor student performance. She told QTV on Aug. 18 that the issue is compounded by low tertiary enrollment in the subject.

Why it mattersThe lack of qualified staff leaves some schools without any mathematics instruction, hindering student academic progress.

Gambia to play Somalia at Independence Stadium

Coach Jonathon McKinstry announced that the first Afcon 2027 qualifier against Somalia will be played at the Independence Stadium next month. This marks the first home match for the team under McKinstry since he took over in May 2024.

Why it mattersThe return to the national stadium allows the Scorpions to play in front of a home crowd for the first time in over two years.

PPA dissolves merger with Gambia For All

The Patriotic Progressive Alliance (PPA), led by Dr. Ousman Gajigo, has ended its merger with Gambia For All. The PPA stated it will instead join the Unite/NUP alliance for the 2026 election.
