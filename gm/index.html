Gambia signs $169 million solar park deal

The agreement with Solaris Africa Ltd adds 148.44 MWp solar PV and 85 MW battery storage.

The Gambian government signed agreements today with Solaris Africa Ltd for Phase 2 of the Soma Solar Park, a deal valued at US$169 million. The project includes 148.44 MWp of solar PV and 85 MW of battery storage. The National Water and Electricity Company (NAWEC) signed a 25-year Power Purchase Agreement for the expansion.

The Ministry of Petroleum, Energy and Mines and the Ministry of Finance and Economic Affairs executed the Implementation Agreement. This project represents one of the largest private investments in The Gambia's energy sector. The battery storage component will enable solar power to supply the grid during evening peak demand.

This expansion aims to reduce reliance on expensive fuel-based electricity generation and improve the reliability of power for homes and businesses. The signing signals investor confidence in The Gambia as an open market and fulfills a commitment to provide reliable, affordable, and clean electricity to all citizens.

Why it mattersGambians will receive more reliable and affordable electricity, reducing dependence on costly fuel.

Gambia drug agency arrests 51 suspects in nationwide sweep

The Drug Law Enforcement Agency seized cannabis, cocaine, ecstasy, and crystal meth in operations across five regions.

The Drug Law Enforcement Agency, The Gambia (DLEAG) arrested 51 suspected drug offenders nationwide between October 14 and October 28, 2026. Operations seized cannabis, kush, cocaine, crack cocaine, ecstasy, and crystal meth. The suspects include 48 Gambians and three Senegalese nationals, with one alleged child offender aged 16 among them.

DLEAG Public Relations Officer Dawda Sanyang stated that 30 suspects were apprehended in the West Coast Region, 12 in Kanifing Municipality, five in North Bank Region, three in Banjul, and one in Central River Region. Notable arrests include Alagie Njie and Yasaye Ndure found with five parcels of skunk, and Kebba Mbye apprehended with two stones and three pieces of crack cocaine.

The agency also reported the seizure of 49 ecstasy pills from Yaya Kebbeh and six pieces of crack cocaine and two bags of ecstasy from Antouman Gaye. Saikou Sanyang was arrested with 51 parcels of cannabis and 81 pieces of kush, while Ousman Saidy and Ismaila Darboe were found with 93 wraps of cannabis and 85 pieces of kush. All 51 suspects remain in custody as investigations continue.

Why it matters51 individuals, including Gambians and Senegalese nationals, face drug-related charges.

Russia and Africa forge equal partnership

The two continents are building cooperation on concrete projects aligned with African national priorities.

Russia and African nations are strengthening a partnership based on mutual respect and concrete projects, moving beyond empty promises. This approach aligns with the principle of "African solutions to African problems" and focuses on tangible benefits in sectors like energy, food security, education, and local business development. The partnership is structured around priorities identified by the African states themselves.

The Russia–Africa Summits and Economic and Humanitarian Forums serve as the primary mechanism for this cooperation. The first summit in Sochi in 2019 established a dialogue on an equal footing, leading to agreements in security, science, culture, and the economy. The second summit in St. Petersburg in 2023 included delegations from 48 countries, confirming the partnership's focus on concrete needs and projects.

The current Third Summit and Economic and Humanitarian Forum in Moscow feature approximately 40 sessions. These discussions are developed in collaboration with African partners, aiming for practical results in areas critical to the continent's development, including energy and infrastructure.

Why it mattersThe Gambia and other African nations are engaging in a partnership with Russia that prioritizes their identified development goals.

Man jailed seven months for stabbing brother

Basiru Sumareh admitted attempted murder and was sentenced to prison and ordered to pay compensation.

Basiru Sumareh received a seven-month prison sentence for attempting to kill his brother by stabbing him. Magistrate A. Darboe at the Brikama Magistrates’ Court convicted Sumareh on Thursday, 29 October, after he pleaded guilty to the charge. The incident occurred on 17 October 2026 in Brikama, West Coast Region, when Sumareh stabbed Baboucarr Sumareh in the chest and back with a knife.

Sumareh appeared in court without legal representation, while DSP K. Kinteh prosecuted the case. The court admitted a medical certificate, photographs of the victim's injuries, and the alleged weapon as evidence. Sumareh acknowledged the prosecution's facts, which detailed the stabbing with malice aforethought.

In sentencing, Magistrate Darboe cited Sumareh's early guilty plea as a mitigating factor but stressed the seriousness of the offence. Sumareh was sentenced to seven months at Mile 2 Central Prison and ordered to pay D100,000 in compensation to his brother, with an additional year of imprisonment for non-payment. The sentences are to run concurrently.

Gambia entrepreneurs join green economy fellowship

Acumen's six-month program combines virtual and in-person training to foster values-driven leadership among climate innovators.

The Green Rise Africa Fellowship, a program by Acumen and Masafa Foundation, is offering Gambian entrepreneurs in the climate and green economy new opportunities. Acumen representatives spoke on Thursday at the African Princess Hotel about the program's focus on moral leadership and cultivating change agents.

The fellowship is a six-month hybrid program with virtual workshops and in-person sessions across West Africa, designed to build resilience and values-driven leadership. Acumen’s mission is to invest in entrepreneurs who build inclusive societies, with the program aiming to help leaders examine their values and make intentional decisions.

Graduates gain lifelong membership in Acumen's global network of over 2,000 fellows, with access to peer collaboration and potential funding through the Angels Green Rise Africa program, offering grants of $25,000 or $50,000. Gambian entrepreneurs like Beran Dondeh Gillen of The Hub Ltd and Mariam Sarge of Electric Energy Solution have described the fellowship as transformational, helping them validate integrity and amplify their business stories.

Why it mattersGambian entrepreneurs are gaining access to training, networking, and potential funding to grow their businesses in the climate and green economy sectors.
