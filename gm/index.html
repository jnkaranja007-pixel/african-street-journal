Team Gambia Concludes Consultations for National Coalition

A five-month nationwide consultation by Team Gambia has concluded, with a final report presented to stakeholders ahead of formal coalition building.

Team Gambia has announced the completion of a five-month nationwide consultation aimed at forming a broad national coalition before the December 2026 presidential election. Co-coordinators Sidia Jatta and Halifa Sallah presented the final report on Saturday at a closed-door meeting at Paradise Suites Hotel. The report synthesizes views gathered from a diverse group of Gambians, including political parties, academics, civil society organizations, trade unions, business leaders, professional bodies, faith leaders, and individuals with disabilities.

Key political figures such as APP-Sobeyaa leader Essa Faal, NUP leader Lamin J Darboe, and lawyer Assan Martin were present at the report's presentation. Participants received copies of the report and were asked to provide feedback by Friday, September 12th. Team Gambia stated that formal coalition building will commence on that same day during the next participant meeting.

The coordinators emphasized that the consultation was not merely a discussion but a deliberate effort to foster unity and establish a shared national agenda. The initiative seeks to reset the political and governance landscape of The Gambia.

Why it mattersTeam Gambia's coalition building initiative affects political parties and civil society by seeking to unify them around a shared national agenda for the upcoming presidential election.

GIRAV Project Donates Vehicles to NAWEC for WASH Services

The donation aims to improve mobility and service delivery for water, sanitation, and hygiene initiatives across The Gambia.

The GIRAV project has strengthened the National Water and Electricity Company (NAWEC) operations through the donation of vehicles. These vehicles, funded by a USD 23 million budget under Component 6 of the GIRAV Project, are intended to boost NAWEC's ability to provide water, sanitation, and hygiene (WASH) services nationwide. This enhancement is particularly crucial for rural and peri-urban areas facing challenges in accessing clean water and sanitation, enabling faster response to issues and better monitoring.

Officials described the donation as timely and strategic, highlighting NAWEC's role in reaching communities. The vehicles will support field operations, improve water quality monitoring, and bolster sanitation programs, contributing to more resilient systems. This initiative is part of a larger collaboration that includes the rollout of smart meters to enhance efficiency and accountability in water and electricity usage across the country.

NAWEC expressed gratitude for the support, noting that the vehicles are tools for transformation that will extend the company's reach and strengthen its monitoring systems. The improved mobility is expected to directly enhance NAWEC's capacity to serve the public effectively, recognizing that access to clean water and reliable electricity is vital for agricultural productivity and community well-being.

Why it mattersFarmers and rural communities will benefit from improved water access and sanitation services due to enhanced NAWEC operational capacity.

Man arrested for insulting President, party, and army

Ibrahima Coly, 28, was detained in Ziguinchor after a video showed him making insults targeting Macky Sall and the Senegalese army.

Ibrahima Coly, 28, was arrested in Ziguinchor following the circulation of a video in which he made insulting remarks. The remarks specifically targeted the Alliance for the Republic (APR), its leader Macky Sall, and the Senegalese army. The Ziguinchor public prosecutor’s office initiated the case due to the video's widespread dissemination. Coly was apprehended by local police and is reportedly charged with public insults, public indecency via electronic means, and making death threats.

Coly, who identified himself as a security guard at the University of Ziguinchor, was taken into custody. The video led to his arrest and subsequent charges. The authorities are pursuing the case based on the content of the widely shared footage. The specific nature of the insults and threats is central to the ongoing legal proceedings.

This arrest highlights the legal consequences for public statements made against political figures and state institutions. The case is being handled by the Ziguinchor public prosecutor’s office, with Coly facing multiple charges. His claim of employment at the University of Ziguinchor has been noted in the proceedings.

Government Recovers D130 Million from Ghost Workers

Civil service reforms have led to the removal of over 2,000 non-reporting employees from the payroll, saving significant public funds.

The Gambian government has recovered 130 million dalasis following the removal of more than 2,000 "ghost workers" from the civil service payroll. Minister Bouy announced the savings on Friday, detailing reforms aimed at improving accountability and efficiency within public institutions. Staff audits conducted in 2024 and 2025 identified thousands of individuals who were either not reporting to their assigned duties or could not be accounted for, leading to their removal from active payment.

These reforms were implemented to address long-standing issues such as inadequate capacity, poor staff performance, and weak record management. The previous manual attendance system, which allowed for proxy sign-ins, contributed to the problem. To combat absenteeism and ensure accurate timekeeping, the Ministry of Public Service has introduced biometric attendance machines in several government offices and is developing a Human Resource Management Information System to digitize personnel records and strengthen payroll management.

Beyond payroll reforms, the government has also streamlined pension administration, ensuring benefits are processed within 30 to 90 days and are now calculated on gross pay for enhanced retirement benefits. Loan schemes for civil servants, including personal, housing, and car loans, have also been maintained, with assessments considering existing financial commitments to prevent excessive debt. These measures aim to provide civil servants with better financial security both during and after their service.

Why it mattersThe government saved 130 million dalasis by removing ghost workers, which directly impacts public finances and the efficiency of state operations.

Football Federation Elects New Leadership

Kemo Ceesay's administration takes the helm of Gambian football, tasked with unifying stakeholders and rebuilding institutional credibility.

Following the conclusion of elections, Gambian football now faces the critical task of restoring ambition and national purpose under the new leadership of Kemo Ceesay and his administration. The focus shifts from electoral competition to collective responsibility for the sport's future. Ceesay's mandate extends to serving the entire football family, including those who supported opposing candidates, emphasizing a need for reconciliation and unity.

The election outcome, while potentially disappointing for some, underscores the importance of continued constructive engagement from all stakeholders. Supporters of Musa Jammeh and Team Falcons are encouraged to remain vigilant and patriotic participants, recognizing that commitment to Gambian football transcends electoral results. Leadership is now defined by governing for all constituents, not just a majority, fostering inclusion over factionalism.

The incoming administration is urged to prioritize institutional transformation, addressing structural deficiencies rather than focusing on personalities. Key areas for development include credible governance, financial transparency, sustainable infrastructure, professional administration, grassroots development, and a competitive domestic league. The establishment of a professional league requires a foundation of financially viable clubs, enforceable contracts, and robust commercial partnerships.

Why it mattersGambian football stakeholders, including administrators and supporters, are affected by the need for institutional transformation and a focus on professional league development.
