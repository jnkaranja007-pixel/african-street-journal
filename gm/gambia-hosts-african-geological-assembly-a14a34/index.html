Gambia hosts African geological assembly

Experts meet in Banjul to discuss managing continent's mineral resources for economic growth.

Source: The Point · 2026-10-28 · 2 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

The Gambia is hosting the 18th General Assembly of the Organisation of African Geological Surveys (OAGS) for the first time. The three-day assembly, which opened on Monday, 26 October 2026, at the Sir Dawda Kairaba Jawara International Conference Centre, brings together geological experts, government officials, and international partners. The event aims to strengthen scientific cooperation and promote sustainable management of Africa’s mineral resources.

Organised by the Ministry of Petroleum, Energy and Mines, the gathering provides a platform for delegates from Africa and Europe to exchange knowledge and explore solutions to challenges facing geological surveys. Alieu Jawo, Director of the Geological Department, stated that the assembly is a vital opportunity to strengthen cooperation among geological institutions. Gabriel Data, OAGS President, highlighted the organisation’s role in advancing geological science and supporting Africa’s socio-economic development.

The assembly occurs as global demand for critical minerals surges, intensifying debate on how Africa can better assess, manage, and benefit from its resources. Minister of Petroleum, Energy and Mines, Hon. Nani Juwara, challenged African countries to end dependence on exporting raw minerals and importing finished products. For The Gambia, hosting the assembly is an opportunity to strengthen ties with regional and international partners while showcasing its geological potential.

Why it mattersAfrican nations are discussing how to better manage their mineral wealth to drive industrialization and job creation.
