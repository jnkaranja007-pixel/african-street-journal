Barrow campaign expects 300,000 supporters at Banjul return

The president is due back in Banjul on Thursday after a tour that the campaign says drew supporters and defections to his party.

Source: The Point · 2026-11-26 · 1 min read

President Adama Barrow’s campaign expects more than 300,000 supporters and sympathisers to line the streets for his return to Banjul on Thursday, after a nationwide tour ahead of the December 5 presidential election. The planned reception will begin at Kanlagie, with the crowd expected to pass through Brikama, Lamin, Tallinding and Westfield on its way to Banjul.

A campaign press release said tens of thousands of supporters of the United Democratic Party and Unite Movement for Change had reportedly defected to Barrow’s National People’s Party during the tour. It attributed their decisions to Barrow’s leadership, development record and commitment to transforming the country.

The campaign predicted a landslide for Barrow in the election, but the release gave no supporting vote figures. It said rallies across the country had focused on infrastructure, public services and national development.

Why it mattersBarrow’s campaign must turn the reported defections and expected turnout into votes at the December 5 presidential election.
