Gambia teachers threaten sit-down strike over unpaid allowances

The education ministry admits delays in hardship and double-shift payments for September to November and expects to pay by 30 November.

Source: The Point · 2026-11-24 · 1 min read

Gambian teachers are threatening a sit-down strike after hardship and double-shift allowances went unpaid for September, October and November. The Ministry of Basic and Secondary Education admits the delay and expects to pay by 30 November.

Teachers say they have kept teaching while waiting for allowances tied to extra work, particularly double-shift duties. One urged the Gambia Teachers' Union to act urgently and call a strike if payment is not made immediately. Another questioned whether a two-day change in the September reopening date was being used to hold back that month's money.

Teachers also said millions of dalasis had gone into the Foundation Learning Programme while the teachers running it were still unpaid. Ministry communications officer Saidou Jallow confirmed the delay and said management had noted the concerns.

Why it mattersTeachers doing extra duties are waiting three months for allowances, and the ministry expects to pay by 30 November.
