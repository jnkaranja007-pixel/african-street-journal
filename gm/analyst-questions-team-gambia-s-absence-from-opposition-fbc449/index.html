Analyst questions Team Gambia’s absence from opposition alliances

Lamin Juwara also says a proposed deal between the UDP and No-To-Alliance could affect the election and the country’s transitional justice system.

Source: Kerr Fatou · 2026-11-23 · 1 min read

Political analyst Lamin Juwara questioned why Team Gambia Grand Coalition has not joined a larger opposition alliance ahead of Gambia’s December presidential election. Speaking on Kerr Fatou’s Brunch show on Saturday, he said he had expected the coalition’s backers to seek a place in an alliance before nominations.

Juwara also identified the return of former President Yahya Jammeh as the No-To-Alliance’s main bargaining demand. He said the group would likely seek Jammeh’s return without accountability or conditions, and questioned which party would accept those terms. He described a possible deal between the United Democratic Party (UDP) and the No-To-Alliance as consequential for the election and transitional justice.

Juwara questioned how much influence No-To-Alliance candidate Almameh Gibba could have in such negotiations, and whether he would follow instructions from Jammeh sent through WhatsApp audio messages or decide independently.

Why it mattersThe No-To-Alliance and the UDP face a decision over whether to negotiate a deal involving Jammeh’s return ahead of the December election.
