Gambia entrepreneurs join green economy fellowship

Acumen's six-month program combines virtual and in-person training to foster values-driven leadership among climate innovators.

Source: The Point · 2026-11-01 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

The Green Rise Africa Fellowship, a program by Acumen and Masafa Foundation, is offering Gambian entrepreneurs in the climate and green economy new opportunities. Acumen representatives spoke on Thursday at the African Princess Hotel about the program's focus on moral leadership and cultivating change agents.

The fellowship is a six-month hybrid program with virtual workshops and in-person sessions across West Africa, designed to build resilience and values-driven leadership. Acumen’s mission is to invest in entrepreneurs who build inclusive societies, with the program aiming to help leaders examine their values and make intentional decisions.

Graduates gain lifelong membership in Acumen's global network of over 2,000 fellows, with access to peer collaboration and potential funding through the Angels Green Rise Africa program, offering grants of $25,000 or $50,000. Gambian entrepreneurs like Beran Dondeh Gillen of The Hub Ltd and Mariam Sarge of Electric Energy Solution have described the fellowship as transformational, helping them validate integrity and amplify their business stories.

Why it mattersGambian entrepreneurs are gaining access to training, networking, and potential funding to grow their businesses in the climate and green economy sectors.
