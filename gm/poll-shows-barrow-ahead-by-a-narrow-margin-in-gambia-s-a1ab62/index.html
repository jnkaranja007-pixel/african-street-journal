Poll shows Barrow ahead by a narrow margin in Gambia's three-way race

The survey by civil society group Gambia Participates found 19% of voters undecided ahead of the 5 December 2026 election.

Sources: Kerr Fatou and The Point · 2026-11-19 · 1 min read

Gambia Participates, a civil society group, has released an opinion poll placing President Adama Barrow just ahead in a tight contest with two rivals for the presidency. Of respondents asked whom they would back if voting took place now, 28% named Barrow.

19% of voters have yet to pick a candidate, a sizeable block with the 5 December 2026 election approaching. The poll sets that undecided share against Barrow's narrow lead.

The reported results do not name the other two leading candidates or give their shares of support.
