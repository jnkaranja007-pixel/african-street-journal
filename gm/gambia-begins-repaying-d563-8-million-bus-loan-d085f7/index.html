Gambia begins repaying D563.8 million bus loan

The government made its first principal and interest payment in May 2026 for buses procured for the Gambia Transport Service Company.

Source: The Point · 2026-10-30 · 1 min read

The Gambian government has started repaying a D563.8 million loan for buses acquired by the Gambia Transport Service Company (GTSC). The first payment covering principal and interest was made in May 2026, according to Finance Minister Seedy Keita.

Minister Keita informed the National Assembly that the D563,779,771.50 financing will be repaid over 10 semi-annual instalments. The repayment schedule began on April 30, 2026, and is scheduled to conclude by October 30, 2030.

The loan was provided by the Social Security and Housing Finance Corporation (SSHFC) to fund the purchase of GTSC buses. The government is expected to continue these bi-annual payments until the loan is fully settled.

Why it mattersThe government is repaying a significant loan for public transport buses, impacting public finances.
