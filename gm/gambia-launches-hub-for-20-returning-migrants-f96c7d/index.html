Gambia launches hub for 20 returning migrants

Kanifing Municipal Council offers services and business support to help returnees rebuild lives.

Source: The Point · 2026-10-30 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

The Kanifing Municipal Council (KMC) launched the Kanifing Migrant Resilience Hub on October 29, 2026, to support 20 returning migrants. The initiative, backed by the Mayors Migration Council's Global Cities Fund 2025, aims to help returnees rebuild their lives and reintegrate sustainably. Lord Mayor Talib Ahmed Bensouda led the launch, emphasizing the council's commitment to inclusive development.

Mayor Bensouda stated the hub addresses the reality of young people leaving Gambia due to perceived lack of opportunity. He praised the courage of those who returned and are now seeking to restart their lives. The Hub will connect participants with services, resources, and opportunities to strengthen their livelihoods and confidence. Businesses supported include waste collection, delivery services, tailoring, and car painting.

The Resilience Hub includes a childcare centre and psychosocial support services to allow participants to focus on their enterprises. KMC aims to foster collaboration with entities like Startup Incubator Gambia to create lasting opportunities. The council seeks to make Kanifing a place where young people can succeed locally by starting businesses and creating jobs.
