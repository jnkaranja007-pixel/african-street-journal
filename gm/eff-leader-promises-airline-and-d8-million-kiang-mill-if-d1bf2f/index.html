EFF leader promises airline and D8 million Kiang mill if Darboe wins

Prince Ebrahim Sanyang says he will launch an airline within 30 days of a Darboe victory and donate D100,000 to youth sports.

Sources: The Alkamba Times and Kerr Fatou · 2026-11-28 · 1 min read

Prince Ebrahim Sanyang, leader of the Economic Freedom Fighters (EFF), pledged to launch Gambia International Airways and establish a D8 million milling company in Kiang if opposition candidate Ousainou Darboe wins the December 5 presidential election. Sanyang made the promises on Thursday at a campaign gathering in Kiang Dumbuto.

Sanyang said the airline would open within 30 days of a Darboe victory. He said milling machines were stored in a Banjul warehouse and ready to be handed to the Kiang Association to manage. He also pledged D100,000 for a youth sports development fund, to be given to Landing B. Sanneh, chairman of Mansa Konko Area Council, to oversee.

Sanyang is part of the UDP-led Gambia First Alliance. He told supporters that investors had lost confidence in the Barrow administration and were seeking change; that was his assessment.

Why it mattersKiang residents and youth sports beneficiaries would receive the promised mill and D100,000 fund only if Darboe wins on December 5.
