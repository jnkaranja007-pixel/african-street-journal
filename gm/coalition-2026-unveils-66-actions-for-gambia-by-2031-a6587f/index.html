Coalition 2026 unveils 66 actions for Gambia by 2031

The manifesto promises constitutional review, increased health spending, and a new development bank.

Source: The Point · 2026-10-26 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Coalition 2026, led by UNITE leader Talib Ahmed Bensouda, launched its manifesto titled ‘The Gambia We Deserve’ on October 22, 2026. The plan outlines 3 pillars, 13 policy points, and 66 priority actions to be completed by 2031. Bensouda stated the manifesto is built around governance and trust, human development, and economic integrity.

The manifesto includes a constitutional review and referendum within two years and aims to raise health spending to 15% of the budget. It also proposes establishing a National Development Bank to drive job creation and economic growth. Bensouda highlighted that Gambians face a high cost of living, youth unemployment, and challenges in farming.

The coalition's vision is structured around three pillars: governance and trust, human development, and economic integrity. Bensouda assured that the manifesto contains specific actions with proposed deadlines, aiming to deliver what matters with discipline, transparency, and results.

Why it mattersCoalition 2026's manifesto proposes reforms and actions that directly affect Gambian citizens if elected.
