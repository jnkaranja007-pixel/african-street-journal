Gambia lets 2-1 lead slip to draw with Ivory Coast

Musa Barrow scored a 79th-minute penalty, but Ivory Coast equalised with the final kick after Musa Badamosi was sent off.

Source: The Alkamba Times · 2026-11-18 · 1 min read

The Gambia drew 2-2 with Ivory Coast at Independence Stadium after surrendering a 2-1 lead in the closing minutes. Yankuba Minteh equalised in the 68th minute, and Musa Barrow converted a penalty in the 79th to put the Scorpions ahead.

Musa Badamosi was shown a red card after a late confrontation, leaving The Gambia with 10 players. Coach Jonathan McKinstry said the team had lost its discipline, and said Ivory Coast scored with the final kick of the match after stoppage time stretched toward nine minutes.

McKinstry said he would review video of the incident that led to Badamosi’s dismissal, while declining to use the decision as an excuse. He said The Gambia had played well enough to earn the lead but gave it away through a collective failure of discipline.
