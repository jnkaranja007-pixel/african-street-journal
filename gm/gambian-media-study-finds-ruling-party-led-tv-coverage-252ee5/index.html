Gambian media study finds ruling party led TV coverage before vote

The ruling National People's Party held up to 91.3% of party airtime on state broadcaster GRTS, while the opposition United Democratic Party's press mentions rose.

Source: The Alkamba Times · 2026-11-22 · 1 min read

A baseline media report on Gambia found the ruling National People's Party (NPP) took most party-related television airtime in the six months before the official campaign for the 5 December 2026 presidential election. The analysis covered 316 evening news bulletins from state broadcaster GRTS and private stations QTV (Quantum Television) and Star TV between April and September.

On GRTS, the NPP held 91.3% of party airtime in April to June and 85.6% in July to September, while President Adama Barrow alone accounted for roughly 32% of tracked airtime in both periods. The report, published by Gambian computer scientist Muhammed Njie of the analytics firm Cubixio, counts a politician's airtime toward their party, including coverage of official duties.

In press articles mentioning a party or leader, the United Democratic Party (UDP) rose from 22.5% to 33.5% of such articles between the two quarters. The report cautions that the mix of outlets changed between quarters, so the figures should not be read as a direct like-for-like trend.

Why it mattersVoters and rival parties go into the 5 December vote with a documented gap in television airtime favouring the ruling party.
