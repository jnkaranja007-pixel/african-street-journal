Gambia's opposition coalition vows to hold rally despite warnings

Ousainu Darboe, the UDP leader and coalition candidate, says the Boiram meeting will proceed under the timetable the electoral commission approved, after the Janjanbureh meeting.

Sources: The Point and Kerr Fatou · 2026-11-26 · 1 min read

Ousainu Darboe, leader of the United Democratic Party (UDP) and presidential candidate of the UDP-led coalition, said the group would hold its planned campaign rally in Boiram despite warnings of possible interference. Speaking at a political gathering in Bantanto, he told the Independent Electoral Commission, the police and the armed forces not to prevent the meeting.

Darboe said the coalition had cancelled a scheduled meeting in Janjanbureh but would go ahead in Boiram under the campaign timetable approved by the electoral commission. He said the coalition had received information suggesting its activities could be obstructed, and warned that blocking the meeting would undermine democratic participation.

At the same gathering, speakers raised rising prices of basic goods, security sector reform and youth empowerment. The presidential election is scheduled for December 5.

Why it mattersVoters in the Central River Region will see whether the Boiram meeting goes ahead, with the electoral commission, police and armed forces now answerable to Darboe's warning.
