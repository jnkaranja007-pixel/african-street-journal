Bensouda pledges Banjul-Barra bridge before 2036 if elected

The Coalition 2026 presidential candidate says the link would strengthen trade and help make the North Bank Region The Gambia's commercial capital.

Source: Kerr Fatou · 2026-11-18 · 1 min read

Talib Ahmed Bensouda, the presidential candidate of Coalition 2026, has pledged to build a bridge linking Banjul and Barra before 2036 if elected. He made the promise at spot meetings in Essau and Pakau Njoku and at a rally in Munyagen, in Jokadou.

Bensouda said the North Bank could become The Gambia's commercial capital, given its location near Senegal and regional cities such as Dakar and Bamako. He said the bridge is a key commitment in his coalition's manifesto and that without such a link the region would struggle to reach its full economic potential.

He said the bridge was something the government under President Barrow could not deliver. "Within 10 years, we will bridge Banjul and Barra," Bensouda said. "Before 2036, there will be a Banjul-Barra Bridge."

Why it mattersCommercial activity on the North Bank depends on the link, Bensouda says, so the bridge would decide whether the region grows into a trading hub.
