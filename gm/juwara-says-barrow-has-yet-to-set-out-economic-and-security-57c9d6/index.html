Juwara says Barrow has yet to set out economic and security policies

The political analyst says the president’s campaign has focused on roads and infrastructure ahead of The Gambia’s December 5 presidential election.

Sources: Kerr Fatou and The Alkamba Times · 2026-11-27 · 1 min read

Political analyst Lamin Juwara says President Adama Barrow has not outlined specific economic or security policies as he campaigns for a third term in The Gambia’s December 5 presidential election. Juwara made the comments on Kerr Fatou’s Politic Kaacha show.

Juwara said Barrow’s campaign speeches have focused on road construction and other infrastructure projects, with little attention to economic hardships or growing security concerns. He said the president was repeating themes from the 2021 campaign and had not presented measures to ease hardship.

The campaign is under way nationwide. Juwara did not identify specific proposals or set out a next step.

Why it mattersVoters weighing Barrow’s bid for another term are being asked to assess a campaign that, Juwara says, has not detailed responses to economic hardship or security concerns.
