Sarjo Mballow Barrow links Bafuloto development to third-term vote

The Second Lady told residents their development requests would be met if Adama Barrow wins the December 5 presidential election.

Sources: Kerr Fatou, The Point and The Alkamba Times · 2026-11-21 · 1 min read

Sarjo Mballow Barrow, The Gambia’s Second Lady, urged Bafuloto residents to re-elect President Adama Barrow for a third term at a campaign gathering ahead of the December 5 presidential election. She said the community’s development needs would be met if her husband returned to office.

Mrs. Barrow told residents that the president would provide what they had requested if he won, and said Gambians could count on him to respond to requests while he remained in office. She described him as the only leader capable of developing the country.

She said she was confident Bafuloto residents would vote for Barrow. The campaign appeal did not specify which development requests residents had raised.

Why it mattersBafuloto residents seeking local development are being asked to back Barrow before the December 5 presidential election.
