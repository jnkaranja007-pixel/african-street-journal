Bensouda pledges separate Area Council and university for Foni

The Unite Party leader and coalition presidential candidate says the changes would bring administration closer to residents and keep young people in the region for tertiary.

Sources: The Point and Kerr Fatou · 2026-11-27 · 1 min read

Talib Ahmed Bensouda, leader of the Unite Party (UNITE) and flagbearer of the Unite Coalition 2026, pledged during a campaign tour of Foni to establish the region as a separate Area Council or administrative area and build a university there. He made the promises in Foni Bondali, saying residents deserved greater administrative recognition and development attention.

Bensouda said a separate council would bring governance closer to communities and improve services. He said young people should be able to pursue tertiary education in Foni rather than travel elsewhere. He also pledged to improve healthcare, pointing to Bondali Hospital's limited maternity facilities, including one maternity bed in a large ward.

He criticised President Adama Barrow's administration over irregular migration, linking it to unemployment, poverty and poor leadership, and promised more jobs for young people. Bensouda also pledged better salaries and living conditions for public-sector workers and informal traders.

Why it mattersFoni residents would have a local Area Council and a university to access under Bensouda's pledges, which he would need to deliver if elected.
