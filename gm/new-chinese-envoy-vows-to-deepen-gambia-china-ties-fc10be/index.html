New Chinese envoy vows to deepen Gambia-China ties

Ambassador Xiao Yewen pledged to boost cooperation in trade, infrastructure, agriculture, health, and education.

Source: The Point · 2026-11-17 · 1 min read

The new Chinese Ambassador to The Gambia, Xiao Yewen, has pledged to strengthen ties between Beijing and Banjul. At a reception on Thursday, 12th November 2026, marking the start of his official duties in Banjul, he said he is ready to work alongside the Government, Parliament, political parties, businesses, youth groups and every sector of society.

Speaking at the Sir Dawda Kairaba Jawara International Conference Centre, Xiao Yewen said the aim is to widen exchanges in trade, infrastructure, agriculture, health, education and vocational training. Government officials, diplomats and guests attended the event, which included cultural performances. He noted that China offers a zero-tariff policy to The Gambia and other African diplomatic partners, and that the Greater Banjul Area Electricity Improvement Project will soon break ground.

Over the past decade, China has funded study places for around 400 outstanding young Gambians in China, he said, and the Confucius Institute at the University of The Gambia is a channel for learning about each other. He also pledged closer coordination with Gambia in multilateral forums to defend the shared interests of developing countries.

Why it mattersThe new Chinese Ambassador pledged to deepen cooperation in trade, infrastructure, agriculture, health, and education, with a zero-tariff policy and an upcoming electricity project benefiting Gambians.
