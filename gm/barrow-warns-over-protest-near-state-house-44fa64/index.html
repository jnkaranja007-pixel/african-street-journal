Barrow warns over protest near State House

President Adama Barrow described a candlelight march towards the State House as a treasonable offence.

Source: Foroyaa · 2026-10-23 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

President Adama Barrow warned Banjul North National Assembly Member Momodou Lamin Bah against organising future protests, describing a recent candlelight march towards the State House as a treasonable offence. The President stated on 19 October 2026 that the protest, organised after a 48-hour electricity outage in Banjul, crossed a red line. He cautioned that any repeat would result in severe consequences, contrasting his government's response with that of former President Yahya Jammeh.

Mr Barrow also highlighted government development projects in Tobacco Road, suggesting the NAM had not assisted his constituents or supported government efforts. He urged voters to consider electing a different representative. The President made these remarks during his Meet the People Tour in Banjul, where he also visited skills training centres, a mini-stadium renovation, and a water storage tank.

Hon. Bah responded by rejecting the President's claims, calling the protest peaceful, lawful, and dignified. He stated it was organised to demand accountability and improved service delivery, with candles held instead of weapons. Bah asserted the demonstration followed required notification and was protected under the constitution guaranteeing freedom of assembly and expression.

Why it mattersPresident Barrow's strong reaction to a protest affects the political climate for citizens and their representatives.
