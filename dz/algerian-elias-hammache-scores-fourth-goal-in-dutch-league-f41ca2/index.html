Algerian Elias Hammache Scores Fourth Goal in Dutch League

The 23-year-old midfielder netted a crucial equalizer for his club Cambuur against AZ Alkmaar in a 2-2 draw.

Source: Echorouk Online · 2026-10-21 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Algerian player Elias Hammache continues his strong performances in the Dutch league, scoring another goal for his club Cambuur. The 23-year-old, who joined the team last summer from Schalke 04, has found his form in recent weeks after a shaky start. He has now scored four goals this season, contributing to Cambuur's recent 2-2 draw against AZ Alkmaar with a remarkable strike.

Hammache's goal against AZ Alkmaar adds to his tally, which includes previous goals against Twente and a brace against NEC Nijmegen. The promising forward is looking to revive his career in the Dutch league after an unsuccessful stint in Germany with Schalke, which included a loan spell at Amiens in France last season. He previously showed early promise with Valenciennes.

Despite having represented France at youth levels, Hammache's primary objective remains to play for the Algerian senior national team as soon as possible. His recent performances in the Netherlands indicate a player regaining confidence and form, potentially putting him on the radar for national team selection.
