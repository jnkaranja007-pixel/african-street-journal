Algeria schedules El Fadjr director’s trial for 29 November

Hadda Hazem was charged after criticising an initiative encouraging children to attend dawn prayers at mosques.

Sources: TSA Algerie and Algerie360 · 2026-11-23 · 1 min read

Hadda Hazem, director of Algeria’s El Fadjr newspaper, is scheduled to stand trial on 29 November over comments about the “Men of El Fadjr” initiative, which encourages children to perform dawn prayers in mosques. Hazem criticised the initiative in a TikTok message on 13 November.

The dispute drew a response from Louisa Hanoune, secretary-general of the Workers’ Party, who called at a rally in Oran on 21 November for authorities to close the case and stop prosecuting Hazem. Hanoune also criticised the lack of an official response to a gathering organised by Egyptian Hala Samir during the 2026 Algiers International Book Fair.

Algeria’s Council of Imams had called on judicial authorities to investigate Hazem’s statements in a communiqué published on 15 November. On the same day, lawmaker Omar Maamer of the Movement of Society for Peace urged the justice minister to intervene.

Why it mattersHazem must appear in court on 29 November over her comments on the children’s mosque-prayer initiative.
