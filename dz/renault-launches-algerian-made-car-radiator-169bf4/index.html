Renault launches Algerian-made car radiator

The Motrio brand radiator, produced in partnership with FB Radiateur, will be available through Renault's network starting November 2026.

Source: TSA Algerie · 2026-11-04 · 1 min read

Renault Algérie announced on Tuesday, November 3, 2026, the launch of a new Motrio radiator manufactured locally with FB Radiateur. These radiators will be progressively sold through Renault's authorized network starting this month.

The new product meets Groupe Renault's quality, performance, and durability standards, showcasing Algerian manufacturers' ability to meet international automotive industry requirements. Hichem Nacer-Bey, General Director of Renault Algérie, stated this launch reinforces their after-sales offer for customers.

FB Radiateur's General Director, Salah Eddine Bouali, expressed pride in contributing their industrial expertise to the Motrio brand. FB Radiateur, part of the Bouali group, specializes in automotive cooling systems and operates a factory in Ain M’lila, Oum El Bouaghi wilaya.

Why it mattersAlgerian car owners will soon have access to locally produced radiators for Renault vehicles, improving parts availability.
