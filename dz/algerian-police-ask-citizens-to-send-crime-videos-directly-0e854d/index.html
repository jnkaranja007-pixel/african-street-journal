Algerian police ask citizens to send crime videos directly to them

An officer says footage of offences and dangerous driving helps investigators, while police and gendarmerie urge people to submit videos rather than post them online.

Source: TSA Algerie · 2026-11-19 · 1 min read

Algeria's police are urging citizens to film offences and assaults and send the videos to the force's Facebook page. Rabah Kaci, a traffic regulation and control officer with Algiers' regional police, made the appeal on Radio Algeria's Channel 2 on Tuesday, 17 November.

Kaci said citizen footage had helped police work, including videos showing crimes and dangerous overtaking by buses. Security services have arrested several alleged road-code violators after videos were recorded and shared online. In Ouargla, police opened an investigation after footage of an assault in June and arrested four suspected attackers.

Police arrested a suspect in a car assault in late September after a video circulated online. In October, a police unit in Ouled Fayet announced an arrest linked to assault footage; in August, gendarmes in Tebessa arrested a man in a kidnapping case based on a citizen's video. Police and gendarmerie say citizens should send footage to them instead of publishing it on social media.

Why it mattersResidents who film offences now face a choice between sharing footage online and sending it to investigators, and the authorities prefer the second.
