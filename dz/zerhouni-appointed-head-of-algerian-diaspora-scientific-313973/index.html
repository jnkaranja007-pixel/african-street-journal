Zerhouni appointed head of Algerian diaspora scientific council

President Tebboune names the Algerian-American professor to lead the newly created body aimed at leveraging expatriate expertise for national development.

Source: TSA Algerie · 2026-11-15 · 1 min read

Professor Elias Zerhouni has been officially appointed president of the High Council of the Algerian Community Abroad, following a presidential decree by Abdelmadjid Tebboune. The decision, published in the official gazette, names the Algerian-American radiologist and medical professor to lead the newly established scientific council.

The High Council of the Algerian Scientific Diaspora, initiated by the head of state, aims to involve expatriate Algerian scientists in the country's development efforts. The council held its first meeting in Algiers in June, with Zerhouni serving as coordinator, and its missions were detailed in a decree published on September 7. Zerhouni stated that the goal is to collaborate on national development, bridging the knowledge of those abroad with those within Algeria.

Zerhouni, born in Nedroma in 1951, built a significant medical and professorial career in the United States, including a tenure as director of the National Institutes of Health (NIH) from 2002 to 2008. The council, comprising 39 members, met with President Tebboune in late September. Its mandate includes strengthening the participation of expatriate scientific expertise and proposing ways to connect them with the national higher education and research system.
