Algerian exporter offers Bangladesh dates at $2 to $5 a kilogram

Algeria's ambassador says shipments could reach Bangladeshi ports in 20 to 30 days once procedures are completed, while Bangladesh seeks supplies before Ramadan 2027.

Source: TSA Algerie · 2026-11-26 · 1 min read

An Algerian private exporter has offered to supply dates to Bangladesh at $2 to $5 per kilogram, including transport, Algeria's ambassador to Bangladesh, Abdelouahab Saidani, told Commerce Minister Khandakar Abdul Muktadir in a meeting on the direct import of Algerian dates by public and private sectors. Bangladesh wants to secure its market before Ramadan 2027.

Saidani said Algeria is in its date season with substantial stocks, and that the agriculture ministry had already sent a proposal to supply dates at competitive prices. Shipments could reach Bangladeshi ports in 20 to 30 days if the necessary procedures are completed.

Saidani also briefed the minister on Algeria's new regulations on exporting halal products. Algeria exported 185,000 tonnes of dates in 2025, earning $184 million, while Tunisia earned $347 million from 147,000 tonnes, according to trade data cited by the report.

Why it mattersBangladeshi buyers face a proposed price of $2 to $5 per kilogram, with transport included, and shipments of 20 to 30 days depend on procedures being completed.
