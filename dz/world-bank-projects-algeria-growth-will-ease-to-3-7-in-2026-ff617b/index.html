World Bank projects Algeria growth will ease to 3.7% in 2026

The forecast follows 3.8% growth in 2025, supported by public investment and domestic demand as hydrocarbon revenues fell.

Sources: Algerie360 and TSA Algerie · 2026-11-21 · 1 min read

Algeria’s economy grew 3.8% in 2025, led mainly by activity outside hydrocarbons, according to the World Bank’s autumn 2026 report. The bank forecasts growth will slow to 3.7% in 2026 as energy markets remain volatile and investment moderates.

Public investment and strong domestic demand supported growth last year despite lower hydrocarbon export receipts. Inflation fell to 1.4% for 2025, while the budget deficit narrowed to 10% of gross domestic product from 14% in 2024, the report said.

The current-account deficit reached $25.3 billion in 2025 as imports rose and hydrocarbon export earnings fell with lower prices and volumes. The bank expects higher oil prices to help reduce current-account and budget deficits in 2026, but says rising domestic energy use, imports and public spending will limit the improvement. It says stronger, more job-creating growth requires gradual reforms to diversify the economy and improve budget discipline, governance and public-company performance.

Why it mattersPublic spending and domestic energy use are among the pressures limiting the forecast improvement in Algeria’s budget and current-account deficits in 2026.
