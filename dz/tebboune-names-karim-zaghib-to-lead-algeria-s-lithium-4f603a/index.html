Tebboune names Karim Zaghib to lead Algeria's lithium-battery factory

The Algerian scientist, who spent 28 years at Hydro-Québec in Canada, will transfer his expertise to build battery manufacturing at home.

Sources: Echorouk Online and Algerie360 · 2026-11-25 · 1 min read

Algerian President Abdelmadjid Tebboune has appointed Karim Zaghib to lead the country's lithium-battery factory project, with a mandate to transfer his expertise and develop the industry locally. Zaghib, born in Constantine in 1963, has worked on lithium batteries for more than three decades.

His record includes more than 450 scientific publications and participation in more than 970 patents. At Hydro-Québec, the Canadian company, he spent 28 years helping develop rechargeable batteries that were commercialised, according to Echorouk Online.

His work included lithium iron phosphate batteries, now a major option for electric vehicles and electricity storage. The report gives no construction schedule or production target for the Algerian factory.
