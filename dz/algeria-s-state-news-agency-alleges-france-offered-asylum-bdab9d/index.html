Algeria's state news agency alleges France offered asylum for testimony against Algeria

The Algerian Press Service (APS), the state news agency, says consular agent Smaïl R. has been held in France for 596 days without valid reason.

Sources: TSA Algerie and Algerie360 · 2026-11-26 · 1 min read

The Algerian Press Service (APS), the state news agency, alleged on Wednesday, 25 November, that French authorities offered asylum to Smaïl R., an Algerian consular agent detained in France since April 2025, in exchange for testimony against the Algerian state. The agency said he was subjected to humiliation and mistreatment in prison to pressure him to testify.

The APS said a judge threatened Smaïl R. and the Algerian government at his first hearing. It described the investigation as resting on what it called an insignificant telephone geolocation, and said the agent was arrested in the street in a manner it called ostentatious. It also linked the case to former French interior minister Bruno Retailleau, whom it described as having a central role. These are the agency's claims; the report includes no response from French authorities.

The APS noted that Retailleau is now a candidate in the 2027 presidential election and has not been interior minister since October 2025. It said the detention continues, citing 596 days. It also invoked the 1961 Vienna Convention and the 1963 Vienna Convention on Consular Relations as protections it says apply to Smaïl R.

Why it mattersAlgeria's government faces the APS's allegations of an asylum offer tied to testimony against the state, while the agent's detention has reached 596 days.
