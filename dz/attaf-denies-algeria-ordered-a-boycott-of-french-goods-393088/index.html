Attaf denies Algeria ordered a boycott of French goods

The foreign minister said French suppliers lost ground on price and competition, and urged Paris to use the EU-Algeria Association Council to discuss the dispute.

Sources: TSA Algerie and Algerie360 · 2026-11-27 · 1 min read

Algerian Foreign Minister Ahmed Attaf denied on Thursday in Algiers that the government had ordered French products excluded from its market. France has decided to take the issue to the European Commission, according to media reports cited by the account.

Attaf said French operators’ declining market share reflected economic factors, not political instructions. He said Algeria was willing to discuss the dispute at the EU-Algeria Association Council, which he described as the institutional forum for disagreements between the parties to their agreement.

Attaf said Algeria changed its wheat supplier rules in 2021, opening procurement to new operators, including Romania, because of price and competition. He also said Algeria now buys milk powder from the Netherlands and Poland on terms he described as more acceptable than France’s. He said data through October 2026 contradicted French allegations.

Why it mattersAlgerian and French officials now have the Association Council as the stated forum for resolving their trade dispute.
