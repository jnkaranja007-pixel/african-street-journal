Brahimi scores twice to lead Al-Gharafa to victory

Algerian international Yacine Brahimi scored two goals in the final 10 minutes of the match.

Source: Echorouk Online · 2026-10-31 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Algerian international Yacine Brahimi starred for his club Al-Gharafa in their Qatar Stars League match against Qatar SC. Al-Gharafa defeated Qatar SC by two goals to nil, with Brahimi scoring both goals and earning man of the match honours.

Brahimi opened the scoring in the 74th minute and added his second goal in the 84th minute. The match also featured fellow Algerian internationals Adam Ounas and Ismael Bennacer, who both delivered strong performances.

The victory came in the eighth round of the Qatari league. Brahimi's brace secured the win for Al-Gharafa against Qatar SC.
