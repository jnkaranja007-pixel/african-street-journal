Algeria bars construction and fuel freight during local elections

The Gendarmerie says the restriction runs until 05:00 on 29 November, while food deliveries remain exempt.

Sources: TSA Algerie and Algerie360 · 2026-11-27 · 1 min read

Algeria’s National Gendarmerie has barred trucks carrying construction materials or fuel, and freight trains, from operating from midnight on 27 November until 05:00 on 29 November, during local elections scheduled for 28 November.

The restriction covers cargo such as gravel, sand and wood, but not vehicles supplying food, including milk, flour, meat, fruit and vegetables, the Gendarmerie said in a statement on its Tariki Facebook page. The locally competent wali, or governor, can grant exceptional permits for fuel tanker movements.

The Interior Ministry’s order of 16 November provides for restrictions on freight vehicles during the 27–29 November period, the Gendarmerie said.

Why it mattersFood suppliers can continue deliveries of staples, while fuel tanker operators need an exceptional permit from the locally competent wali to travel during the restriction.
