Storms in Algeria injure two people and bring down a wall and four trees

Civil protection services recorded the damage in several wilayas on Thursday morning as strong winds swept through the country.

Source: Echorouk Online · 2026-11-12 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Civil protection services reported material damage in several Algerian wilayas on Thursday morning after volatile weather and strong winds. In Mascara, a wall collapsed on a house in the municipality of Mascara, damaging the roof of the building and leaving two people in shock.

In Blida province, strong winds brought down two trees in the municipality of Blida and another in Boufarik. In the wilaya of Medea, one tree fell in the municipality of Medea because of the strong winds.

The incidents come as several regions of the country face unstable weather, and warnings about strong winds remain in place.
