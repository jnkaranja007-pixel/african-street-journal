Algeria seeks diaspora help to reach 8 million tourists by 2029

Tourism Minister Houria Medahi urged Algerians abroad to invest, share expertise and promote the country as the government targets more visitors.

Source: TSA Algerie · 2026-11-22 · 1 min read

Algeria is asking its diaspora to help develop tourism as the country targets 8 million visitors by 2029. Tourism Minister Houria Medahi and Sofiane Chaib, the secretary of state responsible for Algerians abroad, chaired a meeting with diaspora business operators, investors, tourism professionals and startup representatives on Thursday.

Medahi outlined investment opportunities in hotels and other accommodation, desert and mountain tourism, wellness, leisure facilities, tourist transport and digital services, according to a Tourism Ministry statement. She said diaspora involvement should extend beyond financing to transferring expertise, modernising management, building international partnerships and attracting tour operators.

The minister also called for promotion of Algeria as a destination in foreign markets and for local products and crafts to be included in tourism routes.

Why it mattersDiaspora investors and tourism professionals are being asked to provide financing, expertise and partnerships toward the 2029 target of 8 million visitors.
