Algeria reports 63 % rise in online payments to 391 billion DA

Government figures show card-terminal payments also rose, while cash withdrawals from ATMs fell during the first nine months of 2026.

Source: TSA Algerie · 2026-11-19 · 1 min read

Online payments in Algeria reached 391 billion dinars (DA) in the first nine months of 2026, up 63 % from the same period in 2025, the government said after a meeting chaired by Prime Minister Sifi Ghrieb on Wednesday, 18 November.

Payments through electronic payment terminals totalled 133 billion DA, up 48 %, while mobile payments reached 56,4 billion DA. ATM withdrawals fell 14 % to 3 777 billion DA, according to a statement from the prime minister's office.

The government reviewed progress on a roadmap covering regulatory and security rules, electronic-payment services and sector-specific payment methods. The plan also seeks to modernise public banking and financial systems, reduce cash use and improve tracking of transactions nationwide.
