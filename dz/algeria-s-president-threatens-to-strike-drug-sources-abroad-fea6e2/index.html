Algeria's president threatens to strike drug sources abroad

President Abdelmadjid Tebboune called the flood of drugs into Algeria a real war and warned the country could be forced to act at the source.

Sources: TSA Algerie and Algerie360 · 2026-11-16 · 1 min read

President Abdelmadjid Tebboune has warned that Algeria may be forced to go to the source of drug manufacturing, calling the flood of narcotics into the country a real war against its people and youth. He made the remarks on Sunday, 15 November, at the opening of the 2026-2027 judicial year at the Supreme Court in Algiers, before a gathering of magistrates.

Tebboune said any such action would be taken under the right of legitimate defence. The military review El Djeich, published by the National People's Army (ANP), made a similar charge in its November issue, listing drug trafficking among hostile efforts against Algeria alongside disinformation campaigns and sabotage.

Earlier, on 3 October, Tebboune had said drugs had become a means of war against Algeria, describing it as a hybrid war waged by saboteurs targeting young people. Large quantities of drugs, mainly from Morocco and the southern borders, are regularly seized by ANP units and security services.

Why it mattersTraffickers moving drugs into Algeria face the threat of action at their source, and the state has already converted a Sahara military barracks at Tanezrouft into a prison for drug traffickers.
