Algerian army breaks up drug network and arrests 17

Army security services seized 19 quintals of processed cannabis from Morocco, along with vehicles, motorcycles and 273 million centimes.

Sources: TSA Algerie and Algerie360 · 2026-11-22 · 1 min read

Algerian army security services dismantled a drug-trafficking network during the week and arrested 17 people, the Defence Ministry said Saturday 21 November. The network was trying to bring 19 quintals of processed cannabis from Morocco into Algeria, the ministry said.

The operation also seized 50 utility vehicles, five motorcycles, a jet ski, mobile phones and 273 million centimes, according to the ministry. It said the security services acted in the army’s 2nd Military Region, but did not identify the arrested people or say what legal steps they would face.

Separately, Algerian border guards shot dead two Moroccan drug traffickers in the Naâma region on Friday after they refused to stop while attempting to bring more than 50 kilograms of cannabis into Algeria, the ministry said.

Why it mattersThe 17 arrested people face the consequences of the network’s dismantling, while the army security services seized 50 utility vehicles and 273 million centimes.
