Algeria’s parallel market asks 27 700 dinars for 100 euros

Currency dealers offered 27 450 dinars to buy 100 euros on Tuesday, against the central bank’s official rate of 150,49 dinars per euro.

Source: Algerie360 · 2026-11-25 · 1 min read

Currency dealers in Algeria asked 27 700 dinars for 100 euros on the parallel market on Tuesday 24 November, while offering 27 450 dinars to buy the same amount. The selling quote equals 277 dinars per euro, according to rates reported by Algerie360.

The Bank of Algeria set the official euro rate at 150,49 dinars per euro. The two prices leave a wide gap between the parallel market and the banking circuit for people exchanging euros.

Dealers quoted 24 600 dinars to buy 100 US dollars and 24 900 dinars to sell them. For 100 pounds sterling, the buying quote was 29 500 dinars and the selling quote was 30 000 dinars.
