Algeria signs Belarus tractor assembly deal as farmers face machinery shortages

The agreement provides for gradual local integration of Belarus tractor components, alongside plans to develop harvesters for difficult terrain.

Source: TSA Algerie · 2026-11-20 · 1 min read

Algeria's state tractor company, the Entreprise publique des tracteurs agricoles (ETRAG), and the Minsk Tractor Plant signed an agreement on November 18 to assemble high-powered Belarus tractors in Algeria, with components to be integrated locally in stages. The deal was one of three agreements signed at a bilateral cooperation commission meeting in Minsk, co-chaired by Agriculture Minister Sid Ali Khaldi.

A second agricultural machinery agreement, between Sampo Algérie and Gomselmash, covers a study on developing and producing self-propelled combine harvesters suited to difficult terrain. Khaldi said the agreements moved projects from commitments to operational implementation. The report gives no production start date.

Algerian farmers faced a shortage of combine harvesters during the 2026 grain harvest, while some potato growers dug crops up with traditional tools and large numbers of poorly paid workers. The report says the shortages directly weigh on agricultural yields.

Why it mattersFarmers who lacked combine harvesters during the 2026 grain harvest are waiting for equipment projects that have no announced production start date.
