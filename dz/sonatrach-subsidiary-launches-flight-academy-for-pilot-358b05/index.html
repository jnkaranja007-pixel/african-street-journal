Sonatrach subsidiary launches flight academy for pilot training

Tassili Travail Aérien opens applications for three licensed courses beginning in early 2027.

Source: TSA Algerie · 2026-11-26 · 1 min read

Tassili Travail Aérien (TTA), a wholly owned subsidiary of the state hydrocarbon group Sonatrach, has launched TTA Flight Academy to train future pilots. The initiative follows the receipt of two new Cessna aircraft equipped for aeromedical missions. First courses begin on 2 January 2027.

The academy is approved by Algeria's National Civil Aviation Agency (ANAC). It offers three tracks: Private Pilot Licence, Commercial Pilot Licence, and Airline Transport Pilot Licence. Training combines theory, flight simulation, and practice on single- and twin-engine planes under experienced instructors.

Candidates must submit proof of nationality, birth certificate, ID copy, criminal record extract, and secondary school or baccalaureate results. Pre-registrations are open. TTA specializes in oil sector transport, medical evacuations, pipeline surveillance, and forest fire control.

Why it mattersAspiring aviators can now enroll in ANAC-approved courses at TTA Flight Academy, which starts training on 2 January 2027.
