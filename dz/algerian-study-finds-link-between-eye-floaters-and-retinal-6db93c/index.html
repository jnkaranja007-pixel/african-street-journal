Algerian study finds link between eye floaters and retinal tears

A study of 896 people found 9.9% with posterior vitreous detachment also had retinal tears.

Source: Echorouk Online · 2026-11-05 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

A study of 896 people in Algeria found that 89 individuals, or 9.9%, experienced retinal tears alongside posterior vitreous detachment.

Researchers also recorded 8 cases of retinal detachment and observed new tears in 3% of patients within two months who had no tears initially, according to pmc.

The findings highlight the importance of a retinal examination when new floaters appear, especially if accompanied by flashes of light or blurred vision.

Why it mattersAlgerians experiencing new floaters, flashes of light, or blurred vision should seek a retinal examination to detect potential tears or detachments.
