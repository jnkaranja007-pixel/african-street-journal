Algeria launches electronic land document certification

The national land directorate's digital portal now issues legally recognized electronic land documents.

Source: TSA Algerie · 2026-11-16 · 1 min read

Algeria's Directorate General of National Land (DGDN) has made land documents downloaded from its "Fada El Djazair" portal legally certified. The DGDN launched the third version of its digital portal on November 1, introducing electronic signatures that give documents official state recognition. This advancement aims to strengthen trust in digital land records and simplify procedures for citizens and institutions.

The electronic certification applies to cadastral extracts, copies of deeds, negative certificates, and cadastral copies. These PDF documents are signed electronically according to the mechanism recognized by the Electronic Certification Government Authority (AGCE). Users can verify the authenticity of these documents using Adobe Acrobat Reader, which shows a green indicator for valid signatures and content integrity.

The DGDN stated that electronically signed documents hold the same legal value as printed copies and do not require further printing for use. The "Fada El Djazair" portal has recorded over 8.8 million visits since the start of 2026, indicating significant public interest in these digital land services.

Why it mattersCitizens can now use legally recognized electronic land documents without needing to print them, simplifying administrative procedures.
