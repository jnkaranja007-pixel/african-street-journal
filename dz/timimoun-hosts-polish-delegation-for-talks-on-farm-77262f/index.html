Timimoun hosts Polish delegation for talks on farm investment

The visitors examined prospects for investment in strategic crops and partnerships with Algerian operators at the Tinerkouk El-Kebir farm area.

Source: TSA Algerie · 2026-11-28 · 1 min read

A high-level Polish delegation visited the Tinerkouk El-Kebir agricultural area in Timimoun province on Wednesday 25 November to examine farming opportunities, Algeria’s local agricultural services directorate said. The visit was organised on the agriculture minister’s instruction with local authorities and the head of the office for developing industrial agriculture on Saharan land.

Local agriculture officials, representatives of the agriculture chamber and investors from the province received the delegation. Discussions covered investment in strategic crops, technical expertise-sharing and possible direct or joint ventures between Algerian and Polish economic operators, the directorate said.

The visit follows an Italian partnership led by Bonifiche Ferraresi for durum wheat, cereals, pulses and food processing in Timimoun.
