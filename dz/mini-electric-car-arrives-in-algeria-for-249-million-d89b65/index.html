Mini electric car arrives in Algeria for 249 million centimes

The Livan electric car, designed for city use, has sparked debate among Algerian car enthusiasts.

Source: Algerie360 · 2026-10-29 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

A new mini electric car, the Livan, has entered the Algerian market, imported by individuals and appearing in private showrooms for approximately 249 million centimes. This price has generated significant online discussion, ranging from mockery to genuine curiosity.

The Livan is a four-seater, compact city car with an advertised range of about 120 kilometers per charge, making it suitable for short urban trips like commuting or errands. Its unusual design has divided opinions, with some finding it futuristic and others comparing it to vehicles from old science fiction films.

The car's 100% electric nature and zero fuel consumption are appealing arguments for potential buyers. It is marketed towards students, new drivers, or households seeking a second, economical vehicle for daily urban use.

Why it mattersAlgerians interested in purchasing a new, affordable, and electric vehicle for urban commuting are directly affected by the arrival and pricing of the Livan car.
