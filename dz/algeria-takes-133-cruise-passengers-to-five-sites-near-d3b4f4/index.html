Algeria takes 133 cruise passengers to five sites near Algiers

Le Champlain called at Algiers after Annaba, with a tour spanning the capital's landmarks and Tipasa's UNESCO-listed archaeological site.

Source: TSA Algerie · 2026-11-25 · 1 min read

Le Champlain, a cruise ship carrying 133 tourists of different nationalities, called at the port of Algiers 48 hours after docking in Annaba. The Algiers stop includes a tour of five sites in the capital and Tipasa, a coastal city.

The itinerary covers Algiers' Great Mosque, whose minaret is 265 metres tall, the Martyrs' Memorial, the Bardo National Museum and the neo-Moorish Grande Poste. Visitors are also due at Tipasa's archaeological site, listed as a UNESCO World Heritage site. Algeria's National Tourism Office welcomed the ship and gave foreign tourists promotional materials about the country.

The stop was organised by Liledo Travel, a tourism agency. Algeria has set a target of attracting 8 million foreign tourists by 2029.
