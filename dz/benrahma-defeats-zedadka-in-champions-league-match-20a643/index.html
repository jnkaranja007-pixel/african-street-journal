Benrahma defeats Zedadka in Champions League match

Algerian international Saïd Benrahma's team, Lyon, secured a 2-1 victory over Hakim Zedadka's Qarabag in the third round of the Champions League group stage.

Source: Echorouk Online · 2026-10-21 · 1 min read

Algerian international Saïd Benrahma's team, Lyon, secured a 2-1 victory over Hakim Zedadka's Qarabag in the third round of the Champions League group stage on Tuesday evening. Benrahma started the match for Lyon, while Zedadka was also in the starting lineup for Qarabag. Benrahma was substituted in the 72nd minute.

The win gives Lyon six points in the group, placing them in third position. Qarabag, with three points, is currently in fourth place. The top eight teams in the group advance directly to the knockout stage, while teams ranked ninth through twenty-fourth compete in playoffs for a spot in the next round. Teams ranked twenty-fifth through thirty-sixth are eliminated.

This result is significant for the group standings as Lyon aims to advance in the prestigious European competition. Both Algerian players featured for their respective clubs in this crucial fixture, highlighting their roles in European football.

Why it mattersAlgerian footballers Saïd Benrahma and Hakim Zedadka competed against each other in a European club competition.
