Cairo passers-by answer Algerian with football chant

A young man said he was Algerian on Al-Muizz Street, prompting sellers and passers-by to chant the slogan associated with Algeria’s football supporters.

Source: TSA Algerie · 2026-11-21 · 1 min read

Sellers and passers-by on Al-Muizz Street in central Cairo answered an Algerian visitor with a football chant on Sunday evening. After the young man introduced himself as Algerian, people around him spontaneously shouted, “One, two, three, viva l’Algérie,” a slogan used by Algerian supporters in stadiums.

Al Jazeera Egypt shared a video of the scene on X, and other media outlets picked it up. Online comments from Egyptians and Algerians exchanged messages of affection, while some users also recalled the tensions around the teams’ 2010 World Cup qualifying play-off.

One commenter recalled being given a free meal by an Algerian restaurateur during a stop at Algiers airport in 2010. Another said he still remembered Algerian defender Antar Yahia’s goal against Egypt in Omdurman on 18 November 2009.

Why it mattersThe Algerian visitor and Egyptian vendors and passers-by shared a public football chant, while users revisited memories of the 2010 qualifying crisis.
