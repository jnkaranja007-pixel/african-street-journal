Algerian gendarmes dismantle armed drug network across five wilayas

Authorities arrested 31 suspects and seized cocaine, cannabis, more than one million psychotropic pills and weapons in the two-week operation.

Sources: TSA Algerie, Echorouk Online and Algerie360 · 2026-11-28 · 1 min read

Algeria’s National Gendarmerie dismantled an armed network trafficking drugs and psychotropic substances across Béjaïa, Batna, Sétif, Bouira and Algiers during an operation lasting two weeks, the Defence Ministry said on Friday, 27 November. The operation led to 31 arrests.

The ministry said investigators seized 160 kilograms of cocaine, 150 kilograms of processed cannabis and more than one million Pregabalin pills. They also recovered 44 passenger and utility vehicles, seven motorcycles, a jet ski, two automatic pistols and a shotgun. The suspects were brought before the competent judicial authorities.

The ministry said a related money-laundering network transferred $1.98 million in criminal proceeds from drug trafficking to the United Arab Emirates. Investigators also recovered cash in Algerian and Tunisian currency, euros and counterfeit banknotes.

Why it mattersThe 31 suspects have been brought before judicial authorities, while investigators traced $1.98 million in drug proceeds transferred to the United Arab Emirates.
