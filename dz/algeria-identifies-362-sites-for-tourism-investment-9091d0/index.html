Algeria identifies 362 sites for tourism investment

Minister Houria Messaoudi announced the availability of land parcels across 26 provinces to boost the hospitality sector.

Source: Echorouk Online · 2026-10-30 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Algerian Minister of Tourism and Handicrafts, Houria Messaoudi, has identified 362 real estate plots suitable for tourism investment across 26 provinces. The minister chaired a coordination meeting to assess the status of tourism real estate and track efforts to inventory available plots for investors via the Algerian Agency for Investment Promotion's platform.

Messaoudi stated that developing domestic and Saharan tourism depends on providing prepared, exploitable land parcels free from technical and administrative obstacles. During the meeting, it was revealed that 150 of these plots are currently being developed within sectoral programs for tourism expansion zones.

The meeting also reviewed 240 plots made available to the Algerian Agency for Investment Promotion across 30 tourism expansion zones, with 31 land shares already granted to investors. Efforts are underway to demarcate these zones to protect them from encroachment and update land data in coordination with state property services and local authorities.

Why it mattersThe availability of 362 real estate plots aims to support tourism investment and improve the investment climate in Algeria.
