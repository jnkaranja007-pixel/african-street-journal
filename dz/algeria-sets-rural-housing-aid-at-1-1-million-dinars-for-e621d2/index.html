Algeria sets rural housing aid at 1.1 million dinars for mountain communes

Housing Minister Mohamed Tarek Belaribi says a decree listing eligible communes is imminent, following the increase from 700,000 dinars.

Sources: Echorouk Online and Algerie360 · 2026-11-21 · 1 min read

Algeria's housing minister, Mohamed Tarek Belaribi, said on Thursday that a ministerial decree listing eligible mountain communes in the northern wilayas and High Plateaus would be published soon. The decree will set which communes qualify for rural housing aid of 1.1 million dinars.

The increase raises support from 700,000 dinars for mountain communes in those regions and from 1 million dinars for some communes in the far south. Belaribi said the list will determine the appropriate aid for each area. Revising commune classifications required changes to regulations, and a joint working group involving the housing, finance and interior ministries was formed in 2025.

The 2027 finance law includes the higher aid and provides for rural housing and self-build support in social subdivisions. Belaribi also cited an instruction setting deadlines for each stage of aid payments, intended to address delays in allocating programmes, approving beneficiary lists and issuing building permits.
