Algerian man films women on TikTok, sparking privacy outrage

A 43-year-old man was arrested for secretly filming women and girls at a bus station and broadcasting live on TikTok with offensive commentary.

Source: Echorouk Online · 2026-10-20 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Authorities in Setif arrested a 43-year-old man for allegedly filming women and girls without their consent at the city's main bus station. The suspect is accused of broadcasting live footage on TikTok, accompanied by indecent remarks, targeting the dignity of families and societal values. This incident has ignited public debate regarding individual privacy and ethical conduct in the digital age.

The man reportedly exploited the busy environment of the transportation hub, which sees thousands of travelers daily, to carry out his actions. The live streams, intended to gain high viewership, allegedly included offensive commentary that violated public decency. The act has been described as criminal, exploiting personal privacy for online engagement.

This case highlights concerns about the misuse of smartphones and social media platforms, particularly TikTok, in Algeria. The incident has drawn attention to the vulnerability of citizens, especially women, to digital exploitation and the erosion of privacy in public spaces. Investigations into the full extent of the alleged violations are ongoing.

Why it mattersThe incident directly affects Algerian citizens, particularly women, by violating their privacy and dignity through non-consensual filming and online broadcasting.
