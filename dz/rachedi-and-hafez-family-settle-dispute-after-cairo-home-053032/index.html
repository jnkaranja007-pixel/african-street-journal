Rachedi and Hafez family settle dispute after Cairo home visit

The Algerian filmmaker visited the late Egyptian singer’s home with his nephew, who said a misunderstanding had been cleared up.

Source: TSA Algerie · 2026-11-23 · 1 min read

Algerian filmmaker Ahmed Rachedi visited the Cairo home of late Egyptian singer and actor Abdel Halim Hafez on 21 November, resolving a dispute with Hafez’s family over Rachedi’s comments about their past relationship. The visit came during the 47th Cairo International Film Festival.

Hafez’s nephew Mohamed Chabana had challenged Rachedi’s account of his ties to the singer and said he intended to take legal action. At the home, Chabana welcomed Rachedi and said there had been a misunderstanding over something the director said without thinking. Egyptian director Khaled Toufik was also present.

The dispute began at a 18 November masterclass, where Rachedi discussed an unrealised film project about Hafez. Rachedi said Hafez had inspired his interest in Egyptian cinema and had wanted a project showing the capabilities of Egypt and Algeria.

Why it mattersRachedi and the Hafez family have ended their public dispute, and Chabana said the director was welcome at the singer’s home.
