Dollar reaches 248 dinars on Algeria’s parallel market

The rate at Algiers’ Port-Saïd exchange square matched the dollar’s record, while the euro held at 276 dinars.

Sources: TSA Algerie and Algerie360 · 2026-11-22 · 1 min read

The US dollar traded at 248 dinars on Algeria’s parallel currency market on Saturday 21 November, matching its record against the dinar. The rate at Port-Saïd square in Algiers, the country’s main parallel exchange market, was two dinars below the 250-dinar threshold.

The dollar was quoted at 24 800 dinars per 100 dollars, a new record, after trading at 247.5 dinars on Tuesday and 245.5 dinars on 9 November, TSA Algerie reported. The euro remained at 276 dinars, unchanged from 10 days earlier.

On the official market, the dollar rose to 136.96 dinars on Friday 20 November, while the euro edged down to 150.43 dinars. The dollar’s official high was 147.17 dinars, set on 7 February 2022.

Why it mattersPeople exchanging dollars on Algeria’s parallel market paid 248 dinars per dollar on 21 November, two dinars short of 250.
