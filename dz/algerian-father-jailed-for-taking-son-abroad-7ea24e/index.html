Algerian father jailed for taking son abroad

A father was sentenced to 10 months for leaving his three-year-old son in Algeria, blocking the mother's visitation rights.

Source: Algerie360 · 2026-11-12 · 1 min read

A 34-year-old Algerian father received a 10-month prison sentence for taking his son to Algeria without his ex-wife's consent. The Lons-le-Saunier court in France convicted him on November 9 for failing to present the child, thereby preventing the mother from exercising her legal visitation rights.

The couple divorced in 2025, with the court granting custody to the father and visitation rights to the mother. In July 2026, the father took their three-year-old son to a village in Kabylie and left him with his ill grandmother, enrolling the child in a local school. He returned to France alone in mid-September.

The mother reported being unable to see her son, prompting legal action. During the hearing, the father claimed he would rectify his mistake, but his explanations regarding the child's situation and return plans appeared contradictory to the court. This is a first-instance ruling, and the father can appeal the decision.

Why it mattersA father's actions have resulted in a prison sentence and separated a child from his mother.
