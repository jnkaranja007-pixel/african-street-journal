Al Salam Bank Algeria offers firms leasing for equipment and premises

The Islamic bank pitched its financing, leasing and digital services at the TASSHIIL 2026 investment fair in Algiers.

Source: TSA Algerie · 2026-11-19 · 1 min read

Al Salam Bank Algeria presented financing, leasing and digital banking services to businesses at the TASSHIIL 2026 fair in Algiers, held from 16 to 18 November at the SAFEX Exhibition Palace. The Islamic bank said its offer is aimed at companies and professionals seeking investment finance and easier day-to-day banking.

Its Al Salam Leasing product covers professional equipment, such as public-works and transport machinery and medical equipment, as well as business premises. The bank said the firm chooses the asset and supplier, and the bank pays the supplier directly, with fixed rents for the full contract.

Contracts run for three to five years, and the business can become owner of the asset at the end of the term. The bank said the product is approved by its Sharia Committee and follows Islamic finance principles.

Why it mattersSmall and medium-sized firms can spread the cost of equipment and premises over three to five years at fixed monthly rents.
