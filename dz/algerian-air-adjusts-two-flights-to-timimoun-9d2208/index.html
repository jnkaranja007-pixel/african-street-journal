Algerian Air adjusts two flights to Timimoun

Flights AH6156 and AH6157 will depart later due to a temporary airport closure.

Source: Echorouk Online · 2026-10-28 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Algerian Air announced changes to two flights between Algiers and Timimoun, scheduled for October 29 and November 2. The airline stated the adjustments are due to the temporary closure of Timimoun airport during the original flight times.

Flight AH6156 from Algiers to Timimoun will now depart at 20:00, arriving at 21:30. The return flight, AH6157 from Timimoun to Algiers, will depart at 22:30 and arrive in Algiers at midnight. The company advised customers to check their flight times via commercial agencies or the contact center.

The airline did not specify when Timimoun airport will reopen.

Why it mattersAlgerian Air customers travelling between Algiers and Timimoun on October 29 and November 2 will experience altered flight schedules.
