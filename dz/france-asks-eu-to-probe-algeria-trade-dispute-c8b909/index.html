France asks EU to probe Algeria trade dispute

Paris claims Algiers is violating its association agreement by blocking French imports, escalating economic tensions.

Sources: TSA Algerie and Algerie360 · 2026-11-14 · 1 min read

France has asked the European Commission to investigate Algeria's alleged violation of its association agreement with the EU, following unconfirmed reports of a ban on French imports. The French foreign ministry stated on Friday that the move stems from complaints by French companies about a collapse in their exports to Algeria. This action escalates a growing economic disagreement between the two nations.

The dispute intensified after French businesses reported a significant drop in sales to Algeria. Algeria, in turn, has accused the EU of blocking its steel exports, with President Abdelmadjid Tebboune demanding reciprocity in trade relations on December 18, 2025. He specifically mentioned the issue of low quotas for Algerian steel exports to the EU during an event at the national production fair.

Relations between France and Algeria have been strained, with a recent diplomatic chill following a Paris court's decision on July 9 to deny provisional release to an Algerian consular agent. The EU-Algeria Association Council has not met in several years, further complicating trade discussions. The French government is now seeking a European-level examination of Algeria's trade practices.
