Yousef Rabhi wins Ann Arbor mayoral race with 97% of votes

The 38-year-old Democrat, of Algerian origin, will lead the Michigan university city for four years, taking office on 1 December.

Source: Algerie360 · 2026-11-18 · 1 min read

Yousef Rabhi, a 38-year-old Democrat of Algerian origin, won Ann Arbor’s mayoral election with 97% of votes counted across 41 polling stations. He will lead the Michigan university city for four years.

No opponent appeared against Rabhi on the official ballot; Elizabeth Kurtz, an activist from homeless communities, ran a write-in campaign. His win followed an August 4 Democratic primary in which he defeated Christopher Taylor, who had led the city for 12 years.

Rabhi’s term begins at noon on 1 December under a revised Michigan election code that delays the start of local mandates. Three newly elected city councillors are also due to take office on that date.

Why it mattersAlgerian-origin residents and their families have a new mayor representing them in Ann Arbor for a four-year term beginning on 1 December.
