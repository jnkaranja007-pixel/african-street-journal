Bomare Company targets export markets

The Algerian electronics manufacturer held an event in Algiers to discuss export strategies and industrial excellence.

Source: Algerie360 · 2026-11-11 · 1 min read

Bomare Company held its second Afterwork event in Algiers on November 9 to focus on export as a driver for industrial performance. The company argued that export ambition can fuel economic growth, linking industry, compliance, innovation, and customer experience.

The event, attended by journalists and industry figures, explored how local companies can expand beyond national borders. Bomare Company, with export experience since 2007, emphasized that sustainable industry requires skills, logistics, and after-sales service, not just assembly.

The company's new factory can produce three million televisions annually, supporting its STREAM brand and enabling OEM/ODM partnerships. To enter European markets, Bomare Company integrates CE marking and RoHS directive compliance, reflected in its product durability and repairability score of 6.8/10.

Why it mattersAlgerian electronics manufacturers like Bomare Company are working to meet international standards for export.
