Algerian parliament examines 2027 finance bill

National Assembly members reviewed the draft budget, proposing changes for citizens abroad.

Algeria's National People's Assembly (APN) reconvened on Monday, November 2, to review the 2027 finance bill. Finance Minister Mohamed Lamine Lebbou presented the budget's main points, projecting state revenues to exceed 9,251.1 billion dinars, a 7.4% increase from 2026. Total expenditures are planned at 17,251.6 billion dinars in commitments and 17,882.6 billion dinars in payments, with a projected growth rate of 4.2%.

The APN's Finance Commission has put forward 26 amendments to the 2027 finance bill. One proposed amendment specifically addresses a special arrangement for Algerians living abroad. The commission's rapporteur highlighted this amendment in their preliminary report. The bill also allocates 4,356.64 billion dinars in commitments and 4,415.35 billion dinars in payments for transfer expenses, such as subsidies.

The APN's work on the finance bill continues as it examines the proposed amendments. The budget aims to manage state finances for the upcoming year, with specific attention given to potential changes affecting citizens residing outside Algeria.

Why it mattersAlgerians living abroad are directly affected by a proposed special arrangement within the 2027 finance bill.

Renault launches Algerian-made car radiator

The Motrio brand radiator, produced in partnership with FB Radiateur, will be available through Renault's network starting November 2026.

Renault Algérie announced on Tuesday, November 3, 2026, the launch of a new Motrio radiator manufactured locally with FB Radiateur. These radiators will be progressively sold through Renault's authorized network starting this month.

The new product meets Groupe Renault's quality, performance, and durability standards, showcasing Algerian manufacturers' ability to meet international automotive industry requirements. Hichem Nacer-Bey, General Director of Renault Algérie, stated this launch reinforces their after-sales offer for customers.

FB Radiateur's General Director, Salah Eddine Bouali, expressed pride in contributing their industrial expertise to the Motrio brand. FB Radiateur, part of the Bouali group, specializes in automotive cooling systems and operates a factory in Ain M’lila, Oum El Bouaghi wilaya.

Why it mattersAlgerian car owners will soon have access to locally produced radiators for Renault vehicles, improving parts availability.

Algeria women's team nominated for African best team award

The Algerian women's national football team is among 10 nominees for the 2026 African Team of the Year award.

The Algerian women's national football team is among 10 nominees for the 2026 African Best National Team award.

The Confederation of African Football (CAF) awards ceremony will take place on December 7 in Lagos, Nigeria. The Algerian team won the bronze medal at the Africa Cup of Nations and the fair play award in 2026. This third-place finish secured their historic qualification for the 2027 Women's World Cup in Brazil.

Two Algerian players are also nominated for individual CAF awards: team captain Marine Dafeur for Best Player and goalkeeper Chloe Nakkache for Best Goalkeeper.

Why it mattersThe Algerian women's national football team's achievements in 2026 have led to nominations for continental awards, including a historic World Cup qualification.

Algerian prosecutors seek 12 years for milk price manipulators

12 years in prison and a five million dinar fine are sought for defendants accused of diverting subsidized milk.

Algerian prosecutors are seeking a maximum penalty of 12 years in prison and a five million dinar fine for each defendant in a case involving the manipulation of subsidized milk. The accused appeared before the El Harrach court on Tuesday, November 3, facing charges related to disrupting food supplies and market prices.

The prosecution's charges include illicit speculation on state-subsidized milk powder, conducting commercial transactions outside legal distribution channels, and operating without a business registration. Additional accusations concern public hygiene, including transporting perishable goods in unrefrigerated vehicles and storing sensitive products improperly.

The case also involves operating a classified facility without administrative authorization and lacking the mandatory health accreditation for handling animal products. The public prosecutor requested that the fine be immediately enforceable.

Why it mattersAlgerians face potential disruptions to the prices and availability of subsidized milk, a staple food.

Sport · 2026-11-04

Nadia Massé coaches Algerian gymnastics

French-Algerian coach Nadia Massé has been developing Algerian gymnastics since 2018.

Nadia Massé, coach to Olympic champion Kaylia Nemour, has been working with Algerian gymnastics since 2018. Born in France in 1978 to an Algerian father from Oran and a Spanish mother, Massé's connection to Algeria deepened when she began volunteering with the Algerian Gymnastics Federation. She seeks to contribute to the sport's development in the country.

Massé's personal history is linked to Algeria through her father, Noureddine Boudjellal, who emigrated from Oran to France in 1967. Although her first name, Nadia, was inspired by Romanian gymnast Nadia Comăneci, her surname is from her marriage. She has three children and maintains a strong bond with her Algerian heritage.

In 2022, Massé visited Oran during the Mediterranean Games, attempting to locate her grandmother's grave. Her involvement with Algerian gymnastics began after a professional disagreement with the French Gymnastics Federation. She aims to use her experience to benefit Algerian athletes.
