Oran receives 14 burn victims from Jijel fires

Fourteen specialized ambulances transported injured individuals from a military aircraft to a burn treatment center in Oran.

The Health and Population Directorate of Oran mobilized fourteen equipped ambulances to transfer fourteen burn victims. These individuals were evacuated by an Algerian People's National Army aircraft from the hospital in Jijel to the specialized burn hospital in Oran for advanced medical care.

The operation involved fourteen fully equipped ambulances, staffed by specialized medical and paramedical teams, alongside civil protection ambulances and personnel. The injured were initially airlifted from Jijel's hospital before their transfer to Oran's specialized facility, ensuring continuity of care tailored to their health conditions.

This medical transfer is part of a comprehensive mobilization effort by various health services and hospitals. It ensures the necessary human, logistical, and medical resources are deployed for rapid evacuation and transfer, maintaining the highest standards of care for the injured.

Algeria Issues Heavy Rain Alert Across 17 Provinces

The National Meteorological Office warns of intense thunderstorms and heavy rainfall affecting numerous wilayas from Sunday afternoon until midnight.

The National Meteorological Office has issued a Level One alert for heavy thunderstorms and rain across seventeen Algerian provinces. The warning, effective from three in the afternoon until midnight on Sunday, signals potentially severe weather conditions.

Provinces specifically named in the alert include Souk Ahras, Bordj Bou Arréridj, M'Sila, Bordj Badji Mokhtar, Tiaret, Mila, and Oum El Bouaghi. The bulletin also covers Tebessa, Guelma, Batna, Sétif, Djelfa, Constantine, Khenchela, Médéa, Tissemsilt, Saïda, Bouira, and In Guezzam.

This meteorological advisory highlights the immediate threat of intense rainfall and thunder, prompting caution for residents and authorities in the affected regions. The duration of the alert underscores the sustained nature of the expected weather event.

Why it mattersFarmers in seventeen provinces face potential crop damage and disruptions to agricultural activities due to heavy thunderstorms and rainfall.

Algerian Player Adel Boulbina Scores for Saudi Club Al-Draih

Adel Boulbina opened his scoring account for Al-Draih in the Saudi league on Saturday evening, netting the second goal in a match.

Algerian international player Adel Boulbina scored his first goal for his new Saudi club, Al-Draih, on Saturday evening. The forward found the net in the fifty-third minute of the match against hosts Al-Ettifaq, adding Al-Draih's second goal. He was substituted six minutes later.

This match is part of the fourth round of the new Saudi league season. Al-Draih was leading Al-Ettifaq by three goals to nil at the time of this report. Boulbina's contribution helped secure his team's strong position in the game.

Boulbina's move to Al-Draih marks a new chapter in his career, and his immediate impact in scoring highlights his potential value to the team. The Saudi league continues with its competitive fixtures as teams vie for top positions.

Sport · 2026-08-30

Local Coaches Dominate Algerian Football League

Algerian clubs are relying heavily on local coaching talent, supplemented by Tunisian, Egyptian, and Senegalese expertise for the new football season.

The Algerian National First Division football clubs are kicking off their new season with a mix of financial and technical challenges, but a shared ambition to achieve positive results. A significant number of teams have opted to place their trust in local coaches, either newly appointed or continuing from the previous season. This trend is complemented by clubs seeking expertise from Tunisian, Egyptian, and Senegalese football schools, as well as Algerian coaches trained abroad.

Several clubs have made notable coaching appointments. Mouloudia Oran will continue with Cherif El Ouazzani for a second season. JS Kabylie has appointed Karim Belhoussin, and RO. Ruisssat has signed Ghilali Bahloul, who previously managed clubs like JS Kabylie and USM Khenchela. Newly promoted teams like CR Temouchent have hired young coach Mostafa Ghalit, while JS El Biar relies on Mohamed Turki.

Other appointments include Nadir Leknaoui at Biskra, replacing Samir Zaoui. Abdelhak Ballaid continues with AS Chlef, and Mounir Zegdoug with NC Magra. USM Khenchela has Bilal Daziri, and JS Saoura has signed Lotfi Bouzra, who previously managed the club. The experienced Abdelkader Amrani has been appointed at Olympique Akbou, signaling a strong presence of local and regional coaching talent.

Why it mattersAlgerian football clubs are prioritizing local and regional coaching talent for the upcoming season, impacting team strategies and development.

Business · 2026-08-30

Algerian Player Adam Ounas Leaves Mouloudia for Qatar Club

Ounas is in Qatar discussing a new contract after becoming dissatisfied with administrative handling of his transfer negotiations.

Algerian international Adam Ounas is currently in Qatar to finalize terms on a new contract with a local club, signaling a significant shift in his transfer negotiations. Mediators had been actively trying to reopen dialogue with Mouloudia Alger, but Ounas's move suggests a definitive departure from the Algerian club.

Sources close to Ounas indicate his dissatisfaction stems from the administrative approach to his case. Parties connected to the player expressed annoyance over direct contact attempts bypassing agreed official channels and delays in sending his travel ticket to Algiers. The financial offer in Qatar reportedly matches previous agreements.

The calm negotiation environment in Doha has led Ounas to favor the Qatari offer, increasing the likelihood of the deal with the Algerian team collapsing entirely. This development points to a potential end to his discussions with Mouloudia Alger.

Why it mattersAdam Ounas is affected by administrative issues and negotiation delays, leading him to consider a Qatari offer over staying with Mouloudia Alger.
