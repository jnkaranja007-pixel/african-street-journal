Dollar climbs to 136.84 dinars officially as parallel rate nears 250

The US currency rose in both Algerian markets over 10 days, while the euro fell officially and held steady on the parallel market.

The US dollar rose against Algeria’s dinar in both the official and parallel markets, reaching 136.84 dinars on the official market on Tuesday 17 November and 247.5 dinars at Algiers’ Port-Saïd exchange square.

The official rate has risen in recent days and is now a few centimes below 137 dinars, with the dollar approaching the psychological threshold of 140 dinars. On the parallel market, the rate is closing in on 250 dinars, up two dinars in 10 days.

The euro moved the other way on the official market, falling to 150.78 dinars on Tuesday, while it stayed at 276 dinars on the parallel market. The report links the narrowing gap between the euro and dollar to international markets, where they traded between 1.09 and 1.1 dollars per euro, and the dollar is at its highest level since May 2025, supported mainly by expectations on US Federal Reserve interest rates.

Why it mattersDollar buyers at Port-Saïd paid 247.5 dinars per dollar on Tuesday, up from 245.5 on 9 November.

Sport · 2026-11-18

Togo leads Algeria 3-0 at half-time in CAN 2027 qualifier

Klidje scored twice and Boateng added a third in Lomé, leaving Algeria's hopes of securing qualification on Tuesday under pressure.

Togo led Algeria 3-0 at half-time on Tuesday, 17 November, in Lomé, in the fourth round of qualifying for the 2027 Africa Cup of Nations. Klidje scored in the 18th and 32nd minutes, and Boateng headed Togo's third before the break.

TSA Algerie reported that Algeria fielded a heavily changed side and struggled to threaten, while Togo nearly scored a fourth before half-time. Coach Brahim Hemdani was left to rally his team after the three goals.

Algeria had been in a position to secure qualification on Tuesday, the report said. It suggested Hemdani could bring on more experienced attackers Amine Gouiri, Mohamed Amoura and Fares Chaibi; the report did not give the final score.

Why it mattersAlgeria's chance to secure qualification on Tuesday was at risk after the team trailed 3-0 at half-time in Lomé.

Politics · 2026-11-18

Yousef Rabhi wins Ann Arbor mayoral race with 97% of votes

The 38-year-old Democrat, of Algerian origin, will lead the Michigan university city for four years, taking office on 1 December.

Yousef Rabhi, a 38-year-old Democrat of Algerian origin, won Ann Arbor’s mayoral election with 97% of votes counted across 41 polling stations. He will lead the Michigan university city for four years.

No opponent appeared against Rabhi on the official ballot; Elizabeth Kurtz, an activist from homeless communities, ran a write-in campaign. His win followed an August 4 Democratic primary in which he defeated Christopher Taylor, who had led the city for 12 years.

Rabhi’s term begins at noon on 1 December under a revised Michigan election code that delays the start of local mandates. Three newly elected city councillors are also due to take office on that date.

Why it mattersAlgerian-origin residents and their families have a new mayor representing them in Ann Arbor for a four-year term beginning on 1 December.
