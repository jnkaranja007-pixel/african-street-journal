Algerian police recover 23 billion centimes from criminal network

Authorities arrested 25 individuals involved in counterfeiting, fraud, and money laundering across multiple regions.

Algerian police dismantled a criminal network involved in counterfeiting, fraud, and money laundering on October 29. The operation, led by the central service for combating organized crime, recovered 23 billion centimes from the group's illicit activities. The network operated across several regions of Algeria.

The Directorate General of National Security (DGSN) reported that the criminals used over 400 fictitious business registrations for fraudulent transactions to move money outside legal channels. This activity caused an estimated loss of 9200 billion centimes to the public treasury. Police seized trucks, cars, seals, and administrative documents during the operation.

25 members of the network were arrested and presented to the national economic and financial crimes division. Investigations supervised by the competent prosecutor's office revealed the methods used to deceive control services.

Why it matters25 individuals face charges for their involvement in a criminal network that defrauded the public treasury of 9200 billion centimes.

Algeria seizes 2.4 million psychotropic pills

10 suspects arrested and charged with drug trafficking in Algiers

Algerian authorities seized 2.4 million psychotropic pills in a major operation against drug trafficking networks. 10 individuals were arrested on Thursday and placed under detention orders by an investigating judge at the Sidi M’hamed tribunal.

The suspects, identified by the prosecution as Rachid Merabet, 44, Hocine Touil, 37, Nabil Ghazal, 32, Fouad Ghazal, 29, Nassim Touil, 39, Ahamed Benaziza, 37, Akram Benaziza, 26, Oussama Tifoura, 31, Ziad Asnine, 23, and Idriss Gharbi, 44, were caught in possession of the Pregabalin 300 mg tablets. The prosecution also stated that the identities of five other individuals who are currently fugitives have been established.

The operation targeted networks involved in the trafficking of these psychotropic substances. The judge ordered the detention of the 10 suspects on charges related to organized crime and transnational terrorism.

Why it matters10 individuals face detention and charges for involvement in a large-scale drug trafficking operation.

Algerian businesses explore trade opportunities in China

Linyi city hosts international buyers conference to foster economic and investment cooperation.

Algerian businesses are exploring new avenues for trade and partnership at the fourth International Buyers Conference in Linyi, China. The event, running from October 28-30, aims to boost commercial exchange and economic cooperation, aligning with the Belt and Road Initiative. Officials and business representatives from China and other nations are attending to discover commercial, industrial, and logistical potentials.

The conference provides a platform to expand international partnerships and develop cross-border e-commerce, connecting global markets with Chinese production centers. Yu Shengtao, Deputy Secretary of the CPC Linyi Municipal Committee and Mayor, highlighted the city's historical legacy, economic capacity, and integrated logistics services as key strengths for wholesale trade and distribution. He encouraged foreign investors to leverage these opportunities for mutual benefit.

Chen Baiwei, Director-General of the Foreign Affairs Office of the CPC Shandong Provincial Committee, noted that previous conferences have successfully fostered economic and trade exchanges, attracting international buyers. He expressed hope that this event will lead to new partnerships between Chinese and foreign businesspeople, supporting international trade development and economic communication.

Algeria launches major non-hydrocarbon export drive

The operation on November 7 will see companies like Condor and Faderco ship goods to multiple countries.

Algeria is preparing an unprecedented export operation for non-hydrocarbon products, involving companies from various regions. Coordinated by the Ministry of the Interior, the initiative aims to boost non-oil and gas exports and showcase national industrial and artisanal potential. The operation is scheduled for Saturday, November 7.

Several companies are participating, including Condor, Iris, and Faderco. Condor, an appliance giant, will send 400 containers of household goods and metal structures to Tunisia, Egypt, Jordan, Libya, and Mauritania. Faderco, a hygiene product leader, plans to export 50 containers of hygiene items and raw materials to the United States, the United Kingdom, Tunisia, and Ivory Coast.

This event follows a similar export drive on April 11 that reached 19 countries. Algeria exported $4 billion in non-hydrocarbon products in the first eight months of 2026 and aims for $5 billion by year-end. Companies from 13 wilayas, including Bordj Bou Arreridj and Algiers, are involved.

Why it mattersAlgerian companies are expanding their reach to international markets, increasing their export volumes and diversifying beyond oil and gas.

Algeria identifies 362 sites for tourism investment

Minister Houria Messaoudi announced the availability of land parcels across 26 provinces to boost the hospitality sector.

Algerian Minister of Tourism and Handicrafts, Houria Messaoudi, has identified 362 real estate plots suitable for tourism investment across 26 provinces. The minister chaired a coordination meeting to assess the status of tourism real estate and track efforts to inventory available plots for investors via the Algerian Agency for Investment Promotion's platform.

Messaoudi stated that developing domestic and Saharan tourism depends on providing prepared, exploitable land parcels free from technical and administrative obstacles. During the meeting, it was revealed that 150 of these plots are currently being developed within sectoral programs for tourism expansion zones.

The meeting also reviewed 240 plots made available to the Algerian Agency for Investment Promotion across 30 tourism expansion zones, with 31 land shares already granted to investors. Efforts are underway to demarcate these zones to protect them from encroachment and update land data in coordination with state property services and local authorities.

Why it mattersThe availability of 362 real estate plots aims to support tourism investment and improve the investment climate in Algeria.
