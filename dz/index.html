Agriculture · 2026-10-23

algeria launches probe into food waste

videos show produce dumped outside cities to prevent price drops, prompting judicial action.

Algeria's judicial system is investigating the deliberate destruction of food, particularly tomatoes, to artificially keep prices high. The public prosecutor's office at the Court of Algiers ordered immediate and exhaustive inquiries on Thursday, October 22, following the circulation of videos on social media. These videos depict individuals discarding edible food and vegetables outside urban areas from trucks.

The investigations aim to identify all parties involved in these actions, including those who incited, financed, or profited from them, regardless of their position. The judicial authorities also seek to uncover the networks and distribution channels behind these practices. Those found guilty will face prosecution with the full force of current legislation.

This action follows President Abdelmadjid Tebboune's recent order to the Prime Minister to investigate fraudulent practices and speculation in the marketing of imported meats. The president had previously directed investigations into similar issues surrounding imported meat sales during a Council of Ministers meeting.

Why it mattersAlgerian authorities are investigating the deliberate destruction of agricultural products to manipulate market prices, affecting food availability and potentially farmer incomes.

Business · 2026-10-23

new leaders appointed to algerian ports

Riad Hedjal takes over Arzew port, Aman Eddine Khaled Bouabida leads Ghazaouet port following management changes.

Riad Hedjal officially took charge as the new director general of the Arzew Port Company on Thursday, October 22, replacing Kaidari Bensalem. Aman Eddine Khaled Bouabida also became the new director general of the Ghazaouet Port Company in a similar management shift.

These changes aim to inject new energy into management and improve port efficiency and performance, according to a statement from the Ministry of Transport. The port of Arzew is a key hydrocarbon export hub in Algeria and has undergone expansion work costing 45 billion Algerian dinars, funded by the company's budget.

A total of 60 billion Algerian dinars is allocated for equipping and modernizing Algerian ports during 2025 and 2026, as stated by Ali Boularès, CEO of the port services group Serport.

Why it mattersNew leadership has been appointed to two major Algerian ports, Arzew and Ghazaouet.

Algeria launches first national content creator festival in Tlemcen

The festival, running October 21-25, aims to use digital creativity to combat drug abuse and promote national identity.

Algeria's first National Festival for Content Creators officially opened in Tlemcen on Thursday, October 22, 2026. The event, themed 'Purposeful Creativity and Awareness,' will run until October 25.

Minister of Youth and Youth Council Affairs, Mustapha Hidaoui, addressed the festival via a phone call, according to the ministry. The festival aims to employ digital creativity for societal benefit, focusing on fighting drug abuse and fostering national spirit.

The initiative encourages young people to produce responsible, creative content that spreads awareness and reinforces citizenship, belonging, and pride in national identity.

Why it mattersAlgerian youth are encouraged to create digital content that promotes national identity and combats drug abuse.
