Algerian gymnast Kaylia Nemour wins second gold medal

The 19-year-old secured the world championship title in the balance beam event in Rotterdam.

Algerian gymnast Kaylia Nemour won her second gold medal at the 2026 World Gymnastics Championships in Rotterdam, Netherlands. The 19-year-old secured the world title on the balance beam on Sunday, October 25, after winning the uneven bars competition the previous day.

Nemour scored 14.566 points on the balance beam, tying with China's Qinqying Zhang but winning on execution. Zhang, the defending champion from Jakarta 2025, finished with silver, while Brazil's Flavia Saraiva took bronze with 14.300 points. This victory marks Nemour's third world championship gold medal since 2025.

The Algerian athlete also placed fourth in the all-around competition, which was won by American Blakely Skye on October 23. Nemour's wins in Rotterdam follow her Olympic gold in Paris in 2024 and her previous world title in Jakarta in 2025 on the uneven bars. She is now preparing for the 2028 Los Angeles Olympics.

Stellantis recalls 6 Opel models in Algeria over faulty airbags

Owners of Astra H, Astra J, Cascada, Meriva B, Mokka, and Zafira C vehicles produced between 2008 and 2017 must stop driving and contact dealers for free replacement.

Stellantis Algeria has issued an urgent recall for six Opel models, including Astra H, Astra J, Cascada, Meriva B, Mokka, and Zafira C, manufactured between 2008 and 2017. The automotive group is instructing owners to immediately stop driving these vehicles and contact a dedicated number to arrange for a free replacement of Takata airbags.

The chemical components in Takata airbags can degrade over time, especially in hot and humid climates, posing a risk of rupture with excessive force during deployment. Stellantis stated that this could lead to severe or fatal injuries in the worst-case scenarios. Customers can verify if their vehicle is affected via a VIN check on the official Opel Algeria website.

Stellantis, which plans to launch a car and engine production plant in Algeria, apologized for the inconvenience. The company assured that its teams are actively working to resolve the issue as quickly as possible, prioritizing customer safety and well-being.

Why it mattersOwners of specific Opel models in Algeria face potential injury or fatality due to defective airbags requiring immediate replacement.
