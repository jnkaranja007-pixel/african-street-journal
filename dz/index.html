Algerian Security Services Launch Coordinated Campaign Against Neighborhood Gangs

Algeria's security services have initiated a more unified and forceful campaign against neighborhood gangs, moving beyond previous sporadic operations by police and gendarmerie.

Algeria's security services have launched a coordinated campaign against neighborhood gangs, following directives from President Abdelmadjid Tebboune. This new strategy aims to dismantle criminal networks in urban areas that have been threatening citizens' safety, spreading fear, and expanding into drug distribution.

The operations involve police and gendarmerie units conducting targeted raids on organized criminal group hideouts across the country. In Algiers, large-scale operations on Monday night, coinciding with the Prophet Muhammad’s birthday, targeted 13 administrative security districts.

These raids resulted in the arrest of numerous suspects and the seizure of bladed weapons, drugs, psychotropic substances, and criminal proceeds. Algiers security authorities reported dismantling six criminal networks comprising 42 people, confiscating 58 prohibited bladed weapons, cocaine, flares, marine distress signals, tear-gas canisters, and 218,000 Algerian dinars in cash.

Why it mattersThis campaign directly affects urban residents by aiming to reduce crime, drug trafficking, and violence in their neighborhoods, thereby improving their safety and social peace.

Netanyahu, Katz Threaten Gaza Eviction Over Children's Toy Kites

Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu and War Minister Yisrael Katz have threatened to displace entire Gaza neighborhoods if children continue launching toy kites towards.

Netanyahu and Katz convened a "situation assessment session" on Sunday, August twenty-third, to discuss what they termed the "threat of launching balloons and paper planes from Gaza." This meeting included the Chief of Staff of the Israeli army, Eyal Zamir, the head of the National Security Council, and other senior military officials.

Katz reportedly directed the army to "act forcefully" against those launching the paper planes, evacuate neighborhoods and areas from which they are launched, and target "terrorist infrastructure" in those locations. He considers each launch a "full-fledged act of war," despite the kites carrying no explosives or incendiary materials.

The officials' reaction to children's toy kites, which they equate to military drones, has drawn surprise and ridicule. They have also threatened to expand assassinations against Hamas leaders if the kite launches do not cease, linking the children's activities to broader security concerns.

Why it mattersPalestinian residents of Gaza face potential displacement and increased military action due to the Israeli government's response to children's toy kites.

Windstorm capsizes pleasure boat, kills two and injures six in Béjaïa

A sudden weather deterioration with powerful waves and strong wind gusts caused a small pleasure craft to overturn off the west coast of Béjaïa.

Two vacationers died and six others were injured Monday evening when a pleasure boat capsized near the west coast of the wilaya of Béjaïa. The incident occurred around nineteen hours thirty as the weather conditions rapidly worsened, trapping the small vessel amidst strong waves and sustained wind gusts.

On the beach, witnesses and citizens quickly mobilized to assist the occupants of the overturned boat. However, the intensity of the waves and the strong currents hindered these initial rescue efforts by those present at the scene.

Civil Protection rescuers were promptly dispatched to the location, taking over to secure the area and provide care for the survivors. The event disrupted the calm of the seaside resort, leading to a spontaneous chain of mutual aid among those nearby.

Why it mattersTwo vacationers died and six others were injured, directly affecting their families and the local community in Béjaïa.

Algerian Customs expands border control with new guard centers and inspectorates

Public authorities are tightening regulations at land, sea, and air borders to combat smuggling and enhance control over incoming goods.

Algeria has established new Customs guard centers at its land borders and increased the number of quality control and fraud suppression inspectorates to 53. The Director General of Customs, Major General Bakhouche, approved a decree on June 1, 2026, creating these centers to provide continuous field presence and operational support to Customs teams.

These new centers will operate day and night through a continuous patrol system, ensuring permanent guarding of adjacent border strips and coordinating with National Gendarmerie units to combat cross-border crimes. They are also tasked with securing seized goods until delivery to the competent Customs collector.

In coordination with the Ministry of Finance, the Ministry of Internal Trade and National Market Regulation has increased the deployment of quality control and fraud suppression services. This expansion involves replacing the previous list of inspectorate locations with a new one covering more outlets and sensitive sites across various provinces.

Why it mattersBusinesses involved in importing or exporting goods will face increased scrutiny and potentially altered logistics due to intensified border controls and expanded inspection points.

Aïssa Mandi delivers strong performance for Levante, earning high marks

Aïssa Mandi, a key defender for the Algerian national team, received a high rating of 8.1 in Levante's recent match against Osasuna, highlighting his consistent form.

Aïssa Mandi, the Algerian national team defender, demonstrated a strong defensive performance for his club Levante in their recent away match against Osasuna. His contribution helped secure a valuable goalless draw, earning him a match rating of 8.1, second only to his team's goalkeeper.

This performance comes as the national team prepares for official African matches in a month and the Africa Cup of Nations 2027 in a few months. Mandi is currently considered the most active and stable defender for the national team, especially compared to other players facing injuries, reserve roles, or club issues.

Levante's Portuguese coach, Castro, praised Mandi as an experienced player who performs his duties without needing instructions, contributing significantly to the team's efforts to avoid relegation and deliver strong matches.

Why it mattersAlgerian football fans and the national team are directly affected by Mandi's consistent performance, as it suggests his continued importance for upcoming international competitions.
