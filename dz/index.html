Mauritania thanks Algeria for OIC Secretary General election support

Mauritania's Foreign Minister, Mohamed Salem Ould Marzouk, expressed gratitude to Algeria for its backing of Ismail Ould Cheikh Ahmed's election as the new Secretary General.

Mauritania acknowledged Algeria's support for its candidate, Ismail Ould Cheikh Ahmed, who was elected Secretary General of the Organization of Islamic Cooperation (OIC). This appreciation was conveyed by Mauritanian Foreign Minister Mohamed Salem Ould Marzouk during discussions with Algerian Minister of State for Foreign Affairs, National Community Abroad, and African Affairs, Ahmed Attaf, on Thursday, August twenty-seventh.

The meeting took place in Jeddah, Saudi Arabia, on the sidelines of the twenty-third extraordinary session of the OIC Council of Foreign Ministers. Ould Marzouk highlighted Ould Cheikh Ahmed's competence and experience, stating these qualities would enable him to fulfill his duties effectively and enhance the organization's work and objectives.

In response, Attaf congratulated Mauritania on the election of its candidate and reaffirmed Algeria's readiness to collaborate and coordinate with the new Secretary General. This cooperation aims to serve the OIC's goals and principles, strengthening its role in advocating for the issues and interests of the Islamic world's peoples, according to the Algerian Ministry of Foreign Affairs.

Why it mattersThis event affects the diplomatic relations between Algeria and Mauritania, potentially influencing future political and economic cooperation within the OIC framework.

US Embassy offers condolences to Algeria after deadly fires

The US Embassy in Algeria expressed sympathy for the victims of tragic fires that claimed 12 lives across several provinces, prompting a three-day national mourning period.

The US Embassy in Algeria extended its condolences on Thursday to the country following tragic fires that affected several provinces. The embassy conveyed its sincere sympathy and solace to the families of the victims and wished a swift and full recovery to all those injured.

The fires resulted in the deaths of 12 people, as confirmed by Interior Minister Said Sayoud on Wednesday evening from Bejaia. The President of the Republic, Abdelmadjid Tebboune, had earlier declared a three-day national mourning period in remembrance of those who perished.

The declaration of national mourning underscores the severity of the incident and the impact on the affected communities. The US Embassy's message highlights international acknowledgment of the tragedy and support for Algeria during this difficult time.

Why it mattersFamilies in several provinces are directly affected by the loss of 12 lives and injuries caused by the fires, leading to a national mourning period.

Algeria observes deep partial lunar eclipse on Friday

A very deep partial lunar eclipse will be visible from Algeria on Friday, August twenty-eighth, two thousand twenty-six, according to the Astronomy, Astrophysics and Geophysics.

The moon will enter the Earth's penumbra at two twenty-three AM local time in Algeria, followed by its entry into the Earth's shadow around three thirty-three AM. The eclipse will reach its maximum at five thirteen AM local time, when the moon will be very low in the sky.

The moon will set around six twenty-two AM, before the partial phase is expected to end around six fifty-one AM. This eclipse is considered a distinctive astronomical phenomenon for observation and photography, provided there is a western horizon completely free of obstructions.

The Astronomy, Astrophysics and Geophysics Research Center stated that the lunar eclipse can be safely observed with the naked eye without any risk to the eyes, and without the need for special glasses. The eclipse will not be total, with about ninety-six point three percent of the moon's disk falling within the Earth's shadow at the peak of the astronomical event.

Why it mattersResidents of Algeria will have the opportunity to observe a rare astronomical event safely and without special equipment.

Wildfires Close Roads in Skikda, Annaba, and El Tarf

Multiple roads, including sections of the East-West Highway, were closed on Thursday, August twenty-seventh, due to fires in Skikda, Annaba, and El Tarf provinces.

The National Gendarmerie's Traffic Information and Coordination Center announced road closures across several provinces due to ongoing fires. In Skikda, the East-West Highway was closed at the Ain Charshar interchange towards Annaba, diverting traffic to National Road 44. Another section of the East-West Highway at the Ghadir interchange, also towards Annaba, saw traffic rerouted to National Road 44A.

In Annaba province, the East-West Highway was closed at the Ain El Barda interchange towards Skikda and El Tarf, with traffic diverted to National Roads 21 and 84. Additionally, fires closed Provincial Road 107 between the municipalities of Triat and Chetaibi, National Road 84 between National Roads 44 and 21 in El Alma municipality, and Provincial Road 167 between Berrahal and Oued El Annab municipalities.

El Tarf province also experienced closures, with the East-West Highway shut down at the Dhraan interchange towards Annaba, diverting traffic to National Road 84. National Road 82, connecting the municipalities of El Tarf and Zitouna, was also closed, with traffic rerouted to Provincial Road 105 towards the municipalities of Asfour and Bouhadjar in El Tarf province, according to Echorouk Online.

Why it mattersResidents and businesses in Skikda, Annaba, and El Tarf provinces are directly affected by significant travel disruptions and detours due to multiple road closures caused by wildfires.

Niger's Prime Minister Concludes Official Visit to Algeria

Ali Mahamane Lamine Zeine, Prime Minister of Niger, departed Algeria on Thursday evening following an official visit with a significant delegation.

Prime Minister Ali Mahamane Lamine Zeine of Niger concluded his official visit to Algeria on Thursday, August twenty-seventh, departing from Houari Boumediene International Airport. He was seen off by Algerian Prime Minister Seifi Gharib.

The Algerian delegation bidding farewell included several key ministers: Mohamed Arkab, Minister of State and Minister of Hydrocarbons; Abedkarim Bouzard, Minister of Finance; Mourad Adjal, Minister of Energy and Renewable Energies; Sid Ali Zerrougui, Minister of Post and Telecommunications; and Abedlkader Djellaoui, Minister of Public Works and Basic Infrastructure.

Also present were Karima Bekir Tafer, Secretary of State to the Minister of Mines and Mining Industries; Major General Mohamed Saleh Ben Bicha, Secretary General of the Ministry of National Defense; Boualem Chebihi, Director General for Africa at the Ministry of Foreign Affairs; and Ahmed Saadi, Algeria's Ambassador to Niger, underscoring the importance of the visit.

Why it mattersThis event affects Algerian and Nigerien citizens by demonstrating continued high-level diplomatic engagement between their governments.
