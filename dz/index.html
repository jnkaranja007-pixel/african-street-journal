Algeria Launches National AI Doctorate Competition

A new national doctorate program will integrate three computer science schools to advance artificial intelligence research.

Algeria's Ministry of Higher Education and Scientific Research is set to launch a national doctorate competition focused on artificial intelligence. This initiative will consolidate three national computer science schools in Algiers, Bejaia, and Sidi Bel Abbes into a single doctoral program. The move is part of the nation's strategy to bolster AI development within the higher education sector.

The program aims to structure a national doctoral school covering all AI-related fields, with specific thematic doctorates planned for priority economic sectors such as health, energy, water, transport, agriculture, and public administration. An announcement for 102 positions in the integrated computer science doctoral school is expected soon. Discussions also covered a draft executive decree for a specific AI research program, with calls for project participation scheduled for early November.

The strategy seeks to involve economic actors, both public and private, in AI development by exploring cooperation avenues and providing financial and logistical support. This includes enhancing high-performance computing infrastructure and backing scientific research and innovation in AI. The overarching goal is to strengthen Algeria's technological sovereignty through open-source government AI models, superior computing capabilities, and enhanced national talent development.

Why it mattersAlgerian students pursuing advanced studies in computer science will have new opportunities to specialize in artificial intelligence.

Algerian Elias Hammache Scores Fourth Goal in Dutch League

The 23-year-old midfielder netted a crucial equalizer for his club Cambuur against AZ Alkmaar in a 2-2 draw.

Algerian player Elias Hammache continues his strong performances in the Dutch league, scoring another goal for his club Cambuur. The 23-year-old, who joined the team last summer from Schalke 04, has found his form in recent weeks after a shaky start. He has now scored four goals this season, contributing to Cambuur's recent 2-2 draw against AZ Alkmaar with a remarkable strike.

Hammache's goal against AZ Alkmaar adds to his tally, which includes previous goals against Twente and a brace against NEC Nijmegen. The promising forward is looking to revive his career in the Dutch league after an unsuccessful stint in Germany with Schalke, which included a loan spell at Amiens in France last season. He previously showed early promise with Valenciennes.

Despite having represented France at youth levels, Hammache's primary objective remains to play for the Algerian senior national team as soon as possible. His recent performances in the Netherlands indicate a player regaining confidence and form, potentially putting him on the radar for national team selection.

Benrahma defeats Zedadka in Champions League match

Algerian international Saïd Benrahma's team, Lyon, secured a 2-1 victory over Hakim Zedadka's Qarabag in the third round of the Champions League group stage.

Algerian international Saïd Benrahma's team, Lyon, secured a 2-1 victory over Hakim Zedadka's Qarabag in the third round of the Champions League group stage on Tuesday evening. Benrahma started the match for Lyon, while Zedadka was also in the starting lineup for Qarabag. Benrahma was substituted in the 72nd minute.

The win gives Lyon six points in the group, placing them in third position. Qarabag, with three points, is currently in fourth place. The top eight teams in the group advance directly to the knockout stage, while teams ranked ninth through twenty-fourth compete in playoffs for a spot in the next round. Teams ranked twenty-fifth through thirty-sixth are eliminated.

This result is significant for the group standings as Lyon aims to advance in the prestigious European competition. Both Algerian players featured for their respective clubs in this crucial fixture, highlighting their roles in European football.

Algeria's Right Wing Crowded with High-Caliber Players

The Algerian national team faces a surplus of talented right wingers, creating a selection dilemma for coaches.

The Algerian national team is experiencing an unprecedented abundance of high-caliber and even world-class players vying for the right-wing position, a situation that has become a significant challenge for any coach. This depth of talent emerged after Riyad Mahrez's departure, a player known for his consistency and lack of injuries, which previously limited opportunities for stars like Adam Ounas and Rachid Ghezzal.

With Mahrez now retired, the position was widely expected to go to Feyenoord's Anis Hadj Moussa. However, Billel Beloumi has emerged as a strong contender, dividing fan opinion between those who favor Hadj Moussa and those who see Beloumi as more effective. Other notable talents include Ilan Kebbal, whose team Paris FC leads the French league, and Badredine Bouanani, who recently scored for Glasgow Rangers. Adam Ounas, excelling in Qatar, also hopes to join the competition.

Adding to the crowded field is the 20-year-old Bodach from Lyon, who displays impressive speed, dribbling, and attacking flair. These players, representing diverse leagues from the Netherlands and Italy to the UK, France, and Qatar, are all eager to play for Algeria. This wealth of talent at right wing contrasts sharply with other positions, such as goalkeeper and defensive midfield, where Algeria reportedly lacks depth, raising concerns about potentially losing these valuable players due to the intense competition for a single spot.

Why it mattersAlgerian football fans are affected by the intense competition for the right-wing position, leading to debates about player selection and potential talent loss.

Algerian man films women on TikTok, sparking privacy outrage

A 43-year-old man was arrested for secretly filming women and girls at a bus station and broadcasting live on TikTok with offensive commentary.

Authorities in Setif arrested a 43-year-old man for allegedly filming women and girls without their consent at the city's main bus station. The suspect is accused of broadcasting live footage on TikTok, accompanied by indecent remarks, targeting the dignity of families and societal values. This incident has ignited public debate regarding individual privacy and ethical conduct in the digital age.

The man reportedly exploited the busy environment of the transportation hub, which sees thousands of travelers daily, to carry out his actions. The live streams, intended to gain high viewership, allegedly included offensive commentary that violated public decency. The act has been described as criminal, exploiting personal privacy for online engagement.

This case highlights concerns about the misuse of smartphones and social media platforms, particularly TikTok, in Algeria. The incident has drawn attention to the vulnerability of citizens, especially women, to digital exploitation and the erosion of privacy in public spaces. Investigations into the full extent of the alleged violations are ongoing.

Why it mattersThe incident directly affects Algerian citizens, particularly women, by violating their privacy and dignity through non-consensual filming and online broadcasting.
