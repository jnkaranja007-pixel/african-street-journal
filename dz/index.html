Authorities Seize 1000 Billion Centimes From Criminal Network

Authorities have successfully dismantled a criminal network, seizing 1000 billion centimes in an operation described as a major blow against organized crime. The confiscation is the result of meticulous investigative work that commenced in January. The operation, which is being hailed as a landmark event in the fight against major banditry, involved extensive efforts to uncover illicit activities. Details regarding the specific nature of the criminal enterprise or the individuals involved were not immediately released. This significant seizure represents a substantial financial recovery and a victory for law enforcement agencies in their ongoing efforts to combat criminal organizations operating within the country. The full impact of this operation on the broader criminal landscape is yet to be determined.

Why it mattersThe seizure of 1000 billion centimes impacts investors by potentially disrupting illicit financial flows and indicating a crackdown on criminal economic activities.

Algeria Seeks Italian Firms for Agricultural Modernization

Algeria is intensifying its pursuit of food sovereignty, with an upcoming influx of fifteen Italian companies poised to redefine agricultural work in the country. This initiative signals a significant shift in the nation's approach to farming and food production. The arrival of these Italian firms represents a key opportunity for modernization within Algeria's agricultural sector. Their expertise is anticipated to introduce new methods and technologies aimed at enhancing efficiency and output. This collaboration is part of a broader economic strategy to strengthen domestic food security and reduce reliance on imports. The engagement with foreign expertise underscores Algeria's commitment to transforming its agricultural landscape.

Amateur Football League Postpones Season Start to September 11-12

The National Amateur Football League announced on Friday that the 2026-2027 Second League season will now commence on September 11 and 12, a shift from its originally planned September 4 start. This decision is part of the league's organizational and preparatory measures for the upcoming season. According to a statement released on the league's official website, the postponement is intended to facilitate the implementation of directives aimed at supporting clubs and ensuring thorough administrative and sporting preparations. The league management emphasized the importance of these steps for a smooth competition launch. In conjunction with the schedule change, the player registration period has been extended until September 26, 2026. This extension provides clubs with additional time to finalize player signings and complete necessary administrative procedures before the season officially begins.

Why it mattersThe postponement of the football season and extension of player registration directly affects clubs and players by altering their timelines for preparation and team finalization.

Algerie Ferries Adjusts Passenger Schedule for August 25th

Algerie Ferries has issued a specific instruction to passengers booked on the Alger-Marseille ferry service scheduled for Tuesday, August 25, 2026. The company is asking all affected travelers to present themselves at the Port of Algiers earlier than usual. The directive specifies that passengers should arrive at the port starting from 6:00 AM on the day of departure. This change in arrival time is communicated to ensure smooth operations and timely boarding for the intended crossing. This announcement directly impacts individuals holding tickets for the Alger-Marseille route on the specified date. No further details regarding the reason for the early arrival request were provided by the ferry operator.

Why it mattersThis affects passengers traveling between Algeria and France, requiring them to adjust their travel plans and arrive at the port earlier.
