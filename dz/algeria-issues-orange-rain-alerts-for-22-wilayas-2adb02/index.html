Algeria issues orange rain alerts for 22 wilayas

The meteorological office forecasts 20 to 40 mm in some areas, after farmers raised concern over a lack of rain during ploughing.

Source: TSA Algerie · 2026-11-27 · 1 min read

Algeria’s National Office of Meteorology issued orange rain alerts for 22 wilayas on Friday 27 November, forecasting up to 40 mm in some regions. The first alert covered 11 wilayas, where 20 to 30 mm was expected from 03 du matin to 15h00.

A second alert covered Tizi Ouzou, Béjaïa, Jijel, Skikda, Annaba and El Tarf, with 20 to 40 mm forecast between 06h00 and 23h00. Biskra, Batna, Khenchela, Tébessa and El Oued were under alert from 13h00 to 22h00, with 20 to 30 mm expected.

The office issued the warnings after a prolonged hot summer and concern among farmers over a shortage of rain during the ploughing period. The report also said forest fires had occurred in November.

Why it mattersFarmers concerned about dry conditions during ploughing have rain forecast across 22 wilayas, with up to 40 mm in some regions on Friday 27 November.
