Algeria advances $14.6 billion railway to Tamanrasset

The route is planned to carry passengers and freight, with the full line scheduled for delivery by the end of 2028.

Source: TSA Algerie · 2026-11-24 · 1 min read

Algeria is advancing a railway from Algiers to Tamanrasset, a project valued at 14.6 billion dollars and spanning 2 036 km. The government ordered work to begin on 18 October, and the line is scheduled for completion at the end of 2028.

The Public Works Ministry says the first section, from Laghouat through Ghardaïa to El Meniaa, is backed by the African Development Bank and the Asian Infrastructure Investment Bank. The second section, from El Meniaa through In Salah to Tamanrasset, is financed entirely by the state budget. The railway is designed for passenger and freight traffic.

A separate 3.5 billion-dollar dairy complex is being developed by Qatar’s Baladna and Algeria’s investment fund on a 219 000-hectare site in Adrar. At full capacity, its planned 270 000 dairy cows are expected to produce 1.7 billion litres of milk a year.

Why it mattersAlgeria’s state budget and the two development banks must finance their assigned railway sections, with the full line scheduled for delivery at the end of 2028.
