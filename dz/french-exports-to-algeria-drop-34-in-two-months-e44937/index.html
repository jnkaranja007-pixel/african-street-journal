French exports to Algeria drop 34% in two months

Steel and food sectors are among those affected by the significant decline in trade between France and Algeria.

Source: Algerie360 · 2026-11-16 · 1 min read

French exports to Algeria fell by 34% in August and September 2026 compared to the same period in 2025, according to French customs statistics reported by Le Monde.

This decline represents a loss of 241 million euros, with exports dropping from 707 million euros in 2025 to 466 million euros in 2026.

The figures also show a 43% decrease over two years, as exports in August and September 2024 reached 819 million euros.

Why it mattersFrench companies exporting to Algeria lost 241 million euros in two months, affecting sectors from steel to food.
