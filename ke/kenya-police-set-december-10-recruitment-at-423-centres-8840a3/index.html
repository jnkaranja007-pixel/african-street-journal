Kenya police set December 10 recruitment at 423 centres

Applicants must meet age, education and fitness rules, and the service says applying is free.

Sources: Capital FM and Kenyans.co.ke · 2026-11-20 · 1 min read

Kenya’s National Police Service will recruit police constables on December 10 at 423 centres nationwide, inviting qualified citizens to apply for training. Applicants must report by 8am; the exercise is scheduled to run until 5pm.

The service’s notice requires applicants to be Kenyan citizens aged 18 to 28, with a valid national identity card and at least a D+ in the Kenya Certificate of Secondary Education, including English or Kiswahili. Candidates must meet physical and medical standards, have no criminal record or pending charges, and bring original documents and copies of their certificates.

Application forms are available through police service websites and Huduma Centres. The service says recruitment is free and warns that demanding money, bribery or false information are criminal offences; suspected malpractice can be reported to a police station or the Directorate of Criminal Investigations hotline, 0800 722 203.

Why it mattersApplicants must present their documents in person by 8am on December 10, and the service warns them not to pay anyone for a recruitment place.
