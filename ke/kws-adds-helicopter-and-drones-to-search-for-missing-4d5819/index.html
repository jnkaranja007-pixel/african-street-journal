KWS adds helicopter and drones to search for missing Aberdare hiker

The Kenya Wildlife Service says aerial equipment will help teams cover more ground in dense forest as they search for Elder David Kenneth Kihuna.

Sources: Capital FM and Kenyans.co.ke · 2026-11-22 · 1 min read

The Kenya Wildlife Service (KWS) has deployed a rescue helicopter and drones to find Elder David Kenneth Kihuna, an elderly man missing in Aberdare National Park. The agency said Sunday that the search was still under way, with teams looking across the park’s terrain.

KWS said the operation includes its rangers, the Mountain Rescue Team from Mount Kenya National Park, local community guides and other partners. Aerial surveillance is intended to help them cover more ground and reach areas obscured by dense forest canopy.

Kihuna went missing on November 14, according to Kenyans.co.ke, and search efforts have intensified more than a week after his disappearance. KWS said it would issue further updates as the operation progresses.

Why it mattersKWS rangers, mountain rescuers and community guides must continue the search, now using aircraft and drones after Kihuna went missing on November 14.
