Kenya probes looting of refugee offices in Dadaab

Government offices were vandalized and property looted following protests by Somali refugees.

Sources: Capital FM and Anadolu Africa · 2026-11-16 · 1 min read

The Kenyan government is investigating the destruction and looting of Department of Refugee Services offices in Hagadera, Dadaab, which occurred on November 14, 2026. A group of refugee youth allegedly carried out the acts, prompting an appeal for calm from acting Commissioner for Refugee Affairs Mercy Mwasaru. The incident disrupted infrastructure serving both refugees and the host community.

Mwasaru warned against retaliatory attacks and urged refugee and host communities to exercise restraint, stating that the incident threatened peaceful coexistence. She called on community leaders to discourage retaliation and reject inflammatory information, emphasizing that those responsible would be held accountable under Kenyan law. Security agencies are working to recover stolen property and identify those involved.

The Department of Refugee Services is seeking information from the public to aid the investigation, with reports being accepted confidentially by the National Police Service or DRS officials. The government stressed that maintaining peace in Hagadera is a shared responsibility for all residents.

Why it mattersThe government has launched an investigation into the destruction and looting of refugee offices in Dadaab, with security agencies pursuing those responsible.
