Kenya restricts Diwali fireworks to four hours

Mining CS Joho mandates permits and limits shell size for celebrations from November 6 to 8.

Source: Capital FM · 2026-11-03 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Mining Cabinet Secretary Hassan Joho has limited fireworks displays during Diwali celebrations to a four-hour window, from 7pm to 11pm, between November 6 and 8. Organisers must obtain permits and adhere to safety regulations, with fireworks shells capped at four inches in urban areas unless an exemption is granted. These directives aim to regulate displays and minimise public disruption during the Hindu festival.

Joho stated that only qualified, experienced, and sober individuals can handle aerial fireworks, and dealers must sell products only to permit holders. Applications for display authorisations can be made at Madini House or county mining offices. Displays are restricted to approved locations, including hotels, clubs, temples, sports grounds, and school compounds cleared by an Inspector of Explosives.

The new rules follow President William Ruto's pledge in October 2024 to consider Diwali as a national holiday, a proposal that requires parliamentary approval. Contravention of Joho's directives risks a fine not exceeding Ksh10,000 or imprisonment for up to two months, or both.

Why it mattersKenyans celebrating Diwali face restrictions on fireworks displays and potential fines for non-compliance.
