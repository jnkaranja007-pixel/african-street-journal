Kenyan veterinarian named Wildlife Vet of the Year

Dr. Mukami Ruoro-Oundo received the award in London for her work with endangered species.

Source: Farmers Review Africa · 2026-10-23 · 1 min read

Kenyan veterinarian Dr. Mukami Ruoro-Oundo was named Wildlife Vet of the Year at the 2026 International Fund for Animal Welfare Animal Action Awards in London on October 15, 2026.

Dr. Mukami, a Senior Veterinary Officer at the Kenya Wildlife Service, was recognised for her outstanding contributions to wildlife rescue, veterinary medicine, and conservation, treating animals with severe injuries in remote locations.

Her work includes developing specialised treatment protocols for rescued pangolins, strengthening efforts to improve their survival rates, and treating elephants, rhinos, and lions.

Why it mattersKenyan wildlife veterinarians are recognised for their critical role in protecting endangered species.
