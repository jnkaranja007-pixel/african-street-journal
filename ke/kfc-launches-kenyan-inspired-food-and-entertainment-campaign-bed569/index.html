KFC launches Kenyan-inspired food and entertainment campaign

The fast-food chain introduces a limited-time menu and retail activations with local creatives in Nairobi.

Source: Food Business Africa · 2026-10-22 · 2 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

KFC has launched Kentucky Town 254, a Kenyan-inspired food and entertainment campaign running from October 20 to November 15, 2026. The initiative combines locally influenced menu innovations with retail activations and partnerships with Kenyan creatives. The campaign introduces a limited-time menu at selected KFC restaurants in Nairobi, featuring items like The Colonel’s Mandazi Burger, The Colonel’s Mandazi Pops, The Kentucky Choma Burger, Samosa Crunch, and Catch These Masala Fries, alongside new beverages.

The campaign extends beyond the menu with pop-up experiences at The Junction Mall, The Hub Karen, Galleria Shopping Mall, and Garden City Mall, featuring games, live performances, and music. A limited-edition merchandise collection, developed with Kenyan fashion and design brand JDHAA | home Apparel 254, is also part of the offering. KFC Africa stated that the initiative was developed around local creators, suppliers, and talent, with execution led in Kenya, which is its second-largest market in Africa with 48 restaurants.

This campaign reflects a localisation strategy adapting international quick-service restaurant offerings to regional tastes and creating opportunities to collaborate with local suppliers, designers, and entertainment talent. KFC Africa aims to build a restaurant brand that reflects African consumers, local partnerships, and regional creativity. The approach highlights the potential of limited-time menus to test new flavour combinations, generate customer engagement, and connect global brands with local food cultures.

Why it mattersKenyan consumers are affected by new menu items and entertainment experiences that incorporate local flavours and culture.
