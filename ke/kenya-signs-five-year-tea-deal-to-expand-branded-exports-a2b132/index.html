Kenya signs five-year tea deal to expand branded exports

KEPROBA and EATTA will promote packaged and specialty teas and seek buyers across Africa, Asia, Europe, the Middle East and the Americas.

Source: Food Business Africa · 2026-11-21 · 1 min read

Kenya’s Export Promotion and Branding Agency (KEPROBA) and the East Africa Tea Trade Association (EATTA) have signed a five-year partnership to develop branded tea products and expand export markets. The agreement is intended to reduce reliance on bulk exports by supporting packaged and specialty teas that can attract higher prices internationally.

The organisations will help exporters adopt modern packaging and branding, and promote specialty, orthodox, green, purple and premium teas. They will develop the ‘Kenya Tea Story’ under the Made in Kenya brand. The International Trade Centre says tea exports reached KSh185.8 billion (US$1.44B) in 2025, up 2.6% from KSh180.6 billion (US$1.40 billion) in 2024; bulk black tea accounts for 96.3% of exports.

KEPROBA Acting Chief Executive Austin Macheso said the partnership would reposition Kenya as a source of branded, differentiated products. EATTA Managing Director George Omuga said producers, exporters and other tea businesses would gain opportunities through the agreement.

Why it mattersExporters and tea businesses are expected to develop higher-priced packaged and specialty products through a five-year agreement.
