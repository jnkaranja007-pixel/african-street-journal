Taita Taveta targets US$4.3M potato industry growth

The county government aims to boost production by improving seed access, farmer training, and market links.

Source: Food Business Africa · 2026-11-02 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Taita Taveta County plans to grow its Irish potato industry, currently worth US$4.3 million annually, to increase farmer incomes and create jobs. The county cultivates 750 hectares of potatoes, producing 11,250 tonnes each year. Farm-gate prices average US$0.39 per kilogram, offering economic benefits.

The county government is enhancing the sector by improving access to certified seeds, farmer training, research, and reliable markets. Through the National Agricultural Value Chain Development Project, 21 farmer groups received support for seed potatoes, and nine demonstration plots were established. Agripreneur Gift Kisombe connected farmers in Mbale Ward with a seed supplier who bought KSh131,600 worth of certified Markies seed potatoes.

The County Department of Agriculture, with NAVCDP and AGRICOS, is introducing Irish potato farming in Taveta Sub-County and plans to train stakeholders in sustainable practices. The Kenya Agricultural and Livestock Research Organization is identifying suitable potato varieties for the county's diverse ecological zones. County Executive Committee Member for Agriculture, Dawson Katuu Mzenge, stated that potato farming can increase household incomes and strengthen food security.

Why it mattersFarmers in Taita Taveta County will benefit from improved access to quality seeds, training in better farming techniques, and stronger market connections, increasing their household incomes.
