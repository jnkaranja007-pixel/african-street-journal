Ruto orders breastfeeding rooms and flexible hours at State House

The president directed staff to make spaces for mothers after Kenya signed a law on workplace support for pregnant and breastfeeding women.

Sources: Capital FM and Kenyans.co.ke · 2026-11-26 · 1 min read

Kenyan President William Ruto ordered State House staff in Nairobi to provide breastfeeding spaces and flexible working arrangements for mothers employed there. He announced the directive on Nov. 26 after signing the Breastfeeding Mothers Bill, 2024, into law.

Ruto said women should be able to breastfeed and care for their children while continuing their jobs. The law, championed by nominated MP Sabina Chege, seeks to support mothers who are pregnant or breastfeeding. A separate report said State House faces fines of up to KSh1 million for failing to comply with the new law.

Ruto also urged Parliament to speed up a pending bill on tertiary education placement and funding for university, technical and vocational training, and medical training students.

Why it mattersState House staff must provide breastfeeding facilities and flexible schedules, with non-compliance carrying fines of up to KSh1 million.
