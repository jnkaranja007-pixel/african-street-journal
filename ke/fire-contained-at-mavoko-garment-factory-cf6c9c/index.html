Fire contained at Mavoko garment factory

Property worth millions of shillings was destroyed in a late Sunday night blaze along the Nairobi-Mombasa Highway.

Sources: Capital FM and Kenyans.co.ke · 2026-11-16 · 1 min read

Late on Sunday night, a fire swept through a garment factory in Mavoko, beside the Nairobi-Mombasa Highway, and destroyed property worth millions of shillings. Firefighters, police and volunteers from the Kenya Red Cross Society joined the response, with teams from Mavoko and Machakos County, Nairobi City County, and the National Youth Service (NYS) all taking part.

The Kenya Red Cross said its Machakos Action Team was among the responders and that the blaze had been brought under control. "The fire has been contained, and no injuries were reported," the organisation said in an update. The exact extent of the losses has yet to be established, though the fire destroyed a significant section of the factory.

An assessment of the damage is due to be carried out on Monday to establish the full extent of the losses.

Why it mattersOwners of the garment factory face losses worth millions of shillings, and the full scale of the damage will only be known once Monday's assessment is complete.
