Kiambu court order halts 72 million litres of daily water supply

The county government obtained interim orders barring Kenya's national water agency from supplying six providers that serve homes and businesses.

Sources: Capital FM and Kenyans.co.ke · 2026-11-28 · 1 min read

Kenya's national bulk water agency, the Athi Water Works Development Agency (AWWDA), has stopped bulk water deliveries to six water service providers in Kiambu County after a High Court order barred direct supply. The shutdown affects about 72 million litres a day, the agency said in a Friday statement.

The Kiambu High Court issued interim orders on November 18, 2026, after the Kiambu county government applied to stop the deliveries. AWWDA chief executive Joseph Kamau said the agency had complied and would pursue lawful options to resolve the dispute. The six providers distribute water to households, businesses and institutions.

Ruiru-Juja Water and Sewerage Company receives 47.5 million litres daily, while the other five providers receive between one million and 10 million litres each. The agency did not set an end date for the shutdown or specify alternative supply arrangements.

Why it mattersThe six providers serving Kiambu households, businesses and institutions must find an alternative to 72 million litres of daily deliveries, with no end date specified.
