Minnesota lawmaker urges Kenyan women to contest 2027 election

Huldah Momanyi, re-elected with 71.8% of votes in Minnesota, made the appeal at a school in Nyamira County.

Source: Capital FM · 2026-11-23 · 1 min read

Huldah Momanyi, a Minnesota House of Representatives member who represents District 38A, urged Kenyan women to seek elective office during her homecoming tour, which concluded at Getembe Comprehensive School in Nyamira County on Nov. 23. She said women should overcome barriers to political participation and compete for leadership positions.

Momanyi won re-election on Nov. 3 with 9,852 votes, or 71.8% of votes cast, against Republican challenger Charlie Kistler’s 3,840. She was serving her second term and told women that her own election in the United States showed they could contest seats.

The visit also included talks with Nyamira Governor Amos Nyaribo on diaspora partnerships in early childhood digital learning, healthcare and opportunities for young people abroad. Momanyi had earlier led US healthcare professionals to discuss a possible framework for recruiting Kenyan nurses to Minnesota; a pilot in September placed a group of Kenyan nurses in healthcare facilities there.

Why it mattersKenyan women considering elective office were urged to enter the race as the country approaches the 2027 General Election.
