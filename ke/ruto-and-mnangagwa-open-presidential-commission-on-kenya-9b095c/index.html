Ruto and Mnangagwa open presidential commission on Kenya-Zimbabwe ties

The leaders are due to review existing agreements and trade barriers, with Kenya targeting US$70 million in annual bilateral trade.

Source: Capital FM · 2026-11-27 · 1 min read

Zimbabwean President Emmerson Mnangagwa began a two-day state visit to Kenya on Friday with a ceremonial 21-gun salute at State House in Nairobi. He inspected a guard of honour before talks with Kenyan President William Ruto.

The presidents are scheduled to co-chair the first Zimbabwe-Kenya Bi-National Commission, elevating cooperation from a joint permanent commission to presidential-level engagement. The commission is expected to review commitments and identify economic opportunities, as the two governments seek to ease cross-border trade barriers and increase private-sector participation.

Kenya and Zimbabwe have signed 17 bilateral agreements covering areas including agriculture, investment promotion, transport and education. Further agreements are expected to be considered during the commission.

Why it mattersKenyan and Zimbabwean traders face a presidential review of cross-border barriers as Kenya targets US$70 million in annual bilateral trade.
