Kenya and IFAD sign $2.08 million climate resilience grant

The funding will integrate climate information systems into agricultural value chains to help farmers manage climate risks.

Source: Capital FM · 2026-10-29 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Kenya and the International Fund for Agricultural Development (IFAD) have signed a 2.08 million US dollar grant to boost climate resilience in agriculture. Treasury Cabinet Secretary John Mbadi signed the Climate Smart Agribusiness Partnerships for Resilience (CSAPR) agreement with IFAD's new Country Director, Matteo Marchisio, in Nairobi.

The funding, provided by the Bill & Melinda Gates Foundation through IFAD, will support the Integrated Natural Resources Management Programme (INReMP). This initiative aims to integrate climate information systems into agricultural value chains, helping farmers and agribusinesses anticipate and manage climate-related risks.

The programme is expected to improve agricultural productivity by enabling producers to incorporate climate information into their farming and business decisions. Mbadi reaffirmed the government's commitment to deepening cooperation with IFAD, while Marchisio pledged to strengthen the partnership.

Why it mattersKenyan farmers will receive support to use climate data for better decision-making and improved productivity.
