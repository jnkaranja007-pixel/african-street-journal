Kenya names bidders for three mineral projects, pending licences

A US-led consortium won the Mrima Hill tender, while a Chinese joint venture was chosen for projects in Samburu and Tharaka Nithi.

Source: Capital FM · 2026-11-19 · 2 min read

Kenya has named winning bidders for three strategic mineral projects after completing tender reviews, Mining Cabinet Secretary Hassan Ali Joho said on Thursday. The Frontier Minerals Consortium, made up of Frontier Minerals LLC, Bunker Hill Mining Corp, ReElement Technologies and American Resources Corporation, won the Mrima Hill niobium, rare earth elements and other minerals project in Kwale County. Fangda Special Steel Technology and Sinohydro Corporation, operating as a joint venture, were awarded the Kang'ura chromite project in Samburu County and the Kamacabi copper project in Tharaka Nithi County.

Joho said the ministry declared the highest-scoring responsive bidders the intended winners, but the awards do not allow prospecting to begin. Each winner must engage host communities, landowners and county leaders, obtain the required consents, and apply for a prospecting licence through the ministry's online cadastre portal. The Mineral Rights Board will scrutinise the applications before Joho decides whether to grant them.

The tender produced no qualifying bids for the Lali Hills manganese project in Tana River County or the Kiritiri coltan project in Embu County, and the ministry said those projects would be handled separately under the law. Bidding began with an expression of interest in March, and bids were opened on October 21.
