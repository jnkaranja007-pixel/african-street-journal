Ruto and Tshisekedi agree to use diplomacy to ease Kenya-DRC rift

The presidents also pledged economic cooperation and called for joint action against the current Ebola epidemic after talks in Oyo.

Sources: Africanews and Anadolu Africa · 2026-11-24 · 1 min read

Kenyan President William Ruto and Democratic Republic of Congo President Félix Tshisekedi agreed on Sunday to prioritise diplomatic channels for resolving disputes between their countries. Congolese President Denis Sassou Nguesso hosted the leaders in Oyo, in northern Congo-Brazzaville, for a mini-summit.

A statement from the presidency in Brazzaville said the two presidents would use diplomacy to address shared concerns and resolve potential disputes peacefully. Their relations have soured over the crisis between Rwanda and the DR Congo: Tshisekedi has accused Kenya of favouring Kigali, an allegation Nairobi has denied. Togo's Faure Gnassingbé mediated for the African Union, and Radio France Internationale (RFI) reported that the talks lasted more than two hours.

Kenya and the DRC also pledged further economic partnerships and cooperation on peace, security and regional integration. The leaders called on countries to work together to eradicate the current Ebola epidemic.

Why it mattersKenyan and Congolese officials are to pursue further economic partnerships and cooperation on peace, security and regional integration.
