Kenya's election commission will follow court rules on 2027 results

Chairperson Erastus Ethekon says the commission will tally and verify results collectively, while only he can declare the winner.

Sources: Capital FM and Kenyans.co.ke · 2026-11-24 · 1 min read

Kenya’s Independent Electoral and Boundaries Commission (IEBC) will follow the Supreme Court’s interpretation of election law as it prepares for the August 10, 2027 General Election, chairperson Erastus Ethekon said in Nairobi on Tuesday. He rejected concerns that the National Tallying Centre could be used to alter results declared at polling stations.

Ethekon said the commission would work within the legal framework and had no plan to introduce a system outside it. In a September 5, 2022 ruling on petitions over President William Ruto’s election, the Supreme Court said tallying and verification at the national centre are the responsibility of the commission collectively; the chairperson alone declares the final presidential result.

The ruling also bars the chairperson from excluding other commissioners from tallying and verification. The court’s decision followed a dispute in 2022, when four commissioners disowned results announced by then-chairperson Wafula Chebukati.

Why it mattersIEBC commissioners must conduct national tallying and verification collectively under the Supreme Court’s ruling as preparations continue for the August 10, 2027 election.
