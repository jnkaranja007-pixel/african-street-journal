Kenya and DR Congo agree to restore full diplomatic ties

Ruto and Tshisekedi reached the agreement at a summit in Oyo, after Kinshasa recalled its envoy and rejected Kenya’s ambassador nominee.

Sources: BBC Africa and Anadolu Africa · 2026-11-23 · 1 min read

Kenyan President William Ruto and Democratic Republic of Congo President Félix Tshisekedi agreed on Sunday to restore full diplomatic ties, following tensions over the conflict in eastern Congo. They met in Oyo, in the Republic of Congo, at a summit hosted by President Denis Sassou Nguesso, with Togo’s President Faure Essozimna Gnassingbé acting as mediator.

A joint statement said the leaders affirmed respect for sovereignty, territorial integrity and non-interference in other states’ internal affairs. The two countries’ relations worsened after DR Congo accused Kenya in 2023 of hosting leaders launching a rebel alliance that included M23. Kinshasa then recalled its envoy to Nairobi and later refused to accredit Kenya’s nominated ambassador.

Anadolu Africa reported that the leaders agreed to appoint new ambassadors. They also pledged good neighbourliness, peaceful dispute settlement and stronger cooperation through regional mechanisms; both countries belong to the East African Community.

Why it mattersKenya and DR Congo must appoint new ambassadors, replacing the diplomatic representatives disrupted by the dispute.
