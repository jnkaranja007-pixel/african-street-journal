Ledama defends shooting threat over KSh365,000 employment claim

The Narok senator says alleged auctioneers entered a company linked to him and took the keys to its delivery truck under a court order.

Sources: Capital FM and Kenyans.co.ke · 2026-11-22 · 1 min read

Narok Senator Ledama Olekina defended a threat to shoot men filmed in a confrontation near a vehicle, saying alleged auctioneers had entered private company premises and taken the keys to its delivery truck. He said they were seeking KSh365,000 over a former Senate employee’s compensation claim, and denied that his personal vehicle was being auctioned.

In a statement on Saturday, November 21, Olekina said the dispute stemmed from a staff member dismissed in 2017 and a Labour Court order authorising property attachment. The former employee, Zakayo Rotiken, worked as a Narok North Constituency Coordinator from 2017 to 2020; the Employment and Labour Relations Court ruled in his favour on October 31, 2023, over his dismissal and unpaid salary arrears.

Olekina said Senate employment obligations are funded through the Parliamentary Service Commission, not individual senators, and accused political opponents and social media platforms of misrepresenting the video. He questioned the legal basis for attaching assets of a separate private company.

Why it mattersOlekina must contest the attachment of a company’s delivery truck over the KSh365,000 claim while the court dispute concerns Senate employment obligations.
