First Circle Capital closes $26 million fund for African fintech startups

The Kenya-based firm will invest at pre-seed and seed stages in six areas, from lending and insurance to climate fintech and regulatory technology.

Source: Farmers Review Africa · 2026-11-19 · 1 min read

First Circle Capital, a Kenya-based venture capital firm, has closed its Africa Fund I at $26 million to invest in early-stage fintech startups across Africa. The fund will back companies at pre-seed and seed stages.

The female-led firm will focus on six areas: insurance technology, alternative lending, financial infrastructure and interoperability, climate fintech, fintech software as a service, and regulatory technology. First Circle Capital has offices in Nairobi, Kampala, Casablanca and Cape Town.

The firm said it is also placing greater emphasis on AI-native startups, which build artificial intelligence, automation and data-driven operations into their businesses from the start. Co-founder and managing partner Selma Ribica said the firm wants to identify these companies early and work with founders as they scale.

Why it mattersEarly-stage fintech founders across Africa can seek backing from the $26 million fund, with investments planned at pre-seed and seed stages.
