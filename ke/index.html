Ronald Karauri joins UDA for Nairobi governor race

Kasarani MP Ronald Karauri has officially joined the United Democratic Alliance, aiming to challenge for the Nairobi governor seat in 2027.

Kasarani MP Ronald Karauri officially joined the United Democratic Alliance (UDA) on November 9, 2026, positioning himself for a 2027 Nairobi governor bid. Karauri, a former SportPesa chief executive, previously announced his intention to run for the city's top seat in February. He entered Parliament in 2022 as an independent candidate after winning the Kasarani constituency seat.

Karauri’s move follows Governor Johnson Sakaja’s defection from UDA to the Orange Democratic Movement (ODM) on November 2. Sakaja, elected on a UDA ticket in 2022, is expected to seek a second term with ODM. Karauri had previously criticized Sakaja’s move, stating the governor was seeking a new platform to avoid scrutiny of his performance. Karauri promised to address issues like poor roads, garbage collection, and water shortages.

The Kasarani legislator plans to audit building approvals, improve revenue collection, and redirect county spending. He believes Nairobi can enhance service delivery without new taxes, citing uncollected revenue and expenditure weaknesses. Karauri also pledged to improve accountability in county spending, address staffing shortages in health facilities, and clear drainage systems.

Nairobi court suspends online alcohol sales

High Court orders strict licensing and age verification for digital alcohol vendors.

The High Court in Nairobi has suspended online sales and delivery of alcohol via digital platforms. Justice David Mburu issued interim orders on Monday, November 9, 2026, requiring operators to meet strict licensing and age verification rules before selling alcohol online.

Online alcohol vendors must now possess relevant licenses and list drinks in restricted digital sections requiring secure customer authentication. Access is barred to anyone under 18, with age verification mandated before purchase and delivery. The court also restrained digital advertising that bypasses these safeguards.

These orders follow Nairobi City County's proposed Alcoholic Drinks Control and Licensing Bill, 2026, aimed at regulating online sales and delivery services. Chief Officer Lydia Mathia stated the reforms intend to protect young people and ensure transparent operations, addressing concerns over underage drinking and illicit liquor.

Why it mattersNairobi residents who purchase alcohol online will now face stricter age verification processes and potential delays in delivery.

Kenya sugar production jumps 56.7% to 690,521 tonnes

Domestic supply improved and imports declined, leading to lower retail prices for sugar.

Kenya's sugar production surged by 56.7% in the first nine months of 2026, reaching 690,521 tonnes. This increase, an additional 249,869 tonnes from the same period in 2025, was driven by improved domestic supply and reduced imports. Local millers also recorded higher sales, processing 7.76 million tonnes of sugarcane, a 56.2% rise from the previous year.

The national average retail sugar price dropped to Sh155 per kilogramme in September 2026, down from Sh171 in September 2025. Sugar imports fell by 31% to 251,690 tonnes, while local millers' sales increased by 45.6% to 635,672 tonnes. Sugar stocks at the end of September stood at 70,773 tonnes, a significant rise from 14,033 tonnes a year earlier.

Kenya Sugar Board Chief Executive Jude Chesire is helping resolve a leadership stalemate at the International Sugar Organization, which he believes will create export market opportunities. Kenya projects 900,000 tonnes production against one million tonnes consumption, aiming for export surpluses by next year. The country is also exploring cooperation with Brazil on biofuels and ethanol production, with molasses output up 62.1%.

Why it mattersKenyan farmers and consumers will benefit from increased sugar production, lower prices, and potential new markets for exports and biofuels.

Three suspects escape Oyugis police cell after assaulting officer

Suspects attacked an officer with a water bucket before fleeing Oyugis Police Station in Homa Bay County on Sunday night.

Three suspects escaped from Oyugis Police Station cells in Rachuonyo South Sub-County, Homa Bay County, on Sunday night. The escape occurred after the suspects assaulted Police Constable Robert Aminga, who was responding to a request for drinking water for 16 detainees.

According to Rachuonyo South Sub-County Police Commander Philemon Saera, the incident happened around 8pm. After Aminga distributed water, three suspects remained and attacked him with the bucket. The officer sustained injuries to his chest, left knee, and left shoulder and was taken to Rachuonyo Sub-County Hospital.

The escapees are identified as Erick Onyango (arrested for alleged defilement), Collins Otieno, and James Otieno (held for alleged robbery with violence and rape). Police have launched a manhunt for the three men and are investigating the escape.

Why it mattersThree men accused of serious crimes escaped from police custody in Homa Bay County after injuring an officer.
