IEBC requires public officers to resign by Feb. 10

Public officers seeking elective posts must resign by February 10. The Independent Electoral and Boundaries Commission (IEBC) stated that campaigning will close on August 7, 2027, 48 hours before the polls.

Why it mattersGovernment employees must leave their current roles by the February deadline to legally run for office.

IEBC targets 28.5 million voters for 2027 election

The Independent Electoral and Boundaries Commission (IEBC) is targeting a voter register of 28.5 million people. The commission is also doubling the number of countries where diaspora voting is available.

Why it mattersThe expanded register would add more than six million people to the voting rolls ahead of the general election.

Ministry proposes split of tourism levy collection and management

The Ministry is proposing the separation of tourism levy collection from funds management. Tourism PS Julius Bitok said the move mirrors the roles of the KRA and the CBK.
