Kenyan fugitive extradited to US for 2013 manslaughter conviction

Sancho Kibutu, a fugitive for 13 years, was arrested in Nairobi on September 9 and handed over to US authorities.

Kenyan fugitive Sancho Kibutu was extradited from Nairobi to California on October 23, 2026, to face sentencing for a 2013 gross vehicular manslaughter conviction while intoxicated. The Director of Public Prosecutions confirmed Kibutu was handed over to US authorities by officers from the ODPP and Directorate of Criminal Investigations after being arrested in Nairobi on September 9, 2026.

Kibutu, who also went by aliases Sancho Kibuto and Njoroge Kibuto, had been a fugitive for 13 years after failing to appear for sentencing following his conviction in the USA on September 19, 2013. A bench warrant was issued on October 28, 2013, for the then 32-year-old who was driving along Golden State Boulevard when he crossed a double yellow line, colliding with another vehicle. The crash killed a 16-year-old passenger and injured three others.

The extradition process, handled through Kenyan courts and facilitated by the DPP and other agencies, took four weeks. The ODPP stated the extradition underscores its commitment to international cooperation in criminal justice and ensuring wanted individuals face relevant authorities.

Ruto hands COMESA chairmanship to Mnangagwa

Kenya's president urged African nations to fund their own development by using African financial institutions.

President William Ruto handed over the chairmanship of the Common Market for Eastern and Southern Africa (COMESA) to Zimbabwean President Emmerson Mnangagwa on Thursday in Harare. Ruto called on African governments to increase their use of African financial institutions for development projects and to hold more reserves with them. He noted that Africa holds over $4 trillion in domestic financial assets, much of which is held in foreign accounts.

During Kenya's tenure, the priority was leveraging digitalization to deepen regional value chains, which Ruto stated has become a programme. He highlighted that Kenya has increased its shareholding in Afreximbank, Africa Finance Corporation, and ATIDI. Ruto also presented the COMESA 2026-2030 Medium-Term Strategic Plan and the 2025 Annual Report, which focuses on accelerating intra-African trade and eliminating non-tariff barriers.

Ruto expressed confidence in Mnangagwa's leadership to advance COMESA's goals under the theme 'One Market, One Future'. He stated that the $16 billion East Africa refinery planned for Kenya will be funded almost entirely by African financial institutions. The summit also included Zambian President Hakainde Hichilema and COMESA Secretary-General Chileshe Mpundu Kapwepwe.

Why it mattersKenyan President William Ruto is pushing for African nations to finance their own development through African financial institutions.

Kenyan veterinarian named Wildlife Vet of the Year

Dr. Mukami Ruoro-Oundo received the award in London for her work with endangered species.

Kenyan veterinarian Dr. Mukami Ruoro-Oundo was named Wildlife Vet of the Year at the 2026 International Fund for Animal Welfare Animal Action Awards in London on October 15, 2026.

Dr. Mukami, a Senior Veterinary Officer at the Kenya Wildlife Service, was recognised for her outstanding contributions to wildlife rescue, veterinary medicine, and conservation, treating animals with severe injuries in remote locations.

Her work includes developing specialised treatment protocols for rescued pangolins, strengthening efforts to improve their survival rates, and treating elephants, rhinos, and lions.

Why it mattersKenyan wildlife veterinarians are recognised for their critical role in protecting endangered species.

Kenya confirms nine Ebola cases

Four relatives, three health workers, and a cleaner tested positive after contact with the first victim.

Kenya has confirmed nine cases of Ebola, its first outbreak of the disease, after the initial patient travelled from DR Congo and died two weeks ago. The confirmed cases include four relatives of the first victim, three health workers, and a hospital cleaner, all linked to him. Nearly 5,000 healthcare workers have been trained to manage the disease.

The government is working to assure citizens amid rising concerns about further spread, while neighbouring countries like Uganda, Ethiopia, and Somalia are on alert. The US has advised its citizens against travelling to Kenya. This outbreak follows a deadly epidemic in DR Congo where more than 4,000 people have died since May.

Kenyans have voiced worries about the potential for wider transmission of the virus. The current outbreak is the second most deadly in Ebola's history.

Why it mattersNine Ebola cases have been confirmed in Kenya, affecting relatives, health workers, and a cleaner.
