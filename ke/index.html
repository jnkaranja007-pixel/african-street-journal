Kenya threatens Africa CDC action over Saudi travel ban

Nairobi protests disproportionate Ebola travel restrictions that suspend visas and bar arrivals.

Kenya's Ministry of Foreign and Diaspora Affairs has threatened to escalate its concerns over Saudi Arabia’s suspension of travel from Kenya to the Africa Centres for Disease Control and Prevention (Africa CDC). The ministry described the restrictions, which include suspending visa issuance and barring travellers who have been in Kenya within the previous 21 days, as disproportionate and potentially harmful to Kenya’s economic and diplomatic interests.

The ministry, headed by Musalia Mudavadi, stated on October 30 that it would work with other affected African countries to present the matter to Africa CDC. Kenya argues that restrictions not supported by scientific evidence could set a concerning precedent for the continent. The Ministry of Foreign Affairs and Ministry of Health maintain that Kenya has contained the Ebola outbreak, with no new cases reported since October 19.

Saudi Arabia suspended travel by its citizens to Kenya and stopped issuing visas over Ebola concerns. Kenya is demanding Saudi Arabia review and lift the measures, challenging the kingdom to submit scientific justification to the World Health Organization. Seven patients receiving treatment are expected to be discharged on November 3.

Why it mattersKenya's economic and diplomatic interests are threatened by Saudi Arabia's travel ban and visa suspension.

Sifuna demands transparency on Mrima Hills mining project

Linda Mwananchi Movement party leader questions benefits for Kwale residents from rare earth mineral extraction.

Linda Mwananchi Movement party leader Edwin Sifuna has demanded full disclosure of the proposed Mrima Hills mining project in Kwale. Sifuna stated that all mining agreements and contracts should be made public, arguing that communities in mineral-rich areas must know the terms under which investors operate and how benefits will be shared. The project involves extracting niobium and rare earth minerals, used in aerospace, electronics, and electric vehicle manufacturing.

Sifuna questioned what had been agreed between the government and prospective investors, insisting residents have a right to know the proposed arrangements. He raised concerns over land ownership, compensation, and revenue sharing, warning that residents could lose land without adequate compensation. Mining Cabinet Secretary Hassan Joho stated that five bidders submitted proposals on October 21, 2026, and the government expects to award the project within two to three weeks.

The mineral deposit is believed to contain tens of billions of dollars’ worth of rare earth minerals and niobium. Sifuna’s concerns follow previous operations in Kwale where Base Titanium operated for over a decade, with Sifuna claiming communities did not receive promised benefits. Two American consortia, a US-linked group, an Australian consortium, and a Kenyan-British joint venture are among the five contenders.

Why it mattersKwale residents are at risk of losing their land and not receiving adequate compensation or promised benefits from the proposed Mrima Hills mining project.

Taita Taveta targets US$4.3 million potato industry growth

The county government is improving access to certified seeds, farmer training, and market linkages to boost production.

Taita Taveta County aims to grow its Irish potato industry, currently worth US$4.3 million annually, by increasing production from its 750 hectares. The county government is implementing measures to boost farmers' incomes and create jobs, particularly for young people.

The county's 750 hectares yield an estimated 11,250 tonnes of potatoes annually, with average productivity at 15 tonnes per hectare. Farm-gate prices average US$0.39 per kilogram, offering economic opportunities. The government is improving access to certified seeds and farmer training through initiatives like Farmer Field and Business Schools, which have supported 21 farmer groups.

Nine half-acre demonstration plots have been established to train farmers in improved production techniques. Agripreneur Gift Kisombe connected farmers with a seed supplier who purchased certified Markies seed potatoes worth US$1,010. The Kenya Agricultural and Livestock Research Organization is identifying potato varieties suited to the county's diverse ecological zones.

Why it mattersTaita Taveta County's initiative to boost potato production aims to increase household incomes, strengthen food security, and create jobs for young people.

First body recovered from Nairobi building collapse

16 people remain missing after an 18-storey building collapsed in Nairobi last Friday.

A woman's body was recovered Friday from the rubble of an 18-storey building that collapsed a week ago in Nairobi's Eastleigh district, as 16 people remain missing.

Nairobi Governor Johnson Sakaja stated the building lacked required construction permits and that renting ground and basement floors before an occupancy certificate was illegal. Families have gathered at the site for a week, with hopes fading after the critical 72-hour survival window passed.

Thermal imaging cameras, supplied by a Chinese team, are being used in the search, having previously detected two individuals beneath the debris. No survivors have been rescued since Saturday, though four people were previously pulled from the wreckage.

Why it mattersFamilies of 16 people are uncertain about the fate of their loved ones following the building collapse.

Ruto appoints former minister and ex-senator to state agencies

President William Ruto has appointed a former minister and an ex-senator to lead state agencies as he streamlines government operations.

President William Ruto has appointed a former minister and an ex-senator to lead different state agencies. These appointments are part of a move to streamline and enhance government operations. The appointments come ahead of the 2027 General Elections.

The former minister and ex-senator will now lead state agencies. This action is intended to improve the efficiency of government functions. The opposition is also preparing for the 2027 elections, with leaders rallying to unite against Ruto.

The opposition movement has opened a new office in Kwale County as part of its strategy to build county-level structures. This move is also in preparation for the 2027 General Election.

Why it mattersPresident Ruto is appointing new leaders to state agencies and the opposition is building its structures for the 2027 General Election.
