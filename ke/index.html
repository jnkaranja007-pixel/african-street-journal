IEBC requires public officers to resign by Feb. 10

Public officers seeking elective posts must resign by February 10, according to the IEBC. Campaigning for the elections will end on August 7, 2027.

Why it mattersThe deadline forces government employees to choose between their current salaries and running for office.

NANIB seizes Sh3.3 million cannabis in Narok

National Anti-Narcotics Investigations Bureau detectives seized cannabis valued at Sh3.3 million. Three suspects were arrested following intelligence on the storage and distribution of the narcotics.

Why it mattersThe arrests disrupt the local distribution network of narcotics in the Narok area.

Kenya schools to open for Term III on Aug. 24

Schools will open on August 24 as scheduled, according to Education CS Julius Ogamba. The minister refuted social media reports that the date had been postponed.

Why it mattersParents must ensure students return to classrooms by the 24th despite conflicting reports on social media.

Ministry proposes split of tourism levy collection and management

The Ministry of Tourism proposes separating the collection of the tourism levy from its funds management. Tourism PS Julius Bitok said the roles would mirror the functions of the KRA and the CBK.

Why it mattersThe change shifts how tourism funds are handled by introducing specialized roles for collection and management.
