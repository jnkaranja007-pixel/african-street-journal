Former LSK president demands prosecution for Migori attack

Opposition leaders Kalonzo Musyoka, Eugene Wamalwa and Justin Muturi were attacked on November 5, 2026, in Migori.

Former Law Society of Kenya President Faith Odhiambo demanded the prosecution of attackers who disrupted an opposition tour in Migori on Thursday, November 5, 2026. The attack targeted opposition leaders Kalonzo Musyoka, Eugene Wamalwa, and Justin Muturi, damaging several vehicles in their convoy. Odhiambo stated that the incident violated the leaders' constitutional rights to political participation and freedom of expression.

Odhiambo expressed concern over recurring political violence and insufficient action by authorities, noting that shattered windows can be replaced but a life lost cannot. She called for police to identify and investigate those who organised or financed the violence, and for an independent inquiry into police conduct during the confrontation. The leaders were in the South Nyanza region for political mobilisation ahead of the 2027 General Election.

The convoy was pelted with stones by approximately 10 youths, causing damage to vehicles including Muturi's and Musyoka's cars. Running battles between suspected attackers and police, who used teargas, followed the incident. Despite the disruption, the opposition leaders vowed to continue their political engagements.

Why it mattersOpposition leaders and their teams were subjected to violence and property damage during a political tour in Migori.

UN-Habitat, IGAD launch El Niño flood guide for Horn of Africa cities

The new framework helps city leaders prepare for heavy rainfall and protect vulnerable communities before disasters strike.

UN-Habitat and the Intergovernmental Authority on Development (IGAD) launched a preparedness framework on November 6 to help cities in the Greater Horn of Africa prepare for El Niño flooding. The guide, developed with ICPAC, aims to shift city authorities from disaster response to proactive preparation using climate forecasts.

The advisory, titled El Niño Preparedness: An Anticipatory Action Framework for City Leaders, offers practical steps for municipal governments to mobilize resources, strengthen warning systems, and protect infrastructure. It addresses heightened risks in informal settlements and flood-prone neighborhoods amid expectations of heavy rainfall.

The framework outlines how city leaders can translate forecasts into concrete decisions by using impact-based scenarios, pre-agreed action triggers, anticipatory financing, and stronger coordination. It also identifies sector-specific measures to reduce the effects of flooding and disease outbreaks on urban populations.

Why it mattersCities and vulnerable urban communities in the Horn of Africa face heightened risks from El Niño-related flooding and extreme weather events.

Kenya targets sugar export market in three years

The Bura Irrigation Scheme aims to boost sugarcane production with yields double those of other regions.

Kenya aims to enter the international sugar export market within three years through a public-private partnership at the Bura Irrigation Scheme in Tana River County. The government has leased 135,000 acres of the 176,000-acre scheme to investors, including Sukari Industries and Tana River Sugar, who will cultivate sugarcane on 50,000 acres each. This expansion is supported by a new 26-kilometre gravity canal delivering water from the Tana River.

Head of Public Service Felix Koskei stated that the coastal region was chosen for its land and water resources, noting sugarcane matures in 10 months there, faster than in Western Kenya or Nyanza. Irrigation Principal Secretary Ephantus Kimotho added that the scheme could achieve 50 tonnes per hectare, double the yield elsewhere, with the 100,000 acres for sugarcane expected to produce over five million metric tonnes per harvest.

This project is part of efforts to increase sugarcane production and strengthen Kenya's regional sugar market position, reversing a 24.5% drop in domestic production in 2025. The government anticipates meeting domestic demand and creating a surplus for export within four years.

Why it mattersKenyan farmers and investors in the Tana River County's Bura Irrigation Scheme will benefit from increased sugarcane yields and faster crop maturation, leading to export opportunities.

Kenyan pathologists find over 20 wounds on murdered 86-year-old

Three doctors examined Dorita Lasinder Macharia after her death on October 30, 2026, at her Loresho home.

Pathologists found more than 20 injuries on Dorita Lasinder Macharia, 86, who died on the night of October 30, 2026, at her Loresho home. Three doctors conducted a post-mortem examination at Lee Funeral Home, revealing multiple stab wounds to the neck that caused severe bleeding. The examination also noted a fracture on her left upper arm and other sharp-force injuries across her body. Investigators are working to determine the exact weapon used and are examining collected samples.

The post-mortem findings were revealed on Tuesday, November 2, as five people, including the deceased's daughter Regina Wambui Gitao, alias Regina Re, were arrested. Detectives from Gigiri sought 15 days to detain the suspects for further investigation, but the court granted them 10 days. Police Constable David Njoroge stated that preliminary findings suggest a disagreement between Dorita and her granddaughter, Thandie Malaika Wanjiku Gitao, over Thandie's boyfriend, Alton Jimmy Macharia, preceded the incident.

The investigator told the court that Dorita had asked Thandie to leave the house after finding her in a compromising situation with Alton. Dorita then called her son, Jimmy, to assist in evicting her granddaughter. Although Thandie reportedly left, she later returned and stayed with Dorita for about an hour before the elderly woman was found dead. Investigators are analyzing CCTV footage from the estate to piece together the events.

Why it mattersThe murder of 86-year-old Dorita Lasinder Macharia and the subsequent arrest of five individuals, including her daughter, has left her family searching for answers.
