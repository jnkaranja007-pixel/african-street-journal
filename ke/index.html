News · 2026-10-24

woman rescued from collapsed Nairobi building

Emergency teams search for seven missing people after a 16-storey building collapsed in Eastleigh.

A woman was pulled from the rubble of a 16-storey building that collapsed in Nairobi's Eastleigh area on Friday afternoon. Rescue operations are ongoing as emergency teams search for seven people feared trapped beneath the debris. The building's underground construction collapsed, causing panic in the clothing district.

Nairobi Regional Police Commander Issa Mohamud stated that rescue efforts are complicated by reports of a second person heard crying for help, whose cries have since gone silent. The building had not been cleared for occupation, raising questions about why investors allowed it to be occupied. The number of casualties remains unconfirmed.

Authorities are expected to investigate the building's structural condition and compliance with safety requirements. The immediate priority is the search for survivors and providing medical assistance to those rescued. Further updates are expected as teams establish the full impact of the collapse.

Why it mattersA woman was rescued and seven people are missing after a building collapse in Nairobi.

Health · 2026-10-24

Kenya records second Ebola death

A 65-year-old woman, a contact of the first patient, died after developing symptoms on October 13.

Kenya has recorded its second death from Ebola, a 65-year-old woman who was a close family member of the country's first patient. The Ministry of Health confirmed the death on Friday evening, bringing the total fatalities to two among nine confirmed cases.

The woman developed symptoms on October 13 while under quarantine and was admitted to an infectious disease unit the same day. She received supportive care but died early Friday. Health officials are working with her family on a safe burial. Seven patients remain under treatment in isolation facilities.

All nine confirmed cases are linked to a single cluster of known contacts, and the ministry stated there is currently no evidence of community transmission. Authorities urged the public to avoid contact with bodily fluids of sick people and to practice regular handwashing.

Why it mattersKenya's health system is managing nine confirmed Ebola cases and two deaths, with seven patients still undergoing treatment.

Kenya secures Chinese market for dried chillies

Kenyan farmers and exporters can now sell dried chillies to China after meeting strict quality and safety rules.

Kenyan farmers, processors, and traders will now export dried chillies to China, a major consumer market. The Kenya Plant Health Inspectorate Service (KEPHIS) announced this market access on October 16, 2026.

KEPHIS stated that this development allows Kenyan producers to diversify export markets and increase trade. Exporters must register their facilities with China's General Administration of Customs (GACC) through KEPHIS and meet stringent phytosanitary and food safety requirements.

All farmers, drying facilities, and packhouses must also register with KEPHIS. Each shipment to China requires a KEPHIS Phytosanitary Certificate to confirm compliance with the set standards.

Why it mattersKenyan farmers and traders can now export dried chillies to China, creating new income opportunities.
