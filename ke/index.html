Ruto Expands Internship Program by 50,000 Places

President William Ruto announced an expansion of the National Internship Programme by 50,000 places starting January 2027, offering monthly stipends to graduates.

President William Ruto has announced plans to significantly expand the National Internship Programme, adding 50,000 new places starting January 2027. Successful participants will receive monthly stipends. The initiative aims to provide young people completing their studies at universities, technical colleges, and other institutions with practical work experience.

The President stated that placements will be managed through a transparent, digital, and merit-based system. Additionally, incentives will be offered to private employers to provide meaningful work experience. This program is designed to address the challenge graduates face in securing employment without prior experience, which is often a prerequisite for entry-level jobs.

This internship expansion is one of four measures announced by the President, alongside proposed changes to income tax, stamp duty on land transactions, and import duty on mobile phones. The government is expected to finalize the necessary administrative and financing arrangements for the program's rollout.

Why it mattersYoung people completing their studies will gain work experience and receive stipends, addressing the difficulty of finding jobs without prior experience.

Kenyan court convicts cult leader of 191 starvation deaths

Pastor Paul Mackenzie and 28 others were found guilty of murder for inciting followers to starve themselves to death.

A Kenyan court on Monday found cult leader Paul Mackenzie and 28 associates guilty of murdering 191 people, primarily children, in the Shakahola Forest Massacre. The judge stated the prosecution proved the case for the deaths of 190 children and one adult, rejecting claims that the victims voluntarily fasted. Hundreds of bodies have been exhumed from the cult's base near Malindi since 2023, with the total number of victims exceeding 400 over three years.

Mackenzie, who allegedly told his followers to starve to meet Jesus, remained impassive in the Mombasa courtroom as the verdict was delivered. He faces separate charges of manslaughter and terrorism. Relatives of those who died, many from rural coastal communities, were present in court to hear the judgment.

The Shakahola Forest Massacre has revealed a grim pattern of deaths, with bodies of men, women, and children unearthed over the past three years. The court's decision marks a significant step in holding those responsible accountable for the widespread loss of life attributed to the cult's extreme practices.

Why it mattersRelatives of victims are affected by the court's guilty verdict in the starvation cult case.

Kenya Ebola Cases Prompt Travel Advisories

The US has advised citizens to reconsider travel to Kenya following confirmation of nine Ebola cases, including one death.

Kenya has confirmed nine cases of the Ebola virus, with one fatality, all linked to a single patient cluster. The outbreak has prompted international concern, with the United States advising its citizens to reconsider travel to the country.

Despite the travel advisory, the Kenyan government is working to reassure the public about the situation. The confirmed cases are contained within a single cluster, suggesting a limited spread from the initial patient.

The emergence of Ebola cases in Kenya has led to countries beginning to impose restrictions on travel and trade with the nation. The government is focused on managing public perception and controlling the outbreak.

Why it mattersInvestors face potential disruption and increased costs due to international travel advisories and potential restrictions impacting trade and business operations.
