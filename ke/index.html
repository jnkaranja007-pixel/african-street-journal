Ruto returns to Parliament with Sh5 trillion plan under scrutiny

The National Infrastructure Fund holds Sh310.6 billion in starting capital, against a wider ambition to raise Sh5 trillion over a decade.

President William Ruto addresses Parliament on Wednesday in his 2026 State of the Nation Address, nearly a year after unveiling a Sh5 trillion transformation blueprint. Its four priorities cover people, net exports, water storage and irrigation, and energy and transport.

Kenya's National Treasury, in its Draft 2027 Budget Policy Statement, says the National Infrastructure Fund began with Sh310.6 billion: Sh106.3 billion from the Kenya Pipeline Company share sale and Sh204.3 billion from the partial Safaricom stake sale. Treasury papers show no binding commitments from outside investors at the size the plan needs.

Officials aim to raise up to Sh5 trillion over time, with about US$2 billion in international co-investment sought through certified funds. A High Court ruling nullifying the partial Safaricom sale is under appeal by the State. The World Bank puts gross public debt at 69.9 per cent of GDP at the end of June 2026.

Why it mattersThe State is appealing the High Court decision nullifying its partial Safaricom divestiture, which supplied Sh204.3 billion to the fund.

Thugge backs Treasury plan to centralise county deposits at CBK

The proposed law would require counties to get written Treasury approval before opening or closing accounts and move their funds into a single account.

Central Bank of Kenya Governor Kamau Thugge has backed proposed amendments to the Public Finance Management Act that would centralise county government deposits at the central bank. He presented his support to the Senate Standing Committee on Finance and Budget in Nairobi on Nov. 18.

The plan would require counties to obtain written approval from the National Treasury before opening or closing bank accounts, and consolidate county funds in a Treasury Single Account at the CBK. Thugge said the changes would improve oversight, reduce commercial bank accounts and lower borrowing costs by giving the Treasury access to idle public funds.

Thugge also proposed limiting each county to one commercial bank imprest account, with main operational and project accounts held at the CBK. He told senators that keeping county deposits at the central bank would avoid interest payments when government resources were already available.

Why it mattersCounty governments would need Treasury approval to open or close accounts and keep their main operational and project funds at the CBK under the proposed changes.

Kenya hosts global parliamentary hunger summit

The 16-17 November meeting brings parliamentarians and development partners together to discuss food security, nutrition and agrifood systems.

Lawmakers, parliamentary networks and development partners from around the world are meeting in Kenya from 16 to 17 November for the 2026 Global Parliamentary Summit Against Hunger and Malnutrition.

The summit is jointly organised by the Food and Agriculture Organization of the United Nations, the Spanish Agency for International Development Cooperation and Kenya's Parliament, with other parliamentary partners. Delegates are discussing the right to food, access to healthy diets and resilient, sustainable agrifood systems.

Gender equality, rural communities and access to safe, affordable, nutritious food are also on the agenda. Earlier editions were held in Spain in 2018 and Chile in 2023, and the organisers say the aim is to turn international commitments into action through legislation, oversight and accountability.

Why it mattersParliaments are expected to address food access through legislation and oversight, with discussions covering rural communities and sustainable agrifood systems.

Gachagua presses IEBC for a full day of election technology talks

The opposition leader says Thursday’s two-and-a-half-hour meeting is too short to discuss Smartmatic and voter registration ahead of the 2027 polls.

Rigathi Gachagua, leader of the Democracy of the Citizens Party, has asked Kenya’s Independent Electoral and Boundaries Commission (IEBC) to extend Thursday’s consultation with opposition leaders to a full day. The talks are to address whether to renew election technology provider Smartmatic’s contract or choose another way to continue voter registration ahead of the 2027 polls.

Gachagua said the scheduled two-and-a-half hours would not give him enough time to present concerns raised by Kenyans. He said he needed at least one and a half hours to address commissioners and had requested a full day for consultation. The IEBC invited opposition principals after Gachagua called over the weekend for political parties and other stakeholders to discuss the technology for the election.

Gachagua also urged the Registrar of Persons to meet opposition leaders over what he alleges is bias in issuing national identity cards. He claimed applicants in areas seen as supporting President William Ruto received IDs in two to three days, while those in opposition strongholds waited up to seven months. He said he would take the matter to the registrar, arguing the delays disadvantage young people as voter registration continues.

Why it mattersYoung people waiting for national identity cards in areas perceived to support the opposition face delays of up to seven months while voter registration continues.

Politics · 2026-11-18

Sifuna's Linda Mwananchi Movement takes US tour to seven states from November 21

The movement plans town halls, community dinners and meetings with elected officials, with Kenyan diaspora communities at the centre, until December 7.

Nairobi Senator Edwin Sifuna's Linda Mwananchi Movement has unveiled a United States tour running from November 21 to December 7, with events in New Jersey, Pennsylvania, Maryland, Washington DC, Texas, Washington state and California. The movement said the tour begins with the opening of its office in Hackettstown, New Jersey, at noon on November 21.

According to the movement's itinerary, the team will hold a town hall meeting at The Palace Center in Allentown, Pennsylvania, from 2pm to 6pm on November 22, and a community dinner at Spark Hotel in Maryland on November 23. The tour also includes meetings with Gen Z groups and elected officials, and a Thanksgiving dinner in Dallas on November 27.

The schedule runs through Houston, where a women's caucus is set for November 29, and then Seattle-area events on December 3 and 4. The final listed engagement is a dinner in Silicon Valley on December 7, scheduled to begin at 5pm.

Why it mattersKenyans in the United States can attend the movement's town halls and dinners in their own cities through early December.
