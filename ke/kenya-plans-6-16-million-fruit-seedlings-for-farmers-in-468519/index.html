Kenya plans 6.16 million fruit seedlings for farmers in 2027/28

The draft budget includes avocado, mango, citrus, coconut and date palm, alongside macadamia, cashew nut, tea and coffee seedlings.

Source: Food Business Africa · 2026-11-25 · 1 min read

Kenya plans to distribute 6.16 million seedlings to farmers in the 2027/28 financial year, according to its draft 2027 Budget Policy Statement. The programme covers avocado, mango, citrus, coconut, date palm, macadamia, cashew nut, tea and coffee.

The Treasury says the distribution is intended to move farmers towards higher-value crops. Date palms are designated for growers in Arid and Semi-Arid Lands, where cultivation will focus on crops suited to local conditions. The draft also proposes more processing and cold-chain infrastructure for avocados and mangoes.

The statement gives no programme cost, procurement process, county delivery schedule, crop-by-crop allocation or eligibility rules. Kenya’s avocado harvest reached 694,000 tons in 2025; the Agriculture and Food Authority projects 727,000 tons in 2026.

Why it mattersFruit farmers are due to receive seedlings in 2027/28, but the draft sets no county delivery schedule or eligibility rules.
