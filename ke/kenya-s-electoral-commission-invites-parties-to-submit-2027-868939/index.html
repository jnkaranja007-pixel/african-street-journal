Kenya's electoral commission invites parties to submit 2027 poll reforms

The invitation followed an opposition walkout from a Nairobi consultation before the commission presented its election-preparedness plans.

Sources: Capital FM and Kenyans.co.ke · 2026-11-21 · 1 min read

Kenya’s Independent Electoral and Boundaries Commission (IEBC) invited political parties to submit election-reform proposals formally after opposition leaders left a consultation in Nairobi on Thursday. The meeting was about preparations for the August 10, 2027 General Election.

IEBC Chairperson Erastus Ethekon said in a Friday statement that the commission had adjusted the programme, including its start time, to accommodate opposition requests. He said the leaders departed after presenting demands but before the commission’s presentation or substantive discussions, missing information on preparedness, challenges and measures underway.

The commission said it would review proposals within its legal mandate and involve other institutions where necessary. The opposition leaders had called for their reform demands to be addressed before IEBC presented its preparations.

Why it mattersOpposition parties must now submit their proposed reforms formally for IEBC review within its legal mandate.
