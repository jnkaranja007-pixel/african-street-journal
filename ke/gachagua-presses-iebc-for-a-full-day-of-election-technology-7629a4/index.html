Gachagua presses IEBC for a full day of election technology talks

The opposition leader says Thursday’s two-and-a-half-hour meeting is too short to discuss Smartmatic and voter registration ahead of the 2027 polls.

Source: Capital FM · 2026-11-18 · 1 min read

Rigathi Gachagua, leader of the Democracy of the Citizens Party, has asked Kenya’s Independent Electoral and Boundaries Commission (IEBC) to extend Thursday’s consultation with opposition leaders to a full day. The talks are to address whether to renew election technology provider Smartmatic’s contract or choose another way to continue voter registration ahead of the 2027 polls.

Gachagua said the scheduled two-and-a-half hours would not give him enough time to present concerns raised by Kenyans. He said he needed at least one and a half hours to address commissioners and had requested a full day for consultation. The IEBC invited opposition principals after Gachagua called over the weekend for political parties and other stakeholders to discuss the technology for the election.

Gachagua also urged the Registrar of Persons to meet opposition leaders over what he alleges is bias in issuing national identity cards. He claimed applicants in areas seen as supporting President William Ruto received IDs in two to three days, while those in opposition strongholds waited up to seven months. He said he would take the matter to the registrar, arguing the delays disadvantage young people as voter registration continues.

Why it mattersYoung people waiting for national identity cards in areas perceived to support the opposition face delays of up to seven months while voter registration continues.
