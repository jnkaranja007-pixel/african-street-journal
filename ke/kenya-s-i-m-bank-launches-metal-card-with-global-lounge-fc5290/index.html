Kenya's I&M Bank launches metal card with global lounge access

The Mastercard World Elite card adds travel and medical insurance, concierge support and curated hotel and lifestyle offers to its benefits.

Sources: Capital FM and Business Daily Africa · 2026-11-25 · 1 min read

I&M Bank, a Kenyan lender, has introduced a Mastercard World Elite Metal Credit Card aimed at affluent professionals, executives and frequent travellers. The card offers its benefits with no minimum spending requirement.

Cardholders receive unlimited airport lounge access worldwide for themselves and one guest, plus fast-track services at selected airports. A concierge service handles travel arrangements and dining reservations, and Mastercard's Priceless platform gives access to experiences in dining, sport, culture and entertainment.

The bank says the card also includes travel and medical insurance, emergency assistance, fraud monitoring and purchase protection. Curated hotel partnerships and retail, hospitality and golf offers are included as well.

Why it mattersPremium cardholders in Kenya can use insurance, lounge access and lifestyle perks without meeting any minimum spend.
