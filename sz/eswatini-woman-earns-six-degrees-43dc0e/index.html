Eswatini woman earns six degrees

Siphiwe Vilakati holds degrees in law, international economic relations, aviation, and business management.

Source: Times of Eswatini · 2026-11-08 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Siphiwe Vilakati from kaVelezizweni, Eswatini, has achieved six academic degrees and is pursuing a seventh. Her qualifications include a Bachelor of Laws from the University of Zululand and master's degrees from Loyola University of Chicago and Toulouse 1 Capitole University.

Vilakati also holds a Master of Laws in International Economic Law from UNISA, a Postgraduate Diploma in Taxation from UNESWA, and a Global Executive Master of Business Administration in Aerospace Management from Toulouse Business School. She is currently a Doctor of Laws candidate in Mercantile Law at UNISA.

Reflecting on her journey, Vilakati stated that experiencing both rural and urban life instilled in her an understanding of life's complexities. She believes her achievements can inspire others to pursue their dreams, emphasizing that with faith, anything is possible.

Why it mattersSiphiwe Vilakati, an Eswatini national, has earned six degrees in diverse fields, showcasing advanced educational attainment.
