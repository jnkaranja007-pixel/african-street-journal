Eswatini volleyball chief joins global congress that approves new rules

The International Volleyball Federation approved a new constitution and rules for indoor and beach volleyball at its 40th World Congress in the Dominican Republic.

Source: Times of Eswatini · 2026-11-24 · 1 min read

Eric Mseni Gama, president of the Eswatini National Volleyball Association, represented the country at the 40th International Volleyball Federation (FIVB) World Congress in Punta Cana, Dominican Republic, from November 20 to 22, 2026. The congress adopted a new constitution and official rules for indoor and beach volleyball.

The gathering also approved rebranding the FIVB as World Volleyball and confirmed 232 affiliated members. Doha, Qatar, was confirmed as host of the 41st World Congress in November 2028.

Confederation of African Volleyball President Bouchra Hajij outlined the continent's progress and ambitions. The FIVB and the African confederation signed a memorandum to strengthen indoor and beach volleyball across Africa.

Why it mattersEswatini's volleyball players and clubs will play under the revised indoor and beach rules, and its association now has to apply the new constitution.
