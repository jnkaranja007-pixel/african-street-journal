Eswatini central bank holds discount rate at 7%

The decision keeps the prime lending rate at 10.50% as October inflation rises to 3.4%.

Source: Times of Eswatini · 2026-11-22 · 1 min read

The Central Bank of Eswatini kept its discount rate at 7% on Friday, leaving the prime lending rate at 10.50% until its next monetary policy meeting. The decision came as headline inflation accelerated by 0.5 percentage points to 3.4% in October, from 2.9% in September.

The bank’s Monetary Policy Consultative Committee chose not to tighten monetary policy, in line with economists’ expectations. The Central Statistical Office said monthly inflation rose to 0.9% in October from 0.2% in September, with transport and housing-related costs driving pressure on household spending.

Transport inflation reached 6.9% annually, while housing, water, electricity, gas and other fuels rose 6.8%. Food and non-alcoholic beverages recorded annual inflation of negative 0.1%.

Why it mattersPeople and businesses borrowing from commercial banks face no immediate increase in borrowing costs; the prime lending rate stays at 10.50% until the next monetary policy meeting.
