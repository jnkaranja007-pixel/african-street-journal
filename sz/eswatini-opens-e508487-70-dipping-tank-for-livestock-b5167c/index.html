Eswatini opens E508487.70 dipping tank for livestock services

The Mayithukuse facility will offer vaccinations and treatments, with auctions planned to give Ndlalambi farmers another way to sell animals.

Source: Eswatini Positive News · 2026-11-20 · 1 min read

Eswatini opened the Mayithukuse Dipping Tank No. 459 in Ndlalambi, Hhohho Region, on Thursday, November 19. The facility was built with combined contributions of E508487.70 and is intended to provide livestock health services and a market for farmers.

Acting Principal Secretary Nhlanhla Motsa said the tank would offer vaccinations, animal treatments, branding, ear tagging and blood sample collection. Micro Projects contributed E323982.02; local farmers provided materials and labour valued at E184505.68. Motsa said livestock auctions would let farmers sell through competitive bidding, and urged them to work with agricultural officials to put the initiative into operation.

Motsa also warned that El Niño-related dry conditions could reduce grazing and water availability, advising farmers to prepare feed, including by buying grass bales.

Why it mattersNdlalambi livestock owners can use the planned competitive auctions to sell animals, while the facility also brings veterinary services closer to their farms.
