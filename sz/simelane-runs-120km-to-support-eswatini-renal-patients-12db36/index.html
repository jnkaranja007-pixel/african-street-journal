Simelane runs 120km to support Eswatini renal patients

The Royal Eswatini Sugar employee finished a solo route from Siteki to Lavumisa in under 24 hours, with funds raised still to be tallied.

Source: Times of Eswatini · 2026-11-25 · 1 min read

Andreas Simelane, an ultra-marathon runner and Royal Eswatini Sugar Corporation employee, completed a 120km solo run from Siteki to Lavumisa on Tuesday to raise awareness for men’s health. He finished at about 6pm, in under 24 hours, despite adverse weather.

The initiative, run with the Ministry of Health, will benefit the Renal Association Eswatini. Simelane said he wanted to give hope to the association’s 300 patients. Several corporate sponsors have backed the drive, and organisers have left it open to further partners before announcing the final fundraising total.

The run doubled the 60km distance set by the annual Move for Movember challenge, which encourages participants to cover 60km during November. Simelane also completed a 161km race in South Africa in August and a 109km solo run the following month.

Why it mattersThe Renal Association Eswatini’s 300 patients are the stated beneficiaries, while additional sponsors can still join before the fundraising total is announced.
