Eswatini tourism authority urges 228 accommodation businesses to report online

The authority says the sector grew from 165 establishments in 2019, but better occupancy and guest data are needed to guide policy and investment.

Source: Eswatini Positive News · 2026-11-25 · 1 min read

Eswatini’s accommodation sector grew from 165 establishments in 2019 to 228 in 2026, and the Eswatini Tourism Authority is urging operators to submit monthly business data online. The Central Statistical Office introduced the platform in collaboration with the authority, replacing paper questionnaires.

Authority chief executive Vusie Dlamini said operators should make online submissions part of their normal business routines and ensure staff understand the reporting requirements. He said figures on room occupancy, guest numbers, length of stay and facility use help assess the sector’s performance and guide government policy and private investment.

National economic statistics put tourism’s contribution to gross domestic product at 1.4% in 2025; accommodation and food services accounted for 0.8% of that contribution. Dlamini said those figures cover direct activity, not tourism’s full value across other sectors.

Why it mattersAccommodation operators must move to monthly online questionnaires and ensure staff know how to report business information.
