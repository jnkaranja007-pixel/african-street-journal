Sugar surge lifts Eswatini manufacturing growth

Food processing rose 27 per cent in the second quarter of 2026, driven by a 53.5 per cent increase in sugar production.

Source: Times of Eswatini · 2026-11-15 · 1 min read

Eswatini's manufacturing sector grew 14 per cent in the second quarter of 2026, with food processing up 27 per cent, the Central Bank of Eswatini reports. The Central Bank attributed most of that gain to a 53.5 per cent jump in sugar production, which was supported by a larger area of harvested sugar cane and better sucrose and sugar yields.

Across the economy, output expanded by 6.5 per cent year-on-year in the quarter ended June 2026, when measured by Gross Domestic Product (GDP), after 5.9 per cent growth in the first quarter. Quarter-on-quarter growth, however, eased to 0.7 per cent from a revised 2.6 per cent in March. Primary, secondary and tertiary sectors all grew from a year earlier.

Non-food manufacturing expanded 11.1 per cent, held up by chemical products and cement, though both grew more slowly than in the first quarter. Textile and wearing apparel makers contracted 3.4 per cent amid uncertain conditions in their key export markets.

Why it mattersSugar millers and cane growers are the clearest winners, while textile and apparel firms that export face a contraction tied to weaker demand abroad.
