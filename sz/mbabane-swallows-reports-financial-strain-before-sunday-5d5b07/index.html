Mbabane Swallows reports financial strain before Sunday league clash

The club says it has no sponsor for salaries and other expenses, while players reportedly refused to train after transport and meal problems.

Source: Times of Eswatini · 2026-11-19 · 1 min read

Mbabane Swallows, an Eswatini Premier League club, is facing financial and logistical problems before its scheduled match against defending champions Nsingizini Hotspurs at Somhlolo National Stadium on Sunday. A source close to the club said players refused to train at a session after the team struggled to fund bus fuel and provide meals.

Swallows public relations officer Knowledge Ngwenya said on national radio on Monday that running the club costs about E250 000 a month and that it has no sponsor to cover salaries and other expenses. He said the team had attended training despite the strain. A source said players nearly missed Tuesday’s training because the club lacked fuel money for its bus to Ezulwini, and that Sunday’s training was cancelled after players refused to take part.

The club’s supporters approved proposed changes to ownership and governance at a meeting on Saturday. Ngwenya said an advisory board was expected to discuss the proposals with Swallows (Pty) Ltd.

Why it mattersSwallows players face disrupted training and meals, while club officials say they must find a way to meet monthly costs of E250 000 without a sponsor.
