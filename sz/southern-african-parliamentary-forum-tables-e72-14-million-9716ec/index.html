Southern African Parliamentary Forum tables E72.14 million budget for 2027/28

The proposed budget was presented to the 59th Plenary Assembly, which Eswatini hosted, as member parliaments debate the body's financial sustainability.

Sources: Times of Eswatini and Eswatini Positive News · 2026-11-24 · 1 min read

The SADC Parliamentary Forum, a body of parliaments from across Southern Africa, has tabled a proposed budget of E72.14 million for the 2027/2028 financial year. The budget was presented before the 59th Plenary Assembly in Eswatini, where member parliaments are deliberating on the organisation's financial sustainability and future operations.

Lobamba Member of Parliament Michael Masilela called for an increase in mandatory contributions by member states, arguing that the Forum should strengthen its own funding base rather than rely heavily on external donors. The discussions also come as regional legislators weigh reforms and the future establishment of a fully functional SADC Parliament.

The proposed budget is now before the Plenary Assembly for consideration, with member parliaments weighing the Forum's financial requirements against the need for sustainable funding.

Why it mattersMember parliaments must now decide whether to fund a budget that depends on mandatory contributions rather than donors, and whether to raise those contributions as Masilela proposed.
