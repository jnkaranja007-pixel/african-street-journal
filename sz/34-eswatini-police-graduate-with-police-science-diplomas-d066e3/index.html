34 Eswatini police graduate with police science diplomas

The officers gained skills to tackle emerging crimes like cybercrime through a UNESWA and police academy partnership.

Source: Times of Eswatini · 2026-10-26 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

34 police officers in Eswatini have graduated with diplomas in Police Science from the University of Eswatini (UNESWA) and the Eswatini Police Academy. The graduation ceremony took place at Kwaluseni Campus on Saturday.

National Commissioner of Police Vusi Masango stated that the joint training equips officers to address evolving criminal activities driven by technological advancements. He thanked UNESWA for the partnership, calling it a step towards a more professionally equipped police service.

The police service aims to expand this partnership with UNESWA to include more academic programmes addressing other crime and policing areas. Further training opportunities are also planned to support officers' personal development and career advancement.
