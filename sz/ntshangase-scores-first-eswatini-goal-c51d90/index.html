Ntshangase scores first Eswatini goal

The former Kaizer Chiefs midfielder's lone strike secured Amawele a 1-0 victory in the MTN Premier League.

Source: Times of Eswatini · 2026-10-24 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Siphelele Ntshangase scored his first goal since arriving in Eswatini today, netting the only goal in a match against Madlenya at Killarney Sports Ground. The former Kaizer Chiefs player's strike secured a 1-0 win for his club, Amawele, in the fifth week of the MTN Premier League season.

This victory places Amawele, owned by South African businessman Chief Ally Kgomongwe, on nine points with their third win of the campaign. Ntshangase's goal marks his return to professional football after a three-year break, having joined Amawele from SAFA Limpopo ABC Motsepe League side Mohlabile United.

Amawele's win follows a 1-0 victory over Pigg’s Peak Professionals last week. The league's top goalscorer this season is guaranteed a revised E15 000 prize.
