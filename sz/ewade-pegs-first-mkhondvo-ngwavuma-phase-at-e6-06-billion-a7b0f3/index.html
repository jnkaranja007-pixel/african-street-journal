EWADE pegs first Mkhondvo-Ngwavuma phase at E6.06 billion

The estimate covers four infrastructure components; a proposed E987 million BADEA loan still needs parliamentary approval.

Source: Times of Eswatini · 2026-11-19 · 1 min read

Eswatini’s first phase of the Mkhondvo-Ngwavuma Water Augmentation Project is estimated to cost E6.06 billion, according to the Eswatini Water and Agricultural Development Enterprise (EWADE). The plan covers Mpakeni Dam, a main conveyance system and irrigation infrastructure.

The African Development Bank has committed E2.629 billion for the dam and E2.084 billion for the conveyance system. The OPEC Fund committed E360 million for irrigation works in Maloma I and II. EWADE proposes using a E987 million loan from the Arab Bank for Economic Development in Africa (BADEA) for irrigation distribution serving St Philips and Mconcwane/Mcathuvane; parliament has yet to approve it.

Private-sector funding is planned for on-farm development in those latter areas. EWADE chief executive Samson Sithole said the project is intended to expand agricultural production and create employment.

Why it mattersParliament must decide whether to approve the E987 million BADEA loan for distribution infrastructure serving St Philips and Mconcwane/Mcathuvane.
