Magagula’s Maniema Union hosts Singida in CAF group stage

The Eswatini midfielder’s Congolese club has eight points from five matches and needs a top-four finish to reach the Championship Group.

Source: Times of Eswatini · 2026-11-26 · 1 min read

Eswatini midfielder Junior “Shisa Gesi” Magagula’s CAF Confederation Cup campaign resumes on Sunday, November 29, when AS Maniema Union host Tanzania’s Singida Black Stars at the Stade des Martyrs in Kinshasa. Kick-off is scheduled for 3pm.

Maniema sit sixth in Group B with eight points from five matches and have lost once since the new campaign began. Only the top four of the group’s 13 teams advance to the Championship Group. Magagula joined the Democratic Republic of Congo club on a three-year contract in July.

Maniema travel to face Zambia’s Red Rhinos on December 5. The club reached the group stage for the third consecutive time after beating Angola’s Kabuscorp 8-2 on aggregate; Magagula scored after coming off the bench in the second-leg 4-1 win. The run guarantees Maniema more than E6.4 million in prize money.

Why it mattersMagagula has two group-stage fixtures scheduled, with Maniema’s next match away to Zambia’s Red Rhinos on December 5.
