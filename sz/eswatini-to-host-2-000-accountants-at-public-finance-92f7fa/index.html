Eswatini to host 2,000 accountants at public finance convention

Delegates from 34 African countries are registered for the November 30-December 4 gathering at Ezulwini Pavlazzo.

Source: Times of Eswatini · 2026-11-23 · 1 min read

Eswatini will host about 2,000 accountants from 34 African countries at a public finance convention in Ezulwini from November 30 to December 4. The gathering will be held at Ezulwini Pavlazzo.

African Association of Accountants General chief executive Frederick Riaga said the registration figure was recorded by the close of business last Friday. The convention will bring together finance leaders to discuss protecting public and taxpayer funds, reporting on them and managing financial-loss risks, Accountant General Nomsa Simelane said.

Simelane said participating countries would exchange lessons on putting public finance reforms into practice. Eswatini’s Finance Ministry and the Office of the Accountant General are organising the event.

Why it mattersThe Finance Ministry and Accountant General’s office must host 2,000 registered delegates from 34 African countries over five days, starting November 30.
