US challenges Mauritius and Seychelles over blocked Eswatini flight

Washington submitted a paper to the International Civil Aviation Organisation Council after the island states withdrew permits for Lai Ching-te’s April flight.

Source: Times of Eswatini · 2026-11-26 · 1 min read

The United States has challenged Mauritius and Seychelles at the International Civil Aviation Organisation (ICAO) over their withdrawal of overflight permits for a charter carrying Taiwan President Lai Ching-te to Eswatini in April. Washington submitted a working paper to the 36-member ICAO Council in Montreal for its November 2026 session, according to Reuters, which saw the document.

The permits for the China Airlines aircraft were revoked days before Lai was due to arrive on April 22 for King Mswati III’s 40th anniversary celebrations. Taiwan postponed the visit; Lai later travelled to Eswatini from May 2 to May 4 on a private aircraft sent by the king. The US paper questions the island states’ authority to revoke clearances for routes over the high seas.

The paper argues that administrative responsibility for Flight Information Regions does not give a state sovereignty over international airspace. ICAO assigns those regions to states for air-traffic control, safety monitoring and search-and-rescue coordination.

Why it mattersTaiwan’s postponed state visit was rescheduled for May 2-4, with Lai travelling on a private aircraft sent by Eswatini’s king.
