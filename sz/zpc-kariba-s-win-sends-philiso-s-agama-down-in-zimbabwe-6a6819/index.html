ZPC Kariba’s win sends Philiso’s Agama down in Zimbabwe

Agama beat Highlanders 2-1 on Wednesday, but the result could not keep the Eswatini defender’s club in the Premier Soccer League.

Source: Times of Eswatini · 2026-11-26 · 1 min read

Eswatini defender Newman “Strong” Philiso was relegated with Agama after ZPC Kariba beat Herentals College 2-0 in Zimbabwe’s Premier Soccer League on Wednesday, November 25. Agama had beaten Highlanders 2-1, but ZPC Kariba’s victory sealed its drop from the top flight.

Agama needed to win by at least 13 goals and rely on ZPC Kariba losing to survive, according to Times of Eswatini. The club won promotion at the start of the campaign, and Philiso joined shortly after the season began. His future with Agama was uncertain when the report was published.

The final-day fixtures had been postponed from November 21 after players at Agama and Highlanders stopped work over unpaid wages. The Footballers Union of Zimbabwe notified authorities, and the league rescheduled the matches for Wednesday.

Why it mattersPhiliso’s future at Agama is uncertain, and the club will compete in Zimbabwe’s lower tier after Wednesday’s results.
