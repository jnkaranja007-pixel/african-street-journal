Eswatini healer survives KZN hijacking

Two armed men shot at a traditional healer and stole his friend's car in South Africa's KwaZulu-Natal province.

Source: Eswatini Positive News · 2026-11-16 · 1 min read

A traditional healer from Eswatini escaped unharmed after two armed men shot at him and hijacked a vehicle in Tongaat, KwaZulu-Natal, on Sunday afternoon, November 15, 2026. The incident occurred along the R102 near Gopallal Road.

The healer had travelled from Eswatini for his mother’s funeral and agreed to help two men with a Zulu traditional healing matter. After providing assistance, he offered them a ride back in his friend's white Toyota Yaris, registration NDOSI 9 ZN. The suspects, seated in the back, produced firearms and fired two shots at the healer before stealing the car, four cellphones, and a blank-firing firearm.

Reaction Unit South Africa (RUSA) responded to the call for assistance at 4:22pm but were unsuccessful in tracing the stolen vehicle. The suspects fled towards Gopallal Road and Noodsberg Road, and remain at large.

Why it mattersA traditional healer from Eswatini was targeted in a hijacking and shooting in South Africa, with his vehicle and cellphones stolen.
