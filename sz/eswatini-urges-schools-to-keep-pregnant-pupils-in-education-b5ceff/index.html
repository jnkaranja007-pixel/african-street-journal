Eswatini urges schools to keep pregnant pupils in education

Senator Linda Nxumalo says schools must prevent stigma and discrimination and support teenage mothers returning to class under the national policy.

Source: Eswatini Positive News · 2026-11-24 · 1 min read

Eswatini’s government has called on schools to protect pregnant pupils from stigma and discrimination and help them continue their education. Senator Dr Linda Nxumalo spoke during consideration of a regional anti-sexual-harassment policy at a Southern African Development Community Parliamentary Forum plenary assembly in Ezulwini.

Nxumalo said schools must put into practice the National Policy on Prevention and Management of Learner Pregnancy. She called for teenage mothers to be supported in returning to school and completing their education, and said Parliament should oversee the policy’s implementation.

The policy recognises the risk that pregnancy could leave girls facing a lasting educational disadvantage, Nxumalo said. She urged each school to understand and fulfil its responsibility to protect affected learners.

Why it mattersPregnant pupils and teenage mothers depend on schools to support their continued education and return to class, while Parliament is being urged to oversee implementation.
