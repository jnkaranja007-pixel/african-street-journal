Eswatini prepares two climate reports for December 31 submission

The reports will cover greenhouse gas emissions, adaptation and climate finance as droughts, erratic rain, heatwaves and storms affect the country.

Source: Eswatini Positive News · 2026-11-26 · 1 min read

Eswatini is preparing its Second Biennial Transparency Report and Fifth National Communication for submission to the United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change by December 31, 2026. Tourism and Environmental Affairs Minister Jane Mkhonta-Simelane announced the deadline on Wednesday at the opening of a three-day national validation workshop in Mbabane.

The reports will set out national greenhouse gas emissions, progress on the country’s second climate pledge, climate impacts and adaptation measures, and financial, technological and capacity-building support received and still needed. Mkhonta-Simelane said droughts, erratic rainfall, heatwaves and storms were affecting water, agriculture, health, infrastructure and livelihoods.

A separate gender and climate assessment will examine differing effects on women, men, young people and vulnerable groups. The minister said accurate reporting would inform government planning and budgeting and help Eswatini access international climate finance and technical support.

Why it mattersGovernment planners will use the reports to shape climate budgets, while Eswatini must submit them by December 31 to document support received and still needed.
