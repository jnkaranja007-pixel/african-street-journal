Eswatini secures air cargo licence as security compliance reaches 85%

Principal Secretary Thulani Mkhaliphi said the licence was obtained in the week before he spoke, after the country raised aviation security compliance from about 35%.

Source: Eswatini Positive News · 2026-11-27 · 1 min read

Eswatini has secured an air cargo carrying licence after improving its aviation security compliance from about 35% to around 85%, Principal Secretary Thulani Mkhaliphi said. The licence followed progress in meeting regulatory requirements for the country's aviation operations.

Mkhaliphi announced the licence at a Swaziland Editors Forum open forum on development financing and debt sustainability at the Mountain View hotel in Mbabane on Thursday. He said reaching this stage took considerable time because several aspects of aviation operations had to be aligned with required standards. International aviation players were taking notice of the country's progress, he said.

Mkhaliphi also said Eswatini should not let its lack of a coastline keep it from maritime trade, noting that about 80% of international trade moves by sea. The Ministry of Public Works and Transport is also developing a QR code system so transport inspectors can verify public transport permits electronically.

Why it mattersEswatini's aviation authorities must maintain regulatory compliance to use the cargo licence, with security compliance reported at around 85%.
