Agama’s relegation decider delayed until November 25 after wage dispute

The Zimbabwe Premier Soccer League postponed both relegation fixtures after unpaid wages led Highlanders players to refuse travel and Agama’s squad to protest.

Source: Times of Eswatini · 2026-11-22 · 1 min read

Agama, the Zimbabwe club of Eswatini international Newman “Strong” Philiso, will play its relegation decider against Highlanders on November 25 after unpaid wages disrupted Saturday’s fixtures. Highlanders players refused to travel to the match at Wadzanai Stadium, while Agama’s players had also protested over unpaid dues.

The Zimbabwe Premier Soccer League Emergency Committee said the disputes could not be resolved amicably and postponed both matches. Agama have 31 points and must beat Highlanders while ZPC Kariba, on 33, lose to Herentals; a ZPC Kariba draw would leave Agama needing a 13-goal win. The fixtures are scheduled for 2pm and will be played simultaneously.

Highlanders chairman Kindman Ndlovu said players had gone four months without salaries; chief executive Denzil Mnkandla put the arrears at two to three months. The club said the dispute could affect preparations for the Chibuku Super Cup final.

Why it mattersPhiliso and Agama’s players must wait for the November 25 match to learn whether the club stays in Zimbabwe’s top league.
