Pupil, 14, drowns in Mbabane River after leaving school to swim

Police and fire rescue divers recovered the body of the Form I pupil from Hermann Gmeiner (SOS) High School several hours after the drowning was reported.

Source: Times of Eswatini · 2026-11-17 · 1 min read

A 14-year-old pupil at Hermann Gmeiner (SOS) High School drowned in the Mbabane River at Manzana, behind the Sidvwashini Correctional Facility, at about 12:45pm after an examination, police said. The deceased, identified as Ryan, was a Form I pupil from Ngwenya, and police say he had left school with other pupils to go swimming.

Chief Police Information and Communication Officer Senior Superintendent Phindile Vilakati confirmed the incident. Community member Banele Vilakati said he and a colleague found the pupil's school uniform and shoes at the scene and entered the river to search, but failed to find him. Police divers and fire rescue personnel began the search between 4:40pm and 5pm, and recovered the body after relatives were brought from Ngwenya.

Community members questioned the delay, which police attributed to divers attending another drowning at Gebeni. Vilakati urged parents and communities to protect children from swimming in rivers, particularly during hot weather.

Why it mattersParents of pupils at the school now face a direct risk, as a teacher said pupils had previously escaped through a hole in the fence to swim in the river.
