Eswatini stars close in on Zimbabwe league title

Hardrock FC, featuring five Eswatini internationals, needs three wins to secure the Castle Lager Premier Soccer League championship.

Source: Times of Eswatini · 2026-10-22 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Hardrock FC stands three wins away from clinching the Castle Lager Premier Soccer League title in Zimbabwe, with five Eswatini international players central to their campaign. The team currently holds a five-point lead over defending champions Scottland FC, who have a game in hand and are competing in the CAF Champions League. Hardrock's remaining fixtures include home games against MWOS and FC Platinum, and an away match against Simba Bhora.

Scottland FC, aiming to retain the crown, will face stiff competition in their final matches against CAPS United, Chicken Inn, Herentals, and Triangle United. Hardrock's coach, Kelvin Kaindu, has cautioned his squad against complacency despite their strong position and an eight-match unbeaten streak. He emphasized the nine points still available and the need for consistency, recalling past near-misses in title races.

Kaindu, who previously lost titles on goal difference, highlighted the significance of their current five-point cushion. He stressed the importance of maintaining focus and playing one game at a time to achieve the championship. The race remains open with only nine points left to play for, making each upcoming fixture critical for both Hardrock and Scottland.

Why it mattersEswatini international footballers are directly involved in a championship race that could bring them individual and team accolades.
