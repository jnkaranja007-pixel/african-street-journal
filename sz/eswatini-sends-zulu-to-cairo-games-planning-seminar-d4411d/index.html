Eswatini sends Zulu to Cairo Games planning seminar

The chef de mission is receiving updates on qualification, athlete services and preparations for the 2027 African Games in Egypt.

Source: Times of Eswatini · 2026-11-21 · 1 min read

Eswatini’s Chef de Mission Zakhele N. Zulu is representing the country at a planning seminar in Cairo for the 2027 African Games. Zulu is also treasurer and a board member of the Eswatini Olympic and Commonwealth Games Association.

The seminar covers governance, operational readiness, athlete services, qualification pathways and accreditation, according to the Times of Eswatini. Zulu said sessions and inspections of proposed venues gave Team Eswatini planning information, including for qualification and delegation forecasts. Boxing and armwrestling are among the sports in the programme that Eswatini is focused on.

The Games are scheduled for August 20 to September 3, 2027, and are expected to host approximately 10 000 athletes from up to 54 African countries across 40 sporting disciplines. 17 sports will offer qualification opportunities for the Los Angeles 2028 Olympic and Paralympic Games.

Why it mattersEswatini’s Games planners must prepare qualification pathways and forecast the delegation ahead of the August 20 to September 3, 2027 event.
