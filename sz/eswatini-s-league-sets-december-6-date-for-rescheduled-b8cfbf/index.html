Eswatini's league sets December 6 date for rescheduled Charity Cup final

Mbabane Highlanders AM and Nsingizini Hotspurs will meet at Somhlolo National Stadium, with the winner taking E400 000 from a E1.675 million tournament.

Source: Times of Eswatini · 2026-11-28 · 1 min read

The Premier League of Eswatini, the country's top football league, has rescheduled its Charity Cup final for Sunday, December 6, at Somhlolo National Stadium. Mbabane Highlanders AM will face Nsingizini Hotspurs at 4pm. The original match was postponed after rain left the pitch waterlogged.

More than 3 000 supporters had attended the abandoned fixture, according to Times of Eswatini. Both finalists have already guaranteed at least E200 000, and the winner will collect E400 000. The day will open with the unfinished National First Division match between Nkwene Sundowns and Lozitha Spurs at 1:30pm.

The league said it will distribute dignity packs containing sanitary pads, roll-on, deodorant and chocolate to schoolgirls in selected communities from November 30 to December 4. The community outreach programme begins on December 7, a day after the champions are crowned.

Why it mattersMbabane Highlanders AM and Nsingizini Hotspurs each have at least E200 000 guaranteed, and the team that wins on December 6 collects E400 000.
