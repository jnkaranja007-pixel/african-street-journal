UN committee urges Eswatini to adopt national jobs policy

The recommendation follows concerns that youth unemployment reached 58.2% and that existing programmes are not producing sustainable work.

Source: Times of Eswatini · 2026-11-19 · 1 min read

A United Nations committee has urged Eswatini to adopt a national employment policy, citing persistent unemployment and unequal access to jobs. The call followed the country’s first review under the International Covenant on Economic, Social and Cultural Rights, held in Geneva on November 3, 2026.

At the review, committee expert and country rapporteur Asraf Ally Caunhye said youth unemployment had reached 58.2% and asked how government was helping young people move from training into decent work. The committee highlighted limited opportunities in rural communities, particularly the Shiselweni Region, and called for better vocational training and stronger implementation of the Youth Employment Opportunities Project.

Government told the committee that infrastructure funding was expected to generate approximately 10 000 employment opportunities for young people; the figure is projected, not a count of jobs already created. It also cited youth skills programmes and a revolving fund offering business loans.

Why it mattersYoung people in rural communities, particularly Shiselweni, face limited job access, and the committee has called for a national policy and improved vocational training.
