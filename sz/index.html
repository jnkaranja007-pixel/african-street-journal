MTN Eswatini Wins MTN Group Y'ello Care Campaign Award

MTN Eswatini received the top honor for its community health initiatives, including expanding a medical drone network and digital health access.

MTN Eswatini has been named the Overall Winner of the 2026 MTN Group 21 Days of Y’ello Care Campaign, receiving the Group’s highest award for its community health work. The announcement came at the MTN Group Ambassadors’ Appreciation Dinner in Sandton, South Africa. This marks MTN Eswatini’s fifth time winning this award, highlighting its ongoing commitment to social impact.

During the campaign, MTN Eswatini employees focused on improving healthcare access and health technology. Key projects included expanding The Luke Commission’s medical drone network to serve an additional health facility and approximately six thousand people, reducing medicine delivery times to as little as thirty minutes. They also supported the NotsA Digital Health Platform for over twenty-five thousand people and conducted a community outreach program for close to two thousand residents.

Further initiatives involved a Next STEM Generation Digital Health Fair engaging seventy-five young people in health technology and innovation. The campaign also refurbished Hospice at Home, upgrading its water system, donating eight palliative care beds, and providing critical medication to improve patient care.

Why it mattersMTN Eswatini's award recognizes its significant investment in community health infrastructure and services.

IMF: Lower rates may not reduce loan costs for Eswatini banks

Commercial banks might widen lending margins to offset risks from diverging interest rates with South Africa, negating monetary easing benefits.

Eswatini's central bank may not achieve cheaper loans for businesses and households despite lowering interest rates, according to an International Monetary Fund (IMF) study. Commercial banks could increase their lending margins to protect profitability, especially when Eswatini's discount rate falls below South Africa's policy rate. This divergence creates risks for banks, prompting them to adjust rates on new loans to cover funding costs and market conditions.

The IMF report, "Monetary Policy Transmission Through Banking: Evidence from Eswatini," suggests that banks have discretion over margins for new loans, unlike existing variable-rate loans which adjust quickly to changes in the central bank's discount rate. This mechanism, where the Prime Lending Rate is set at 3.5 percentage points above the discount rate, has been in place since September 2003. However, banks can still influence overall borrowing costs by adjusting margins on new lending.

This situation is partly due to Eswatini's membership in the Common Monetary Area, which pegs the Lilangeni to the South African Rand and links domestic financial conditions to those in South Africa. Persistent interest rate differences can also encourage large companies to seek better returns or financing in South Africa, potentially weakening the intended stimulus of lower domestic rates and impacting economic activity.

Why it mattersInvestors may face higher borrowing costs and reduced investment opportunities if banks increase lending margins despite lower central bank rates.

Zinia Explores Love's Complexities on Debut EP

Eswatini-born vocalist Zinia releases a six-track project exploring heartbreak, longing, and the difficult process of letting go.

Eswatini-born vocalist Zinia is set to release her debut EP, "Devotion & Other Mistakes," on October 23, 2026. The six-track project delves into the less acknowledged aspects of love, such as lingering attachment and the uncomfortable journey of self-prioritization. Zinia aims to present an honest portrayal of heartbreak, finding beauty in the truth rather than romanticizing the pain.

The EP navigates the tension between devotion and misguided persistence, tracing a path from surrender through uncertainty to the challenging work of release. Sonically, the project leans into R&B and Neo-soul, incorporating influences from soul, house, Afro-house, and African rhythms. Zinia's vocals are central, moving between vulnerability and strength across the tracks, which feature fellow Eswatini artist Oriiginelle.

Produced by Fezi Simelane, with mixing and mastering by Lethumusa Simelane, "Devotion & Other Mistakes" marks a significant step for Zinia, whose musical journey began in Mbabane. After years of performing at Eswatini festivals like MTN Bushfire and Standard Bank Luju Food & Lifestyle Festival, this EP represents a cohesive artistic statement, showcasing her growth from early musical beginnings to her studies in Music Business and Performance.

Why it mattersThe EP's release offers a new cultural product from an Eswatini artist, potentially impacting the local music scene and providing content for diaspora audiences interested in contemporary African arts.

Manzini Council Seeks Resident Input for 2027/28 Budget

The Municipal Council is inviting submissions from residents and stakeholders to shape development priorities and budget allocations for the upcoming fiscal year.

Manzini residents have been invited by the Municipal Council to influence the city’s development priorities and budget for the 2027/2028 financial year. Mayor Xolani Masuku stated the municipality is committed to citizen involvement to ensure programs meet actual needs. The call for submissions was made during the 2026 Stakeholder Budget Consultation Meeting, where ratepayers were encouraged to offer practical suggestions for resource allocation.

Mayor Masuku emphasized the importance of these consultations for public input into municipal planning and priorities. He acknowledged that while all suggestions are taken seriously, limited financial resources and competing needs mean not every proposal can be accommodated. The municipality aims to implement some of the submitted ideas where feasible, according to Masuku.

During the meeting, Thulile Dlamini Msane from Ward 2 raised concerns about ratepayers who may no longer have the capacity to pay municipal rates. The council is gathering these diverse perspectives as it prepares its financial plan, balancing resident needs with fiscal realities.

Why it mattersManzini residents are directly affected by the municipal budget process as it determines how city resources are allocated for development and services.
