King Mswati III pushes Eswatini toward E4 trillion economy

The monarch urged self-sufficiency and regional supply capabilities, citing a new fuel reserve facility as a model for development.

King Mswati III has challenged Eswatini to aim for a E4 trillion economy, urging the nation to produce most of its own goods and supply neighboring markets. Speaking at the 58th Eswatini International Trade Fair, the King highlighted the Strategic Oil Reserve Facility at Phuzumoya as an example of strengthening national security and economic ambition.

The E5.2 billion fuel reserve, developed by the Eswatini National Petroleum Company, will hold 80 million liters, providing 60 days of national fuel cover. This facility is designed to mitigate disruptions from supply chain issues or market fluctuations, with plans to eventually extend coverage to 90 days. The King noted regional interest in Eswatini's proactive approach to fuel security.

His Majesty emphasized Africa's need to leverage its vast natural resources by developing refining and processing capabilities, rather than relying on imports for value addition. He cited South Korea's success in building a significant petroleum industry through imported crude oil as a model for creating wealth and development within the continent.

Why it mattersThe King's call for economic self-sufficiency and increased production directly impacts farmers by potentially creating new markets for agricultural outputs and reducing reliance on imported goods.

Eswatini Midfielder Scores Brace in Pre-Season Victory

Junior Magagula netted two goals for AS Maniema Union in a friendly match against FC Les Aigles in the Democratic Republic of Congo.

Eswatini international Junior Magagula has scored two goals for his new club, AS Maniema Union, in a pre-season friendly. The midfielder found the net twice in the Democratic Republic of Congo powerhouse's 3-2 victory over FC Les Aigles at the Centre FIFA in Ignié. This performance marks an early success for Magagula, who recently completed a three-year transfer to the club after more than six years playing in Eswatini's MTN Premier League.

The transfer to Maniema Union, who had previously pursued Magagula, was finalized after they submitted an offer exceeding E200 000. FIFA-accredited agent Alimasi Saleh confirmed the deal but did not disclose specific financial terms. Magagula reportedly earns a minimum of US$1 500, which is over E20 000, per month, excluding potential winning bonuses. He is expected to feature again when Maniema Union plays AS Vita Club on Wednesday, September 2.

Magagula's brace offers a promising glimpse of his offensive capabilities for AS Maniema Union as he aims to establish himself at the prominent DRC club. The match against AS Vita Club is scheduled for 9am in Kinshasa and 10am in Kindu. This early success could be a significant step in his first overseas playing career, ahead of the 2026/27 season.

Why it mattersEswatini's Junior Magagula is affected by his new club's pre-season performance, scoring two goals and earning a reported minimum of US$1 500 per month.

Local Wins Grand Prize Bike at Swazi Rally

Tyra Rose, a young woman from Eswatini, was awarded a Suzuki 8S motorcycle valued at over E180,000 at the 33rd annual Swazi Rally.

The 33rd Swazi Rally concluded with a significant win for a local resident, Tyra Rose, who took home the grand prize motorcycle. The Suzuki 8S, valued at over E180,000, was awarded to Rose, who expressed shock and speechlessness at her win. She noted that while not a professional biker, the prize might signal a new direction for her.

The Swazi Rally is a key event that promotes Eswatini's culture and attracts tourists. This year's rally featured entertainment from DJ Stokie and various activities like an egg game for participants. The event aims to foster unity, perseverance, and excellence, inspiring young people and providing a platform for local entrepreneurs and artists.

Beyond the racing and entertainment, the rally contributes to the kingdom's economy and cultural pride. The grand prize motorcycle symbolizes achievement and adventure, reflecting the event's commitment to rewarding participants and encouraging them to pursue their passions. Khwezi Tsabedze also won a bike during the Kiddies raffle draw.

Why it mattersLocal residents like Tyra Rose benefit directly from winning prizes at the Swazi Rally, which also promotes Eswatini's culture and economy.

Eswatini Stock Exchange to Host Business Seminar on Capital Markets

The Eswatini Stock Exchange will host a seminar on Thursday at the ongoing Trade Fair to explore how capital markets can unlock growth and investment for businesses.

The Eswatini Stock Exchange (ESE) is set to host a business seminar titled ‘Unlocking Business Growth Through Capital Markets’ on Thursday. The event, scheduled from 1pm to 3:30pm at the UNDP Sustainable Centre during the Eswatini International Trade Fair, aims to educate businesses and entrepreneurs on leveraging capital markets for expansion and wealth creation.

The seminar will unpack strategies for mobilizing capital, discussing how companies can access funding beyond traditional bank loans to finance growth, improve operations, and explore new opportunities. It will also highlight investment avenues for the public and focus on investment-readiness for small and medium enterprises (SMEs), emphasizing the importance of financial management, governance, and credible growth strategies.

Discussions will also cover the relationship between capital markets and bank finance, clarifying how these funding sources can complement each other. The ESE aims to provide businesses with a deeper understanding of how to become attractive to potential investors and financiers, thereby fostering economic activity and employment.

Why it mattersBusinesses and entrepreneurs in Eswatini can learn how to access capital for expansion and growth through capital markets.

Kwakhe Thwala scores as Scottland FC dominates MWOS Under-19

Eswatini international Kwakhe Thwala scored a goal in his team's 6-0 victory, preparing to face his former club Nsingizini Hotspurs.

Eswatini international Kwakhe Thwala announced his return to the starting lineup with a goal in Scottland FC's 6-0 win over MWOS Under-19 in the Munhumutapa Challenge Cup. Thwala, who joined the Zimbabwean champions from Nsingizini Hotspurs in January 2026, scored the third goal in the 50th minute. This performance serves as a warning to his former Eswatini club ahead of their Confederation of African Football (CAF) Champions League encounter.

Scottland FC will face Nsingizini Hotspurs in the CAF Champions League preliminary round on Sunday, September 6, at the National Stadium in Zimbabwe. Thwala's current team is also competing in Zimbabwe's richest domestic cup, the US$5 million per season Munhumutapa Challenge Cup. Meanwhile, Nsingizini Hotspurs have secured a quarter-final spot in the Eswatini 8Bet Trade Fair Cup, gaining match fitness.

Despite Nsingizini's domestic success, the CAF Champions League presents a significant challenge, particularly with Thwala in sharp form for Scottland FC. The former Nsingizini star's performance in the Munhumutapa Challenge Cup indicates he will be a key threat for his new team when they meet his former employers in Harare.

Why it mattersFootball fans and investors in Zimbabwean and Eswatini football leagues are affected by the performance of Kwakhe Thwala and the upcoming continental match.
