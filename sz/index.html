Green Mamba fans plan 3 500km Angola road trip

Green Mamba supporters are organizing an overland journey covering over 3 500km to attend their team's CAF Confederation Cup match in Angola on September 12, 2026.

Green Mamba's loyalists are preparing for a cross-continental road trip to support their team in the second leg of the CAF Confederation Cup preliminary tie against Clube Desportivo Primeiro de Agosto. The journey to Luanda, Angola, from Mbabane will cover over 3 500km and is expected to take more than two days of travel.

The Correctional Services outfit has invited interested supporters to register for the road trip at a cost of US$200, which is E3 200 in Eswatini currency. The Eswatini CAF envoys will first host the Angolan powerhouse at Somhlolo National Stadium on September four, 2026, before the reverse fixture in Angola.

The most direct overland route for fans will cross through three countries: exiting Eswatini via the Oshoek/Ngwenya border, traveling through Gauteng towards Botswana, then crossing into Namibia via the Mamuno/Trans-Kalahari border, and finally entering southern Angola through border posts like Oshikango/Santa Clara or Ruacana before reaching Luanda.

Why it mattersGreen Mamba fans are directly affected by the cost and logistical challenges of traveling over 3 500km across multiple countries to support their team.

Eswatini darts duo advances to Region V gold final

Sandziso Nhleko and Mbali Sihlongonyane secured a spot in the final after a decisive 3-0 victory over Zambia in the Under-25s darts competition.

Eswatini's Under-25s darts team has reached the gold medal final at the African Union Sports Council (AUSC) Region V games in Gaborone. The duo of Sandziso Nhleko and Mbali Sihlongonyane achieved this milestone with a dominant 3-0 win against Zambia. Their teammates, Neliswa Mhlanga and Siphamandla Dladla, were eliminated in the semi-finals after a 3-0 loss to Zambia.

The competition, which began with an official opening ceremony on Tuesday, is being held at the Aquamarine Tide Hotel in Gaborone. Five nations, including Eswatini, initially kicked off the games, competing against players from Zimbabwe, Botswana, South Africa, and Namibia. Zambia joined the event today, August 27, after missing the initial team events.

Team Eswatini experienced mixed results on day one, Wednesday, August 26. The Senior Men's team bounced back from a 12-24 defeat to Botswana with a 23-13 win over Namibia. However, they later suffered a 30-6 defeat to South Africa, though this result showed an improvement from a 31-1 loss to South Africa in 2024.

Why it mattersEswatini's national darts team members are directly affected by their progress to the gold medal final, which could bring them regional recognition.

Central Bank of Eswatini plans new E10 commemorative coin for 2028

The Central Bank of Eswatini announced a limited-edition E10 circulating commemorative coin to mark the country's 60th independence anniversary in September 2028.

Eswatini will introduce a new E10 circulating commemorative coin in 2028, as announced by Central Bank of Eswatini (CBE) Governor Dr. Phil Mnisi. The coin will celebrate the country's 60th Anniversary of Independence on September 6, 2028. The CBE launched a design concept competition for the coin yesterday at its Ezulwini complex, inviting artists, students, designers, cultural enthusiasts, and the public to submit concepts by October 26, 2026.

The initiative aims to provide a lasting symbol of independence and national pride, while also allowing the CBE to assess public acceptance of higher denomination coins. The E10 denomination was chosen because its banknote is widely used and experiences significant wear and tear, typically lasting one to five years. Coins, in contrast, remain in circulation for 20 to 50 years, according to Dr. Mnisi.

This shift to a more durable E10 coin is intended to improve the efficiency of the currency system by reducing the frequency of replacement due to physical deterioration. The minting of the commemorative coin is an approved operational project for the bank's 2026/27 financial year, supporting the CBE's mandate to issue and redeem currency and maintain its quality and reliability for the public.

Why it mattersEmaSwati will be directly affected by the introduction of a new E10 coin, which will change how they handle and use this common denomination in daily transactions.

Eswatini designers showcase commercial fashion potential at IP Runway event

The Eswatini Intellectual Property Office hosted a runway event to highlight the commercial viability of local fashion, furniture, and arts for international markets.

Eswatini's creative economy demonstrated its readiness for global markets at the recent IP Runway event, hosted by the Eswatini Intellectual Property Office (EIPO) under the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Trade. The event, titled 'IP Runway: The Business of Fashion, Furniture and Arts,' showcased local talent in the presence of Her Royal Highness Princess Sakhizwe.

Designers presented collections that blended traditional Eswatini motifs with modern styles, emphasizing the commercial potential of legally protected designs. Categories included Roots and Heritage, Contemporary and Streetwear, Formal and Evening Wear, NextGen, Upcycle and Innovation, and Fashion Complementaries, featuring brands like Kotini, Guzzani, Designs by Yanda, and Goodness Fashion.

The showcase served as a workshop for designers and underscored the need for structural investment, cross-border licensing, and international industrial scale within Eswatini's fashion ecosystem. Princess Sakhizwe noted that domestic support for Eswatini brands is crucial before expanding to global audiences.

Why it mattersEswatini designers and artists are directly affected by the event's focus on commercial viability and intellectual property protection for their work.

Eswatini government rejects China's security claims, defends sovereignty

The Kingdom of Eswatini has stated it will not be coerced or destabilized by China's advisory for its citizens to leave due to security risks, asserting its sovereign right.

The Government of Eswatini has rejected claims by the People's Republic of China that the kingdom poses an exceptional security risk, following a Chinese travel advisory for its citizens. Acting Spokesperson Thabile Mdluli acknowledged China's right to issue consular guidance but regretted any suggestion that Eswatini is generally unsafe.

Mdluli clarified that a recent law enforcement operation, primarily involving Chinese nationals, targeted suspected organized cyber-related criminal activity. She stated this operation demonstrates the vigilance of Eswatini's law enforcement, not a failure of public security, and that the country remains peaceful and stable.

Eswatini maintains that its foreign policy and diplomatic relations are matters of sovereign national determination, seeking friendship with all nations. China's Foreign Ministry Spokesman Lin Jian cited rampant telecommunications fraud and online gambling in Eswatini as reasons for their travel advisory, stating risks to public order and safety are extremely high.

Why it mattersThis event affects Chinese nationals in Eswatini who are advised to leave due to perceived security risks, and the Eswatini government, which is defending its national image and sovereignty.
