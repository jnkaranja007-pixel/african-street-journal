Eswatini fuel prices surge 51.2% since April

The Ministry of Economic Planning and Development warns of increased production costs and weakened export competitiveness.

Fuel prices in Eswatini have risen by 51.2 per cent since April 2026, placing significant cost pressures on the economy. This increase threatens business competitiveness, reduces household purchasing power, and hinders economic recovery efforts.

The Ministry of Economic Planning and Development stated in its FY2026/27 Second Quarter Performance Report that persistent fuel price hikes, driven by Middle East tensions and global supply chain disruptions, have raised production costs. This situation significantly weakens Eswatini's export competitiveness and fuels inflation, which is currently limiting consumer spending.

Despite these challenges, Eswatini's economy grew by 6.5 per cent in the second quarter of 2026 compared to the same period in 2025. The primary sector expanded by 9.3 per cent, driven by agriculture and mining, while the industrial sector grew by 11.6 per cent, with manufacturing output up 14 per cent.

Why it mattersBusinesses face higher production costs and reduced export competitiveness due to rising fuel prices.

Highlanders and Swallows clash in PLE Charity Cup

The two Mbabane rivals meet for the PLE Charity Cup just 60 days after Highlanders won their last encounter 4-0.

Mbabane Highlanders and Mbabane Swallows face off this afternoon in the Premier League of Eswatini (PLE) Charity Cup. This derby comes exactly 60 days after Highlanders defeated Swallows 4-0 in the 8Bet Trade Fair Cup quarter-finals.

Both teams have new coaches, Thembela Dlamini for Highlanders and Mduduzi ‘Sikhalo’ Nxumalo for Swallows, who took over last month. Highlanders and Swallows are tied with four Charity Cup titles each, with only Manzini Wanderers having more with five.

This match is a play-off ahead of the next league derby. Highlanders, currently one point ahead of Swallows in the league, will rely on midfielder Smiso Gumede and Captain Sandziso Dlamini, while Swallows look to Thobani Kunene, who has scored in his last three games.

Why it mattersFootball fans in Eswatini will watch their two biggest clubs, Mbabane Highlanders and Mbabane Swallows, compete for the PLE Charity Cup.

Eswatini sprinter Mahlalela wins two top awards

Bongiwe Mahlalela received Sportswoman of the Year and International Participation awards, earning E10,000 and a vacation.

Eswatini sprinter Bongiwe Mahlalela secured two major honours at the Prestige Sports Awards 2026 on October 30. She was named Sportswoman of the Year and received the International Participation Award.

Mahlalela's awards included E10,000 and a seven-day vacation in South Africa. She stated her sporting success is built on hard work, patience, discipline, sacrifice, and perseverance.

This follows Mahlalela being crowned Sportswoman of the Year at Tshwane University of Technology (TUT) three weeks prior. TUT has supported her academic and sporting journey, covering tuition fees since her first year.

Eswatini government seeks E3.79 billion in loans

The Ministry of Finance tabled three loan bills in the House of Assembly to finance development projects.

The Eswatini government is seeking E3.79 billion through loans to fund infrastructure and social development projects. Minister for Finance Neal Rijkenberg presented three loan Bills to the House of Assembly on Thursday.

One Bill seeks to authorise a loan of up to E1.6 billion from Standard Bank of South Africa Limited and other lenders. This borrowing is for projects agreed upon between the government and the arranger. The total public debt stood at E43.04 billion as of September 29, 2026.

The proposed loan will incur interest at a margin of 4.95 per cent per annum, plus arranging fees and facility management charges. An arranging fee could amount to E20.8 million based on the maximum facility. An annual management fee of E380,000 is also stipulated.

Why it mattersEswatini's government is borrowing money, which will affect public finances and development projects.
