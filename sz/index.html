Eswatini fuel prices climb 51.2%

The Ministry of Economic Planning and Development warns of rising production costs and weakened export competitiveness.

Fuel prices in Eswatini have risen by 51.2 per cent since April 2026, increasing production costs and weakening export competitiveness. The Ministry of Economic Planning and Development reported these pressures in its FY2026/27 Second Quarter Performance Report, attributing the increases to the Middle East crisis disrupting global supply chains and driving up crude oil prices. This situation poses a challenge for Eswatini, which relies on imported energy.

Despite rising fuel costs, Eswatini's economy grew by 6.5 per cent in the second quarter of 2026 compared to the same period in 2025. All three major economic sectors showed positive performance. The primary sector grew by 9.3 per cent, driven by agriculture and mining. The industrial sector expanded by 11.6 per cent, with manufacturing up 14 per cent and electricity supply up 21.8 per cent, though construction contracted by 7.9 per cent.

The services sector expanded by 4.1 per cent, with accommodation and food services growing by 19.6 per cent and information, communication, and technology seeing a 55.6 per cent expansion. Professional services also increased by 8.1 per cent. The report highlights both the economy's resilience and emerging vulnerabilities.

Why it mattersEswatini's economy faces mounting cost pressures from increased fuel prices, impacting business competitiveness and household purchasing power.

Eswatini launches youth jobs project

The Eswatini Youth Employment Opportunities Project will offer training and support for entrepreneurship in agriculture and construction.

Young emaSwati aged 18 to 35 can now apply for the Eswatini Youth Employment Opportunities Project (EYEOP) to gain employment and business skills. The government initiative, supported by the World Bank, focuses on agriculture and construction to build resilient livelihoods. Applications opened on October 13, 2026, and close on November 13, 2026, for residents of Hhohho, Manzini, Shiselweni, and Lubombo regions.

EYEOP will provide beneficiaries with accredited skills training, apprenticeships, and on-the-job experience. The project also offers enterprise development and start-up support, along with mentorship and connections to finance, markets, and jobs. Young women and youth with disabilities are especially encouraged to apply.

The project supports on-farm and off-farm agriculture, including climate-resilient crops and agri-businesses. In construction, opportunities cover trades like bricklaying, carpentry, plumbing, and electrical installation. Applicants need to submit ID documents and any supporting papers online or at an Inkhundla Centre.

Why it mattersYoung emaSwati aged 18 to 35 seeking employment or business opportunities will benefit from skills training and support through the EYEOP project.

Sport · 2026-11-02

Eswatini taekwondo team wins nine medals in Mozambique

The nine-member Next Generation Academy team secured four gold, four silver, and three bronze medals at the AUSC Region V Test Event.

Eswatini’s taekwondo team claimed nine medals, including four gold, four silver, and three bronze, at the AUSC Region V Test Event in Mozambique yesterday. The nine-member team from Next Generation Academy finished second overall, preparing for next month’s Region V Games in Maputo and Matola.

Bonkhe Mthethwa won gold and was named Best Junior Male Player, while Saneliso Masuku, Velile Gwebu, and Lindokuhle Fakudze also secured gold medals. The team's performance provides a significant boost for Eswatini’s preparations for the regional showpiece.

The test event also assessed Mozambique’s readiness to host the taekwondo competition during the Maputo 2026 African Union Sports Council (AUSC) Region V Games. Facilities and technical arrangements were examined, with COSATA Technical Delegate Sizwe Mabuza expected to comment on preparations.

Why it mattersEswatini's taekwondo athletes won nine medals at a regional test event in Mozambique, boosting their preparation for upcoming games.

Sport · 2026-11-02

Mbabane Highlanders AM beat Mbabane Swallows 2-0

Zwelihle Phewa scored a penalty in the 43rd minute and Bhekani Mthembu added a stoppage-time goal.

Mbabane Highlanders AM defeated rivals Mbabane Swallows 2-0 at Mavuso Sports Centre yesterday to reach the Premier League of Eswatini (PLE) Charity Cup semi-finals. Zwelihle Phewa converted a penalty in the 43rd minute, and Bhekani Mthembu secured the win with a goal in the 90+3 minute. Over 3 000 fans watched the match.

Highlanders President Shauwn ‘Mamkhize’ Mkhize celebrated with the team after the victory. This win marked Head Coach Thembela Dlamini's first major derby since taking over. Mbabane Swallows, coached by interim Coach Mduduzi ‘Sikhalo’ Nxumalo, will miss the semi-finals for the first time.

The two teams last met on September 2, when Highlanders won 4-0 in the 8Bet Trade Fair Cup quarter-finals. Yesterday's match saw Mbabane Swallows wearing their new red kit and creating early chances through Thobani Kunene.

Why it mattersFootball fans in Eswatini will miss Mbabane Swallows in the PLE Charity Cup semi-finals after their loss to rivals Mbabane Highlanders AM.

Health · 2026-11-02

Bullet lodged in girl's foot after shooting incident

An eight-year-old girl in Ekupheleni had a pistol bullet removed from her foot after weeks of medical attention failed to heal the injury.

An eight-year-old girl in Ekupheleni had a pistol bullet lodged in her foot for weeks before a hospital scan revealed the cause of her persistent pain. The child sustained the injury last month while playing with siblings outside a gogo centre, initially believing she had stepped on sharp wire.

Medical attention at the hospital involved cleaning the wound and administering antibiotics, but the injury worsened and began to swell, causing the girl difficulty walking. A scan this month identified the bullet, prompting the girl to recall hearing a gunshot and seeing a resident shooting at cats that day.

Police visited the resident, seized a pistol for ballistic testing, and the individual admitted to firing the weapon but denied shooting at the child. The circumstances of the injury remain under police investigation, pending the ballistic examination results.

Why it mattersAn eight-year-old girl in Eswatini suffered a gunshot wound to the foot that required surgical removal of a bullet.
