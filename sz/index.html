Eswatini calls for regional action on supply chain woes

The kingdom's foreign minister urged stronger cooperation to counter rising costs and trade disruptions affecting COMESA nations.

Eswatini's Minister of Foreign Affairs and International Cooperation, Pholile Shakantu, has called for enhanced regional collaboration to address the impact of global supply chain disruptions. Speaking at the 21st COMESA Meeting of Ministers of Foreign Affairs in Harare, Zimbabwe, Shakantu highlighted how these issues are driving up the cost of living through increased fuel and shipping expenses, and delays in essential goods movement.

Shakantu emphasised that the consequences of these disruptions extend beyond trade, posing significant risks to economic stability, food security, and the general welfare of citizens across the COMESA region. The meeting also reviewed the regional peace and security situation, acknowledging its critical role in fostering economic development and shared prosperity.

The ministers recognised that collective action and sustained collaboration are necessary to tackle the region's challenges. Strengthening regional partnerships, they concluded, will better equip COMESA member states to safeguard their economies, support vulnerable populations, and promote a more stable and prosperous future for all.

Why it mattersEswatini citizens are affected by rising costs of living and potential food insecurity due to global supply chain disruptions.

Eswatini stars close in on Zimbabwe league title

Hardrock FC, featuring five Eswatini internationals, needs three wins to secure the Castle Lager Premier Soccer League championship.

Hardrock FC stands three wins away from clinching the Castle Lager Premier Soccer League title in Zimbabwe, with five Eswatini international players central to their campaign. The team currently holds a five-point lead over defending champions Scottland FC, who have a game in hand and are competing in the CAF Champions League. Hardrock's remaining fixtures include home games against MWOS and FC Platinum, and an away match against Simba Bhora.

Scottland FC, aiming to retain the crown, will face stiff competition in their final matches against CAPS United, Chicken Inn, Herentals, and Triangle United. Hardrock's coach, Kelvin Kaindu, has cautioned his squad against complacency despite their strong position and an eight-match unbeaten streak. He emphasized the nine points still available and the need for consistency, recalling past near-misses in title races.

Kaindu, who previously lost titles on goal difference, highlighted the significance of their current five-point cushion. He stressed the importance of maintaining focus and playing one game at a time to achieve the championship. The race remains open with only nine points left to play for, making each upcoming fixture critical for both Hardrock and Scottland.

Why it mattersEswatini international footballers are directly involved in a championship race that could bring them individual and team accolades.

Business · 2026-10-22

OK Foods boss offers space to young entrepreneurs

Lincoln Motsa has opened his new commercial premises to three young entrepreneurs, creating 29 jobs in total.

OK Foods Eswatini boss Lincoln Motsa has opened his new commercial premises at Ngwane Park to three young entrepreneurs. The initiative provides a platform for a car wash and a shisanyama to operate alongside his L’s Chillers Liquor and Restaurant. This setup has created 29 jobs in total, supporting the businesses of Thabang Maphalala, who runs MAPS Village Car Wash, and Sibusiso Shabangu and Sandile Mkhulisi, operators of SISAJ Shisanyama.

Motsa, who is also the Linac Group of Companies Chairperson, stated that the project aims to uplift young people and help them establish successful businesses. The arrangement allows the entrepreneurs to operate within an established commercial setting, benefiting from shared customer traffic and mutual business activity. The establishment employs nine people at the car wash, six at the shisanyama, and 10 at the restaurant, with an additional four staff for cleaning and security.

Maphalala expressed gratitude for the opportunity, highlighting it as a significant step for MAPS Car Wash's growth and brand development. He noted that many young people face challenges due to a lack of opportunity, despite their ambition. This new location is the car wash's second in the Manzini Region, supporting its expansion goals and aspirations to create more employment and establish outlets across Eswatini.

Why it mattersYoung entrepreneurs in Eswatini are provided with business space and job creation opportunities.

Pupil accuses teacher of sexual assault at high school

Four teachers at Sikhunyane High School are suspended amid allegations of inappropriate relationships with students, including a sexual assault claim.

A 17-year-old pupil at Sikhunyane High School has accused a female teacher of sexual assault, contributing to the temporary suspension of four teachers. The suspensions, involving three male teachers and one female teacher, are a safety measure while investigations into alleged romantic relationships with pupils proceed. The school committee was initially informed by the late head teacher about two pupils reporting such relationships.

The allegations surfaced throughout the year, with one case involving a mother discovering romantic conversations between her child and a teacher on a mobile phone. A Ministry of Education and Training official met with the school committee and teachers, warning them to cease any inappropriate conduct. The committee expressed shock and anger, with parents demanding the teachers' suspension, but the ministry advised patience for ongoing investigations.

The school community, including pupils, has been traumatized by the incidents, with a senior teacher stating the need for psychological support. Despite the disruption, the temporary removal of teachers has not caused disturbances, and remaining staff are working to reassure students. The incidents occurred during a critical period as pupils are writing external examinations.

Why it mattersPupils at Sikhunyane High School are affected by the alleged inappropriate relationships and sexual assault, leading to teacher suspensions and community trauma.

Eswatini theatre vice-chair speaks at Johannesburg music event

Sizo Hlophe will address a cultural policy summit and a main conference panel at the ACCES event, focusing on music industry growth.

Sizo Hlophe, Vice-Chairperson of the Eswatini Theatre Club, is set to speak at the ACCES event in Johannesburg, a major pan-African music industry gathering. Hlophe, who is also involved in rejuvenating Eswatini’s oldest theatre, will participate in two sessions. His first appearance will be at a cultural policy summit focused on promoting fair culture and exchange in the performing arts, bringing together global leaders to connect policy with practical work.

Hlophe's involvement stems from his experience as an ecosystem builder and co-founder of Antidote Music and Antidote Culture Foundation, organizations that have worked to bring local music online and develop cultural programs. He has supported initiatives like the Music Cities Convention since 2023, aiming to strengthen music ecosystems globally. His work aligns with UNESCO conventions on the status of the artist and the promotion of cultural diversity.

In addition to the policy summit, Hlophe will join an international panel at the main ACCES conference. This session, moderated by Keith Kuhudzai from Kay Media, will explore how communities and independent music businesses can build sustainable international careers, focusing on developing artists and cultural organizations from smaller markets. Hlophe believes a collaborative ecosystem is crucial for industry advancement and creative growth.

Why it mattersEswatini's creative sector and its professionals are affected by gaining international exposure and insights into strengthening music ecosystems and cultural policy.
