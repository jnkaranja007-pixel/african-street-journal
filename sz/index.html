Ex-deputy sheriff sentenced to 124 years for murder

Judge Titus Mlangeni condemned Vusi Charles Mncina's ease in shooting five unarmed women, four fatally.

Former Deputy Sheriff Vusi Charles Mncina received a 124-year prison sentence yesterday for murdering four women and attempting to kill a fifth. The killings occurred at Goje Township, Ezulwini, on October 28, 2023. Judge Titus Mlangeni noted Mncina targeted defenceless women after avoiding two men at the same gathering.

Judge Mlangeni stated that Mncina easily pulled the trigger on the unarmed women who pleaded for mercy. He emphasized the need for general deterrence against violent crime, calling victory against it a "forlorn hope" without it. The judge acknowledged the case had rattled the country due to the number of victims and the circumstances of their deaths.

The judge observed that Mncina, a first offender, committed five serious offences in a single incident, permanently scarring the victims' families and society. He described the killings, which included three members of the same family, as an example of extreme callousness. The case highlights the diminishing value of life amid persistent violent crime in Eswatini.

Why it mattersFour women were murdered and a fifth seriously injured, leaving their families devastated and society shaken by the violent crime.

Eswatini drops to 30th in Africa investment rankings

The kingdom's investment attractiveness score fell to -0.54, placing it below the African average.

Eswatini has fallen to 30th place in the 2026/27 Rand Merchant Bank Where to Invest in Africa report, assessed against 29 other African economies. The kingdom recorded an overall score of -0.54, below the hypothetical African average of zero. This represents a one-place decline from the previous year's 29th position.

The report compares 31 African countries using 20 indicators including economic performance and human development. Eswatini's score places it just above Zimbabwe, which ranked 31st with -0.65. Regional peers Namibia, Botswana, and Zambia all ranked higher, while Lesotho improved to 26th.

The RMB report cautions that no single ranking determines suitability for all investors. The importance of factors like market size and economic growth depends on the specific investment. The report aims to reveal structural characteristics affecting Eswatini's attractiveness.

Why it mattersEswatini's investment attractiveness has declined, affecting its position relative to other African economies.

Eswatini U-15s advance to classification matches

The junior squad will play classification matches to determine their final global standing in the tournament.

Eswatini’s Under-15 national football team secured a place in the tournament’s final placement matches after a 3-0 victory over the Maldives and a subsequent 2-1 loss to Palestine. Samkelo Tsabedze, Mnotfo Simelane, and Junior Shabangu scored in the win against the Maldives. The team competed in the 8-a-side developmental format in Azerbaijan.

Despite the loss to Palestine, Eswatini will continue playing in classification matches as no teams are eliminated early from the FIFA tournament model. The squad will play against other Round of 16 eliminated teams to determine their final global ranking. Their next match is a semi-final against The Gambia on Thursday, October 29, 2026.

This classification semi-final will help sort teams within the 145th to 160th ranking zone. The global festival concludes on November 1, 2026, at the Hovsan Competition Complex and Baku Crystal Hall. Eswatini's next match kicks off at 10:45 am Eswatini time.

Why it mattersEswatini's Under-15 football team is competing in an international tournament in Azerbaijan and will play classification matches to determine their final global standing.

South African duo win Ingwenyama Cup awards

Mandla Qhogi named Coach of the Tournament, Senzo Ndlovu Player of the Tournament.

A South African coaching duo has excelled in Eswatini's Ingwenyama Cup. Mandla Qhogi, formerly of Orlando Pirates DDC, was named Outstanding Coach of the 2026 edition, while his striker Senzo Ndlovu, a former AmaZulu marksman, received the Player of the Tournament award. Qhogi led the Nsingizini Hotspurs team to their first-ever title in the Sincephetelo Motor Vehicle Accidents Fund-sponsored competition.

For his coaching achievement, Qhogi received a E10,000 replica cheque and credited club President Derrick Shiba, the technical team, players, and fans. He expressed that receiving an award from His Majesty King Mswati III was a special feeling for him as a South African. Ndlovu pocketed E15,000 for his influential role in the title-winning run, narrowly missing the top scorer award.

Nsingizini Hotspurs are now a target for other teams this season following their treble-winning campaign last term.

Why it mattersSouth African coach Mandla Qhogi and player Senzo Ndlovu received awards at the Ingwenyama Cup tournament in Eswatini.

Sphiwo Ndoni single tops 50 000 views in a week

Eswatini gospel artist Sphiwo Ndoni's new song 'Ngeke Ngisuke Kuye' achieved over 50 000 views on digital platforms within seven days.

Eswatini gospel artist Sphiwo Ndoni has reached a significant milestone as his new single, ‘Ngeke Ngisuke Kuye’, garnered more than 50 000 views in its first week. The song was released after a social media campaign where fans voted for the cover art. The track, recorded live at the TLC Events Centre, carries a message of commitment to God, inspired by the Book of Peter.

The live recording aims to foster spiritual connection and includes a prayer session. This success comes ahead of Ndoni’s annual event, The Mount Zion Experience, scheduled for tomorrow. The single is expected to be a highlight of his performance at the event, which will feature South African artists Oncemore Six, Reginald Jabu, and Takie Ndou.

Ndoni is a prominent figure in contemporary Gospel music in Eswatini and the Southern African region. His previous popular songs include ‘Insindiso Iyangisebenzela’, ‘Waze Wetsembeka’, and ‘Sinomvuzo’. He continues his work of creating music intended to heal and encourage listeners.

Why it mattersEswatini gospel artist Sphiwo Ndoni's new single 'Ngeke Ngisuke Kuye' has surpassed 50 000 views in one week, with his annual event The Mount Zion Experience scheduled for tomorrow.
