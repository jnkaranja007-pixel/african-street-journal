Court stops Eswatini National Provident Fund board inauguration

Business and labour federations challenged the appointment process, leading to an interim court order.

The High Court has issued an interim order stopping the inauguration and public announcement of the newly formed Eswatini National Provident Fund (ENPF) Board. Labour and Social Security Minister Phila Buthelezi had announced the Board at SibaneSami Hotel just a day before the court order. The halt follows an urgent application by business and labour groups contesting the selection process.

The application, filed by the Federation of Eswatini Business Community (FESBEC), Business Eswatini, and the Trade Union Congress of Swaziland (TUCOSWA), challenges the exclusion of two nominees and the appointment of Ministry of Labour Principal Secretary Makhosini Mndawe. Applicants argue that decisions to exclude FESBEC nominee Themba Dlamini and Business Eswatini nominee E. Nathi Dlamini, and to appoint Mndawe, were irregular and violated representation arrangements.

Applicants stated that an ordinary court process could take approximately three months, and allowing disputed appointments to stand could prejudice their interests. They received electronic notification on Tuesday evening, October 27, 2026, for an inauguration the following morning, leaving insufficient time for a standard review before the event.

Police allege PUDEMO president coordinated 2021 unrest by phone

Mlungisi Makhanya and Zweli Martin Dlamini allegedly directed ground activities and received stolen firearms during the 2021-2023 unrest, according to court papers.

Eswatini police allege that Mlungisi Makhanya, president of the proscribed People’s United Democratic Movement (PUDEMO), and Swaziland News Editor Zweli Martin Dlamini coordinated the 2021/22 unrest via phone calls. Assistant Superintendent Bhekani Shiba stated in court papers that stolen firearms were surrendered to Makhanya. These allegations are part of an application for the extradition of Ntokozo Kenneth Vilakati, also known as ‘Putin’, who is reportedly arrested in South Africa.

Shiba, the investigating officer, claims Makhanya and Dlamini gave instructions to individuals on the ground, linking them to the activities of a group called the ‘underground solidarity forces’. The investigator alleges Vilakati acted on orders from this group’s commander, Thabo Kunene, and others, including Makhanya. The police account connects the group to alleged offences across Eswatini’s four regions between 2021 and 2023.

The court papers detail alleged operations involving Vilakati, including incidents at Dumako, the homestead of the late Chief Justice Bheki Maphalala, and properties near Pigg’s Peak. These include alleged cattle killings, house burnings, and the burning of a truck and forest belonging to Rockland Company. The Central Transport Administration block and Pigg’s Peak Magistrates Court were allegedly burnt the following morning.

Why it mattersThe allegations in court papers directly implicate the president of a proscribed political movement and a news editor in coordinating unrest and receiving stolen firearms.

Eswatini attracts E1.85 billion in new investments

The Eswatini Investment Promotion Authority facilitated projects expected to create 147 jobs.

Eswatini attracted E1.85 billion in new investment projects and E170 million in expansion projects during the second quarter of the 2026/27 financial year. These projects are expected to support 147 jobs once at full capacity. The Eswatini Investment Promotion Authority (EIPA) facilitated these investments, according to the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Trade’s Second Quarter Performance Report.

The report, covering July to September 2026, was presented under Minister Manqoba Khumalo. EIPA also conducted five outward investment promotion missions to Zimbabwe, South Africa, and Zambia. Cumulative potential export orders increased to E3 million, and the authority supported six additional exporters.

EIPA launched the buyeswatini.shop platform, which onboarded 29 sellers and recorded 40 transactions. The Mavuso Trade and Exhibition Centre website and digital booking platform were also completed. The Registrar of Companies registered 790 companies, including 637 Emaswati-owned businesses.

Why it mattersEswatini attracted significant new investments and expansion projects, creating potential jobs and boosting export orders.

South African duo win Eswatini Ingwenyama Cup awards

Mandla Qhogi named Coach of the Tournament, Senzo Ndlovu Player of the Tournament.

A South African coaching and playing duo at Nsingizini Hotspurs have won top awards at the Ingwenyama Cup in Eswatini. Mandla Qhogi was named Outstanding Coach of the 2026 edition, while his striker Senzo Ndlovu received the Player of the Tournament award. Qhogi led the Shiselweni team to their first-ever title in the Sincephetelo Motor Vehicle Accidents Fund-sponsored competition.

Qhogi received a E10,000 replica cheque for his coaching achievement and thanked club president Derrick Shiba, the technical team, players, and fans. He noted the special feeling of receiving an award directly from His Majesty King Mswati III. Ndlovu pocketed E15,000 for his influential role in the title-winning run, coming close to the top scorer award.

Nsingizini Hotspurs are now a target for other teams this season following their treble-winning campaign last term.

Why it mattersSouth African coach Mandla Qhogi received an award directly from the King of Eswatini, highlighting cross-border recognition.

Mbabane derby to decide Charity Cup

Mbabane Highlanders and Mbabane Swallows will clash in the PLE Charity Cup final on November 8.

Mbabane Highlanders will face Mbabane Swallows in the Premier League of Eswatini (PLE) Charity Cup final on November 8 at Mavuso Sports Centre. The derby match will kick off at 4:15pm, following an earlier game between Green Mamba and Ubombo Sugar. The winners of the elite league tournament will receive E1 million.

Under businesswoman Shauwn ‘MaMkhize’ Mkhize, Highlanders aim to extend their winning streak against Swallows, having beaten them 4-0 and 1-0 in previous matches. The PLE secured E1.5 million in sponsorship for the Charity Cup, which returns after being last staged in 2019. Highlanders won that tournament, defeating Manzini Wanderers 3-1.

The play-offs for the tournament are scheduled for November 1, with semi-finals and the final on November 8. These dates were moved from September 27 and October 4 to accommodate FIFA fixtures. The National First Division will also have a final on finals day with E200 000 for the competition. Proceeds from the event will support non-governmental organisations.

Why it mattersFootball fans in Eswatini will witness a major derby match with a substantial prize for the winning club, and proceeds supporting charitable causes.
