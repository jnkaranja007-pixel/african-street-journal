Eswatini woman earns six degrees

Siphiwe Vilakati holds degrees in law, international economic relations, aviation, and business management.

Siphiwe Vilakati from kaVelezizweni, Eswatini, has achieved six academic degrees and is pursuing a seventh. Her qualifications include a Bachelor of Laws from the University of Zululand and master's degrees from Loyola University of Chicago and Toulouse 1 Capitole University.

Vilakati also holds a Master of Laws in International Economic Law from UNISA, a Postgraduate Diploma in Taxation from UNESWA, and a Global Executive Master of Business Administration in Aerospace Management from Toulouse Business School. She is currently a Doctor of Laws candidate in Mercantile Law at UNISA.

Reflecting on her journey, Vilakati stated that experiencing both rural and urban life instilled in her an understanding of life's complexities. She believes her achievements can inspire others to pursue their dreams, emphasizing that with faith, anything is possible.

Why it mattersSiphiwe Vilakati, an Eswatini national, has earned six degrees in diverse fields, showcasing advanced educational attainment.

Eswatini proposes new state mining company

Government faces economic debate over establishing another state-owned entity amid concerns over existing parastatals.

The Government of Eswatini is considering establishing a national mining company, adding to its portfolio of approximately 50 public enterprises. This proposal emerges at a time when several existing state-owned entities struggle financially, relying on government support.

The debate centers on the economic viability of expanding the public enterprise sector into mining, an industry demanding significant capital, specialized skills, and market knowledge. Supporters argue a national company could ensure citizens benefit more from mineral wealth and boost national development.

The Coordinating Assembly of Non-Governmental Organisations (CANGO), through its Executive Director Thembinkosi Dlamini, supports the initiative, stating State participation is overdue. Commissioner of Mines Dr Noah Nhleko acknowledged differing professional opinions as normal and important for public debate, with an official announcement pending strategy development.

Why it mattersEswatini citizens could receive a greater share of wealth from mineral resources and benefit from employment creation if the proposed national mining company is established.

Business · 2026-11-08

Eswati capital markets stall with no trades in October

Share prices held steady but bond values declined on the Eswatini Stock Exchange.

Eswatini’s capital markets saw no activity in October, with share prices remaining unchanged for all 10 listed companies on the Eswatini Stock Exchange (ESE). No equity trades occurred, resulting in zero trading volume and turnover for the month. The total market capitalisation stayed at E7.065 billion on October 31, 2026, the same as in September.

The ESE All-Share Index also held at 502.65 points, mirroring its September close. First National Bank of Eswatini maintained its share price at 1 483 cents, and Royal Eswatini Sugar Corporation Limited and Nedbank Eswatini Limited stayed at 1 800 cents each. First National Bank of Eswatini represented 27.92 per cent of the market capitalisation.

Despite the month's inactivity, the market has seen modest annual growth, with capitalisation up 3.96 per cent from October 2025. The All-Share Index increased by 3.80 per cent year-on-year. Government and corporate bond values both fell during October.

Why it mattersInvestors in Eswatini's stock market saw no changes in share values or trading activity in October, though bond values decreased.

MTN Eswatini boosts PLE Charity Cup sponsorship to E1.675 million

MTN Eswatini added E100 000 to the Premier League of Eswatini's Charity Cup, bringing the total sponsorship to E1.675 million.

MTN Eswatini has committed E100 000 to the Premier League of Eswatini (PLE) Charity Cup, increasing the total sponsorship to E1.675 million. This new funding follows a E75 000 contribution from Nedbank Eswatini the previous week. The PLE announced the initial E1.5 million sponsorship for the tournament at its launch.

PLE Brand and Marketing Manager Nqobile Simelane confirmed that the funds from MTN Eswatini will cover tournament prizes and event costs. She stated that any acquired sponsorship is directed towards these expenses, as the prize money was initially funded by the PLE. Individual awards include E9 000 for Player of the Series, E6 000 for Top Scorer, and E5 000 for Best Goalkeeper.

PLE Chief Executive Officer Pat Vilakati clarified that only statistics from the semi-finals and final matches will be considered for individual awards, excluding performances from the play-offs. The tournament reaches its climax on Saturday at Somhlolo National Stadium, the same venue where Mbabane Highlanders last won the trophy in 2019.

Why it mattersFootball fans in Eswatini will benefit from the increased prize money and event costs for the PLE Charity Cup.

Politics · 2026-11-08

Eswatini PM wants better public service

Prime Minister Russell Dlamini stated that customer experience is central to the country’s development agenda.

Prime Minister Russell Dlamini has called for improved customer service in government offices, emphasizing its importance for Eswatini's development. Minister for Education and Training Owen Nxumalo delivered the PM's message at the 11th Customer Experience Awards in Ezulwini.

The Prime Minister stated that good customer service involves all staff, not just frontline employees, and is influenced by leadership, culture, and systems. He noted that the service sector contributes over half of Eswatini's GDP, making customer experience vital for economic performance.

Dlamini urged businesses and public institutions to support customer service initiatives and called for the movement to extend to all economic sectors. The Clinic Group won an award in Healthcare and Medical Services, MTN Momo in Money Transfer/Fintech, and Garden Inn in Hotels.

Why it mattersEswatini's Prime Minister aims to improve public service delivery, which affects all citizens interacting with government offices.
