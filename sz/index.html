NGO criticised for advertising unpaid skilled roles in Eswatini

Eswatini's coordinating body for NGOs has warned against exploiting unemployed graduates through unpaid positions.

An NGO in Eswatini has come under fire for advertising unpaid volunteer positions that require specialised medical qualifications and professional skills. The advertisement offered roles in critical care, emergency care, medical data and research, and has raised questions about whether NGOs are taking advantage of unemployed graduates.

The Coordinating Assembly of Non-Governmental Organisations (CANGO), an umbrella body for more than 70 NGOs, has warned against exploiting graduates through unpaid positions. Its executive director, Thembinkosi Dlamini, said organisations often cite a lack of funding for unpaid roles, but he expressed concern that some volunteers were expected to work beyond agreed hours and duties without clear pay arrangements.

Dlamini said genuine volunteer programmes should have clear agreements that define responsibilities and separate volunteer roles from those of permanent employees. He said CANGO opposes exploitation in the workplace, and that the Employment Act and Industrial Relations Act mainly cover formal employer-employee relationships.

Taiwan partners Eswatini on diabetes prevention

A World Diabetes Day campaign at Siphocosini Constituency Centre focused on early action and preventive measures.

Taiwan and Eswatini's Ministry of Health partnered for a World Diabetes Day campaign at Siphocosini Constituency Centre, focusing on diabetes prevention and early detection. Taiwan's Ambassador to Eswatini, Ching-Lin Tsuei, stated his country supports Eswatini in managing non-communicable diseases, emphasizing proactive health measures.

Minister of Health Mduduzi Matsebula highlighted the dangers of poorly managed diabetes and urged residents to adopt healthier routines, including increased physical activity and reduced alcohol consumption. He also stressed the importance of regular medical check-ups for early identification of complications.

The campaign, held under the 2026 World Diabetes Day theme 'Early action on diabetes,' provided residents with health services and information. World Diabetes Day is observed annually on November 14.

Why it mattersResidents of Siphocosini Constituency are encouraged to adopt healthier lifestyles and undergo regular medical check-ups to prevent diabetes and its complications.

Eswatini seeks UK help on border security

Minister Shakantu met with UK Minister Turley to discuss managing migration and asylum seekers.

Eswatini's Minister for Foreign Affairs and International Cooperation, Senator Pholile Shakantu, met with United Kingdom Minister of State for Border Security and Asylum, The Rt Hon Anna Turley MP, in Mbabane to discuss strengthening border security and asylum processes. The Kingdom of Eswatini is experiencing an increase in refugees and asylum seekers and seeks to manage the situation proactively.

The two ministers explored cooperation in managing migration fairly, orderly, and securely, including sharing experiences, improving asylum processes, combating human trafficking, and enhancing information sharing. Eswatini aims to strengthen its laws, policies, systems, and controls to safeguard national security and uphold human dignity while meeting international obligations.

Senator Shakantu also observed proceedings in the UK Parliament during her visit. The discussions highlighted Eswatini's commitment to its friendship with the United Kingdom and its desire to deepen cooperation on these critical issues.

Why it mattersEswatini is strengthening its laws, policies, and controls to manage an increase in refugees and asylum seekers, following talks with the United Kingdom.

Eswatini hands regional trade leadership to Namibia

Mluleki Dlamini, Eswatini's Director of MSMEs, outlined past initiatives and regional trade challenges at a Windhoek meeting.

Eswatini has transferred its leadership of the Southern African Bureau of the Intergovernmental Committee of Senior Officials and Experts (ICSOE) to Namibia. The handover occurred during a high-level economic meeting in Windhoek from November 10 to 13, 2026, organised by the Government of Namibia and the United Nations Economic Commission for Africa (UNECA).

Mluleki Dlamini, Eswatini's Director of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs) and outgoing Chairperson of the Southern African Bureau, presented initiatives from Eswatini's tenure. These included support for Angola's African Continental Free Trade Area (AfCFTA) strategy, MSME development in Eswatini, and capacity-building for small businesses in Lesotho. Dlamini also highlighted trade barriers such as differing regulations, documentation, inadequate transport investment, and insufficient digital infrastructure.

The meeting aimed to strengthen regional trade and economic integration, with discussions focusing on persistent obstacles affecting cross-border trade. Dlamini called for harmonised regulations among regional economic communities to facilitate the smooth movement of goods and services as African countries implement the AfCFTA.

Why it mattersEswatini's businesses face challenges with transportation costs, border procedures, and regulatory differences, impacting their access to larger markets.

Mbabane equips 48 youths with business, crochet skills

The Municipal Council of Mbabane partnered with two organisations to train young people in entrepreneurship and crocheting.

Graduates from a Mbabane programme now hold business and craft certificates: 48 young people completed training backed by the Municipal Council of Mbabane (MCM). Of them, 23 took entrepreneurship courses run with Kwakha Indvodza, covering tax compliance, financial management and other business basics over one week. 25 more learned crocheting through Crafty Hook, a local crochet business.

At the graduation, Mayor Thulani Mkhonto, city councillors and MCM Chief Executive Officer Gciniwe Fakudze presented the certificates. Fakudze told the graduates the training should be a starting point, and urged young women in particular to earn extra income with their hands. Mkhonto said a skill cannot be taken away from its holder, and urged graduates to use theirs to improve their livelihoods.

Buyiswa Nkambule, one of the entrepreneurship graduates, thanked Kwakha Indvodza and the council for the training. A separate Eswatini Positive News report says Ntombikayise Dlamini received a certificate for The Cozy Crochet Experience on 13 November 2026, and that Sisekelo Dlamini was the only male on that five-week course.

Why it mattersGraduates now hold recognised skills they can use to earn income, and Mayor Mkhonto has offered to help those ready to show their products at the first Mbabane Fashion Show.
