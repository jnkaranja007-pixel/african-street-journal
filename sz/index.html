Eswatini farmer speaks at UN climate conference

Sonia Paiva will present on women farmers' role in sustainable agriculture and green businesses at COP31 in Türkiye.

Sonia Paiva, Founder and Director of the Woman Farmer Foundation (WFF), is representing Eswatini at the 2026 United Nations Climate Change Conference (COP31) in Antalya, Türkiye, starting today. Her participation highlights women farmers' contributions to sustainable agriculture and rural economic development.

Paiva will speak as a panellist at a COP31 side event organised by the Commonwealth Climate Finance Access Hub (CCFAH) on "Restoring Land, Creating Jobs: Women Farmers Building Green Businesses Across the Commonwealth." She will share insights from over three decades of experience on how small-scale women farmers can develop commercially viable, climate-resilient businesses.

The presentation will showcase the WFF's work, including its Woman Farmer of the Year Competition and climate-smart youth training. Paiva's experience as Managing Director of Eswatini Kitchen will also inform discussions on challenges women farmers face in accessing finance and markets.

Eswatini runners mourn South African athlete's marathon death

Antonie van Zijl, 37, collapsed and died during the Kaapsehoop Marathon 4-in-1 in South Africa.

South African runner Antonie van Zijl, 37, died after collapsing during the Kaapsehoop Marathon 4-in-1 on Saturday. Medical personnel on site provided immediate care but could not revive him. The Nelspruit Marathon Club, which organized the event, confirmed the death and expressed condolences.

Several Eswatini athletes had traveled to South Africa for the popular annual event, with many achieving strong results. News of van Zijl's passing spread quickly through official club channels, turning celebrations into mourning for the local participants. Runners from Eswatini took to public platforms to share their grief and offer prayers to the family.

The host organization, Nelspruit Marathon Club, stated that details regarding memorial arrangements would follow consultations with the family. The club also urged the public to respect the family's privacy and refrain from speculation about the cause of death.

Why it mattersEswatini athletes participating in the Kaapsehoop Marathon in South Africa were affected by the death of a fellow runner.

Eswatini adopts new two-injection HIV prevention drug

Lenacapavir offers protection through two doses annually, expanding choices for those struggling with daily medication.

Eswatini has become one of the first countries in the region to introduce Lenacapavir, a new long-acting injectable drug for HIV prevention. This medication requires only two injections per year, providing an alternative to daily oral pills. The drug is expected to increase prevention options, especially for adolescent girls and young women who may find daily medication challenging.

UNAIDS Strategic Information Advisor Nsindiso Dlamini highlighted the drug's introduction at the 2026 Regional World AIDS Day Commemoration in the Hhohho Region on Friday, November 6. He stated that Lenacapavir's two-injection schedule could simplify prevention for many. However, Dlamini cautioned that it must be offered alongside existing methods like condoms, oral PrEP, VMMC, and HIV testing.

The introduction of Lenacapavir supports Eswatini's goal to end AIDS as a public health threat by 2030. Dlamini stressed that medical advances must reach those in need and called for continued investment in existing services. He emphasised the importance of community involvement and respecting the privacy of users when shaping HIV prevention programmes.

Why it mattersEswatini residents seeking HIV prevention now have a new option with fewer doses than daily pills.

Mumcy Dlamini appointed acting chief justice

Judge Dlamini becomes the first woman to hold the judiciary's top post after King Mswati III's appointment.

High Court Judge Mumcy Dlamini has become the first woman to serve as acting Chief Justice of Eswatini. His Majesty King Mswati III appointed her to the position, filling the vacancy left by the death of Chief Justice Bheki Maphalala.

Minister of Justice and Constitutional Affairs Prince Simelane announced the appointment, and Attorney-General Sifiso Mashampu Khumalo presided over the swearing-in ceremony. Her husband, retired Supreme Court Judge Majahenkaba James Dlamini, attended the event.

Majahenkaba Dlamini, who previously served as Attorney-General and helped develop the 2005 Constitution, witnessed his wife's historic elevation. She now leads the Judiciary during a period of transition.

Why it mattersJudge Mumcy Dlamini's appointment as acting Chief Justice is a significant milestone for women in Eswatini's legal profession.

Politics · 2026-11-10

Eswatini MPs want chiefs stripped of stamp issuing powers

Lawmakers debated the Youth Enterprise Revolving Fund, raising concerns about underfunding and land insecurity.

Members of Parliament in Eswatini have called for chiefs to lose the power to issue umphakatsi stamps for young entrepreneurs. They proposed that these stamps should instead be available at tinkhundla centres. MPs voiced concerns yesterday during the debate on the Finance Committee’s report on the Youth Enterprise Revolving Fund (Amendment) Regulations, 2025.

Lawmakers highlighted fragmentation in youth empowerment, insufficient funding, and land insecurity as major obstacles to tackling youth unemployment. They advocated for a unified governance strategy to improve coordination and ensure funds reach young people effectively. The Youth Enterprise Revolving Fund, they noted, only reached a few hundred micro-enterprises, leaving thousands of applicants without necessary capital.

The Finance Committee Chairperson, Marwick Khumalo, stated that Regulation 7 has been amended to address the stamp issue, clarifying that the process begins at the chiefdom and does not require a stamp initially. Young people also proposed allocating underutilized farmland to them for agricultural development and food security.

Why it mattersMembers of Parliament in Eswatini are seeking to reform how young entrepreneurs access necessary documentation and resources, while also addressing broader issues of youth unemployment and land access.
