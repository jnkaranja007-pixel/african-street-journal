Eswatini inflation rises to 3.4 per cent ahead of CBE meeting

Annual inflation climbed from 2.9 per cent in September, ahead of the Central Bank's final Monetary Policy Consultative Committee meeting on Friday.

Eswatini's annual inflation rose to 3.4 per cent in October, up from 2.9 per cent in September, just before the Central Bank's final Monetary Policy Consultative Committee meeting on Friday.

Month on month, prices rose 0.9 per cent, according to the October Consumer Price Index report from the Central Statistical Office, faster than the 0.2 per cent recorded the month before. Housing and utilities, along with transport, together added 3.0 percentage points to the headline rate.

In September the bank lifted its benchmark rate by 25 basis points to 7.0 per cent, which pushed the prime lending rate to 10.25 per cent. Policymakers will weigh the direction of rates at the Friday meeting.

Why it mattersBorrowers already pay a prime lending rate of 10.25 per cent, and the bank will decide on Friday whether that rate moves again.

Eswatini brings in 104 grass bales for livestock farmers

The National Agricultural Marketing Board bought the feed in South Africa as farmers prepare for an anticipated El Niño drought.

Eswatini has received 104 grass bales from South Africa to provide feed for livestock farmers ahead of an anticipated El Niño drought. The National Agricultural Marketing Board (NAMBOARD) procured the batch for the Ministry of Agriculture.

Agriculture Minister Mandla Tshawuka said the bales are the first part of a larger order of thousands secured in South Africa to support the national herd. He urged livestock farmers to form groups and buy feed collectively to make it more accessible and cost-effective.

The Food and Agriculture Organization has provided E5 million for agricultural interventions to mitigate the dry spell. Tshawuka also urged crop farmers in areas that have received early rains to start planting while moisture is available.

Why it mattersLivestock farmers are being urged to organise group purchases, while the ministry's feed order extends to thousands of bales.

Climate · 2026-11-18

UNDP to run E508.5 million climate programme across three countries

The Adaptation Fund approved the regional funding for projects in Eswatini, Zambia and Zimbabwe, with work scheduled from 2027 to 2032.

Eswatini will share a E508.5 million (US$30 million) climate adaptation programme with Zambia and Zimbabwe, to be implemented by the United Nations Development Programme (UNDP) from 2027 to 2032. The Adaptation Fund approved the regional initiative in April 2026.

The programme will finance locally designed catchment projects to improve water security, restore damaged ecosystems and protect agricultural livelihoods. Smallholder farmers and herders are among its intended beneficiaries as Eswatini faces an El Niño-related drought threat in the 2026/2027 agricultural season, with below-normal rainfall and higher temperatures expected to pressure crops, livestock and water supplies.

Eswatini’s Ministry of Tourism and Environmental Affairs is the country’s executing entity. The programme will also explore payment for ecosystem services and partnerships with national banks to help fund climate-resilient agriculture. UNDP said droughts in 2015/2016 and 2023/2024 affected about 40 million and 60 million people across Southern Africa, respectively.

Why it mattersSmallholder farmers and herders are targeted for locally designed projects addressing water security and agricultural livelihoods during the 2026/2027 drought threat.

Dlamini says South Africa athletics reform is on track to avoid suspension

The World Athletics-appointed reform overseer says a new constitution is due for ratification on November 28 before submission to the global body.

Athletics Eswatini President Zakhele Dlamini said South Africa’s athletics federation is on course to avoid international suspension after a two-day reform meeting with its board in Kempton Park, Johannesburg, at the weekend. Dlamini is the World Athletics representative overseeing governance changes at Athletics South Africa (ASA).

Dlamini said ASA remains in good standing with World Athletics and is not suspended. The talks produced a roadmap for a new constitution, which is scheduled for ratification at an ASA Council Meeting on November 28, 2026, before submission to World Athletics ahead of fresh elections.

South Africa’s acting sports minister, Gayton McKenzie, and the South African Sports Confederation and Olympic Committee backed the process. The confederation seconded Jacques Faul and Nomsa Mahlangu to Dlamini’s task force. ASA Acting President Jean Verster said the overhaul is intended to protect athletes from missing World Athletics deadlines.

Why it mattersASA’s council is due to consider the new constitution on November 28, 2026, before it is sent to World Athletics ahead of fresh elections.

Culture · 2026-11-18

Vibes on Main brings Mr JazziQ to Mbabane on 28 November

The South African DJ will lead the festival’s Phase One bill at Prince of Wales Stadium, alongside South African and Eswatini performers.

Vibes on Main Eswatini will stage a music festival at Prince of Wales Stadium in Mbabane on Saturday, 28 November, with South African DJ Mr JazziQ leading its Phase One line-up. Organisers also named CowBoii and the duo Mellow & Sleazy among the performers.

The bill includes South African artists Optimistic, Freddy K, Chley and Bob Mabena, alongside Eswatini performers Young Zesh and Gifted Kosie. Organisers said tickets are affordable, but did not give prices. The event is part of the VOM Tour, linked to Johannesburg lifestyle hub Vibes on Main.

Mr JazziQ, whose name is Tumelo Manyoni, founded Black Is Brown Entertainment and was appointed a brand ambassador for GAC Motor South Africa in August 2026. In early October, Malkerns Country Club hosted the Eswatini Kota Festival, another South African event, where local performers had an opportunity to appear on an international stage.

Why it mattersEswatini performers Young Zesh and Gifted Kosie are billed alongside South African artists at the Mbabane show on 28 November.
