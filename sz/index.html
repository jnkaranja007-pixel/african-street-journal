Tribunal rules for ITQNET in E8.8m tax dispute

Eswatini Revenue Service failed to respond to ITQNET's objection within a reasonable period.

The Revenue Appeals Tribunal Eswatini ruled in favour of ITQNET Investments (Pty) Ltd in a tax dispute over E8 844 147.70. The tribunal found the Eswatini Revenue Service (ERS) did not respond to ITQNET's objection within a reasonable time. The ruling on October 8, 2026, stated ITQNET's objection was allowed by operation of law after ERS took about 10 months to decide.

The dispute began after an ERS audit disallowed expenses ITQNET claimed as deductions. This led to a revised assessment of E8 844 147.70 in additional tax and penalties. ITQNET, an IT services provider to the Eswatini government, argued the expenses were incurred to produce income. The company lodged its objection with ERS on April 16, 2025.

ERS issued its decision on February 19, 2026, which ITQNET challenged. The company argued ERS failed to act within the legally required reasonable period. The tribunal also examined ERS's 'pay now, argue later' principle, stating it does not override ERS's obligation to determine objections.

Why it mattersITQNET Investments (Pty) Ltd, a company providing information technology services to the Eswatini government, won a tax dispute of E8 844 147.70.

Eswatini strengthens disease surveillance amid ebola threat

The Ministry of Health is using the Integrated Disease Surveillance and Response strategy to prepare for outbreaks.

Eswatini is enhancing its public health systems to detect and manage disease outbreaks, including the threat of ebola. Nkosinathi Dlamini from the Epidemiology and Disease Control Unit (EDCU) stated this during a workshop in Manzini.

Dlamini explained that the country employs the Integrated Disease Surveillance and Response (IDSR) strategy to identify, report, and respond to priority diseases. This strategy involves case identification, reporting, data analysis, and investigations, utilizing both Indicator-Based Surveillance and Event-Based Surveillance.

The IDSR strategy aligns with Eswatini's obligations under the International Health Regulations (IHR) for public health emergency preparedness. Dlamini emphasized the need for community communication as part of these preparedness efforts.

Why it mattersEswatini's population is at risk of public health emergencies, including ebola, and the government is preparing to respond.

Eswatini names Malta friendly squad

Interim coach Dominic Kunene recalls midfielder Quality Dlamini for November 12 match.

Eswatini's national football team, Sihlangu, will play Malta in a friendly on November 12. Interim Head Coach Dominic Kunene is expected to include Nsingizini Hotspurs midfielder Quality Dlamini in the squad. Dlamini's inclusion follows his improved performances in the MTN Premier League, including a recent goal against Mbabane Highlanders AM. The team will also likely feature Justice Figuareido, who plays for Chippa United in South Africa.

Kunene, who was appointed interim head coach on October 30, will lead Sihlangu for the first time since his return to the technical bench. He will be assisted by Caleb Ngwenya, with Siza Dlamini and Thokozani Mamba serving as goalkeeper and fitness coaches, respectively. The Eswatini Football Association (EFA) stated this interim arrangement allows preparations to continue while a permanent head coach is finalised.

The EFA is reportedly in discussions with a Moroccan coach for the substantive head coach position, with a potential memorandum of understanding with Moroccan football authorities. The incoming permanent coach may join the team during the November international window and could travel to Malta. The match against Malta will be Eswatini's first international friendly against a European nation, kicking off at 6:30pm at the Ta’ Qali National Stadium.

Why it mattersEswatini footballers and fans will be directly affected by the selection and upcoming international friendly match.

Eswatini Competition Commission probes EswatiniMed

Medical aid fund EswatiniMed faces investigation for alleged anti-competitive practices against rival providers.

The Eswatini Competition Commission is investigating EswatiniMed for alleged anti-competitive practices that disadvantage rival healthcare providers. Complaints from Paramed Laboratories and the Eswatini Private Doctors Association (EPDA) led to the probe. The commission ruled on July 31, 2026, that EswatiniMed’s activities, including its ownership of Ezulwini Private Hospital, fall under the Competition Act No. 8 of 2007.

Paramed Laboratories claims EswatiniMed refused to issue practice numbers and withdrew existing ones for some providers. EPDA accused EswatiniMed of using a 'non-price predatory strategy' to harm competitors of its associated healthcare providers. The commission’s Secretariat issued an investigation notice on September 26, 2024, and its report on February 10, 2025, concluded EswatiniMed holds a dominant market position and engaged in anti-competitive practices.

The Secretariat recommended an administrative penalty of 4.398 per cent of EswatiniMed’s total turnover, to be paid within 90 days of the Board’s decision. EswatiniMed consultant Peter Simelane raised concerns about the Board’s composition. The commission’s investigation began after EswatiniMed objected to its jurisdiction on November 12, 2024.

Why it mattersEswatiniMed, a dominant medical aid fund, is being investigated for actions that could harm competing healthcare providers and potentially affect patient choice and access to services.

Sport · 2026-11-03

Eswatini swimmer Fraser improves personal best in Dakar

Luca Fraser, 16, swam the 200m breaststroke in 2:32:05, shaving two seconds off his previous record.

Eswatini swimmer Luca Fraser competed in the 200 metres (m) breaststroke heat at the Dakar Youth Olympic Games on Monday, finishing sixth in his race with a time of 2:32:05. Fraser, who also carried the national flag, improved his personal best by two seconds in the unfamiliar event. Japan's Lori Miyazaki won the heat in 2:16:35.

At 16 years old, Fraser was the second youngest competitor in his heat. He was initially scheduled to race in his preferred 50m breaststroke event. The Dakar 2026 Youth Olympic Games are the first Olympic sporting event hosted in Africa.

Team Eswatini's campaign continues with sprinter Knowledge Mkhwanazi competing in the 100m and taekwondo athlete Simisile Thwala in the -55kg category.

Why it mattersEswatini athletes are representing the nation at the first Olympic sporting event hosted in Africa.
