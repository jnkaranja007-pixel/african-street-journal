Eswatini open schools football campaign against Mozambique

The boys’ and girls’ teams face Mozambique on December 4, then Lesotho, in a nine-nation youth tournament in South Africa.

Source: Times of Eswatini · 2026-11-25 · 1 min read

Eswatini’s boys’ and girls’ teams open their CAF African Schools Football Championship COSAFA (Council of Southern African Football Associations) Qualifier campaigns against Mozambique on December 4 in Saldanha Bay, South Africa. The three-day tournament runs through December 6 and brings together nine Southern African nations.

The boys play at 10am at Saldanha Bay Municipality Grounds, while the girls meet Mozambique at 11am at Weskus School of Skills. Eswatini are in Group C with Mozambique and Lesotho in both divisions, and return to face Lesotho that afternoon, at 3:30pm for the boys and 4:30pm for the girls.

The three group winners and best-placed runner-up in each division advance to the semi-finals on December 5. Finals are scheduled for December 6, with the girls’ match at 1pm and the boys’ at 2:30pm.

Why it mattersEswatini’s teams have two group matches on December 4, and only the group winners and best-placed runner-up advance to the semi-finals.
