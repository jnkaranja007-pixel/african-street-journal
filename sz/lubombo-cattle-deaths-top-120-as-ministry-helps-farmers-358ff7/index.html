Lubombo cattle deaths top 120 as ministry helps farmers find buyers

Gall sickness killed more than 90 cattle on the Lubombo Plateau in two months, while drought and poor grazing threaten herds elsewhere.

Source: Times of Eswatini · 2026-11-20 · 1 min read

More than 120 cattle have died in Eswatini’s Lubombo region amid drought and gall sickness, regional veterinary officer Dr Noel Chikuni said. About 30 died around Siphofaneni and Sithobela amid drought, while gall sickness killed more than 90 on the Lubombo Plateau over the past two months.

Chikuni said the sickness, also known as inyongo, affected cattle at Mkhalamfene, Maphungwane and Sitsatsaweni. Ministry of Agriculture extension officer Lungelo Mncube said officers were helping farmers find buyers and arrange sales, but the number of buyers remained limited. He urged farmers to sell cattle before shortages of grazing and water weakened them.

Chikuni said veterinary authorities had not seen a significant overall rise in cattle deaths, although losses had increased slightly in the Lowveld. Mncube said buyers had shown interest in cattle from Lubombo and neighbouring Shiselweni.

Why it mattersLubombo farmers are being urged to sell cattle before grazing and water shortages weaken them, but the ministry says buyers are limited.
