Road accident kills three in Chad's Mandoul

A tyre burst caused a vehicle to overturn on the Nderguigui axis, resulting in multiple injuries.

Source: Alwihda Info · 2026-11-08 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Three people died and several others were injured in a road accident on November 7, 2026, in Chad's Mandoul province. The crash occurred on the Nderguigui axis.

Mandoul central prefect Oumar Issaka Fouzari stated that a tyre burst caused the vehicle to lose control and overturn. The victims, mostly traders heading to the Bedjondo market, received initial treatment at the Péni health centre.

The bodies of the deceased were taken to the Koumra morgue. This accident has reignited discussions about road safety and the need for drivers to be more cautious.

Why it mattersThe accident directly affected traders and their families in Chad.
