Abuja Iyoto Galy builds a rap career in Chad

The artist blends rap, trap and afro-drill, using music for expression and denunciation in a scene she describes as largely male.

Source: Tchadinfos · 2026-11-22 · 1 min read

Aboudja Singli Josué, who performs as Abuja Iyoto Galy, is developing a career in Chad’s music scene, blending rap, trap and afro-drill. She says she wants to use music to express herself, denounce issues and assert her identity in a field that remains largely male.

Galy began her artistic career as a professional dancer in 2020. The experience helped her develop a sense of rhythm and stage presence, according to Tchadinfos. In 2023, she moved into rap and began shaping a musical identity around incisive delivery and engaged lyrics.

She says she is committed to staying authentic as she develops her sound.

Why it mattersGaly’s next step is to develop her rap career, following her move from professional dance into rap in 2023.
