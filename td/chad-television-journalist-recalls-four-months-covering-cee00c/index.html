Chad television journalist recalls four months covering 1993 conference

Annouar El Sadat Amin joined the Arabic-language newsroom in 1989 and now heads the state broadcaster’s station in Sarh.

Source: Tchadinfos · 2026-11-23 · 1 min read

Annouar El Sadat Amin, a journalist with Chad’s National Office of Audiovisual Media (ONAMA) and head of its provincial station in Sarh, has worked in television since 1989. He joined the Arabic-language newsroom to report and present television news.

Amin said he covered the 1993 Sovereign National Conference for four months despite limited resources. He was among 19 young recruits selected for the civil service after a 1991 recruitment competition, and later spent about 20 years in the broadcaster’s programmes department.

His career included presenting, producing and making documentaries. Amin said Chad’s first television broadcasts dated to 1987, when images appeared on a screen while sound came through a radio. Satellite broadcasting began in 2006, and he said ONAMA now has more modern technical equipment.

Why it mattersONAMA’s Sarh station is headed by a journalist whose account covers four months of reporting at the 1993 national conference.
