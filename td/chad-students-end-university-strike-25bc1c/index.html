Chad students end university strike

The National Union of Chadian Students (UNET) lifted a three-day strike at the University of N'Djamena on November 13, 2026, but maintained its demands.

Source: Tchadinfos · 2026-11-14 · 1 min read

The National Union of Chadian Students (UNET) at the University of N'Djamena announced on November 13, 2026, that it had ended a strike that began three days earlier. The student organization cited actions taken by the National Centre for University Services (CNOU) as the reason for lifting the strike, while affirming that their demands remain unresolved.

After three days of protests, UNET has called for students to resume their university activities. In a statement, the organization thanked the striking students for their mobilization and sense of responsibility. It urged everyone to return to classes calmly and with respect.

UNET also issued a call for unity and responsibility, encouraging students to continue working towards improving their living and study conditions. This objective remains a priority for the organization despite the lifting of the strike.

Why it mattersStudents at the University of N'Djamena are returning to classes after a three-day strike, with their demands for improved living and study conditions still pending.
