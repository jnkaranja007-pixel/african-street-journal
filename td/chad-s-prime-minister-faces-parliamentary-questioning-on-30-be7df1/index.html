Chad's prime minister faces parliamentary questioning on 30 November

Deputy Mahamat Ahmat Lazina initiated the plenary-session questioning, whose official subject has not yet been announced.

Source: Tchadinfos · 2026-11-21 · 1 min read

Chad’s Prime Minister Allah-Maye Halina is scheduled to appear before the National Assembly at 10 a.m. on Monday, 30 November, for questioning in a plenary session initiated by lawmaker Mahamat Ahmat Lazina, Tchadinfos reported.

The exact subject has not been officially disclosed, the outlet said, citing informed sources. Several sources told Tchadinfos that lawmakers could ask about the government’s midterm record, including progress on its commitments and results of its work.

The session is scheduled for the National Assembly, but the government has not formally confirmed what the questioning will cover.

Why it mattersHalina must answer in the National Assembly at 10 a.m. on 30 November, while the subject of the questioning remains unannounced.
