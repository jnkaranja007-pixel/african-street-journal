Chad prefect urges peace between herders and farmers

Aboudeïa prefect Mahamat Ismaïl Hassan Kari met with residents in Am-Habilé on October 28, 2026.

Aboudeïa prefect Mahamat Ismaïl Hassan Kari visited Am-Habilé on October 28, 2026, urging residents to foster peaceful coexistence between herders and farmers. The meeting, held 80 km east of Aboudeïa in the Salamat province, drew a large crowd. Kari, accompanied by security officials and community leaders, also encouraged mass school enrollment and an end to violence against women.

The prefect's message directly addresses land disputes in a region where fields and pastures often overlap. Noh Idriss Hamid, the sous-préfet of Am-Habilé, welcomed Kari and his delegation. The gathering included all segments of the population, highlighting the importance of the prefect's address on community harmony.

Kari's visit aimed to promote stability and development in the Salamat province. The call for peace between agricultural and pastoral communities is important for the region's economic activities. Residents were also urged to prioritize education for their children.

Why it mattersFarmers and herders in the Salamat province of Chad are directly affected by the prefect's call for peaceful coexistence over land use.

Chad court sentences rebel leader to 15 years

The FDPC movement calls the September 9, 2026 conviction of General Abdoulaye Miskine a heavy blow.

Chad's justice system sentenced General Abdoulaye Miskine, leader of the Front démocratique du peuple centrafricain (FDPC), to 15 years in prison on September 9, 2026. The FDPC announced its deep concern on October 29, 2026, from Gamboula, calling the ruling heavy with human, political, and judicial consequences.

The movement questions the fairness of the trial and demands that judicial authorities ensure Miskine's access to legal recourse, his safety, and medical care. The FDPC has asked international organizations and human rights groups to monitor the case while respecting Chad's judicial independence.

The FDPC stated it will contest the sentence through legal, diplomatic, and peaceful means, emphasizing its commitment to peace and dialogue. The group insists on the need for an unimpeachable procedure that respects defense rights and fair trial principles.

Why it mattersThe conviction of FDPC leader General Abdoulaye Miskine to 15 years in prison by Chadian justice affects the political stability and human rights of individuals involved.

N'Djaména mayor joins cemetery cleanup

Djamal Yaya Moussa participated in a cleanup at Farcha cemetery ahead of All Saints' Day.

The mayor of N'Djaména's 1st arrondissement, Djamal Yaya Moussa, joined a cleanup operation at Farcha cemetery on October 29, 2026. He worked alongside Catholic parishioners from two local churches and attended with priests, preparing for the upcoming All Saints' Day celebration.

During discussions with religious leaders, Mayor Moussa expressed concern about protecting the cemetery from fires. He stated he would instruct the municipal police to ensure the site's security during this period.

The cleanup aimed to prepare the cemetery for the influx of visitors expected for All Saints' Day.

Why it mattersThe mayor's action ensures the security of a public space for religious observance.

Politics · 2026-10-30

Chad launches national forum for traditional leaders

Traditional authorities from 23 provinces gathered in Mongo to identify causes of conflict and propose solutions.

Chad's national forum for traditional authorities opened on October 29 in Mongo, Guera province. Vice-Premier Minister Limane Mahamat, representing the head of state, launched the three-day event aimed at identifying conflict causes and proposing solutions for peaceful coexistence.

Traditional leaders from across Chad's 23 provinces are discussing the roots of clashes, including farmer-herder disputes, intercommunal violence, and land conflicts. The forum seeks to develop recommendations and establish frameworks to curb ongoing violence.

The president of the High Council of Traditional Chiefdoms, Tamitah Djidingar, stressed the role of traditional leaders in fostering unity and protecting communities. He stated that chiefs should reconcile adversaries and preserve human life.

Why it mattersTraditional leaders from across Chad are meeting to address conflicts that affect communities and fragile social cohesion.
