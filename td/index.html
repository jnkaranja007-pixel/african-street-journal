Chad students strike over food and transport

University campuses in N'Djaména are deserted as students protest lack of meals and buses.

Students at Université de N’Djaména began a strike on November 3, leaving lecture halls empty across the Ardep Djoumal, Farcha, and Toukra campuses.

The University National Union of Chad (UNET) issued a five-day strike notice citing two main grievances: the closure of university restaurants since the academic year began and insufficient bus services to campus.

UNET representative Ortoroum Mathurin expressed frustration, stating that authorities had not initiated any discussions to resolve the issues.

Why it mattersStudents at Chad's national university are protesting conditions that affect their daily learning and well-being.

Chad drops complaint over guard shot by expelled student

A prosecutor has closed the complaint of a guard shot at a N'Djamena high school, while the 21-year-old suspect is still missing.

A fourth substitute prosecutor of the Republic in Chad has closed the complaint brought by the family of a school guard wounded by automatic weapon fire. The shots came from a 21-year-old student who had been expelled from the courtyard of the Mamadou Sidibe high school in Farcha, N'Djamena. The family said the decision left them stunned and dejected.

The suspect cannot be found. The Judicial Police Coordination is leading intensive searches and expects to send the file to the prosecution despite his absence. Investigators traced his phone to Djarmaya with help from mobile operators, but did not find him there. His two guardians were arrested after the attack and released two days later after promising to bring the student back.

The guard remains in hospital. His family rejects proposals for an amicable settlement and demands the suspect's arrest and trial.

Why it mattersThe guard remains hospitalized, and the family's demand for arrest and trial now rests on a suspect who is still at large.

Airtel Tchad and ARCEP inspect network sites in N'Djamena

The operator's director general and the telecoms regulator's director general toured four sites after a formal notice of default.

The director general of Airtel Tchad, Brahim Nallar, and the director general of the ARCEP, Moussa Souleymane Chérif, visited several of the operator's mobile sites on Wednesday, November 11, 2026. The sites are in Farcha, Djambal Bar, Sabangali and Moursal, all in Chad's capital area. The visit followed a formal notice of default issued by the regulator, which had found shortcomings in Airtel Tchad's passive and active network infrastructure.

Nallar said closer cooperation with the regulator would help the company direct its investments in line with the authorities' wishes, and said Airtel Tchad, present in Chad for more than 20 years, planned to keep investing heavily in information and communication technology. Chérif said the regulator was satisfied with progress on passive network components, ground equipment, towers, maintenance and a hybrid solar energy system, but said more work was needed on the active network and service quality.

Chérif said the ARCEP would continue its controls on a regular basis. The visit ended at the headquarters of Energy Vision, the technical partner on the operation.

Why it mattersAirtel Tchad's mobile customers are affected because the regulator has ordered the operator to improve active network infrastructure and service quality, and inspections will continue.

Politics · 2026-11-12

Chad's president holds security talks in Abéché

Mahamat Idriss Déby Itno met administrative, military and religious leaders in the Ouaddaï provincial capital on Wednesday, November 11, 2026.

President Mahamat Idriss Déby Itno, a marshal, visited Abéché on Wednesday, November 11, 2026, as the fourth stop of his security tour of Chad. The presidency said he held talks on arrival with local administrators, military commanders, notables and religious leaders.

The local delegation was led by Ismaël Yamouda Djorbo, the government's delegate general for Ouaddaï province, who holds the rank of police inspector general. The discussions focused on assessing the local security situation, strengthening peaceful coexistence between communities and reinforcing respect for state authority.

The packet does not report any decisions, announcements or next steps from the meeting.

Why it mattersResidents of Ouaddaï province are directly affected, since the talks covered the local security situation and relations between communities in the province.

Education · 2026-11-12

Logone Occidental launches training for 200 volunteer agents

The 200 recruits, drawn from education, health, environment and agriculture, will take part in a programme run with the national volunteering agency.

Denenoudji Sylla, the first secretary of the Logone Occidental Provincial Council, formally launched the training of 200 volunteer agents on Tuesday, November 10, 2026, in the council's meeting room in Moundou. The volunteers were recruited in education, health, environment and agriculture.

The training is part of a partnership between the National Agency for Volunteering in Chad (ANVOLT) and the Provincial Council. ANVOLT mission chief Zamzam Nime praised the collaboration and said the course prepares volunteers to work with the communities they will serve, urging participants to make full use of the learning and exchanges on offer.

Sylla said the agreement reflects a shared aim to promote civic engagement, value the skills of Chadian youth and mobilise human resources for territorial development. The packet gives no further details on the length of the course or when the volunteers will be deployed.

Why it mattersYoung volunteers in Logone Occidental will be prepared to work in communities across education, health, environment and agriculture.
