N'Djamena police clear illegal site at Milezi forest

Municipal police and national security forces cleared an illegally occupied public space in the 1st arrondissement on Aug. 20. The operation took place at the Milezi forest. The site is designated for future development.

Why it mattersThe clearance removes irregular occupants to allow the city to proceed with planned construction on the site.
