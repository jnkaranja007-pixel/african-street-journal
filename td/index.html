Chad prepares entrepreneurial village for FNE 2026

The ONAPE and seven incubation partners are structuring a space for business creators and project leaders.

Chad's National Office for Employment Promotion (ONAPE) and its seven incubation partners are organizing the Entrepreneurial Village for the upcoming National Employment Forum (FNE) 2026. This dedicated space aims to support and foster innovation among business creators and project leaders. A working meeting on Thursday, October 22, 2026, led by the head of the Self-Employment Desk, finalized plans for this initiative.

The Entrepreneurial Village is designed to stimulate innovation, learning, and personalized support for entrepreneurs. Discussions focused on how incubation structures will lead the Entrepreneurial Clinic and masterclasses. Priority themes were identified to address entrepreneurs' needs, enhance their skills, and ensure their initiatives are sustainable.

The Entrepreneurial Village is set to be a key event for discovering new ideas, acquiring practical tools, and connecting with experts. Separately, the organizing committee for the second Ngourkosso forum launched an appeal on October 22, 2026, for funds from the department's natives, with a fundraising assembly scheduled for October 31, 2026.

Why it mattersEntrepreneurs and business creators in Chad will gain access to innovation, learning, and practical support.

Chad groups launch budget transparency watchdog

Oxfam and CIERGED organised a workshop in Koumra to revitalise citizen budget monitoring.

Oxfam and the Centre for Studies and Research on Governance, Extractive Industries and Sustainable Development (CIERGED) organised a workshop in Koumra on Wednesday, October 21. The event aimed to revitalise citizen observatories for budget control.

The initiative seeks to boost citizen participation in monitoring local public finances, focusing on transparency, accountability, and reducing inequality in Chad. The workshop gathered youth and women's organisations, people with disabilities, religious leaders, and media professionals.

CIERGED Director General Professor Maoundonodji Gilbert stated the effort should make the observatory more operational and strengthen its role in citizen oversight of communal resource management.

Why it mattersChadian citizens will have a stronger mechanism to monitor local public finances.

Chad launches special road safety brigade

The National Road Safety Office is deploying a new unit to enforce vehicle regulations and driver compliance.

Chad's National Road Safety Office (ONASER) has activated its Special Brigade to enforce road safety regulations for transporters. Director General Zam-zam Ahmat Karambal met with transport unions on Thursday, October 22, 2026, at the Abou Hamama travel agency and the Tacha Barra goods transport hub. The brigade, operational since September 2, 2026, aims to ensure the safety of road users and drivers.

Karambal highlighted that some drivers lack proper licenses or use counterfeit documents, and stressed the importance of vehicle condition, including headlights, seatbelts, and tires. The brigade's initial focus will be on non-compliant lighting systems. Transport unions requested a two-week period to inform their members and remove illegal 'laser' headlights.

The transport unions proposed that the brigade issue detailed inspection reports for non-compliant vehicles, to be given to drivers to share with their companies. ONASER, established by law n° 003/PR/2017, is central to the country's road safety strategy and will maintain an active presence on the ground.

Why it mattersTransporters and their unions in Chad are directly affected by new road safety enforcement measures.
