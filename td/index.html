Chad Shares Universal Health Insurance Experience with Central African Republic

Chad's National Health Insurance Fund hosted a workshop to discuss challenges and financing of universal health coverage with a Central African delegation.

The National Health Insurance Fund (CNAS) in N'Djamena organized a capitalization workshop on October 20, 2026, to share Chad's experience with the progressive implementation of universal health insurance. The event brought together Chadian health officials and a delegation from the Central African Republic. The primary goal was to learn from difficulties encountered in implementing and financing universal health insurance policies in countries like Togo, Ivory Coast, Chad, and Ghana, to aid the successful rollout of this policy in the Central African Republic.

CNAS Director General Sing-yabe Barnabas stated that Chad has been committed for several years to an ambitious process of building universal health coverage. This initiative underscores Chad's role in regional knowledge sharing for public health policy development. The workshop focused on identifying obstacles and successful strategies in establishing and funding national health insurance schemes.

The discussions aimed to facilitate a successful implementation of universal health coverage in the Central African Republic by leveraging the experiences of other nations. This exchange highlights a collaborative approach to addressing public health challenges across borders and improving healthcare access for citizens.

Why it mattersCentral African Republic health officials are affected by learning how to implement and finance universal health insurance policies.

Chad Official Visits Conflict Zone

A government delegate and security forces deployed to the border region of Salamat to quell farmer-herder tensions.

Souleymane Issakha Abdoulaye, the government delegate for the Salamat province, traveled to Kouzi Wahit on the night of October 18-19, 2026. He was accompanied by security, judicial, and administrative officials, as well as the provincial council president. The area, located near the Sila province border, has been experiencing unrest stemming from conflicts between herders and farmers.

Security and defense forces are currently operating in the region under the command of Major General Fouzari Hassan, head of Military Zone No. 10. Their mission includes strengthening surveillance, protecting residents and their property, and restoring order. General Hassan has urged his troops to remain vigilant and firm in carrying out their duties.

This deployment aims to de-escalate the ongoing conflict between agriculturalists and herders in the Bahr-Azoum department. The presence of high-ranking officials and military personnel underscores the seriousness of the situation and the government's commitment to resolving the dispute.

Why it mattersFarmers and herders in the Salamat province face escalating conflict over land and resources.

Chad launches agricultural project to boost food security

The TARSPro II Lot 2 initiative aims to enhance legume and fodder production, focusing on women's empowerment and nutritional security in Mayo-Kebbi Ouest.

The TARSPro II Lot 2 project, focused on intensifying legume and fodder value chains for women's empowerment and nutritional security, has officially launched in Pala, Mayo-Kebbi Ouest province. Spearheaded by the Consortium Agri-Résilience Mayo-Kebbi (CARMK), the initiative seeks to bolster rural community production capacities, develop income-generating activities, and promote the economic autonomy of women and youth.

Neyom Toukoua Djaba, CARMK's lead, urged beneficiaries to embrace the project and leverage its opportunities for sustainable agricultural development. Provincial Governor General Abdelmanane Khatab praised the initiative for its local development prospects, highlighting Maralfalfa cultivation for fodder improvement, local agricultural product processing, and the promotion of the Maradi red goat.

Activities commenced with a symbolic Maralfalfa planting on a 200-hectare site in Sorga. TARSPro II Lot 2 intends to improve agricultural and fodder output, generate income, and strengthen rural household resilience through an integrated approach, contingent on beneficiary involvement and local actor mobilization.

Why it mattersFarmers in Mayo-Kebbi Ouest will benefit from enhanced production capacities, income generation, and improved fodder availability through the TARSPro II Lot 2 project.

Commercial Bank Tchad partners for youth employment

The bank will mobilize job and internship opportunities and provide technical support to young entrepreneurs.

The Commercial Bank Tchad (CBT) and the organizing committee of the National Employment Forum (FNE 2026) have formalized a partnership to boost employment, training, and entrepreneurship for Chadian youth. This agreement, signed on Wednesday, October 20, 2026, designates CBT as the official and exclusive banking partner.

Under the agreement, CBT commits to actively seeking job and internship placements for young people. The bank will also offer technical guidance to young entrepreneurs participating in the PROJIACE initiative. This collaboration aims to bridge the gap between the banking sector and job seekers.

This initiative reflects a shared objective to increase private sector involvement in promoting decent employment in Chad. The partnership is intended to leverage public-private collaboration as a tangible tool for professional integration and to shape a better future for the nation's young population.

Why it mattersYoung job seekers and entrepreneurs in Chad will benefit from new employment and training opportunities facilitated by the bank.

Central African Republic Official Denies Border Breach in Chad Operation

Dimitri Sytyi stated military actions against Noureddine Adam's fighters remained within Central African territory, citing drone data and maps.

Dimitri Sytyi, a special advisor to the Central African Republic's president, asserted that a recent military operation against Noureddine Adam's fighters did not cross into Chad. Sytyi, who is also the director of the Maison Russe Cultural Center in Bangui, addressed concerns raised by Chadian authorities, stating that the operation was conducted with precision within Central African borders. He suggested that the Chadian reaction might stem from misinformation from local sources.

The Central African Ministry of Defense had previously announced on October 10, 2026, that its forces, supported by Russian allies, had conducted a successful operation in the Vakaga prefecture, near the border region of the Central African Republic, Chad, and Sudan. This operation reportedly neutralized several fighters and seized military equipment. Sytyi presented drone footage and administrative maps as evidence to support his claim that the incident occurred on Central African soil.

Sytyi called for strengthened bilateral cooperation, emphasizing that transborder armed groups pose a shared threat that neither state can effectively combat alone. He noted that Noureddine Adam's own statement acknowledged his camps were located within the Central African Republic, further corroborating the official account of the operation's location.

Why it mattersThe official's statements address potential border security concerns between Chad and the Central African Republic, impacting regional stability.
