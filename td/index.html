Chad women learn breast cancer prevention in Abéché

The Association of Midwives for Sexual and Reproductive Health raised awareness about early detection and dangers.

In Abéché, Chad, the Association of Midwives for Sexual and Reproductive Health (ASFSSR) held a breast cancer awareness event on Sunday, October 25, 2026. The session took place at the Mabrouka center in the 7th arrondissement.

ASFSSR President Hatiyé Sougoudou led the initiative, informing young women and adult women about the disease's dangers, essential prevention measures, and the vital importance of early screening. Participants pledged to follow the advice and share prevention messages with their communities.

This action by the ASFSSR reinforces its commitment to women's health and strengthening local prevention efforts. The association aims to inform women about breast cancer, a disease that can also affect men, though less commonly.

Why it mattersWomen in Abéché, Chad, received information on breast cancer prevention and early detection.

NdjamVi festival aims for professional artists

The 19th edition of the NdjamVi festival will take place from November 25-28, 2026, in N'Djamena.

The 19th edition of the NdjamVi International Festival is scheduled for November 25-28, 2026, at the Olympe Africa Centre in N'Djamena. This year's theme, "Creative Youth, Sustainable Future," signals a strategic shift towards building lasting careers for artists, moving beyond mere talent discovery. The festival, which began in 2007, has hosted 784 Chadian music groups and awarded 58 prizes.

Festival director Nguinambaye Ndoua Manassé announced the plans on October 24, 2026, emphasizing the need to professionalize artists and foster cultural entrepreneurship. The event will feature concerts, competitions, and new segments like "Éclat de Femmes: Voix de l’Engagement," showcasing female artists. A training program in innovative entrepreneurship with Mali's Kôré Arts Institute and a market village for artisans are also planned.

The festival aims to contribute to social cohesion and provide employment opportunities through culture. Organizers are seeking increased support from public authorities, while acknowledging current partners including the GIZ Lél Afé project, the German Embassy, and the European Union. The event has previously conducted cultural decentralization activities in locations such as Kélo, Moundou, and Doba.

Why it mattersThe NdjamVi festival aims to professionalize artists and develop cultural entrepreneurship in Chad.

Chad official promotes peace after farmer-herder clash

Ahmat Choua Rozi met with community representatives in Salamat to foster reconciliation and prevent future conflict.

In Chad's Salamat province, government delegate Ahmat Choua Rozi visited Am-Djoudjoul on Sunday, October 25, 2026, following a conflict between herders and farmers in Kouzi-Wahit on October 18. Rozi met with community representatives to encourage reconciliation and social cohesion.

The delegate urged communities to learn from recent events and avoid further confrontation, emphasizing dialogue and adherence to established rules. Participants expressed regret over the recent clashes and called for reconciliation and peaceful coexistence between herders and farmers.

Earlier in the week, on October 21, the prefect of Aboudeïa department, Mahamat Soumaïne Hassan, began a sensitisation tour in eight sub-prefectures, accompanied by six canton chiefs, to prevent community conflicts.

Why it mattersFarmers and herders in the Salamat province of Chad are affected by tensions that require reconciliation efforts.

three die in N'Djamena road accident

A vehicle overturned near Milezi forest after swerving to avoid a motorcycle.

A serious road accident occurred on Sunday evening in the Farcha neighborhood, within N'Djamena's 1st arrondissement. A vehicle overturned multiple times near the Milezi forest.

The driver was attempting to avoid a motorcyclist when the crash happened. Initial reports indicate that three people lost their lives in the incident.

The accident took place in the evening hours of Sunday. The location was specifically the Farcha area of the capital city.

News · 2026-10-26

Haiti force receives human remains after soldier disappearance

The Gang Suppression Force (GSF) received human remains suspected to be those of four missing Salvadoran soldiers.

The Gang Suppression Force (GSF) in Haiti announced on October 25, 2026, that it received human remains through a third-party humanitarian organization. These remains are suspected to belong to four Salvadoran soldiers who disappeared on October 18 after an attack in the Santo area of Croix-des-Bouquets.

Jack Christofides, the GSF's special representative, stated that the force cannot yet formally confirm the identity of the remains and offered condolences to the families of the missing soldiers and the Salvadoran government. The GSF also clarified that a Chadian military ID card circulating on social media belongs to a soldier who lost it during search operations; the soldier is healthy and not among the missing.

The GSF assured that further official information will be released once verified and highlighted the ongoing cooperation with Haitian authorities, troop-contributing countries, and the Salvadoran government as essential to combatting armed groups.

Why it mattersChad contributes soldiers to the GSF in Haiti, and one Chadian soldier's ID was misplaced during operations.
