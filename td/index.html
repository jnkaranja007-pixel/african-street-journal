Chadian court postpones General Miskine's trial to August 2026

The trial of General Abdoulaye Miskine and his co-accused has been rescheduled by the N’Djamena high court due to heavy rainfall and a public holiday.

The trial of General Abdoulaye Miskine, also known as Martin Koumtamadji, and his co-accused has been postponed to August 26, 2026. The N’Djamena high court cited heavy rainfall on August 24, 2026, and the commemoration of Mawlid an-nabaoui on August 25, 2026, as reasons for the delay. Miskine has been detained for nearly seven years without judgment at Klessoum prison.

Miskine, along with Adoum Rakhis, Ringo Djouma, and Abdel-Aziz Gros, faces serious charges including assassinations, rapes, and insurrectional movements in the border area between Chad and the Central African Republic. The Chadian state is a civil party in this case, which is also closely watched in the Central African Republic.

A team of 10 lawyers will defend the accused, who benefit from the presumption of innocence. Miskine's defense has repeatedly raised concerns about his critical health, noting kidney failure and significant weight loss during his detention.

Why it mattersThe postponement of this high-profile trial affects General Miskine and his co-accused, who remain in detention, and delays justice for the victims of the alleged crimes.

News · 08/26/2026

CNDH urges calm after intercommunal violence in Oum-Hadjer

Chad's National Commission for Human Rights (CNDH) called for restraint and legal resolution after deadly intercommunal clashes in Oum-Hadjer, Batha province.

The National Commission for Human Rights (CNDH) expressed deep concern regarding the situation in Oum-Hadjer, Batha province, following violent intercommunal clashes that resulted in deaths, injuries, and arrests. In a communiqué published on August twenty-fourth, two thousand twenty-six, the CNDH stated it is closely monitoring these events and urged all parties to show restraint and work towards peace.

The Commission encouraged affected communities to use legal and judicial channels to resolve disputes instead of resorting to violence. It also called on administrative, judicial, security, and traditional authorities to continue efforts to identify and prosecute those responsible for the crimes committed by all sides.

CNDH President Belngar Larmé Jacques signed the communiqué, which also extended condolences to the bereaved families and wished a speedy recovery to the injured. The institution emphasized the need to preserve peace, guarantee fundamental rights, and allow justice to establish accountability.

Why it mattersResidents of Oum-Hadjer are directly affected by the violence, which has caused deaths, injuries, and arrests, disrupting their safety and community stability.

Tech · 08/26/2026

Chad plans national forum on artificial intelligence

A national forum on Internet governance, themed "Artificial Intelligence for Chadian Development," will convene in N'Djamena on September twenty-third and twenty-fourth, 2026.

The organizing committee for the National Forum on Internet Governance in Chad announced the event on Tuesday, August twenty-fifth, 2026. Mahamat Alifa Abouna, president of the organizing committee, stated that the forum aims to gather digital sector stakeholders, including national and international experts, public institution representatives, private sector actors, civil society organizations, and young innovators.

Participants will discuss digital challenges, the evolution of Internet governance, and the impact of artificial intelligence on Chadian society and economy. The forum also plans training activities to enhance digital skills for citizens and professionals.

Organizers intend to foster reflection on responsible regulation and use of new technologies to contribute to sustainable, inclusive, and equitable digital development in Chad. The initiative seeks to create a framework for exchanges among digital actors and encourage technological solutions tailored to Chad's needs and challenges.

Why it mattersThe forum directly affects Chadian citizens and professionals by offering training and skill-building opportunities in digital technologies and artificial intelligence.

MENA FOOD CHAD unveils two new ZEINA food products

The Chadian agro-food company introduced ZEINA Tomato 70g and ZEINA Macaroni 500g at a commercial meeting in N'Djamena, aiming to expand its national market presence.

MENA FOOD CHAD, a Chadian agro-food company, launched two new products under its ZEINA brand on Monday, August twenty-fourth, 2026. The new offerings, ZEINA Tomato 70g and ZEINA Macaroni 500g, were presented at a commercial meeting held in a capital hotel. The company emphasized its commitment to local production to serve food sovereignty and the local economy.

The ZEINA Tomato 70g is a compact and economical concentrated tomato format, designed to meet the purchasing power and daily needs of households, particularly for single-unit purchases common in retail and informal markets. ZEINA Macaroni 500g is a family-sized pasta, presented as a locally manufactured alternative to imported products, focusing on quality, cooking texture, and price.

MENA FOOD CHAD, based in N'Djamena, specializes in consumer goods like pasta and tomato concentrate. The company plans to strengthen its distribution network in N'Djamena and secondary cities including Moundou, Sarh, Abéché, Kélo, Doba, Oum-Hadjer, Massakory, and Mongo. It also intends to support wholesalers and retailers with commercial tools adapted to the Chadian market.

Why it mattersChadian households will have access to new locally produced food options, potentially reducing reliance on imported goods and offering products tailored to their consumption habits and purchasing power.

Moov Money Tchad crowns Al-Afia champion of 2026 Lycéens Trophy

The Lycée Al-Afia from Abéché won the 2026 Lycéens Trophy, a competition supported by Moov Money Tchad that highlights student talent and teamwork.

The Lycée Al-Afia from Abéché secured the 2026 Lycéens Trophy on Saturday, August twenty-second, defeating Lycée Acyl Ahmat Akhabach from Ati in the final. This competition, which celebrates talent, effort, and teamwork among Chadian schools, concluded after several weeks of challenges and performances.

Moov Money Tchad, the official partner of the Lycéens Trophy, reaffirmed its commitment to youth through this edition. The company views supporting young people as an investment in future talent, encouraging innovation and contributing to the development of an ambitious and responsible generation.

The event served as a platform for sharing, creativity, and self-improvement, with students demonstrating maturity and mastery in their disciplines. Moov Money Tchad’s involvement highlights its goal to promote excellence, education, and initiatives that build confidence and development among Chadian youth.

Why it mattersThis event directly impacts Chadian youth by providing a platform to showcase their talents and receive recognition, fostering their development and confidence.
