Health · 2026-10-28

Moyen-Chari health officials meet to boost vaccination

Provincial health officials and UNICEF are holding a four-day workshop in Moyen-Chari to improve vaccination coverage and disease detection.

Provincial health officials in Chad's Moyen-Chari province are holding a strategic workshop from October 27 to 30, 2026, to improve vaccination campaign results and disease detection. The meeting, supported by UNICEF, brings together chief physicians from 10 health districts and traditional leaders to assess progress, identify challenges, and plan actions. Interim provincial health delegate Dr. Nehoulné Gaston highlighted the workshop's importance for child protection against preventable diseases.

The workshop aims to measure progress in vaccination and epidemiological surveillance between January and September 2026. Dr. Gaston noted efforts to reach remote populations but cited obstacles including unvaccinated children, access difficulties, vaccine stockouts, and poor health data collection. Moyen-Chari provincial secretary general Fidèle Kodé Ngolo urged participants to propose concrete solutions to enhance district health performance.

The recommendations from this workshop will be used to strengthen health interventions and improve access to vaccination services across the Moyen-Chari province. The provincial health delegation and UNICEF are working together to address the identified issues and boost overall health outcomes for the region's residents.

Why it mattersChildren in Moyen-Chari province are at risk of preventable diseases due to challenges in vaccination coverage.

Chad launches 18-month project for peace and tolerance

The CELIAF, with EU and ACRA support, aims to foster unity among women and youth in N'Djamena and Logone Occidental.

The Cellule de Liaison et d’Information des Associations Féminines (CELIAF) launched an 18-month project on October 27, 2026, in N’Djamena to promote peace and coexistence. The initiative, supported by the European Union and the NGO ACRA, targets women and youth in N’Djamena and the Logone Occidental region.

CELIAF president Denodji Robertine stated that peace requires daily construction through dialogue and rejecting prejudice, aiming to break down invisible barriers. Mariam Abdraman, representing the Delegate General of the Government of N’Djamena, emphasized that diversity should be a source of strength, not division, and highlighted the role of women and youth in conflict prevention.

The project includes creating inclusive dialogue spaces, training workshops, awareness campaigns, and cultural and sports events. This initiative aligns with President Mahamat Idriss Déby Itno's social cohesion policy and the national development plan 'Chad Connexion 2030'.

Why it mattersThe project aims to build peace and tolerance among communities in Chad, directly affecting women and youth.

Culture · 2026-10-28

Musik Mouv Tchad launches second phase for young artists

The project aims to build skills and promote social cohesion through music over six months.

The second phase of the Musik Mouv Tchad project began on October 27, 2026, aiming to enhance the abilities of young artists and foster unity through music.

Black Mind Musik launched the initiative, themed "Youth, creativity and social cohesion through music," which will involve rappers, singers, and slam poets in six months of training and creation workshops.

Participants will collectively write, compose, and produce works addressing themes like tolerance and solidarity, with artistic director Xavier Baoundoh stating that music can bridge divides and open discussions between communities.

Why it mattersYoung Chadian artists will have their skills strengthened and their creative output used to promote social cohesion.

Politics · 2026-10-28

Niger summons Chad ambassador over drone strike

Niger's interim foreign minister met Chad's ambassador following a deadly drone strike on October 24, 2026.

Niger summoned Chad's ambassador to Niamey on Tuesday, October 27, 2026, after a drone strike in the Agadez region killed two civilians and injured several others. The attack occurred on October 24, 2026, near Emiloulou, and Nigerien authorities allege it was launched from Chadian territory with foreign assistance.

Alio Daouda, Niger's Justice Minister acting as interim Foreign Minister, received the Chadian envoy. Niger's government condemned the strike as a grave violation of national sovereignty and international law. They plan to report the incident to the UN Security Council and the African Union's Peace and Security Council, demanding an independent international investigation.

Niamey seeks explanations for the incident while asserting its commitment to defending its fundamental interests and protecting civilians. The government reiterated its dedication to peace and good neighborly relations, emphasizing that national sovereignty and civilian protection are absolute imperatives.

Why it mattersNiger summoned Chad's ambassador following a deadly drone strike on October 24, 2026, which killed two civilians and injured several others.
