Chad Governor Demands End to Road Racket and Border Crime

Mayo-Kebbi Ouest provincial governor orders increased patrols and strict enforcement against illegal tolls and banditry.

Provincial Governor Abdelmanane Khatab has ordered security forces in Mayo-Kebbi Ouest to increase vigilance and proactively address threats to local stability. The directive came during an expanded security meeting in Pala, attended by provincial and departmental officials, as well as security chiefs. Khatab emphasized that no laxity would be tolerated, instructing commanders to bolster their presence on the ground and anticipate potential dangers.

Specific instructions were given to intensify border surveillance and dynamic patrols along the frontiers, targeting clandestine crossing points. These measures aim to combat cross-border banditry, illegal arms trafficking, and infiltration, while also better managing migration flows. The governor also encouraged cooperation with neighboring countries' security forces where necessary. Additionally, measures were outlined to manage the upcoming transhumance period, ensuring official corridors are secured and traditional authorities are involved to prevent conflicts between herders and farmers.

The governor also addressed the issue of road extortion, ordering an immediate end to the practice by certain agents and reminding security forces of their duty to protect citizens. Controls must now adhere strictly to the law, with any agent caught extorting road users facing immediate suspension and disciplinary action. The circulation of unregistered motorcycles was also identified as a security concern, prompting orders for large-scale control operations and vehicle seizures.

Why it mattersRoad users and residents in Mayo-Kebbi Ouest are directly affected by the governor's orders to stop illegal tolls and improve security.

Chad Education Ministry Drafts 2027 Budget

National education officials are meeting to develop a medium-term spending framework and draft budget for 2027, aligning with national development goals.

Chad's Ministry of National Education, Bilingualism, and Civic Promotion is holding a week-long workshop from September 3 to 9, 2026, to prepare the 2027-2029 Medium-Term Expenditure Framework (CDMT) and the draft 2027 budget. This initiative is part of the government's public finance reforms and the "Chad Connexion 2030" National Development Plan, aiming to guide the education sector's planning and budgeting.

The 2027 budget exercise is deemed particularly important, with national authorities emphasizing a results-oriented approach focused on accelerating development, transforming the economy, and improving living conditions. Ministries are tasked with submitting realistic, sustainable, and executable budget proposals that align with national priorities. Participants will analyze sector performance, identify priorities, assess intervention costs, and define indicators for expected results.

UNICEF Chad, a partner in the process, stressed the importance of prioritizing education in budget choices, viewing it as a key investment in human capital and the future of Chadian children. The organization encourages the formulation of realistic, equitable, and measurable budget proposals, particularly focusing on access, learning quality, teacher training, infrastructure, digitalization, and the schooling of vulnerable children, especially girls.

Why it mattersThe Ministry of Education is preparing a budget that will affect the allocation of funds for educational resources, infrastructure, and teacher training.

Saudi Arabia Updates Hajj 2027 Rules for Chadian Pilgrims

New regulations announced by the Saudi Ministry of Hajj aim to enhance pilgrim safety and comfort for the 2027 Hajj season.

The Organ for the Management of Pilgrimage (ORGEP) in Chad has announced new directives for the 2027 Hajj, issued by the Saudi Ministry of Hajj. These changes, communicated in a September 3 statement signed by ORGEP President General Idriss Dokony Adiker, are intended to improve the safety and living conditions for Chadian pilgrims undertaking the journey.

Key modifications include the full payment of the Hady fee through the Massar platform, the establishment of shuttle services connecting hotels to the Haram, and the introduction of a dedicated clinic for pilgrim health. These measures aim to streamline the pilgrimage process and provide better support for attendees.

Further enhancements focus on improving catering services in Mecca and Medina. The 'D' package for Machair has been eliminated, replaced by packages A, B, and C, which will have a cost difference of 600 riyals. These adjustments are designed to offer more structured and potentially cost-differentiated options for pilgrims.

Why it mattersChadian pilgrims are directly affected by new payment, transport, health, and catering regulations for the 2027 Hajj.

Kagame Urges Risk Sharing for Youth Entrepreneurship

Rwandan President Paul Kagame called for governments to absorb some financial risks to improve credit access for young entrepreneurs.

Rwandan President Paul Kagame has advocated for governments to share financial risks to make credit more accessible to young entrepreneurs. Speaking at the Africa Food Systems 2026 Forum, Kagame stated that Africa's financial systems currently favor those with existing capital, creating a barrier for many.

He proposed that public authorities act as intermediaries by taking on a portion of the financial risk. This state intervention, he argued, would facilitate access to loans for individuals currently excluded from the financial system. Kagame noted that governments supporting young borrowers through banks see a high return on investment.

Entrepreneurs who receive this support can generate income, improve their social standing, and contribute to national economic development, according to Kagame. He believes these mechanisms are achievable with political will and proper organization from authorities.

Why it mattersYoung entrepreneurs in Chad could benefit from easier access to credit if governments adopt risk-sharing mechanisms to support their ventures.
