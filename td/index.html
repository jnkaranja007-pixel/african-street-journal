Chad delegate mourns canton chief death

Ahmat Choua Rozi visited Mirekika village to offer condolences for Issa Hamid Douloum, who died November 4.

Ahmat Choua Rozi, the delegate general for Salamat province, traveled to Mirekika village on Saturday, November 7, 2026, to offer condolences. The village is located 37 km east of Am-Timan. He was accompanied by administrative officials and security forces to mourn the canton chief who died a few days prior.

The delegate expressed sadness, stating that the family, losing a respected notable, was not alone in their grief. He described Issa Hamid Douloum, born January 1, 1941, as a man of value who served his canton with seriousness, wisdom, and integrity throughout his life. Douloum, who led his community since 1961, died on Wednesday, November 4, 2026, after a short illness.

Issa Hamid Douloum leaves behind three wives and 26 children, with his son Tom Issa Hamid designated to succeed him according to his will. The family thanked the authorities for their presence and support during their mourning period.

Why it mattersThe death of a canton chief and the official visit to his family affects the local community and its leadership structure.

ACRA project mobilises Chad communities for child education

The ALAPAJ project is running a campaign in six Chad locations to promote schooling and protect girls.

The ALAPAJ project, led by ACRA and partners, has begun a large awareness campaign in six Chad locations. The initiative aims to promote children's education and the protection of girls.

The campaign is active in Goré Nord, Beboni, Bembaïtada, Bekondjo, Komé Ndolébé, and Bengamia. Its theme is "Investing in children's education and girls' protection is investing in the Nation's future," aiming to gather support for schooling and girls' rights.

Organisers want to raise awareness and encourage parents, community leaders, and local authorities to engage. This effort seeks to build a protective environment for children and combat harmful practices affecting girls, fostering a more inclusive society.

Why it mattersChildren in six Chad locations will benefit from increased focus on their education and the protection of girls.

Chad's president names interim CEN-SAD executive secretary

Aboubakar Assidick Tchoroma will lead the Sahel-Saharan community until a new leader is elected.

Aboubakar Assidick Tchoroma has been appointed interim executive secretary of the Community of Sahel-Saharan States (CEN-SAD). Chad's President Mahamat Idriss Deby Itno, who also chairs the institution, made the decision on November 9, 2026.

This interim appointment will last until a new executive secretary is elected at the 13th ordinary session of the CEN-SAD Heads of State and Government Conference. Tchoroma will assume his duties at the organization's headquarters in Tripoli, Libya.

Tchoroma previously served as diplomatic advisor to the Chadian presidency and held the position of Minister of National Education. The decision was signed in N'Djamena.

Why it mattersChadian Aboubakar Assidick Tchoroma has been appointed to lead an international organisation based in Libya.

Chad sultanate installs youth and women's bureaus

The Dar Ouaddaï abbasside Sultanate officially installed 23 members each into its new youth and women's secretariats on Sunday, November 8, 2026.

The Dar Ouaddaï abbasside Sultanate held an official ceremony on Sunday, November 8, 2026, to install members of its Youth and Women's Secretariats. Sultan Chérif Abdelhadi Mahadi presided over the event, which gathered administrative and traditional leaders to emphasize the importance of the Maba community's heritage and cultural identity.

The newly installed Women's Secretariat has 23 members and is led by Mme Djawahir Hissein Kittir, while the Youth Secretariat, also with 23 members, is headed by M. Abdoulaye Youssouf Bourma. Bourma stated that the Youth Secretariat is an integral institution of the Sultanate, focused on peace, social cohesion, and development in the Ouaddaï province.

Sultan Chérif Abdelhadi Mahadi highlighted the Sultanate's historical role and the need to preserve cultural identity and promote solidarity. The appointments aim to strengthen the Sultanate's institutional framework and encourage youth and women's engagement in peace, tradition, and community development within Ouaddaï.

Why it mattersYouth and women in the Ouaddaï province of Chad are affected by the establishment of new institutional bodies within the Sultanate aimed at their engagement in community development.

Politics · 2026-11-09

World Bank team inspects Abéché development sites

A delegation led by an urban development specialist visited bus station locations, a municipal slaughterhouse, and a school sports field.

A World Bank delegation inspected strategic sites in Abéché on Sunday, November 8, 2026, as part of a mid-term review for the RESI-Tchad project. The team examined locations for future urban developments, including two sites for new bus stations on the Simaral Djané and N'Djaména roads.

Led by urban development specialist Emilie Jourdan, the group included Abéché mayor Dr. Abdel-Mahmoud Adam Yaya and Ouaddaï provincial council president Mahamat Ali Rahama. They also visited the municipal slaughterhouse and the sports field at the Lycée national franco-arabe, which are also slated for improvements. Mayor Yaya provided details on the objectives and prospects for each site.

The mission will continue on Monday with inspections of other locations to assess the implementation and local impact of the RESI-Tchad project. The project aims to foster territorial development and resilience in Chad.

Why it mattersResidents of Abéché will see improvements to urban infrastructure including new bus stations, a slaughterhouse, and a sports field.
