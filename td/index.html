Chad raises 29.2 billion FCFA in regional bond market

Chad's Public Treasury secured 29.2 billion FCFA in public securities from the CEMAC market in August 2026, achieving 83.6% of its 35 billion FCFA target.

The Public Treasury of Chad successfully raised 29.2 billion FCFA on the public securities market of the Economic and Monetary Community of Central Africa (CEMAC) between August 5 and 19, 2026. This mobilization represents 83.6% of the 35 billion FCFA the country aimed to secure during this period.

These funds were acquired through three operations on the regional market. The capital raised by the Chadian government is intended to support its financial needs and public spending within the CEMAC zone.

The partial fulfillment of the target indicates a significant, though not complete, success in Chad's efforts to attract regional investment for its public finances. This activity reflects ongoing financial engagement within the CEMAC economic bloc.

Why it mattersThe Chadian government and its public services are directly affected by the funds raised, which will support national financial needs.

CNDH urges calm after intercommunal violence in Oum-Hadjer

Chad's National Commission for Human Rights (CNDH) called for restraint following violent clashes in Oum-Hadjer that resulted in deaths, injuries, and arrests.

The National Commission for Human Rights (CNDH) expressed deep concern over the situation in Oum-Hadjer, Batha province, where intercommunal violence led to fatalities, injuries, and multiple arrests. In a statement released on August twenty-fourth, two thousand twenty-six, the CNDH stated it is closely monitoring these tragic events.

The CNDH urged all parties to exercise restraint and work towards peace, encouraging affected communities to use legal and judicial channels to resolve disputes instead of violence. The commission also called on administrative, judicial, security, and traditional authorities to continue efforts to identify and prosecute those responsible for crimes committed.

CNDH President Belngar Larmé Jacques signed the statement, which also extended condolences to the families of victims and wished a swift recovery to the injured. The commission emphasized the need to preserve peace, guarantee fundamental rights, and allow justice to establish accountability for the events.

Why it mattersResidents of Oum-Hadjer are directly affected by the violence, which has caused deaths, injuries, and arrests, disrupting community safety and stability.

MENA FOOD CHAD unveils two new ZEINA food products

The N'Djamena-based company introduced ZEINA Tomato 70g and ZEINA Macaroni 500g, aiming to expand its market presence and reduce import reliance.

MENA FOOD CHAD, a Chadian agro-food company, launched two new products under its ZEINA brand: ZEINA Tomato 70g and ZEINA Macaroni 500g. The company presented these items at a commercial meeting in the capital on Monday, August twenty-fourth, two thousand twenty-six. This move is part of the company's strategy to develop the ZEINA brand nationally and promote local production.

The new ZEINA Tomato 70g is a compact, economical tomato concentrate format designed for daily household needs and single purchases in retail and informal markets. ZEINA Macaroni 500g is a family-sized pasta, locally manufactured as an alternative to imported products, focusing on quality, cooking texture, and price.

MENA FOOD CHAD plans to strengthen its distribution network in N'Djamena and secondary cities like Moundou, Sarh, and Abéché. The company also intends to support wholesalers and retailers with commercial tools adapted to the Chadian market, aiming to position ZEINA as a credible local alternative to imported goods.

Why it mattersChadian households will see new locally produced food options, potentially affecting their purchasing choices and access to affordable staples.

Chad plans national forum on artificial intelligence

The organizing committee for the National Internet Governance Forum in Chad announced that the event will take place on September twenty-third and twenty-fourth in N'Djamena.

Chad is preparing to host a national forum focused on artificial intelligence (AI) and Internet governance. The event, scheduled for September twenty-third and twenty-fourth, 2026, in N'Djamena, aims to bring together various stakeholders from the digital sector, including national and international experts, public institution representatives, private sector actors, civil society organizations, and young innovators.

Mahamat Alifa Abouna, president of the organizing committee, announced the forum, which will operate under the theme "Artificial Intelligence for Chadian Development." Participants will discuss digital challenges, the evolution of Internet governance, and AI's impact on Chadian society and economy.

The forum will also offer training and digital skills development activities for citizens and professionals. Organizers intend to foster discussions on the regulation and responsible use of new technologies to contribute to sustainable, inclusive, and equitable digital development in Chad, creating a framework for exchanges and encouraging technological solutions tailored to the country's needs.

Why it mattersThis event affects Chadian citizens and professionals by offering training and discussions on digital skills and the responsible use of new technologies.

National Microfinance Committee reviews financial inclusion challenges

The National Microfinance Committee met to address structural barriers limiting access to formal financial services and coordinate public interventions.

The National Microfinance Committee convened on Monday, August twenty-fourth, two thousand twenty-six, at the Ministry of Finance and Budget conference room to discuss challenges in the microfinance sector. The meeting, chaired by Yannis Stéphane Koyangbanda, focused on improving financial inclusion as a key economic driver.

Attendees, including Valentin Mayere Yolonguere, Director of Financial, Monetary, Insurance, and Microfinance Affairs, explored ways to create a more resilient ecosystem. The committee aims to support small and medium-sized enterprises, entrepreneurship, and savings mobilization through microfinance.

The Ministry emphasized the importance of governance and regulation, highlighting that stronger dialogue between public authorities and financial institutions is essential for sustainable growth. These efforts seek to equip the country with effective institutional and operational tools, positioning microfinance at the center of economic transformation and income generation in the Central African Republic.

Why it mattersThe Central African Republic's economic actors, especially small and medium-sized enterprises and entrepreneurs, are affected by efforts to improve access to financial services and support income generation.
