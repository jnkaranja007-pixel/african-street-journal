Agriculture · 2026-11-15

Chad officials urge herders to respect farming calendar

Loug-Chari authorities conduct field missions to prevent farmer-herder conflicts by promoting dialogue and awareness.

Authorities in Chad's Loug-Chari department are intensifying awareness campaigns to prevent conflicts between farmers and herders as the agricultural season ends. The departmental secretary-general, Sougouma Hassaballah Ahmat, led a field mission this November to engage nomadic herders and ensure respect for the official timeline, which prohibits early herd arrivals until December 15, 2026.

Accompanied by local officials and traditional leaders, Ahmat visited herder camps in Boudamassa and Moualé-Kousiwahit. The mission aimed to sensitize herders to administrative rules, explaining that a rainfall deficit delayed harvests, necessitating that livestock remain away from cultivated areas. This measure seeks to allow farmers to complete their harvests without damage and avoid tensions.

The delegation stressed three key points: strict adherence to the December 15, 2026, date for herd movement into grazing areas, prevention of damage to crops which often sparks disputes, and strengthening social cohesion between the complementary farming and herding communities.

Why it mattersHerders in Loug-Chari must wait until December 15, 2026, to move livestock into farming areas to prevent crop damage and community disputes.

Politics · 2026-11-15

Déby orders action on Am-Timan hotel and market

The president inspected three public buildings in Am-Timan, the capital of Salamat province, during a regional tour on Saturday.

President Mahamat Idriss Déby Itno made a field visit to Am-Timan, in Salamat province, on his tour of the regions, to inspect three key public infrastructures. First was the Hôtel Moderne, which received more than 3 billion CFA francs from the public treasury but now looks like a worn, neglected old building, marked by soot and general abandonment.

At the Hôtel Moderne the president expressed his displeasure and ordered immediate measures to restore the site to its original purpose. He then visited the provincial hospital in Am-Timan, where he praised the medical staff and the cleanliness of the premises, which he called exemplary for the upkeep of a public building. The visit ended at the new modern market, which is finished, but traders have so far refused to move in.

The president instructed the mayor and the government's general delegate to install the traders and to ensure the market is maintained and kept clean. The report does not give a date for the move.

Why it mattersTraders who have refused to move into the finished Am-Timan market are now under orders from the mayor and the government's general delegate to occupy it, and the Hôtel Moderne must be restored after its 3 billion CFA franc investment.

Health · 2026-11-15

Culturama offers free diabetes screening in N'Djamena

The cultural association Culturama organized free diabetes testing at the Idriss Mahamat Ouya stadium on World Diabetes Day.

The cultural association Culturama hosted a free diabetes screening event on November 14 at the Idriss Mahamat Ouya stadium in N'Djamena. The initiative, held on World Diabetes Day, was a collaboration with the Ministry of Public Health and Prevention, supported by a Chinese medical team and the Maison du diabète.

This event aimed to raise public awareness about diabetes risks and promote early detection, allowing participants to check their health status and receive guidance for appropriate medical care. Culturama president Claudine Alingué stated the project, named 'Assapa Village,' was inspired by her mother's personal experience with diabetes and her advocacy for screening and sports.

The screening offered participants a chance to understand their health and receive necessary referrals. The partnership with the Ministry of Public Health and Prevention ensured access to medical expertise for those needing further treatment.

Why it mattersResidents in N'Djamena can get free diabetes screenings and health guidance at the Idriss Mahamat Ouya stadium.

Business · 2026-11-15

Al Damane real estate agency opens in N'Djamena

The agency says it will handle property management, land registry paperwork and contract-based transactions for residents and investors.

The Al Damane real estate agency officially began operations on Saturday, 14 November 2026, at an opening ceremony in N'Djamena. The president of its organising committee, Mahamat Moussa, said the firm was created to offer residents, businesses and investors close-to-home property services based on professionalism, transparency and trust.

The secretary-general, Ali Abba, said the agency wants to ease transactions, secure operations and provide tailored support. It will manage properties, help clients with cadastre services and contribute to land security, a sensitive issue in the capital where land and property matters are central to daily life.

The agency also plans to build a network of partners, improve its communication and strictly honour its contractual commitments. The ceremony was also a forum for professionals, partners and investors active in the sector.

Why it mattersBuyers, sellers and tenants in N'Djamena now have a local firm to handle land registry paperwork and property transactions.

Culture · 2026-11-15

Ramzy-14K aims to elevate Chadian music

The N'Djaména-based artist blends Afrobeat and Amapiano to create urban music for youth.

Ramzy-14K, a young artist from N'Djaména, is working to establish his musical identity and promote Chadian music internationally. He performs urban music, drawing inspiration from Afrobeat and Amapiano to create a sound with catchy rhythms and melodies.

The artist focuses on his energetic stage presence and ability to connect with audiences through his style and flow. He aims to deliver accessible music that is danceable while conveying his generation's outlook.

Ramzy-14K has performed at major cultural events in Chad, including the Festival Dary on May 4, 2024, April 9, 2025, and December 30, and the Festival Haguina on May 27, 2026. His motto, "The street, my pen, my future!", reflects his ambition to build a significant musical career.

Why it mattersRamzy-14K's ambition to promote Chadian music internationally affects the nation's cultural output.
