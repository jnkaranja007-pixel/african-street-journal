Chad officials review local investment priorities

Moundou hosts annual meeting to improve public investment planning and territorial development.

Officials in Moundou, Chad, met from November 4 to 6, 2026, to review the Annual Investment Programme (PAI). The meeting, organised by the ministries of Finance and Territorial Administration, aimed to strengthen territorial planning and boost public investment efficiency for local populations.

The review focused on improving public investment planning and programming, with attention to local development needs. Yahya Mahamadou Bellout, Director of Cabinet for the Logone Occidental Governorate, stated that PAI's effectiveness depends on accurate needs identification, resource availability, realistic objectives, and rigorous project monitoring.

The meeting also addressed the challenge of translating plans into concrete actions. Solkam Bachir Soucat, head of the mission, highlighted that PAI instruments help align local priorities with the national development plan and Sustainable Development Goals, reinforcing accountability of autonomous collectivités and improving citizen input in public decisions.

N’Djamena hosts Mouroum cultural festival

The Festival of Arts and Culture of the Mouroum Space will run from November 18 to 22 in N’Djamena.

N’Djamena will host the Festival of Arts and Culture of the Mouroum Space (FACEM) from November 18 to 22. The event, held at the Talino Manu space, aims to promote cultural heritage, identity, and traditions.

An organizing committee officially launched communication for the festival on Thursday, November 5, 2026. They stated the festival is intended as a gathering place to value cultural heritage and pass on traditional values to younger generations.

The festival's theme is "Valorisation de notre patrimoine culturel, de notre identité et de nos traditions." It is not meant to be just a festive event but a framework for transmitting values.

Why it mattersChadians in N'Djamena will participate in a festival celebrating their cultural heritage and traditions.

Algerian minister visits Chad to boost ties

Ahmed Attaf met with Chadian officials to advance security and economic cooperation between the two nations.

Algerian Foreign Minister Ahmed Attaf visited Chad's capital, N'Djamena, on Tuesday, November 3, to continue discussions from recent months. While no agreements were formally signed, the meeting emphasized the importance of ongoing security and economic cooperation between Algiers and N'Djamena.

The visit aimed to strengthen diplomatic relations and explore avenues for collaboration. Both nations acknowledged the need for continued partnership in critical areas, building on previous engagements.

Politics · 2026-11-06

Chad mediator calls for end to Tibesti fighting

The Republic's Mediator urges belligerents in Miski to cease hostilities and resume dialogue by November 7, 2026.

The Republic's Mediator expressed deep concern over ongoing clashes in Miski, Tibesti region. The institution warned on November 5, 2026, in N'Djaména, that the violence could harm peace efforts.

Mediator Ambassador Saléh Kebzabo appealed to all parties for an immediate halt to fighting starting November 7, 2026. He stated that armed conflict is not a lasting solution and damages trust.

The 2025 Bardaï agreement is recalled as a key achievement that must remain a priority. The Mediator's office is available to support any initiative for a peaceful resolution.

Why it mattersOngoing violence in Miski, Tibesti region, threatens peace efforts and human security in Chad.
