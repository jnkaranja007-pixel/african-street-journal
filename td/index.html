Chad security forces receive praise and equipment

Police officers in N'Djamena were recognized for arrests and dismantling a criminal network.

Chad's security forces were honored on Monday, November 2, 2026, in N'Djamena for recent successes in combating crime. Director of Public Security, Hachim Haroun Déby, specifically praised three police officers for arresting two wanted criminals in the capital. A local company donated three vehicles, 20 motorcycles, and 50 gas cylinders to support police operations.

Further recognition came after the dismantling of a cross-border criminal network. Agents from Public Security Commissariat No. 16 in Gassi received congratulatory letters from the Minister of Public Security and Immigration for their professionalism. The Director General of the National Police, accompanied by the first deputy prosecutor and a local deputy, visited the Gassi station to acknowledge these efforts.

The Director of Public Security also thanked six officials at Public Security Commissariat No. 8 for their daily commitment. These acknowledgments highlight the attention authorities and the public are giving to security efforts in the capital.

Why it mattersSecurity forces in N'Djamena were praised and received equipment for their work in arresting criminals and dismantling a network.

Chad trains 34 agents for reproductive health data

NGO OASIS-Tchad completed a two-week training for 34 agents in Moundou to collect family planning data.

34 agents, including 24 female data collectors and 10 team leaders, have been trained to gather reproductive health information in Moundou. The training, organized by NGO OASIS-Tchad under the Family Planning Impact Lab project, concluded on November 4, 2026, after two weeks of capacity building. The agents will now collect data on family planning and reproductive health across Chad.

The closing ceremony in Moundou was led by Dr. Djimadoum Dionadji, the chief medical officer for the Moundou health district. He was joined by Dougssa Rita, the country representative for OASIS-Tchad, and reproductive health focal points from the Logone occidental and Mandoul health delegations. This initiative aims to ensure the collection of reliable data for reproductive health services.

The training began on October 19, 2026, and focused on preparing the selected agents for their fieldwork. The collected data will support family planning efforts in the country.

AfrotroniX named finalist for Quebec arts award

Chadian musician Caleb Rimtobaye is competing for the Prix Charles-Biddle 2026 for immigrant contributions to Quebec culture.

Chadian musician Caleb Rimtobaye, known as AfrotroniX, is a finalist for the 2026 Prix Charles-Biddle in Quebec. This award recognizes the cultural and artistic contributions of immigrants to the province.

Rimtobaye, a musician, composer, and cultural entrepreneur, is one of three finalists in the national/international category. He is competing against illustrator Gérard Dubois and artistic director Victor Quijada.

The award ceremony will take place on November 6, 2026, during the Quebec Week of Intercultural Encounters.

Why it mattersChadian artist Caleb Rimtobaye has been recognized for his contributions to Quebec's cultural and artistic landscape.

Politics · 2026-11-05

Chadian president meets Algerian minister

Mahamat Idriss Deby Itno and Ahmed Attaf discussed cooperation and regional security in N'Djamena.

Chadian President Mahamat Idriss Deby Itno met Algerian Foreign Minister Ahmed Attaf in N'Djamena on Tuesday. The leaders discussed enhancing bilateral cooperation and implementing agreements from Deby's visit to Algiers last year.

Attaf stated that Chad and Algeria share common goals for development and security in their Sahel-Saharan region. Algeria recently deployed troops to Niger, a country bordering Chad, as it seeks to increase its influence in the Sahel.

Mali's army leader General Assimi Goita is also expected to visit Algeria soon, following recent exchanges between the prime ministers of Algeria and Mali.

Why it mattersThe meeting between the Chadian president and the Algerian foreign minister concerns Chad's bilateral relations and regional security.
