Chad election agency names six legislative candidates

The National Election Management Agency announced the provisional list for the Assoungha partial election on November 9, 2026.

Chad's National Election Management Agency (ANGE) revealed a provisional list of six candidates for a partial legislative election in Assoungha. ANGE President Ahmed Bartchiret announced the names on Monday, November 9, 2026, in N'Djaména. The election follows the death of the previous representative, Hassan Moussa Yacoub.

Six political parties submitted applications after a call for candidates on October 29. A technical commission reviewed the submissions against electoral law criteria. The provisional candidates are Adam Abdou Bichara (MUR), Ali Kidjiber Mougadam (MPS), Mahamat Ahmat Mahamat (RNDT Le Réveil), Grenne Mahamat Saleh (CONAPO), Tidjani Markhous Adoum (PLD), and Aboubakar Mahamat Ali (UNDR).

The provisional list will go to the Constitutional Council for final approval. The election campaign will run for 21 days, from November 21 to December 11, 2026. Only voters in the Assoungha constituency will participate in the December 13, 2026 election.

Why it mattersSix political parties and their candidates are directly involved in the upcoming partial legislative election in Assoungha.

Moov Africa Tchad to help 300 youths find jobs

The telecommunications company will collect CVs and select candidates for professional integration in N'Djamena.

Moov Africa Tchad announced it will support the professional integration of 300 young people in N'Djamena. This initiative was revealed during the second National Employment Forum (FNE) held from November 10 to 12, 2026.

The company's director general, Mourad Hadraoui, stated that Moov Africa Tchad will collect CVs and select candidates in collaboration with the Forum organizers and the National Office for Employment Promotion (ONAPE). Hadraoui also discussed employment prospects linked to Chad's digital development.

This commitment aims to strengthen Moov Africa Tchad's role in youth employment. The company also oversees Moov Money Tchad as part of its operations.

Why it matters300 young people in N'Djamena will receive support for professional integration through Moov Africa Tchad's initiative.

Agriculture · 2026-11-10

Chad government backs off-season crops amid drought

Minister Keda Ballah announced support for producers facing a disrupted agricultural season due to irregular rains.

Chad's government is supporting farmers with seeds, inputs, and solar boreholes to counter a disrupted agricultural season. Minister of Agricultural Production and Industrialization Keda Ballah presented these measures to the National Assembly on Monday, November 9, 2026. The initiative aims to reduce the country's reliance on rainfall for farming.

Ballah described the 2026-2027 agricultural campaign as challenging for Chadian producers due to late planting, inconsistent rain, and dry spells during critical growth periods. He attributed the poor distribution of rainfall, which affected pastures and food crops, to climate change. A multi-ministerial mission, including international partners, surveyed 11 affected provinces to assess the impact.

Following the assessment, short and medium-term actions will be implemented by relevant government departments. Ballah emphasized concrete, immediately available support over projections when addressing deputies' questions about aid. The government is focusing on securing necessary supplies and stocks for producers.

Why it mattersChadian farmers face crop losses and reduced pasture due to climate change-induced erratic rainfall, impacting their livelihoods.

Sport · 2026-11-10

Koutoukagbe wins Bébédjia youth football tournament

Koutoukagbe defeated Dangdin 4-1 on penalties after a 0-0 draw in the Bébédjia youth football tournament final.

The Bébédjia youth football tournament concluded with Koutoukagbe emerging victorious over Dangdin in a final match that went to penalties. 16 teams competed in the tournament, which saw Koutoukagbe and Dangdin reach the final after intense competition. The match ended 0-0 after regular time, with strong defenses and goalkeeping preventing any goals.

Koutoukagbe secured the trophy by winning the penalty shootout 4 goals to 1. Mbaigolmem Arnaud, president of the organizing committee, stated the competition aimed to foster solidarity and tolerance among youth. The departmental football league president, Nadjimbaye Doumsangar, and youth advisor Alladoumbeye Rodrigue also praised the initiative.

The canton chief of Bébédjia, Mara Emmanuel, announced his upcoming enthronement on December 5, 2026. He encouraged support for rural teams and congratulated the finalists. The event concluded with the awarding of prizes to the participating teams.

Why it mattersYoung footballers in the Bébédjia canton of Chad participated in a tournament aimed at building solidarity.
