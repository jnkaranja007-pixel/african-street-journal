Chad football federation faces governance questions

20 months after promising order, the Chadian Football Federation's leadership is criticized for inconsistent application of rules and player disputes.

The Chadian Football Federation's governance is under scrutiny, 20 months after its president, Tahir Oloy Hassan, promised an end to disorder. His program included installing confidence, auditing the federation, and supporting provincial leagues, but critics point to unresolved issues. For example, the national team's selection and player disputes have led to controversy, including five players being dropped in June 2026 for alleged betrayal, only to be reinstated 10 days later after mediation by the Ministry of Sports.

Further governance concerns have arisen with recent league decisions. On October 24, 2026, Foullah Édifice, the N'Djamena champion, was removed from play-off contention by its league for appealing directly to the Court of Arbitration for Sport, a move permitted by federation statutes. Additionally, a November 10 match was halted due to a penalty and pitch invasion, yet the league's subsequent decision to award a 3-0 loss to Mont-Guéra, rather than a replay, has been questioned by the club.

Federation president Oloy Hassan also heads FC Yves, which benefited from the controversial decision in the match against Mont-Guéra. Provincial league presidents, promised 10 million CFA francs annually, report receiving only five million. The federation's president also issued a June 28 note requiring prior written agreement from league presidents for any gala tournament or match.

Why it mattersFootball clubs and provincial leagues face inconsistent rulings and financial shortfalls, impacting the sport's development.

Vehicle crashes into home in N'Djaména, killing one

A car left the road in the capital's Kallatchou district on Sunday, hitting a house and leaving one dead and two seriously injured.

A vehicle left the road in the Kallatchou district of N'Djaména's 10th arrondissement on Sunday, November 15, 2026, and came to rest inside a residence. The provisional toll is one dead and two seriously injured.

According to early information, the driver lost control before leaving the carriageway and struck a house violently, causing heavy damage to the property. The two seriously injured were taken to hospital urgently.

The exact circumstances of the crash are still to be established, and no official statement has yet given its cause or named the victims.

Why it mattersThe family of the person killed and the two injured people's relatives now face hospital costs and a loss of income while the cause is established.

President Déby ends security tour in Chad

Mahamat Idriss Déby Itno returned to N'Djamena after visiting six provinces.

President Mahamat Idriss Déby Itno returned to N'Djamena on Sunday, November 15, 2026, concluding a security tour across six Chadian provinces. The tour covered Ennedi-Est, Ennedi-Ouest, Ouaddaï, Salamat, Batha, and Hadjer-Lamis. The president's arrival at Hassan Djamous International Airport was met by Prime Minister Allah-Maye Halina.

High-ranking civil and military officials also greeted the head of state on the tarmac. Among them were the Chief of Staff of the Armed Forces, General Abakar Abdelkerim Daoud, the Minister of State and Secretary-General of the Presidency, and the Minister Director of the Civil Cabinet. Their presence underscored the importance of the president's security-focused itinerary.

The president's extensive tour aimed to assess and bolster security measures across these key regions. The visit involved direct engagement with local authorities and military personnel to address ongoing security challenges within the country.

Why it mattersPresident Déby's return to N'Djamena marks the end of a security tour across six provinces, with high-ranking officials present.

New bishop shares pastoral vision for Sarh diocese

Monseigneur Jean Miguina aims for an engaged Church focused on education, poverty, and peace.

Monseigneur Jean Miguina, the new bishop of Sarh, met local journalists on Saturday, November 14, 2026, to outline his pastoral vision. He expressed a desire to build a Church closer to its faithful and more involved in community life, aiming to consolidate unity and encourage participation.

The bishop's pastoral plan prioritizes education, Christian formation, social communication, poverty reduction, and supporting vocations. He stated the Church's mission extends beyond worship to actively seeking solutions for population difficulties, emphasizing education, peace, and solidarity.

Miguina also highlighted the importance of involving youth and fostering collaboration between generations. Addressing farmer-herder conflicts, he urged dialogue and mutual understanding, calling for intensified awareness campaigns to prevent clashes and ensure peaceful coexistence.

Why it mattersThe new bishop of Sarh has prioritized education, peace, and solidarity, impacting the community's social and spiritual well-being.

Culture · 2026-11-16

Chadian Quran memorizer advances to semi-finals in Qatar

Al-Goni Mahamat Adam Mahamat is among 42 contestants from 19 countries still competing in the third "Awwal Al-Awaïl" international Quran contest.

Al-Goni Mahamat Adam Mahamat, a Chadian candidate, has advanced to the semi-finals of the third "Awwal Al-Awaïl" international Quranic competition held in Qatar. The result gives Chadian Quran memorizers a fresh chance to be seen on the world stage.

He won first place last year at the King Abdelaziz international competition in Saudi Arabia, which tests memorization of the Quran according to the 10 authentic readings. Every contestant in this year's field of 42 has already taken a top place in an earlier international Quran contest.

The Qatari Ministry of Awqaf and Islamic Affairs runs the contest to encourage memorization and recitation of the Quran. Judges rely on a patented electronic scoring system that the organisers say ensures precise and transparent assessments.

Why it mattersChadian memorizers now have a rare international showcase, with the semi-final stage narrowing a field of 42 contestants from 19 countries.
