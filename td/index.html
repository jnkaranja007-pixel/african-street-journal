Health · 2026-10-24

Chad parliament launches breast and cervical cancer screening

Lawmakers commit to improving healthcare access and funding for women's health programs.

Chad's National Assembly hosted a "Pink October" awareness day on October 23, 2026, focusing on fighting breast and cervical cancers. Dr. Mahamat Abakar Kori, the 4th vice-president, spoke at the event, which included voluntary screening sessions. The assembly pledged to strengthen healthcare services, improve financial access to treatment, and support prevention programs nationwide.

Dr. Kori highlighted that many Chadian women suffer in silence, often seeking medical help at advanced stages of cancer due to societal taboos, lack of information, and limited access to early detection, especially in rural areas. He emphasized that screening is an act of courage and responsibility, saving lives and preventing family tragedies.

The National Assembly committed to equipping health facilities with modern diagnostic tools, exploring universal health coverage mechanisms, enhancing medical staff training, and ensuring sustained funding for health initiatives. This multisectoral effort calls for the involvement of traditional and religious leaders to combat stigma surrounding women's cancers.

Why it mattersChadian women are directly affected by limited access to cancer screening and treatment.

two men tried for posing as generals in Chad

Chad police officer and another man face charges of impersonating military officers and fraud in N'Djamena.

Two men, aged 36 and 45, appeared in Chad's Tribunal de grande instance in N'Djamena on October 22, 2026, accused of impersonating army generals. The charges include title usurpation, illegal possession of uniforms and weapons, and fraud. The men allegedly used military attire and firearms to defraud citizens.

The 45-year-old, a police officer, claimed he was a "collateral victim" and only associated with his co-accused. Both defendants pleaded not guilty to all charges. The court postponed the case to October 29, 2026, due to the absence of the civil party, and ordered the two men to remain in provisional detention at Klessoum prison.

The trial's next hearing is scheduled for October 29, 2026. The accused men are currently held in provisional detention. The charges stem from their alleged use of military uniforms and weapons to commit fraud.

Cultural figure Mortordé Djimrangar Clément buried in N'Djaména

The journalist and cultural animator died on October 15, 2026, after an illness.

Mortordé Djimrangar Clément, a cultural animator, journalist, and communicator, was buried on Friday, October 23, 2026, at the Toukra cemetery in N’Djaména. He passed away on October 15, 2026, following an illness.

Born in 1985, Clément was remembered for his engagement in cultural animation, journalism, and communication. A cultural evening was held on October 22, 2026, to pay final respects before his burial.

The artistic vigil served as a tribute to Clément's contributions before his interment. His passing leaves behind memories of his work in multiple fields.

Education · 2026-10-24

APET launches activities to boost Chad's education system

The non-profit association aims to improve access to quality education for all Chadian children and youth.

The Association Agir pour l'Éducation au Tchad (APET) officially launched its activities in N'Djamena on Friday, October 23, 2026. The non-profit, apolitical, and non-confessional organization aims to transform interest in education into concrete contributions for the country's development.

APET's president, Allayanmal Kladegue, outlined the association's vision to ensure every Chadian child and young person has access to quality education, covering formal schooling, vocational training, literacy, and intergenerational learning. The organization's work will focus on six priority areas, including promoting access for vulnerable groups and girls, supporting local initiatives, and strengthening the capacities of educators.

The association plans to build a network of skills, forge institutional partnerships, and implement pilot actions like tutoring and mentoring. APET also announced the upcoming creation of a Citizenship and Education Laboratory and has a platform for public membership to support its mission.

Why it mattersThe Association Agir pour l'Éducation au Tchad (APET) launched activities to improve educational access and quality for Chadian children and youth.
