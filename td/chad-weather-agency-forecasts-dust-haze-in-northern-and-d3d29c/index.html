Chad weather agency forecasts dust haze in northern and central provinces

The 20 November bulletin also forecasts maximum temperatures of 26°C to 39°C, with Sarh expected to reach the highest reading.

Source: Tchadinfos · 2026-11-20 · 1 min read

Chad’s National Meteorological Agency (ANAM) forecast dust haze in several northern and central provinces during the night of 19-20 November and on 20 November 2026. Its daily bulletin, published on 19 November, also expects cool nights and mornings across nearly all the country, with persistent daytime heat.

Maximum temperatures are forecast to range from 26°C to 39°C. N’Djamena and Abéché are expected to reach 36°C, while Mongo, Massenya, Am-Timan, Bongor and Laï are forecast at 38°C; Sarh is expected to reach 39°C. Amdjarass, Bardaï and Faya are forecast at 26°C, 27°C and 31°C, respectively.

ANAM warned that the temperature shifts carry risks of climate-sensitive illnesses. It advised people to drink water regularly, avoid intense exercise during the hottest hours and protect children and older people from the nighttime chill.

Why it mattersResidents in the forecast areas must take precautions against dust and temperature-related illness, with the agency urging water intake and protection from nighttime cold.
