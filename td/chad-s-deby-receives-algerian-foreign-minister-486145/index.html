Chad's Déby receives Algerian foreign minister

Mahamat Idriss Déby Itno and Ahmed Attaf discussed bilateral cooperation and Sahel security in N'Djamena.

Source: Alwihda Info · 2026-11-04 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Chad's leader, Marshal Mahamat Idriss Déby Itno, met with Algeria's Foreign Affairs Minister Ahmed Attaf in N'Djamena on March 11, 2026. Attaf delivered a written message from Algerian President Abdelmadjid Tebboune to Déby Itno. The meeting took place at the Toumaï palace.

Discussions focused on strengthening bilateral relations and cooperation between Chad and Algeria. The two officials also addressed security and political challenges within the Sahel-Saharan region.

Separately, Israeli Foreign Affairs Minister Gideon Sa’ar invited Chadian counterpart Ahmat Kogri to visit Israel to enhance bilateral relations. Sa’ar announced this invitation on social media platform X after their discussion.
