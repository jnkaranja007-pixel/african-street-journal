Chad allocates CFA420 billion and 90 billion in external funds to roads

The 2027 budget sets total investment at 1 458,132 milliards de FCFA, up 24%, with roads taking more than 53% of domestic investment resources.

Source: Alwihda Info · 2026-11-25 · 1 min read

Chad’s parliament approved a 2027 budget on Tuesday that allocates 420 milliards de FCFA from domestic resources and 90 milliards of external financing exclusively to road infrastructure. Total investment spending is set at 1 458,132 milliards de FCFA, a 24% increase.

The parliamentary report says the road programme is intended to link production areas with markets and improve movement of people and goods. Investment financed from the state’s own resources totals 795,5 milliards de FCFA, compared with 618 milliards in 2026; externally financed investment is 662,6 milliards.

The government’s plan, Tchad Connexion 2030, puts infrastructure at the centre of development. Technical and financial partners will scrutinise whether local companies and the administration can execute the investment budget within one calendar year.

Why it mattersLocal companies and the administration are expected to execute 1 458,132 milliards de FCFA in investment spending within one calendar year.
