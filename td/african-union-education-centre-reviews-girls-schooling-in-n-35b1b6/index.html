African Union education centre reviews girls’ schooling in N’Djamena

The delegation examined school re-entry measures, programmes for girls out of school and the results of the #AfricaEducatesHer campaign during a 11-13 November mission.

Source: Alwihda Info · 2026-11-18 · 1 min read

A delegation from the African Union International Centre for the Education of Girls and Women in Africa completed an evaluation mission in N’Djamena, Chad, from 11 to 13 November 2026. It reviewed the situation of girls’ education and the implementation of the continental #AfricaEducatesHer campaign.

Led by programme officer Boubakar Yougbare, the team met Chad’s education officials and visited schools, a girls’ vocational training centre and a centre for survivors of gender-based violence. The delegation also examined school re-entry arrangements and programmes for girls who are not enrolled, while authorities outlined ongoing initiatives and progress.

The mission included discussions with international organisations and civil-society groups. The centre said the visit was supported by the World Bank through the East Africa Girls’ Empowerment and Resilience project; the delegation also identified mothers’ education associations, hygiene kits and listening centres among practices in use.

Why it mattersChadian education officials and schools were reviewed on measures to return girls to school and serve those who are not enrolled during the 11-13 November mission.
