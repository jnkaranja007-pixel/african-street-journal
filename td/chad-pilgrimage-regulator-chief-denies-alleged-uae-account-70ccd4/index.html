Chad pilgrimage regulator chief denies alleged UAE account transfers

A judicial document cites transfers of 20 million and 16.5 million dirhams from the regulator’s accounts to a personal account at First Abu Dhabi Bank.

Source: Tchadinfos · 2026-11-28 · 1 min read

Idriss Dokony Adiker, president of Chad’s pilgrimage regulator, denied on Friday that he made, ordered or benefited from two alleged transfers to a personal account in the United Arab Emirates. A judicial document dated 23 October cites transfers of 20 million and 16.5 million dirhams from the regulator’s accounts.

In a statement released on 27 November, Adiker said the allegations appeared in a prosecutor’s request issued to carry out an order by the Supreme Court president. He said the regulator’s Saudi account is used for electronic payment of Hajj services under Saudi authorities’ control, and that the institution’s 2025 financial balances are documented in its accounts and audit report.

Adiker said he had received no official notice or request for an explanation from the government or his superiors. He said he was prepared to provide supporting documents to judicial authorities and reserved the right to take legal action.

Why it mattersAdiker says he is ready to provide supporting documents to judicial authorities, while he reports receiving no official request for an explanation.
