Chad's teachers' union leader vows to press labour campaign after release

Djimoudouel Faustin, president of the Independent Teachers' Union of Chad (SIET), says he was arrested while campaigning for teachers' living and working conditions.

Source: Tchadinfos · 2026-11-20 · 1 min read

Djimoudouel Faustin, president of the Independent Teachers' Union of Chad (SIET), was released on Monday 9 November 2026 after several months held at Klessoum prison. In a statement on social media, he said he would continue the union's campaign.

Faustin said he was arrested on 6 January and that his salary was suspended the next day, 7 January. He said he and fellow union members were running a peaceful campaign for better living and working conditions for Chadian teachers.

Faustin also described his conviction and the difficult conditions of his detention. His statement did not specify a next step for the campaign.

Why it mattersFaustin's salary was suspended the day after his arrest, leaving him without pay after months in detention.
