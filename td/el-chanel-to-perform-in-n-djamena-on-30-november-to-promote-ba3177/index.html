El Chanel to perform in N'Djamena on 30 November to promote « Nagada »

The Cameroonian singer's 10-track album, released on 31 October, deals with love, social realities and gratitude to God.

Source: Tchadinfos · 2026-11-27 · 1 min read

Singer El Chanel was scheduled to perform on 30 November 2026 at Hotel Persévérance in Chagoua, N'Djamena, to promote her new album, « Nagada ». The album was due to be released on 31 October.

The 10-track album addresses love, social realities and gratitude to God, according to Tchadinfos. El Chanel, whose real name is Frida ASDE Denise, was born in Yagoua, Cameroon, and wants to promote Massa arts and cultures on both sides of the Chad-Cameroon border, including gourna music.

Her songs also cover the search for peace and the defence of women's rights. She previously released a 13-track debut album, « Mon premier pas ».

Why it mattersFans of Massa music on both sides of the Chad-Cameroon border can hear the album live at the N'Djamena concert.
