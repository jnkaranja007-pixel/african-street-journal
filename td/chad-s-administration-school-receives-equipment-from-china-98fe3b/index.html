Chad's administration school receives equipment from China

The Chinese embassy donated computer and office tools to the national school, alongside a discussion on lessons from China's development.

Source: Alwihda Info · 2026-11-28 · 1 min read

Chad's National School of Administration (ENA) received computer and office equipment from China's embassy on Wednesday, 25 November, during an event in N'Djamena on lessons from China's development. The school trains future public administrators.

ENA director-general Mahamat Borgou Hassan received the donation. China's ambassador, Wang Xining, called it the first step in his mission's support for the school. The discussion covered China's economic development and industrialisation, and speakers examined how Chad could develop its agricultural potential.

The Chinese delegation said it was ready to support Chad's agricultural production and processing, taking local climate conditions into account. The school said it streamed the event on its official Facebook page.

Why it mattersENA students and staff received computer and office equipment, and the Chinese ambassador described the donation as the first step in his mission's support for the school.
