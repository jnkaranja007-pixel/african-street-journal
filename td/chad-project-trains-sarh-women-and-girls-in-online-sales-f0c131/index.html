Chad project trains Sarh women and girls in online sales

The session covered phone basics and internet safety, with participants learning to market products on social media without a shop.

Source: Tchadinfos · 2026-11-19 · 1 min read

The Chad Digital Transformation Support Project and consultancy Ebenyx held a digital skills workshop for women and girls in Sarh on 17 November 2026. The training took place at the Ngarta Tombalbaye Palace of Arts and Culture and covered ways to use digital tools in daily life, economic activities and communication.

Layibé Patalet, a cybersecurity engineer at Ebenyx and a trainer, said some women were not yet familiar with basic phone functions, so the course began with phone use before moving to more advanced subjects. Its modules included internet safety and selling online.

Participants learned to promote products through social media and run small businesses without a physical shop. Patalet said a phone alone could help develop a profitable activity and generate income.
