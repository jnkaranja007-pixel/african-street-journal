Chad's employment office and Moov Africa shortlist 400 young people for 300 jobs

A joint committee has begun the first screening of applicants, with interviews to pick the final beneficiaries before 27 November 2026.

Source: Alwihda Info · 2026-11-24 · 1 min read

The National Office for the Promotion of Employment (ONAPE) in Chad and the telecoms firm Moov Africa have started selecting young people for a programme to place 300 of them in jobs after the 2026 National Employment Forum. Their joint committee met on Monday and began an initial screening to reduce the pool to 400 candidates.

The committee validated the programme's operational timetable, which covers candidate screening, interviews, training, support and final placement, Alwihda Info reported. The 400 shortlisted candidates will be interviewed by the joint committee to identify the 300 final beneficiaries.

The final list of selected young people is due by 27 November 2026. The meeting was chaired by Brahim Djorbo, head of ONAPE's self-employment desk.

Why it mattersSome 300 young Chadians will be chosen from 400 shortlisted candidates through interviews, with the final list due by 27 November 2026.
