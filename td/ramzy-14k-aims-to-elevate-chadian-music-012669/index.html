Ramzy-14K aims to elevate Chadian music

The N'Djaména-based artist blends Afrobeat and Amapiano to create urban music for youth.

Source: Alwihda Info · 2026-11-15 · 1 min read

Ramzy-14K, a young artist from N'Djaména, is working to establish his musical identity and promote Chadian music internationally. He performs urban music, drawing inspiration from Afrobeat and Amapiano to create a sound with catchy rhythms and melodies.

The artist focuses on his energetic stage presence and ability to connect with audiences through his style and flow. He aims to deliver accessible music that is danceable while conveying his generation's outlook.

Ramzy-14K has performed at major cultural events in Chad, including the Festival Dary on May 4, 2024, April 9, 2025, and December 30, and the Festival Haguina on May 27, 2026. His motto, "The street, my pen, my future!", reflects his ambition to build a significant musical career.

Why it mattersRamzy-14K's ambition to promote Chadian music internationally affects the nation's cultural output.
