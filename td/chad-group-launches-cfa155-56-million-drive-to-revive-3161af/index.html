Chad group launches CFA155.56 million drive to revive science classes in Wadi Fira

The three-year programme targets teacher shortages and the lack of science streams, with CFA46.75 million allocated for 2026-2027.

Source: Tchadinfos · 2026-11-21 · 1 min read

The Association OUM AL KOUL (an Chadian civil-society group) launched Nahdatil Taalim, a programme to improve secondary education and promote science subjects, on Friday 20 October at Arada bilingual secondary school in Wadi Fira province, eastern Chad. The group plans to spend CFA155.56 million over three years.

The association says Al Biher department has only one secondary school, and a shortage of teachers has left it without science classes from seconde. Pupils have instead been directed into literary streams regardless of their abilities or career ambitions. In the 2026 baccalaureate, the department's average pass rate was about 25%, and no candidates sat the science C and D streams because they were not offered.

The 12-month pilot is expected to directly benefit 560 pupils, 25 teachers and five administrative officials. Its budget for the 2026-2027 school year is CFA46.75 million.
