Chad names Daouda Ahmat Mahamat to head N'Djaména university

A 20 November presidential decree installs the professor in place of Mahamat Saleh Daoussa Haggar, who was suspended from the post.

Source: Alwihda Info · 2026-11-21 · 1 min read

Daouda Ahmat Mahamat has been appointed president of the University of N'Djaména by a decree signed on 20 November 2026. He succeeds professor Mahamat Saleh Daoussa Haggar, who held the post before being suspended.

The appointment was made under presidential decree No. 3199/PR/PM/MESRSFP/2026, which the Alwihda Info report cites. The decree is part of a renewal of governance at the institution.

The report gives no further details on when Mahamat will take up the role or what led to Haggar's suspension.

Why it mattersThe university's leadership changes hands, so students and staff now deal with a new president.
