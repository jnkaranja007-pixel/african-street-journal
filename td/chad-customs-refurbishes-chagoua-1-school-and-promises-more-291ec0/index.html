Chad customs refurbishes Chagoua 1 school and promises more equipment

The works added 200 desk-benches, water points and power to 17 classrooms, while the customs director announced further supplies.

Source: Alwihda Info · 2026-11-26 · 1 min read

Chad’s customs service has fully refurbished Chagoua 1 school in the 6th arrondissement, officials said at a handover ceremony on Wednesday, 25 November. The work included 62 windows for 12 classrooms, 23 doors, electricity in 17 classrooms and the schoolyard, and 200 desk-benches.

The project also added hand-operated and electric water wells with taps, three-compartment toilets, two gates and 41 trees. Workers raised the boundary wall from 1.90 metres to 3 metres and filled the schoolyard to prevent flooding. School director Djaoused née Tourmel Nangar had described the former buildings as lacking usable toilets and furniture, with damaged roofs and flooded rooms.

Customs director-general Ousman Brahim Djouma announced a further donation of desk-benches, solar panels and drilling equipment. Finance Minister Tahir Hamid Nguilin presided over the ceremony and urged pupils to attend school regularly.

Why it mattersChagoua 1 pupils and staff have improved classrooms and facilities, and the customs service has promised additional desks, solar panels and drilling equipment.
