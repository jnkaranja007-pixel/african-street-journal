Chad hands six Kouba fire suspects to justice

Authorities say the 20 November blaze burned a large area of bush in the Salamat province village.

Source: Alwihda Info · 2026-11-26 · 1 min read

Chad’s government delegate for Salamat province, Ahmat Choua Rozi, presented the main suspect in a deliberate bush fire at Kouba to the press on Wednesday, 25 November. The fire started on 20 November in the village, in Haraze-Mangueigne department, and burned a large area of bush, according to authorities.

Six people, including the main suspect, were arrested in the case, authorities said. After the presentation, the suspect and the others were handed to the justice system for proceedings. Provincial environment delegate Souleymane Yaya Labadry praised the security forces’ work.

Provincial council president Souleymane Issakha Abdoulaye urged residents to remain vigilant, saying bush fires are prohibited by law. Rozi said the fire’s widely shared images had caused strong public emotion and alleged that the suspects had also called for hatred and violence.

Why it mattersThe six people arrested in the Kouba case are now in the hands of the justice system for proceedings.
