Chad football shows signs of revival

Journalist Mbaigolmem Aubain says a true renaissance requires unity among football stakeholders.

Source: Tchadinfos · 2026-11-11 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Chad's national football league is restarting after years of difficulty, with the national team, the Sao, entering competitions. The new federal team has also stated ambitions, creating hope for the sport.

However, sports journalist Mbaigolmem Aubain stated on Tchadinfos that these steps are more of a "surge of pride" than a full revival. He noted positive signals but stressed that a true renaissance depends on all football actors speaking with one voice.

Aubain identified a lack of cohesion and conflicting interests among football stakeholders as the main barrier to rebuilding the sport. He described the current environment as "clan-like" and divisive, hindering development efforts.

Why it mattersChad's football stakeholders must unite for the sport to truly recover.
