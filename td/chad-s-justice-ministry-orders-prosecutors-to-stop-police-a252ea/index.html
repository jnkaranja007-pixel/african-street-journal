Chad's justice ministry orders prosecutors to stop police custody in civil disputes

A 25 November circular tells prosecutors general to summon judicial police officers and enforce the ban on custody in civil, commercial and social cases.

Source: Alwihda Info · 2026-11-27 · 1 min read

Chad's Ministry of Justice has ordered prosecutors general to enforce a strict ban on judicial police handling civil, commercial and social disputes or using custody in those cases. Justice and Human Rights Minister Ndolenodji Alixe Naimbaye issued the circular on 25 November 2026.

The ministry said abuses persist in police stations and gendarmerie brigades despite earlier circulars in 2023 and January 2026. Prosecutors general are to summon every judicial police officer in their territory and formally forbid custody and interference in such disputes.

The ministry also told judicial authorities to apply law No. 011/AN-SENAT/2025, amending the rules, with the prohibition enforced in full rigour.
