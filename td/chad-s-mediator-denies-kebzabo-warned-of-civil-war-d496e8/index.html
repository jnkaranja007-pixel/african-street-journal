Chad's mediator denies Kebzabo warned of civil war

The institution rejected an online outlet's account of his remarks and urged media and social-media users to report them accurately.

Source: Alwihda Info · 2026-11-28 · 1 min read

Chad's national mediator denied on Friday, 27 November, that its head, Saleh Kebzabo, had warned of a risk of civil war. The institution rejected an online outlet's attribution of a warning about a generalised armed civil war.

In a statement, the mediator said the account distorted Kebzabo's intervention. He had met civil-society figures, journalists, academics and think-tank representatives in N'Djamena on Wednesday, 25 November, to present the institution's peace-engagement strategy. The discussion covered weapons circulation and conflicts between communities.

The statement said Kebzabo had called for all parts of society to support social cohesion, and it urged media and social-media users to report his remarks accurately.

Why it mattersReporters and social-media users who repeat the outlet's account now face a public correction from the mediator, which calls for accurate reporting of Kebzabo's remarks.
