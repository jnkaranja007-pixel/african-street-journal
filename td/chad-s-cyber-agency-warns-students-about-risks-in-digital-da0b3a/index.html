Chad’s cyber agency warns students about risks in digital books

At a N’Djamena university, officials cited malware, phishing and data theft, and explained where victims of online abuse can seek help.

Source: Tchadinfos · 2026-11-23 · 1 min read

Chad’s National Agency for Information Security and Electronic Certification held a cyber-safety discussion for students at the African University of Management and Innovation in N’Djamena on Friday, 20 November. The event formed part of the 10th edition of the Month of Books and Reading and focused on downloading and reading digital books safely.

Ali Barkai, the agency’s director of personal data control and protection, said digital books widen access to knowledge but warned of malware, phishing and personal-data theft. Dr Zakit Sabit, its information-systems security director, said personal devices connected to workplace networks can expose institutions and businesses to cyber risks.

Sabit said victims of online scams, cyberbullying or cyberattacks can report to the agency, the prosecutor’s office or the Judicial Police Coordination, presenting concrete evidence. He said the agency works to secure Chad’s cyberspace and pursue cybercriminals.

Why it mattersStudents downloading digital books were warned about malware, phishing and data theft, while victims were told which three authorities accept reports.
