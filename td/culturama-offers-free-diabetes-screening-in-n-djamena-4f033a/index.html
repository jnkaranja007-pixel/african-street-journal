Culturama offers free diabetes screening in N'Djamena

The cultural association Culturama organized free diabetes testing at the Idriss Mahamat Ouya stadium on World Diabetes Day.

Source: Tchadinfos · 2026-11-15 · 1 min read

The cultural association Culturama hosted a free diabetes screening event on November 14 at the Idriss Mahamat Ouya stadium in N'Djamena. The initiative, held on World Diabetes Day, was a collaboration with the Ministry of Public Health and Prevention, supported by a Chinese medical team and the Maison du diabète.

This event aimed to raise public awareness about diabetes risks and promote early detection, allowing participants to check their health status and receive guidance for appropriate medical care. Culturama president Claudine Alingué stated the project, named 'Assapa Village,' was inspired by her mother's personal experience with diabetes and her advocacy for screening and sports.

The screening offered participants a chance to understand their health and receive necessary referrals. The partnership with the Ministry of Public Health and Prevention ensured access to medical expertise for those needing further treatment.

Why it mattersResidents in N'Djamena can get free diabetes screenings and health guidance at the Idriss Mahamat Ouya stadium.
