Chad prepares Al-Djazira site for Mahamid expelled from Niger

More than a thousand people from Niger’s Diffa region have crossed into Chad, and others are still arriving at the border.

Sources: RFI Afrique and Jeune Afrique · 2026-11-26 · 1 min read

Chad’s ministries of Social Action, Solidarity and Humanitarian Affairs have prepared the Al-Djazira reception site in Lake province for Mahamid people expelled from Niger. The site is a few kilometres from the border. More than a thousand members of the community from Niger’s Diffa region have crossed into Chad, while others are still arriving.

RFI Afrique reported that women at the site include many without identity papers from either Chad or Niger. Niger’s authorities have given the community until 31 December to leave the country’s southeast and settle in Chad.

Jeune Afrique reported that the expulsions of several thousand Mahamid Arabs have again strained relations between Niamey and N’Djamena.

Why it mattersMahamid women at Al-Djazira without papers from either country face a 31 December deadline to leave Niger and settle in Chad.
