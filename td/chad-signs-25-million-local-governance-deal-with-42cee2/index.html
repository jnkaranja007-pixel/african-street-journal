Chad signs $25 million local-governance deal with Switzerland and the UN's development...

The project aims to build the capacity of local authorities and bring administration closer to citizens.

Source: Tchadinfos · 2026-11-24 · 1 min read

Chad's Ministry of Territorial Administration, Switzerland and the United Nations Development Programme (UNDP), the UN's global development agency, signed a financing framework on 23 November 2026 for a $25 million local-governance project. The project is called Strengthening Governance for Local Development (RGDL).

The agreement was signed by Vice-Prime Minister Limane Mahamat, Finance Minister-delegate Saleh Bourma, UNDP resident representative Francis James and Swiss foreign affairs official Philipp Stalder. Switzerland is the financing partner, with UNDP supporting implementation. Mahamat chaired the project's first steering committee meeting after the signing.

RGDL is intended to build the capacity of autonomous local authorities, encourage residents to take part in local decisions and improve public services.

Why it mattersAutonomous local authorities are the intended beneficiaries of the $25 million project, which is designed to build their capacity and improve public services.
