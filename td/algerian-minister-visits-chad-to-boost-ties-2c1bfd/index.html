Algerian minister visits Chad to boost ties

Ahmed Attaf met with Chadian officials to advance security and economic cooperation between the two nations.

Source: RFI Afrique · 2026-11-06 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Algerian Foreign Minister Ahmed Attaf visited Chad's capital, N'Djamena, on Tuesday, November 3, to continue discussions from recent months. While no agreements were formally signed, the meeting emphasized the importance of ongoing security and economic cooperation between Algiers and N'Djamena.

The visit aimed to strengthen diplomatic relations and explore avenues for collaboration. Both nations acknowledged the need for continued partnership in critical areas, building on previous engagements.

Minister Attaf's trip focused on security and economic cooperation.
