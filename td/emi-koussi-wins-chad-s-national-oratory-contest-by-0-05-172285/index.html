Emi Koussi wins Chad's national oratory contest by 0.05 point

The university scored 16.85 out of 20 in the final, edging the National Higher School of Information and Communication Technologies.

Source: Alwihda Info · 2026-11-22 · 1 min read

The University Emi Koussi won Chad’s third National Oratory Contest in N’Djamena on Saturday, 21 November 2026, scoring 16.85 out of 20. It beat the National Higher School of Information and Communication Technologies, known as ENASTIC, which scored 16.80.

The final was organised by the Association of Leaders for a Prosperous Chad and chaired by Youth and Sports Minister Naïr Abakar. ENASTIC argued for the need for great leaders, while Emi Koussi argued for more responsible citizens, under a debate theme asking what Chad needs.

Association president Youssouf Togoï described the contest as a forum for learning, critical thinking and civic training. Abakar said his ministry supported initiatives that promote young people’s talents.

Why it mattersStudents at the two universities contested the national title, with Emi Koussi’s 16.85 score beating ENASTIC’s 16.80 on 21 November.
