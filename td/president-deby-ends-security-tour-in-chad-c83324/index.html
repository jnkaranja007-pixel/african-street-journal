President Déby ends security tour in Chad

Mahamat Idriss Déby Itno returned to N'Djamena after visiting six provinces.

Source: Alwihda Info · 2026-11-16 · 1 min read

President Mahamat Idriss Déby Itno returned to N'Djamena on Sunday, November 15, 2026, concluding a security tour across six Chadian provinces. The tour covered Ennedi-Est, Ennedi-Ouest, Ouaddaï, Salamat, Batha, and Hadjer-Lamis. The president's arrival at Hassan Djamous International Airport was met by Prime Minister Allah-Maye Halina.

High-ranking civil and military officials also greeted the head of state on the tarmac. Among them were the Chief of Staff of the Armed Forces, General Abakar Abdelkerim Daoud, the Minister of State and Secretary-General of the Presidency, and the Minister Director of the Civil Cabinet. Their presence underscored the importance of the president's security-focused itinerary.

The president's extensive tour aimed to assess and bolster security measures across these key regions. The visit involved direct engagement with local authorities and military personnel to address ongoing security challenges within the country.
