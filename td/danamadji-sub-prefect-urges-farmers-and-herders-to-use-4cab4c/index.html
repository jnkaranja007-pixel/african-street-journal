Danamadji sub-prefect urges farmers and herders to use legal dispute channels

Tougondjide Tedebaye Léonard raised grazing routes, poisoned fields, bush fires and relations between village chiefs and residents during a visit to Kalgoua.

Source: Alwihda Info · 2026-11-20 · 1 min read

Tougondjide Tedebaye Léonard, the sub-prefect of Danamadji in Chad, met residents in Kalgoua village, in Moyo canton, to discuss preventing tensions between farmers and herders. He sought residents’ concerns and ways to address local difficulties with their participation.

The discussions covered respecting corridors reserved for seasonal livestock movement and keeping animals out of cultivated land. Tedebaye warned farmers against using toxic products or poison to settle disputes involving animals, and urged both groups to use legal mechanisms. He also called on residents to avoid practices that can cause uncontrolled bush fires, which he said threaten land, natural resources and economic activities.

Tedebaye also asked village chiefs and residents to build mutual understanding and trust. The visit focused on explaining rules for community life and preventing disputes.

Why it mattersFarmers and herders in Kalgoua were asked to keep livestock out of cultivated land and settle animal-related disputes through legal channels.
