Commercial Bank Tchad partners for youth employment

The bank will mobilize job and internship opportunities and provide technical support to young entrepreneurs.

Source: Alwihda Info · 2026-10-21 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X

The Commercial Bank Tchad (CBT) and the organizing committee of the National Employment Forum (FNE 2026) have formalized a partnership to boost employment, training, and entrepreneurship for Chadian youth. This agreement, signed on Wednesday, October 20, 2026, designates CBT as the official and exclusive banking partner.

Under the agreement, CBT commits to actively seeking job and internship placements for young people. The bank will also offer technical guidance to young entrepreneurs participating in the PROJIACE initiative. This collaboration aims to bridge the gap between the banking sector and job seekers.

This initiative reflects a shared objective to increase private sector involvement in promoting decent employment in Chad. The partnership is intended to leverage public-private collaboration as a tangible tool for professional integration and to shape a better future for the nation's young population.

Why it mattersYoung job seekers and entrepreneurs in Chad will benefit from new employment and training opportunities facilitated by the bank.
