Al Damane real estate agency opens in N'Djamena

The agency says it will handle property management, land registry paperwork and contract-based transactions for residents and investors.

Source: Alwihda Info · 2026-11-15 · 1 min read

The Al Damane real estate agency officially began operations on Saturday, 14 November 2026, at an opening ceremony in N'Djamena. The president of its organising committee, Mahamat Moussa, said the firm was created to offer residents, businesses and investors close-to-home property services based on professionalism, transparency and trust.

The secretary-general, Ali Abba, said the agency wants to ease transactions, secure operations and provide tailored support. It will manage properties, help clients with cadastre services and contribute to land security, a sensitive issue in the capital where land and property matters are central to daily life.

The agency also plans to build a network of partners, improve its communication and strictly honour its contractual commitments. The ceremony was also a forum for professionals, partners and investors active in the sector.

Why it mattersBuyers, sellers and tenants in N'Djamena now have a local firm to handle land registry paperwork and property transactions.
