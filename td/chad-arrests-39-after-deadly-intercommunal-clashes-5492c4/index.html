Chad arrests 39 after deadly intercommunal clashes

Government ministers visited Ngoura after 25 people died and 22 were injured in violence between two communities.

Source: Alwihda Info · 2026-11-02 · 1 min read

Government ministers visited the Ngoura department in Hadjer-Lamis on Saturday, October 31, 2026, following deadly violence between two communities. 39 people were transferred to N'Djamena to face judicial authorities after the clashes. The violence, which occurred between the Hadjer-Lamis and Bahr El-Ghazal provinces, resulted in 25 deaths and 22 injuries.

The delegation, led by the Minister of Public Security and Immigration, General Ali Ahmat Aghabach, and the Minister Delegate for Decentralization, Dr. Ahmat Oumar Ahmat, arrived the day after the confrontations. Authorities stated their commitment to fully investigate the events, identify responsibilities, and ensure justice is served. Security measures are being implemented to de-escalate tensions and protect residents.

The government aims to foster dialogue between the communities to achieve lasting peace. The intervention follows intercommunal clashes that have caused significant loss of life and injury.

Why it mattersIntercommunal violence in Chad has led to deaths, injuries, and arrests, with government intervention to restore peace.
