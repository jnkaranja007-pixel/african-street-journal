AfrotroniX named finalist for Quebec arts award

Chadian musician Caleb Rimtobaye is competing for the Prix Charles-Biddle 2026 for immigrant contributions to Quebec culture.

Source: Tchadinfos · 2026-11-05 · 1 min read

Chadian musician Caleb Rimtobaye, known as AfrotroniX, is a finalist for the 2026 Prix Charles-Biddle in Quebec. This award recognizes the cultural and artistic contributions of immigrants to the province.

Rimtobaye, a musician, composer, and cultural entrepreneur, is one of three finalists in the national/international category. He is competing against illustrator Gérard Dubois and artistic director Victor Quijada.

The award ceremony will take place on November 6, 2026, during the Quebec Week of Intercultural Encounters.
