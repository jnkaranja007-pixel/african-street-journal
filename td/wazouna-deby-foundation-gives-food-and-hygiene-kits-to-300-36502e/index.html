Wazouna Déby Foundation gives food and hygiene kits to 300 households

The Chadian NGO distributed rice, oil, pasta, soap and mosquito nets in Machaga and Birbété to households identified with local leaders.

Source: Alwihda Info · 2026-11-22 · 1 min read

The Wazouna Déby Foundation, a national non-governmental organisation in Chad, distributed food and hygiene kits to 300 vulnerable households in Machaga and Birbété on Saturday 21 November 2026. The households were identified in collaboration with local leaders.

The food packages contained rice, oil and pasta, while the hygiene kits included soap and mosquito nets. Hamid Mahamat, the foundation’s national secretary-general, said the aid was intended to ease families’ daily needs and protect children, pregnant women and older people. He said the kits were meant to help prevent dengue and malaria.

The foundation’s delegation also visited Birbété, Ambetawa and Ada Sakine to extend the operation. Salima Ahmat Mbamta, who leads an association working with the foundation on breast, cervical and uterine cancer, praised its attention to women facing illness and poverty.
