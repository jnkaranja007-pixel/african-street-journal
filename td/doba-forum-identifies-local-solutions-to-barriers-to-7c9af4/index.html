Doba forum identifies local solutions to barriers to inclusive education

Education actors, community leaders and students discussed obstacles facing children in Logone Oriental province, including girls and those with disabilities.

Sources: Alwihda Info and Tchadinfos · 2026-11-20 · 1 min read

A community forum on inclusive, gender-sensitive education was held on Thursday, 19 November, at the diocesan continuing education centre in Doba, Chad. Education actors, community leaders and students discussed barriers to schooling in Logone Oriental province.

Project leader Michel Rija Christian said children with disabilities, girls facing social barriers, and children from vulnerable households and remote rural areas remain among those excluded from school. Participants mapped obstacles in their departments and identified locally agreed responses involving administrators, teachers and communities.

The meeting was part of the Improving Learning for Youth Empowerment project (ALAPAJ, its French acronym), implemented by the ACCRA consortium with the Jesuit Refugee Service (JRS), the Association Chadienne pour l'Autopromotion et le Developpement Rural (ACHDR) and Handicap International (HI), with funding from the French Development Agency (AFD). Its stated goal is to promote and sustain inclusive, gender-sensitive practices in targeted middle schools in the province.

Why it mattersAdministrators, teachers and communities are to be involved in local responses to barriers affecting children with disabilities, girls and pupils in remote areas.
