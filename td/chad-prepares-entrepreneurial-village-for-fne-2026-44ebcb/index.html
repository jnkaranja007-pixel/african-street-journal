Chad prepares entrepreneurial village for FNE 2026

The ONAPE and seven incubation partners are structuring a space for business creators and project leaders.

Source: Alwihda Info · 2026-10-23 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Chad's National Office for Employment Promotion (ONAPE) and its seven incubation partners are organizing the Entrepreneurial Village for the upcoming National Employment Forum (FNE) 2026. This dedicated space aims to support and foster innovation among business creators and project leaders. A working meeting on Thursday, October 22, 2026, led by the head of the Self-Employment Desk, finalized plans for this initiative.

The Entrepreneurial Village is designed to stimulate innovation, learning, and personalized support for entrepreneurs. Discussions focused on how incubation structures will lead the Entrepreneurial Clinic and masterclasses. Priority themes were identified to address entrepreneurs' needs, enhance their skills, and ensure their initiatives are sustainable.

The Entrepreneurial Village is set to be a key event for discovering new ideas, acquiring practical tools, and connecting with experts. Separately, the organizing committee for the second Ngourkosso forum launched an appeal on October 22, 2026, for funds from the department's natives, with a fundraising assembly scheduled for October 31, 2026.

Why it mattersEntrepreneurs and business creators in Chad will gain access to innovation, learning, and practical support.
