Chad launches nationwide clean-up to fight dengue and malaria

Prime Minister Allah Maye Halina began the campaign at Dembé market, with work targeting mosquito breeding sites, rubbish dumps and public spaces.

Source: Alwihda Info · 2026-11-22 · 1 min read

Chad’s Prime Minister, Allah Maye Halina, launched the National Sanitation Week on Saturday 21 November 2026 at Dembé market in N’Djaména’s 7th arrondissement. The campaign aims to step up the fight against dengue and malaria across the country, with clean-up work planned in the capital’s 10 arrondissements and all provinces.

The operation focuses on removing mosquito breeding sites, clearing illegal rubbish dumps and cleaning public spaces, Alwihda Info reported. After the launch, Halina visited several neighbourhoods and joined the work with a shovel. He urged residents to set aside every Saturday to clean neighbourhoods, schools and markets.

Government members, N’Djaména’s mayor, security forces, Red Cross volunteers and artists took part in the launch. The campaign’s slogan is “Put dengue out!”

Why it mattersResidents and local organisers across the capital’s 10 arrondissements and all provinces are being asked to carry out clean-ups every Saturday.
