Chad opens reception site for Arabs expelled from Niger

More than a thousand people from Niger’s Diffa region have crossed the border, while Niamey has given them until 31 December to leave.

Sources: RFI Afrique and Jeune Afrique · 2026-11-27 · 1 min read

Authorities in Chad’s Lake province have set up a reception site for Arab communities arriving from Niger, where they have faced forced departures and violence for several weeks. More than a thousand people have crossed the border, according to RFI Afrique.

Niger’s authorities have given the communities until 31 December to leave Niger and settle in Chad. Local authorities in Lake province are having to accommodate the arrivals and have prepared a site for them.

Jeune Afrique reported that the expulsion of several thousand Arabs Mahamid has again strained relations between Niamey and N’Djamena.

Why it mattersLocal authorities in Lake province must accommodate more than a thousand arrivals before Niger’s 31 December departure deadline.
