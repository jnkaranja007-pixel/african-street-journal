Chad security forces receive praise and equipment

Police officers in N'Djamena were recognized for arrests and dismantling a criminal network.

Source: Alwihda Info · 2026-11-05 · 1 min read

Chad's security forces were honored on Monday, November 2, 2026, in N'Djamena for recent successes in combating crime. Director of Public Security, Hachim Haroun Déby, specifically praised three police officers for arresting two wanted criminals in the capital. A local company donated three vehicles, 20 motorcycles, and 50 gas cylinders to support police operations.

Further recognition came after the dismantling of a cross-border criminal network. Agents from Public Security Commissariat No. 16 in Gassi received congratulatory letters from the Minister of Public Security and Immigration for their professionalism. The Director General of the National Police, accompanied by the first deputy prosecutor and a local deputy, visited the Gassi station to acknowledge these efforts.

The Director of Public Security also thanked six officials at Public Security Commissariat No. 8 for their daily commitment. These acknowledgments highlight the attention authorities and the public are giving to security efforts in the capital.
