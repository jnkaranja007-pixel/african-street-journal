Chad launches land dispute chambers

New judicial bodies aim to secure property rights and reduce conflicts over land.

Source: Alwihda Info · 2026-11-11 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Chad officially launched its land chambers on Tuesday, November 10, 2026, to secure property and improve dispute resolution. The Ministry of Justice, in partnership with the Ministry of Urban Planning, established these chambers within judicial jurisdictions across the country.

Justice Minister Ndolenodji Alixe Naïmbaye stated that the chambers will facilitate real-time information exchange between courts and land services. She emphasized that while technical expertise will inform judges, the courts will retain independent decision-making power, urging citizens to use legal channels instead of private justice or violence.

The Minister of Urban Planning, Mahamat Assileck Halata, described the reform as a response to the President's concerns about recurring land conflicts. He expressed hope for the future creation of a dedicated land tribunal, noting the commitment of the land registry and magistrates involved.

Why it mattersThe new land chambers will directly affect Chadian citizens by providing a legal framework for resolving property disputes and preventing violence.
