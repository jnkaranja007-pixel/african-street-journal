Chad bars exam-year students from transferring schools

The immediate ban covers public and private schools nationwide, including students in the 3e and Terminale classes.

Source: Tchadinfos · 2026-11-23 · 1 min read

Chad’s education minister, Dr Mahamat-Ahmad Alhabo, barred students in 3e and Terminale from transferring between schools nationwide under a circular signed on 30 October 2026. The immediate rule applies to public and private schools.

In N’Djamena, Abakar Zakaria said the ban stopped him from sending his daughter to live with her paternal uncle in Doba and prepare for the baccalaureate there. She had already failed the exam once at a private lycée. Zakaria said his family now has to enrol her in two lycées, in N’Djamena and Doba, so she can sit the exam at her former school.

The ministry says the measure is intended to stabilise exam classes and has directed school officials at every level to enforce it. Zakaria called for exceptions to be considered case by case.

Why it mattersFamilies planning a school change for a student in an exam class must keep the student at the current school under the immediate nationwide rule.
