Tandjilé-Ouest prefect urges dialogue as herds move through farming areas

At a 23 November meeting in Kélo, officials also formed committees to organise Chad’s 36th anniversary of Freedom and Democracy Day.

Source: Alwihda Info · 2026-11-24 · 1 min read

Colonel Ali Mahamat Sebey, prefect of Tandjilé-Ouest department, brought local leaders and representatives together at his residence in Kélo on 23 November to address pastoral and security concerns. The meeting focused on the movement of transhumant herders, possible tensions with farmers and bushfire prevention.

The prefect urged community authorities to promote dialogue and amicable settlement of disputes, citing a difficult agricultural campaign. Sub-prefects, security and civil-service officials, traditional chiefs, religious and political leaders, and representatives of traders, herders, farmers and unions attended. The group also discussed preparations for Chad’s 36th anniversary of Freedom and Democracy Day, and formed organising subcommittees.

Traditional chiefs and civil-society representatives pledged to share messages of peace and take part in the official parade. A further assembly is scheduled for 27 November to assess preparations.

Why it mattersFarmers and herders in Tandjilé-Ouest are being urged to settle disputes through dialogue as herds move through the area, with officials set to review preparations on 27 November.
