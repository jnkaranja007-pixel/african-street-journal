Chad football federation faces governance questions

20 months after promising order, the Chadian Football Federation's leadership is criticized for inconsistent application of rules and player disputes.

Source: Alwihda Info · 2026-11-16 · 2 min read

The Chadian Football Federation's governance is under scrutiny, 20 months after its president, Tahir Oloy Hassan, promised an end to disorder. His program included installing confidence, auditing the federation, and supporting provincial leagues, but critics point to unresolved issues. For example, the national team's selection and player disputes have led to controversy, including five players being dropped in June 2026 for alleged betrayal, only to be reinstated 10 days later after mediation by the Ministry of Sports.

Further governance concerns have arisen with recent league decisions. On October 24, 2026, Foullah Édifice, the N'Djamena champion, was removed from play-off contention by its league for appealing directly to the Court of Arbitration for Sport, a move permitted by federation statutes. Additionally, a November 10 match was halted due to a penalty and pitch invasion, yet the league's subsequent decision to award a 3-0 loss to Mont-Guéra, rather than a replay, has been questioned by the club.

Federation president Oloy Hassan also heads FC Yves, which benefited from the controversial decision in the match against Mont-Guéra. Provincial league presidents, promised 10 million CFA francs annually, report receiving only five million. The federation's president also issued a June 28 note requiring prior written agreement from league presidents for any gala tournament or match.

Why it mattersFootball clubs and provincial leagues face inconsistent rulings and financial shortfalls, impacting the sport's development.
