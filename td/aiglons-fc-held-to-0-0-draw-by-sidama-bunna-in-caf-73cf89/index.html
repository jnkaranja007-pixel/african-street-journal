Aiglons FC Held to 0-0 Draw by Sidama Bunna in CAF Champions League Opener

Chad's Aiglons FC dominated possession and created chances but could not score against Ethiopia's Sidama Bunna in the first leg of their CAF Champions League preliminary round.

Aiglons FC of N'Djamena played to a goalless draw against Ethiopian club Sidama Bunna on Saturday, September 5, 2026, at the Maréchal Mahamat Idriss Deby Itno stadium. The match was the first leg of the preliminary round of the CAF Champions League. Despite controlling the game for the full 90 minutes and creating several clear opportunities, the Chadian team's forwards lacked finishing ability, preventing them from securing a lead.

The home team displayed strong physical commitment from the start, taking control of play with better ball possession. However, their inability to convert chances meant Sidama Bunna remained largely untested. The Ethiopian side focused on containing Aiglons FC's attacks and only managed one shot on target in the second half. The defensive efforts of Sidama Bunna ultimately held firm against increased pressure from the Chadian club late in the game.

This 0-0 scoreline leaves the qualification for the next round entirely open heading into the return leg. Both teams will be playing for their survival in the competition during the second match. The intense duels throughout the game highlighted the high stakes for both clubs as they aim to advance in the continental tournament.

Why it mattersFootball fans in Chad will be disappointed by the inability of their national team, Aiglons FC, to score a goal at home, leaving their progression in the CAF Champions League uncertain.
