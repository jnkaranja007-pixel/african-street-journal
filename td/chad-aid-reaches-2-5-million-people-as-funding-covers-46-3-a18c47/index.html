Chad aid reaches 2.5 million people as funding covers 46.3% of needs

The response had reached 75% of its initial target by 30 September, but nearly one million people in need remained without assistance.

Source: Alwihda Info · 2026-11-28 · 1 min read

Humanitarian agencies in Chad had provided aid in at least one sector to about 2.5 million people by 30 September 2026, despite a shortfall in funding for the 2026 response plan. The United Nations Office for the Coordination of Humanitarian Affairs reported that about 4.5 million people needed assistance in the country.

The plan had targeted 3.4 million people, but budget restrictions led aid groups to narrow their focus to 1.9 million people with the most critical needs. Of those reached, 1.4 million had priority needs, equal to 74% of the reprioritised group.

Partners had raised $456.3 million, or 46.3% of the plan’s total funding requirement. Coverage was lowest for coordination and camp management at 6.9%, refugee response at 10.1% and nutrition at 29.3%. More resources are needed to reach half a million people in areas of high humanitarian tension.

Why it mattersAid groups must find more funding to reach half a million people in high-tension areas, while nearly one million people in need remain without assistance.
