Vitória de Riboque hosts undefeated Caixão Grande

The championship title in Sao Tome and Principe's 1st division will be decided on Sunday.

Source: STP Press · 2026-11-07 · 1 min read

Vitória de Riboque will host Caixão Grande on Sunday in the final match of the 1st division season. Caixão Grande enters the game undefeated, while Vitória de Riboque is one point behind the leaders. The championship title remains undecided among three teams: 6 de Setembro, Caixão Grande, and Vitória de Riboque, separated by one or two points.

The league standings show Despotivo 6 de Setembro in first place with 45 points, followed by B.U. Caixão Grande with 44 points and a game in hand. Vitória F.C. de Riboque is third with 43 points. Ké-Morabeza and Aliança Nacional have already been relegated to the 2nd division.

Other matches on Saturday include Juba against Agro Sport Monte Café and UDRA against Inter FC Bombom. Trindade FC will play UDESCAI on Saturday. On Sunday, Sporting Praia Cruz faces Aliança Nacional, and Ké Morabeza plays 6 de Setembro.
