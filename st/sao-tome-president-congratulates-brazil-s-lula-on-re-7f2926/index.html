Sao Tome president congratulates Brazil's Lula on re-election

Carlos Vila Nova sent a message to his Brazilian counterpart wishing him health and success.

Source: STP Press · 2026-10-28 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Sao Tome and Principe President Carlos Vila Nova congratulated Brazil's re-elected president, Lula da Silva, on his victory in Sunday's presidential elections. The congratulatory message was sent via the official presidency page of Sao Tome. Vila Nova expressed wishes for health and success in Lula's mission, stating the victory would strengthen historical relations and friendship between the two nations.

The Sao Tomean president affirmed that Lula da Silva's re-election reflects the Brazilian people's trust and hope in his leadership for development and prosperity. Lula da Silva secured his fourth presidential term with 51.7% of the vote in the second round, defeating Flávio Bolsonaro who received 48.23%.

Brazil is a key cooperation partner for Sao Tome and Principe, involved in sectors such as health, education, culture, and food security. This re-election solidifies existing cooperation ties between the two CPLP member states.
