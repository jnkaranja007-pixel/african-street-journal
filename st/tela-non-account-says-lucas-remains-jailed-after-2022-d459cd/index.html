Tela Non account says Lucas remains jailed after 2022 military incident

The artist says soldiers beat him, while then-colonel Virgílio Pontes intervened to divert a shot aimed at his head.

Source: Tela Non · 2026-11-28 · 1 min read

Tela Non reported that Lucas, an artist and family head, remains imprisoned after an incident involving soldiers in São Tomé on 24 November 2022. He was at a kiosk near the central Dr Ayres de Menezes hospital after work, the account says.

Lucas said he had gone there after an argument with his wife. The report says soldiers beat him and that Armindo Rodrigues, then deputy chief of the armed forces’ general staff, pointed a pistol at his head and fired. Virgílio Pontes, then a colonel, struck Rodrigues’s hand, diverting the shot into a wall, the account says.

Pontes later became chief of the armed forces’ general staff, according to Tela Non. The report gives no court ruling or official response.
