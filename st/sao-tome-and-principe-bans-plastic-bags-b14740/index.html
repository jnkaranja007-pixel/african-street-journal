Sao Tome and Principe bans plastic bags

Law 8/2020 prohibits production, import, sale, and free distribution of plastic bags.

Source: Tela Non · 2026-11-02 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Sao Tome and Principe banned plastic bags on September 21, 2020, through Law 8/2020. The law, published in the Official Gazette, prohibits the production, import, sale, and free distribution of these items. This legislation aims to curb plastic pollution across the island nation.

Neighboring countries offer comparative approaches to plastic regulation. Cabo Verde's Law 43/X/2024 expands a ban to all single-use plastics like cups and cutlery. Angola's Decree 170/25 implements a phased ban, starting with thin plastic bags and straws, and later including disposable cups and bottles.

The analysis suggests Sao Tome and Principe could expand its ban to include plastic cups and cutlery in future revisions. The law also mandates government incentives for private investment in alternative products, aligning with Cabo Verde's experience. Ensuring the quality of these alternatives is a stated requirement.

Why it mattersThe ban on plastic bags directly affects the daily lives and consumption habits of all residents in Sao Tome and Principe.
