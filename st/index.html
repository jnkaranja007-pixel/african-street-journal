Mozambique, São Tomé and Príncipe Expand Trade and Training Cooperation

Mozambican Prime Minister Benvinda Levi proposed broadening bilateral ties beyond diplomacy to include commerce, agriculture, and fisheries.

Mozambique aims to expand cooperation with São Tomé and Príncipe into commercial areas, agriculture, and fisheries, according to Prime Minister Benvinda Levi. Speaking in São Tomé and Príncipe, Levi stated that while political-diplomatic ties are strong, other sectors, particularly trade, remain underdeveloped despite mutual potential. She highlighted cocoa and coffee from São Tomé and Príncipe as products with potential for increased circulation, while Mozambique offers agricultural capabilities to meet São Toméan market needs.

The Prime Minister, representing President Daniel Chapo at the inauguration of re-elected President Carlos Vila Nova, emphasized the existing effective cooperation in training. São Toméan students and professionals benefit from training in Mozambique, particularly in medicine, police, and justice sectors, with opportunities and scholarships expected to increase. Levi also identified fisheries as a key area for enhanced cooperation, leveraging São Tomé and Príncipe's expertise and Mozambique's extensive coastline.

To facilitate these expanded collaborations, Levi advocated for the creation of joint working mechanisms to identify specific needs and priority areas. This structured approach, she believes, will enable both nations to advance cooperation more securely and effectively. The proposed expansion aims to build on existing strengths and address complementary needs between the two countries.

Why it mattersInvestors may find opportunities as Mozambique and São Tomé and Príncipe plan to boost trade in agricultural products like cocoa and coffee, and potentially in fisheries.

Vila Nova Begins Second Term, Pledges Renewed Commitment

Reelected President Carlos Vila Nova took the oath for his second five-year term, vowing to prioritize the nation's future and effective governance.

Carlos Vila Nova was sworn in for a second term as President of São Tomé and Príncipe on Thursday, following his reelection with 55.88% of the vote on July 19. The inauguration ceremony was attended by several heads of state and government. Vila Nova stated that his victory signifies a renewal of commitment and responsibility to the nation, promising to work with greater determination for a brighter future for all citizens.

During his inauguration speech, the head of state reiterated his campaign slogan, “Primeiro São Tomé e Príncipe” (First São Tomé and Príncipe), emphasizing it as a deep conviction and a promise to be fulfilled. He called for hard work, effort, dedication, and persistence in national development, advocating for an efficient and swift justice system and political stability grounded in accountability.

Vila Nova stressed that leadership power is a temporary service to the community, not personal property. He urged courage in confronting challenges truthfully, without hiding difficulties or allowing them to diminish hope. He asserted that hope, combined with work, competence, and determination, becomes a force for change, and that São Tomé and Príncipe possesses the conditions for a better future.

Why it mattersThe President's renewed commitment to national development and effective governance directly impacts all citizens of São Tomé and Príncipe.

Law Firm Urges Photronics Investors to File Claims Before September 4 Deadline

Rosen Law Firm is reminding buyers of Photronics, Inc. securities who experienced losses to act by September 4, 2026, to join a class action lawsuit.

Investors who purchased Photronics, Inc. (NASDAQ: PLAB) securities between December 10, 2025, and May 27, 2026, have until September 4, 2026, to file as a lead plaintiff in a securities class action. Rosen Law Firm, representing investors, stated that eligible individuals may be entitled to compensation without out-of-pocket costs through a contingency fee arrangement.

The lawsuit alleges that Photronics provided overly optimistic statements while concealing adverse facts about its product portfolio, customer timelines, and demand stability. According to the complaint, the company faced a critical bottleneck in its project launch pipeline, hindering its future growth expectations.

Rosen Law Firm encourages investors to select legal counsel with a proven track record in leadership roles for securities class actions. The firm highlighted its own experience, including achieving a significant settlement against a Chinese company and consistently recovering substantial sums for investors.

Why it mattersInvestors who bought Photronics securities during the specified period may be eligible for compensation due to alleged misrepresentations by the company.

Tchiloli Theatre Actors Champion Justice in Sao Tomé

Actors in traditional dress perform medieval European tragedies in open-air Tchiloli theatre, a tradition popular with locals and tourists.

Open-air Tchiloli theatre, introduced to Sao Tomé over 500 years ago by Portuguese colonists, continues to draw crowds with its performances of medieval European tragedies. Actors, adorned with beards, wigs, and hats, present these historical dramas in the island nation off Africa's west coast, maintaining a tradition that resonates with both residents and visiting tourists.

The enduring popularity of Tchiloli theatre is attributed by performers to its strong message of justice. Marc Carvalho, a troop member for 35 years, stated that the plays speak about justice being impartial, regardless of wealth or social standing. Spectator Maribel Borja echoed this sentiment, emphasizing that fairness is a universal concern, irrespective of religion or class, and that negative actions lead to negative consequences.

Performing these intense plays requires significant dedication, with actors spending hours on stage. Despite the effort, Jardel Moniz Fernandez finds the work energizing and plans to involve his own children in the tradition. He intends to begin training his eldest son to perform the role of Kata, a character responsible for delivering a letter within the play, ensuring the continuation of the theatrical heritage.

Why it mattersThe Tchiloli theatre tradition is sustained by performers and audiences who find its themes of justice and fairness relevant to their lives.

IGR São Tomé Opens Portuguese Language Course Registrations

New classes for foreigners and nationals begin September 24, 2026, offering specialized Portuguese language training.

The Instituto Guimarães Rosa São Tomé has announced that registrations are now open for its upcoming Portuguese language courses. The new academic term is scheduled to commence on September 24, 2026, with several distinct programs available to cater to different learning needs.

Offerings include Portuguese as a Foreign Language (PLE) for non-native speakers aiming to improve communication, and Portuguese as an Intercultural Language (PLI) for nationals seeking to refine their language skills and qualify for the PEC-G program. A preparatory course for the Celpe-Bras exam is also available for those looking to understand its structure and requirements.

Interested individuals are encouraged to secure their spots promptly. For inquiries and registration, prospective students can contact the institute via WhatsApp at +239 2223796 or by email at igr.saotome@itamaraty.gov.br. The institute aims to facilitate better communication and cultural understanding through language acquisition.

Why it mattersForeign nationals and São Toméan citizens can improve their Portuguese language skills through specialized courses offered by the Instituto Guimarães Rosa São Tomé.
