Américo Ramos sworn in as Sao Tome and Principe prime minister

Ramos took office despite a challenge from the MLSTP, the party that won the most seats in the September 27 election.

Américo Ramos, president of the Acção Democrática Independente (ADI), was sworn in as prime minister of Sao Tome and Principe at the Palácio do Povo, in a ceremony presided over by President Carlos Vila Nova. Ramos said he already has his list of members for the new government and is making final checks before announcing it.

The ADI won 23 seats in the September 27 election, behind the Movimento de Libertação de São Tomé e Príncipe (MLSTP), which won 24. Ramos reached a deal with the Movimento de Cidadãos Independentes-Partido Socialista/Partido de Unidade Xavier (MCI-PS/PUN), which has six elected deputies, giving the coalition an absolute majority of 29 seats. Ramos said on Monday that the rule of the majority is decisive.

On Friday, a group of MLSTP deputies asked the Constitutional Court to annul the decree appointing Ramos, arguing the President did not hear the parties with parliamentary representation as the constitution requires. Their spokesman, Cílcio dos Santos, said the President based the appointment mainly on parliamentary sustainability and that all acts violating the constitution can be revoked.

Sao Tome and Angola to expand cooperation

Ministers expressed intent to revitalize dialogue and broaden partnerships in strategic sectors.

Sao Tome and Principe and Angola intend to boost bilateral cooperation by resuming dialogue, implementing signed agreements, and expanding partnerships into strategic development areas. This intention was stated by Sao Tome and Principe's Minister of State for Foreign Affairs, Cooperation and Communities, Ilza Amado Vaz, and Angola's Ambassador to Sao Tome and Principe, Fidelino Pelicanga, during Angola's 51st Independence Day celebrations on November 11.

Minister Vaz advocated for leveraging the countries' complementary strengths and experiences, focusing cooperation on training, institutional capacity building, and youth empowerment. She highlighted agreements signed in 2025 concerning vocational training, employment promotion, labor inspection, and social security, as well as a 2026 protocol on higher education.

The minister proposed revitalizing business relations through bilateral legal instruments to stimulate investment and create a favorable business environment, identifying opportunities in energy, oil, health, agriculture, tourism, and the blue economy. She also noted the need for increased coordination and information sharing on maritime security in the Gulf of Guinea.

Why it mattersSao Tome and Principe's foreign minister proposed expanding cooperation with Angola into strategic sectors like energy and agriculture, aiming for concrete benefits for both populations.

Trindade beats Inter de Bombom on penalties to reach STP Cup final

Trindade Futebol Clube won the shootout 4-3 after a goalless 90 minutes and will face 1.º de Maio da Ilha do Príncipe for the trophy.

Trindade Futebol Clube reached the final of São Tomé and Príncipe's Cup by beating Inter de Bombom 4-3 on penalties. Neither side scored in the 90 minutes of regulation play, and the match was decided from the penalty spot.

Inter de Bombom missed two of its penalties, and Grande took the decisive kick for Trindade and scored, setting off celebrations among players, staff and fans. Trindade returned to the country's top division recently, after more than two decades out of the elite.

Trindade will meet 1.º de Maio da Ilha do Príncipe in the final for the national cup.
