Sao Tome installs 8 water systems to reduce scarcity

Eight modular treated water collection and distribution systems are being installed across Sao Tome. In Agostinho Neto, the systems will capture 100 cubic meters of water per hour, according to Tela Non.

Why it mattersThe installation aims to alleviate water shortages for residents in several affected localities.

Politics · 08/21/2026

Constitutional Court delays party list for Sept. 27 elections

The Constitutional Court of Sao Tome and Principe has not yet validated the list of political parties admitted to the Sept. 27 legislative elections. The court has 11 business days to decide on candidacies under Article 41 of the electoral law, according to Tela Non.

Why it mattersThe delay leaves the official field of candidates undetermined as the legislative election date approaches.

Tela Non
Sport · 08/21/2026

6 de Setembro leads national division with 30 points

The 15th round of the Sao Tome 1st division championship begins Friday, Aug. 21, with Agrosport de Monte Café facing Inter do Bombom. League leader 6 de Setembro currently holds 30 points, according to STP Press.

Why it mattersThe upcoming matches on Aug. 22 will determine if 6 de Setembro can maintain its lead over rivals Caixão Grande and V.Riboque.

Culture · 08/21/2026

Director Jessica Lima joins FilmLab Mozambique

Sao Tome director Jessica Adelaide Lombá da Silva e Lima has been selected for the first edition of FilmLab Mozambique. The project development laboratory begins Aug. 24 in Maputo, according to Tela Non.

Why it mattersThe program aims to strengthen cinematic ties and project development between the two nations.

Business · 08/21/2026

Government organizes Gastronomic Fair for Aug. 23

The Ministry of Environment, Youth and Sustainable Tourism will hold the 'Grande Feira Gastronómica' this Sunday, Aug. 23, starting at 12:00. The event will take place at Praça Yon Gato, according to STP Press.

Why it mattersThe fair is designed to promote local tourism and culinary offerings in the capital.
