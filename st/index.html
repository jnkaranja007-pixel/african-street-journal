Fuel Ship Arrives Wednesday With Gasoline, Jet Fuel

A vessel carrying gasoline, jet fuel, and petroleum is set to dock Wednesday, ending a 15-day shortage that halted transportation and fishing.

A ship carrying gasoline, jet fuel, and petroleum is scheduled to arrive Wednesday around 10 a.m., according to Minister of State for Economy and Finance Gareth Guadalupe. The arrival is expected to replenish stocks that have been depleted for over 15 days, significantly impacting taxi drivers, fishermen, and motorcyclists who have been unable to work.

Minister Guadalupe acknowledged the hardship caused by the shortage, stating that governing involves making difficult decisions. He assured that the incoming fuel supply is sufficient to last until next year. The government, led by Américo Ramos, faces public discontent over the fuel crisis, but Guadalupe confirmed a strategy is in place to prevent future shortages.

The government's strategy includes reducing diesel imports, a primary source for electricity generation. Diesel costs currently account for over 50% of import expenses due to the nation's reliance on it for power. Minister Guadalupe expressed confidence that by the end of 2026, São Tomé and Príncipe will begin generating solar energy to further decrease diesel dependency.

Why it mattersTaxi drivers, fishermen, and motorcyclists are affected by the fuel shortage, which prevents them from working and earning income.

UNECA Launches Climate Resilience Initiative in Sao Tome and Principe

The UN Economic Commission for Africa has initiated RESIslands to bolster climate risk assessment and investment readiness.

The UN Economic Commission for Africa (UNECA) has launched the RESIslands initiative in Sao Tome and Principe, aiming to enhance the nation's preparedness for climate change impacts. This project is part of a larger program involving eight other African countries. The initiative focuses on strengthening climate risk evaluation, institutional capacity, and readiness for climate-related investments, with a specific emphasis on safeguarding communities and essential services.

Funded by the Green Climate Fund (GCF) and implemented by UNECA, the 30-month RESIslands project supports nine member states of the African Island States Climate Commission (AISCC). Sao Tome and Principe will receive support to improve national capacities for risk assessment, enhance climate information, and prepare investment proposals aligned with national priorities. The goal is to translate national priorities into tangible actions and lasting benefits for the population.

UNECA program manager Kjartan Fjeldsted highlighted the country's natural resources, including its marine environment, forests, and biodiversity, underscoring the importance of protecting the foundations of agriculture, fisheries, and sustainable tourism. He also acknowledged Sao Tome and Principe's active contribution to regional cooperation through the AISCC.

Why it mattersFarmers are affected by the initiative's focus on protecting the foundations of agriculture and fisheries from climate change impacts.

Fuel Shortage Halts Water Sales in Sao Tome

A lack of gasoline has stopped motorbike taxis from delivering water, leaving residents without a vital commodity.

The water distribution system installed by EMAE in the peripheral neighborhoods of Sao Tome city has been dry for some time, forcing residents to buy water. Previously, citizens paid over 1000 dobras for 1000 liters of water delivered by motorbike. This trade became profitable, especially in and around the capital, as people returned from work to find no water for cooking or bathing.

However, a gasoline shortage has paralyzed this business. Motorbike taxis are now stationary, leaving the population that relied on them for water frustrated. Drivers report being unable to work, leaving their families hungry. Residents in areas like Bobô Fôrro, Água Porca, Penha, and Boa Morte, who have had no running water for three months, are now unable to access even this costly alternative.

Agostinha da Glória, a resident of Bobô Fôrro, stated that the piped water system in her neighborhood has been dry for three months, requiring her to travel kilometers for water. Antónia Fernandes Moreno from Boa Morte echoed this, noting that EMAE installed pipes but no water flows, making basic hygiene difficult. The paralysis of motorbike taxis means even fetching water from nearby rivers is no longer an option for many.

Why it mattersResidents are unable to purchase water for essential needs due to a fuel shortage halting delivery services.

Trindade FC advances to São Tomé Cup final

Trindade FC defeated Desportivo de Guadalupe 2-0 in the semi-final, with striker Peter Ube scoring both goals.

Trindade Futebol Clube secured their spot in the São Tomé and Príncipe Cup final for the São Tomé region by beating second-tier side Desportivo de Guadalupe 2-0. The decisive semi-final match took place at the Estádio Nacional 12 de Julho.

Striker Peter Ube opened the scoring in the first half and later added a second goal in the second half to seal the victory for Trindade FC. They will now face Inter de Bombom in the regional final.

Inter de Bombom reached the final by defeating Caixão Grande 1-0 in their semi-final. Meanwhile, in the Príncipe Autonomous Region, 1.º de Maio and Operários have also qualified for their regional final. The winners of the São Tomé and Príncipe finals will compete in the national final, with Operários being the current holders of the trophy.

Why it mattersFootball fans in São Tomé will attend the regional cup final between Trindade FC and Inter de Bombom.

Beninese Footballer Adnan Aziz Pursues Argentina Dream

Despite a serious knee injury, Adnan Aziz remains determined to play for Chacarita Juniors in Argentina.

Beninese footballer Adnan Aziz harbors a deep ambition to play for Argentina's Chacarita Juniors, a dream that has persisted despite significant obstacles. His connection to the club transcends mere professional interest, embodying a personal aspiration that has fueled his journey.

A severe knee injury previously disrupted Aziz's plans, making his path more challenging. However, his resolve to reach Argentina and experience the Chacarita Juniors environment firsthand has remained unwavering. His story is gaining attention from media outlets in both Argentina and Africa.

Aziz's narrative highlights the human elements of football, including ambition, identity, and resilience. His determination to represent Chacarita Juniors underscores the powerful bond between a player and a club, with the next chapter of his career yet to be written.
