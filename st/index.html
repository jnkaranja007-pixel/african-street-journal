Lusíada University honors former rector Fernanda Pontífice

The Lusíada University of São Tomé and Príncipe published a tribute today to Professor Fernanda Pontífice, who served as rector for sixteen years.

The Lusíada University of São Tomé and Príncipe today honored Professor Fernanda Pontífice, who passed away recently. The university published a message on its digital page in the capital, recognizing her as a figure of merit and a reference in education and higher learning in the country.

Professor Pontífice dedicated sixteen years to the institution as rector, leaving a lasting impact. During her tenure, she was also a teacher, mentor, leader, and inspiration for many students, faculty, and staff. Her work is associated with a commitment to training, valuing knowledge, and advocating for a university that contributes to national development.

Her legacy extends beyond the university, encompassing public service as a National Deputy and Minister of Education. The university's message, signed by Chancellor Liberato Moniz and Rector Maria das Neves, expresses profound condolences to her family, friends, and the academic community, stating that her work and example will endure.

Why it mattersStudents, faculty, and the broader academic community in São Tomé and Príncipe are affected by the loss of a prominent educator and leader who shaped the country's higher education landscape.

Health · 08/26/2026

WHO reports Ebola outbreak accelerates with 90 daily cases in DR Congo

The World Health Organization announced that the Ebola outbreak in the Democratic Republic of Congo has reached an unprecedented pace, with nearly 90 new cases confirmed daily.

After 100 days since its official declaration, the Ebola outbreak in the Democratic Republic of Congo is accelerating significantly faster than previous regional outbreaks, according to the World Health Organization. The agency reported on Monday that the average daily confirmed cases reached almost 90 during the first quarter of epidemiological surveillance.

This rapid progression is severely straining the local health system and international response teams. Challenges include high transmission rates, logistical difficulties in hard-to-reach areas, and barriers to contact tracing and rapid isolation of infected individuals. The epidemic is now present in a sixth province, with Ituri remaining the epicenter, accounting for about 85% of cases and 79% of deaths.

Health authorities are emphasizing the urgent need to strengthen border surveillance and expand mass vaccination campaigns to prevent the epidemic from spreading to neighboring countries. The mortality rate remains alarming, with about 60% of the 260 weekly deaths in the last six weeks occurring outside Ebola treatment centers, indicating significant delays in detection and treatment.

Why it mattersThe accelerated Ebola outbreak directly affects the health and safety of communities in the Democratic Republic of Congo and potentially neighboring countries, increasing mortality and straining healthcare resources.

China hosts World Humanoid Robot Games, showcasing robotics advancements

Beijing is hosting the second World Humanoid Robot Games from August twenty-second to twenty-sixth, featuring over 2,056 robots from 666 teams across 16 countries competing in.

The second World Humanoid Robot Games opened in Beijing on August twenty-second, highlighting China's rapid development in the robotics sector. The event, held at the National Speed Skating Ring, features humanoid robots competing in sports like table tennis, boxing, racing, football, rope skipping, and weightlifting, demonstrating advanced industrial robotics capabilities.

China's leadership in robotics innovation is underscored by a recent industry report from the 2026 World Robotics Conference, which states that China delivered over 40,000 humanoid robots in the first half of this year, securing 97% of global deliveries. These robots are increasingly performing tasks in various sectors, from cooking to cleaning and delivery services.

The games also serve as a testing ground for new robotic creations, with one Chinese humanoid robot setting a new 100-meter record of 9.39 seconds, surpassing Usain Bolt's record of 9.58 seconds. The event, organized by the Beijing municipal government, China Media Group, the World Robotics Cooperation Organization, and the RoboCup Asia-Pacific International Council, promotes technological exchange and collaboration in the robotics field.

Why it mattersThe event showcases the increasing integration of humanoid robots into daily life and industry, affecting how people work and interact with technology.

Politics · 08/26/2026

Gabon donates 300 transparent ballot boxes to Sao Tome and Principe

The National Electoral Commission received 300 transparent ballot boxes from Gabon to support the upcoming legislative, municipal, and regional elections on September 27.

Sao Tome and Principe's National Electoral Commission (CEN) received 300 transparent ballot boxes this weekend, a donation from the Gabonese Republic. This contribution aims to bolster CEN's capacity for the legislative, municipal, and regional elections scheduled for September 27.

Horácio Sousa, the Sao Tomean Minister of Defense and Internal Order, confirmed that the donation fulfills a request made by President Vila Nova and Head of Government Américo Ramos to Gabonese President Brice Oligui. Sousa stated that the 300 ballot boxes address a deficit and are the exact quantity requested.

The Minister further explained that three elections will occur simultaneously, and the existing number of ballot boxes was insufficient. He assured that financial and technical conditions are now in place for the scrutiny, attributing the prompt assistance to the strong friendly and cooperative relations between the two nations, which date back to Sao Tome and Principe's liberation struggle.

Why it mattersCitizens of Sao Tome and Principe are directly affected as the donation ensures the necessary equipment is available for the upcoming elections, impacting their ability to participate in the democratic process.

Prime Minister Ramos recognizes fire service challenges, urban reform need

Prime Minister Américo Ramos acknowledged the growing number of fires and the fire department's difficulties during a visit to security sectors, emphasizing the need for urban.

Prime Minister Américo Ramos identified significant challenges for the fire department in São Tomé and Príncipe, including an increasing number of fires. During a visit to defense and security sectors, part of a United Nations initiative for Peace Maintenance and Conflict Prevention, Ramos stated the need for help to improve the fire service's response to recent catastrophes.

Ramos also highlighted the necessity for a comprehensive reform of the urban sector. He noted that firefighters often face obstacles reaching fire locations due to infrastructure placement and site conditions. The Prime Minister stressed that this prevents them from effectively combating blazes.

The head of government advocated for increased public awareness regarding construction types and the quality of materials used in buildings. This push for reform and public education aims to address the underlying issues contributing to the fire department's difficulties and the rising incidence of fires.

Why it mattersResidents of São Tomé and Príncipe are directly affected by the increasing number of fires and the fire department's limited ability to respond, impacting their safety and property.
