Universidade Lusíada honors former Rector Fernanda Pontífice

The Universidade Lusíada in São Tomé and Príncipe published a tribute today to Professor Fernanda Pontífice, who served as Rector for sixteen years.

The Universidade Lusíada in São Tomé and Príncipe has paid posthumous tribute to Professor Fernanda Pontífice, acknowledging her significant contributions to education and higher learning. The university's digital page today featured a message expressing profound sadness at her passing, recognizing her as a figure of merit and a reference in the field.

During her sixteen years as Rector, Professor Pontífice demonstrated dedication and a high sense of mission, leaving a lasting mark on the institution. She was also a teacher, mentor, leader, and inspiration for many generations of students, professors, and staff, according to the university's statement.

Her legacy is associated with a commitment to training, valuing knowledge, and advocating for a university that contributes to national development. The note, signed by Chancellor Liberato Moniz and Rector Maria das Neves, extends condolences to her family, friends, and the entire academic community.

Why it mattersThe academic community, including current and former students, professors, and staff, is affected by the loss of a key leader and educator.

Ebola outbreak accelerates in DR Congo, averaging 90 daily cases

The World Health Organization reports that the Ebola outbreak in the Democratic Republic of Congo has reached an unprecedented pace, with nearly 90 cases confirmed per day over.

The Ebola outbreak in the Democratic Republic of Congo has significantly accelerated, with an average of almost 90 cases confirmed daily in the first three months, according to the World Health Organization. This speed of contagion surpasses previous outbreaks in the region, placing extreme pressure on local health systems and international response teams. The epidemic is now present in a sixth province, with Ituri remaining the epicenter.

The rapid spread is compounded by logistical challenges in hard-to-reach areas, hindering contact tracing and isolation efforts. The WHO and its partners have expanded contact tracing from nine percent to eighty-four percent and established nineteen laboratories to combat the virus. However, instability, conflicts, attacks on health infrastructure, population displacement, and community resistance continue to impede the response.

Health authorities are emphasizing the urgent need to strengthen border surveillance and expand mass vaccination campaigns to prevent the epidemic from spreading to neighboring countries. The mortality rate remains alarming, with about sixty percent of the two hundred sixty weekly deaths occurring outside Ebola treatment centers in the last six weeks, indicating barriers to early detection and treatment.

Why it mattersThe accelerated Ebola outbreak directly affects residents of the Democratic Republic of Congo through increased illness and death, and threatens neighboring populations with spread.

Beijing hosts World Humanoid Robot Games, showcases robotics advancements

The second edition of the World Humanoid Robot Games, held in Beijing from August 22 to 26, 2026, highlighted China's rapid development in the robotics sector.

Beijing is hosting the second World Humanoid Robot Games from August 22 to 26, 2026, featuring over 2,056 humanoid robots from 666 teams across 16 countries. The opening ceremony at the National Speed Skating Ring on August 22 showcased robots competing in sports like table tennis, boxing, racing, football, rope skipping, and weightlifting, illustrating the advanced capabilities of these machines.

The event underscores China's leadership in robotics innovation, with a recent sectoral report indicating China delivered over 40,000 humanoid robots in the first half of this year, accounting for 97% of global deliveries. Chinese humanoid robots are now performing various tasks from cooking to cleaning and delivery, demonstrating their practical applications across multiple industrial sectors.

The games also serve as a testing ground for new robot designs, with one Chinese humanoid robot setting a new 100-meter record of 9.39 seconds, surpassing Usain Bolt's 9.58 seconds. Organized by the Beijing municipal government, China Media Group, the World Robotics Cooperation Organization, and the RoboCup Asia-Pacific International Council, the event promotes technology exchange and international cooperation in robotics.

Why it mattersThis event directly affects technology developers and industrial sectors globally by showcasing advanced robotics and fostering international collaboration and competition in the field.

Gabon donates 300 ballot boxes to Sao Tome and Principe for upcoming elections

Sao Tome and Principe's National Electoral Commission received 300 transparent ballot boxes from Gabon to support the legislative, municipal, and regional elections on September.

The Gabonese Republic provided 300 transparent ballot boxes to the Sao Tomean government this weekend, according to Horácio Sousa, the Sao Tomean Minister of Defense and Internal Order. This donation aims to bolster the National Electoral Commission's capabilities for the upcoming legislative, municipal, and regional elections scheduled for September twenty-seventh.

Minister Sousa stated that the 300 ballot boxes fulfill a request made by President Vila Nova and Head of Government Américo Ramos to Gabon's President Brice Oligui. He explained that the quantity received addresses a deficit, as the country will hold three simultaneous elections, and the existing ballot boxes were insufficient.

Sousa confirmed that financial and technical conditions are in place for the elections. He attributed Gabon's prompt response to the long-standing friendly and cooperative relations between the two countries, which date back to Sao Tome and Principe's liberation struggle.

Why it mattersCitizens of Sao Tome and Principe will benefit from the improved capacity of the National Electoral Commission to conduct the upcoming legislative, municipal, and regional elections.

Politics · 2026-08-25

Prime Minister Ramos addresses firefighter challenges, urban reform needs

Prime Minister Américo Ramos identified the need for urban planning reform and support for firefighters in São Tomé and Príncipe due to increasing fires.

Prime Minister Américo Ramos recently visited defense and security sectors, including the fire department, as part of a United Nations initiative for Peacekeeping and Conflict Prevention. During his visit, Ramos acknowledged the growing number of fires and the challenges faced by firefighters in responding to these incidents.

Ramos emphasized the necessity of implementing an already approved instrument to address these issues, stating that firefighters require assistance to better meet the demands of recent catastrophes. He noted that current urban conditions, including infrastructure location, often hinder firefighters' access to fire sites.

The Prime Minister also advocated for a comprehensive reform of the urban planning sector and called for increased public awareness regarding construction types and material quality. This initiative aims to improve emergency response capabilities and mitigate the impact of fires across the country.

Why it mattersThe Prime Minister's focus on urban reform and firefighter support directly impacts residents by addressing safety concerns related to fires and inadequate infrastructure.
