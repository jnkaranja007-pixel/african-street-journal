President names ADI leader Américo Ramos prime minister

Carlos Vila Nova chose Ramos after the MLSTP, which won the most seats, could not show it had enough parliamentary support to govern.

President Carlos Vila Nova has appointed Américo Ramos, leader of the ADI party, as Prime Minister and Head of Government of São Tomé and Príncipe, according to Presidential Decree No. 32/2026, which the presidency made public on Thursday. The appointment followed a 24-hour deadline the president had set for ADI to name a prime minister.

The presidency said the MLSTP, the most-voted party, had not provided the elements needed for political stability and governability. ADI, which won 23 seats in the 27 September legislative elections, signed a parliamentary agreement with the MCI-PUN, led by António Monteiro and holding six seats, giving them a combined 29 deputies. The MLSTP, with 24 seats but no absolute majority, had urged the president to reject an ADI-led government.

The Movimento BASTA criticised the decision and called for a government of national unity, arguing the appointment rests only on guarantees of parliamentary support.

Why it mattersRamos now has to put a government together and win votes in a parliament where the ADI-MCI-PUN bloc holds 29 seats, and the MLSTP is contesting the result.

Politics · 2026-11-14

MLSTP fails to form government after election win

The ADI party, which finished second, secured a 29-seat majority with the MCI-PS/PUN coalition, and its leader Américo d'Oliveira dos Ramos was named Prime Minister on November 12.

The Movimento de Libertação de São Tomé e Príncipe (MLSTP) failed to form a government despite winning the most seats in the September 27 legislative elections. The party secured 24 deputies, while the ADI party won 23 seats, and the MCI-PS/PUN coalition took 6. The MLSTP's inability to consolidate its victory led to its exclusion from governing.

While the MLSTP celebrated its narrow win, the ADI party formed a stable parliamentary majority of 29 deputies by agreeing with the MCI-PS/PUN coalition. President Carlos Vila Nova, after consulting political parties, gave the MLSTP 30 days to form a government, a deadline they could not meet. Consequently, the President invited the ADI to present a candidate for Prime Minister.

On November 12, 2026, Dr. Américo d’Oliveira dos Ramos was appointed Prime Minister, leading the ADI party. The MLSTP also lost leadership positions in the National Assembly to the ADI and MCI-PS/PUN. Political analysts suggest the MLSTP leader should consider stepping down and calling an extraordinary congress to elect new leadership to avoid further political isolation.

Business · 2026-11-14

Central bank asks public to take part in emigration and currency survey

The Central Bank of Sao Tome and Principe is collecting views on emigration, the foreign exchange market and inflation expectations until November 22.

The Central Bank of Sao Tome and Principe is carrying out a survey between November 9 and November 22 on emigration, the foreign exchange market and inflation expectations. It aims to learn what people think, feel and have experienced on these subjects.

The bank is calling on everyone to take part, saying the contributions will be essential to reaching the survey's goals. Its appeal did not set out how the results will be published or used.

Other items in the same feed cover the Dobra banknote, which was produced in the Netherlands, and a Central Bank and academies initiative to support economic growth in the country.

Why it mattersResidents who want their views on the currency and prices counted have until November 22 to respond.
