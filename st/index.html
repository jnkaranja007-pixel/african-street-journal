Sao Tome and Principe awaits presidential decree for health institute

National Institute of Public Health decree-law and statutes are ready but await presidential promulgation after two years.

Sao Tome and Principe is one of two Portuguese-speaking African countries without a National Institute of Public Health, a strategic health system institution.

Bonifácio Sousa, director of the National Center for Endemias, stated that the decree-law and statutes for the institute have been prepared with support from the CPLP network and submitted for approval.

Sousa noted that the country has a National Reference Laboratory capable of producing scientific evidence for the future institute, equipped with human resources and diagnostic tools for molecular biology and other analyses.

Why it mattersSao Tome and Principe's health system needs a National Institute of Public Health to strengthen its response to health challenges, including continental outbreaks like Ebola and MPOX.

Sao Tome court to launch digital accounting system in 2027

The Court of Accounts will introduce an electronic system for public entities and companies to submit financial reports digitally.

The Court of Accounts in Sao Tome and Principe will launch an electronic system in 2027 for public entities and companies to submit financial reports digitally. President Ricardino Costa Alegre announced that a new instruction will be issued for digital submission, replacing paper-based processes. The tool is in its final stages of development.

Costa Alegre stated that the system is expected to be operational by April 30, allowing all entities subject to reporting to do so digitally. The Court of Accounts, established in June 2003, is the supreme and independent body responsible for controlling public revenue and expenditure and auditing state accounts.

The Court of Accounts also holds the power to conduct preventive audits of public contracts. The digital system aims to streamline the accountability process for public sector organizations.
