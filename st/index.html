Sao Tome installs 8 water systems to cut shortages

The government is installing 8 modular water collection and distribution systems across the country. In Agostinho Neto, the systems will capture 100 cubic meters of treated water per hour, according to reports.

Why it mattersThe new infrastructure aims to reduce water scarcity for residents in affected localities.

Politics · 2026-08-21

Constitutional Court delays party list for Sept. 27 polls

The Constitutional Court of Sao Tome and Principe has not yet validated the list of political parties admitted to the Sept. 27 legislative elections. Under Article 41 of the electoral law, judges have 11 working days to decide on the admission of candidacies.

Why it mattersThe delay leaves the final field of competing parties unconfirmed as the election date approaches.

Sport · 2026-08-21

6 de Setembro leads national division with 30 points

The 15th round of the 1st division National Championship begins Friday, Aug. 21, with Agrosport de Monte Café facing Inter do Bombom. League leader 6 de Setembro holds 30 points heading into its Saturday match, according to STP Press.

Why it mattersThe upcoming matches determine if 6 de Setembro can maintain its lead over challengers like Caixão Grande and V.Riboque.

Director Jessica Lima joins FilmLab Mozambique

Sao Tomean director Jessica Adelaide Lombá da Silva e Lima has been selected for the first edition of FilmLab Mozambique. The intensive project development laboratory begins Aug. 24 in Maputo.

Why it mattersThe program aims to strengthen cinematic ties and professional development for the director in Mozambique.

Government organizes gastronomy fair for Aug. 23

The Ministry of Environment, Youth and Sustainable Tourism will hold the 'Grande Feira Gastronómica' this Sunday, Aug. 23. The event begins at 12:00 at Praça Yon Gato in the capital.
