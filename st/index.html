Sao Tome and Principe records second-worst health indicator decline in Africa

The nation's health system faces infrastructure and staffing challenges, exacerbated by rising non-communicable diseases.

São Tomé e Príncipe registered the second-largest deterioration in health indicators in Africa between 2016 and 2025, according to the 2026 Ibrahim Index of African Governance. This decline contrasts with the continental trend, where 40 out of 54 African countries improved their health system performance during the same period.

Health Minister Celso Matos acknowledged the long-evident degradation, citing fragile infrastructure, a shortage of health professionals, and an increase in non-communicable diseases like stroke and diabetes. Financial limitations hinder the provision of adequate care for these conditions.

The minister identified urgent challenges including infrastructure, equipment, medical supplies, human resources, endemic malaria, the changing epidemiological profile, health financing, and the organization of the national health system, all requiring resource mobilization.

Why it mattersThe health of citizens in São Tomé e Príncipe is deteriorating due to systemic issues and rising non-communicable diseases.

Sao Tome and Principe doubles number of women in parliament

18 women now hold seats in the National Assembly, representing 32.7% of deputies after the 2026 elections.

18 women won seats in the São Tomé and Príncipe National Assembly in the 2026 legislative elections, more than double the eight elected in 2022. These deputies now represent 32.7% of the 55 members of Parliament. The final election results were validated by the Constitutional Court on October 6, 2026.

The United Nations Development Programme (UNDP) confirmed the figures, noting this represents an advance in women's political participation. The elected women include Adjoa Soares, Arminda Candô, and Ayza da Silva, among others representing various constituencies and parties like MLSTP and ADI.

Despite the increase, the UNDP stated that gender parity has not yet been achieved. The organization highlighted the need to ensure women can fully exercise their mandates, take leadership roles, and participate in political decisions.

Why it mattersWomen in São Tomé and Príncipe have increased their representation in the National Assembly, affecting their ability to participate in political decisions.

Elackcio Afonso takes charge of Cantagalo district

The new district council president promised rigorous management and service recovery.

Elackcio Afonso was sworn in on Wednesday as the new President of the Cantagalo District Council. He pledged to transform the district into a model of perfect local government through rigorous management, internal controls, and accountability.

Afonso, who led the MLSTP list to victory in the September 27 local elections, announced immediate plans to restore waste collection, repair vehicles, and organize effective cleaning services in coordination with communities. He aims for transparent governance in his new role.

The new president outlined a strategic combination of agriculture, fishing, culture, and tourism to generate structural resources for the district's economy. He stated his commitment to building a better future for Cantagalo's city, towns, and communities.

Why it mattersThe new president of Cantagalo district, Elackcio Afonso, promises to improve waste collection, repair vehicles, and organize cleaning services.

Health · 2026-11-07

Pharmacist returns to disillusionment in Sao Tome

Evanilde Espírito Santo found pharmacies lacking medicine and hospitals in a worrying state upon her return from Portugal.

Evanilde Espírito Santo, a pharmaceutical sciences graduate, returned to Sao Tome and Principe after studying in Portugal but found the country disappointing. She stated that pharmacies lack medicines and hospitals face similar shortages, while roads are degraded and the health system is in a concerning situation.

Santo, who spent four years abroad, said she now better understands why many young Santomeans seek opportunities outside the country. Cristiano Dondo, president of the Federation of Non-Governmental Organizations of Sao Tome and Principe, added that employment is a primary challenge, alongside difficulties in accessing education, housing, and land.

The federation also noted issues with vocational training systems. Young people in Sao Tome and Principe face multiplying challenges with slow-arriving solutions.

Why it mattersEvanilde Espírito Santo returned to Sao Tome and Principe to find a health system lacking essential medicines and facing worrying conditions.

Sport · 2026-11-07

Vitória de Riboque hosts undefeated Caixão Grande

The championship title in Sao Tome and Principe's 1st division will be decided on Sunday.

Vitória de Riboque will host Caixão Grande on Sunday in the final match of the 1st division season. Caixão Grande enters the game undefeated, while Vitória de Riboque is one point behind the leaders. The championship title remains undecided among three teams: 6 de Setembro, Caixão Grande, and Vitória de Riboque, separated by one or two points.

The league standings show Despotivo 6 de Setembro in first place with 45 points, followed by B.U. Caixão Grande with 44 points and a game in hand. Vitória F.C. de Riboque is third with 43 points. Ké-Morabeza and Aliança Nacional have already been relegated to the 2nd division.

Other matches on Saturday include Juba against Agro Sport Monte Café and UDRA against Inter FC Bombom. Trindade FC will play UDESCAI on Saturday. On Sunday, Sporting Praia Cruz faces Aliança Nacional, and Ké Morabeza plays 6 de Setembro.
