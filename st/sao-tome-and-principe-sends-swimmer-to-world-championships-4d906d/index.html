São Tomé and Príncipe sends swimmer to world championships in Beijing

World Aquatics invited the country to compete from 1 to 6 December, after a two-year project built local swimming capacity.

Source: Tela Non · 2026-11-28 · 1 min read

São Tomé and Príncipe will compete at the World Swimming Championships in Beijing from 1 to 6 December 2026, following an invitation from World Aquatics, the sport’s international governing body.

The São Tomé and Príncipe Swimming Federation said the entry followed two years of work by its “São Tomé and Príncipe Swimming” project with Portugal’s Association of Swimming Coaches. The project trained coaches, supported a local club and swimming school, and gave swimmers international competition opportunities. José Dias, a swimming teacher and swimmer trained in São Tomé and Príncipe, prepared in Portugal during October with several clubs.

The federation’s president, Angélico dos Santos, said the participation involved local and international partners, volunteers and coaches. The championships run through 6 December.

Why it mattersJosé Dias and the national federation will take part in the Beijing championships from 1 to 6 December after the invitation.
