Sao Tome youth council demands effective public policies

The National Youth Council seeks real job opportunities, training, and housing for young people facing emigration.

Source: Jornal Tropical · 2026-11-09 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

The Sao Tome and Principe National Youth Council (CNJ) on Thursday demanded "effective public policies" to create real opportunities for young people. The council stated that young people need concrete results, including employment programs, vocational training aligned with market needs, entrepreneurship support, access to credit, affordable housing, and effective participation in decision-making processes.

The CNJ expressed its disappointment with the government's handling of Youth Day, withdrawing from the central event due to communication and organizational failures. The council emphasized the need for a National Youth Strategy that moves beyond paper to effect tangible change, urging institutions to listen more to youth concerns and commit to clear goals, resources, and deadlines. They called for youth to be placed at the center of national priorities.

The organization highlighted that many young Sao Tomeans lack employment, dignified opportunities, and the means to realize their life projects, often lacking hope for their future in their homeland. This reality, coupled with persistent issues in basic services like water, energy, health, and housing, forces many young people to see emigration not as an option but as a necessity, representing a loss of national growth and development capacity.

Why it mattersYoung Sao Tomeans are facing a lack of opportunities, leading many to consider emigration as a necessity rather than a choice.
