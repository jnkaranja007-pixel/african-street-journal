Sao Tome and Principe government seeks communications advisor

The government is hiring a communications assistant for a youth employment project funded by the International Development Association.

Source: Tela Non · 2026-10-23 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

The Government of Sao Tome and Principe (GoSTP) is seeking to recruit a communications assistant consultant for the Youth Employment Opportunities Project (POEJ). This recruitment is funded by a grant from the International Development Association (IDA).

The IDA grant will cover project costs for the POEJ, which aims to create employment opportunities for young people. The consultant will support communication efforts related to this initiative. Other related procurements include vehicles for the Directorate of Technical and Vocational Education and Training and a specialist consultant for micro, small, and medium enterprises.

The Project SIAS also announced an extension for applications for an agro-economy technician. These recruitments and extensions indicate efforts to develop various sectors within the country.

Why it mattersThe government is hiring a communications assistant for a youth employment project, impacting young people seeking jobs.
