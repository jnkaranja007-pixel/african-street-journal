São Tomé swears in ADI leader Américo Ramos as prime minister

President Carlos Vila Nova appointed Ramos after his party presented an agreement giving it support from 29 lawmakers, despite MLSTP objections.

Sources: STP Press, Noticias Lusofonas and Jornal Tropical · 2026-11-18 · 1 min read

Américo Ramos, leader of the Independent Democratic Action party (ADI), was sworn in again as prime minister of São Tomé and Príncipe on Monday, 16 November, at a ceremony led by President Carlos Vila Nova. ADI came second in the 27 September legislative election with 23 seats, behind the Movement for the Liberation of São Tomé and Príncipe (MLSTP), which won 24.

Vila Nova’s decree said he appointed Ramos because ADI demonstrated parliamentary support through an agreement that gave it an absolute majority of 29 lawmakers. The MCI-PUN party grouping won six seats and BASTA won two. MLSTP contested the appointment and called on the president to reject any government led by ADI.

MLSTP lawmakers have also asked the Constitutional Court to annul the presidential decree. The party argued that the president failed to follow the constitutional rule requiring him to consider election results after consulting parliamentary parties; the presidency said MLSTP had not provided a written guarantee of governing support when consulted first.

Why it mattersThe Constitutional Court is being asked to rule on the appointment, after MLSTP challenged the decree that installed a prime minister backed by 29 lawmakers.
