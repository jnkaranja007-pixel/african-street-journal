Príncipe swears in Nascimento's five-secretary regional government

Prime Minister Américo Ramos pledged central-government support as the new administration takes office after winning six of nine regional seats.

Source: STP Press · 2026-11-28 · 1 min read

São Tomé and Príncipe's Prime Minister Américo Ramos swore in the new regional government of Príncipe on Friday, 27 November, with Filipe Nascimento as its president and five regional secretaries. Ramos said the central government would provide the support needed for the island to grow, with more opportunities, more jobs, better infrastructure and the protection of its natural heritage.

Nascimento said his administration would open a new cycle of progress and transformation for Príncipe, based on a change of mindset and greater demands at work. He said the regional government was counting on the Republic's government and defended a responsible, constructive and solidary autonomy. He pledged greater transparency and strict management of public resources, saying the government wanted action and rigour rather than promises.

Nascimento's UMPP, the Unidade Mais Pela Pátria party, won six of the nine regional seats in the 27 September election, while the Movimento Verde, led by Nestor Umbelina, took three. The five secretaries cover finance and economic coordination, infrastructure, environment and land use, education, culture and sport, health, equality and social protection, and agriculture, fisheries and trade.

Why it mattersNascimento's administration must now manage public resources under its stated commitment to transparency, with support from the central government in Tomé.
