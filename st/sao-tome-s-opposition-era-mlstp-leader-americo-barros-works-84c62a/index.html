Sao Tome's opposition-era MLSTP leader Américo Barros works the room at a party assembly

A Tela Non columnist who had dismissed Barros as a politician took a different view after watching him speak to members while walking among them.

Source: Tela Non · 2026-11-19 · 1 min read

Américo Barros, the economist who leads the Movement for the Liberation of São Tomé and Príncipe (MLSTP), a long-standing party in the country, addressed members at a district assembly, according to a column published by the outlet Tela Non on Tuesday.

The columnist said Barros moved around the hall and kept his eyes on the members as he spoke, rather than sitting at the party's stage decorated with its flag. The writer, who had earlier criticised Barros for his alliance with the prime minister, said the approach changed his opinion.

The column gave no date for the assembly or its exact location, and did not describe any decisions or announcements made at it.

Why it mattersParty members at the assembly, and voters judging the party ahead of the elections, are shown a leader who works among them in person rather than from a stage.
