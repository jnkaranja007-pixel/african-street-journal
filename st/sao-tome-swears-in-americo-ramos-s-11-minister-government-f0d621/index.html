São Tomé swears in Américo Ramos’s 11-minister government

President Carlos Vila Nova installed the cabinet on 19 November, with eight new ministers and three ministers retained from the previous government.

Sources: STP Press and Jornal Tropical · 2026-11-20 · 1 min read

São Tomé and Príncipe’s 20th government, led by Prime Minister Américo Ramos, was sworn in on 19 November by President Carlos Vila Nova. The cabinet has 11 ministers and one secretary of state; eight ministers are new, while three served in the previous government.

The returning ministers are Nelson Mário de Carvalho Rosa Cardoso, for public works, transport and natural resources; Nilton Garrido de Sousa Pontes, for agriculture, fisheries and the blue economy; and Isabel Maria Correia Viegas de Abreu, for education, culture and science. Ramos said he would serve all São Toméans, including people who voted for other parties or abstained.

Ramos’s party, the Independent Democratic Action (ADI), won 23 seats in the 27 September election, while the Liberation Movement of São Tomé and Príncipe (MLSTP) won 24. ADI secured an agreement with the Citizens’ Movement of Independents–Socialist Party/Party of Unity Xavier, which has six seats, giving the government’s supporters 29 lawmakers. MLSTP asked the Constitutional Court to annul Ramos’s appointment.

Why it mattersRamos’s government must rely on the 29 lawmakers supporting its agreement to govern, after the MLSTP asked the Constitutional Court to annul his appointment.
