Sao Tome president gives MLSTP 24 hours to name prime minister

President Carlos Vila Nova wants proof that the nominee would have enough support in the National Assembly to pass the government's programme.

Sources: STP Press and Tela Non · 2026-11-13 · 1 min read

President Carlos Vila Nova has invited the MLSTP party, which won the most seats in the September 27 legislative elections but without an absolute majority, to name a prime minister within 24 hours. The president's office said the nominee must be sent in writing and accompanied by evidence of parliamentary backing.

The presidency said the nominee must show enough political support in the National Assembly to secure approval of the government's programme and allow it to carry out its duties. The MLSTP, led by Américo Barros, won 24 seats and has claimed the right to form the next government. The presidency said the demand was made to safeguard national interests and promote political stability.

The outgoing XIX Constitutional Government remains in office until a new prime minister is appointed and sworn in, and may only carry out acts needed for routine public business. The government's resignation followed the opening of the new legislative term, which began on the same day the presidency issued its statement.
