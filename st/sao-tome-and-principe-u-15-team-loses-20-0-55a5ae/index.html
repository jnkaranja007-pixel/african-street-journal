Sao Tome and Principe U-15 team loses 20-0

The national U-15 football team conceded 45 goals in five matches during recent international competition.

Source: Tela Non · 2026-11-02 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Sao Tome and Principe's U-15 football team suffered a 20-0 defeat in a recent international competition, scoring only two goals across five games.

Humiliating results have become a pattern for the island nation's teams in international competitions, mirroring a 1976 loss where the senior team was defeated 11-0 by the People's Republic of Congo.

Despite receiving millions of dollars from FIFA, the Sao Tomean Football Federation faces criticism for a lack of strategic options and organization, with a failure to host national championships for youth age groups.

Why it mattersSao Tome and Principe's national U-15 football team's poor performance in international competitions affects national pride and raises questions about the country's sports development.
