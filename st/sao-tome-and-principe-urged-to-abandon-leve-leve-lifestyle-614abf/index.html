Sao Tome and Principe urged to abandon 'leve-leve' lifestyle

A writer reflects on the island nation's relaxed pace of life and calls for a reevaluation of how the country is built.

Source: Tela Non · 2026-10-26 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

A writer in Sao Tome and Principe is calling for a reevaluation of the nation's approach to life, work, and development, suggesting it is time to move beyond the 'leve-leve' (slowly, slowly) philosophy.

The author expresses admiration for poet Dona Alda do Espírito Santo and musician Kalu Mendes, whose work embodies the Santomean identity of tranquility and living without constant pressure. Observing life in Angolares, the writer notes the unhurried movements of people and fishermen, who wait for the right moment and the right wave before acting.

While acknowledging the beauty and intelligence found in patience and respecting natural rhythms, the writer questions whether this relaxed attitude hinders progress and the building of the country's future.
