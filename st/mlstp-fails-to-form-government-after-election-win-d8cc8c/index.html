MLSTP fails to form government after election win

The party secured the most seats but could not secure a parliamentary majority.

Source: Tela Non · 2026-11-16 · 1 min read

The Movement for the Liberation of Sao Tome and Principe (MLSTP) failed to form a government after winning the most seats in the September 27 legislative elections. The MLSTP secured 24 deputies, while the ADI party followed with 23, and the MCI-PS/PUN coalition with 6.

President Carlos Vila Nova gave the MLSTP 30 days to secure a parliamentary majority, a task they could not complete. The ADI, in contrast, formed an alliance with the MCI-PS/PUN coalition, securing 29 deputies. This allowed the ADI to present Dr. Américo d’Oliveira dos Ramos for Prime Minister, who was appointed on November 12, 2026.

The MLSTP also lost out on leadership positions in the National Assembly to the ADI and its coalition partner. The article suggests that the MLSTP leader should consider stepping down and calling an extraordinary congress to elect new leadership to avoid further political isolation.

Why it mattersThe MLSTP, despite winning the most seats, failed to form a government, leading to the appointment of a Prime Minister from the second-place party.
