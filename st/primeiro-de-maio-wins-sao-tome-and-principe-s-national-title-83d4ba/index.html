Primeiro de Maio wins São Tomé and Príncipe’s national title

The Príncipe team beat 6 de Setembro 3-0 in the second leg, taking the two-match final 3-2 after losing the first game 2-0.

Sources: Tela Non and STP Press · 2026-11-24 · 1 min read

Primeiro de Maio, a football team from the Autonomous Region of Príncipe, won São Tomé and Príncipe’s national championship after beating 6 de Setembro 3-0 in the second leg at the National 12 de Julho Stadium. The club had lost the first leg 2-0, so the two-match final ended 3-2 on aggregate.

Jaymilson scored twice and Tó added the other goal in the first half. The additional report from STP Press said Jaymilson scored in the 18th minute and again in the 33rd, with Tó scoring in the 23rd. 6 de Setembro captain Tinho missed a penalty in the second half, which would have sent the final to extra time.

Jaymilson finished the competition as its top scorer, with 22 goals during the season.

Why it mattersJaymilson finished as the competition’s top scorer with 22 goals, while Primeiro de Maio’s players and supporters celebrate the national championship.
