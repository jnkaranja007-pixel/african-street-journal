Sao Tome opposition takes government dispute to Constitutional Court

The Movement for the Liberation of Sao Tome and Principe (MLSTP) says the government formed after the election does not reflect how voters voted and vows further legal action.

Source: Tela Non · 2026-11-21 · 1 min read

The Movement for the Liberation of Sao Tome and Principe (MLSTP), the main opposition party, has asked the Constitutional Court to rule on whether acts it says distorted voters' will are constitutional, in the process that led to the formation of the XX Constitutional Government. The party says its election results give it the legitimacy to govern.

Party president Américo Barros said the MLSTP would wait calmly and with respect for the courts' independence for the will of voters to be respected. In a political statement read to parliament after Friday's plenary agenda was changed, the party said it would use every constitutional and legal mechanism to defend what it called democratic truth.

At the first plenary session of the XIII Legislature, lawmakers elected MLSTP deputy Arlindo Barbosa vice-president of the National Assembly, and set up the Permanent Commission and the composition of the parliament's standing specialist committees.

Why it mattersVoters who backed the MLSTP will see whether the Constitutional Court accepts the party's challenge to how the government was formed.
