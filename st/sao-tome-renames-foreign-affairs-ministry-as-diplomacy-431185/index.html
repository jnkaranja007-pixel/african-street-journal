São Tomé renames foreign-affairs ministry as diplomacy ministry

Presidential Decree No. 34/2026 sets the new government’s composition and changes the ministry’s name to Diplomacy, Cooperation and Diaspora.

Source: Tela Non · 2026-11-21 · 1 min read

São Tomé and Príncipe’s new government has renamed the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, Cooperation and Communities as the Ministry of Diplomacy, Cooperation and Diaspora. Presidential Decree No. 34/2026, dated 18 November, sets the composition of the XX Constitutional Government.

In an analysis published by Tela Non, a contributor argues that “diplomacy” describes only one tool of the ministry. The article says its responsibilities also include setting and carrying out foreign policy, managing bilateral and multilateral ties, negotiating international agreements, representing the country abroad and providing consular services to citizens.

The ministry’s stated remit also covers external economic relations and the country’s political and cultural profile. The article compares its new title with foreign-affairs or international-relations ministry names used in Portugal, Angola, Brazil, Spain, France and South Africa.

Why it mattersCitizens abroad rely on consular services provided by the ministry, whose title has changed under the new government.
