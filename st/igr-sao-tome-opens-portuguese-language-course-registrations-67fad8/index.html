IGR São Tomé Opens Portuguese Language Course Registrations

New classes for foreigners and nationals begin September 24, 2026, offering specialized Portuguese language training.

The Instituto Guimarães Rosa São Tomé has announced that registrations are now open for its upcoming Portuguese language courses. The new academic term is scheduled to commence on September 24, 2026, with several distinct programs available to cater to different learning needs.

Offerings include Portuguese as a Foreign Language (PLE) for non-native speakers aiming to improve communication, and Portuguese as an Intercultural Language (PLI) for nationals seeking to refine their language skills and qualify for the PEC-G program. A preparatory course for the Celpe-Bras exam is also available for those looking to understand its structure and requirements.

Interested individuals are encouraged to secure their spots promptly. For inquiries and registration, prospective students can contact the institute via WhatsApp at +239 2223796 or by email at igr.saotome@itamaraty.gov.br. The institute aims to facilitate better communication and cultural understanding through language acquisition.

Why it mattersForeign nationals and São Toméan citizens can improve their Portuguese language skills through specialized courses offered by the Instituto Guimarães Rosa São Tomé.
