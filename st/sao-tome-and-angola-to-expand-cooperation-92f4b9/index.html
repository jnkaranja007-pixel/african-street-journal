Sao Tome and Angola to expand cooperation

Ministers expressed intent to revitalize dialogue and broaden partnerships in strategic sectors.

Source: Tela Non · 2026-11-17 · 1 min read

Sao Tome and Principe and Angola intend to boost bilateral cooperation by resuming dialogue, implementing signed agreements, and expanding partnerships into strategic development areas. This intention was stated by Sao Tome and Principe's Minister of State for Foreign Affairs, Cooperation and Communities, Ilza Amado Vaz, and Angola's Ambassador to Sao Tome and Principe, Fidelino Pelicanga, during Angola's 51st Independence Day celebrations on November 11.

Minister Vaz advocated for leveraging the countries' complementary strengths and experiences, focusing cooperation on training, institutional capacity building, and youth empowerment. She highlighted agreements signed in 2025 concerning vocational training, employment promotion, labor inspection, and social security, as well as a 2026 protocol on higher education.

The minister proposed revitalizing business relations through bilateral legal instruments to stimulate investment and create a favorable business environment, identifying opportunities in energy, oil, health, agriculture, tourism, and the blue economy. She also noted the need for increased coordination and information sharing on maritime security in the Gulf of Guinea.

Why it mattersSao Tome and Principe's foreign minister proposed expanding cooperation with Angola into strategic sectors like energy and agriculture, aiming for concrete benefits for both populations.
