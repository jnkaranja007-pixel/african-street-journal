Sao Tome and Principe culture showcased at Portugal festival

The Artes à Solta festival in Coimbra, Portugal, will feature traditional theatre, dance, and music from the island nation.

Source: Jornal Tropical · 2026-11-05 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

The Artes à Solta festival in Coimbra, Portugal, will highlight the culture and heritage of Sao Tome and Principe from November 13 to 15. The event will include traditional Sao Tomean theatre, known as tchiloli, which is recognized by UNESCO as Intangible Cultural Heritage, and the popular dance bulawê. The festival, in its second edition, previously focused on Cape Verde.

Diogo Coelho, executive director of Golden Skill, the company organizing the event, stated that conferences will cover Sao Tome and Principe's historic plantations called roças and its biosphere reserve. Santomean researcher and musician Hector Costa will present on tchiloli, followed by a concert described as an 'acoustic journey' through the archipelago's sounds. The festival is a partnership with the Sao Tome and Principe Embassy in Portugal.

Esterline Gonçalves Género, the Sao Tome and Principe ambassador to Portugal, attended the press conference and noted the festival's role in promoting the country's culture and heritage. The festival program, which is free to attend, is still being finalized and will be announced on the event's social media channels soon.

Why it mattersThe festival in Coimbra will present Sao Tome and Principe's cultural heritage, including UNESCO-recognized tchiloli theatre, to an international audience.
