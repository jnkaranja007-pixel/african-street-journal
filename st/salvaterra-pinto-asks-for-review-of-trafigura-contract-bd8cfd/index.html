Salvaterra Pinto asks for review of Trafigura contract process

The request to the attorney general's office and the state contracts body concerns the process associated with the company's hiring.

Source: Tela Non · 2026-11-23 · 1 min read

Luisélio Salvaterra Pinto has asked São Tomé and Príncipe's attorney general's office and the Cossil, the national contracts oversight body, to verify the process associated with the hiring of Trafigura. The report describes the matter as a request for verification, not a finding about the process.

The request concerns a contract process involving Trafigura, but the report gives no details of the contract, its value or the grounds for the request. It does not say whether either body has begun a review or responded.

The report also carries commentary on political power and a statement about the nature of a presidential candidate's independence; it does not link either item to the request concerning Trafigura.

Why it mattersThe attorney general's office and the Cossil must now decide whether to examine how Trafigura was hired.
