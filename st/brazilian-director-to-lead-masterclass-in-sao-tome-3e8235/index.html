Brazilian director to lead masterclass in São Tomé

Juliane Almeida will discuss character creation in fiction on November 13.

Source: Jornal Tropical · 2026-11-11 · 1 min read

The Instituto Guimarães Rosa São Tomé will host Brazilian filmmaker Juliane Almeida for a masterclass on Friday, November 13. The session, titled “Construção de Personagens na Ficção” (Character Construction in Fiction), will explore the creative processes behind developing fictional characters.

Almeida will guide participants through the techniques and considerations involved in bringing fictional beings to life. Following the masterclass, attendees will have the opportunity to watch the series “Criança de Raíz” (Child of Root), which was directed by Almeida.

The event aims to foster creative development within São Tomé and Príncipe's artistic community. The Instituto Guimarães Rosa serves as a cultural bridge, offering educational and artistic exchanges.

Why it mattersFilmmakers and aspiring writers in São Tomé and Príncipe can learn about character development.
