São Tomé prime minister promises digital channels for public input

Américo Ramos made the pledge after the first meeting of the 20th government, while saying officials would explain decisions and accept responsibility.

Source: STP Press · 2026-11-26 · 1 min read

São Tomé Prime Minister Américo Ramos said the 20th government would create regular digital channels for public suggestions and complaints after its first cabinet meeting in São Tomé on Tuesday. He said the government would focus on carrying out its plans and showing progress step by step.

Ramos said officials would explain decisions in clear language and take responsibility when things went wrong. He also said the government would explain difficult measures and make the project available to young people seeking opportunities without leaving the country.

Ramos expressed solidarity with families affected by Sunday's rains and said he was mobilising resources to support those worst hit.

Why it mattersPeople affected by Sunday's rains are to receive support as Ramos mobilises resources for those worst hit.
