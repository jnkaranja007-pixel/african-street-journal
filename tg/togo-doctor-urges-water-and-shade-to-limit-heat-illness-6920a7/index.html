Togo doctor urges water and shade to limit heat illness

General practitioner Jean Claude Bakpatina advises adults to drink two to three litres daily in hot weather, adjusted for health and activity.

Source: 24heureinfo · 2026-11-19 · 1 min read

Togo general practitioner Jean Claude Bakpatina has advised people to drink water regularly, limit time in direct sun and watch vulnerable groups during periods of high temperatures. Children, pregnant women, older people, people with chronic illnesses and outdoor workers face particular risks, he told the Togolese News Agency in an interview.

Bakpatina recommends seeking shade and moving strenuous activity to cooler hours. Outdoor workers who cannot stop work should take more shade breaks and drink regularly. He also advises closing curtains during hot hours and airing rooms when outside temperatures fall. Signs of dehydration include intense thirst, dry mouth, dark or reduced urine, headache, unusual fatigue and dizziness.

For adults, he gives an indicative daily intake of two to three litres of drinks, adjusted for activity, weather and health. People with heart or kidney conditions who have fluid restrictions should follow their doctors’ instructions. Confusion, severe illness, convulsions or loss of consciousness require immediate medical help.
