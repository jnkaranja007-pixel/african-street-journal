Togo launches third male health campaign

Novembre Bleu Togo aims to reach rural communities with prostate cancer awareness and screening.

Source: Icilome · 2026-11-09 · 2 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Togo has launched the third edition of its national "Novembre Bleu Togo" campaign, which began Friday in Lomé with a hybrid webinar. Running until November 30, 2026, the initiative, organized by Bébé Trésor and Smart Group Togo, focuses on male health, particularly prostate cancer, under the theme "From the city to the village, let's act for men's health!". This year, the campaign specifically targets rural communities.

Christelle Agnindom, coordinator of Novembre Bleu, highlighted the campaign's purpose, comparing it to "Pink October" for women and noting that male health initiatives are less numerous. She described prostate cancer as a "silent killer" often diagnosed late, leading to preventable deaths. Agnindom expressed satisfaction with progress since the campaign's 2024 start, observing increased partner engagement and men beginning to discuss their health and seek medical advice.

According to the Plan Cancer Togo 2022-2025, prostate cancer accounts for 18.5% of cancer cases in the country, with Hôpital Dogta-Lafié diagnosing over 500 new cases annually. Dr. Sokpoli Jule, a sexologist, identified age (over 60) and family history as unmodifiable risk factors, while diet, physical activity, and exposure to pollution are modifiable. He urged men to recognize symptoms like frequent urination and seek screening via a PSA blood test from age 40.
