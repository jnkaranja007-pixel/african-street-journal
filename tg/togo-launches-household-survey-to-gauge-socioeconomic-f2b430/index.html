Togo Launches Household Survey to Gauge Socioeconomic Conditions

The National Institute of Statistics will collect data on income, employment, education, and health from September to December 2026.

The National Institute of Statistics and Economic and Demographic Studies (INSEED) will begin a new household living conditions survey across Togo on September 7, 2026. This data collection effort, scheduled to run until December 2026, is part of the third Harmonized Survey on Household Living Conditions (EHCVM). The initiative is supported by the West African Economic and Monetary Union (UEMOA) and the World Bank.

Mobilized teams will gather information on household incomes and expenditures, employment status, educational attainment, and health indicators. The collected data aims to provide a more accurate measurement of the socioeconomic realities faced by Togolese populations. This operation is designed to update national poverty statistics and equip public authorities with reliable information.

The anticipated results will help refine national poverty statistics and offer policymakers concrete elements for better targeting and directing development policies and programs. The survey covers the entire Togolese territory, ensuring a comprehensive overview of living conditions nationwide.

Why it mattersThe survey will provide data on household incomes and expenditures, which is useful for understanding the economic situation of Togolese households.
