Togo power outages linked to gas pipeline maintenance

Electricity cuts across Togo are due to maintenance on the gas pipeline supplying power plants, with normal service expected by month's end.

Source: Icilome · 2026-10-22 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Several regions in Togo are experiencing electricity disruptions because of maintenance work on the gas pipeline that fuels some power plants. The Compagnie énergie électrique du Togo (CEET) stated that these operations, initially scheduled for October 12 to October 27, 2026, have been postponed multiple times. The CEET's director general, Ilagou Ayeva, indicated that a gradual return to normal power supply is anticipated before the end of October.

Despite efforts to use alternative energy sources to mitigate the impact, further power interruptions are possible during the maintenance period. The CEET is advising customers to unplug their electrical appliances during outages to prevent potential damage. This measure is part of an effort to manage the situation while awaiting improved electricity provision across the affected areas.

The maintenance aims to ensure the continued reliable operation of the gas pipeline, which is crucial for electricity generation. The CEET is urging users to exercise caution and patience as the work progresses. The company expects the situation to stabilize once the maintenance concludes, restoring consistent power supply to all customers.

Why it mattersBusinesses and households in Togo are affected by power outages that disrupt daily operations and could damage electrical equipment.
