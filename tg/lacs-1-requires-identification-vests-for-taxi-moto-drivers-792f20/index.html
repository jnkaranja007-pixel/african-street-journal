Lacs 1 requires identification vests for taxi-moto drivers

The municipal council approved the rule, and joint teams from the town hall and police will begin field checks after 30 November.

Source: L-Frii · 2026-11-26 · 1 min read

The municipality of Lacs 1 in Togo has made identification vests compulsory for all taxi-moto drivers operating in the commune. Drivers have until 30 November 2026 to comply.

Mayor Me Alexis John C. D. Aquereburu announced the requirement in a statement published on 23 November, following a municipal council resolution. The town hall said joint teams from the municipality and police will check drivers in the field after the deadline.

Drivers found without the required vest face sanctions under current regulations. The municipality said the measure is intended to improve organisation of the taxi-moto sector, protect users and strengthen public order.

Why it mattersTaxi-moto drivers in Lacs 1 must meet the 30 November deadline before joint municipal and police checks begin, with sanctions for non-compliance.
