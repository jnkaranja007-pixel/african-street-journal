Togo president attends Francophonie summit in Cambodia

Faure Essozimna Gnassingbé joined other leaders in Phnom Penh for the 20th Francophonie Summit, focusing on peace and dialogue.

Sources: Icilome and 24heureinfo · 2026-11-16 · 1 min read

Togo's President Faure Essozimna Gnassingbé attended the 20th Francophonie Summit in Phnom Penh, Cambodia, on Sunday, November 15, 2026. Leaders from the French-speaking world gathered to discuss peace, dialogue, solidarity, and partnership.

The summit's opening ceremony was presided over by the King of Cambodia, Norodom Sihamoni, and included leaders such as French President Emmanuel Macron and Cambodian Prime Minister Hun Manet. A key moment was the official handover of the summit's presidency from France to Cambodia.

Discussions at the summit focused on preventing conflict, strengthening cooperation, promoting cultural diversity, and addressing youth employment. Leaders also held closed-door consultations on the Organisation internationale de la Francophonie's (OIF) activities and strategic orientations.

Why it mattersTogo's president participated in high-level discussions on peace and cooperation within the Francophonie space.
