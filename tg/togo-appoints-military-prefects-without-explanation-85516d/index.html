Togo appoints military prefects without explanation

The government has appointed military officers to lead several prefectures, sparking calls for transparency.

Source: Icilome · 2026-11-14 · 1 min read

Togo has appointed military officers to head several prefectures without providing official explanations for the choices.

The newspaper Sika’a suggests these appointments may be linked to the delicate security situation in the sub-region, particularly the threat of terrorism. While military experience can be an asset against terrorism, leading a prefecture also requires administrative, local development, and population engagement skills beyond security concerns.

The government has been urged to account for its decisions, as citizens have a right to understand the motivations and expected outcomes of these appointments, which should prioritize effective governance and citizen well-being.
