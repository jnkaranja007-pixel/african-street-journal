Togo launches third prostate cancer awareness campaign

Novembre Bleu Togo aims to reach rural communities with health information and screenings until November 30.

Source: Icilome · 2026-11-10 · 1 min read

Togo has launched its third annual "Novembre Bleu Togo" campaign to raise awareness about male health, specifically prostate cancer. The initiative, which began on Friday in Lomé, will run until November 30, 2026, with the theme "From city to village, let's act for men's health!" This year's campaign focuses on reaching rural communities.

Christelle Agnindom, coordinator of Novembre Bleu, highlighted that prostate cancer is a "silent killer" often diagnosed late, leading to preventable deaths. She noted progress since the campaign's 2024 inception, with increased partner involvement and men beginning to discuss their health and seek consultations. The campaign aligns with UN Sustainable Development Goals 3, 5, and 10.

Activities include a "Blue Space" for consultations and screenings in Grand Lomé on November 14-15, and awareness tours in various regions until November 26. A digital campaign runs from November 10-25, and the campaign culminates on November 28 with a morning run and an evening event. A final press briefing is scheduled for December 5.

Why it mattersTogolese men, particularly in rural areas, are targeted for health information and screenings to combat late diagnoses of prostate cancer.
