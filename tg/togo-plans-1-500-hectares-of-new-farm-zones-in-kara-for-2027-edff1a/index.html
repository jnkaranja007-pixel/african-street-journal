Togo plans 1 500 hectares of new farm zones in Kara for 2027

The state programme also provides for 1 500 hectares of lowlands, support for 155 existing zones and the opening of seven processing centres.

Source: 24heureinfo · 2026-11-23 · 1 min read

Togo's 2027-2029 state programme budget provides for 1 500 hectares of new planned agricultural development zones in the Kara region in 2027, alongside development of 1 500 hectares of identified lowlands. The plan also includes upgrades to 155 existing zones, targeting agricultural basins in Oti and Kara.

The programme says the investments are intended to improve producers' capacity and working conditions. It also sets a 2027 goal of putting seven already-built agricultural processing centres into operation and establishing three rice-processing units.

Plans include infrastructure at the agro-park and contracts linking producers, processors and exporters. The Senate approved an agricultural framework bill on 19 November 2026, after its first reading in the National Assembly.

Why it mattersProducers in the Oti and Kara basins are included in a programme for new and upgraded farm zones, with seven processing centres targeted to begin operating in 2027.
