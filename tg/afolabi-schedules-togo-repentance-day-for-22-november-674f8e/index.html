Afolabi schedules Togo repentance day for 22 November

The nationwide event calls on Togolese to seek forgiveness and pray for peace, with its closing gathering at the Centre for Sports and Recreation (CERFER) grounds in Lomé.

Source: Icilome · 2026-11-21 · 1 min read

Habib Barth Afolabi, a Christian servant and ambassador based in Togo, has initiated a National Day of Repentance and Reconciliation for Sunday, 22 November 2026, across the country. The initiative asks Togolese to acknowledge their wrongdoing, seek forgiveness and pray for peace and the country's restoration.

The day's theme is "Sincere repentance attracts God's mercy." Afolabi's appeal names corruption, injustice, divisions, hatred and tribalism as problems facing society, and invites all parts of society to take part.

The day's climax is scheduled at the CERFER grounds in Lomé from 15h to 18h. Afolabi said spiritual awakening begins in people's hearts, not in stadiums.

Why it mattersAfolabi and participants are due to gather at the CERFER grounds in Lomé from 15h to 18h on 22 November.
