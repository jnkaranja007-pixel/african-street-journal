Togo opens 17-day Lomé trade fair with India as guest of honour

The event brings exhibitors, investors and business operators from Togo and other countries to CETEF TOGO, a conference and exhibition centre in Lomé, through 13 December.

Source: Icilome · 2026-11-28 · 1 min read

Togo's 21st Lomé International Fair, the Foire internationale de Lomé (FIL), opened on Friday, 27 November, at CETEF TOGO, a conference and exhibition centre in Lomé, launching 17 days of business meetings through 13 December. India is the guest of honour, and exhibitors, investors and business operators from Togo and several other countries are attending.

Economy Minister Badanam Patoki said the fair should connect businesses, promote investment and support private-sector development. He urged Togolese companies to use it to reach new markets. Alexandre de Souza, director-general of CETEF TOGO, said the fair's success should be judged by opportunities created, not only visitor numbers.

This edition includes e-FIL digital services, payment security, stand geolocation and accessibility measures. Delegations from India, China and Benin are present; organisers say they want meetings to produce lasting partnerships and investment opportunities.

Why it mattersTogolese companies have until 13 December to use the fair to pursue new markets, while investors can meet businesses from several countries.
