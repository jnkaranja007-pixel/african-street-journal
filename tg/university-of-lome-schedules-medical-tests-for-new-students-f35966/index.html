University of Lomé schedules medical tests for new students

Students must register online and pay analysis fees at Ecobank before attending the 7 a.m. appointment on Monday, 23 November.

Source: 24heureinfo · 2026-11-22 · 1 min read

The University of Lomé expects new students at the Centre des œuvres universitaires de Lomé (COUL), the university's student services centre, on Monday, 23 November, for medical tests required to complete registration for the 2026-2027 academic year. Appointments begin at 7 a.m. at the Bella Bellow stage.

The university says the tests apply to new high-school graduates and new students at all levels. Students must first book through the university's PAUL online platform, in its "Medical analyses" section, and download the required forms. They then pay the analysis fees at an Ecobank branch and return to the platform to retrieve their payment and appointment forms before going to the centre.

The medical visit is a prerequisite for receiving a student card, according to the university.
