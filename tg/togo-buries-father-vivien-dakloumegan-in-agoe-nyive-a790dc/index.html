Togo buries Father Vivien Dakloumegan in Agoè-Nyivé

The Lomé archdiocese priest, who died aged 51 on 19 October, was buried at the cemetery for priests of Petit Séminaire Saint Pie X.

Source: 24heureinfo · 2026-11-21 · 1 min read

Father Vivien Dakloumegan, a priest of the Archdiocese of Lomé, was buried on Friday, 20 November 2026, at the priests’ cemetery of Petit Séminaire Saint Pie X in Agoè-Nyivé, Togo. He was 51 when he died on 19 October.

The funeral Mass took place at Cristo Risorto parish in Hédzranawoé, attended by relatives, friends, worshippers and clergy. The funeral ceremonies ran from 18 to 20 November, beginning with a requiem Mass at Notre-Dame de la Miséricorde parish in Anyron and including a prayer-and-song vigil in Hédzranawoé.

Dakloumegan was ordained on 22 December 2012 and served as parish priest at Notre-Dame de la Miséricorde. He also shared religious teachings on social media.

Why it mattersTogo’s Catholic community completed the priest’s funeral rites over three days, from 18 to 20 November, with his burial in Agoè-Nyivé.
