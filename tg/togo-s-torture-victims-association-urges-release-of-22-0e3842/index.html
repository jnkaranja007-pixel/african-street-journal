Togo's torture victims' association urges release of 22 detainees

The Association of Victims of Torture in Togo (ASVITTO) says 18 court decisions have not been enforced and asks for detainees' release and compensation before June 2027.

Source: Icilome · 2026-11-23 · 1 min read

The Association of Victims of Torture in Togo (ASVITTO) told United Nations member states that 22 people remain arbitrarily detained in Togo, and urged their release and compensation before June 2027. Its president, Kao Atcholi, spoke on 17 November at a pre-session of the Universal Periodic Review focused on Togo.

ASVITTO counts 18 people whose court decisions have not been enforced, two whose cases are covered by an unimplemented opinion from the UN Working Group on Arbitrary Detention, and two held because of their opinions. The group noted that several detainees were freed on 31 December 2025.

On torture allegations linked to the June 2025 demonstrations, ASVITTO said about 10 victims had filed complaints at Lomé's military court and paid bail costs from September 2025, but no action had followed. It asked for an investigation within a year and reparations before June 2027.

Why it matters18 detainees whose court orders have not been carried out, and two held for their opinions, depend on the government freeing them and paying compensation before June 2027.
