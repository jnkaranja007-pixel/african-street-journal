Togo assigns road company maintenance target of 1 700 km for 2027

The target is in the 2027-2029 programme budget, while the government has not specified the cost of the work.

Source: 24heureinfo · 2026-11-25 · 1 min read

Togo’s government has set a 2027 target for maintaining 1 700 km of roads, according to the state’s 2027-2029 programme budget, as reported by Togo First. The work falls to the new National Road Financing Company, SONAFIR, which replaced the former road-maintenance financing company.

SONAFIR has been operating for a few weeks and has been led by Naka Gnassingbé de Souza since October 2026. The cost of maintaining the targeted roads has not been stated. The 2027 finance bill has not yet been finally adopted.

The government also plans studies on nearly 2 000 km of other routes. Road maintenance had previously relied mainly on fuel excise duties and toll revenue, which accounted for 70% and 30% of the former company’s resources, respectively.

Why it mattersSONAFIR must deliver its 2027 maintenance plan, but the work’s cost is unspecified and the finance bill still awaits final adoption.
