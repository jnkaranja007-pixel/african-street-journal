ECOWAS and AfDB launch 500 million dollar rice project

The regional initiative aims to boost local rice production and reduce import dependency across 14 countries.

Source: 24heureinfo · 2026-11-17 · 1 min read

The Economic Community of West African States (ECOWAS) and the African Development Bank launched a 500 million dollar initiative called the REWARD project on November 11, 2026, in Abuja, Nigeria. The programme forms part of a regional roadmap running from 2025 to 2035 to develop resilient rice value chains and improve food security.

The broader investment platform seeks to mobilise 850 million dollars across 14 member countries, reaching more than one million farmers with a focus on women and young people. Processing capacities, competitiveness, and sector organisation also form part of the plan.

Member countries including Togo face the task of translating the regional strategy into concrete investments for local farming operations, infrastructure, and processing facilities.

Why it mattersMore than one million farmers across 14 member countries, including Togo, are targeted by the investment platform aiming to mobilise 850 million dollars.
