Grand Lomé surveys households on waste collection in 13 communes

The survey compares current services with 2022 benchmarks set after the second phase of the Lomé Urban Environmental Project (PEUL II), which the French Development Agency.

Source: 24heureinfo · 2026-11-23 · 1 min read

Togo's National Institute of Statistics and Economic and Demographic Studies (INSEED) began a household survey on 21 November across the 13 communes of the Autonomous District of Grand Lomé, assessing waste collection and residents' sanitation practices. The fieldwork runs through 7 December.

Interviewers are asking residents about the quality of rubbish collection, how households dispose of waste, their sorting habits and waste recovery. The survey follows the second phase of the Lomé Urban Environmental Project (PEUL II), implemented with support from the French Development Agency, and will measure results against benchmarks set in 2022.

The district says enumerators will carry identification badges in neighbourhoods and asks residents to check each badge before answering. The findings are intended to show remaining service gaps and guide future waste-management work.

Why it mattersGrand Lomé households are asked to check interviewers' badges and report their waste-collection experience before the survey closes on 7 December.
