Togo launches first national industry week

The event aims to showcase domestic production from raw materials to finished goods.

Source: Icilome · 2026-11-10 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Togo is hosting its first National Industry Week from November 16 to November 22 in Lomé. The event, announced by the Republic's official website, seeks to answer the question of what the country produces and how. Visitors will tour factory production lines and meet industrialists, investors, and small and medium-sized enterprises.

The initiative comes six decades after independence and amid a long-standing family rule, prompting questions about the timing of such an inquiry into national production. The government's economic chronicle highlights this self-examination, which some view as a communication strategy for an industry that may be struggling, likening it to a stranded whale on a beach.

This event occurs as Togo receives clothing for schoolchildren from Turkey, raising questions about a nation celebrating its industry while relying on foreign imports for basic needs. The week aims to showcase what is manufactured locally, while the Turkish clothing implicitly answers that the country primarily produces speeches and ceremonies.

Why it mattersThe National Industry Week in Lomé aims to reveal what Togo produces domestically, affecting citizens' access to locally made goods.
