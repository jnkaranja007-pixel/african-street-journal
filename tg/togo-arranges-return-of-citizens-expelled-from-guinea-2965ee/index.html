Togo arranges return of citizens expelled from Guinea

Lomé is working with Burkina Faso and Mali and with its diplomatic and consular missions to bring home citizens held in reception centres after anti-illegal-mining operations.

Sources: 24heureinfo and Icilome · 2026-11-21 · 1 min read

Togo's government is arranging the return of citizens who had been living in Guinea and are now staying in reception centres in Burkina Faso and Mali, the foreign ministry said in a statement dated 19 November 2026. The ministry linked their distress to operations against illegal gold mining in Guinea.

Togolese officials are in talks with their counterparts in Burkina Faso and Mali, as well as the relevant diplomatic and consular missions, to provide support and organise a secure return. The government said arrangements were under way but gave neither the number of people involved nor a return timetable.

The government urged citizens, especially young people, to take care when travelling abroad and to follow national and international rules on migration and mining.

Why it mattersFamilies of Togolese stranded in Burkina Faso and Mali are left waiting for a return date that the government has not yet fixed.
