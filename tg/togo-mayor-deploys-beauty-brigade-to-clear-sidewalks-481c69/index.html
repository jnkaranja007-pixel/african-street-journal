Togo mayor deploys beauty brigade to clear sidewalks

Golfe 5 commune launches a two-week operation to remove goods and structures from priority streets.

Source: Icilome · 2026-11-03 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Golfe 5 Mayor Kossi Aboka launched a special street clearing operation on November 2, 2026, in Lomé. Instead of bulldozers, a new 'beauty brigade' will enforce government orders to clear priority roads in the Grand Lomé area until November 20.

The brigade, led by the third deputy mayor Koubalo Badjamla, includes local officials, city agents, and security representatives. Their task is to remove goods, containers, stalls, and other obstructions from sidewalks and road edges, a move aimed at improving the city's appearance.

The operation targets items like merchandise, fuel sales, and construction material deposits on public spaces. While the goal is a cleaner commune, the brigade has not yet publicly stated where displaced vendors will relocate.

Why it mattersResidents of Golfe 5 will experience cleaner streets, while vendors may face disruption to their livelihoods.
