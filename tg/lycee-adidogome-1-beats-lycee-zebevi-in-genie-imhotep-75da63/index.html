Lycée Adidogomé 1 beats Lycée Zébévi in Génie Imhotep

Adidogomé 1 scored 410 points to defeat Zébévi's 370 in the third season of the 'Génie Imhotep' quiz competition.

Source: Icilome · 2026-11-01 · 1 min read

Lycée Adidogomé 1 defeated Lycée Zébévi on Saturday, October 31, 2026, in the fourth round of the third season of the 'Génie Imhotep' quiz competition. The match took place at the Prof James studio of SOS Docteur TV in Lomé.

Students from Lycée Zébévi, located in the Lacs prefecture, scored 370 points, while their counterparts from Lycée Adidogomé 1, from the Grand Lomé region, achieved 410 points. This loss follows a narrow victory for Zébévi against Tokoin 2 the previous week.

This third edition of the competition, supported by the United Nations Population Fund (UNFPA) and promoted by Professor Serge Michel Kodom of Aimes-Afrique, now includes six schools from the Maritime region alongside six from Lomé. The next match will feature Lycée Adidogomé 1 against Lycée Attitogon.

Why it mattersStudents in Togo are participating in an educational quiz competition that tests their knowledge.
