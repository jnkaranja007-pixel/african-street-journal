Algeria beat Togo 1-0 in CAN 2027 qualifier in Tizi Ouzou

Ramy Bensebaïni's header in the 76th minute decided the match at the Hocine-Aït-Ahmed stadium on Saturday, 14 November 2026.

Sources: 24heureinfo and RFI Afrique · 2026-11-16 · 1 min read

Togo's national team, the Sparrowhawks, lost 1-0 to Algeria on Saturday, 14 November 2026, in the third round of qualifying for the 2027 Africa Cup of Nations. The match was played at the Hocine-Aït-Ahmed stadium in Tizi Ouzou.

The Togolese side held firm for much of the game before Algerian defender Ramy Bensebaïni headed in the only goal in the 76th minute, from a cross by Hadj Moussa. Algeria took the three points in front of their own fans, while the result left Togo's players with a bitter taste, despite a solid organisation that kept the match close for most of the game.

Algeria now lead Group I of the qualifiers, RFI Afrique reported. The Sparrowhawks must turn the page quickly and prepare for the next stage of their qualifying campaign, with their chances of reaching the tournament still at stake.

Why it mattersTogo must now recover from this defeat and prepare for the next qualifying matches, with its chances at the 2027 tournament still at stake.
