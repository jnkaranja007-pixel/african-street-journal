Togo influencer raises 2 million FCFA for house fund

Maa Bio's online fundraiser for her home project collected nearly 2 million FCFA in two hours.

Source: L-Frii · 2026-10-30 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Influencer Maa Bio launched a fundraiser to symbolically finance the construction of her house, collecting nearly 2 million FCFA in just two hours. She announced that 15% of the collected amount will be donated to autistic children.

Maa Bio explained that her subscribers wanted to continue contributing to her project, viewing their financial support as a symbolic way to gift her a house. She stated that contributions would continue.

The influencer humorously addressed her financial situation, suggesting that acquiring property might be part of her future. The fundraiser was conducted via the Tikerama platform.

Why it mattersTogo's influencer Maa Bio is raising funds for her house and donating a portion to autistic children.
