ECOWAS and AfDB launch 500 million dollar rice project

The regional initiative aims to boost local rice production and reduce import dependency across 14 countries.

The Economic Community of West African States (ECOWAS) and the African Development Bank launched a 500 million dollar initiative called the REWARD project on November 11, 2026, in Abuja, Nigeria. The programme forms part of a regional roadmap running from 2025 to 2035 to develop resilient rice value chains and improve food security.

The broader investment platform seeks to mobilise 850 million dollars across 14 member countries, reaching more than one million farmers with a focus on women and young people. Processing capacities, competitiveness, and sector organisation also form part of the plan.

Member countries including Togo face the task of translating the regional strategy into concrete investments for local farming operations, infrastructure, and processing facilities.

Why it mattersMore than one million farmers across 14 member countries, including Togo, are targeted by the investment platform aiming to mobilise 850 million dollars.

Togo adds Ewe and Kabye as optional BAC 2 subjects

Candidates for the June 2027 baccalaureate exam can now sit papers in two national languages, Ewe and Kabye.

Candidates for the second part of Togo's baccalaureate, the BAC 2, will be able to sit their exam in Ewe or Kabye, two national languages. Until now, the optional language choices were foreign languages: Russian, Arabic, Latin, Spanish and German.

The measure was set out in a note from the director general of the baccalaureate office, addressed to school heads, teachers and candidates for the June 2027 session. The note says the aim is to promote and enhance these national languages. With the addition, the optional language pool rises to seven subjects.

Under the note, candidates may choose at most two optional subjects, and one of them must be a language. The second must come from the arts group of sewing, drawing and music. School heads have been asked to register specialist teachers who wish to apply to mark the papers, under criteria set out in a letter dated 9 November 2026.

Why it mattersStudents sitting the June 2027 baccalaureate can now offer Ewe or Kabye as their language subject instead of a foreign language.

Togo targets 1 000 vulnerable women for ID documents

The social action is part of the 2027-2029 budget-programme to reduce administrative barriers.

The government of Togo plans to provide identity documents to 1 000 vulnerable women and girls as part of its social actions for the year 2027. According to the ministry in charge of solidarities, the initiative falls under the 2027-2029 budget-programme projections designed to remove administrative obstacles faced by disadvantaged individuals. Official papers are necessary for citizens to access public services, secure official procedures, and claim their legal rights.

The ministry's projections also include distributing school kits to 4 500 orphans and children from impoverished families across the country. Authorities plan to place 72 young people vulnerable to trafficking into apprenticeships and provide care for 1 000 children who have experienced violence, abuse, and exploitation.

These planned measures are scheduled for implementation throughout the course of the year 2027. Officials stated that lacking official papers complicates administrative steps, schooling access, and social safety nets for vulnerable populations.

Politics · 2026-11-17

Gilbert Houngbo secures second term leading ILO

The former prime minister won 51 votes after his sole challenger withdrew from the race.

Gilbert Fossoun Houngbo, a former prime minister, will continue leading the International Labour Organization (ILO) after the governing body confirmed his reappointment on Monday, 16 November 2026. He secured 51 votes from the 58 eligible members on the board, which includes representatives from governments, workers, and employers. His new five-year term will officially begin on 1 October 2027 and run until 2032, following his initial election in 2022.

Yolanda Diaz, Spain's labor minister and second vice-president, withdrew her opposing candidacy shortly before the vote to preserve international union unity. Although she stepped down, Diaz criticized current management practices, alleging the institution experienced a deep crisis affecting social dialogue and workers' rights. She thanked several African and European countries, the Brazilian government, and Spanish unions for their support.

Following his re-election, the ILO director-general intends to focus on artificial intelligence as a central element for global social justice. 58 board members were authorized to participate in the electoral process, which concluded following multiple candidate withdrawals.

Why it mattersThe ILO director-general starts his new five-year term on 1 October 2027, following a vote of 51 out of 58 eligible board members.
