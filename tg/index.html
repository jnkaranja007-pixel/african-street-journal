Climate · 2026-11-13

Gnassingbé takes Togo's climate ambitions to COP31 summit in Antalya

Togo's president will push its energy transition and climate finance commitments among world leaders gathered in Türkiye.

Faure Gnassingbé, President of the Council, attended the opening of the World Leaders Summit of COP31 in Antalya, Türkiye, on Wednesday. The meeting brings together several heads of state and government and UN Secretary-General António Guterres to address climate change.

Gnassingbé was welcomed at the EXPO Antalya site by Turkish President Recep Tayyip Erdoğan and Guterres. Leaders at the opening called for greater international mobilisation, with emphasis on easing countries' access to climate finance and speeding up the energy transition.

Togo intends to present its commitments on energy transition, environmental protection and climate finance mobilisation, along with its sustainable development initiatives. Its aims include better protecting its people from climate impacts. The reports do not say what concrete actions will follow the pledges.

Why it mattersTogo's commitments on energy transition and climate finance are now on the table with international partners, the first step toward any funding it can secure.

Sport · 2026-11-13

Togo opens 2026 national Vététré championship with 13 clubs

13 clubs split across three zones will contest a traditional sport whose final round is set for 13 December.

The 2026 national Vététré championship, a traditional and popular discipline in Togo, opens on 15 November. 13 clubs are competing for the title across three geographical zones: Zio, Avé and Grand Lomé.

In the Zio zone, four clubs are entered: Volcan de Gapé Avélébé, Assilassimé de Gamé Nyativé, Dzobibi de Gapé Agokope and Chemin de Dieu de Gapé Kpodji. The Avé zone also has four: Dzobibi de Noèpé, Les Héros de Kovié Gbavé, Dieu Merci d’Ando Agové and Aménouvévé de Koudassi Gare.

The Grand Lomé zone is the largest, with five clubs: Aigle d’Approche, Blague à Part de Madjikpéto, Colombe de Sogbossito, Vainqueur d’Agoè Cacavéli and Wait And See de Légbassito. The final is scheduled for 13 December, after nearly a month of matches.

Why it mattersClubs from all three zones must win enough matches over the month to reach the final on 13 December.

Education · 2026-11-13

University of Lomé launches 2026-2027 doctoral year

The ceremony brought together researchers, doctoral students and partner universities, including Kara, Datcha and Winneba in Ghana, around the theme of interdisciplinarity.

The University of Lomé officially launched its 2026-2027 doctoral year on Wednesday, November 11, 2026, at its Auditorium. The ceremony brought together teacher-researchers, doctoral students, institutional leaders and delegations from partner universities.

Under the theme "Interdisciplinarity, innovation and artificial intelligence: scientific research serving the socio-economic transformation of the Togolese nation," the universities of Kara, Datcha and Winneba in Ghana took part. University president Kossivi Hounaké urged doctoral students to direct their work toward national realities and priorities while keeping to scientific rigour. Minister Delegate for Higher Education and Research Gado Tchangbedji called for better alignment between university research, national needs and the economic sector.

Three scientific presentations covered the future of higher education, interdisciplinarity and innovation, and artificial intelligence, the last of which flagged algorithmic bias, faulty references and ethical concerns. Six doctoral students from Lomé and Kara received awards for the quality of their work.

Why it mattersDoctoral students at Lomé and Kara must now align their theses with national priorities, and six of them have been recognised for the quality of their research.
