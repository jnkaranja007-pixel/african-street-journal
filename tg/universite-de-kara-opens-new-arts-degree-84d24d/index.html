Université de Kara opens new arts degree

The new plastic arts and drawing degree will train teachers for secondary schools.

Source: L-Frii · 2026-10-29 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

The University of Kara will launch a new Bachelor's degree in Plastic Arts and Drawing starting in the 2026-2027 academic year. The program, offered by the ISPAU, aims to train professionals capable of teaching these subjects at the junior secondary level.

This new specialization will allow students to master fundamental plastic arts practices, develop their creative and analytical abilities, and learn effective pedagogical methods for teaching art. The university's president approved the creation of this degree.

The degree is part of the University of Kara's 2026-2030 Strategic Development Plan. The program seeks to promote and disseminate artistic culture and creation within Togo.

Why it mattersTogolese students will have a new educational opportunity to specialize in visual arts and pursue careers in teaching.
