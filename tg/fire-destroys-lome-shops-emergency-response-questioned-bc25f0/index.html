Fire Destroys Lomé Shops, Emergency Response Questioned

Flames engulfed multiple businesses in the Tokoin Ramco neighborhood, prompting a community response amid reports of delayed emergency services.

Source: Icilome · 2026-10-21 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X

An intense fire broke out Monday in Lomé's Tokoin Ramco district, directly opposite the Bomaco bakery, destroying several shops and causing significant material damage. Local residents joined firefighters in battling the blaze, but difficulties were reported in reaching emergency services via the 118 emergency number.

Gendarmerie officers were present to secure the area and potentially investigate the fire's origin. The exact cause of the fire and the total value of the losses remain unknown at this time. This incident highlights ongoing challenges in accessing emergency services during disasters.

The fire's origin and the full extent of the financial losses are still under investigation. The event underscores the critical need for reliable and swift emergency response systems in urban areas.

Why it mattersShop owners and local businesses are affected by the destruction of their commercial spaces and inventory.
