Lomé airport cancels Royal Air Maroc flight after technical incident

The service to Casablanca was cancelled on 24 November, and 111 passengers were accommodated in a local hotel while new-aircraft routing began.

Source: L-Frii · 2026-11-26 · 1 min read

Royal Air Maroc flight RAM 551 from Lomé to Casablanca was suspended and then cancelled on 24 November 2026 after a technical incident, the Lomé-Tokoin Airport Company said.

The airport company said 111 affected passengers were housed in a local hotel after routine formalities. It apologised for the disruption and said the passengers were being rerouted aboard a replacement aircraft.

The company also urged airlines to arrange swift and considerate care for passengers when technical incidents occur.

Why it mattersThe 111 passengers were housed in a Lomé hotel while the airport company said their rerouting aboard a replacement aircraft was under way.
