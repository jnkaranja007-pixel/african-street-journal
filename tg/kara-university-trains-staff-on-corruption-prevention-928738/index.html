Kara University Trains Staff on Corruption Prevention

The University of Kara hosted a training session aimed at embedding anti-corruption principles and ethical conduct into Togolese university curricula.

The University of Kara recently hosted a training session organized by HAPLUCIA for its teacher-researchers. This initiative focuses on integrating corruption prevention, ethics, deontology, responsibility, and good governance into university programs across Togo.

HAPLUCIA's goal is to empower teacher-researchers to instill values of integrity and transparency in students. The program seeks to position universities as central players in combating corruption by fostering responsible citizenship and a culture of integrity within higher education.

This effort aims to cultivate a generation of students who understand and uphold ethical standards, contributing to a more transparent and accountable society in Togo. The training emphasizes the university's role in shaping future leaders committed to good governance.

Why it mattersTeacher-researchers at the University of Kara are affected by training that integrates corruption prevention and ethical conduct into their teaching programs.
