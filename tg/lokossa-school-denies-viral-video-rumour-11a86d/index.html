Lokossa school denies viral video rumour

CEG 3 administration states no student by the name circulated is enrolled at the institution.

Source: L-Frii · 2026-11-06 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

The administration of CEG 3 in Lokossa, Benin, has formally denied rumours circulating on social media about a private video allegedly involving one of its students. The school stated that the name mentioned in the viral posts does not appear in their student registers.

School officials issued a statement to counter the widespread online speculation, emphasizing that no such incident has occurred within the college premises. This denial aims to protect the reputation of the institution, its learners, and its staff from unverified information.

The CEG 3 management urged internet users to exercise caution and verify information before sharing it online. The school is located in Lokossa, Benin, and the rumour spread widely on social media platforms.
