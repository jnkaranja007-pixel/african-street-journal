Lokossa school denies viral video rumour

The CEG 3 administration stated that the named student is not registered and no such incident occurred.

Source: L-Frii · 2026-11-02 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

The administration of CEG 3 in Lokossa, Benin, has formally denied rumours circulating on social media about a private video involving a student from the school. The school's management stated that the name mentioned in the viral post does not appear in their student registry.

Officials at CEG 3 confirmed that no incident of this nature has taken place within the school premises. This clarification aims to protect the reputation of the institution, its students, and its staff from unverified information.

The school administration urged internet users to exercise caution and verify information before sharing it online. They emphasized that the viral content is not linked to their establishment.

Why it mattersThe reputation of CEG 3 in Lokossa, Benin, its students, and staff is at risk due to unverified rumours circulating online.
