Togo proposes 2 684,1 milliards of FCFA budget for 2027

The draft is 2,5 % below the 2026 initial budget, while 921,4 milliards of FCFA is set aside for debt service.

Source: 24heureinfo · 2026-11-26 · 1 min read

Togo’s Council of Ministers adopted a proposed 2027 budget of 2 684,1 milliards of FCFA on 22 October 2026. The proposal balances revenue and spending at that amount and is now awaiting examination by the National Assembly.

The Finance Ministry’s published figures allocate 921,4 milliards of FCFA to debt service, or 34,3 % of the budget. That includes 719,9 milliards for principal repayments and 201,5 milliards for interest; the ministry responsible for education is allocated 213,6 milliards.

The government attributes the proposed budget’s 2,5 % decrease from the 2026 initial budget of 2 751,5 milliards to the international economic context. The Assembly must examine and vote on the draft.

Why it mattersThe National Assembly must examine and vote on the draft, which assigns 921,4 milliards of FCFA to debt service.
