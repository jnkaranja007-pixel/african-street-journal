John Meyer’s Odysseus sets South African auction record at R5 947 500

The Cape Town painter’s work was the third-highest auction price for a living South African artist, while Strauss & Co’s evening sale raised R52 123 640.

Sources: The South African and Daily Maverick · 2026-11-19 · 1 min read

Cape Town realist painter John Meyer’s acrylic painting Odysseus sold for R5 947 500 at Strauss & Co’s Evening Sale in Johannesburg on Tuesday, 17 November, setting a world auction record for the artist. The price was the third-highest paid at auction in South Africa for a work by a living South African artist.

The live-virtual sale raised R52 123 640 over three and a half hours, with 72% of lots sold, Strauss & Co said. A telephone buyer won Odysseus after competing bids. Irma Stern’s Still Life with Strelitzias, a Songe Mask and a Songe Nkisi Figure (1946) led the sale at R8 578 125 after 13 bids.

Strauss & Co’s online-only Day Sale raised R7 916 653, selling 78% of lots. The auction house’s director and global head of art, Alastair Meredith, said the results reflected continuing demand and pointed to further market momentum into 2027.

Why it mattersSouth African art-market sellers and bidders now have a R5 947 500 benchmark for Meyer’s work, alongside a R8 578 125 result for Stern.
