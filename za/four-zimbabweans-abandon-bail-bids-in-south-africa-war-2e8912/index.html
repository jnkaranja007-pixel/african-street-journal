Four Zimbabweans abandon bail bids in South Africa war-recruitment case

The fifth accused, Abel Muchenje, plans to seek release, but prosecutors told the Kempton Park court they will oppose his application.

Sources: The Citizen SA and Mail & Guardian · 2026-11-18 · 1 min read

Four of five Zimbabwean nationals accused of trying to leave South Africa to join the Russia-Ukraine war abandoned their bail applications at Kempton Park Magistrate’s Court on Tuesday, 17 November. The fifth, Abel Muchenje, 40, said he intends to apply for bail.

National Prosecuting Authority spokesperson Magaboke Mohlatlole said prosecutors would oppose Muchenje’s application. The five were arrested on 8 November after an intelligence-led investigation, while allegedly preparing to board flights out of South Africa. They face charges under the Regulation of Foreign Military Assistance Act 15 of 1998.

The court postponed the case to 24 November for possible plea and sentence negotiations under the Criminal Procedure Act. The four who dropped their applications are Weight Mudhanyane, 23, Javangwe Munyaradzi, 41, Wesley Sothole, 24, and Knowledge Ndunduma, 46.

Why it mattersMuchenje faces prosecutors’ opposition to his bail application, with the court due to consider possible plea and sentence negotiations on 24 November.
