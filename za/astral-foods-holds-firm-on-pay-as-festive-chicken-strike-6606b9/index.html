Astral Foods holds firm on pay as Festive Chicken strike spreads

The poultry group says it cannot meet workers’ demands for increases above 74%, while some employees are seeking more than 100%.

Sources: The Citizen SA and GroundUp · 2026-11-18 · 1 min read

Astral Foods is enforcing “no work, no pay” as employees at its Festive Chicken factory in Olifantsfontein, Gauteng, continue a strike that has spread to workers at some group farms. The strike, led by the Simunye Workers’ Forum (SWF), has lasted more than two weeks.

Astral chief executive Gary Arnold told The Citizen that some factory workers have returned, while others remain on strike. SWF members seek a monthly salary of R13 500, up from R6 500 for the lowest-paid workers. Arnold said the group is still recovering from severe losses caused by load shedding and cannot provide the demanded increase of more than 74%; some workers seek increases above 100%.

Arnold said workers approached the Commission for Conciliation, Mediation and Arbitration (CCMA) over the pay dispute and received a certificate of non-resolution. He said they had no picketing rules and were acting under the Public Gatherings Act. A 2023 change to CCMA rules clarified commissioners’ responsibilities in disputes that could lead to strikes, according to law firm Cliffe Dekker Hofmeyr, which was not commenting on this strike.

Why it mattersThe strike involves workers seeking R13 500 a month from R6 500, while Astral says it cannot provide the demanded increase of more than 74%.
