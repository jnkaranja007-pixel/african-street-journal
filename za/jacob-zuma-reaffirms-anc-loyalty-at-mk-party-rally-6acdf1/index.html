Jacob Zuma reaffirms ANC loyalty at MK party rally

Former president Jacob Zuma addressed thousands of supporters at the MK party's final election rally in Durban on Sunday.

Source: The Citizen SA · 2026-11-02 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Former president Jacob Zuma declared his lifelong loyalty to the ANC at the MK party’s final election rally in Durban on Sunday. The 84-year-old spoke to thousands of supporters at the Princess Magogo Stadium in KwaMashu, days after his hospital discharge.

Zuma launched a strong attack on the Government of National Unity (GNU), stating that its alliance with parties representing white interests showed South Africa was not yet free. He warned that the nation was “dying a second death” and vowed to fight the GNU until it ended.

The MK party leader expressed confidence in his party’s electoral chances, predicting substantial gains in local government. Zuma assured supporters that they should not fear, as the party is currently governing and will continue to do so.

Why it mattersFormer president Jacob Zuma's declaration of ANC loyalty while leading the breakaway MK party highlights a political paradox for voters.
