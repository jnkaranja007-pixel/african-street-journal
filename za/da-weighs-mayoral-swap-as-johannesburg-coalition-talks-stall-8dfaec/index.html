DA weighs mayoral swap as Johannesburg coalition talks stall

The proposal would send party leader Geordin Hill-Lewis to Johannesburg and Helen Zille to Cape Town, but Zille says she is not taking it seriously.

Sources: The South African and Daily Maverick · 2026-11-18 · 1 min read

Helen Zille has dismissed talk of replacing Geordin Hill-Lewis as Cape Town mayor, as reports describe a proposed swap that would make Hill-Lewis Johannesburg mayor. The idea has been raised with ActionSA during coalition negotiations with the Democratic Alliance (DA), according to sources in both parties.

The DA won 91 of Johannesburg’s 270 council seats in the 4 November local government elections, leaving it short of a majority. ActionSA leader Herman Mashaba has said his party will not back Zille for Johannesburg mayor and offered to leave the race if she does. Hill-Lewis told 702 that ActionSA’s demand to drop Zille was difficult for the DA to accept, but he has not ruled out working with the party.

Zille, who was Cape Town mayor from 2006 to 2009 before becoming Western Cape premier, said she was not taking the rumour seriously. She has previously said she would lead the DA’s opposition caucus in Johannesburg if she did not secure the mayoralty; the DA has not announced changes to its mayoral candidates.

Why it mattersJohannesburg’s 270-seat council has no DA majority, and ActionSA has offered to withdraw its mayoral candidate if Zille does the same.
