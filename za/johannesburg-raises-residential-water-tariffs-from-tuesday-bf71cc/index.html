Johannesburg raises residential water tariffs from Tuesday 1 December

The new Level 2 rates lift the monthly water charge for a household using 10 kilolitres from R134.28 to R196.72, excluding other charges.

Sources: The South African, Mail & Guardian and GroundUp · 2026-11-28 · 1 min read

The City of Johannesburg will raise stepped water tariffs for residential accounts from Tuesday, 1 December, under Level 2 restrictions introduced to protect the municipality's water supply system and prevent reservoir depletion. The changes apply to household and multi-dwelling accounts, while commercial and industrial tariffs are excluded.

The City says a household using 10 kilolitres a month will pay R196.72 for water, up R62.44 from the normal R134.28, before VAT and other charges. The first six kilolitres, previously charged at R0.00 per kilolitre, will cost R7.80 per kilolitre. Registered indigent and Expanded Social Package households retain approved Free Basic Water relief.

The City says it is consuming 1 709 million litres of water a day against a bulk supply allocation of 1 524 million litres, an excess of roughly 185 million litres daily. Deemed-consumption and prepaid customers are not subject to the stepped tariff adjustment; those areas will instead receive a 20% flow reduction on zone supply meters.

Why it mattersHouseholds on stepped residential tariffs pay more from Tuesday 1 December; a 10-kilolitre monthly bill increases by R62.44 before other charges.
