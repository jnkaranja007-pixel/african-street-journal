Cyril Ramaphosa turns 74 during Silicon Valley working visit

South Africa's president marks his 74th birthday in California on a working visit focused on artificial intelligence.

Sources: The South African and Daily Maverick · 2026-11-17 · 1 min read

President Cyril Cyril Matamela Ramaphosa celebrated his 74th birthday on Tuesday, 17 November, while leading a delegation on a working visit to Silicon Valley in the United States. According to the Presidency, the trip began on Saturday, 14 November, and spans a week of engagements with technology companies, investors and artificial intelligence experts. The president also called on South African-born billionaire Elon Musk to give back to his country of birth.

The working visit focuses on data centres, cloud infrastructure and the artificial intelligence economy alongside MTN testing a Telkom takeover. The African National Congress dismissed speculation of a resignation last week, following his re-election by the National Assembly in June 2024 after succeeding Jacob Zuma on 15 February 2018.

The president was born in Johannesburg on 17 November 1952, obtained his law degree from Unisa in 1981, founded the National Union of Mineworkers in 1982, and established the Shanduka Group in 2001. He is scheduled to return from the United States this weekend.

Why it mattersPresident Cyril Ramaphosa is in California for a week-long working visit ending this weekend, focusing on artificial intelligence and technology investments.
