Johannesburg teams suspend recovery search for 11-year-old in Soweto

Rain and risks to divers halted the operation for Anqobile Jiyela, who was swept away by floodwaters in Dobsonville on Tuesday.

Sources: The Citizen SA, Mail & Guardian and The South African · 2026-11-19 · 1 min read

Johannesburg's Emergency Management Services (EMS), a city rescue agency, and police suspended the search and recovery operation for 11-year-old Anqobile Jiyela in Dobsonville, Soweto, on Wednesday afternoon, after rain threatened divers. Jiyela was swept away by floodwaters on Tuesday afternoon, after school transport dropped her off.

EMS, police water and dog units, private security and community members searched near a river stream. The operation shifted from rescue to recovery, EMS spokesperson Robert Mulaudzi said, because the chance of finding her alive was “very slim”. A private security company's drone unit and a police helicopter also assisted.

The search was expected to resume on Thursday, weather permitting. The South African Weather Service had issued a yellow level 2 warning for severe thunderstorms in Gauteng. Mulaudzi urged residents to avoid flooded roads, low-lying bridges and river streams.

Why it mattersThe emergency teams must return to the river search on Thursday, weather permitting, while supporting Jiyela's family in seeking closure.
