Western Cape High Court sets aside Phala Phala report

The ruling halts the report that had cleared the way for Parliament’s impeachment proceedings against President Cyril Ramaphosa.

Sources: The Citizen SA, Daily Maverick and Mail & Guardian · 2026-11-26 · 1 min read

South Africa’s Western Cape High Court on Wednesday, 25 November 2026, set aside in full the Section 89 independent panel report on President Cyril Ramaphosa and the Phala Phala farm. The judgment, delivered in Cape Town, followed Ramaphosa’s legal challenge to the panel’s findings.

Judge President Nolwazi Penelope Mabindla-Boqwana ruled that the report was fundamentally flawed. The panel had investigated the theft of approximately $580 000 (about R9.6 million) from Ramaphosa’s game farm in Limpopo on 9 February 2020. The report had paved the way for Parliament’s impeachment proceedings against the president.

A separate account of the ruling said it dissolved Parliament’s impeachment committee, while opposition appeals kept the political dispute alive.

Why it mattersRamaphosa and Parliament must now respond to the court’s decision setting aside the report that underpinned impeachment proceedings.
