Johannesburg council to elect mayor as coalition deal gives ANC bloc control

A reported four-party pact includes the ANC, ActionSA, MK and the EFF, with Herman Mashaba in line to lead the city.

Sources: The South African, Daily Maverick and The Citizen SA · 2026-11-23 · 1 min read

Johannesburg’s new municipal council is scheduled to elect a mayor at its inaugural sitting on Monday, 23 November, as coalition talks conclude. ActionSA leader Herman Mashaba is reportedly in line for the post. The sitting, set for 13:00 at the Connie Bapela Council Chambers, will also swear in councillors and elect a speaker and other office-bearers.

The Daily Maverick reported that the African National Congress (ANC), ActionSA, MK, which is the uMkhonto weSizwe party, and the Economic Freedom Fighters (EFF) have agreed to govern Johannesburg, Tshwane and Ekurhuleni. No party won an outright majority in Johannesburg. The Democratic Alliance (DA) received the largest vote share there, with 34.84%, but is expected to sit in opposition.

The DA has asked Johannesburg City Manager Floyd Brink to postpone the sitting, according to TimesLIVE, arguing coalition negotiations are continuing.

Why it mattersJohannesburg residents will have a new mayor and council leadership that depends on whether the ANC-led pact holds against the DA’s postponement push.
