Cape Town shooting leaves five dead

Eight people were shot in a gang-related incident near Steenberg and Lavender Hill on the Cape Flats.

Source: The Citizen SA · 2026-10-26 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Five people died and three were injured in a mass shooting near Cape Town on October 25, 2026. The incident occurred at a home on the Cape Flats, near Steenberg and Lavender Hill.

Police are investigating the shooting as a gang-related incident. Eight people in total were shot during the event.

The Special Investigating Unit also conducted a raid at businessman Hangwani Maumela’s home on October 9, 2025, while SACP general secretary Solly Mapaila spoke out against political leaders who demean women.
