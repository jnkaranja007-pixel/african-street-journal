Durban remains South Africa's cheapest metro for buying property

Outside the city centre, the average home there sells for R920 000, while Johannesburg's average is R1.1 million, according to a property report.

Source: The South African · 2026-11-27 · 1 min read

Durban is the cheapest metro in South Africa to buy property outside the city centre, where the average sale price is R920 000, according to The South African. Johannesburg's average sale price across the rest of the city is R1.1 million, while flats in Cape Town's central business district cost upwards of R1 million.

In Durban's city centre, the average sale price is R420 000, and a studio flat needs at least R280 000. Statistics South Africa puts the expanded unemployment rate in eThekwini, the municipality that includes Durban, at 39.2% as of the third quarter. Property prices in Durban rose 5.1% between 2021 and 2025.

In Pretoria, the average sale price in the broader city is R989 000, up 16.8% over five years. The report says Johannesburg's average price outside the centre was unchanged from 2024.

Why it mattersBuyers with a fixed budget can compare Durban's R920 000 average with Pretoria's R989 000 and Johannesburg's R1.1 million before choosing a metro.
