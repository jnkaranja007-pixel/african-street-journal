Johannesburg council delays mayor vote to Friday amid coalition talks

ActionSA leader Herman Mashaba is in line for the post, while the Democratic Alliance has challenged the council sitting as negotiations continue.

Sources: The South African, Daily Maverick and The Citizen SA · 2026-11-24 · 1 min read

Johannesburg’s first council sitting for the 2026–2031 term was pushed from Monday to Friday as coalition negotiations continued, according to the Daily Maverick. ActionSA leader Herman Mashaba is reportedly in line to become mayor after parties reached agreements in several hung municipalities.

No party won an outright majority in Johannesburg. The Democratic Alliance (DA) received 34.84% of the vote, the highest share, but is expected to sit in opposition. The city had scheduled the inaugural council meeting for 13:00 on Monday at the Connie Bapela Council Chambers to swear in councillors and elect a speaker and other office-bearers.

The DA sent City Manager Floyd Brink a lawyer’s letter seeking a postponement, arguing that negotiations were still underway, TimesLIVE reported. ANC Greater Johannesburg secretary Sasabona Manganye said 18 parties in the council had agreed to work with the ANC, while the DA had expressed discomfort.

Why it mattersJohannesburg councillors must now settle the coalition and choose the mayor and other office-bearers at Friday’s sitting.
