Premier FMCG revenue rises 38.8% to R14.3 billion after Rainbow Food Group deal

The South African food maker’s results include its culinary business, while net finance costs rose 17.1% to R139 million after it took on debt.

Source: Food Business Africa · 2026-11-21 · 1 min read

South African food manufacturer Premier FMCG reported revenue of R14.3 billion for the period ended 30 September 2026, up 38.8% after its March acquisition of RFG, formerly known as Rainbow Food Group, brought the culinary category into its business. Net profit rose 62.1% to R1.2 billion, while earnings before interest, tax, depreciation and amortisation (EBITDA) increased 51.4% to R2.0 billion.

Millbake, which generated 66% of group revenue, grew sales 1.3% to R9.4 billion despite maize prices falling 20% and wheat prices 5%. Its EBITDA rose 23.7% to R1.6 billion. Groceries revenue was R4.9 billion, including Culinary, and its EBITDA reached R513 million, with a 10.4% margin.

Net finance costs climbed 17.1% to R139 million following the inclusion of debt assumed in the RFG transaction. Premier said cash generation would fund capital spending, debt reduction and possible share buybacks; it expects operational integration and supply-chain benefits to increase in the second half.

Why it mattersPremier FMCG shareholders are exposed to R139 million in net finance costs as the company directs cash towards reducing debt and funding capital spending.
