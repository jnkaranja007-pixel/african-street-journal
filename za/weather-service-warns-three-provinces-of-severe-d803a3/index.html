Weather service warns three provinces of severe thunderstorms

The Level 4 alert ran until 23:59 on Monday, with heavy rain, hail and damaging winds forecast across parts of Gauteng, Limpopo and Mpumalanga.

Sources: The South African and The Citizen SA · 2026-11-24 · 1 min read

South Africa’s weather service issued a Yellow Level 4 warning for severe thunderstorms in Gauteng, Limpopo and Mpumalanga on Monday, 23 November. It took effect at 13:00 and remained valid until 23:59.

The service warned that heavy rain, hail and strong winds could cause flooding in low-lying areas, settlements and roads, and damage property and infrastructure. It said storms were expected in central and southern Gauteng and south-western Mpumalanga; the warning also covered Limpopo.

The City of Johannesburg’s emergency management spokesperson, Robert Mulaudzi, urged residents to avoid flooded roads, low-lying bridges, rivers and dams. A separate Level 4 warning was issued for parts of KwaZulu-Natal, southern Mpumalanga, eastern Free State and North West on Tuesday, 24 November.

Why it mattersResidents in low-lying areas and motorists faced possible flooding and route closures before the warning expired at 23:59 on Monday.
