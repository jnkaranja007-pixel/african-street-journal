Ramaphosa orders flags at half-mast for Mlambo-Ngcuka through 28 November

South Africa’s first female deputy president will receive a Category 2 state funeral, with a memorial service scheduled for Tuesday in Johannesburg.

Sources: The Citizen SA, Mail & Guardian and The South African · 2026-11-22 · 1 min read

Phumzile Mlambo-Ngcuka, South Africa’s first female deputy president and a former United Nations under-secretary-general, died on Friday, 20 November, aged 71. President Cyril Ramaphosa confirmed she will receive a Category 2 state funeral.

The Presidency said the funeral will include ceremonial elements by the South African National Defence Force and take place on Saturday, 28 November, at Middledrift in the Eastern Cape. Ramaphosa ordered the national flag flown at half-mast at flag stations from Sunday morning, 22 November, until the evening of 28 November.

A state memorial service is scheduled for 14:00 on Tuesday, 24 November, at Our Lady of Lourdes Catholic Church in Rivonia, Johannesburg. Mlambo-Ngcuka had also served as minister of minerals and energy and chancellor of the University of Johannesburg.

Why it mattersSouth Africans will see flags lowered at flag stations nationwide through 28 November, while the state funeral is scheduled for that day in the Eastern Cape.
