First-time buyers make up 48.5% of South African home-loan applications

Ooba data show first-time buyers averaged an 8.5% deposit, while approval rates reached 83.9% this year.

Source: The South African · 2026-11-22 · 1 min read

First-time buyers made up 48.5% of South African home-loan applications in October, according to ooba Home Loans data cited by Pam Golding Properties. Their share averaged 46.9% for the year to date.

The buyers put down an average deposit of 8.5% of the purchase price, compared with 12.8% across all homebuyers in the year to October. Effective loan approval rates averaged 83.9% this year, and ooba secured an average rate of prime minus 0.77% in October, the data showed.

Applications including legal, tax and administrative costs made up 10.1% of applications in September and October. The South African Reserve Bank left the repo rate unchanged at its November meeting, with the prime lending rate at 10.75%.
