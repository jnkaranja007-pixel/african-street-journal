Tshwane council elects Nasiphi Moya mayor with 115 votes

ActionSA’s Moya defeated DA candidate Cilliers Brink in a secret ballot at the City of Tshwane’s inaugural council meeting on Thursday.

Sources: The Citizen SA, Daily Maverick and Mail & Guardian · 2026-11-27 · 1 min read

ActionSA’s Nasiphi Moya won election as executive mayor of the City of Tshwane on Thursday, 26 November, securing 115 votes in the council’s inaugural meeting. DA candidate Cilliers Brink received 87 votes.

The council reported 209 votes cast, including seven abstentions. Moya’s return follows the 2026 local government elections, as parties form municipal governments and contest leadership posts.

The African National Congress won the council speaker position, with its candidate Naeem Patel elected. The Economic Freedom Fighters’ Omphile Maotwe was tipped for deputy mayor in a separate report.

Why it mattersTshwane councillors must now work under Moya’s executive leadership after she secured 115 votes in the mayoral ballot.
