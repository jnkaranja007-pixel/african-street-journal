Weather service warns of Saturday storms across South Africa

Yellow Level 2 alerts cover parts of nine provinces, with flooding risks and extremely high fire danger in areas of three others.

Sources: The South African and The Citizen SA · 2026-11-21 · 1 min read

South Africa’s weather service has issued Yellow Level 2 warnings for severe thunderstorms on Saturday, 21 November, across parts of nine provinces. The alerts cover the Free State, central North West, KwaZulu-Natal, southern Gauteng, most of Mpumalanga, central and southeastern Limpopo, and eastern parts of the Eastern Cape.

The service warns that heavy downpours, damaging winds, hail and excessive lightning could flood vulnerable roads, low-lying areas and bridges. Extremely high fire danger is forecast in central and eastern Northern Cape, northeastern Western Cape, and parts of central and northern Eastern Cape.

The forecast also calls for hot and humid conditions in eastern KwaZulu-Natal, which the service says will be extremely uncomfortable. Scattered showers and thundershowers are expected in parts of the country over the weekend.

Why it mattersResidents and motorists in warned areas face possible flooding on susceptible roads, low-lying ground and bridges during Saturday’s storms.
