Indalo plans R2.5 billion, 35-storey Cape Town development

ThirtyFour18 will include a five-star hotel, 101 apartments and office space, with construction scheduled to begin in April 2027 subject to approvals.

Source: The South African · 2026-11-21 · 1 min read

Indalo Hotels and Leisure Group and its development partners plan a R2.5 billion, 35-storey mixed-use project on Heerengracht Street in Cape Town’s Foreshore. ThirtyFour18 is scheduled to break ground in April 2027, subject to approvals, and take four years to build.

The 1 680-square-metre site sits in front of CTICC 2, the second building of the Cape Town International Convention Centre. The project will include a five-star hotel, 101 apartments, premium-grade offices, dining and entertainment facilities, and a rooftop bar and swimming pool. Afrirent Holdings founder Senzo Tsabedze said construction could create up to 2 500 jobs, with 400 permanent positions expected once the building is operating.

The foundations are expected to need about 90 piles, each extending around 90 feet underground. ThirtyFour18 is scheduled to be launched to the public in January 2027, and completion is targeted for April 2031.
