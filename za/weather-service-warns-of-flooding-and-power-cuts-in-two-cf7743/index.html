Weather service warns of flooding and power cuts in two provinces

A Yellow Level 2 alert covers severe thunderstorms in parts of the Free State and North West on Wednesday, 18 November.

Sources: The South African and The Citizen SA · 2026-11-18 · 1 min read

South Africa's weather service issued a Yellow Level 2 warning for severe thunderstorms in parts of the Free State and North West on Wednesday, 18 November. The storms are expected to bring localised flooding, property damage and disruptions to power and communication services.

The service said the warning covers northern and northeastern parts of the Free State and southeastern parts of the North West. It forecast isolated to scattered showers and thundershowers in the central and eastern Free State, and isolated showers and thundershowers in eastern North West.

The service forecast extremely high fire danger in western parts of both provinces, as well as the Northern Cape and parts of the Western Cape. Very hot to extremely hot conditions are also expected in the interior of the Namakwa District and along parts of the Western Cape coast and interior.

Why it mattersResidents and businesses in the warning areas face expected localised power and communication disruptions alongside flooding on Wednesday, 18 November.
