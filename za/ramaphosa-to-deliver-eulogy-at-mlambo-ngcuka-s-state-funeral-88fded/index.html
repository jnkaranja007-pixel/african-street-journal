Ramaphosa to deliver eulogy at Mlambo-Ngcuka’s state funeral

South Africa’s first female deputy president will receive Category 2 honours, including military ceremonies, at a service in the Eastern Cape.

Sources: The South African, Daily Maverick and Mail & Guardian · 2026-11-26 · 1 min read

President Cyril Ramaphosa will deliver the eulogy at the state funeral of Phumzile Mlambo-Ngcuka, South Africa’s first female deputy president, in the Eastern Cape on Saturday, 28 November. The service is scheduled for eXesi Village in Middledrift.

The Presidency said Mlambo-Ngcuka will receive a State Funeral Category 2, which includes ceremonial honours by the South African National Defence Force. The category is designated for serving and former deputy presidents and acting presidents. Mlambo-Ngcuka died on 20 November 2026, aged 71, after complications from a recent medical procedure.

Presidency spokesperson Vincent Magwenya said she was an activist in the liberation struggle and later served as executive director of UN Women from 2013 to 2021.

Why it mattersThe state funeral places her among those honoured for the highest public office, with military ceremonial honours prescribed for the service.
