South African Weather Service keeps Orange Level 5 storm warning for three provinces

The warning covers parts of Gauteng, Limpopo and Mpumalanga until Sunday night, with risks of flooding, falling trees and power cuts.

The South African Weather Service (SAWS) has kept an Orange Level 5 warning in place for parts of Gauteng, Limpopo and Mpumalanga until Sunday night. The warning raises the risk of flooding, falling trees, power disruptions and damage to homes, vehicles and infrastructure.

Residents are urged to clear gutters and stormwater drains, bring garden furniture and bins indoors, and keep an emergency kit with a torch, batteries, drinking water, food, medication and a fully charged phone. Those living near rivers and streams should watch water levels and move to higher ground if flooding threatens their homes.

SAWS also issued a Level 2 warning for severe thunderstorms in parts of North West, the Free State, KwaZulu-Natal and other areas, and a Level 1 warning for damaging winds between Cape Columbine and Cape Agulhas. Residents are told to follow municipal disaster teams and emergency services.

Why it mattersHouseholds without a stocked emergency kit or cleared drains are most exposed if power and water supplies are cut during the storms.

Agriculture · 2026-11-15

South Africa vaccinates over 90% of cattle herd against FMD

The vaccination rate surpasses the government's target, signaling progress in controlling the foot-and-mouth disease outbreak.

More than 90% of South Africa’s cattle herd has been vaccinated against foot-and-mouth disease (FMD), surpassing the government’s target of 80% by the end of December 2026. Agriculture minister Willie Aucamp announced this progress in Johannesburg on Friday, noting that all provinces are now administering booster doses to maintain immunity. Over 22 million FMD vaccine doses have been distributed, with the private sector vaccinating about four million cattle and the state vaccinating just under 11 million.

Aucamp described reaching the 90% vaccination rate as a significant achievement in fighting FMD, demonstrating effective collaboration between government and the private sector. He stated that this joint effort is rapidly controlling the disease's spread, protecting South African livelihoods and the country’s red meat export markets. New FMD outbreaks have declined, and the department is working with farmers to ensure the fight against the disease is won.

State veterinary services will continue large-scale vaccination campaigns, and farmers can still voluntarily register online to vaccinate their own cattle. The department emphasizes the importance of an uninterrupted vaccine supply, with farmers able to order vaccines online for delivery within 24 hours. Producers are urged to register on the department of Agriculture, Red Meat Industry Services (Rims), or Buffalo Analytics websites to procure vaccines for self-vaccination.

Why it mattersOver 90% of South Africa's cattle herd has been vaccinated against FMD, protecting the country's livestock industry and export markets.

Sport · 2026-11-15

France beat South Africa 21-19 in Paris Nations Championship Test

The Springboks trailed 21-7 at halftime and rallied, but Manie Libbok missed a conversion that could have won them the match.

France beat South Africa 21-19 in a Nations Championship Test at the Stade de France in Paris on Friday. It was France's second win over the world champions in their last 11 meetings.

The Springboks trailed 21-7 at halftime. Replacement front rowers Wilco Louw, Malcolm Marx and Ox Nche came on before the break and dominated the scrums in the second half. Cobus Reinach and Marx scored tries for South Africa, but Libbok missed the conversion that would have given them the lead.

South Africa are next scheduled to face Ireland in Dublin next Saturday. Jasper Wiese was shown a yellow card that was later upgraded to red, and both teams lost players to the sin bin for high tackles.

Why it mattersSouth Africa's loss to France means the Springboks have lost top spot in the World Rugby rankings.
