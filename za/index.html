Court halts Ramaphosa impeachment inquiry as EFF fights ruling in ConCourt

A South African court has paused the parliamentary committee investigating a possible impeachment case against President Cyril Ramaphosa tied to the Phala Phala farm scandal, according to eNCA. The Economic Freedom Fighters has since asked the Constitutional Court to reinstate the impeachment proceedings, arguing the committee must continue its work, while the impeachment committee met amid the ongoing legal battle.

Why it mattersThe outcome will determine whether South Africa's head of state faces a formal impeachment process, a first-order political risk for markets and governance.

SARB holds repo rate at 7% as inflation jumps to 5%, a two-year high

The South African Reserve Bank kept its benchmark repo rate unchanged at 7% on July 23, even after Statistics South Africa reported annual consumer inflation accelerated to 5% in June from 4.5% in May. The Monetary Policy Committee voted 4-2 to hold, with two members pushing for a 25-basis-point hike, and Governor Lesetja Kganyago said the bank is setting policy to return inflation to a 3% target over time.

Why it mattersBorrowing costs stay put for now, but a hotter inflation print keeps pressure on the rand and on households' loan repayments.

Eskom marks 441 days without load shedding, its best run since 2018

South Africa has gone 441 consecutive days without power cuts since 16 May 2025, Eskom said, with electricity demand met 100% of the time between April and July. Unplanned outages on 26 July fell to 4,562MW, the lowest since June 2018, and diesel spending dropped 84% year-on-year to R746.41 million, while six of nine provinces have been fully removed from load reduction schedules.

Why it mattersA stable grid removes one of the biggest drags on business investment and household costs, though rising Eskom tariffs still squeeze budgets.

ANC opens talks to expand 10-party GNU coalition after budget rifts

The African National Congress will hold discussions over two to three weeks on expanding its coalition government following disputes with the Democratic Alliance over the budget, secretary-general Fikile Mbalula said. The GNU currently controls more than 70% of parliamentary seats through the ANC, DA, Inkatha Freedom Party and seven smaller parties, and any new entrant requires approval from existing members under the coalition's founding agreement.

Why it mattersA wider or reshuffled coalition would reset the balance of power ahead of local elections and shape how far pro-market reforms can go.

Electricity tariff hikes of up to 9% squeeze households despite stable grid

South Africa's energy regulator NERSA approved an 8.76% tariff increase for Eskom's direct customers effective April 1, 2026, and a further 9.01% rise for municipal customers from July 2026, with another 8.83% hike already approved for 2027/28. Industry group the Energy Intensive Users Group has said the escalation is already forcing some operations to cut back, even as power supply itself has stabilised.

Why it mattersCheaper blackout risk is being offset by pricier electricity bills, keeping cost-of-living pressure high for consumers and industry.
