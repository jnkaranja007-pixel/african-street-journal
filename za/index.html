Police arrest four suspects in Zolani Tete murder investigation

Eastern Cape police have arrested three additional suspects, bringing the total to four, in connection with the killing of former boxing world champion Zolani Tete.

South African police have arrested four individuals in connection with the murder of former two-division boxing world champion Zolani Tete. The arrests follow the shooting death of Tete outside his home in Mdantsane on Friday, 21 August. One suspect is scheduled to appear in court on Monday to face charges of murder and attempted murder, according to Africanews and BBC Africa.

Tete, born on 8 March 1988 in Mdantsane, was a celebrated boxer known for his power and precision. He turned professional in 2006 and achieved world titles, including the IBF junior-bantamweight title in 2014 and the WBO bantamweight title in 2017. His career included 29 wins out of 34 professional fights, with 22 knockouts.

A notable moment in Tete's career was his 11-second knockout of Siboniso Gonya in November 2017, setting a Guinness World Record for the fastest knockout in a world title fight. Despite a four-year suspension in 2022 for a banned substance, Tete was preparing for a comeback when his suspension was set to end in July 2026.

Why it mattersThe arrests in the murder case of Zolani Tete directly affect his family and the boxing community in South Africa, providing progress in the investigation into his death.

Dolly Parton dies at 80, ending six-decade music career

Country music icon Dolly Parton, known for hits like “Jolene” and “I Will Always Love You,” passed away on Tuesday at the age of 80, her family confirmed.

Dolly Parton, the legendary country music singer, songwriter, and actress, has died at 80, her family announced on Tuesday. Her death concludes a career spanning almost six decades, during which she became one of the most influential figures in popular music, selling over 100 million records worldwide.

Parton's career began with her debut album in 1967 and included numerous hit songs, film roles in movies such as "9 to 5," and the establishment of Dollywood, a theme park in Tennessee. Her influence extended to Broadway, television, and theatre, with a musical based on her life in development.

Beyond entertainment, Parton was known for her philanthropic work, including the Dolly Parton’s Imagination Library, which provides free books to children globally. She also contributed $1 million to Moderna’s Covid-19 vaccine research. Just days before her passing, her music continued to gain new global sales certifications across 14 countries, including South Africa.

Why it mattersFans of Dolly Parton and those who benefited from her philanthropic efforts, such as children receiving free books, are directly affected by her passing.

US National Institutes of Health lifts South Africa funding ban

The US National Institutes of Health has reversed its nearly 18-month ban on South Africa, allowing funding for "meritorious research projects" to resume.

The US National Institutes of Health (NIH) has lifted its ban on South Africa, which had halted many clinical trials and led to the retrenchment of numerous researchers. The decision comes after 12 months of negotiations between the NIH and the South African Medical Research Council (SAMRC), according to SAMRC president and CEO Ntobeko Ntusi.

An internal memo dated August seventeen, signed by NIH director Jay Bhattacharya, confirmed the lifting of the ban, stating that funding for research projects in South Africa may now proceed. The memo, marked "Not for distribution outside government," clarifies that NIH grants are distinct from traditional foreign aid.

The ban, initiated in February, had created a crisis for HIV and TB research in South Africa, impacting critical research initiatives and decades of investment in research infrastructure. Research in South Africa, often led by local scientists with NIH funding, has historically shaped global health policies for TB and HIV prevention, diagnosis, treatment, and care.

Why it mattersThe lifting of the ban directly affects South African researchers and patients by restoring funding for critical HIV and TB research and clinical trials.

Johannesburg panel urges youth inclusion for city renewal

A Metro Lives dialogue highlighted that Johannesburg's urban renewal efforts will fail without the active participation of its young people, who face food insecurity and exclusion.

Johannesburg's renewal efforts depend on the meaningful participation of its young people, according to speakers at a Metro Lives dialogue hosted by the DG Murray Trust at Constitution Hill. The discussion on Monday, August twenty-fourth, emphasized treating youth as co-authors of the city's future, not merely as beneficiaries, to address issues like food insecurity, public safety, and exclusion.

Matshidiso Lencoasa, a budget analyst at SECTION27, stated that young people possess ideas and solutions that need to be foregrounded for Johannesburg to work for all. She joined Dr. Tlaleng Mofokeng, Nomzekelo Mohamed, Mark Heywood, and Ayanda Allie in exploring how local governments can build safer, healthier, and more inclusive communities by centering children and young people.

The conversation also touched on the city's failing urban food system, with one high school learner sharing her experience of going to school hungry. Mark Heywood, co-founder of Union Against Hunger, suggested practical steps like identifying municipal land for food cultivation, ensuring water and security, and viewing the city as a food-producing space, citing the demolition of an inner-city farm as a counterproductive action.

Why it mattersJohannesburg's young people are directly affected by the city's renewal plans, as their exclusion could lead to continued food insecurity and lack of safety.

Sport · 08/25/2026

Simbine wins Sport Star of the Year at 19th SA Sport Awards

Sprinter Akani Simbine received the top individual honor, while the Protea Men’s Test Cricket team was named Team of the Year at the South African Sport Awards.

Akani Simbine was crowned Sport Star of the Year at the 19th South African Sport Awards held at the Sun City Superbowl in North West. The sprinter was recognized for his consistent performance and ability to inspire millions.

The Protea Men’s Test Cricket team secured the Team of the Year award. Their victory in the ICC World Test Championship in 2025 against Australia at Lord’s marked a significant achievement, ending decades of near-misses for the team.

The event, themed “Crowned in Greatness,” celebrated 19 years of sporting achievement, honoring athletes, teams, coaches, administrators, and volunteers who contribute to South Africa’s presence on the world stage.

Why it mattersSouth African citizens are affected by the recognition of their athletes and teams, fostering national pride and unity across various communities.
