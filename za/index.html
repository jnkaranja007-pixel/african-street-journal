Mosimane faces McCarthy in crucial AFCON qualifiers

South Africa's new coach Pitso Mosimane will meet Kenya's Benni McCarthy twice in November during the 2027 Africa Cup of Nations qualification campaign.

Pitso Mosimane has been officially appointed as the new head coach of South Africa's men's national football team, Bafana Bafana. This marks Mosimane's second permanent stint in charge, succeeding Hugo Broos. His initial competitive matches are scheduled for September against Guinea and Eritrea. However, the November international window presents a significant challenge as South Africa prepares to host Kenya on November 11 and then travel to face them four days later.

These upcoming fixtures against Kenya, led by fellow South African football icon Benni McCarthy, are particularly critical. Kenya, as co-hosts of the 2027 AFCON, automatically qualify for the tournament. This means only one other team from their qualification group will secure a spot at the continental finals, placing immense pressure on Bafana Bafana to secure positive results.

Mosimane, a highly decorated coach with numerous honours from his time with Mamelodi Sundowns and Al Ahly, now faces a crucial test early in his tenure. The double-header against McCarthy's Kenyan side represents a pivotal moment in the qualification campaign, with dropping points potentially having severe consequences for South Africa's chances of reaching the 2027 AFCON.

Why it mattersSouth African football fans and investors in the sport will be affected by the performance of Bafana Bafana in these high-stakes qualification matches.

Education · 2026-09-02

South African Schools Close for 12-Day Holiday

Learners will break for the September holidays after the shortest school term of the year, returning in October.

The third school term in South Africa concludes on Wednesday, September 23, granting students a 12-day break that includes the Heritage Day public holiday. This term, comprising 46 school days, is one of the shortest of the year. Students will resume classes for the fourth term on Tuesday, October 6.

The Department of Basic Education structures the academic year with four terms, aiming to maintain a consistent framework for teaching and learning. The 2026 school year began on January 14 and will conclude on December 9, encompassing a total of 199 school days and 28 school holidays, in addition to six public holidays.

This break comes as matriculants prepare for their mock matric exams, a crucial part of their preparation for final assessments. The academic calendar is reviewed annually to ensure no loss of teaching and learning days, providing a predictable schedule for students and educators nationwide.

Why it mattersStudents are directly affected by the school calendar, which dictates their periods of study and rest.

Health · 2026-09-02

Public Health Congress Confronts Climate Crisis Impacts

Delegates meeting in Cape Town will connect health crises to energy justice and a transition away from fossil fuels.

The 18th World Congress on Public Health convenes in Cape Town this September, offering a platform for global health professionals to address the escalating health impacts of the climate crisis. The congress aims to guide Africa's pursuit of energy justice and a just transition away from fossil fuels, linking various crises from conflict to extreme weather. Attendees will share expertise on illnesses linked to unsafe work, poverty, pollution, and political neglect, all exacerbated by conditions like the super El Niño.

The congress's theme, 'Health Without Borders: Equity, Inclusion, and Sustainability,' calls for understanding and connecting crises often viewed in isolation. The ongoing conflict in the Strait of Hormuz, impacting global oil supplies, serves as a stark example of how fossil fuel dependence creates widespread public health emergencies. Rising oil prices, increased transport and food costs, and fiscal strain on importing nations further pressure already strained health systems.

This gathering provides a crucial opportunity for the public health community to identify shared systems driving ill health, including conflict, energy insecurity, rising costs, pollution, climate change, and inequality. Public health's role extends beyond direct disease causes to examining underlying political, commercial, social, and environmental factors that determine access to essential resources and influence overall well-being.

Why it mattersThe public health community is affected by the interconnected crises of climate change, energy insecurity, and geopolitical conflicts that strain health systems and increase the cost of essential goods and care.

News · 2026-09-02

Madlanga Commission Demands Medical Proof for Khan Testimony Delays

The commission will not postpone suspended officer Feroz Khan's testimony further without independent medical verification of his inability to appear.

The Madlanga Commission has set a deadline of September 28 for suspended Crime Intelligence officer Major General Feroz Khan to testify, making any further delays conditional on an independent medical assessment. Khan's testimony has been repeatedly postponed due to medical excuses, most recently a hospital admission over the weekend. His legal representative argued against an independent assessment, fearing it could worsen his client's condition.

Commission chair Justice Madlanga ruled that while Khan's medical condition details remain private, his ability to testify must be independently verified. This decision follows scrutiny over altered and backdated sick notes. Khan was injured in a shooting on June 28, shortly before a previous scheduled appearance.

The commission will not force Khan to undergo a medical examination, adopting an approach similar to a previous case. Khan's ongoing medical issues are the latest development in an inquiry where his name has frequently surfaced, including scrutiny of his relationship with former Investigating Directorate Against Corruption head Andrea Johnson.

Why it mattersMajor General Feroz Khan's testimony is delayed due to medical excuses, impacting the progress of the Madlanga Commission's inquiry.

Sport · 2026-09-02

Messi Retires From International Football

The 39-year-old Argentina captain announced his retirement on August 31, 2026, after a career spanning 207 caps and 125 goals.

Lionel Messi, 39, has announced his retirement from international football on August 31, 2026, ending a career that included 125 goals in 207 appearances for Argentina. The decision follows Argentina's runner-up finish at the 2026 World Cup and comes amid personal grief from the recent death of his father, Jorge Messi.

Messi's international journey began in 2005, marked by near-misses in major finals before finally securing the World Cup in 2022, alongside two Copa América titles and the Finalissima. His career was also defined by overcoming a childhood growth hormone deficiency, with his treatment funded by Barcelona.

The legendary forward's departure marks the end of an era for the Argentina national team, leaving a significant void. While his international career concludes, Messi, who plays for Inter Miami CF, will continue his club career through the 2028 season.

Why it mattersFootball fans worldwide, particularly those who follow Argentina, will be affected by the retirement of one of the sport's most iconic figures.
