South African court postpones Sibiya case to 9 February

Investigators are seeking more time to examine four devices allegedly wiped before the former deputy police chief’s arrest and reconstruct a hotel scene.

Sources: The South African and Mail & Guardian · 2026-11-25 · 1 min read

A Randburg magistrate’s court postponed former deputy national police commissioner Shadrack Sibiya’s case to 9 February 2027, after the State sought more time for its investigation. Sibiya, 59, remains in custody and faces six charges, including rape, human trafficking and the sexual grooming of a minor.

State prosecutor Zwelethu Mata told the court on Tuesday, 24 November, that investigators had hired an expert to recover information from four seized digital devices and were awaiting a report. Prosecutors alleged during Sibiya’s October bail proceedings that he wiped his phones before his arrest and asked a 16-year-old complainant to delete their messages. Sibiya denies the allegations.

Investigators are also waiting for a Sandton hotel floor plan to complete a reconstruction and photographic record of the alleged scene involving an 18-year-old woman. Prosecutors are considering moving the case to the Johannesburg High Court for trial.

Why it mattersInvestigators must complete the device analysis and hotel-scene reconstruction before the case returns to court on 9 February 2027.
