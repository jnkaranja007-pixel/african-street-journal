South Africa reach 140/4 against Australia

Proteas hold a 40-run lead after Australia were bowled out for 429 in the third Test in Cape Town.

Sources: The Citizen SA, Daily Maverick and The South African · 2026-10-30 · 1 min read

South Africa ended day three of the third Test against Australia in Cape Town on Thursday at 140/4, leading by 40 runs. Matthew Breetzke was 19 not out and Ryan Rickelton was unbeaten on eight. Australia were bowled out for 429 in their first innings, a lead of 100 runs.

Spin bowler Simon Harmer led the Proteas attack with 4/104, removing Josh Inglis (102) and Alex Carey (80) who had put on 185 runs for the fifth wicket. Seamer Marco Jansen took 3/60 as South Africa's bowlers claimed the last five Australian wickets for 59 runs. Captain Temba Bavuma scored 31 off 115 balls before being bowled by Nathan Lyon.

The Proteas need a strong performance on day four to stay in the series, which is currently level at 1-1. Bavuma and Marques Ackerman (62) attempted to stabilise the innings after South Africa fell to 95/3. Wiaan Mulder, Marco Jansen, and Simon Harmer are still to bat for the hosts.

Why it mattersSouth African cricket fans will be watching to see if the Proteas can secure a series win at home.
