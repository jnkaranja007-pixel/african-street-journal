Johannesburg runners raise cancer funds

Thousands of men and boys wore purple Speedos for the 5km Hollard Daredevil Run charity event.

Source: The Citizen SA · 2026-10-26 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Thousands of men and boys ran in purple Speedos for the Hollard Daredevil Run in Johannesburg on October 23, 2026. The annual 5km charity event took place at Zoo Lake to raise awareness and funds for prostate and testicular cancer.

Participants ran to support cancer-related causes, with the event aiming to highlight the importance of early detection and treatment. The Hollard Daredevil Run is a well-established fixture in the city's calendar.

The event also featured a colourful 32nd Cape Town International Kite Festival at Youngsfield Military Base on October 24, 2026. This festival is Africa’s oldest kite festival and aims to raise mental health awareness.
