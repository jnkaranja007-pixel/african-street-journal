foreign national beaten to death in Durban

Police are investigating the murder of a man assaulted by about 50 people on Dr Pixley kaSeme Street.

Source: The Citizen SA · 2026-10-23 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Police in KwaZulu-Natal have opened a murder case after a foreign national was allegedly beaten to death in Durban CBD on Thursday, 22 October 2026. The man, believed to be in his late 30s or early 40s, was allegedly assaulted by a group of about 50 people and was declared dead at the scene.

Information suggests a group of people was marching along Dr Pixley kaSeme Street when two males broke away and approached the victim. CCTV footage showed the pair speaking to the man before he was later found lying on the ground with swelling at the back of his head. The motive for the killing is unknown, and police are following several leads.

Investigators are working to trace those responsible for the assault, which occurred on Dr Pixley kaSeme Street. Police have urged community members not to spread unverified information as the investigation continues.
