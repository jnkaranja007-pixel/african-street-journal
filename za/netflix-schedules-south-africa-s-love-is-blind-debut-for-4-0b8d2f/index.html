Netflix schedules South Africa’s Love Is Blind debut for 4 December

The first six episodes of the dating series will premiere on that date, with two more released on 11 December and the finale on 18 December.

Source: Mail & Guardian · 2026-11-18 · 1 min read

Netflix will launch the first South African edition of Love Is Blind on 4 December, with 27 single contestants trying to find a spouse without seeing their potential partners. The series is hosted by Dineo and Solo Langa.

Six episodes are scheduled for the opening release, followed by two on 11 December and the final episode on 18 December. Contestants date in specially designed pods, where they can speak through a wall; they must get engaged to progress and meet face to face.

Cape Town production house afrOkaans produces the show. After the pod dates, couples who progress travel to a location where they can spend time together in person and meet the other couples.

Why it mattersThe 27 contestants must get engaged before they can meet their potential partners face to face.
