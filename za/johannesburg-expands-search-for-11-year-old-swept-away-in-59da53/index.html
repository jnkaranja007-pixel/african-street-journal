Johannesburg expands search for 11-year-old swept away in Soweto floods

Teams are searching back from a debris blockage about 4 km downstream, with police dog units, divers and community members joining the operation.

Sources: The Citizen SA, Mail & Guardian and The South African · 2026-11-20 · 1 min read

Johannesburg rescue teams expanded their search on Thursday for Anqobile Jiyela, an 11-year-old swept away by floodwaters in Soweto on Tuesday afternoon. The search resumed in Dobsonville Extension 3 after worsening weather suspended it on Wednesday.

Emergency Medical Services spokesperson Robert Mulaudzi said crews were working from a debris blockage about 4 km downstream toward the point where Anqobile entered the water, as well as checking nearby drainage channels. Two police dog teams, two rescue-technician teams, divers and community members joined the operation, which covered an area between two bridges.

A police helicopter assisted with drone work, while crews cleared reeds to improve access. Mulaudzi told eNCA that teams planned to use machetes and knives on Friday to clear more reeds and move into the targeted area.
