Mulder’s 100 and seven wickets power South Africa to 2-0 series win

The all-rounder became the first South African to score a century and take seven wickets in one Test innings, beating Bangladesh in Centurion.

Sources: The Citizen SA and The South African · 2026-11-26 · 1 min read

Wiaan Mulder scored 100 and took seven wickets in Bangladesh’s second innings as South Africa won the second Test by an innings and 219 runs in Centurion on Wednesday. He also took one wicket in Bangladesh’s first innings, giving him eight in the match, and caught a batter to end the game.

Mulder became the first South African to make a century and claim a seven-wicket haul in one Test innings. He said captain Temba Bavuma had challenged him after he scored a duck and took no wickets in the first Test in Johannesburg. Bavuma’s conversations reminded him to stick to his game plan and start each innings afresh, Mulder said.

The result completed a 2-0 Test series sweep. South Africa’s next fixtures against Bangladesh are three one-day internationals and three Twenty20 matches in December, followed by a home series against England.

Why it mattersMulder and Bavuma must now carry the lessons of their first-Test discussions into South Africa’s three one-day internationals and three Twenty20 matches against Bangladesh in December.
