Springboks to meet France in inaugural Nations Championship final

France's 45-17 win over Argentina sent them through, while South Africa reached the decider despite losing 20-17 to Ireland in Dublin.

Sources: The Citizen SA, BBC Africa and The South African · 2026-11-23 · 1 min read

France's 45-17 win over Argentina on Saturday sealed their place in next weekend's Nations Championship final against South Africa at Twickenham. The bonus-point result gave France top spot in the northern hemisphere pool, ahead of England and Ireland, while Argentina slipped to fourth in the southern hemisphere table.

South Africa lost 20-17 to Ireland at the Aviva Stadium in Dublin, but their bonus point for a defeat by fewer than eight points kept them top of the southern hemisphere standings on points difference, ahead of New Zealand. France needed a full five points to reach the final after England beat New Zealand earlier on Saturday.

Argentina, who were well in the match until two second-half cards cost them, will play Scotland for seventh place next week. The final is scheduled for Sunday, 29 November, at 18:40 South African time.

Why it mattersSpringboks supporters in South Africa and abroad can now plan for the final on Sunday, 29 November, at Twickenham.
