ANC and ActionSA prepare 18-party Johannesburg coalition

Herman Mashaba is expected to become mayor, while the ANC is set to retain the speaker and finance posts; the deputy mayor remains undecided.

Sources: Daily Maverick, The Citizen SA and The South African · 2026-11-25 · 1 min read

The African National Congress (ANC) and ActionSA are finalising a coalition arrangement to share power in Johannesburg, South Africa’s economic hub. ActionSA leader Herman Mashaba is expected to become mayor when councillors elect office-bearers on Friday. The ANC is expected to retain the speaker’s position and finance portfolio.

The deputy mayor’s post remains unresolved, with the ANC, the uMkhonto Wesizwe party and the Economic Freedom Fighters discussed as possible contenders, Daily Maverick reported. The proposed framework brings 18 parties together, excluding the Democratic Alliance, Freedom Front Plus and African Christian Democratic Party. The council’s first sitting on Monday swore in councillors but postponed the office-bearer elections to give parties more time to negotiate.

The ANC won 20.25% of the vote and 64 seats in the 270-member council, while ActionSA won 16.27% and 42 seats. The Democratic Alliance took 91 seats, the most of any party, but fell short of the 136 required for a majority. The wider ANC-ActionSA agreement would give ActionSA the mayoralties in Johannesburg and Tshwane, while the ANC keeps Ekurhuleni’s mayoralty.

Why it mattersJohannesburg councillors must settle the deputy mayor’s post and elect office-bearers on Friday after their first sitting postponed the vote.
