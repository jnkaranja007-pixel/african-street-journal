Johannesburg reverses Rea Vaya bus suspension after payments

Commuters will not need alternate transport after operators received partial funds.

Sources: The Citizen SA and Daily Maverick · 2026-11-17 · 1 min read

The City of Johannesburg reversed its decision to suspend Rea Vaya Bus Rapid Transit services after resolving payment issues with operating companies on Monday. Bus companies received partial payment on Monday afternoon, allowing the subsidized public transport network to continue running on Tuesday, 17 November.

The municipality had initially announced the shutdown due to financial troubles, mirroring chlorine shortages that forced public pools to close from Monday. Tabisa Mogapi, an Economic Freedom Fighters caucus whip, stated that worsening municipal financial challenges have contributed to recurring disruptions in public services relied upon by working-class communities.

In a separate incident on Monday morning, an empty Rea Vaya bus crashed along the Soweto Highway near Immink Drive before 5am. The driver was rushed to Tshepo Themba hospital for a medical check-up after the vehicle, which was travelling between Thokoza and the Soweto depot without passengers, collided.

Why it mattersCommuters relying on the Rea Vaya bus network retain their daily transport service instead of arranging alternate travel for Tuesday, 17 November.
