Springboks reach Nations Championship final despite 20-17 loss to Ireland

Ireland beat South Africa in Dublin, but the Springboks' losing bonus point puts them through to the final at Twickenham.

Sources: The Citizen SA, Daily Maverick and BBC Africa · 2026-11-22 · 1 min read

Ireland beat South Africa 20-17 in Dublin on Saturday, but the Springboks earned a losing bonus point for losing by fewer than seven points and qualified for the Nations Championship final. The match was at the Aviva Stadium, and the defeat was the Springboks' second in a row after losing to France the previous weekend.

Ireland led 17-10 at halftime and scored three tries to South Africa's two. Ireland won the lineout ball, dominated the breakdown and kept the Springboks pinned in their own half for much of the game. Rassie Erasmus, the banned head coach, watched from the team hotel.

The final is at Twickenham next Sunday, when South Africa are due to meet either France or England. France were scheduled to play Argentina later on Saturday, and England's win over the All Blacks on the same day means they will qualify if South Africa lose.

Why it mattersThe Springboks must prepare for the Twickenham final next Sunday, with France or England as their opponent.
