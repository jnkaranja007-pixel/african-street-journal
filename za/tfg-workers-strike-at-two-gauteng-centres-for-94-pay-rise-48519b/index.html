TFG workers strike at two Gauteng centres for 94% pay rise

Hundreds downed tools at the retailer’s Kempton Park and Midrand distribution centres after wage negotiations stalled.

Sources: The Citizen SA and GroundUp · 2026-11-24 · 1 min read

Hundreds of workers at The Foschini Group (TFG) distribution centres in Kempton Park and Midrand, Gauteng, went on strike on Monday, 23 November, seeking a salary increase of at least 94%. They submitted 48 hours’ notice on 20 November after wage negotiations deadlocked.

Esther Mwasi, a worker and representative of the Simunye Workers Forum, said workers had gathered at the centres from Monday morning. She estimated no more than 100 were on site, although a district figure put the workforce at about 295. TFG said it had asked the Commission for Conciliation, Mediation and Arbitration to facilitate talks, but employees declined; the company said it remained open to engagement.

The forum is also seeking a Monday-to-Friday, 45-hour workweek, overtime pay for weekend hours and clear pay levels for each position. It alleges some workers earn as little as R6 200 a month.

Why it mattersTFG workers are pressing for overtime pay on Saturdays and Sundays, which the forum says they currently do not receive.
