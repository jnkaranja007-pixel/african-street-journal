Floods wash away shacks and close roads in Gauteng and Mpumalanga

Storm damage flooded informal settlements, disrupted public services and left a bridge on the R542 near Boomplaas damaged.

Sources: The South African and The Citizen SA · 2026-11-26 · 1 min read

Heavy rain on Tuesday and Wednesday flooded homes, swept away shacks and damaged roads across Gauteng and Mpumalanga. In Bethal, Mpumalanga, two teenage boys drowned in separate incidents. Emergency teams rescued motorists as floodwater submerged roads and bridges.

The City of Ekurhuleni recorded 20 storm-related incidents, including 15 water rescues and five fallen-tree cases. Most shacks at Gugulethu informal settlement were reportedly washed away, while homes in Skomplaas, Springs, were flooded. Johannesburg emergency services spokesperson Robert Mulaudzi said blocked stormwater drains contributed to flooding. The Johannesburg Roads Agency closed Nasrec Road after infrastructure collapsed.

In Mpumalanga, flooding damaged offices and equipment at Evander police station and disrupted power and technical services at KwaMhlanga station. A bridge on the R542 near Boomplaas was damaged, and authorities diverted traffic while assessing it. Routes in Moloto became inaccessible, and the local hospital was also affected.

Why it mattersResidents in Ekurhuleni and Johannesburg are dealing with washed-away homes and road closures, while traffic near Boomplaas is diverted during a bridge assessment.
