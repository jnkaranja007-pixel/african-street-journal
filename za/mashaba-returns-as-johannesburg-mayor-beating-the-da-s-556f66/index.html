Mashaba returns as Johannesburg mayor, beating the DA's Helen Zille

The ActionSA leader was elected at a council sitting, after coalition talks involving the ANC, ActionSA and the Economic Freedom Fighters (EFF) party.

Sources: The Citizen SA, Mail & Guardian and The South African · 2026-11-28 · 1 min read

Herman Mashaba has returned as executive mayor of the City of Johannesburg, after councillors elected him at a council sitting in the Johannesburg Council Chamber on Friday, 27 November. He beat Helen Zille of the Democratic Alliance (DA), the party he served under as mayor from 2016 to 2019. He now leads ActionSA.

Councillors voted after reports of a coalition agreement involving the African National Congress (ANC), ActionSA and the Economic Freedom Fighters (EFF). The DA won the most seats in the 4 November local government elections, with 91 of the 270 seats, followed by the ANC with 63 and ActionSA with 43, but it fell short of an outright majority.

Zille had said that last-minute coalition negotiations were still underway ahead of the vote, and she declined to say whether the DA was negotiating with ActionSA.

Why it mattersJohannesburg residents now have a mayor from ActionSA, elected by councillors after coalition talks among several parties.
