Water commission portal targets municipal losses as non-revenue water hits 47.4%

The resource hub includes field-tested tools, while a planned sharing feature would let councils pass on approaches that worked in their networks.

Source: Mail & Guardian · 2026-11-28 · 1 min read

The Water Research Commission is developing a portal to help South African municipalities tackle non-revenue water, which the department’s national water balance puts at 47.4%. The commission identified 84 resources for the hub, research psychologist Leonie Vorster said at a webinar in Durban on 25 November.

Vorster said the next phase would let registered users share approaches that had worked in their networks. The portal has been in development for about two years, and some municipalities have started using it. It will bring together guidelines, tools and information on technologies tested in the field, water specialist Dr Manjusha Sunil said.

The national water balance, drawing on the department’s 2023 No Drop assessment, estimated water losses at 40.8%. Non-revenue water also includes supplies that generate no revenue; officials need to distinguish leaks, which require repairs, from inaccurate readings or billing errors.

Why it mattersMunicipal officials must identify whether losses come from leaks or measurement and billing errors, with the national water balance estimating physical water losses at 40.8%.
