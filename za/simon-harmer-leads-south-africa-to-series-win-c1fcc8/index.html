Simon Harmer leads South Africa to series win

Spin bowler Simon Harmer took career-best figures of 6/16 in the final innings to secure a 176-run victory against Australia.

Source: The Citizen SA · 2026-11-01 · 2 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Spin bowler Simon Harmer played a key role for South Africa in Cape Town on Saturday, taking career-best figures of 6/16 in Australia’s second innings. The 37-year-old’s performance helped the Proteas to a 176-run victory in the Test series decider at Newlands, securing a 2-1 series win. Harmer also achieved 10 wickets in the match for the first time in a five-day game, earning him the Player of the Match award.

Harmer, playing in his 17th test, credited his lengthy domestic career experience for his performance, stating he focused on dealing with expectations. He had struggled in the first two matches of the series, taking only two wickets in each. "It’s been a tough few weeks but I will enjoy tonight," Harmer said after the match. The Proteas secured their first Test series victory over Australia since 2018.

South Africa scored 329 in their first innings, with contributions from Marques Ackerman (95) and Aiden Markram (78), before Australia replied with 429, led by Josh Inglis (102). The Proteas then posted 387 in their second innings, featuring centuries from Wiaan Mulder (115) and Ryan Rickelton (101), setting Australia a target of 288. Australia were bowled out for 111 in their second innings on October 31, 2026.

Why it mattersSouth African cricket fans can celebrate a significant Test series victory over rivals Australia.
