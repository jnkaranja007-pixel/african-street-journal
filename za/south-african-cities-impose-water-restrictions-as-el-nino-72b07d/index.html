South African cities impose water restrictions as El Niño strengthens

The US Climate Prediction Centre puts the chance of a strong-to-very-strong event through January-March 2027 above 79%.

Source: Daily Maverick · 2026-11-20 · 1 min read

Tshwane, Johannesburg and Cape Town have imposed water-use restrictions as El Niño strengthens, while the Western Cape as a whole also faces curbs. The restrictions follow Agriculture Minister Willie Aucamp’s call last month for proactive measures to address the weather pattern.

The US government’s Climate Prediction Centre said in a forecast released early this week that a strong-to-very-strong El Niño was likely through January-March 2027, with the chance remaining above 79%. The report said the event would compound municipal water problems linked to poor maintenance of critical infrastructure.

Dam levels remain above 90% in every province except the Western Cape and KwaZulu-Natal, according to the Department of Water and Sanitation’s latest weekly report; five provinces are above their levels at the same time last year. The article says R10-billion in funding is available to address the crisis.
