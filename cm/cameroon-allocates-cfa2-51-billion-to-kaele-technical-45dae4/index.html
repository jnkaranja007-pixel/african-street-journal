Cameroon allocates CFA2.51 billion to Kaélé technical school works

A tender assigns CFA1.08 billion, or 43.1% of the proposed budget, to a garden, perimeter wall and playground.

Source: Business in Cameroon · 2026-11-19 · 1 min read

Cameroon’s Far North reconstruction program has set aside an estimated CFA2.51 billion for construction and renovation at Kaélé Technical High School in Mayo-Kani Division. The project is divided into eight lots, according to a tender dated November 11, 2026.

The tender, published on November 17 by the Public Contracts Regulatory Agency, assigns CFA1.08 billion, or 43.1% of the total, to a public garden, perimeter wall and playground. The garden is allocated CFA423.5 million for 10 hectares; the two-kilometer wall is budgeted at CFA374 million, and the playground at CFA285 million.

Technical workshops and classrooms together have CFA829.8 million, less than the three outdoor facilities. The workshop budget is CFA441.1 million and covers construction and rehabilitation of vocational training facilities.

Why it mattersThe program must distribute the school’s CFA2.51 billion allocation across eight tender lots, including vocational facilities and outdoor works.
