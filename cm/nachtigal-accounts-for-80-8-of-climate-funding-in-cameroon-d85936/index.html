Nachtigal accounts for 80.8% of climate funding in Cameroon review

Three operations tied to the hydropower project totaled $48.2 million of $59.7 million in receipts covered by the government’s review.

Source: Business in Cameroon · 2026-11-26 · 1 min read

Nachtigal, the hydroelectric plant, makes up 80.8% of the climate financing Cameroon received in a sample of operations that the government reviewed. The country’s First Biennial Transparency Report and Fourth National Communication on Climate Change, sent to the United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change on November 13, 2026, puts three operations linked to the plant at $48.2 million, or about CFA28.6 billion, out of $59.7 million, or CFA35.4 billion, in reported receipts.

The figures, on page 235 of the report, were drawn from 80 financing operations across 11 funding providers. The United Kingdom supplied $18.5 million in 2020 and $17.7 million in 2021 through two operations, which the report classes as other official flows, excluding export credits. France lent about $12.1 million in 2020 in a third operation, classed as official development assistance.

The report stresses that the totals are not the full climate financing mobilised in Cameroon, nor the full cost of the project.

Why it mattersFunders and lenders in the 420 MW plant now have a government record showing how much of Cameroon’s climate money they supplied.
