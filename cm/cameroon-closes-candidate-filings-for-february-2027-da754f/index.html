Cameroon closes candidate filings for February 2027 elections

Elections Cameroon received at least 200 files from 27 parties in Yaoundé, while parties submitted candidacies across the country.

Sources: Journal du Cameroun and Actu Cameroun · 2026-11-25 · 1 min read

Elections Cameroon closed submissions for the February 2027 legislative and municipal elections at 23:59 on 24 November 2026. Its Yaoundé headquarters received at least 200 candidacy files from 27 political parties, and territorial offices took submissions across the country.

The governing Cameroon People's Democratic Movement submitted more than 100 files for legislative elections. The Cameroon Renaissance Movement filed seven legislative candidacies in Mfoundi, represented by its former president, Maurice Kamto. In the Littoral region, the governing party submitted more than 30 files in Wouri, while opposition lawmaker Jean-Michel Nintcheu led the list for Wouri-East.

Electoral offices must centralise and send the files to the national directorate within five days of the close. The Electoral Council is due to begin examining each candidacy on 29 November and publish accepted and rejected lists on 09 December 2026, opening the pre-election dispute process.
