SCR Maya brings in Côte d’Ivoire-based group to support modernization

The Cameroonian vegetable oil producer has not named the investor or disclosed the value or structure of the deal announced in Douala.

Source: Business in Cameroon · 2026-11-22 · 1 min read

Cameroonian vegetable oil producer Société camerounaise de raffinage Maya & Cie (SCR Maya) has brought a Côte d’Ivoire-based industrial group into its ownership structure, the company announced in Douala on November 19, 2026. SCR Maya said the deal is intended to support its development and modernization.

Hazim Chehade Hazim, SCR Maya’s chairman and chief executive, announced the transaction. The company has not identified the new shareholder, disclosed the amount involved or said what percentage of equity changed hands. It has also not clarified whether the investor bought shares from existing owners or subscribed for newly issued shares.
