Golar LNG books CFA3.23 billion in Cameroon exit costs

The gas company took the charges after its Perenco contract expired and its floating gas vessel sailed out of the country.

Source: Business in Cameroon · 2026-11-22 · 1 min read

Golar LNG has booked $5.4 million, about CFA3.23 billion, in employee termination costs after its Hilli Episeyo floating liquefied natural gas facility stopped operating in Cameroon. The charges show up in the company's financial statements for the first nine months of 2026, published on November 18.

In the segment results, Golar said Cameroonian labour law governed the charges, which followed the expiry of its contract with Perenco and the vessel's departure. It declined to say how many workers were hit or how much had already been paid out. The total climbed from $2.5 million at March 31 to $4.8 million at June 30 and $5.4 million at September 30.

The third-quarter addition of $600,000 was up 12.5% on the June total. The disclosures do not show how much cash has actually gone to departing staff.

Why it mattersDeparting workers are owed termination pay under Cameroonian labour law, yet the company has not said how many are affected or how much it has paid.
