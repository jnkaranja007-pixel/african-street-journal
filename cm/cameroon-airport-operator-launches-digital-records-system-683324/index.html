Cameroon airport operator launches digital records system

State-owned ADC says MAARCH will reduce paperwork and speed procedures across the company, which manages seven airports.

Source: Business in Cameroon · 2026-11-20 · 1 min read

Cameroon's state-owned airport operator, Aéroports du Cameroun (ADC), launched a digital document platform on November 17 to reduce paperwork and administrative delays. Managing Director Thomas Owona Assoumou introduced MAARCH, which is designed to let departments share, process and store documents electronically.

ADC manages seven airports and handles work including infrastructure maintenance, aircraft ground handling and facility development. The company expects electronic files to shorten processing times, improve access to information and limit paper use, while strengthening transparency and reducing administrative risks.

MAARCH is intended for internal use. ADC said the system is part of its efforts to modernize company operations.

Why it mattersADC staff coordinating airport maintenance, ground handling and facility development will use the internal platform to share and process records electronically.
