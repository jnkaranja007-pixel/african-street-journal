Cameroon opens 82.252-kilometre road serving 22 villages

The Awae-Esse-Soa route links three communes and connects to national roads 1, 4 and 10, with access toward the Central African Republic.

Sources: Journal du Cameroun and Actu Cameroun · 2026-11-27 · 1 min read

Cameroon’s public works minister, Emmanuel Nganou Djoumessi, officially opened the 82.252-kilometre Awae-Esse-Soa road on Thursday, linking three communes in the Mefou and Afamba department. The road serves 22 villages and joins national roads 1, 4 and 10.

The Ministry of Public Works said the new route will help move macabo, cassava and plantain to national and regional markets faster. The project, funded with CFA76.61 billion from the public investment budget, was built by China First Highway Engineering Company Cameroon and generated 943 jobs, including 919 for Cameroonians.

The works also included a two-by-two-lane urban crossing in Esse, 5,000 square metres of parking in Soa and 25 hand-pumped wells along the route. Connections through the national roads extend toward the Central African Republic.

Why it mattersFarmers and traders in 22 villages can move macabo, cassava and plantain faster to national and regional markets.
