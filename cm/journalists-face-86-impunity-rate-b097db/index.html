Journalists face 86% impunity rate

UNESCO reports global impunity for journalist murders rose to 86% in 2026, reversing a downward trend.

Source: Journal du Cameroun · 2026-11-04 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Globally, six out of seven murdered journalists see no justice, with the UNESCO reporting an 86% impunity rate in 2026. This figure marks an increase from previous years, reversing a decline that began in 2012 when the rate was 95%.

The report criticizes governments for inaction and complicity, stating that excuses like conflict or terrorism are used to avoid accountability. It highlights that when journalists are killed, it silences whistleblowers and terrorizes newsrooms, ultimately blinding entire populations.

The UNESCO data indicates that impunity for journalist murders is a deliberate policy, sending a message to others to remain silent. This failure of justice is presented as a direct reflection of political courage and the state of the rule of law.

Why it mattersJournalists in Cameroon and globally face a high risk of murder with no perpetrators brought to justice, impacting freedom of expression.
