Cameroon opens first international auto expo

The event in Douala runs until November 15, bringing together automotive businesses and transport operators.

Source: Business in Cameroon · 2026-11-15 · 1 min read

Cameroon's first international automobile, transport and mobility exhibition, the Cameroon Auto Expo 2026, opened in Douala on November 11. Transport Minister Jean Ernest Masséna Ngallè Bibéhè inaugurated the event at the Camtel Club in Bépanda. The expo runs until November 15 and gathers automotive businesses, transport operators, logistics companies, and public institutions.

The exhibition features vehicle displays and business meetings, with discussions covering financing, insurance, customs, and taxation. Its theme focuses on infrastructure, innovation, and regional integration. This event occurs in a market where Cameroon imported CFAF375 billion worth of transport equipment in 2024, a 76.8% increase from 2019, according to National Institute of Statistics figures.

Beyond vehicle sales, the programme addresses the movement of goods through Cameroon, including sessions on multimodal logistics and freight corridor performance. The exhibition aims to foster discussions on the growing market for imported transport equipment.

Why it mattersThe exhibition brings together automotive businesses and transport operators to discuss financing, insurance, customs, and taxation until November 15.
