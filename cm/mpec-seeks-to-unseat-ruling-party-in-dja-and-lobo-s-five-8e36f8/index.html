MPEC seeks to unseat ruling party in Dja and Lobo’s five seats

Professor Jean-Calvin Aba’a Oyono leads a six-person slate challenging the ruling party’s hold on the department’s National Assembly seats.

Source: Journal du Cameroun · 2026-11-28 · 1 min read

Professor Jean-Calvin Aba’a Oyono has filed to contest Cameroon’s 2027 legislative elections in Dja and Lobo, leading a Mouvement populaire pour l’émergence du Cameroun (MPEC) slate seeking all five National Assembly seats held by the ruling Rassemblement démocratique du peuple camerounais (RDPC). The department is in the South region.

The list includes MPEC national president Martine Danielle Befolo Essono, Thomas Dydime Tomo Ze, Edjimbi Epse Nkoumou Nkoumsu and Jean Cyrille Abondo Ngono, as well as their respective substitutes. The Electoral Council must decide whether to accept the slate before the candidates can compete.

Aba’a Oyono was detained for a month after protests over the results of the 12 October 2025 presidential election, the report says. He later filed appeals against external intelligence services and Defence personnel.

Why it mattersMPEC’s candidates and their substitutes must wait for the Electoral Council’s ruling before they can contest the department’s five seats in 2027.
