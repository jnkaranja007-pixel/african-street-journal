Parlym wins CFAF 11.14 billion LPG contract in Cameroon

The French engineering group's subsidiary will build a storage and distribution hub in Douala for the state-owned petroleum company.

Source: Business in Cameroon · 2026-11-16 · 1 min read

Douala is set to get a new liquefied petroleum gas (LPG) storage and distribution hub, after Parlym Cameroun, a subsidiary of French engineering group Parlym, won a CFAF 11.14 billion construction contract. The facility will be built for Société camerounaise des dépôts pétroliers (SCDP), Cameroon's state-owned petroleum storage company, under a deal signed on October 29, 2026.

The Public Contracts Regulatory Agency (ARMP) lists the deal as No. 029/M/DG/DTEC/SDPEST/SST/CIPM-SCDP/2026. At a project kickoff meeting in Douala on November 11, SCDP confirmed it had chosen Parlym. The CFAF 11,140,906,208 figure, including taxes, excludes a separate technical assistance contract estimated at CFAF 417.375 million.

The construction award came in CFAF 93.1 million below the CFAF 11.234 billion budgeted in the international tender issued on August 17, 2026. Investir au Cameroun had estimated the two contracts together at CFAF 11.65 billion in September, before either was awarded.

Why it mattersTraders and gas distributors who depend on LPG supply in Douala will see the new hub's capacity and timing shaped by this contract.
