Cameroon's 2025 budget deficit reaches CFA535 billion

Revenue fell CFA176 billion short of projections while spending exceeded forecasts, widening the deficit by 72.7%.

Source: Business in Cameroon · 2026-11-15 · 1 min read

Cameroon closed 2025 with a budget deficit of CFA535.2 billion, or 1.6% of gross domestic product (GDP). That is CFA225.3 billion, or 72.7%, above the initial estimate of CFA309.9 billion.

Revenue came in at CFA5,174.8 billion against a projection of CFA5,350.8 billion, a gap of CFA176 billion. Government spending rose to CFA5,729.7 billion, CFA83.4 billion more than forecast, and special-purpose accounts ran a CFA19.5 billion surplus.

Figures for the year are set out in a draft budget settlement law that the government put before the National Assembly on 12 November 2026. The Assembly's Conference of Presidents has ruled the bill admissible, but lawmakers have yet to debate it. Total financing needs, including debt repayments, are put at CFA1,925.2 billion.

Why it mattersCameroon's government must find CFA1,925.2 billion in total financing, which includes the CFA535.2 billion deficit and debt repayments, before lawmakers take up the draft settlement law.
