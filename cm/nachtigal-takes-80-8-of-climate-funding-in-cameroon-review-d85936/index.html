Nachtigal takes 80.8% of climate funding in Cameroon review

Three financing operations for the 420 MW plant totalled $48.2 million, within a government sample of 80 operations.

Source: Business in Cameroon · 2026-11-25 · 1 min read

Nachtigal hydroelectric project financing accounted for 80.8% of climate funding received in a sample of operations reviewed by Cameroon’s government. Three operations associated with the plant totalled $48.2 million, or about CFA28.6 billion, out of $59.7 million in reported receipts.

The figures are in Cameroon’s First Biennial Transparency Report and Fourth National Communication on Climate Change, submitted to the United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change on November 13. The review covered 80 financing operations involving 11 funding providers. The report says the amounts are neither the full climate financing mobilised in Cameroon nor the project’s total cost.

The United Kingdom provided $18.5 million in 2020 and $17.7 million in 2021, both classified as other official flows excluding export credits. France provided about $12.1 million in 2020 as an official development assistance loan.

Why it mattersThe government’s review covers 80 operations from 11 providers, and its recorded receipts total $59.7 million, including three Nachtigal operations.
