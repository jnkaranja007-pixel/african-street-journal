Yaoundé to host African hospital forum on building resilient hospitals

The Central Hospital of Yaoundé will hold the forum at the Palais des Congrès from December 8 to 10, 2026, with about 300 health professionals expected.

Source: Journal du Cameroun · 2026-11-15 · 1 min read

The Central Hospital of Yaoundé is organising the African Hospital Forum from 8 to 10 December 2026 at the Palais des Congrès in Yaoundé, marking the hospital's 95th anniversary. The forum, themed "Towards the resilience of the African hospital", aims to help modernise African hospitals facing economic, technological and health challenges.

At a press conference on 12 November 2026, the hospital's director, Professor Pierre Ongolo Zogo, presented the meeting as a platform for reflection and exchange. Professor Elie Claude Ndam Ndjitoyap, chair of the scientific committee, named inclusive and transparent governance, infrastructure modernisation, digital transformation and better quality of care as the main levers. Professor Serge Billong, president of the Cameroonian Society of Public Health, said about 300 health sector actors from Cameroon, other African countries and France are expected.

Doctors, managers, researchers, decision-makers and partners are invited to share experience and propose concrete solutions to health crises, financial constraints and technological change. The hospital says the anniversary is intended as a step towards its centenary.

Why it mattersHospital managers, doctors and public health partners from Cameroon and abroad are invited to bring concrete solutions to the forum on health crises and financial constraints.
