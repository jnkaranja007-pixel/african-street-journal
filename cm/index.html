Cameroon parliament to debate 2027 budget

Lawmakers will review spending and revenue proposals aiming for increased public investment.

Cameroon's Parliament will convene on November 10, 2026, to begin its regular November session, with the 2027 budget bill as a central agenda item. Lawmakers will review the government's proposed spending and revenue measures. Cabinet ministers are expected to defend budget proposals before parliamentary committees.

The discussions will follow an October 12 presidential circular that set economic and fiscal guidelines for the 2027 budget preparation. Authorities aim to increase public investment while managing financing needs and maintaining debt sustainability.

The government has set a target of allocating at least 30% of the general budget to public investment in 2027. Cameroon's 2026 public investment budget stands at CFAF 2,031.15 billion, which is approximately 23.4% of the general budget.

Why it mattersCameroonian lawmakers will review the 2027 budget, which includes plans for public investment and debt management.

Cameroon banks lend CFA5 billion to SMEs

AFG Bank and GBC will provide loans of up to CFA75 million with interest capped at 7.75% for women and youth entrepreneurs.

Cameroon's Deposit and Consignment Fund (CDEC) has partnered with AFG Bank Cameroon and General Bank of Cameroon (GBC) to disburse CFA5 billion in loans to small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs) led by women and young entrepreneurs. AFG Bank will manage CFA3 billion of the total, while GBC will provide CFA2 billion. The agreements were signed on November 4, 2026.

Businesses can secure financing of up to CFA75 million each through installment loans or equipment leasing, with repayment periods ranging from 12 to 48 months. The annual interest rate is capped at 7.75%, excluding taxes. CDEC Managing Director Richard Evina Obam, AFG Bank Cameroon Managing Director Éric Valéry Zoa, and GBC Managing Director Victor Noumoué signed the agreements, which outline terms for a pilot program.

This financing is part of a pilot program announced in April 2026, a collaboration between CDEC and the African Guarantee Fund (AGF). The CFA5 billion represents the program's initial allocation. Participating banks will be responsible for reviewing and approving loan applications.

Why it mattersWomen and youth entrepreneurs in Cameroon can access loans to invest in their small and medium-sized businesses.

Business · 2026-11-10

Cameroonian CEOs invited to U.S.-Africa summit

The U.S. Embassy in Yaoundé is seeking applications from eligible Cameroonian companies for the December 6-9 summit in Mauritius.

The U.S. Embassy in Yaoundé has invited chief executives of eligible Cameroonian companies to apply for the 18th U.S.-Africa Business Summit in Mauritius from December 6-9, 2026. The event aims to help Cameroonian businesses forge commercial partnerships and attract U.S. investment in priority sectors like energy, infrastructure, health, agribusiness, finance, and technology. Applications for the summit, organized by the Corporate Council on Africa, close on November 16.

The summit's theme is “Advancing New Frontiers in U.S.-Africa Prosperity,” and it will feature business-to-business meetings and investment discussions. This invitation follows the August 27 Cameroon-U.S. Bilateral Economic and Commercial Dialogue in Yaoundé, which identified approximately $7 billion in potential trade and investment opportunities with American companies.

The Corporate Council on Africa organizes the summit to connect companies with potential investors and commercial partners. The dialogue identified opportunities in energy and mining, among other sectors.

Why it mattersCameroonian business leaders can apply to attend a summit in Mauritius to seek U.S. investment and partnerships.

Sport · 2026-11-10

André Onana stars in Trabzonspor win

The Cameroonian goalkeeper achieved 100% aerial duels won and 92% successful passes in Sunday's match.

Cameroonian goalkeeper André Onana delivered a strong performance on Sunday, helping Trabzonspor secure a 2-0 victory over Alanyaspor. Onana was decisive in aerial duels and accurate with his passes, keeping a clean sheet throughout the match.

The win followed a disappointing European competition result on Thursday. Onana, formerly of Manchester United, made two crucial saves and maintained his team's advantage. He completed 92% of his passes, including 67% of long balls, and won his only aerial duel.

This victory allows Trabzonspor to solidify their second position in the league standings, three points behind leaders Galatasaray. The team will now head into the November international break with renewed confidence.

Why it mattersCameroonian footballer André Onana's performance directly impacts his team's standing and his own confidence.
