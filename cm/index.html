Léonide Mfoum resigns from UNDP after twelve years of activism

Léonide Mfoum, a national communication cell member, departed the National Union for Democracy and Progress citing a lack of debate and poor leadership after the 2025.

Léonide Mfoum resigned from the National Union for Democracy and Progress (UNDP) on August twenty-fifth, 2026, after twelve years as an activist. Mfoum stated his refusal to continue being led by "incompetents" and described the party as having "no horizon, no perspective."

His departure is motivated by what he calls an "impossibility of debate" within the party, which he claims hinders self-assessment and future planning. Mfoum characterized the UNDP as a "very sick patient" suffering from the "cancer" of absent discussions.

Mfoum attributed the party's failure in the 2025 presidential election, where candidate Bello Bouba Maigari placed fourth with 2.45% of votes, to leaders who resist debate and self-criticism. He also criticized the campaign director, stating that nothing has changed within the UNDP since the election, despite the poor result.

Why it mattersThis event directly affects political actors within Cameroon by altering the composition of a major political party and highlighting internal dissent.

Canyon Resources delays bauxite exports indefinitely amid financial dispute

Canyon Resources has postponed the Minim-Martap bauxite project indefinitely, citing a frozen credit line of 82 billion FCFA and a hostile takeover bid.

Canyon Resources announced on Monday, August twenty-fourth, two thousand twenty-six, that it has indefinitely postponed its Minim-Martap bauxite project, originally slated for first exports in the fourth quarter of two thousand twenty-six. The delay stems from AFG Bank Cameroon freezing an 82 billion FCFA (140 million dollar) credit line essential for the project's launch.

The financial freeze coincides with an aggressive stock market maneuver by A2MP Investments, the main shareholder, which already holds 55.56% of Canyon Resources' capital. A2MP Investments, led by businessman Gagan Gupta, launched a hostile takeover bid at 0.05 Australian dollars per share, arguing that the project is no longer profitable due to escalating logistical costs.

Despite these financial and corporate challenges, the physical infrastructure for the project is largely complete. The mining road is finished, and 60 wagons ordered from China are expected to arrive in Douala in October. The Minim-Martap deposit, containing 1.1 billion tonnes of resources, is ready for extraction, according to the Journal du Cameroun.

Why it mattersThe delay of the Minim-Martap bauxite project means the Cameroonian state will not receive anticipated revenue to offset declining oil production, directly impacting national economic planning.

SCDP inaugurates three new energy infrastructure projects in Douala

The Société Camerounaise des Dépôts Pétroliers (SCDP) launched a gas sphere, a pipeline, and a new headquarters to boost national energy security and storage capacity.

The Société Camerounaise des Dépôts Pétroliers (SCDP) inaugurated three major infrastructures on Friday, August twenty-first, 2026, in Douala. These include a new domestic gas sphere in Bonabéri, a pipeline connecting the Port of Douala to the Bessengué depot, and a new headquarters for the company. The Minister of Water and Energy, Gaston Eloundou Essomba, presided over the ceremony alongside SCDP Director General, Véronique Manzoua épouse Moampea Mbio.

The new gas sphere in Bonabéri increases Cameroon's national gas storage capacity by forty percent, aiming to prevent shortages of cooking gas for households. The pipeline from the Port of Douala to Bessengué is expected to accelerate the unloading of oil tankers, streamline the logistics chain, and reduce fuel supply tensions across the country.

Minister Eloundou Essomba stated that these investments signify a shift from reactive management to an approach based on anticipation, resilience, and energy sovereignty. The new headquarters symbolizes the transformation of the public company, reinforcing efforts to secure daily energy supplies for Cameroonians.

Why it mattersCameroonian households will experience more stable access to cooking gas and fuel due to increased storage capacity and improved logistics.

Yaoundé General Hospital seeks family for unaccompanied patient

Yaoundé General Hospital is searching for relatives or acquaintances of a patient, Mrs. Ngo Tonye, who was admitted to the emergency department without an escort.

Yaoundé General Hospital has issued an urgent appeal to locate the family or acquaintances of Mrs. Ngo Tonye, a patient currently receiving care. Mrs. Tonye, identified as being around 40 years old and residing in Nkongmondo, Douala 1er, was admitted to the hospital's emergency department and has since been stabilized by medical teams.

According to a statement from the hospital's communication unit, Mrs. Tonye has not been accompanied by any family member, friend, or acquaintance since her admission. The hospital is asking anyone who knows Mrs. Ngo Tonye, or who might be looking for her, to contact the emergency department immediately.

The hospital has also specifically requested that Mr. Tonye Tonye Franck, mentioned as a potential contact in the statement, or anyone with information that could help identify or locate the patient's family, reach out to the hospital services. Medical care for Mrs. Tonye is ongoing.

Agriculture · 2026-08-25

CENEEMA delivers Ngouang seed farm to boost maize and soybean production

The National Center for Agricultural Mechanization Studies and Experimentation (CENEEMA) has completed a 123-hectare seed farm in Ngouang, aiming to reduce imports of hybrid.

CENEEMA has delivered the Ngouang seed farm, located in Yoko, Mbam-et-Kim department, Centre region, with 123 hectares developed out of a planned 100. This project, financed by the PIISAH program with 400 million FCFA, aims to produce hybrid maize and soybean seeds and decrease reliance on imports.

The farm, supported by RBH GROUP Sarl and funded by the Ministry of Agriculture and Rural Development (MINADER), is designed as a technological showcase for producers. CENEEMA deployed its Yoko Mechanization Pool, including bulldozers, tractors, and precision planters, to develop the site and demonstrate the impact of mechanization on yields.

The Ngouang farm will serve as an integrated hub, bringing quality inputs closer to agricultural basins along the Ntui-Yoko-Lena-Tibati-Meiganga corridor. The project has already created direct and indirect jobs and stimulated local transport and commerce, contributing to the government's food self-sufficiency goals outlined in the SND30 strategy.

Why it mattersThe project directly affects local producers by providing access to quality hybrid seeds and demonstrating advanced mechanization techniques, potentially increasing their yields and income.
