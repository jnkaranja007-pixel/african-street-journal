Douala BRT project faces 104.6 billion CFA franc funding gap

The Douala Urban Mobility Project requires an additional 104.6 billion CFA francs to maintain its current scope. As of July 17, 2026, the total cost to complete the project rose to 365.4 billion CFA francs from an initial budget of 260.8 billion CFA francs, according to Business in Cameroon.

Why it mattersThe shortfall may lead to a downsizing of the Bus Rapid Transit system and the removal of 66 kilometers of planned feeder roads.

Cameroon signs Bolongo offshore block deal with Octavia Energy

Octavia Energy Corporation committed to an exploration program of up to $41 million under a production sharing contract signed Aug. 14. The agreement covers a 381.56-square-kilometre block, according to Business in Cameroon.

Why it mattersThe contract moves the offshore block into the exploration phase to determine potential hydrocarbon production.

Cameroon reports 45.5% BTS and 54.79% HND pass rates

The Ministry of Higher Education published official 2026 results on Aug. 19, showing a 45.50% success rate for the BTS and 54.79% for the HND. These figures contradict earlier reports of a 14% pass rate, according to the Journal du Cameroun.
