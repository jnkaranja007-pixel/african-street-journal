Douala BRT project faces CFAF104.6 billion funding gap

The Douala Urban Mobility Project requires an additional 104.6 billion CFA francs to maintain its current scope. The total cost to complete the project is estimated at 365.4 billion CFA francs, up from an initial budget of 260.8 billion CFA francs. The shortfall represents 40.1% of the original budget.

Why it mattersLack of new funding or tax relief could lead to a downsizing of the Bus Rapid Transit corridor and the removal of 66 kilometers of planned feeder roads.

Business · 2026-08-21

CAMWATER targets CFAF2.35 billion in monthly collections

Cameroon Water Utilities Corporation has set a revenue collection target of CFAF2.35 billion for Douala and Yaoundé this month. The target includes CFAF1.1 billion for Douala and CFAF1.25 billion for Yaoundé. Other regional divisions must increase collection performance by at least 15%.

Why it mattersThe state-owned utility is attempting to increase internally generated revenue to improve its financial performance.

Education · 2026-08-21

Cameroon reports 45.5% BTS and 54.79% HND pass rates

The Ministry of Higher Education announced official 2026 pass rates of 45.50% for the BTS and 54.79% for the HND. These figures contradict earlier reports of a 14% success rate. The results were released on August 19.

Why it mattersThe official figures resolve a public controversy regarding the failure rate of national technical diplomas.

Islamic Development Bank discusses 2027-2029 framework with Cameroon

The Islamic Development Bank will meet in Yaoundé on Sept. 14 to discuss a new engagement framework for 2027-2029. Cameroon's Economy Ministry stated in June 2026 that the bank's active portfolio in the country is worth nearly $1 billion.

Why it mattersThe meeting will determine the final list of projects and the financial package for the three-year partnership.

Business · 2026-08-21

Kribi Port water project reaches 95% completion

The potable water supply project for the Autonomous Port of Kribi has reached a 95% execution rate. The Ministry of Water and Energy confirmed the status on August 19. Remaining work includes technical tests and commissioning.

Why it mattersThe project is entering its final phase to provide drinking water infrastructure for the port.
