Cameroon gas assets part of US-Russia Ukraine talks

Lukoil's 30% stake in Cameroon's Etinde gas field is a bargaining chip in negotiations to end the Ukraine war.

Source: Actu Cameroun · 2026-11-09 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Cameroon's Etinde gas field has become a point of negotiation between the United States and Russia as part of broader discussions to resolve the conflict in Ukraine. Russian multinational Lukoil, facing U.S. sanctions imposed in October 2025, is reportedly discussing the divestment of its international assets, including its 30% share in the Cameroonian project.

According to a New York Times investigation, Russian President Vladimir Putin has directly raised the issue of these strategic assets, specifically the Etinde stake, in meetings with American envoys. This central African gas prospect is now being used as an economic lever to facilitate a global compromise between Moscow and Washington.

The inclusion of Cameroonian natural resources in the Ukraine crisis discussions raises questions about the nation's economic sovereignty, as operational slowdowns at the Etinde project are linked to international sanctions. Cameroonian authorities have not yet issued an official statement on the ongoing preparations for a deal between Lukoil and Washington, leaving the government with limited room to defend its industrial interests.

Why it mattersCameroon's economic sovereignty and its ability to choose energy partners are impacted by international negotiations over its gas assets.
