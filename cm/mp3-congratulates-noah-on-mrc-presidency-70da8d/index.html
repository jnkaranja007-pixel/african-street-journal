MP3 congratulates Noah on MRC presidency

The Mouvement patriotique pour la prospérité du peuple (MP3) has sent its best wishes to Tiriane Balbine Noah following her election as the new leader of the Mouvement pour la.

Source: Journal du Cameroun · 2026-10-22 · 2 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

The Mouvement patriotique pour la prospérité du peuple (MP3) on October 20 sent enthusiastic congratulations to Tiriane Balbine Noah, who was elected president of the Mouvement pour la renaissance du Cameroun (MRC) on October 17. Noah replaces Maurice Kamto and her election has prompted reactions from opposition parties. The MP3, led by Hiram Samuel Iyodi, praised Noah and the new national bureau, calling the MRC's convention a lesson in democracy for other political parties.

The MP3 expressed hope that this new chapter for the MRC will strengthen democratic pluralism, debate, and citizen participation in Cameroon. The party also highlighted a shared responsibility with the MRC to work towards a more just, free, and prosperous Cameroon where public action prioritizes the general interest. This shared goal is exemplified by both parties' refusal to recognize the official results of the October 12, 2025, presidential election, asserting the need to respect the people's will.

This shared stance and potential for collaboration could lead the two distinct parties to form strategic alliances. The upcoming legislative and municipal elections present the first opportunity for the MP3 and MRC to work together. The MP3 wishes the new MRC leadership success in continuing their predecessors' work and navigating challenges that may affect the party's influence.

Why it mattersCameroonian opposition parties, specifically the MP3 and MRC, are affected by the leadership change within the MRC and may collaborate on future political strategies.
