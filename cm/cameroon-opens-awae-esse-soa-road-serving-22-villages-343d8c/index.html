Cameroon opens Awae-Esse-Soa road serving 22 villages

The 82,252 km route links three communes to national roads and routes into the Central African Republic, with faster transport for farm produce.

Source: Journal du Cameroun · 2026-11-26 · 1 min read

Cameroon’s government officially opened the Awae-Esse-Soa road on Thursday, connecting three communes in the Mefou and Afamba department. The route serves 22 villages and links to national roads n°1, n°4 and n°10, as well as routes through the Central African Republic.

The Ministry of Public Works said the 82,252 km project cost CFA76,61 billion from the public investment budget. It was built by China First Hihgway Engineering Company Cameroon and generated 943 jobs, including 919 for nationals. The project also included 25 hand-operated boreholes along the route and four rubbish bins in Soa.

The ministry said macabo, cassava and plantain can now be transported faster to national and regional markets. The road also includes a 2x2-lane crossing through Esse and 5000 m2 of parking in Soa.

Why it mattersFarmers and traders in 22 villages can move macabo, cassava and plantain faster to national and regional markets using the opened road.
