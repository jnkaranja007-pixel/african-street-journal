Sofitoul convenes Cameroon tourism forum on private investment

The Yaoundé meeting will examine regulatory, administrative and financial barriers to tourism projects and possible incentives for investors.

Source: Business in Cameroon · 2026-11-21 · 1 min read

Travel services company Sofitoul is hosting a forum on public-private partnerships at the Hilton Hotel in Yaoundé on November 20–21, seeking ways to attract private investment to Cameroon's tourism infrastructure. The event is part of the company's 20th anniversary celebrations.

A press kit released on November 14 says government officials, project finance specialists and tourism professionals will discuss regulatory, administrative and financial obstacles to tourism projects. The forum is expected to be chaired by the Minister of Tourism and Leisure.

Participants will consider tax incentives, public guarantees and joint financing by government and private operators. They will also review financing approaches used in other countries for possible adaptation to Cameroon.

Why it mattersTourism officials, finance specialists and businesses must assess investment barriers and possible tax incentives, guarantees and joint project financing at the two-day forum.
