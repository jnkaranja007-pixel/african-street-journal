Cameroon lifts domestic share of public investment funding to 62.6%

Finance Minister Louis Paul Motaze said domestic funding rose by CFA261 billion in 2025, while external funding fell by CFA13.9 billion.

Source: Business in Cameroon · 2026-11-27 · 1 min read

Cameroon financed 62.6% of its Public Investment Budget from domestic resources in 2025, Finance Minister Louis Paul Motaze told the National Assembly’s Finance and Budget Committee on November 25. The share was 55.9% in 2024 and 46.8% in 2023, according to the Ministry of Economy, Planning and Regional Development.

Motaze said domestic funding allocated to public investment rose by CFA261 billion from 2024, while external funding declined by CFA13.9 billion. Domestic resources overtook external financing in 2024, when they accounted for more than half of the investment budget for the first time in five years, the ministry said.

The ministry linked the shift partly to a new government budget classification system and reforms begun in 2022 to change how externally financed projects are budgeted. Domestic funding also had higher spending execution rates, according to the government’s budget execution report.

Why it mattersPublic investment allocations are increasingly funded from domestic resources, which supplied 62.6% of the budget in 2025.
