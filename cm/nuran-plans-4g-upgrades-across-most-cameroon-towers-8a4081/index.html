NuRAN plans 4G upgrades across most Cameroon towers

The Canadian telecom infrastructure provider says pilot tests raised revenue at some sites by up to 40%, but has not disclosed their number or locations.

Source: Business in Cameroon · 2026-11-24 · 1 min read

NuRAN Wireless, a Canadian telecom infrastructure provider, plans to equip most of its towers in Cameroon with 4G after pilot tests reportedly lifted revenue at some sites by up to 40%. The company announced the initiative on November 12, 2026, to earn more from rural infrastructure that largely supports 2G services.

NuRAN has not said how many pilot towers recorded the increase, where they are or how long the results were measured. The company said it upgraded 2G capacity at 57 Cameroon towers with Orange over the past 18 months to handle higher mobile traffic; those works are separate from the planned 4G equipment.
