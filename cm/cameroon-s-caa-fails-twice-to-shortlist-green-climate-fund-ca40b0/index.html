Cameroon’s CAA fails twice to shortlist Green Climate Fund legal consultant

The public institution’s latest recruitment ended unsuccessfully, leaving it without a candidate for the legal opinion required in its accreditation preparations.

Source: Business in Cameroon · 2026-11-22 · 1 min read

Cameroon’s Autonomous Sinking Fund (CAA) has failed for a second time in three months to shortlist a consultant for a legal opinion needed in its preparations for Green Climate Fund accreditation. The public institution declared the latest recruitment unsuccessful in a notice signed on November 13 and published four days later by the Public Procurement Regulatory Agency.

The CAA’s previous attempt produced the same result on August 14. Its latest call for expressions of interest, issued on September 21, covered two assignments: an institutional integrity policy and complaints mechanism, and an external opinion on whether the fund has the capacity to act as an accredited entity.

The legal review was intended to assess the CAA’s administrative and financial autonomy. The institution remains without a selected candidate for that assignment.

Why it mattersCAA officials must continue preparations without a selected legal consultant after the second failed shortlisting attempt in three months.
