Cameroon's deposits fund gives Afriland eight days to pay CFA141.96 billion

The demand covers principal, interest and penalties tied to a dispute involving MTN Cameroon and companies linked to businessman Baba Ahmadou Danpullo.

Source: Business in Cameroon · 2026-11-21 · 1 min read

Cameroon's Deposits and Consignments Fund (CDEC) has given Afriland First Bank eight days from receipt of its November 18 demand to transfer CFA141.96 billion tied to a dispute involving companies linked to businessman Baba Ahmadou Danpullo and MTN Cameroon. CDEC Director General Richard Evina Obam warned the bank that compulsory debt recovery proceedings would follow if it misses the deadline.

The demand comprises CFA126.63 billion in principal and CFA15.33 billion in interest and penalties. The funds relate to precautionary seizures in September 2022 on accounts associated with MTN Cameroon and Mobile Money Corporation, among others, sought by Danpullo-linked companies operating as Bestinver.

The Douala-Bonanjo Court of First Instance ordered Afriland in November 2023 to set aside CFA126,629,176,833 immediately, with a CFA50 million daily penalty for delay. The CDEC demand concerns the bank's handling of funds subject to that order.

Why it mattersAfriland's handling of the CFA141.96 billion, including MTN Cameroon and Mobile Money Corporation accounts, now faces compulsory recovery proceedings after the eight-day deadline.
