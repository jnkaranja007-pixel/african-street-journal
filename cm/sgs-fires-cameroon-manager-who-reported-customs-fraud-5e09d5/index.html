SGS fires Cameroon manager who reported customs fraud

A Swiss inspection group says its internal investigation found employees who allegedly altered duty calculations and issued fake invoices to move money abroad.

Source: Business in Cameroon · 2026-11-16 · 1 min read

SGS, the Swiss inspection and certification group, says an internal investigation at its Cameroon subsidiary uncovered customs fraud. Employees allegedly manipulated duty calculations and issued fake invoices to move funds abroad without importing any goods.

The case began in May 2023, when a whistleblower alerted the group to possible breaches of its Code of Integrity. SGS sent members of its corporate security team to Cameroon, who interviewed employees and reviewed documents, identifying several staff suspected of the misconduct.

The allegations are set out in a November 28, 2025, response to Switzerland's National Contact Point for Responsible Business Conduct, reproduced in a report dated May 5, 2026. The dispute involves a former manager at the subsidiary who was fired after reporting the suspected misconduct.

Why it mattersInvestors dealing with SGS Cameroon face a company under scrutiny over alleged fraud, with its handling of the whistleblower now under review.
