Sports betting models show varying predictions

Two models can offer different win probabilities for the same match, with differences explained by calculation methods and data used.

Source: Journal du Cameroun · 2026-10-31 · 1 min read

WhatsApp X Telegram

Sports betting models can present significantly different win probabilities for the same match, with one model giving a team 55% chance of victory while another shows only 42%. These discrepancies do not necessarily indicate a faulty model, but can stem from varied calculation methods, data inputs, and update timings.

The reliability of a prediction depends on the input data, ranging from simple past results to more complex metrics like shots, chance quality, team lineups, and expected goals (xG). The xG indicator, for example, measures the probability of a shot resulting in a goal based on the chance's characteristics, with Opta's model using nearly one million historical shots.

A trustworthy model's performance should be verifiable against past matches using pre-game probabilities, with evaluations gaining relevance over hundreds of games. The publication date of a probability is crucial, distinguishing pre-match estimates from those recalculated after the final whistle and definitive statistics.

Why it mattersCameroonian sports bettors may encounter differing odds for the same match due to variations in prediction models.
