Cameroon hosts African customs intelligence workshop

Yaoundé is hosting a week of geospatial intelligence training for 28 customs participants from 14 countries, opened by Cameroon's customs director general.

Source: Journal du Cameroun · 2026-11-17 · 1 min read

Yaoundé is hosting the third customs master-trainer workshop on geospatial intelligence (GEOINT) from 16 to 20 November 2026. Edwin Nuvaga, Cameroon's director general of customs, opened the sessions on Monday 16 November.

The workshop is run by the World Customs Organization and Japan's International Cooperation Agency (JICA), and brings 28 participants from 14 countries together with eight experts from the United States, France, Japan, Uzbekistan and the Netherlands. Cameroon is represented by Captain Hugues Amba Bontsebe and Warrant Officer Caeli Ami Ndula. Nuvaga told the gathering that the customs security mission is now as imperative as its fiscal mission, given terrorism and organised crime.

The sessions will end on Friday with a roadmap for embedding GEOINT in national border security strategies. Earlier editions were held in Nairobi in February and Dakar in May.

Why it mattersCustoms trainers from 14 countries will return home to teach satellite imagery and spatial analysis to border staff, with a roadmap due on Friday.
